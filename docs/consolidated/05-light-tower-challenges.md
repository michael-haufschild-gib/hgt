## 4. The honest verdict

The light tower is a **coherent research map, not a proof** (its own §0, §8). Consolidating the
challenges:

1. **The proven supports are BRIDGE** — they reduce to classical mathematics; the native content
   is uniformly the open core. The map is correct about *where* the wall is, not yet *across* it.
2. **The experimental supports are untested against the surrogate ladder** (synthesis §4) — until
   the refined statistics separate genuine HT `Λ` from an FE-exact correct-zeros fake, they support
   "analytic ≠ truncation," not native HT.
3. **The wall is tool-independent** — orthogonality (no Source-B positivity), affine confinement
   (no flow crossing), the DSS no-go (no native lower bound), and the ~10-names reduction all say
   the *same* thing: RH-positivity is the one archimedean direction, and no prime-side / flow /
   variance / measured object reaches it.
4. **The genuine open cores are two, joined only at the seam:** Schanuel-for-prime-logs (value
   axis) and `κ₋=0` / uniform-in-`d` Hankel positivity (RH axis). `ω=0 ⇏ RH` keeps them distinct.

None of this falsifies the light tower's *picture*; it sharply bounds what the picture has so far
*earned*. The decisive next action the corpus names is the **§4 surrogate-ladder experiment**,
which adjudicates LT1/LT4/LT5/LT7 in one stroke.

## Appendix — The no-go acceptance standard (incorporated from the GPT consolidation pass)

The corpus's no-goes are best read not as scattered refutations but as a single **acceptance
standard** a future bridge object must meet. Each pattern below is a way a candidate route fails; the
conjunction is the bar.

- `[NO-GO]` **Measured positivity is too weak.** Any route proving only density, type-II trace
  positivity, averaged positivity, or finite-statistical regularity can miss dust off the line. RH
  needs a *faithful signed index* (`κ₋`), not a density.
- `[NO-GO]` **Margin proofs are mis-shaped.** RH is a zero-margin statement; a proof requiring a
  hidden positive gap, uniform slack, or domination reserve is attacking a stronger (likely false)
  object.
- `[NO-GO]` **Equidistribution-only / Baker-(log-independence)-only routes are transverse-blind.**
  They do not produce the quadratic Weil response or the faithful signature needed to see off-line
  pairs.
- `[NO-GO]` **Raw pole + archimedean-reference positivity is false-shaped.** The completed
  distribution does **not** split into independently positive prime and archimedean pieces;
  positivity, if true, is a *completed kernel / polarization* fact.
- `[NO-GO]` **Positive-residue Herglotz is the wrong carrier.** The live target is indefinite
  Hermite–Biehler / generalized Nevanlinna / Krein–Langer control with the correct completed carrier.
- `[NO-GO]` **Generic de Branges positivity is only a restatement unless source-built.** Naming a
  Hilbert space or positivity criterion does not cross the wall; the source construction and
  fake-family separation are the content.
- `[NO-GO]` **Eisenstein / scattering analogies are doubled or boundary-contaminated** unless a
  Stokes or boundary correction is named.
- `[NO-GO]` **Finite Jacobi / membrane positivity can be fake-blind** — both ζ and Davenport–Heilbronn
  may pass at finite depth; finite gates are falsifiers/shadows, not proof.

> **The standard (the conjunction):** a light-tower invariant or bridge object must be **faithful,
> all-level, completed, source-built, and fake-family separating**. This is the bar `09-preprint-draft.md`
> §5 and `03-theorems-toward-rh.md` (Appendix) hold any candidate to.
