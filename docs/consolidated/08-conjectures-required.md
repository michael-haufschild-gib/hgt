## §1 — The carrier-synthesis conjectures C1–C6 (synthesis §3)

Each bundles several `[OBSERVED]` catalog items into one statement and names the experiment that
adjudicates it and the observation that falsifies it.

**C1 — The carrier is a single *oriented 2-form*, not a scalar. `[CONJECTURE]`**

projections of `∬ω`.
- *Kill:* if the sign reversal is reproduced by any **FE-exact / correct-zero surrogate**, `ω` is
  FE/zero content, not HT.

**C2 — The carrier is the *holonomy of the affine weld connection*, trivialized on `σ=½` by the
Γ-cocycle. `[CONJECTURE]` (the load-bearing synthesis).**
The weld phase carrier **is** the holonomy / 1-cocycle of the flat-curvature-modulo-universal affine
`(∂_s, M_p)` connection; the Γ-completion is the **additive boundary cocycle** that trivializes the

- *Kill:* if the "holonomy" is just `∮ d arg = 2πi(#zeros−#poles)` (argument principle), it reduces;
  the conjecture **requires** the carrier to be the **non-integer / continuous** part, orthogonal to
  the winding number.

**C3 — Long-run coherence = the H¹/Sobolev regularity gap of one meromorphic phase. `[CONJECTURE]`**
The run-length tail and edge-softness are monotone functionals of a **phase-Sobolev seminorm**
`‖∂_t arg(Γ∞+P)‖`; the true-vs-finite gap equals the gap in that seminorm; one regularity functional
**predicts all of Group B**.
- *Kill:* if the regularity gap is reproduced by a **smooth amplitude-matched non-HT analytic
  surrogate**, Group B is "analytic ≠ truncated," a known fact.

**C4 — The weld is 2-D rigid: a divergent cross-lane correlation length `ξ_σ`. `[CONJECTURE]`**
The true carrier has a cross-σ correlation length `ξ_σ(σ,t)` **large/divergent in the critical
strip, `O(1)` at `σ>1`**, because `∂_s` (Cauchy–Riemann) couples neighbouring σ-lanes while finite
Euler lanes factorize per-lane. The sheets and σ–t spanning are the same divergent `ξ_σ`.
- *Kill:* if `ξ_σ` is **set by zero-spacing alone**, it reduces to zero-statistics.

**C5 — A genuine crossover scale `σ* ∈ (½,1)` — a "critical temperature" of the weld. `[CONJECTURE]`**
There is a sharp crossover `σ*` (coherence alive→dead), possibly tied to the
`Σ_p p^{−2σ}~loglog`-marginality region or `σ* = 3/4`; the transition is **non-analytic in the
statistic** (a kink, not a smooth fade).
- *Kill:* if the fade is smooth/analytic with no distinguished `σ*`, this is just continuity of the
  Dirichlet series.

**C6 — The whole program is one inverse problem: realize `Γ∞+ζ'/ζ` from primes + a boundary
cocycle. `[CONJECTURE]` (= C2 as an inverse problem; the native frontier of `𝓜⁺`).**

counterterm / no scalar `c_N` / no order-commuting limit" certify it nontrivial and nonlocal.

  genuine HT obstruction (it would be differential-algebraic, contradicting Hölder — so this
