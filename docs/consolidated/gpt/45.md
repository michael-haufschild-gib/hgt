## What is clearly not new

The following are mostly **known SOTA or classical material under our language**.

### 1. “RH is positivity”

This is absolutely not new. RH as Weil positivity, Hilbert–Pólya/de Branges-style positivity, Laguerre–Pólya/Jensen hyperbolicity, Hankel or moment positivity, and faithful Krein-index positivity is a very old and heavily studied family of equivalences. The corpus itself calls these “one wall, many names” and says naming such an equivalence is not progress unless the object is source-built.

### 2. Jensen/Pólya route

The Jensen-polynomial story is SOTA, not ours. Pólya’s old criterion says RH is equivalent to hyperbolicity of all relevant Jensen polynomials; Griffin–Ono–Rolen–Zagier proved strong fixed-degree/density-one/fixed-degree results and established hyperbolicity for all (d\le 8), but the uniform-in-(d) statement remains RH-level. ([arXiv][1])

So when we say “uniform Hankel/Jensen apex,” that is not a new route. It is a restatement of a known frontier.

### 3. Connes–Consani / archimedean positivity

The corpus correctly flags that our “source-built faithful-index object” language overlaps strongly with the Connes–Consani programme. Their arithmetic-site / Riemann–Roch / Jacobian / semilocal trace-formula / prolate-operator work is precisely the kind of archimedean-positive, source-built machinery our language keeps circling around. Connes–Consani’s arithmetic site appeared in Adv. Math. 2016, their Riemann–Roch for (\mathrm{Spec},\mathbb Z) is listed as 2023, their prolate wave operator paper is 2023/2024, and their “Jacobian of (\overline{\mathrm{Spec},\mathbb Z})” preprint appeared in 2026. ([Alain Connes][2])

So if we say “the likely proof object is archimedean, source-built, positivity/polarization, not prime-flow,” that is very much aligned with existing SOTA, not a private discovery.

### 4. Uvarov / Christoffel / Sherman–Morrison / boundary triple algebra

The identities

[
s_d=a_*+\frac1{m_d},
\qquad
s_d=\frac1{K_{\nu'}(y_*,y_*)},
\qquad
s_d=-\frac{\det H^0_d}{\det B_d}

are not deep discoveries by themselves. Once the rank-one split

[

is written down, Sherman–Morrison, Schur complements, Uvarov formulas, and Christoffel kernels give these identities mechanically.

The **algebra is standard**. What may be less standard is the particular way it is being attached to the reciprocal-seam zero divisor and the first Γ-trivial atom.

### 5. “Bare primes are not enough”

Also not new. The explicit formula is distributional and completed; trying to approximate the RH-positive object with raw prime truncations is known to be structurally dangerous. Your comp73 did not discover a new theorem; it confirmed the expected failure mode:

[

That is useful calibration, not new theory.
