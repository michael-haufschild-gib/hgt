## II. What Is Still Blocking Route FZZ-P?

Despite these advances, three tightly linked structural blockers still prevent closing RH via FZZ-P:

### Blocker 1 (Producer Side): The Cutoff $t = 1/(N+1)$ Head-vs-Tail Dilemma & Truncated Möbius Leakage
Why can't we combine `FermiNymanWitnessGermRigidity` (which says $\{\varphi_a\}$ is dense on $(\delta, 1]$) with the Möbius/log-Möbius approximants?
1. **Local bulk approximants blow up on the tail:** Approximating $h$ on $(\delta, 1]$ by step combinations of $\varphi_a$ ($a \le N$) gives zero error on $(1/(N+1), 1]$, which is exactly what the **raw Möbius approximant** $c_d = -\mu(d)$ does (`FermiNymanRawMobius`). On $(1/(N+1), 1]$, $\sum_{d \le N} \mu(d)\lfloor 1/(dt)\rfloor = \sum_{m \le 1/t} \sum_{d \mid m} \mu(d) = 1$, so the head residual vanishes identically!
2. **Why raw Möbius fails on the tail $(0, 1/(N+1)]$:** For $t < 1/(N+1)$, the integer $m = \lfloor 1/t \rfloor > N$ has divisors $d > N$ that are **missing** from the truncated sum $\sum_{d \le N} \mu(d)\lfloor 1/(dt)\rfloor$. As proven in `FermiNymanRawMobiusTailFloor`, this causes the tail integral $\int_0^{1/(N+1)} |h - \Phi_N^{\text{raw}}|^2\,dt$ to be bounded *below* by $\frac{N+1}{6} H_N^2$ ($H_N = \sum_{d \le N} \mu(d)/d$), hitting Wall 1 ($H_N = o(N^{-1/2})$).
3. **Why log-Möbius ($c_d = -\mu(d)\frac{\log(N/d)}{\log N}$) trades tail blowup for Chebyshev head error without closing either:**
   - Smoothing $\mu(d)$ by $\frac{\log(N/d)}{\log N}$ kills the top-octave harmonic shadow ($H_N^{\log} \to 0$ unconditionally by PNT) and softens the cutoff at $d = N$.
   - However, on the **bulk head** $(1/(N+1), 1]$, replacing $\mu(d)$ by $\mu(d)\frac{\log(N/d)}{\log N}$ turns the exact identity $\sum_{d \mid m} \mu(d) = \delta_{m,1}$ into $-\sum_{d \mid m} \mu(d)\log d = \Lambda(m)$, introducing the non-zero Chebyshev head residual $\frac{1}{(\log N)^2} \int_{1/(N+1)}^1 (2\psi(1/(2t)) - \psi(1/t))^2\,dt$ ([`logMobiusFermiHeadEnergy_eq_chebyshev`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean#L254-L266)).
   - Worse still, on the **origin tail** $(0, 1/(N+1)]$ ($x = 1/t > N+1$), the log-Möbius sum evaluates to the **truncated Selberg/divisor sum**
     $$\sum_{m \le x} \Bigl(\sum_{\substack{d \mid m \\ d \le N}} \mu(d)\frac{\log(N/d)}{\log N}\Bigr),$$
     where for $m > N$ the inner divisor sum is restricted to $d \le N$ and no longer equals $\Lambda(m)$! Neither `RHTower` nor `BiScaleUncertainty` currently has an upper bound on this $t < 1/(N+1)$ tail integral (or on the off-diagonal Báez-Duarte / Franel integral $\int_0^{1/(N+1)} \tilde{\varphi}_a(t)\tilde{\varphi}_b(t)\,dt$).

### Blocker 2 (Gram / Shear Side): Truncated Measure $dt$ on $(0, 1]$ vs. Periodic Franel Measure, and Sign-Sensitive Off-Diagonal Cancellation
1. **Measure mismatch at the $t = 1$ boundary:**
   - In [`FoldFreeKernelGramAndFranelGcdCancellation.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/FoldFreeKernelGramAndFranelGcdCancellation.lean), the exact GCD formula $\langle \psi_a, \psi_b \rangle = \frac{\gcd(a,b)^2}{12ab}$ holds for $x \in [0, 1]$ with uniform measure $dx$, which under $x = 1/t$ corresponds to $\int_0^\infty \{1/(at)\}\{1/(bt)\}\,dt$ weighted by $t^{-2}$, **not** the FZZ-P inner product $\int_0^1 \varphi_a(t)\varphi_b(t)\,dt = \int_1^\infty \varphi_a(1/x)\varphi_b(1/x)\,\frac{dx}{x^2}$.
   - On $L^2((0,1], dt)$, the inner product $\langle \varphi_a, \varphi_b \rangle_{L^2(0,1]}$ is the classic Báez-Duarte / Vasyunin correlation, which contains non-periodic boundary/harmonic terms from the cutoff at $x = 1$ alongside the $\gcd(a,b)$ arithmetic core.
2. **Lack of sign-sensitive off-diagonal cancellation in the Abel shear sum:**
   - In [`CoprimeShearArithmeticWeightsAndChebyshevCellIncrement.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/CoprimeShearArithmeticWeightsAndChebyshevCellIncrement.lean#L291-L303), we reduced the Nyquist-cell Chebyshev sum $\sum_{n=1}^N \frac{(\psi(n)-n)^2}{n(n+1)}$ to the linear-times-increment cross term:
     $$2 \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \frac{(\Lambda(k+2)-1)\bigl(\psi(k+1)-(k+1)\bigr)}{k+2} + \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \frac{(\Lambda(k+2)-1)^2}{k+2}.$$
   - Look closely at the diagonal square term: $\sum_{k \le N} \frac{\Lambda(k)^2}{k} \sim \frac{1}{2}(\log N)^2$ by PNT! Yet under RH, the entire left-hand side $\sum_{n=1}^N \frac{(\psi(n)-n)^2}{n(n+1)}$ is $O(\log N)$ (or $o((\log N)^2)$ for FZZ-P).
   - **What this means mathematically:** The bilinear cross-term $2\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \frac{(\Lambda(k+2)-1)(\psi(k+1)-(k+1))}{k+2}$ is **not merely small—it is asymptotically negative of size $-\frac{1}{2}(\log N)^2$**, cancelling the diagonal $\sum \frac{\Lambda(k)^2}{k}$! Every existing tool in `BiScaleUncertainty` (Cauchy–Schwarz, Bessel, Krein compression, Sobolev clamps) bounds inner products by their absolute values, which turns $-\frac{1}{2}(\log N)^2$ into $+\frac{1}{2}(\log N)^2$ and destroys the exact cancellation between the prime jump $\Lambda(n+1)-1$ and the accumulated error $\psi(n)-n$.

## III. What Is Missing in `BiScaleUncertainty` to Overcome These Blockers

To bridge the remaining gap and close FZZ-P, `BiScaleUncertainty` needs **four specific positive mathematical components** that go beyond receiver floors:

### 1. The Vasyunin / Báez-Duarte Gram Matrix on $L^2((0,1], dt)$ and Selberg Sieve Tail Weights
- **What is missing:** Our Franel Gram module (`FoldFreeKernelGramAndFranelGcdCancellation.lean`) computes $\langle \psi_a, \psi_b \rangle$ on the periodic torus $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$. In $L^2((0,1], dt)$, the exact inner product $\langle \tilde{\varphi}_a, \tilde{\varphi}_b \rangle_{L^2(0,1]}$ is governed by Vasyunin's cotangent/reciprocity formula.
- **Why it is needed for Blocker 1:** In the Selberg $\Lambda^2$-sieve, one chooses weights $\lambda_d$ ($d \le N$) to minimize the quadratic form
  $$\sum_{m \ge 1} w_m \Bigl(\sum_{\substack{d \mid m \\ d \le N}} \lambda_d\Bigr)^2.$$
  The small-$t$ origin tail $\int_0^{1/(N+1)} |h(t) - \sum_{d \le N} c_d \varphi_d(t)|^2\,dt$ is **literally** a Selberg $\Lambda^2$-sieve quadratic form over $m = \lfloor 1/t \rfloor > N$ with weight $w_m = \frac{1}{m(m+1)}$! Formalizing the Selberg diagonalization (via Möbius inversion on $\gcd(a,b)$, extending our `FoldFreeKernelGramAndFranelGcdCancellation` framework from $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ to $\sum_{m > N} \frac{1}{m(m+1)} \mathbf{1}_{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b) \mid m}$) is the exact tool required to bound the $t < 1/(N+1)$ tail from above.

### 2. Sign-Sensitive Martingale / Mean-Reversion Identity for the Abel–Chebyshev Cross Term
- **What is missing:** In [`nyquistCellWeightedSum_eq_abel_vonMangoldt_shear`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/CoprimeShearArithmeticWeightsAndChebyshevCellIncrement.lean#L291-L303), we proved:
  $$\sum_{n=1}^N \frac{E(n)^2}{n(n+1)} = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \frac{2\delta_{k+2} E(k+1) + \delta_{k+2}^2}{k+2} - \frac{E(N+1)^2}{N+1}, \qquad E(n) := \psi(n)-n,\; \delta_m := \Lambda(m)-1.$$
  Notice that $\delta_{k+2} = E(k+2) - E(k+1)$, so the numerator is $E(k+2)^2 - E(k+1)^2$! When we expand $E(k+1) = -\sum_{d \le k+1} \mu(d)\log d \lfloor \frac{k+1}{d} \rfloor - (k+1)$ and $\delta_{k+2} = -\sum_{d' \mid (k+2)} \mu(d')\log d' - 1$, the off-diagonal sum over $d, d'$ couples divisors of $k+2$ to floor quotients at $k+1$.
- **Why it is needed for Blocker 2:** Because $\gcd(k+1, k+2) = 1$, for every $d' \mid (k+2)$ ($d' > 1$), the residue $k+1 \equiv -1 \pmod{d'}$ is **maximally negative** ($\{(k+1)/d'\} = 1 - 1/d'$)! That systematic residue bias $\{ (k+1)/d' \} - 1/2 = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{d'} > 0$ at every divisor of $k+2$ is the exact arithmetic reason why the jump $\Lambda(k+2)$ anti-correlates with the sawtooth error in $\psi(k+1)-(k+1)$ and produces the $-\frac{1}{2}(\log N)^2$ cancellation against $\sum \frac{\Lambda(k)^2}{k}$. Formalizing this **coprime residue anti-correlation identity** ($\{(n-1)/d\} = 1 - 1/d$ for $d \mid n$) in `BiScaleUncertainty` is the missing link between coprime shear and sign-sensitive upper bounds.

### 3. Non-Multiplicative / Additive Perturbation of the Witness Equation to Break $\zeta(\rho) = 0$
- **What is missing:** In `FermiNymanWitnessGermLaws` and `FermiNymanWitnessContinuationRigidity`, every witness equation $\langle w, \varphi_a \rangle = 0$ is purely multiplicative ($t \mapsto at$), so its Mellin transform factors as $a^{-s} \cdot \Phi(s)$ and is annihilated whenever $\zeta(1-z) = 0$.
