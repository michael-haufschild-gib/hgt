## "The same theorem also proves that even the full derived topology is blind to orientation surgery."

It means an RH proof cannot get the critical line from the unpolarized algebraic/derived object alone.

More precisely, the surgery family keeps all of this fixed:
- the generated differential field;
- every finite differential relation;
- the exact constraint ideal;
- every syzygy, Tor group, and derived/cohomological boundary class.

Yet it changes the signed residue/Krein orientation through every finite negative index. So any argument whose decisive invariant factors only through that fixed derived topology is incapable of distinguishing “positive” from “indefinite.” It may explain functional-equation symmetry, multiplicity, continuation obstruction, or a symmetric degeneracy set—but it cannot force the degeneracies onto the self-dual locus.

This does not show RH is impossible, and the surgery sources are not deformations of zeta. It is a control theorem for proof architecture. A viable RH proof needs an additional source-canonical datum that the surgery changes, such as a signed Hermitian polarization, an arithmetic order/positive cone, or an Euler–archimedean coupling that uniquely selects the zero-negative-index chamber.

The sharp audit for any proposed RH proof is: "If I apply an orientation surgery that preserves its entire algebraic and derived input, which exact step of the proof changes?"
-> If no step changes, the argument cannot prove an orientation-sensitive conclusion such as critical-line confinement. If one step changes, that step—not the derived topology—is the real RH-bearing theorem.

The positive research target is therefore very specific: construct the zeta object before its zeros, prove that its prime–archimedean source coupling canonically selects a positive polarization, and prove that the relevant analytic/spectral realization is conservative for that polarized object. Then self-duality supplies symmetry and the new positivity theorem could supply fixed-line confinement. Without that selector, different RH chains can indeed be reaching the same unpolarized boundary and failing for the same structural reason.
