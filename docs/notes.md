BiScaleUncertainty and Wall 1
BiScale's tools (Krein compression, Nyquist packing, Bessel/√-cancellation, producer–consumer duality) all produce receiver floors. In fold language they are instances of the fold–Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, which bounds the error from below. The fold shows the only non-arithmetic obstruction is the one-dimensional self-similar shadow, already matched by the Haar target. What remains is the ker P error, which for the log-Möbius approximant is the mean square of the Chebyshev dyadic difference 2ψ(x/2) − ψ(x) in dx/x². That is Wall 1 exactly, and no fold, compression, or Bessel argument can bound it from above.

### 1. What Went Wrong? (The Directionality Inversion: Consumer Floors vs. Producer Upper Bounds)

The core issue is that **`BiScaleUncertainty` built tools on the *consumer* side of Cauchy–Schwarz (which bound errors from below), whereas overcoming Wall 1 requires bounding an arithmetic residual from *above*.**

In both the FZZ-P / Nyman–Beurling setting ([`FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean)) and the Weil / `BiScaleUncertainty` setting ([`ProducerConsumerDualityAndNymanBeurlingBridge.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/ProducerConsumerDualityAndNymanBeurlingBridge.lean)), the bridge between test functions ("consumers" / "witnesses" $w$) and arithmetic approximants ("producers" $\sum c_n g_n$ for a target $p$) is the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality ([`consumer_polar_sq_le_producer_error`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/ProducerConsumerDualityAndNymanBeurlingBridge.lean#L104-L111), [`norm_sq_fold_pairing_le`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean#L592-L604)):

$$\underbrace{\frac{|\langle w, p - \sum c_n g_n \rangle|^2}{\|w\|^2}}_{\text{Consumer / Fold Floor}} \;\le\; \underbrace{\left\| p - \sum_{n} c_n g_n \right\|^2}_{\text{Producer Error Energy}}.$$

Look at what happened on both sides of the correspondence:

1. **On the FZZ-P / Fold Side (`ker P` vs. Self-Similar Shadow):**
   - The dyadic fold $(Pf)(u) = \sum_{j \ge 0} 2^{-j} f(2^{-j}u)$ splits $L^2(0,1] = \ker P \oplus \mathcal{S}$, where $\mathcal{S}$ is the space of dyadically self-similar functions ($w(t/2) = w(t)$).
   - Every alternating generator $\varphi_a(t) = \{1/(at)\} - 2\{1/(2at)\}$ folds to a 1D self-similar shadow $(P\varphi_a)(u) = -\frac{1}{au}$ ([`hasSum_dyadicFold_fermiNymanGen`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean#L145-L208)), while the Haar target $h = -\mathbf{1}_{(0,1/2]} + \mathbf{1}_{(1/2,1]}$ has $Ph = 0$, so $h \in \ker P$ ([`dyadicFold_dyadicHaar`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean#L493-L494)).
   - Testing against the self-similar shadow gives the **harmonic penalty lower bound** ([`dyadicHaar_approximation_harmonic_penalty`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean#L758-L772)):
     $$\int_0^1 \left|h(t) - \sum_j c_j \varphi_{a_j}(t)\right|^2 dt \;\ge\; \frac{1}{2}\left|\sum_j \frac{c_j}{a_j}\right|^2.$$
   - For the **raw Möbius approximant** $c_d = -\mu(d)$ ([`FermiNymanRawMobiusTailFloor.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanRawMobiusTailFloor.lean)), the head error on $(1/(N+1), 1]$ is zero ([`rawMobiusFermiApprox_eq_dyadicHaar`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanRawMobiusTailFloor.lean#L200-L206)), but the uncancelled harmonic shadow $H_N = \sum_{d \le N} \mu(d)/d$ creates a tail floor $\ge \frac{N+1}{6}H_N^2$ ([`rawMobiusFermiErrorEnergy_lower_bound`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanRawMobiusTailFloor.lean#L302-L313)).
   - Passing to the **logarithmically smoothed Möbius approximant** $c_d = -\mu(d)\frac{\log(N/d)}{\log N}$ ([`FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean#L89-L92)) cancels the $1/t$ harmonic shadow so the error lies in $\ker P$. Once the shadow is matched, every fold/Bessel/Krein lower bound gives $0$, and what remains on $(1/(N+1), 1]$ is the exact $\ker P$ head error ([`logMobiusFermiErrorEnergy_decomposition`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean#L193-L199)):
     $$\frac{1}{(\log N)^2} \int_{1/(N+1)}^1 \bigl(2\psi(1/(2t)) - \psi(1/t)\bigr)^2\,dt \;=\; \frac{1}{(\log N)^2} \int_1^{N+1} \bigl(2\psi(x/2) - \psi(x)\bigr)^2 \frac{dx}{x^2}.$$

2. **On the Weil / `BiScaleUncertainty` Side (Wrong Sign & Gauge Reduction to the Same Integral):**
   - **Sign mismatch in Nyquist collar bounds:** In [`global_vonMangoldt_all_scale_signed_coercivity`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/VonMangoldtWeilExplicitFormulaAndChebyshevBridge.lean#L284-L290), Krein's increment inequality and the $O(n^{-3/2})$ origin-budget telescoping prove:

     Here the prime-power sum enters with a **$+$ sign** (and is suppressed by the cell width $\Delta \log n \asymp 1/n$), whereas the actual Weil explicit-formula functional requires bounding $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{arch}}(f) - 2\sum_{n \ge 2} \frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt{n}} h_f(\log n)$ with a **$-$ sign** from below.
   - **Gauge representation lands on Wall 1:** When [`HyperbolicGaugeChebyshevEnergyRepresentation.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/HyperbolicGaugeChebyshevEnergyRepresentation.lean#L224-L284) and [`HyperbolicGaugeMeanSquareChebyshevWeilSynthesis.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/HyperbolicGaugeMeanSquareChebyshevWeilSynthesis.lean#L135-L186) bound the actual signed prime–polar packet $P_{f,N}(u) = \sum_{n \le N+2} \frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt{n}} f(u+\log n) - \int_0^L 2\cosh(t/2)f(u+t)\,dt$, Stieltjes integration by parts reduces the packet energy upper bound (`smoothGaugeBound`) to:

     That integral $\int_1^{N+2} \frac{(\psi(x)-x)^2}{x^2}\,dx$ (or its discrete Nyquist-cell form [`discreteChebyshevCellSum`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/HyperbolicGaugeMeanSquareChebyshevWeilSynthesis.lean#L62-L65)) is the exact same Wall 1 Chebyshev mean-square integral.

### 2. What Is the Blocker Here?

The blocker is twofold: **analytic equivalence to RH** and **structural blindness of Hilbert-space/positivity tools to the arithmetic of $\mathbb{Z}$**.

1. **The Integral *Is* the Square-Root Barrier (Wall 1):**
   In $2\psi(x/2) - \psi(x) = 2(\psi(x/2) - x/2) - (\psi(x) - x)$, the linear main term $x$ cancels identically, leaving only the prime-counting error.
   - **If RH holds:** $|2\psi(x/2) - \psi(x)| \asymp x^{1/2}$ in mean square, so the integrand $(2\psi(x/2) - \psi(x))^2 / x^2$ has size $\frac{(x^{1/2})^2}{x^2} = \frac{1}{x}$. Its integral over $[1, N]$ is $O(\log N)$, so dividing by $(\log N)^2$ gives $O(1/\log N) \to 0$.
   - **If an off-line zero $\rho = \beta + i\gamma$ ($\beta > 1/2$) exists:** Since $2^{1-\rho} - 1 \ne 0$ for $\beta \in (1/2, 1)$, the zero contributes $\Omega(x^\beta)$ to $2\psi(x/2) - \psi(x)$. The integrand is $\asymp x^{2\beta - 2}$, and the integral grows like $N^{2\beta - 1} \gg (\log N)^2$.
   - **Unconditionally:** The best known zero-free regions (Vinogradov–Korobov) only give savings of $\exp(-c(\log x)^{3/5})$, leaving the integral exponentially larger than $(\log N)^2$.

2. **No Test Function Is Left to Vary (And Beurling Countermodels Satisfy the Axioms):**
   - Once Stieltjes integration by parts or finite Möbius floor inversion ([`sum_moebius_log_div_mul_floor`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean#L67-L86)) isolates $\int_1^N \frac{(2\psi(x/2)-\psi(x))^2}{x^2}\,dx$, **there is no test function $f$ left inside the integral**. It is a fixed deterministic number. Functional analysis on $f$ (Krein's inequality, Schur complements, Bessel projections, Nyquist cell partitioning) can only manipulate the $f$-dependent prefactor $\|f' - f/2\|_1^2$, never the growth rate of the Chebyshev integral itself.
   - Worse, any argument that only uses $0 \le \Lambda(n) \le \log n$ (`vonMangoldtLogRatio_le_one`), multiplicative divisibility $m \mid n \implies \log n - \log m = \log(n/m)$, and Stieltjes integration by parts applies verbatim to **Beurling generalized number systems** ([`BeurlingRegularIntegersOffLineZeros`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/docs/reports/walls-ledger.md#L48-L50)) and **non-tempered shifted zeta pairs** $\zeta(s-a)\zeta(s+a)$ ([`NonTemperedShiftedZetaPairCountermodel`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/docs/reports/walls-ledger.md#L273-L281)), where off-line zeros exist and the integral diverges as $N^{2\beta - 1}$.

### 3. What Needs to Happen to Get That Bound from Above?

To bound the $\ker P$ error (or the signed prime–polar discrepancy) from above, a new mechanism cannot just repackage Cauchy–Schwarz, Bessel's inequality, or Stieltjes integration by parts on test functions. One of the following must happen:

1. **Exploit Non-Orthogonal Gram Cancellation in $\ker P$ (Rather than Fixed Log-Möbius Coefficients):**
   - In [`FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanLogMobiusHead.lean), the log-Möbius approximant fixes the coefficients $c_d = -\mu(d)\frac{\log(N/d)}{\log N}$ *a priori*, which forces the head error to be the point-by-point Chebyshev difference $(2\psi(x/2)-\psi(x))/\log N$.
   - However, RH only requires that the **infimum** over all coefficients $c_d$ in the span of the fold-free generators $\tilde{\varphi}_d = \varphi_d - P^*(P\varphi_d) \in \ker P$ tends to zero ([`riemannHypothesis_iff_foldFree_approximable`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean#L498-L516)).
   - While `BiScaleUncertainty` only extracted diagonal/clique Bessel floors from orthogonal dyadic cosets $d = m \cdot 2^k$, the fold-free generators $\tilde{\varphi}_a, \tilde{\varphi}_b$ have a rich **off-diagonal Gram matrix** $\langle \tilde{\varphi}_a, \tilde{\varphi}_b \rangle_{L^2(0,1]}$ governed by classical Franel/gcd sawtooth correlations $\int_0^1 \{1/(at)\}\{1/(bt)\}\,dt$, which depend on $\gcd(a,b)$ and Bézout's identity in $(\mathbb{Z}, +, \times)$. Getting an upper bound on the minimum-norm projection in $\ker P$ requires bounding the quadratic form of this full gcd-Gram matrix from above (or its inverse from below on $\ker P$), rather than restricting to orthogonal cliques where cross-coset cancellation is discarded.

2. **Couple Coprime Shear ($n \leftrightarrow n+1$) to the *Arithmetic Weights* ($\mu, \Lambda$), Not Just the *Test Function* $f$:**
   - `BiScaleUncertainty` introduced coprime shear ($\gcd(n, n+1) = 1$) in `CoprimeShearAndMultiplicativeGraphUncertainty.lean` and `InfiniteCoprimeTraceProjection.lean` to evade Trap 6 (Beurling countermodels), but it only applied additive differences $v(n+1) - v(n)$ to abstract consumer vectors $v \in \ell^2(\mathbb{N})$ or continuum Sobolev clamps on $f$.
   - It never coupled the additive step $n \mapsto n+1$ (where $\lfloor \frac{n+1}{d} \rfloor - \lfloor \frac{n}{d} \rfloor = \mathbf{1}_{d \mid (n+1)}$) to the **Dirichlet convolution identity** $\Lambda = \mu \star \log$ or $\sum_{d \mid m} \mu(d) = \delta_{m,1}$.
   - For the discrete cell sum $\sum_{n=1}^{N+1} \frac{(\psi(n) - m_n)^2}{n(n+1)}$ in [`HyperbolicGaugeMeanSquareChebyshevWeilSynthesis.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/HyperbolicGaugeMeanSquareChebyshevWeilSynthesis.lean#L62-L65), the step from $n$ to $n+1$ changes $\psi(n)$ by $\Lambda(n+1)$ while shifting the multiplicative divisor set from $\{d : d \mid n\}$ to the coprime set $\{d : d \mid (n+1)\}$. Any upper bound on the mean square must come from a bilinear identity on $\psi(n)^2$ (or on the Möbius floor sums) that uses the coprime orthogonality of divisors of $n$ and $n+k$ to force cancellation across the off-diagonal terms $\sum_{d_1 \ne d_2} \mu(d_1)\mu(d_2) \dots$, rather than treating $\psi(x) - x$ as an opaque black-box function in $L^2$.

3. **Target the Small-$t$ Tail + Head Jointly via Witness Germ Rigidity (`FermiNymanWitnessGermLaws`):**
   - On the dual side of $\ker P$, [`FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FermiNymanDyadicFoldGeometry.lean#L788-L802) proves that RH is equivalent to showing that every $L^2$ witness $w \perp \{\varphi_a\}$ satisfies the **linear origin-mass bound** $\int_0^x |w(t)|^2\,dt \le 2\|w\|_2^2 x$ (equivalently, $w$ is a.e. dyadically self-similar, so the projection of the witness space onto $\ker P$ is $\{0\}$).
   -Proving $\int_0^x |w(t)|^2\,dt \le C x$ for $w \in \{\varphi_a\}^\perp$ is the exact dual of bounding the $\ker P$ producer error from above: instead of guessing explicit coefficients $c_d$ and hitting the pointwise Chebyshev error, it asks you to use the **infinitely many orthogonality equations** $\int_0^1 w(t)\varphi_a(t)\,dt = 0$ for all $a \ge 1$ simultaneously to prevent $w$ from concentrating mass $x^{2(1-\beta)}$ ($1-\beta < 1/2$) near $t \to 0^+$.
