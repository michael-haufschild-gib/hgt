- **The Receiver ([`CHMDChebyshevDiscrepancyDerivativeTransportLaw.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CHMDChebyshevDiscrepancyDerivativeTransportLaw.lean), [`CHMDPrimeKernelDerivativeUniformBound.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CHMDPrimeKernelDerivativeUniformBound.lean)):**
  Stieltjes integration by parts expresses the causal Klein–Gordon prime wave in terms of the classical critical-line Chebyshev discrepancy
  $$\Delta(s) := \sum_{\log n < s} \frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt{n}} - 2e^{s/2}$$
  paired against the exponentially localized odd derivative kernel $\partial_s K_y(s)$:
  $$\mathrm{RH} \iff \sup_{y \ge 0} \pm \int_0^\infty \partial_s K_y(s)\,\Delta(s)\,ds < \infty$$
  (or equivalently a uniform one-sided bound on the folded Möbius wave or signed $(\Lambda(n)-1)$ lattice-replacement wave in [`CHMDLatticePoleReplacementAndSignedPrimeWave.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CHMDLatticePoleReplacementAndSignedPrimeWave.lean)).

  In [`HyperbolicGaugeChebyshevEnergyRepresentation.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/HyperbolicGaugeChebyshevEnergyRepresentation.lean) and [`GaugeDualNormAndDivisorSampledResponse.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/GaugeDualNormAndDivisorSampledResponse.lean), we expressed the finite-cutoff signed prime–polar pairing as $-\int R_N(t)(h'(t) - \tfrac{1}{2}h(t))\,dt$, where $R_N(t) = e^{-t/2}(\psi(e^t)-e^t) + (1-t)e^{-t/2}$ is the causal response. However, our current producers bound $\int_0^{L_N} R_N(t)^2\,dt = \operatorname{laplaceEnergy}(N)$ only by $O(N)$ (`laplaceEnergyProducerBound N = O(N)`), which by Cauchy–Schwarz gives an $O(\sqrt{N}) = O(e^{y/2})$ bound on the pairing instead of an $O(1)$ (or one-sided) bound as $y \to \infty$.
