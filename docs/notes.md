### The radical reversal: locality itself is the interaction

An integer has two radically different descriptions:

- In additive coordinates, \(n\mapsto n+1\) is one local step.
- In factorization coordinates, \(n=\prod_p p^{k_p}\), multiplication by one prime is one local step.

But **addition by one can reorganize the entire factorization**. Conversely, changing one prime occupation dilates the additive position.

My interpretation is that arithmetic cancellation may be enforced by this incompatibility. Neither coordinate system can sustain an independently chosen large-scale bias while remaining the exact same integer system in the other.

That is stronger than “addition and multiplication must be compatible.” The static compatibility identities already exist and do not select anything. The proposed missing mathematics concerns **how correlations are forced to reorganize across scale when both notions of locality remain present**.

### What RH would become

The squarefree, alternating occupation sector has the literal observable

\[
H(t)=\sum_{n\ge1}\mu(n)e^{-tn^2}.
\]

A decisive consequence of the proposed cancellation law would be

\[
|H(t)|\le C_\varepsilon t^{-1/4-\varepsilon}
\quad(t\downarrow0)
\]

for every \(\varepsilon>0\).

The RH consequence is exact: on \(\Re s>1\),

\[
\int_0^\infty H(t)t^{s/2-1}\,dt
=\frac{\Gamma(s/2)}{\zeta(s)}.
\]

That bound would extend the integral holomorphically throughout \(\Re s>1/2\), excluding zeta zeros there; reflection finishes RH.

**Writing this estimate down is not progress.** It is the terminal check. The possible new mathematics would be an independently derived, scale-dependent cancellation identity in the full arithmetic response hierarchy that yields it.
