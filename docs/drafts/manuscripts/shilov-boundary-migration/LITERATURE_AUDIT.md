## Novel theorem package

The following conjunction is the novelty claim—not any imported component in isolation:

1. canonical escape/deficit boundary-exchange algebras for a complete order quotient;
2. escape as exact obstruction to canonical C*-envelope functoriality, with boundary-representation
   and nc spectral-closure equivalences;
3. deficit as exact kernel-generation failure, positive matricial lifting criterion, and arbitrary
   separable unital C*-algebra realization even for split quotients;
4. maximal zero-escape quotient category and right-exact deficit composition law;
5. natural Roos sequence for universal Shilov ideal towers;
6. universal `lim¹`-acyclicity and countable continuity of local C*-envelopes;
7. split zero-escape towers with any prescribed sequence of noncommutative deficits, showing
   pointwise defects can be arbitrary while the global derived obstruction vanishes;
8. faithful local boundary representation and representable K-theory consequences.

## Residual novelty risk

No bibliographic search proves novelty. The main residual risk is obscure pro-C* or local
operator-space literature using different terminology. Risk is reduced because searches covered
both `inverse` and `projective`, both `C*-envelope` and `Shilov`, both `operator system` and `local
operator system`, and both pointwise exactness and `lim¹`. A submission should still invite an
operator-system specialist to check priority before posting.
