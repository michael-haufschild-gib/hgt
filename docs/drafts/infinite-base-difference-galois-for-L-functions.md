## 0. Why the field must exist (and why it doesn't)

- **Hölder (1887):** `ζ` (like `Γ`) satisfies *no* algebraic differential equation — it is **hypertranscendental
  / non-holonomic**.
- Classical transcendence theory (Siegel–Shidlovsky, the `E`/`G`-function formalism, differential Galois theory,
  the period ring) lives **entirely on holonomic functions**. So the zeros of `ζ` are *outside every tool* — we
  cannot prove a single `γ_n` irrational.
- **There is one prototype beyond holonomic: Mahler's method.** Mahler functions satisfy a *functional/difference*
  equation `f(x)=R(x,f(x^k))`, are routinely non-holonomic, and Mahler's method *still proves transcendence of
  their values* (Thue–Morse; revived by Adamczewski–Bell, Philippon). It replaces the **differential** equation
  with a **difference** equation and the differential Galois group with a **difference Galois group** (van der
  Put–Singer).
- `ζ` is not a Mahler function — but only because it has **infinitely many independent bases, one per prime**
  (`x_p := p^{-s}`). The needed field is **Mahler/difference-Galois theory in infinitely many independent bases,
  with an arithmetic functional equation.** That is what is unbuilt.

## 1. The objects (definitions)

Let `L(s)=∏_p L_p(p^{-s})` be an Euler product with `L_p` rational (e.g. `ζ`: `L_p(x)=(1-x)^{-1}`), completed
to `Λ(s)=L_∞(s)L(s)` with functional equation `Λ(s)=Λ(1-s)`. Introduce one variable **per prime**,
`x_p:=p^{-s}`, so `L` is a function on the infinite polydisc `𝔻^∞` pulled back along the **diagonal**

acts by `x_p ↦ p^{-1}x_p`), and `L_p` satisfies a first-order local difference relation. `L` is the diagonal of
infinitely many independent local difference systems.

## 2. Lemma 1 (derivable, not conjectural): the functional equation is a simultaneous inversion, and the critical line is its fixed-modulus locus

```

```

So **RH `=` "every zero lies on the `σ`-fixed-modulus locus of the infinite-base difference system."** This
recovers, from the difference-Galois side, the `σ`-real / BPS picture of the program (off-line zeros are
