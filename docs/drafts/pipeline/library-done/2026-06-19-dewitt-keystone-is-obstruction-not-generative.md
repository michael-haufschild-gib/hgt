## The principle (suggested note for 00-status §A and/or the charter §5)

A **founding/generative principle** — a rule from which the object follows by deduction — **is** an algebraic
differential equation. **Hypertranscendence is exactly the absence of one.** So a generative founding theorem
for the field is **self-defeating**: a hypertranscendental object has none, by definition. **Hölder 1887 is the
founding theorem and it is negative** — it certifies that no generative rule exists.

**Consequence for C5 (resolves the long search):** a theory of hypertranscendental functions can only have a
keystone of two kinds:
1. **Obstruction theory** — *why no rule exists* (Hölder; G1 no-difference-equation; G3 corank=∞ /
   non-holonomicity). At the **function/linear level these are local/classical ⟹ BRIDGE** (the archimedean Γ
   alone obstructs; `{log p}` linear independence is #1). This is why every "Λ-satisfies-no-equation" C5
   attempt (G1, the weld `lambda-difference-differential-hypertranscendence`, the cross-term, generators, and
   DeWitt's refuted `flow-constraint-asymmetry`/`hypertranscendence-weld-c5`) came back BRIDGE — they were
   disguised searches for a generative rule, which cannot be native.
2. **Value-relations (transcendence)** — *relations among the values/zeros* (G2 multi-q Siegel–Shidlovsky;
   DSS). **Only here is the infinite simultaneous σ_p structure irreducibly load-bearing** (the joint
   algebraic independence of the zeros).

**So the C5 keystone is necessarily DSS / multi-q Siegel–Shidlovsky** — the value-relations obstruction
computed via the difference-Galois group `G_L` (orbit-dim = trdeg of the zeros). This is the exact analogue of
the differential-Galois keystone (Airy = `SL₂` ⟹ no Liouvillian solution: itself a *negative* theorem computed
via the Galois group). **"C5 is the apex (DSS)" is the correct answer, not a failure** — it is the only form a
hypertranscendental theory's keystone can take.
