# B24 — a profinite Galois group acting on the zeros (disproven)

- **Statement (disproven):** a profinite Galois group acts on the `ζ`-zeros (the zeros as a Galois
  orbit / "dessin of the primes").

## Disproof
DSS implies the zeros are transcendental. A Galois orbit (of `Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)`) is finite and consists of
algebraic numbers — the opposite of a generic transcendental set. So no such action exists over `ℚ`.

## What it became
The **absence** of this action over `ℚ` is exactly the missing absolute Frobenius / F₁: over a function
field the action exists (Frobenius = the spectral Galois action, Weil), and `G_L` (acting on the primes)
is the only surrogate over `ℚ`, reaching the zeros only through the open carrier reconstruction. The
content is redirected to the seam / missing-Frobenius framing
(`../../door/bucket-the-seam-archimedean-obstruction.md`), not pursued as a Galois action.
