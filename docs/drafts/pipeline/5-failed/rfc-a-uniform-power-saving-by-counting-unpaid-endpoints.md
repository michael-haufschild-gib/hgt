# Counting the unpaid endpoints of a single global involution

- **Stage** 5-failed
- **Failed estimate** \(U_F(X)\ll X^\theta\) at every cutoff for a single one-prime to two-prime involution and some \(\theta<1\).

Products of many comparable primes have large unchanged cofactors. In any interval shorter than that cofactor, a prescribed product is fixed or pays at one of the two cutoff boundaries. Counting prime products over a grid gives unsigned exponent exactly one for every global involution.

**Positive survivor, proved on paper and in Lean.** The finite two-cutoff inequality locates a witness uniformly over all choices of matching. One global involution simultaneously has \(U_F(X)=o(X)\) and exact power exponent one. This identifies the attained boundary and directs the prefix estimate to the signs of the unpaid vertices.
