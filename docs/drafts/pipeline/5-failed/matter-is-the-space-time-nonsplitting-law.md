## Hostile attack: splitting is not a representation-independent physical predicate

The conjecture fails before its proposed particle, gravity, or horizon consequences.

`(MST2)` requires one invariant predicate
\[
  \operatorname{NonSplit}(q_{\mathfrak W,O})
\]
before specifying the category, admissible product, morphisms, or equivalence relation in which
splitting is evaluated. No such category-free predicate exists.

The obstruction is exact:

1. in vector spaces over a field, every short exact sequence splits;
2. in Hilbert spaces, every closed subspace has an orthogonal complement, so the corresponding
   bounded linear short exact sequences split;
3. in groups, rings, \(C^*\)-algebras, modules over general rings, sheaves, and tensor categories,
   non-split extensions may exist, but their extension classes depend on the chosen category and
   notion of equivalence; and
4. a physical system can be dilated, represented by a different observable algebra, or transported
   across an equivalent presentation without preserving a naive block or extension splitting.

Matter and interaction already exist in finite-dimensional Hilbert-space quantum mechanics, where
the underlying linear extension theory is split. Therefore matter cannot equal linear
non-splitting. Choosing a richer category can create nontrivial classes, but unless that category
is independently forced and its class is proved invariant under every physical equivalence, the
choice manufactures the proposed matter.

The equation also has no independent right-hand side. “Matter” is assigned to whatever information
prevents a chosen spatial and temporal projection from reconstructing the complete theory. If the
obstruction retains masses, couplings, gauge representations, and gravitational response, it is
the residual complete theory by definition. The displayed identity then says only:

> physical information omitted by the two selected shadows is the omitted physical information.

That is a decomposition convention, not a universal law.

The nearby parent theories sharpen the problem rather than solve it. Almost-commutative spectral
geometry selects a particular algebra, Hilbert representation, and Dirac operator; locally
covariant QFT selects a category of spacetimes and a theory functor; extension and superselection
theories select observable categories. Their nontrivial content begins after those structures are
specified. None supplies a category-independent `NonSplit`.
