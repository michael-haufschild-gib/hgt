## Hostile attack ledger

- **Collapse by tailored operators.** If arbitrary operators are allowed, every source admits an
  artificial one-relation grammar.  The representation is therefore restricted to
  source-independent base actions.
- **Ore-algebra rediscovery.** Relation ideals for operator orbits and changes of operator
  generators may already be standard difference-algebra or module data.  No novelty claim is
  permitted until the exact comparison is complete.
- **Abstract-ideal collapse.** The two displayed principal ideals are affinely isomorphic after
  forgetting which base endomorphism produced them.  The positive datum, if any, must retain the
  operator action and its conjugacy class; ideal shape alone does not separate the benchmarks.
- **Trivial-conjunction risk.** Pure differential homogeneity plus two known functional equations
  may yield only a useful bridge.  Scientific significance requires a functorial mixed law or a
  classification theorem, not the conjunction itself.
- **Periodic-gauge counterexample.** `GammaPointedSourceNonEffectivity.lean` proves that
  `exp(2*pi*i*z) Gamma(z)` obeys the same unit-shift equation and agrees with Gamma at `z=1`, but
  is not Gamma.  The depth-one ideal classifies a periodic-gauge torsor, not a source.
- **Carrier and derivation mismatch.** Gamma is global meromorphic under translation and uses the
  ordinary derivative; the thermal series is a disc/formal germ under `z |-> z^p` and naturally
  uses the Euler derivative.  A common spectrum requires typed carrier maps and an explicit
  triangular derivation comparison.
