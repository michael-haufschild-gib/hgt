## Retired route

The route identified the three nonidentity elements of the minimal projective binary source square
directly with the three components of the colour fundamental, then promoted arbitrary unitary
changes of their basis to `U(3)` and its determinant-one part to colour `SU(3)`.

This is false when source composition is fixed. The three projective-source directions carry the
Pauli/quaternion cross bracket. Lean proves that

\[
U=\operatorname{diag}(i,-i,1)
\]

is unitary with determinant one but does not preserve that bracket. Thus full `SU(3)` is not the
automorphism group of the fixed source-composition law.

## What was missed

Dimension was mistaken for structure. Three different objects were collapsed:

```text
three source-composition directions
three complex consequence amplitudes
three-dimensional coherent-frame freedom.
```

The first has a multiplication tensor. The second is only a vector space until further structure
is derived. The third changes bases. A dimension count cannot prove they are the same physical
representation.

## Positive survivor

`[PAPER-PROVED] [LEAN-FORMALIZED]`

The \(3\times2\) profile census and colour-Gram orbit theorem survive as vector-space and
positive-descent statements. What fails is only the claim that colour `SU(3)` is already the
fixed-composition automorphism group of the three source directions.

Two live repairs remain:

1. colour is an independent three-dimensional consequence multiplicity generated by, but not
   identical to, the source-direction bracket; or
2. local source composition is a covariant tensor transported with its frame, so `SU(3)` acts on
   both amplitudes and composition data.

The second repair is the stronger universal-law candidate. It says physical gauge fields may be
the obstruction to gluing not merely local frames but local rules of composition. This makes the
composition tensor a field rather than hidden background structure.

The finite repair is already paper- and Lean-proved. Transporting the bracket by
\[
[a,b]_U=U[U^\dagger a,U^\dagger b]
\]
makes every unitary \(U\) exactly equivariant from the old bracket to the transported one. For
the explicit no-go witness, the transported tensor differs from the fixed tensor. What remains
open is the physical law forcing this transport and its continuum curvature.

## Re-entry gate

Do not reassert “three source directions are colour” from \(3=3\). A replacement route must prove
one of:

- a source-only construction of an independent complex three-dimensional multiplicity with
  effective `SU(3)` symmetry; or
- a covariant composition bundle whose complete local frame group is `SU(3)`, whose fixed Pauli
  bracket is one legitimate reduction, and whose curvature has a physical colour interpretation.

It must also distinguish its colour triplet from the rotation/adjoint triplet naturally carried by
the fixed Pauli bracket.
