# Hostile audit: autogenic time law

Status: `[FALSIFIED AFTER CORPUS AUDIT] [NATURE: FAIL]`

Target: `autogenic-time-law-conjecture.md`.

## Antagonist verdict

This is now an Einstein-scale change of primitives. It is not yet science.

The headline is larger than the recurrence theorem because it denies one arena containing all
possible futures. But almost every phrase surrounding that denial has occupied parents:

- evolving laws and state--law unification;
- temporal naturalism;
- observer-first “law without law” programmes;
- process ontologies and dynamical causal structure;
- higher-order quantum maps and recursive operation types;
- variable Hilbert bundles and geometric quantum evolution;
- constructor-theoretic possible/impossible tasks; and
- background-independent or self-referential cosmology.

The conjecture's possible novelty is therefore not “laws evolve,” “events matter,” “causal
structure changes,” or “operations act on operations.” It is the exact conjunction:

```text
source-generated successor operation types
  + no history-independent physical totalization
  + observer time reconstructed from their distinguishability
  + one continuation curvature yielding recurrence, horizons, and gravity
  + one finite discriminator.
```

No item in that conjunction is presently proved.

## Strongest immediate attack: totalization

Any family of sets, spaces, Hilbert spaces, categories, operation types, or transition rules can
usually be placed inside a larger set-theoretic, categorical, direct-sum, Fock, or universal
carrier. The existing corpus proves this explicitly for arbitrary linear event networks and for
countable separable Hilbert carriers.

Therefore the sentence “the future carrier changes” has no invariant content until the conjecture
names physical structure that every such totalization destroys. “Source provenance” is only a
label unless a finite protocol reads it.

If the missing invariant cannot be found, the central claim is false or metaphysical.

## Second attack: meta-law regress

If one fixed rule says how each event generates a new rule, the fixed generator is itself an
outside successor law. If that generator also changes, the theory risks an infinite regress or an
ordinary fixed point in a larger meta-theory.

A successful construction must prove all three:

1. each finite law language is genuinely exceeded;
2. one source principle still gives quantitative predictions;
3. the source principle does not already encode every later operation extensionally.

Hypertranscendence might express the first clause. It does not automatically solve the second or
third.

## Third attack: empirical equivalence

If every finite history generated by the theory can be reproduced by a fixed-carrier hidden-state
model, no finite experiment separates the ontologies. Nature would reject a manuscript whose sole
difference appears only after an inaccessible completed infinity.

Consequence C2 must therefore be sharpened into one finite contextual continuation experiment:

1. two preparations are identical for a tomographically complete family of present experiments;
2. their admissible future experiment types differ;
3. no enlarged fixed quantum state and fixed higher-order process reproduces the difference;
4. an observed probability separates them.

This requirement may be inconsistent. That is a proper fatal test.

## Fourth attack: derivational collage

The current map from one continuation mechanism to quantum probability, time, space, gravity,
matter, forces, horizons, and the Big Bang is motivational. Nothing forces these projections to
be parts of one object. Without an equivalence or rigidity theorem, the card is a collage with a
strong slogan.

The first acceptable theorem must connect at least three domains by one invariant and prove that
their separate definitions coincide. Parallel analogies do not count.

## External collision boundary

Primary literature already establishes close but weaker parent ideas:

- Lee Smolin, *Unification of the state with the dynamical law*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2632>, proposes cosmological evolution of a merged state--law entity.
- Lee Smolin, *Temporal naturalism*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.8539>, treats real time and
  evolving laws as foundational.
- Markus Müller, *Law without law*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01826>, derives apparent external
  worlds and probabilistic laws from observer states and universal induction.
- Bisio and Perinotti, *Theoretical framework for Higher-Order Quantum Theory*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.09554>, constructs a recursive hierarchy of operation types and
  higher-order causal structure.
- Artacho and O'Regan, *Quantum mechanics in an evolving Hilbert space*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05300>, develops connection and curvature for varying quantum
  spaces.

These sources prevent novelty claims about evolving laws, recursive operation types, or curvature
of changing Hilbert spaces. No source in this initial audit was found proving source-generated
operation types with a finite no-totalization discriminator. Absence of a hit is not a priority
certificate.

## What would make the conjecture scientific

The next work is not to explain more phenomena in its vocabulary. It is to solve one hard
discriminator:

> Construct a finite operational history whose continuation structure is source-natural but
> cannot be reproduced by any fixed-carrier theory preserving the same physical composition and
> observer data.

Then prove:

1. a classification of precisely when fixed totalization is possible;
2. a nonzero obstruction outside that class;
3. a finite witness of the obstruction;
4. its transformation law under observer change;
5. one exact recurrence or clock consequence;
6. one physical realization not engineered from the desired conclusion.

Until then, the conjecture is appropriately bold but the paper remains unwritable.

## Post-audit correction

The requested non-totalizability theorem cannot exist for the stated carrier data. The corpus
already proves the opposite for arbitrary typed linear networks and records Hilbert and
completely-positive extensions. The hostile concern above is therefore promoted from “missing
theorem” to exact falsification.

Do not revive autogenic time by restricting the number of event types, adding higher-order
operation labels, or moving to countably many Hilbert carriers. Those mechanisms are already
covered by the totalization boundary.

The positive survivor is not literal creation of an unrepresentable future. It is invariant
dynamics of the source-derived law of independent composition, tracked in
`../1-in-progress/event-relative-independence-spacetime-law.md`.
