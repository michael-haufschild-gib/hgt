## Convention and exact three-body equation

Let `R(u)` denote the physical quantum matrix, let `F` be the tensor flip, and put

```text
B(u)=R(u)F,                 B(0)=-I.                         (1)
```

For the right-flipped adjacent braid convention, an intertwiner to the identity-zero anchor must
satisfy

```text
J_n(theta) B_k(theta_(k+1)-theta_k)
  = -J_n(theta^tau_k).                                      (2)
```

This is not obtained by mechanically right-multiplying the displayed Alazzawi--Lechner equation;
the flips on the two sides must be tracked through the permutation of rapidities.  At particle
number two, Alazzawi's normalized phase has

```text
R(u)=-F exp(2 i rho(u)),
q(u)=exp(i rho(u)),       q(0)=I,
q(x) B(-x)=-q(-x).                                          (3)
```

The harmless global minus in the published `I_2` has been removed.

For three rapidities put

```text
a=theta_1-theta_2,
b=theta_2-theta_3,
c=a+b.
```

Equation `(2)` becomes exactly

```text
J_3(a,b) B_12(-a)=-J_3(-a,c),
J_3(a,b) B_23(-b)=-J_3(c,-b).                              (4)
```

These two equations are the first nontrivial all-particle compatibility gate.

## Failure of the lexicographic complete-pair word

Take the most direct ansatz

```text
J_3(a,b)=q_12(a) q_13(c) q_23(b).                           (5)
```

Using `(3)`, equations `(4)` are equivalent to

```text
q_13(c)q_23(b)B_12(-a)
  =B_12(-a)q_13(b)q_23(c),                                 (6)

q_12(a)q_13(c)=q_12(c)q_13(a).                             (7)
```

At the collision `a=0`, equation `(7)` says

```text
q_13(b)=q_12(b),                                            (8)
```

which is false for every non-scalar `O(N)`/Brauer factor.  Thus the obstruction is projective and
cannot be repaired by a scalar normalization.



## Divisor warning from standard Brauer factors

The standard Brauer Yang--Baxter factors of Isaev--Molev--Ogievetsky have the form

```text

```

and satisfy the three-factor quantum Yang--Baxter equation, but are projectively singular at
`z=0` and obey


zero and matrix pole form one divisor-framed object; these standard fusion factors are not regular
scalar-unit relative half-twists and cannot be inserted into the Hardy argument without a new
collision analysis.
