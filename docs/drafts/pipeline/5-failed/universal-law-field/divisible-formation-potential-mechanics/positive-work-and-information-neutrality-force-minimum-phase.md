## Countermodel

Take a two-atom positive Dirichlet packet with target partition function

\[
 Z(s)=1+2\,2^{-s}
\]

and inherited partition function (Z_{\mathrm{in}}(s)=1).  For every real (\sigma),

\[
 \alpha(\sigma)=\frac1{1+2\,2^{-\sigma}}\in(0,1),
 \qquad
 W(\sigma)=-\log\alpha(\sigma)>0.
\]

This is a finite positive GNS formation with exact Bernoulli information splitting.  Any split
packet lift preserves its information exactly.

Nevertheless,

\[
 Z\left(1+\frac{(2k+1)\pi i}{\log2}\right)=0
 \qquad(k\in\mathbb Z).
\]

Hence the survival transfer (1/Z) has strict-right poles.  Positivity on a real convergence ray,
finite GNS realization, KL conservation, and information-neutral split transport do not imply
minimum phase.
