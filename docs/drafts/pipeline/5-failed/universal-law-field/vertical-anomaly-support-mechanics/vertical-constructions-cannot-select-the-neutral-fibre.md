# Vertical constructions cannot select the neutral fibre — failed conjecture

- **ID:** `ULF-VASM-F1`
- **Stage:** 5-failed
- **Type:** FALSIFIED
- **Source:** whole-corpus anomaly-support reinterpretation
- **Statement:** Any construction assembled from maps over the horizontal-grade base preserves
  inhabited fibres and therefore cannot restrict Xi occurrence to neutral grade.
- **Status:** `[FALSIFIED] [ELEMENTARY EQUALIZER COUNTEREXAMPLE] [BRIDGE SURVIVOR] [NOT RH]`

## 1. Big-picture higher law that was sought

The proposed reinterpretation treated every route organ as a family over
`a = 2 Re(s) - 1`. Faithful recovery, functional-equation transport, positivity, recurrence, and
completion were to be vertical operations on this family. The hoped-for meta-law said that no
vertical construction can reduce grade support, so RH selection would require a genuinely
transverse arithmetic operation.

## 2. Corpus facts used before attack

- `[CORPUS]` The common recovery and functional-equation lift conserve horizontal information.
- `[CORPUS]` Products, fusions, positive shadows, completion classes, and route transgressions
  repeatedly reconstruct the same signed grade or its square.
- `[CORPUS]` Many terminal RH criteria are equalities of two grade-dependent responses, including
  FE/Schwarz branch equality, Euler-flux equality, source-dagger descent, and formation
  equalizers.
- `[LOCAL-RESEARCH]` `STATUS.md` `F05`–`F10`, `F65`–`F81`, and `F107` give the compressed audit.

## 3. Claimed RH closure

Had the claim held for the complete intervention grammar, every existing route fusion would have
been incapable of selecting `a=0`. A proof-producing field would then have had to construct an
operation changing anomaly support rather than another vertical reporter.

## 4. Exact killing counterexample

Let the base be `A = R`. In the slice category `Set/A`, put

\[
 X=A\times\{*\},\qquad Y=A\times R.
\]

Define two maps over `A` by

\[
 f(a,*)=(a,a),\qquad g(a,*)=(a,0).
\]

Both preserve the base coordinate. Their equalizer is

\[
 \operatorname{Eq}(f,g)=\{(a,*):a=0\},
\]

so its projection to `A` has support exactly `{0}`. Hence base-preserving maps can select one fibre
as soon as the grammar permits equalizers, fixed loci, compatibility loci, or vanishing loci.

This counterexample is already the abstract shape of many corpus endpoints. The conjecture was
false because it conflated vertical transport with all constructions internal to a slice.

## 5. Strongest positive survivor

`[BRIDGE]` Pure transport and relation formation are categorically different.

- Fibrewise products of inhabited families remain inhabited.
- Fibrewise surjective images and split recoveries preserve inhabited support.
- Equalizers have fibre at `a` equal to `Eq(f_a,g_a)` and may therefore have arbitrary smaller
  support determined by failure of pointwise compatibility.

Thus selection enters exactly when a source supplies a relation whose fibrewise solvability is not
automatic. This is standard slice-category reasoning, not new scientific knowledge and not worth a
new Lean theorem.

## 6. Stronger replacement constraint

The first replacement—require the relation to be **endogenously generated before occurrence
specialization**—also fails as a discriminator. `SourceCoherentInterventionSynchronization`
already gives source response packets and abstract equalizer selection. More decisively,
`ConstitutiveEndogenousCompatibility` proves that one state-indexed compatibility test realizes an
arbitrary transition relation exactly, with conjugacy naturality. Unconstrained “endogeneity” can
therefore store the desired answer.

The surviving restriction is syntactic as well as semantic: source generation needs a fixed,
occurrence-independent operation signature and a uniform derivation rule. Even that is only an
admissibility gate, not a candidate RH mechanism; the corpus already contains multiple fixed
execution grammars whose selectors still require a source premise.

## 7. No-go closure

- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:** the explicit equalizer above is vertical and supported only
  at neutral grade.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** transport preserves support; relation formation can cut support.
- **New insight:** neither verticality nor the word “endogenous” is the discriminator. Transport
  laws conserve support; relation formers may cut it; unconstrained state-indexed relations can
  encode any cut. A noncircular selector must be derivable in a fixed literal-source calculus.
- **Status:** counterexample and survivor are dependency-closed on paper, but standard and
  scientifically insignificant; no Lean formalization planned.
