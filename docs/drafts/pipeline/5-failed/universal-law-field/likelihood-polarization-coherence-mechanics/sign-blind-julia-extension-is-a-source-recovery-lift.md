## Conjecture

For a reflected binary experiment, likelihood polarization gives a Julia isometry

\[
 C f=(af,df):L^2(\eta)\longrightarrow L^2(\eta)\oplus L^2(\eta),
\]

and reflection gives

\[
 F_+=U\oplus U,
 \qquad
 F_-=U\oplus(-U).
\]

The conjecture treated both as source-derived lifts of the common Markov-recovery action and used
their mismatch as a source coherence current.

## Exact failure

The common-recovery action is a Markov kernel on an observer/history product `X times H`.  It
recovers the observer coordinate, applies reflection there, discards old history, and regenerates
history from the common conditional kernel.

The Julia operators `F_+` and `F_-` act instead on the Hilbert double
`L^2(eta) plus L^2(eta)`.  No source map

\[
 X\times H\longrightarrow L^2(\eta)\oplus L^2(\eta)
\]

or intertwining comparison was constructed.  Likelihood parity proves only

\[
 F_-C=CU.
\]

It does not prove that `F_+` is the image of the Markov lift.  Therefore `F_+C-F_-C` is an exact
operator comparison but not a source-produced coherence current.

## Strong positive survivor

The failure identifies the correct primitive.  For an actual Markov observation `K:X->Y`, balanced
posterior contrast is transported by

\[
 d_Y(Y)=\mathbb E[d_X(X)\mid Y].
\]

The source-typed vertical current is

\[
 j_K=d_X(X)-d_Y(Y),
\]

and it satisfies the exact conservation law

\[
 \mathcal W(P,Q)=\mathcal W(PK,QK)+4\|j_K\|_2^2.
\]

Serial currents form an orthogonal martingale-difference history; endpoint plus all currents is an
isometric proper reconstruction.  This survivor is proved in ULF-PMPG-1.

## No-go closure

- **Killing mechanism:** same formulas and codomain do not make two operators images of one source
