1. **Same-species additive thinning:** one law is represented as a convolution of independent roots

2. **Sufficient-macro formation:** the law of a sufficient statistic reconstructs an independent
   sample through a parameter-independent conditional kernel.  The macro distribution may leave the
   one-particle species and need not be infinitely divisible.

The literal normalized theta cusp likelihood `P_B` kills the conjecture.  Corpus Lean theorems prove

baseline product along the sum supplies a kernel `kappa_d`, independent of `B`, with

\[

Thus exact independent formation survives while same-species divisibility fails.
