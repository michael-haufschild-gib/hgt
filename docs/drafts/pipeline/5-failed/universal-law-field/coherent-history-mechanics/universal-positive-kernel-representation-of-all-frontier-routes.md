## Conjecture

Every live RH route and every law-forming mechanism can be represented, on its native carrier, by a
packet of complex histories followed by positive linear kernels.  The marked-variation defect would
then be a universal complete invariant.

## Killing theorem

The conjecture already fails for the native law

\[
 f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R,\qquad f(x)=x^2.
\]

If a linear operator `T:R->R` agreed with `f`, then `T(1)=1`.  Linearity would give

\[
 T(2)=2T(1)=2,
\]

whereas `f(2)=4`.  Hence no linear operator on the native carrier represents the law.

Feature, Koopman, free-vector-space, or Dirac-measure lifts can linearize arbitrary functions, but
the choice of lift is additional representation data.  It need not preserve source admission,
cost, topology, adaptive observers, or the meaning of a route's endpoint.  Therefore existence of
some linearization does not prove a source-normal common mechanism.

Multivalued relations provide a second obstruction: one positive linear map chooses numerical
branch weights that are absent from a bare relation.  Adaptive null-face laws provide a third: the
observer itself changes with the current state, so one fixed kernel is ill-typed.

## Why the representation failed

The conjecture confused a powerful chart with a universal ontology.  Positive kernels require
additivity, fixed source/target spaces, and predeclared numerical weights.  The full frontier also
contains nonlinear generation, changing admission, multivalued execution, and adaptive
orientation.

## Strongest positive survivor

When a literal source construction **does** provide:

1. a marked additive history packet;
2. coherent complex execution;
3. positive observer kernels;

the marked-variation defect is canonical and obeys exact fusion, observer-chain, refinement, serial
budget, source/splice, recurrence-rigidity, and carrier-distance laws.  This survivor is the
paper-proved theorem `ULF-MVD-1`.

The survivor changes the research question from

> Can the route be embedded in some linear positive space?

to

> Does the literal source itself generate the marked packet and positive observer, and does its
> normalized splice density vanish cofinally?

That is a falsifiable source theorem rather than a representation trick.
