## Failed conjecture

Let `v_a` be any family in one real normed space. Suppose every finite packet admits a common
norming functional. The failed conjecture proposed that the whole family might nevertheless lack a
common normer, thereby explaining the corpus's cofinal obstructions inside fixed norming-face

## Exact countertheorem

For each `a`, let

\[
 N(v_a)=\{\varphi:\|\varphi\|\le1,\ \varphi(v_a)=\|v_a\|\}.
\]

The dual unit ball is weak-star compact by Banach--Alaoglu. Every `N(v_a)` is weak-star closed,
and Hahn--Banach makes it nonempty. If every finite packet has a common normer, the family
`N(v_a)` has the finite-intersection property. Compactness gives

\[
 \bigcap_aN(v_a)\ne\varnothing.
\]

Therefore failure of global calibration in a fixed normed source always has a finite witness.
Purely cofinal failure is impossible in this model.
