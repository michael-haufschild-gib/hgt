## Killing representation

Static compatibility data impose no special topology.  Given any finite simplicial complex `K` on
a vertex set `V`, declare exactly the faces of `K` to be jointly executable law packages.  The
resulting compatibility complex is `K` by construction.  Equivalently, its minimal failed packages
are the minimal nonfaces of `K`, an arbitrary clutter.  The elementary closure construction in
`static-failure-kernel-exchange-universality.md` realizes every such clutter as the minimal failure
kernels of an extensive, monotone, idempotent consequence doctrine.

Therefore compatibility complexes can have arbitrary homotopy type, arbitrary Betti numbers, and
arbitrary minimal-nonface incidence.  Neither simplicial topology nor static circuit data alone can
force exchange, positivity, admission, recurrence, hypertranscendence, or a source selector.

The corpus already contains the stronger relevant machinery:

- Stanley--Reisner vertex relations and hollow-simplex cohomology;
- Hochster cycle realization and proper-face interaction fibres;
- hypertranscendental source-fracture hypercubes;
- arbitrary failure-kernel clutter realization.

Thus the proposed topology would redress an existing arbitrary-presentation layer, not explain why
one law changes the future executability of another.
