## Failed conjecture

The coefficientwise completion of finite source jets was expected to have a small strict primitive
extension and a nonzero quotient

\[
 \operatorname{Prim}(\widehat A)/
 \overline{\operatorname{Prim}(A_{\mathrm{fin}})}.
\]

This is false for the natural product completion.

## Countertheorem

For an injective clock \(\lambda:I\to\mathbb C\) and source space
\(V\le\mathbb C^I\), the product closure of

\[
 \operatorname{span}\{\lambda^nv:v\in V,n\ge0\}
\]

is the complete product of all functions supported on the source-active coordinates. In the
prime-power realization, one nonzero source coefficient on a valuation layer makes finite clock
jets dense in every prime-dependent current on that layer.

Hence no active completed current defines a nonzero quotient-by-closure fracture class.

## Positive survivor

Closure saturation does not erase formation complexity. For the source resolvent

\[
 J=(1-D)^{-1}a,
\]

the least polynomial formation depth matching an \(N\)-coordinate packet is exactly \(N-1\).
The normalized order-\((N-1)\) divided difference is nonzero and annihilates every lower-depth
formation. Thus the replacement invariant is the cofinal lift-depth profile, not a closure quotient.

## Exact lesson

Product completion confuses reachability with generation. A viable universal law field must retain
source lift cost, operation order, or a stronger receiver scale. Merely adjoining completion points
cannot select a law.
