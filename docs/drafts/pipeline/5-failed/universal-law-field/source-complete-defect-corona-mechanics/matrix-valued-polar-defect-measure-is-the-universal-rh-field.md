## 1. Retired conjecture

The proposed field variable was one matrix-valued polar defect measure on a compactification of
source frequency/history space.  Its finite part would record route-visible mass, its angular part
would retain phase and orientation, and its point at infinity would record concentration or
noncompact escape.  Route obstructions were to become projections of this measure.

This proposal is retired as the new field's primitive.

## 2. Why it looked plausible

One positive operator-valued measure can simultaneously retain:

- scalar mass and signed cross-phase through matrix entries;
- local oscillation and frequency direction;
- finite versus escaping support;
- positive Gram matrices and determinant shadows;
- weak limits and concentration defects.

That packet covers more corpus organs than a scalar obstruction, a single curvature, or a single
homology class.

## 3. Decisive failure

Representation is not mechanism.  The measure says where a defect is observed but does not imply:

1. why source formation makes it invariant;
2. why an invariant finite part must vanish;
3. why mass at infinity is forbidden;
4. which formation cost is proper;
5. why functional equation or common recovery preserves the selection law.

Any prescribed defect packet can be placed into such a measure.  Without independent source laws,
the construction is a lossless archive, not a field equation or selector.
