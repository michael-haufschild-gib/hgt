### Conjecture B — reflection-doubled Cartier determinant triviality kills the anomaly

Let `A_a=exp(-a kappa)` on a source triangle.  Reflection sends the centered rate `a` to `-a`, so


**Verdict: false.**  `diag(t,t^(-1))` has determinant one for every positive `t`.  It preserves the
hyperbolic Stokes pairing for every `t`, so reflection reciprocity and indefinite conservation do
not imply `t=1`.

### Conjecture C — a positive same-fibre Cartier line is Stokes-flat
