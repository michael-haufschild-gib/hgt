## Public law

> **The universe has no absolute parts. Space is the consistency law between
> all operationally valid ways of dividing it.**

Temporal form:

> **Different observers may divide the universe differently and therefore
> inhabit different dimensions of quantum time. Physical law is what remains
> invariant when those divisions are changed.**

This is not the statement that coordinates on one supplied tensor product
are arbitrary. It denies that one observer-independent tensor product,
subsystem list or local-algebra net is fundamental.

## Frozen truth-hunch

For every complete physical process \(\mathfrak P\), there is no generally
preferred physical factorization. Instead there is an intrinsic
**division-relativity object**
\[
  \mathfrak D(\mathfrak P)
\]
whose objects are all operationally valid ways of identifying systems,
observers, environments and independently variable controls, and whose
arrows are complete physical translations between those divisions.

Every division \(F\) has its own observer-relative temporal completion
\[
  \mathbb T_F(\mathfrak P),
\]
including its recurrent, constrained, hidden and transient temporal
directions. A valid change of division transports the complete temporal law,
not merely states or coordinates.

Physical spacetime is not one member \(F\). It is the invariant compatibility
geometry of the entire family
\[
  F\longmapsto\mathbb T_F(\mathfrak P).
\]
Spatial regions, causal adjacency, horizons and proper-time comparison are
stable features of this compatibility geometry. They are not properties of
one privileged factorization.

This hunch is frozen before choosing:

- a temporal completion functor;
- a descent or holonomy invariant;
- a continuum construction;

## Ontological reversal

Ordinary quantum theory permits basis changes while keeping fixed what counts
as a system. Quantum mereology asks which tensor-product structure should be
preferred. The conjecture makes a stronger reversal:

1. there need not be one preferred subsystem structure;
2. “system,” “environment” and “observer” are chart-dependent identifications;
3. an observer's temporal dimension is computed inside that chart;
4. changes of chart can alter subsystem number, entanglement, locality and
   visible temporal dimension;
5. complete physical predictions must transport between charts;
6. space is the global compatibility law of these chartwise descriptions;
7. gravity, if the conjecture reaches spacetime physics, is curvature or
   nonclosure of physical chart transport rather than a field inside one
   fixed division.

The analogy with relativity is structural, not rhetorical. Relativity did not
select the true coordinate system. This conjecture says fundamental physics
does not select the true division of the universe into systems.

## Why the predecessor failed and why this is not its repair

The predecessor tried to infer one preferred factorization from coordinate

  z_1z_2=\frac{(z_1+z_2)^2-(z_1-z_2)^2}{4}

shows that joint source support changes under invertible source mixing.
Either the mixing is gauge, so inferred locality is gauge-dependent, or it is

The present conjecture does not seek a more sophisticated sparsity
functional. It takes the no-go literally: both presentations may be valid

invariants.

## Universal statement

The conjecture asserts all of the following.

1. **Division covariance.** Every valid division change induces a reversible
   physical translation of complete predictions on its domain.
2. **Temporal covariance.** Recurrent, constrained, hidden and transient
   temporal sectors transport as one object. Their individual dimensions may
   change, but transported observable return laws agree.
3. **No absolute subsystem count.** Operationally valid divisions may
   disagree on how many subsystems exist and on which degrees of freedom
   belong to an observer.
4. **Invariant physical geometry.** A division-independent compatibility
   invariant determines the physical causal/spatial geometry seen in every
   regular classical chart.
5. **Chartwise locality.** Locality is meaningful inside a division, while
   physical locality is the equivalence class of its compatible appearances
   across divisions.
6. **Chartwise temporal dimension.** The scalar clock parameter is a path
   through \(\mathbb T_F\); the dimension of that completion is
   observer-and-division dependent.
7. **Interaction transport.** The creation of joint temporal directions by
   interaction in one chart is equivalent to a redistribution of temporal,
   constraint and boundary sectors in every other valid chart.
8. **Horizon descent.** A physical horizon is a failure of global reversible
   division transport that persists after every regular chart change; mere
   detector loss or a weak link is chart-removable or finite-depth.
9. **Classical limit.** Ordinary spacetime regions and approximately stable
   objects arise as robust common strata of many division charts, explaining
   why classical observers experience an effectively fixed world of parts.
10. **Dynamics.** If gravity is included, the law relating matter
    interventions to deformation of the compatibility geometry must be
    derived from the same division-relative temporal object, not supplied as
    an independent metric equation.

Deleting item 4 leaves only subsystem relativity. Deleting items 2 and 7
disconnects the conjecture from quantum recurrence. Deleting item 10 leaves
pregeometry without physical dynamics.

## New risky consequences

1. **Subsystem-number relativity.** A controlled quantum-reference-frame
   protocol can change the operational subsystem count while transporting all
   complete predictions exactly.
2. **Temporal-dimension relativity.** The same protocol can change visible
   projective temporal dimension, with the change balanced by hidden,
   constrained or boundary directions.
3. **Return-law covariance.** Once return observables and tolerances are
   transported correctly, two division charts predict identical measured
   return distributions even when their phase-torus dimensions differ.
4. **Entanglement is insufficient.** Division charts with identical
   entanglement spectra can have different temporal completions; complete
   recurrence transport distinguishes them.
5. **Nontrivial division holonomy.** A closed sequence of physically
   implementable division changes may return all ordinary state coordinates
   while leaving a measurable temporal-completion residue.
6. **Classical-space reconstruction.** Stable common invariants of a
   sufficiently rich division atlas determine classical causal regions
   without selecting any chart as fundamental.
7. **Horizon invariance.** A genuine causal horizon survives all valid
   division changes, whereas ordinary open-system loss can be removed in a
   larger valid division.
8. **Gravitational target.** In a continuum theory, infinitesimal
   division-holonomy must reproduce classical spacetime curvature and its
   intervention response without inserting a Levi--Civita connection by
   definition.

Items 5, 6 and 8 distinguish the conjecture from a reinterpretation of
quantum reference frames.

quantum reference frame and controllable reassignment of degrees of freedom

For each division chart:

1. reconstruct the observer-visible spectral-character action;
2. measure its temporal rank and projective return distribution;
3. transport the return reporter and tolerance through the physical chart
   change;
4. close a nontrivial sequence of chart changes.

The conjecture predicts:

- chartwise subsystem count and visible temporal dimension may differ;
- the complete transported return distribution is invariant; and
- a nonzero closed-chart residue, if present, lies in temporal
  completion/constraint data rather than ordinary coordinate mismatch.

Failure of return-law covariance kills the temporal clause. Universal
triviality of every closed-chart residue kills the holonomy clause.

## Post-freeze priority attack — foundational core already occupied

`[RETIRED 2026-07-30]`

Primary literature contains the proposed foundational reversal in materially
the same form.

1. Ahmad--Galley--Höhn--Lock--Smith, *Quantum Relativity of Subsystems*
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01232>), proves that quantum-reference-frame
   perspectives induce different subsystem observable algebras and that
   subsystem locality is frame-dependent.
2. Höhn--Kotecha--Mele, *Quantum Frame Relativity of Subsystems,
   Correlations and Thermodynamics*
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09131>), explicitly states that internal
   quantum frames partition a system differently, compares this with the
   relativity of space/time decomposition, and shows that correlations,
   interactions, open versus closed dynamics, temperature, heat, work and
   entropy flow can all be frame-relative.
3. Cepollaro et al., *The sum of entanglement and subsystem coherence is
   invariant under quantum reference frame transformations*
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19448>), supplies a nontrivial conserved
   redistribution law across frames.
4. Ludescher--Mekonnen--Galley--Mueller, *Quantum reference frames beyond
   subsystems*
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24976>), extends frames beyond tensor-factor
   subsystems to covariant quantum instruments and obtains relational clocks
   even with interacting subsystems and frequency superselection sectors.

Consequently the following are not available as new foundational claims:

- no absolute subsystem division;
- different frames induce different subsystem structures;
- locality and open/closed character can change with frame;
- resources redistribute while complete predictions remain covariant; and
- relational clocks need not coincide with a tensor factor.

The remaining clauses in the frozen conjecture are:

1. attach an observer-relative recurrence completion to each frame;
2. find nontrivial closed-frame temporal holonomy; and
3. identify compatibility of all frames with physical spacetime and gravity.

Clause 1 is a plausible new theorem programme but is a local extension of
existing QRF covariance. Clauses 2 and 3 were not independently supported by
the facts that motivated the conjecture; they were appended to lift a known
framework to Nature altitude. No theorem or discriminating physical
prediction currently forces them. The whole proposal can therefore be
described as “known subsystem relativity plus recurrence transport plus a
spacetime hypothesis,” violating the conjecture-distance gate.

This is a novelty retirement, not a proof that every mathematical clause is
false.

  non-identifiability, weak-coupling rank discontinuity and recurrence
  density—not a known relativity framework decorated with those results.
