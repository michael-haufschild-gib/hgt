## Abstract consumer theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Let \(H\) be a Hilbert space, \(U\in B(H)\), and suppose
\[
 K=U^*U-I
\]
is compact. If an orthonormal sequence \((x_m)\) satisfies
\[
 Ux_m=\lambda x_m
\]
for one complex number \(\lambda\), then \(|\lambda|=1\).

Indeed \(x_m\rightharpoonup0\), so compactness gives
\(\|Kx_m\|\to0\). But
\[
 \langle Kx_m,x_m\rangle
 =\|Ux_m\|^2-\|x_m\|^2
 =|\lambda|^2-1.
\]
The left side tends to zero, hence \(|\lambda|=1\).

Apply this to `(2)`--`(5)`. For every nontrivial zero \(\rho\),
\[
 \left|p^{\rho-1/2}\right|=1,
\]
so
\[
 p^{\Re\rho-1/2}=1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Re\rho=\frac12.
\tag{6}
\]

The abstract proof is standard operator theory and is not a Lean target. All RH content lies in
constructing the source object and proving its four clauses.
