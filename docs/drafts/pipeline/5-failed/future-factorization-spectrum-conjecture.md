## Frozen public conjecture

> **The universe is not made of objects evolving inside spacetime.**
>
> **Objects and spacetime are the factorization geometry of possible futures.**

More operationally:

> **Space records which futures can still be independent. Time records the
> order in which that independence fails. Recurrence is the symmetry left
> after every observable failure has been resolved.**

The earlier sentence

> space is the delay before observers share time

is now only a proposed first-distance corollary. It is not the primitive law.

## The category change

The conventional ontology begins with:

```text
objects + state space + subsystem split + spacetime + time parameter
  -> dynamics
  -> observations and recurrence.
```

The conjectured ontology begins with:

```text
source-complete possible continuations
  -> exact factorization and nonfactorization of their observable future laws
  -> objects, locality, causal order, metric, and recurrence.
```

The primitive is neither a state, Hamiltonian, graph, manifold, clock, nor
observer. It is the complete law of which counterfactual continuations can be
varied independently and at what operational order their independence first
becomes observably false.

## Frozen technical conjecture

Let a physical process supply only:

1. admissible sources and reporters;
2. sequential composition;
3. an independently admissible joint-composition operation;
4. probabilities or expectation values for every finite controlled history;
5. operational equivalence of source and reporter presentations.

No tensor decomposition, interaction graph, metric, Hamiltonian, external
time coordinate, or spacetime is supplied.

For every finite source interface \(A\), let \(\Gamma(A)\) be the complete
abelian group of observer-visible temporal characters of controlled future
histories sourced in \(A\), after quotienting operationally invisible
presentations. Joint composition induces comparison maps
\[
  \Gamma(A)\oplus\Gamma(B)\longrightarrow\Gamma(A\boxtimes B).
\]
Define the future-factorization defect
\[
  \mathfrak D(A,B)
  =
  \Gamma(A\boxtimes B)/
  \bigl(\Gamma(A)+\Gamma(B)\bigr).
\]
Filter every defect by the least controlled-history or interaction order at
which a representative has a nonzero response. Retain the complete leading
response form at every birth order, not only its rank.

Write
\[
  \mathfrak F(P)=
  \bigl(\Gamma,\boxtimes,\mathfrak D,v,\operatorname{in}_v\bigr)
\]
for this source-complete valued future-factorization object.

The conjecture is:

1. **Completeness.** \(\mathfrak F(P)\) determines \(P\) up to operational
   physical equivalence. Two inequivalent physical processes cannot have the
   same complete future-factorization object.
2. **Objects.** Persistent indecomposable factors of \(\mathfrak F(P)\), not
   presupposed tensor factors, are the physical systems or objects of the
   process.
3. **Space.** The prime factorization spectrum of \(\mathfrak F(P)\) is the
   process's spatial locale. Exact independence is separation; failure of
   factorization is adjacency.
4. **Causality and distance.** Directed causal order is the orientation of
   defect birth. The least birth valuation of
   \(\mathfrak D(A,B)\) is directed path distance. The entire filtered initial
   form, rather than one scalar distance, determines causal geometry.
5. **Time.** Observable temporal succession is the valuation order on new
   nonfactorizable future distinctions. A clock is a faithful one-dimensional
   reporter of this order, not an external parameter supplied to it.
6. **Recurrence.** The terminal temporal-character quotient is the character
   group of the complete observer-visible recurrence hull. Every return law is
   a harmonic functional of that terminal quotient.
7. **Irreversibility.** Birth and deletion of future-factorization characters
   define an oriented persistence object. The arrow of time is nontrivial
   orientation of this persistence, not clock reversal on a closed orbit.
8. **Horizons.** An operational horizon is a one-way prime boundary across
   which local future laws remain nontrivial but every directed joint
   factorization defect has infinite birth valuation.
9. **Continuum.** For locally finite families with a nondegenerate scaling
   limit, the factorization spectrum and oriented valuation recover conformal
   Lorentzian geometry; calibrated leading forms recover metric scale and
   volume.
10. **Gravity.** Gravitational connection and curvature are the obstruction to
    gluing local future-factorization spectra and valuations into one global
    product of independently synchronized futures. This clause includes a
    physical claim only if the induced continuum response is shown to obey
    universal free fall and gravitational field dynamics.

No clause is presently a result. The claim is frozen without choosing a
proof architecture. In particular, it may not later be weakened into
Hamiltonian learning, graph reconstruction from signal arrival, a supplied
Haag--Kastler net, entanglement geometry, or ordinary process tomography.

## Why this is a larger conjecture than shared-time reconstruction

Shared-time reconstruction assumes identifiable observer regions and asks
when joint temporal characters appear. The present conjecture removes the
regions themselves from the input. It says that:

- physical objects are indecomposable future factors;
- space is their intrinsic factorization spectrum;
- distance and causal orientation are valuations on its defects;
- recurrence is its terminal symmetry;
- horizons and irreversibility are failures of extension and persistence of
  the same object.

Thus the conjecture does not put recurrence into a pre-existing spatial
network. It claims that the network and its recurrence law are two
projections of one pregeometric invariant.

The hunch is that these are shadows of one missing structure: valued

## First hostile result — full-process completeness is false

`[KILLED 2026-07-30]`

Clause 1 fails for the stated temporal-character object.

Consider a static one-state operational device with no nonconstant temporal
characters and one binary reporter whose probability of outcome \(1\) is
\(p\in(0,1)\). For every \(p\):

\[
  \Gamma=0,\qquad
  \mathfrak D=0,
\]

and there are no nontrivial birth valuations or initial forms. Nevertheless,
devices with different \(p\) are operationally inequivalent because the
binary reporter separates them in one trial. Thus the complete valued
temporal-factorization object as defined above does not determine the
physical process.

Including the entire zero-order probability functional in
\(\mathfrak F(P)\) removes this counterexample only by creating a dilemma:

1. if enough controlled probabilities are retained to distinguish every
   process, completeness is process tomography by construction;
2. if only temporal-character and factorization data are retained, static
   operational information is lost and full-process completeness is false.

The literature attack independently sharpens the boundary:

- Zanardi, Lidar and Lloyd already show that tensor-product structure can be
  induced by operationally accessible interactions and observables;
- quantum causal unravelling already reconstructs multipartite causal
  interaction structure under bounded-loss and nonzero-strength conditions;
- locally covariant algebraic QFT already treats locality, independence and
  tensor structure through functors and local observable algebras;
- order-and-number reconstruction already recovers smooth Lorentzian
  spacetime under its continuum hypotheses.

Therefore “observables determine subsystems,” “process data determine causal
structure,” “factorization defines locality,” and “causal order reconstructs
spacetime” are occupied parent claims.

## No-go closure

- **Exact killing mechanism:** nontrivial static probability data can coexist
  with the trivial temporal-character and factorization object.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** temporal future-factorization may still be
  a complete invariant of a narrower **causal-recurrence geometry**, while
  ordinary probability and matter data live as non-geometric fibers over it.
- **New insight:** a temporal invariant cannot be both a nontrivial quotient
  and a complete invariant of all physics. The scientific target must be an
  exact maximality theorem: determine precisely which geometric and
  recurrence information the quotient retains, rather than hiding process
  tomography inside “source completeness.”
- **Formal status:** counterexample paper-level only; a reusable finite
  operational-process formalization is not yet present.

The frozen conjecture is retired rather than weakened. A successor must keep
the ontology-changing claim while asking for exact spacetime and recurrence
reconstruction, not full-process reconstruction.
