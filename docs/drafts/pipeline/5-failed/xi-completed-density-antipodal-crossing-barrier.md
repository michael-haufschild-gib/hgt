## Hostile verdict

### 1. Regular no-return is automatic and insufficient

On a chart where `E!=0`, write

```text
W_E=(E#)'E-E#E'.
```

Then `R'=W_E/E^2`.  Orient a regular real-level arc by the tangent

```text
V_E=E^2 conjugate(W_E).
```

Exact differentiation gives

```text
dR(V_E)=|W_E|^2>0.                                  (1)
```

Hence `R` is already strictly increasing on every regular negative-ray arc.  Such an arc cannot
take the value `-1` twice.  This is generic holomorphic level-set geometry, not theta-source
selection.

An off-axis Xi zero can nevertheless lie once on an unanchored regular component whose real image
contains `-1`.  No return occurs and `W_E` need not vanish.  The old clause saying that unanchored
components avoid `-1` therefore contained the full unresolved problem.

### 2. `C_F` is not the ratio connection

The actual projective derivative is generated by `W_E`, not by the earlier full-density connection
`C_F`.  Corpus algebra now proves

```text

C_F = 8z XiC F + 4z^2 (XiC' F - XiC F').             (3)
```

```text

C_F=4z^2 XiC' F,
W_E-q C_F=-z^2 XiC'.                                 (4)
```

Thus at every noncentral simple collision the two connections are provably different.  Nonvanishing
of `C_F` cannot certify the flow of `E#/E`.
