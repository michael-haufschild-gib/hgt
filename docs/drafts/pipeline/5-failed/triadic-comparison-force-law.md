## Hostile verdict

triadic data. Multilevel quantum mechanics already supplies the killing counterstructure.

Mukunda--Arvind--Chaturvedi--Simon show that a complete invariant set for an \(n\)-level unitary
contains, in addition to geometric phases, \(\frac12(n-1)(n-2)\) algebraically independent
four-vertex Bargmann invariants. Modern Bargmann-invariant work treats arbitrary-order quantities
\(\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_1\cdots\rho_n)\) as genuine invariants with distinct operational uses.

Thus three perspectives are not universally sufficient even before gravity and particle physics
are added. The failure hits pre-registered kill condition 6.
