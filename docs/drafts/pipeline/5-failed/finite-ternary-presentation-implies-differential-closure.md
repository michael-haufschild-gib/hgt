## Exact killing mechanism

Neither structure determines the other.

Let \(h(z)=\Gamma(z)\), Hypertranscendental over \(\mathbb C(z)\), and let \(P\) be a fixed nonzero
rectangular partial isometry, so \(PP^\dagger P=P\).  For every regular parameter value \(z\), put

\[
X_z=\mathbb C P,\qquad D(z)=h(z)P.
\]

Every \(X_z\) is the same one-dimensional TRO:

\[
X_zX_z^\dagger X_z\subseteq X_z.
\]

Nevertheless the section \(z\mapsto D(z)\) carries the arbitrary Hypertranscendental scalar
\(h(z)\).  Fixed finite fiber dimension, exact ternary closure, reciprocal squares, and a trivial
bundle do not constrain its differential-algebraic type.

The field form is even sharper.  With \(F=\mathbb C(z)\),

\[

theorem would make \(S\) derivation closed and force \(\Gamma\) to be differentially algebraic.
Therefore

\[
\Gamma'\notin F(\Gamma).
\]

Finite ordinary generation and finite ternary presentation do not imply derivative closure.
