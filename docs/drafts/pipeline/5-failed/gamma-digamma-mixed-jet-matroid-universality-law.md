## Proof

`[PROVED HERE]` Regard a mixed polynomial as a polynomial in the Gamma coordinate with coefficients
in the algebra generated by finitely many digamma jets.  Represent each coefficient
by its unique multivariate polynomial using digamma algebraic independence.  A zero
of this polynomial at Gamma gives a cleared `GammaPowerRel`; Gamma-power elimination
forces every coefficient to vanish.  This proves the mixed basis.

The Bell identity then places each positive Gamma jet in the algebra generated by
Gamma and the lower digamma jets.  Conversely, `Gamma' = Gamma * digamma` gives a
nonzero polynomial relation on the triple `(Gamma, Gamma', digamma)`, producing the
first circuit.  No numerical computation is applicable or used.

Finitarity upgrades every finite result to the countably infinite basis
`{Gamma} ∪ {digamma^(j) : j ≥ 0}`.
