# PTR angular-only helical distributional route: no-go

- **Chain attacked:** rotating-cylinder Maxwell source → infinite grade/OAM boundary map → prime conormal distribution → independent confinement → RH. This card retires only the *angular-only, source-free, positive-loss, real-frequency* reduction, not the cylinder or square-lattice PTR route.
- **New result (Lean):** `PhotonicHelicalAngularDistributionalNoGo` proves that the two-index recurrence with real diagonal angular dispersion `ℓ²`, adjoint helical shifts `(n,ℓ)↔(n±1,ℓ±m)`, nonzero real grade frequencies, and strictly positive loss has no nonzero jointly polynomial-growth coefficient field. This permits moving OAM `ℓ=q+mn`; it is stronger than the prior fixed-spatial-profile and finite-energy no-gos. `ptrJointSquarePolyGrowth` is the explicit Fourier-coefficient growth class; no spectral truncation or summability is assumed.
- **Mechanism:** integer shear `q=ℓ−mn` preserves joint polynomial growth (`ptr_helical_shear_joint_growth_iff`) and separates the recurrence into every conserved `q` sector. Grade-dependent real angular eigenvalues do not enter the imaginary flux balance. Each sector's positive loss forces exponential mass growth unless zero, contradicting joint polynomial growth.
- **Positive survivor (Lean-proven):** polynomial growth is *exactly invariant* under the helical shear, so moving angular momentum alone cannot evade the loss-flux obstruction. Escape in the actual source must use a term absent here: radial/interface flux, square-lattice spatial mixing that breaks exact `q` conservation, non-passive/causal frequency-dependent material response, forcing, or nonreal Floquet parameter. This is a necessary alternatives list, not a claim any one is sufficient.
- **Limit:** [Mai--Yang--Wang, arXiv:2609.08748v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08748v1) has a rotating cylinder in a square lattice, radial structure, and a finite Floquet truncation. Its full Maxwell/interface Cauchy-data relation has not been constructed. The present angular equation is a controlled reduction, not that relation. Therefore no full PTR route kill or RH result follows.
- **Next falsifier:** derive the literal all-grade interior/exterior Cauchy-data relation and its Green identity. Test whether a prime conormal trace can enter the source-free interface intersection while boundary flux absorbs the loss current. A counterexample to a proposed distribution-to-interior-mass estimate is a legitimate outcome.
