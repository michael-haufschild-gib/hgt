## Failed claim

For clock-derived smeared curvature reporters `K_f,K_g`, consider

```text
E = exp(i lambda Z_A K_f) exp(i mu Z_B K_g)
      exp(-i lambda Z_A K_f) exp(-i mu Z_B K_g).
```

If a free quantum-gravity model has

```text
[K_f,K_g] = i Omega(f,g) I,
```

then

```text
E = exp(-i lambda mu Omega(f,g) Z_A Z_B) tensor I_grav.
```

The discarded overclaim was:

> Probe entanglement from this echo proves, independently of all model assumptions, that gravity
> cannot be classical.

## Exact killing mechanism

Two distinct notions had been conflated.

1. For every **prescribed classical metric history** independent of the probe branch, `K_f,K_g`
   are commuting numbers. The four factors cancel pointwise before stochastic averaging.
2. A **hybrid classical mediator** is a dynamical theory for coupling classical and quantum
   sectors. Its generators need not be represented by multiplication by commuting classical
   random variables.

Galley--Giacomini--Selby prove the classical-mediator no-go inside a generalized probabilistic
framework whose classical systems have simplex state spaces, hypercube effects, and standard
tensor-product composition. Their conclusion is conditional on that operational definition of
classicality and local mediation.

Ulbricht--Bermúdez Manjarres--Reginatto construct a 2026 countermodel in hybrid van Hove mechanics.
Classical Hamiltonian functions are represented by van Hove generators whose commutator realizes
the Poisson Lie algebra. These generators do not form the ordinary product algebra of classical
observables: in general

```text
O_(F^n) != (O_F)^n.
```

Within that framework a classical oscillator mediates two-spin entanglement. Whether that hybrid
theory is an acceptable model of classical gravity remains disputed, but its existence is enough
to kill the word **model-independent**.

The central echo phase also equals a Peierls/symplectic response kernel determined by the causal
Green function. Its numerical value is not uniquely quantum; only its realization inside a
declared operational product algebra can carry a nonclassicality conclusion.

## Strongest positive survivor

The following exact null remains:

> The echo is the identity for every prescribed, possibly stochastic, commuting metric history
> independent of the probe branch.

The following conditional witness remains:

> Under simplex classicality, standard product composition, local mediation, and initially
> factorized probes, an entangling clock echo excludes a classical mediator.

The stronger research target is no longer entanglement alone. A complete clock protocol must
tomographically test both:

```text
antisymmetric sequential composition -> Lie/causal bracket,
symmetric and repeated composition   -> physical product/Jordan structure.
```

A genuinely nonclassical gravitational clock algebra must exhibit a noncommutative associative
product law for the same gauge-invariant reporters that reconstruct Riemann curvature. A hybrid
model that preserves only the classical Poisson Lie algebra but not the observable product law
must then make a different operational prediction.

## No-go closure

- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:** hybrid van Hove mechanics supplies a claimed classical
  mediator with noncommuting generators and mediated two-spin entanglement; the universal
  classical-mediator inference therefore requires extra composition assumptions.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** pointwise classical-history cancellation, conditional GPT
  witness, and the stronger complete product-algebra discriminator.
- **New insight:** commutator, quantumness, and classicality are three different types unless the
  physical product structure is measured. A Lie bracket alone can encode a classical Poisson
  algebra.
- **Lean status:** no new unconditional mathematical theorem isolated here. The exact central
  group-commutator factorization and clock-word reconstruction remain formalization targets in
  the parent card.
