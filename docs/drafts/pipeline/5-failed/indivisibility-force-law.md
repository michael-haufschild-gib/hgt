## Semantic commitments

1. **Physical division** means an operational claim that two sets of degrees of freedom have
   independently specifiable states and reversible dynamics. It is stronger than drawing a spatial
   boundary and weaker than asserting zero statistical correlation.
2. **Failure** is structural, not merely state entanglement. An entangled state in a theory with an
   exact subsystem composition does not by itself count as a force.
3. **Same indivisibility** means gravity and gauge interactions must descend from one physical
   comparison datum. Placing unrelated connections in a direct sum does not satisfy the
   conjecture.
4. **Field curvature** refers to the local interaction strength. A flat connection may retain
   global holonomy; the global division can therefore remain inexact when local curvature
   vanishes.
5. **Exactly when** is a biconditional. A realization proving only that decomposition failure can
   source a force, or only that force obstructs a particular factorization, is insufficient.
