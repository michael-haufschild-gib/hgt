# Failed route: derived eternity from a Jones recovery tower

Status: `[FAILED REALIZATION] [ABSTRACT CONJECTURE NOT KILLED] [POSITIVE SURVIVOR EXTRACTED]`

## Target

Realize the temporal anomaly
\[
  \varprojlim\nolimits^1 H_n
\]
canonically in a black-hole recovery tower built from nested operator algebras, Jones basic
constructions, conditional expectations, or exact recovery maps.

## Fatal obstruction

The natural bonding maps supplied by conditional expectations are surjective onto the included
subalgebras: a conditional expectation restricts to the identity on its range. A countable
surjective tower admits compatible lifts. Its first inverse-limit obstruction therefore vanishes.

Finite-dimensional Jones/recovery stages are still more hostile: their descending image chains
stabilize, so the corresponding towers satisfy the Mittag--Leffler condition. Passing to the
standard black-hole operator-algebra construction does not create the proposed anomaly.

Thus the natural Jones-tower realization does not merely lack a proof. Its proposed carrier kills
the effect.

## External collision

Crossed-product horizon algebras, Type II horizon factors, Jones basic constructions, canonical
shifts, index, and entropy already have established mathematical roles in black-hole information
and subfactor theory. Renaming their tower behavior a temporal anomaly would not create new
physics.

## Positive survivor

The failure isolates a sharper fact:

> The obstruction is not hidden in an inverse limit of ordinary recovery maps. It can occur only
> when two physically meaningful completion operations fail to commute.

Candidate completion operations include infinite observation time, thermodynamic size,
semiclassical scaling, zero noise, and unbounded spatial resolution. The relevant invariant is
therefore a comparison defect between completions, not a raw \(\varprojlim^1\) of the natural
recovery tower.

This survivor motivates a larger conjecture: no particular completion of finite operational
history is physically absolute.

## Formal status

Existing formal corpus proves the surjective-tower compatible-lift theorem and the relevant
finite-stage stabilization boundaries. No nonzero black-hole temporal anomaly is formalized or
claimed.

## Decision

Do not reuse Jones or conditional-expectation towers as evidence for derived eternity. Any future
revival must use a genuinely noncommuting pair of completion functors and must produce a finite
operational discriminator.
