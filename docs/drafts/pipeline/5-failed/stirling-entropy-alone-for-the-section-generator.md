# Stirling entropy alone for the section generator

Exact zero Stirling entropy places no size constraint on the ordinary section generator. Every positive rational is a balanced factorial ratio of entropy zero, and the actual generator has such a presentation at horizon at most one above the prime window, subject to the minimum horizon ten. Its logarithmic value then lies entirely in the Stirling remainder.

At fixed representable value and horizon at least six the possible entropies are dense. The infimum of the larger absolute term is $|\log q|/2$. The joint prime-coordinate theorem classifies exact attainment at all horizons from ten onward. Quantitative estimates on the coefficients or the signed remainder remain open.
