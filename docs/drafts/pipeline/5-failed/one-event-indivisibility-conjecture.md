## Semantic commitments

The conjecture is intended as physics, not a metaphor.

1. **One event.** “Event” means the complete physical actuality from which observable alternatives
   and correlations arise. It is not located at a point or time; points and times belong to derived
   descriptions.
2. **No independent parts.** No exact fundamental description admits two nonclassical subsystems
   whose states and reversible dynamics can be specified independently. Approximate subsystem
   independence may emerge for observers.
3. **Every spacetime.** Distinct Lorentzian spacetimes, topologies, causal orders, and putative
   multiverse branches are partial descriptions of the same event, not fundamentally separate
   containers.
4. **Every force.** Gravity, electromagnetism, weak interaction, and strong interaction are not
   four primitive couplings. A successful realization must derive them as distinct observable
   aspects of comparison among decompositions.
5. **No globally exact decomposition.** Any division into regions, particles, fields, clocks, or
   universes loses or hides physical structure that cannot be reconstructed inside that division
   alone.

Weakening “one event” to “one universal wavefunction on a supplied spacetime”, or weakening “no
independent parts” to ordinary entanglement, changes the conjecture and does not test it.

## Why this is presently a hunch

The following jointly motivate the conjecture but do not prove it:

- quantum theory makes correlations fundamental and exact subsystem factorization representation
  dependent;
- general relativity makes localization and temporal comparison dynamical rather than absolute;
- gauge descriptions use overlapping local representatives whose redundancy has no direct physical
  counterpart;
- black-hole and holographic results strain a naive identification of spatial regions with
  independent information containers;
- the exceptional quantum candidate already present in this programme is distinguished by failure
  of nonclassical composition;
- the HGT corpus proves exact cases in which all relative reporter data fail to recover a global
  common mode.

The conjecture is not inferred deductively from these facts. It is the proposed explanation of why
they point in the same direction.

## Incompatible world

The strongest competing ontology is:

> **Reality fundamentally consists of many local quantum systems embedded in, or assigned to,
> independently existing spacetime regions; gravity is a separate dynamical geometry.**

The two pictures must eventually make different finite predictions. If the one-event conjecture
cannot supply such a discriminator, it is scientifically inert even if mathematically consistent.

## Pre-registered kill conditions

The conjecture is false or scientifically unusable if any of the following survives serious audit:

1. a complete, empirically adequate fundamental theory requires exact independent nonclassical
   subsystems rather than merely approximate ones;
2. observer decompositions cannot recover Lorentzian causal order, quantum probabilities, and
   localized records without inserting spacetime or subsystem structure;
3. gravity and the Standard Model gauge forces require logically independent fundamental actions
   or unrelated state spaces;
4. the conjecture is equivalent, after definitions are removed, to a universal wavefunction,
   block-universe monism, ordinary relationalism, holography, or another existing parent theory;
5. every possible finite observation agrees with general relativity plus the Standard Model;
6. making “event”, “internal view”, or “decomposition” exact makes the statement circular or
   unfalsifiable.

## Hostile verdict

**Verdict: `REDISCOVERED`.**

The killing mechanism is not a counterexample. It is normalization into established parent
positions:

- priority monism already treats the cosmos as the one fundamental whole and ordinary objects as
  derivative;
- wavefunction monism already treats one universal quantum state or wave as the sole fundamental
  physical entity;
- quantum-first gravity already treats tensor-factor subsystem localization as non-fundamental and
  seeks a network of alternative quantum structures from which spacetime emerges;
- gravitational factorization problems and holographic emergence already deny that spatial regions
  straightforwardly define independent fundamental Hilbert factors;
- relational quantum mechanics already gives observer-relative events and approximately stable
  facts.

The clauses about all spacetimes and all forces are not an achieved novelty because this card
supplies no law deriving them. Without a discriminator, “one event” is a metaphysical
strengthening—not new physics.
