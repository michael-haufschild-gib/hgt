# Stein palettes classify finite full HT constraint geometry

- **ID:** `stein-palette-exact-ht-dimension-classification`
- **Stage:** 5-failed
- **Type:** FALSIFIED ROUTE / OVER-RESTRICTIVE HT QUOTIENT CLASSIFICATION
- **Scientific status:** `[RETIRED]`
- **Statement:** The intrinsic analytic dimension of a finite full Stanley--Reisner HT constraint
  geometry equals its Stein-palette dimension: the least dimension in which every facet complement
  is the kernel pattern of an exponential-linear quotient and the integral translation lattice of
  the facet embeds faithfully in that quotient.

## Terminal verdict

The proposed Stein reduction is not a necessary consequence of hypertranscendence.  The decisive
open lemma is contradicted by the parent geometry it was meant to refine: noncompact Cousin
quasi-tori may admit nondegenerate meromorphic functions, and the meromorphic field of a
noncompact quasi-torus is either constant or has infinite transcendence degree.  Capocasa--Catanese
further record that the quotient field generated by powers of one fixed tame line bundle can have
uncountable transcendence degree.  Thus absence of nonconstant holomorphic functions does not give
finite meromorphic translation-orbit capacity.

The correct quotient class is quasi-Abelian, characterized by generalized Riemann relations, not
Stein.  The surviving exact-classification question is continued separately in
`quasi-abelian-palette-exact-ht-dimension-classification.md`.

## Why this is the required strengthening

Finite constraint universality proves that every finite simplicial geometry can occur, but it does
not classify the analytic cost of occurrence.  The current necessary and sufficient invariants
leave a gap

```text
peDim(Delta) <= htDim(Delta) <= ppDim(Delta).                (1)
```

Noncompact period escape is too weak: a noncompact connected Abelian complex Lie group may contain
a Cousin direction that supplies no freely generated meromorphic differential orbit.  A periodic
pencil is too restrictive: it allows only one `C*` character for each facet, whereas a scalar
meromorphic source may use several additive or multiplicative Stein coordinates simultaneously.

The proposed invariant isolates the missing middle object.  Equality with `htDim` would turn the
realization theorem into an exact necessary-and-sufficient classification rather than another
upper or lower bound.

## Stein-palette separations

Let `Delta` be a finite simplicial complex on a nonempty essential vertex set `V`.  A
**Stein-palette separation in ambient dimension `d`** consists of `Q`-linearly independent vectors

```text
a_v in C^d,  v in V,                                       (2)
```

and, for every facet `A`, nonnegative integers `p_A,q_A` with `p_A+q_A>0` and complex-linear maps

```text
L_A:C^d -> C^(p_A),
M_A:C^d -> C^(q_A).                                        (3)
```

Write

```text
Phi_A(z)=(L_A z, exp(2 pi i M_A z))
  in C^(p_A) times (C*)^(q_A).                              (4)
```

The conditions are:

```text
Phi_A(a_v)=e  iff  v notin A;                              (5)
Z^A -> C^(p_A) times (C*)^(q_A),
n   |-> Phi_A(sum_(v in A)n_v a_v)                          (6)
```

is injective.  Define

```text
spDim(Delta)=min{d:a Stein-palette separation exists}.      (7)
```

Condition (5) is exact facet incidence.  Condition (6) is not merely point separation: it says the
whole integral structural orbit inside a facet survives in its Stein quotient.

Every periodic-pencil separation is a Stein-palette separation with `(p_A,q_A)=(0,1)`, so

```text
spDim(Delta)<=ppDim(Delta).                                 (8)
```

In dimension one, (5) forces the already proved simplex-or-pure-principal phase: a nonzero additive
character cannot kill a nonzero complement step, while two globally `Q`-independent complement
steps cannot both be periods of one nonzero exponential character.  Thus `spDim=1` has the same
boundary as `htDim=1` and `ppDim=1`.

## Main classification `[CONJECTURE]`

For every finite essential simplicial complex,

```text
htDim(Delta)=spDim(Delta).                                  (9)
```

Consequently the complete dimension hierarchy sharpens to

```text
peDim(Delta) <= htDim(Delta)=spDim(Delta) <= ppDim(Delta)
                                               <= ffsDim(Delta).          (10)
```

The theorem has two logically different halves.

### Sufficiency: a Stein-palette orbit theorem `[CONJECTURE]`

Let `S_j=C^(p_j) times (C*)^(q_j)` and let `H_j` be a finitely generated subgroup of `S_j`.
For finitely many nonconstant exponential-linear maps `Phi_j:C^d->S_j` whose restrictions to the
chosen integral orbit lattices are injective, there exist holomorphic functions `h_j` on `S_j`
such that all distinct functions

```text
h_j(Phi_j(z) h),  j fixed and h in H_j,                     (11)
```

are jointly differentially algebraically independent over `C(z_1,...,z_d)` for one constant
directional derivation.

The intended proof is coefficient-uniform Baire avoidance on the Stein manifolds `S_j`.  For each
finite relation support, choose evaluation nodes escaping every prescribed compact set.  Distinct
group translates give distinct nodes; Cartan interpolation on Stein manifolds prescribes the
finite jets; the directional-jet map is triangular with nonzero top diagonal after choosing the
derivation outside finitely many kernels.  Countably many relation patterns are then removed by
Baire category.

Given (11), put

```text
Y_A(z)=h_A(Phi_A(z)),
F(z)=sum_(A in Fac(Delta)) Y_A(z).                           (12)
```

Equations (5)--(6) make the facet restrictions exact.  The established localized
Stanley--Reisner and differential-operator matroid theorem then gives the complete nonlinear
matroid, proving `htDim<=spDim`.

### Necessity: Cousin directions have finite orbit capacity `[OPEN CORE]`

Let `F` be an exact full HT realization.  The established facet separator gives, for every facet,

```text
G_A=q_A(T)F,                                                (13)
```

whose full integral facet orbit is differentially algebraically independent and whose exact period
group contains precisely the complementary vertex steps.  Put

```text
X_A=C^d/Per(G_A).                                           (14)
```

The quotient is a connected Abelian complex Lie group and `G_A` descends to it.  Let

```text
rho_A:X_A -> S_A=C^(p_A) times (C*)^(q_A)                   (15)
```

be its maximal Stein quotient.  The decisive missing theorem is:

> **Cousin finite-orbit-capacity lemma `[OPEN CORE]`.** If a nonzero finitely generated translation
> subgroup lies in `ker rho_A`, then the orbit of any one meromorphic function under that subgroup
> has finite differential transcendence degree over the rational coordinate field.

The full facet orbit in (13) has unbounded differential transcendence degree.  The lemma would
therefore force the integral facet lattice to inject into `S_A`.  Lifting `rho_A` to the universal
cover gives the exponential-linear map (4), while exact periods give (5).  Hence
`spDim<=htDim`, closing (9).

The lemma must be proved for one function and its translation orbit, not asserted from the weaker
fact that a Cousin group has no nonconstant holomorphic functions.  The likely mechanism is that
the meromorphic field of the Cousin kernel is a finite-transcendence-degree extension and every
individual meromorphic function on the total group uses only finitely many Stein-coefficient
functions; kernel translations fix those coefficients and cannot create an infinite free
differential orbit.

## Attacks already survived or failed

1. `[FALSIFIED AS A GENERAL TARGET]` `htDim=peDim`.  Noncompactness alone does not remove Cousin
   directions and therefore does not supply a free meromorphic orbit.
2. `[DOWNGRADED TO PARENT THEORY]` Reflection polarization forcing Gorenstein or Poincare-dual
   Stanley--Reisner geometry.  Perfect duality implies the familiar Gorenstein-star condition, but
   deriving that perfect pairing from a functional equation is the actual missing theorem.
3. `[DOWNGRADED AS CIRCULAR]` Euler locality forcing a flag complex.  If locality is defined as
   pairwise generation, flagness is built into the definition rather than discovered.
4. `[REJECTED AS TOO WEAK]` More examples with `htDim=2`.  They enlarge the realized family but do
   not close the necessary/sufficient gap.
5. `[LIVE ATTACK]` The Stein palette may secretly compress to one periodic pencil per facet after
   redesigning the translation vectors.  A strict example with `spDim<ppDim`, or a proof that the
   two invariants always agree, is required to determine whether (9) creates a genuinely new
   stratum or upgrades the existing periodic-pencil theorem to an exact classification.

## RH-facing consequence `[CONJECTURE]`

If (9) holds, a finite prime/Euler HT route cannot choose its analytic carrier dimension freely.
Its exact relation complex determines a minimal system of additive and multiplicative Stein escape
channels.  In particular, every claimed one-coordinate RH carrier must lie in the pure-principal
phase, while every nonpure relation geometry must display at least two intrinsic Stein escape
directions.  To distinguish zeta from universal HT realizations, the Euler and functional-equation
markings must act on this minimal palette and sew its upper and lower ends; adding arbitrary
ambient coordinates cannot replace that intrinsic quotient geometry.

This is a carrier constraint, not an RH proof and not a claim that zeta currently realizes a finite
Stanley--Reisner complex.
