## Frozen truth-hunch

For every physically complete process or region \(\mathfrak P\) and every complete operational
observer \(O\), there exists one representation-independent **distinction object**
\[
  \mathbb D(\mathfrak P,O)
\]
which is prior to a spacetime manifold, a tensor-product decomposition, a Hamiltonian, and an
external time parameter.

It has two canonical, non-equivalent polarizations:

1. a **coexistence polarization** \(\mathsf S(\mathfrak P,O)\), whose physical content is which
   distinctions admit independent simultaneous composition; and
2. a **recurrence polarization** \(\mathsf T(\mathfrak P,O)\), whose physical content is which
   distinctions persist as independent temporal characters and how their complete return
   collisions are distributed.

The universal law is not that either polarization determines the other in isolation. It is that
physically admissible pairs are exactly the two polarizations of one \(\mathbb D\), related by a
canonical involutive reciprocity
\[
  \boxed{
  \mathfrak R_{\mathbb D}:
  \operatorname{Def}\!\bigl(\mathsf S(\mathfrak P,O)\bigr)
  \;\simeq\;
  \operatorname{Char}\!\bigl(\mathsf T(\mathfrak P,O)\bigr).
  }
  \tag{CR}
\]
Here \(\operatorname{Def}(\mathsf S)\) is the complete obstruction to faithful independent
coexistence and \(\operatorname{Char}(\mathsf T)\) is the complete persistent recurrence-character
object. Neither side is presently defined by known mathematics in the required generality.
Equation `(CR)` is the frozen physical hunch; selecting a category that makes it true is forbidden
unless that category is independently forced by operational physics.

Under every physically valid change of observer, subsystem division, or local reference frame,
\(\mathbb D\) is transported without loss. What one description counts as simultaneously
independent, another may count as temporal, constrained, or boundary structure, but the full
distinction object and `(CR)` remain invariant.

Standard physics starts with things arranged in space and evolves them through time. This

```text

but:    one law of physical distinction
                   |

                   |
```

An event is a change of polarization of \(\mathbb D\), not motion inside a pre-existing time.
Interaction is conversion between coexistence and recurrence structure, not an extra relation
between pre-existing things.

## First hostile attack: fixed coexistence, different temporal character rank

Equation `(CR)` is false before any continuum or gravitational issue appears.

Use the same two-qubit Hilbert space
\[
  \mathcal H=\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^2
\]
with the same tensor factors, commuting local observable algebras, computational product basis,
and independently executable local controls. Compare the diagonal Hamiltonians
\[
  H_{\rm rat}=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2,4),
  \qquad
  H_{\rm irr}=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2,\sqrt2).
\]
For a state having nonzero weight on all four basis vectors, the persistent energy-gap character
group of \(H_{\rm rat}\) has rational rank \(1\), while that of \(H_{\rm irr}\) has rational rank
\(2\). The first group is generated over \(\mathbb Z\) by one energy unit; the second contains the
rationally independent generators \(1\) and \(\sqrt2\).

The physical independent-composition structure is identical. Both Hamiltonians are diagonal in the
same product basis and both differ from an additive two-site Hamiltonian only through a diagonal
two-site interaction coefficient. Thus a coexistence polarization whose content is which
distinctions compose independently cannot distinguish their temporal character ranks.

There are only two escapes, and both abandon `(CR)`:

1. if \(\operatorname{Def}(\mathsf S)\) retains only independent-composition structure, the two
   left-hand sides agree while the right-hand sides have different rank; or
2. if it is enlarged to retain exact interaction coefficients and the resulting spectrum, it no
   longer describes coexistence and makes the reciprocity true by inserting the temporal dynamics.

Verdict:
