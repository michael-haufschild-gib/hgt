## Exact obstruction

Let `L(p) = log p`.  Differentiation of a prime-supported Dirichlet carrier multiplies its
coefficient stream by `-L`.  Hence the differential field generated by a source class `[c]` sees
all classes `[L^k c]`, not only the complex line through `[c]`.

For example, the bounded streams

```text

d(p) = 1
```

are complex-linearly independent modulo finite support, whereas the derivative of the carrier of
`c` is a nonzero scalar multiple of the carrier of `d` (up to the fixed rotated-derivative
convention).  Thus item 2 of the former conjecture is false, and its field-classification items
cannot follow.
