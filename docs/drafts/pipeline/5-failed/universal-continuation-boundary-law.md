## Reinterpretation

Physics conventionally begins with states in spacetime and gives laws for their evolution.
Reverse that order.

The primitive physical object is the oriented family of possible continuations of an event. States,
particles, fields, and spacetime are not inputs. They are the stable public boundary data left when
the full continuation family is quotiented to what one observer or experiment can compare.

Two possible continuations \(A\) and \(B\) need not compose in the same order. Their closed return
defect is

\[
  \Omega(A,B)=ABA^{-1}B^{-1}.
\tag{UCB1}
\]

This is not initially a loop in a pre-existing spacetime and not initially an internal gauge
holonomy. It is a source-level disagreement between possible next physical acts.

## Modal physical content

The source object classifies processes, not only the state produced in one realized run. Let
\(\Phi_\gamma\) denote the operational channel associated with a continuation history. It is
possible for two channels to be different as processes while agreeing on the state actually
present:

\[
  \Phi_{\gamma_1}\ne\Phi_{\gamma_2},
  \qquad
  \Phi_{\gamma_1}(\rho)=\Phi_{\gamma_2}(\rho).
\tag{UCB2}
\]

Standard state-sourced physics permits the unused process distinction in `(UCB2)` to have no
further effect. The present ontology conjectures that a gauge-invariant difference of physical
continuation capacity is itself real. If it survives in the source return class, the jointly
faithful boundary must expose it even when the realized state and every standard source record
coincide.

This is stronger than saying that all Feynman paths contribute to an amplitude. It says two
available transformation laws that are indistinguishable on the actual input can differ
gravitationally because they act differently on unrealized inputs. This modal claim is the sharp
worldview break and a primary kill target.

## Preliminary primary-source collision audit

1. Oreshkov--Costa--Brukner construct operational quantum processes without a definite global
   causal order and explicitly suggest emergence of spacetime in a classical limit
   ([arXiv:1105.4464](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.4464)). This kills any novelty claim based only
   on “quantum operations before causal order.”
2. Markopoulou's quantum causal histories attach Hilbert spaces and local evolution maps to a
   causal pre-spacetime ([arXiv:hep-th/9904009](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9904009)). The present
   conjecture must derive, not assume, causal order and Hilbert structure to remain distinct.
3. Hardy's causaloid framework starts with operational probabilities, allows non-fixed causal
   structure, and organizes a theory by physical compression
   ([arXiv:gr-qc/0608043](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0608043)). “Operational composition first”
   is therefore not itself new.
4. Oeckl's general-boundary formulation assigns quantum state spaces to arbitrary spacetime
   boundaries ([arXiv:hep-th/0306025](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0306025)). Boundary-first
   language is not new; the proposed distinction is a boundary of continuation possibility rather
   than a boundary of an assumed spacetime region.
5. The quantum no-hiding theorem says information bleached from one subsystem resides in the rest
   of the universe ([arXiv:1004.5073](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.5073)). It does not select gravity,
   a physical boundary, or four force projections. `(UCB5)` is nontrivial only if it strengthens
   no-hiding in exactly those directions.
6. Harlow--Ooguri establish, within AdS/CFT, no bulk global symmetries, compact internal gauge
   groups, and completeness of charged representations
   ([arXiv:1810.05337](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05337),
   [arXiv:1810.05338](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05338)). These are strong known shadows of a
   faithful physical boundary, not evidence for exactly the Standard Model projections.
7. Marolf argues that the on-shell gravitational Hamiltonian is a boundary term and that suitable
   asymptotic observable algebras are complete in perturbative gravity
   ([arXiv:0808.2842](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2842)). Raju conjectures/argues related
   state-determination by boundary observables
   ([arXiv:1903.11073](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11073),
   [arXiv:2110.05470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05470)). Thus “gravity makes bulk state
   information public at a boundary” is already an active principle. Novelty requires faithfulness
   for operationally distinct continuation histories plus the exact cross-sector decomposition.
8. Donnelly--Giddings show that, to first gravitational order, exterior measurements can be
   insensitive to localized quantum information beyond total Poincare charges
   ([arXiv:1805.11095](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.11095)); Giddings further emphasizes dressing
   flexibility and asymptotic insensitivity to internal details
   ([arXiv:1903.06160](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.06160)). This is direct hostile pressure on the
   gravitational child prediction, not supporting evidence.
9. Standard displacement memory is fixed by radiation energy flux and BMS supertranslation data
   ([arXiv:1411.5745](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5745)). A memory of matched-source quantum order
   would be a new charge channel, not a relabelling of established memory.
10. Larger-group gauge/gravity unifications already combine spin and internal connections, for
    example through an enlarged Lorentz group
    ([arXiv:1602.02295](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.02295)). The present conjecture survives this
    collision only if the four sectors are forced as quotient faces of a source object rather than
    embedded by hand in a chosen parent group.

The ontology does not survive its first physical realization.

1. Indefinite causal order, process matrices, quantum causal histories, general-boundary
   formulations, no-hiding, boundary completeness, gravitational dressing, asymptotic memory, and
   anomaly/descent theory already occupy the weaker claims that processes precede causal order,
   local data can fail to globalize, and missing bulk information can reside at a boundary.

   in a programmable processor require distinguishable program records
   (Nielsen--Chuang, <https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9703032>). Including controls and apparatus
   turns the modal distinction into an actual carried distinction.
4. If every actual carrier is restored and the complete gravitational influence functional is
   matched, unitarity and standard source coupling leave no private process residue. If a record
   remains, ordinary physics may couple to that record and `(UCB5)` has not discovered a new
   boundary law.
5. The proposed exact four projections are inserted from the observed force list; no source axiom
   selects them.

Thus “gravity weighs unrealized possibilities” is not an admissible physical interpretation.
Unrealized channel action on unused inputs has no independent stress source, while a physically
implemented program has an actual record. This activates kill conditions 1, 2, 4, 5, and 8.
