## Why this currently feels physically possible

This is abductive motivation, not a proof sketch.

1. In gravity, causal placement is relational: matter and clocks participate in the geometry used
   to say when an event occurred.
2. In gauge theory, complete internal identity is relational: comparing phase, weak orientation,
   or colour at separated events requires physical transport.
3. Quantum theory makes relational questions capable of being incompatible rather than merely
   unknown.
4. Gravity and gauge theory both make comparison path-dependent, yet treating their symmetries as
   an unexplained direct product has not yielded a unique theory of everything.
5. A complementarity law would explain why unification has resisted treating spacetime and
   internal symmetry as simultaneously independent classical structures.

None of these points entails the frozen statement.

## Hostile reviewer objections

1. **Observable ambiguity.** “Complete identity” and “complete causal order” are not established
   observables. Without source-pure operational reporter families, complementarity is unfalsifiable.
2. **Gauge-coordinate error.** Internal orientation is gauge redundancy. A commutator involving
   an unphysical frame coordinate has no empirical content.
3. **Generic-noncommutativity error.** Choosing one noncommuting identity reporter and one clock
   reporter proves nothing universal. Completeness and a state-independent obstruction are
   essential.
4. **Direct-product counterpressure.** Standard Model QFT on a classical spacetime permits sharp
   charge sectors and fixed causal structure. The conjecture must show why this is only an
   approximation to the exact gravity-coupled theory.
5. **Coleman--Mandula pressure.** A literal nontrivial mixing of ordinary bosonic spacetime and
   internal \(S\)-matrix symmetries contradicts the theorem's hypotheses. The new law must locate
   and physically justify the failed hypothesis, then recover direct-product scattering in its
   domain.
6. **Indefinite-order false positive.** Quantum switches already exhibit causal-order phenomena

7. **Group insertion.** Writing \(S(U(2)\times U(3))\) in the conjecture does not derive its global
   quotient, hypercharges, chirality, generations, Higgs sector, or anomaly cancellation.
8. **No dynamics.** An uncertainty relation does not by itself produce Einstein or
   Yang--Mills--Higgs equations.
9. **No scale.** No lower bound, coupling relation, or finite signal is known.
10. **No evidence.** Existing experiments do not demonstrate identity-conditioned causal order.

The conjecture is therefore not presently a result, theorem, or manuscript claim.

## First kill-or-survive models

### A. Flat-holonomy pressure test

In classical Einstein--Yang--Mills theory, \(F_A=0\) makes the Yang--Mills stress tensor vanish,
while a flat connection on a non-simply-connected bundle may still have nontrivial holonomy.
[de Lima](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05373) explicitly treats a Poincaré--Einstein metric together
with a flat connection as the trivial Einstein--Yang--Mills background.

Thus classical causal geometry need not determine global internal identity. The frozen conjecture
allows a classical limit, but it dies if quantization of such a sector admits physical states for
which complete relational geometry and complete holonomy identity become jointly sharp or if
\(\mathfrak K_R=0\) at finite gravitational and gauge coupling.

### B. Perturbative gravitational-splitting pressure test

[Giddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.06160) constructs perturbative dressings for which
gravitational observations outside a region depend only on total Poincaré charges, not on detailed
internal matter data. Consequently, the proposed matched-source cross experiment has no generic
first-order signal supplied merely by gravitational dressing.

The conjecture survives only if:

1. its causal reporter is not an arbitrary asymptotic dressing observable;
2. a dressing-independent cross-response appears beyond the perturbative splitting; and
3. two states with identical Poincaré data but distinct complete internal identity are separated by
   that response.

If gravitational splitting with this independence persists for complete causal reporters in the
full theory, the conjecture is false.

### C. Constraint-algebra pressure test

The canonical Einstein--Yang--Mills constraint algebra is not a naive direct sum:
[Ghalati](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3651) finds gauge-generator contributions in the gravitational
constraint brackets. This is not evidence for the conjecture. Constraint mixing may disappear on
physical gauge-invariant observables and supplies neither complementarity nor the Standard Model
group.

### D. Exceptional same-corner pressure test

The corpus proves unconditionally in Lean that a concrete \(SU(2)\) half-turn acts nontrivially on
the same \(H_2(\mathbb C)\) corner used as a four-dimensional Minkowski-vector model. Hence that
corner cannot simultaneously carry an independent internal weak kernel and the standard spatial
rotation action.

This obstruction is consistent with the hunch that weak identity and causal orientation are not
independent readings. It does not prove complementarity: literally identifying weak \(SU(2)\) with
spatial rotation is phenomenologically false, and an ordinary matrix noncommutator is not a
universal force law. A viable realization needs physically distinct gauge-invariant reporters whose
incompatibility is derived from one exceptional object.
