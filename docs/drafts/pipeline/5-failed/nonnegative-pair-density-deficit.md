# Nonnegative pair-density deficits cannot exclude seventy percent

- **Stage:** 5-failed
- **Route:** `Aδ₀+(1−g)dx` with integrable `0≤g≤1` and
  `ĝ(α)=A−|α|` on `[-1,1]`.
- **Obstruction:** Parseval forces `A≤(3+√(11/3))/4<13/10`.
- **Positive survivor:** allowing signed `g≤1`, the quadratic and linear
  mass of its negative part is at least `2A²−3A+2/3`; at `A≥13/10`,
  at least `11/75`. The general energy statement assumes `g∈L¹∩L²`.
- **Paper proof and Lean:** complete in
  [Pair-density excess is forced](../3-formalized/pair-density-excess-is-forced.md).

The obstruction concerns nonnegative deficits only. It leaves signed
deficits and the damped quadrature candidate open.
