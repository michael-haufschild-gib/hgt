# Constraint-reflecting inner functions

- **ID:** constraint-reflecting-inner-functions
- **Stage:** 5-failed
- **Type:** RETIRED · PARENT-THEORY ABSTRACTION
- **Status:** `[RETIRED — HYPOTHESES ENCODE THE PROOF]`
- **Statement:** An inner function `g` with a superpolynomial affine microscope whose limiting map
  has injective differential-jet pullback reflects differential algebraicity: any algebraic
  differential relation for `u ∘ g` forces a nonzero local polynomial jet relation for `u` on the
  limiting image.  Consequently every locally hypertranscendental outer germ remains
  hypertranscendental after composition with such a `g`.

## Proposed static object

Let `aₙ` be microscope centers, `sₙ ≠ 0` microscope scales, and suppose

\[
g(a_n+s_nx)\longrightarrow \phi(x)
\]

locally with all jets on a fixed domain `U`.  Require that the scale defeats every coefficient
growth generated by translating a rational-coefficient differential equation to `aₙ`; the Gamma
instance is `aₙ=-n` and `sₙ=(-1)^{n+1}/n!`.  Require also that substitution of the jets of
`u ∘ φ` into polynomial jet constraints is injective back to the jets of `u` on `φ(U)`.

These are static source constraints.  No carrier, transport law, or generative flow is introduced.

## Retirement verdict

The proposed class does not presently define an independent mathematical phenomenon.  Its
``scale defeats every coefficient growth'' and ``jet pullback is injective'' clauses package the
two hard conclusions needed by the Gamma proof, while the remaining compact-open convergence,
initial-form selection, and affine rescaling are standard parent mathematics.  Weakening the
hypotheses enough to become intrinsic reopens the entire dominance problem; keeping them strong
enough for the proof makes the theorem routine.

Accordingly this abstraction is retired rather than promoted.  The concrete factorial Gamma
microscope and its `Γ ∘ Γ` consequence remain independently live results; no novelty is claimed for
placing them inside a bespoke class.

## Expected theorem

For each nonzero differential polynomial annihilating `u ∘ g`, the dominant joint valuation in
coefficient growth and derivative scale selects a nonzero autonomous initial form annihilating
`u ∘ φ`.  Injectivity of the `φ`-jet pullback then produces a nonzero polynomial-coefficient
relation for `u` on `φ(U)`.

The Gamma factorial-composition theorem is the first proven instance, with `U` the left half-plane,
`φ(x)=-1/x`, and `φ(U)` the right half-plane.
