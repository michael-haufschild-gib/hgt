# Failed route: HB finite-local-angular threshold cancellation

Status: killed; Lean formalized

## Route

Use one source-adapted zero-frequency threshold atom generated by a finite-order local angular
differential operator.  After separation, its coefficient is `P(Q)` for one polynomial `P` in the
Teukolsky angular eigenvalue.  Require it to cancel the chiral AIME mass jet on every positive real
Hardy-line Xi fibre.

## Obstruction

The AIME coefficient is exactly `-12 / (Q + 2)`.  Since `Im Q(t) = t / 4`, arbitrarily large
packets of distinct positive Hardy zeros yield equally large packets of distinct `Q` values.  A
polynomial agreeing with `12 / (Q + 2)` on all of them would make `(X + 2)P(X) - 12` identically
zero, contradicted at `X = -2`.

Closed Lean certificate:
`RHTower.d4HBFiniteLocalAngularThresholdCancellationRouteKilled`.

## Positive survivor

Successful source-adapted threshold cancellation must leave the polynomial angular functional
calculus.  The exact required coefficient exposes three survivors:

1. the genuinely nonlocal angular resolvent `(Q + 2)⁻¹`;
2. an infinite-order angular response not represented by any polynomial;
3. fibre-adaptive data that is not one universal local operator.

This is sharper than the negative statement: finite differential order itself is the obstruction,
while the reciprocal resolvent is the exact replacement target.  No claim is made here that any
survivor follows from the literal Einstein--Teukolsky source; that remains the hard HB PDE burden.
