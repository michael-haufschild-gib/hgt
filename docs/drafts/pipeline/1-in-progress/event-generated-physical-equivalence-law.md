## Frozen technical conjecture

Primitive physical source data do not come with a fixed state space, gauge group, tensor
factorization, observable algebra, spacetime, causal order, or equivalence relation saying which
presentations describe the same physical possibility.

For every finite source history \(H\), physical law source-generates:

1. a moduli object \(\mathfrak C^+(H)\) of positive, spectator-free continuations;
2. a physical congruence \(\sim_H\) on their presentations;
3. a descended report object \(\mathfrak R(H)\); and
4. complete noninvertible comparison data for every event
   \(e:H\rightsquigarrow H'\), relating \(\sim_H\) to \(\sim_{H'}\).

The congruence is not arbitrary. Conjecturally, it is the maximal source-compatible identification
that simultaneously:

1. leaves no nontrivial report sector untouched by every admissible future;
2. leaves no nontrivial fixed idempotent representing a permanently disconnected spectator;
3. preserves positive composition and complete source records; and
4. keeps every non-gauge distinction separable by some admissible future continuation.

An objective event therefore changes both physical data and the rule deciding which changes are
mere presentation. Time is the ordered, non-horizontalizable history of those changes. Internal
gauge structure is source-preserving automorphism data of local descended reports. Spacetime is
the public compatibility geometry of event-relative physical congruences. Gravity and the three
gauge forces must be derived as the four stable continuum response sectors of the **complete
comparison law**, including boundary self-law and reciprocal transfer—not identified with bare
curvature or nonfactorization.

This paragraph is one conjecture. No physical clause is proved.

## Hostile correction: equivalence is downstream of composition

noncommutative quantum algebra, noncentral projections can represent ordinary physical
alternatives rather than disconnected sectors. Restricting to central idempotents detects direct
sums but misses tensor spectators: tensoring by an independent factor can preserve factoriality,
trivial centre, and the entire selected vertical invariant while changing the physical theory.

to that supplied finite commutative candidate family.

construction relative to a supplied behavior and intervention alphabet. It can minimize or
identify a theory once its future questions are known; it does not select nature's questions,
composite outcomes, or physical source.

Therefore:

```text

  is not the first missing variable;

  must come first;

physical equivalence
  is the quotient induced by that generated law.
```

The stronger post-failure mathematical frontier is frozen in
[`consequence-trirepresentability-universal-law.md`](consequence-trirepresentability-universal-law.md):

> **Every event turns possibility into fact, fact into law, and law into the next possibility.
