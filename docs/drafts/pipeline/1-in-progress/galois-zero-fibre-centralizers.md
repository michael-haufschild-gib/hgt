# The infinite zero action and its finite centralizers

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CANDIDATE
- **Question:** Which permutations of the complete source fibres extend to field automorphisms?

Let `K` be the complete-fibre source field and `L=ℚ(all w)`. The
[formalized fibre theorem](../3-formalized/galois-zero-full-fibre-field.md)
shows that `Gal(L/K)` preserves each finite ordinate fibre and commutes with
conjugation. Every real zero is fixed individually.

On a fibre with `r` distinct nonreal conjugate pairs, its permutation image
therefore lies in `C₂ ≀ S_r`. Construct the faithful whole-field action in
the product of these finite centralizers, with no height cutoff. Retain
analytic multiplicity and distinguish support permutations from actions on
labelled copies.

Use the normal fields generated by complete fibres up to increasing ordinate
height as finite stages. Their union must be proved to equal the entire zero
field, and compatible finite automorphisms must extend uniquely. Identify the
actual Galois group with this inverse limit and with a closed subgroup of the
product of finite fibre centralizers.

Determine which algebraic relations among fibres constrain the image and
which compatible finite actions extend. Equality with the full product is
not asserted. No theorem on this card is claimed formalized.
