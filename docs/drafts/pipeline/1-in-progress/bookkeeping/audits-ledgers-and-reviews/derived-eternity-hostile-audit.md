# Hostile audit: derived eternity conjecture

Status: `[CONJECTURE SURVIVES AS A QUESTION] [NO PHYSICAL REALIZATION] [NATURE: FAIL]`

Target:
`derived-eternity-conjecture.md`.

## Antagonist verdict

The ontological jump is large enough. The science is not yet earned.

The sentence

> information can be absent from every finite view and survive in how all finite views fail to
> fit together

has an exact mathematical parent: derived inverse limits, towers of torsors, phantom maps, Milnor
sequences, and obstruction to globalization. None of that mathematics is new by itself.

The candidate-new physics would be:

1. a canonically generated observer tower in an accepted physical theory;
2. a nonzero derived obstruction forced by that theory;
3. an observer-invariant conservation or transfer law;
4. a finite or asymptotic signature distinguishing the obstruction from ordinary hidden state;
5. a black-hole, cosmological, or quantum-field realization where the obstruction answers a live
   question.

No item in that list is proved.

## Immediate mathematical attacks

### A. Natural coarsening towers often kill the anomaly

If every restriction map is surjective, compatible choices can be lifted recursively. The corpus
already proves this exact statement for arbitrary towers of nonempty types.

### B. Finite-dimensional stages are hostile to nonstabilization

At a fixed finite-dimensional stage, descending images stabilize. Finite-dimensional affine
splitting towers are therefore Mittag--Leffler. A nonzero \(\varprojlim^1\) requires an
infinite-dimensional, topological, nonclosed-range, or otherwise non-Mittag--Leffler boundary.

### C. A name does not construct a defect

An inverse system of acyclic objects remains zero under a derived limit. A valid anomaly must come
from a nonexact comparison—such as failure of completion, specialization, recovery, or
base-change to commute—not from relabeling zero finite fibers.

### D. Ordinary state restriction is probably the wrong tower

Finite quantum density matrices admit surjective partial-trace extensions after adjoining a fixed
ancilla state. Compatible local states also have standard extension mechanisms in operator
algebraic settings. A black-hole anomaly cannot be asserted for the naive state tower.

### E. Operational invisibility threatens falsifiability

A class invisible at every finite stage is not automatically physical. The theory must prove a
consequence for stabilization rates, asymptotic recovery cost, limit-order dependence, entropy,
or another measurable structure. Otherwise the class is metaphysical surplus.

## Corpus evidence

These are useful infrastructure. They do not supply a new physical law.

## External collision boundary

Initial primary-source search found no direct use of \(\varprojlim^1\) as a black-hole information
or temporal-observer invariant. That is not a complete priority audit.

Closely adjacent occupied fields include:

- operational general relativity built from scalar coincidences;
- relational quantum clocks and changes of clock;
- causal and metric reconstruction from operational or propagator data;
- algebraic quantum field theory net cohomology;
- clock-dressed crossed-product algebras in black-hole and de Sitter physics;
- Type II horizon algebras and generalized entropy;
- standard phantom-map and derived-limit obstruction theory.

The crossed-product literature is especially relevant because adding an observer clock changes
the algebraic type and makes entropy well-defined in important gravitational models. A derived
observer-tower law must add a theorem not already supplied by modular flow or crossed products.

## Live physical towers to test

### 1. Nested recovery-map tower

At radiation cutoff \(n\), let \(R_n\) be the space of recovery channels satisfying a declared
exact operator-algebra reconstruction task. Restrictions need not be surjective. Required result:
all finite tasks solvable, no coherent exact recovery, and obstruction calculated independently.

Risk: finite-dimensional channel spaces may force stabilization; task must be field-theoretic or
have a genuine infinite boundary.

### 2. Nested horizon-algebra tower

Use a decreasing family of exterior regions approaching a Killing horizon and the induced local
algebras, modular flows, or clock-dressed crossed products. Required result: identify a canonical
short exact tower whose boundary morphism has entropy or recoverability content.

Risk: standard AQFT intersection, split-property, or crossed-product theorems may already make the
candidate obstruction vanish or reduce it to known superselection data.

### 3. Discrete winding-clock tower

Use topological charge or clock-winding groups with non-surjective multiplication maps, not real
linear state spaces. Required result: a physical protocol where failure of coherent infinite root
choice changes an asymptotic interference or recurrence law.

Risk: engineered topology may be mathematically clean but physically irrelevant to gravity.

### 4. Infinite local-interaction recovery tower

Let each stage expose a larger local region or higher replica order. Required result: every finite
visible recurrence/recovery constraint is satisfiable while the completed constraint system is
not, with failure tied to an accepted infinite-system phase.

Risk: this may be ordinary thermodynamic noncommutation rather than a new derived invariant.

## Decision gate

Do not promote derived eternity to master-paper law until one live tower passes:

1. canonical construction;
2. unconditional nonzero obstruction;
3. vanishing theorem for a complementary phase;
4. operational signature;
5. external novelty;
6. physical significance independent of the formal apparatus.

Until then, use the conjecture to search for a physical tower, not to decorate the recurrence
paper.
