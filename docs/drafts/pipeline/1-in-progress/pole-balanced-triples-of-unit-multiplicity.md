# Pole-balanced triples of unit multiplicity

On a signed divisor with two positive unit graphs and one negative unit graph, let the positive square-root conductors be \(u\le v\). The negative square-root conductor must be \(u+v\). Set \(x=\log(v/u)\), \(d=2\log(1+e^{-x})\), \(K=\Re S\). Its degree is
\[
3K(0)-2K(d)+2[K(2x)-K(2x+d)].
\]
At actual zero width \(w\), the spline derivative on \([0,t]\) is bounded by \(Le^{wt}\). Since \(0\le d\le2e^{-x}\), the degree differs from \(K(0)\) by at most \(C e^{-(1-2w)x}\). The known strip gives \(1-2w>0\). Thus arbitrarily separated conductors recover the single-graph degree. The proposed bound for their ordinary prime products should hold with one base for all unit triples.
