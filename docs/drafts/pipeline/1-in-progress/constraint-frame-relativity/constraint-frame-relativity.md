## 0. Direct answer

The candidate “something” casting the corpus shadows is not a hidden scalar, determinant, operator,
matroid, topology, or source class. CFR conjectures a **stratified source-law space whose observer
fibres have four formally distinct directions of change**:

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal K=\int_{s\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{law}}}\mathcal F(s),
 \qquad
 \mathcal F(s)=
 \bigl(
 \text{coefficient realization},
 \text{prolongation index},
 \text{cofinal aperture}
 \bigr),
 \quad
 \mathcal H\rightrightarrows\mathcal K.}
\tag{CFR.1}
\]

The source-law direction is not a fifth symmetric axis. It is the stratified base: its morphisms change the
relation, differential, contact, domain, exactness, or inertia law while the four observer-fibre
coordinates may remain fixed. The four fibre directions are kinematics. A source-normal
constitutive section is the analogue of a dynamical field: its jet says which law deformation an
arithmetic intervention actually produces. Route formulas are projections of that constitutive
jet. Without it, a four-axis atlas can package unrelated routes but predicts nothing. Semidirect
product notation is justified only on strata where an actual law action on observer fibres is
constructed; no such global action is assumed.

A literal Euler-shell HGT object carrying all four directions is constructed. The power morphism
`P_r(k)=k^r` now supplies one restricted three-doctrine source-law event; its phase/history response
is Gibbs-determined and adds no independent invariant. Its non-surjective complement is now a
strict marked split-defect functor, and a Kummer torsion-phase observer detects that complement by
Haar/Parseval laws. Both transports are flat. No full HGT constitutive section with
coherent cross-route comparison cells is known. Current results show that the listed shadows can
coexist and that several forgetful maps are nonconservative. They do not prove every route is a
realization of one object.

The GR analogy is a structural research heuristic:

- route mathematics treats one observer doctrine and its scalar endpoint as absolute;
- CFR treats the source law object and its transition system as invariant;
- route formulas are coordinates;
- invertible route comparisons are gauge changes;
- noninvertible comparisons are quotient/horizon maps, not gauges;
- admitted noncommuting comparisons have curvature/interchange defects;

- missing domains and non-Hausdorff limits are defect loci; a stratification theorem remains open;
- a source field equation must constrain the whole transition system, not patch one route endpoint.

### 2.2 Local frames and gauges

A route qualifies as a **constraint frame** only when it is equipped with a realization natural
under a declared generating family of source interventions. A qualified finite chart is often a matrix, complex,
relation, moment packet, or finite source graph. Its matroid is the analogue of a tangent frame:
it records which finite directions are independent but not the global geometry.

Two route charts are gauges of one another only when an admitted invertible comparison transports
their source marking, relation equations, reporter pairing, and relevant topology. A quotient,
completion, scalarization, positive Gram passage, or forgotten affine extension is not a gauge
unless it is invertible on the declared source stratum.

Thus the honest replacement for “all routes are local gauges” is:

> **Constraint-frame equivalence principle.** Two qualified route frames are local
> gauges of one law only where their full marked comparison is an equivalence. Else their
> transition is a physical/mathematical observer reduction whose defect is part of the geometry.

### 3.1 Coefficient realization

One finite source relation can be realized over different coefficient doctrines:

- ordered or Lorentzian realizations retain positivity/inertia;

### 3.2 Prolongation

Differential depth changes the ground data rather than merely its coefficients. For a named

\[
 r_f(N)=\operatorname{trdeg}_K K(f,Df,\ldots,D^Nf).
\tag{CFR.5}

Affine extension charts also belong here: they retain first-unseen relation or moment coordinates

### 3.3 Cofinal aperture

Finite frames need not determine their limit. Aperture transitions carry declared topology,

### 3.4 History transport

## 4. Curvature, holonomy, and singularity

### 4.1 Constraint uncertainty as vertical-kernel geometry

The corpus's Heisenberg-like phenomenon has an exact first-order form. At a source law, combine a
declared family of calibrated observer jets into `A` and let `B` be another observer jet. Define

\[
 \mathcal U_{A\to B}
 =\inf\{C\ge0:\|Bh\|\le C\|Ah\|\text{ for every source tangent }h\},
\tag{CFR.7a}
\]

with value `infinity` if no such `C` exists. Then:

- `ker A` is the source tangent invisible in the calibrated frames;
- algebraic prediction of `B` requires and, in finite dimension, is equivalent to
  `ker A subset ker B`;
- continuous prediction on a declared completion is equivalent to `U_(A->B)<infinity`;
- finite-aperture predictability can coexist with `U_(A_N->B_N)->infinity`.

Thus “making one route precise makes another imprecise” means that the chosen observer quotient or
completion enlarges a vertical kernel or makes the constitutive factor unbounded. It is not a
universal commutator inequality. The exact instances include:

- Gram/positive reduction kills unsquared phase;
- a current Hankel face kills its first affine extension sign;
- some Hausdorff completions kill algebraic reporters;
- a compact phase endpoint kills lifted winding/action;
- every fixed finite jet observer misses later HT directions;
- the ramification-diagonal Boolean observer is a positive exception: a natural chosen variance
  geometry yields the finite Route-E Schur capacity and its intervention derivative.

This is the first common invariant interpretation spanning coefficient, extension, topology,
history, HT, and Route-E capacity. It does not assert that the literal observer maps are already
constructed in every route.

### 4.2 Comparison defects

For two axes `u,v`, an admitted comparison square has an interchange cell

\[
 \chi_{u,v}:u_*v_*\mathfrak C\Longrightarrow v_*u_*\mathfrak C.
\tag{CFR.7}
\]

If two path transports are equivalences with common endpoints, their comparison automorphism is
curvature/holonomy data. If `chi_(u,v)` is noninvertible and both composites live in a stable
coefficient chart, its pointwise fibre/cofibre is an **interchange obstruction**, not curvature.
Outside stable fibres, no common fibre/cofibre construction is defined. Doctrine-specific
candidates include extension torsors, lattice defects, inertia jumps, closure radicals, and
multivalued relations; each requires a separate construction and invariance proof.

Invertible transport around an actual loop gives holonomy; noninvertible loop transport is a
monodromy correspondence. Three-axis coherence does not automatically give Bianchi: triviality of
the boundary composite of a comparison cube is a separate conjecture/axiom.

Not every no-go is literally curvature. CFR distinguishes:

| Corpus obstruction | Coordinate-free type |
|---|---|
| two admitted composites disagree | comparison automorphism if invertible; interchange obstruction otherwise |
| transport around route loop fails to close | holonomy |
| current face has unfixed next rung | affine extension torsor |
| observer map is undefined/nonclosable | admission horizon |
| finite tower loses quotient at completion | topology/strictness singularity |
| positivity erases sign/phase | nonconservative coefficient base change |
| integer span changes closure | period/monodromy fracture |
| prolongation rank never stabilizes | non-finite-type constraint motive |

CFR conjectures that a selected source-natural subset are shadows of one transition geometry;
calling all of them curvature would be false.

## 8. Candidate axioms and construction gates

CFR is not complete until the source supplies equations, not fitted arrows.

### Gate 0 — law formation

Construct `pi_law:S_law->S_src` and a source-normal constitutive section `kappa`. Every route
response must be a projection of the same section on declared raw-source interventions.
An absent source-to-incidence or source-to-contact map is a formation gap, not a curved

assumed.

### Gate I — source normalization

Construct `C` and its marking before Xi-zero specialization. Every realization and comparison must
be natural under declared source interventions.

### Gate II — joint conservativity

A bounded source probe category must satisfy: if every declared realization sends a morphism to an
equivalence, then that morphism is an equivalence. No
single route need be conservative. Serial observer loss cannot be repaired downstream; parallel
frames may jointly reconstruct.

### Gate III — observer-fibre comparison

Coefficient change, prolongation, aperture, and transport must have represented comparison cells
with coherent composition wherever declared admitted. Their defects are computed, not named.

The escort--logit--phase square supplies one strict flat calibration cell. It does not pass the
nontrivial-cell requirement because it is an analytic coordinate conjugacy, has zero interchange

anchor--defect support observer supplies a nonzero direct/stagewise interchange defect and Bianchi

history gauge adds no independent invariant. Gate III therefore remains open at the next level:
construct a collision-labelled totalization whose carry is coherent under source composition and

### Gate IV — extension/flux balance

Seek a natural transformation from a specified source transition to both first unseen affine
extension and retained boundary flux. No such transformation is currently formulated.

### Gate V — physical cost properness

For a physical realization, bounded observed history plus bounded action must have compact

source edge mode is added.

These are acceptance criteria, not field equations. None is assumed to prove RH.

## 9. Risky predictions

1. **Phase-before-positive prediction.** Any successful proof requiring coorientation must carry an
   unsquared phase/extension reporter before Gram, norm, determinant-square, or positive scalar
   reduction.
2. **Two-depth prediction.** Determinant/Fitting order alone does not control cofinal strictness;
   any literal theorem claiming otherwise must add singular-spectrum or filling-energy hypotheses.
3. **Extension prediction.** A route whose live obstruction is first-unseen orientation cannot be
   closed by any theorem depending only on the complete current face.
4. **Cofinal-birth prediction.** For a fixed finite-frame functor, a limit admission wall invisible
   at every stage requires additional transition/topology data not present in that functor.
5. **HT prediction.** Unbounded differential rank forces failure of every fixed finite-rank
   **conservative** realization, but does not force noncompact observed dynamics. Any universal
   chaos claim is false.
6. **Recurrence prediction.** Compact return statistics never determine lifted action without a
   marked cocycle/cover and cost. Endpoint-only wormhole claims admit finite information-theoretic
   countermodels.
7. **Source-field research bet.** A useful RH-bearing theorem would constrain a source-derived
   compatibility law across multiple axes. This is not yet falsifiable enough to count as a field
   prediction.
8. **Withheld-organ test.** A proposed source transition calibrated in three organs must predict a
   fourth organ's response before that fourth formula is used. Failure falsifies the transition,
   not merely one route.
9. **Constitutive-factor prediction.** At first order, a withheld response is predictable only if
   its differential annihilates the common calibrated vertical kernel; at cofinal level it must
   also satisfy a uniform response inequality. These conditions are exact and independently
   falsifiable.

### Falsifiers

- CFR contracts if one declared observer doctrine is conservative on the literal HGT sector.
- The four-axis ontology contracts if one axis is functorially determined by the other three on
  the literal source.
- A defect construction fails if source-equivalent presentations give different defect classes.
- CFR fails its explanatory test if a three-organ calibration does not predict a withheld fourth
  response.
- A physical extension fails if its cost/holonomy law is fitted after the target history is known.

## 10. Physics and holography

CFR permits the hunch but sharply limits it. A black-hole boundary can be an observer quotient;
interior time can be a lifted history; an edge mode can restore information lost by quotient; a
wormhole can represent a correspondence between two boundary frames. Yet geometry becomes
physical only after construction of:

1. a source-derived symplectic/duality pairing;
2. conjugate edge charge;
3. Hamiltonian or action;
4. reconstruction map;
5. cost/length operator.

Compact recurrence plus open lift alone is not evidence for a wormhole: finite GHZ and torus
models already realize it. In CFR terms, a boundary readout `A` determines an interior/history
response `B` only if the constitutive factor norm `U_(A->B)` is finite. If it diverges, an added
edge mode must either shrink the vertical kernel or carry infinite relaxed cost; this is a precise
conditional prediction, not a wormhole identification. If arithmetic and black-hole systems share
CFR geometry, their common invariant should therefore be a constitutive factor/edge anomaly or
costed holonomy, not a common spectrum or rank-one phase portrait.
