# Signed variation of the actual state weight

In [§2.16](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#216-programme-to-close-ecs), fix $u=e^t$, $h=\sqrt{u\log u}$, $b=\lfloor u+h\rfloor$ and $w_j=m\,\Phi(\log(u/j))/\sqrt j$. Write $A_j=\sum_{u<n\le j}(\Lambda(n)-1)$ and $Q_j=A_j-A_b$ for $j\ge b$. The target is a one-sided estimate for the weighted tail on the original centers, including $w_LQ_L$ and the full $-m^2/2$ charge.

**Adjacent band.** For fixed $B>1$, uniformly on $u+h<j\le u+Bh$, the state wall law gives $hw_j\to\gamma$. For $b<L\le u+Bh$, $|Q_L|+\sum_{b<n\le L}|\Lambda(n)-1|\ll_B h$ by the interval sieve. Subtract $\gamma/h$ before summation by parts. Both the endpoint error and the signed variation $\sum_{b<j<L}Q_j(w_j-w_{j+1})$ tend to zero uniformly over all these endpoints. Indeed the signed variation equals $\sum_{b<n\le L}(\Lambda(n)-1)(w_n-\gamma/h)-Q_L(w_L-\gamma/h)$. Each term is bounded by $O_B(h)\sup_{b<n\le L}|w_n-\gamma/h|=o(1)$. This holds on the original set $A$.

**Remote rising variation.** [Lean proof](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheRemoteVariationOfTheStateWeights.lean). Confinement gives $|w_j|\ll_\Phi u^2 j^{-5/2}$. The true prefix satisfies $|Q_j|\ll j$ for $j\ge b$, by the linear Chebyshev bound. Thus $|w_LQ_L|\ll_\Phi u^2 L^{-3/2}$ and the sum of $|Q_j|[w_{j+1}-w_j]_+$ over $j>N\ge b$ costs $O_\Phi(u^2/\sqrt N)$. At $N\ge u^6$ this is $O_\Phi(u^{-1})$ for every center. Its integral against $K(T-t)$ over $t>T/6$ costs $O_\Phi(e^{-T/6})$, including the centers outside $A$.

**Open middle.** Between $u+Bh$ and $u^6$ a [two-sided prime-prefix bound](../2-fully-proven/ecs-two-sided-prime-prefixes-at-the-wall.md) gives upper and lower control outside an exceptional set of relative measure tending to zero. Paying this bound against the actual increasing and decreasing state weights, or proving signed cancellation between them, remains open. The endpoint left after freezing the weight is $\gamma Q_L/h$; its sign still depends on the centered prime count. The early absolute cost is $O_\Phi(e^{-T/12})$ at $U=T/6$. A strict improvement for the complete fractional test, including its remaining late mass outside $A$, is open.
