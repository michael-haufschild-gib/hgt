## Frozen technical conjecture

Let an operational quantum process supply source interventions, local
reporters, composition, and a notion of interaction order, without supplying
a background spatial metric. For ordered reporter regions \(A,B\), form the
complete controlled response histories from sources in \(A\) to readouts in
\(B\). Filter their temporal character module by the least interaction order
at which each character has a nonzero source-to-readout coefficient:
\[
 \Gamma_{A\to B}^{(\le r)}
 =
 \langle\text{temporal characters born by interaction order }r\rangle .
\]
Characters already generated separately by the source and reporter are
removed. The remaining quotient
\[
 \Delta_{A\to B}^{(\le r)}
 =
 \Gamma_{A\vee B}^{(\le r)}
 \big/
 \bigl(\Gamma_A^{(\le r)}+\Gamma_B^{(\le r)}\bigr)
\]
is the **shared-time defect**. Define
\[
 d_{\rm sh}(A\to B)
 =
 \inf\{r:\Delta_{A\to B}^{(\le r)}\ne0\},
\]
with value \(+\infty\) when no genuinely joint temporal character is ever
born.

The conjecture is that, after the operational completeness and generic
noncancellation conditions are stated intrinsically:

1. \(d_{\rm sh}\) reconstructs the directed causal adjacency and path
   distance of every finite locally generated quantum process, without being
   given its interaction graph;
2. the full filtered modules, not only their first birth order, reconstruct
   the process up to operational causal equivalence;
3. a continuum scaling limit of this filtration determines conformal
   Lorentzian geometry, while calibrated leading response coefficients fix
   the remaining metric scale;
4. the terminal temporal-character group is the Pontryagin dual of the
   observer-visible recurrence hull, so ordinary recurrence dimension and
   return laws are terminal shadows of the same filtration;
5. irreversible dynamics removes characters from the persistent terminal
   group, producing an oriented filtration; an operational horizon is a
   boundary across which \(d_{\rm sh}=+\infty\) in one direction while local
   temporal modules remain nontrivial;
6. gravitational curvature is the obstruction to gluing the locally
   reconstructed shared-time filtrations into one globally synchronized
   temporal factorization.

No clause is presently a result. The conjecture is frozen before proof
search. A later proof may refine definitions but may not replace “shared
temporal-character birth reconstructs causal geometry” by ordinary signal
arrival, a supplied graph metric, or a renamed Lieb--Robinson bound.

## New experiment whose answer is not assumed

Construct an unlabeled programmable quantum network. The analysis receives no
interaction graph, spatial coordinates, or coupling layout. It may apply a
complete local source family and record local response histories. From the
interaction-order filtration of newly born nonfactorizable temporal
characters alone, reconstruct:

1. directed adjacency;
2. graph distance;
3. deliberately inserted bottlenecks and disconnected components;
4. an engineered one-way analog horizon;
5. terminal recurrence rank.

The conjecture predicts exact agreement between reconstructed shared-time
geometry and causal connectivity for generic controls, and a specific
failure signature when completeness or noncancellation is violated. Standard
signal-arrival time, spatial labels, and fitted Hamiltonian matrices are not
allowed as inputs.

A stronger test varies couplings without changing topology. Birth order must
remain topological while leading coefficients change the calibrated metric

generic noncancellation does not recover paths, or if identical complete
filtrations coexist with operationally inequivalent causal networks, the
conjecture fails.

## Immediate hostile attacks

1. **Lieb--Robinson relabeling.** A speed bound on already spatially local
   dynamics is not a derivation of space. The conjecture must recover the
   causal graph without being given support geometry.
2. **Hamiltonian-learning relabeling.** Reconstructing a matrix and then
   reading its sparsity is not shared-time reconstruction. Geometry must be
   an invariant of the filtered history algebra.
3. **Spectral nonuniqueness.** Isospectral nonisomorphic graphs are direct
   pressure. Complete controlled histories, not the bare spectrum, must
   separate them or the conjecture dies.
4. **Genericity escape hatch.** “Generic” must be a proved full-measure or
   residual statement on an explicit physical family, with exceptional
   mechanisms classified.
5. **Continuum overreach.** Finite graph recovery does not imply Lorentzian
   geometry, gravity, or black holes. Those clauses remain conjectural until
   a scaling and gluing theorem exists.
6. **Gauge dependence.** Temporal characters and birth valuations must be
   invariant under energy origin, basis change, source reparametrization, and
   operationally invisible dilation.
7. **No operational completeness.** A restricted reporter family can create
   false infinite distances. Completeness must be independently testable.
8. **Static-entanglement false positive.** Shared time must be created by
   controlled dynamical response, not inferred from an entangled state alone.

## Kill conditions

Retire or sharply weaken the conjecture if any of the following is proved:

1. two finite locally generated processes have identical complete
   shared-time filtrations but inequivalent directed causal structures;
2. generic path coefficients can cancel at every order despite
   source-complete controls;
3. the filtration is equivalent only after supplying the interaction graph;
4. continuum limits with the same filtration admit inequivalent conformal
   causal structures;
5. the proposed horizon criterion labels an ordinary weakly coupled but
   causally connected region as infinitely distant;
6. gravity requires independent geometric data not recoverable from any
   complete shared-time filtration.

## First external parent attack — 2026-07-30

Checked primary parents already occupy several weaker statements:

- Wilming and Werner, *Lieb--Robinson bounds imply locality of interactions*
  (2020), arXiv:2006.10062, proves an equivalence between exponential
  Lieb--Robinson behavior and spatial decay of interactions, already using
  single-site two-point behavior. Thus “propagation data characterize
  locality” is occupied.
- Zhang and Sarovar, *Quantum Hamiltonian identification from measurement time
  traces* (2014), arXiv:1401.5780, reconstructs parameterized Hamiltonians
  from restricted observable time traces. Thus “time records can recover an
  unknown interaction model” is occupied.
- Bairey, Arad and Lindner, *Learning a local Hamiltonian from local
  measurements* (2018), arXiv:1807.04564, recovers local Hamiltonians from
  local observables with linear resource scaling. Thus local reconstruction
  itself is occupied.
- Chiribella and Kristjansson, *Quantum causal unravelling* (2022), develops
  causal-graph recovery from signalling structure of multipartite quantum
  processes. Thus “operational interventions reveal quantum causal order” is
  occupied.
- Seiberg's *Emergent Spacetime* review (2006) and broad emergent-spacetime
  programmes occupy the worldview claim that spacetime/locality may be
  emergent.

The frozen conjecture is not killed, but its novelty residue is narrower and
harder: causal geometry must be equivalent to the **birth filtration of
nonfactorizable temporal characters**, without reconstructing a Hamiltonian
first, without being given spatial decay, and with terminal recurrence and
infinite-distance horizons recovered from the same invariant. Continuum
Lorentzian and gravity clauses remain unsupported conjectures.
