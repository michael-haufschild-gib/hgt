## Statement

Let `Q_a` be the orthogonal projection onto the even physical-and-Fourier Sonin carrier and

`L_(a,p) = (1-Q_a)(U_p+U_p^(-1))Q_a`, `a>0`, `p>1`.

The canonical principal shell of the positive Gram operator `L_(a,p)^* L_(a,p)` is

`Sigma_(a,p) = (A_(pa)-A_a) + (R_(pa)-R_a)`,

the sum of its physical and Fourier cutoff shells. Define the principal-shell residual

`K_(a,p) = L_(a,p)^* L_(a,p) - Sigma_(a,p)`.

`[PROVED IN LEAN]` The existing exact Calkin shell law implies that `K_(a,p)` is compact. Both
terms are self-adjoint, hence `K_(a,p)` is self-adjoint. Consequently

`T_(a,p) = K_(a,p)^* K_(a,p) = K_(a,p)^2`

is a positive compact source operator.

contains a stronger ideal statement. The projection correction

`C_a = Q_a-(1-A_a-R_a)`

is a finite sum of bounded two-sided multiples of the mixed finite time-band blocks `A_a R_a`
and `R_a A_a`. These blocks are Hilbert--Schmidt integral operators: after Fourier conjugation
their kernels are the bounded Fourier kernel restricted to a finite rectangle. The
Hilbert--Schmidt ideal is two-sided, so `C_a` is Hilbert--Schmidt. The exact lower-corner residual

`R_(a,p) = (1-Q_a)(U_p+U_p^(-1))Q_a

with `Q_(0,a)=1-A_a-R_a` is therefore Hilbert--Schmidt, and
`R_(a,p)^*R_(a,p)` is trace class. The separate even Gram residual `K_(a,p)` above remains proved
compact and self-adjoint; upgrading that particular Gram-shell difference to the same semantic
Hilbert--Schmidt interface is not needed for the all-prime construction below and remains a
distinct strengthening.

## All-prime assembly

`[PROVED IN LEAN]` The all-prime object begins after removal of the linear prime shell. The raw
positive Euler coefficient is `p^(-1/2)`, whose squared prime energy is `sum_p 1/p` and diverges,
exactly as the formal finite-family Calkin lower bound proves. The regularized prime-power tail

`{(p,k) : p prime, k>=2}`, with weight `p^(-k/2)`,

has square energy `sum_p sum_(k>=2) p^(-k)<infinity`. `SoninPrimePowerRegularization.lean`
formalizes this double summability, constructs the square-root amplitudes, and proves that their
countable diagonal kernel is Hilbert--Schmidt. It also formalizes that the deleted linear prime
energy is not summable.

`SoninAllPrimeResidualKernel.lean` first performs an auxiliary block-diagonal assembly from the
**actual** weighted lower-corner residual blocks. It proves the complete residual energy is
Hilbert--Schmidt and gives an unconditional positive trace-class Gram. This diagonal operator is
not the canonical arithmetic assembly: declaring distinct prime powers orthogonal erases every
mixed prime word.

`SoninCoupledPrimeResidual.lean` constructs the canonical common-domain column

`C_a : SoninL2 -> ℓ²((prime power) × Sonin basis)`, `C_a x = (A_α x)_α`.

The already proved sum `sum_α ||A_α||_HS² < infinity` proves `C_a` Hilbert--Schmidt with no new
convergence hypothesis. Its Gram

`T_mix(a) = C_a^* C_a`

is positive, self-adjoint, and trace class through its explicit rectangular Hilbert--Schmidt
factorization. The exact infinite second-moment identity is now proved:

`Re Tr(T_mix(a)^2) = sum_(α,β) ||A_α A_β^*||_HS²`.

The double family is summable. Splitting its diagonal gives the convergent nonnegative invariant

`I₂(a) = sum_(α != β) ||A_α A_β^*||_HS²`,

and Lean proves `Re Tr(T_mix(a)^2) = diagonalSecondMoment(a) + I₂(a)`. It also proves that one
nonzero off-diagonal residual overlap implies `I₂(a)>0`. The logarithmic Euler coefficients are
all nonzero, so this gate is exactly the unweighted geometric statement
`R_α R_β^* != 0`; no coefficient hypothesis remains.

The semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh square supplies the complementary structural fact. For every two
places `p,q>1`, the full place-adjoining Gram product is invertible, nonzero, and noncompact on
the common weighted line. In contrast, every principal-subtracted cross overlap is
Hilbert--Schmidt and compact. Therefore the residual coupled moment cannot by itself be the full
semilocal/Weil functional. The deleted principal shell must re-enter through a relative trace,
regularized logarithmic derivative, or an equivalent semilocal comparison.

An ordinary determinant of the block-diagonal residual introduces extra powers and does not
reproduce the logarithmic Euler series term-by-term. Calling either assembled residual operator
the Xi determinant would be false.

`[PROVED IN LEAN]` Taking the scalar logarithm of the finite-place Hardy--Titchmarsh Gram before
the residual Gram assembly restores the unsquared Euler modes exactly:

`log |1-p^(-1/2-is)|^2 = -2 Re sum_(m>=1) p^(-m/2-ims)/m`.

For finite unordered place sets these logarithmic metrics satisfy an exact disjoint-union descent
law. There is no all-prime pointwise sum: even after deleting `m=1`, the `m=2` central mode is
`-1/p`, so its absolute prime sum diverges. This rules out a naive global sum of local operator
logarithms and forces a completed prime--archimedean or relative construction.

`SoninRelativeGramDefect.lean` now restores the missing linear interface. If `P` is the formal
principal corner, `R` the actual Hilbert--Schmidt residual, and `c` a logarithmic Euler

`(P+cR)^*(P+cR)-P^*P = c P^*R + conj(c) R^*P + |c|^2 R^*R`.

The full relative defect and its linear cross term are unconditionally Hilbert--Schmidt; the
quadratic remainder is trace class. Thus the coefficient-squaring obstruction belongs to the
