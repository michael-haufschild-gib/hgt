# Prime-valuation profile hypertranscendence law

- **ID:** `prime-valuation-profile-hypertranscendence`
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** `[CONJECTURE] [GENERAL HT LAW] [NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]`
- **Source:** Shapiro--Sparer arithmetic-function Jacobians, logarithmic derivation, and the
  prime-power valuation decomposition
- **Statement:** For prime-power-radial Dirichlet carriers, joint differential dependence is
  exactly linear dependence of their valuation profiles; consequently an infinite Euler
  completion is hypertranscendental over every finite valuation aperture.

## Goal

Classify differential dependence for arithmetic functions supported on prime powers whose value
at `p^r` depends on `r` but not on `p`.  This is a general hypertranscendence problem.  The zeta
Euler logarithm is a downstream unit-profile specialization.

The target is not the already-known Jacobian criterion, not Hilbert's hypertranscendence theorem
for zeta, and not finite prime-power block independence.  It is an exact new dictionary between
two structures:

```text
linear matroid of valuation profiles u : positive integers -> C
    =
differential matroid of the corresponding prime-power-radial Dirichlet carriers.
```

## Setup

For a profile `u : N_{>0} -> C`, define the formal arithmetic carrier

```text
K_u(p^r) = u(r)/r,        r >= 1,
K_u(n)   = 0             away from prime powers.
```

The logarithmic derivation satisfies

```text
D^k K_u(p^r) = (r log p)^k u(r)/r.
```

After the `p`-basic derivation, the coefficient at `p^(r-1)` is

```text
(r log p)^k u(r).
```

Thus valuation degree probes the profile variable `r`, while the prime clock `log p` probes jet
order `k`.

## Master conjecture

**[CONJECTURE] Prime-valuation profile classification.**  Let `(u_i)_(i in I)` be any family of
complex valuation profiles.  The following are equivalent:

1. every finite subfamily of the profiles is linearly independent as functions on the positive
   integers;
2. the carriers `(K_{u_i})_(i in I)` are jointly differentially independent for the logarithmic
   derivation over `C`;
3. the complete differential relation ideal of the carrier family is generated by the prolonged
   linear relations among the profiles.

Equivalently, the assignment `u |-> K_u` is a faithful map of linear matroids to differential
matroids.  It preserves rank, circuits, closure, and restriction to finite profile families.

The essential positive law is:

```text
every differential relation among prime-power-radial carriers
must already be a prolonged linear relation among valuation profiles.
```

## First theorem target: vertical completion externality

Let `delta_r` be the profile supported at valuation degree `r`, and let `1` be the constant unit
profile.  Then `1` is linearly independent from every finite collection of `delta_r`.  The master
law predicts:

**[CONJECTURE] Euler completion escape.**  The canonical all-valuation carrier

```text
K_1(p^r) = 1/r
```

is hypertranscendental over the differential field generated by any finite collection of pure
prime-power layers `K_{delta_r}`.  Stronger, the completion and every finite valuation window are
jointly differentially independent.

For `Re(s)>1`, the analytic image is

```text
K_1(s) = log zeta(s),
K_{delta_r}(s) = P(r s)/r,
```

where `P` is the prime zeta function.  Hence the analytic corollary would say that `log zeta` is
hypertranscendental over every finite differential field generated by prime-zeta dilates.

## Proof architecture

1. Prove a separated-prime determinant coefficient law: when row `i` of an
   arithmetic-function matrix is supported on powers of a distinct prime `q_i`, the coefficient
   of the determinant at `prod_i q_i^e_i` is the determinant of the scalar coefficient matrix at
   those powers.
2. For any finite jet packet, choose valuation probes on which the profile-jet evaluation matrix
   has full rank and choose distinct prime clocks avoiding the resulting nonzero polynomial
   determinant.
3. Apply the Shapiro--Sparer Jacobian criterion to obtain algebraic independence of every finite
   jet packet.
4. For the completion theorem, enlarge an arbitrary finite aperture to the initial window
   `1,...,d`.  Subtract those layers from `K_1`.  The residual begins in the fresh degree `d+1`;
   its leading prime-power block is a Vandermonde packet in `log p`.  This makes the Jacobian
   block triangular and proves joint differential independence.
5. Linear profile relations plainly prolong to differential relations.  The independent quotient
   basis and affine differential pivot elimination must prove that there are no other relations.

## Anti-theatre attacks

1. **Jacobian-corollary attack.**  If the master law is merely a one-line restatement of the
   Shapiro--Sparer criterion, it is not a significant new theorem.  Survival requires the exact
   converse classification of the whole relation ideal and the valuation-profile matroid.
2. **Symmetric-function attack.**  Ghost coordinates and `log zeta = sum_r P(rs)/r` are classical.
   The claimed content is relative differential externality and exact profile classification, not
   the ghost expansion.
3. **Finite-window attack.**  Independence of every finite set of pure layers does not imply
   independence of their infinite completion.  The completion theorem needs its own residual
   leading-block proof.
4. **Support attack.**  Pong's theorem over finitely generated prime support does not apply: every
   pure valuation layer already involves all primes.  If another theorem covers this all-primes
   vertical regime, the present claim must be compared to it directly.
5. **Terminology attack.**  Names such as `valuation profile`, `vertical HT`, or `completion
   escape` carry no novelty.  Only the exact equivalence and relative HT theorem count.
6. **Significance attack.**  A proof only for `log zeta` is a special-function corollary.  The
   general profile classification is required for a strong general-HT paper.

## Downstream RH consequence

If the completion theorem holds, every finite valuation aperture is positively incomplete: the
all-valuation Euler logarithm supplies a new differential transcendence direction over it.  Any
prime-side route to RH that is exact at the Euler-product level must therefore use an operation on
the completed valuation profile, not a finite collection of prime-power layers.  This is a
constraint on possible RH architectures, not an RH proof.

## Formal status

The first formal milestone is the separated-prime determinant coefficient law, followed by the
