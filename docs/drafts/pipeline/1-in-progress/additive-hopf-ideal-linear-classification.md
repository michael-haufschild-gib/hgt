# Additive Hopf ideals are generated in degree one

- **ID:** additive-hopf-ideal-linear-classification
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CANDIDATE
- **Source:** Schanuel algebraic-group route
- **Statement:** Over a characteristic-zero field, every Hopf ideal of `Sym(V)` is generated by
  its intersection with the degree-one image of `V`.

## Goal

Classify closed subgroup schemes of finite-dimensional additive groups as linear subspaces.  This
is the additive factor theorem required by algebraic Goursat and therefore by the connected
subgroup splitting step in the algebraic-coefficient defect proof.

## Gates

- [x] 0 → 1 in-progress: well-posed; minimal-degree derivative proof exists in differential form.
- [ ] 1 → 2 fully-proven: ordinary-polynomial proof and generation induction dependency-closed.
- [ ] 2 → 3 formalized: exact Lean theorem, exact-module build, axiom audit.
- [ ] 3 → 4 publication-ready.

## Approach

1. Generalize the existing `DifferentialHopfPrimitive` minimal-total-degree argument from jet-index
   polynomials to `MvPolynomial σ k`.
2. Bridge Mathlib's tensor-quotient `Ideal.IsHopfIdeal` condition to the doubled-variable coideal
   condition via the canonical tensor/polynomial equivalence.
3. Extract a nonzero degree-one relation from every nonzero additive Hopf ideal.
4. Quotient by that primitive direction and induct on finite dimension to prove generation by all
   degree-one relations.
