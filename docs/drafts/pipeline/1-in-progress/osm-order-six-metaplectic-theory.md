## Approach
Thesis: Hasse–Davenport makes every first moment of order six a twisted Rankin–Selberg convolution of
the cubic theta function on the cubic cover (STCC engine). Order of attack:
1. Bridge `γ₂ = normalizedCubicUnitGauss`; row-root series = conjugate twisted squarefree theta-square
   series (column 1). Transfer of STCC's simple pole: a coherent main term at `2/3`.
2. Columns: `Φ_n(c) = (n/c)₃(n/c)₂Φ(c)`; cubic part = shift `T(nc)T(1) = (n/c)₃T(n)T(c)`.
3. Isolation of the untwisted squarefree series: `O/3O` translates of θ_s normalize `Γ(3)` and keep κ;
   `(1/9)Σ_τ C_τ` projects onto `ν ∈ 3O` (layers `m ≥ 2`, constant term included); `D − (1/9)Σ_τ C_τ`
   is the unit layer `2Σ_I T(gen I)²…` = `S_1` times `Z₀(3w−1)`. Double poles of `C_τ` from the cusps fixed by
   the translation.
4. Smoothed first moments: incomplete Eisenstein series with entire rapidly decaying Mellin transform,
   polynomial growth of the resolvent pairing (`‖R(λ)‖ ≤ 1/|Im λ|`), contour shift to `Re s = 1 + ε`;
   squarefree part through `1/Z₀(3w − 1)` and the seed.
5. Sextic large sieve (Heath-Brown recursion at order six).

Diagonal elements `δ_a ∈ Γ(3)`, `δ_a ≡ diag(a, a⁻¹)` mod `q²`, give `δ_a⁻¹T_xδ_a = γ''T_{xδ²}` with `γ'' ∈ Γ(3) ∩ Γ(q)` and `κ(γ'') = 1` (lower row `(−9tq³m₀², 1 ∓ y + y²)`, `y ≡ 0` mod 9 for the choice `c(δ_a) = 3q²m₀`, `t ∈ 3O`), so `J(ta⁻²) = J(t)`: `J` depends only on the square class of `t` and `Res L_λ = ((N − 1)/2)(J₊ − J₋)`. Units are squares when `N ≡ 1` mod 4; the `GL₂` element `diag(a, 1)` at `q` swaps the two components of the `GL₂` quotient, so the `GL₂` period vanishes trivially and does not constrain `J₊ − J₋`. No symmetry kills the quadratic grade; the residue is a twisted trilinear period (`Hom_{GL₂(K_q)}((θ_q ⊗ λ_q) ⊗ θ_q ⊗ θ_q, ℂ)` and its value), a global input. For the cubic twists the same symmetry gives `ψ(a)²` and kills only the spectral part, consistent with C29–C34. (b) *Hecke trace at `λ`* (`A = diag(λ, λ⁻¹)`, `Γ_A = {b, c ∈ λ⁴O}`, index 81, cosets `T_{3β}L_{3δ}`, multiplier `κ²` since the `λ`-supplements cancel): the nine `δ = 0` cosets give the full dilate correlation at 9 with trivial phase (`9⟨ξ_k, x⟩ ∈ 3ℤ`); the cosets with `−ω²δ` rational mod 3 reduce to the same product by `SL₂(ℤ)`; the others put `θ(L_{−ω²δ}Aw)` at a ramified cusp, whose expansion is the 3-adic datum `T(uλ^m)`. The ramified cosets contribute an unknown Rankin–Selberg series, so this trace meets the same wall as C28. (c) *Conjugate twist at 2.* At `q = 2` the fractional trace degenerates: `χ(−4) = χ(0) = 0` kills `K(s)`, and `J = Σχ(1 − s²) = Σχ(1 − s)² = 0` in characteristic 2. Opposite translations double the phase (`e(2⟨ξ_k, x⟩)`), so they cannot select frequencies mod 2. A 2-adic selector needs translations by quarters (a fractional trace at 4, level 16). This is the 2-adic analogue of C28. Survivor: none in Lean; (a) is the precise statement of the decision.
