## Finite-perturbative no-go

Many finite cosmological diagrams lie in finite holonomic systems:

- de Sitter Ward identities give finite differential equations for fixed exchange correlators;
- differential representations obtain tree wavefunctions from seed functions by finite
  differential operators;
- twisted-integral and positive-geometry formulations produce finite canonical differential
  systems graph by graph;
- fixed flat-space Feynman integrals satisfy finite systems of differential or Picard--Fuchs
  equations in standard algebraic kinematic families.

Every component of a finite linear differential system is differentially algebraic.  Finite sums,
products, derivatives, and algebraic substitutions remain DA.  Thus, within these explicitly
holonomic finite-diagram classes, HT cannot appear at one fixed finite perturbative stage.

The positive physical interpretation is:

> A verified HT cosmological wavefunction shape is a certificate of an irreducibly infinite
> completion in the chosen kinematic direction: nonperturbative resummation, an infinite species
> tower, an infinite graph sector, or another source not compressible into the finite holonomic
> system.

This statement is deliberately limited to classes for which the finite differential system is
proved.  It is not a theorem about every cosmological QFT or arbitrary time-dependent background.

## Observable and gauge attacks

1. **Wavefunction versus in-in correlator.**  A wavefunction coefficient is not automatically an
   observable late-time correlator.  Complex conjugation, phase cancellation, and boundary terms
   can erase information.  A separate theorem must show survival in a gauge-invariant in-in
   quantity.
2. **Field redefinitions.**  Local nonlinear redefinitions can move contact terms and manufacture
   non-Gaussian shapes.  Use the face-complete relative profile and quotient the admissible local
   base.
3. **Transfer functions.**  Late-time radiation/matter transfer and survey response can add
   differential complexity.  The primordial invariant must be defined before those maps, with a
   separate injectivity theorem for observation.
4. **Initial-state engineering.**  An arbitrary HT Bogoliubov coefficient would be input, not a
   discovery.  The HT law must follow from locality, unitarity, crossing/cutting, and a static
   global constraint.
5. **Finite-graph theatre.**  Rewriting a known hypergeometric or polylogarithmic shape does not
   produce HT; those functions satisfy finite differential equations.

## Observation-stability gate

The concurrent conservativity-spectrum theorem adds a separate obstruction.  Model the map from a
primordial response fibre to late-time or survey data by a bounded reporter `A`.  If the residual
primordial HT gauge is infinite-dimensional and `A` is compact or smoothing, then even an
injective `A` has nonclosed range and admits unit response directions whose observed norm tends to
zero.  Thus a compact transfer can be algebraically faithful but never stably reconstruct the full
HT response.

This yields a required dichotomy for any phenomenological proposal:

```text
finite-dimensional physical HT sector,
or a natural noncompact transverse observational channel,
or unavoidable approximate blindness of the infinite HT sector.              (4)
```
