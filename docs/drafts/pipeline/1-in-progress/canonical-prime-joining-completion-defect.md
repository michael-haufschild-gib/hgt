## Proven starting boundary

The parent theorem closes the finite problem:

```text
local source constraint K
  + chosen finite joining C
  = complete finite correlation geometry,

unrestricted Dirichlet multiplication
  = maximal Cartesian joining plus collision symmetrization.
```

Every countable subdirect finite joining is realizable by a locally maximal prime source. Hence no
law choosing one proper joining follows from local tail completeness alone.

The Prime-Walsh/Stone theorem separately shows that a compatible family of all finite pattern
chambers reconstructs its compact product-topology inverse limit. Thus the next invariant cannot be
advertised as “information lost by taking all finite marginals.” At the topological level, no such
information is lost.

## Conjectural master law

`[CONJECTURE]` There is a natural category of prime constraint joinings and a partial analytic
completion correspondence

```text
Comp : prime constraint joining  -/->  meromorphic completion data
```

with the following dichotomy.

1. **Topological completeness.** Compatible finite joining restrictions determine the full compact
   joining uniquely.
2. **Analytic singularity.** On a universal HT family such as Helson zeta functions, continuation
   and divisor readouts do not extend to a continuous natural transformation from the compact
   joining inverse limit.
3. **Polarized rigidity.** A source-defined reflection/positivity polarization may cut the singular
   completion correspondence down to a unique or rigid stratum. The polarization must be stated
   independently of the target divisor.

The desired positive law is:

> Global analytic structure is a singular polarization of a completely reconstructed prime
> joining. It is not a missing finite marginal and not the output of unrestricted multiplication.

## Existing evidence

- The formal joining dichotomy proves complete finite freedom at fixed local geometry.
- Prime-Walsh/Stone reconstruction proves that all finite Boolean chambers determine the compact
  inverse limit.
- The Helson continuation-seam theorem gives a dense/codense, boundary-everywhere continuation
  stratum in the prime-phase product topology and arbitrary continued divisors on the continuable
  side, conditional only on established analytic theorems from the literature.
- Functional-equation and archimedean coupling are absent from general Helson deformation, marking
  them as candidate polarizations rather than consequences of the prime joining.

## Anti-theatre gate

The following are not new results:

- inverse limits reconstructing compatible finite restrictions;
- Stone duality;
- Furstenberg joinings or probability couplings;
- the statement that analytic continuation can be unstable;
- the separate Helson theorems used as classical inputs.

The route survives only if it produces a canonical completion correspondence and proves a
nontrivial regularity/rigidity dichotomy on it. Renaming discontinuity as a “defect” is not enough.

## RH pressure test

The parent theorem says an RH-capable prime correlation must be a chosen joining. This conjecture
adds that even the full joining is only topological data: RH-relevant continuation must be selected
by a singular analytic polarization. For zeta, the plausible source is the Euler--Gamma functional
equation with positivity. No selection or RH implication is currently proved.

is used only to search for the correct mathematical polarization.
