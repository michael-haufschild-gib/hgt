# Prime trace descent to the actual zero divisor

- **ID:** galois-zero-prime-trace-descent
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CANDIDATE
- **Questions:** Arithmetic ground field; automatic spectral compatibility; actual zero action.

## Target

For every symmetric height window, retain the traces of every finite product
of prime translations, with analytic zero multiplicities. Let `B` be the
subfield of `ℂ` generated by all these traces and `E` the full prime spectral
field. The proposed arithmetic model is the normal algebraic extension `E/B`.

An embedding fixing `B` should automatically permute the actual zero divisor
in every height window, through one common permutation at every prime. This
would discharge the prime-spectrum compatibility condition rather than assume
it. Ordinary conjugation fixes the trace field and acts nontrivially.

## Proof mechanism

Each zero defines a multiplicative character on the free prime group; two
prime coordinates distinguish these characters. Distinct multiplicative
characters are linearly independent. Equality of all mixed traces therefore
identifies the complete finite character multiset after a field embedding.
The induced zero permutation is independent of the height window containing
the zero and retains multiplicity. Applying it to all prime coordinates gives
the additive action on the entire integer zero group.

Power sums at multiples of one prime vector recover the coefficients of the
finite characteristic polynomial by Newton identities. Every prime eigenvalue
is thus algebraic over `B`, and every polynomial splits in `E`. This gives
algebraicity and normality of the full generated field, with no terminal height
or finite set of primes.

## Boundaries to audit

These are actual spectral traces. A prime-side contour-source formula must be
proved before calling them independently computable prime-side data.
The trace field may impose strong orbit restrictions. Neither its size nor
normality supplies purity or determines the full permutation group.
