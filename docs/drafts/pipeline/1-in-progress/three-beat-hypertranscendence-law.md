## Physical source and observable

Let `H|n,a> = E_n|n,a>` with multiplicity `g_n`, and let

```text
Z_c(s) = sum_(n>=0) g_n exp(-s E_n),    Re(s)>0.
```

The normalized one-particle thermofield-double state at inverse temperature `beta>0` is

```text
|TFD_beta> =
  Z_c(beta)^(-1/2)
```

Its one-sided Loschmidt amplitude is

```text
A_c,beta(t)
  = <TFD_beta| exp(-i t H_L) |TFD_beta>
```

Both real quadratures are standard interferometric observables. No initial amplitudes are selected
to manufacture a hypertranscendental series: the coefficients are the spectrum's multiplicities

## Definitions

`DA_C(t)(F)` means that some nonzero polynomial over `C(t)` annihilates a finite jet
`(F,F',...,F^(r))`. `HT_C(t)(F)` is its negation.

For the theorem below, the weights satisfy:

1. `g_n` is nonzero for every `n`;
2. `G(z)=sum_(n>=0) g_n z^n` is rational.

Sphere-harmonic degeneracies satisfy both conditions. The HT half needs only nonzero coefficients;
the rational-generating condition is used to close the `c=0` half.

## Theorem T1: three-frequency sign law

Assume `q>=0`, `R>0`, and either `c=0` or `q^2+c>0`. Then

```text
sign(E_2 - 2 E_1 + E_0) = sign(c).
```

In particular,

```text
E_2 - 2 E_1 + E_0 = 0  <->  c=0.
```

### Proof

For `c!=0`, set `f(x)=sqrt(x^2+c)` on `[q,q+2]`. Direct differentiation gives

```text
f''(x) = c/(x^2+c)^(3/2).
```

The exact second-difference identity

```text
f(q+2)-2f(q+1)+f(q)
  = integral_0^1 integral_0^1 f''(q+u+v) du dv
```

has the sign of `c`. For `c=0`, `f(x)=x` on the nonnegative half-line, including `q=0`,
and the second difference vanishes. Division by positive `R` preserves the sign. `[PROVED HERE]`

## Theorem T2: arithmetic-progression square-root rank
`[VERIFIED-KNOWN; PROOF ROUTE REPRODUCED]`

Let `q=a/d` with integers `a>=0`, `d>0`. Let `c` be real and nonzero, with all radicands
`(n+q)^2+c` positive for `n>=0`. Then

```text
Q-rank {sqrt((n+q)^2+c) : n>=0} = infinity.
```

### Priority collision

Peter Müller and Will Sawin gave proofs in a February 2025 MathOverflow answer of the stronger
statement: values of the square root of any real quadratic polynomial that is not a polynomial
square contain an infinite `Q`-linearly independent subset. Their theorem directly covers

```text
p(n)=(n+q)^2+c
```

for every real `c!=0`; only an eventually positive integer tail is needed. T2 is therefore prior
mathematics, not a new theorem of this project. The forum source is not peer-reviewed, so a paper
must reproduce a complete proof and cite the priority.

### Specialization proof route

It is enough to show that, for every finite real family `x_1,...,x_r`, infinitely many values
`sqrt(p(n))` lie outside its rational span.

Suppose otherwise. Let `R` be the finitely generated integral domain over `Z` generated by the
`x_j` and the coefficients of `p`, localized so that the nonzero quadratic discriminant remains
invertible. Every sufficiently large `sqrt(p(n))` then lies in `Frac(R)`.

The weak Nullstellensatz supplies a characteristic-zero specialization of `R` into a number field
`F` that preserves the nonzero discriminant. Exclude finitely many denominator, discriminant, and
dyadic primes. There are infinitely many rational primes splitting completely in `F`. At such a
prime, reduction lands in `F_p`.

For the reduced nonsquare quadratic

```text
x^2+A x+B,
```

the equation

```text
y^2=x^2+A x+B
```

cannot have a solution for every `x in F_p`. Indeed, when `B-A^2/4!=0`,

```text
(y-x-A/2)(y+x+A/2)=B-A^2/4
```

parametrizes exactly `p-1` pairs `(x,y)` by the first nonzero factor. If every quadratic value were
a square, there would be at least `2p-2` pairs because at most two values vanish, a contradiction.

Choose a nonsquare residue `x_0` and arbitrarily large integers `n=x_0 mod p`. The specialized
`p(n)` is nonsquare in `F`; hence the original `sqrt(p(n))` could not have belonged to the chosen
rational span. Induction constructs an infinite independent subset.

### Formal-proof obligations

1. formalize specialization of a finitely generated real domain to a number field while preserving
   one specified nonzero element;
2. formalize existence of infinitely many completely split primes, or an adequate weaker theorem;
3. formalize the finite-field solution count;
4. close the induction from escape from every finite rational span.

T2 is paper-valid as a cited known result, but not yet unconditional in Lean.

## Theorem T3: exact DA/HT response dichotomy


nontrivial algebraic differential equation has exponents contained in a finitely generated

change of the independent variable and multiplication by a nonzero constant preserve DA and HT.

## Closed-universe specialization

Consider a free scalar on the `(d+1)`-dimensional Einstein cylinder
`R_time x S^d_R`. Scalar harmonics have

```text
lambda_l = l(l+d-1)/R^2,
g_l = (2l+d-1)(l+d-2)!/(l!(d-1)!),
```

with the standard `d=1` multiplicity convention. For mass `m` and curvature coupling `xi`,

```text
E_l^2 = (1/R^2)
  [l(l+d-1)+(mR)^2+xi d(d-1)].
```

Set

```text
q=(d-1)/2,
c=(mR)^2+xi d(d-1)-q^2.
```

Then `E_l=R^(-1)sqrt((l+q)^2+c)`. At conformal coupling
`xi_conf=(d-1)/(4d)`,

```text
c=(mR)^2.
```

Thus a massless conformal scalar lies exactly on the three-beat/DA surface, and any nonzero mass
lies on the positive-defect/HT side.

The harmonic generating function is

```text
sum_(l>=0) g_l z^l = (1+z)/(1-z)^d.
```

At `c=0`,

```text
Z_d,0(s)
  = exp(-q s/R)
    (1+exp(-s/R))/(1-exp(-s/R))^d,
```

an explicit DA response. This provides one formula valid in every sphere dimension.

## Hostile Nature review: strongest objections

### R1. “This is a toy-model classification, not a law of physical reality.”

**Valid.** Current result covers a free one-particle scalar sector on exactly static compact
backgrounds. Calling it a universal law without the class qualifier would be false.

### R2. “The underlying field already obeys a finite Klein--Gordon PDE.”

**Valid and central.** HT concerns elimination to one global scalar return signal, not absence of a
finite microscopic equation. “Time has no equation” is inadmissible. The strongest defensible
phrase is “this clock signal has no finite algebraic differential closure.”

### R3. “Once the square-root dispersion is assumed, measuring one calibrated line determines
`c`; three lines add no physics.”

**Partly valid.** Three lines remove an unknown affine energy offset and test curvature directly,
but the inference still depends on prior model validation. The three-beat statistic is an elegant
classifier, not yet a new physical mechanism.

### R4. “Exact HT is experimentally undecidable near `c=0`.”

**Valid without a resolution statement.** The `4 epsilon` bound gives a falsifiable threshold but
cannot resolve arbitrarily small `c`. No finite experiment establishes exact zero.

### R5. “Applying a century-old theorem to a textbook spectrum lacks outstanding importance.”

**Currently unanswered.** A Nature case needs a consequence that changes physical reasoning:
an interaction-stable boundary, a causal/cosmological constraint, a demonstrated experiment, or a
new quantitative prediction. Formal depth and a public headline do not substitute for this.

### R6. “The headline confuses a chosen observable with time itself.”

**Valid.** Public language must say “quantum clock” or “return signal,” and the main text must state
the observable and coefficient field before using “finite equation.”

## Nature significance gate
