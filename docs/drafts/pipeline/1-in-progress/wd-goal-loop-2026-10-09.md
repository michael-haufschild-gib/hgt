## Programme: single-height RH criterion (genus-0 Edrei–ASW by deflation)

Target: RH ⟺ the coefficients `b` of `1/Ξ(√y)` (or of `1/(Ξ(t+σ)Ξ(t−σ))` at one height) have all consecutive-row Toeplitz minors `≥ 0`.
- A. Bordered limit (HTGeometry): if `b_{N+1}/b_N → ρ`, then consecutive-row `m`-minors of `b' = b − ρ·shift b` are limits of `(m+1)`-minors of `b` with an extra far column `N`, divided by `b_{N−i₀−m}`. Hence consecutive-row TP passes to `b'`.
- B. Ratio limit: log-concave positive coefficients of a function analytic on `|y|<D` with a pole on `|y| = D` have `b_{N+1}/b_N → 1/D`.
- C. Step: the minimum-modulus law gives the pole at `+D`. `G = dslope F (−D)` has reciprocal coefficients `D·(b − b'/D)`; A and B give TP of the new reciprocal.
- D. Induction on the zero count in the closed disk of radius `|y₀|`: every zero is negative real. A non-real pair on a circle is excluded once the positive pole there is exhausted.
- E. ξ: the centre function `F(y) = ξ(½+√y)` as an entire power series; RH ⟹ reciprocal PF∞ via geometric factors; the converse via D.
- Later: Jacobi complementary minors to state it for the slice coefficients themselves.
