## Research question

Assume individual Xi zeros have no independent reason: the divisor is globally coupled and local
certificates are shadows of cross-zero compatibility. Find an unconditional, source-defined cover
of the off-line strip whose chartwise producer obligations are individually weaker, genuinely
complementary, and not a renamed RH endpoint.

No claim is frozen yet. This log precedes conjecture selection. It records the complete internal
novelty boundary needed to avoid rediscovery and endpoint theatre.

## Acceptance test

A surviving architecture must satisfy all of the following.

1. **Source-first:** charts and producer data are defined without selecting, enumerating, or tuning
   against Xi zeros.
2. **Cross-zero:** essential law couples at least two spectral locations, two charts, or a finite
   packet under a non-factorizing compatibility condition.
3. **Strictly local burden:** no fixed chart obligation implies RH, and this weakness is proved in
   a parent class or by an exact information-factorization theorem.
4. **Complementarity:** joint force comes from an explicit overlap/refinement/cocycle/conservation
   law, not from the tautology that every point lies in some box.
5. **No endpoint redressing:** replacing chart vocabulary by zero-set, Hankel, Weil, Li, Jensen,
   or positivity vocabulary must not reveal a pre-existing RH equivalence with identical content.
6. **Producer surface:** at least one source-side statement is stronger than the divisor conclusion
   and has a plausible attack independent of the target zero configuration.
7. **Closed Lean target:** first formal theorem is unconditional and closed; it introduces no RH
   premise, producer hypothesis, or new axiom.

## Working hypothesis, not yet a conjecture

### Governing thesis

Existing RH programmes do not contain the sought producer. Suzuki/de Branges positivity, Deninger
cohomology and dynamics, Connes semilocal trace geometry, Weil/Hankel forms, Jensen/Laguerre--Polya
coefficient tests, Ward currents, and zero statistics are shadows of a larger and qualitatively
different law, object, or constraint. Their repeated arrival at exact RH-equivalent endpoints is
evidence of projection loss: each route can recognize a valid divisor after receiving it, but none
retains the pre-divisor compatibility that generates admissible divisors.

The audit therefore does **not** seek the best existing route or a hybrid conjunction of route
endpoints. It seeks an unknown parent category and the information destroyed by each shadow map.

Write provisionally

```text
                         unknown parent source object P
                       /       |        |        \
              Suzuki shadow  Deninger  Connes   Weil/Ward/... shadows
                       \       |        |        /
                         the same Xi divisor data
```

Each lower route may be faithful after universal quantification and still be useless for production.
The target datum lies in the kernel of every individual projection but not in the kernel of their
pre-scalarized joint compatibility. Candidate forms include a higher cocycle, descent obstruction,
transition 2-cell, determinant-line orientation, refinement anomaly, noncommutative incidence law,
or a new structure not represented by present vocabulary.

Zeros are not coordinates of `P` and do not receive separate proofs. They are singular events or
observable shadows forced by one global configuration of `P`. “Different reasons” may appear only
because different local probes expose different faces of the same parent constraint.

### Conceptual correction: zeros are failures, not outputs

The parent object must not be a mechanism that *creates* critical-line zeros. A zero is an error
locus: failure of invertibility, exactness, descent, comparison, factorization, or global
completion. The unknown law classifies which failures are admissible. RH should therefore read:

> every failure of the completed arithmetic object is neutral/balanced.

Different zeros may be genuinely different obstruction classes and may fail for different local
reasons. What is universal is not their producer but the conservation/compatibility law restricting
every admissible failure to the neutral grade. Consequently:

- a spectrum, determinant, or canonical product is downstream unless it independently constructs
  the object whose exactness or invertibility can fail;
- explaining why zeros exist or how they are distributed is not yet the target;
- a viable parent theory defines an error type before inserting the Xi divisor;
- critical-line symmetry must restrict error classes, not label already known zeros;
- an off-line contradiction must say that a non-neutral failure violates a global source law, not
  merely that a scalar endpoint becomes negative.

This is a hard audit criterion for every route below.

### Candidate interpretation under audit: completed topological defects

“Topological defect” is a mathematically testable interpretation; “soliton” is not yet earned.
Current corpus evidence suggests the following provisional model.

- Finite arithmetic stages are zero-free and carry trivial local monodromy; defect charge can be
  born only under infinite completion.
- A zero is a nonzero annihilator/error class of the completed source presentation. Multiplicity is
  its jet length or local integral charge.
- Ordinary local winding is insufficient: it records multiplicity but not horizontal position.
  Each defect therefore needs both an integral charge and a duality grade
  `Re(ρ) - 1/2` (or an intrinsic replacement for that scalar shadow).
- RH would state that every completed defect is self-dual/neutral-grade. Distinct zeros may carry
  different obstruction cocycles while satisfying the same global neutrality law.

To promote “defect” to “soliton”, the audit must independently find a source field/configuration
space, nonlinear evolution, localization, finite energy/action, topological sector, and stability.
Absent those data, soliton language is rejected as metaphor. A viable defect theory must also beat
the local-winding no-go by recovering the duality grade from global transition data.

### Consequences for corpus audit

For every route, record four additional items.

1. **Shadow map:** exact declaration where source structure is scalarized, decategorified, traced,
   determinant-reduced, or converted to divisor data.
2. **Lost kernel:** deformations invisible after that map, especially formalized countermodels.
3. **Shared invariant:** datum also appearing in at least one unrelated route before scalarization.
4. **Lift obstruction:** what would be required to reconstruct a common parent object from route
   shadows, and whether corpus proves such reconstruction impossible or incomplete.

Hybridizing terminal inequalities is forbidden. Only pre-shadow compatibility may survive.

This hypothesis survives only if corpus pass finds unconditional source constructions before the
divisor and a joint compatibility law not already equivalent to RH by scalarization.
