## Claim

For self-adjoint idempotents `P,Q`, define their reflections and quantum walk by

```text
R_P = 2P - 1,
R_Q = 2Q - 1,
W   = R_P R_Q.
```

Then

```text
W^* W = 1,
W W^* = 1,
(W-1)^*(W-1) = 4 (P-Q)^*(P-Q),
W-W^* = 4 (PQ-QP),
W+W^* = 2 - 4 (P-Q)^*(P-Q).
```

The first two equalities discharge both orientations of unitarity from the projection laws.  The
third says that the walk scattering-defect master constraint is exactly the projection-mismatch
master constraint, with the sharp factor four.  The fourth places the noncommuting orientation
channel in the anti-Hermitian part of the same unitary, and the fifth is its Hermitian signal.

There is also a direct localization-block value law:

```text
Q (W+1) Q = 2 Q P Q.
```

Thus the standard alternating/prolate compression is not merely analogous to the quantum walk:
it is exactly the second-sector compression of its `+1` shift.

Define the denominator-free cosine recurrence

```text
C_0(X) = 2,
C_1(X) = X,
C_{n+2}(X) = X C_{n+1}(X) - C_n(X).
```

Then the two-sided inverse relation between `W` and `W^*` gives, at every order,

```text
C_n(2 - 4 (P-Q)^*(P-Q)) = W^n + (W^*)^n.
```

Thus the entire symmetric walk-phase hierarchy is a triangular polynomial repackaging of the
projection-mismatch energy.  It is not an independent family of source constraints.

The odd companion

```text
S_0(X) = 0,
S_1(X) = 1,
S_{n+2}(X) = X S_{n+1}(X) - S_n(X)
```

similarly gives

```text
W^n - (W^*)^n
  = S_n(2 - 4 (P-Q)^*(P-Q)) 4(PQ-QP).
```

So higher antisymmetric powers do not provide a new orientation generator either: they are mixed
commutator--mismatch observables generated by the first-order commutator.

These are static operator identities.  No time evolution, numerical experiment, spectral
decomposition, or asymptotic computation is used.

## Physics provenance

Szegedy's quantization of a Markov chain defines the walk as a product of two reflections and
derives the spectrum of the product.  This is the primary quantum-walk source for the operator
used here:

- M. Szegedy, *Spectra of Quantized Walks and a sqrt(delta epsilon)-Rule*, 2004,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0401053>; conference version, *Quantum Speed-Up of Markov Chain
  Based Algorithms*, FOCS 2004, <https://doi.org/10.1109/FOCS.2004.53>.

The present contribution to the corpus is not the product-of-reflections construction itself.
It is the exact simultaneous polarization of the two operator values already used here:
projection mismatch in the Hermitian walk defect and the projection commutator in the
anti-Hermitian walk defect.

Low and Chuang's qubitization framework treats a Hermitian signal encoded in a unitary walk and
organizes powers of the walk through phase/Chebyshev polynomials.  The general primary source is:

- G. H. Low and I. L. Chuang, *Hamiltonian Simulation by Qubitization*, 2019,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06546>; *Quantum* 3, 163,
  <https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2019-07-12-163>.

The corpus relation is deliberately weaker in prerequisites and sharper for the present use: it
uses only the algebraic two-sided inverse equations and proves the exact denominator-free
recurrence without spectral decomposition, functional calculus, or ancillary qubits.

The even/odd polynomial organization is developed systematically in quantum singular-value
transformation:

- A. Gilyén, Y. Su, G. H. Low, and N. Wiebe, *Quantum Singular Value Transformation and Beyond*,
  2019, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01838>.

Here it is consumed only as an algebraic value relation: no quantum circuit or algorithmic claim
is imported into the corpus.
