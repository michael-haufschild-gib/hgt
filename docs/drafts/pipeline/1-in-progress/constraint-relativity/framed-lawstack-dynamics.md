## 1. Founding verdict

No single carrier, cohomology group, metric, relation lattice, or completion defect explains the
corpus. Hostile audit finds eight logically independent gates. Collapsing them into one word such
as “admission” is unfalsifiable.

The proposed field is therefore not geometry of a relation on one space. It is geometry and
dynamics of a **fully structured source law object together with its heterogeneous observer
stack**.

> **Framed Lawstack Dynamics.** A mathematical object is specified by the source operations which
> generate questions and relations, the structures those laws must preserve, and the entire stack
> of admissible observations. Arithmetic, spectral, geometric, and operational representations
> are charts of this lawstack. Curvature is failure of a source-generated law to admit one jointly
> conservative structured realization, not failure of a scalar equation.

“Lawstack” is a working name. An ordinary stack is not assumed sufficient.

which squares an odd class is not an equivalence of law objects.

functors.


scalar consequence afterward.
