  every safe base `sigma > 1`, the moving power

  the imaginary axis.  Its literal complex-damped Möbius column is in `ell²`
  with norm square exactly `1`.
- **MSC-T2 (new, formal):** Among all real power exponents at a fixed safe
  base, `r = sigma - Re(rho)` is exactly equivalent to preservation of the
  source boundary-mass parameter at `sigma`.  Threshold tracking is forced by
  the source mass law, not a chosen normalization.

  physical column multiplied by `n^(-r(sigma))`, but every real power shifts
  its affine spectral target from `rho` to `rho+r`.  Threshold tracking lands
  at `sigma + i Im(rho)`, a Xi-zero-free Euler-safe point.

  simultaneously preserve the physical source mass parameter at `sigma` and
  preserve the divisor target `rho`.  Thus the apparent norm-one escape does
  **not** retain conormal incidence; it closes the complete diagonal real-power
  branch, including moving exponents.
- **MSC-O1 (remaining hard gate):** A viable source-framed horizontal estimate

  restating vanishing of the critical Ward coefficient.
