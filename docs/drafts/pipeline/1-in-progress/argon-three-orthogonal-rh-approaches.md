## A. Gauss–Mayer and loop-Eisenstein scattering

`TheLoopEisensteinReflectionCarriesTheHodgeDefect.lean` defines `reflectionPoles` as denominator zeros, not analytic poles. Its RH equivalence is proved. Numerator noncancellation and the bridge to the Mayer operator require separate verification.

`SelbergScatteringNativeResidual.lean` already proves that every actual Xi scattering energy has strictly positive optimal residual in the original modular self-adjoint domain, including critical zeros. Reusing self-adjoint spectral exclusion would therefore reject the desired zeros too. This existing obstruction is not a new result.

`SelbergScatteringPolynomialXiObstruction.real_on_all_xi_copies_iff` further classifies fixed polynomial changes of the native Casimir: only real constants send all actual Xi copies into a real spectrum. Arbitrarily high genuine critical-line scattering poles defeat every nonconstant polynomial. Thus polynomial recentering does not repair the domain obstruction.

**Open target:** Identify a source-specific resonance exclusion mechanism surviving this domain obstruction.

**Theorem A1:** A rational function of the native Casimir is real on infinitely many regular critical-line Casimirs iff it is a real constant. The classification, quantitative degree bound, sharp four-point example, and actual scattering-pole obstruction are Lean-certified and axiom-audited. See [the formalized theorem](../3-formalized/rational-scattering-reality-degree-bound.md). Totalized division at poles is excluded explicitly.

**Proof:** Write `C(t)=3/16+t²/4+it/4`. In a reduced fraction `P/Q` with monic `Q`, set `A=P∘C`, `B=Q∘C`. Infinite reality implies `ĀB=AB̄`. Coprimeness forces `B | B̄`; equal degree and the real leading coefficient of `B` give `B=B̄`. The existing polynomial classifier makes `Q=1`, then makes `P` a real constant. The nonzero cross polynomial has degree at most `2(deg P+deg Q)`, bounding the number of regular real matches. The bound four is attained for degree-one numerator and denominator by `i(z-3/2)/(z-1/2)` at `t=±√7/2, ±√15/2`. Hardy's theorem supplies arbitrarily high genuine scattering poles outside the finite exceptional set, where the rational transform is regular and nonreal.

**Surviving changes of spectral variable:** For `λ=(ρ/2)(1-ρ/2)` and `E=(ρ-1/2)²`, `ρ=E+4λ-1/4` gives a quadratic relation. Separately, the corpus already constructs a single-valued entire transform `F` with `F(C(t))=cosh(4πt)` (`SelbergScatteringEntireTransform`). Its actual operator packets have zero limiting defect iff the original zero is critical (`SelbergScatteringEntireXiPackets.xiEntirePacket_residual_zero_iff`). Recovering a real-looking scalar therefore does not supply the missing source-specific spectral occurrence. A1 concerns fixed rational scalar transports, not general Padé approximation of transfer operators.

**Theorem A2:** An irreducible algebraic correspondence real at infinitely many critical Casimirs and infinitely many native spectral values `x>1/4` is a nonzero scalar multiple of a real horizontal line. Quadratic-cover factor orbits and two parallel reality lines give the rigidity. The irreducible correspondence `(4z−3/4−w)²+w=0` contains every critical Casimir with `w=t²`, and its real base locus is exactly `z≤1/4`. Both the obstruction and this sharp nonconstant survivor are Lean-certified with clean axiom audits. [Exact theorem and paper proof](../3-formalized/algebraic-scattering-two-reality-loci.md). This closes simultaneous algebraic recentering of both loci, not the broader resonance route.

**Theorem A3, actual Mayer operators:** On the Banach disc algebra `|z−1|≤3/2`, the genuine first-`N` Gauss branches with exponent `w=2s` converge in operator norm after subtracting `N^(1−w)/(1−w)` times the norm-one projection `f↦f(0)·1`. The explicit error is `O(N^(−Re w))` for `Re w>0`, `w≠1`, and the result agrees with the ordinary branch sum for `Re w>1`. For `0<Re w<1`, the normalized raw operators converge to `P/(1−w)`, their norms grow exactly as `N^(1−Re w)/|1−w|`, and this leading correction is unique among all bounded operators. Hence raw cutoffs diverge at every actual zeta scattering parameter, including critical zeros. [Paper proof and certified declarations](../3-formalized/gauss-mayer-renormalized-cutoff-operator.md); [precise obstruction and positive survivor](../5-failed/raw-gauss-mayer-cutoffs-at-zeta-resonances.md). ## B. Böttcher and arithmetic period rigidity

`AlgebraicVerticalFirstJets.lean` detects all polynomial partial derivatives only at algebraic torus points with algebraic coefficients. Applying it at a zeta zero requires an independently proved arithmetic realization. The proposed nonlinear equation and realization have not been specified.

Thus actual Xi zeros cannot supply the algebraic torus point required by the cited Baker first-jet theorem on three or more prime coordinates. This is already formalized mathematics, not a new result of this investigation.

`BottcherContinuation.basin_monodromy_image_eq_dyadicRootsOfUnity` gives the same full dyadic-root multiplier group for every disconnected quadratic basin. The unmarked group alone cannot distinguish parameters within this class; a proposed zero-location detector needs additional marked periods and a proved map from zeta to those data. Both the canonical coupling and its source trivialization remain open. `BottcherPrueferXiCharge.lean` proves only the multiplicity-character consumer; replacing its missing coupling by the arbitrary character `ζ_n^ord(Xi)` is explicitly a closed shortcut.

**Open target:** Construct an exact arithmetic realization or prove an obstruction for a precise proposed class. Audit existing Böttcher monodromy and hypertranscendence results before formulating that class.

## C. Two-place Krein reciprocity

`TheGluingFormulaAtEveryPlace.lean` gives the full index identity. Its state is defined on rational affine transformations: the successor term uses the p-adic norm, but `ratCorr` remains a global, archimedean-continuous dilation correlation. Compact Fourier support of local indicators does not establish compact support or even p-adic continuity of this correlation.

**Theorem C1:** Every p-adic principal-unit neighborhood, viewed as a subgroup of positive rational slopes, retains the entire negative index of `ratCorr`.

**Paper mechanism:** Weak approximation for the inequivalent real and p-adic absolute values makes each principal-unit group dense in positive real slopes. Its logarithms are therefore dense in ℝ. `negIndex_orbitKernel_comp` then preserves the exact zero-count index. This proves an obstruction to removing negative squares by localizing slopes; it does not exclude a genuinely global two-place argument.

**Positive survivor:** Exact preservation of the spectral index at every p-adic resolution. Lean proofs, direct file checks, transitive axiom audits, and umbrella imports are complete. See [the formalized theorem](../3-formalized/every-padic-neighborhood-carries-the-whole-weil-index.md).

**Theorem C2:** Every principal-unit neighborhood approximates every global value of `ratCorr`. Since its coprime mixed difference is nonzero, a fixed positive oscillation survives in all such neighborhoods. This rules out p-adic continuity at the identity unconditionally, including under RH. The Lean proof has the same clean axiom audit.

**Theorem C7, simultaneous local conditions and exact support boundary:** For finite prime sets `S,T`, positive `S`-units satisfying arbitrary principal-unit depths at every prime in `T` equal the same group with support `S\T`. Their logarithms are dense exactly when at least two free primes remain. No free prime gives the trivial group; one free prime `q` gives precisely `d log(q)·ℤ` for some `d>0`. On the dense side, the full negative and mass-zero Weil indices survive, every global correlation value is approximable, and one positive oscillation works uniformly across all supports, places and depths. This resolves C3's finite-intersection question through a common Euler power. [Paper proof and certified declarations](../3-formalized/principal-s-unit-logarithmic-support-boundary.md); all five production audits use only the standard three axioms. The one-prime logarithmic lattice may still detect the full finite zero stratum; density is not asserted necessary for index preservation. These finite intersections are not finite-idèle neighborhoods.

The complementary boundary is already formalized in `FieldOneFiniteStageBruhatDenseRange.fieldOneSUnit_abs_eq_one_of_localNorm_one`: unit conditions at the selected places reduce an `S`-unit to a sign. Its dense half-density construction retains an independent positive real Schwartz factor. `DeningerSUnitEndpointPage.deningerRat_eq_one_of_valuations` proves the all-prime positive-rational rigidity directly.

**Theorem C4, native affine state:** Restricting `weilPadicState p c` (`c>0`) to affine transformations with slope in `H(p,n)` and arbitrary rational translation retains `negIndex(ratCorr)+1`. The new input is full negative-index preservation on the mass-zero hyperplane inside every subgroup with dense logarithms, including `H(p,n)`. The paper proof, Lean declarations, and clean axiom audits are in [the affine restriction card](../3-formalized/principal-unit-affine-state-index.md).
