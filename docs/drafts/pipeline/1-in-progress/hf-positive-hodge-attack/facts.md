- **HF-F1 [corpus, L]:** Conformal (flow-isometric) completions of Meyer's `M_ζ` are blind off the critical line. `DeningerMeyerHalfDensityL1Completion` keeps Ξ-evaluation continuous exactly at critical zeros. `DeningerMeyerHalfDensityXiGraphClosure` makes off-line evaluations maximally multivalued. **Route:** Deninger–Singhof Kähler positivity, which lives on such a completion, sees only critical zeros.
- **HF-F2 [Lean, general; `RHTower.ConformalHodgeBlindness.not_bounded_of_conformal_offLine`, axioms clean]:** Take a seminorm `p` with `p(V_c u)=e^{κc}p(u)` and a character `E` with `E(V_c u)=e^{λc}E(u)`, `Re λ≠κ`, `E≠0`. Then `E` is not `p`-bounded: `w_c=e^{−λc}V_c u₀` has `E(w_c)=E(u₀)` and `p(w_c)=e^{(κ−Re λ)c}p(u₀)→0` along one direction of `c`. **Route:** blindness is structural for every conformal Hodge norm, not a feature of one completion.
- **HF-F3 [corpus, L]:** A faithful positive flow-invariant Hermitian form exists on a semisimple defect core iff the divisor is real (`EntireMultiplierDefectUniversality.nonempty_positiveInvariantForm_iff`). Conjugate-pair blocks `[[0,c],[c̄,0]]` are the obstruction. **Route:** the Hodge gate has no geometric freedom beyond faithfulness.
- **HF-F4 [paper]:** Complementarity.
  - Acausal completion (`L²` on `Re s=½`): the flow is a unitary group, the Poisson range closure is reducing, the quotient is conformal, and the spectrum is the critical zeros.
  - Causal completion (Hardy space of `Re s>½`, functions supported in `(0,1]`, the `Pg` range invariant): the flow is an isometric semigroup, and by Beurling the range closure is `I_ζ·H²` (`I_ζ` = inner factor of ζ). The quotient is the model space `K_{I_ζ}` with the compressed contraction semigroup, whose spectrum is the zeros with `Re ρ>½`.
  - RH ⟺ `K_{I_ζ}=0` ⟺ Nyman–Beurling density (corpus [L] `NymanBeurlingCriterion`).
  - **Route:** positivity plus conformal quotient ⇒ blind; positivity plus faithful ⇒ non-conformal (compressed) quotient. Purity needs an invertible conformal flow on all of `H¹`, which is RH.
- **HF-F5 [corpus]:** Half-port HB is dead (`theta-half-line-port-alternating-orientation`): both modulus orders fail at critical zeros with alternating crossing signs. - **HF-F7 [paper]:** J-pairing loop. On `K₊ ⊕ K₋` (`K₋ = J K₊`, `J` the functional-equation reflection), the flow-natural pairing of the `ρ`-mode with the `1−ρ̄`-mode scales by `e^{c(ρ−½)}·e^{c(½−ρ)} = 1`, so it is conformal and faithful. - **HF-F8 [paper]:** Resolvent core. On `M_ζ` (Mellin side `F mod ζ·G`), `(λ−Θ)` is bijective iff `ζ(λ)≠0` (take `G(λ)=F(λ)/ζ(λ)`). - **HF-F10 [paper]:** Outer form. The finite Euler products `ζ_X` are outer on `Re s>½`, so their Poisson averages of `log|ζ_X|` from `s=1` equal `log ζ_X(1)` exactly. For ζ itself the Balazard–Saias–Yor excess `I = ∫log|ζ̃(½+it)|dt/(¼+t²) ≥ 0` vanishes iff RH. **Route:** HF's causal side is the uniform integrability of `log|ζ/ζ_X|` on the critical line; the Euler product diverges pointwise there (`Σ_{p≤X}p^{−½−it}` oscillates), so no limit passage is available.
