## Mathematical boundaries

The phrase “every statistic neither gauge-invariant nor finitely determined”
does not define a valid universal typicality principle. Those properties do
not constrain values at the distinguished point. A useful theorem must specify
an arithmetic family and its normalization.

Unique additive invariance is qualitative. Finite integer observations cannot
see prime coordinates beyond their cutoff. Quantitative successor control and
the exact unresolved tail are the first coupling problem.

Adams fixed loci impose root-of-unity identities coordinatewise. Their
vanishing ideal and jet cost must be determined before claiming a producer.
An elementary tensor product giving quadratic dimension is not a surface
effectivity theorem.
