# W4 — the singularities of the reciprocal torus locus

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CANDIDATE
- **Statement:** On `Σx_i=Σx_i⁻¹=0` with nonzero coordinates, dependence of the two differentials is equivalent to equality of all `x_i²`. After normalizing one coordinate to one, every critical coordinate is `±1`.

## Quadratic structure

At a normalized critical point, the reciprocal equation along a sum-zero tangent vector `v` has quadratic term `Σx_i v_i²`. The associated diagonal pairing has radical exactly the scaling line on `Σv_i=0`. Fixing the leading coordinate removes this radical. In characteristic different from two, the transverse Hessian is therefore nondegenerate.

## Proof mechanism

The annihilator of the sum-zero hyperplane consists of constant covectors, tested on differences of coordinate vectors. Apply this to the reciprocal differential with coefficients `−x_i⁻²`. For the quadratic pairing, apply it to coefficients `x_i v_i` and use `x_i²=1`. The formal geometric series for `(x_i+t v_i)⁻¹` identifies the linear and quadratic terms.

## Scope

The target is the exact differential and transverse quadratic-form classification. A cohomological weight theorem, boundary analysis, and an arithmetic family containing ζ remain separate obligations.
