# Arithmetic-projection Leray--Rost--Schmid resolution

- **ID:** `deninger-arithmetic-projection-leray-rost-schmid-resolution`
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** SOURCE CONJECTURE
- **Label:** `DAC_ARITHMETIC_PROJECTION_LERAY_ROST_SCHMID_RESOLUTION`
- **Parent:** `deninger-coarse-invariant-sheaf-rost-schmid-descent`
- **Statement:** derived pushforward of Deninger's corrected coarse
  invariant sheaf to the arithmetic curve has two analytic rows, each
  resolved by the length-one Rost--Schmid complex; the real signature
  adds the unique degree-two orientation.

## Goal

Replace broad analytic-realization and quotient-stack comparisons by
one source-native theorem on Deninger's actual coarse coefficient.
Together with `DAC_TWO_COLUMN_HYPERCOHOMOLOGY_AMPLITUDE`, this theorem
constructs the three-term arithmetic cohomology required by
`docs/Deninger program.md`.

## Source objects

Specialize to the arithmetic curve
\[
 S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z,\qquad
 j:\eta=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q\hookrightarrow S,\qquad
 i_p:\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_p\hookrightarrow S.
\]
Let \(a:X_W\to S\) be the arithmetic projection induced by
Deninger's rational-Witt character construction.  On the suspension
cover let
\(\widetilde{\mathscr A}_{\mathrm{geom}}^M\) be the genuine
Tate--Milnor sheaf constructed in
`DAC_LEAFWISE_KUMMER_SHEAF_AMALGAMATION_EXISTENCE`.  Define on the
coarse quotient, degreewise,
\[
 \mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}}
 =
 (\pi_*\widetilde{\mathscr A}_{\mathrm{geom}}^M)
 ^{\mathbb Q_{>0}}.                                                \tag{1}
\]
Existence and operation descent for `(1)` are
`DAC_COARSE_INVARIANT_SHEAF_EXISTENCE`.

Set
\[
 \mathscr L_{\mathrm{Den}}=Ra_*\mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}}.          \tag{2}
\]
This is an ordinary derived direct image of the already-formed
coarse sheaf.  No derived rational-power invariants occur in `(2)`.

## Main conjecture

`[CONJECTURE]
[DAC_ARITHMETIC_PROJECTION_LERAY_ROST_SCHMID_RESOLUTION]`

There is a complete locally convex enhancement of `(1)` with the
following properties.

1. **Two analytic rows.**
   \[
   \mathcal H^q(\mathscr L_{\mathrm{Den}})=0
   \quad(q\notin\{0,1\}).                                         \tag{3}
   \]
   Both surviving sheaves are Hausdorff and carry the descended
   flow, Galois, Frobenius, Verschiebung, and Tate actions.
2. **Unramified Milnor identification.**  For \(q=0,1\), the sheaf
   \(\mathcal M^q=\mathcal H^q(\mathscr L_{\mathrm{Den}})\) is the
   analytic realization of the corresponding real Milnor homotopy
   module generated by Deninger's Kummer regulator.  On the generic
   pro-Stein chart it agrees with
   `DAC_PRO_STEIN_RELATIVE_CELL_GLOBAL_SECTIONS`.
3. **Length-one source resolution.**  Each \(\mathcal M^q\) has a
   functorial exact Rost--Schmid resolution
   \[
   0\longrightarrow\mathcal M^q
   \longrightarrow j_*\mathcal M^q_\eta
   \xrightarrow{\oplus_p\partial_p}
   \prod_p' i_{p,*}\mathcal M^q_p\langle-1\rangle
   \longrightarrow0,                                              \tag{4}
   \]
   where \(\langle-1\rangle\) is the Rost--Schmid Tate/normal-line
   shift and not a change in analytic row \(q\).  Both right-hand
   terms are acyclic for global sections.
4. **Mixed topology and closedness.**  The topology in `(4)` is the
   mixed \(L^1/L^2\) Kummer topology: \(L^1\) makes product formula
   and residue map closed; \(L^2\) gives positive local energy.  The
   reduced differential has closed range.
5. **Operation compatibility.**  Residues satisfy projection formula
   and commute with the descended flow and finite power
   correspondences.  Prime powers affect local trace, not
   cohomological length.
6. **Real boundary.**  The Milnor--Witt signature comparison adjoins
   one acyclic skyscraper
   \(i_{\infty,*}\mathbb R(-1)[-2]\).  The finite-plus-infinite
   product formula identifies the degree-two cokernel with
   \(\mathbb R(-1)\).
7. **Regulator edge.**  Deninger's source regulator into
   \(H^1(\widetilde X,Q_0(1))^{\widetilde G}\) maps to the generic
   degree-one edge of `(4)`; it is not declared to be all of
   \(H^1_{\mathrm{arith}}\).

## Consequence

Clauses 1--6 give
\[
 R\Gamma_{\mathrm{arith}}(X_W,\mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}})
 \simeq
 \operatorname{Tot}\!\left(
   R\Gamma(S,j_*\mathcal M^\bullet_\eta)
   \xrightarrow{\oplus_p\partial_p}
   R\Gamma(S,\prod_p'i_{p,*}\mathcal M^\bullet_p\langle-1\rangle);
   \mathbb R(-1)[-2]\right).                                      \tag{5}
\]
By `DAC_TWO_COLUMN_HYPERCOHOMOLOGY_AMPLITUDE`, `(5)` has a
three-term representative and no cohomology outside degrees
\(0,1,2\).  Deninger's connectedness theorem gives
\(H^0=\mathbb R\); the real Arakelov endpoint theorem gives
\(H^2=\mathbb R(-1)\).  The middle group is defined intrinsically as
\(H^1\) of `(5)`, with no quotient-stack comparison.

## Conjecturing decomposition

The main conjecture splits into four independently falsifiable gates.

### A. Coarse-fibre analytic contraction

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_COFINITE_SUSPENSION_SECTION_AMPLITUDE]`

For every nonzero \(N\in\mathbb Z\), let
\[
 U_N=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/N]\subset S.
\]
Derived sections
\[
 R\Gamma(a^{-1}U_N,\mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}})
\]
are Hausdorff and vanish outside analytic degrees zero and one.
The contractions are compatible under \(U_M\subseteq U_N\) and with
finite correspondences.  By
`DAC_COFINITE_BASE_DIRECT_IMAGE_AMPLITUDE_REDUCTION`, these exact
cofinite-section statements imply clause 1.

No local slice or quotient-adapted Stein basis for the nonproper
\(\mathbb Q_{>0}\)-action is asserted.

`DAC_DENINGER_COFINITE_OPEN_BASE_CHANGE` closes the source-localization
part of A.  Deninger's character space, common Frobenius colimit,
suspension, and underived invariant coefficient restrict canonically to
each \(U_N\):
\[
 X_{U_N}\cong a^{-1}U_N,\qquad
 \mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}}|_{a^{-1}U_N}
 \cong
 \left(
   \pi_{U_N,*}
   \widetilde{\mathscr A}_{\mathrm{geom}}^M|_{\pi^{-1}a^{-1}U_N}
 \right)^{\mathbb Q_{>0}}.
\]
Thus the cofinite comparison computes the restriction of Deninger's
global source coefficient, not a separately chosen quotient object.

`DAC_COFINITE_DIVISOR_SPLIT_STRICTNESS` closes the arithmetic
generic-to-prime algebra after the analytic row has been identified:
for \(S_N=\{p:p\mid N\}\),
\[
 0\to
 \bigoplus_{p\mid N}\mathbb R[p]
 \to
 \bigoplus_p\mathbb R[p]
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_{p\nmid N}}
 \bigoplus_{p\nmid N}\mathbb R[p]
 \to0
\]
is canonically split exact, and stays strict after the mixed weighted
\(L^1/L^2\) completion.  Thus A has one exact analytic open core:

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_COFINITE_COARSE_ANALYTIC_DIVISOR_COMPARISON]`

For every \(N\), ordinary derived sections of the already-formed
coarse invariant sheaf on \(a^{-1}U_N\) are functorially
quasi-isomorphic to the two-row analytic Kummer complex whose
weight-one horizontal row is the split divisor sequence above.
The comparison is compatible with restriction in \(N\), finite
correspondences, and the flow.  It is a comparison of source
coarse-sheaf sections, not a comparison with derived group
invariants.

The ordinary-analytic Cartier version of prime purity is retired:
Deninger constructs \([p]\) only as a generalized monoid-valued
divisor.  `DAC_ARITHMETIC_PRO_LOG_KUMMER_DIVISOR_RESOLUTION` supplies
the exact positive replacement.  Finite divisorial log structures on
\(S\) give
\[
 0\to\mathscr O_S^\times
 \to\mathscr M_P^{\mathrm{gp}}
 \xrightarrow{\oplus_{p\in P}\operatorname{ord}_p}
 \bigoplus_{p\in P}i_{p,*}\mathbb Z
 \to0,
\]
their pullbacks give disjoint generalized-log prime normal lines on
Deninger's space, and rational-power scaling is cancelled by the Tate
twist.  Proposition 11.7 identifies their supports with
\(\operatorname{div}[p]\).

The remaining comparison decomposes into one local analytic purity
statement and one coarse-section statement.

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_GENERALIZED_LOG_PRIME_ANALYTIC_PURITY]`

The Tate--Milnor analytic coefficient satisfies purity for the
pulled-back pro-log divisor: the localization boundary of the Kummer
class of \(p\) is the unit generator in
\(i_{p,*}\mathbb R(-1)\), compatibly with the flow, finite
correspondences, and rational powers.  This asserts compatibility of
the analytic coefficient with an already constructed generalized-log
normal line; it does not turn \([p]\) into an ordinary analytic
function.

`DAC_SPECZ_COFINITE_RECOLLEMENT_BASIS` removes the need for an
auxiliary prime-star analytic site.  The actual topology of
\(S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\) is the cofinite generic-prime
star, and every finite prime set \(P\) gives the source recollement
\[
 P\hookrightarrow S\hookleftarrow S\setminus P.
\]
Open base change pulls it back to Deninger's actual source space.

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_COFINITE_PRO_RECOLLEMENT_ANALYTIC_COMPARISON]`

For the filtered system of finite \(P\) disjoint from \(S_N\), the
localization triangles of the restricted source coefficient satisfy:

1. generic terms are computed by
   `DAC_PRO_STEIN_RELATIVE_CELL_GLOBAL_SECTIONS`;
2. prime-support terms are computed by
   `DAC_GENERALIZED_LOG_PRIME_ANALYTIC_PURITY`;
3. finite disjoint sums pass strictly to the weighted direct-sum
   residue row;
4. the filtered transition cone has no derived-limit defect.

The resulting normalized pro-recollement complex is functorially the
two-row Kummer divisor complex.  This is a statement about actual
source sheaves and finite localization triangles.  Treating chartwise
global cochains as the coefficient would repeat the retired
`deninger-chart-cochains-as-coefficient-sheaf` type error.

### B. Milnor row identification

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_COARSE_MILNOR_ROW_IDENTIFICATION]`

The two sheaves left by A are the realizations of the degree-zero and
degree-one real Milnor homotopy-module rows, including every relative
Steinberg cell.  This is a sheaf statement, not a stalk-amplitude
claim.

### C. Strict Rost--Schmid realization

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_STRICT_ROST_SCHMID_ANALYTIC_REALIZATION]`

Milnor residues realize as `(4)`, and the mixed \(L^1/L^2\)
completion preserves exactness, acyclicity, and closed range.

### D. Real signature comparison

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_REAL_SIGNATURE_SKYSCRAPER_COMPARISON]`

The framed real Gysin line realizes as the unique skyscraper in
clause 6 and satisfies finite-plus-infinite reciprocity.

A--D imply the main conjecture.

## Attack

1. Use Deninger's finite-flat sheafification of
   \(W_{\mathrm{rat}}\) on Dedekind rings and its finite
   correspondence description to build the arithmetic projection and
   its generic/closed specialization maps on \(S\).
2. Extend character evaluation to the generated relative
   Tate--Milnor cell category; no realization on unrelated motives is
   required.
3. Use `DAC_DENINGER_COFINITE_OPEN_BASE_CHANGE` to work on the actual
   restricted source coefficient.  Use
   `DAC_ARITHMETIC_PRO_LOG_KUMMER_DIVISOR_RESOLUTION` for the
   generalized-log prime normal lines and prove analytic Kummer purity
   against those lines.
4. Use `DAC_SPECZ_COFINITE_RECOLLEMENT_BASIS` and pull its finite
   open--closed decompositions back to the coarse suspension.  Prove
   `DAC_COFINITE_PRO_RECOLLEMENT_ANALYTIC_COMPARISON`, including
   strict filtered passage and vanishing of the derived-limit defect.
5. Deduce `DAC_COFINITE_COARSE_ANALYTIC_DIVISOR_COMPARISON`, compose
   it with `DAC_COFINITE_DIVISOR_SPLIT_STRICTNESS`, and apply
   `DAC_COFINITE_BASE_DIRECT_IMAGE_AMPLITUDE_REDUCTION`.
6. Identify the contracted rows through Kummer symbols and the
   complete Steinberg cell filtration.
7. Use dimension-one Rost--Schmid exactness over \(S\), then prove
   strictness after mixed completion.
8. Glue the real signature line and apply the existing two-column
   totalization, cup, flow, and endpoint theorems.

## Falsifiers

- Chartwise amplitude does not imply A; the proof must compute sections
  on every \(a^{-1}U_N\) after coarse sheafification.
- A local-slice or Stein-basis argument is invalid because Deninger's
  rational-power action is not properly discontinuous.
- Finite-constructible exodromy does not apply: separating the generic
  point from all prime points of \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\) is
  not a finite constructible stratification.  Exodromy is therefore
  neither A nor a proof of A.
- Stalk Tor may survive even if A holds and cannot be discarded.
- Bare \(L^2\) completion fails the product-formula closedness gate.
- Derived \(\mathbb Q_{>0}\)-invariants, a bar complex, or
  quotient-stack cohomology are not `(2)` and cannot prove the
  conjecture.
- Any realization defined with Xi zeros, RH, Weil positivity, or a
  zeta spectral model is circular.
- If `(3)` fails or `(4)` has a nonclosed range, arithmetic amplitude
  \([0,2]\) is not established.
