1. For each prefix `P_(i,n)`, take the local deck pseudogroup of the finite branched cover and all
   of its jet prolongations.  An element lies in `P_(i,n)^*F_(i+n)` exactly when it is invariant
   under that deck relation.
2. Let `Gamma_i` be the differential pseudogroup generated by all prefix deck relations.  Then

   ```text
   T_i=F_i^(Gamma_i).                                      (1)
   ```

3. If `T_i` is nonconstant, the differential-Zariski closure `G_i` of `Gamma_i` is a proper
   transitive differential groupoid on `P1_i`.
4. Apply the Lie--Cartan/Casale classification of proper transitive differential groupoids on a
   curve.  Its rational geometric structure is one of finite rank:

   - a rational tensor/parallelism;
   - a rational affine connection;
   - a rational projective connection.
5. Define the finite singularity defect of the connection or projective connection.  Pullback by a
   prefix replicates every terminal defect over all inverse sheets.  Prove a degree-amplified
   inequality analogous to the tensor law

   ```text
   Defect_i >= degree(P_(i,n)) Defect_(i+n).                (2)
   ```

   Hence the late structure is defect-free/parabolic.
6. On `P1`, a defect-free expanding affine or projective structure has Euclidean orbifold
   holonomy.  Expansion quantizes the local holonomy exactly as in the shifted tensor-order
   argument.  A finite holonomy power yields a rational transported tensor and therefore a

7. Invoke the proved first-order converse to identify the complete tail.
