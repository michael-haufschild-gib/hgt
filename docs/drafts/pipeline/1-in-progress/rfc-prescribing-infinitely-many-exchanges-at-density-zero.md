# Prescribing infinitely many exchanges at density zero

- **Stage** 1-in-progress
- **Target** Enforce every prescribed edge or fixed vertex on any set of natural density zero inside one global restricted involution with sublinear unpaid prefixes.

Let \(F\) be the short density-one involution and let \(T\) be the prescribed set. Use the prescribed involution \(G\) on \(T\). Outside \(T\), fix each former partner of \(T\) and keep all remaining old pairs. The two-cycle law follows from invariance of \(T\) under \(G\) and involutivity of \(F\). Every prescription holds, including fixed-vertex prescriptions.

At cutoff \(X\) put \(Y\ge\max_{n\le X}F(n)\). The new unpaid count is at most the old count plus \(\#(T\cap[0,X])+\#(T\cap[0,Y])\). The second term bounds the displaced partners by the injection \(n\mapsto F(n)\). Eventually one may take \(Y=2X\) since the old displacement is sublinear. Thus the new unsigned unpaid count is still \(o(X)\). If the prescribed displacements are sublinear, so are the displacements of the combined involution. No condition on the sign, prime factors or distribution of the prescribed vertices beyond their stated density and the legal moves is required.
