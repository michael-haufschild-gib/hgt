# Source-logarithmic boundary representation theorem

- **ID:** `source-logarithmic-boundary-matroid`
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / formalization open
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED HERE] [CANDIDATE NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Statement:** For multiplicative observables governed by an arbitrary, possibly noncommuting
  family of compatible differential-field automorphisms, the complete differential-algebraic
  matroid is represented by their logarithmic source increments modulo simultaneous
  coboundaries. Every nonlinear differential dependence is therefore detected by one finite
  linear differential character on a source boundary quotient.

## Setup

Let `C -> K -> L` be a tower of ordinary differential fields of characteristic zero. Assume that
`C` is a differential constant field. Let

```text
tau_i   : K ~= K,
sigma_i : L ~= L                         (i in I)
```

be differential-field automorphisms satisfying

```text
sigma_i(k) = tau_i(k),
sigma_i(c) = c,
L^(all sigma_i) = C.
```

No composition law or pairwise commutativity is imposed on the family. Let `x_a in L^*` and
`b_(i,a) in K^*` satisfy

```text
sigma_i(x_a) = b_(i,a) x_a.              (1)
```

Write `D` for the derivation and define the logarithmic source increment

```text
omega_a(i) = D(b_(i,a))/b_(i,a).         (2)
```

The simultaneous source boundary module is

```text
Q_Sigma(K) = K^I / {(tau_i(k)-k)_i : k in K}.             (3)
```

Because every `tau_i` commutes with `D`, the denominator in (3) is a `C[D]`-submodule. Hence
`Q_Sigma(K)` is a `C[D]`-module. Put

```text
V_Sigma(K) = Q_Sigma(K) tensor_(C[D]) C(D).               (4)
```

## Theorem A: exact boundary criterion

For every finite `S subset A`, the family `(x_a)_(a in S)` is differentially algebraically
dependent over `K` if and only if there are differential operators

```text
P_a(D) in C[D], not all zero,
```

and one `k in K` such that, simultaneously for every `i in I`,

```text
tau_i(k)-k = sum_(a in S) P_a(D) omega_a(i).              (5)
```

Equivalently, `(x_a)_(a in S)` is differentially algebraically independent over `K` if and only
if the boundary classes

```text
[omega_a] in V_Sigma(K), a in S,
```

are linearly independent over `C(D)`.

## Theorem B: complete matroid representation

For every finite `S subset A`,

```text
dtrdeg_K K<x_a : a in S>_D
  = dim_(C(D)) span{[omega_a] : a in S}.                  (6)
```

Thus the differential-algebraic matroid of the observable family is representable over the
ordinary field `C(D)`. Its circuits are exactly the finite-support simultaneous coboundary
relations (5). This is a complete nonlinear-to-linear collapse: no additional nonlinear
differential circuit is invisible to the source boundary module.

For a single observable `x`, hypertranscendence over `K` is equivalent to non-torsion of
`[omega_x]` in `Q_Sigma(K)`:

```text
P(D)[omega_x] != 0 for every nonzero P(D) in C[D].        (7)
```

This is the precise sense in which infinite algebraic clock capacity is concentrated on a thin
constraint boundary. The observable is not time. Its capacity is the non-torsion differential
character carried by its source increment class.

## Proof

Put

```text
ell_a = D(x_a)/x_a.                                      (8)
```

The differential fields generated by `(x_a)_(a in S)` and `(ell_a)_(a in S)` have the same
differential transcendence degree over `K`. One inequality follows from `ell_a in K<x_a>_D`.
For the other, each `x_a` satisfies the first-order differential equation

```text
D(Y)-ell_a Y=0
```

over `K<ell_a>_D`, so `K<x_a>_D` is differentially algebraic over `K<ell_a>_D`.

Commutation of `sigma_i` with `D` and (1) give

```text
sigma_i(ell_a) = ell_a + omega_a(i).                     (9)
```

Assume first that `(x_a)_(a in S)` is differentially dependent. By the equality of differential
transcendence degrees, `(ell_a)_(a in S)` is differentially dependent. Apply the already proved
arbitrary-automorphism-family additive Ostrowski theorem to the full jet family of the `ell_a`.
It gives constants `c_(a,n) in C`, finitely supported and not all zero, and one `k in K` such that

```text
tau_i(k)-k = sum_(a,n) c_(a,n) D^n(omega_a(i))            (10)
```

for every `i`. Taking

```text
P_a(D)=sum_n c_(a,n)D^n
```

proves (5).

Conversely, assume (5) and put

```text
g = sum_a P_a(D)ell_a-k.                                 (11)
```

Equations (5) and (9) give `sigma_i(g)=g` for every `i`. Exactness of the common fixed field gives
`g in C`. Equation (11) is then a nonzero linear differential relation among the `ell_a` over
`K`. Hence the `ell_a`, and therefore the `x_a`, are differentially dependent. This proves
Theorem A.

Applying Theorem A to every subset identifies the independent subsets on both sides of (6).
Differential transcendence degree and vector-space dimension are the corresponding matroid rank
functions, proving Theorem B.

## Order-zero positive residue

The same minimal-support method without derivations gives a separate algebraic theorem. If
`sigma_i(x_a)=b_(i,a)x_a`, every Laurent relation over `K` among the `x_a` is generated by
binomials. The algebraic relation lattice is exactly

```text
{m in Z^(A) : exists k in K^*,
  product_a b_(i,a)^m_a = tau_i(k)/k for every i}.        (12)
```

Consequently the algebraic matroid is represented over `Q` by the multiplicative classes of the
`x_a` in `(L^*/K^*) tensor_Z Q`. This order-zero theorem is useful infrastructure, but it is not
the main novelty claim: one-operator `Pi`-extension theory and toric difference geometry are close
parents.

## Novelty gate

Closest parents located:

1. Hardouin--Singer, *Differential Galois Theory of Linear Difference Equations*, Corollary 3.3,
   proves the single-operator multiplicative criterion by logarithmic derivatives and
   parameterized Picard--Vessiot theory.
2. Hardouin, *Hypertranscendance des systemes aux differences diagonaux*, treats diagonal
   difference systems for one structured difference operator.
3. Karr and Schneider's `Pi`-extension theory gives one-operator product telescoping and
   algebraic-independence criteria.
4. Gao--Huang--Yuan and Gao--Huang--Wang--Yuan develop binomial difference ideals and toric
   difference varieties from prescribed difference lattices.
5. The existing project theorem `noncommutative-automorphism-family-ostrowski` gives the additive
   arbitrary-family dependence-to-telescoping theorem used in the proof.

No checked source states the arbitrary, possibly noncommuting automorphism-family equivalence
(5), the single simultaneous base element in every source direction, or the complete matroid
representation (6). The novelty delta is therefore the arbitrary-source exactness and the full
matroid representation, not logarithmic differentiation or the one-operator criterion.

This is affirmative evidence, not proof of absence. Independent MathSciNet/Zentralblatt and
specialist review remain open.

## Significance gate

- The theorem classifies every differential-algebraic circuit of the multiplicative source class,
  rather than proving hypertranscendence of one function.
- Arbitrary and infinite source families are allowed, while every obstruction remains a finite
  differential character with one simultaneous base primitive.
- Pairwise commutativity, a parameterized Picard--Vessiot torsor, differential closure of the
  fixed field, and a source group law are absent from the hypotheses.
- The boundary module (3) is an actual invariant whose rank is observable differential
  transcendence degree. It is not a metaphorical topology.

The theorem is significant only within the multiplicative source class. It does not show that an
arbitrary hypertranscendental function admits equations (1), and it does not yet identify spectral
positivity or self-adjoint boundary defects with classes in (3).

## Bold conjecture register

### `[PROVED HERE]` Split source-boundary universality

Let

```text
G = G_a^r x G_m^s
```

and let an arbitrary, possibly noncommuting family of compatible differential-field
automorphisms act on a finite family of `G`-valued observables by base-field increments. Then the
complete differential-algebraic matroid of the observables is represented by the simultaneous
source-coboundary classes of all differential characters of `G`. Equivalently, every mixed
nonlinear dependence among additive and multiplicative primitives is forced by either a finite
additive differential character, a finite multiplicative logarithmic character, or a linear
combination of these in the character module. There are no character-invisible mixed circuits.

The additive arbitrary-family Ostrowski theorem and Theorems A--B prove the two pure factors. The
mixed assertion follows by replacing each multiplicative coordinate with its logarithmic
derivative: this preserves differential transcendence degree and makes the entire mixed family
additive. The complete proof is recorded in
`split-source-boundary-universality`.

### `[CONJECTURE]` Character-radical decomposition

For a commutative algebraic differential group `G`, let

```text
X_D(G) = differential characters G -> G_a,
R_D(G) = intersection_(chi in X_D(G)) ker(chi).           (13)
```

There is a functorial two-layer source boundary:

```text
linear character corona  +  character-invisible radical core R_D(G).  (14)
```

Every differential-algebraic dependence of a `G`-valued source primitive is detected either by a
simultaneous coboundary in the character corona or by a relation entirely inside an
`R_D(G)`-torsor. In particular, failure of character-boundary completeness is not unstructured;
it is concentrated in one canonical radical.

For split vector groups and tori the radical should be trivial, recovering the exact linear
boundary theorem. For abelian and semiabelian differential groups the radical is expected to be
the Manin-kernel-type core.

**Falsifier:** a source dependence whose image under every differential character is trivial but
which does not factor through the differential-character radical.

### `[CONJECTURE]` Hypertranscendence is non-torsion capacity

For every finite-source observable admitting a faithful commutative differential-group
presentation, hypertranscendence is equivalent to non-torsion of its source class under the full
differential-character ring, after quotienting by the radical core. Joint differential
transcendence degree is the localized character-boundary rank plus the differential dimension of
the radical component.

Thus “infinite algebraic clock capacity” is not infinite elapsed time and not merely infinitely
many jets. It is the impossibility of killing the observable's source class by any finite
differential character. The thin boundary is the finite-character test surface on which that
infinite non-torsion capacity is visible.

**Falsifier:** a faithful finite-source observable which is hypertranscendental although its
character class is torsion and its radical component is differentially algebraic, or the converse.

### `[CONJECTURE]` Five obstruction faces are two-layer realizations

Continuation failure and monodromy are realizations of the linear character corona. Spectral
incompleteness, positivity failure, and noncanonical self-adjoint boundary conditions are
realizations of the character-invisible radical core after it is equipped with a self-dual
polarization. The five failures are not equal predicates, but their obstruction classes are images
of one two-layer boundary object under different realization functors.

**Falsifier:** two rigorously source-matched realization chains whose obstruction classes cannot be
joined by any common character/radical boundary object even up to derived equivalence.

### `[CONJECTURE]` Representation-independence of the obstruction spectrum

Different valid source presentations of the same hypertranscendental observable produce derived
Morita-equivalent two-layer boundary objects. Their character ranks, radical differential
dimensions, and polarized degeneracy spectra are invariant. This is the proposed topology: not a
topology on finite jets, but a descent theory on presentations of the source boundary object.

**Falsifier:** two faithful source presentations of one observable with inequivalent localized
character ranks or inequivalent radical degeneracy spectra.

### `[CONJECTURE]` Zeta as a self-dual boundary determinant

The Riemann zeta function is not conjectured to be the clock or even the underlying boundary
object. Its completed form is conjectured to be a determinant section of a polarized realization
of the radical source boundary attached to the prime/archimedean constraint system. A critical
zero is a parameter where the comparison from the source boundary to its dual loses rank. The
functional equation supplies the duality involution; the critical line is its fixed locus.

The critical zeros are therefore conjectured to be the common self-dual degeneracy spectrum seen
through spectral incompleteness, continuation, positivity, monodromy, and boundary-condition
realizations. RH would assert that the entire degeneracy spectrum of this determinant section lies
on the fixed locus. This is a research hypothesis, not an RH result.

**Falsifier:** a construction of the proposed common boundary determinant whose off-fixed-locus
degeneracies are compatible with every required source, duality, and positivity structure, or a
proof that no common determinant realization can simultaneously recover two of the five chains.

## RH pressure test `[CONJECTURE]`

If continuation, monodromy, spectral, positivity, and boundary-condition RH chains can each be
factored through one source-logarithmic boundary class, then their apparently different failures
would be different realizations of torsion or degeneracy in one boundary module. The critical
zeros would then be a common self-dual degeneracy spectrum of an additional polarized realization
of that module. No such factorization or polarization is proved here.
