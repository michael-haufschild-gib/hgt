## 1. The object one level above an HT constraint

Let `R` be a commutative structural difference-differential operator algebra, `M` an ambient
meromorphic `R`-module, and `D subset M` the submodule of admissible DA gauges.  For a
source-defined self-dual observable `q` in `M/D`, put

```text
J_q=Ann_R(q),
Cyc(q)=R q subset M/D.                                   (1)
```

The first isomorphism theorem always gives

```text
Cyc(q) ~= R/J_q.                                         (2)
```

Equation `(2)` alone is `[BRIDGE]` and contains no geometry.  The new datum is an independently
given finite source law `f in R`, obtained from the source recurrence before `q` or its HT behavior
is inspected.  Define its intrinsic critical ideal

```text

```

The annihilator `J_q` is **conormally integrable** when

```text

```

for such a source law, modulo unit/contact equivalence of source presentations.  Only `(4)`, not
the tautology `(2)`, identifies the analytic obstruction module with cotangent singular geometry:

```text

```

Thus the proposed higher object is not a clock and not an arbitrary topology on HT functions.  It

For `N>=1`, positive rationally independent periods, and



