## Citation-context findings

All statements directly carrying citations were checked against cited
abstracts or publisher descriptions. After corrections listed above, no
cited sentence attributes result absent from its cited source. Every one of
45 references retained in manuscript are cited in body; no uncited
bibliography padding remains.

The new recurrence-desert comparison uses only a claim stated explicitly in
Kotowski--Oszmaniec v3: tight recurrence bounds for physical dynamics induced
by local noncommuting Hamiltonians remain an open direction.  Manuscript
limits its contribution to deterministic existence/consecutive-gap lower-bound aspect and does
not claim universal optimal first-return bounds.

New physical local-path first-return comparison was checked against primary
texts.  Campos Venuti studies average crossing spacing, Gupta--Short prove
deterministic Dirichlet upper bounds, and Kotowski--Oszmaniec prove packing
upper/random-diagonal lower results while explicitly leaving local
noncommuting dynamics open.  Manuscript claims exact almost-everywhere
physical fidelity exponent only for full finite nearest-neighbour path
coefficient family and asymptotic small deficit.  One common conull set works
for every normalized state, with exact exponent `max(s-2,0)/2` determined by
nonzero spectral-support size.  It does not claim every local Hamiltonian or
experimentally uniform finite-resolution bounds across states or coefficients.

Vanishing-tail comparison was checked against Kotowski--Oszmaniec v3's
effective-support construction and all-state deterministic bounds.  That
source proves uniform closeness after deleting a spectral tail and uses
effective support for upper estimates; manuscript does not attribute exact
exponent instability to it.  Searches also separated monitored
first-detection discontinuities, which insert repeated measurements, from
present unmeasured survival-fidelity law.  Novelty wording is restricted to
conjunction of uniform all-time `4η` invisibility, exact coarse cutoff,
maximal exact-resolution exponent activation and static nearest-neighbour
local-family realization.

New physical first-return horizon-instability comparison was checked against
fixed-Hamiltonian recurrence bounds, truncated-Diophantine torus filling and
monitored first-detection recurrence.  None of these primary sources states
same-state non-identifiability under uniform operator-norm control of full
propagator on every bounded interval.  Manuscript claims novelty only for
that conjunction; rational-spectrum density, metric extremality and Duhamel
continuity are explicitly treated as established ingredients.

Same-field thermodynamic continuous-circuit occupation theorem introduces no
new citation.  Its comparison is limited to already verified
Oszmaniec--Kotowski--Horodecki--Hunter-Jones random-circuit/stochastic-
Hamiltonian result, Kotowski--Oszmaniec finite-dimensional/random-Hamiltonian
frontier, and Cui--Schuster--Mao--Huang--Brandao static-local non-Haar
obstruction.  Manuscript does not attribute deterministic static same-field
theorem to these sources and does not infer full-unitary Haar randomization.
Earlier finite-alphabet Choi occupation statement remains in paper as separate
resolved-target clause.
