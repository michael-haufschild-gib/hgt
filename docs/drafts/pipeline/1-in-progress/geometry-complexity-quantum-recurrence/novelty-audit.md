## Capstone novelty boundary

Capstone is now the universal stationary survival-recurrence law for arbitrary
probability spectral measures. Its single physical input is the spectral
measure of a normalized state. It proves: centered-time survival-fidelity laws
converge weakly; complete limiting measure equals normalized pure-point law
scaled by squared total pure-point mass; support is one interval ending at
that square; and total pure-point mass is recovered from observable upper
edge.

Classical boundary is explicit. Wiener--Eberlein theory gives first stationary
moment as sum of squared atomic masses, and Eberlein decomposition identifies
the strongly almost-periodic component with the pure-point part of the
underlying measure. Pure-point selection and its polynomial-statistic
consequences are therefore not claimed new. Jessen--Wintner gives
support-as-range-closure ancestry for uniformly almost-periodic pure-point
motion. Candidate contribution is narrowed to the joined no-gap interval
support and exact inverse-edge law for arbitrary mixed spectral type,
including absolutely continuous and singular continuous parts. Exact-object
searches found no source stating that result. This is absence evidence, not
proof of priority; manuscript makes no absolute-first claim.

The finite-spectrum identity `alpha + rho = D - 1` is demoted to a geometric
corollary. It is local Haar dimension plus rank--nullity, not headline physical
law. Gao, Barreira--Saussol and Saussol are cited as direct
recurrence--dimension antecedents. Half-integer physical first-return
exponents later require explicit quadratic target geometry and stated metric
Diophantine genericity.

   quantum systems. Current paper studies completed orbit geometry and
   completed-time occupation.  It does not infer a first hit from time zero,

   inverse-occupation deserts into lower bounds on actual consecutive-return

3. Riddell--Pagliaroli--Alhambra study recurrence and concentration; Riddell--
   Bertini study rationally independent free-fermion spectra and form-factor
   moments. Campos Venuti--Odavic--Hamma derive all-order spectral-form-factor

   Present candidate novelty is discharging maximal projective independence
   generically for interacting local-network families and joining it to exact
   graph, interaction and countable-spectrum laws.
4. Huang proves generic nondegenerate gaps for geometrically local
   Hamiltonians. Current graph theorem removes every nonconstant balanced
   rational spectral relation, simultaneously for all induced subsystems, and

5. Oszmaniec et al. prove complexity saturation and recurrence for random
   circuits and stochastic local Hamiltonian evolution. Kotowski--Oszmaniec--

   Hamiltonians. Brown--Susskind's second law is an argued typical growth

   onsite-plus-edge Hamiltonians after generic spectral discharge and gives

10. Wiener--Eberlein theory and modern treatments of Eberlein decomposition

    The bounded claim left here is connectedness of full survival-law support,

## Candidate novelty residues

### New core. No-gap stationary recurrence and bound-state-mass inversion

Exact residue: for every probability spectral measure, including arbitrary
mixtures of pure-point, absolutely continuous and singular-continuous parts,
stationary survival-fidelity support is exactly one closed interval
`[rho,p^2]`; `p` is total pure-point mass.  Hence full stationary law recovers
`p` as square root of support maximum.  Lower edge is scaled least fidelity
on exact compact relation hull.  No simplicity, finite support, rational
independence, decay or genericity enters.

Preliminary verdict: useful mixed-spectrum extension, no longer novelty
anchor.  Classical Wiener--Eberlein mean uses `sum w_i^2`; theorem extracts
`(sum w_i)^2` from full-law support.  Jessen--Wintner already supplies
support-equals-range-closure mechanism behind pure-point no-gap topology.
Candidate novelty is exact mixed-spectrum reduction, interval endpoints and
inverse formula.  Priority remains provisional pending independent specialist
search.

### Core. Universal observer-action information hierarchy

Exact residue: finite or countable phase-law convergence is equivalent to
convergence of every finite-support character action coefficient; zero-action
characters are exact constraints and split projective capacity with temporal
dimension; scalar survival laws have exact maximal system quotient given by
spectral difference measure and exact maximal clock quotient given by
reflection symmetrization; complete resolved replica detectors recover full
projective relation lattice. One explicit uniform nine-level pair proves
scalar quotient is strict under every probability clock.

Preliminary verdict: strongest paper spine. Each harmonic-analysis or
homometry ingredient has literature ancestry. Candidate novelty is exact
quantum closure of all five clauses, especially two maximal quotient
characterizations, resolved relation-lattice completeness and their strict
finite witness, joined to carrier, locality and finite-record laws. Search
found no matching theorem package; this is provisional absence evidence, not
priority proof.

### O. Physical first-return horizon-instability law

**Closed theorem.**  Every bounded-time operator-norm neighborhood of every
finite Hermitian Hamiltonian in dimension at least three contains two
pairwise commuting simple-spectrum Hamiltonians sharing one explicit
orthonormal eigenbasis.  Uniformly over same finite interval, both complete
propagators are arbitrarily close to original.  Nevertheless, for every one
fixed normalized state cyclic in common basis, periodic branch has physical
survival-fidelity first-return exponent zero and non-VWA branch has maximal
exponent `(D-2)/2`.

**Frontier comparison.**  Gupta--Short and Kotowski--Oszmaniec bound recurrence
for fixed Hamiltonian.  Campos Venuti studies fidelity-level crossing
spacing, not actual first hit.  Dumas--Fischler prove optimal filling-time
bounds from truncated Diophantine data.  Gr\"unbaum--Vel\'azquez--Werner--Werner
study monitored discrete-time recurrence, where projective measurement is
inserted after every step.  None states local instability of unmeasured
physical first-return exponent under uniform full-propagator control, same
eigenbasis and same cyclic state.

**Priority verdict.**  Strong, likely novel identifiability theorem.  Exact
conjunction was not located as of 28 August 2026.  Priority remains absence
evidence, not bibliographic proof.  Established ingredients explicitly not
claimed new: density of commensurate spectra, nullity/extremality of VWA
packets, finite-dimensional spectral theorem and Duhamel continuity.

**Experimental boundary.**  Finite process tomography can test finite-window
closeness and observe subsequent divergence at accessible thresholds; it
cannot certify non-VWA arithmetic or asymptotic exponent.  This is theorem's
physical message rather than a defect in protocol.
