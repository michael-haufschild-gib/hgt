## Objections and dispositions

### “Wiener already determines atomic content from average recurrence”

Only weighted collision sum $\sum_jw_j^2$ is determined by first stationary
moment.  It does not determine total atomic mass $\sum_jw_j$.  New theorem
uses topology of full stationary distribution: support is one interval and
its maximum is $(\sum_jw_j)^2$.  Jessen--Wintner already gives
support-equals-range-closure mechanism for uniformly almost-periodic
pure-point orbit.  Novelty claim is only exact arbitrary mixed-spectrum
scaling, endpoints and inverse edge law, not pure-point no-gap topology or
Wiener averaging.

### “The path theorem still proves only a winding surrogate”

No.  New capstone first deletes zero-weight levels by an exact
spectral-decomposition identity, then applies two-sided quadratic comparison
between retained positive-weight fidelity deficit and projective phase
displacement.  It proves first time after any positive cutoff at which actual
orbit survival deficit is below $\delta$ has logarithmic exponent
`max(s-2,0)/2` for every state simultaneously.  Winding variable is proof
clock, not theorem endpoint.  Scope remains almost every Hamiltonian in full
finite path coefficient family; state-uniform finite-$\delta$ constants remain
open because arbitrarily small positive weights can delay asymptotic onset.

### “An experiment can prove the support edge and therefore bound-state mass”

No finite record proves exact infinite-time support.  Experimental proposal
compares independently calibrated bound-state population with empirical
upper edge across widening windows and gives one-sided falsifiers.  Exact law
remains mathematical consequence of spectral model; finite convergence
needs platform-specific detuning and leakage bounds.

### “An arbitrarily small tail is physically irrelevant, so exponent jump is an artefact”

At any fixed resolution above `4η`, it is indeed irrelevant: theorem proves
state never leaves corresponding return window for any real time.  At exact
resolution it is not irrelevant because every positive coordinate contributes
a projective phase direction and gives maximal exponent.  This is precise
order-of-limits classification, not a finite-resolution prediction that tiny
tails must be observed.  Experimental protocol tests uniform envelope and
crossover for deliberately injected resolvable tails; it cannot establish
`η=0` from finite data.

### “This is only Kronecker--Weyl repackaging”

Equidistribution and character determination of compact measures are not
claimed new. New candidate is exact quantum closure: arbitrary-regime
finite/countable action criterion, capacity conversion, two maximal scalar
quotients, complete resolved relation tomography and strict separation,
followed by local and inferential realizations.

### “The nine-level example is classical homometry”

Product/reflection homometry is classical and explicitly credited. Example is
not headline novelty. It proves strictness of quantum hierarchy after scalar
system and clock quotients have been characterized exactly. Resolved theorem
identifies precisely what extra recurrence data recovers: full projective
spectral relation lattice.

### “Arbitrarily weak interaction cannot change observable physics”

No fixed-time discontinuity is claimed. For perturbation norm at most
`delta`, every bounded Heisenberg observable differs by at most
`2|t|delta||O||`. Discontinuity concerns completed orbit closure, temporal
rank and infinite-time collision statistics.

### “Genericity is being sold as a concrete physical ensemble”

No. Statement is dense, residual and full Lebesgue measure in finite
onsite-plus-edge coefficient space. It excludes translation-invariant
subfamilies and arbitrary singular coefficient laws unless separately stated.

### “Same-field theorem supplies an explicit experimental Hamiltonian”

No. Coefficient boxes and background are explicit, but simultaneous ambient
selection is existential.  The theorem proves compatibility and existence,
not a numerical coefficient list or hardware prescription.  Experimental
section reserves direct device proposal for explicit finite witnesses.

### “Thermodynamic recurrence is an infinite-volume return”

No. Time limit is taken at each fixed finite volume before size limit.
Exhaustion and same-field theorems supply one ambient coefficient assignment
whose every finite restriction is generic.  Same-field theorem additionally
constructs bounded self-adjoint UHF generator and compact-time convergence of
finite dynamics.  Neither result proves return of infinite-volume orbit.

### “Vanishing return density does not imply long return-free intervals”

Correct in isolation.  Paper does not make that inference alone.  Every
narrow operator-norm return has fixed positive time thickness inside wider
anchored target, because same-field generator norms are eventually bounded by
one speed scale.  Disjoint-cell packing then turns exact completed wide-target
occupation into arbitrarily late continuous narrow-target deserts.  Explicit
choice `Lambda_n=delta/(2 beta_n)-delta` makes packing contradiction strict
and `Lambda_n→∞`.  Return tube is closed; syndetic recurrence supplies visits
on both sides.  Taking last pre-desert visit and first post-desert visit gives
literal consecutive returns separated by at least `Lambda_n`.

### “Circuit-hard local Hamiltonian means bounded-degree geometry”

No such inference is needed for same-field theorem: its target Hamiltonians
are literally nearest-neighbour on one-dimensional path, while approximating
two-qubit gates may be nonlocal.  Separate bounded-weight Pauli theorem remains
all-to-all in support and is labeled accordingly.

### “Hard epochs imply Haar randomization or a monotone ramp”

No. Separate bounded-weight Pauli evolution remains above an exact local
two-query Haar benchmark at all times.  Same-field circuit target is complete
continuous family below stated gate budget, but its occupation is measured on
spectral phase torus, not full unitary Haar space.  No first hard time,
monotone growth, design formation or random-matrix ramp is claimed.
Finite-volume capstone now controls pointwise hardness on selected
exponential-width intervals, but not every late time, maximal saturation or
monotonicity. Same-field continuous-circuit theorem controls centered completed-time
occupation and its thermodynamic envelope.  Finite-aperture companion now
controls occupation on every translated finite window for arbitrary finite
spectrum. Exact retained resonances contribute persistent census term; only
nonzero sheets receive `1/(T Δ_N^nz)` correction. This is not a mixing theorem:
large resonance census may make bound weak, and result still gives neither
pointwise hardness at every late time nor rate uniform in volume.

### “Linear upper and lower bounds prove a literal second law of complexity”

False.  Envelopes compare complexity with linear functions; they do not order
every pair of times.  Explicit nested path now proves decisive counterexample
inside same window: at $s=2\pi-10^{-6}$ exact complexity is at least `1514`,
while at later $t=2\pi$ it is at most `1513`.  Lower bound permits arbitrary
nonlocal and non-diagonal two-qubit gates.  Upper bound is explicit and uses
free scalar/one-qubit centering.  Paper states coarse scale-separated order,
nearby backtracking and exact return reflection as three distinct facts.

### “Graph reconstruction derives physical space from time”

No. Tensor-factor decomposition and subsystem restrictions are supplied.
Curvature reconstructs interaction adjacency relative to that input.

### “Finite records determine long-time behavior with enough precision”

No uniform inference follows at a strict earlier horizon: one exact spectrum,
one fixed protocol, common finite moments and uniformly close earlier records
remain compatible with every terminal probability. Compact complete moments
cross a different determinacy boundary.

### “Infinite-volume dynamics means infinite-volume recurrence”

No. Explicit l-bit field defines bounded self-adjoint multiplication
Hamiltonian and strongly continuous unitary group on every selected
$L^2(\Omega,\mu)$.  Same-field Pauli construction separately defines standard
qubit UHF $C^*$ dynamics and proves compact-time finite-prefix convergence.
No global infinite-volume return, infinite-system circuit complexity or
microscopic MBL realization is inferred.
