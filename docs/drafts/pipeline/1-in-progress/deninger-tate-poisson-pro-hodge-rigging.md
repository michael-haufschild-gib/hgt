## Prime-packet theorem

Fix a rational prime \(p\).  Put
\[
 L_p=\log p,\qquad
 \Gamma_p=B_p\times S_p,\qquad
 S_p=\mathbb R/L_p\mathbb Z.
\]
Use normalized Haar measure.  On the circle-oscillatory packet space
\[

 =
 L^2(B_p)\widehat\otimes L^2_0(S_p)
\]
let
\[
 A_p=|D_{S_p}|,\qquad
 \widehat A_p=L_pA_p.
\]
The \(k\)-th circle Fourier mode of \(A_p\) has eigenvalue
\(2\pi|k|/L_p\), so
\[
 \widehat A_p\big|_k=2\pi|k|.                              \tag{1}
\]
Tate normalization removes every dependence on \(p\) from the local
spectrum.

For \(r>0\), define
\[
 G_{p,r}(f,g)
 =
 \left\langle
   f,\widehat A_p e^{-2r\widehat A_p}g
 \right\rangle_{L^2(\Gamma_p)}.                            \tag{2}
\]

`[THEOREM] [DAC_TATE_NORMALIZED_PRIME_POISSON_FORM_SUMMABILITY]`

Let
\[

 =
 \left\{
   f=(f_p):
   \sum_p(\log p)^2\|f_p\|_2^2<\infty
 \right\},
\qquad
 \|f\|_\beta^2
 =
 \sum_pL_p^2\|f_p\|_2^2.                                   \tag{3}
\]
Then for every \(r>0\):

1. the series
   \[
    G_r(f,g)=\sum_pG_{p,r}(f_p,g_p)                         \tag{4}
   \]

### Proof

For \(\lambda\ge0\),
\[
 \lambda e^{-2r\lambda}\le\frac1{2er}.                    \tag{5}
\]
Functional calculus and `(1)` give
\[
 \left\|
   \widehat A_pe^{-2r\widehat A_p}
 \right\|
 \le\frac1{2er}                                           \tag{6}
\]
uniformly in \(p\).

Relative to the weighted scalar product `(3)`, the \(p\)-block
representing `(2)` is
\[
 K_{p,r}
 =
 L_p^{-2}\widehat A_pe^{-2r\widehat A_p},
\]
so
\[
 \|K_{p,r}\|
 \le
 \frac1{2er\,L_p^2}
 \longrightarrow0
 \quad(p\longrightarrow\infty).                           \tag{7}
\]
The blocks have mutually orthogonal supports.  Hence for a finite
prime set \(F\),
\[
 \left\|
   \bigoplus_{p\notin F}K_{p,r}
 \right\|
 =
 \sup_{p\notin F}\|K_{p,r}\|
 \longrightarrow0.                                       \tag{8}
\]
Equations `(7)`--`(8)` prove convergence in the continuous-bilinear-map
norm, not merely pointwise scalar convergence.

Every nonzero circle mode has
\[
 (2\pi|k|)e^{-4\pi r|k|}>0.
\]
Thus each local form is strictly positive on
\(E_p^{\mathrm{osc}}\), and a nonzero all-prime vector has a nonzero
coordinate with positive energy.  This proves positivity and exact
kernel detection.
