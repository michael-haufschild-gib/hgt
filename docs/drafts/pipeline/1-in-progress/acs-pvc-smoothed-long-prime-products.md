# The signed conductor prefix of the upper coprime product

**ACS × PVC · In progress.** Put \(R_n=T_n(n\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor,n)\), where \(T_n(U,M)\) is the capped reading of the actual two-dilation spline, with ordinary first prime, coprime cofactor \(v\le M\), and \(pv>U\). The target is the upper bound \(\sum_{n\le N}n^2 R_n\ll_\eta N^{3+\eta}\) at every terminal \(N\), for every \(\eta>0\). The intermediate full-prefix exponent \(11/3\) would give \(\Theta\le5/6\). [Revised programme](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#programme)

**Transfer.** Write \(P_N\) for the square-generator product. The mesh and common-window errors in \(\log q_n-n^2D_n\) cost \(O(n^2)\). For \(U=n\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor\), \(M=n\), the two cutoff costs are each \(O(1+\log(2n))\). The remaining count error costs six logarithms. Summing the absolute transfer errors gives \(|\log P_N-\sum_{n\le N}n^2 R_n|\ll N^3(1+\log(2N))^6\). Lean verification of the complete cutoff and prefix transfer is pending.

**Interior shell.** Set \(V_n=\lfloor n^{7/4}\rfloor\) and \(B_n=T_n(V_n,n)-T_n(2V_n,n)\). For fixed \(q=pv\), sum only over \(N<n\le M\le2N\) satisfying \(v\le n\) and \(V_n<q\le2V_n\). On this range the horizon endpoints \(q\) and \(\lceil q/2\rceil^2\) lie outside the spline support for all sufficiently large \(N\). At unit stencils the horizon weight is periodic, of mean zero, with period a fixed multiple of \(q\). A second bounded periodic primitive of size \(O(q^2)\) and the third mesh difference \(O(n^{-7})\) would charge each pair \(O(q^2/n^5)\). The ordinary-prime reciprocal sum then gives \(O(N^{13/4}\log(2N))\) for every terminal fragment of this shell. The second summation and both endpoints require formal verification. [First primitive](../3-formalized/acs-pvc-clipped-product-horizon-means.md)

**Complete-row recurrence.** Fix a prime \(\ell>4\). At \(n=\ell m\), the full \(v=\ell^2\) fibre has coefficient −1 and the mesh scales by \(1/\ell\). With \(A(N)=\sum_{n\le N} n^2D_n\) and \(H(N)\) the complete-row prefix with that fibre deleted only at multiples of \(\ell\), this gives \(A(N)=H(N)-\ell A(\lfloor N/\ell\rfloor)+O_\ell(N^3(1+\log(2N))^6)\). Iterating twice gives positive feedback with normalized coefficient \(\ell^{-4-2\eta}\). The required upper bound on \(H(N)-\ell H(\lfloor N/\ell\rfloor)\) is open. Clipping the square fibre introduces a separate boundary.
