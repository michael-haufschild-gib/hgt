# The signed conductor prefix of the upper coprime product

**Target.** Set \(R_n=T_n(n\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor,n)\), \(S_N=\sum_{n\le N}n^2 R_n\), \(L_n=1+\log(2n)\). The row retains primes \(p>4\) with weight \(\log p\), cofactors \(1\le v\le n\) coprime to \(p\), and products \(pv>n\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor\). Use the positive sign and every terminal \(N\). The target \(S_N\ll_\eta N^{3+\eta}\) for all \(\eta>0\) gives RH. The intermediate bound \(S_N\ll N^{11/3}\) gives \(\Theta\le5/6\). Both global bounds remain open. [Programme](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#programme)

**Verified transfer.** For the actual square-generator product, \(|\log P_N-S_N|\ll N^3L_N^6\). Both dilations, ordinary-prime weights, coprimality and the two sharp cutoffs are included. [Paper proof and Lean theorem](../3-formalized/acs-pvc-square-generator-to-capped-conductor-prefix.md)

**Interior shell.** For \(V_n=\lfloor n^{7/4}\rfloor\), the signed shell \(V_n<pv\le2V_n\) costs \(O(N^{13/4}L_N)\) on every \((N,M]\subseteq(N,2N]\). Both horizon endpoints lie outside the spline support for large \(n\). A second Abel summation then charges each pair \(O((pv)^2/n^5)\). Lean verification is pending. [Paper proof](../2-fully-proven/acs-pvc-interior-shell-at-seven-fourths.md)

**Square-fibre recurrence.** For a fixed prime \(\ell>4\), let \(A(N)=\sum_{n\le N}n^2D_n\) and \(H(N)\) be the complete-row prefix after deleting \(v=\ell^2\) at multiples of \(\ell\). Two substitutions give positive feedback \(+\ell^2 A(\lfloor N/\ell^2\rfloor)\). The one-sided bound on \(H(N)-\ell H(\lfloor N/\ell\rfloor)\) remains open. In the actual capped row at \(n=\ell m\), the singleton and its proper multiples carry \(-D_m/\ell\) and \(+D_m/\ell\), respectively, up to six logarithms. The cap forces \(p>\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor\), so the exceptional prime \(p=\ell\) drops out. [Paper proof and Lean status](../2-fully-proven/acs-pvc-cancellation-on-square-multiple-cofactors.md)

**Full-range squarefree test.** Seek a uniform bound \(O(L_n^A)\) for the capped contribution of \(r^2\mid v\), for all \(2\le r\le\sqrt n\) coprime to 6. Möbius inversion would discard every cofactor divisible by the square of a prime above 3 at prefix cost \(O(N^{7/2}L_N^A)\), within the \(11/3\) target.

For RH precision, first restore all cofactors in the full signed row at cost \(O(L_n)\). At \(X=\lfloor k/r^2\rfloor\), the divided coprime row and first-prime row carry the same \(\psi(X)-X\). The omitted primes divide \(r\) and have total log weight at most \(\log r\). The proposed \(O(\sqrt{k}\,L_k^A/r)\) cost sums harmonically. The product cutoff on the resulting squarefree mask costs one logarithm. This approach leaves the upper cofactor cap lifted on the selected row.
