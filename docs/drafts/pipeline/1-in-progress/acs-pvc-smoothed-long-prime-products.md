# The signed conductor prefix above the seventeen-ninths cutoff

Put \(U_n=\lfloor n^{17/9}\rfloor\) and \(B(N)=\sum_{1\le n\le N} n^2T_n(U_n,n)\). Keep the ordinary factors \(p>4\) with weight \(\log p\), coprime cofactors \(v\le n\), and the original moving product endpoint. At every terminal, \(|\log P(N)-B(N)|\ll N^3(1+\log(2N))^6\). The open intermediate target \(B(N)\ll N^{11/3}\) gives \(\Theta\le5/6\). The one-sided \(B(N)\ll_\eta N^{3+\eta}\) for every \(\eta>0\) gives RH. **Fixed products (§2.3).** Every admissible first prime of \(q=pv\) has inner weight \(\mathrm{innerJump}(1,q)+\mathbf1_{q\text{ prime}}\). Its total log mass is \(\log q\) less four disjoint omissions. At the last conductor \(b\) there are at most two cofactor entries. Two imply \(q=prt,\ qt\le b^2,\ t<b^{1/9}\). Lean verified both terminal quotient samples and the replacement by \(\Lambda(q)-1\) at cost \(O(M^3(1+\log(2M)))\) on every \(J<n\le M\), uniformly in the moving cutoff. [Divisor inversion and fragment transfer](../3-formalized/acs-pvc-divisor-inversion-at-the-moving-product-boundary.md)

**Returned endpoint (§2.18).** Starting at the first positive quotient returns \(+w_n(q)\) with the same far endpoint. Every fixed subquadratic power cutoff eventually retains the full ordinary-prime price, up to \(O(M^3)\) on every fragment. A superquadratic cutoff eventually removes it. The completed Mangoldt mesh costs \(O((1+\log(2n))/n)\) per conductor. At every quadratic divider \(2\le K\le L\), the two prime prices have opposite terms \(\pm n^3 A(K/n^2)\) with uniform \(O(n^{25/9})\) error, where \(A(v)=\int_0^v\mathrm{dyadicSpline}\). These bounds passed Lean. On paper \(A(v)>0\) exactly on \((e^{-8},4e^8)\). The poles at four cancel at \(K=n^2\), and the returned endpoint at \(17/9\) retains each right-zero residue. [Endpoint bounds](../3-formalized/acs-pvc-ordinary-prime-mass-at-quotient-zero.md) · [Pole proof and audit status](../2-fully-proven/acs-pvc-the-quadratic-product-divider.md)

**Which cofactors remain at \(11/3\)?** Thinning the cap to \(\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor\) costs \(O(N^{7/2}\log(2N))\) on every terminal fragment. The remaining \(pv>n^{17/9}\) force \(p>n^{25/18}\) and have one prime divisor above the cap. The two signed prefixes therefore have equivalent \(N^{11/3}\) upper bounds. Lean audit pending. [Cap loss and unique prime](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheUniqueLargePrimeAboveTheProductCut.lean)

**Removing the moving cofactor cap (§2.18).** Put \(F_n=\max(n,\lfloor L_n/(n+1)\rfloor)\), with \(L_n\) the last spline horizon. The first primes \(p\le F_n\) cost \(O(M^3(1+\log(2M)))\) on every fragment on paper. The two endpoint meshes have bounded harmonic variation against coprime cofactor prefixes, and \(\sum_{p\le F_n}\log p\le7F_n\ll n\). For every remaining prime, \(v\le\lfloor k/p\rfloor\le n<p\). Thus the upper cap and coprimality both disappear, and \(p\) is recoverable from the product. The one-sided upper bound for these primes remains open. On a right-zero probe the four adjacent sharp cofactor caps average to one with error \(O_\rho(K^{-1-\Re\rho})\). [Paper proofs and Lean status](../2-fully-proven/acs-pvc-first-primes-beyond-the-conductor.md)

**Subquadratic bands.** The \(19/10\) shell and the entire intervening band cost \(O(N^3(1+\log(2N)))\) on paper, including partial octaves. Lean verified the rescaled prime intervals. More generally Abel summation gives a proposed cost \(O_{\alpha,\beta}(M^3\log(2M)+M^{2+\alpha\beta})\) up to fixed \(n^\alpha\), \(17/9<\alpha<2\), for \(\beta>\Theta\). Choosing \(\beta<2\Theta/\alpha\) puts this band below the critical exponent for \(w>0\). **Cutting after conductor summation.** At \(K=\lfloor N^{17/9}\rfloor\), the integrated prime parts have leading terms \(\pm cN^{34/9}/2\), with \(c=\mathcal M(\mathrm{squarePrimeWeight})(-1)>0\). Adding \(cK(K+1)/2\) to the high-prime part gives \(H(N)\). On paper its error from \(\log P(N)\) is both \(O(N^{289/81})\) and \(O(N^{3+2\max(0,w-1/40)})\), giving the equivalent open target \(\Re H(N)\ll N^3\). The Mellin and one-sided deductions await kernel audit. [Fixed-cut criterion](../2-fully-proven/acs-pvc-the-quadratic-product-divider.md)

**Quartet and index alternative (§2.3).** For \(\Theta>1/2\), a bounded radial trial forces exactly one conjugate pair on the attained extremal line. The audited theorem allows arbitrary multiplicity and zeros below that line. A universal Gram cap still needs nonnegativity on the common kernel of two fixed functionals. The cofactor cap itself has unbounded rank across products. Choose one prime \(4B<p\le8B\). For odd \(B<t\le2B\), increasing the cap from \(b-1\) to \(b\le2B\) gives the signed coefficient \(-\log p\,\mathbf1_{b=t}\) at \(pt\). This realizes every odd-cofactor trace by linear combination.
