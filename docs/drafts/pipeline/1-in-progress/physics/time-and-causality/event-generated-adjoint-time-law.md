probability until an effect, normalization, exclusivity rule, and compositional Born evaluator
have been derived. Calling every positive scalar “the Born rule” would hide the state/effect

### Modular-time collision

A state-dependent modular flow still assumes a \(C^*\)- or von Neumann algebra and its involution.
The event source must generate the relevant partial adjoint before modular theory applies.

### Weak--Lorentz collapse

causalization must be distinct physical representations. Treating one supplied \(SU(2)\) as both
is a fatal same-carrier identification.

### Static-category collapse

ordinary dagger process theory.

### No dynamics or prediction

Changing inner products does not imply Einstein equations or Standard Model dynamics. A viable
