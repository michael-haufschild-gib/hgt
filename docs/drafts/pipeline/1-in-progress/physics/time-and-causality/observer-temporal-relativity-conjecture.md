> **Time does not have a fixed number of dimensions. An observer's spacetime

## Missing primitive

For every physical process \(P\) and operational observer \(\mathcal O\),
there should exist a canonical **observer-temporal geometry**
\[
  \mathfrak T(P,\mathcal O)
\]
whose points are not instants and whose coordinates are not assumed clock
readings. Its primitive content is the complete pattern of distinctions an
observer can make between compatible histories, indexed by experimental
resolution, observation horizon, probe complexity and intervention.

Clock time is one path or one evaluation character through
\(\mathfrak T(P,\mathcal O)\). It is not the ambient object.

The word *canonical* is essential. A construction manufactured to package
known invariants does not satisfy the conjecture. The geometry must be
uniquely forced, up to operational equivalence, by the complete
distinguishability structure.

## Temporal relativity principle

The frozen conjecture asserts existence and uniqueness of
\(\mathfrak T(P,\mathcal O)\) with the following inseparable structure.

1. **Operational completeness.** Every probability law obtainable by
   \(\mathcal O\) from finite histories factors through a bounded region of
   \(\mathfrak T(P,\mathcal O)\), and the family of such laws separates its
   operational points.
2. **Observer covariance.** Changing observer is a change of temporal
   geometry, not a change of an underlying observer-independent temporal
   dimension. Every admissible observer transformation acts functorially
   and preserves shared operational predictions.
3. **Distance.** The infinitesimal metric of temporal geometry is forced by
   optimal distinguishability of nearby histories. Familiar quantum
   information metrics are coordinate shadows of this metric.
4. **Dimension.** At each resolution, horizon and probe complexity, temporal
   dimension is the independent rank of distinctions that can be resolved.
   It is allowed to change with observer and scale.
5. **Interaction-curvature equivalence.** Temporal geometry is flat across
   an operational decomposition exactly when the complete process
   factorizes across that decomposition. Its mixed curvature is the
   invariant obstruction to factorization, not merely a diagnostic
   correlated with interaction.
6. **Geometry reconstruction.** The support and propagation of temporal
   curvature determine the observer's minimal causal/spatial incidence
   geometry. Spatial adjacency is therefore derived from which temporal
   distinctions can be created jointly, not supplied as a background graph.
7. **Horizon-persistence equivalence.** A horizon for \(\mathcal O\) is a
   boundary beyond which a family of temporal distinctions ceases to be
   operationally recoverable. Persistent, metastable and terminated modes
   belong to one intrinsic persistence structure.
8. **Arithmetic boundary.** Exact infinite-time recurrence is the
   arithmetic completion of temporal geometry. It can be discontinuous even
   when every bounded operational region varies continuously.
9. **Open/closed unity.** Closed recurrence, dissipative decay, monitored
   return and subsystem leakage are not separate theories. They are
   different persistence strata of the same observer-temporal geometry.
10. **Finite/infinite compatibility.** Thermodynamic and continuous-spectrum
    limits arise from completion of compatible finite-resolution temporal
    geometries; no finite terminal rank is imposed.

These are not ten loosely conjoined laws. The conjecture is that one
canonical representation theorem makes every clause a forced aspect of one
object. If independent choices are required for the clauses, the conjecture
fails.

## Einstein-distance content

The reversal is not “time measurements depend on observers.” That is old.
It is not “information geometry can be placed on quantum states.” That is
old. It is not “spacetime may emerge from entanglement.” That is an existing
research programme.

The hunch is stronger:

- dimensionality itself is observer- and resolution-relative;
- interaction is literally the curvature obstruction of temporal
  factorization;
- spatial/causal geometry is reconstructible from that curvature without a
  presupposed spatial metric;
- horizons are death loci in the same temporal object;
- exact eternity is a different, arithmetic boundary of that object; and
- these identifications are forced by a uniqueness theorem rather than
  proposed analogies.

General relativity replaced force in a fixed arena by geometry. Temporal
relativity would replace evolution in a fixed spacetime by the geometry of
distinguishable change.
