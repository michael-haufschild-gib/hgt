## The mathematical conjecture `[CONJECTURE]`

### 1. Constraint data before derivation

A **pretemporal constraint datum** is

```text
U=(G,F,K,a,X),
```

where:

1. `G` acts on a field `F` by automorphisms;
2. `K subset F` is a `G`-stable field of local constraint coefficients;
3. `a:G -> K^*` is a multiplicative cocycle;
4. `X in F^*` is a global section satisfying `g(X)=a_g X`;
5. the simultaneous fixed field `F^G` is the constant field.

No derivation, time coordinate, Hamiltonian, or flow occurs in this definition.  The global
constraint system determines `X` up to multiplication by a constant: the ratio of two solutions is
`G`-fixed.

The ambient analytic, local, or operator-algebraic category of `F` fixes a regularity class
`RegDer_(G,K)(F)` independently of `X`.  An **admissible temporalization** is chosen only afterwards:
it is a nonzero member `delta` of this class, hence a regular derivation which commutes with `G` and
preserves `K`.  The admissible class must contain a nonzero derivation and must be classified from
the ambient category; it cannot be selected after seeing `X`.  Let `B_delta` be the subfield of
elements differentially algebraic over `K` for that derivation.

The datum is **pretemporally hypertranscendental** when

```text
X notin B_delta
```

for every admissible `delta`.  Equivalently, for every such derivation, the same pre-existing
cocycle has

```text
[a] != 0 in H^1(G,B_delta^*),
[a]  = 0 in H^1(G,F^*).
```

The first equivalence is the pointwise application of the base-trivialization theorem already
formalized without any differential assumptions in `MultiplicativeConstraintTrivialization`.
Hypertranscendence is therefore not “complicated time evolution.”  It is the obstruction to every
finite differential evolution law after the global state has already been selected.  Quantifying
over arbitrary field derivations would be neither intrinsic nor physically honest: wild derivations
can be manufactured from transcendence bases, while an empty admissible class makes the definition
vacuous.  The classification and non-vacuity of `RegDer_(G,K)(F)` are part of the theorem.

The right side is the **constraint-superverse**: every character gives a globally consistent


## Whole-universe conjecture `[CONJECTURE: PHYSICS, NO EVIDENCE CLAIMED]`

The **Pretemporal Universe Conjecture** is that the exact wavefunctional of the universe is a
pretemporally hypertranscendental global section of a constraint datum.  What physics calls time is
then a derived presentation: a compatible nonzero derivation used to organize observations of a
state that was not produced by that derivation.

This differs from the Wheeler--DeWitt proposal in a sharp mathematical way.  The Wheeler--DeWitt
state is selected by a finite functional differential equation.  A pretemporally
hypertranscendental state satisfies no nonzero algebraic differential equation for any admissible
derivation.  It must be selected by global consistency, boundary, cocycle, or gerbe data which is
not compressible into a finite local differential constraint.

The **Everett--Galois Conjecture** is that, when the response-field automorphism group acts
faithfully on the physical observable algebra, its characters label genuine decoherent sectors.
This is not established by the mathematics above.  A physical many-worlds claim additionally
requires:

1. a Hilbert-space or operator-algebra representation of the Galois action;
2. a decoherence functional separating character sectors;
3. a positive state or measure producing branch weights;
4. recovery of the Born rule or a different falsifiable probability law.

Until these are constructed, **superverse** means the moduli of algebraically consistent
completions, not parallel physical universes.
