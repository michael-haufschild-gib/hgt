## Falsity audit

The conjecture fails at its claimed generality.

1. **Accidental return is not compact recurrence.** Infinite-dimensional unitary systems can have
   singular-continuous rigid spectra. Their return amplitude can approach its initial value along a
   sparse sequence although the observed orbit is not almost periodic and has no compact recurrent
   phase packet. Thus one subsequential near-return does not identify a compact recurrent orbit.
2. **Covering complexity is not a fixed-world clock.** Two dense translations of the same compact
   torus have identical covering numbers but radically different finite-time return behaviour.
   Diophantine arithmetic controls the waiting time of a fixed spectrum. Covering geometry controls
   target complexity and spectrum-uniform minimax exponents; it cannot give a two-sided universal
   fixed-system deadline.
3. **No universal canonical split exists at the stated scope.** Arbitrary open evolutions on
   arbitrary infinite state spaces need not carry the Hilbert/reflexive, normality, compactness, or
   weak-almost-periodicity structure required for one canonical peripheral/transient decomposition.

These are kill conditions 1, 3, and 4. The frozen conjecture is retired rather than weakened
silently.
