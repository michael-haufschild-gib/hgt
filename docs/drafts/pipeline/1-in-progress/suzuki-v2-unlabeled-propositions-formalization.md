## Section 8.2 closed: the inverse Neumann Laplacian

Two new Lean modules.

### `GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/TriangularIntervalFubini.lean`

Reusable exchange lemma with no continuity, boundedness or compact-support hypothesis:

`∫_p^q F(x) (∫_x^q G) dx = ∫_p^q (∫_p^t F) G(t) dt`   (`intervalIntegral_triangle_swap`)

Both sides are the integral of `F(x)G(t)` over the triangle `p < x < t ≤ q`; the proof runs through
the corpus's product-majorant Fubini theorem `integral_swap_of_dominated_prod`.

### `GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/SuzukiInverseNeumannLaplacian.lean`

Kernels:

* `suzukiPrefixGramKernel a x y = a - max x y`;
* `suzukiNeumannGreenKernel a x y = (x² + y²)/(4a) - |x - y|/2 + a/6`.

Mechanism.  The two kernels differ by the **separable** expression `φ(x) + φ(y)` with
`φ(t) = t²/(4a) + t/2 - 5a/12` (`suzukiNeumannGreenKernel_sub_prefixGramKernel`, needing no
hypothesis on `a` at all).  A separable perturbation contributes `φ(x)·∫u + (∫φu)·∫ū` to the
quadratic form, so it is invisible on mean-zero data.  On the prefix Gram side, one triangular
exchange gives

`∫_{-a}^a (a - max(x,y)) u(y) dy = ∫_x^a v(t) dt`,  `v(x) = ∫_{-a}^x u`,

and a second exchange turns the resulting form into `∫ |v|²`.

Closed statements:

* `suzuki_equation_802` — `‖v‖²_{L²} = ⟨N u, u⟩` for mean-zero `u`;
* `suzuki_neumannGreen_form_eq_ofReal`, `suzuki_neumannGreen_form_nonneg` — the form is the real
  nonnegative `L²` mass, i.e. `(-Δ_N)^{-1}` is positive;
* `intervalIntegral_suzukiNeumannGreenKernel_right` — `∫_{-a}^a N(x,y) dy = 0`; the constant `a/6`
  is exactly what forces this;
* `intervalIntegral_suzukiNeumannGreenOperator_eq_zero` — the operator maps `L²₀` into `L²₀`,
  proved by two further triangular exchanges instead of a plane Fubini;
* `hasDerivAt_suzukiNeumannGreenOperator` — `(K_a u)'(x) = -v(x)` at interior points;
* `hasDerivAt_deriv_suzukiNeumannGreenOperator` — `(K_a u)'' = -u` wherever `u` is continuous;
* `tendsto_deriv_suzukiNeumannGreenOperator_left`/`_right` — the Neumann boundary condition
  `(K_a u)'(±a) = 0`, obtained from `v(-a) = 0` and `v(a) = ∫u = 0`;
* `suzukiPrefixPrimitive_hasDerivAt_and_vanishes`, `intervalIntegral_eq_zero_of_dirichlet` — the two
  halves of the bijectivity of `D : H_0^1(-a,a) → L²₀(-a,a)`;
* `suzuki_equation_804` — the generalized-eigenvalue Rayleigh quotient.

Hypotheses are only `0 < a`, interval integrability of `u` (implied by `u ∈ L²(-a,a)` on a bounded
interval) and, where the source requires it, `∫_{-a}^a u = 0` (i.e. `u ∈ L²₀`).  No positivity, no
regularity beyond integrability, no Riemann-hypothesis input.

## Section 7 closed: the de Branges deficiency kernel

`GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/SuzukiDeBrangesDeficiencyKernel.lean`.

The de Branges space of Section 7 is generated by `E(z) = ξ(1/2-iz) + ξ'(1/2-iz)`, which in the
corpus's centered coordinate is exactly `xiHedenmalmDeBrangesE = ΞC + i ΞC'`.  Everything Suzuki
computes about its reproducing kernel is unconditional algebra once the four deficiency values are
known:

`E(-i) = ξ(3/2) - ξ'(3/2)`, `E(i) = ξ(3/2) + ξ'(3/2)`,
`E♯(-i) = ξ(3/2) + ξ'(3/2)`, `E♯(i) = ξ(3/2) - ξ'(3/2)`,

which follow from `ΞC(-i) = ξ(3/2)`, the chain rule `ΞC' = i ξ'(1/2 + i·)`, evenness of `ΞC`, and
Schwarz reality (`suzukiXiThreeHalves_conj`, `suzukiXiDerivThreeHalves_conj`).

Closed statements:

* `suzukiDeBrangesKernel` — the kernel `(E(z)conj E(w) - E♯(z)conj E♯(w))/(2πi(w̄ - z))`;
* `suzukiDeBrangesKernel_conj_left` and `sub_mul_suzukiDeBrangesKernel_conj_left` — the spectral
  vector `K(z̄,·)` in closed form and the cancellation of its boundary factor, valid at the
  singular point because the numerator vanishes there;
* `suzukiDeBrangesKernel_neg_I_self`, `suzukiDeBrangesKernel_I_self` —
  `‖K(∓i,·)‖² = K(∓i,∓i) = ξ(3/2)ξ'(3/2)/π`;
* `suzukiDeBrangesBoundaryForm` — for every real `θ` and **every** `z ∈ ℂ`,

  `(z+i)K(z̄,-i) + e^{-iθ}(z-i)K(z̄,i) = -(e^{-iθ/2}/(πi))(C_θ E(z) - conj(C_θ) E♯(z))`,
  `C_θ = ξ(3/2)cos(θ/2) + i ξ'(3/2) sin(θ/2)`;

* `suzukiDeBrangesBoundaryForm_pi` — at `θ = π` the right-hand side is `(2i/π)ξ'(3/2)ΞC(z)`;
* `suzukiDeBrangesSpectralRelation` and `suzukiDeBrangesSpectralRelation_pi` — the companion
  identity for `f̂_w = K(w̄,·)/E`,

  `(z-i)f̂_{+i}(z) + e^{iθ}(z+i)f̂_{-i}(z) = (i e^{iθ/2}/π)(C_θ - conj(C_θ) E♯(z)/E(z))`,

  and its `θ = π` form `= -(2i ξ'(3/2)/π)·ΞC(z)/E(z)`, on `z ∉ {i,-i}` with `E(z) ≠ 0`.

The half-angle bookkeeping is isolated in `suzukiHalfPhase`/`suzukiHalfPhasePos` together with the
two exponential identities `w(2cos(θ/2)) = 1 + w²` and `w(∓2i sin(θ/2)) = w² - 1`, from which the
two combination lemmas `(p∓q) + w²(p±q) = 2w·C_θ^{(conj)}` follow by `linear_combination`.

Note on normalization: the source displays the last identity with `π²` in place of `π`.  The extra
factor there is carried in from the `⟨·,·⟩_{A_∞} = 2⟨·,·⟩_{L²}` normalization used earlier in
Section 7; computed directly from the definitions of `K` and `f̂`, the constant is `π`.  Both the
structure and the `θ = π` collapse agree with the source.

## The digamma integral representation behind (2.7)–(2.8)

Two modules.

`GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/EulerMascheroniGaussKernel.lean`:

* `integral_geometricGaussKernel_Ioi` — `∫_ε^∞ e^{-t}/(1-e^{-t}) dt = -log(1-e^{-ε})`;
* `integral_expNegDiv_Ioi_small` — `∫_ε^∞ e^{-t}/t dt = -log ε + Ein(ε) - γ` for `0 < ε ≤ 1`;
* `abs_ein_le`, `tendsto_ein_nhdsWithin_zero` — `|Ein(ε)| ≤ ε`;
* `integral_gaussKernel` — **`∫_0^∞ (e^{-t}/t - e^{-t}/(1-e^{-t})) dt = -γ`**, the two logarithmic
  divergences cancelling because `(1-e^{-ε})/ε → 1`.

`GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DigammaIntegralRepresentation.lean`:

* `sum_gaussDigammaLaplaceTerm` — the geometric partial sum
  `∑_{n<N}(e^{-(n+1)u} - e^{-(s+n)u}) = (1 - e^{-Nu})·(e^{-u}-e^{-su})/(1-e^{-u})`;
* `norm_exp_neg_sub_exp_neg_mul_le` — `‖e^{-u} - e^{-su}‖ ≤ (1+‖s‖)u`, by the fundamental theorem
  of calculus; together with `1 - e^{-u} ≥ u e^{-u}` this bounds the dominating profile by
  `e(1+‖s‖)` on `(0,1]`;
* `integral_gaussLaplaceKernel_eq_tsum` — dominated convergence turns the corpus's Gauss *series*
  into `∑_n (1/(n+1) - 1/(s+n)) = ∫_0^∞ (e^{-u}-e^{-su})/(1-e^{-u}) du`;
* `digamma_eq_integral_gauss` — **`ψ(s) = ∫_0^∞ (e^{-u}/u - e^{-su}/(1-e^{-u})) du`** for
  `Re s > 0`;
* `integral_archimedean_gaussKernel` — the source's archimedean specialization
  `∫_0^∞ (e^{-u/4}cos(zu/2)/(1-e^{-u}) - e^{-u}/u) du = -Re ψ(1/4 + iz/2)`.

What remains of (2.7)–(2.8) is the *other* half of the source's split,
`∫_ε^R (e^{-u} - cos(zu/2))/u du → log|z| - log 2`.  That integrand is only conditionally
convergent at infinity, so it is not a Bochner integral on `(0,∞)`; the honest statement is a
truncated limit built on the cosine integral `Ci`.  Note the source's `r_1''` is likewise not `L¹`,
which is why the corpus's own route to (2.6) goes through Abel limits rather than a literal Fourier
transform.

## The cut-off logarithmic window (the `I_ε → 0` step of Theorem 1.1)

`GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/SuzukiCutoffLogWindow.lean`.

* `log_add_three_le` — `log(3+z) ≤ log(3+R) + log z - log R` for `z ≥ R > 0`, because
  `3 + z ≤ (3+R)z/R`;
* `integral_logMajorant_Ioi` — the closed evaluation
  `∫_R^∞ (log(3+R) + log z - log R)/z² dz = (log(3+R)+1)/R`, by the primitive
  `-((log(3+R) - log R + 1 + log v)/v)`;
* `integral_logWindow_tail_le` — hence `∫_R^∞ log(3+z)/z² dz ≤ (log(3+R)+1)/R`;
* `integral_logWindow_head_le` — `∫_{-R}^R log(3+|z|) dz ≤ 2R log(3+R)`;
* `setIntegral_Iic_neg_eq_Ioi_of_even` — reflection invariance of an even majorant;
* `integral_logWindow_le` — **the assembled estimate**: a frequency profile with
  `|ŵ(z)| ≤ 2ε` and `|ŵ(z)| ≤ M/|z|` satisfies
  `∫_ℝ log(3+|z|)|ŵ(z)|² dz ≤ 8ε log(3+1/ε) + 2M²(ε log(3+1/ε) + ε)`;
* `tendsto_cutoffLogWindowBound` — that bound tends to `0` as `ε → 0+`.

Together with (3.3)–(3.4) from `SuzukiApertureFourierDecay` this is exactly the source's
`I_ε ≪ ε log(1/ε) → 0`.

## The cosine-integral half of (2.7)–(2.8)

`GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CosineIntegralAsymptotic.lean`.

The source's step `E₁(ε) + Ci(|z/2|ε) = log|z| - log 2 + O(ε)` is about an integral that is only
*conditionally* convergent at infinity, so it is not a Bochner integral on `(0,∞)`.  The module
builds it as a genuine improper limit.

* `intervalIntegral_cos_div` — integration by parts against `t⁻¹`:
  `∫_1^y cos t/t dt = sin y/y - sin 1 + ∫_1^y sin t/t² dt`, with `sin t/t²` absolutely integrable;
* `cosTailValue`, `tendsto_intervalIntegral_cos_div` — the truncated integrals converge to
  `-sin 1 + ∫_1^∞ sin t/t² dt`;
* `intervalIntegral_damped_cos_div`, `integral_damped_cos_div_Ioi` — the same parts formula for the
  damped kernel `e^{-εt}cos t/t`, which *is* Lebesgue integrable;
* `integral_exp_neg_mul_Ioi`, `integral_exp_neg_mul_div_Ioi_le` — `∫_1^∞ e^{-εt}/t dt ≤ log(1/ε)+1`,
  by splitting at `1/ε`;
* `tendsto_dampedCosTail` — **the Abel limit** `∫_1^∞ e^{-εt}cos t/t dt → cosTailValue`: the extra
  parts term carries the factor `ε·∫_1^∞ e^{-εt}/t dt ≤ ε(log(1/ε)+1) → 0`, and the remaining
  `sin t e^{-εt}/t²` converges by dominated convergence against `1/t²`;
* `integral_dampedFrullani` — the real part of the corpus's complex Frullani integral at rates `1`
  and `ε - i`:  `∫_0^∞ (e^{-t} - e^{-εt}cos t)/t dt = log √(ε²+1)`;
* `gaussCosineIdentity` — **letting `ε → 0` gives `∫_0^1 (e^{-t} - cos t)/t dt + ∫_1^∞ e^{-t}/t dt
  = cosTailValue`**, i.e. the improper integral `∫_0^∞ (e^{-t} - cos t)/t dt` vanishes;
* `cinNeg`, `abs_cinNeg_le`, `intervalIntegral_cos_div_head`, `neg_cinNeg_one`,
  `cosIntegralTail`, `tendsto_cosIntegralTail` — the regularized head `∫_0^x (cos t - 1)/t dt` and
  the closed form of the improper tail;
* `tendsto_expIntegralTail_sub_cosIntegralTail` — **the source's identity**: for `a > 0`,

  `∫_ε^∞ e^{-u}/u du - ∫_{aε}^∞ cos u/u du → log a`  as `ε → 0+`.

  Both terms diverge like `-log ε`; the divergences cancel and the constants collapse by
  `-cinNeg 1 = γ + cosTailValue`, which is `gaussCosineIdentity` combined with
  `γ = frullaniHead - frullaniTail`.
