# A finite prime-multiple count at the quarter exponent

For $X>0$ put $T(y)=\sum_{1\le m\le y}(1-m/y)$ and
$$A(X)=\sum_{pm\le X}(\log p)(1-pm/X)-\sum_{n\le X}(\log n)(1-n/X)
       +\sum_{r=2,3}\sum_{k^r m\le X}(1-k^r m/X).$$
Here $p$ is prime and $k,m,n$ are positive integers. The proposed arithmetic criterion is $|A(N)-2A(N/2)|=O_b(N^b)$ at every integer cutoff for every $1/4<b<1/2$. All three terms use finite sums. There is no fitted density.

Let $w(d)=\Lambda(d)\mathbf 1_{d\text{ nonprime}}$, $u(d)=w(d)-\#\{(k,r):r\in\{2,3\},\ k^r=d\}$. Then $A(X)=-\sum_d u(d)T(X/d)$. Write $H(y)=T(y)-2T(y/2)$. On $2j\le y\le2j+1$, $H(y)=j/y$; on $2j+1\le y\le2j+2$, $H(y)=1-(j+1)/y$. In particular $H$ is continuous, zero through one, and $|yH'(y)|\le1$ on every open cell. Under RH the square and cube prime weights have cumulative mass $x^{1/2}+x^{1/3}+O_b(x^b)$, and all higher powers together cost $O(x^{1/4}\log x)$. Thus $\sum_{d\le x}u(d)=O_b(x^b)$. Partial summation against $H(N/d)$ gives the claimed bound since $|H(N/d)-H(N/(d+1))|\le1/d$.

For the converse the Mellin symbol before the triangular mean is
$$-(1-2^{1-s})\zeta(s)\left(\sum_d w(d)d^{-s}-\zeta(2s)-\zeta(3s)\right).$$
If $a(n)$ are its coefficients, then $X[A(X)-2A(X/2)]=\sum_{n\le X}(X-n)a(n)$ is linear on each unit interval. Integer bounds therefore imply the same real bounds. Mellin integration gives a holomorphic continuation of the symbol divided by $s(s+1)$ to $\Re s>1/4$. The explicit lattice terms have only real poles in this region. The dyadic factor has no zeros in $1/4<\Re s<1/2$. At half a right zero the remaining residue is proportional to $\zeta(s)(\operatorname{ord}_{2s}\zeta/2+\operatorname{ord}_{3s}\zeta/3)$. Vanishing of all such residues would force $\zeta(\rho/2)=0$ for every right zero, which the reflection-and-halving theorem excludes. [Prime-power continuation and residues](prime-divisor-logarithmic-moments-at-a-finite-top.md)

A single exponent gives a zero-free region. If the finite count has exponent $1/4<b\le1/3$, any zero with real part $\beta>2b$ must satisfy $2-4b\le\beta\le3b$. Indeed, halving and reflecting exclude $2b<\beta<2-4b$. If $\beta>3b$, vanishing of the residue at $\rho/3$ forces a zero there, and its reflection has real part strictly between $2b$ and $2-4b$. For $b<2/7$ the surviving interval is empty, so $\Theta\le2b$.

Under a single quartet generated by a right zero $\rho$, neither $\rho/2$ nor $3\rho/2$ is a zero. The half-height residue is exactly $\zeta(\rho/2)m(\rho)/2\ne0$. Each exponent strictly above $\Re\rho/2$ bounds the normalized finite mean by the coefficient estimate, and every smaller exponent fails by this pole. An independent estimate below that threshold would exclude the quartet. The cumulative coefficient has a direct inverse. Write $U(x)=\psi(x)-\theta(x)-x^{1/2}-x^{1/3}$ and $E(x)=\psi(x)-x$. The Costa–Pereira inequalities give
$$0\le U(x)-E(x^{1/2})-E(x^{1/3})\le(\log4+4)x^{1/5}.$$
Substituting $x=y^2,y^{4/3},y^{8/9}$ with alternating signs cancels the two intermediate errors. The last term $E(y^{8/27})$ is $O(y^{8/27})$ by Chebyshev. Hence
$$E(y)=U(y^2)-U(y^{4/3})+U(y^{8/9})+O(y^{2/5}).$$
For every $a\ge2/5$, a bound $E(x)=O(x^a)$ is therefore equivalent to $U(x)=O(x^{a/2})$, including the endpoint. The unconditional strip gives $U(x)=O_b(x^b)$ for every $b>20999/48000$. The two lattice counts change its prefix by at most two. [Three-count inversion](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThreeProperPrimePowerCountsRecoverThePrimeError.lean) · [Discrete Abel bound for the finite mean](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/AdjacentDenominatorsInDiscreteQuotientMeans.lean)

Neither sign of $U(x)$ admits a constant-times-quarter-power bound. A one-sided bound would force $\Theta\le1/2$, since $U(y^2)=E(y)+O(y^{5\Theta/6})$ and each sign of $E$ exceeds every exponent below $\Theta$. At $\Theta=1/2$, the tracking error is $O(y^{5/12})$, while Littlewood gives excursions of both signs of size $\sqrt y\log\log\log y$. This obstruction concerns the coefficient prefix; the averaged count has a different endpoint question.
