## Parent construction

Abdesselam--Chandra--Guadagni construct a scale-invariant non-Gaussian generalized stochastic
field on `Q_p^3`, together with a squared composite field, anomalous dimension, space-dependent
renormalization group, and removed-cutoff correlations.

Two different structures in that construction must not be conflated.  First, for the compact
elementary source,

```text
F(z)=S^T(z 1_(Z_p^3),0),
F''''(0)=<phi^4>-3<phi^2>^2 < 0.                                  (1)
```

Thus a connected four-point interaction reporter exists before any HT conjecture is made.  This
strictly negative fourth cumulant proves that the field is non-Gaussian.  It does **not** select the
unstable RG coordinate and does not imply HT of an RG generator.

Second, the one-dimensional unstable direction is the mass, or `phi^2`, direction.  The unstable
manifold is parametrized in the parent construction by the physical mass coefficient `mu`:

```text
v=(delta g_u(mu),mu,R_u(mu)),
e_u=(delta g_u'(mu_*),1,R_u'(mu_*)),
D RG(v_*) e_u=alpha_u e_u,              alpha_u>1.               (2)
```

Their partial analytic linearization at the non-Gaussian fixed point has the form

```text
Psi(v_*,w)=lim_(n->infinity) RG^n(v_*+alpha_u^(-n)w),
RG(Psi(v_*,alpha_u^(-1)w))=Psi(v_*,w),                            (3)
```

with `D_w Psi(v_*,0)=Id`.  Equation `(3)` supplies an exact analytic scaling chart, not a proof
that this chart or the corresponding beta field is DA.

## Parent-derived mass-coordinate theorem

Define the scalar mass component of the nonlinear scaling chart by

```text
m(z)=[Psi(v_*,z e_u)]_mu.                                        (4)
```

Then `m(0)=mu_*` and `m'(0)=1`, because the `mu` component of `e_u` is one.  Hence `m` is locally
invertible without any fourth-cumulant assumption.  Let `f` be the analytic scalar RG map obtained
by restricting `RG` to the unstable curve and reading its `mu` component.  Equation `(3)` gives

```text
f(m(z))=m(alpha_u z).                                             (5)
```

Thus `m` is the normalized Poincare function of the **physical mass RG germ** `f`.  If
`kappa=m^(-1)`, its exact local beta field is

```text
beta_mu(mu)=(log alpha_u) kappa(mu)/kappa'(mu).                    (6)
```

Equivalently, the exact physical mass-to-scale clock is

```text
tau_mu(mu)=log(kappa(mu))/log(alpha_u),
tau_mu(f(mu))=tau_mu(mu)+1,
tau_mu'(mu)=1/beta_mu(mu).                                       (6a)
```

The theorem `exact-rg-clock-hypertranscendence-equivalence.md` proves that `tau_mu` is HT exactly
when `beta_mu` is HT.  This is the operational QFT meaning of the conjecture: no finite ADE
calibrates physical mass to elapsed block scale.

The parent construction also identifies the long-distance `phi^2` source with this direction: its
linear response has a nonzero projection proportional to `e_u`.  This ties the exact scaling
field to the composite mass operator; it still does not decide differential algebraicity.

## Why this is the next QFT target

The diamond-Ising theorem proves the exact mechanism in an interacting hierarchical statistical
field model:

```text
rational discrete RG + repelling fixed point + no rational Ritt cocycle
  => exact beta HT throughout the DA scheme class.                (7)
```

The p-adic parent already supplies what that model lacks: a rigorous non-Gaussian continuum field,
an exact mass scaling limit, and a proved connected four-point interaction witness.  What it does
not supply is the finite rational base used in `(7)`.

This is therefore not another request to prove that a Koenigs coordinate is HT for a rational map;
that problem is source-settled.  The new mathematical core is a differential-Galois obstruction
for a physically normalized analytic RG germ arising from an infinite-dimensional constructive
field theory.

## Exact proof obligations

1. **DA source atlas.** Define the admissible local counterterm and source-coordinate category and
   prove that polynomial local-potential changes act by DA germs.
2. **Global continuation.** Determine the maximal complex analytic continuation of `m`, `kappa`,
   or `f`.  A nonexceptional rational continuation closes the problem by Ritt; a transcendental
   entire continuation closes it by Bergweiler's incoherence theorem.  A boundary of the
   Banach-domain proof is not yet a singularity of the germ.
3. **Analytic obstruction.** Either prove that `f` is algebraic over the physical coupling field
   and extend the Ritt cocycle test, or derive a global singularity/backward-orbit obstruction to
   every equation

   ```text
   (kappa')^r=(mu-mu_*)^j A(kappa)                               (8)
   ```

   with `A` in the admitted DA base.
4. **RG-tower incoherence alternative.** If direct continuation is inaccessible, use
   `rg-tower-differential-incoherence-obstruction.md`: differential algebraicity of `m` forces one
   autonomous ADE shared by every iterate `f^n`.  Contradict that uniform equation from the exact
   all-scale `phi^2` source or shell hierarchy.
5. **Interaction separation.** Keep `(1)` as the independent certificate that the parent is an
   interacting field rather than a Gaussian scaling model.  Do not use it as an RG coordinate.
6. **Scheme theorem.** Prove HT throughout the DA atlas and state explicitly that the analytic
   scaling coordinate itself linearizes the flow by exporting, rather than destroying, the HT.
7. **Archimedean bridge.** Determine which part of the obstruction survives an Archimedean
   hierarchical or local-QFT limit.  Do not call the p-adic field a cosmological theory.

## Current hard obstruction

The available parent theorem gives analyticity of `Psi` and of the extended RG.  Analyticity alone
does not supply a finite algebraic-differential base.  Ritt's rational theorem cannot be applied to
`f` without first proving algebraicity or a new analytic-map replacement.

The parent paper computes finite Taylor information about the scaling chart, including

```text
Psi(v_*,z e_u)=v_*+z e_u+z^2 Theta+O(z^3),
alpha_u^2 Theta=D RG(v_*)[Theta]+(1/2)D^2 RG(v_*)[e_u,e_u].       (9)
```

But `finite-rg-jet-blindness-to-hypertranscendence.md` proves that every finite jet of an analytic
one-coupling RG and its exact beta field has an exact algebraic completion.  Therefore `Theta`, any
finite-loop beta function, and any finite extension of `(9)` are logically blind to HT.  Computing
more coefficients is a ruled-out route.

This no-go is positive residue: the exact diamond-Ising proof identifies the structure to seek
globally--an infinite divisor or critical-preimage tree incompatible with a rational/DA invariant
differential.  The p-adic task is to prove a genuine singularity or all-scale source obstruction
for `m`; the finite radius of a constructive estimate alone is not enough.
