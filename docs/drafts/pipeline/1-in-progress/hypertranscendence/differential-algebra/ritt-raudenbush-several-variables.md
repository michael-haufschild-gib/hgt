# Ritt–Raudenbush in several differential indeterminates

**Status**: in progress.  Opened 2026-08-29.

## Why this card exists

The Bessel card (`2-fully-proven/bessel-order-intensity-hypertranscendence.md`) has exactly one
piece of *general theory* left in its residue: the hypothesis `Algebra.FiniteType C E` carried by

* `HTGeometry/DifferentialGaloisSeparation.eq_zero_of_forall_mem_tensorDifferentialPoints`
* `HTGeometry/DifferentialGaloisSeparation.isomonodromy_of_no_riccati_of_differentiallyClosed`
* `HTGeometry/GaloisMonoidDichotomyEndgame.isomonodromy_or_sl2_galoisMonoid(_detOne)`

and used only to reach `HTGeometry/KolchinNullstellensatz.exists_differentialAlgHom_ne_zero`.

`E` is `comparisonJetAlgebra Z w` — the `C`-algebra generated by **all `∂`-jets** of the entries of
the comparison matrix together with `1/det`.  It is *differentially* finitely generated (five
differential generators) but its algebraic finite type is exactly the statement that the jets
stabilise, i.e. that the parameterized group is finite dimensional.  That is a consequence of the
differential-algebraicity assumption, not an unconditional fact, so the present hypothesis is the
wrong one.

**The correct hypothesis is differential finite generation**, and the theorem that makes it work is
Kolchin's Nullstellensatz in its real form, whose engine is the Ritt–Raudenbush basis theorem in
*several* differential indeterminates.  The corpus owns the theorem in **one** indeterminate over a
**field**:

```
HTGeometry/RittBasisTheorem.radDiffFG_differentialPolynomial
    K a differential field of characteristic zero =>
    every radical differential ideal of K{y} is the radical differential ideal of a finite set
```

## The route

Induction on the number of indeterminates through the *base ring*, never through a ranking on
`Fin k x N`:

```
C{y_1,...,y_k}  ~  ( C{y_1,...,y_{k-1}} ){y_k}
```

so the whole content is one step:

> **(R)** `R` a differential domain of characteristic zero in which every radical differential ideal
> is `IsRadDiffFG`  ==>  the same holds in `R{y}`.

### Steps

1. **Transfer.** `IsRadDiffFG` travels along a differential ring isomorphism.  Immediate from
   `map_mem_radicalDifferentialIdeal` applied both ways.
2. **Re-indexing.** The differential ring isomorphism
   `DifferentialPolynomial (Fin (k+1)) C ~ DifferentialPolynomial Unit (DifferentialPolynomial (Fin k) C)`,
   i.e. `MvPolynomial ((Fin (k+1)) x N) C ~ MvPolynomial (Unit x N) (MvPolynomial (Fin k x N) C)`,
   with the two derivations matching.
3. **Contraction to zero.**  If `M` is a maximal non-`IsRadDiffFG` radical differential ideal of
   `R{y}` and `p = C^{-1}(M)`, then `R/p` is again a differential domain of characteristic zero in
   which every radical differential ideal is `IsRadDiffFG`, and the image of `M` in `(R/p){y}` is
   again a maximal non-`IsRadDiffFG` radical differential ideal, now with zero contraction.  Needs
   `MvPolynomial.quotientEquivQuotientMvPolynomial` promoted to a *differential* isomorphism, and
   the descent "`p` and `M/p` both `IsRadDiffFG` ==> `M` is", which follows because
   `ker <= radicalDifferentialIdeal T0` for a finite `T0` generating `p`.
4. **Minimal rank over a domain.**  Replace `hItop : I /= top` by `forall r /= 0, C r not-in I` in the two rank
   selection lemmas.  With zero contraction that hypothesis is automatic.
5. **Irreducibility of the minimal-rank element over `L = Frac R`.**  A factorisation in `L{y}`
   descends by Gauss to a factor of no larger rank inside `M`.
6. **The oracle.**  `P := S^{-1}M` is a prime differential ideal of `L{y}` containing the differential
   ideal of `A`, and minimality of the rank of `A` gives the divisibility property `hPdvd`, so
   `RittRingGeneralComponent.exists_oracle_initial_separant_powers_mem_diffPolyIterateIdeal`
   applies to every `Q` in `M`.
7. **Finish.**  `h := initial A * separant A` is not in `M` because `M` is prime and both have lower
   rank; `RittBasisFinishingStep.isRadDiffFG_of_saturation` with `N = {A}` closes the contradiction.

### Then

8. **Kolchin's Nullstellensatz, differential finite type.**  Present the algebra as `C{y_1..y_k}/I`
   with `I` radical differential; Ritt–Raudenbush gives `I = {T}` for finite `T`; the Rabinowitsch
   variable turns "`e /= 0`" into one more equation; `IsDifferentiallyClosed.exists_point` produces a
   `C`-solution, and evaluation there is the differential point not killing `e`.
9. **Rewire.**  Replace `[Algebra.FiniteType C E]` by a differential-finite-generation hypothesis in
   `DifferentialGaloisSeparation` and `GaloisMonoidDichotomyEndgame`, and discharge it for
   `comparisonJetAlgebra` by construction.

### Two things came out stronger than planned

* **The contraction descent needs no domain hypothesis on the base.**  `Ideal.Quotient.isDomain`
  only wants the contraction to be prime, which it is because the maximal counterexample is prime.
  So `radDiffFG_differentialPolynomial_of_base` holds for *every* commutative differential
  ℚ-algebra, not only for differential domains.
* **The corpus already owned the hard half.**  `PseudoDivisionCommRing.exists_initial_separant_powers_mem_diffPolyIterateIdeal`
  is stated over `[CommRing K] [IsDomain K] [CharZero K]` and for an arbitrary index type, so the
  Ritt reduction never had to be redone; only the two rank-selection lemmas were field-specific, and
  only because a nonzero constant is a unit in a field.

### The consequence for the Bessel card

`Algebra.FiniteType C E` is no longer needed anywhere on the item-(b) path.  The endgame now runs on

```text
adjoin_comparisonJetGeneratorsTwo :
  Algebra.adjoin C (range fun p : Fin 5 × ℕ => tensorJet p.2 (![Z₀₀, Z₀₁, Z₁₀, Z₁₁, w] p.1))
    = comparisonJetAlgebra Z w
```

which is a *theorem*, proved from `Z.det * w = 1` alone.  The hypothesis that used to encode "the
jets stabilise" — a consequence of differential algebraicity, not an unconditional fact — is gone.
