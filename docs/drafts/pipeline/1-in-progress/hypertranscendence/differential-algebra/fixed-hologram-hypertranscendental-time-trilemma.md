## Exact theorem T1 `[PAPER-PROVED] [LITERATURE/BRIDGE]`

Let `H` be a Hermitian operator on a finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space and let
`rho,A_1,...,A_k` be fixed operators. Any scalar correlation formed from finitely many Heisenberg
operators

```text
C(t)=Tr(rho A_1(t)...A_k(t)),
A_j(t)=exp(i t H) A_j exp(-i t H),
```

is a finite sum

```text
C(t)=sum_(alpha in S) c_alpha exp(i alpha t).
```

Consequently `C` is annihilated by the nonzero constant-coefficient operator

```text
prod_(alpha in S) (d/dt-i alpha)
```

and is differentially algebraic over `C(t)`. Therefore an HT scalar correlation has no exact
realization of this form.

### Proof

Diagonalize `H`. Every matrix coefficient of `A_j(t)` is its fixed coefficient multiplied by
`exp(i(E_p-E_q)t)`. Expanding the finite matrix product and trace produces a finite sum of
exponentials. Applying one factor `(d/dt-i alpha)` for every distinct frequency annihilates the
sum. ∎

The same conclusion holds for any finite algebraic combination or quotient whose denominator is
not identically zero, because differentially algebraic elements form a field.
