## The formal/global distinction

Let a linearized source constraint have analytic symbol

```text
F(lambda)=lambda^r U(lambda),
U(0) != 0.                                                  (1)
```

Its Taylor series `U_hat(D)` is a unit in the formal power-series algebra. Every finite nilpotent
clock truncation therefore treats `U_hat(D)` as invertible and reduces the constraint to the
finite operator `D^r`.

If the source tangent remains free on every finite clock window, the finite-observable conormal
theorem forbids a nonzero conormal carried by `D^r`, and the descent-anomaly theorem forbids any
nonzero finite operator from exactly representing the source constraint. Hence the carrier must
include both the failure of formal representation and the failure of the formal inverse
`U_hat(D)^(-1)` to act in the source test-space category.

This motivates the **source-unit defect**

```text
Def_Phi(U)=formal inverse of U - source-topological inverse of U.  (2)
```

Expression `(2)` denotes a comparison class, not subtraction of two operators in an unspecified
space. Concretely it is carried by the kernel/cokernel, localization boundary, or determinant
divisor of the source realization `U_Phi`.

## Essential correction: formal action is not finite descent

Formal invertibility alone does not explain Gamma finite-jet freedom. If the truncated formal
operator `T_k` were an exact descent of translation through the first `k` jets `q_k`, then

```text
q_k S = T_k q_k,                                           (7)
```

where `S h=h(.+1)-h`. But period-one Fourier packets lie in `ker(S)` and map surjectively under
`q_k`. The finite descent-anomaly range theorem therefore gives

```text
range(T_k q_k-q_k S | ker(S)) = range(T_k).                (8)
```

Hence `(7)` forces `T_k=0`. For every nonzero formal truncation, its entire range is generated by
failure of descent on actual periodic solutions. The infinite Taylor tail is therefore not a
small remainder omitted by truncation; it is the carrier of the source constraint.

This replaces the formal unit alone by the coherent comparison data

```text
alpha_k = T_k q_k-q_k S.                                  (9)
```

The unit defect controls where inversion fails globally. The descent anomaly controls why no
finite formal representation is the source constraint. Both are needed in the general object.

## Collision audit

The following are parent theories and absorb generic versions of the dictionary:

- formal power-series units and inversion;
- infinite-order constant-coefficient differential operators and convolution equations;
- mean-periodic functions, spectral synthesis, and characteristic varieties;
- analytic Fredholm and determinant-line theory;
- difference Galois criteria proving differential transcendence of difference-equation solutions.

In particular, Suzuki--Ricotta--Fesenko already connect mean-periodicity with meromorphic
continuation and functional equations of zeta functions
(https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.2821), and Meyer constructs a nuclear-Frechet spectral realization of
the zeta zeros (https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412277). Arreche--Dreyfus--Roques develop differential
transcendence criteria for linear difference equations (https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.05416).

Therefore neither mean-periodicity, convolution spectra, nor a spectral interpretation of zeta is
claimed new. The candidate-new content is the necessity chain

```text
HT finite freedom
  -> zero finite conormal
  -> no nonzero finite shadow descends from the source constraint
  -> formal source cofactor is invisible as a unit
  -> obstruction equals the comparison cone of its failed source realization.  (11)
```

Targeted searches found no exact theorem making `(11)` the common invariant of constrained HT
sources. This is a provisional absence claim, not a priority claim.
