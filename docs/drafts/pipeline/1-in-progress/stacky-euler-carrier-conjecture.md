- **Statement:** On a fixed-divisor critical-disc fibre, the maximal Hausdorff continuous Euler

  rather than a coarse quotient.

## Exact object

Let `V_r` be the real topological vector space of analytic functional-equation-odd,
Schwarz-real regular germs on the closed critical disc of radius `r<1/2`.  For a deleted finite
prime set `S` and `epsilon>0`, let

```text
G_(r,S,epsilon) <= V_r
```

be the additive group generated by the genuine real degree-one Euler-unit increments of parameter
size `<epsilon`.  The existing nonlinear density theorem gives

```text
closure(G_(r,S,epsilon))=V_r.                              (1)
```

Define the translation action groupoid `E_r,S,epsilon` by

```text
Obj(E)=V_r,
Hom_E(x,y)={g in G : y=x+g},                               (2)
```

with composition induced by addition in `G`.  Mark the zero germ as base object.

## Positive stacky carrier law `[PAPER PROOF]`

For every topological real vector space `V` and additive subgroup `G<=V`:

1. the translation action groupoid is a groupoid;
2. the pointed total source fibre is canonically the acting group,

```text
coprod_(y in V) Hom(0,y)  ~=  G,                           (3)
```

by `g |-> (g, proof that g=0+g)`, with inverse given by the arrow label;
3. the translation action is free;
4. if `closure(G)=V`, every continuous `G`-invariant map from `V` to a Hausdorff space is
   constant, while `(3)` remains unchanged.

Thus coarse quotient and pointed groupoid contain categorically different information:

```text
Hausdorff coarse quotient V/G  -> one point,
pointed action groupoid        -> exact group G.            (4)
```

In the standard Borel model, contractibility of `V` further gives

```text
V //^h G ~= B(G_discrete),                                 (5)
```

so the fundamental group of the derived quotient is `G`, even though its coarse Hausdorff quotient
is a point.  Equation `(5)` is a standard equivariant-topology consequence; `(3)` is the elementary
model to formalize first and does not require a developed theory of stacks in Lean.

## Actual Euler specialization

Take `V=V_r` and `G=G_(r,S,epsilon)`.  Equation `(1)` plus the existing maximal-continuous-quotient
theorem proves the first line of `(4)`.  Equation `(3)` identifies the exact information retained by
the pointed quotient: not an invented auxiliary label, but the actual nonlinear group generated by
arbitrarily small Euler units after deleting any finite prime set.

This changes the carrier question.  A scalar reader on germs must collapse, but a functor, cocycle,
local system, or representation of `E_r,S,epsilon` may read the Euler arrows.  The source-to-divisor
problem should therefore be posed as a pointed incidence functor

```text
Euler action groupoid
  -> completed global source/divisor incidence groupoid
  -> trace-moment target,                                      (6)
```

not as a continuous function on the coarse germ quotient.

## Coupling to the two other boundary laws

The carrier in `(6)` must satisfy three independent non-collapse conditions.

1. **Euler-germ boundary:** retain the arrow group after the coarse fixed-divisor quotient becomes
   one point.
2. **Archimedean boundary:** transport an infinite arithmetic phase carrier through the one-point
   projective boundary of every finite commensurable Gamma product.
3. **Cold Fisher boundary:** retain more than the first two prime-power energies `log 2,log 3`,
   because ordinary scalar Boltzmann curvature has the formalized bottom-two-level selection law.

The resulting positive target is a pointed global incidence stack with

```text
actual Euler arrows
  + one finite archimedean valuation direction
  + distinguished divisor/trace moments.                       (7)
```

## Falsification attacks

1. **Standard-theory attack.** The abstract equivalence `(5)` is standard and cannot by itself
   pass the novelty gate.  Novelty can only lie in the exact arithmetic specialization and a
   source-to-divisor incidence functor.
2. **Label-overcount attack.** A free presentation on prime symbols would be artificial if its
   action has a kernel.  The first carrier is therefore the actual generated subgroup `G`, not a
   free group imposed by hand.
3. **RH-blindness attack.** The groupoid of one fixed-divisor fibre still does not know which
   divisor is the zeta divisor.  The marked global incidence functor in `(6)` is load-bearing.
4. **Completion attack.** Appending the Gamma factor as a scalar weight does not couple it to the
   arrow group.  Archimedean transport must be a proved functor/cocycle.
5. **Cold-collapse attack.** Scalar Fisher curvature factors through the first two energy levels;
   it can diagnose the clock end but cannot serve as the incidence functor.
