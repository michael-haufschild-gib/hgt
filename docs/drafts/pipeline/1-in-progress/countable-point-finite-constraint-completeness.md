# Countable point-finite constraint completeness of one positive HT source

- **ID:** `countable-point-finite-constraint-completeness`

## Medalist lens

The finite theorem says that one fixed positive hypertranscendental source realizes every finite
Stanley--Reisner differential matroid.  Passing to infinitely many facets is not a formal direct
limit: the finite proof sums one coordinate over every facet, so an infinite facet family creates
an undefined infinite analytic sum even when each observation polynomial is finite.

The exact question is therefore:

> Which infinite constraint geometries still make every finite observation a finite analytic
> operation on the same fixed HT source?

The proposed answer is point-finiteness, together with a separate global constant mode.

## Setup

Let `V` be countable and let `A` be a countable family of finite facets in `V`.  Assume:

1. `A` is an antichain;
2. every vertex belongs to at least one facet;
3. `A` is **point-finite**: every vertex belongs to only finitely many facets.

Let `S=C[delta][x_v : v in V]`.  Let `I_A` be the squarefree monomial ideal generated by finite
sets not contained in any facet.  For a facet `F`, let

```text
rho_F:S -> C[delta][x_v : v in F]
```

be restriction, and let `epsilon:S -> C[delta]` be the constant-term map.

## Candidate theorem

`[CONJECTURE]` Define the unitized finite-support facet map

```text
Theta(P)=(epsilon(P), (rho_F(P)-epsilon(P))_(F in A)).
```

Then:

1. the second component of `Theta(P)` has finite facet support for every polynomial `P`;
2. `ker Theta=I_A`;
3. the induced map from `S/I_A` into the unitization of the direct sum of the facet augmentation
   ideals is an injective `C[delta]`-algebra map;
4. one fixed positive prime-log Herglotz source `g_*`, independent of `V`, `A`, and the structural
   family, realizes the exact nonlinear differential matroid of every finite family in `S/I_A`;
5. the realization uses only finitely many translated source coordinates for each finite
   structural family, despite the constraint geometry having countably many facets.

Equivalently, the quantifier strengthening is

```text
there exists one positive HT source g_* such that every countable point-finite
Stanley--Reisner constraint geometry is realized exactly by finite observations of g_*.
```

## Candidate moves considered

1. `[BRIDGE]` Arbitrary algebraic constraint matroids on abstract differential-generic points.
   This is standard differential algebra and does not retain one analytic source or a translation
   sewing law.
2. `[BRIDGE]` Direct enumeration of allowed monomials into independent source coordinates.  This
   realizes a linear matroid but loses the requirement that the relations arise from one coherent
   facet/translation action.
3. `[REJECTED]` Naively sum the finite facet coordinates over countably many facets.  The constant
   monomial is seen by every facet, and even nonconstant terms may have infinite facet incidence.
4. `[SELECTED CONJECTURE]` Split off the one global constant mode and require point-finiteness.
   Every nonconstant monomial then meets only finitely many facets, so every polynomial has a
   finite-support coordinate packet.

## Paper proof obligations

### A. Finite-support law

For a nonconstant monomial with nonempty support `J`, choose `v in J`.  Every facet containing
`J` contains `v`; point-finiteness therefore makes the set of such facets finite.  A polynomial
has finitely many monomials, so the union of their visible facet sets is finite.  Subtracting the
constant term is load-bearing.

### B. Exact kernel

Every nonface monomial restricts to zero on every facet and has zero constant term, so `I_A` is
contained in `ker Theta`.  Conversely, if a monomial support `J` is a face, choose a facet
containing `J`.  Its coefficient is unchanged by restriction to that facet.  Thus `Theta(P)=0`
forces every face-monomial coefficient of `P` to vanish, leaving exactly `I_A`.

### C. Multiplicative unitization

Writing `P=c+p_F` and `Q=d+q_F` in each facet gives

```text
rho_F(PQ)-cd = c q_F + d p_F + p_F q_F.
```

The right side again has finite facet support.  This is precisely multiplication in the
unitization of the direct sum of facet augmentation ideals, so `Theta` is an algebra map rather
than only a linear embedding.

### D. Analytic realization

The index set consisting of the global constant coordinate and all facet--integral-orbit
coordinates is countable.  Embed it into the already formalized universal countable shift palette.
The fixed gauge `universalFacetOrbitGauge` makes that whole source family differentially
independent.  For every finite structural family, finite-support law A converts its coordinates
to finite polynomial differential-operator rows.  The existing exact operator-row theorem then
identifies differential independence of the analytic outputs with `C[delta]`-linear independence
of their classes in `S/I_A`.

## Attack

1. **Finite-complex collapse.**  If `A` is finite, the theorem specializes to the existing
   single-source finite constraint-completeness theorem.  The new content must therefore use an
   actually infinite point-finite family and a coherent global realization.
2. **Constant-mode obstruction.**  Without the separate `epsilon(P)` coordinate, the constant
   monomial has infinite facet support.  This falsifies the naive direct-sum claim.
3. **Point-finite boundary.**  If one vertex lies in infinitely many facets, the coordinate packet
   of `x_v` itself has infinite support.  The finite-observation realization fails before any
   differential-algebra question.  A topological/convergent completion would be a different
   theorem.
4. **Parent-theory attack.**  Erasing HT vocabulary leaves the standard embedding of a
   Stanley--Reisner ring into a product of its facet rings.  The possible new residue is the
   point-finite restricted-product refinement plus the one-fixed-positive-source exact
   differential-matroid realization.  If the restricted-product theorem or the analytic
   realization already appears in infinite Stanley--Reisner or differential-matroid literature,
   downgrade accordingly.
5. **Anti-theatre test.**  A mere direct limit or countable reindexing is not progress.  Promotion
   requires the exact constant-mode repair, finite-support theorem, multiplicative unitization,
   coherent infinite facet sewing, and exact nonlinear differential matroid.

## Small and boundary examples

- Infinite disjoint edges are point-finite and genuinely infinite.  Every nonconstant monomial is
  visible in at most one facet; the constant mode is the only global coordinate.
- An infinite star is not point-finite at its centre.  The centre variable already requires
  infinitely many facet coordinates, so the theorem correctly excludes it.
- A locally finite infinite triangulated surface is point-finite and lies in the intended class.
- The empty face is contained in every facet; it is exactly the global constant mode isolated by
  `epsilon`.

## RH-impact audit

`[CONJECTURE]` If proved, the theorem would strengthen the finite-selector no-go.  Even a cofinal
countable family of locally finite combinatorial constraints, together with positivity, the full
prime-log pole carrier, and one fixed source moment marking, would still not select zeta: the same
non-zeta HT source would carry every such point-finite geometry.  An RH-relevant selector would
therefore have to use at least one of:

1. a non-point-finite cofinal incidence law;
2. a genuinely convergent infinite sewing not reducible to finite-support observations;
3. a compatibility tying the sewing to functional-equation polarization or the actual Euler
   source, rather than relation geometry alone.

This is an exclusion/requirements result for RH research, not a route to RH.
