## Source geometry

Let `P` be the primes and put

```text
Q = C^P / C^(P),
```

where `C^(P)` denotes the finitely supported streams.  Let `T : Q -> Q` be induced by

```text
(T u)(p) = (log p) u(p).
```

This is well defined because multiplication by `log p` preserves finite support.  Regard `Q` as a
module over `R = C[X]` by letting `X` act as `T`.  For a bounded source family
`c : I -> (P -> C)`, define its clock module

```text
M(c) = span_C { T^k [c_i] : i in I, k >= 0 }
     = R-span { [c_i] : i in I } <= Q.
```

Fix a complex shift `a`.  Let

```text
F_a(c)(s) = sum_p c(p) p^(-(s+a))
```

on its right half-plane, and let `B_a` be the differential field generated over `C(s)` by every
single-prime exponential `p^(-(s+a))`.  Write

```text
K_a(c) = B_a < F_a(c_i) : i in I >_diff.
```

The diagonal shift multiplier `p^(-a)` is nowhere zero and commutes with `T`, so the module does
not depend on the chosen common shift.

## Exact master theorem

For arbitrary bounded source families `c : I -> (P -> C)`, `d : J -> (P -> C)`, and
`g : H -> (P -> C)`:

1. **Clock torsion-freeness.**  `Q` is torsion-free over `C[X]`.  Equivalently, every
   infinite-support prime source is hypertranscendental over `B_a`.
2. **Relative clock-module criterion.**  The family `(F_a(g_h))_h` is differentially
   algebraically independent over `K_a(c)` iff the natural module map

   ```text
   directSum_h C[X] -> Q / M(c),
   (P_h)_h |-> sum_h P_h(T)[g_h]
   ```

   is injective.
3. **Exact field order.**

   ```text
   K_a(c) <= K_a(d)  iff  M(c) <= M(d).
   ```

   Consequently `K_a(c) = K_a(d)` iff `M(c) = M(d)`.
4. **Rank law.**  For every finite new family, the relative differential transcendence degree is
   the `C[X]`-rank of the image of its clock module in `Q / M(c)`.
5. **Complete relation presentation.**  The differential-relation ideal over `K_a(c)` is generated
   by the affine linear differential relations obtained from the kernel of the module map in item
   2; the affine term is the carrier of the finite-support and `M(c)` residual.  The quotient is
   the symmetric algebra of the resulting complex jet space, equipped with the induced derivation.
   It is a free differential-polynomial algebra precisely on every free `C[X]` summand; torsion
   summands retain their corresponding linear differential equations.
6. **Finite prime-power grading.**  For any finite valuation palette, replace `Q` by the direct sum
   of one copy `Q_r` for every layer `p^r` and let `X` act on layer `r` by `r T`.  Items 1--5 hold
   verbatim.  Unique factorization makes the layers disjoint, and the nonzero scalar `r` changes no
   single-layer module rank.

## Paper proof

The proof is divided into six lemmas.  No analytic assertion below uses the false plain-tail
criterion.

### Lemma 1: clock torsion-freeness

Let `P(X) != 0` and suppose `P(T)[u] = 0` in `Q`.  Then

```text
{p : P(log p) u(p) != 0}
```

is finite.  A nonzero complex polynomial has finitely many roots, and distinct primes have
distinct logarithms.  Outside a finite set, therefore, `P(log p) != 0`, so `u(p) = 0`.  Thus `[u]
= 0`.  This proves torsion-freeness.

For one source `[u] != 0`, a nonzero differential polynomial linear in the jets would give a
nonzero `P` with `P(T)[u]=0`.  The converse analytic freeness follows from Lemma 4 below.  Hence an
infinite-support source is hypertranscendental over `B_a`.

### Lemma 2: finite-prime plus tail Jacobian packet

Let `S` be a finite set of primes and let `v_1,...,v_n` be coefficient columns linearly independent
modulo finite support.  Tail independence supplies primes `q_1,...,q_n` outside `S` for which the
evaluation matrix `(v_j(q_i))` is invertible.  Add the Kronecker columns at the primes in `S` and
evaluate first on `S`, then on the `q_i`.  The resulting matrix has the block form

```text
[ diagonal nonzero    * ]
[        0            V ]
```

with `V` invertible.  It is invertible.  The prime-coordinate Jacobian criterion therefore makes
the corresponding arithmetic functions algebraically independent over `C`.  Faithful analytic
Dirichlet realization and transcendence of the upper-end coordinate then make their convergent
Dirichlet series algebraically independent over `C(s)`.

### Lemma 3: finite character of the Euler base

Every element of `B_a` is contained in the differential field generated by finitely many
single-prime exponentials.  Indeed `B_a` is the ordinary field adjoin of the set of all jets of
those exponentials, and field adjunction has finite character.  Every jet of a single-prime
exponential is a nonzero scalar multiple of that same exponential.  A polynomial relation has
finitely many coefficients, so one finite prime set `S` contains the activity of all of them.

### Lemma 4: exact relative differential-independence criterion

First take `c` empty.  If the module map for `g` is not injective, choose a nonzero finite family
of polynomials `P_h` whose clock combination has finite support.  Termwise differentiation gives

```text
sum_h P_h(-d/ds) F_a(g_h) in B_a.
```

This is a nonzero affine linear differential-polynomial relation over `B_a`, so the analytic
family is not differentially independent.

Conversely, injectivity says that every finite family of jet columns

```text
p |-> (log p)^k g_h(p)
```

is independent modulo finite support.  A putative differential-polynomial relation uses finitely
many jets and, by Lemma 3, finitely many Euler-base primes.  Lemma 2 makes those jets jointly
algebraically independent with the finite base generators over `C(s)`, hence the selected jets are
algebraically independent over the finite base field.  This contradicts the relation.

For nonempty `c`, any putative relation involves only finitely many old generators and their jets.
Replace their clock classes by a basis of their finite-dimensional complex span in `M(c)`.  Module
injectivity modulo `M(c)` says that adjoining the selected new jet classes preserves tail-linear
independence.  Lemmas 2 and 3 again give joint algebraic independence; the standard pure-
transcendental base-change criterion gives algebraic independence of the new jets over the old
field.  Conversely, a kernel vector has a residual in `M(c)` plus finite support.  Both residual
carriers lie in `K_a(c)`, producing the required nonzero affine differential relation.  This proves
item 2.

### Lemma 5: field reconstruction

If `M(c) <= M(d)`, every class `[c_i]` is a finite `C[X]`-linear combination of the `[d_j]` modulo
finite support.  Termwise differentiation and finite-support descent show
`F_a(c_i) in K_a(d)`, and therefore `K_a(c) <= K_a(d)`.

Conversely suppose `K_a(c) <= K_a(d)` and fix `i`.  If `[c_i]` were not in `M(d)`, its image in
`Q/M(d)` would be nonzero.  Torsion-freeness is not required here: the order-zero carrier is
algebraically independent from every finite packet of old source jets whose source span omits
`[c_i]`, by Lemmas 2 and 3.  Any field expression for `F_a(c_i)` uses only such a finite packet,
which is impossible.  Thus `[c_i] in M(d)`.  Stability under `T` gives `M(c) <= M(d)`.  This proves
item 3.

### Lemma 6: rank, relation ideal, and valuation grading

For a finite new family, its image module in `Q/M(c)` is finitely generated over the principal
ideal domain `C[X]`.  The structure theorem splits it as a free module of rank `r` plus a torsion
module.  Lift a basis of the free summand to polynomial combinations of the new sources.  Lemma 4
makes their carriers differentially independent over `K_a(c)`.  Every torsion generator satisfies
a nonzero inhomogeneous linear differential equation over the field generated by that free packet,
so it contributes no differential transcendence.  Hence the relative differential
transcendence degree is exactly `r`.

On the free complex jet vector space, the analytic evaluation map has linear kernel equal to the
clock-module relations, with its finite-support and old-module residual recorded in the
coefficient field.  The symmetric-algebra quotient by a linear kernel is the symmetric algebra of
the image.  Lemma 4 makes a complex basis of that image algebraically independent, so no nonlinear
generators remain in the kernel.  Hence the full differential-relation ideal is generated by the
affine linear kernel relations.  The induced clock action preserves the image; its free
`C[X]` summands give differential indeterminates, while its torsion summands give exactly the
linear differential equations already present in the kernel.

For a finite set of valuation layers, unique factorization gives disjoint coefficient support at
`p^r`.  The preceding packet proof is therefore block diagonal by `r`.  Differentiation multiplies
the layer-`r` coefficient by `-r log p`, which is exactly the announced action `X |-> rT`.
Repeating Lemmas 1--5 on the finite direct sum proves item 6.

## Hostile audit

- The former plain-Grassmann statement is not used; the reciprocal-log counterexample is absorbed
  because `T[c]=[d]` places both sources in the same clock module.
- The one-source hypertranscendence consequence is classical Ostrowski/Reich/Pong territory and is
  not claimed as new.
- Shapiro--Sparer supplies the Jacobian conversion, not the module classification.
- Pong's shift--log derivative theorems give broad sufficient independence criteria for one source
  and special operator families.  They do not, in the sources checked so far, give the arbitrary
  multi-source iff criterion, equality classification of generated differential fields, or the
  complete relation ideal over the all-prime Euler base.
- The rank statement requires care when quotienting by `M(c)`: the quotient can have torsion.  The
  asserted rank is the fraction-field rank of the image module; relative differential algebraic
  torsion is allowed and is recorded by the relation ideal.
- The analytic theorem is only for convergent bounded seeds and finite valuation palettes.  It
  makes no unsupported continuation claim and does not identify the infinite prime-power
  completion used by `log zeta`.
