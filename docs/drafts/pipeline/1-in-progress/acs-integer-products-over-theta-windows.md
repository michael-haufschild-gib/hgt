# Integer products over theta windows

Let $q_n=d_{\rm ord}(nD_n)$. Seek fixed positive integers $K,B$ and explicit integer weights $0\le k_{N,n}\le K$, independent of $q_n$, with $k_{N,n}\ge1$ for $N\le n\le2N$, such that the finite rational product $\prod_n q_n^{k_{N,n}}$ lies between $B^{-N^3}$ and $B^{N^3}$ for every sufficiently large $N$. This would control a product containing every conductor in the central band at the natural cubic scale. The sharp prefix still uses unit weights through its endpoint. [Signed prefix criterion](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheSignedProductOverSquareConductors.lean)

At center $T=2\log N$, let $a_n$ be the theta mass assigned to conductor $n=\lceil e^{(T+x)/2}\rceil$. For fixed large $A$, round $A N^3a_n/n^2$ by taking successive differences of floors of its partial sums. Every prefix rounding error is at most one. The theta source is positive on the compact central band and satisfies $\Phi(x)\ll e^{3x/2}$, giving uniform upper multiplicities and a positive central lower multiplicity. Truncate at $M=\lceil N^{1+\delta}\rceil$ with $(1+\delta)(2+2w)<3$; all absolute exponential theta moments exist. Abel summation against $n^2 L(2\log n)$ costs $O(M^{2+2w})$ by the trace increment bound. The pointwise $O(n^2)$ difference from $-\log q_n$ costs $O(N^3)$ under either weight, since the same prefix rounding applied to $n^2$ costs only $O(M^2)$. The unrounded long trace has zero theta mean.
