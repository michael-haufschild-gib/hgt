# The RH Route–Literature Exchange Frontier at 1 August 2026

## Abstract

This paper compares every proof, disproof, secondary, and retired route in the current
`HTGeometry`, `HilbertMetric`, and `RHTower` corpus with primary external literature available by
1 August 2026. It asks two questions route by route: what the formal corpus contributes beyond the
exact results located externally, and what external work contributes that is both absent from Lean
and capable of narrowing the route's first open source premise.

The answer is asymmetric. The corpus contains many source-specific identities, completeness laws,
sharp boundaries, and no-go classifications for which no exact external collision was located.
These are provisional priority assessments, not proof of novelty. External 2025–2026 work supplies
several major route architectures—especially the CCM finite spectral models, Suzuki's finite screw
operators, Hedenmalm's theta boundary pair, Groskin's finite Guinand–Weil dictionary, and
Liflandsky's Möbius–Laplace implication. Most downstream logical content is already formalized.
The principal remaining literature-to-Lean debts are concrete operator/source constructions, not
additional abstract RH equivalences.

No external theorem reviewed supplies a missing Tier-1 source estimate, and no local theorem closes
one. RH and its negation therefore remain unproved.

## 1. Standards and verdict language

Lean declaration types, not names or prose, determine local content. A result is credited as an
advance only when unconditional. An implication with an open premise, an RH equivalence, a theorem
assuming RH or simple zeros, and numerical evidence are classified separately.

“No collision located” means targeted comparison with primary sources found no theorem having the
same hypotheses and conclusion. It does not establish legal or historical priority. “Already in
Lean” requires a source-specific declaration or an exact logical/analytic connector; generic
functional analysis is not called a formalization of a paper's concrete operator.

Formalization is recommended only when an external unconditional theorem is absent, narrows a
named open core, and is not merely another coordinate equivalent to RH.

## 2. Route-by-route exchange

### Route A — first clock

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** No external source was found proving that one
fixed published logarithmic clock is multiplicity-complete for every off-critical Xi divisor,
classifying its false-RH power/abscissa spectra, or reducing RH to its eventual lower floor and
Bohr-asymptotic source laws. This fixed-scalar tomography package is a plausible local contribution;
priority remains specialist-review dependent.

**External contribution.** CCM finite spectral triples and Connes's 2026 survey motivate the clock
and finite-to-infinite limit, but leave rigorous convergence open. In particular, Theorem 1.1 of
[Zeta Spectral Triples](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22755) proves self-adjointness and the
regularized-determinant formula for the finite rank-one perturbation under its finite simple/even
ground-state hypothesis. That finite theorem is absent as a concrete Lean object. It does not prove
the Route-A source floor, tail lower bound, or continuous Bohr asymptote.

**Lean action.** Formalize CCM Theorem 1.1 source-faithfully and identify its normalized finite
determinant coefficient with the local published first clock. This supplies a concrete finite
baseline and a bridge into the local tomography package; the convergence and lower-floor premises
must remain explicit.

### Route B — fixed-width rectangular Weil and positive quadrature

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** No collision was found for the conjunction of
one predetermined positive-width rectangular profile, exact prime–Gamma evaluation, complete
off-critical divisor/multiplicity reconstruction, false-RH two-sided exponent and negative-mass
spectra, and the exact positive completed-lag versus prime-wall quadrature. Grouped Mangoldt
transport, apex-lock, and no-free-splice classifications also appear local.

**External contribution.** Classical Guinand–Weil and Weil positivity provide the parent formula
and endpoint. Groskin's [finite Guinand–Weil dictionary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02828) gives an
exact vector-to-bandlimited-test and zero-sum dictionary (Theorem 2.5), quotient dimension
`2N+1` (Corollary 2.4), a noncollapsing pole-neutral family (Corollary 2.7), and a
positive-definite, strictly totally positive Cauchy–Stieltjes archimedean-tail increment with
cutoff budget (Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.3).

**Lean action.** Formalize Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.3 in the literal rectangular basis, then
compare the resulting positive tail budget with the local boundary charge. This is not a duplicate:
it is the strongest external finite-cutoff theorem directly testing whether omitted archimedean
mass can pay that charge. It still does not resolve grouped apex/flank capacity-one transport.

### Route C1 — complete Xi–Weil positivity

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The route endpoint is Weil's classical
criterion. Formal multiplicity-aware polarization and exact typed equivalence are verification
contributions, not claimed new mathematics.

**External contribution.** Weil, Guinand, Barner, Yoshida, Bombieri, Connes–Consani, Suzuki, and
Groskin supply the established explicit-formula, localization, and finite-operator framework.
Suzuki's 2022 screw-function theorem makes every localized kernel operator trace class
unconditionally and characterizes RH by nonnegativity/nondegeneracy of those concrete localized
forms.

**Lean action.** Formalize Suzuki's unconditional trace-class theorem for the literal localized
screw kernel and identify the localized form as a concrete subcarrier of `xiWeilPolarization`.
Then prove the cofinal carrier-density statement. This adds a source-defined compact carrier but
does not establish positivity; the complete source positivity wall survives.

### Route C2 — theta center-mixed positivity

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The corpus goes beyond the order-two
log-concavity literature: it proves source log-concavity, strict fourth-Turán and sixth-jet laws,
cofinal and coalesced order-three positive chambers, and global three-point center-mixed positivity.
It also proves that the center-mixed Fourier current is the transverse derivative of `‖XiC‖²`, so
the matrix, Fourier-sign, and logarithmic-derivative formulations are one condition; no exact
external collision was located. The center-mixed criterion is corpus-originated on present
evidence. It must not be attributed to Hedenmalm, whose paper contains no center-mixed matrix or
positive-definite criterion. Separately, the corpus classifies continuous positive source-core
forms by divisor-supported spectral measures and proves dense-radical/nonclosability obstructions
for Hedenmalm's actual theta pair.

**External contribution.** Hedenmalm's
[spectral interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17494) supplies the concrete theta boundary
problem and formal self-adjoint-pair strategy. The 2026 theta log-concavity preprints supply only
order-two statements; computation-dependent or axiomatized perturbation claims are excluded.

**Lean action.** Hedenmalm's normalized boundary-value equivalence is already formalized but is not
the source of the center-mixed criterion. The actionable external input is the independently
checker-replayable Platt–Trudgian verification that all zeta zeros through height
`3,000,175,332,800` lie on the critical line. Once replayed rather than assumed, the existing
current identity transfers it to center-mixed nonnegativity on the corresponding finite band. It
does not prove simplicity and leaves the infinite high-frequency sign open.

### Route D — prime–archimedean Hankel reconstruction

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The exact fixed-node/all-degree source Hankel
reconstruction equivalence, HilbertMetric unisolvent handoff, finite-degree tail obstruction, and
source-specific moment cones have no located external match as one package.

**External contribution.** Classical Hamburger/Stieltjes moment theory and Hankel positivity give
generic machinery, while Weil/Barner explicit formulae identify the arithmetic source. They do not
prove positivity or reconstruction at the corpus's literal nodes. Suzuki's 2022 Theorem 1.8 gives
an RH criterion through two Hankel determinant families built from screw moments; those moments are
not visibly identical to the local reciprocal-central source moments.

**Lean action.** Suzuki's determinant criterion is a literature-completeness import, not a
frontier priority: formalizing it and proving the exact transform or noncollision with the local
moment family would clarify provenance, but would not prove the missing source PSD or
reconstruction.

### Route D2 — prime–archimedean high-frequency ceiling

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The exact equivalence between RH and any finite
uniform ceiling above frequency four, together with the false-RH cofinal-unboundedness
contrapositive, appears corpus-specific. No external collision was located.

**External contribution.** Platt–Trudgian prove that the first `12,363,153,437,138` nontrivial
zeros, through exact height `3,000,175,332,800`, lie on the critical line. This does not supply the
continued-source tail ceiling and does not assert simplicity, but it enlarges the certified central
band by twelve orders of magnitude.

**Lean action.** Replay the published verification in an independently checkable certificate and
instantiate the existing generic `RHVerifiedThrough` interface. This advances the route's finite
band but leaves the RH-equivalent high-frequency ceiling unchanged. A theorem that merely assumes
the published certificate is not an acceptable import.

### Route E — critical Möbius/Mertens cancellation

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The sparse Gamma–Hankel bounded-return endpoint,
the finite-aperture Euler-normal divergence, and the monotone cofinal finite-prime normal-cone
diagonal—including Gamma completion and projectivized reciprocal-square limit on `Re(s)>1`—have no
located external collision.

**External contribution.** Liflandsky's
[discrete Möbius–Laplace criterion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09797) proves unconditionally that a
half-power decay bound implies RH; its explicit zero formula assumes simple zeros and its converse
needs extra hypotheses. Classical Mertens, Nyman–Beurling, and Báez–Duarte criteria provide related
equivalences. Mangerel's 2024 strict bound for Liouville reflection correlations is the closest
growing antidiagonal theorem located, but it gives only a strict trivial saving and uses the wrong
arithmetic weight for the one-sided Möbius Gamma–Hankel covariance.

**Lean action.** Liflandsky's transform, convergence, tail, Mellin identity, and unconditional
decay-to-RH implication are already formalized without importing the simple-zero hypothesis.
No external result continues the proved Plücker diagonal from `Re(s)>1` to critical Möbius data.

### Route F — all-prime Sonin transfer

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The shell-cut completion classification,
positive divergent lost-layer energy, exact physical-band vanishing, explicit packet norm floor,
sharp three-term gap-tail budget, closed-form slack laws, and polynomially weighted tail vanishing
have no located external counterpart as an arithmetic Sonin-to-prime transfer theorem.

**External contribution.** Burnol's 2002 Theorems 3.1–3.3 prove sharp qualitative
completeness/minimality laws for evaluator systems, Sonine evaluators including the critical
`a=1` case, and the functions `ζ(s)/(s-ρ)^l`; Theorem 6.3 identifies exact critical dual systems.
Connes–Consani's semilocal/scaling analyses provide a related finite-prime Sonin carrier. These
results can settle qualitative carrier closure but provide no quantitative convergence rate,
all-prime shell schedule, or ideal-error energy control.

**Lean action.** Formalize Burnol's qualitative completeness/minimality and co-Poisson complement,
then identify its carrier with the local `evenSonin`/shell-cut carrier. This removes a qualitative
literature debt. The route's decisive quantitative schedule remains new work.

### Route G — Suzuki Hermite–Biehler, anti-Pick, and Ward realization

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** Beyond Suzuki's finite interval theorem, the
corpus proves projective defect boundaries, two-sided escape no-gos, prime tangent and Clark-atom
tomography, flat shift holonomy, phase compactification, one-sided Carleman parity corrections,
source-specific wall/light-cone Gram completions, sharp depth floors, unique least negative-part
completion, and exact Ward/source obstructions. No exact external collision was located for these
source-specific refinements.

**External contribution.** Suzuki's
[Weil quadratic form via the screw function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09096) proves the concrete
Friedrichs realization, continuity of the lowest eigenvalue, small-aperture positivity/simplicity
and evenness, deficiency indices `(1,1)`, self-adjoint extensions, and entire characteristic
functions `W(a,θ;z)` whose zeros are exactly their real simple spectra. The proposed
finite-to-`z²ξ/ξ′` limit is conjectural. The July finite-element
[numerical realization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24830) proves an archimedean Mellin head
coefficient but labels the spectral convergence, ground-state, and zero observations numerical and
universal rather than new arithmetic.

**Lean action.** Formalize Suzuki Theorems 1.1 and 1.5 source-faithfully: concrete screw Hilbert
space, closed form domain, Friedrichs operator, deficiency vectors, self-adjoint extensions, and
literal `W`. Abstract Hermite–Biehler, Livšic, Donoghue, and real-zero consequences are already in
Lean, but cannot stand in for Suzuki's objects. Corollary 1.6 must remain conditional; the corpus
proves that its proposed entire `z²ξ/ξ′` limit target has unavoidable real poles. Numerical
finite-element claims are not imported.

### Route G2 — relative-Volterra phase source-germ weld

### Route L — Deninger/Hodge/cohomological realization

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The corpus adds explicit Hilbert/Kummer

**External contribution.** Deninger's

**Lean action.** Formalize those concrete prime-fibre theorems, the normalized-Haar retraction,

### Route M — Gamma–Jacobi singular reader

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The singular normalization, head/residual

**External contribution.** Inoue's
[Meixner–Pollaczek expansion theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.1220) proves compact-uniform

**Lean action.** Formalize Inoue Theorem 2.3 and Proposition 4.1, then the source-specialized
inner/outer convergence statement needed by the local Gamma–Jacobi normalization. These results
provide the missing analytic passage from finite Jacobi readers to a holomorphic limit; an
additional proof must still show that the outer factor is Xi rather than only prime-node data.

### Route N — Hedenmalm prime-normal estimate

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The exact Wick/Fock boundary-energy reduction
and sub-log-square source-domain threshold are corpus-specific.

**External contribution.** Hedenmalm's Theorems 3.3.1 and 3.3.3 identify the real and complex

**Lean action.** The normalized boundary-value equivalence and source-polarization consequences
already cover this conditional mechanism. Do not add another abstract endpoint. The next theorem
must be the unconditional source-domain all-prime `o((log X)²)` estimate, including Jordan-tower
terms; no such external theorem exists.

### Route O — Connes/CCM restricted-Weil and prolate limit

**Local contribution beyond located literature.** The corpus proves extensive constrained-prolate

**External contribution.** Connes–Consani–Moscovici's

**Lean action.** Formalize the concrete CCM finite operator and determinant theorem together with
Connes–van Suijlekom's exact isolated-simple-even-ground-state real-zero theorem. The real-zero
transfer and abstract constrained-prolate machinery are already present, but the source-defined
finite family is not. Do not encode the numerical finite-to-Xi convergence as a theorem.

## 3. Secondary criteria

Li–Keiper, Nyman–Beurling/Báez–Duarte, Salem, Hardy, Colombeau, theta-infinity, mollifier,
Burnol co-Poisson, OPUC, determinant-line, spectral-flow, de Bruijn–Newman, Jensen-polynomial,
Lagarias, and related criteria add coordinates rather than source producers.

The 2026 Salem improper-integral criterion and Liflandsky one-way Möbius implication are already
formalized. Hedenmalm's normalized boundary problem and generic Hardy evaluation obstruction are
formalized. Exact absent literature packages are:

- Li's full analytic criterion, not merely a finite or sequence-level abstraction;
- Hardy's 2026 concrete evaluation functions and their density/obstruction theorems;
- the 2026 automorphic theta-at-infinity carrier and its density consequences;
- Burnol's evaluator/co-Poisson completeness and exact dual systems;
- the concrete Suzuki, CCM, Inoue, Deninger, Groskin, and Polson theorems named above.

Full Li, Hardy, and theta-at-infinity formalizations improve corpus completeness but do not narrow a
live source premise beyond an equivalent positivity, density, or closure wall. They are therefore
secondary to the concrete operator/carrier imports. Colombeau remains low priority: no located
unconditional theorem improves a route's first open core.

## 4. Retired routes and external SOTA

No primary source found by the cutoff revives a retired mechanism at the quantifier level killed in
the corpus. External work supports several of the same boundaries:

- finite/local positivity does not extrapolate automatically to complete Weil positivity;
- scalar, finite-moment, fixed-degree, and finite-rank data cannot control an unrestricted source;
- Suzuki's conjectural entire limit is not established, and the literal `z²ξ/ξ′` target has real
  poles independently of RH;
- CCM and Suzuki numerical convergence is not an analytic finite-to-infinite theorem;
- zero-density, critical-line proportions, gap statistics, and large-value estimates do not
  eliminate every off-line zero;
- criteria assuming RH, simple zeros, localization, independence, or arbitrary-length moment
  bounds cannot supply those hypotheses.

The corpus nevertheless contributes sharper positive survivors: exact fixed-width tomography,
refinement conservation, source boundary charges, light-cone Gram decompositions, quantitative
leakage thresholds, compactification residual classifications, normal-cone diagonals, and explicit
rate deficits. No exact external collision was located for most of these survivors.

## 5. Disproof frontier

The local corpus has unusually explicit typed disproof certificates: negative finite Weil/Hankel
forms, first-clock or rectangular divergence, positive Ward index, nonzero SEM charge, source-sector
failures, and right-half divisor/ladder witnesses. Their logical sufficiency is formalized; none is
produced unconditionally.

External literature supplies no rigorously certified off-critical zeta zero and no unconditional
negative certificate. Numerical verification of many critical-line zeros is evidence against, not a
disproof route. Claimed proof/disproof manuscripts are not imported without independent complete
proof audit.

## 6. Formalization priorities

External results that genuinely strengthen a live route, in dependency order, are:

1. Suzuki Theorems 1.1 and 1.5: concrete finite screw space, Friedrichs and deficiency operators,
   self-adjoint extensions, and literal `W` (Routes G/G2).
2. CCM Theorem 1.1 and Connes--van Suijlekom Theorem 6.1: concrete finite restricted-Weil operator,
   determinant, and exact finite real-zero transfer (Routes A/O).
3. Groskin Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.3: positive archimedean tail and cutoff budget in the
   rectangular basis (Route B).
4. Burnol Theorems 3.1--3.3: qualitative Sonin/evaluator completeness and co-Poisson complement,
   followed by CCM's semilocal Sonin isomorphism and Fourier–Mellin factor law (Route F and prime
   seam).
5. Deninger Theorem 7.10, Theorem 10.2, and Proposition 11.7: concrete prime-fibre topology and
   divisor support (Route L).
6. Inoue Theorem 2.3 and Proposition 4.1: compact-normal Jacobi expansion and zero convergence
   (Route M).
7. Suzuki 2022 Theorem 1.5: trace-class localized screw carrier and its Weil-polarization handoff
   (Route C1).
8. Polson Theorem 21: safe `α>1` Thorin/GGC comparison, stopped at the quarter-tilt barrier
   (Route P).
9. Platt--Trudgian's checker-replayable verified-height certificate (Routes C2/D2), only through an
   independently audited certificate and not by an assumed or newly computed fact.

Suzuki's screw-moment Hankel criterion, full Li, Hardy, theta-at-infinity, and Romik's coefficient
asymptotic are corpus-completeness imports, not first-order route advances. Bellotti, Mangerel, fixed
Jensen degrees, numerical Suzuki/CCM convergence, and conditional critical Thorin statements do not
meet a current route premise and should not be formalized for frontier leverage.

## 7. Present conclusion

The corpus's strongest plausible new science lies mainly in source-specific finite identities,
complete scalar tomography, obstruction classifications, and exact survivor theorems. External
SOTA contributes powerful finite operator and boundary-value architectures, but no published
unconditional theorem closes a current source-level RH premise.

The next rational exchange is therefore not to add more RH equivalences. It is to complete the
ordered finite operator/carrier imports above, then attack the independently named
convergence/source estimates. Until one such estimate is proved—or an unconditional negative
certificate is constructed—the research frontier remains open on both proof and disproof sides.
