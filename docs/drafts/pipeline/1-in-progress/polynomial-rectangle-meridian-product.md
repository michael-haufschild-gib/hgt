## Strip cancellation — complete paper proof [PROVED HERE]

Let B_i=h_0⋯h_{i−1} and K_i=k_0⋯k_{i−1}, with paths written in traversal order. Define the positive cell loop L_i=h_i v_{i+1} k_i⁻¹ v_i⁻¹. Native monodromy therefore gives the same ascending product of cell permutations. No commutativity or unproved plane presentation is used.
