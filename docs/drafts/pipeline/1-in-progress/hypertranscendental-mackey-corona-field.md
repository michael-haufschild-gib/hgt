## 1. Raw clock and finite freedom

Let `J_raw,x` be the unrestricted linearized infinite observable clock of a constrained source
`x`, filtered by its finite jet windows `J_<=N`. Let `W_x` be the homogeneous source tangent and let

```text
J_fin^vee = union_N (J_<=N)^vee                              (1)
```

be the finite-coordinate dual.

Hypertranscendental finite freedom is represented by

```text
W_x -> J_<=N  surjective for every N.                        (2)
```

The Lean-formalized product-density theorem gives

```text
closure_product(W_x)=J_raw,x.                               (3)
```

Consequently every Hausdorff product-continuous linear functional annihilating `W_x` is zero.
Thus no nonzero source constraint is visible in `J_fin^vee` or in the raw product topology.

## 2. Product-topology no-go and positive residue

The product topology is not merely too weak for one proof technique. Under `(2)`, it collapses
every separated constraint quotient. Therefore it cannot be the topology of constrained HT
geometry.

This no-go has the exact positive replacement formalized in Lean. If a nonzero conormal

```text
lambda : J_raw,x -> Q,        lambda(W_x)=0                 (4)
```

takes values in a Hausdorff space, then the minimal topology generated by the product clock and
`lambda` is strictly finer than the product topology. Moreover it is minimal among all refinements
making `lambda` continuous.

Hence a source constraint contributes open sets. It is not an equation later placed inside a
pre-existing completion.

## 3. Raw-domain no-go and source rigging

An actual infinite conormal series need not be defined on every raw clock. Gamma's factorial

The positive object is a source-rigged clock

## 4. Single-topology no-go and the Mackey interval

A conormal dual does not generally determine one locally convex topology. Different compatible
topologies can have the same continuous dual, and choosing one by hand would merely rename the
noncanonical-boundary-condition obstruction.

The positive object is therefore an interval, not a distinguished topology.

Let `A_x` be the intrinsic source operator algebra and let `Lambda_x` be the minimal
source-canonical `A_x`-module of continuous conormals generated by the nonlocal constraint and
closed under reflection and the anomaly cocycle. Put

```text

```

The dual pair `(E_x,E_x^natural)` has a weak topology and a Mackey topology. Define the
**hypertranscendental topology interval**

```text
I_x = [ sigma(E_x,E_x^natural), tau(E_x,E_x^natural) ].      (6)
```

The existence of an interval of compatible topologies is parent functional analysis. The proposed
new object is the source construction of `(5)`, its clock filtration, boundary current, reflection,
and analytic framing as one indivisible package.

**[CONJECTURE -- SOURCE-CORONA EXISTENCE AND MINIMALITY]** Every regular source-minimal constrained
HT object has a nonzero finitely generated `A_x`-module `Lambda_x`, intrinsic under source
equivalence, satisfying

```text
Lambda_x intersection J_fin^vee = 0.                        (7)
```

It is the smallest source-algebra- and reflection-stable conormal module for which the original
global constraint is continuous and its source-selected analytic section exists.

**Falsifier.** Two source-equivalent minimal constraints produce nonisomorphic modules `Lambda_x`,

source-operator generators or has unbounded boundary-symbol width.

## 5. The corona conormal

Equation `(7)` places every source conormal entirely beyond finite observation. Define

```text

B_x      = image(Lambda_x -> Corona_x).                      (9)
```

`B_x` is the **corona conormal**. It need not be finite-dimensional over the constant field:
differentiating one nonlocal constraint already produces infinitely many conormal equations. It is
thin when it is finitely generated over `A_x` and its associated boundary symbol has uniformly
finite rank, even though the finite jet field has unbounded transcendence degree.

This gives a precise version of the founding conjecture:

```text
infinite algebraic clock capacity = unbounded independent finite observables,
thin constraint boundary         = finite generator rank and boundary width
                                   of the corona conormal B_x.                (10)
```

Hypertranscendence is about observables of a clock. The clock is not time. The source conormal is
the relation among the entire observable tower which no finite clock can see.

The positive replacement is the pairing, not the ranks. Let


**Falsifiers.** A differentially algebraic source with unavoidable tail-faithful minimal

## 9. Five obstruction faces of one interval

**[CONJECTURE -- MACKEY-CORONA FIVE-FACE THEOREM]** Let `I_x` carry the source framing, reflection,
and boundary current. Then the five recurring proof-chain obstructions are representations of one
failure of a self-dual polarized section of `I_x`:

1. **continuation failure:** the source conormal or its framed section does not extend under domain

2. **noncanonical boundary conditions:** more than one source-admissible compatible topology in

3. **monodromy:** local source polarizations extend but return with nontrivial automorphism;
4. **spectral incompleteness:** the chosen compatible topology is not complete for the

5. **positivity failure:** no compatible topology supports a reflection-positive real
   polarization.

These are the same obstruction only if comparison functors preserve `B_x`, the transgressions
`theta_N`, the seam current, reflection, and framing. Agreement of spectra alone is insufficient.
