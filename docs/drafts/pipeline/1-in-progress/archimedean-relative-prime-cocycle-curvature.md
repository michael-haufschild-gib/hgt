- **Statement:** For two distinct primes `p,q`, the Riemann zeta function is differentially
  transcendental over the differential field generated by all Gamma translates along

## Relation to the HT value geometry

This cocycle statement is not the scientific novelty unit.  It asks whether the universal HT
relation geometry admits a canonical arithmetic pointing when the distinguished value is `zeta`.

The candidate-new object remains the geometry already isolated by the realization and dimension
theorems: arbitrary finite simplicial complexes occur as exact HT relation geometries, analytic
dimension one has only the principal pure stratum, and a connected nonpure two-thick complex first
occurs in analytic dimension two.  If the present conjecture is proved, it contributes a possible
source-sensitive marking of that geometry; it is not promoted as a second founding geometric law.

## The constraint

Fix distinct primes `p,q` and put

```text
alpha=log p,   beta=log q,
Lambda=Z alpha+Z beta.                                     (1)
```

Unique factorization makes `alpha,beta` linearly independent over `Q`.  Define the shift-stable
archimedean differential field

```text
K_(Gamma,Lambda)
 =C(s)<Gamma(s+lambda):lambda in Lambda>_D.                 (2)
```

Let `K_(Gamma,Lambda)^da` denote the meromorphic elements differentially algebraic over (2).  It is
a shift-stable differential field.  The zeta translation cocycle is

```text
m_zeta(lambda;s)=zeta(s+lambda)/zeta(s).                    (3)
```

The conjecture is

```text
dtrdeg_(K_(Gamma,Lambda)) K_(Gamma,Lambda)<zeta>_D=1.       (4)
```

## Exact gauge reformulation `[PROVED HERE]`

Equation (4) is equivalent to the nonexistence of

```text
b in (K_(Gamma,Lambda)^da)^x,
chi:Lambda->C^x a character                              (5)
```

such that, for `lambda=alpha,beta`,

```text
m_zeta(lambda;s)=b(s+lambda)/b(s) chi(lambda).              (6)
```

If zeta were DA over (2), take `b=zeta` and `chi=1`; then (6) holds.  Conversely, if (6) held, put
`g=zeta/b`.  Then

```text
g(s+lambda)=chi(lambda)g(s),   lambda=alpha,beta.           (7)
```

The two-shift quasi-period-rank law makes `g` DA over `C(s)`.  Since `b` is DA over (2), zeta would
be DA over (2), contradicting (4).  Thus (4) and DA-gauge nonflattenability are equivalent. `QED`

This reduction is exact: the conjecture asks whether zeta's relative gauge curvature survives
after every archimedean translate available to the same prime-log action has already been adjoined.

## Prime-log Gamma differential-coordinate and fixed-field law `[PROVED HERE] [BRIDGE]`

Before derivatives are adjoined, the coefficient field in (2) has an exact positive description.
Write

```text
K^alg_(Gamma,Lambda)
  = C(s)(Gamma(s+lambda):lambda in Lambda).                 (8)
```

Then:

> **Prime-log Gamma coordinate law.** The family
> `{Gamma(s+lambda):lambda in Lambda}` is algebraically independent over `C(s)`.  Consequently
> `K^alg_(Gamma,Lambda)` is the pure rational function field on a set of coordinates indexed by
> `Lambda`.

Indeed, Wang--Yao prove that every finite family of Gamma translates whose shifts are pairwise
incongruent modulo `Z` is algebraically independent over `C(s)`.  If two elements of `Lambda`
differed by an integer, there would be an identity

```text
a log p+b log q=n,   a,b,n in Z.                            (9)
```

The prime-log independence-modulo-one theorem forces `a=b=n=0`.  Thus distinct lattice elements
are incongruent modulo `Z`, and Wang--Yao applies to every finite subfamily. `QED`

There is a stronger all-jet version.  Let `F` be the field of one-periodic meromorphic functions.

> **Prime-log Gamma differential-coordinate law.** The family
> `{Gamma(s+lambda):lambda in Lambda}` is differentially algebraically independent over `F(s)`.
> Hence it is differentially algebraically independent over `C(s)`, and
> `K_(Gamma,Lambda)` is a differential rational function field on differential coordinates indexed
> by `Lambda`.

Hardouin--Singer's multiplicative dependence criterion applies to the equations

```text
Gamma(s+lambda+1)=(s+lambda) Gamma(s+lambda).               (10)
```

For a finite set `E subset Lambda`, differential dependence over `F(s)` would give a nonzero
constant-coefficient homogeneous linear differential polynomial `L` such that

```text
L((s+lambda)^(-1):lambda in E)=g(s+1)-g(s),   g in C(s).    (11)
```

Every derivative of `(s+lambda)^(-1)` has its sole pole at `-lambda`.  Distinct `lambda` lie in
distinct pole orbits modulo `Z`.  At the highest pole order occurring in `L`, the discrete residue
on each orbit is therefore one nonzero scalar coefficient.  A rational difference
`g(s+1)-g(s)` has zero discrete residue at every orbit and every pole order.  All highest-order
coefficients must vanish; descending on pole order makes `L=0`, a contradiction. `QED`

This free differential-coordinate description has a dynamical consequence.

> **Prime-log Gamma fixed-field law.** For every nonzero `mu in Lambda`, translation
> `tau_mu:f(s) |-> f(s+mu)` is an automorphism of `K_(Gamma,Lambda)` and
>
> ```text
> (K_(Gamma,Lambda))^(tau_mu)=C.                            (12)
> ```

Under the coordinate identification, `tau_mu` sends `s` to `s+mu` and the coordinate indexed by
`lambda` to the coordinate indexed by `lambda+mu`, preserving derivative order.  For a differential
rational function `R`, let its essential block support be the finite set of Gamma translates whose
jets cannot be removed from its smallest differential rational coordinate field.  Differential
independence makes this support intrinsic.  Translation carries it from `S` to `S+mu`.  If `R` is
fixed, uniqueness of essential support gives `S=S+mu`.  A nonempty finite subset of the torsion-free
group `Lambda` cannot be invariant under a nonzero translation, so `S` is empty and `R in C(s)`.
A rational function with a nonzero real period is constant, proving (12). `QED`

The positive resource statement is therefore exact: a nonconstant observable fixed by a prime-log
translation cannot be manufactured from any finite differential expression in the
translation-saturated Gamma field.  Such an observable requires an additional arithmetic generator.
This does not prove (4): a zeta--Gamma mixed differential relation need not itself be invariant.

The arithmetic collision input is already formalized, axiom-clean, as
`RHTower.primeLog_indep_mod_one` in
`GaloisForLFunctions.RHTower.WeldCircleFaithfulness`.  It proves the stronger statement that an
arbitrary finite integer combination of all prime logarithms cannot be an integer unless every
coefficient and the integer vanish.  The all-jet orbit obstruction is formalized, axiom-clean, as
`simplePoleDifferentialOperatorFamily_ne_finiteOrbitCoboundary` in
`GaloisForLFunctions.PrincipalPartDifferentialOperator`: a finite family of simple poles on distinct
translation orbits retains a nonzero orbit residue after any nonzero constant-coefficient
differential operator.  The Hardouin--Singer dependence criterion and the differential-rational
fixed-field argument remain imported paper steps rather than Lean theorems.

## What is already known and why it does not close (4)

1. Classical Hilbert--Ostrowski hypertranscendence proves zeta is DT over `C(s)`.  This gives
   nonflatness only over the ordinary DA gauge field.
2. The proved single-Gamma and finite archimedean-product value laws give

   ```text
   zeta is DT over C(s)<G>
   ```

   for one Gamma function or one finite positive commensurable Gamma product `G`.  The field (2)
   contains the individual translates as separate generators and is much larger than the field of
   their product.
3. Wang--Yao's 2023 theorem closes the derivative-free Gamma-shift layer.  Hardouin--Singer's
   multiplicative dependence criterion, plus the orbitwise discrete-residue calculation above,
   closes every Gamma jet layer and computes the translation fixed field.  Neither theorem includes
   zeta jets.
4. Wang--Yao's 2024 differential theorem settles Markus's conjecture for the single pair
   `Gamma,zeta`; Lue's 2025 theorem extends that single shift-equation solution mechanism.  The
   accessible statements do not assert independence of zeta jets from an arbitrary finite family
   of nonintegral Gamma-shift jets.  A proof of (4) requires precisely that finite-family statement,
   because any putative differential relation uses only finitely many generators.
5. Chiang--Feng, Chen--Wang, and Lue prove further algebraic difference-independence results over
   small-growth coefficient fields or for distinguished polynomial shapes.  None of the located
   statements has the full family (15) below.

No inference from a single-Gamma theorem to (4) is valid without this finite-family upgrade.

## Exact value-relation ledger

The established relations are:

1. distinct prime-log Gamma translates have no algebraic relation over `C(s)`;
2. all jets of distinct prime-log Gamma translates have no algebraic relation over the larger
   one-periodic meromorphic base `F(s)`;
3. every nonzero prime-log translation fixes exactly the constant values inside the generated
   differential field;
4. distinct Gamma Laurent monomials have distinct finite signed-moment, hence complete Stirling,
   value signatures.

For the fourth relation, enumerate a finite set of distinct shifts as
`lambda_1,...,lambda_r`.  For an exponent vector `e in Z^r`, define its signed moments

```text
M_k(e)=sum_(i=1)^r e_i lambda_i^k,   0<=k<r.               (14)
```

> **Finite Stirling-signature separation.** The map
> `e |-> (M_0(e),...,M_(r-1)(e))` is injective.  Consequently two distinct Laurent monomials
> `product_i Gamma(s+lambda_i)^(e_i)` have distinct complete formal Stirling signatures.

The first statement is exactly Vandermonde invertibility: the `lambda_i` are distinct, so a signed
weight vector annihilating the first `r` powers is zero.  In the logarithmic Stirling expansion,
the coefficient at successive inverse powers of `s` is a Bernoulli polynomial in `lambda_i`, whose
top term is `lambda_i^k`; the triangular Bernoulli-to-power change of coordinates therefore turns
equality of complete signatures into equality of the moments (14). `QED`

The Vandermonde core is already formalized in mathlib as
`Matrix.eq_zero_of_forall_pow_sum_mul_pow_eq_zero`; the repository's `PowerSumTorelli` supplies the
equivalent unsigned multiset formulation.

The sole unclassified relation in this card is the following finite value question.  For every
finite `E subset Lambda` and jet orders `m,n`, determine the algebraic relations of

```text
{zeta^(j)(s):0<=j<=m}
 union {Gamma^(k)(s+lambda):lambda in E,0<=k<=n}            (15)
```

over `C(s)`.  Absence of every relation nontrivial in the zeta jets, for all `E,m,n`, is equivalent
to (4).  If a published theorem already classifies exactly (15), the statement is `[SEARCH]`;
otherwise it remains `[OPEN CORE]`.  No auxiliary construction is part of this question.

## RH-facing significance

The two outcomes identify complementary positive value laws.  If the curvature is absorbed,
the translation-saturated archimedean differential field already contains the two-prime zeta
cocycle relation.  If the curvature survives, zeta has a prime-relative gauge class beyond
every finite or countably translation-generated archimedean completion.  The latter outcome gives
a canonical separation of the two HT sources:

```text
archimedean Gamma translation field  !=  prime cocycle curvature of zeta.  (16)
```

That still would not prove RH.  The same relative value relation may occur for many Euler or
Dirichlet sources.  Equation (4) classifies only whether the zeta value
is differentially algebraic over the stated archimedean value field.
