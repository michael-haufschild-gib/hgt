# One density-zero family of actual exchanges detecting every Mertens power

- **Stage** 1-in-progress
- **Target** A single family of disjoint genuine exchanges whose moved integers have natural density zero and whose signed moving prefix has exactly the full set of positive Mertens power exponents, including its endpoint.

Choose disjoint successive intervals of support. In stage \(j\) take one pair \(p\leftrightarrow w=qr\) of relative displacement at most \(1/j\), with all three primes above \(j\). For \(A<c\le B\) coprime to \(Q=pqr\), exchange \(cp\) and \(cw\). The signed prefix of this stage is the difference, up to sign, of \(M_Q\) evaluated at \(\max(A,\min(B,\lfloor X/p\rfloor))\) and \(\max(A,\min(B,\lfloor X/w\rfloor))\). It vanishes before the first endpoint and after the last. At a given cutoff only one stage can contribute.

**Uniform upper bound.** At any fixed \(\theta>0\), deleting three primes costs at most \((1-2^{-\theta})^{-3}\) in the all-cutoff power-bound norm. This follows one prime at a time by the floor-dilation recurrence at the unscaled cutoff. Thus every power bound for \(M\) passes to the moving prefix uniformly across all stages, irrespective of the selected primes or interval lengths.

**Lower witnesses.** Take a nondecreasing sequence cofinal among positive exponents where \(M\) has no power bound, repeating the maximal failure exponent if it exists. The corpus theorem `mertens_omega_pm_sqrt` ensures failure already at exponent one quarter, so the family has actual nontrivial exchanges at infinitely many stages. At stage \(j\) first choose a cutoff beyond the previous support where \(M\) exceeds \(jX^{\theta_j}\) in magnitude. Start the stage above that cutoff. For the selected pair, \((D_p-D_w)M_Q\) fails at \(\theta_j\) by sharp dilation inversion. Take a witness exceeding \(jX^{\theta_j}\) beyond \(\max(p,w)A\), and then take \(B\) large enough to retain both quotient cutoffs at this witness. The fixed prefix equals \(M\) in each gap. Thus both the moving prefix and its fixed complement retain every failure as well as every bound.

**Density.** In the occupied part of stage \(j\), the number of possible moved integers through \(X\) is at most \((1/p+1/w)X\). Start stage \(j\) beyond \((j+2)\) times the preceding upper endpoint. At cutoff \(X\), take the latest stage that has started. All its predecessors together contain at most \(X/(j+2)\) integers. The current stage contributes at most \(2X/j\), which gives natural density zero. The displacement is \(o(n)\). The signed moving prefix is exactly the sum on those of its lower endpoints whose partners still cross the sharp cutoff.

Such marked exchanges can also be imposed on the known density-one involution. Replace each old pair meeting a marked endpoint by the prescribed pair and fix the displaced partners. The old map moves integers by \(o(n)\), so the displaced partners of a density-zero population still have density zero. This would embed the same signed detector in one globally dense matching while preserving its sublinear unpaid count and displacement.
