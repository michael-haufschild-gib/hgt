# PTR passive multimode loss-flux threshold

- **Stage:** arbitrary-spatial-Hilbert flux theorem Lean-checked; distributional and source-specialization bridges open.
- **Chain:** rotating-cylinder Maxwell source → infinite `(grade, spatial mode)` boundary map → prime conormal eigen-distribution → independent confinement → RH.
- **Corpus/source basis:** `PhotonicConormalFiniteEnergyNoGo` rules out only `ℓ²` conormal output; `PhotonicMultimodeGeometricFloquetRigidity` rules out fixed spatial profile, not moving profiles. [Mai--Yang--Wang, arXiv:2609.08748v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08748v1), Eqs. (1)--(5), uses real travelling-wave modulation plus strictly positive material loss `ε''` and a full `(n,σ)` Floquet expansion.
- **Proved abstract result:** `ptr_passive_multimode_tempered_loss_overlap_zero` in `PhotonicPassiveMultimodeFluxNoGo.lean` handles an arbitrary complex spatial Hilbert space and arbitrary grade-dependent spatial profiles, with no spatial-mode truncation. For the real-frequency three-operator recurrence, Hermitian static/spatial forms, adjoint neighboring couplings, positive loss, a local coupling/loss form bound, and polynomial growth of cumulative **spatial-Hilbert loss mass**, every grade has zero loss overlap. The stronger `ptr_factorized_multimode_tempered_loss_zero` derives both adjointness and the exact local bound from `G=B*B`, `M₊=aB*UB`, `M₋=aB*U*B` for any contraction `U` and any Hilbert spaces; it concludes `Bcₙ=0` at every grade. This is strictly stronger than the corpus's fixed-profile and finite-energy no-gos.
- **Literal material overlap now proved:** `PhotonicRotatingCylinderMaterialOverlap.lean` constructs the measurable cylinder cutoff `B` and unit-modulus angular multiplier `U` on the full spatial `L²`, with `B*=B`, `U*=U₋`, and almost-everywhere identities `B*UB f=1_C e^{imθ}f`, `B*U*B f=1_C e^{-imθ}f`. Its two `ptrCylinder*OverlapIntegral` theorems give the exact unrestricted spatial overlap integrals underlying the paper's Eq. (4), with no finite mode basis or truncation. This discharges the helical-overlap/adjointness layer. It still does not derive Maxwell's differential/interface equation or the trace topology.
- **Still conjectural:** the exact all-grade rotating-cylinder Maxwell recurrence and its interface law satisfy those form identities; and a PTR conormal **boundary distribution** enforces polynomial growth of interior loss mass. The latter does not follow formally from distributionality: escape to arbitrarily high spatial modes can hide large Hilbert norm. Thus the theorem does **not** yet retire the full PTR route. It also does not exclude gain-balanced/complex-frequency/forced realizations.
- **Why trace growth is genuinely missing:** on a space-time torus, Fourier data `cₙ(x)=2ⁿe^{i2^{2n}x}` for `n≥0` are polynomially bounded in joint frequency `(n,k)` (hence define a finite-order distribution), while `‖cₙ‖₂=2ⁿ`. Adding a low-spatial-mode time Dirac comb preserves a nonzero conormal port trace. This is not a Maxwell solution; it refutes only the inference “distributional port ⇒ polynomial interior Hilbert mass” without a PDE estimate.
- **Falsifier:** a polynomial-growth all-grade solution with nonzero lossy-region norm satisfying that exact recurrence and its interface conditions.
- **Proof mechanism:** pair the grade-`n` recurrence with its state, divide by real nonzero `(ω+nΩ)²`, and take imaginary parts. The exact balance is `γ gₙ = Jₙ₊₁ − Jₙ`; the local form bound controls `|Jₙ|` by adjacent loss masses. Cumulative positive loss must then grow exponentially every two grades unless zero, contradicting polynomial mass growth. This proof is closed in Lean for arbitrary spatial Hilbert space. Next hard step: derive the source's all-grade modal/interface law and a distribution-to-loss-mass estimate, or construct an ultraviolet-escape counterexample.
- **Physical-scope warning:** the cited paper computes with a finite Floquet truncation and writes a constant `+iε''` loss term. Its extension to all positive and negative sidebands is not a causal broadband constitutive law. The [Lean causal-loss audit](../5-failed/ptr-uniform-bilateral-complex-loss-causal-no-go.md) now proves the precise failure: real-response Schwarz symmetry forbids uniformly positive imaginary permittivity at all signed frequencies; the corpus's conductive replacement has `ν²χ=ν²ε−iσν/ε₀` and an exact *bulk modulation-work* term in its all-mode current. Thus the theorem here concerns the literal extrapolated model, not a passive dispersive Maxwell material or open scattering with radiation flux.
- **Positive survivor if proved:** a prime conormal landing needs gain/loss balance, nonreal Floquet parameter, non-tempered sidebands, or a zero-overlap port. The boundary flux itself is the exact obstruction, not finite-energy failure.
- **Angular-only subroute closed:** [joint-tempered helical no-go](../5-failed/ptr-angular-only-helical-distributional.md) proves in Lean that moving OAM does not save a source-free angular model: `q=ℓ−mn` separates every polynomial-growth Fourier sector. The actual radial/interface and square-lattice terms remain outside this theorem; they are now the decisive tests.
