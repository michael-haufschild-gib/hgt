# Common prime factors of the unreduced square-generator prefix

Write $q_j=U_j/V_j$ in lowest terms. Determine the leading order of $\log\gcd(\prod_{j\le N}U_j,\prod_{j\le N}V_j)$ and of both reduced factors of $\prod_{j\le N}q_j$. The conjecture is a strictly positive quartic leading term in each case. The gcd records actual cancellation between distinct conductors. [Unweighted prefix](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheSignedProductOverSquareConductors.lean)

Let $W(t)=2H(t)-H(t-\log4)$ and $f(r,y)=2r^2W(\log(y/r^2))/\sqrt y$ for $0<r\le1$ and $y>0$, extended by zero at the axes. The prime profile of $q_j$ at $p$ equals $Nf(j/N,p/N^2)$. Summing the absolute valuation errors costs $O(N^3)$ in log mass. Put $h(s)=2s^2W(-2\log s)$ on $s>0$, extended by zero. The function $h$ is compactly supported and Lipschitz. Since $f(r,y)=\sqrt y\,h(r/\sqrt y)$ for $y>0$, one Lipschitz constant in $r$ works for all $y$. The upper-endpoint Riemann sum costs $O(1/N)$ uniformly. Chebyshev’s bound then gives total primewise log error $O(N^3)$ for the prefix valuation profile $N^2g(p/N^2)$. [Valuation error](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/ThePrimeExponentsOnTheSquareDiagonal.lean)

Put $g(y)=\int_0^1 f(r,y)\,dr$ and $v_+=\max(v,0)$. After division by $N^4$, the predicted reduced numerator and denominator log limits are $\int g_+$ and $\int(-g)_+$. The predicted gcd log limit is $\int[\int_0^1 f(r,y)_+\,dr-g(y)_+]\,dy$. For $0<y<e^{-8}$ the root interval meets both strict signs of $W$, giving strict cancellation. Here $g(y)=y\int h=\tfrac{15}{8}y\int H(u)e^{-3u/2}\,du$ exactly, so the predicted cumulative valuation of every prime $p<e^{-8}N^2$ is a fixed positive constant times $p$. The remaining ACS estimate asks for the signed difference of the reduced logs to be $O(N^3)$ at every endpoint. [Dilated prime measure](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/DilatingTheOrdinaryPrimeMeasure.lean)

For a direct lower bound on individual inner valuations put $B_j=j^2H(\log(j^2/p))$. Past the short lags the integrated exponent is exactly $(2/\sqrt p)[2B_j-(j/(2j+1))^2 B_{2j+1}]$. After the root cutoff $C\sqrt p$, the negative indices inject into the positive indices, giving a lower bound $(2/\sqrt p)\sum B_j$. On $\sqrt p\le j\le2\sqrt p$, $B_j\gg p$ by strict positivity of $H$ on $[0,\log4]$. Only $O(\sqrt p)$ roots can contribute to the floor and mesh error by exact [mesh support](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/OrdinaryPrimeSectionModuli.lean). This reduces exclusion of all sufficiently large inner denominator primes to the individual row bound and finite-sum injection.
