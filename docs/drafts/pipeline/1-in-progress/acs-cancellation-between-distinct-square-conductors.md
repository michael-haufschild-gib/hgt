# Common prime factors of the unreduced square-generator prefix

Write $q_j=U_j/V_j$ in lowest terms. Determine the leading order of $\log\gcd(\prod_{j\le N}U_j,\prod_{j\le N}V_j)$ and of both reduced factors of $\prod_{j\le N}q_j$. The conjecture is a strictly positive quartic leading term in each case. The gcd records actual cancellation between distinct conductors. [Unweighted prefix](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheSignedProductOverSquareConductors.lean)

Let $W(t)=2H(t)-H(t-\log4)$ and $f(r,y)=2r^2W(\log(y/r^2))/\sqrt y$ for $0<r\le1$ and $y>0$, extended by zero at the axes. The prime profile of $q_j$ at $p$ equals $Nf(j/N,p/N^2)$. Summing the absolute valuation errors costs $O(N^3)$ in log mass. On the support, $r\asymp\sqrt y$, so $f$ extends continuously to the compact rectangle $[0,1]\times[0,4e^8]$. Upper-endpoint Riemann sums in $r$ therefore converge uniformly in $y$. [Valuation error](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/ThePrimeExponentsOnTheSquareDiagonal.lean)

Put $g(y)=\int_0^1 f(r,y)\,dr$ and $v_+=\max(v,0)$. After division by $N^4$, the predicted reduced numerator and denominator log limits are $\int g_+$ and $\int(-g)_+$. The predicted gcd log limit is $\int[\int_0^1 f(r,y)_+\,dr-g(y)_+]\,dy$. For $0<y<e^{-8}$, the root interval meets both strict signs of $W$, giving strict cancellation. The remaining ACS estimate asks for the signed difference of the reduced logs to be $O(N^3)$ at every endpoint.
