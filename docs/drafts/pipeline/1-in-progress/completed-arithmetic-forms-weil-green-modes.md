# Arithmetic modes of the actual complete Weil inverse

For the tilted unit spline at fixed overlap $c>0$, let $A_N$ be its complete
Weil matrix, $r_{m,N}$ its logarithmically normalized Ramanujan vector, and
$E_m(c)>0$ its completed conductor energy. The actual source satisfies
$$\|A_Nr_{m,N}-E_m(c)r_{m,N}\|\longrightarrow0,$$
and one positive floor controls $A_N$ for large $N$.

For a positive matrix $A\succeq eI$, $e,\lambda>0$,
$$\|A^{-1}v-\lambda^{-1}v\|
\le(e\lambda)^{-1}\|Av-\lambda v\|.$$
Indeed $A(A^{-1}v-\lambda^{-1}v)=-\lambda^{-1}(Av-\lambda v)$;
coercivity and Cauchy–Schwarz give $e\|w\|\le\|Aw\|$.

The corresponding source target is
$$\|A_N^{-1}r_{m,N}-E_m(c)^{-1}r_{m,N}\|\longrightarrow0,$$
$$\langle A_N^{-1}r_{m,N},r_{l,N}\rangle
\longrightarrow\mathbf1_{m=l}E_m(c)^{-1}.$$
Every fixed finite conductor packet then has the exact limiting dual form
$$\left\langle A_N^{-1}\sum_m b_mr_{m,N},\sum_m d_mr_{m,N}\right\rangle
\longrightarrow\sum_m\frac{b_md_m}{E_m(c)}.$$
The normalization includes the pole, prime, Gamma, successor-shell and mixed
terms of the original matrix. Conductors, packet support and overlap are
fixed in these limits; no uniform growing-conductor assertion follows.
