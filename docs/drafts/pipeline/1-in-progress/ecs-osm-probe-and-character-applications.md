# Five applications of probe and character calculus

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CANDIDATE

## Statements

1. Every nonzero real polynomial filter of the critical state's tail moments
   has polynomial-square tests of both signs. Its positivity cone is exactly
   the almost-everywhere sign cone of the filtered state density.
2. Every Chebyshev order of the finite probe signal has an exact norm-loss
   identity, with equality precisely when the reflection walk returns to the
   probe span.
3. The actual Weil probe matrix splits into positive Gram matrices of the two
   reflection sectors; its quadratic form lies between the negative and
   positive ordinary probe Gram forms.
4. Cubic unit rotation admits three character projectors and a complete
   polynomial calculus. The actual Weil-phase pairing selects one eigenvalue.
5. The conjugate Weil phase determines the unique minimum-norm class vector
   with a prescribed signed pairing; the residual has zero pairing and an
   exact orthogonal energy decomposition.

## Proof mechanisms

Bounded-coordinate sign detection and finite polynomial root sets;
orthogonal projection and norm-preserving zero reflection; finite cyclic
character algebra; projection onto the line spanned by the actual phase.
All arithmetic objects are those already defined in ECS and OSM.
