# Arithmetic logarithmic derivative of factorial entropy

For a positive rational $q$ put $d(q)=\sum_p v_p(q)/p$. This is its arithmetic derivative divided by $q$. Its reduced denominator is exactly
$$\prod_{p\nmid v_p(q)}p.$$
Indeed after multiplication by the product of all primes in the support, reduction modulo one such $p$ leaves only its own valuation times a unit. For every sufficiently large factorial horizon, the exact entropy group therefore consists of the $E$ with $d(E)\in\mathbb Z$. Partial fractions identify the quotient of all positive rationals by the entropy group with the subgroup of $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ consisting of squarefree-denominator classes. An exact half-price presentation of $q$ exists precisely when $q=r^2$ with $r>0$ rational and $d(r)\in\mathbb Z$.

On the square diagonal the denominator of $d(q_n)$ contains every prime in the negative outer window. Its logarithm has order $n^2$: the lower bound is the outer-window mass, and every denominator prime lies below $O(n^2)$, where Chebyshev bounds their total logarithmic mass. Multiplying by arbitrary factorial entropies preserves this denominator exactly.
