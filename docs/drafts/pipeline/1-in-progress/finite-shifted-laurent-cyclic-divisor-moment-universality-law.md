## Theorem generated by the sources

For distinct nonzero nodes `lam : Fin r → ℂ`, every consecutive length-`r`
integer Laurent-moment window

`q = b, b + 1, ..., b + r - 1`

separates arbitrary complex weights. If a permutation `σ` rotates the nodes by
a primitive `d`th root `ζ`, its pushforward fixes a weight vector exactly when
all moments in any such window whose exponents lie in nontrivial characters
modulo `d` vanish.

This is not obtained by predicting a target. It is the closure of:

1. ordinary Vandermonde separation of the first `r` natural moments;
2. invertible diagonal rescaling by `lam i ^ b` on nonzero nodes;
3. cyclic covariance at integer exponents;
4. primitive-root character detection by integer divisibility.

## Scope and non-patching check

- The same Laurent-moment map occurs in both directions.
- The shifted-window separator is required to recover equality of pushed and
  original weights.
- The cyclic covariance and primitive-root character law are required to turn
  fixedness into precisely the vanishing conditions.
- The nonzero-node hypothesis is sharp for negative moment orders and for
  invertibility of the diagonal shift.
- No disjoint case split or unrelated theorem conjunction is used.

## Novelty status

Post-proof searches covered finite-support moment reconstruction, consecutive
Laurent moments, Prony/Vandermonde methods, cyclic invariant measures, and
root-of-unity character vanishing.

Closest sources:

- Kunis--Peter--Roemer--von der Ohe, *A multivariate generalization of Prony's
  method*, arXiv:1506.00450, reconstructs finitely supported complex measures
  from moment data.
- Meyer--Nadareishvili, *A universal coefficient theorem for actions of finite
  groups on C*-algebras*, arXiv:2406.11787, uses cyclotomic idempotents and
  cyclic character decomposition.

No source found states the exact theorem proved here: arbitrary complex weights
on fixed distinct nonzero nodes, separation by every consecutive length-`r`
integer Laurent window, and equivalence between cyclic pushforward fixedness and
nontrivial-character vanishing inside any one such window.

Novelty claim remains deliberately narrow: joint packaging above, not
Vandermonde inversion, Prony reconstruction, Laurent moments, or cyclic
character theory individually.
