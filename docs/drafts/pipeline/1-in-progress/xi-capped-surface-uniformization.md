## Immediate deduction: exclude bounded analytic embeddings

Let G:C→M be the checked actual analytic lift, and suppose u:M→C is analytic, injective and has bounded image. Then u∘G is a bounded entire function, hence constant by the corpus Liouville theorem. Injectivity makes G constant, so Xi=p∘G is constant. This contradicts an actual Xi zero and xi(0)=1/2. Thus the selected surface admits no bounded injective analytic coordinate. In particular a disk uniformization is impossible. This deduction is complete on paper; formalization is next. It does not construct a plane uniformization or prove the plane/disk dichotomy.
