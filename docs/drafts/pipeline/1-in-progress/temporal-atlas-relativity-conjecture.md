## Public conjecture

> **There is no spacetime shared in advance. Each observer generates a time;
> spacetime is the atlas describing how those times can agree.**

More personal:

> **Your clock does not measure a universal line already surrounding you. It
> traces one path through the changes available to you. Other observers
> trace different paths, and the lawful overlap of all those times is what
> appears as a common world.**

The strongest short form is:

> **Space is how observers' times fit together.**

## Proof-blind truth-hunch

The primitive physical object is a complete operational process \(P\):
allowed interventions, outcomes, compositions and their probabilities. It
does not initially include a preferred clock, Hamiltonian, subsystem
factorization, metric or spacetime manifold.

Each observer \(\mathcal O\), at each finite observational resource
\(r\), has an operational temporal quotient
\[
  \mathbb T_{\mathcal O,r}(P),
\]
obtained by identifying clock labels that no protocol available at resource
\(r\) can distinguish and then completing the resulting history image.
Resource refinement supplies comparison maps. The observer's physical time
is not one terminal space but the complete pro-object
\[
  \mathbf T_{\mathcal O}(P)
  =
  \{\mathbb T_{\mathcal O,r}(P)\}_{r\in\mathcal R_{\mathcal O}} .
                                                        \tag{1}
\]

Observer restriction supplies canonical maps between these pro-times.
The total assignment
\[
  \mathcal O\longmapsto\mathbf T_{\mathcal O}(P)         \tag{2}
\]
is the **temporal atlas** of \(P\).

The conjecture is that the causal-recurrence spacetime of a process is the
canonical gluing geometry of `(2)`. No single observer time is spacetime.
Spacetime is the compatibility law of all observer times.

This is frozen before choosing whether the exact realization uses
pro-compacta, locales, sheaves, stacks, enriched categories, operator
systems, process tensors or a new construction. Those are possible proof
architectures, not part of the conjecture.

Conventional kinematics begins with

The reversal is analogous in logical shape to replacing gravitational force

The compatibility structure of observer times is the proposed background.

## What the atlas must recover

The conjecture concerns causal-recurrence geometry, not every physical
datum.

1. **Observer time.** Every observer has a canonical resource-indexed
   temporal pro-object satisfying a universal representation property.
2. **Shared time.** Common temporal distinctions are the maximal common
   quotient of observer pro-times. Joint distinctions are their minimal
   compatible refinement.
3. **Temporal dimension.** Dimension is a resource-dependent rank or
   covering spectrum, not one globally fixed integer.
4. **Space.** Spatial separation is the obstruction to identifying or
   synchronizing observer temporal quotients; adjacency is first failure of
   temporal factorization.
5. **Causal order.** Direction is the order in which temporal distinctions
   can be transported through allowed interventions.
6. **Recurrence.** Recurrent behavior is the compact group stratum of a
   temporal chart.
7. **Irreversibility.** The arrow of time is the noninvertible semigroup
   stratum and the failure of temporal comparison maps to reverse.
8. **Horizons.** A horizon is directional failure of temporal descent:
   distinctions present on one side have no faithful image in the other
   observer's atlas.
9. **Infinite systems.** Infinite and continuous-spectrum time are encoded
   by the pro-system itself; no fictitious finite terminal rank is assigned.
10. **Matter fibers.** Static probabilities and other non-geometric process
    data live as fibers over the atlas. The atlas is not asserted to
    reconstruct all physics.

The final clause is essential. A prior conjecture was falsified because a
temporal-character quotient cannot distinguish static devices with
different probabilities. Temporal geometry may be a nontrivial quotient of
physics without being a complete invariant of physics.

## Running dimension of time

For a metrized operational history map \(\Phi_{\mathcal O}\), define the
finite-horizon covering profile
\[
  N_{\mathcal O}(\epsilon,T)
  =
  N_\epsilon\!\left(
    \Phi_{\mathcal O}([0,T])
  \right).                                           \tag{3}
\]
One candidate scale-dependent temporal dimension is
\[
  D_{\mathcal O}(\alpha)
  =
  \limsup_{\epsilon\downarrow0}
  \frac{
    \log N_{\mathcal O}(\epsilon,\epsilon^{-\alpha})
  }{
    \log(1/\epsilon)
  }.                                                 \tag{4}
\]

Equation `(4)` is a realization candidate, not a definition imposed on the
conjecture. Its role is to make the proposed jump falsifiable:

- at bounded horizons a regular clock image is curve-like;
- increasing horizon can resolve independent temporal phases;
- exact completion dimension is the terminal arithmetic invariant;
- exceptional Diophantine spectra can produce different dimension-growth
  profiles even when exact terminal rank agrees; and
- open dynamics can add an irreversible approach stratum to a recurrent
  future boundary.

Thus the number of visible time dimensions should **run with observational
scale**. “Time has dimension \(d\)” is predicted to be an endpoint statement,
not a complete physical description.

For finite closed dynamics, observer-visible gaps span a rational vector
space \(\Gamma_{\mathcal O}\). The current finite theorem shadow says:

1. exact observer-time dimension is
   \(\dim_{\mathbb Q}\Gamma_{\mathcal O}\);
2. every positive admissible exact dimension occurs densely;
3. exact dimension is nowhere continuous once two gap coordinates exist;
4. bounded finite-horizon histories remain operator-norm stable;
5. joint and shared observer dimensions obey

     d(1\vee2)+d(1\wedge2)=d(1)+d(2).                \tag{5}

These results cannot be geometry of ordinary open strata. They instead look

## New predictions

These are conjectures, not manuscript claims.

1. **Temporal renormalization.** Every finite operational process has a
   canonical dimension flow from clock-curve dimension to exact completion
   dimension as horizon and precision increase.
2. **Arithmetic universality classes.** Finite systems with equal exact
   temporal rank can have inequivalent finite-resource dimension flows,
   classified by simultaneous approximation exponents.
3. **Observer polymatroid.** For every finite observer family, joint visible
   temporal dimensions form a monotone submodular rank function. The
   two-observer conservation law `(5)` is its modular linear shadow.
4. **Open-time boundary.** Every finite contractive quantum evolution has a
   temporal chart with a transient one-way interior and a compact recurrent
   boundary determined by its peripheral modes.
5. **Horizon discriminator.** A weak link and a true one-way horizon differ
   in the inverse system: every finite weak link eventually becomes
   faithful under refinement, while a horizon leaves a permanent
   nontrivial kernel.
6. **Common-world criterion.** A family of observers admits one common
   effective spacetime chart exactly when its finite temporal comparisons
   satisfy an effective descent condition.
7. **Black-hole information criterion.** Information is operationally lost
   to an exterior observer exactly when a nontrivial temporal distinction
   remains in the interior atlas but maps to zero at every exterior
   resource level. Approximate thermality alone neither proves nor disproves
   this condition.
8. **Infinite-time completion.** Continuous-spectrum and thermodynamic
   systems are distinguished by growth and obstruction invariants of the
   temporal pro-object, not forced into finite torus rank.

## Why this is not the failed asymptotic-boundary conjecture

The failed boundary conjecture demanded ordinary open strata on which exact
temporal dimension was locally constant. Dense rational-rank
interpenetration disproves that demand.

The temporal atlas instead keeps two layers:

1. stable finite-resource quotients; and
2. an exact arithmetic completion reached only by the full inverse system.

No finite fan or manifold-with-corners is claimed to carry exact recurrence
rank.

## Why this is not the failed future-factorization conjecture

The failed factorization conjecture claimed that temporal data reconstruct
the complete physical process. Static binary devices disprove this.

The temporal atlas claims only to reconstruct causal-recurrence geometry.
Matter probabilities and other non-geometric information are retained as
fibers. It does not hide complete process tomography inside the word
“geometry.”

## First antagonistic priority attack — 2026-07-30

Two proposed ingredients have direct prior-art parents.

1. Guérin and Brukner, *Observer-dependent locality of quantum events*
   (arXiv:1805.12429), associate causal reference frames and
   observer-dependent time descriptions to events, impose global
   consistency and prove equivalence with pure process matrices. Therefore
   observer-dependent time plus compatibility of local descriptions is not
   new.
2. Anikushin, *Dimension Theory Approach to the Complexity of Almost
   Periodic Trajectories* (arXiv:1710.03052), studies Diophantine dimension,
   inclusion-length growth, quasiperiodic trajectories and the Liouville
   phenomenon. Earlier work cited there already relates orbit dimension to
   frequency count and recurrent dimensions to Diophantine approximation.
   Therefore a horizon-dependent dimension profile for a quasiperiodic
   trajectory is not new by itself.
3. Function-algebra hulls, process tensors, quantum reference frames,
   sheaf/topos approaches to causal sets and detector-based spacetime
   reconstruction occupy further pieces of the proposed architecture.

The conjecture is not killed, because the search did not locate the exact
joined law: a resource-indexed operational temporal atlas with observer
rank conservation, arithmetic completion instability, open-system
group/semigroup boundary and a permanent-kernel horizon discriminator.
This is not a novelty clearance.
