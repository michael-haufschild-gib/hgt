# Exceptional frequency ratios for two top ordinates

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Question:** For two top ordinates, can failure of positive CCM balance accumulate at a positive frequency ratio?
- **Conjecture:** At fixed depth, first ordinate and positive masses, only finitely many ratios on each compact subinterval of (0,∞) can fail.

Away from coalescence, the two Rayleigh extrema vary continuously with both nodes and both phases. Some unrestricted phase has positive balance, by exclusion of opposite eigenvalue pairs and half-turn antisymmetry. At a reference ratio p/q, advance the aperture in steps fixing the first phase. After q steps the second phase advances by 2πq times the change of ratio. For sufficiently small nonzero change it enters the positive phase interval. At irrational ratios the phase orbit is dense; near ratio one the concentration theorem applies.
