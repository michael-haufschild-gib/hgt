## 1. General cyclic-radical theorem

Let `H` be a topological vector space, let `(U_t)` be a family of linear maps, and let `k in H`.
Write

```text
Cyc(U,k) = closure span{U_t k:t in R}=H.                 (1)
```

If a closed linear subspace `N` contains every `U_t k`, then `(1)` gives `N=H`.  Equivalently:

> If `k` is cyclic and `N` is a proper linear subspace containing its complete orbit, then `N`
> is not closed in the ambient topology.                              (2)

The exact abstract statement `(2)` is proved in
`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CyclicRadicalCollapse.lean`, including both family and orbit
forms.  It uses no zeta, zero, positivity, or spectral hypothesis.

## 2. The theta source is `L²`-cyclic `[PROVED ON PAPER AND IN LEAN]`

Let

```text
k(x)=2 VarTheta_00(i exp(2x)).                            (3)
```

The current formal corpus proves that `k` is a nonzero Schwartz function and, in the normalized
Fourier convention,

```text
Fourier(k)(xi)=Xi(xi).                                    (4)
```

The real zero set of `Xi` is countable, hence Lebesgue-null.  Therefore

```text
Fourier(k)(xi)!=0 for almost every xi.                    (5)
```

The `L²` Wiener cyclicity theorem says that the closed span of the translations of a function
`f in L²(R)` is all of `L²(R)` exactly when its Fourier transform is nonzero almost everywhere.
Equations `(4)`--`(5)` therefore give

```text
closure span{k(·+t):t in R}=L²(R).                      (6)
```

For completeness, the direct proof of the nontrivial direction is short.  Under Plancherel,
translations become multiplication by the characters `exp(2 pi i t xi)`.  If `h` is orthogonal
to every translated source, then the finite complex measure

```text
dnu(xi)=h(xi)conj(Xi(xi))dxi
```

has identically zero Fourier transform.  Uniqueness of Fourier transforms of finite measures gives
`nu=0`; `(5)` gives `h=0`.  Thus the orthogonal complement of the orbit span is zero, proving `(6)`.

## 5. Attacks and boundary

1. This is not an RH proof.  It forces the topology of a successful metric but does not construct
   the singular conservative rigging.
2. It does not exclude Hedenmalm's proposal.  It proves that the proposed form cannot be an ordinary
   `L²` completion; a rigged or divisor-sampling Hilbert space remains viable.
3. Cyclicity uses only that `Xi` is nonzero almost everywhere, not RH.  Off-line zeros do not affect
   the proof because the real Fourier zero set remains countable unconditionally.
4. The abstract cyclic-radical theorem is routine.  Scientific content, if any, lies only in its
   exact application to the newly proposed theta pair and in the resulting topology classification.
5. The result cannot orient the Bezoutian: it specifies where orientation must live, namely in a
   topology discontinuous from ordinary `L²`.
