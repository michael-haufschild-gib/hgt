## Proof

`[PROVED HERE]` Bundle each sign orientation as a bounded prime source with norm bound one, then take its quotient
class modulo finite support.  For a finite family, a linear combination of quotient classes
vanishes exactly when the corresponding coefficient stream has finite support.  This is precisely
`PrimeOrientationTailIndependent`; hence finite tail independence is equivalent to ordinary
linear independence of the quotient classes.

Apply finite character to the full Boolean-sequence family.  Every finite set of indices is
represented by an injective finite subtype family, so sparse-prefix tail independence makes the
whole continuum family linearly independent.  Its cardinality gives the module-rank lower bound.

For each analytic aperture, source linear independence plus disjointness of the frame span from
the all-jet realization kernel implies independence of the images.  Conversely, independence of
the images forces that disjointness.  This yields an iff and identifies the exact remaining
analytic faithfulness obstruction without assuming injectivity of the entire quotient map.

No numerical computation is used.

## Post-proof literature audit

Performed only after exact Lean source check passed.

- Piotr Koszmider and Niels Jakob Laustsen, *A Banach space induced by an almost disjoint family,
  admitting only few operators and decompositions*
  ([arXiv:2003.03832](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.03832)), studies subspaces of `ℓ∞` generated by
  `c₀` and almost-disjoint characteristic functions.  It does not construct the present signed
  prime-tail continuum modulo finite support or its analytic Euler all-jet kernel criterion.
- David Chodounský, Vera Fischer, and Jan Grebík, *Free sequences in P(ω)/fin*
  ([arXiv:1808.05930](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05930)), studies Boolean-algebra free sequences
  modulo finite sets, not complex linear independence in a bounded coefficient quotient followed
  by analytic Dirichlet realization.
- Sanoli Gun, Neelam Kandhil, and Patrice Philippon, *On linear independence of Dirichlet L
  values* ([arXiv:2212.00366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00366)), proves arithmetic linear
  independence for special values of character `L`-functions, not a continuum family of source
  classes or a span/kernel iff for all jets.
- Wei Chen and Qiong Wang, *Algebraic differential independence concerning the Euler Γ-function
  and Dirichlet series*
  ([DOI:10.1007/s10473-020-0411-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10473-020-0411-3)), concerns
  differential independence between Gamma and classes of Dirichlet series, without quotienting
  coefficient streams by finite support or computing source rank.

Searches combining bounded sequence quotients, finite-support gauges, sparse Boolean prime
orientations, continuum linear independence, and analytic Dirichlet all-jet realization found
adjacent Boolean/Banach and Dirichlet-independence subjects, not the theorem's
tail-independence → continuum-rank quotient frame → exact analytic kernel criterion.  Door 2
remains open rather than claimed closed.
