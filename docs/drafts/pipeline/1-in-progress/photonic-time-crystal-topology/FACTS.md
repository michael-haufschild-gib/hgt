- **Physical object.** A photonic time crystal has externally periodic electromagnetic coefficients. Its temporal circle and quasifrequency circle are dual objects. Neither is the complex plane of a Mellin exponent. [Quantum PTC account, §III.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30850v1#S3.SS1).
- **Monodromy.** In the ideal homogeneous, lossless two-mode model, one-period transport has form `M=[[a,b],[conj b,conj a]]`, with `|a|²-|b|²=1`. Its eigenvalues are reciprocal. Unimodular multipliers describe oscillatory modes; momentum gaps contain growing/decaying modes. Same source, equations 18–21.
- **Band topology.** The temporal Zak phase is Berry holonomy over the quasifrequency Brillouin circle; temporal inversion quantizes it in the binary model. It is not the argument of one Floquet multiplier. [Lustig–Sharabi–Segev](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.08731), equation 5.
- **Interface localization.** Temporal interface states have tails decaying toward both temporal ends and lie in bulk momentum gaps. **Route consequence:** their constituent bulk multipliers are not neutral; identifying the RH line with neutral bulk transport while identifying zeros with these interface modes conflates spectral variables. Same paper, temporal-interface construction.
- **Quantum geometry.** Projective mode evolution acts on the Poincaré disk by Möbius transformations. Stable/unstable/critical orbit types and geometric phase are distinguished there. **Route consequence:** this geometry must not be silently replaced by a modular quotient with an inserted arithmetic lattice. [Eswaran–Kopaei–Sacha](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17317).
- **Cover counting.** The circle's connected finite covers, up to isomorphism over the circle, have one class of each positive degree. Counting these classes with weight `degree^(-s)` gives the Riemann zeta series for `Re s>1`. This is established cyclic subgroup-zeta mathematics, not new photonic physics. [Cimetta–Lucchini](https://www.research.unipd.it/retrieve/e14fb269-2fb8-3de1-e053-1705fe0ac030/perfect.pdf).
- **Measure choice matters.** The degree-`n` circle cover has `n` deck transformations. Counting cover groupoids with inverse-automorphism weight instead gives `ζ(s+1)`. **Route consequence:** optics must determine the counting/normalization convention before a numerical critical line can be claimed native.
- **Holonomy-sensitive count.** A degree-weighted cover observable retaining scalar holonomy `h` is `Σ h^n/n^s=Li_s(h)`. At `h=1` it gives `ζ(s)`; at `h=-1` it gives `-(1-2^(1-s))ζ(s)`. The identities are classical. The construction below specifies a Laplace–Mellin response realizing the parabolic case; its status as a preferred physical observable remains unproved. [NIST polylogarithm definition](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.12.E10).
- **Two holonomies.** Berry holonomy on the band bundle and dynamical holonomy around a physical time cell are distinct. **Route consequence:** winding either circle gives a different candidate observable; their identification is an additional theorem, not notation.

The Fourier-dual lattice explanation uses Gaussian/heat and Mellin structure in addition to circle topology. **Route consequence:** putting `s=1/2+ik` into a transfer matrix does not derive this structure. [NIST reflection formulas](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4).
