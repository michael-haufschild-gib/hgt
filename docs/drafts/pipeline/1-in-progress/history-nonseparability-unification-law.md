### Transported-split no-go `[PAPER PROOF; NOT YET LEAN]`

A dynamics cannot choose the subsystem split relative to which it is declared nonseparable. Let
\(\mathcal H=\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B\) be any supplied finite tensor factorization and let
\(U\) be any unitary dynamics. Transport the input local algebras to

\[
\mathcal A'_A=U\big(\operatorname{End}(\mathcal H_A)\otimes I\big)U^\dagger,\qquad
\mathcal A'_B=U\big(I\otimes\operatorname{End}(\mathcal H_B)\big)U^\dagger.
\]

The two output algebras still commute, generate the full matrix algebra, and are isomorphic to the
two input local algebras. Relative to these transported input/output subsystem structures, \(U\)
is local by construction. Therefore every unitary can be made non-entangling if its own dynamics
is allowed to select the output split. Any non-vacuous dynamical nonseparability invariant needs
independently supplied operational ports, local algebras, or intervention classes.

This obstruction is consistent with the observable-induced tensor-product structures of

In gravity the missing datum cannot generally be replaced by ordinary local commuting algebras:

## Exact internal-group recognition bridge

[Krasnov's pure-spinor characterisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05088) gives an exact

1. \(\Psi_1\) and \(\Psi_2\) are orthogonal;
2. every linear combination of them is pure;
3. \(\Psi_1\) is fixed and the line \([\Psi_2]\) is fixed.

The first two conditions give commuting orthogonal complex structures whose product splits
\(\mathbb R^{10}=\mathbb R^6\oplus\mathbb R^4\), equivalently

\[

\cong \frac{SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)}{\mathbb Z_6},
