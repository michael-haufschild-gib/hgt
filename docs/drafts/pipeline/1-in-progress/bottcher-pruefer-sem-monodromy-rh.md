## Why the detour is mathematically nonempty

The Böttcher coefficient object is not a generic supply of roots of unity.  Its exact Prüfer tower
is the period group of `d log phi_c`, generated by harmonic measures of clopen symbolic cylinders,
and survives every finite cover.  The Xi integer is not target-labelled either: it is the winding
of the actual completed finite member.  The open problem is whether their external product is
selected by a common constraint, not whether either endpoint exists.

2. `[CONJECTURE]` A morphism from the integral SEM divisor-jet boundary into the character dual of

The second is the smallest precise construction.  Naturality under the two squaring maps must
determine the pairing uniquely; otherwise the coupling is arbitrary.

## Firewalls and positive residues

### An arbitrary external product proves nothing

One can always define `zeta_n^m` after reading `m`.  That is the formal consumer already recorded.
The source construction must produce the pairing before expanding the Xi divisor and must identify
its SEM value by a comparison theorem.

**Positive residue:** the missing object is a natural transformation between two explicit dyadic
towers, with a prescribed value on their canonical generators.

### Böttcher monodromy is nontrivial, not self-annihilating

The exact Prüfer theorem deliberately proves nontrivial monodromy of every large dyadic order.  No
Böttcher-only theorem can trivialize the product.

**Positive residue:** any cancellation must be a mixed Xi--Böttcher reciprocity law, and its mixed
term is the only potential new science in this route.

### This is not a new proof of the dyadic ladder

After the coupling is supplied, triviality at all depths is algebraically equivalent to infinite
dyadic divisibility of the local order.  Calling the existing RH ladder a Böttcher character does
not advance RH.

**Positive residue:** the route survives only if the mixed determinant line has an independent
source null-homotopy unavailable in the Xi Kummer tower alone.

### Critical-line zeros must not be killed

The local consumer excludes any zero to which it is applied.  Therefore the source coupling must
be oriented to the right-half SEM boundary before reflection completion; a global coupling to the
entire Xi divisor would incorrectly demand zero multiplicity on the critical line too.
