# Full square sections in every late logarithmic window

Does every late window $|2\log n-T|\le C\log(T+2)$ contain a conductor with $Q(D_n)\ge\delta>0$ for a common $\delta$? This would give at least $2\lfloor e^{\gamma(n^2+m^2)}\rfloor+1$ original sections for every positive multiplicity on one conductor of each window. Under RH every sufficiently late conductor has a positive reserve. Off RH, filter the two long trace lags simultaneously by the positive theta deformation retaining one right zero and its quartet. Their finite transform has multiplier $2-4^z$, nonzero on every retained mode with $\Re z<1/2$. A finite conjugate spectrum without real frequencies has relatively dense positive excursions at its greatest real exponent. The filter has logarithmic moment bounds, so these excursions force the unfiltered long trace to exceed any fixed constant in each late logarithmic window.
