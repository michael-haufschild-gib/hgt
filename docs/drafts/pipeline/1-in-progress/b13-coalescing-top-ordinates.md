# Parity at a collision of top ordinates

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Question:** B13-J6 asks whether two distinct, nearly coincident top ordinates with an odd relation can have nonpositive CCM balance at every aperture.
- **Conjecture:** At fixed depth and positive height, one neighborhood of the height works for all finite splittings of the ordinate and all positive multiplicities.

At an aperture of strict singleton balance, compare the quadratic forms on the common real unit sphere in half-line L². A frequency displacement changes the exponential by at most the displacement times the damped first moment. Summing with normalized positive masses preserves the same bound independently of the number of atoms. The spectral gap persists under splitting even though the coefficient Gram matrix becomes singular at collision.

The general finite-stratum implication and the arithmetic ground-state parity remain open.
