## Goal

Replace the finite-range hypothesis in the existing valuation--Veronese theorem by the intrinsic
condition

```text
{p : prime | x(p) is not carried by an infinite profile fibre} is finite.       (CR)
```

Unlike finite range, `(CR)` permits infinitely many recurrent profile values and hence
positive-dimensional projective constraint geometry. The intended master invariant is no longer
a finite point set but the graded homogeneous coordinate algebra of the recurrent profile locus.

The theorem is about prime-supported hypertranscendental systems in general. RH is only a
downstream pressure test.

## Master theorem

Let `sigma` be finite, let

```text
x : Primes -> C^sigma,
R_x = {v | x^(-1)(v) is infinite},
```

and assume `(CR)`. For every degree `m`, let `I_x(m)` be the homogeneous polynomials of degree
`m` vanishing on `R_x`, and let

```text
H_x(m) = dim_C Sym^m(C^sigma) / I_x(m).
```

For a homogeneous observable `Q` of degree `m`, place the prime column `p |-> Q(x(p))` in the
`p^m` arithmetic valuation layer, with any finite palette of exponent shifts having pairwise
distinct real parts.

The proposed theorem has five parts. Parts 1--3 are now unconditionally formalized. Part 4 is
formalized at the level of the complete labelled homogeneous relation layers but still needs the
projective-Zariski reconstruction interface. Part 5 remains `[CONJECTURE]`.

1. **Cofinal recurrent kernel `[PROVED]`.** The degree-`m` column is supported on only finitely many primes
   exactly when `Q` lies in `I_x(m)`.
2. **Valuation--Hilbert rank `[PROVED]`.** A basis of `Sym^m/I_x(m)` gives a differentially independent
   basis of the degree-`m` carrier field. Across any finite set of valuation degrees, the exact
   differential transcendence degree is the shift-weighted sum of the numbers `H_x(m)`.
3. **Complete relations `[PROVED]`.** Every differential relation among all selected monomial carriers is
   generated by prolonging the homogeneous coordinate reductions, with finite-prime base
   corrections. There are no additional nonlinear relations and no relations mixing valuation
   degrees.
4. **Projective reconstruction `[OPEN INTERFACE]`.** Equality of the labelled homogeneous relation layers is
   equivalent to equality of the homogeneous vanishing ideals and therefore of the projective
   Zariski closures of the recurrent profile loci.
5. **Realization `[CONJECTURE]`.** Every complex projective algebraic set admitting a normalized affine chart is
   realized by such a cofinally recurrent prime profile. Consequently its Hilbert function and
   reduced homogeneous coordinate algebra occur as exact invariants of a prime-supported
   hypertranscendental differential field.

The positive law is:

```text
nonlinear source constraints must reappear as linear relations in matching prime-valuation
degree, while differential algebra adds no cross-degree constraints of its own.
```

## Why this is stronger than the finite theorem

The existing formal theorem treats a finite recurrent point set. It therefore has an eventually
constant Hilbert function and cannot carry positive-dimensional algebraic geometry. `(CR)` keeps
the fibrewise logarithmic rigidity needed for hypertranscendence while allowing an infinite
recurrent locus. The resulting valuation-rank spectrum can have genuine Hilbert-polynomial
growth and can encode dimension, degree, and the full reduced homogeneous ideal.

Finite-range tomography is the zero-dimensional special case. The ordinary valuation-profile
matroid is its degree-one shadow.

## Proof architecture

1. Prove the cofinal recurrent observable kernel by observing that a function vanishing on
   `R_x` can be nonzero only on the finite nonrecurring locus.
2. Show that a homogeneous evaluation basis on `R_x` has recurring coefficient patterns spanning
   its full coefficient space. This invokes the already formalized fibrewise shift--clock
   rigidity directly and does not require finite range.
3. Generalize the valuation-layer basis theorem from finite coefficient range to recurring-pattern
   spanning.
4. Reuse prime-power block separation and differential pivot elimination to obtain the exact rank
   and complete relation ideal.
5. Define the homogeneous closure of `R_x` from all vanishing layers and prove reconstruction.
6. Construct a cofinally recurrent prime coloring of a countable Euclidean-dense subset of a
   complex projective algebraic set, with every selected point assigned an infinite prime fibre.

## Antagonistic attacks

1. **Generic algebraic-geometry attack.** Hilbert functions, homogeneous coordinate rings, and
   projective reconstruction are classical. The candidate novelty is only their exact realization
   as complete differential relation systems of prime-power arithmetic carriers.
2. **Generic differential-field attack.** Any free differential field can be decorated with a
   chosen algebraic variety. That does not prove this theorem. The result survives only if the
   arithmetic valuation degree itself realizes polynomial degree and the embedded carrier's full
   differential relation ideal is computed.
3. **Clock-encoding counterexample.** Tail independence alone is insufficient: a coefficient can
   encode `log p` and collide with a derivative. `(CR)` is designed to prevent this by supplying
   infinite constant-profile fibres on which exponential-polynomial uniqueness separates every
   jet.
4. **Scheme attack.** Point evaluations reconstruct only a reduced Zariski closure. Nilpotent
   scheme structure is not claimed.
5. **Analytic-transfer attack.** The formal arithmetic-function field is not automatically a field
   of analytic Dirichlet germs. Analytic realization is a separate theorem unless proved.
6. **Priority attack.** No novelty claim is permitted until primary literature on arithmetic
   function fields, differential Hopf algebras, universal differential-field realizations, and
   Hilbert-series reconstruction has been checked.

## RH pressure test

For zeta, the scalar prime profile has one-dimensional homogeneous quotient in every positive
valuation degree. The theorem would therefore force any RH-bearing prime-side constraint to live
in an infinite completion or in a coupling to the archimedean reflection data: every finite
valuation window is already exhausted by its projective coordinate relations and contains no
hidden cross-degree differential law.

This is a downstream architecture constraint, not an RH theorem.
