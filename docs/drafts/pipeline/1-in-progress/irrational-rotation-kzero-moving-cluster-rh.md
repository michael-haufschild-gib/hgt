## Claim

The moving root cluster need not be forced into a fixed finite-dimensional Weyl representation and
its determinant holonomy need not be algebraic.  It may instead remain a finite projective module
over the noncommutative torus generated by the exact prime--Weyl pair.

For a prime `p` and positive integer `q`, put

```text
theta_(p,q) = log(p)/(2*pi*q).                                  (1)
```

The rational-imaginary source relation is

```text
T_(I/q) M_p = exp(-2*pi*i*theta_(p,q)) M_p T_(I/q).             (2)
```

Lean proves that `theta_(p,q)` is irrational.  The proof is stronger than a logarithmic
independence argument: if `theta_(p,q)` were rational, the multiplier in `(2)` would be a root of
unity and therefore algebraic, whereas Gelfond--Schneider makes it transcendental.

For the irrational rotation algebra `A_theta`, the canonical trace on a `K_0` coordinate
`(r,c) in Z^2` has the standard form

```text
tau_*(r,c) = r + theta*c,                                      (3)
```

while the second coordinate `c` is the Chern pairing.  Therefore

```text
tau_*(r,c) is an integer  ->  c=0.                             (4)
```

The proposed source functor sends a finite moving right-half root cluster to a projective
`A_theta`-module `E_W` such that

```text
tau_*[E_W] = analytic cluster rank d_W in Z,
chern[E_W] = off-line incidence charge C_W.                   (5)
```

If `C_W` is the positive local order, or the already formalized quadratic free-orbit charge, then
`(4)` forces `C_W=0`.  Exhausting right-half windows proves RH.

## Why this is not the fixed-fiber Weyl route

A non-root-of-unity Weyl pair has no nonzero finite-dimensional representation: determinants would
force a nontrivial power of the scalar multiplier to equal one.  That kills any proposal which
compresses both generators to endomorphisms of one fixed finite hole space.

Irrational rotation algebras do have nontrivial finite projective modules.  They are the natural
home for moving fibers with nontrivial curvature.  The route therefore accepts exactly what killed
the fixed-fiber shortcut.  It extracts the obstruction from the trace/Chern pairing rather than
from a finite determinant.

## Positive statement from the fixed-fiber and AF no-gos

The fixed-fiber no-go does not say that a moving prime--Weyl cluster is impossible.  It says that
the correct carrier must be projective over the crossed product.  The finite-prime AF no-nucleation
theorem then says a regular tensor-product bulk cannot create a new divisor charge.  Combining the
two gives a positive classification:

> a nonzero off-line charge must occur as the Chern coordinate of a source-defined crossed-product
> projective module or as a singular boundary index; it cannot be padding in a finite code or a
> charge nucleated in an AF norm limit.

For the irrational prime parameter, an integer analytic trace annihilates the first possibility by
`(4)`.  A surviving construction must therefore violate the trace/rank comparison or move into the
singular boundary-index category.  This is a useful bifurcation even if Open Core C fails.

## Attacks and firewalls

1. **The `K_0` trace range is parent theory.** Equation `(3)` is standard and contributes no new
   science.  The proof-bearing content is the source functor and both comparisons in `(5)`.
2. **An arbitrary projective module proves nothing.** Irrational rotation algebras contain many
   classes.  `E_W` must be constructed functorially from the completed prime--Gamma moving cluster.
3. **Trace is not automatically analytic rank.** The canonical noncommutative trace can be
   irrational.  Open Core B is precisely the theorem forcing it to equal the integer root census.
4. **Chern is not automatically multiplicity.** Assigning `c=d_W` after reading a zero is the formal
   consumer and is RH-equivalent.  Open Core C must be a local index theorem from a source
   projection or Toeplitz boundary map.
5. **Total divisor charge is reflection-blind.** The Chern pairing must be relative or
   stabilizer-corrected so critical-line fixed clusters contribute zero and right-half free clusters
   contribute positively.
6. **The moving action may be only a groupoid action.** A groupoid `C*`-algebra or Morita-equivalent
   crossed product is allowed, but the trace and Chern pairing must survive the equivalence with
   the same normalization.
7. **The scalar cannot be gauged away.** Gauge changes conjugate the projective module data; they do
   not change the trace/Chern pairing.  Passing only to an unframed principal ideal loses the class.
8. **No external tensor padding.** Adjoining a standard Heisenberg module by hand manufactures a
   Chern class unrelated to Xi.  The projection must arise from the actual moving SEM complex.
9. **No AF regular-limit shortcut.** Existing no-nucleation results force any regular finite-prime
   class to have a finite ancestor.  The required class must come from the crossed-product clock or
   a singular completion, with that category derived from the source.
10. **Known critical zeros are a decisive control.** Any supposedly universal module that assigns
    positive Chern charge to every root cluster is false.  The right-half-relative polarization is
    load-bearing.
