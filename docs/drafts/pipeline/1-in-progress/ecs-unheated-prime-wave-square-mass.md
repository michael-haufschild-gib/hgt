# Square mass of the unheated prime wave

For $\kappa=\Theta-1/2$ and $d>\kappa$, seek $e^{-dx}F^+\in L^2(0,\infty)$ for the actual critical state.

The [reflected logarithmic moments](../3-formalized/ecs-reflected-logarithmic-moments.md) give square integrability of $N(1/2+c-it)\mathcal LF(-c+it)$ for $\kappa<c\le1$. The truncated prime tail has square-integrable trace for $\operatorname{Re}p>-4$: on $u<-\ell$ its tilt satisfies $e^{2s(u+\ell)}\le e^{-8(u+\ell)}$.

For $\kappa<c<1/2$, assemble the reflected Euler, divided difference, Lévy head, prime tail and polar terms on $\operatorname{Re}p=-c$. Divide the continued transform by $(1+\varepsilon w)^2$ in the reflected half-plane and apply the Poisson bound. The Hardy datum recovers $e^{-cx}F^+$ by Laplace uniqueness after damping both data to $c=1$. Lean verification of this last argument is pending.

State exclusion still requires independent one-sided cancellation for the ordinary primes after polar subtraction.
