# Square mass of the unheated prime wave

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Source:** ECS programme, unheated mean-square growth of the profile beyond the wall.

For $\kappa=\Theta-1/2$ and every $d>\kappa$, seek $\int_0^\infty |F^+(x)|^2e^{-2dx}\,dx<\infty$ for the actual critical state. The missing frequency estimate is square integrability of $\mathcal LF(-d+it)\,\zeta'/\zeta(1/2+d-it)$ up to the zero-free boundary.

On $0\le d\le1$, put $G(u)=e^{-du}F(u)$. At step $h=k^2$ the difference $D(u)=F(u)-e^{-dh}F(u+h)$ has square mass $O(h)$ and bounded confinement mass. On the negative half-line $k e^{-2du}\le 1+k^2e^{-8u}$. Hence $\int|G(u)-G(u+h)|^2\,du=O(k)$. Plancherel at $h=4^{-(n+1)}$ bounds the Fourier mass on $4^n\le|t|\le4^{n+1}$ by $O(2^{-n})$. This pays every fixed power of the logarithm in the reflected Euler multiplier. Recovering the time datum requires a Hardy or contour-shift argument. The stage-five arithmetic bound requires subexponential growth for both signs or for one sign alone.
