## 1. Object and reduction target

Let

```text

lambda_j>0,  m_j in Z\{0}.                                 (1)
```

Put

```text
Psi=G'/G,
Tau=Psi'
   =sum_j m_j lambda_j^2 psi'(lambda_j s+mu_j).            (2)
```

Remove every relation generated by:

1. permutation of factors;
2. integer Gamma recurrence on the same affine lattice;
3. Gauss multiplication after rational rescaling of slopes;
4. exact cancellation of the resulting signed factors.

Call the remaining datum **multiplication-reduced**. For commensurable slopes, the signed orbit-charge
card proves that a nonempty reduced datum makes `Tau`, `Psi`, and `G` hypertranscendental. This card
asks for the positive carrier replacing the common-shift orbit residue when the multiplicative group
generated by the `lambda_j` has rank greater than one.

## 3. Candidate mechanisms

### A. Multi-shift parameterized Galois group

`[CONJECTURE]` Treat the factors as a module for the commuting shifts

```text
rho_j:s|->s+1/lambda_j.                                    (6)
```

The first factor is rationally multiplied under `rho_1`, while every other factor is translated by
`lambda_k/lambda_1`. A parameterized Galois theorem for the generated shift group should identify
the differential character lattice with the multiplication relations removed in Section 1.

**Attack.** Existing one-operator rational-or-HT theorems do not apply: under `rho_j`, the other
Gamma factors are not governed by rational multipliers. A genuine several-operator theorem or a
descent to a common difference field is required.

### B. Borel singular-lattice character

`[CONJECTURE]` The Borel transform of `(2)` has singular lattices scaled by the `lambda_j`. A
multiplication-reduced combination has a nonzero signed lattice character. Differential algebraicity
should force this character into a finitely generated convolution closure incompatible with an
incommensurable primitive lattice.

**Attack.** Nonlinear differential equations become convolution equations after Borel transform;
sums of incommensurable lattices can generate a dense additive subgroup. Density alone is not a
contradiction and may enlarge rather than obstruct the allowed singular support. The missing object
must be a signed alien residue or quotient character, not the bare singular set.

### C. Nevanlinna-small coefficient isolation

`[SEARCH]` Bank--Kaufman prove Gamma hypertranscendental over the field of meromorphic functions of
Nevanlinna characteristic `o(r)`. One could try to isolate a Gamma factor and treat the remaining
product as a coefficient.

**Attack.** Every other affine Gamma factor has characteristic of order `r log r`, comparable to the
chosen factor. It is not a small coefficient. Existing small-function theorems therefore do not
directly prove `(2)`.
