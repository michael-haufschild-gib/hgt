and demand that every event have a greatest causal slice containing every other antichain through
that event. Then such greatest slices exist exactly when augmented incomparability

\[
x\mathrel{\sim}y \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \neg(x<y)\ \wedge\ \neg(y<x)

linearly ordered: the causal order is a strict weak order, an ordinal stack of simultaneity layers.
