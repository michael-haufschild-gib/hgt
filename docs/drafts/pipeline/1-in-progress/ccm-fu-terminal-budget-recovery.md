remaining prime terminal. Its singleton has absolute value at most
(3/5) J sqrt(M). Thus the remaining square energy is at most
(9/25) J² M times the number of primes below M, uniformly in the processed
core. Prime sparsity makes this o(M²).

then fit any fixed positive quadratic budget at sufficiently large cutoff.

Proof obligations: formal terminal estimate, quantitative head repair,
actual window transfer for arbitrary enlarged cores, and a uniform
asymptotic producer including the compulsory set. No experiment is needed:
these claims follow deductively from existing source bounds.

does not prepare that front from the initial state, renew exhausted
sources, or bound the final signed Abel sum. Existing terminal ordering
retains that final sum as an endpoint obligation.
