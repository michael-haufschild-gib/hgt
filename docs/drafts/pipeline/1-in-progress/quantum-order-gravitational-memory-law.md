## Matched-source construction routes

### Route A — symmetry-degenerate control

`[LEADING CANDIDATE] [NOT YET A CLOSED PHYSICAL CONSTRUCTION]`

Use a protected degenerate internal sector and two noncommuting controls \(A,B\) related by an
exact internal symmetry under which the complete stress tensor is a singlet. Arrange equal pulse
envelopes, gaps, durations, and control-field invariants for

\[
  ABA^{-1}B^{-1}
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  BAB^{-1}A^{-1}.
\]

Prepare the protected sector maximally mixed. Both return channels then agree on the actual state
while remaining different channels on counterfactual inputs. A holonomic implementation is
kinematically plausible because non-Abelian gates can act within a degenerate eigenspace
([Zanardi--Rasetti](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9904011)); geometric trapped-ion controls give
a concrete experimental family ([Duan--Cirac--Zoller](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0111086)).

Missing step: the apparatus, control fields, emitted radiation, and their stress-tensor
correlations must be included. Equality of pulse energy or \(\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle\) is
insufficient; the two protocols must induce the same complete influence functional for gravity.

### Route B — topological logical holonomy

`[BACKUP] [LOCAL INDISTINGUISHABILITY INSUFFICIENT]`

Use a topologically protected degenerate code space, where local observables cannot distinguish
logical states, and implement different non-Abelian logical holonomies by adiabatic code
deformation. Such holonomic computation with a maintained gap exists in explicit topological-code
models ([Cesare et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2690)).

Failure pressure: braid or code-deformation world histories generally differ in their control and
momentum flow. Local indistinguishability of code states does not imply equality of the complete
gravitational source history.

### Route C — quantum-switch process witness

`[BACKUP] [ORDINARY IMPLEMENTATIONS DO NOT SUPERPOSE SPACETIME ORDER]`

Photonic quantum switches realize nonseparable operation order while making the operations
indistinguishable by spatial or temporal location
([Goswami et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04302)). This is useful for certifying a process
difference rather than a state difference.

Failure pressure: ordinary switches can be embedded in a fixed causal spacetime and do not by
themselves superpose spacetime causal order; gravitational-switch proposals require superposed
gravitational fields ([Paunkovic--Vojinovic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.09682)). They therefore do
not supply a matched-gravity null experiment without further construction.
