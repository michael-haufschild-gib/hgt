## Rank-one mechanism

For `g=[[a,b],[c,d]]∈SL₂(F)`, division by whichever of `c,d` has larger
valuation produces an integral determinant-one elimination matrix.
The upper factor has lower diagonal value `max(v(c),v(d))`.
Integral matrices and their inverses preserve this maximum. These rank-one
statements passed direct Lean and transitive axiom audits in
[`ValuedSL2Iwasawa`](../3-formalized/valued-rank-one-iwasawa-has-an-exact-torus-value.md).
Applying the two actual simple embeddings and vertex generation gives
`G=K₀B`, where `K₀` is generated by the integral simple groups.
Both Iwasawa conventions and actual integral–central–finite-torus–unipotent
factors passed direct Lean and transitive axiom audits in `ValuedLoopIwasawa`.

## Completed integral subgroup

`K₀` has Laurent-polynomial projections. The completed adelic subgroup
must also contain coefficient-integral positive power-series unipotents.
Define `P₀(O)` from the positive-loop splitting over `SL₂(O[[X]])` and
central valuation units; define `P₁(O)` by the adjacent-lattice shift.
The required common intersection is the literal integral Borel `B(O)`.

The shift lifts to the actual symbol extension; direct Lean and transitive
axiom audits passed in `SymbolDiagonalRescaling`. For diagonal rescaling `R_r`,
let `D_r(g)=T(r,d(g))` on the triangular chart and `1` otherwise.
The tame Kubota cocycle satisfies
`σ(R_rg,R_rh)/σ(g,h)=D_r(gh)/(D_r(g)D_r(h))` by its five Bruhat cases.
The exact shifted-positive splitting formula and its agreement with the
actual affine simple splitting also passed direct Lean and transitive axiom
audits in `ShiftedPositiveSplitting`. The standard integral vertex and both
unit-valued Borel coordinates passed the same audits in `IntegralPositiveVertex`.
For `r=X⁻¹`, the shifted positive splitting differs on the common Iwahori by
central residue `d(0)`, a valuation unit for an integral matrix. The common
vertex–Borel intersection and both unit-valued torus coordinates passed
direct Lean and transitive axiom audits in `IntegralAdjacentVertex`.

Reduced alternating vertex words have a unique nonzero extreme Laurent
coefficient: lower-left order zero at one vertex, upper-right order minus
one at the other. The full completed matrix-word obstruction passed direct Lean and transitive
axiom audits in `FullVertexMatrixWords`. Together with the integral vertex
intersections, it gives `⟨P₀(O),P₁(O)⟩∩B=B(O)`.
