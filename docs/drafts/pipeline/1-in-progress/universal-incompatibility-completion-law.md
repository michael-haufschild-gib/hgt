## Known-parent audit

### Constructor theory

Constructor theory already formulates fundamental laws through possible and impossible physical
transformations, and its theory of time explicitly avoids putting time into fundamental law.
Therefore neither “impossibility is fundamental” nor “time is derived from timeless
counterfactuals” is a novelty claim.

Missing from constructor theory, relative to `(UIC1)`--`(UIC4)`, is a theorem that the complete
incompatibility object uniquely generates a dagger ternary amplitude space, reciprocal
quantum/gravity coefficient algebras, their calibrated spectrum, and Standard Model plus
Lorentzian factorization.

### Quantum combs and process theories

Quantum combs, memory channels, and categorical process theories compose supplied admissible
quantum maps.  They explain higher-order processes and causal structure after positivity and
system interfaces are given.  They do not derive both interfaces, their positive cones, and
spacetime from a source incompatibility nerve.

### TRO/Morita/linking theory

Ternary rings of operators already generate the two coefficient algebras and linking algebra in
`(UIC2)`.  Reciprocal spectra are standard singular-value/supersymmetric mathematics.  This entire
middle layer is parent theory.

Missing is **source-generated positive bar-to-ternary rigidity**: existence, uniqueness,
calibration, exact bar recovery, and non-terminating composition from \(\mathcal I_{\rm src}\).

### Noncommutative geometry

Spectral action models can recover Standard Model fields coupled to gravity from a supplied
four-dimensional manifold, finite noncommutative space, representation, grading, real structure,
and Dirac data.  They do not derive this input package uniquely from incompatibility, and their
finite internal component cannot by itself provide the non-terminating source.

### Verdict

Each visible shadow has a strong parent.  Novelty can survive only in one rigidity theorem that
forces all shadows simultaneously.  A collage of constructor language, TROs, moment theory, and
spectral action fails the hostile-review test.
