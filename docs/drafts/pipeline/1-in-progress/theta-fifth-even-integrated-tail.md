`R_lead(q) ≥ E(q)L(q) + E(q)(3/(32q²)−3/(128q³))A`,

`1/5+3/(50q)+3/(500q²)−3/(5000q³)` and
`1/5+3/(50q)+3/(500q²)`; both endpoint matrices and the literal integral
identification are formalized.
