# Estimating the dyadic smooth-count difference

At each outer cutoff $X$, fix the single set of primes at most $y=\lfloor\sqrt X\rfloor$. Can its exponent vectors give the centered first-mean bound for $2S(n,y)-S(2n,y)$ independently of the prime-count error? Each $S$ counts nonnegative vectors in the simplex $\sum_{p\le y}v_p\log p\le\log n$. The required bound and its equivalence to RH are formalized. [Exact identity and criterion](../3-formalized/dyadic-differences-of-square-root-smooth-counts.md)

At $y^3\ge 2n$ the second inclusion–exclusion term counts distinct pairs $p,r>y$ for which $\lfloor 2n/(pr)\rfloor$ is odd.
