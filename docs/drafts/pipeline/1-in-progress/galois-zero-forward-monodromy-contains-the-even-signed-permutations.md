# The forward monodromy group contains the even signed permutations of the zeros

- **ID:** gtz-forward-monodromy-even-signed
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Source:** programme card `gtz-galois-theory-of-zeros-programme.md`, open core item 5 ("the full
  group: which restricted signed permutations lie in `G₊`"); review-1311 §2.19 question 1.
- **Statement:** for every forward time `b` off the branch times,
  (a) for zeros `p, q, r` of `H_b` in three distinct pairs, `(p q r)(−p −q −r) ∈ G₊(b)`;
  (b) for zeros `p, q` in two distinct pairs, `(p −p)(q −q) ∈ G₊(b)`.

## Goal

`G₊(b)` is a group of permutations of the zeros with finite support that commute with
`z ↦ −z`. Known: transitive, generated by the turns. The elements (a), (b) generate the
subgroup `W⁰` of the signed permutations that are even on the zeros and even on the pairs; it has
index `4` in the group `W` of all signed permutations with finite support. So `W⁰ ≤ G₊ ≤ W`, and
`G₊` is determined by two characters: the sign on the zeros (the monodromy of `√c`) and the sign
on the pairs.

## Notation

Base time `1/8`: zeros real, simple, `≠ 0`. Strip index `n(x) ∈ ℤ` of a zero `x`: the number of
zeros in `(0, x)` if `x > 0`, and `−1 −` the number of zeros in `(x, 0)` if `x < 0`;
`n(−x) = −1 − n(x)`. Pair coordinate `π(x) = μ(n(x))`, `μ(j) = j/2` for even `j`,
`μ(j) = −(j+1)/2` for odd `j`; `π(x) = π(x')` iff `x' = ±x`.

Signed cycle `τ(p,q,r) = (p q r)(−p −q −r)`; double flip `e(p,q) = (p −p)(q −q)`.

## Analytic input

**A1 (several zeros at once).** Let `γ` be a continuous path of forward times with ends off the
branch times, and `w_i`, `i` in a finite set, continuous lifts of zeros along `γ` through simple
zeros. Then one forward path with the same ends transports `w_i(0)` to `w_i(1)` for all `i`.

*Proof.* As for one zero. Property `𝒢(s)`: for every `ε > 0`, every time `τ'` near `γ(s)` off
the branch times is reached from `γ(0)` by one forward path that transports each `w_i(0)` to a
zero within `ε` of `w_i(s)`. Charts of the finitely many simple zeros `(γ(s₀), w_i(s₀))` over one
disc of times; two times of the disc off the branch times are joined inside the disc by an arc
off the branch times, and its lifts are the charts. So `𝒢` holds near every parameter at which it
holds once; it holds at `0`; at `s = 1` the zeros are isolated. ∎

**A2 (the strip is the index).** At a real saddle `σ` of a window `R` (one zero in each strip,
all zeros of the window in strips), let a lift along the real times from `1/8` to `τ(σ)` end in
the strip `j` of the window. Then it starts at the zero of index `n = j`.

*Proof.* `j ≥ 0`. Lifts keep sign and order, and transport is a bijection of the fibres. The
positive zeros below the end are in strips (window) with index `< j` (one zero per strip), one
in each. So exactly `j` positive zeros lie below the start. `j < 0`: negate. ∎

**A3 (window elements).** For every `K` there are `a, b ∈ G₊(1/8)` with: for every zero `x` with
`|π(x)| ≤ K`: `π(ax) = −π(x) − 1`, `π(bx) = −π(x)`.

*Proof.* Window `R` with the strips `|j| ≤ 2K + 3`, real saddle `σ` far out, signed loops
`a_ε` beyond it. For a strip `j` let `x_j` be the zero of index `j` at `1/8` and `W_j` the lift
from `x_j` along `C` = (real times to `τ(σ)`, real saddles, vertical segment to the base of the
loop); it ends at the zero `v_j` of the strip `j` at the base (A2, a zero stays in its strip).
For `(−1)^j = −ε` the lift along the loop from `v_j` ends in the strip `j − 1` (exchange), at
`v_{j−1}`. So `x_j` is followed through simple zeros along `C · loop · C⁻¹` to `x_{j−1}`, for all
such `j` of the window along the same path. By A1 one `g_ε ∈ G₊(1/8)` has `g_ε x_j = x_{j−1}` for
these `j`. Every pair of the window has a member with `(−1)^j = −ε` (`n(−x) = −1 − n(x)`), and
`g_ε` commutes with `z ↦ −z`. `ε = 1`: `j` odd, `μ(j) = −(j+1)/2 ↦ μ(j−1) = (j−1)/2
= −μ(j) − 1`. `ε = −1`: `j` even, `μ(j) = j/2 ↦ μ(j−1) = −j/2`. ∎

## Group theory

Setting: a set `Ω`, an involution `ν` without fixed point, `π : Ω → ℤ` with `π(ω) = π(ω')` iff
`ω' ∈ {ω, νω}`, a group `G` of permutations of `Ω` with
(H1) every `g ∈ G` commutes with `ν`; (H2) every `g ∈ G` moves finitely many points;
(H3) for every `K` there are `a, b ∈ G` with `π(aω) = −π(ω) − 1`, `π(bω) = −π(ω)` for
`|π(ω)| ≤ K`; (H4) some `g ∈ G` has `gω = νω` for some `ω`.

**Theorem.** `G` contains `τ(p,q,r)` for all `p, q, r` with distinct `π`, and `e(p,q)` for all
`p, q` with distinct `π`.

Every `g ∈ G` induces a map of pairs: `π(gω)` depends only on `π(ω)`.
`M(g) = {ω : π(gω) ≠ π(ω)}` is finite, stable under `g`, `g⁻¹`, `ν`.

**Step 1 (a signed cycle exists).** Let `g = a` of (H3), `K = 1`: `M(g) ≠ ∅`. Let `m₀ < m₁` be
the least and largest value of `π` on `M(g)`, `t = m₁ − m₀`. By (H3) for a large window,
`h = (ab)^t` has `π(hω) = π(ω) − t` on `M(g)`. Then `g' = hgh⁻¹` has `M(g') = hM(g)`, and
`M(g) ∩ M(g')` is the pair `π = m₀`. Let `q` be in it and `k = gg'g⁻¹g'⁻¹`. Following `q`, `gq`,
`g'q` through the four factors: `π(kq) = π(gq)`, `π(k·gq) = π(g'q)`, `π(k·g'q) = π(q)`, and
`π(kω) = π(ω)` for the other pairs. So `k³` and, off the three pairs, `k` preserve every pair:
`k⁶ = 1` on the three pairs and `k² = 1` off them. `T = k⁴` fixes every point off the three
pairs, permutes the three pairs cyclically and `T³ = 1`: `T = τ(q, Tq, T²q)`.

**Step 2 (triples of pairs).** `U` = set of triples `{i, j, k}` of distinct integers such that
`τ(p,q,r) ∈ G` for some `p, q, r` with `π = i, j, k`. Conjugation: `gτ(p,q,r)g⁻¹ =
τ(gp,gq,gr)`. Hence:
- (translation, reflection) `U` is stable under `m ↦ m ± 1` (`ab`, `ba`) and `m ↦ −m` (`b`);
- (exchange) if `A, B ∈ U`, `a ∈ A ∩ B`, `b ∈ B ∖ A`, then `A − a + b ∈ U`: conjugate by the
  cycle on `B`, oriented so that it carries `a` to `b` and, if the third point of `B` lies in
  `A`, read from the point of `A` it moves;
- (break) for some `L`: `{0, j, k} ∈ U`, `j, k ≥ L` ⇒ `{−1, j, k} ∈ U`: conjugate by `ab` of the
  window `0`; it carries the pair `0` to the pair `−1` and moves finitely many points.

Claim: `U` is the set of all triples.
1. (Euclid) `{0, α, α+β} ∈ U`, `α < β` ⇒ `{0, α, β} ∈ U`: exchange with the reflected triple
   `{0, β, α+β}` at `a = α+β`, `b = β`. With the reflection `(α, β) ↦ (β, α)` the gaps descend to
   `(d, d)`, `d = gcd(α, β)`.
2. (classes) if `{0, d, 2d} ∈ U` then every triple of one class mod `d` is in `U`:
   `{m, m+d, m+nd}` by induction on `n` (exchange with `{m+(n−1)d, m+nd, m+(n+1)d}`), then
   `{i, i+nd, k}` by induction on `n` (exchange with `{i+nd, i+(n+1)d, i}`).
3. Step 1 and 1. give `d`; by 2. `{0, j, j+d} ∈ U` for `j = Ld`; by (break) `{−1, j, j+d} ∈ U`,
   with gaps `(j+1, d)` of gcd `1`; by 1. `{0, 1, 2} ∈ U`; by 2. all triples.

**Step 3 (signs).** For every `p` some `g_p ∈ G` has `g_p p = νp`: conjugate (H4) by an element
that carries its pair to the pair of `p` (Step 2). Let `t, s` be pairs off the support of `g_p`
and `τ(p', t', s') ∈ G` with `p' ∈ {p, νp}` (Step 2). Conjugation by `g_p` gives
`τ(νp', t', s') ∈ G`, and `τ(p',t',s') τ(νp',t',s')⁻¹ = e(p,t')`. For `p, q` in distinct pairs
and `t` off the supports of `g_p`, `g_q`: `e(p,q) = e(p,t) e(q,t)`. Finally
`e(p,t) τ(p,q,r) e(p,t)⁻¹ = τ(νp,q,r)` for `t` off the three pairs, so all sign patterns of the
cycle on a triple of pairs lie in `G`. ∎

For `G₊(1/8)`: (H1) transports commute with `z ↦ −z`; (H2) finite support; (H3) is A3; (H4) the
sign flip at a centre time. Other base times: conjugation by the transport along a forward path,
which commutes with `z ↦ −z`.

## Remarks

- The windows alone cannot decide the two characters: the sign on the zeros is the parity of the
  order of `c` at the centre times enclosed by a loop, the sign on the pairs counts the exchanges.
- `G₊` acts on the pairs as a group that contains every 3-cycle: it is highly transitive on the
  pairs and primitive; the pairs are the only nontrivial system of imprimitivity on the zeros.
