# Mixed intersections of signed arithmetic correspondences

**Target.** Control the difference between the integral graph contact and its pole product after signed gluing on growing supports, including the real-place finite part.

**First question.** At separation `X = y²`, add the ordinary von Mangoldt contact to its Liouville twist. The odd prime-power exponents cancel. Does the remaining contact, without normalization, converge to twice the product of the unweighted masses of the two profiles? An exact pullback to the ordinary contact at scale `y` would identify the next term even when both original contacts have size `√X`.

**Uniformity.** For translated constituents the relevant separation is that of each pair after gluing. Recover the signed coefficient at each common displacement before bounding the tail. Determine which profiles can still contribute when the minimum separation fails to increase.

**Arithmetic question.** The twist-even combination suppresses ordinary primes. Control of the complementary signed intersection must retain them. A bound below RH should cover an increasing support and give its test norm explicitly.
