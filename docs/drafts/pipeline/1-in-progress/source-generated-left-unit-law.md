## What the law says

Let \(e\) be a primitive event specified only by complete counterfactual distinctions available
at its source. The law asserts that \(e\) determines, without a supplied target Hilbert space,
operator algebra, norm, dagger, tensor product, order unit, time parameter, or spacetime:

1. a future operational object \(B_e\);
2. a positive source-to-future passage \(X_e:A_e\rightsquigarrow B_e\);
3. a universal completion \(\lambda_e\) that acts as the missing left unit of that passage; and
4. the physical equivalence class under which all presentations of this triple agree.

The generated triple must be unique up to the law's intrinsic equivalence and initial among all
outcome-consistent continuations of \(e\).

The resulting physical-history structure is conjectured to be **right-unital but not globally
left-unital**:

- after an event, doing nothing is neutral;
- before an event, no source-independent “do nothing” passage preserves the event;
- the event itself generates the completion needed to make its future composable.

That asymmetry is not an arrow placed in background time. It is the proposed origin of time.
