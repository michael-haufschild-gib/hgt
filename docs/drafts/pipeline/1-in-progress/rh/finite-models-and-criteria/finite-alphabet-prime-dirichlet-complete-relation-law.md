- **Statement:** For a finite family of finite-range prime coefficient columns, every differential
  relation among any finite translate palette is generated by the affine differential-linear
  relations coming from finite-support tail relations; equivalently, a tail basis is a complete
  differential transcendence basis and the exact rank is the number of shifts times the tail
  dimension.

## Goal

Upgrade the independence iff theorem from a yes/no matroid classifier to a presentation theorem.
After choosing a basis of the source span modulo finite prime support, every remaining source is an
affine base-field linear combination of that basis at every shift and derivative order.  The
relation kernel should contain nothing beyond these displayed jetwise linear relations.

## Approach

1. Split a finite source index into a tail basis and dependent sources.
2. Express every dependent shifted jet as a base-field correction plus a constant linear
   combination of the basis jets.
3. Define the polynomial elimination map which fixes the basis jets and substitutes these affine
   expressions for the dependent jets.
4. Prove by polynomial induction that the kernel of elimination is generated by the displayed
   affine linear relations.
5. Use differential independence of the tail basis to identify the analytic evaluation kernel
   with the elimination kernel.
6. Read off the rectangular rank `card(A) * rho` and the nonrectangular sum of local ranks.

## Open core

The mathematical core is the exact kernel computation, not the already-proved independence iff.
The internal formal statement will use all jet-level generators; when the base correction is a
genuine differential orbit, these are precisely the successive derivatives of the order-zero
tail relations.
