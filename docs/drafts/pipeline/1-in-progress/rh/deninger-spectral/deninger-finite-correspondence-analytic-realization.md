# Finite-correspondence analytic realization

- **ID:** `deninger-finite-correspondence-analytic-realization`

- **Label:** `DAC_FINITE_CORRESPONDENCE_ANALYTIC_REALIZATION`

## Proved algebraic input

Let \(X_0\) be a regular arithmetic curve and
\(\operatorname{Cor}_{X_0}\) the standard Suslin--Voevodsky category:
objects are separated finite-type \(X_0\)-schemes and morphisms are
universally integral relative cycles finite over source components.

Deninger's 2025 results give:

1. on normal Noetherian affine \(X\),
   \[
     W_{\mathrm{rat}}(\mathcal O(X))
     \cong
     \underline{\operatorname{Corr}}_{X_0}(X,\mathbb A^1),          \tag{1}
   \]
   the represented transfer presheaf
   \(\mathbb Z_{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathbb A^1)/
     \mathbb Z_{\mathrm{tr}}(0)\);
2. on Dedekind rings, \(W_{\mathrm{rat}}\) is the finite-flat
   sheafification of the reduced multiplicative monoid algebra;
3. after the stated characteristic localization on normal schemes over
   a field, its \(h\)- and \(qfh\)-sheafifications agree;
4. for \(\pi_n:x\mapsto x^n\),
   \[
     F_n=(\pi_n)_*,\qquad V_n=\pi_n^*.                              \tag{2}
   \]
5. on the rational-Witt character spaces used in Deninger's
   arithmetic dynamical construction, multiplicative evaluation
   extends to
   \[
     W_{\mathrm{rat}}(A)\longrightarrow C(X_A,\mathbb C).           \tag{2a}
   \]

The standard category, not a new Witt topos, is the algebraic source.
The length-one Rost--Schmid direction comes from
\(\dim X_0=1\).

## Source coefficient

Let \(\mathscr K^M_*\) be the Tate-graded Milnor homotopy module over
\(X_0\).  This is the coefficient forced by the
Kucharczyk--Scholze Steinberg defect and
the sheaf-level construction
`DAC_LEAFWISE_KUMMER_SHEAF_AMALGAMATION_EXISTENCE`.  Its
arithmetic-curve Rost--Schmid complex has the source form
\[
 0\longrightarrow
 \mathscr K^M_*(K)
 \xrightarrow{\oplus_p\partial_p}
 \prod_p'
 \mathscr K^M_{*-1}(\kappa(p))
 \longrightarrow0.                                                  \tag{3}
\]
Its logarithmic Kummer realization is
`DAC_ADELIC_L1_L2_ROST_SCHMID_HODGE_COEFFICIENT`.  The
Milnor--Witt/signature refinement belongs to the real Arakelov
boundary.  Ordinary finite correspondences in `(1)` do not
automatically supply that orientation; framed/Gysin lifts of the power
maps are known, but their real-boundary comparison remains separate.

## Conjecture

`[CONJECTURE] [DAC_FINITE_CORRESPONDENCE_ANALYTIC_REALIZATION]`

There is a symmetric monoidal realization
\[
 \operatorname{Real}_{\mathrm{Den}}:
 D(\operatorname{Shv}_{\mathrm{tr}}
      (\operatorname{Cor}_{X_0},\tau))
 \longrightarrow
 D_{\mathrm{clc}}(X_W)                                             \tag{4}
\]
on the full subcategory generated by `(1)`,
\(\mathscr K^M_*\), Tate twists, and their relative cells,
such that:

1. **Coefficient image.**
   \[
   \operatorname{Real}_{\mathrm{Den}}
      (\mathscr K^M_*)
   \simeq
   \mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}},                                      \tag{5}
   \]
   where \(\mathscr E_{\mathrm{Den}}\) is
   `DAC_TRUE_TO_APPROXIMATE_DERIVED_COEFFICIENT`; on the generic fibre
   this is the Tate--Milnor unit-amalgamated sheaf, not raw
   rational-Witt cohomology and not a truncation of its global
   cochains.
2. **Represented Witt evaluation.**  On `(1)`, `(4)` is induced by
   Deninger's multiplicative character-evaluation functions and
   agrees with finite-flat/qfh descent wherever the source theorems
   apply.
3. **Residues.**  The realization of `(3)` is the generic/prime-packet
   sheaf differential of
   `DAC_COARSE_INVARIANT_SHEAF_ROST_SCHMID_DESCENT`.
4. **Real MW lift.**  The ordinary power correspondences `(2)` admit
   compatible framed/Gysin lifts at the real boundary; determinant
   twists, signatures, proper Gysin maps, and the projection formula
   survive `(4)`.  No global replacement of Milnor by Milnor--Witt
   coefficients, and no orientation of every ordinary cycle in `(1)`,
   is assumed.
5. **Mixed topology.**  The Kummer restriction of `(5)` is the mixed
   \(L^1/L^2\) coefficient, and place-average residues agree with
   `DAC_ADELIC_PLACE_AVERAGE_RESIDUE`.
6. **Derived sections and amplitude.**  Realization commutes with the
   generic and closed-point derived sections in `(3)`.  Each realized
   term has reduced internal amplitude \([0,1]\).
7. **Regulator.**  Deninger's \(Q_0(1)\)-regulator is the realized
   degree-one Kummer edge map, not the whole spectral \(H^1\).

Then `(3)` realizes the two-column localization triangle and yields
three-term arithmetic cohomology after adjoining the real orientation.

## Construction program

1. Work in the standard finite-correspondence category and identify
   Deninger's \(W_{\mathrm{rat}}\) with the represented coefficient
   `(1)` on the normal affine basis.
2. Use `(2a)` as the degree-zero realization of the represented Witt
   coefficient and prove its sheafwise completed-locally-convex
   continuity.
3. Choose the finite-flat/\(h\)/\(qfh\) topology only where Deninger's
   comparison theorems apply; prove the required arithmetic-base
   descent rather than extrapolating from the field case.
4. Insert Milnor Rost--Schmid residues.  At the real boundary, use the
   existing framed finite-syntomic power maps and homotopy-coherent
   Gysin operations; construct only the comparison of their
   determinant/signature lines with `(1)`.
5. Add pro-Stein analytic and mixed adelic factors by completed
   external tensor product.
6. Extend the degree-zero map from the represented Witt coefficient to
   relative motivic cells by derived Kan realization on the generated
   subcategory.
7. Prove `(5)` on a quotient-adapted chart basis.
8. Prove residue, projection-formula, Frobenius/Verschiebung, and
   signature compatibility.
9. Establish internal amplitude and reduced closed-range comparison.

## Falsifiers

- Theorem 5.1 alone does not construct `(4)`.
- Ordinary finite correspondences do not imply a real
  Milnor--Witt-oriented lift.
- \(h/qfh\) results over fields cannot silently be promoted to the
  integral arithmetic base.
- Ordinary topological dimension of \(X_W\) does not control amplitude;
  dimension control must pass through `(3)` and `(5)`.
- Sheafification must impose the generic Steinberg correction and must
  not erase the regulator, mixed product-formula topology, or real
  orientation.
- Any realization using Xi zeros or RH-equivalent positivity is
  circular.
