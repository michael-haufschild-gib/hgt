## Risky consequences

The conjecture predicts more than RH.

1. **Multiplicity is microlocal intersection length.**  The order \(m_\rho\) is the length of the
   corresponding real characteristic-cycle component under radial projection.
2. **Prime transfer is a real-symbol calculus.**  The exact scalar
   \[
     q_p(\rho)=p+1-p^\rho-p^{1-\rho}
   \]
   is the radial image of a real prime-normal endomorphism.  In particular \(q_p(\rho)\) is real
   on every nontrivial Xi conormal.
3. **Two-prime reality suffices.**  Reality of \(q_2(\rho)\) and \(q_3(\rho)\), together with
   \(\Im\rho\ne0\), forces \(\Re\rho=\tfrac12\).  Thus a successful construction need not first
   establish a full all-prime unitary norm.
4. **Quartet surgery has excess microlocal support.**  An inserted off-line quartet in a
   generalized-prime model cannot be the radial image of the same literal integral
   prime-incidence characteristic cycle; it must add a source-unproduced component or break
   real incidence descent.
5. **The global Weil test is a pushforward theorem.**  Weil positivity should arise as positivity
   of the real characteristic cycle under a source-generated common-test pushforward, not as an
   independent inequality on scalar test functions.

Any failure of consequences 1--5 falsifies the stated conjecture rather than merely one proposed
proof.

## Mandatory falsifiers

1. **Generic-product falsifier.**  If every positive self-dual generalized-prime quartet system
   admits the same literal integral prime-incidence lift, the conjecture does not distinguish zeta
   and is false.
2. **Projection-censorship falsifier.**  If the source cycle can be real while some Xi conormal
   component maps to zero, RH does not follow and clause 5 of the frozen hunch is false.
3. **Target-fed falsifier.**  A cycle defined from \(\operatorname{div}\Xi\), its zeros, a
   zero-adapted Weil test, or a metric calibrated after specialization is circular.
4. **Scalar-repackaging falsifier.**  A construction factoring through radial Mellin data before
   retaining prime incidence cannot establish the conjecture.
5. **Complex-wavefront falsifier.**  If the natural source microlocal category necessarily has
   complex rather than real characteristic directions, “reality” is an added RH axiom.
6. **Finite-Rees falsifier.**  If the construction is implementable by a fixed finite-Rees
   multiplier, it contradicts the proved all-orders-flat superselection law.
7. **Multiplicity falsifier.**  Failure to retain top conormal jets makes the construction
   divisor-support detection, not the claimed characteristic-cycle theorem.
