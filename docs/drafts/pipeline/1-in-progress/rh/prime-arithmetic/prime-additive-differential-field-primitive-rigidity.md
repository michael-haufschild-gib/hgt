- **Statement:** If a positive-prime-power-supported arithmetic source belongs to the differential
  field generated by canonical prime-radial sources, then it is a finite complex-linear
  combination of logarithmic jets of those sources.

## Goal

Let `P` be the complex vector space of arithmetic functions vanishing away from positive prime
powers, let `D` be the rotated logarithmic derivation, and let `R(V)` be the canonical prime-radial
realization of a profile subspace `V <= C^N`.  Write

```text
K_V = C<R(V)>
```

for the embedded differential field generated by `R(V)`.  The proposed exact intersection law is

```text
K_V cap P = C[D] R(V),                                      (PR)
```

where the right side means finite sums `sum_j D^j R(v_j)` with `v_j in V`.

Equivalently: a rational differential operation on finitely many collective prime-radial
observables cannot create a new prime-additive observable.  Every prime-additive output is forced
to be differential-linear with constant coefficients.

The statement is source-pure.  It concerns the arithmetic-function differential field itself and
does not assume an analytic continuation, a zero set, or the Riemann hypothesis.

## Why this is not the existing profile-matroid theorem

The profile-matroid theorem classifies other prime-radial inputs.  Formula `(PR)` quantifies over
all positive-prime-power-supported outputs, including sources whose coefficients depend on the
prime.  Derivatives of radial sources already have this prime dependence through powers of
`log p`, so `(PR)` identifies the entire additive intersection rather than only the radial slice.

The reverse inclusion is immediate.  The open content is the rigidity inclusion

```text
K_V cap P <= C[D] R(V).
```

## Attack

1. Partition the primes into disjoint infinite packets.  Restriction to a packet preserves
   Dirichlet convolution and commutes with the logarithmic derivation.
2. A positive-prime-power-supported source is additive under disjoint packet union.
3. A field expression in finitely many generator jets therefore induces a rational function
   satisfying the multivariate Cauchy identity

   ```text
   F(X + Y) = F(X) + F(Y).
   ```

4. Over characteristic zero, a rational homomorphism from an additive algebraic group to the
   additive group is linear.  Equivalently, the primitive rational elements of the symmetric
   field are degree one.
5. Joint algebraic independence of the two packet copies transfers the abstract rational identity
   back to the arithmetic-function field and identifies the coefficients with a finite
   `C[D]`-combination of the original sources.

The denominator step is closed in
`../2-fully-proven/universal-law-field/primitive-formation-rigidity-mechanics/two-packet-rational-primitive-rigidity-and-finite-formation-no-new-current-law.md`.
Two disjoint infinite packet copies are jointly algebraically free on every finite jet palette, so
every nonzero formal denominator stays nonzero under all three required packet evaluations.  The
resulting rational Cauchy identity is differentiated in independent coordinates and is forced to be
linear in characteristic zero.

## RH pressure test

`(PR)` would impose a positive architecture law on Euler-side proof mechanisms: within a finite
prime-radial differential field, every additive Euler observable must be a constant-coefficient
differential-linear combination of the admitted sources.  Thus a mechanism capable of producing
new additive data must use a new profile direction, non-radial prime coupling, a multiplicative
state followed by logarithmic extraction, or a genuinely infinite completion.  This is a route
constraint, not an implication of RH.
