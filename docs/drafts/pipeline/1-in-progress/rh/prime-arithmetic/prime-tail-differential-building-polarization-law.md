## Candidate law

Let

```text
Q_tail = C^Primes / C^(Primes),
```

where `C^(Primes)` is the finitely supported subspace.  Fix a finite-dimensional subspace
`V` of `Q_tail` represented by bounded prime-coefficient sequences, and assume the signed
prime-Dirichlet theorem identifies every basis of `V` with a differentially algebraically
independent carrier family over the finite-prime differential field `E_fin`.

For `u in V`, write

```text
P_u(s)=sum_p u(p)p^(-s),                 Re(s)>1,
```

where changing the representative of `u` changes `P_u` by an element of `E_fin`.  For every
linear subspace `U<=V`, define the embedded differential field

```text
K(U)=E_fin<P_u:u in U>_D.                                  (1)
```

The target theorem is the following exact geometry.

### Differential-building theorem `[PROVED ON PAPER]`

For all subspaces `U,W<=V`,

```text
K(0)=E_fin,
K(U+W)=K(U)K(W),
K(U intersect W)=K(U) intersect K(W),                       (2)

dtrdeg_(E_fin) K(U)=dim_C U,
dtrdeg_(K(U)) K(U+W)=dim_C W-dim_C(U intersect W).           (3)
```

Moreover every `A in GL(V)` induces a unique `E_fin`-differential automorphism

```text
sigma_A:K(V)->K(V),       sigma_A(P_u)=P_(Au),               (4)
```

and

```text
sigma_A(K(U))=K(AU).                                        (5)
```

Thus `U |-> K(U)` is a lattice embedding of the full subspace lattice of `V` into the lattice of
intermediate differential fields.  Its proper nonzero members form the spherical projective
building of `PGL(V)`.  Passing through all finite-dimensional tail subspaces gives a canonical
ind-building attached to the prime-tail carrier.

For Boolean orientations, finite changes vanish in `Q_tail` and global sign fixes the projective
line.  Consequently the raw same-field coordinate is not merely a quotient set: it is the
projective point

```text
C[epsilon] in P(Q_tail),                                   (6)
```

and finite families of carriers generate the complete projective subspace geometry spanned by
their tail points.

## Proof

Choose an adapted basis

```text
V=A direct_sum B direct_sum C direct_sum D,
U=A direct_sum B,
W=A direct_sum C,
A=U intersect W.                                            (7)
```

Choose bases `a`, `b`, `c`, and `d` of the four summands in `(7)`.  Their union is a basis of
`V`.  By the prime-tail differential-independence theorem, the complete jet family

```text
{D^r P_e:e in a union b union c union d, r>=0}              (8)
```

is algebraically independent over `E_fin`.  Therefore the evaluation map from the rational
function field on the formal variables in `(8)` to `K(V)` is an isomorphism.  Under this
isomorphism,

```text
K(U)=E_fin(D^r P_e:e in a union b, r>=0),
K(W)=E_fin(D^r P_e:e in a union c, r>=0).                    (9)
```

Put `F=E_fin(D^r P_e:e in a,r>=0)`.  The `b`-jet and `c`-jet variable sets are disjoint
transcendence bases over `F`.  The standard disjoint-transcendence-basis intersection lemma gives

```text
F(D^r P_b) intersect F(D^r P_c)=F.                           (10)
```

For completeness, that lemma follows from linear disjointness: polynomial algebras on disjoint
indeterminate sets tensor injectively into the joint polynomial algebra; localization preserves
injectivity, so the two rational function fields are linearly disjoint over `F`.  Linearly
disjoint subfields have intersection `F`.  Equation `(10)` is exactly the meet law in `(2)`.

The join law follows because adjoining the union of two source families is the same as adjoining
their linear span; `P_(u+w)=P_u+P_w`.  The first dimension law follows because a basis of `U` is
differentially independent and generates `K(U)`.  For the relative law, extend a basis of
`U intersect W` first to a basis of `U` and then by a complementary family from `W` to a basis of
`U+W`.  Algebraic independence of the union of all jets remains algebraic independence of the
new complementary jets over the rational field generated by the `U` jets.  Their number is
`dim W-dim(U intersect W)`, which proves `(3)`.

Let `A in GL(V)`.  In a fixed basis its matrix acts by the same invertible constant substitution
on every jet order.  The inverse matrix gives the inverse substitution on the differential
rational function field, and

```text
D(sum_j A_(ij) D^r P_j)=sum_j A_(ij) D^(r+1)P_j,
```

so the substitution commutes with the derivation.  This proves `(4)`.  Applying it to generators
proves `(5)`, and uniqueness follows because those generators and all their jets generate `K(V)`.

Finally, changing a representative of `u in Q_tail` adds a finite Dirichlet polynomial.  Every
jet of that correction lies in `E_fin`, so base translation does not alter the generated embedded
differential field.  Thus `(1)` and the entire construction depend only on the tail subspace.

## Positive necessity for HT carriers

The projective building is homogeneous: `PGL(V)` is transitive on subspaces of each fixed
dimension.  Therefore an invariant of the unpointed differential field and its finite-prime base
cannot distinguish the zeta tail line from another line in the same finite building orbit.

> **Polarization necessity.**  A canonical arithmetic HT carrier must be a polarized differential
> building.  It must add data that reduces the full linear-change symmetry to a stabilizer of one
> arithmetic projective point.  In the zeta setting the required polarization must simultaneously
> retain the positive prime orientation, the nonlinear Euler plethysm, and the scale-one
> archimedean functional equation.

This is a positive structural requirement, not the statement that a field-only route is
impossible.  The carrier must be a projective tail field together with a symmetry-breaking
polarization.

## Attacks

1. **Renaming attack.**  Projective buildings and automorphisms of differential rational fields
   are classical.  Scientific novelty can only lie in the exact analytic prime-tail realization,
   especially the meet law `(2)`, not in the word `building`.
2. **Rank-is-not-intersection attack.**  The existing differential matroid gives dimensions of
   joins but does not by itself prove the embedded-field intersection law.  Equation `(8)` must be
   proved explicitly; no transcendence-degree count may substitute for it.
3. **Representative attack.**  A finite change of a coefficient sequence changes `P_u` by an
   `E_fin` element.  The definition of `K(U)` must be shown independent of all representative
   choices.
4. **Automorphism scope.**  The automorphisms act on the generated differential field `K(V)`, not
   on the entire meromorphic function field and not on analytic continuation.  No broader action
   is claimed.
5. **Parent-theory attack.**  If Shapiro--Sparer, Pong, or differential-field literature already
   states the complete subspace/intermediate-field lattice for these Dirichlet carriers, the
   candidate is a bridge and must be retired.
6. **RH upcast attack.**  The theorem does not locate zeros.  Its RH consequence is the precise
   symmetry that an additional arithmetic polarization must break.
