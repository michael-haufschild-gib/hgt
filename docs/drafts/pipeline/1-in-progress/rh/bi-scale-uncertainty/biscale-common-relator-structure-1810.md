## Exact algebraic candidate

On an abelian group `Gamma`, consider real even amplitudes `a(s)=a(−s)`. Observe products `∏a(s_i)` over every finite zero-sum tuple. Two-tuples `(s,−s)` determine the magnitudes and support `S`. On `S`, equal data therefore give a sign ratio `eta:S→{−1,1}`. The candidate all-orders classification is:

`same zero-sum products ↔ same magnitudes and b(s)=chi(s)a(s) on S`,

where `chi` is a character of the subgroup generated by `S` into `{−1,1}`. Zero coordinates require no ratio.

Construction: express a subgroup element as a finite signed sum of elements of `S`, and multiply the corresponding signs. Two expressions differ by a zero-sum word. Symmetry of `S` and `eta(−s)=eta(s)=eta(s)⁻¹` convert negative multiplicities into permitted entries, so equality of the observed products makes the character independent of the expression. Conversely, a character multiplies every zero-sum product by one.

It extends to an ambient complex translation character, even when it does not extend to an ambient real character.
