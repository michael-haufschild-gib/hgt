## Hostile Audit

1. **Ordinary symmetric Frobenius is insufficient.**
   The local Artin algebra \(\mathcal O_\rho/(\Xi)\) is already a symmetric Frobenius algebra for
   every zero, including hypothetical off-line zeros. Its algebraic Nakayama automorphism is
   trivial but it has no positive analytic dagger and does not see `(1)`.
2. **Vanishing only in cohomology is insufficient.**
   A modular cocycle may be a nontrivial coboundary between distinct objects. The RH consumer
   uses its value on a same-object prime-clock arrow, where every coboundary is one. Clause 3 is
   essential.
3. **Properness is not assumed.**
   Proper Lie groupoids are unimodular, but prime flows have infinite clock isotropy and need not
   be proper. The conjecture asks for a constructed invariant transverse density, not for a false
   properness slogan.
4. **Finite unitarity is insufficient.**
   Every finite truncation may be unitary while the cofinal divisor functional leaves the normal
   sector. Kakutani singularity and the flat-radical theorem are direct falsifiers.
5. **Local residue pairing is insufficient.**
   A complex-bilinear perfect residue pairing can pair reflected stalks with unequal raw grades.
   Clause 5 requires positive Hermitian continuity of the unnormalized grades.
6. **Generalized-prime falsifier.**
   The explicit off-line generalized-prime Euler systems have functional equation, entire
   completion, hypertranscendence, source positivity, and local principal parts. If one also
   satisfies clauses 1--5 with its own source, the conjecture is false.
7. **No target-fed metric.**
   A density defined from Xi zero coordinates, a zero-indexed Hilbert sum, or the requirement
   that `(5)` hold is circular.
