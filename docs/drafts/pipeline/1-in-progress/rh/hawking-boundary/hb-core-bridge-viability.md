## What would count as progress

The task is to test whether a concrete physical mechanism can support HB, not to accumulate
supporting regularity lemmas. Proving both global bridges unconditionally, together with the
existing RH receiver, would already prove RH. A viability assessment must therefore distinguish
an exact conditional mechanism, its physical evidence, and its still-unproved identification.
Absence of a discovered contradiction is not confirmation that the identification can be proved.

The corpus has a precise conditional variational mechanism, actual local physical inputs,
and an unconditional cancellation for a specified odd angular current and its complete finite
rectangle boundary. Its distinct coexact/coexact channel is even (V17--V18). These results do
not yet establish simultaneous realization of the selective diagnostic and extinction law
by the complete original causal action.

The relative-reader horizon obstruction now has a concrete positive survivor in the
inspected corpus: the raw radial current has no finite limit, but a unique leading-power
subtraction, fixed by the modes, leaves zero (V22--V23). Its full angular reader difference
still survives in the normalized stream trace (V24). This is a candidate boundary input,
not a completed correspondence with the original action's boundary prescription.

Own paper attacks P5--P7 additionally derive the complete finite-cusp reader-difference
balance for the incoming SV--SV sector and show that the half-square Hermitian balance
is a weighted scalar-current identity. These deductions are **not Lean-formalized** and
must not appear as certified manuscript results. They locate missing identifications;
they do not provide an independent extinction selector.

These are existing **conditional Lean statements**, not a premise-free physical construction

## Reframing the identification test

**[BRIDGE TARGET — UNPROVED; NOT A NEW PHYSICAL LAW]**

For the original action, determine its reflected orbit before seeking equality of each
individual oriented coefficient with a BM candidate. A sufficient target would be

```
C_phys(q) + C_phys(q*) = -A_phys(q) * |D_clock(q)|^2,
A_phys(q) > 0.
```

Here `C_phys` must be obtained from the original full action and its fixed causal prescription;
it is not a new definition of the right-hand side. Its existence, normalization, source
coupling and complete boundary treatment are part of the unproved bridge. `A_phys` must be
derived independently from that calculation, not defined by division by `D_clock`.
No assertion is made here that such a factor exists.

This formulation allows individual oriented radiation coefficients to survive on the line,
provided their reflected sum vanishes. It also allows additional contributions whose odd
reflection law is proved for the same full action. Thus a nonzero source, a nonzero Green
block, or an individual surviving boundary term does not by itself refute this orbit target.
Conversely, simply dropping such terms would not establish it.

This is a refinement of the existing orbit receiver, not a claimed new invariant or a solved
bridge. The methods' novelty/distance gate therefore labels it a **bridge target**. It does
not justify inventing a conservation law tailored to the desired square.

## Applying the two methods to the actual frontier

**Where does the calculation know `xi(rho)=0`?** The physical reader calibration removes one
Weyl coefficient. The strict-right physical source evaluation retains three affine Xi
incidences. The spin-two completed zero and its first live derivative are different objects.
The identification must explain the arithmetic input at these exact steps, rather than
appeal to covariance of an arbitrary spectral family.

**Which correspondence was being used informally?** A linear QNM testing current, an
algebraic packet cut, and a pairing of two genuine quadratic sources were being treated as
interchangeable. They have different definitions. Source exchange alone does not identify
them. The actual source/reader parameters are `rho-1/2` and `(rho-1)/2` (V09--V10).

**What nuisance must become part of the object?** The complete boundary contribution and the
reader-change remainder. Their orbit parity is relevant; individual vanishing is stronger
than needed. No parity for the completed remainder has been proved by this investigation.

**Am I working on the right problem?** Another local eigen-reader boundary simplification
does not decide this bridge. The next decisive object is the physical radiative pairing of
the two original quadratic sources, with its actual retarded/advanced prescription. A proposed
identification must be attacked on that object before spending effort on unrelated support
lemmas. A nonzero lower-order field or a formal RH equivalence is not a substitute.
