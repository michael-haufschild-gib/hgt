## Why this is not the Thorin route in disguise

The resulting moment sequence belongs to the same Stieltjes cone used by the completed-seam Thorin
card.  Therefore an arbitrary existence assertion for `nu_src` is no more explanatory than an
abstract Thorin measure and cannot count as the missing proof.

The additional, falsifiable restriction here is factorization through the concrete family of Sonin
spectral measures:

```text
mu_PG = integral mu_a dnu_src(a),
mu_a = spectral trace law of T_a.                   (5)
```

Thus the route is useful only if `nu_src` is given before the target is invoked.  It is in general
stronger than RH or abstract Stieltjes representability because not every positive spectral law
need lie in the positive convex cone generated by `(mu_a)_(a>0)`.

## Source attack

The Gamma completion has scale-integral formulae, while the Sonin model has exact cutoff--dilation
covariance.  These facts suggest a multiplicative Calderon-type decomposition in the logarithmic
aperture variable.  A valid proof must establish a nonlinear trace identity, not only a vector or
projection reproducing formula:

1. derive a concrete density `w_src(a)>=0`, preferably with `dnu_src(a)=w_src(a) da/a`, from the
   theta--Gamma completion and the completed prime residual;
2. keep the polar and principal-shell counterterms inside the same source packet;
3. prove integrability of every `a |-> Tr(T_a^(r+1))` against `nu_src`;
4. sum the resolvent series on a common open disk and prove `(3)` there;
5. extract coefficients only after the analytic identity is established.

The existing dilation identities move the physical and Fourier cutoffs in reciprocal directions,
so they do not make `T_a` unitarily constant in `a`.  They preserve aperture as a genuine
uncertainty coordinate.  They also stop short of `(3)`: pointwise cutoff annihilation does not
commute automatically with powers, traces, or aperture integration.

The source comparison need not be attacked as opaque trace powers.  The formal adjacent-norm
tomography identities give, pointwise for every positive aperture,

```text
Q_a(X^n) = Tr(T_a^(2n+1)),
Q_a(X^n+X^(n+1))
  = m_(2n)(a) + 2 m_(2n+1)(a) + m_(2n+2)(a).          (6)
```

After the corresponding integrability bounds, the aperture averages of these two explicit
squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm families reconstruct every moment in `(4)` by polarization.  This is
a narrower positive producer target than direct coefficientwise comparison and is compatible with
a Calderon-scale attack.

## Hard attacks and positive residues

1. **One aperture curve is too rigid.**  Requiring the fixed infinite target vector to be a cluster
   point of the one-dimensional curve `a |-> (Tr(T_a^(r+1)))_r` may fail even when a positive
   Sonin factorization exists.
   **Positive residue:** membership in the positive barycentric hull generated by the aperture
   spectral laws suffices.  The barycenter theorem formalizes this weaker producer; a further
   coordinatewise-limit theorem would cover the closure of that hull.
2. **An arbitrary aperture measure is target fitting.**  Recovering `nu` from the numbers
   `m_r^PG` is a moment problem equivalent to the sign wall.
   **Positive residue:** require a formula for `nu_src` from theta integers, Gamma scale packets,
   prime powers, and polar terms before comparing any moments.
3. **Independent prime-block weights destroy the Euler source.**  Separate weights can interpolate
   moments while changing the completed logarithmic derivative.
   **Positive residue:** one common aperture law acts simultaneously on every prime-power block;
   all arithmetic weights remain the canonical logarithmic Euler weights.
4. **A Calderon vector identity is insufficient.**  Even a positive resolution of the identity
   over dilations does not imply the nonlinear resolvent-trace identity `(3)`.
   **Positive residue:** the exact source target is the operator resolvent trace, or equivalently
   the two integrated adjacent-norm families in `(6)` with one common dominating estimate.
5. **A zero-fed law is circular.**  A measure assembled from the Xi divisor merely transports the
   known RH-equivalent spectral law to aperture space.
   **Positive residue:** define the law solely from completed source packets and invoke analytic
   uniqueness only after equality on the Euler--Gamma convergence domain.
6. **Abstract Stieltjes or Thorin existence is not the producer.**  It names the positive cone but
   supplies none of its orientation.
   **Positive residue:** `(5)` is a restricted Sonin factorization problem.  A source formula for
   `nu_src` would prove the needed orientation rather than assume it.
