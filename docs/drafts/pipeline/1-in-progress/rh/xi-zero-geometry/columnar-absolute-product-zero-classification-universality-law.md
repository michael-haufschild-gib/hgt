## Theorem generated by the sources

Suppose on a vertically shift-closed region a carrier factors as

`F(s) = psi(b^(-s)) * R(s)`

with `b ≥ 2`. At a point where `R` is represented by an absolutely convergent
product `∏ (1 + f_n(s))` inside the half-ball:

1. `R(s)` is nonzero;
2. `F(s) = 0` exactly when the column factor vanishes;
3. every such zero propagates through the full vertical lattice of period
   `2π / log b`;
4. consequently `F` has no aperiodic zero at that point.

Thus any aperiodic zero of a completed carrier must lie beyond at least one of
the two local interfaces: column factorization or absolute residual-product
representation.

## Scope and non-patching check

- Every premise and conclusion concerns the same carrier, factorization, point,
  and vertical orbit.
- Residual-product nonvanishing is necessary to recover the column factor from
  a carrier zero.
- Column periodicity is necessary for the zero-lattice conclusion.
- `AperiodicZeroAt` is discharged by that exact lattice, not by an unrelated
  contradiction.
- No disjoint case split or mere theorem conjunction is used.

factors, vertical progressions, almost-periodic Dirichlet series, and zeros of
combinations of Euler products.

  of Dirichlet series*, arXiv:2311.02728, studies zero sets of absolutely
  convergent Dirichlet series and almost-periodic structure.

No source found states the exact local theorem formalized here: for one
exponential column times an arbitrary half-ball absolutely convergent residual
product, carrier zero iff column zero plus its full explicit vertical lattice,
with aperiodic-zero exclusion as a corollary.

Novelty claim remains narrow: this joint representation-boundary
classification, not classical nonvanishing of absolutely convergent products,
Euler-product zero-free regions, or elementary periodicity of a local factor.
