## Headline

> **The slightest mass defeats every finite clockwork.**

Precise public sentence:

> **A massless quantum clock fits one time equation. Give it any mass, and no finite algebraic
> time equation survives.**

The word `algebraic` is essential. The theorem does not exclude algorithms, integral equations,
infinite-order equations, or a finite formula that inserts the answer as a coefficient.

## Physical experiment

Consider the one-particle sector of a free relativistic scalar field on a spatial circle of
radius `R>0`. In units `c=hbar=1`, momentum eigenstates `|n>` have energies

```text
E_n(m) = (1/R) sqrt(n^2 + c),    c=(mR)^2,    n in Z.       (1)
```

For `beta>0`, put two copies in the normalized thermofield state

```text
|TFD_(m,beta)> =
  Z_m(beta)^(-1/2)
  sum_(n in Z) exp(-beta E_n(m)/2) |n>_L |n>_R,             (2)

Z_m(s) = sum_(n in Z) exp(-s E_n(m)),    Re(s)>0.           (3)
```

The phase-resolved one-sided return amplitude is

```text
A_(m,beta)(t)
  = <TFD_(m,beta)| exp(-i t H_L) |TFD_(m,beta)>
  = Z_m(beta+i t)/Z_m(beta).                                (4)
```

Absolute convergence of `(3)` on every right half-plane makes `(2)` a legitimate unit vector and
`(4)` analytic in the half-plane `Im(t)<beta`. Equation `(4)` is a standard
Loschmidt/thermofield overlap. A Hadamard or Ramsey interferometer reads its real and imaginary
parts as two ancilla expectation values. Intensity alone is not yet claimed to retain HT.

No coefficient sequence is chosen to force the result. The nonzero coefficients are the
canonical Boltzmann weights generated by the same Hamiltonian whose time law is tested.

## Paper proof v1

### 1. Massless closure

At `m=0`, `E_n=|n|/R`. Therefore

```text
Z_0(s)
  = 1 + 2 sum_(n>=1) exp(-n s/R)
  = coth(s/(2R)).                                           (5)
```

Write `C=Z_0(beta)`. Then

```text
A_(0,beta)(t) = Z_0(beta+i t)/C
```

and direct differentiation of `(5)` gives

```text
2 R C A'_(0,beta)(t)
  + i (C^2 A_(0,beta)(t)^2 - 1) = 0.                       (6)
```

Thus the massless thermofield clock closes by one autonomous Riccati state.

### 2. Positive-mass frequency rank

For `m>0`, set `c=(mR)^2>0`. The supporting card proves:

```text
dim_Q span_Q {sqrt(n^2+c) : n in N} = infinity.             (7)
```

This theorem is not new. Peter Müller and Will Sawin proved the stronger statement for every
real quadratic polynomial that is not a square, in a February 2025 MathOverflow answer. Their
finite-field specialization argument is the correct dependency route:

- assume all sufficiently large square roots lie in a finite rational span;
- place the coefficients and span generators in a finitely generated real domain and specialize
  to a number field while preserving the nonsquare discriminant;
- reduce at completely split odd primes;
- count points on `y^2=x^2+A x+B` using
  `(y-x-A/2)(y+x+A/2)=B-A^2/4`;
- obtain `p-1` points when the right side is nonzero, contradicting the at least `2p-2` points that
  would exist if every quadratic value were a square;
- choose nonsquare residue classes at arbitrarily large integers and induct outside every finite
  rational span.

This covers every real positive mass, not only rational or generic masses.

### 3. Ostrowski transfer

The series `(3)` is a convergent generalized Dirichlet series with all coefficients nonzero.
Ostrowski's necessary condition says that exponents of a convergent generalized Dirichlet series
satisfying an algebraic differential equation must lie in the integer span of finitely many
exponents. Equation `(7)` violates that condition. Hence `Z_m` is HT for every `m>0`.

The invertible affine substitution `s=beta+i t` and multiplication by the nonzero constant
`1/Z_m(beta)` preserve DA and HT. This gives positive-mass half of `(M0)`.

### 4. Finite-state exclusion

Let a finite algebraic clock have states `x_1,...,x_d` with

```text
x_j' = F_j(t,x_1,...,x_d),
y    = G(t,x_1,...,x_d),
```

where `F_j,G` are rational, allowing a finite algebraic extension. The differential field
generated by states has transcendence degree at most `d`. Therefore

```text
y,y',...,y^(d)
```

are algebraically dependent over `C(t)`. Every output of such a clock is DA. The HT conclusion in
`(M0)` excludes every finite `d`, proving `(M1)`.

### 5. Two real interferometric outputs

Hadamard/Ramsey overlap measurement returns

```text
X_m(t)=Re A_(m,beta)(t),    Y_m(t)=Im A_(m,beta)(t)
```

in two fixed ancilla bases. If one finite rational/algebraic dynamical model jointly generated
both records, then both outputs would be DA after complexification. Differentially algebraic
functions form a differential field, so `X_m+iY_m=A_(m,beta)` would be DA, contradicting `(M0)`.
This proves `(M2)`.

The conclusion concerns a phase-referenced pair of ordinary real expectation values. It does not
require Hardy projection or inference of phase from intensity. Determining which single fixed
quadrature is HT, or proving the echo probability `|A|^2` is HT, remains open.

## What changes physically

Both `m=0` and `m>0` clocks have countably many exact energy levels. Mass does not create the
infinity. Relativistic dispersion bends the harmonic ladder

```text
|n|/R
```

into

```text
sqrt(n^2+(mR)^2)/R.
```

At zero mass, infinitely many modes collapse into one Riccati law. At every positive mass,
however small, the bent spectrum has no finite rational basis and no finite nonlinear algebraic
state closure. Differential type is discontinuous at `m=0` even though finite-time numerical
values vary continuously with `m`.

This is the nontrivial content. “An infinite spectrum needs infinitely many states” would be
false here at the level of nonlinear differential realization because the massless infinite
spectrum closes exactly.

## Finite survivor: the first three beats crack

Put `a=mR` and subtract ground energy:

```text
delta_n(a)
  = E_n(m)-E_0(m)
  = (sqrt(n^2+a^2)-a)/R.                                  (7a)
```

At `a=0`, the ladder is exactly additive:

```text
delta_(n+k)(0)=delta_n(0)+delta_k(0).
```

For every `a>0` and positive integers `n,k`, it is strictly superadditive:

```text
delta_(n+k)(a)>delta_n(a)+delta_k(a).                      (7b)
```

`[PROVED HERE]` Let

```text
f_a(x)=sqrt(x^2+a^2)-a
      = x^2/(sqrt(x^2+a^2)+a).
```

For `x>0`,

```text
f_a(x)/x = x/(sqrt(x^2+a^2)+a)
```

is strictly increasing when `a>0`; direct differentiation has positive numerator
`a(a+sqrt(x^2+a^2))/sqrt(x^2+a^2)`. Hence

```text
f_a(n+k)/(n+k)>f_a(n)/n
and
f_a(n+k)/(n+k)>f_a(k)/k.
```

Multiplying by `n` and `k` and adding proves `(7b)`.

In particular, the first three nonnegative momentum lines obey

```text
m=0:  E_2+E_0=2 E_1;
m>0:  E_2+E_0>2 E_1.                                     (7c)
```

Thus three resolved beats expose the first exact fracture of harmonic closure. This is a finite,
noise-margin-ready spectroscopy target. If each measured line has absolute error at most
`epsilon`, then

```text
| (Ehat_2+Ehat_0-2 Ehat_1) - (E_2+E_0-2 E_1) | <= 4 epsilon.   (7d)
```

Therefore a reported defect above `4 epsilon` certifies the positive-mass side of `(7c)` within
the model. This is not an HT certificate: one strict defect rules out the massless Riccati
ladder, not every finite algebraic model. Its role is to tie the infinite theorem to a finite
observable without overclaiming that finite data proves HT.

## Finite observational hierarchy

HT itself cannot be certified from finite noisy data. The admissible empirical consequence is a
growing model-rejection hierarchy:

```text
for every proposed finite algebraic state order d,
some finite jet relation required by that model fails for m>0.          (8)
```

Equation `(8)` is not yet a uniform noise-aware theorem. A Nature-level result needs an explicit
family of residuals or determinants with:

1. finite sample or finite jet requirements;
2. lower bounds at every `m>0`;
3. controlled degradation as `mR -> 0`;
4. stability under bandwidth limits and measurement noise.

The singular small-mass scaling is likely decisive: any fixed finite-order residual may vanish
rapidly as `mR -> 0`, while the full infinite hierarchy remains nonzero. Exact law and feasible
observation must not be conflated.

## Experimental carrier audit

Two current capabilities make the ingredients nonfictional:

1. Loschmidt amplitudes have been phase-sensitively measured on a 32-qubit trapped-ion device,
   with explicit noise and error-mitigation analysis.
2. A 2026 cold-atom experiment realizes relativistic collective fields with a tunable mass gap
   in a two-dimensional box.

Neither experiment realizes `(1)`--`(4)` as stated. The trapped-ion work does not implement the
relativistic circle or TFD source; the cold-atom work does not report the circle thermofield
overlap or the all-mode differential boundary. A credible experimental route must combine:

```text
periodic spatial spectrum
+ tunable relativistic mass gap
+ phase-resolved overlap
+ enough resolved modes to test the growing residual hierarchy.
```

This is a platform-design target, not an accomplished experiment.

## Hostile reviewer attacks

1. **“Finite equation” is rhetoric.** Headline must define algebraic differential law and state
   excluded coefficient field.
2. **Thermofield state is engineered.** Boltzmann weights are canonical, but a one-particle TFD
   is still a chosen preparation. Show another local equilibrium correlator with same theorem.
3. **Two settings are not one scalar readout.** Standard ancilla interferometry closes direct
   access to `(Re A, Im A)` and `(M2)` excludes their joint finite-state realization. A theorem
   for one fixed quadrature or for echo probability would be stronger.
4. **Exact infinity is experimentally inaccessible.** Derive finite residual and noise laws.
5. **Free ring is a toy model.** Establish robustness or a universal local-QFT extension.
6. **Small mass is continuously invisible at finite time.** Quantify observation-time/order cost
   as `mR -> 0`.
7. **Ostrowski already did the mathematics.** Novelty must be the source-forced physical phase
   transition and its operational consequence.
8. **A nonlinear finite model may use nonalgebraic primitives.** Correct; theorem excludes the
   audited algebraic model class, not arbitrary finite software.
9. **Weak interactions destroy exact line spectrum.** Identify a protected observable or prove a
   deformation theorem.
10. **No broad physical conclusion changes.** Until a recognized inference, experiment, or
    universality class changes, Nature significance remains open.
