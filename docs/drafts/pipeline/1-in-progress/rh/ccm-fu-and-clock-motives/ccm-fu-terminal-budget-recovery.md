# CCM-FU: recovering a prepared terminal budget

- Stage: 1-in-progress.
- Target: a bounded complete path through an actual terminal core under a small quadratic envelope.
- Full chain: divisor source → renewable signed reserve → exhaustive prime selection → uniform signed Abel bound → RH.

For a fixed quotient bound J, a sufficiently large cutoff makes every
remaining prime terminal. Its singleton has absolute value at most
(3/5) J sqrt(M). Thus the remaining square energy is at most
(9/25) J² M times the number of primes below M, uniformly in the processed
core. Prime sparsity makes this o(M²).

The producer processes every prime outside a positive quotient-seven
window and a negative late window. Existing quantitative PNT bounds give
the final signed aggregate little-o of M sqrt(M)/log(M), while each
reserved window has a positive lower mass on that scale. The exact
terminal sum identity therefore places the new head within the interval
that the two opposite supplies can cancel. A finite mass cover reduces
the head to within one source weight, with no assumed initial energy
bound. The remaining square energy and complete successor envelope then
fit any fixed positive quadratic budget at sufficiently large cutoff.

The [unconditional endpoint construction](../../../3-formalized/ccm-fu-small-terminal-core.md)
is formalized: every prime outside the two windows is processed, all
square-root-scale primes are processed, the head is at most 5 sqrt(M),
and the complete successor envelope is at most ε M² for any fixed ε>0
eventually. The new density estimate, discrete head cover, actual source
transfer, and mass comparison close that endpoint construction.

The construction supplies a prepared endpoint. It does not supply a
uniformly bounded path from the initial state to that endpoint, renew
exhausted sources, or bound the final signed Abel sum. Existing terminal
ordering retains that final sum as an endpoint obligation.
