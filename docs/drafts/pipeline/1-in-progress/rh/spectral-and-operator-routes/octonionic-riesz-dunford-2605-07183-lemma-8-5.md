# Real invertibility of the octonion Sylvester map (Huo--Ren--Sabadini--Xu, Lemma 8.5)

- **ID:** `octonionic-riesz-dunford-2605-07183-lemma-8-5`
- **Stage:** `1-in-progress/not_new_scientific_knowledge`
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Source:** arXiv:2605.07183v1, Lemma 8.5; Tian, *Matrix representations of octonions and their applications*, Theorem 2.14.
- **Statement:** The real-linear map `x ↦ x*s - q*x` on real octonions is invertible exactly when `s` is not in the conjugacy sphere of `q`.

## Source correction

The paper's displayed determinant factors are consistent with the stated invertibility criterion.  Its last phrase says the zero-determinant equality of real part and imaginary norm is “i.e. `s notin [q]`”; this is reversed.  The source-faithful mathematical target is:

```text
det(R_s - L_q) = 0  ↔  Re(s) = Re(q) and |Im(s)| = |Im(q)|  ↔  s ∈ [q],
```

and therefore real-linear invertibility is equivalent to `s ∉ [q]`.
