# Weight-two rank-contraction theorem

- **ID:** goncharov-weight-two-rank-contraction
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CONJECTURE
- **Parent:** `goncharov-motivic-complex-conjecture`
- **Statement:** `[CONJECTURE]` For every field `F`,
  `H₂(PGL₂(F); St₂∞(F)) = 0` over `ℚ`, where `St₂∞(F)` is the rank-two infinite Steinberg
  module. Equivalently, `K₄⁽²⁾(F)ℚ = 0`.

## Why this exact claim

Kupers--Rudenko--Sierra, arXiv:2606.23863v1, Proposition 10.5(v), identify
`K₄⁽²⁾(F)ℚ` with `H₂(PGL₂(F); St₂∞(F))`. Their Corollary 10.14 proves that universal
vanishing of this group is equivalent to the first open Goncharov comparison,
`K₅⁽³⁾(F)ℚ ≅ H¹(Γ₃(F))`, for every field. Garkusha, arXiv:2007.09044v4,
Corollary 3.13, gives the independent semilocal presentation
`K₄⁽²⁾(F)ℚ ≅ H₃(K₂ᴹ(Δ̂_F^•))ℚ`.

## Concrete complex

Let `Dec(V) = V* \ {0}` with left-zero multiplication `h₁h₂ = h₁`, and let `FC(V)` be
the projective `ℚ[GL(V)]`-module of formal correlators. Decomposition operators `D_h`
make `FC(V)` a `Dec(V)`-module. For `V = F²`, the projective resolution

`⋯ → ℚ[Dec(V)]⊗² ⊗ FC(V) → ℚ[Dec(V)] ⊗ FC(V) → FC(V) → St₂∞(F) → 0`

reduces the claim to exactness in degree two of the coinvariant complex

`C_k(F) := (ℚ[Dec(F²)]⊗k ⊗ FC(F²))_{PGL₂(F)}`.

The active constructive target is: every `z ∈ ker(d : C₂(F) → C₁(F))` has an explicit
finite-support filler in `C₃(F)`, natural under field embeddings. A fixed-functional bar
contraction does not descend to coinvariants; required new object is its second coherent
equivariance homotopy.

## Rank-two exterior reduction

Let `P_F = ℚ[P¹(F)]`, with augmentation `ε : P_F → ℚ`, and let `I_F = ker ε`. The
rank-two ordinary Steinberg module is `I_F`. In rank two,

`St₂∞(F) ≅ Λ² I_F`.

Indeed, `St₂²(F) = I_F ⊗ I_F`, and each rank-one decomposition contributes the square
`([x]-[y])⊗²`. Fixing `∞` and writing `v_x=[x]-[∞]`, the squares of `v_x` and
`v_x-v_y` span `Sym² I_F` by polarization. Thus quotient by decomposables is exactly
`(I_F⊗I_F)/Sym² I_F = Λ²I_F`.

Contraction by `ε` gives an exact exterior Koszul tail

`⋯ → Λ⁶P_F → Λ⁵P_F → Λ⁴P_F → Λ³P_F → Λ²I_F → 0`.

For `r ≥ 3`, `ΛʳP_F` is projective over `ℚ[PGL₂(F)]`: it is a direct sum over orbits of
oriented `r`-element configurations, and each stabilizer is finite because its action on any
three distinct points is faithful. Consequently the exact open core is the elementary
configuration syzygy

`ker((Λ⁵P_F)_{PGL₂(F)} → (Λ⁴P_F)_{PGL₂(F)})
 = im((Λ⁶P_F)_{PGL₂(F)} → (Λ⁵P_F)_{PGL₂(F)})`.

The map from four- to three-point configurations vanishes on coinvariants by odd stabilizer
symmetry. Four-point orbits are cross-ratio generators; five-point boundaries are the classical
five-term relations. Hence the displayed equality says precisely: every rational syzygy among
five-term relations is generated by alternating boundaries of six-point configurations.

## Algebraic-closure reduction

Universal vanishing reduces to algebraically closed fields.  For every field `F`, restriction

`K₄⁽²⁾(F)ℚ → K₄⁽²⁾(F̄)ℚ`

is injective.  If a class dies over `F̄`, filtered-colimit compatibility of algebraic `K`-theory
shows that it dies over a finite subextension `E/F`.  Finite-flat transfer composed with
restriction is multiplication by `[E:F]`, so the original rational class is zero.  Restriction
commutes with Adams operations, hence the argument applies to the weight-two eigenspace.

This is a genuine reduction, but not a proof: algebraically closed fields of positive
transcendence degree remain.  The transcendence-degree-zero cases are known because `F̄_p` is a
filtered colimit of finite fields and `Q̄` is a filtered colimit of number fields, while the first
possible counterexample has positive transcendence degree.

## Configuration spectral-sequence placement

Let `G = PGL₂(F)` and use the augmented oriented configuration complex
`C_p = Λ^(p+1) P_F`.  Its equivariant spectral sequence has

`E¹_(p,q) = H_q(G; C_p) ⇒ H_(p+q)(G; ℚ)`.

For `p ≥ 2`, configuration stabilizers are finite, so `E¹_(p,q) = 0` for `q > 0` over `ℚ`.
The remaining stabilizers are the Borel `B` at `p = 0` and the torus normalizer `N(T)` with
orientation character at `p = 1`.  Over an algebraically closed field,
`H_q(B; ℚ) ≅ H_q(T; ℚ)`, while

`H_q(N(T); ℚ_sign) ≅ (Λ^q(F×_ℚ) ⊗ sign)^(C₂)`.

Weyl inversion acts by `(-1)^q`; therefore the latter group is zero for even `q`, and for odd
`q` the first differential to the Borel column is multiplication by `±2`.  In particular, every
possible outgoing higher differential from

`E²_(4,0) = H₂(G; Λ²I_F)`

has zero target: `(2,1)` for `d₂`, `(1,2)` for `d₃`, and `(0,3)` for `d₄`.  There are no incoming
differentials.  Thus the obstruction survives to `E∞` and is a top-filtration quotient of
`H₄(PGL₂(F); ℚ)`.  This rules out killing it by Borel/torus cancellation alone; it does **not**
give an injection into ordinary group homology or prove vanishing.

## Hostile conjecture loop

1. `[FALSIFIED]` Contract all of `K₂ᴹ(Δ̂_F^•)ℚ`. This would also kill
   `H₂ ≅ K₃ind(F)ℚ`, which is generally nonzero. Positive survivor: contraction may begin
   only in simplicial degree three.
2. `[FALSIFIED]` Use inverse-transpose duality as `-1` on the obstruction. KRS Theorem 8.3
   and Lemmas 9.5--9.6 put both `K₄⁽²⁾` and the rank-two column in the `+1` eigenspace;
   rank-two duality is inner on `PGL₂`.
3. `[FALSIFIED]` Force the product
   `K₄⁽²⁾(F)ℚ ⊗ F×ℚ → K₅⁽³⁾(F)ℚ` to be zero. KRS Proposition 10.15 proves that any
   counterexample to vanishing produces a field where this product is neither zero nor injective.
4. `[FALSIFIED]` Use simplex reversal to obtain `-1` on degree three. Raw reversal satisfies
   `d r_q = (-1)^q r_{q-1} d`; the sign correction required to make it a chain map cancels
   the orientation sign rather than creating an odd eigenspace.
5. `[ACTIVE CONJECTURE]` A fixed-functional contraction of the `Dec(F²)` bar resolution has
   a finite, explicit second homotopy measuring failure of `PGL₂(F)`-equivariance. On a
   degree-two coinvariant cycle, the equivariance defects cancel and the homotopy supplies a
  degree-three filler. Degree one retains the uncancelled first defect, exactly the Bloch group.
6. `[POSITIVE SURVIVOR]` Rank two eliminates new `E∞` terminology:
   `St₂∞(F) ≅ Λ²St₂(F)`, and the obstruction is degree-two homology of the oriented
   `PGL₂(F)` configuration quotient. Any valid contraction must act on five-point cycles,
   preserve four-point cross-ratio homology, and resolve only syzygies of five-term relations.
7. `[FALSIFIED]` Base-change to `F(t)` and cone every configuration from the generic point.
   The cone is not defined on coinvariants: its equivariance defect is another configuration
   class.  Simultaneous dilation by `diag(t,1)` does not remove that defect because every
   cross-ratio is unchanged.  Naive collision specialisation would also kill the nonzero Bloch
   group in degree one; the genuine specialisation retains the cross-ratio class.
8. `[ACTIVE CONJECTURE]` Resolve the generic-cone equivariance defect by the all-place Milnor
   residue profile.  For a degree-two configuration cycle, construct a functorial variable
   `K₂ᴹ(F(t))` symbol whose finite residues are the individual coherence defects.  Prove the
   cycle identity forces the global norm-sum to vanish, use projective-line approximation to
   realize the profile, and subtract that realization.  The corpus theorem
   `rationalFunctionMilnorKTwo_allResidue_eq_zero_iff_constant` then reduces the remaining
   defect to a constant class.  Missing step: an explicit chain map from five-point
   configuration syzygies to these Milnor residue profiles; no such map exists in the corpus or
   audited literature.
9. `[POSITIVE SURVIVOR]` It suffices to prove the theorem for algebraically closed fields.
   Restriction to an algebraic closure is rationally injective by finite-flat transfer and
   filtered-colimit compatibility.  This removes descent as a source of counterexamples, but
   leaves algebraically closed fields of positive transcendence degree.
10. `[FALSIFIED]` Kill the obstruction using the equivariant configuration spectral sequence and
    vanishing of positive rational homology of finite stabilizers.  The Borel and pair columns do
    cancel the odd torus target groups, but this makes `E²_(4,0)` a permanent cycle.  The
    obstruction survives as a top-filtration quotient of `H₄(PGL₂(F); ℚ)` rather than vanishing.
11. `[FALSIFIED]` Use one-variable projective-line residues alone to contract
    `H₃(K₂ᴹ(Δ̂_F^•))ℚ`.  Corpus exactness reconstructs a symbol from a compatible residue profile
    only modulo a constant `K₂ᴹ(F)` class.  Showing that constant remainder is a simplicial
    boundary is exactly the original degree-three semilocal homology problem, so the proposed
    argument is circular without an additional coherent moving identity.
12. `[ACTIVE CONJECTURE]` Galois-trace-zero rigidity in minimal transcendence degree.  After the
    algebraic-closure and pure-transcendence reductions, any minimal counterexample descends to a
    finite separable extension `E/k(t₁,…,t_d)` and lies in a nontrivial rational representation of
    its Galois closure (the invariant part descends and vanishes).  Construct a configuration-level
    specialization homotopy proving that this trace-zero summand is zero.  Required identity must
    retain degree-one Bloch classes and therefore can only begin at the five-point syzygy level.
13. `[FALSIFIED]` Remove Beilinson--Soulé vanishing from Kimura's Bloch--Kriz filler by passing to
    the reduced bar construction.  In arXiv:2604.17671 the two weight-two fillers have the same
    differential, but their difference `D_new²-D_old² ∈ N⁰(2)` is killed by choosing
    `E² ∈ N⁻¹(2)` with `dE²=D_new²-D_old²`; existence is exactly the assumed weight-two
    Beilinson--Soulé vanishing.  A bar word can kill the product of this difference with a later
    factor, not the closed difference itself.  Positive survivor: the obstruction is coherent
    filler monodromy, not failure to construct either individual filler.
14. `[FALSIFIED]` Replace admissible higher Chow cycles by Bolbachan's support-free complex
    `Λ(K,2)` and apply strong Suslin reciprocity.  arXiv:2311.07567 proves the Totaro comparison
    only in degrees `1,2` for weight two.  The present obstruction is `H⁰`; extending the
    comparison one degree lower requires every closed ruled-surface four-function symbol to be a
    threefold boundary.  After Theorem 4.1 and factorization, its four-moving-factor part is the
    same five-point/six-point configuration syzygy.  Strong Suslin reciprocity supplies the
    one-dimensional homotopy and its surface reciprocity law, but not the required
    two-dimensional filler.
15. `[POSITIVE SURVIVOR]` The minimal-transcendence trace-zero problem has a precise motivic form.
    Let `k` be algebraically closed and let `C/k` be a smooth projective connected curve.  Removing
    closed points does not change `CH²(-,4)_ℚ`, because both adjacent support groups are
    `CH¹(k(x),4)_ℚ=CH¹(k(x),3)_ℚ=0`.  Hence
    `K₄⁽²⁾(k(C))_ℚ ≅ H⁰(C,ℚ(2))`.  A rational point splits
    `M(C)=ℚ ⊕ h¹(C) ⊕ ℚ(1)[2]`; the first summand is the constant-field group and the
    last contributes `H⁻²(k,ℚ(1))=0`.  Thus the new core is exactly
    `Hom_DM(k)(h¹(C),ℚ(2)[0])=0`.  Induction on finite transcendence degree plus filtered
    colimits makes these curve statements equivalent to universal weight-two vanishing.  Motivic
    duality rewrites this group but does not force it to vanish; treating the negative shift as a
    Chow-weight separation would assume the desired Beilinson--Soulé connectivity.
16. `[POSITIVE SURVIVOR]` Every primitive curve-motive obstruction is smash-nilpotent with an
    explicit genus bound.  For `C` of genus `g`, the motive `h¹(C)` is Kimura-odd and
    `Sym²ᵍ⁺¹ h¹(C)=0`.  If `f : h¹(C) → ℚ(2)[0]` is an obstruction, the Tate target is
    even and invertible, so `f^{⊗(2g+1)}` factors through this zero symmetric power.  Thus
    `f^{⊗(2g+1)}=0`.  This does not imply `f=0`: nonzero smash-nilpotent motivic morphisms are not
    excluded in a non-semisimple triangulated category.  The sharpened target is vanishing of the
    tensor radical specifically in `Hom(h¹(C),ℚ(2)[0])`, not a general realization-conservativity
    claim.
17. `[ACTIVE CONJECTURE]` Covariant Hessian contraction with pair-level terminal correction.  For
    a squarefree binary form `F_S` of degree `r`, its Hessian has degree `2(r-2)` and is disjoint
    from the root configuration whenever `r-1` is invertible: at a root, write `F_S=XG`; then
    `det Hess(F_S)=-(r-1)²G²`.  Its normalized zero divisor therefore gives a finite
    `PGL₂`-covariant cone point for configurations of sizes `3,4,5` in characteristic different
    from `2,3`.  For a deletion `T⊂S`, the normalized-divisor defect is principal, represented
    explicitly by
    `Hess(F_T)^(2(|S|-2)) / Hess(F_S)^(2(|T|-2))` after the common-degree normalization;
    all square deletion coherences telescope at the rational-function level.  The remaining
    flag-homotopy term is supported at ordered pairs.  It cannot be removed by another finite
    covariant zero-cycle: the pair stabilizer torus acts transitively on the complement of the
    pair, so it has no nonzero finite invariant zero-chain there.  Required new step is a
    semilinear generic-pair homotopy whose field-automorphism defect is proved null on five-point
    syzygies while retaining the degree-one Bloch group.  Characteristics `2,3` additionally need
    replacement covariants.
18. `[FALSIFIED]` Use the multiplication-isogeny tail of a Jacobian to descend a translation-
    invariant obstruction to the constant field.  The corpus already proves that finite
    multiplication pullback levels are torsion-deck fixed fields and identifies their stable
    intersection.  This controls rational functions, not arbitrary `K`-classes.  The classical
    countermodel is `Pic⁰(A)_ℚ`: its classes are invariant under every translation and satisfy
    `[m]^*x=mx`, yet need not descend from the ground field.  The primitive weight-two class has
    exactly this isogeny eigenpattern, so dense torsion invariance alone is insufficient.
19. `[FALSIFIED]` Combine `[-1]^*=-1` on the primitive `h¹(A)` summand with `+1` K-theory
    duality on Adams weight two.  Pullback by group inversion is the Hopf antipode, whereas
    vector-bundle dualization is a distinct involution.  They agree on group-like line bundles
    only after the relevant Fourier--Mukai/Grothendieck-duality shift; that shift reconciles the
    apparent signs.  Identifying the two operations on the present higher class would assume a
    new higher-Picard vanishing theorem.
20. `[POSITIVE SURVIVOR]` The curve obstruction is a two-step derived-tensor defect.  Since
    `ℚ(1)[1]≅G_m⊗ℚ`, one has
    `ℚ(2)[0]≅((G_m⊗ℚ)⊗ᴸ(G_m⊗ℚ))[-2]`.  Therefore the exact curve core is
    `Hom_DM(h¹(C), ((G_m⊗ℚ)⊗ᴸ(G_m⊗ℚ))[-2])`.  A length-one flat or
    configuration resolution of `G_m⊗ℚ` would kill it while leaving the one-step Bloch group,
    but no such resolution exists in the corpus or audited literature.  This is the sheaf-level
    form of the required second coherent homotopy, not an independent shortcut.
21. `[FALSIFIED]` Apply the coordinate homotopy `u ↦ 1+t(u-1)` uniformly to the two
    `G_m` coordinates of the weight-two correspondence complex.  For a fixed simplex point, the
    transformed units are nonzero on an endpoint neighborhood.  Rational contractibility of
    `Cor(Δʳ×-,G_m^∧2)` requires a single open in `X×A¹` uniform over all of the nonproper
    affine simplex `Δʳ`; projection of the bad locus need not be closed.  Compactification shows
    the exact survivor: a projective-infinity correspondence whose alternating face boundary is
    the second coherence defect.  The dedicated supporting card
    `goncharov-weight-two-rational-kernel-contraction.md` now foregrounds its cancellation.

## Falsification targets for active conjecture

- dependent pairs of functionals, where stabilizers are parabolic rather than trivial;
- finite fields and fields with two or three elements, where generic-functional choices fail;
- compatibility of decomposition operators `D_{h₁}D_{h₂}=D_{h₁}` with the proposed second
  homotopy;
- a surviving stabilizer-homology class in degree two;
- hidden appeal to Beilinson--Soulé vanishing, rank-spectral-sequence degeneration, or a
  faithful regulator.
- the generic-cone residue profile may merely re-encode the original `K₄⁽²⁾` class; constant
  remainder must be shown null by a chain identity, not by homotopy invariance alone.
- Galois action is nontrivial on lower-weight Bloch groups, so trace-zero rigidity cannot be
  assumed from algebraic closedness or transfer; it needs a degree-two syzygy-specific proof.
- a Bloch--Kriz bar boundary may annihilate the obstruction after multiplication while leaving the
  primitive `N⁰(2)` class nonzero; bar decomposability is not weight-two vanishing.
- Bolbachan's proved comparison stops one degree above the obstruction.  Any proposed extension
  must explicitly fill the four-moving-factor ruled-surface class, not cite moving freedom.
- Chow--Künneth decomposition isolates the primitive `h¹(C)` group but does not vanish it by
  weights; negative motivic shifts are exactly where the connectivity conjecture is open.
- smash-nilpotence of the primitive curve class is a theorem, but tensor-radical vanishing is not;
  semisimplicity or faithful realization cannot be imported.
- Hessian covariants solve finite equivariant point selection only down to triples.  At pairs the
  torus stabilizer forbids a finite invariant cone, so any completion must control a semilinear or
  bar-homological defect rather than postulate another covariant point.
- multiplication-tail descent applies to field elements, not translation-invariant higher
  `K`-classes; `Pic⁰` is a direct counterexample to that inference.
- inversion and vector-bundle duality are different operations on an Abelian variety.  Their sign
  mismatch cannot be used without proving the missing higher-Picard comparison.
- a target-level rational homotopy must be uniform over the simplex variable.  Ignoring the
  projective-infinity locus silently assumes the kernel-preserving contraction.
