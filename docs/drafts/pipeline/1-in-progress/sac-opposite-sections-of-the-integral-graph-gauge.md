# Range of the integral lower sections

The lower section map is injective and each lower divisor has an integral upper dual. [Integral duality](../3-formalized/sac-duality-of-integral-endpoint-sections.md)

**Range question.** Each basis polynomial $x^{X-i}S_i(x)^2$ is reciprocal about degree $X-1$. At distance $h$ from the center its coefficient is $(i-h)_+$. The predicted image consists of all integral reciprocal arrays supported through $2X-2$, with inverse $u_{h+1}=a_h-2a_{h+1}+a_{h+2}$. Surjectivity onto this sublattice requires the converse reconstruction.
