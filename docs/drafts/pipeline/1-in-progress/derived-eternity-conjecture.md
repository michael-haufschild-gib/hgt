# Derived eternity conjecture

Status: `[CONJECTURE FROZEN BEFORE TARGETED LITERATURE SEARCH] [TRUTH HUNCH] [PROOF ROUTE UNKNOWN]`

## Public statement

> **Eternity is not an extremely long duration. It is a different mathematical phase from every
> finite observation.**

Stronger black-hole form:

> **Information can be absent from every finite view and survive in how all finite views fail to
> fit together.**

## Reinterpretation

Let an observer's resource levels form a directed tower. At level \(r\), the observer has a finite
or controlled history object \(H_r\). Coarsening maps compare what survives between levels.

The standard move is to call the ordinary inverse limit

\[
  H_\infty=\varprojlim_r H_r
\]

the exact history. The conjecture says this loses the decisive physics. Exact time is the
**derived temporal completion** of the tower. Its degree-zero part contains compatible observable
histories; its higher obstruction contains failures of compatible finite reconstructions to
globalize.

For a countable abelian tower, the first candidate obstruction is

\[
  \mathfrak A_{\mathrm{time}}=\varprojlim\nolimits^{1}_r H_r .
\]

Call this the **temporal anomaly**.

This is not a metaphorical use of cohomology. The proposed physical distinction is exact:

- degree zero: information represented by one compatible completed history;
- degree one: information represented only by the incompatibility of all attempted finite-depth
  reconstructions;
- vanishing anomaly: every compatible reconstruction problem glues globally;
- nonvanishing anomaly: every finite level can look recoverable while exact recovery fails.

## Why this is a jump

The usual black-hole question asks where information is stored: outside, inside, radiation,
remnant, correlations, or another universe. The conjecture adds a logically different answer:
information need not be stored in any single observational layer. It may be a global obstruction
class of the entire observer tower.

The usual recurrence question asks how long one waits. The conjecture says exact recurrence may
not be the limit of longer finite-horizon recurrence tests at all. It can change when the ordinary
resource tower is replaced by its derived completion.

The usual problem of time asks which variable is the clock. The conjecture says the deeper issue is
which completion functor turns all finite clocks into exact time.

## Joined conjectural law

> **Derived temporal conservation law.** For every physically closed universe and every observer
> tower, information is conserved in the total derived temporal completion. Change of observer can
> move information between visible histories, invisible kernels, and gluing-obstruction classes,
> but cannot destroy the total derived class.

If true, ordinary rank conservation is only the degree-zero shadow. Horizon information transfer
is a connecting morphism. Exact irreversibility is failure of an observer semigroup to become
cancellable in degree zero while remaining conservative after derived completion.

## Risky consequences

### C1 — finite recovery does not imply exact recovery

There must exist physically admissible observer towers in which every finite-depth reconstruction
problem is solvable but no compatible exact reconstruction exists. The obstruction must be
calculated by the temporal anomaly rather than inserted by definition.

### C2 — a sharp no-paradox class

For towers satisfying a concrete exactness condition, expected to be a Mittag--Leffler or
uniform-splitting condition, the temporal anomaly must vanish. In that class, compatible finite
recoverability must imply exact recoverability. No permanent information paradox can then arise
from taking the observational limit.

### C3 — recurrence instability is an obstruction phenomenon

Dense discontinuity of exact recurrence dimension, despite uniform stability on every fixed
finite horizon, must be realizable as a change in the completed or derived tower. A theorem must
separate ordinary arithmetic instability from a genuinely nonzero derived anomaly.

### C4 — horizon formation is a boundary map

For a globally reversible system split into outside and hidden observer towers, horizon formation
must induce an exact-sequence boundary morphism carrying lost visible information into a hidden or
obstruction degree. Evaporation must return that class, transfer it, or leave a nonzero residual
anomaly.

### C5 — “before time” becomes noncircular

A pre-temporal influence is not an event at negative external time. It is a nonzero class that
vanishes in every later finite clock quotient but survives in the derived completion. If no such
class exists, “before time” is observationally and mathematically empty for that universe.

### C6 — order of idealized limits becomes measurable structure

Infinite observation time, infinite spatial resolution, zero noise, and thermodynamic size must
define comparison maps between different towers. Noncommuting idealized limits must produce a
calculable anomaly rather than an informal warning about limit order.

## Fatal tests

Reject the conjecture if any of these occurs:

1. the proposed anomaly is always zero for physically relevant observer towers;
2. nonzero classes are artifacts of a chosen algebraic presentation and lack observer-invariant
   meaning;
3. known black-hole information measures cannot be functorially related to the tower;
4. recurrence discontinuity remains entirely degree zero and supplies no derived signal;
5. the theory permits “hidden information” without any possible consequence, reducing it to
   metaphysics;
6. derived limits already have this complete physical interpretation in external literature;
7. the finite theorem shadow requires assuming the desired information conservation.
