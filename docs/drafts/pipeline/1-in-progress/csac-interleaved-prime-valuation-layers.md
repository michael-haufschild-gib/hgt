# Intersections between two prime valuation layers

At a prime p, pair p-free indices a with indices pb for p-free b, and dilate both supports by d. Positive integer-ratio prime-power contact forces a=b. The poles and the off-diagonal real jet scale by 1/d. Determine the resulting sign and mixed energy bound on the full Weil form, with the prime contact also tested against its Liouville twist. Quantify dependence on the cutoff when the two supports interleave and grow.
