# QIC radial exclusion at the extremal abscissa

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Source:** [QIC](../../../reports/rh-current-frontier-map.md#qic)

The global radial exclusion law is equivalent to absence of a lone off-line quartet. Bounded discrepancy pins the trial exponent, excludes every other right zero and gives the exact odd-harmonic frequency and multiplicity relation. [Formalized classification](../3-formalized/qic-the-extremal-trace-of-the-ordinary-prime-radius.md)

**Open arithmetic step.** Exclude that last pair and its reflection. A genuine quartet predicts its own ordinary-prime radius within O(√x log²x) at its frequency and multiplicity. One possible contradiction would bound the translated prime pairing of one fixed pole-neutral exponential triple from one side for every nonnegative translation. The quartet predicts unbounded excursions of both signs. [Two-interval amplitude](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/QuadratureOfTwoConsecutivePrimeIntervals.lean#L132) · [Translated pairing](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/TranslatedProbeTentDecompositionAndPoleNeutralTriples.lean#L869)

**Confined index.** Window index counts distinct earlier crossings. Each bounded range contains finitely many. Under failure of RH the index equals two on an interval and the second state detects every right ζ zero. A third state would detect at least one right zero and could miss at most one conjugate pair. Failure of RH without a lone quartet forces a third crossing. Three crossings give a combination annihilating the Euler and prime-square charges whose ordinary-prime wave has infinite exponentially damped mass of both signs. [Prime excursions](../3-formalized/qic-the-ordinary-prime-wave-on-the-third-confined-span.md) Its exclusion remains open. **Zero traces.** At every crossing the Laplace values are square-summable over all ζ zero copies. The Cauchy transform of the reflected off-line products has strictly negative real part on Re p > 4. For both rungs of a lone quartet, critical-line mass within one unit of height y is O((1 + y²)⁻¹), and (w + 5)LF(w) is bounded throughout Re w ≥ −4. It tends uniformly to zero at infinity on each Re w ≥ b > −4. Both states have vanishing mean-square wall trace. For every sufficiently large y, the boundary power and critical-line traces within one absolute radius of y together contribute at least 2aM/y², where aM is the confinement energy. **Ground-state cusp.** The cosine loss of the reflected samples over the [whole zero divisor](../3-formalized/qic-the-cosine-cusp-of-the-whole-zero-divisor.md) has slope $4aM$, where $aM$ is the confinement energy. For a finite off-line divisor, the critical-line samples alone carry this slope and their [first absolute ordinate moment diverges](../3-formalized/qic-critical-line-cusp-of-the-ground-state.md).
