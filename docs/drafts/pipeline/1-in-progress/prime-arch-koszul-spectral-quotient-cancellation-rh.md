## Why the scalar producer is dead

The scalar tensor proposal asked for a positive `K_infinity` with moments `q_n`.  Direct
theta-source Taylor computation gives negative shifted Hankel minors at the canonical exponent
two, including

```text
det(q_(2+i+j))_(0<=i,j<6) < 0,
det(q_(8+i+j))_(0<=i,j<4) < 0.
```

Therefore no positive measure and no positive compact operator can have the full residual tower.
This is not an accidental numerical pathology.  Lean proves in the two-atom model that pointwise
division of positive moment towers creates a negative ghost term and can produce a negative
`3 x 3` Hankel determinant, while pointwise multiplication remains a positive tensor spectrum.
