and let `L_epsilon` be the differential field generated over the finite-prime Dirichlet field by

The question is no longer whether the inverse limit exists.  It is: what geometry is forced on the

## Competing moves

### A. Tail-subspace field lattice

`[CONJECTURE]` For a family `E subset X`, let `U_E` be the complex span of its sign classes modulo
finite support, and let `L(E)` be the field generated by all complete palettes in `E`.  Then

```text
L(E) subset L(F)  <->  U_E subset U_F,
L(E) L(F)          =   L(U_E + U_F),
L(E) intersect L(F)=   L(U_E intersect U_F).
```

For a finite shift set `A` and finite-dimensional `U`, the corresponding field has differential
transcendence degree `card(A) * dim(U)`.  Thus the finitely generated tail-subspace lattice embeds
as a modular lattice of differential fields; its projective points are precisely the individual
orientation fields.

**First attack.**  Once the shifted tail differential matroid is available, this is close to the
standard intersection law for rational function fields on independent variable blocks.  It may be
a strong geometric organization but not a new mechanism.  It must not be promoted on elegance
alone.

### B. Maximal profinite/field incompatibility

`[PROVED]` Give `X` its canonical product topology.  Every same-field class is countable and
dense, the quotient topology on `X / (= field)` is indiscrete, and every continuous same-field
invariant with values in a `T0` space is constant.

**Boundary cases.**

- Countability: a class consists of finite sign changes of `epsilon` and `-epsilon`.
- Density: every finite cylinder meets every class by changing only its prescribed coordinates.
- Indiscreteness: a nonempty saturated open set contains a cylinder; every class meets that
  cylinder, hence saturation gives all of `X`.

This is a positive carrier law: any nonconstant field-sensitive selector must be tail-discontinuous
at every orientation for the profinite topology, or must enrich the carrier with source-sensitive
topology.

### C. Exact `E_0` groupoid law

After enumerating the primes, let `E_0` be eventual equality on Boolean sequences and let
`E_0^+-` be eventual equality up to global complement.  The field equivalence is `E_0^+-`.

`[PROVED]` It is continuously, hence Borel, bireducible with `E_0`:

1. `E_0^+- <=_B E_0` by the discrete derivative
   `D epsilon(n) = epsilon(n) xor epsilon(n+1)`;
2. `E_0 <=_B E_0^+-` by anchored duplication
   `A(x)(2n)=x(n)`, `A(x)(2n+1)=0`.

The first map works because equality of discrete derivatives on a tail forces the relative sign to
be eventually constant.  The second map prevents eventual complement by its fixed odd coordinates.
Consequently the same-field relation is hyperfinite and nonsmooth.  There is no Borel complete
invariant in a standard Borel space and no Borel global representative selector.

## Paper proof

### 1. The tail-subspace field functor

Let

```text
V_fr = Span_C {[epsilon] : epsilon in X} subset C^Primes / C^(Primes).
```

Every class in `V_fr` has a bounded finite-range representative.  For `v in V_fr`, choose such a
representative `v_tilde` and put

```text
P_v(s) = sum_p v_tilde(p) p^(-s).
```

Changing the representative adds a finite Dirichlet polynomial and therefore does not change any
field generated over `E_fin`.  For a subspace `U subset V_fr`, define

```text
L(U) = E_fin < P_v(s+a(q)) : v in U, q in script-U >.
```

This does not depend on a basis.  If `v = sum_i c_i u_i` in the tail quotient, then for every shift
`a`

```text
P_v(s+a) - sum_i c_i P_u_i(s+a) in E_fin.
```

Thus any basis of `U` generates `L(U)`.

The signed shifted-tail theorem extends from sign vectors to finite families in `V_fr`: put all
vectors into the span of finitely many signs, choose a tail-independent subfamily of those signs,
and apply the existing theorem followed by a full-row-rank linear change of coordinates, separately
at every shift.  Hence a tail-independent family of `r` vectors on a finite nonempty shift set `A`
gives `r * card(A)` differentially algebraically independent carriers.

If `U` is not contained in `W`, choose `v in U \ W`.  Any expression placing one coordinate of
`P_v` in `L(W)` uses only finitely many vectors of `W`; together with `v` they are tail-independent
after choosing a basis, contradicting the preceding paragraph.  Therefore

```text
L(U) subset L(W)  <->  U subset W.                         (1)
```

Joins are immediate from generators.  For meets, choose a basis `C` of `U intersect W`, extend it
by `A` to a basis of `U` and by `B` to a basis of `W`.  The union `C union A union B` is linearly
independent.  All of its shifted jets are algebraically independent over `E_fin`, so evaluation
identifies the generated field with a rational function field on three independent variable
blocks `X_C, X_A, X_B`.  The standard rational-field intersection identity gives

```text
E_fin(X_C,X_A) intersect E_fin(X_C,X_B) = E_fin(X_C).
```

This proves the meet law in finite dimension.  In general, any element of a field intersection
uses finitely many generators on both sides, so the finite-dimensional result applies.  Therefore

```text
L(U + W)       = L(U)L(W),
L(U intersect W) = L(U) intersect L(W).                    (2)
```

For finite-dimensional `U` and finite nonempty shift set `A`, the same basis argument proves

```text
dtrdeg_E_fin L_A(U) = dim(U) * card(A).                    (3)
```

Equations (1)--(3) give the claimed modular field lattice.  Individual sign fields form a
distinguished subset of its projective points; they coincide exactly on eventual equality or
eventual opposition.

### 2. Dense same-field strata and indiscrete quotient

Fix `epsilon in X`.  Its same-field class consists of `epsilon` and `-epsilon` followed by arbitrary
finite sign changes.  It is countable.  Given a nonempty finite cylinder prescribing signs on
`F`, modify `epsilon` only on `F` to meet the prescription.  Hence every field class meets every
cylinder and is dense.

Let `O subset X` be nonempty, open, and saturated under same-field equivalence.  It contains a
finite cylinder `C`.  Every field class meets `C`, and saturation therefore puts every point of
`X` in `O`.  Thus the orbit quotient has only the empty and total open sets.  Any continuous
same-field invariant into a `T0` space factors through this indiscrete quotient and is constant.

### 3. Exact `E_0` classification

After enumerating primes, write Boolean orientations as `x in 2^N`.  Define

```text
D(x)(n) = x(n) xor x(n+1).
```

If `x` and `y` are eventually equal or eventually complementary, then `D(x)` and `D(y)` are
eventually equal.  Conversely, eventual equality of the derivatives makes

```text
r(n) = x(n) xor y(n)
```

eventually constant, because `D(x)(n)=D(y)(n)` is exactly `r(n)=r(n+1)`.  The constant is `0` or
`1`, giving eventual equality or eventual complement.  Thus `D` continuously reduces same-field
equivalence to `E_0`.

For the reverse reduction define

```text
A(x)(2n)=x(n),       A(x)(2n+1)=0.
```

Eventual equality of `x,y` gives eventual equality of `A(x),A(y)`.  Eventual complement of
`A(x),A(y)` is impossible because both have value `0` at every odd coordinate.  Therefore
same-field equivalence of `A(x),A(y)` forces eventual equality of `x,y`.  This continuously reduces
`E_0` to same-field equivalence.

The relation is consequently Borel bireducible with `E_0`.  The classical nonsmoothness theorem
for `E_0` implies that no Borel map to a standard Borel space can be a complete same-field
invariant, and no Borel transversal can choose one orientation in every embedded-field class.

### 4. Canonical replacement

Let

```text
Gamma = (direct sum over Primes of Z/2Z) times Z/2Z.
```

The first factor acts by finite sign changes and the last factor by global reversal.  The action is
free and every orbit is dense.  Its transformation groupoid has exactly the embedded-field classes
as its orbits.  Unlike the indiscrete orbit set, this countable hyperfinite groupoid retains both
the finite-chamber approximants and the exact infinite same-field relation.  The all-positive
orientation is a distinguished positive-definite object of this groupoid.
