return record of any physical reporter for which
\[
 C_{\mathcal O}(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{it\omega}\,

\]

Write
\[

 b_{\mathcal O}=\nu_{\mathrm{pp}}(\mathbb R).
\]
Let \(K_{\mathcal O}\) be the compact closure of the phase orbit generated by
the atoms of \(\nu_{\mathrm{pp}}\), let \(m_{\mathcal O}\) be its normalized
Haar measure, and let
\[

   =\sum_{\omega\in\operatorname{At}(\nu_{\mathcal O})}
      \nu_{\mathcal O}\{\omega\}\,z_\omega .
\]
Then the following statements hold for every such record.

1. **Complete recurrence-distribution law.** For every bounded continuous
   \(g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R\),
   \[
   \lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1{2T}\int_{-T}^{T}
      g(C_{\mathcal O}(t))\,dt
   =
   \int_{K_{\mathcal O}}g(P_{\mathcal O}(z))\,
      dm_{\mathcal O}(z).
   \]
   Thus every long-time statistic of the return record is determined by its
