## Stage-1 theorem extracted: the arithmetic interval is Krein, not Hilbert

For one logarithmic action \(L>0\), put
\[
 \Theta_L(x,y)
 =\frac{e^{(x-1/2)L}-e^{(y-1/2)L}}{x-y},
 \qquad
 \Theta_L(x,x)=L e^{(x-1/2)L}.
\]
On \(L^2(0,L)\), let \(J_Lf(t)=f(L-t)\) and
\(v_x(t)=e^{(x-1/2)t}\). Then
\[
 \boxed{\Theta_L(x,y)=\langle v_x,J_Lv_y\rangle_{\rm bil}}
 \tag{K1}
\]
for all complex \(x,y\), with the diagonal supplied by the removable value.
Thus the exact Gamma factorial/co-Poisson interval cell has a common-test
factorization, but its metric is the reflection involution \(J_L\).

This is not a hidden positive Gram. For distinct real \(x,y\), writing
\(u=(x-y)L/2\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \det
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \Theta_L(x,x)&\Theta_L(x,y)\\
  \Theta_L(y,x)&\Theta_L(y,y)
 \end{pmatrix}
 &=
 L^2e^{(x+y-1)L}
 \left(1-\left(\frac{\sinh u}{u}\right)^2\right)<0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{K2}
\]
Indeed \(|\sinh u|>|u|\) for \(u\ne0\). Hence every nontrivial action cell has
exact inertia \((1,1)\) on every two-character packet. No incidence-preserving
primewise Hilbert factorization exists, even before taking an infinite limit.

For the ordinary von-Mangoldt source, let \(L_n=\log n\) and, for
\(\Re s,\Re w>1\),
\[
 K_\Lambda(s,w)
 =
 \frac{\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)n^{-w}
       -\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)n^{-s}}{s-w}.
\]
Absolute convergence and `(K1)` give the exact source-global Krein
factorization
\[
 \boxed{
 K_\Lambda(s,w)
 =\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)
   \int_0^{L_n}e^{-st}e^{-w(L_n-t)}\,dt.}
\tag{K3}
\]
The canonical left and right feature families are not vectors of the ordinary
orthogonal direct-sum Hilbert space: near \(t=0\) one leg has no decay in
\(n\), while reflection moves the missing decay to the other endpoint.
Nevertheless their codiagonal `(K3)` converges. This is the literal
singular, infinite, source-pointed common-test factorization of the prime
current.
