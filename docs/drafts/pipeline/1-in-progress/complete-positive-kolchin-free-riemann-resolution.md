## Question closed by the theorem

Can every coefficient of the Riemann zeta function be assigned to exactly one positive arithmetic
channel so that

1. every channel carries the complete zeta divisor with multiplicity on the RH half-plane; and
2. after the common zeta factor is removed, the complete family has maximal differential rank
   even over the differential field generated by zeta; while
3. analytic completion nevertheless forces one exact infinite relation?

The earlier high-exponent frame answered this only for a tail of the coefficients.  The missing
head is not an error term: it is the `K`-free Euler channel.  Adding that channel turns the frame
into a complete positive resolution.

## Candidate theorem

For an integer `K >= 12`, put

```text
A_(K,0) = {n >= 1 : no p^K divides n},
A_(K,m) = {n >= 1 : max_p v_p(n) = m},       m >= K.
```

Let

```text
F_(K,0)(s) = sum_[n in A_(K,0)] n^(-s),
F_(K,m)(s) = sum_[n in A_(K,m)] n^(-s).
```

Then:

### A. Exact positive resolution

The sets `A_(K,0), A_(K,K), A_(K,K+1), ...` are pairwise disjoint and partition the positive
integers.  Hence, coefficientwise and on `Re s > 1`,

```text
zeta(s) = F_(K,0)(s) + sum_(m>=K) F_(K,m)(s).            (1)
```

Every coefficient in every channel is `0` or `1`.

### B. One complete divisor in every channel

Write

```text
R_m(s) = 1 / zeta(ms),
H_m(s) = R_(m+1)(s) - R_m(s).
```

The exact Euler identities are

```text
F_(K,0) = zeta R_K,
F_(K,m) = zeta H_m.                                     (2)
```

On `Re s > 1/2`, `R_K` is holomorphic and zero-free, and the proved head-dominance theorem makes
every `H_m`, `m>=K`, holomorphic and zero-free.  Therefore every channel in `(1)` has exactly the
zeta zero divisor there, including multiplicities.

### C. Maximal relative differential rank of the complete resolution

The family

```text
{R_K} union {H_m : m >= K}                              (3)
```

is jointly differentially algebraically independent over

```text
C(s)<zeta>.
```

Equivalently, after the common divisor is adjoined and removed, no polynomial differential
identity involving any finite set of normalized channels remains.

The proof is forced and triangular.  The dilated Möbius carriers

```text
R_m(s) = sum_(d>=1) mu(d) d^(-ms)
```

have distinct lowest total-prime-factor grades `m`.  One prime derivation lowers grade `m` to
`m-1` and leaves a single pure-prime ray; finite jet Jacobians are prime-log Vandermonde blocks.
Thus `{R_m:m>=K}` is jointly differentially independent over `C(s)<zeta>`.  For every finite
prefix, the change of coordinates

```text
(R_K,H_K,...,H_M) <-> (R_K,R_(K+1),...,R_(M+1))
```

is unitriangular.  Differential independence is therefore preserved, proving `(3)`.

### D. Kolchin-free analytic partition of unity

The normalized channels obey no differential-polynomial relation involving finitely many
coordinates, even after adjoining zeta and all its derivatives.  Nevertheless their ordered
analytic completion is exactly

```text
R_K(s) + sum_(m>=K) H_m(s) = 1.                         (4)
```

Indeed every finite prefix telescopes:

```text
R_K + H_K + ... + H_M = R_(M+1),
```

and `R_m(s) -> 1` as `m -> infinity` for every `Re s>0`.  More strongly, convergence is uniform
on each closed half-plane `Re s>=delta>0`.  Thus `(4)` is a locally normal analytic relation that
cannot be detected by any finite differential-algebraic expression.

This finite/infinite phase transition is the conceptual theorem: a countable family can be
maximally Kolchin-free in every finite direction and simultaneously resolve the identity after
analytic completion.  Equations `(1)` and `(4)` are its arithmetic and analytic faces.

### E. Universal cutoff law

The conclusion holds for every `K>=12`, not one fitted decomposition.  Changing `K` merely fuses
the finite block

```text
A_(K,0), A_(K,K), ..., A_(L-1,L-1)
```

into the `L`-free head.  Thus zeta carries a nested countable family of complete positive,
isodivisorial, Kolchin-free resolutions.

## RH consequence

If RH is false, every off-critical zero, with its exact analytic multiplicity, occurs
simultaneously in every member of every resolution `(1)`, despite:

- pairwise disjoint positive coefficient supports;
- exact fusion to the original all-ones coefficient stream;
- maximal finite differential-algebraic independence after zeta and all its derivatives are
  admitted as coefficients.

This is not zero exclusion.  It proves that a zeta zero is compatible with a complete positive
resolution into arithmetically disjoint channels and cannot be explained or eliminated by any
finite differential-algebraic coupling among those channels.  Any successful attack through the
resolution must use a genuinely infinite analytic operation.  Equation `(4)` identifies that
operation exactly: locally normal ordered completion.  It converts the earlier vague escape clause
into a proved boundary theorem.

## Projective-tomography attack and hostile rejection

The high-shell limit admits a complete coefficient-extraction flag.  Put

```text
c_n(s)=mu(n)(n^(-s)-1),
P_(m,Q)(s)=sum_(n<Q) c_n(s)n^(-ms).
```

For every `Re(s)>0` and every positive integer `Q`,

```text
lim_(m->infinity)
  Q^(ms) (H_m(s)-P_(m,Q)(s))
    = mu(Q)(Q^(-s)-1).                                  (5)
```

The proof expands `H_m` as the difference of two absolutely convergent Möbius series once
`m Re(s)>1`, removes the first `Q-1` terms, and applies dominated convergence to

```text
c_(Q+k)(s) ((Q/(Q+k))^s)^m.
```

The full `Q`-hierarchy and its `Q=2` smallest-prime specialization passed an exact standalone
formal check.  It is mathematically correct and gives an elegant projective boundary for the
shell tower.

It is **rejected as headline novelty**.  Equation `(5)` is ordinary Dirichlet-series coefficient
uniqueness written as a successive asymptotic blow-up.  Specializing the recovered coefficients
to Möbius does not constrain zeta zeros, improve a zero-free region, or create a new RH mechanism.
It may appear only as a supporting asymptotic interpretation if a stronger theorem independently
passes the significance gate.

## General Euler-product extension

The `Omega`-leading-echelon proof does not depend on the values `mu(d)` being exactly `+-1` except
for nonvanishing of the prime leading coefficients.  It should extend to completely multiplicative
Euler weights with nonzero prime coordinates:

```text
Z_a(s) = product_p (1-a_p p^(-s))^(-1),
R_(a,m)(s) = product_p (1-a_p^m p^(-ms)).
```

Whenever a domain supports the meromorphic continuation of `Z_a` and the high-level gauges
`R_(a,m+1)-R_(a,m)` are units, the same coefficient partition is a complete isodivisorial
Kolchin-free resolution.  This extension is optional for the first paper; it may not be claimed
until proved both on paper and formally.
