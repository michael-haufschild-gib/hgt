## Frozen public conjecture

> **Space is the geometry of return.**

> **Your place in the universe is the complete pattern of what can leave you,
> affect something else, and come back.**

> **A horizon is not merely far away. It is where return ceases to exist.**

This is not the familiar statement that signals travel through a supplied
spacetime and can return. It reverses the dependency:

```text
rooted physical return laws
  -> adjacency, distance, spatial topology, horizons, and recurrence.
```

The graph or spacetime is an output, not an input.

## The ontological jump

Conventional recurrence theory asks whether a state evolving in a known
system returns close to itself. The conjecture says that the family of all
rooted return possibilities is prior to both “state” and “where”:

- a physical location is an equivalence class of roots with the same return
  atlas;
- adjacency is the first nonfactorizable closed history shared by two roots;
- distance is the minimum order required to close a history through another
  root;
- a connected component is a maximal mutually returning sector;
- a horizon is a directed failure of round-trip closure;
- long-time quantum recurrence is the terminal phase symmetry generated by
  the same closed histories.

Return is therefore not one phenomenon occurring in space. Spatial geometry
and recurrence are the short-history and infinite-history faces of one
object.

## Frozen finite-network conjecture

Let a finite operational quantum process supply:

1. distinguishable source and reporter ports;
2. source composition and controlled deformations;
3. rooted return reporters;
4. no subsystem grouping, interaction graph, metric, Hamiltonian, or spatial
   coordinates.

For a root \(x\), form the complete multivariate germ of every rooted return
response under independently switchable ports. Apply source and reporter
contrasts so that a sector indexed by a finite port packet \(S\) contains only
closed histories that genuinely use every label in \(S\). Let
\[
  b_x(S)\in\mathbb N_0\cup\{\infty\}
\]
be the least total control order of a nonzero initial form in that sector,
minimized over a fixed source-complete probe family. Retain the full labelled
initial form, not merely \(b_x(S)\).

The conjecture is that for every finite locally generated process:

1. **Return reconstruction.** The complete rooted return atlas determines the
   minimal interaction hypergraph relevant to the ports, up to operational
   gauge and removal of dynamically silent structure.
2. **Exact metric law.** For every bidirectionally coupled pair of roots
   \(x,y\),
   \[
     b_x(\{y\})=d(x,y)+d(y,x).
   \]
   In a Hermitian undirected local process this becomes
   \[
     \boxed{b_x(\{y\})=2d(x,y).}
   \]
3. **No genericity escape.** Exact equality is obtained by the
   source-complete labelled initial form or an explicit minimization over
   probes. “Almost every Hamiltonian” is insufficient.
4. **Topology.** Finite return order is mutual reachability. The strongly
   connected components and their condensation order are recovered from the
   atlas.
5. **Horizon.** A directed operational horizon from \(x\) across \(B\) occurs
   exactly when histories from \(x\) can enter \(B\), local return within
   \(B\) is nontrivial, but no source-complete history through \(B\) can close
   back at \(x\).
6. **Terminal recurrence.** The exact projective character relations among
   all rooted return phases determine the compact observer-visible
   recurrence hull, and every return distribution is a harmonic functional
   of it.
7. **One object.** Items 1--6 are functorial projections of one valued
   labelled closed-history object. Graph reconstruction followed by a
   separately supplied recurrence calculation does not satisfy the
   conjecture.

The finite statement is deliberately stronger than the trivial graph fact
that a shortest closed walk through \(y\) has round-trip length. The unknown
content is that complete physical return responses isolate exactly those
walks without prior access to vertices, edges, a matrix model, or a fitted
generator, and that the same invariant supplies terminal quantum recurrence.
