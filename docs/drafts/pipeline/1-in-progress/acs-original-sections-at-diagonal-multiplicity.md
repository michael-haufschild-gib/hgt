# Original sections at diagonal multiplicity

For $D_n=e_1+2e_{n^2}-e_{(2n+1)^2}$, does the full section lattice at $m=n$ contain $\exp(cn^2)$ sections on arbitrarily late conductors? On a cofinal set the signed Weil degree $Q(D_n)$ is nonnegative. At every sufficiently large conductor the actual ordinary floors save at least $\delta n^2$. It suffices to show that the radius minus the full log generator, after removal of the ordinary floor loss and division by $n^2$, differs from $Q(D_n)$ by $o(1)$. The coefficient-independent quadrature estimate applies at every positive prime-power lag. Proper-power floors cost at most $\psi(L)-\theta(L)$, with $L=O(n^2)$. The positive continuous pole vanishes. [Prime-power quadrature](../3-formalized/acs-the-mesh-error-at-diagonal-multiplicity.md)

The remaining analytic comparison concerns the regularized degree-point pairing at the moving horizon. An integer dilation $d$ replaces the resolution $r$ by $dr$ for a single translated packet. The two high packets have relative dilation $(2n+1)^2/n^2\to4$. One can try to freeze this ratio, control the change using spline derivatives, and bound the interaction with the low packet using the decay of the jet kernel. The fixed-packet limit then applies separately to both clusters.
