## Public sentence

The prime--Gamma source is one static constraint with one coherent completion. Completion cannot
turn its unique lift into two inequivalent FE/Schwarz lifts. An off-line Xi zero is exactly such a
`1 -> 2` completion error.

## Reinterpretation

Do not model a zero as a particle, resonance, spectral level, flow orbit, or object produced by an
action. A zero is a failure of constraint hyperdescent at the unique arithmetic end.

Finite arithmetic presentations form a refinement category. Before physical completion, one
literal reflected-minus-forward source relation has one coherent lift. At a completed Xi fibre,
functional-equation and Schwarz specialization give two candidate lifts. On the critical line they
are the same lift. Off the line they form a discrete two-point fibre, detected by the transverse
dual-number class, Route-R completion phantom, prime-seam charge, Gamma-six curvature, and Noether
orientation charge.

The proposed new field is arithmetic constraint hyperdescent: study when completion of an
arithmetic constraint diagram preserves the homotopy type of its lift fibres at infinity.

## Frozen conjecture shape

Let `C_arith` be the target-free refinement category built from literal finite prime support,
valuation depth, theta/Mobius rung, Gamma sewing, and internal jet base `J_m`. Let
`Lift_m : C_arith^op -> Type` be the groupoid of coefficient-faithful lifts of the fixed source
relation over `J_m`. Let `Box_arith` be physical source completion.

`[CONJECTURE: ACH]` On the full subcategory generated by the literal Xi source relation,
`Box_arith` is a left-exact end-hypercompletion and the comparison from hypercompleted lift data to
the physical FE/Schwarz lift fibre is an equivalence.

Equivalent local form for the one required diagram:

```text
Box_arith (Eq(source forward, source reflected))
    ~= Eq(Box_arith source forward, Box_arith source reflected).
```

Equivalent fibre form: a contractible finite-source lift fibre remains contractible after physical
completion. It cannot become `S^0`, the two-point reflected fibre.

This is not accepted as a theorem by defining `Box_arith` to be sheafification. The physical
comparison map must be constructed coefficient-faithfully from the existing Mobius--theta,
Euler--Gamma, valuation-depth, and source-marked completion maps, then proved an equivalence without
using any RH-equivalent endpoint.
