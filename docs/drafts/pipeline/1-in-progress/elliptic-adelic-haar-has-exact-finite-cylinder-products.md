# Elliptic adelic Haar has exact finite-cylinder products

- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Parent:** [Arithmetic unfolding](finite-field-eisenstein-unfolding.md).
- **Target:** On the open subgroup integral outside a finite place set `S`,
  actual adelic Haar equals the product of normalized Haar on the fields in
  `S`, Haar probability on the remaining integer rings, and infinity Haar.

## Mechanism

Coordinate assembly identifies the cylinder with the stated product as a
topological additive group. Both measures are Haar and assign mass one to
the same full integral product, so Haar uniqueness identifies them.
A radial product kernel equals one on the compact tail. Fubini then gives
its cylinder integral as a finite product of the audited local integrals.

Finite-place cylinders exhaust the actual adeles. Nonnegative monotone
convergence and the audited analytic Euler product would identify the global
integral. Complex absolute domination is a further step. Arbitrary-idele
Haar scaling, needed for root-coordinate Jacobians, also remains.

Topology, measure identification and the global integral require Lean proofs.
