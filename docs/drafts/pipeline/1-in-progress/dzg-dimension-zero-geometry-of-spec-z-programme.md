## Status
- §2.8 as posed is Weil positivity: descent from any dominating family; the prime-blind family
  `kD + 2R_A(k)‖·‖²` dominates iff `Q ≥ 0`; null tests force the 2-jet to pay Selberg's `Λ₂`.
- Real Tate twist = shifted pair `Λ(s−k)Λ(s+k)` = tilt of the jump measure by `cosh(kt)`; negative on
  every window for large `k`, positive on windows `A ≤ 3/5` for `|k| ≤ 1/200` (critical Tate
  dimension of a short window in `[1/200, ∞)`). Imaginary twist = unitary modulation, tilt by
  `cos(θt)`, positivity preserved window by window.
- Jordan family: positive for `k > 0`, sign `(−1)^{ω(n)}` for `k < 0`, vanishing order `ω(n)` at `0`.
  Wall = Dirichlet inversion: `J_{−k}(n) = (−1)^{ω}rad^k n^{−2k}J_k(n)`, centred `K_{−k} * K_k = 1`,
  `K_{−k}(n) = μ(n)K_k(n)` on squarefree `n`; `K_k = exp(c_k)` on windows, `c_k = 2ℓ sinh(k log n/2)` odd.
  Shifted datum Tate positive in all real `k ≥ 0` with non-Weil-positive weight: Tate positivity does
  not descend.
- Tate flow equation `∂_kJ_k = DJ_k + J_k*Λ`; on windows `U′ = [M,U] + Uρ(Λ)`; all jets `ρ(Λ_j)` lie in
  the algebra generated by `M` and `ρ(Λ)`.
- Hecke similitudes of `Q` in the window algebra are scalars; isometries `±1`; `σ_A` an isometry.
- Archimedean place alone not Weil positive; finite-level cone local (`n ≤ e^{2A}`), convex, weight-free
  below the first prime, one weight at the first fold. Archimedean Tate cocycle `Γ_ℝ(s−k)/Γ_ℝ(s)`,
  finite in even dimension. Completed centred family unimodular on the critical line (exact balance
  of the two places by the functional equation).
- Stage step: exact ground-state representation in every positive gauge.
- Zero modes: radical, translation stable, touch both ends at a first critical length; at an edge
  `Q^{(k)} = Q(H(cosh−1)) − Q(H sinh)`, `Q^{(iθ)} = Q(H(cos−1)) + Q(H sin)`.
