# A gap for integrable functional-equation defects

- **ID:** integrable-fe-defect-gap
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CONJECTURE
- **Source:** Γ74
- **Statement:** A countable Beurling system with sufficiently small integrable uncompleted Riemann functional-equation defect is exactly the rational-prime system.

## Proposed mechanism

Write `ρ=2β` for the pole residue. Radial residues identify the Abel cosine spectrum: value `ρ/(2π)` at zero, atomic multiplicity divided by `2π` at each generalized integer, and zero below the first positive frequency 1.

If two distinct frequencies differ by less than 1, positivity of the three-point Gram with centre 0 gives `ρ²≥2`. If `ρ<√2`, each atomic multiplicity is an integer at most `ρ<2`, hence one, and consecutive frequencies are separated by at least 1. Their Abel density is then at most 1. Unit-density rigidity forces the ordinary spectrum. Thus the possible residues lie in `{1}∪[√2,∞)`.

Existing small-defect rounding and tent-mass estimates should force `ρ<√2` for an absolute defect threshold. Their exact constants and the passage from a countable Beurling system to its ordered norm spectrum remain to be proved.

## Open proof steps

- Formalize the three-point Gram inequality and the Abel density bound for a separated spectrum.
- Derive an explicit upper bound for `ρ` from the existing small-defect theorem.
- Discharge the ordered-spectrum representation for the actual Beurling integer measure.
