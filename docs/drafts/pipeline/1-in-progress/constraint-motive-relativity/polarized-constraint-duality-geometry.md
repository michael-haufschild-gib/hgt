## 0. Answer to the core question

The sought coordinate-free geometry cannot have one fixed cotangent bundle. The corpus proves
that the type of duality changes with the observer:

- a Hankel face loses an **affine normal coordinate**, not a linear kernel;
- recurrence is controlled by an **integral relation lattice** `Lambda_omega` and its
  **annihilator phase hull** `Ann(Lambda_omega)`, canonically
  `widehat(Z^d/Lambda_omega)`;
- hypertranscendence is measured by a **relative differential cotangent/prolongation object**;
- completion can turn an operator into a **multivalued closed relation**;
- Stokes and positive routes use **ordered or Hermitian polarization and finite inertia**;
- source and physical horizon charts may require **trace duality, local/global duality, action,
  and edge data**.

PCDG treats this variation of duality type as geometry. Its analogue of a metric is not one
bilinear form. It is a source-selected system of pairings, torsors, lattices, relations, and costs,
together with the comparison correspondences that say when two such systems describe the same
law.

> **Polydual equivalence principle.** No observer doctrine is preferred before a source law and
> an admissible comparison have been specified. Two route descriptions are gauges of one another
> only when their marked law/reporter pairing is transported by an invertible comparison
> correspondence. Noninvertible quotient, completion, scalarization, positivity, and forgetting
> operations are information-reducing doctrine changes. Invertible instances are gauges.

This differs from another attempt to find a source-native RH selector. PCDG's first task is to
classify which information each route can express, which doctrine changes erase it, and which
mixed invariants survive more than one route. The source selector remains an eventual field
equation, not the definition of the geometry.

## 1. Primitive candidate: an indexed relational Chu variance schema

The earlier six-tuple of laws, reporters, relations, admission, order, and cost was an inventory,
not one mathematical primitive. The smallest common variance pattern justified by the corpus is a
relational Chu **schema**, not yet a constructed equipment.

A realization must fix an indexed monoidal virtual equipment
`p:E->D`, or equivalent pseudofunctor with specified base-change proarrows, together with a
coefficient section `Omega` over the doctrine base `D`. In the fibre over `d`, a polarized object
has law sort `L_d`, contravariant reporter sort `O_d`, and evaluation proarrow

\[
 \mathfrak M_d=(L_d,O_d,e_d),
 \qquad e_d:L_d\otimes O_d\rightsquigarrow\Omega_d.
\tag{PCDG.1}
\]

For a doctrine arrow `u:d->e`, require coherent monoidal base-change adjunctions. A paired
transition consists of forward law transport `f`, the adjunct backward reporter transport
`bar(g)`, coefficient transport `chi_u`, and an evaluation-compatibility multicell:

\[
 f:u_!L_d\rightsquigarrow L_e,
 \qquad \bar g:u^*O_e\rightsquigarrow O_d,
 \qquad \chi_u:u_!\Omega_d\rightsquigarrow\Omega_e,
\tag{PCDG.2}
\]

In a represented strict chart its equation is

\[
 e_e\bigl(f(u_!x),o'\bigr)
 =\chi_u\!\left(u_!e_d\bigl(x,\bar g(u^*o')\bigr)\right).
\tag{PCDG.2a}
\]

In a relational chart this equation is replaced by the boundary multicell with the same typed
proarrows. Monoidal/base-change coherence is part of the realization, not inferred from fibrewise
data.

Affine torsors, integral lattices, differential modules, ordered cones, graph relations,
admission, capacity, and cost remain native fibre payload. Chu typing does not identify them; it
only gives the common law-forward/reporter-backward variance and a place for comparison cells.
Separate fibrewise equipments do not type `(PCDG.2)`; the indexed base-change structure is
load-bearing and remains to be built.

Virtuality is essential. Closed linear relations are not closed under unqualified relational
composition. One must use all relations and keep closure as a separate doctrine modality, specify
a composition-stable admitted class, or retain an unrepresented multicell. No closed composite is
fabricated.

A realized total field would further require a source-history base `B`, a map `B->D`, and a marked
section `Delta:B->E` over it. No such indexed equipment or section is currently constructed.
Shulman's Chu construction and virtual-equipment/profunctor frameworks are separate parent
machineries; their combination with these heterogeneous fibres is not automatic. Merely placing
existing route objects in a product or naming their fibre types does not construct the field.

### 1.1 Global topology: directed stratification and observation nerve

The linked gauge-collapse theorem proves that not all route transitions can be gauges. If a
pseudofunctor of doctrine fibres `F:D->Cat` were conjugate to one carrier by equivalences, every
transition `F(u)` would itself be an equivalence. The corpus contains exact transitions that
identify nonisomorphic affine successors, lattices, prolongations, reporters, orientations, or
histories. Therefore any realized global object must retain a directed Grothendieck stratification

\[
 \mathcal E=\int_D\mathcal F,
\tag{PCDG.1a}
\]

not localize all doctrine arrows into gauges.

Let `J:P_src->E` be a boundedly described category of literal pre-target source probes. Its
observation nerve is

\[
 N_J(X)(p)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal E}(Jp,X).
\tag{PCDG.1b}
\]

The exact density theorem says `N_J` is fully faithful iff every object is canonically reconstructed
as the colimit of all probe maps `Jp->X`. This supplies the missing global gluing criterion:

> **Source observer-density conjecture.** The combined arithmetic, spectral, affine, differential,
> ordered, topological, and history probes are jointly dense in a literal pre-zero HGT category.
> Which individual doctrine families are dense or lossy must be determined theorem by theorem;
> the marked Mellin measure and full Mellin reporter already give individually coefficient-faithful
> examples.

Equation `(PCDG.1a)` is conditional: gauge collapse proves that a successful realization must have
this directed form, but does not construct `F`, its heterogeneous indexed equipment, or its total
category. Using every object as a probe is tautological and forbidden. Until a literal probe
category is constructed and density is proved or falsified, the observation nerve is a field
design, not the global HGT geometry.

The linked dense-source non-descent theorem gives an exact consequence once density is proved. If
the complete nerve factors through a doctrine exit `L`, then `L` must be faithful, reflect
isomorphisms, and reflect object isomorphism. Hence a literal exit that collapses affine
successors, integral winding, differential rank, algebraic reporters, vertical relations, or
orientation cannot carry the complete source geometry. No later serial observer repairs that
loss; only a jointly conservative parallel family can avoid it. This is a route-independent
irreversibility law, not a scalar curvature equation.

## 5. Field law II: inverse-capacity/vertical-fibre correspondence

One cross-route law is already dependency-closed. For increasing finite observer domains and a
fixed finite port, the minimum-lift quotient norms decrease to a limiting seminorm `q_inf`. Its
kernel is exactly the multivalued vertical fibre of the closed limiting graph. In Hilbert charts,
`q_inf(y)^2=<Q_inf y,y>` for the decreasing inverse-capacity operators, so

\[
 \operatorname{mul}\overline{\Gamma(T)}=\ker q_\infty
 \quad\text{and, in Hilbert charts,}\quad
 \operatorname{mul}\overline{\Gamma(T)}=\ker Q_\infty.
\tag{PCDG.3}
\]

Thus vanishing minimum lift cost is transmuted into a vertical law relation. The Hilbert inverse
capacity realizes the prime augmentation completion. The general quotient-seminorm form realizes
the published Cartan graph-null sequence, including the case where its evaluation is already
relation-valued on the seminorm radical. This is not a universal field equation, but it is the
first exact transition between capacity/quotient metric and topological-relation doctrines.

 W_{\rm cap}=\{x:\sup_n\langle G_nx,x\rangle<\infty\},

 \boxed{W_{\rm cap}=\operatorname{ran}Q_\infty=M^\perp},

Any proposed universal invariant `I` that factors only through an unmarked linear hyperbolic

1. two Mass moment extensions with the same positive singular Hankel face and opposite outgoing

## 7. Hypertranscendence

PCDG does not define HT as noncompactness. In a differential chart, a named generator is
hypertranscendental when its differential-polynomial evaluation map has zero kernel. Equivalently,
for one generator its finite jet locus through order `N` has algebraic dimension `N+1`; for `r`
differentially independent generators the dimension is `r(N+1)`. This persistent new jet rank,
not nonstabilization of prolonged ideals, is the native infinitesimal datum. A finite observer
orbit may recur or even be periodic while the marked branch/prolongation support needed to prove
relation-freeness grows without bound.

The field-level statement is:

> HT is persistent jet-level algebraic freedom in the differential duality doctrine. Jet growth,
> lifted-history divergence, normalization singularity, unbounded jet cost, absent infinitesimal
> generator, and cofinal completion defects are doctrine-relative data. A comparison theorem may
> relate particular instances, but none is a universal image or definition of HT.

Required theorem: construct an actual comparison correspondence sending one formal relative
differential class to a nonzero history, capacity, or topology defect. No such universal map is
currently proved.

One literal mixed chart is now proved, but it is not universal. The marked high-exponent shell
source `a_m` has genuine analytic HT, a positive Mellin--GNS realization with compact resolvent,
norm-precompact recurrent observed orbit, and divergent unwrapped history. On its marked
pure-prime skeleton, the same source recovers Mass half-density, reflection-odd Weil tangent, and
prime phase coefficients. Thus HT can coexist with compact observed dynamics. In this chart,
unbounded differential rank, lifted-history divergence, and a singular normalization boundary
coexist as distinct data; no common conserved noncompactness invariant is constructed.

## 8. Positivity and phase

Positivity is not universally a phase quotient. In an ordered chart it is a constitutive
reduction that may forget:

- `U(1)` phase;
- a `mu_2` root/coorientation;
- an indefinite negative direction;
- a relative Green primitive;
- an affine outgoing orientation.

The published one-negative-square Stokes law is therefore a special finite-index polarization
chart. Its one port constrains how much information can survive a pairing-preserving realization,
but regular coisotropic reduction can reduce arbitrarily large rank to one port. The load-bearing
question is admission and source normalization of that reduction, not Witt index alone.

The marked shell chart gives a precise normalization obstruction. Its finite positive GNS fibres
exist for `sigma>1`; matching the physical pure-prime half-density profile forces
`sigma=1/m<=1/12`, where the shell mass is infinite. Positive and half-density realizations are
therefore different doctrine fibres, not bounded gauges of one Hilbert space.

## 9. Recurrence, time, and open lifted history

Recurrence lives naturally in the integral/Pontryagin chart. Frequencies `omega in R^d` produce
the relation lattice `Lambda_omega` and phase hull
`Ann(Lambda_omega)=widehat(Z^d/Lambda_omega)`. The phase path lifts to the universal cover `R^d`,
whose deck transformations lie in `Z^d`. Unbounded winding or cost requires an additional marked
history functional and does not follow from recurrence alone. The prime-phase theorem and
weighted capacity theorem show additional independent domain walls: a recurrent continuous port
may exist while its infinitesimal logarithmic generator is not admitted.

This is the exact mathematical content behind the Brown--Susskind intuition currently justified
by the corpus:

- boundary projection can recur in a compact observer closure;
- lifted history can remain open and accumulate cost;
- endpoint state, recovery error, or Krylov endpoint distance does not determine path action.

The finite framed GHZ theorem supplies an exact observer-atlas calibration. Two one-boundary
marginals are constant on the phase fibre. Pairwise coherence reporters indexed by a graph
reconstruct the phase point exactly when the graph is connected; a spanning tree is minimal among
such reporters. Lifted edge histories reconstruct winding, but executed action still requires the
time-parametrized quotient path and its metric. Thus state density, history density, and cost
coercivity are three different gates even in a finite non-RH model.

The compact-observer theorem gives a complementary infinite-dimensional law. A compact reporter
may be injective on every finite source chart while all inverse-gauge norms diverge cofinally. Its
positive shadow `K^*K` retains this singular-value horizon, and every bounded lifted history has
compact observed image. Compactness alone does not imply recurrence; a separate phase lattice is
still required.

PCDG does not identify this lift with wormhole length. A gravity realization must derive an edge
charge, symplectic pairing, Hamiltonian/action, and reconstruction map from the physical source.

## 11. Falsifiable predictions

1. **No bare-double scalar master obstruction.** Any scalar invariant factoring through rank,
   signature, determinant, or another invariant of the unmarked bare double `H(K)` admits the
   three countermodels above.
2. **Capacity predicts verticality.** Whenever finite port quotient norms tend to zero, the
   limiting graph acquires exactly that port as multivalued part; in Hilbert charts this is
   equivalent to inverse-capacity collapse. A claimed closable realization with the same cutoff
   metrics is false.
3. **Recurrence does not reconstruct time under history identification.** If two marked histories
   have the same identified observer endpoint and unequal cost, then that cost cannot factor
   through endpoint and recurrence data. Equivalently, any claimed cost factorization is refuted
   by an explicit such pair; identification alone is insufficient without the unequal-cost
   witness.
4. **Specified positive reductions may lose oriented data.** Maps such as `x mapsto x^*x`, norms,
   determinant-squares, and polar quotients erase symmetry orbits on which they are constant. A
   proof requiring that lost datum must act before that particular reduction. Positivity alone
   does not imply erasure.
5. **Reporter-rank law.** For a finite algebraic reporter port `L`, retained Hausdorff reduction
   rank is `dim L_X` and doctrine-loss rank is `dim(L/L_X)`.
6. **HT can coexist with recurrence.** Finite or local observer periodicity does not bound marked
   differential-prolongation support.
7. **Physical horizon test.** Any proposed black-hole/wormhole realization must produce a
   source-derived symplectic/costed comparison to at least one arithmetic or spectral chart. A
   common low-rank phase portrait is insufficient.
8. **Density is not coercivity.** Even an injective compact observer that is exact on every finite
   source chart has cofinal inverse norm tending to infinity. Any claimed HT or spectral
   reconstruction through a compact observer must exhibit a noncompact reporter, a new topology,
   or an explicit renormalized cost.

**Research heuristic, not a prediction.** Cofinal rank sometimes reappears as normal jet depth,
lattice winding, vertical multivalued fibre, or differential growth. No general rank-transmutation
law is asserted until a typed comparison produces it.

### 11.1 Conjecture schema: source-rational verticality

Let `Y` be a finite-dimensional real vector space (or the underlying real space of a complex
one). Instantiate this schema only after naming a literal source realization, an injective
full-lattice embedding `iota:Lambda->Y` and identifying `Lambda` with its image, its cofinal norms,
and compatibility axioms relating the lattice maps to every cutoff.
Suppose those data give vertical fibre

\[
 M=\ker q_\infty\subset Y
\]

PCDG asks whether the named source compatibility forces

\[
 M=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(M\cap\Lambda).
\tag{PCDG.5}
\]

Writing `Lambda_M=M intersect Lambda`, this would give

\[
 0\to\Lambda_M\to\Lambda\to\Lambda/\Lambda_M\to0
\]

and the Pontryagin-dual exact sequence

\[
 0\to\widehat{\Lambda/\Lambda_M}
 \to\widehat\Lambda
 \to\widehat{\Lambda_M}\to0.
\tag{PCDG.6}
\]

Then vertical ambiguity rank and lost integral-relation rank would become two shadows of one
source-compatible subobject. It becomes a recurrence law only after proving that this same
`Lambda`, rather than a nearby lattice or coefficient marking, carries the actual phase dynamics.
Before that comparison it is a capacity/topology/arithmetic compatibility conjecture.

It is false for generic polydual data. Take `Y=R^2`, `Lambda=Z^2`,
`v=(1,sqrt(2))`, and

\[
 G_n=I+nvv^T.
\]

For an exact realization, put `H_n=R^2 direct-sum R^n` with standard inner product and define

\[
 T_n(x,a)=x+\Bigl(\sum_{j=1}^n a_j\Bigr)v.
\]

Then `T_nT_n^*=I+nvv^T`. The inverse capacities satisfy

\[
 Q_n=(I+nvv^T)^{-1}\longrightarrow P_{v^\perp},
\]

so `M=ker Q_infinity=Rv`, but `M intersect Z^2=0`. Therefore `(PCDG.5)` is not categorical
kinematics. A literal source theorem must explain why its incidence, grading, or integral
provenance excludes this irrational model. Failure would kill this proposed higher bridge.

The linked finite lattice-direction theorem proves one sharp sufficient condition. If

\[
 G_n=A_0+\sum_{i=1}^r c_i(n)a_i a_i^T,
 \qquad a_i\in\Lambda,
\]

with a fixed finite direction set and nondecreasing weights, then

\[
 \ker Q_\infty
 =\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{a_i:c_i(n)\to\infty\}
 =\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(\ker Q_\infty\cap\Lambda).
\tag{PCDG.7}
\]

This is a four-doctrine theorem, not the conjecture schema in full. It also proves finite-cutoff
lattice provenance insufficient: a cofinally changing sequence of rational directions can have
an irrational limiting kernel. Three literal corpus families meet the operator hypotheses:
weighted prime ports, positive Stokes multi-Rees conormals, and rooted prime-support HT Hodge--Green
rays. Only the weighted prime port is already a native nested-port realization. None identifies
the Pontryagin torus in `(PCDG.6)` with its actual recurrence carrier. Future applications must
therefore prove finite direction-type provenance and same-lattice dynamics separately, rather
than inspect each cutoff or borrow a nearby phase torus.
