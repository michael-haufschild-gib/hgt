## 1. Direct answer to the core question

The strongest current answer is:

> **Field conjecture.** Proof routes admitting a CMR realization are local charts of a stratified
> geometry of source laws, observer admissions, and law-changing events. Invertible overlaps are
> gauge transformations; noninvertible doctrine exits are event or admission maps, not gauges.
> Current evidence consists of three nonisomorphic exact charts; no all-route realization theorem
> is claimed.

The field is not a manifold of zeta zeros. Its points are source-law presentations together with
admissible observer structures. It has two qualitatively different motions.

1. **Horizontal source response:** deformation remains inside one equivalence stratum. A
   connection transports law classes, reporters, normalization, phase, and determinant lines.
   Invertible chart changes are local gauges.
2. **Vertical law event:** admission, exact structure, rank, contact order, domain, or topology
   changes at a discriminant. Nearby/vanishing-cycle, crossing-form, connecting-morphism, or
   derived base-change data measure what is born or lost. These arrows are not gauges.

This distinction is the missing dynamics in earlier CMR/PCDG cards. It is also the precise reason
the request “differentiate Stokes inertia under a coefficient intervention” failed: inertia is
locally constant horizontally and changes only through a vertical event.

Three tempting identifications are too strong and are not field axioms:

1. Hypertranscendence is persistent unbounded prolongation rank; it need not make the observed
   orbit noncompact. Noncompactness may instead occur in an unwrapped history lift or singular
   normalization/completion boundary.
2. Positivity is a phase or scale quotient only for a declared degree-zero or scalar reporter.
   Full positive Gram/covariance data may retain relative phase.
3. Recurrent compact observation has an open lifted history only after a marked cover, action, or
   cost is supplied; the same recurrent observation can also have a compact lift.

The sought geometry must type these alternatives and predict which one a source selects. It may
not promote a useful model into a universal equivalence.

No novelty claim for constructible sheaves, spectral flow, Stokes moduli, information geometry,
Gauss--Manin transport, or vanishing cycles is required. This program deliberately permits a
Frankenstein of known machinery. Acceptance criterion is stronger: one literal source must produce
the structure maps and at least one out-of-sample prediction.

## 3. Field laws

### Law H — horizontal covariance

Inside one stratum, source operations transport the full marked law object. A route change is a
gauge only if that transport is invertible and respects all marked ports, domains, and pairings.
Raw moving-ideal Gamma transport is an exact example.

### Law A — observer admission

Each physical route chooses a fixed observer doctrine. Transport in the moving source fibration
need not descend conservatively to that fixed fibre. Its defect is a typed admission holonomy, not
ordinary curvature by default.

### Law E — event transgression

Signature, nullity, homology, exactness, domain, or contact order changes only through a wall event.
At a regular one-port quadratic wall, the event orientation is the crossing form. For chain
exactification it is a connecting morphism or coframe-extension anomaly. For HT degeneration it is
nearby/vanishing-cycle or derived base-change data.

### Law P — polarization separates shape from central orientation

Degree-zero normalized scalar reporters live on projective/relative directions. Determinant,
trace, or orientation lines retain central scale. Reflection may square a central unsquared defect
into a positive energy, but that energy does not select its vanishing. General positive
operator-valued reporters need not erase relative phase.

### Law C — conservative change requires anomaly accounting

Given a pointed linear event map, a reporter separating a killed class cannot descend through that
map. When an exact event triangle exists, this descent failure may be represented by transgression
to the event object. No transgression dichotomy is asserted without such exact comparison data. A
moving gauge which makes the class horizontal does not establish vanishing in the fixed observer.

## 4. Exact six-chart evidence

### 4.1 Mellin normalization: horizontal shape response

For a positive coefficient source `c`, normalization has

\[
 DP_c[h]=Z_c^{-1}(\mu_h-H_0(h)P_c),
\tag{SRG.4}
\]

with radial kernel `R c`. Phase response is its Fourier transform and bounded-score response is a
covariance. This is horizontal, projective response inside one source cone.

### 4.2 Stokes: vertical contact event

Inside one Puiseux contact stratum, all derivatives of the coefficient Cartan form, capacity gap,
and inertia vanish. Activating one higher cross-cluster coefficient changes the law by

\[
 \Delta Q
 =\frac{\Delta}{2}\bigl((X_U-X_V)^2-M^2\bigr),
\tag{SRG.5}
\]

while keeping determinant port `M` fixed. At a smooth rank-one model wall,

\[
 u^T\dot Qu=c^2\dot\Omega,
\tag{SRG.6}
\]

so spectral-flow sign is `sgn(dot Omega)`. This is vertical event response, not smooth inertia
differentiation.

### 4.3 Kummer--Gamma corona: fixed-observer admission holonomy

Raw Gamma scaling `G_t=M_(p^(-2t))` is a two-sided group generated by `-2 log p`. In the fixed
corona `C^P/ell^1` it is admitted only for `t>=0`, and for `t>0` it is surjective but noninjective.
Its finite holonomy is

\[
 G_tF_t-F_t=F_{-t}-F_t=\beta_t.
\tag{SRG.7}
\]

If the ideal moves as `I_t=G_tI`, transport becomes tautologically flat and the class horizontal.
The nonzero Bockstein arises from returning to one fixed physical observer. Thus “curvature” here
is not raw source curvature; it is failure of conservative descent to the fixed observer.

These three charts do not prove a common geometry. They show that any common geometry covering
them must distinguish horizontal transport, vertical events, and fixed-observer admission.
Horizontal covariance alone misses Stokes events. Event geometry alone misses fixed-observer
admission. A scalar total obstruction class is not yet justified.

### 4.4 Valuation coframe: raw event-rank budget

For `N` independently coframed source classes, any chain event killing all `N` classes has
reporter-transport anomaly rank at least `N`. A one-dimensional raw port cannot exactify them.
The top exterior anomaly is nevertheless a line isomorphism. This is the precise mathematical
reason a physical one-port object must be a determinant/orientation receiver after a full-rank
event, rather than a faithful linear compression of the source.

### 4.5 Fredholm: genuine pre-zero spectral events

Two nonidentical spectral receivers are now exact.

1. A divisor-weighted coefficient stream `a` has a coefficient-faithful trace-class Helson
   operator `K_a(j,k)=a_(jk)`. At a simple eigenvalue wall, its crossing covector is the
   independently determined divisor convolution

   \[
   h\longmapsto-\sum_n h_n\sum_{jk=n}\overline{u_j}u_k.
   \tag{SRG.7a}
   \]
2. The completed theta/Gamma source canonically constructs a compact source-Grushin relative
   operator before choosing a divisor point. Kernel of the affine source pencil is equivalent to
   a reciprocal eigenmode of that fixed operator.

The first gives exact coefficient response but does not source-select its threshold/eigenline. The
second source-selects the Fredholm object but its global determinant trace/multiplicity bridge and
local Stokes comparison remain open. Neither is the full field functor.

### 4.6 Gamma--Rees: local event equivalence and cofinal singularity

For every prime, the signed Gamma Bockstein coordinate and adjacent-depth Rees normal coordinate
are analytically equivalent event germs:

\[
 \beta_p(t)=-\widetilde g_p(t)w_p(t),
 \qquad
 \widetilde g_p(0)=
 \frac{4p^3(\log p)^2}{(p+1)^2(p-1)(2p-1)}.
 \tag{SRG.7b}
\]

The comparison constants tend to zero like `2(log p)^2/p`. Hence the primewise charts have no
uniformly bounded inverse on the unweighted all-prime `ell^2`, `c0`, or `ell^infinity` models.
Local event equivalence therefore forces a source-selected weighted/pro topology or a nonclosed
global correspondence. This is the first literal source example in which an exact local law event
creates a cofinal topology requirement.

The literal arithmetic Stokes twist is common to every block:

\[

It leaves Stokes contact data, coarea, capacity gap, and inertia unchanged. Only determinant

Likewise, projectivizing the prime coefficient source multiplies the fixed ideal by a nonzero

This is a genuine failed conjecture loop, not lack of calculation.

## 6. Narrowed open conjecture: arithmetic determinant-compatible Fredholm--Stokes event functor

The open problem is no longer to construct *some* pre-zero Fredholm response. Section 4.5 supplies
two. It is to source-select one response, lift it through the full reporter-rank determinant, and
identify its line orientation with the local Gamma--Rees/Stokes event before scalar
trivialization.

Let `B_EG` be a pre-divisor category of ordinary Euler--Gamma coefficient sources and signed finite
interventions. Seek a source-natural functor

\[
 \boxed{

 \longrightarrow
 \mathsf{FStokes}^{\mathbb R}_{1\text{-port}}}
\tag{SRG.9}
\]

to determinant-oriented real one-port Fredholm Stokes polarizations. It must be defined without a
chosen Xi zero.

For source `c`, let

\[
 \pi_c=Z_c^{-1}\sum_n c_nn^{-\sigma}\delta_{\log n}.
\tag{SRG.10}
\]

The functor must construct a port gap `widehat Omega_c` and a directed boundary observable `W_c`
such that

\[
 \boxed{
 D\widehat\Omega_c[h]
 =\int W_c\,d(D\pi_c[h]).}
\tag{SRG.11}
\]

In the exact coefficient space of Section 4.1, equation `(SRG.11)` alone has no content if `W_c`
may be an arbitrary element of the continuous dual tangent space: at fixed `c`, `D pi_c` has kernel
`R c` and is onto the mass-zero atomic tangent space, so every continuous covector vanishing on `c`
has such a representation, unique modulo constants.
It counts only if:

1. target category, gap functor, boundary-observable class, topology, and growth bounds are fixed
   before `Phi`;
2. `widehat Omega_c` is constructed from a Fredholm/Stokes operator or polarization without using
   `W_c`;
3. `W_c` is constructed from an independent boundary-current functor without differentiating
   `widehat Omega_c`;
4. the atomwise predictions
   \[
   D\widehat\Omega_c[e_m]
   =\frac{m^{-\sigma}}{Z_c}
     \left(W_c(\log m)-\int W_c\,d\pi_c\right)
   \tag{SRG.11a}
   \]
   are computed before comparison and then extended by continuity;
5. at least one atomwise response is nonzero.

Equation `(SRG.11)` also forces `D widehat Omega_c[c]=0`; if valid along every positive ray, the
gap is scale-invariant. Determinant orientation may retain scale separately. A scale-sensitive gap
falsifies `(SRG.11)`.

The Helson receiver realizes `(SRG.11)` conditionally, with `W_c` fixed by a simple eigenline.
That is a theorem, not the open core. The missing conditions are source selection of the base
operator/threshold/eigenline, the full finite-cutoff coframe determinant upstream of the scalar
wall, and compatibility with a local boundary event. The source-Grushin receiver supplies a
source-normal operator but does not yet supply those three comparisons.

At a transverse wall `widehat Omega_c=0`, `D widehat Omega_c[h] ne 0`, the functor must construct
an isomorphism between the determinant crossing-orientation torsor and the source
Bockstein-orientation torsor. Sign agreement is asserted only under this independently constructed
line-level comparison; agreement after unrelated scalar trivializations does not count.

This is intentionally stronger than naming a vanishing-cycle receiver. It requires an actual
pre-zero source functor, independently defined observable, and one nonzero coefficient-computed
crossing.

### Required non-holonomic scale

The full HT Dirichlet source cannot be faithfully carried by a fixed finite-rank holonomic
horizontal system: its coordinates would satisfy a finite-order linear differential equation.
Therefore a faithful realization of `(SRG.9)` must be pro-infinite, Fredholm/nonholonomic, derived
with unbounded complexity, or explicitly lossy with its kernel classified.

## 7. Universal lemmas, chart consequences, and field tests

### Universal response lemmas

1. If a differentiable reporter satisfies `R(tc)=R(c)` for every `t>0`, then `DR_c[c]=0`.
2. Let `Delta subset X`, and let `I:X setminus Delta -> D` be locally constant with `D` discrete.
   Every continuous path whose endpoints have different `I`-values intersects `Delta`.
3. Let `f:V->W` be linear, `x in ker f`, and `alpha in V*` satisfy `alpha(x) ne 0`. Then no
   `beta in W*` satisfies `alpha=beta circ f`.

The first proof differentiates the scaling identity. For the second, a path avoiding `Delta` has
connected image, so its discrete locally constant reporter is constant. For the third,
`alpha(x)=beta(f(x))=beta(0)=0`, a contradiction. These are route-independent but kinematic.

### Literal chart consequences and open field tests

1. **Stokes staticity.** The common block-scalar twist `lambda_i=delta_n(s)` in the present Puiseux
   chart has zero Stokes crossing response. A claimed nonzero inertia derivative from that twist is
   false.
2. **Positive radial blindness.** Every differentiable degree-zero reporter kills the positive
   radial tangent. Therefore it contains no first-order information about deformations confined to
   that ray. No stronger conclusion about determinant orientation follows without separately
   marked line/scale data.
3. **Bockstein rigidity.** Nonzero scalar normalization, finite-support diagonal changes, bounded
   diagonal multipliers satisfying the proved cofinite lower bound, and moving-ideal transport
   preserve or re-express fixed-corona Bockstein status.
4. **Coframe anomaly.** Any fixed-corona chain intervention killing a nonzero finite prime
   combination while preserving the fixed ideal must make at least one valuation-coframe element
   fail closed/admitted extension.
5. **HT scale requirement.** No fixed finite-rank holonomic horizontal system can contain a
   faithful horizontal coordinate equal to the full HT carrier.
6. **Recurrence requirement.** Recurrence of one fixed unitary clock supplies no law event by
   itself. A crossing requires varying law/admission data and meeting a discriminant.
7. **Mass/affine requirement — unproved.** A first-unseen Mass successor or outgoing flux should be
   vertical extension data, not a horizontal gauge invariant of the current truncated face.
8. **Route-E requirement — unproved.** Capacity-preserving horizontal flow should not create a new
   boundary incidence relation; allocation/seam changes should live in event data.
9. **Reporter-rank requirement.** Exactifying `N` independently coframed prime classes forces
   anomaly rank at least `N`; a one-port receiver can occur only after determinant/contraction
   transgression.
10. **Cofinal-normal requirement.** Primewise Gamma--Rees event equivalence does not extend through
    an unweighted boundedly invertible all-prime diagonal map. Any global chart must expose its
    weights, pro topology, or nonclosed range.

Universal lemmas 1--3 are closed formal consequences. Chart items 1--4 are theorem-backed only in
their named literal charts. Items 5--8 remain field requirements; none is yet a common-primitive
theorem across two routes.

## 9. Falsifiers

The field-dynamics conjecture fails if:

1. source stratification is defined using Xi-zero locations or desired physical support;
2. `W_c` is defined retrospectively from `D widehat Omega_c`;
3. moving observer fibres are identified with a fixed physical fibre without an admitted
   comparison;
4. a smooth path is claimed to change a locally constant rank/contact/inertia object;
5. a wall object is declared but no specialization or connecting map is constructed;
6. all examples are finite classical toy models with no literal source realization;
7. Mass, Route E, recurrence, HT, or Stokes are called gauges despite noninvertible doctrine exits;
8. the only nonzero event is equivalent to RH.
