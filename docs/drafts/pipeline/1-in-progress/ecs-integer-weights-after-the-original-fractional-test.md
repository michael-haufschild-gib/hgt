# The integer weights after the original fractional test

Put $X=e^T$, $m=\int\Phi$, $A=\int e^{-v/2}\Phi(v)\,dv$, $N(s)=\sum_{k\ge1}k^{-1/2}\Phi(s-\log k)$ and $D(s)=N(s)-e^{s/2}A$. The whole tested weight $W_T(x)$ satisfies $W'_T(x)=m x^{-3/2}D(T-\log x)$. At the moving far edge the normalized derivative of the actual complementary weight tends to $-mA$. The sign of this coefficient is open. [Moving-edge theorem](../3-formalized/ecs-tested-state-weight-at-the-moving-far-edge.md).

**Lower integer weights.** Write $a=(m/2)\int e^{v/2}\Phi(v)\,dv>0$. The whole tested weight satisfies $\sqrt X W_T(n)=a+o_\Phi(\sqrt{n/X})$ uniformly as $X/n\to\infty$. At the moving far edge $p=\log(e^r+\sqrt{e^r r})$, the scaled omitted strip is bounded by $|m|\int_{r-p}^0 e^{v/2}|\Phi(v)|\,dv$. This gives the individual truncated-weight limit $a$ for $r,T-p\to\infty$ on paper. [Whole-weight theorem](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheSignedIntegerWeightsBelowTheTestAbscissa.lean#L155).

**Signed time endpoint.** For $t>6T$ the integral of the entire oriented row beyond $e^t+e^{t/2}\sqrt t$, through any terminal time $M\ge6T$, is $O_\Phi(e^{-T/10})$. At fixed $v=t-\log n\le0$, the moving edge and an independent lower integer cutoff leave one terminal interval; its signed reciprocal Mangoldt excess is $O_d(k^{-d})$ for every $d<3001/24000$, $d>0$. [Lean proof](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheLateTimeEndpointOfTheTestedIntegerRows.lean).

**Signed lower integer range.** On $T/6<t\le6T$, the actual complementary row through $N$ contributes $a(\psi(N)-N)/\sqrt X+o_\Phi(1)$ uniformly for $1\le N\le CX^{2/3}$, for every fixed $C>0$. The result includes all centers. [Moving-prefix proof](../3-formalized/ecs-chebyshev-discrepancy-in-the-lower-integer-prefix.md).

For comparison, Abel summation against the [tilted $L^2$ integer error](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/SubcriticalMeanSquareOfTheIntegerQuadrature.lean) gives the candidate upper bound $O_\Phi(X^{-1/2}N^\theta+X^{-\beta}N^{\theta-1/2+\beta})$ for the full weight through $N$, for $\theta>\Theta$ and $0<\beta<1/2$. The first term pays the endpoint. Adding the same wall-strip cost gives $o(1)$ for $N=X^a$, $a<1/(2\Theta)$. This reaches beyond $X^{2/3}$ when $\Theta<3/4$. Its formal Abel estimate is open.

**Verified upper integer range.** On every bounded time interval containing $T$, the infinite signed contribution past $k\ge X^4 e^s$ is $O_\Phi(e^{-s/8})$, uniformly for $T\ge1$ and $s\ge0$. This includes the original middle test with its moving far edge. [Upper integer tail](../3-formalized/ecs-upper-integer-tail-of-the-original-prime-test.md).

**Upper reciprocal term.** For $T=(7/8)\log k$, all $n>k$ contribute $mA\sqrt X\,R(k)+o_\Phi(1)$ to the original middle integral with its moving edge. Here $R(k)=H_k-\sum_{n\le k}\Lambda(n)/n-2\gamma$. Arbitrarily large $k$ satisfy $|R(k)|\le2/\sqrt k$. Along these cutoffs the upper contribution vanishes for either sign of $mA$. [Moving-edge and small-value proofs](../3-formalized/ecs-reciprocal-prime-discrepancy-above-the-moving-edge.md).

**Signed upper weights.** Splitting $\{Y/n\}$ at $Y=Xe^{-v}$ gives a reciprocal term and decreasing floor weights. Abel summation bounds the whole signed error above $k$ by $O_{\Phi,d}(\sqrt X k^{-d}X/k)$ for every $0<d<1-\Theta$. Inside the interval around $X$, proper powers have a limiting distribution in $\log n-T$ and an explicit total charge. [Ordinary-prime deficit and square charge](../3-formalized/ecs-proper-power-charge-inside-the-diagonal-interval.md).
