Open core of question 1:

1. *Non-escape.* Proved for `Re τ > 0` (card `galois-zero-forward-tameness-of-the-heat-cover`).
   For `Re τ ≤ 0` there is no gain from the Gaussian and the statement is of the strength of
   zero-spacing results for ζ.
2. *Collisions away from the centre.* A real collision at a positive time exists iff RH
   fails. Real branch times exist in every neighbourhood of the time `0`, or zeros escape
   there. At nonreal forward times they exist, with `Re τ` arbitrarily large (M4). Not shown:
   that the real branch time is a collision at a real place (needs continuation through
   collisions at nonreal places).
3. *Irreducibility* of the cover over forward times: proved (`G₊(b)` transitive on the zeros).
   Over all times open.
4. *Accumulation* of branch times: none at a forward time (finitely many in a box); open on
   `Re τ ≤ 0`. `c` has simple zeros at forward times of arbitrarily large real part; whether
   every zero of `c` is simple is open.
5. *The group over forward times.* `G₊(τ)` is a group of restricted signed permutations of
   the pairs `{z, −z}`, the same up to conjugation at every base time, a quotient of the
   fundamental group of the forward half-plane without the branch times. It contains `cr` at
   real base times, the turn around every forward branch time, and a sign flip of some pair;
   it does not contain `r`. It preserves all pairs only if RH. The turn is known on the whole
   fibre. `G₊` is generated by the conjugates of the turns, and it preserves all pairs iff no
   collision away from the centre exists at a forward time. Such collisions exist (M4), so
   `G₊` contains exchanges, and `G₊` is transitive on the zeros (card
   `3-formalized/galois-zero-forward-monodromy-is-transitive.md`). `G₊` contains the signed
   3-cycles and the double flips: `W⁰ ≤ G₊ ≤ W`, `[W : W⁰] = 4` (card
   `3-formalized/galois-zero-forward-monodromy-contains-the-even-signed-permutations.md`).
   The sign on the zeros is not trivial on `G₊` (card
   `3-formalized/galois-zero-sign-of-the-zeros-on-the-forward-monodromy.md`), and `G₊` is
   normal of index at most two in `W` (card
   `3-formalized/galois-zero-forward-monodromy-has-index-at-most-two.md`): `G₊` is `W` or one
   of the two subgroups of index two on which the sign on the zeros is not trivial.
   Not yet a theorem:
   - the sign on the pairs. The turn around `τ₀` has the class `f^k e^N` in `W/W⁰`, `k` the
     number of pairs colliding at the centre, `N = Σ ⌊m/2⌋` over the collisions away from the
     centre up to sign. `G₊ = W` iff the exchange of two pairs alone lies in `G₊` iff the two
     classes generated by the turns are independent; sufficient: one forward branch time that
     is not a centre time with `N` odd.

   Proved: conjugation at a real base time is the word of the turns around the real branch
   times in `(t, 1/8)` (card `3-formalized/galois-zero-conjugation-is-the-word-of-the-real-turns.md`).

## Conjecture log

- *Real times suffice for the monodromy.* False as a setting: a loop around a collision time
  leaves the real axis. Pivot: complex time; all modules generalized.
- *Infinitely many real collisions at negative times follow from the infinitely many nonreal
  zeros at negative times.* A nonreal zero may come from infinity as `t ↑ 0`, so compactness
  gives no collision. Proved as a dichotomy: a branch time in `[a, b]` or infinitely many
  escaping zeros (injective transport into the finite nonreal census at `b > 0`).
- *A folded cover without branch points and without escape is a cosine (Picard).* Correct on
  paper (squared zeros entire and never equal ⇒ one similarity ⇒ a second-order equation with
  affine coefficients ⇒ static zeros), but for ξ the real-time dichotomy is stronger and needs
  no Picard. Not formalized.
- *Power sums of the reciprocal centre times lie in `K_J`.* True formally
  (`Σ τ_j^{−2} = (μ₂/μ₀)² − μ₄/μ₀`), but needs a genus-one Hadamard product for the growth
  `exp(C|τ| log²|τ|)`; the corpus product theorem covers `exp(C|τ| log|τ|)` only. Not done.
- *No centre time has `Re τ ≤ 0`.* Proved: `Φ` decreases on `[0, ∞)`
  (`strictAntiOn_riemannKernelReal_Ici`), so `Φ(√u)e^{ru}/√u` decreases for `r ≤ 0`, and the
  sine transform of a decreasing function is positive.
- *Irreducibility through three-point rigidity.* If the folded cover were unramified and
  lifting held, the squared zeros would be entire functions of `τ` never equal, and by
  Picard's theorem they would move by one similarity. Needs lifting and Picard; neither is
  available.

- *An exchange follows from the heat equation and the symmetry.* False: the family
  `(τ−τ₀)^{ν/2} f(z/√(τ−τ₀))`, `f` an even solution of `f'' − (y/2)f' + (ν/2)f = 0`, has the
  sheets `z² = ρ_j²(τ−τ₀)` and no collision off the centre; for polynomials this is the Hermite
  family, and a degree count of the discriminant in `z²` (`n(n−1)` against `Σ k_i(k_i−1)` at
  the centre times) shows that every polynomial family with two distinct centre times has a
  collision off the centre. For `Φ` the proof must use the kernel. Polynomial intuition also
  fails for the position: a real-rooted polynomial has its centre times in `Re τ < 0`, the
  family of `Ξ` has them in `Re τ > 0`.
- *The collision is near a centre time, with `c/S` winding once.* Correct in outline; the
  proof does not locate a centre time. It uses the squared ratio `Q = (2S₀/B₀)²`, which is
  explicit, an explicit base point with `Re f = 0`, and a loop on which `Q cos² ξ₀ ≈ 1`; the
  sign of `2S₀ cos ξ₀/B₀` is constant on the loop but not determined, so the exchanged zeros
  are near `π ± ξ₀` or near `2π ± ξ₀`.
- *Barriers.* Circles or vertical lines free of zeros for all times of a compact set cannot
  exist when the set has interior: each far zero sweeps a region of diameter about
  `(π/2)·diam`, larger than the spacing. Non-escape needs the local asymptotics.

- *A centre time whose central collision is the only collision.* Not reachable with the
  two-term model: where `2S₀ = −B₀` the model has double zeros at every `ζ ∈ 2πℤ` of its range
  at once (where `2S₀ = B₀`, at every `ζ ∈ π + 2πℤ`), and the true collision times differ by
  less than the model error. Pivot: allow other collisions at the same time; the turn is then
  an involution with exactly one flipped pair, and the exchanges are removed in `W⁰`.
- *The sign on the pairs from an explicit loop.* The loops of saddles are known on a window
  only; the parity of the exchanges outside the window (up to the far zeros) is not
  controlled. A single collision time needs the `ζ`-dependence of the two terms to relative
  precision `o(1/‖τ‖)` on the whole fibre.
- *The sign on the pairs from conjugation.* `cr` at a real time is a product of `N(t)`
  exchanges, `N(t)` the number of quartets of nonreal zeros of `H_t` (no zero is imaginary),
  so an odd `N(t)` would give `G₊ = W`; under RH `N(t) = 0`. No unconditional parity.
- *The sign on the pairs from a discriminant.* The sign is the monodromy of a square root of a
  discriminant of the squared zeros; in the two-term model the product of `H'` over the zeros
  is an infinite power of `1 − (B₀/2S₀)²`, so no regularized discriminant separates the
  collisions of one model time.
- *A zero is determined by the others.* False in the continuous sense: the stabilizer of
  finitely many zeros in `G₊` is transitive on the other pairs. Formalized as independence.
