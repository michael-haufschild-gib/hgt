### Finite-complex length identity

- **Lemma:** `[PAPER-PROVED]` For a bounded complex `C•` of finite abelian 2-groups,
  `Σ_i (-1)^i length_{ℤ₂}(Cⁱ) = Σ_i (-1)^i length_{ℤ₂}(Hⁱ(C•))`.
- **Proof:** Put `Zⁱ = ker dⁱ` and `Bⁱ = im dⁱ⁻¹`. The short exact sequences
  `0 → Zⁱ → Cⁱ → Bⁱ⁺¹ → 0` and `0 → Bⁱ → Zⁱ → Hⁱ → 0` give
  `length Cⁱ = length Bⁱ + length Hⁱ + length Bⁱ⁺¹`. Alternating summation cancels the two boundary
  sums after an index shift. No arithmetic input is used.
- **Consequence:** `[PAPER-PROVED]` Once Gates (0) and (1) produce finite truncations, their
  determinant valuation is forced by chain-group lengths. All new arithmetic content lies in the
