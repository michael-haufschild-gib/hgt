## Abstract

A finite spectrum can reveal a system's parameters, but ordinarily cannot decide its entire
infinite-time future. We identify an exception for a broad class of closed relativistic fields.
Let the one-particle energies form a rationally shifted relativistic ladder

```text
E_n = (1/R) sqrt((n+q)^2+c),    n>=0.
```

The second difference of the first three energies has the sign of `c`. If it vanishes, every
many-particle energy lies on one rational frequency grid and the full free-field propagator is
exactly periodic. If it does not vanish, the one-particle frequencies have infinite rational rank,
so no state-independent period and no exact thermal-field return exist at any positive time.
The full thermal partition function is then hypertranscendental: no algebraic differential
equation of any finite order closes it. Thus three measured tones decide both an all-future
recurrence question and an infinite-complexity property of a complete quantum field, within a
source-validated relativistic-ladder class.

## Model

Let `q=a/d>=0` be rational in lowest terms, `R>0`, and assume

```text
(n+q)^2+c > 0
```

for every `n>=0`. Let `g_n` be positive finite mode multiplicities of at most polynomial growth.
The one-particle Hamiltonian has energy `E_n` with multiplicity `g_n`.

Let `F_+` and `F_-` denote bosonic and fermionic Fock spaces, and subtract the vacuum energy:

```text
H_F = sum_(n,alpha) E_n N_(n,alpha).
```

When `E_0>0`, both thermal partition functions converge for `Re(s)>0`:

```text
Z_F,+ (s) = product_n (1-exp(-s E_n))^(-g_n),
Z_F,- (s) = product_n (1+exp(-s E_n))^( g_n).
```

Their thermofield returns are

```text
A_F,plusminus(t)
  = Z_F,plusminus(beta+i t)/Z_F,plusminus(beta).
```

## Theorem R2: full-field periodicity dichotomy

Under the model assumptions:

```text
Delta_3=0
  <-> c=0
  <-> exp(-i T H_F)=Identity for some T>0;

Delta_3!=0
  <-> c!=0
  <-> exp(-i T H_F)!=Identity for every T>0.
```

The claim holds for both bosonic and fermionic Fock dynamics.

### Proof

If `c=0`, then

```text
E_n=(n+a/d)/R=(d n+a)/(d R).
```

Every finite-particle Fock energy is an integer multiple of `1/(dR)`. Hence

```text
T_*=2 pi d R
```

gives `exp(-i T_* H_F)=Identity` on the complete occupation-number basis and therefore on Fock
space.

If `c!=0`, Theorem T2 of `three-beat-hypertranscendence-law.md` gives

```text
Q-rank {E_n:n>=0}=infinity.
```

If a positive period `T` existed on Fock space, it would exist on its one-particle sector, so

```text
T E_n in 2 pi Z
```

for every `n`. Then all `E_n` would lie in the one-dimensional rational vector space generated by
`2 pi/T`, contradicting infinite rank. `[CONDITIONAL ON T2]`

## Theorem R3: exact thermal return and almost-return

Assume `E_0>0` and `beta>0`. If `c!=0`, then

```text
|A_F,plusminus(T)| < 1
```

for every `T>0`. Nevertheless, for every `epsilon>0` and every `T_0>0`, there exists `T>T_0`
such that

```text
|A_F,plusminus(T)-1| < epsilon.
```

Thus the field returns arbitrarily closely but never exactly.

### Exact non-return proof

Expand the normalized thermal trace as a convex combination

```text
A_F(t)=sum_j p_j exp(-i t mathcalE_j),
p_j>0, sum_j p_j=1,
```

over Fock energies, including the vacuum `mathcalE_0=0`. Equality in the triangle inequality gives

```text
|A_F(T)|=1
```

only if every phase with positive weight is identical. The vacuum fixes that common phase to `1`.
In particular `T E_n` is in `2 pi Z` for every one-particle energy, contradicting R2.
`[CONDITIONAL ON T2]`

### Arbitrarily close return proof

Choose a finite set of Fock energies carrying thermal probability greater than `1-eta`. Dirichlet's
simultaneous approximation theorem supplies arbitrarily large times at which all their phases lie
within `eta` of `1`. The omitted thermal tail contributes at most `2 eta`. Choosing `eta` from
`epsilon` proves the result. `[PAPER PROOF V1; QUANTIFIERS TO CLOSE]`

## Theorem R4: massive full-field hypertranscendence

Assume `c!=0`, `E_0>0`, and polynomially bounded positive multiplicities. Then both

```text
Z_F,+ (s),  Z_F,- (s)
```

and their affine real-time thermal returns are hypertranscendental over the corresponding rational
function fields.

### Proof skeleton

Expand either Fock partition function as a generalized Dirichlet series

```text
Z_F(s)=sum_j b_j exp(-s mathcalE_j).
```

After collecting equal Fock energies, every coefficient `b_j` is positive. The support is locally
finite and includes the vacuum and every one-particle energy `E_n`; coincident many-particle
energies only increase coefficients and cannot cancel a one-particle support value. Therefore

```text
Q-rank support(Z_F) >= Q-rank {E_n:n>=0} = infinity.
```

Ostrowski's generalized-Dirichlet theorem rules out every nontrivial algebraic differential
equation. Normalization and the invertible affine substitution `s=beta+i t` preserve HT.
`[CONDITIONAL ON T2 AND AN EXACT LOCAL-FINITENESS/OSTROWSKI AUDIT]`

No claim is made here that the massless full Fock partition is DA in every dimension. The recurrence
dichotomy does not require that stronger statement.

The cosine lies in `[-1,1]`, proving the sharp interval `[1/9,5/9]`.

## Theorem R6: arbitrary higher-sector interactions cannot restore recurrence

Let

```text
H_total = H_free + V
```

act on bosonic or fermionic Fock space. Assume:

1. particle-number sectors reduce `H_total`;
2. `V` vanishes on the vacuum and the complete one-particle sector;
3. the one-particle restriction is the relativistic ladder above with `c!=0`;
4. `H_total` is bounded below with locally finite pure-point spectrum;
5. `exp(-beta H_total)` is trace class for the thermal parameters used.

No restriction is placed on the strength or form of `V` in sectors with two or more particles.
Then:

```text
exp(-i T H_total)!=Identity              for every T>0;
|A_total,beta(T)|<1                      for every T>0;
Z_total(s) and A_total,beta(t) are HT;
F_psi3(pi/|Delta_3|) <= 5/9.
```

### Proof

The one-particle sector is reducing and unchanged, so its energy support retains infinite rational
rank. A global period would restrict to a period there, contradicting R2. The normalized thermal
trace is a positive convex combination containing the vacuum and every one-particle eigenvalue, so
equality of its modulus to one would again force all one-particle phases to one.

After a harmless ground-energy shift, the interacting thermal partition is a convergent generalized
Dirichlet series with positive integer spectral multiplicities. Its support contains the unchanged
one-particle energies. Contributions from interacting sectors can coincide with those energies but
can only add positive multiplicity; they cannot cancel them. Infinite support rank and Ostrowski
therefore force HT. Finally, the three-mode state lies wholly in the protected one-particle sector,
so R5 is unchanged. `[CONDITIONAL ON T2 AND THE OSTROWSKI AUDIT]`

### Scope

This includes arbitrary number-conserving two-body and higher-body interactions that annihilate the
zero- and one-particle sectors, a standard structure in many-body Hamiltonians and quantum
simulators. It does not include relativistic interactions that mix a nominal one-particle state
with multiparticle states of the same conserved quantum numbers.

## Hostile review

### R1. “Commensurate spectra revive; incommensurate spectra do not. This is textbook.”

The parent criterion is textbook. New content, if any, is the three-note equivalence and the
unconditional infinite-rank/HT conclusion for every nonzero algebraic or transcendental mass
parameter. Whether that is enough conceptual advance remains open.

### R2. “The universe in the title is an Einstein-static toy.”

Valid. The admissible headline says “closed quantum field,” not “our Universe.” A Nature paper
needs extension beyond exact ultrastatic free ladders or a compelling experimental realization.

### R3. “No experiment can wait forever or establish exact equality.”

Valid. The theorem makes a model-based all-future inference from finite spectral data. Falsification
is one-sided: an exact revival contradicts the massive branch, while finite non-observation never
confirms it without the source model.

### R4. “Interactions generically destroy revivals already.”

Answered for a broad but not fundamental class by R6. Arbitrarily strong number-conserving
interactions on sectors with at least two particles cannot restore recurrence or differential
closure because the protected one-particle tower remains an infinite-rank subsystem. Relativistic
interactions that mix particle sectors remain outside the theorem.

### R5. “Approximate recurrences make the distinction operationally irrelevant.”

Answered in part by R5. The exact curvature time `pi/|Delta_3|` forces an equal three-mode
superposition below return fidelity `5/9`, giving a finite-time separation. Approximate recurrence
later remains possible. Whether this elementary three-mode bound has broad enough consequence for
Nature remains open.

### R6. “HT adds no prediction beyond irrational spectral ratios.”

Partly valid. Infinite rational rank is enough for no period; HT additionally excludes every finite
algebraic differential closure of the complete thermal response. A manuscript must demonstrate why
that stronger exclusion affects physics.
