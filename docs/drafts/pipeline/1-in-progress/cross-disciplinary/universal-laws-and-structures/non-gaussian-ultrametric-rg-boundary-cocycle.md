## Rigorous parent source

Abdesselam--Chandra--Guadagni construct a scale-invariant non-Gaussian generalized stochastic
process on `Q_p^3`, together with its squared field and anomalous dimension.  Their extended
renormalization group allows space-dependent couplings and relates mixed correlations to the
dynamics near a nontrivial fixed point.

For the elementary field, their analytic cumulant-generating functional satisfies the exact
non-Gaussian witness

```text
F(z) = S^T(z 1_(Z_p^3), 0),
F''''(0)
  = <phi(1_(Z_p^3))^4> - 3 <phi(1_(Z_p^3))^2>^2 < 0.       (1)
```

Thus the parent model already has a connected four-point sector that cannot be erased by calling
the source generalized free.  Reconstructing a non-Gaussian field is parent science here; it is
not the proposed discovery.

The same construction writes the cutoff log moment-generating functional schematically as an
additive shell cocycle

```text
S^T_(r,s)(f)
  = sum_(r <= q < s)
      {delta b[V^(r,q)(f)] - delta b[V^(r,q)(0)]},         (2)
```

where the `V^(r,q)` are successive extended-RG iterates.  After separating ultraviolet, middle,
infrared, free, and boundary pieces, the infrared contribution is an absolutely convergent
one-sided sum over `q >= q_+`; the terminal finite-volume boundary term disappears in the removed-
cutoff limit.

Equation `(2)` is the first parent construction found in which all three needed structures coexist:

```text
non-Gaussian connected sector
+ exact hierarchical shell iteration
+ additive boundary cocycle.                              (3)
```

## The wrong candidate: the bulk fixed-point power law

The limiting field is exactly translation- and scale-invariant.  Its elementary and composite
correlators therefore have fixed homogeneous scaling dimensions.  Such power laws are
differentially algebraic.  Bilateral shell completion similarly tends to strict covariance rather
than a rational scale defect.

Consequently

```text
non-Gaussianity + anomalous dimension + discrete scale covariance
does not imply hypertranscendence.                         (4)
```

Using the anomalous exponent itself as the sought HT observable would be rediscovery and category
error.

## Selected reporter

Remove the Wick face from the compact-source cumulant germ:

```text
I_4(z) = F(z) - F(0) - F''(0) z^2/2.                      (5)
```

The field is centered and even, and `(1)` gives `I_4''''(0) < 0`.  The serious object is not this
single coefficient but the one-sided infrared shell contribution to `(5)`.  Let

```text
I_4^IR(z)
  = sum_(q>=q_+) Delta_4 delta b[V^q(z 1_(Z_p^3))],        (6)
```

where `Delta_4` denotes subtraction of every one- and two-point face contribution before the
shells are summed.

`[CONJECTURE -- NON-GAUSSIAN BOUNDARY-HT]` There is an intrinsic nonlinear scaling coordinate `u`
for the compact source in which `(6)` obeys a finite-defect scale constraint

```text
I_4^IR(u) = B_4(u) + a I_4^IR(lambda u),                   (7)
```

with a nonzero one-shell defect `B_4` that is differentially algebraic over the physical source
field.  The unique regular boundary completion of `(7)` is hypertranscendental and has positive
face-complete Wick--Kolchin profile.

Equation `(7)` is a target, not a fact imported from the parent paper.  Their partial analytic
linearization gives a serious route to the coordinate `u`, but the RG map is infinite-dimensional
and the shell functional `delta b` is analytic rather than presently known to be rational or DA.

## Exact proof obligations

1. **Intrinsic one-sided split.** Prove that `(6)` is independent of arbitrary choices of cutoff,
   RG lift, and the ultraviolet/middle/infrared partition, modulo the admitted local base.
2. **Face subtraction.** Express `Delta_4` using every pair-face pullback required by the compact
   four-source configuration and prove the negative coefficient `(1)` survives.
3. **Finite defect.** Derive `(7)` from one extended-RG step.  Do not posit a geometric shell sum
   after the fact.
4. **Tame shell.** Prove `B_4` is rational or otherwise differentially algebraic in the selected
   source coordinate.  Analyticity alone is insufficient for a rational-or-HT theorem.
5. **Nonrational completion.** Exhibit an infinite divisor, a support-gap obstruction, or another
   exact obstruction to the rational branch.
6. **Relative separation.** Prove `I_4^IR` is not in the differential-algebraic closure of the
   face-complete one- and two-point field.  A nonzero fourth cumulant alone does not imply positive
   relative Kolchin growth.
7. **Reporter invariance.** Prove the conclusion under differential-birational changes of the
   source and under admissible renormalization counterterms.

## Cosmological source route

For a fixed Euclidean de Sitter harmonic amplitude `varphi_l`, the compact-source functional

```text
W_l(varphi_l) = -F(g varphi_l)                             (8)
```

is a mathematically well-defined non-Gaussian influence potential.  Its fourth vertex is nonzero
by `(1)`, unlike the Gaussian ultrametric determinant.  If `(7)` is proved for the same source
coordinate, `(8)` becomes a mode-resolved interacting HT candidate.

This is not yet a cosmological QFT.  The parent construction is Euclidean/probabilistic on
`Q_p^3`; a local product theory with de Sitter spacetime, reflection positivity or Lorentzian
unitarity, gauge-invariant in-in observables, and harmonic recombination has not been constructed.
The full cosmological bridge requires all of those steps rather than a relabelling of `(8)`.

## Anti-theatre and dual gate

The existence of hierarchical non-Gaussian fields, anomalous dimensions, RG cocycles, and
partial Koenigs linearization are established parent theory.  The conjecture is not promoted by
renaming them.

The candidate clears the significance gate only if it proves an interaction-relative HT theorem
for a rigorous continuum field and a source-natural cosmological carrier.  It clears the novelty
gate only after formula-level comparison with hierarchical RG pressure functions, nonlinear
scaling fields, differential algebra of iterative functional equations, and constructive-QFT OPE
literature.  Neither gate is currently cleared.

## Lean boundary

No Lean theorem should be added for `(7)` before the source coordinate, shell defect, and
differential-algebraic class of `B_4` are proved on paper.  The existing formal corpus already
contains the finite face-rank receiver and unconditional affine scale-constraint uniqueness.
Formalizing an abstract equation that merely assumes the missing RG facts would be theorem
theatre.
