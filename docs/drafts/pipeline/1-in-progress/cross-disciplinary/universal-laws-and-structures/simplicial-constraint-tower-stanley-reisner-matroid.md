# Two-thick simplicial constraint towers have Stanley--Reisner differential matroids

- **ID:** `simplicial-constraint-tower-stanley-reisner-matroid`
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** GENERAL THEOREM / CONSTRAINT-TOWER BRIDGE
- **Scientific status:** `[BRIDGE]` -- strictly stronger than the simplex-skeleton theorem, but not
  independently promoted as new scientific knowledge until a non-multiple-gamma
  hypertranscendental realization is constructed.
- **Statement:** Every atomic meromorphic constraint tower indexed by a finite two-thick
  simplicial complex has complete differential-algebraic matroid equal to the linear matroid of
  the localized Stanley--Reisner ring of that complex.

## Setup

Let `V` be finite and let `Delta` be a downward-closed family of subsets of `V`, including the
empty face. No purity assumption is made. Call `Delta` **two-thick** when
every nonmaximal face `J` has distinct vertices `q,u notin J` such that `J union {q}` and
`J union {u}` are faces.

Put

```text
S=C[delta],
R=S[t_v^(+-1):v in V],
x_v=t_v-1,
I_Delta=(x_N : N is a minimal nonface of Delta).
```

Let `D` be a differential field of meromorphic functions and let commuting translations `T_v`
act on a common meromorphic extension. For every face `J`, let

```text
O_J={T_J^a F_J:a in Z^J}.
```

Assume:

1. `D` is stable under the derivation and every structural translation;
2. for every maximal face `J`, `O_J` is differentially algebraically independent over `D`;
3. translation inside a face shifts its orbit freely;
4. if `q notin J` and `J union {q}` is a face, then
   `(T_q-1)T_J^aF_J=T_J^aF_(J union {q})`;
5. if `J union {q}` is not a face, the same difference lies in `D`;
6. the common fixed meromorphic field for the two coface translations selected at every
   nonmaximal face is `C`;
7. after all deeper faces are adjoined freely, a nonzero constant-coefficient differential-linear
   form on one slice of a target coface orbit is not a coboundary in its free shift direction.

## Theorem `[PAPER PROOF]`

The union of all face orbits is differentially algebraically independent over `D`. Consequently,
for every finite `P_1,...,P_m in R`,

```text
dtrdeg_D D<P_1(T)F_empty,...,P_m(T)F_empty>_delta
  =dim_(C(delta)) span{Pbar_1,...,Pbar_m}
```

inside

```text
C(delta) tensor_(C[delta]) (R/I_Delta).
```

Thus every nonlinear differential dependence is generated by the nonface constraints.

## Proof

Start with the maximal faces. They form an inclusion antichain, although their cardinalities may
differ. For a seed `A`, let `Star_max(A)` be the maximal faces containing `A`. If `J` lies in this
star and the star contains another maximal face, then `A` is a proper subset of `J`; otherwise
`A=J` would force every maximal face containing `A` to equal `J`. Choose `q in J\A`, partition a
minimal circuit into the maximal faces containing `q` and those avoiding `q`, and use the free
`T_q`-translates inside `O_J`. Every maximal face avoiding `q` has its `T_q`-difference in `D`,
because adjoining `q` would contradict maximality. Free-block replication gives the usual
contradiction. Induction on `|V\A|` terminates when `A` is itself maximal, when its star is a
singleton. Thus all maximal-face orbits are jointly independent without uniformity or purity.

Descend by face cardinality. Before the descent, keep every maximal-face orbit in the base,
including maximal faces whose cardinality is smaller than the current layer. Let `K_>` be generated
over `D` by all maximal faces and every already-treated nonmaximal face of larger cardinality. For a
current nonmaximal face `J`, choose two distinct coface directions `q,u`. A hypothetical differential
dependence in a finite block of `O_J` gives, by simultaneous multi-translation telescoping, one
constant-coefficient differential-linear form and one `G in K_>` satisfying a `T_q`-coboundary
equation on `O_(J union {q})`. Remove that target orbit from `K_>`. The remaining field is
`T_q`-stable: equality of another coface with the target would imply equality of the underlying
faces after deleting `q`. The target orbit is therefore a free differential-rational shift
extension, and the right side occupies one slice. Assumption 6 excludes `G`. Hence every current
face orbit is independent over `K_>`.

For fixed cardinality, equation 4 or 5 makes every off-face translation DA over `K_>`. Uniform
nested-star gluing applies to the nonmaximal faces in that cardinality layer. The maximal faces of
that size were already adjoined freely and remain in the base. Descending to the empty face proves
the first assertion.

Every Laurent factor decomposes as

```text
S[t_v^(+-1)]=S direct_sum x_vS[t_v^(+-1)].
```

Tensoring over `V` and quotienting by the nonface monomials gives the unique face decomposition

```text
R/I_Delta=direct_sum_(J in Delta) x_JS[t_j^(+-1):j in J].
```

The face-orbit independence turns a finite operator family into a finite matrix of
constant-coefficient differential-linear forms in differential indeterminates. Smith normal form
over `C[delta]` gives the displayed rank formula.

## Pure ridge-thick criterion `[LEAN PROVED]`

Suppose the maximal faces are pure of cardinality `d` and every ridge belongs to two distinct
maximal faces. Every proper face has two immediate coface directions:

- if its codimension in a containing maximal face is at least two, choose two unused vertices of
  that maximal face;
- if it is a ridge, choose the distinct added vertices supplied by two incident maximal faces.

## Sharp boundary

Purity is not a boundary: the antichain-first argument removes it. Two-thickness is the actual
local boundary for this proof. If a nonmaximal face has only one
coface direction. The paired-translation constant collapse is then unavailable, and a nonconstant
periodic constant field can support additional differential relations. A one-boundary complex
therefore requires new analytic input and is not covered by this theorem.

## Research route opened

Search for hypertranscendental constraint systems whose DA boundary incidences form arbitrary
two-thick complexes rather than simplex skeletons. Nonpure complexes now become legitimate targets,
including irregular finite prime-constraint systems. Cycle, pseudomanifold, triangulated-manifold,
and mixed-dimensional incidence types would produce differential matroids not realizable by a
single multiple-elliptic-gamma hierarchy. A concrete realization would promote this bridge into
new scientific knowledge about a broader class of hypertranscendental functions.
