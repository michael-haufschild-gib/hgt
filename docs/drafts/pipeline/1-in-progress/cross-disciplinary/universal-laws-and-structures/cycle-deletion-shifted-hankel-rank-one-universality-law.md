## Survivor

For any real weight sequence nonzero at every positive index, shape-blind cycle deletion is
equivalent to vanishing of every shifted two-row Hankel cross-minor:

`w(m+1) w(n) = w(m) w(n+1)` for all positive `m,n`.

Thus a combinatorial invariance under arbitrary cycle-shape deletion and the algebraic rank-one
moment locus are the same universal condition, not merely parallel consequences of geometric
weights.

## Novelty audit

Post-proof searches covered deletion-consistent random permutations, weighted cycle structures,
rank-one Hankel/Helson matrices, geometric sequences, and prime-power Euler coefficients.

- Betz--Ueltschi--Velenik (`arXiv:0908.2217`) and Ercolani--Ueltschi
  (`arXiv:1102.4796`) study random permutations with arbitrary cycle weights, including the Ewens
  constant-weight case, but do not state this deterministic shape-score/Hankel equivalence.
- Perfekt--Pushnitski, *On Helson matrices: moment problems, non-negativity, boundedness, and finite
  rank* (DOI `10.1112/plms.12068`) gives a much broader finite-rank multiplicative-Hankel theory.
- Sammartano, *s-Hankel hypermatrices and 2 x 2 determinantal ideals* (`arXiv:1210.4230`) studies
  the determinantal ideals generated by such minors.

These sources make each algebraic ingredient classical. Searches found no theorem identifying the
cycle-deletion shape score with the shifted-Hankel locus, nor the resulting reduction of the
degree-one Euler source image to support plus one prime-local normalization plus deletion
blindness. Novelty claim is restricted to that cross-domain equivalence and its Lean
formalization; no priority claim is made for rank-one Hankel or geometric-sequence facts.
