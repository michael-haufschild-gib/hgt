## Theorem A: universal square-root clock-rank law
`[VERIFIED-KNOWN; PROOF ROUTE REPRODUCED]`

For every real `c != 0`, the frequency family

```text
Lambda_c = {sqrt(n^2+c) : n sufficiently large}
```

has infinite dimension over `Q`.  Equivalently, it is not contained in the integer span of any
finite subset of itself.

### Priority collision

Müller and Sawin proved in a February 2025 MathOverflow answer that square roots of values of every
real quadratic polynomial that is not itself a square contain an infinite `Q`-linearly independent
subset.  Their theorem is stronger than Theorem A and directly applies to `X^2+c` for every real
`c!=0`.  The arithmetic theorem is prior work, not candidate novelty.

### Reproduced proof route

Given a finite real family `x_1,...,x_r`, suppose every sufficiently large `sqrt(n^2+c)` lies in
its rational span.  Let `R` be the finitely generated domain over `Z` generated by the `x_j` and
`c`, localized to preserve `c!=0`.  Specialize `R` into a number field `F` while keeping the
quadratic nonsquare.  At infinitely many completely split odd primes, reduction lands in `F_p`.

For a nonsquare reduced quadratic `x^2+A x+B`, the equation

```text
y^2=x^2+A x+B
```

has exactly `p-1` solutions when `B-A^2/4!=0`, by parametrizing the two nonzero factors

```text
(y-x-A/2)(y+x+A/2)=B-A^2/4.
```

If every value were a square, it would have at least `2p-2` solutions because at most two values
vanish.  Hence some residue class is a nonsquare.  Arbitrarily large integers in that class cannot
have their original square roots in the chosen rational span, since specialization would make the
reduced value a square.  Induction yields an infinite independent tail.

At `c=0`, `sqrt(n^2)=n`, so the rank is exactly one.  No general DA claim is made at `c=0`, because
arbitrary coefficients may carry an independent obstruction; Theorem 0 handles the canonical
physical coefficients separately.

The forum proof is not peer-reviewed.  A publication must supply full specialization and
split-prime details.  Lean formalization requires those dependencies unconditionally.

## Finite-observation theorem required

No finite dataset proves that a function is hypertranscendental.  A Nature claim therefore needs
a finite-resolution survivor in addition to `(4)`.

Two exact finite survivors are already available.

### Finite survivor 1: clock-rank lower bound `[PAPER PROOF V1]`

If a quasiperiodic model has `r` fundamental clocks `Omega_1,...,Omega_r`, every Fourier line of
every observable lies in their integer span.  Therefore `q` rationally independent observed lines
force `r>=q`.  This is immediate after tensoring the integer-span inclusion with `Q`.

Likewise, a sum of `q` nonzero distinct complex exponentials cannot satisfy a nonzero
constant-coefficient linear ODE of order below `q`: if `P(D)` annihilates the sum, linear
independence of distinct exponentials gives `P(lambda_j)=0` for all `j`, hence `deg(P)>=q`.
The real covariance carries paired frequencies and consequently requires order at least `2q`
when all `q` pairs are present.

These statements give rigorous finite lower bounds inside, respectively, finite-clock torus models
and linear time-invariant state models.  They do not extend without hypotheses to arbitrary chaotic
or nonlinear finite-dimensional systems.

### Finite survivor 2: three-beat compactification law `[PAPER PROOF V1]`

Consecutive KK clocks obey

```text
mu_(n+1)^2 - 2 mu_n^2 + mu_(n-1)^2 = 2/(R^2 H^2).         (11)
```

Thus three consecutive lines determine `RH`; a fourth line gives an overdetermined falsification
test.  For a massless five-dimensional field the stronger one-parameter law is

```text

```

This suggests the public empirical sentence:

> **Three primordial beats can expose the size of a hidden dimension.**

Equations `(11)`--`(12)` are useful but not by themselves new: they are elementary consequences
of the KK mass formula.  Their role is to make the infinite-rank theorem falsifiable rather than
to carry the paper's novelty.

The remaining quantitative target is:

```text

  force differential-state order at least q,

and the (q+1)st source mode gives an exact or bounded residual
  against every q-clock truncation.                         (6)
```

The likely carrier is a generalized Vandermonde or Wronskian determinant for distinct `mu_n`,

bound for the Hardy projection.  This must yield a noise-aware lower bound on required mode count

## Hostile attacks

1. **Infinite EFT theatre.** A five-dimensional interacting EFT has a cutoff.  Summing formal KK
   modes above it is not physics.  Exact core uses a free compactified QFT; interacting
   cosmological transfer needs a UV completion.
2. **One-sided complex signal.** The observed in-in correlator is real.  HT of one wavefunction
   orientation does not automatically imply HT of its real symmetrization.  A source-natural
   Hardy/dispersion reconstruction or a direct two-sided theorem is mandatory.
3. **Boltzmann invisibility.** Heavy modes are suppressed by `exp(-pi mu_n)`.  Exact nonzero tails
   can be observationally irrelevant.  Equation `(6)` needs survey- and noise-level force.
4. **Finite data cannot prove infinity.** The empirical claim must be a growing lower bound or a
   falsifiable residual hierarchy, never direct experimental certification of HT.
5. **Not unique to extra dimensions.** An infinite string, composite, or nonperturbative tower may
   generate the same certificate.  HT can certify failure of every finite-frequency model in the
   audited class; it cannot by itself identify compact geometry.
6. **Coefficient cancellation.** Ostrowski needs infinitely many nonzero coefficients on an
   infinite-rank exponent subset.  Exact source weights, selection rules, and degeneracies must be
   audited mode by mode.
7. **Analytic/contact pollution.** Local shapes and contact terms can dominate observations.
   Claim must live in the nonanalytic frequency channel and prove its invariance.
8. **Known infinite-tower spectroscopy.** Cosmological collider physics already uses oscillatory
   signals to read masses and spins, and current work searches KK modes directly.  Candidate
   novelty is the all-tower differential boundary and its square-root clock-rank theorem, not
   “inflation can produce KK particles.”
9. **Ostrowski is parent mathematics.** Generalized-Dirichlet finite-basis obstruction dates to
   1920.  Novelty can only lie in deriving its hypotheses from a source-complete physical
   observable and obtaining a finite-resolution consequence.
10. **Coordinate inflation.** HT is asserted only in the physical logarithmic scale/time
    coordinate in which de Sitter masses are clock frequencies, with exact invertible coordinate
    maps stated.  No arbitrary reparameterization is allowed.
11. **Free-theory significance.** An exact Gaussian propagator can be a mathematical-physics
    theorem without being a discovery about our Universe.  Nature significance requires either
    an interacting source-complete transfer or a realistic experiment whose measured lower bound
    cannot be reduced to ordinary multi-mode spectroscopy.
12. **Ideal-symmetry fragility.** Exact de Sitter, fixed radius, and free propagation are ideal
    limits.  Slow roll, radius dynamics, widths, and mode mixing can replace pure clock lines by
    chirps or cuts.  A robustness theorem must identify which deformations preserve an
    infinite-rank asymptotic certificate.
13. **Headline overreach.** Hypertranscendence excludes algebraic differential equations of every
    finite order over the stated coefficient field.  It does not exclude arbitrary algorithms,
    nonlocal equations, or equations with the target signal inserted as a coefficient.  Public
    language must say “finite algebraic differential law,” or explain the allowed operations.
14. **“Infinite KK tower, therefore infinite memory” is obvious.** Mode count alone is not the
    result: at `c_M=0` the same infinite tower compresses exactly to one Riccati state.  The
    candidate-new statement is the discontinuous loss of every finite nonlinear algebraic
    realization at any positive offset.  A reviewer may still judge this compressibility
    distinction mathematically elegant but physically nonessential.
15. **Global-observable artifact.** A spectral trace can hide nonlocal access to the compact
    direction.  On the homogeneous circle, however, the diagonal Poisson kernel is the trace
    divided by compact volume and has the same series.  The law therefore has a local
    fixed-compact-point carrier; the real-time experimental reconstruction remains a separate
    stability problem.
16. **Mori--Zwanzig collision.** Exact projection of unresolved degrees of freedom into a
    generalized Langevin equation with a memory kernel is a standard mechanism in statistical
    mechanics. “Hidden variables become memory” is therefore explanation, not novelty.
    Hypertranscendence would have to yield a new invariant or operational consequence beyond the
    existence of memory.
17. **Nonlocal KK-action collision.** Barvinsky--Kamenshchik--Rathke--Kiefer already constructed a
    lower-dimensional nonlocal braneworld action whose form factors incorporate the cumulative
    effect of bulk KK modes. The current result may sharpen “nonlocal” to “no finite algebraic
    state realization” for a canonical response, but cannot claim discovery of the space-to-memory
    trade itself.
18. **Consistent-truncation counterexample to broad wording.** Finite consistent truncations
    retaining selected massive KK modes exist on special compactifications and symmetry sectors.
    Consistency means every retained-sector solution uplifts; it does not mean the finite theory
    reproduces the complete local spectral response. Accordingly, the exact theorem can exclude
    finite realization only for its full response and audited observable class, never all
    lower-dimensional descriptions.
