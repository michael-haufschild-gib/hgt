## Why several established results look like shadows of one law

### 1. Scattering creates an external particle at a factorization seam

In factorizing S-matrix theory, a direct-channel bound-state pole has residue supported on a
representation \(V_c\subset V_a\otimes V_b\). The residue maps factor through \(V_c\), and the
bootstrap then constructs scattering of the new external particle \(c\) with other particles from
the old amplitudes. This is an exact physical example in which a factorization singularity promotes
a composite channel into a reusable particle port.

It does not prove `(SM1)`. It begins with asymptotic particle spaces, tensor products, spacetime
analyticity, unitarity, crossing, and usually representation theory. Old S-matrix bootstrap and
nuclear democracy also aimed to close particle spectra under such consistency, and modern
bootstrap explicitly records that those principles admit many theories rather than one unique
world:

- [Benincasa--Cachazo, consistency conditions on massless-particle
  S-matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.4305);
- [Kruczenski--Penedones--van Rees, S-matrix bootstrap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02421).

Weinberg's soft theorem is a second seam shadow. Lorentz invariance and pole factorization force
charge conservation for a massless spin-one mediator and universal coupling/equality of
gravitational and inertial mass for a massless spin-two mediator. It still assumes the photon,
graviton, Poincaré kinematics, and S-matrix pole structure rather than generating the cut and seam:

- [Weinberg, photons and gravitons in S-matrix
  theory](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.135.B1049).

### 2. A gauge or gravitational cut creates boundary degrees of freedom

Gauge constraints prevent the physical Hilbert space from factorizing naively by spatial region.
Restoring a local subsystem description introduces edge degrees of freedom. Donnelly--Freidel
derive boundary gauge data for Yang--Mills and surface-location/normal-frame data for gravity:

- [Donnelly--Freidel, local subsystems in gauge theory and
  gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.04744).

Concrete \(2+1\)-dimensional gravity models go further: subdivision produces particle-like edge
modes carrying mass and spin, measuring curvature and torsion, which do not cancel when cells are
glued:

- [Freidel--Girelli--Shoshany, loop quantum gravity on the
  edge](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.04360).

Minimal factorization of Chern--Simons theory can also produce particle degrees of freedom on a
quantum group and, for three-dimensional gravity, gravitational anyonic edge modes:

- [Mertens--Wu, minimal factorization and gravitational anyonic edge
  modes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00501).

These are unusually close physical shadows of `(SM1)`, but they start after a spacetime
dimension, gauge/gravity theory, action, region, and cut have been supplied. They do not show that
all fundamental matter is the source-generated compensation for physical divisibility.

### 3. An obstruction can force a boundary mode

Anomaly inflow shows that an otherwise inconsistent boundary realization may require a bulk phase
or protected boundary degrees of freedom. Four-dimensional chiral gauge theory can occur at a
higher-dimensional topological phase boundary:

- [Hsieh--Tachikawa--Yonekura, anomaly inflow and
  \(p\)-form gauge theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.11550);
- [Kaplan, chiral gauge theory at the boundary between topological
  phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01494).

The anomaly constrains but generally does not uniquely select the completion. It also presupposes
the symmetry, QFT, dimension, and boundary interface. The universal seam law instead claims that
the analogue of the cut, anomaly, completion alphabet, and physical matter mode are generated
together.

### 4. Quantum-gravity cobordism can demand defects

The swampland cobordism conjecture is a broader and closer parent than anomaly inflow. It proposes
that the relevant bordism groups of a consistent quantum gravity are trivial. Potentially
nontrivial cobordism classes must then be trivialized by physical defects; the framework can
thereby require stringy defects or Kaluza--Klein monopoles:

- [McNamara--Vafa, cobordism classes and the
  swampland](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.10355);
- [Andriot--Carqueville--Cribiori, structure in the cobordism
  conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00021).

This already realizes “global sewability requires physical defects” inside candidate quantum
gravity. It is not `(SM1)`--`(SM2)`: a spacetime dimension, compactification background,
geometric/QG structure, bordism problem, and usually a string-theoretic completion are supplied;
the conjecture does not claim that all elementary particles are exactly the irreducible defects of
source-generated operational cuts or uniquely derive GR plus the Standard Model. A seam theory
equivalent only to cobordism trivialization would be a rediscovery.
