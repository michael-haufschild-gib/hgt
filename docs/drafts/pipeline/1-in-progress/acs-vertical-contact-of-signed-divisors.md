# Vertical contacts for signed conductor divisors

- **Stage** 1-in-progress
- **Route** ACS arithmetic section count on integer translates of the cubic spline.

Seek a single integral family of graphs whose vertical contacts have weight one on each prime-power ratio. In the polynomial for conductor n, mark each prime-power divisor qʲ by the monomial qT^(qʲ). On changing only the q-exponent, every changed coefficient is ±q. Changing two prime exponents gives coprime changed coefficients. The vertical part of the resultant of two products of graph equations should therefore count every opposite-sign contact with its prescribed multiplicities, with no higher-order mixing. The signed Weil count still needs same-sign restrictions, the smooth diagonal and both poles in a common effective object.
