# Alternating descent between cofactor conventions

- **Stage** 1-in-progress
- **Question** Can transported negative vertices and previously paired positive vertices be covered in the same matching?

Start at an old positive source absent from the new sources. Follow its old edge, then the inverse new edge if present. Switch to old edges on the reached positive vertices and retain new edges elsewhere. Injectivity makes each continuation unique and forbids merger of walks from distinct starts. If the old edge stays in the even-prefix fibre and the new edge merges the two largest primes, each two-edge step removes two prime factors. The desired result preserves every old positive source and every new negative target, including the transported empty-fibre debt. The remaining signed mass is confined to the two terminal classes.
