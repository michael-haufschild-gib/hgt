## Decision rule

- Lean theorem signatures determine local mathematical content.
- “Beyond external SOTA” means no exact mathematical collision was located in primary sources; it is a provisional priority assessment, never proof of novelty.

- Equivalent criteria, conditional implications, numerical evidence, and generic infrastructure are recorded but not promoted as route advances.
- Any new Lean proof must be computation-free, foreground-built, umbrella-wired, and axiom-audited.
