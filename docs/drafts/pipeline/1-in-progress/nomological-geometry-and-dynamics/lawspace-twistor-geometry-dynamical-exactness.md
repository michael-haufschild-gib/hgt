## 0. Why the previous core regressed

“Construct a source-native class and prove it vanishes” is the common endpoint of many RH routes.
Retyping that endpoint as a relative class, binary cell, or singular reporter is useful diagnosis,
but it is not a new field. Those constructions are calibration problems inside individual charts.

LTG changes the unknown. Exactness, topology, positivity, and time are no longer fixed backgrounds
in which a special object must be found. Their compatible **law frame** is the dynamical object.
Routes are local polarizations or irreversible reductions of that frame. Selection is a global
stability/harmonicity problem for the law frame, not an additional source axiom.

\[

\tag{LTG.R1}
\]

where `R_x subset H_(1,x) direct-sum H_(0,x)` is a source-labeled, possibly closed, unbounded, or
multivalued relation; `theta_x` is a section of a declared pre-target source-data object; `K_x` is

\[
 (q,\theta_x)\in\mathcal R_x.
\tag{LTG.R2}

When their hypotheses hold—closed composition, Fredholm/perfectness, integral lattice, jet

\[

These correspond respectively to recurrence/completion, multivalued horizon, residual boundary,

\[

\tag{LTG.G1}

The homotopy fibre `(LTG.G1)` is the **exactification lift stack**. It is a gerbe only after proving

3. explicit criteria under which it is a banded gerbe;

Without this theorem, LTG remains a vocabulary capable of post-hoc classification.

## 4. Dynamical law frame

In a regular finite-rank chart, let a gauge group `G` act Hamiltonianly on the law fields and let
`mu_h` take values in the coadjoint/current bundle `ad(P)^* tensor Omega^2`. Require the source
current `J_src` to be an independently constructed section of that same bundle and to satisfy the
topological charge constraint imposed by the bundle component. For example, in rank one on a
compact curve of degree `d`, its central integral must equal `2 pi d` in the chosen normalization.
The model equation is then a sourced Hitchin--Simpson system

\[
 F_{\nabla_h}^{0,2}=0,
 \qquad \bar\partial_\nabla\Phi=0,
 \qquad
 \mu_h(\nabla,\Phi)=J_{\rm src},
 \qquad d_\nabla J_{\rm src}=0,
 \qquad [J_{\rm src}]_{\rm central}=c_{\rm top}(P).
\tag{LTG.3}
\]

Here `h` is not a background metric. It is part of the unknown law frame; `mu_h` is the moment map
of the shifted symplectic reduction; and `J_src` is fixed by literal source deformation before
observer specialization. In singular/pro chambers `(LTG.3)` must be replaced by a derived moment-
map section plus relative-admission data.

This is the GR move: `h`, `nabla`, and the admissible exactness polarization respond to the source.
The equation is presently a **field conjecture**, not a theorem and not an RH proof. Even after the
underlying object and stability condition are fixed, polystability generally selects only a
harmonic orbit modulo automorphisms and may leave moduli. Thus `(LTG.3)` does not solve source
selection by vocabulary. Its stronger claim is only that any residual selection problem must be
posed on the full law object and its normalized moduli, rather than inserted as a zero-residue
axiom in one route.

## 5. Equivalence principle

### Conjecture `LTG-EQUIVALENCE`

At every regular lawspace point, a gauge can set the connection coefficient to zero at that point;
along one chosen path it can set the contracted coefficient to zero. It cannot generally kill the
first jet: its antisymmetric part contains curvature. What remains invariant is:

1. curvature around two-dimensional reduction cells;
2. holonomy around loops of exactification choices;
3. Harder--Narasimhan/stability type;
4. admission and nonproperness of the frame;
5. source current and integral/real structure.

This is only a normal-frame statement. It does not make a nonzero cohomology or relation class
exact. If two route comparisons are realized as transverse connection directions, no single normal
frame need flatten their curvature cell.

The finite-dimensional local statement is classical gauge geometry. New work is constructing its
source-indexed pro-wild lawspace and proving that corpus comparisons are its transverse cells.

## 6. Hypertranscendence as lawspace non-finite-typeness

Let `T_n` be the tangent space to the `n`-jet/prolongation law chart and let `O` be a fixed
finite-dimensional observer chart of dimension `d`.

### Proposition 6.1 — observer-kernel growth

For every differentiable observation `f_n:T_n->O`,

\[
 \dim\ker(df_n)\ge \dim T_n-d.
\tag{LTG.4}
\]

Hence if the Kolchin/prolongation dimension tends to infinity, no fixed finite-dimensional
observer is locally quasi-finite on all jet levels; its invisible tangent rank diverges.

#### Proof

Rank--nullity gives
`dim ker(df_n)=dim T_n-rank(df_n)>=dim T_n-d`. ∎

This proves divergent **infinitesimal invisibility**, not noncompact observer fibres. To infer
noncompactness one must additionally integrate the tangent directions to actual points in one
fibre and prove uniform separation there. A map may have infinite-dimensional derivative kernel at
a point while its level fibre is a singleton. Unbounded algebraic rank alone does not force cost,
metric noncompactness, or global escape.

Thus hypertranscendence is not merely “many derivatives.” It forces non-finite-type tangent
geometry for any chart realizing full Kolchin growth. Proving that this infinitesimal growth
integrates to pro-wild noncompactness is a separate LTG theorem, presently open.

## 7. Positivity and recurrence

In a declared Hamiltonian/Gram quotient, positive observation can forget a phase-bearing gauge
orbit. The exact rank-one model `psi -> |psi><psi|` has `U(1)` fibres and differential kernel
`R i psi`; higher-rank Gram maps have larger unitary fibres and stabilizer strata. Separately, the
DVR map `a -> a^2` is a `mu_2` phase quotient, not positivity in general. These examples motivate,
but do not universally prove, that a named positive realization forgets exactification phase. On
the real half-line `a>=0`, squaring is injective and loses none.

Let

\[
 \mathfrak H\longrightarrow\mathfrak L_{\rm hist}
 \xrightarrow{\;p\;}\mathfrak L_{\rm obs}
\tag{LTG.5}
\]

be the history fibration. Recurrence closes in `L_obs`; its lift can end at nontrivial holonomy in
`H` or can wind with positive Moore-path cost. The finite GHZ phase-fibre theorem proves this
separation exactly. A wormhole/interior interpretation is allowed only after an independent bulk
length/charge operator intertwines with this lifted history and matches higher cumulants.

## 9. Route-independent predictions

1. **Common-discriminant synchronization.** When two routes are genuine realizations of one
   lawspace object, jumps of their rank, inertia, radical, or recurrence dimension must pull back
   to one source discriminant. Call it a stability wall only after constructing a stability
   condition whose semistable locus has that boundary.
2. **Conditional instability profile.** In a noetherian law chart with a declared slope and
   Harder--Narasimhan property, failed semistability produces an ordered filtration. LTG predicts
   that named realization functors transport its graded pieces to observer failures. Arbitrary
   failed descent has no canonical ordered filtration.
3. **Conjectural third-face necessity.** In the corpus models now known, arithmetic/Higgs and
   spectral/de Rham data do not select a real law frame; a positive/harmonic real realization adds
   independent constraints, while its phase quotient is nonconservative. A genuine common-lawspace
   example where two faces already determine the third would falsify this prediction.
4. **Conditional cofinal escape law.** If a derived metric is uniformly coercive on each new
   prolongation direction, bounded energy and growing rank force loss of compactness. Without this
   uniform coercivity, shrinking weights can retain compactness; rank alone predicts nothing.
5. **Phase-even characteristic classes.** If a declared positive quotient identifies a phase-
   reversal orbit, every invariant factoring through it is blind to the corresponding odd
   orientation. This is conditional on the actual gauge action.
6. **Recurrence/history split.** No complete set of endpoint observer invariants bounds lifted
   history cost without a properness/coercivity theorem for `(LTG.5)`.
7. **Method wall crossing.** A proof technology whose allowed operations stay inside one lawspace
   chamber cannot cross an exponent/stability wall; improvement requires a reducer changing the
   chamber or its source weights.
8. **Derived-thickening necessity.** Kolchin polynomial and Stokes/monodromy diagram do not
   determine local lawspace singularity: square-zero extensions can preserve both while changing
   nonreduced structure. Any universality classification must include the full derived/nilpotent
   deformation complex.

Each prediction compares independent organs. A counterexample with proved common-lawspace
realization falsifies the relevant law.

## 11. Make-or-break construction programme

### A. Build a non-RH calibration lawspace — kinematic wall passed, twistor gluing open

Use a hypertranscendental family with independently known arithmetic/monodromy, differential-rank,
and positive/recurrent data. Construct its pro-wild twistor stack and prove a correspondence among
at least three faces. The multiple-Gamma path now proves synchronization of differential, integral-
clock, and closed-range faces, but no non-isotrivial `P^1` gluing or real/positive face. RH is
forbidden at this stage.

### B. Prove one stability-wall synchronization theorem — source wall passed, HN dynamics open

Produce one source deformation where a single Harder--Narasimhan wall forces both a differential-
rank jump and a nonisomorphic topological/positive invariant jump. The multiple-Gamma rational-rank
wall already synchronizes differential and topological jumps, but it has no constructed
Harder--Narasimhan category or harmonic dynamics.

### C. Derive, do not declare, a cost law

Construct a natural metric/action from the shifted symplectic and real structures. Prove a
coercivity or noncompactness theorem. Rank alone is insufficient.

### D. Only then construct the zeta lawspace

The zeta object must enter as one source point in an already functioning field. Route R/L/E/G/O,
Mass, Hedenmalm, recurrence, and source-dagger become chart tests. No “missing source-native
selector” may be installed as an axiom.

## 12. Falsifiers

1. No non-RH hypertranscendental family admits a non-isotrivial typed `lambda`-family with at least
   two identified faces and one independently constructed real, topological, or history
   realization.
2. Claimed route changes are merely names; no Lagrangian correspondence or transition functor is
   constructed.
3. Stability walls can be moved arbitrarily without changing source data.
4. Moment-map/source equation is chosen after observing target obstruction.
5. Hypertranscendence does not control the tangent/prolongation complex.
6. A claimed common lawspace has invariant jumps over incompatible source walls.
7. History cost is inserted independently and has no symplectic/metric construction.
8. Zeta/RH is needed to define the field rather than serving as a later example.

Any of 1--4 kills the current field schema as a serious candidate. Route-specific progress cannot
rescue it.
