## 0. Answer to the core question

> The common object is a source-indexed derived lawstack: a moduli stack of possible law frames,
> their symmetries, histories, and compatible exactifications. Proof routes are observer maps or
> local presentations of this stack. Arithmetic relations are isotropy, spectral transport is
> linearized monodromy, geometric zeros are derived incidence, positivity is a coarse/Hamiltonian
> quotient, topology measures separation and properness, and recurrence is a closed observer loop
> whose lift can carry nontrivial holonomy and cost.

“All routes are gauges” is too strong. A gauge is invertible. Many corpus operations—completion,
positive squaring, quotient, finite truncation, closure, scalarization—are irreversible observer
maps. CLG therefore treats routes as **local observer charts**; only invertible overlaps are gauge
changes. Likewise, not every no-go is curvature. Exact types include isotropy, nonseparatedness,
nonproperness, missing morphisms, derived thickness, and holonomy. Calling all of them curvature
would erase the mathematics.

## 1. Why the twistor proposal was not foundational

Lawspace Twistor Geometry correctly asks for non-isotrivial gluing among graded, flat, Betti, and
real faces, but several corpus organs have no known `lambda`-connection. A `P^1` label without a
Rees construction is taxonomy. CLG moves the stack of laws below any particular twistor
enhancement:

\[
 \text{source lawstack}
 \quad\longrightarrow\quad
 \begin{cases}
   \text{twistor/nonabelian-Hodge enhancement},\\
   \text{ordered/Krein enhancement},\\
   \text{topological/pro enhancement},\\
   \text{costed history enhancement}.
 \end{cases}
\tag{CLG.1}
\]

LTG survives as the strongest proposed arithmetic--spectral--positive realization of CLG, not as
the universal primitive.

## 2. Coordinate-free primitive

Fix a source site or higher category `S`. A **constitutive lawstack** consists provisionally of:

1. a source-indexed derived/pro-topological stack

   \[
    \pi:\mathfrak L\longrightarrow S;
   \tag{CLG.2}
   \]

2. a marked deformation--obstruction complex `T_L` or `L_L`, including integral/real source
   structure when present;
3. observer stacks `O_alpha` and a coherent cone of typed morphisms

   \[
    q_\alpha:\mathfrak L\longrightarrow\mathfrak O_\alpha;
   \tag{CLG.3}
   \]

4. an exactification lift stack for every compatible observer tuple `o=(o_alpha)`,

   \[
    \operatorname{Lift}_{\mathfrak L}(o)
    =\operatorname{fib}_{o}
      \left(\mathfrak L\longrightarrow
      \operatorname*{holim}_\alpha\mathfrak O_\alpha\right);
   \tag{CLG.4}
   \]

5. a **constitutive section** `c` in a declared fibration `Con(L)->L` choosing admissibility,
   polarization, connection, or cost where these are not already intrinsic;
6. a history object `Path(L)` with source/target and an independent action only when derived from
   `c` or another normalized source law;
7. optional twistor, symplectic, ordered, operator-algebraic, correspondence-valued, or
   information-geometric enhancements connected by named realization functors. A
   correspondence-valued observer needs a separately defined lax limit and lift object; it is not
   covered by `(CLG.4)`.

The constitutive section is the analogue of the metric field in GR. It is not free decoration. A
valid theory must characterize it before target specialization by a source equation, universal
property, normalized variational principle, or rigidity theorem. If any desired observer behavior
can be installed by choosing `c`, the field is tautological.

These are Morita/presentation-invariant only after the relevant stack and morphism are specified.

## 4. Field laws

### Law I — presentation covariance target

Observer and constitutive constructions must be proved Morita-natural. Only then do equivalent
groupoid, complex, or atlas presentations give equivalent observer diagrams, lift stacks, and
constitutive data. A defect changed by adding contractible summands or admitted coordinates is not
physical law geometry.

### Law II — source-before-observer constitution

The constitutive section is constructed on `S` or `L` before a target zero, route endpoint, or
desired inequality is inspected. It must defeat alternative sections that preserve source data but
change topology, positivity, or cost.

### Law III — conservative observer family

The joint observer map need not be faithful, but its invisible fibre must be computed. A claim that
an obstruction “migrates” among routes requires joint conservativity on the marked source class or
an explicit conservation law in the relative deformation complex.

### Law IV — coarse observation loses stack data

Passing from a stack to a coarse, positive, scalar, or completed observer can erase stabilizers,
orientation, extension classes, and path history. These data are not zero; they remain in inertia,
relative cotangent, or lift fibres.

### Law V — history is not endpoint state

Observer recurrence is a loop in `O`. A lift is a path in `L` whose endpoints may differ by an
isotropy arrow or holonomy. Endpoint observables alone supply no general bound on lifted action;
such a bound requires an additional theorem, for example properness or coercivity of
`Path(L)->Path(O)`.

### Law VI — hypertranscendence is pro-non-finite-typeness

Hypertranscendence supplies unbounded prolongation/Kolchin tangent growth in the marked source
lawstack. It does not by itself prove global metric noncompactness; integrating invisible tangent
directions with uniform separation is an additional theorem.

## 5. First exact lawstack theorem

For the non-RH two-period multiple-Gamma family, the marked period map

\[
 p_m:\mathbb Z^3\to\mathbb C
\]

defines the canonical translation lawstack `[C/Z^3]_(p_m)`. Its exact observer shadows are:

1. `ker p_m` as isotropy/integral relation;
2. `C/im p_m` as coarse orbit topology;
3. marked effective period excess;
4. affine reflection as a groupoid involution.

An independent multiple-Gamma theorem proves that marked excess equals differential-algebraic
depth. No functor from action stacks to special functions is constructed. Another independent
normalization/parity theorem proves the Gamma reflection equation; the groupoid involution alone
does not.

At finite packets, isotropy is zero, depth is one, and coarse quotient is non-Hausdorff. At the
boundary, isotropy has rank one, depth is zero, and coarse quotient is a compact Hausdorff torus.
The exact conservation law is

\[
 \boxed{
 d_{\rm DA}(\Gamma_m)
 +\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}\pi_1(\mathfrak P_m,z)=1.}
\tag{CLG.5}
\]

More generally, for a marked period action `p:Z^N->V` whose real span has dimension `d`,

\[
 \boxed{
 e(p)+\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}\ker p=N-d.}
\tag{CLG.6}
\]

This is the first exact example of the corpus “uncertainty” as a rank-complementarity ledger rather
than scalar loss: finite samples carry one unit of hypertranscendental excess and no isotropy;
the boundary carries no excess and one unit of isotropy. A transport theorem that makes this a
literal dynamical transmutation is still open. The normalized multiple-Gamma reflection survives,
but its normalization is extra function data, not induced by the action stack alone.

The universal Hausdorff reflection theorem sharpens the topological face. Every continuous group
observer of `C/Lambda_m` with Hausdorff target factors through `C/closure(Lambda_m)`. Its real
dimension `h_m` obeys

\[
 d_{\rm DA}+h_m=2,
 \qquad
 h_m-\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}\pi_1(\mathfrak P_m,z)=1.
\tag{CLG.7}
\]

Thus the cofinal ledger replaces one HT/invisible dense direction by one additional Hausdorff
observer direction and records one integral stabilizer at the boundary. No stack transport making
that replacement causal has yet been constructed.

## 6. Second exact chart: recurrence and phase history

The finite framed GHZ family supplies a different lawstack chart. Fixed one-boundary reductions
are coarse observers of a phase torus. Boundary state and recovery defect remain fixed while a
lifted phase history winds, accumulates Berry holonomy and Fisher/Fubini--Study action, and returns
to the same observer point. Thus CLG Law V is an exact finite theorem, not a wormhole metaphor.

Holographic interpretation requires an independently constructed edge-charge/length operator and
an intertwiner. A boundary quotient plus phase fibre alone does not prove black-hole interior or
wormhole length.

### Third exact chart: Hurwitz monodromy closure

For a finite Hurwitz exponent packet, common-loop monodromy defines a cyclic action on the
character torus `T=(C^x)^I`. If `H_q` is its Zariski closure and `R_q` its resonance lattice, then

\[
 \dim H_q+\operatorname{rank}R_q=|I|.
\tag{CLG.8}
\]

The residual affine observer has coordinate ring `C[R_q]`. When it is a point, `H_q=T`; the
published branch-deformation/Vandermonde representation then proves joint differential algebraic
independence of the Hurwitz family. This is a genuine analytic realization mechanism, not merely a
numerical match. Resonant chambers remain one-way: smaller monodromy closure does not by itself
produce differential relations.

## 8. Route-independent predictions

1. **Marked-lattice migration target.** For `p:Z^N->V_R` with fixed `N` and fixed real-span
   dimension `d`, effective-period excess and kernel rank are complementary. Calling this migration
   requires a constructed specialization/transport functor and a relative `K`-, determinant-, or
   Ext-class. No general perfect-complex Euler theorem follows from this mixed `Z`/`R` rank ledger.
2. **Separation/isotropy exchange.** Families may regularize coarse topology by creating stack
   isotropy. Coarse observers will report “improvement” while hidden symmetry increases.
3. **Phase erasure.** Any positive/coarse quotient invariant is blind to the part of orientation
   carried only by its inertia fibre.
4. **Cofinal blindness.** If every finite observer is an equivalence but the pro-observer is not,
   the defect must occur in nonproper descent, a derived inverse-limit term, or failure of uniform
   constitution—not a fixed finite coordinate.
5. **Recurrence split.** Closed observer recurrence with open lifted history occurs precisely when
   `Path(L)->Path(O)` has nontrivial relative homotopy/holonomy; cost still requires a constitutive
   action.
6. **Twistor conditionality.** Arithmetic, spectral, and positive faces form a twistor family only
   when a non-isotrivial Rees/`lambda` construction exists. Otherwise they remain distinct observer
   maps of the same lawstack.
7. **RH as later global section problem.** If a zeta lawstack is eventually constructed, RH must
   become a global statement about its normalized real/positive section. That statement cannot be
   used to define CLG.

## 9. Conjecture / proof / fail loop

1. **One scalar obstruction is the geometry.** Failed: stabilizer, separation, derived thickness,
   cost, and holonomy are nonisomorphic organs.
2. **All no-gos are curvature.** Failed: missing morphisms, nonclosed diagonals, and isotropy are
   defined without a connection.
3. **All routes are gauges.** Failed: coarse quotient and completion are noninvertible.
4. **Twistor `P^1` is universal.** Failed as foundation: many organs lack Rees gluing; decorative
   products are isotrivial.
5. **Period arithmetic determines any desired topology.** Failed for arbitrary incidence weights.
6. **Period action determines canonical coarse topology.** Proved by the multiple-Gamma lawstack.
7. **Defect disappears at the DA boundary.** Failed as a rank ledger: DA depth vanishes while
   stack isotropy appears. Literal transport between them remains open.
8. **Hypertranscendence forces global noncompactness.** Failed without integration/coercivity.
9. **Survivor.** Source lawstack plus normalized constitutive section, observer maps, and history
   geometry is the minimal candidate broad enough for the whole corpus.

## 11. Falsifiers

CLG is theatre if:

1. its invariants change under Morita/presentation equivalence;
2. constitutive data are chosen after the desired route behavior is known;
3. no one source lawstack produces two independently computed nonisomorphic organs;
4. observer rows are only labels with no functors;
5. history cost is arbitrary and unrelated to source constitution;
6. zeta/RH data are needed to define the field;
7. exact counterexamples violate the claimed virtual-rank or descent laws.

The multiple-Gamma, framed-GHZ, and Hurwitz theorems defeat Falsifier 3 locally in three different
sectors; the GHZ chart also derives a normalized Fubini--Study history cost, and Hurwitz supplies a
genuine analytic monodromy realization. They do not defeat the other falsifiers globally.

## 12. Current literature interface

CLG deliberately assembles occupied ingredients. Recent primary literature confirms three pieces
are mathematically substantive rather than metaphors:

1. D'Addezio's 2026 convergent stack packages a cohomology theory as a stack and presents it by a
   formal groupoid in suitable charts. This is a real precedent for one stack having different
   cohomological and flat-connection realizations: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10573>.
2. Proper-good-quotient theory separates stack data from existence, separatedness, and properness
   of coarse moduli through `Theta`-reductivity and `S`-completeness. This supports treating
   nonseparation/nonproperness as distinct organs, not generic curvature:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09912>.
3. Arithmetic Bost--Connes--Marcolli systems already use arithmetic stack-groupoids, convolution
   algebras, time evolution, and zeta functions in one construction:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.07778>.

These works do not state CLG's whole-corpus field laws or the multiple-Gamma rank-complementarity
theorem. They also show why the next step must be a construction, not new terminology.
