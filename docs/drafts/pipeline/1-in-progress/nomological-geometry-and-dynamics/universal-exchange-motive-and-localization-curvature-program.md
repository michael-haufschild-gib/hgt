\[
 \operatorname{cofib}(u)\to\operatorname{cofib}(vu)

\]

## Conjecture/proof/fail loops

### One scalar universal obstruction

**Failed.** Witt index, relation kernel, `K`-class, Kolchin rank, and cost are not elements of one
group. Survivor is one exchange cell with several typed realizations.

### Arbitrary comparison defines curvature

**Failed.** In a stable category, `cofib(0->Z)=Z`; a freely chosen comparison realizes any desired
object. Survivor is the canonical Beck--Chevalley mate of a declared square.

### Source charge equals full curvature

**Failed.** The Euler logarithmic source is flat while completion/reflection can be curved.
Survivor is a contracted localization boundary plus a vacuum/Weyl kernel.

### Bianchi coherence guarantees nonerasure

**Failed.** Zero reducers erase a nonzero source while every cube commutes. Survivor is a jointly
conservative realization family or split detector.

### Infinite rank forces large cost

**Failed.** Infinite algebraic rank admits summable positive metric weights. Survivor is a separate
constitutive map or inequality from a realized defect to cost.

### Completion cofiber creates Route-L curvature

**Failed exactly.** The comparison is an isomorphism in distributions and has zero cofiber. The
tempered restriction loses admission before the endpoint. Survivor is relative exactification:
the differential may be strict although every primitive lies outside the strict coefficient
system.
