## 1. Why this is the field gate

An RH source selector is forbidden here. Success or failure must be visible for a non-RH
hypertranscendental family. The existing paper theorem synchronizes three shadows at one source
wall:

\[
 \text{Kolchin/differential phase},\qquad
 \text{integral period relation},\qquad
 \text{closed-range topology}.
\]

That is only kinematics. The incidence-operator topology depends on a weight `f(|gamma|)`, for a
period coordinate `gamma in Lambda^x`, not determined by the period group; replacing `t/(1+t)` by
`1` destroys the operator wall while preserving arithmetic and clock data. The canonical
translation quotient lawstack now supplies an observer-independent coarse topology, but not a
metric, cost, or twistor gluing. A serious field must derive every additional constitutive coupling
from its law object. Merely placing results in a table is regression.

## 2. Coordinate-free target

Use two explicitly different source objects:

\[
 \widehat S=\operatorname{Spf}\mathbb C[[t]],
 \qquad \tau_1=2i+t,
 \qquad r_0=(0,-2,1),
\tag{MGD.0}
\]

where the marked relation `r_0` vanishes exactly at `t=0`, and the cofinal analytic probe
`S_cof={pi/m:m>=1} union {0}`. The latter is not an analytic discriminant: the full rational-
relation locus in the naive analytic `t`-plane is dense. A comparison from the formal marked germ
to the cofinal bounded/topological realization is part of the theorem, not an identification by
notation.

Construct a source deformation complex `K` on `S_hat`, a filtration `F`, a normalized source
pairing, and a nonsplit mixed class

\[
 \varepsilon\in\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathcal C_{src}}(K_1,K_0),
\tag{MGD.0a}
\]

together with its Rees lawstack

\[
 \pi:\mathfrak L_G\longrightarrow \widehat S\times\mathbb P^1_\hbar
\tag{MGD.1}
\]

with:

1. a Rees or genuine `hbar`-connection construction generated by `K`, not `X times P^1` or a
   product enlarged by unrelated factors;
2. a nonzero presentation-invariant mixed transition/extension class induced by `epsilon`; a
   first-order Kodaira--Spencer class alone is insufficient under ramified reparametrization;
3. an equivalence from the `hbar=0` face to an independently defined filtered/prolongation moduli
   problem;
4. an equivalence from a declared `hbar in C^x` face to an independently defined
   flat/translation moduli problem;
5. one independently constructed real, topological, or history realization;
6. a source-side variational, harmonic, Green, determinant, or covariance equation with a proved
   existence-and-uniqueness statement for the constitutive section in a declared admissible class;
7. for a declared compatible observer tuple `t=(t_alpha)` in the homotopy limit, a
   presentation-invariant exactification lift stack

   \[
    \operatorname{fib}_{t}
    \left(\operatorname{Triv}_{src}
    \longrightarrow\operatorname*{holim}_\alpha\operatorname{Triv}_\alpha\right);
   \tag{MGD.2}
   \]

8. named integral and bounded/bornological realization functors sending the same nonsplit class
   `epsilon` to explicit class objects—not merely properties or projections of an adjoined product;
9. a boundedly invertible or bornologically bicontinuous presentation category and canonical
   compact/finitely presented probes for the finite/cofinal comparison.

The lift stack is not called a gerbe unless descent, local nonemptiness and connectedness, and an
automorphism band are separately proved.

## 3. Make-or-break theorem

### Conjecture `MG-LAWSPACE-DESCENT`

There exist `(MGD.0)--(MGD.2)` and a comparison to the cofinal analytic probe such that:

1. the mixed class `epsilon` is nonsplit and invariant under the admitted source presentations;
2. the integral realization sends `epsilon` to the marked relation class `r_0`;
3. the bounded/bornological realization sends `epsilon` to an explicit nonzero uniform-inverse,
   pro-cokernel, or other computed cofinal class;
4. the constitutive equation uniquely supplies the bounded/topological scale used in Item 3;
5. the Rees transition class of `epsilon` is nonzero, so the `hbar`-family is not a relabeled
   product;
6. canonical compact probes kill the topological realization while its cofinal/pro realization
   remains nonzero;
7. a typed diagram of pairwise comparison transformations and their modification/cocycle condition
   proves coherence among the realizations.

This theorem would be the first genuine non-product **twistor/descent** result above the already
proved translation lawstack: one nonsplit source class would produce multiple class-valued
realizations and a new observer prediction. It would not prove RH.

## 4. Route-independent predictions

1. **Marked-support pullback.** Jumps of the named functors preserving the source support/Fitting
   class must occur on its pullback. “Discriminant” is reserved for a constructed geometric
   support class.
2. **Finite/cofinal separation target.** Compute whether the exact bounded/bornological obstruction
   is a uniform-inverse defect, pro-cokernel, or derived-limit class. Do not infer its type merely
   from finite blindness.
3. **Constitutive rigidity.** The source equation must prove uniqueness up to declared gauge among
   competing weights such as `r^a/(1+r^a)` and the constant weight. Provenance and normalization
   alone are insufficient.
4. **Presentation invariance.** Contractible summands, boundedly invertible/bornologically
   bicontinuous coordinate changes, or logarithmic linearization cannot change the lift-stack
   equivalence type. Arbitrary unbounded rescaling is not an admitted equivalence.
5. **No selector smuggling.** No Xi zero, critical line, positivity assumption tailored to RH, or
   target vanishing condition may enter construction.

## 5. Conjecture / proof / fail loop

1. **Conjecture:** the three-face paper theorem already constructs a lawspace.
   **Fail:** it supplies only synchronized invariants; no `hbar`-family, transition class, or
   derived constitutive weight exists.
2. **Conjecture:** period arithmetic uniquely determines every closed-range operator model.
   **Fail:** weights `t/(1+t)` and `1` give different operator topology for identical period data.
   **Survivor:** the translation quotient stack does determine canonical coarse separation.
3. **Conjecture:** any homotopy fibre of exactifications is the desired gerbe.
   **Fail:** a homotopy fibre can be empty or disconnected and have no band.
4. **Conjecture:** hypertranscendence alone forces noncompact fibres.
   **Fail:** it forces growing infinitesimal observer kernels; integration and uniform separation
   remain extra theorems.
5. **Conjecture:** a product with `P^1` supplies twistor geometry.
   **Fail by definition and falsifier:** zero Kodaira--Spencer/transition class carries no
   interaction between faces.
6. **Survivor:** construct a non-product common-generator Rees family plus a uniquely derived
   constitutive section and prove one nonsplit mixed class has two nonisomorphic realization images.

## 6. Hard questions

1. What universal deformation--obstruction complex simultaneously controls period relations,
   differential prolongations, and admission topology?
2. Does a harmonic metric, Green operator, determinant-line norm, or minimum-action law uniquely
   derive a bounded realization beyond the already canonical coarse quotient topology?
3. Is there an initial/universal compact-probe observer category for which `(MGD.2)` is jointly
   conservative on `epsilon`, rather than one chosen to make conservativity true?
4. Does the rank-drop wall arise as a derived discriminant, a stability wall, or only an arithmetic
   specialization? These are not interchangeable.
5. What exact uniform-inverse, bornological, pro-cokernel, or derived-limit class represents
   finite-probe blindness in the admitted presentation category?
6. What real structure supplies positivity without erasing the phase needed to recover the source
   orientation?
7. Which Moore-path action is forced by the same constitutive section, and does observer recurrence
   close while the lifted history remains open?

## 7. Falsifiers

The programme fails if any of the following is proved:

1. every candidate `hbar`-family is isotrivial after presentation equivalence;
2. the canonical closed-range weight cannot be derived without choosing the desired topology;
3. the alleged common comparison decomposes into unrelated route-specific classes;
4. no one class maps functorially to two nonisomorphic invariants;
5. the wall location changes under a source-preserving presentation;
6. RH-specific data are required to define the geometry.
