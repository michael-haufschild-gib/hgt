## 1. Target theorem

Let `K` contain a primitive cube root of unity `omega`.  There is a three-dimensional internal
state space and a linear operator pencil

```text
A(c0,c1,c2) = c0 X + c1 Y + c2 Z
```

such that

```text
A(c)^3 = (c0^3+c1^3+c2^3) I,                                       (1)
```

and the commutator symbol

```text
exterior^2(K^3) -> End(K^3),   ei wedge ej |-> [Xi,Xj]              (2)
```

is injective.  The Lean-proven shift-suspension theorem then gives an exact finite-width
differential-field horizon

```text
c0^3+c1^3+c2^3 = 0                                                  (3)
```

whose three incompatibility channels are all nonzero and independent.

## 2. Weyl construction

Use the order-three clock and shift representation of the finite Heisenberg group.  Choose three
Weyl monomials whose exponent vectors in `F_3^2` have pairwise symplectic product one.  With a
consistent phase normalization they satisfy

```text
X^3=Y^3=Z^3=I,
XY=omega YX,  XZ=omega ZX,  YZ=omega ZY.                             (4)
```

The quantum multinomial theorem at a primitive cube root cancels every mixed term in the cube,
giving `(1)`.  Furthermore

```text
[X,Y]=(omega-1)YX,
[X,Z]=(omega-1)ZX,
[Y,Z]=(omega-1)ZY.                                                   (5)
```

The three Weyl monomials on the right have different Heisenberg characters and are linearly
independent, proving `(2)`.
