# Sharp spacing threshold and the finite part of the Weil square

On the aligned comb $dS$ let $W_d$ be Weil’s full quadratic form with the coefficients pulled back to $S$. Let $D$ be the base logarithmic diagonal, $P$ the off-diagonal prime form, and $M$ the coefficient mass. Common dilation gives
$$W_d=W_1/d+(1-1/d)(D-P)+M\log d.$$
On fixed-ratio windows the prime row is bounded, and $D=(\log N)M+O_Q(M)$. The uniform leading floor for $d\ge2$ thus has coefficient $1-1/d$. For sharpness use the flat vector on $(N,2N]$. It has no off-diagonal prime contacts and its unit-spaced Rayleigh quotient converges to the finite flat-packet form. Its dilates have the claimed leading coefficient. The flat vector rules out every divergent unit-spaced floor even assuming RH. Existence of a fixed positive floor for every ratio at unit spacing is still the RH criterion.
