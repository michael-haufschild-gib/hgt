# Simultaneous integral sections on conductor thickenings

For conductors in a fixed ratio window, lift arbitrary polynomial jets simultaneously through the graph restriction map. Seek a common multiplier whose integer content grows subquadratically in logarithmic scale on every spline ray.

At branch (i), the product of the other branch equations evaluates to the row product of graph differences. A Bézout identity separates the two thickenings after raising this row product to twice the jet order. Only prime-power ratios give nonunit content. A fixed ratio window bounds the number of such neighbours at each branch independently of mesh size. Primitive horizontal denominators still contribute to the real-place norm.
