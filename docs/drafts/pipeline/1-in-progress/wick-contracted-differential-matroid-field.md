## Finite labeled construction

Fix a differential source field `(K,D)`, an ambient field `L` of analytic response germs, and a finite physical window `w=(O,P,N,r)` consisting of a spacetime region, a finite source-probe family, correlation height `N`, and jet height `r`.

Use labeled ground sets, not sets of values:

- `G_w`: labels for the face-complete Gaussian/Wick base—all admitted local coefficients and counterterms, and every pulled-back one- and two-point connected response jet needed by the selected higher configurations;
- `S_w`: labels for the selected higher connected response jets `D^j C_n`, with `3 <= n <= N` and `0 <= j <= r`;
- `ev_w : G_w ⊔ S_w -> L`: evaluation of each labeled reporter.

Let `A_K(L)` be the algebraic matroid on `L`. Pull it back along `ev_w` to preserve coincident but differently labeled observables:

```text
M_w = A_K(L).comap(ev_w).
```

The finite Wick-contracted interaction matroid is

```text
I_w = (M_w / G_w) | S_w.                                    (1)
```

Here `/ G_w` is matroid contraction and `| S_w` is restriction to the higher connected labels. Its rank function is

```text
r_(I_w)(T)
  = trdeg_K K(ev(G_w), ev(T)) - trdeg_K K(ev(G_w)),          (2)
```

so the existing face-complete Wick--Kolchin profile is the rank shadow of `(1)`. Circuits of `I_w` are minimal algebraic dependencies among higher connected jets modulo all Gaussian face information. Loops are higher reporters already algebraic over the face base.

The use of labels is load-bearing. The existing finite profile erases duplicate values because it only needs a rank. A physical matroid field must remember whether two different probes become parallel, coincide only on a phase boundary, or participate in different circuits.

## Finite structural theorem `[PAPER PROOF TARGET]`

For `(1)` prove the following as one exact package:

1. **Relative-rank law.** Equation `(2)` holds for every finite signal subset.
2. **Wick collapse.** If every signal value lies in the algebraic closure of the field generated by `ev(G_w)`, then every element of `I_w` is a loop.
3. **Counterterm neutrality.** If extra base labels `C_w` evaluate into the closure of `G_w`, then contracting `G_w ⊔ C_w` instead of `G_w` produces exactly the same restricted signal matroid, not merely the same total rank.

Items 1--5 are consequences of algebraic-matroid contraction, closure, and differential independence. Their finite proofs are necessary infrastructure, not by themselves new scientific knowledge.

## Directed local field

A refinement `w <= w'` may enlarge both the signal set and the face base. Enlarging the signal set gives restriction compatibility, but enlarging the contracted base can turn apparent signal independence into dependence. Therefore the physical object is not a naive sheaf of matroids.

The correct refinement map has the shape

```text
I_w  --->  I_(w') | S_w,                                    (3)
```

where `(3)` is generally a matroid quotient/strong map. More resolved Gaussian faces, local laws, or counterterms can explain a finite-stage interaction witness; they cannot create relative transcendence. The continuum interaction field is a directed pro-system under these quotient maps.

This yields the persistence criterion:

> **[CONJECTURE]** A physical interaction law is a circuit, nonloop, or free flat that survives every cofinal admissible refinement. Finite-stage positive rank is not enough.

The intrinsic HT interaction phase is present when some higher connected vertical jet fibre remains free of unbounded rank after passage to the full face-complete base. This is strictly stronger than ordinary HT of a sample, propagator, form factor, or two-point response.

## Locality and cross-region structure

Spacelike commutativity does not imply probabilistic or algebraic independence, so `I_(O_1 union O_2)` must not be postulated to equal `I_(O_1) direct-sum I_(O_2)`. Cross-region circuits can encode exact constraints or entanglement.

The proposed locality data are:

1. isotony by reporter inclusion and base-refinement quotient maps;
2. causal covariance by labeled matroid isomorphisms;
3. face descent for connected configurations;
4. compatible amalgamation on overlaps when the reporters arise inside one global response field;
5. a cross-region connectivity defect whose vanishing characterizes factorization after Wick contraction.

General abstract matroid amalgams can fail to exist. Physical local matroids avoid an existence failure when they are restrictions of a single ambient algebraic matroid, but an intrinsic reconstruction theorem must characterize exactly which local diagrams admit such a global realization.

## Why an ordinary matroid cannot be the physical field

The characteristic-zero Gaussian-mimicry no-go is decisive. Every finite ordinary algebraic matroid of complex reporters, including every finite Wick-contracted minor `(1)`, has the same independence pattern as a finite family of linear Gaussian observables. Thus even an exotic circuit skeleton cannot certify nonlinear interaction. Exact algebraic dependence also forgets the circuit equations and coefficients, the physical coefficient base, correlation and derivative gradings, how rapidly correlations decay, how a determinant approaches zero, and which phase or period closure a circuit carries.

Consequently enrichment is mandatory, not optional. The finite datum must contain the labeled ordinary skeleton together with:

- decorated circuit polynomials or a differential-ideal presentation over the explicit face-complete coefficient base;
- correlation-sector and vertical derivative gradings;
- a valuation recording orders or scales of vanishing and RG asymptotics;
- oriented/phase or tract/pasture data retaining signs, phases, and representation coefficients;
- integral period-closure data whenever global gauge effectivity is physical;
- its directed transition maps, so survival under admissible refinement is part of the object.

The underlying Wick-contracted matroid still gives the exact dependency skeleton and makes contraction laws precise, but the forgetful map to that skeleton destroys the interaction certificate. Its valuation is expected to carry RG scaling, and its phase/period enrichment is expected to carry positivity and topological response. A plain chirotope is independently ruled out as complete physical data by the existing oriented-Gale period-closure fracture theorem.

## Exact negative controls

1. **Random analytic noise.** A jet-nondegenerate Gaussian entire function is almost surely HT, but this is a parent-mechanism bridge and does not imply interaction.
2. **Generalized free field.** The two-point sector may be HT while every higher connected sector vanishes; `I_w` is then all loops.
3. **Polynomial Wick composites.** Higher connected functions can be nonzero yet lie in the face-complete Gaussian algebra; `I_w` is still all loops.
4. **Infinite Gaussian influence hierarchy.** Infinitely many induced vertices can remain in the differential field of the two-point response; their relative interaction matroid is still trivial.
5. **Finite matroid periods.** A fixed finite-rank Gauss--Manin amplitude is DA; intrinsic HT requires an all-scale failure of finite capture.
6. **Tutte state sums.** If amplitudes factor only through finite deletion--contraction characters, the construction is combinatorial parent theory rather than the proposed local interaction field.
7. **Characteristic-zero Gaussian mimicry.** Every finite undecorated algebraic interaction matroid has a linear Gaussian realization. No claim of nonlinear interaction may factor only through its ordinary independent sets or circuit supports.
