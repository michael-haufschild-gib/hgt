## 1. Reading rules and status vocabulary

**Endpoint** is a proved implication or equivalence whose conclusion is RH. It is a receiver, not
evidence for its premise.

**Open core** is first unproved source statement after all proved connectors are removed. An
assumption equivalent to RH is not called progress.

**Closed mechanism** means corpus proves advertised construction cannot provide required
information. Closure is scoped: it does not refute every future mechanism using similar language.

**Positive survivor** is strongest nontrivial theorem exposed by a failed route: rigidity,
classification, exact boundary, or replacement design constraint.

**Independent route** requires a literal source object, an unconditional chain to an RH endpoint,
and a first unresolved producer not identical under the corpus's source-isogeny comparisons to an
existing route.

## Final frontier statement

Corpus has constructed source, completed landing, many positive geometries, and exact RH
receivers. It also proves why bounded label-blind completion, generic positivity, local exactness,
finite moments, isometry, and determinant packaging erase or fail to orient RH-bearing
coordinate.

Current frontier consists of eleven genuinely different lettered source problems plus the
unlettered CCM-FU incidence problem, concentrated in singular descent, oscillatory all-prime
transport, adaptive boundary orientation, whole-space coercivity, and positive carrier existence.
Routes T and U are real additions. Source-horizontal,
Burnol, eigen-pair, centered-prime-tail, Gamma--Lorentz, and universal-law-field additions narrow
existing routes or strengthen receiver/no-go infrastructure; they do not add another lettered
route. The
Euler q-Gamma/abelian-gas process is a genuine new pre-zero source-to-divisor architecture, now
including formal global class-law, expectation, and depth-divisor calculus in addition to
relative-entropy evaluation, hypertranscendence, and zero-free genus-one erasure, but it lacks a
critical-line orientation and RH handoff, so it remains a candidate rather than Route V. The
Gauss-component residue hierarchy is an adjacent local Connes candidate with actual circle
operators, one-step `L²` convergence, and a committed algebraic principal-step factorization;
analytic composition, iteration, multi-place assembly, and Weil handoff remain open. Exact
Hedenmalm source spectrum is a major C1 opening, pending umbrella promotion, not an independent
route. Shilov matrix/tower, now-complete scalar function-system boundary, congruence-symbol no-go,
and constitutive repair laws strengthen receiver and obstruction bookkeeping, not source
production.
Nyman--Beurling finite sections likewise strengthen a classical receiver, while the generalized
Beurling system layer is a Route-E firewall rather than a new producer.

RH remains open. Any claim of progress must now prove one named source theorem above, refute one
route with an exact no-go and positive survivor, or construct a literal new source architecture
with an unconditional handoff not source-isogenous to an existing route.
