# Current Formal Frontier Toward Proving or Disproving the Riemann Hypothesis

## Scope and evidentiary rules

This document is a present-tense topology of the RH research formalized in HTGeometry, HilbertMetric, and RHTower. It records live branch architectures, exact open cores, candidate interfaces, shared structure, and terminal no-gos. It is not chronology, research diary, or catalogue of every theorem.

Evidence surface: declarations indexed from current Lean working tree in HTGeometry, HilbertMetric, and RHTower. Cards and papers are not evidence here. Incomplete intended theorems are not reported as proved.

Status language:

- **Proved** means an unconditional Lean theorem.
- **Endpoint** means a proved implication or equivalence ending in RH; it does not prove its premise.
- **Receiver** means a formal object that detects or classifies off-line zeros after a source law or divisor input is supplied.
- **Producer** means a theorem derived from the literal arithmetic, theta, prime–Gamma, Möbius, or geometric source before selecting a Xi divisor.
- **Live branch label** means source, connector chain, and RH endpoint are formal, with a first independent producer still open. It does not mean the branch is mathematically independent of other labels.
- **Candidate interface label** means a novel formal receiver or source fragment exists, but the source handoff or divisor exhaustion needed for a live branch does not. It may be another realization of an existing open problem.
- **Terminal** means Lean rules out the stated producer or proves every surviving premise equivalent to RH.

No route currently proves or disproves RH.

## Cross-route conclusions

### 1. Source/receiver firewall

Most routes already possess a sharp RH-equivalent receiver. Rewriting that receiver as positivity, passivity, semistability, flatness, coherent approximation, derived exactness, determinant convergence, bounded energy, operator-norm transport, reversible KMS exchange, finite-action replay, protected pole incidence, or spectral completeness does not advance RH unless Lean derives its premise from the literal source before choosing a zero. Relative-completion semistability unifies theta flux, Meyer closability, purity, exceptional-pole collision, Gamma skew, history holonomy, and graph-null collapse, but global componentwise semistability is exactly RH. ### 2. Local exactness is abundant; global selection is scarce

Finite packets, all finite jets, exact finite homology, local indices, divisor multiplicities, finite real-zero determinants, and exact functional-equation passports can be realized or relocated without fixing horizontal position. FD2's source-selected signed Xi occurrence is substantive, but trace-faithful polarization cannot improve its divisor; GTA shows that a realification can move it only by exposing a higher-trace anomaly. A successful selector must therefore use infinite coefficient provenance with a singular/discontinuous, source-marked, order-sensitive datum—not more local data, profield invariants, or compact-open continuity.

Target cardinality is also closed at fixed occupied dimension. One determinant-optimal positive covariance controls capture of an arbitrary bounded, even uncountable, target family with cost equal to occupied dimension times one worst-target quadratic moment; the same law excludes post-ramp re-entry and has a quenched probability form. Thus continuum union bounds and target enumeration are not route cores.
