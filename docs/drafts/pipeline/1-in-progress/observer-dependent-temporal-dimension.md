## Headline, stated without metaphysics

A dynamical law may supply one time parameter, but an observer does not in
general see one periodic phase. The observer sees only the frequencies carried
by its spectral record. Those frequencies generate two canonical objects:

\[
 \Gamma_{\mathcal O}
 =\left\langle \operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O}\right\rangle_{\mathbb Z}
 \subset\mathbb R,\qquad
 \Omega_{\mathcal O}
 =\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}
   \operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O}\subset\mathbb R.
\]

Here \(\mu_{\mathcal O}\) is the positive spectral measure of the observed
displacement signal. The exact effective dimension is the rational rank of
\(\Omega_{\mathcal O}\); the exact return type is the topology of
\(\Gamma_{\mathcal O}\).

This is an operational dimension of the observed temporal orbit. It is not a
claim that spacetime has a variable coordinate dimension.

## Abductive jump — conjecture before proof

**State--observer spectral-time conjecture.** In a globally reversible, or
globally stationary constrained, universe, experienced temporal dimension and
the macroscopic arrow are properties of the pair consisting of a physical
state and an observer algebra, not properties of an external evolution
parameter. The restricted modular spectrum of that pair has a compact
arithmetic sector and a continuous sector. The compact sector fixes how many
independent temporal directions can recur; the continuous sector fixes the
irrecoverable density of nonreturning history. In a thermodynamic or QFT
limit, loss of spectral atomicity—not microscopic nonunitarity—is the universal
transition from recurrent clock time to a macroscopic arrow. Two observers of
the same stationary global state can therefore inhabit temporal histories of
different effective dimensions and different recurrent-versus-arrow type.

This is a conjecture generated from the joined evidence below, not a theorem
selected because a proof route is presently visible. At the moment of writing
it, no proof or formalization route for the full state--observer,
thermodynamic-limit, modular-spectrum, or arrow correspondence is known.
Provability is a downstream question. No manuscript claim may use the
conjecture until each bridge has been separately closed.

### Conjecture discipline

A good conjecture records this exact epistemic state: based on everything
currently known, there is a reasoned hunch that a sharp statement could be
true, but no present account of how it would be proved. Provability is not part
of the conjecture. Ease of proof, present library support, and likely
formalizability play no role in generating it. They enter only afterward.

A statement reverse-engineered from an available proof, or weakened until an
existing method applies, is a theorem candidate rather than a conjectural
jump. It may still be useful, but it cannot supply this project's abductive
novelty.

1. make the abductive jump;
2. state exact falsifiers and predictions;
3. attack truth and prior art;
4. find a paper proof;
5. certify every surviving mathematical claim;
6. delete every clause that does not survive.

compact-local observer excitation orthogonal to the vacuum has atomless

fixed positive-fidelity return set has zero asymptotic time density. Exact

This was posed as a hunch from the conjunction of the spectrum condition,

### Fourth abductive jump — finite histories determine internal time

**Status: falsified after the jump.** The frozen conjecture below omits an
unavoidable similarity/antiunitary gauge of complete operational histories.

**Operational history-reconstruction conjecture.** Let a separable local
quantum theory be represented by a faithful normal state on a local
observable algebra, a covariant internal clock action, and a physically
labelled family of local instruments that is tomographically complete. If an
alternative clock--world description preserves every finite joint
conditional probability for every ordered list of clock readings and every
word of labelled instruments, then it is related to the original description
by exactly one clock-origin transformation followed by an element of the
action-fixed commutant of the labelled algebra. No other clock--world
refactorization exists. If the labels generate a factor and the clock action
is ergodic, only the clock-origin transformation and an invisible global
phase remain.

This is a genuine hunch, not a theorem selected from a visible method. At the
moment of conjecture there is no proof route from equality of all finite
instrument histories to full labelled-multiplier covariance, no construction
that handles continuous clock POVMs without point projectors, and no
clock--world tensor factorization available for a general type-III local
algebra. The algebraic stabilizer discovered afterward identifies what would
follow *if* that reconstruction bridge were proved; it does not prove the
bridge.

Hard falsifiers are:

1. two nonconjugate faithful modular dynamical systems with identical
   probabilities for every finite word in one tomographically complete
   labelled instrument family;
2. a nonspatial or clock--world-mixing refactorization of a local type-III
   algebra that preserves all such histories but violates multiplier
   covariance;
3. a continuous-clock model in which every finite operational history agrees
   while no common origin intertwines the clock action;
4. an ergodic factor whose history-preserving stabilizer contains a
   nontrivial element outside the global phase and clock-origin action.

Any one counterexample kills the conjecture. Weakening “every finite
history” after seeing a proof route would create a different theorem
candidate, not repair this conjecture.

The counterexample mechanism is universal. Let \(S\) be any invertible linear
change of a state-space realization. Transform the initial state by \(S\),
every labelled instrument \(\Phi_a\) by
\(S\Phi_aS^{-1}\), and every terminal report \(r\) by \(rS^{-1}\). For every
finite word \(a_1\cdots a_n\),
\[
 (rS^{-1})(S\Phi_{a_n}S^{-1})\cdots
 (S\Phi_{a_1}S^{-1})(S\rho)
 =r\Phi_{a_n}\cdots\Phi_{a_1}(\rho).
\]
Thus even all labelled histories determine only a simultaneous-similarity
class unless part of the realization is externally anchored. This exact
cardinality-free identity is now certified for arbitrary label and reporter
families and every finite word.

Arbitrary similarities need not preserve the quantum positive cone, but the
physical obstruction survives. Global matrix transposition maps density
matrices and POVM effects to density matrices and effects, and maps a Kraus
instrument \(\Phi(X)=\sum_iK_iXK_i^\dagger\) to the Kraus instrument with
operators \(\overline{K_i}\). Applied to the entire preparation--instrument--
measurement description, it preserves every sequential probability. In
dimension at least two, transposition is not multiplicative and hence is not
an inner clock-origin automorphism or a scalar commutant action. The exact
nonmultiplicativity witness is certified in dimension two. Transposition is
also certified to preserve finite Kraus form, density-matrix positivity and
unit trace, POVM-effect positivity bounds, and the terminal Born trace. The
physical counterexample is therefore dependency-closed.

This is consistent with gate-set tomography, where complete circuit
probabilities identify a gate set only up to a common similarity gauge, and
with Wigner's unitary/antiunitary alternative. A corrected classification
must include operational representation gauges or add independently
calibrated anchors. Either correction is materially weaker than the frozen
conjecture and belongs to known parent theory.

### Fifth abductive jump — nonrepetition fixes black-hole algebra type

**Status: falsified after the jump.** The conjecture remains below in its
frozen, originally proposed form. It must not be weakened retroactively.

**Return-density type-III conjecture.** Let \(\psi\) be a faithful normal
state on a nontrivial von Neumann algebra \(\mathcal M\), and let
\(\sigma^\psi_t\) be its modular flow. Suppose the scalar multiples of the
identity together with all centred observables whose modular spectral
measures are atomless form a von Neumann algebra, and every nonzero centred
element of that algebra has atomless modular spectrum. Then that algebra is a
type-III\(_1\) factor and \(\psi\) has trivial centralizer.

By the all-spectrum collision law, “atomless” is operationally equivalent to
the statement that every fixed positive-fidelity return set has zero
asymptotic time density. The conjecture therefore says:

> If a closed algebra of physical observables never statistically repeats,
> that nonrepetition alone fixes the black-hole-compatible algebraic type.

This is strictly weaker than the known premise that every connected
correlator converges to zero. Mixing implies atomlessness, but atomlessness
allows singular-continuous spectra and Fourier transforms with no pointwise
limit. The conjecture was generated from three facts: finite/type-I systems
have pure-point modular recurrence; nontrivial centralizer elements carry a
zero-frequency atom; and known mixing-algebra theorems force
type III\(_1\). At the moment of conjecture there is no proof that the weaker
zero-density premise excludes type II, type III\(_0\), or type
III\(_\lambda\), and no proof that closure of the recurrence-null class
supplies the missing Connes-spectrum argument.

Hard falsifiers are:

1. a type-I or type-II von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal state whose
   nonzero centred elements all have atomless modular spectra;
2. a type-III\(_0\) or type-III\(_\lambda\), \(0<\lambda<1\), factor with that
   property;
3. a recurrence-null class closed under multiplication and weak limits but
   having a nontrivial modular centralizer;
4. a nontrivial action-fixed centred observable whose spectral measure is
   nevertheless atomless.

Finding any such example kills the conjecture. The known mixing theorem may
be used as evidence and as a comparison target, never as a proof of this
strictly weaker statement.

The first falsifier exists already in dimension two. Take
\(\mathcal M=M_2(\mathbb C)\) with any faithful state. Its modular
representation is finite dimensional, so every modular spectral measure is
pure point. The only centred observable with atomless modular spectrum is
zero. Consequently, the scalars together with all such observables form
exactly \(\mathbb C1\), and the clause about every nonzero centred element of
that algebra is vacuous. All stated premises hold, but \(\mathbb C1\) is not
a type-III\(_1\) factor.

Requiring the resulting algebra to be nontrivial produces a true-looking
replacement but not a new conjecture under the rule above. The proof route is
then visible: any non-scalar centralizer element can be centred to a nonzero
modular fixed point, whose spectral measure has a zero-frequency atom. The
atomless premise therefore makes the restricted state ergodic. The
established ergodic-state theorem says that a nontrivial von Neumann algebra
admitting a faithful normal state with trivial centralizer is a
type-III\(_1\) factor. Marrakchi and Vaes proved the converse under separable
predual and explicitly identify the forward implication as known since early
Tomita--Takesaki theory. Thus the repaired statement is a short corollary of
known operator algebra, not a novelty anchor.

The surviving new task is not to rename weak mixing as nonrepetition. It is
to find a genuinely weaker operational statistic that does not already force
trivial centralizer element-by-element, yet still determines a physically
important algebraic or gravitational phase. No such statistic is currently
proved.

### Sixth abductive jump — time's dimension survives clock ambiguity

**Status after proof search: operational core proved; hidden-representation
clause unresolved; novelty anchor rejected.**

**Operational temporal-invariance conjecture.** Consider two separable
stationary quantum theories with faithful minimal operational realizations,
strongly continuous internal clock actions, and the same physically labelled
preparations, instruments, reports, and clock readings. Suppose they assign
exactly the same joint probability to every finite labelled history. Their
clock realizations need not be related by a clock-origin transformation:
similarity and antiunitary gauges show that this is false. Nevertheless, the
restricted clock representations seen by the cyclic operational subspaces
have the same spectral measure class, up to reversal
\(\omega\mapsto-\omega\). Consequently every observer refinement has the
same:

1. rational visible-frequency rank in the pure-point sector;
2. atomic, singular-continuous, and absolutely-continuous decomposition;
3. collision mass and every fixed-fidelity asymptotic return-density law;
4. recurrent-versus-nonrecurrent temporal phase.

In short:

> A universe may not determine a unique clock, but it determines the
> operational dimension and return type of its time.

This is a conjecture in the strict sense. It is suggested by three pieces of
evidence: common similarities preserve finite-dimensional generator spectra;
global transpose reverses frequencies without changing rational rank or
spectral type; and operationally null sectors must be removed by minimality.
But no proof route is known from equality of all finite probabilities to
equivalence of continuous spectral measure classes. In particular, there is
no known route for nonunitary positive similarities, nonspatial
representations, continuous clocks, or type-III local algebras. The existing
finite-history gauge theorem proves invariance of probabilities, not
invariance of spectral measures.

Hard falsifiers are:

1. two minimal faithful finite-dimensional quantum gate sets with identical
   probabilities for every labelled word but different visible rational
   frequency rank;
2. two standard-form von Neumann dynamical systems with identical operational
   process tensors but mutually singular clock spectral types;
3. a quantum-compatible similarity gauge that changes collision mass or
   converts an atomic clock record into an atomless one;
4. a type-III clock--world refactorization preserving every local history but
   changing the Connes spectral class seen by the operational cyclic
   subspace.

Any one example kills the conjecture. Minimality, faithfulness, and the
complete history quantifier are frozen parts of the hunch, not clauses to be
adjusted after proof search. No manuscript may claim the result unless all
four conclusions and the continuous/type-III bridge are proved
unconditionally.

The scalar spectral core closes much more directly than anticipated. If two
finite real spectral measures have the same return amplitude at every real
time, uniqueness of characteristic functions makes the measures equal
exactly. Temporal dimension, atomicity, collision mass, and every
finite-window high-fidelity density are therefore identical. This
dependency-closed theorem is now certified without finite-dimensional,
pure-point, or absolute-continuity assumptions.

That result is useful but cannot carry novelty: it is Fourier uniqueness
applied to an operational record. For finite minimal process realizations,
similarity equivalence and gauge-invariance of generator spectra are also
established realization theory and gate-set tomography. The genuinely hard
clause is whether equality of all operational histories identifies a hidden
continuous/type-III clock representation rather than only its reported
scalar spectral measures. Claiming that hidden clause would exceed the
evidence; deleting it leaves a known theorem. The jump therefore contributes
a supporting invariant, not the paper's discovery.

### Seventh abductive jump — a horizon changes the dimension of time

**Status: falsified after the jump; model core collides with prior art.**

**Horizon temporal-phase conjecture.** Consider any UV-complete holographic
quantum theory on a compact boundary Cauchy surface, in a strict
semiclassical large-\(N\) limit admitting both a stationary horizonless bulk
phase and a stationary nonextremal black-hole phase. For every centred,
nonconserved simple boundary observable whose bulk excitation reaches the
horizon, the connected boundary-time spectral measure is pure point in the
horizonless phase and atomless in the black-hole phase. Conversely, if a
separating family of such observables changes from pure-point to atomless
connected spectra while the boundary kinematics and conserved charges remain
fixed, the dominant semiclassical bulk phase has acquired a horizon.

Combined with the exact all-spectrum return law, this predicts an
observer-level phase transition:

> **A black hole forms when time becomes infinitely dimensional for outside
> observers.**

More precisely, the temporal dimension of every horizon-coupled probe jumps
from at most countable to continuum, and every fixed positive-fidelity return
set changes from arbitrarily late recurrence to zero asymptotic time density.
The conjecture concerns the strict semiclassical limit; it does not deny
finite-entropy late-time recurrences at finite \(N\).

This statement is frozen before proof search. It is a hunch suggested by the
joint pattern of normal modes in horizonless compact geometries, quasinormal
decay in black-hole geometries, holographic mixing results, and the proved
spectral return law. No present argument establishes either universal
direction, the all-probe quantifier, or the converse. In particular, no
known route derives global horizon existence from boundary spectral type.
The availability of exact BTZ or thermal-CFT calculations plays no role in
the quantifiers above and may not be used to weaken them retroactively.

Hard falsifiers are:

1. a compact-boundary, stationary, horizonless semiclassical holographic
   phase with an atomless connected spectrum for a simple local probe;
2. a stationary nonextremal black-hole phase with a nonconserved
   horizon-coupled simple probe carrying a nonzero spectral atom;
3. a horizon-forming phase transition at fixed boundary kinematics and
   charges with no pure-point-to-atomless transition for a separating probe
   family;
4. a pure-point-to-atomless transition of a separating simple-probe family
   caused by a horizonless dominant bulk phase;
5. dependence of the alleged phase boundary on an arbitrary probe
   normalization, time-origin gauge, or representation choice.

Any one counterexample kills the conjecture. A theorem proved only for BTZ,
generalized free fields, a selected Gamma response, or the Hawking--Page
saddle would be a model theorem and supporting evidence, not proof of this
conjecture.

The conjecture does not survive its frozen quantifiers.

1. Birmingham, Sachs and Solodukhin already established the sharp
   normal-mode/oscillatory versus quasinormal-mode/decaying transition in the
   large-\(k\) AdS\(_3\)/CFT\(_2\) model and identified it with the
   Hawking--Page transition. The strongest easy model case is therefore
   parent physics, not a new horizon law
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0212308>).
2. Horizonless global AdS\(_3\) string theory with NS--NS flux has long-string
   continuous representations. In the UV-complete supersymmetric setting,
   the spacetime dual contains an \(\mathcal N=4\) Liouville factor for
   generic flux, hence a continuum even though the boundary spatial circle
   is compact and the background need not contain a black-hole horizon
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00421>). This defeats the universal
   horizonless-pure-point direction.
3. Nonextremal Kerr--AdS black holes have superradiant onset curves on which
   a nonconserved perturbation has a real, zero-damping mode; black-resonator
   branches grow from those onsets. Thus a horizon does not make every
   nonconserved horizon-coupled probe atomless
   (<https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5323>,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04793>). This defeats the universal
   black-hole-atomless direction.

Adding “Einstein-like,” “stable,” “away from marginality,” “no long strings,”
and “generic probe” would manufacture a different, proof-shaped model
statement. It would not repair the conjecture. The positive survivor is only
the exact AdS\(_3\) Hawking--Page model consequence: in the strict large-\(k\)
limit the oscillatory/decaying correlator transition induces a
pure-point/atomless return-phase transition for the selected response. That
consequence is useful exposition and a calibration example, but it cannot
carry novelty or Nature significance.

### Immediate fractures found after the jump

1. Recurrence exponents and local dimension are related in classical dynamical
   systems only under additional mixing or regularity structure; arithmetic
   rotations supply counterexamples to an unrestricted equality. Prediction 1
   must therefore remain restricted to the finite nondegenerate torus law.
2. Rational span of the *topological support* of a measure is not always the
   dimension of its temporal orbit. A countable dense atomic measure has
   topological support \(\mathbb R\), but its atoms may have rational rank one.
   The arbitrary-measure definition below is therefore only an exact-return
   constraint rank until spectral-type and orbit-closure theorems identify the
   correct general invariant.
3. Thermal time already makes flow depend on state and observable algebra.
   Novelty cannot be “time comes from a state” or qualitative observer
   dependence. It must be an exact dimension/spectral-type law, its universal
   finite-to-infinite transition, and a new physical prediction.
4. Infinite rational rank does not by itself create a time arrow. Every
   summable countable pure-point packet still returns arbitrarily closely at
   every fixed resolution. Prediction 3 is false if “dimension diverges” is
   its only nondegeneracy condition. The universal transition variable must be
   spectral atomicity, not cardinal rank.
5. The noncompact-space conjecture has a stronger known parent at the
   relativistic-representation level. Lorentz covariance plus the spectrum
   condition already imply that every timelike translation generator is
   purely absolutely continuous after removing translation-invariant vectors;
   the corresponding timelike clustering limit is known. Maison proved the
   four-dimensional result using Wigner representation theory, and Kruse
   (2024) gave a Mourre-theoretic proof and broader formulation. Equation (S5)
   is a useful independent measure theorem and covers arbitrary
   countable-fiber dispersions, but neither relativistic dephasing nor
   timelike clustering can be claimed as new physics.

These fractures narrow the proof problem; they do not retroactively narrow the
conjecture.

### New unconditional survivors

For every nonempty finite sector set there exists one faithful normalized
state, fixed independently of the observer, whose modular log-frequency
dimension saturates
\[
 \dim_{\rm time}(G)=|V|-c(G)
\]
for every observer graph \(G\) on that same state. Thus observer dependence is
not produced by changing the dynamics or retuning a state: access structure
alone realizes every graph-allowed dimension. An explicit countable
prime-log family also has temporal dimension exactly \(\aleph_0\).

For every Borel probability spectral measure \(\mu\), with survival amplitude
\(\widehat\mu(t)\), the symmetric-window mean fidelity obeys the exact
all-spectrum law
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1{2T}\int_{-T}^{T}
       |\widehat\mu(t)|^2\,dt
   =(\mu\otimes\mu)\{(x,y):x=y\}.
 \quad}                                                     \tag{S1}
\]
The right side is the spectral collision probability: the probability that
two independent samples from the observer's spectrum agree. It vanishes
exactly when \(\mu\) has no atoms.

Consequently, for every atomless spectrum—including every
singular-continuous spectrum—and every fixed \(\eta>0\),
\[
 \lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{
   |\{t\in(-T,T]:|\widehat\mu(t)|^2\ge\eta\}|}{2T}=0.        \tag{S2}
\]
Thus singular-continuous rigidity may permit rare returns, but never a
positive-density population of fixed-fidelity returns. For a mixed spectrum,
the same collision probability is the exact limiting mean-coherence budget
and gives the universal asymptotic upper budget for every high-fidelity return
density. For an absolutely continuous spectrum the stronger
Riemann--Lebesgue law gives eventual permanent escape below every positive
fidelity threshold.

Equations (S1)--(S2) cover pure-point, singular-continuous,
absolutely-continuous, and mixed spectral types without a finite-dimensional,
bounded-support, or absolute-continuity assumption. Together with the
countable pure-point recurrence theorem, they identify loss of atomicity—not
infinite rank—as the sharp recurrent-versus-density-arrow boundary.

The dimension side has a separate universal cardinal gap. For every real
Borel spectral measure,
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \dim_{\rm time}(\mu)\leq\aleph_0
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \dim_{\rm time}(\mu)=2^{\aleph_0}.
 \qquad}                                                     \tag{S3}
\]
No intermediate uncountable temporal cardinal can occur. If the support is
uncountable, closedness forces it to have continuum cardinality, and its
rational span therefore has continuum rank. In particular every atomless
probability spectrum has
\[
 \dim_{\rm time}(\mu)=2^{\aleph_0},                           \tag{S4}
\]
so the continuous phase is not merely infinite: it jumps directly to
continuum temporal dimension while all fixed positive-fidelity return sets
simultaneously acquire zero time density.

The measure law now has an exact stationary-field bridge. Every continuous
nonnegative momentum-symmetric Schwartz sesquilinear form has one unique
positive temperate spectral measure. Every nonzero Schwartz observer probe
selects a finite weighted spectrum, its normalization is a probability law,
and the probe weight is exactly the intrinsic diagonal value of the original
form. Equations (S1)--(S4) therefore apply to every probe. If the reconstructed
field spectrum is atomless, every nonzero probe has continuum temporal
dimension and zero-density fixed-fidelity returns.

There is also one explicit same-form separation theorem. A single positive
stationary field form with one atomic sector and one separated continuum
sector admits two Schwartz probes: one has unit return fidelity at every
instant, while the other has zero-density returns above every fixed positive
threshold. State and dynamics remain fixed; observer coupling alone changes
the return phase.

The spatial part now has a universal unconditional core. Let \(\nu\) be any
atomless measure in one momentum coordinate, let \(\mu\) be any measure of all
remaining labels, let \(\rho\ll\mu\otimes\nu\) be any correlated joint
observer law, and let \(E(a,p)\) be any measurable dispersion for which
\(\{p:E(a,p)=e\}\) is countable for every \(a,e\). Then
\[
 \boxed{\qquad E_\#\rho\ \text{is atomless}. \qquad}        \tag{S5}
\]
No finiteness or sigma-finiteness assumption is required for either product
factor. Arbitrary nonnegative observer weighting is included because a
weighted product remains absolutely continuous with respect to the original
product.

Whenever the pushed-forward energy law is normalized, (S5) joins (S2) and
(S4): one atomless spatial momentum coordinate forces continuum temporal
dimension and zero-density returns above every fixed positive fidelity.
For the relativistic dispersion
\[
 E(m,p)=\sqrt{m^2+p^2},
\]
each fixed-\((m,E)\) momentum fiber contains at most two points. The joined
conclusion therefore holds for every mass mixture and every normalized
correlated joint law absolutely continuous with respect to an arbitrary mass
law times an atomless momentum law. This closes the arbitrary-mass,
arbitrary-weight product-dispersion theorem; it does not yet prove that every
compact-local interacting QFT observer law has the required absolute
continuity.

The first clock-refactorization survivor is also exact. On every nonempty
finite-dimensional world, choose one nondegenerate labelled coordinate
observable and one fully connected labelled coupling. Their common commutant
contains only scalar matrices. Consequently:

1. if two arbitrary generators induce the same Heisenberg law on those two
   labels, their difference is one scalar energy-origin term;
2. after adjoining an arbitrary finite clock factor, the common commutant of
   the two lifted world labels consists exactly of clock-only operators;
3. every invertible clock--world refactorization preserving both labels by
   conjugation is therefore clock-only—no clock--world mixing survives;
4. if the physical clock labels comprise every basis reading and every
   regular group shift, covariance under one origin change forces the
   residual clock operator to be exactly that group translation times one
   global scalar;
5. if the refactorization is invertible, that scalar is nonzero;
6. combining the world and clock labels gives an if-and-only-if
   classification: every finite physical clock--world refactorization
   preserving the two world meanings and transporting all clock readings and
   shifts by one common origin is precisely one clock-origin translation
   times one nonzero global scalar.

Thus two physical world meanings, rather than the complete matrix algebra,
already extinguish arbitrary finite-dimensional law ambiguity and remove
every finite clock--world mixing transformation. Complete labelled clock
readings and shifts then reduce the residual freedom to one physical origin
change. This is a strict strengthening of the earlier complete-observable
commutant statement. It does not prove that the same minimal label set is
operationally complete, nor prove the conjecture for continuous or type-III
systems.

The discrete theorem is now cardinality-free. Let the clock be any group,
finite, countably infinite, or uncountable, and let its record space be the
full scalar function space on that group. Coordinate-projector covariance
forces every output coordinate to depend on exactly one input coordinate;
regular-shift covariance forces all coordinate weights to coincide. Hence
every linear clock refactorization preserving all readings and shifts is one
left translation times one scalar, with no topology, boundedness, basis
expansion, or summability assumption. If it is bijective, the scalar is
nonzero.

For any nonempty finite labelled world coupled to that arbitrary discrete
clock, the two world observables still force every total linear
refactorization to be clock-only. Combining both classifications gives the
exact if-and-only-if law at arbitrary discrete cardinality: a bijective
clock--world refactorization preserves the two world meanings and transports
all clock readings and regular shifts by one origin exactly when it is that
origin translation times one nonzero global scalar. Thus the countably
infinite clause of the conjecture is closed, and the result is stronger than
countable. Continuous clocks and infinite/type-III world algebras remain
separate problems because point-reading projectors and finite world-sector
decomposition are no longer available in the same form.

A second cardinality-free formulation removes point readings altogether.
Let an arbitrary group act by automorphisms on an arbitrary unital labelled
observable algebra, commutative or noncommutative. A linear refactorization
covariant with every labelled left multiplier and every group shift is
exactly
\[
  T(a)=\alpha_g(a)c,
\]
where \(g\) is one clock-origin transformation and \(c\) is fixed by the
entire group action. Conversely every such map has both covariance
properties. If \(T\) preserves the identity, then \(c=1\) and
\(T=\alpha_g\) exactly. This theorem has no finiteness, cardinality,
commutativity, topology, spectral-type, or factor-type restriction.

For the full scalar function algebra on any group, regular translation has
only constant invariant functions. The exact stabilizer is therefore one
regular translation times one scalar, without decidable equality or point
projectors. This is the algebraic continuous-clock survivor. It applies to
the underlying algebra of continuous-group and type-III models, but it does
not yet prove that equality of operational conditional probabilities implies
full multiplier covariance, nor that the resulting refactorization is normal,
bounded, or spatial on a Hilbert-space representation.

At the purely tomographic level, the scalar-to-algebra bridge is now exact.
For any family of linear reports that separates points of the labelled
observable algebra, equality of every reported multiplier and shift
correlation forces the corresponding algebra-valued covariance identities.
The same origin-times-fixed-element stabilizer therefore follows from scalar
reports alone. This closes the abstract local-tomography implication; it does
not identify finite quantum-instrument conditional probabilities with that
separating linear family.

The mathematical mechanism is close to classical systems of imprimitivity
and crossed-product covariance. It is therefore a bridge theorem, not yet a
novelty claim. Scientific novelty would require the missing operational
reconstruction implication and a physical consequence that existing
imprimitivity theory does not already supply.

These survivors establish an exact observer-spectral return law. They do not
yet establish compact-spacetime locality of the probes, modular spectral
measures for arbitrary local von Neumann algebras, identify the mathematical
density arrow with experienced time, select an arrow orientation, or derive
cosmological time. Those bridges remain separate proof obligations.

## Definition

For an arbitrary positive Borel spectral measure \(\mu_{\mathcal O}\) on the
real frequency axis, define the visible frequency group, visible frequency
module, and observer temporal dimension by
\[
 \Gamma(H,\mathcal O)
 =\operatorname{cl}_{\rm alg}^{\mathbb Z}
   (\operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O}),\qquad
 \Omega(H,\mathcal O)
 =\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}
   (\operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O}),
\]
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \dim_{\rm time}(H,\mathcal O)
 =\dim_{\mathbb Q}\Omega(H,\mathcal O).\quad}
\]
The dimension is a cardinal and may be finite or infinite. No finite spectrum,
finite observer family, or pure-point assumption enters this definition.

For a finite Hamiltonian with distinct energy sectors
\(E_1,\ldots,E_d\), and a finite observer family \(B^{(a)}\) with
nonnegative weights \(\lambda_a\), put
\[
 W_{ij}=\sum_a\lambda_a|B^{(a)}_{ij}|^2.
\]
The coherence graph \(G_{\mathcal O}\) joins \(i\) and \(j\) when
\(W_{ij}>0\) or \(W_{ji}>0\). Its spectral measure is atomic on the
visible gaps, so the general definition reduces exactly to
\[
 \Omega(H,\mathcal O)=
 \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}
 \{E_i-E_j:\{i,j\}\in E(G_{\mathcal O})\}.
\]

Because the visible gaps are differences inside graph components,
\[
 0\leq\dim_{\rm time}(H,\mathcal O)
 \leq d-c(G_{\mathcal O}).
\]
The upper endpoint is the observer's topological phase capacity; the actual
temporal dimension can be smaller because of arithmetic relations among
visible gaps.

## Theorem A — universal lattice--continuum law

For every observer spectral measure, with no cardinality restriction,
exactly one of the following temporal types occurs:

1. **stationary:** \(\Gamma_{\mathcal O}=\{0\}\);
2. **clock-lattice:** there is a unique positive fundamental frequency
   \(\omega_{\mathcal O}\) with
   \(\Gamma_{\mathcal O}=\omega_{\mathcal O}\mathbb Z\);
3. **gapless:** \(\Gamma_{\mathcal O}\) is dense in \(\mathbb R\).

Moreover:

- the observed record is stationary at every time exactly in case 1;
- a positive exact return exists exactly in cases 1 or 2;
- every nonstationary exact return is an integer multiple of
  \(2\pi/\omega_{\mathcal O}\);
- no positive exact return exists in case 3.

Thus every observer sees either no clock, one exact frequency lattice, or a
gapless temporal frequency group. There is no fourth possibility.

### Paper proof

Let \(S=\operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O}\). Exact return at \(T\ne0\)
means
\[
 e^{iT\omega}=1\qquad(\omega\in S).
\]
For the spectral integral this pointwise support statement is equivalent to
zero observed displacement: the integrand is continuous and nonnegative, so
zero integral makes it vanish almost everywhere; its zero set is closed and
therefore contains the support. The converse follows because the support is
conull.

The exponential condition is equivalent to
\[
 S\subseteq (2\pi/T)\mathbb Z,
\]
and hence, by algebraic generation,
\[
 \Gamma_{\mathcal O}\subseteq(2\pi/T)\mathbb Z.             \tag{A1}
\]
Every subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic. Conversely, if
\(\Gamma_{\mathcal O}=\omega\mathbb Z\), then
\(T=2\pi/|\omega|\) is a positive exact return when \(\omega\ne0\);
when \(\omega=0\), every time returns.

Finally every additive subgroup of the Archimedean line \(\mathbb R\) is
either cyclic or dense. The two alternatives are exclusive. Combining this
classification with (A1) proves the trichotomy and every return statement.

## Theorem A2 — dimension and the infinite rank-one fracture

\[
 \dim_{\rm time}=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Gamma_{\mathcal O}=\{0\}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{the observed record is stationary}.
\]
A nonstationary clock-lattice observer has
\(\dim_{\rm time}=1\). Every observer with
\(\dim_{\rm time}\ge2\), finite or infinite, is gapless and has no positive
exact return.

The converse “dimension one implies periodic” holds for finitely generated
visible groups, hence for every finite Hamiltonian/observer packet, but fails
for infinite spectra. A rank-one group such as
\(\mathbb Z[1/2]\) is dense and has no positive exact return. Therefore
infinite temporal structure has two independent invariants:

\[
 \boxed{\text{rational dimension}\quad+\quad
        \text{lattice versus dense type}.}
\]

This is the exact infinite extension. It removes the false inference that
rank one alone guarantees a clock once infinitely many visible frequencies
are allowed.

For a finite observer:

1. \(\dim_{\rm time}=0\) iff its record is stationary;
2. \(\dim_{\rm time}=1\) iff it is nonstationary and exactly periodic;
3. \(\dim_{\rm time}\ge2\) iff it has no positive exact return.

The same Hamiltonian can therefore be timeless, periodic, or gapless to
different observation algebras.

## Theorem B — observer monotonicity and exact quotient

If
\[
 \operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O_1}
 \subseteq\operatorname{supp}\mu_{\mathcal O_2},
\]
then
\[
 \Gamma(H,\mathcal O_1)\subseteq\Gamma(H,\mathcal O_2),
 \Omega(H,\mathcal O_1)\subseteq\Omega(H,\mathcal O_2),
 \qquad
 \dim_{\rm time}(H,\mathcal O_1)
 \leq\dim_{\rm time}(H,\mathcal O_2).
\]
In particular, absolute continuity
\(\mu_{\mathcal O_1}\ll\mu_{\mathcal O_2}\) implies all three conclusions.
Adding observations can reveal temporal dimensions; discarding observations
cannot create them.

For a finite coherence graph, support inclusion reduces to edge inclusion.
At the graph level the full relative-phase space has dimension
\(d-c(G_{\mathcal O})\). The temporal frequency module is the arithmetic
subspace actually traversed by the Hamiltonian.

## Theorem C — finite-resolution dephasing filtration

For a signal threshold \(\theta>0\), define the operational graph at
environmental time \(s\) by
\[
 \{i,j\}\in G_{\theta}(s)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 W_{ij}(s)>\theta\ \text{or}\ W_{ji}(s)>\theta,
\]
and define
\[
 \dim_{\rm time}^{(\theta)}(s)
 =\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}
 \{E_i-E_j:\{i,j\}\in E(G_\theta(s))\}.
\]

If every coherence weight is nonincreasing in \(s\), then
\[
 s\le t\Longrightarrow G_\theta(t)\le G_\theta(s)
\Longrightarrow
\dim_{\rm time}^{(\theta)}(t)
\le\dim_{\rm time}^{(\theta)}(s).
\]
Hence finite-resolution temporal dimension is an integer-valued monotone
filtration.

For exponential pure dephasing
\[
 B_{ij}(s)=
 e^{-\frac12(\ell_i-\ell_j)^2s}B_{ij}(0),
\]
the squared observer weights decay as
\[
 W_{ij}(s)=
 e^{-(\ell_i-\ell_j)^2s}W_{ij}(0).
\]
Every nonzero coherence remains nonzero at every finite \(s\), so exact
zero-threshold dimension does not change at finite time. For every fixed
positive threshold, edges cross at the explicit times
\[
 s_{ij}=
 \frac{\log(W_{ij}(0)/\theta)}
      {(\ell_i-\ell_j)^2}
\]
whenever numerator and denominator are positive. Continuous loss therefore
produces discrete operational changes.

## Theorem D — arbitrary dimension-loss schedules are physically realizable

For every \(r\ge1\), every threshold \(0<\theta<1\), and every ordered list
of positive times
\[
 0<\tau_1\le\cdots\le\tau_r,
\]
there is an \((r+1)\)-level Hamiltonian, a self-adjoint star observer, and one
diagonal Lindblad dephasing operator such that:

1. the \(r\) star gaps are rationally independent;
2. star edge \(j\) crosses threshold exactly at \(\tau_j\);
3. the operational temporal dimension equals the number of edges not yet
   crossed;
4. it therefore follows the prescribed stepwise schedule from \(r\) to
   zero.

### Construction

Use a hub \(0\), leaves \(1,\ldots,r\), and a self-adjoint observer whose
only nonzero off-diagonal entries are the star pairs. For distinct primes
\(p_1,\ldots,p_r\), choose
\[
 E_0=0,\qquad E_j=\log p_j.
\]
Unique factorisation makes the star gaps rationally independent. Give all
star edges common initial squared weight one.
Put
\[
 \ell_0=0,\qquad
 \ell_j=\sqrt{\log(1/\theta)/\tau_j}.
\]
Then star weight \(j\) is
\[
 e^{-\ell_j^2s},
\]
which equals \(\theta\) exactly at \(s=\tau_j\). A diagonal self-adjoint
\(L=\operatorname{diag}(\ell_0,\ldots,\ell_r)\) generates
\[
 \mathcal L(B)=LBL-\tfrac12(L^2B+BL^2),
\]
whose \((i,j)\) entry is
\[
 -\tfrac12(\ell_i-\ell_j)^2B_{ij}.
\]
Thus the displayed exponential is the exact Lindblad solution. Each active
star gap is an independent basis vector, so temporal dimension is exactly
the active-edge count.

## Theorem E — recurrence consequence

The existing observer graph law gives a spectrum-uniform deadline with
exponent \(d-c(G)\). The temporal dimension refines the exponent for a fixed
spectrum:

If every visible gap is represented using \(r=\dim_{\rm time}\) rationally
independent basis frequencies with a finite integer-coordinate height
\(\mathcal H\), simultaneous approximation of only those \(r\) basis
frequencies gives a recurrence deadline of order
\[
 \varepsilon^{-r}
\]
with an explicit factor depending on \(\mathcal H\), not on the number of
invisible sectors. Worst-spectrum constructions attain every \(r\), so the
rank exponent cannot be reduced uniformly.

Under the dephasing filtration, the operational recurrence exponent drops
with \(\dim_{\rm time}^{(\theta)}\). Smooth loss of coherence can therefore
cause abrupt reductions in observable recurrence complexity.
