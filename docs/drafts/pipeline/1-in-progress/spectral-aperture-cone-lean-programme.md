## Design

- `K(H)` and `Q` become non-unital C*-algebras: multiplication is well defined because
  `C_0` histories form a two-sided star ideal; the C*-identity on `Q` follows from the limsup norm
  (`limsup ‖F*F‖ = (limsup ‖F‖)²`), with no general quotient theory.
- The scale germs are values of one isometric non-unital `*`-homomorphism
  `Φ_T : C([0,1], ℂ)₀ → Q`, `g ↦ [ε ↦ g(E_ε(T))]` (isometry: eigenvalue/aperture matching).
  Then `σ(e_c) = [0,1]`, `C*(e_c) = C*(e_1) ≅ C_0((0,1])`, `e_d = φ_{d/c}(e_c)` by uniqueness of
  the continuous functional calculus.
- `J_T = Φ_T(ker ev₁)`; density of the algebra generated by `φ_c − id` via the injective profile
  `κ(t) = t(1−t)(1+it)` and non-unital Stone–Weierstrass.  `K₁` of the suspension ideal: homotopy
  classes of unitaries in its unitization, identified with free loops in `S¹` (winding).
- Wiener–Hopf: regular representation of `C_0(ℝ) ⋊ ℝ` on `L²(ℝ)`; closure of `span M_f λ_g` is
  `K(L²(ℝ))`.
