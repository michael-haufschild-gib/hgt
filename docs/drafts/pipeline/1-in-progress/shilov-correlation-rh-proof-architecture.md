## Impact verdict

The law materially sharpens the existing local-Shilov and Route-R programs but does not presently
constitute an independent RH route. It provides a canonical obstruction carrier even when

finite-prime trivializations can fail cofinally. The first theorem that would promote the idea to
a new route is a source-pure construction of the arithmetic reporter diagram together with a
reflection-odd relative contraction independent of divisor location and Weil positivity.
