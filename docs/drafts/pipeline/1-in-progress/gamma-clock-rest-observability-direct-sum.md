## Motif

Hypertranscendence concerns the observability structure of a clock, not temporal evolution.  A
clock is a factor in an observation algebra.  Its invariant is the algebraic rank function on
finite families of observables, including its circuits and closures, rather than a phase rate or
a determinant magnitude.

For finite sets `S` of clock observables and `R` of rest observables, the desired factorization law
is

\[
 \operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C(S\cup R)
 =\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C(S)
  +\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C(R).                 \tag{1}
\]

Thus the joint algebraic matroid is the direct sum of the clock and rest matroids.  Equation (1)
is the source-pure analogue of a faithful clock/rest Schmidt factorization.

## Gamma source

\[
 z=-n+s_nB_n(T),\qquad w=-1/T=\Gamma(z),\qquad
 D_z=a_n(T)D_w,

where

\[
 a_n(T)=\frac{1}{s_nT^2B_n'(T)}.

The pole label `n` and reciprocal coordinate `T` vary independently on a complete product tail:

## Generic product lemma `[PROVED ON PAPER]`

Let `A⊆C^X` and `B⊆C^Y` be function algebras.  The multiplication map

\[
 A\otimes_{\mathbb C}B\longrightarrow C^{X\times Y},\qquad
 f\otimes g\longmapsto ((x,y)\mapsto f(x)g(y))                 \tag{2}
\]

is injective.  Indeed, write a nonzero tensor as `sum_i f_i tensor g_i` with the `g_i` linearly
independent and some `f_j` nonzero.  Choose `x` where the coefficient vector `(f_i(x))` is nonzero.
The resulting nonzero linear combination of the `g_i` is nonzero at some `y`.

The same finite argument works for eventual sequence germs after increasing the common pole
threshold.  Consequently independent clock coordinates and independent rest coordinates stay
independent as functions of the independent pair `(n,T)`.  What remains is to show that the actual
Gamma pullback realizes exactly this product algebra without specialization collapse.

## Clock observability `[FORMALIZED]`

Define

\[
 \mathcal C_n(T)=\frac{B_n(T)-T}{T^2}.
\]

`GammaInversePoleClockObservability.lean` proves that its formal coefficient-germ series satisfies
no finite algebraic differential relation.  Thus the nonlinear clock factor has the free
finite-resolution matroid required by (1).  This is stronger and more invariant than comparing
the magnitudes of projective Jacobian determinants.

## Triangular observation map `[PROVED ON PAPER, SPECIALIZATION OPEN]`

For a lower-tower observable `F(w)`, the visible `z`-jets are

\[
 D_z^r w=(aD_w)^r w,\qquad D_z^rF(w)=(aD_w)^rF(w).             \tag{3}
\]

The first family is triangular in

\[
 a,D_wa,\ldots,D_w^{r-1}a:
 \quad D_z^{r+1}w=a^rD_w^ra+\text{lower clock jets}.
\]

After recovering those clock jets, the second family is triangular in the rest jets:

\[
 D_z^rF=a^rD_w^rF+\text{lower rest jets}.
\]

Since every inverse branch is noncritical, `a≠0`, so both triangular systems have rational
inverses.  The visible observation algebra therefore recovers the clock factor first and the rest
factor second.  No temporal interpretation is used.

## Falsifiers and attacks

1. **Formal-variable attack.**  A proof that only cites the polynomial ring in
   `GammaInverseBranchLowerJetFreeness` has not reached the actual rest algebra.
2. **One-parameter collapse attack.**  The argument must use the common product collar.  Evaluating
   on one coupled curve without product injectivity can create mixed algebraic relations.
3. **Gauge attack.**  The fixed coefficients `B(0)=0`, `B'(0)=1` are not clock capacity.  The
   observable is the nonlinear deformation `(B-T)/T^2`.
4. **Determinant attack.**  `2^(rn)` versus `1` in projective minors is normalization-dependent;
   both nonsingular blocks have the same algebraic rank.
5. **Dynamics attack.**  WKB phase speed, Page ordering, and BO adiabaticity do not measure (1).
6. **Novelty attack.**  Tensor injectivity and triangular observability are classical.  Candidate
   novelty can only lie in the source-specific actual Gamma specialization and its iterate-tower
   consequence.
