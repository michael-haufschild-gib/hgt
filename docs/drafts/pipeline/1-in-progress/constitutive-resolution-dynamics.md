## 1. Why another framework is justified

The corpus does not lack RH receivers.  It contains many exact selectors, equivalences, local
positive forms, singular carriers, projective charts, and coefficient-faithful finite packets.
They fail at a common transition:

\[
 \text{literal source law}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{signed orientation}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{singular infinite landing}.
\]

Treating the routes as independent searches hides that common transition.  Treating them as
ordinary charts of a pre-existing geometry is also insufficient: the corpus already contains
Rees gradings, blow-up charts, derived classes, Hodge reconstructions, and observer complexes, but
none of those objects by itself generates the missing source law.

The extra structure is **dynamical generation of the resolution itself**.  A source relation is
not merely stored in an ideal.  Legal deformations prolong it to all orders.  When ordinary
formation reaches a simultaneous base locus, the first active law coefficient creates an
exceptional chart.  Products add its depth; nonfaithful observers can erase it; cofinal evolution
either revisits a fixed chart or forces its depth to infinity.

This package is called **Constitutive Resolution Dynamics (CRD)**.

## 2. Basic object

A CRD system is intended to contain the following typed data.

1. A source algebra `A` whose coordinates are formed before any target zero or spectral state is
   selected.
2. A source-law ideal `I ⊂ A`, recording exact relations among those coordinates.
3. A legal Hasse--Schmidt deformation `D=(D_n)_{n≥0}` generating the complete response history of
   every coordinate and law.
4. A formation ideal `J`, generated by response/admission coordinates which may vanish together at
   singular completion.
5. Its Rees/normal history, retaining the first active graded coefficient instead of identifying
   the whole base locus to one point.
6. A family of route observers `O_r`; each route is one partial chart or quotient of the common
   resolved history, not a competing ontology.
7. An involution carrying functional-equation reflection before observation.
8. A cofinal source evolution and an active-order function `κ`, measuring the first nonzero
   admission layer at each stage.

This is a mechanism, not a declaration that every corpus object already fits.  A route is admitted
to one CRD system only after its coordinates and transition laws are constructed from the same
literal source deformation.

## 3. Laws already proved

### CRD-1 — source-law prolongation

Every polynomial source law generates an all-order Hasse tower.  For a product circuit

\[
 q\star m=c h,
\]

the tower is triangular whenever an admission port is active.  This is proved in `ULF-LJPM-1`.

### CRD-2 — observer provenance

Injective coefficient observers reflect the entire prolonged law.  Noninjective observers may
create relations absent from the source.  Therefore a receiver identity is admissible evidence
only after source-level generation or a proved faithful lift.

### CRD-3 — regular withheld-organ reconstruction

If `m_0≠0`, every hidden response coefficient `q_n` is reconstructed recursively from `m,h` and
earlier `q_j`.  This generated the independently checkable Boolean--Mass--Hankel--QFI first-response
identity.

### CRD-4 — singular exceptional reconstruction

If `m_0,…,m_{k-1}` vanish and `m_k≠0`, the same reconstruction begins at the exceptional order:

\[
 q_n=
 \frac{c h_{k+n}-\sum_{j<n}q_jm_{k+n-j}}{m_k}.
\]

Ordinary blow-down reaches a common zero through order `k-1`; the lifted history remains complete.
This is proved in `ULF-SLBR-1`.

### CRD-5 — global algebraic formation faithfulness

For every nonzero admission history `m`, Hasse multiplication

\[
 q\mapsto q\star m
\]

is injective on complete histories.  Vanishing of the ordinary port or any finite prefix is not an
information-theoretic obstruction.

### CRD-6 — additive exceptional grade

If two organs first activate at orders `k` and `ℓ`, their fused organ first activates at `k+ℓ`,
with leading port equal to the product of the two leading ports.  Exceptional depth is therefore
an additive formation grade, while leading exceptional amplitude is multiplicative.

### CRD-7 — cofinal chart/escape dichotomy

For a moving active order `κ_N`, either one finite exceptional chart occurs at arbitrarily late
stages, or `κ_N→∞`.  There is no diffuse third failure mode.  This is the first exact statement in
the framework connecting infinite differential complexity to noncompact escape.

## 5. Route-independent predictions

CRD makes predictions which can be checked without proving RH.

1. **Higher-order response prediction.** Once one circuit and its first active admission port are
   identified, every withheld response coefficient is forced.  An independently formalized fourth
   organ must satisfy the recursion.
2. **Depth additivity.** First-active orders computed independently in two organs must add after
   their source-native fusion; leading ports must multiply.
3. **No finite-prefix retirement.** A no-go killing orders `0,…,K` cannot retire a route unless it
   proves the full admission history zero or proves active-order escape.
4. **Observer fracture localization.** If an identity appears only after positivity, trace,
   determinant, or scalar fusion, its first possible false coefficient is the first active layer
   killed by that observer.
5. **Cofinal obstruction classification.** Failure of fixed-chart properness must manifest as
   `κ_N→∞`; vague “loss at infinity” is not an accepted diagnosis.
6. **Gamma/Cartier normal prediction.** For any common normal Hasse coordinate on the cooriented
   Gamma gauge and transported Cartier port, the first exceptional ratio must equal centered
   horizontal displacement and all higher ratios must satisfy the singular recursion.

## 6. Why this is not regression into a route

The fixed `(2,3)` Gamma factorization already proves a positive gauge times horizontal displacement
and gives a port whose vanishing is equivalent to critical-line placement.  Its Stokes-flatness is
RH-strength.  CRD does not relabel that endpoint as a proof.

Instead, CRD changes the universal completion burden:

- old burden: produce a uniformly invertible ordinary leading port;
- new algebraic theorem: any finite first active port reconstructs the complete history;
- remaining global burden: rule out escape of that active order and prove a source-native
  exceptional landing.

That statement applies simultaneously to Gamma/Cartier, singular Green/Stokes, Hankel contact,
recurrence history, and any future organ with a common Hasse formation.  A single route provides a
laboratory, not the definition.

## 7. RH proof architecture

CRD yields one specific proof architecture, with no claim that its open theorems are already true.

### A. Common normal-formation theorem

Construct before choosing a Xi zero one source deformation whose typed coordinates include:

1. reflection-odd orientation;
2. signed prime/depth incidence;
3. singular admission history;
4. completed source response.

Prove that these coordinates satisfy one prolonged relation and that existing route organs are
faithful charts or observers of it.

### B. Active-order properness theorem

For every source-selected cofinal state, prove the admission history is nonzero and rule out
`κ_N→∞`.  By CRD-7, one fixed finite exceptional chart then recurs cofinally.  Strengthen this to a
closed/proper landing by controlling the inverse leading port on that chart.

### C. Source annihilation theorem

Derive from literal arithmetic/Theta/Euler--Gamma formation—not from a supplied divisor—that the
completed physical zero law annihilates the deciding response coordinate on the common chart.

### D. Selection

CRD-4 reconstructs signed orientation from the annihilated completed response.  Observer
faithfulness prevents a receiver-created relation.  Existing proper RH receivers then force
neutral horizontal displacement.

The final implication is routine only after A--C.  A and B are the new geometric/formation burden;
C is the remaining arithmetic producer.

## 9. Falsifiers

CRD must be retired or revised if any of the following occurs.

1. Three candidate organs cannot be constructed from one pre-target source deformation.
2. Independently computed exceptional orders violate CRD-6 under claimed fusion.
3. A predicted higher response coefficient disagrees with an existing independently certified
   organ theorem.
4. Active-order failure occurs without either a cofinally recurring finite chart or escape to
   infinity.
5. Common observation is nonfaithful at the deciding exceptional layer and no source lift repairs
   it.
6. The only available completed-response annihilation statement is equivalent to RH after a zero
   is supplied.
7. The framework produces no theorem beyond already-known Hasse, Rees, blow-up, or observer facts.

The proven singular reconstruction, depth conservation, and cofinal dichotomy clear falsifier 7
at the abstract level.  The decisive tests are now concrete common formation and arithmetic source
annihilation.
