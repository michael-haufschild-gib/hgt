- **Arithmetic reconstruction of the right-line datum, paper proof; Lean theorem open.** Write `χ(s)=Γℝ(1-s)/Γℝ(s)`, `G(s)=exp((s-1/2)^2/(4π))`, and `a=μ*μ` (Dirichlet convolution). The integral converges absolutely because `Gχ=Fζ²` on that line, `F` has the proved Gaussian `L¹` bound, and the Dirichlet series bounds `ζ` uniformly there. Also `∑|a(n)|n^{-2}≤ζ(2)^2`, so Fubini gives the exact, absolutely convergent source formula `R_2(x)=∑_{n≥1}a(n)K(nx)` for every `x>0`. This is a concrete arithmetic realization of the sewn zero-damping scalar; identifying it as a limit in the literal Green/BV source domain remains open.
- **Reflected residue signature, paper proof; Lean theorem open.** Reflection gives `R_{-1}(x)=x^{-1}R_2(x^{-1})` and hence `D(x):=R_2(x)-R_{-1}(x)` obeys `D(x)=-x^{-1}D(x^{-1})`. For paired poles `ρ,1-ρ`, `Res_{1-ρ}(x^{-s}F(s))=-x^{-1}Res_ρ((x^{-1})^{-s}F(s))`. Thus the paired contribution at `x=e^y` is `-2e^{-y/2}Res_ρ(sinh((s-1/2)y)F(s))`.
