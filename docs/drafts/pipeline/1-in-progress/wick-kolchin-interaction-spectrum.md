- **Statement:** Hypertranscendence of a quantum-field response and interaction are independent

## Goal

Give hypertranscendence a literal physical carrier without confusing it with non-Gaussianity,
interaction strength, chaos, or quantum gravity.  Then define the smallest invariant that removes
the Gaussian spectral contribution and can detect differential complexity created only by higher
connected sectors.

The founding physical question is not

```text
Which special function occurs in a QFT calculation?
```

but

```text
Which part of the differential constraint complexity belongs to the positive spectral measure,
and which part first appears after Wick factorization is removed?
```

## Medalist lens

1. A Källén--Lehmann measure fixes a scalar two-point function by a static positivity and spectral
   constraint.  No Hamiltonian flow needs to generate the response as a function of momentum.
2. A generalized free field may have an arbitrarily complicated positive spectral measure while
   every connected correlator above order two vanishes.
3. Therefore bare hypertranscendence of a two-point response cannot be an interaction invariant.
4. The candidate invariant must be relative to a specified differential source geometry.  An
   arbitrary analytic reparametrization is not admissible: it can change differential-algebraic
   type.  Differential-birational changes and derivation-preserving source embeddings are the
   natural equivalences.
5. The invariant must see connected sectors, not the full moment hierarchy, because the
   moment--cumulant/Bell transform can manufacture arbitrarily high full correlators from Gaussian
   pairings alone.

## Candidate moves

### Candidate A -- spectral hypertranscendence

`[BRIDGE; PROVED ON PAPER]` Put, for `0<a,b<1`,

```text
rho_(a,b) = (1-a) sum_(k>=0) a^k delta_(b^k).                 (1)
```

This is a positive probability measure with compact mass-squared support.  Its normalized
mass-resolvent response is

```text
R_(a,b)(z)
  = integral 1/(1-z t) d rho_(a,b)(t)
  = (1-a) sum_(k>=0) a^k/(1-z b^k).                          (2)
```

It obeys the fixed self-similarity constraint

```text
R_(a,b)(z) = (1-a)/(1-z) + a R_(a,b)(b z).                   (3)
```

The response is nonrational because its meromorphic continuation has the distinct poles
`b^(-k)`.  Equation `(3)` homogenizes to a linear `b`-difference equation.  The main theorem of
Adamczewski--Dreyfus--Hardouin therefore makes `(2)` hypertranscendental over `C(z)`.  The Euclidean
propagator

```text
G_(a,b)(s) = integral 1/(s+t) d rho_(a,b)(t)
```

is hypertranscendental as a germ at infinity because

```text
G_(a,b)(-1/z) = -z R_(a,b)(z),                              (4)
```

and differential algebraicity is preserved by rational substitution.

The positive polynomially bounded measure `(1)` defines a scalar generalized free Wightman field.
Its generating functional is Gaussian and all connected `n`-point functions vanish for `n != 2`.

### Candidate B -- finite-law eviction at the continuum boundary

`[CANDIDATE THEOREM; PARENT FUNCTIONAL ANALYSIS]` Fix derivative order `r`, dependent-variable
degree `d`, and base-coordinate degree `h`.  These bounds define a finite-dimensional vector space
`Law(r,d,h)` of algebraic differential laws.  Evaluation on a response `f` gives a linear map

```text
Ev_f : Law(r,d,h) -> analytic functions on a compact source domain.           (5)
```

If `f_j -> f` in the `C^r` topology, then `Ev_(f_j) -> Ev_f` in operator norm.  If `f` is HT,
`Ev_f` is injective.  Injective continuous linear maps with finite-dimensional domain form an open
set.  Hence

```text
for every fixed (r,d,h), Ev_(f_j) is injective for all sufficiently large j.   (6)
```

Thus an HT continuum limit evicts every fixed finite law window.  Any exact laws of finite-cutoff
approximants must escape through derivative order, nonlinear degree, base-coordinate complexity,
or failure of the required convergence.  For the finite atomic truncations of `(1)`, the escaping
quantity is already visible in the increasing rational denominator degree.

Equation `(6)` is a useful bridge, but its proof is the standard openness of injectivity.  It is
not by itself candidate new scientific knowledge.

### Candidate C -- Wick-reduced Kolchin profile

`[CONJECTURE; SELECTED]` Let `(K,D)` be the differential field of an admissible one-parameter
physical source probe, and let `C_n` be the connected `n`-point response pulled back to that probe.
For finite correlation height `N` and jet height `r`, define

```text
kappa_(<=N)(r)
  = trdeg_K K(D^j C_n : 1<=n<=N, 0<=j<=r),                  (7)

kappa_G(r)
  = trdeg_K K(D^j C_n : 1<=n<=2, 0<=j<=r),                 (8)

iota_N(r)
  = trdeg_(K<D^j C_1,D^j C_2 : j<=r>)
      K<D^j C_n : 1<=n<=N, j<=r>.                           (9)
```

Use relative transcendence degree in `(9)` rather than numerical subtraction when the fields are
not known finite-dimensional.  The sequence `iota_N(r)` is the **Wick-reduced Kolchin profile**.

- A generalized free field has `iota_N(r)=0` for every `N,r`, regardless of the complexity of its
  two-point spectrum.
- The geometric field `(1)` has maximal one-generator jet growth in its two-point response while
  its Wick-reduced profile is identically zero.
- A field has **interaction hypertranscendence at level `N`** only if the higher connected sectors
  contribute positive asymptotic relative Kolchin growth over the Gaussian sector.

This makes spectral HT and interaction HT different coordinates rather than different names for
one phenomenon.

## Attack on the selected candidate

### Attack 1 -- source-coordinate dependence

An arbitrary analytic source change can alter differential-algebraic type.  Consequently `(7)`--
`(9)` are not invariants of a bare collection of functions.

**Positive salvage.**  Treat the differential source probe as part of the physical object and
require invariance only under differential-birational reporter changes and derivation-preserving
source embeddings.  The existing HTGeometry clock-covariance and observable-net theorems supply
the one-generator algebraic model for this restriction.

### Attack 2 -- ordinary rather than functional derivatives

Schwinger--Dyson equations live on an infinite-dimensional source space, while `(7)` uses one
ordinary probe derivation.  A finite slice can hide transverse functional complexity.

**Positive salvage.**  The full theory should use a multivariate Kolchin polynomial for a finite
family of commuting source probes, followed by a directed limit over probe families.  The
one-derivation profile is a measurable shadow, not the final invariant.

### Attack 3 -- Gaussian higher moments masquerade as interaction

Full `n`-point functions of a Gaussian field are nonzero at every even order.

**Positive salvage.**  Use truncated/connected correlators before taking differential fields.
The polynomial Bell transform between full and connected coordinates is infrastructure only; it
cannot contribute to `iota_N`.

### Attack 4 -- local counterterms and field redefinitions

Renormalization schemes change correlators by local differential-algebraic terms.  A raw list of
jets may count the same local counterterm repeatedly.

**Positive salvage.**  Define the profile over the differential field generated by admissible
local counterterms and take relative growth of the nonlocal connected sector.  A viable theorem
must prove scheme invariance under this base extension rather than assume it.

### Attack 5 -- the physical anchor is still parent assembly

The geometric measure, generalized `q`-logarithm, difference-Galois dichotomy, Källén--Lehmann
representation, and Gaussian factorization are all established parent theories.

**Verdict.**  The existence theorem is `[BRIDGE]`, not new science.  Its scientific role is to kill
the wrong invariant and force the Wick reduction.  Candidate novelty begins only with a
scheme- and probe-invariant interaction profile plus a nontrivial continuum-QFT theorem about it.
