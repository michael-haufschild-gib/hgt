# The signed complement beyond the integer wall window

[Section 2.16](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#216-exclusion-of-the-critical-state-programme-ecs) requires a strict improvement over $\operatorname{Re}\zeta(1/2)m^2$ for the full oriented fractional test, with $m=\int\Phi$. Put $X=e^t$ and $h(X)=\sqrt{X\log X}$. The open contribution contains every $n>X+h(X)$ and the entire charge $-m^2/2$. Its sign on favorable centers and its cost on the rest of the test are unknown.

**Verified integer window.** The adverse coefficient mass at width $H$ has order $H/h(X)$. At $H=h(X)$ the signed sum is $\gamma((\psi(X+H)-\psi(X))/H-1)+o(1)$ with $\gamma>0$, and exceeds every prescribed negative level arbitrarily late. [Adverse mass](../3-formalized/ecs-critical-width-of-adverse-integer-mass.md) · [Signed sum](../3-formalized/ecs-signed-chebyshev-excess-at-the-critical-width.md).

**Time and divisor bounds.** The early integral at $U=T/8$ costs $O_\Phi(e^{-3T/16})$. On $t>U$, replacing $S$ by the ordered row $L(t)-m/2$ over $d>e^{t/4}$ costs $O_\Phi(e^{-U/16})$ in absolute integral. [Paper proof](../2-fully-proven/ecs-early-mass-and-the-signed-large-divisor-tail.md).

**Same centers, all horizons.** Favorable centers in every late $(X,RX]$ carry a fixed positive mass under $K(\log X-\log u)\,du/u$. There is a common set where, for every $y\ge u$, $\psi(y)-\psi(u)\ge c(y-u)-c(RX)^{1/4}$, with $c<1$ chosen close to one. At $D=\lfloor u^{1/4}\rfloor$, replacing small-divisor multiples counts by their linear terms costs at most $D\log D$ uniformly in $y$. The literal integer reference remains. Passing to the full state weight requires paying the linear drift and the intervals on which its variation changes sign. [Fractional mass](../2-fully-proven/ecs-positive-measure-of-signed-critical-width-centers.md) · [All future increments](../2-fully-proven/ecs-all-future-prime-increments-at-the-same-centers.md).

**Ordinary-prime transfer.** In log time, remove the subset of favorable centers with $|P-S|>\varepsilon/(2(|m|+1))$. The sign-transfer tail bounds its measure by $O_{\Phi,\varepsilon}(e^{-T/40})$. Since $0\le K\le1$, the remaining set still carries a fixed positive test mass. On that set $mP$ exceeds the full complementary integer row minus $\varepsilon$. The sign of the complementary row itself remains decisive.
