# The signed complement beyond the integer wall window

[Section 2.16](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#216-programme-to-close-ecs) requires a strict improvement over $\operatorname{Re}\zeta(1/2)m^2$ for the original oriented prime test, with $m=\int\Phi$. Put $x=e^t$ and $h=\sqrt{x\log x}$. Up to $o_\Phi(1)$ in weighted absolute integral on every measurable time set, its integrand is the signed row over $n>x+h$, including $-m^2/2$, restricted to $T/6<t\le6T$. Put $X=e^T$. Along an increasing arithmetic sequence $k=X^{8/7}$, the entire signed contribution from $n>k$ tends to zero on this interval. For $1\le N\le CX^{2/3}$ the prefix contributes $a(\psi(N)-N)/\sqrt X+o_\Phi(1)$ with $a=(m/2)\int e^{v/2}\Phi(v)\,dv>0$. At $N=\lfloor\sqrt X\rfloor$ the lower prefix also vanishes. A positive limsup for the remaining band $\sqrt X<n\le X^{8/7}$ on those cutoffs would give the strict gain. **Ordinary-prime deficit.** If RH fails, every $0<\eta\le1$ has tests $X=e^T\to\infty$ and $X^{1/(1+\eta)}\le N\le X$ on which ordinary primes against the unit reference on $(N,\lceil X^{1+\eta}\rceil]$ contribute at most $-cX^g+\operatorname{Re}\zeta(1/2)m^2+o(1)$. The constants $c,g>0$ are uniform in $\eta$. All proper powers in this band supply $-\operatorname{Re}\zeta(1/2)m^2+o(1)$. [Ordinary-prime deficit and square charge](../3-formalized/ecs-proper-power-charge-inside-the-diagonal-interval.md). The adverse sign persists on one integer interval after binary expansion and removal of all denominators below the square root. [Odd quotients in the deficit](../3-formalized/ecs-odd-quotients-in-the-ordinary-prime-deficit.md).

**State-weighted tail.** At a favorable center $u$ put $b=\lfloor u+h(u)\rfloor$, $a_j=\Lambda(j)-1$, $A_j=\sum_{u<n\le j}a_n$ and $w_j=m\,\mathrm{atom}(\Phi,j,\log u)$. The partial sum on $(b,L]$ is
\[
 w_L(A_L-A_b)+\sum_{b<j<L}(A_j-A_b)(w_j-w_{j+1}).
\]
The positive variations $w_{j+1}>w_j$ require an upper prefix. A [two-sided bound](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TwoSidedPrimePrefixesAtAlmostEveryCenter.lean) controls every future prefix from both $u$ and $u+h(u)$ after removing $o(X)$ centers from the original octave. The [variation estimates](ecs-signed-variation-of-the-weighted-complement.md) treat the adjacent band by the actual wall law and the remote tail by confinement. The [original tested weight at the moving far edge](../3-formalized/ecs-tested-state-weight-at-the-moving-far-edge.md) has normalized slope tending to $-m\int e^{-v/2}\Phi(v)\,dv$. Its sign remains open. At integer abscissae far below $e^T$ the limiting weight involves the positive charge at $+1/2$. [Tested integer weights](ecs-integer-weights-after-the-original-fractional-test.md).

**Verified integer window.** The adverse coefficient mass at width $H$ has order $H/h(X)$. At $H=h(X)$ the signed sum is $\gamma((\psi(X+H)-\psi(X))/H-1)+o(1)$ with $\gamma>0$, and its absolute value tends to zero in measure on every late octave. Its absolute integral against the original test tends to zero uniformly over all measurable time sets, including the exceptional centers. Both all-future prefix bounds hold outside the same vanishing exception. [Full first-window estimate](../3-formalized/ecs-first-window-in-the-original-fractional-test.md). **Time and divisor bounds.** The original absolute test costs $O_\Phi(e^{-T/12})$ before $T/6$ and $O_\Phi(e^{-T/10})$ after $6T$ on any measurable selection. [Exterior bounds](../3-formalized/ecs-exterior-mass-of-the-original-fractional-test.md). On $t>U$, replacing $S$ by the ordered row $L(t)-m/2$ over $d>e^{t/4}$ costs $O_\Phi(e^{-U/16})$ in absolute integral. [Paper proof](../2-fully-proven/ecs-early-mass-and-the-signed-large-divisor-tail.md).

**Same centers, all horizons.** Favorable centers in every late $(X,RX]$ carry a fixed positive mass under $K(\log X-\log u)\,du/u$. There is a common set where, for every $y\ge u$, $\psi(y)-\psi(u)\ge c(y-u)-c(RX)^{1/4}$, with $c<1$ chosen close to one. At $D=\lfloor u^{1/4}\rfloor$, replacing small-divisor multiples counts by their linear terms costs at most $D\log D$ uniformly in $y$. The literal integer reference remains. Passing to the full state weight requires paying the linear drift and the intervals on which its variation changes sign. **Ordinary-prime transfer.** Discarding $|P-S|>\varepsilon/(2(|m|+1))$ costs $O_{\Phi,\varepsilon}(e^{-T/40})$ in time measure. In each late octave the remaining set still carries fixed positive fractional-test mass and satisfies $mP>C-\varepsilon$ for the complete oriented complementary row $C$.
