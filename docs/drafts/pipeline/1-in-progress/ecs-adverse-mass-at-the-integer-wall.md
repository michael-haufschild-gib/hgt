# The signed complement beyond the integer wall window

In [§2.16](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#216-exclusion-of-the-critical-state-programme-ecs), write $m=\int\Phi$, $X=e^t$ and $h=\sqrt{X\log X}$. Split the literal row at $X+h$. The [critical-width bound](../3-formalized/ecs-critical-width-of-adverse-integer-mass.md) prices adverse coefficients before averaging. The [signed window calculation](../2-fully-proven/ecs-signed-chebyshev-excess-at-the-critical-width.md) identifies the first window with the local Chebyshev excess and gives arbitrarily late favorable readings.

**Early and late mass.** On $t\le U$, $U\ge0$, confinement gives $|P(t)|+|S(t)|\ll_\Phi e^{2U}$. Since $K(T-t)\le e^{(t-T)/2}$, the absolute early integral is $O_\Phi(e^{(5U-T)/2})$. Take $U=T/8$. On $t>U$ the [logarithmic Möbius sum](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheLargeDivisorMomentsOfThePrimeWave.lean) $L(t)$ over $d>e^{t/4}$ omits centered multiples controlled in $L^2$ with tilt $1/8$. Subtracting the integer quadrature error restores the literal $-1$ reference. Cauchy–Schwarz gives $\int_{t>U}|S(t)-(L(t)-m/2)|\,dt\ll_\Phi e^{-U/16}$.

**Open sign.** The complementary integer row retains every $n>X+h$ and the entire charge $-m^2/2$. Its reading on the same favorable windows is uncontrolled. The endpoint requires a strict improvement over $\operatorname{Re}\zeta(1/2)m^2$ for the full oriented fractional-part integral. The integer window indexes $n$ and the Möbius tail indexes $d$; combining these deletions requires accounting for their intersection.

**From individual centers to time windows.** Put $E(x)=\psi(x)-x$ and $Q(x)=h(x)^{-1}\int_x^{x+h(x)}E(v)\,dv$. The candidate identity $\Delta_h E/h=Q'-(h'/h)(E(x+h)-Q)$ holds away from the jump endpoints. Here $Q=o(x)$ by the prime number theorem and $h'/h=O(1/x)$. Integrating against $dx/x$ on a fixed logarithmic window should give a vanishing signed mean for the initial integer window. This would locate the full fractional-test deficit in the complementary row.
