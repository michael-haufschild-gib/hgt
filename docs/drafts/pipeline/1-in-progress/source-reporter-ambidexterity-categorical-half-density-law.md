\]

\[
  \epsilon:B\longrightarrow A
\]
extract the coefficient of \(x^{n-1}\). Then
\[
  \epsilon(x^ix^j)=
  \begin{cases}
    1,&i+j=n-1,\\
    0,&\text{otherwise},
  \end{cases}
  \qquad 0\le i,j<n.                                    \tag{4d}
\]
Hence the residue pairing \((a,b)\mapsto\epsilon(ab)\) has an anti-diagonal unimodular Gram

\[
  \operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(1)=n,\qquad
  \operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(x^k)=0\quad(1\le k<n).         \tag{4e}
\]
