# Route 1 (sub-quantum chiral passage) — fact log

Scope: `docs/reports/route-proposals/route1.md`. Facts and no-gos only.

- **R1-F1 [paper, derived here]:** Gaussian passage defect. For `x>0`, `Δ_x := logDeriv Φ_x - logDeriv ξ = -(J_x - M_x) - F`, `F(z) = -ζ'/ζ(z) - 1/(z-1)`. Mellin: `J_x - M_x = (1/2πi)∫_(c) Γ(w)(πx)^(-w) F(s+2w) dw`. Residues: `w=0` gives `F(s)`, `w=(ρ-s)/2` gives `-(m_ρ/2)Γ((ρ-s)/2)(πx)^((s-ρ)/2)`, `w=-k` gives `O(x^k)`. So `Δ_x(s) = ½Σ_ρ m_ρ Γ((ρ-s)/2)(πx)^((s-ρ)/2) + O(x)`. Each zero is felt only to its left, with growth `x^(-(β-σ)/2)`. **Whole route:** the passage is the Gaussian-smoothed explicit formula. It converges exactly on `Re s > Θ`, and the critical line is its natural boundary even under RH.
- **R1-F2 [paper, derived here]:** Anchored bubbling exclusion. Let `Ψ_k` be zero-free and holomorphic near a closed disk `B`, and let `Ψ_k → f` locally uniformly on an open `V ⊂ B` with `f ≢ 0`. If the continuous boundary lifts `arg Ψ_k` are bounded in `L¹(∂B)` modulo constants, then `f` is zero-free in `B`. Proof: the Schwarz formula bounds `G_k' = (log Ψ_k)'` locally, Vitali applies, and the limit is bounded below. By Bourgain–Brezis–Mironescu, bounded `H^(1/2)(∂B;S¹)` phase energy implies the `L¹` lift bound. **Whole route:** on every rectangle or disk reaching the safe chart `Re s>1`, bounded phase energy along any sequence `x_k→0` already excludes zeros. Chiral losses always balance (`κ₋=κ₊`), so step 4 (`κ₋ ≤ B_base`) is logically idle.
- **R1-F3 [paper]:** Step 2 (weak `H^(1/2)` convergence through the passage) is equivalent to RH. The forward direction is R1-F2. The converse holds because under RH, R1-F1 gives `C¹` convergence on compacts of `Re s>1/2`. Under ¬RH the phase energy tends to `∞` along every sequence: the failure is diffuse, never quantized. **Whole route:** the route's central premise, "degree changes only in quanta", never engages for holomorphic passages.
- **R1-F4 [paper]:** Fixed-x arm. `Tr T_(D,x) = wind(u_D)` for every `x`, and the only D-dependent identity with an Euler side is Brezis–Nirenberg `E₊-E₋=deg`. Its renormalization relative to `u_(D,x)` returns the chiral losses of R1-F3. **Whole route:** the stop-test fallback reduces to the same receiver.
- **R1-F5 [paper]:** Unanchored bubbles are Koebe factors. A bounded-energy weak limit `u` with interior limit `g` (zero-free) has `u = g/|g|` a.e. Each bubble is a double boundary zero of `g`, with `(1-p̄z)²` having boundary phase `-p̄z` of degree 1. Then `ξ/g` has positive boundary values (a `-Koebe` factor). **Whole route:** a bubble is a boundary double zero of the interior limit, never an interior event.
- **R1-VERDICT:** route 1 is CIRCULAR. Its content reduces to the classical Gaussian-smoothed prime-sum bound (`ψ(X)-X` in explicit-formula form). The sub-unit budget `B_base` cannot enter: holomorphic readers have no sub-quantum regime, only bounded (degree conserved) versus power-growth (`x^(-(β-σ)/2)`).
- **R1-F6 [program-level]:** Test A ("bound below one quantum, uniform in height") is misdirected for holomorphic readers. Their integer is protected by rigidity, so any finite bound per disk suffices, and the only failure mode is growth.
