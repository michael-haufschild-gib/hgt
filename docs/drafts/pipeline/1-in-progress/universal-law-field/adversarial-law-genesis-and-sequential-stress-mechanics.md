## 1. Public conjecture

> A mathematical law is stable only when it knows how to survive the counterexamples that its own
> previous repairs make possible.

The proposed field does not begin with a hidden geometry, law object, route atlas, or universal
obstruction.  It begins with one alternating mechanism:

```text
current legal doctrine
  -> source generates a legal challenge
  -> doctrine responds or repairs
  -> the response changes the next legal challenge space
  -> transported certificates constrain every later response.
```

Ordinary theorem validity is a terminal shadow of this process.  Passing every fixed test and
surviving every source-adaptive test are different mathematical claims.

## 2. Frozen technical conjecture

A constitutive law problem supplies, before any target theorem is inspected:

1. legal histories;
2. a source rule generating challenges from histories;
3. admitted responses and repair transitions;
4. transported certificates retained by later transitions;
5. observer doctrines that may forget challenge or response data; and
6. a source-derived resource filtration on executable strategies.

The conjecture is that the correct universal dynamics is governed by the following seven laws.

### ALG Law I -- quantifier order is constitutive

Pointwise solvability

\[
  \forall c\;\exists r\;R(c,r)
\]

does not imply a uniform response, and a uniform response does not imply a causal strategy against
history-dependent challenges.  Which player moves first is mathematical structure, not notation.

### ALG Law II -- consistent challenge descent

A legitimate counterchallenge must leave at least one source law consistent with the complete
transcript.  Each response therefore produces a nested version doctrine, not an arbitrary sequence
of objections.  Empty version space is finite refutation; nonempty strict descent is law learning.

### ALG Law III -- sequential stress rank

The maximum depth of a source-consistent counterchallenge tree is the intrinsic abstract stress of
the current response doctrine.  Finite stress is equivalent to existence of a finite-mistake
abstract strategy.  Infinite stress means that no bounded sequence of repairs closes the doctrine.

### ALG Law IV -- strategy admission is independent

Abstract finite stress does not guarantee an executable strategy.  Computability, topology,
completion, positivity, locality, and source provenance may exclude the abstract response.  The
field must therefore retain two axes:

\[
  \text{sequential stress}
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  \text{strategy admission/effectivity}.
\]

Neither is a scalar refinement of the other.

### ALG Law V -- observer contraction

An observer that forgets challenge labels, response orientation, or certificate lineage may
contract a challenge tree and create apparent closure.  It cannot manufacture a source strategy
that was absent before observation.  Recurrent observed rulebooks may therefore coexist with a
strictly open lifted strategy history.

### ALG Law VI -- convexification changes damage, not truth

Mixed or positive responses can reduce worst-case loss, but do not by themselves produce a global
source law or an admitted exact response.  Exact closure requires a saddle/effectivity theorem;
averaging is not one.

### ALG Law VII -- failure promotes the challenge language

When every admitted response is defeated, the minimal source-consistent counterstrategy is retained
as a new legal reporter or certificate.  Law genesis is growth of the challenge/response language,
not iteration inside one fixed hypothesis class.

## 3. Why the current frontier points here

After route names are erased, the five current obstruction regimes differ primarily by quantifier
and move order:

| frontier regime | sequential-mechanism reading |
|---|---|
| singular source descent | abstract response exists, but no admitted/global executable strategy |
| all-prime oscillatory transport | cancellation needs signed history; modulus-first feedback erases the challenge label |
| adaptive spectral orientation | fixed tests are weaker than a flag selected after earlier responses |
| whole-space coercivity | every bounded response family admits an escaping counterstrategy |
| positive carrier existence | every finite constraint family may be realizable while no one effective positive strategy realizes all constraints |

This table is evidence, not a route reduction.  No row counts until a source-normal challenge law is
constructed before the target and predicts a withheld response.

## 4. Ontological reversal

Existing approaches ask which object satisfies all constraints.  ALG asks which player can force
which future constraints after seeing which information.  A constraint set is only the depth-zero
shadow of a sequential game.  Compactness, descent, positivity, recurrence, and
hypertranscendence become possible response regimes of the same challenge mechanism rather than
independent coordinates.

The closest parent theories are online learning and Littlestone dimension, game semantics,
model-theoretic stability, counterexample-guided synthesis, dynamic logic, proof search, and
interactive realizability.  ALG is killed if it becomes “apply online learning terminology to
proofs.”  It survives only if source-generated doctrine changes and admission laws force new
cross-organ theorems.

Recent parent boundaries make the two-axis clause load-bearing.  Classical Littlestone dimension
characterizes abstract deterministic mistake bounds.  Effective Littlestone dimension does not in
general suffice for computable online learning, even at small finite dimension:

- Delle Rose--Kozachinskiy--Steifer, *Effective Littlestone Dimension*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15109>;
- Filmus--Hanneke--Mehalel--Moran, *Optimal Prediction Using Expert Advice and Randomized
  Littlestone Dimension*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13849>;
- Malliaris--Moran, *The unstable formula theorem revisited via algorithms*,
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apal.2025.103633>.

These works occupy abstract sequential rank, randomized mistake bounds, and the stability bridge.
The proposed field content is endogenous challenge-language revision plus source-relative
admission, not those parent theorems.

## 5. First subordinate bridge

The first test is deliberately elementary and is not the field conjecture.  For depth \(d\), take
binary branches as source laws and prefix cylinders as legal tests.  The test family is laminar, so
no two fixed tests are shattered.  Nevertheless a source that sees the current prediction can
choose the next branch bit so that:

1. the full transcript remains realizable by one branch;
2. every deterministic response is wrong for all \(d\) rounds; and
3. every fractional response pays at least \(1/2\) loss per round.

Thus static dimension one coexists with exact adaptive depth \(d\), and convexification halves but
does not remove the stress.  If dependency-closed, this becomes an explicit bridge theorem and a
Lean calibration of ALG Laws I, II, III, and VI.

## 6. Risky consequences

1. A doctrine can have uniformly tiny fixed-face complexity and unbounded sequential stress.
2. Finite static reporter completeness cannot settle an adaptive source theorem without an
   independent sequential-rank bound.
3. Positive mixtures can lower response loss while leaving the same infinite source-consistent
   challenge tree intact.
4. If an observer makes an infinite challenge tree finite, some challenge label or certificate
   lineage is necessarily in its kernel pair.
5. A genuinely Hypertranscendental law family should exhibit unbounded **prolongation challenge
   rank**, not merely infinitely many jets or a noncompact orbit.
6. Open law history can arise from finite visible vocabulary only if source certificates retain
   lineage or generate new challenge kinds; otherwise finite-game repetition eventually cycles.

## 7. Hard falsifiers

Retire ALG as universal field if any survives serious attack:

1. adaptive and nonadaptive source tests always have equivalent exact complexity after source
   constraints are imposed;
2. every source-normal challenge process reduces to a fixed hypothesis class with no doctrine
   revision;
3. sequential stress and strategy admission cannot be separated in a native model;
4. challenge trees predict no obstruction beyond existing compactness, descent, or online-learning
   theorems;
5. observer contraction, convexification, and certificate transport must be supplied as unrelated
   annotations;
6. no source-normal instance spans three structurally different mathematical organs; or
7. no withheld theorem is forced before a target route is inspected.
