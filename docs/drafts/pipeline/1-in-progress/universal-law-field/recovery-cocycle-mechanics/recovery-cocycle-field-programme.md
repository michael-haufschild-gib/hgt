## 1. Ontological reversal

The previous packet theorem showed that a functional-equation action can be lifted through a
common recovery kernel without losing relative entropy.  The abelian-relaxation theorem showed
that a finite recurrent observer can conceal a linearly open repair history with quadratic work.
Those facts look opposed only if a packet is treated as a static joint law.

Recovery--Cocycle Mechanics instead treats a packet as a **two-storey execution law**:

1. a common recovery kernel refreshes disposable local latent data;
2. a persistent additive ledger records what the observer quotient forgets;
3. observer dynamics transports both layers;
4. closed observer cycles leave a gauge-invariant ledger holonomy;
5. positive work is the harmonic norm of that holonomy.

The primitive mechanism is

\[
 \boxed{
 \textsf{recover local state}
 \longrightarrow \textsf{act on observer}
 \longrightarrow \textsf{refresh local state}
 \quad+\quad
 \textsf{accumulate persistent ledger}.}
 \tag{RCM.1}
\]

This separates two kinds of hidden state that earlier packet language conflated.  Common recovery
can erase and regenerate local history while a cocycle ledger remains globally open.

### Law V -- harmonic work

\[
 \boxed{

\]

### Law VI -- proper lifted recurrence

On a cycle `C`, `k` turns accumulate ledger `k H_C`.  Bounded lifted history is therefore
equivalent to zero holonomy, zero harmonic work, and cocycle triviality.

### Law VIII -- refinement-covariant work
