## Goal

Develop a mechanics of law birth in which one map

\[
T:K\longrightarrow A
\]

simultaneously governs three different events:

1. **cokernel:** whether a residual admits a law carrier at all;
2. **kernel:** whether a chosen carrier satisfies its next coherence;
3. **closure gap:** whether failed exact birth is nevertheless invisible to every Hausdorff
   observer.

This is not a search for a privileged geometric object. It is a proposed set of laws for how one
law changes type when it is lifted through a finer coefficient or admission doctrine.

## Mechanism

Let a binary compensator \(\kappa\) have associator \(a_\kappa=\delta\kappa\). The ordinary
identity \(\delta a_\kappa=0\) does not decide whether \(a_\kappa\) is a legal ternary law. A
carrier map \(T:K\to A\) creates a second decision:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
a_\kappa\in\operatorname{im}T
  & \text{exact higher carrier exists},\\
a_\kappa\in\overline{\operatorname{im}T}\setminus\operatorname{im}T
  & \text{only approximable higher carrier exists},\\
a_\kappa\notin\overline{\operatorname{im}T}
  & \text{separated higher residual survives}.
\end{array}
\]

If \(h\) is an exact carrier, \(Th=a_\kappa\), then

\[
T(\delta h)=\delta(Th)=\delta a_\kappa=0.
\]

Thus next coherence defect lies in \(\ker T\). Surjectivity controls existence; injectivity
controls coherence reflection; closed range controls exact-versus-approximate birth; dense range
controls Hausdorff visibility. These are different laws.

## Why this is a higher pivot

Prior universal-law-field cards had pieces separately:

- promotion differentiation births an associator;
- exact coefficient refinements turn a coarse cocycle into a kernel-valued higher law;
- admission-relative exactness distinguishes formal from legal primitives;
- unseparated cofibers distinguish algebraic from Hausdorff quotients.

Missing transition was:

> What happens when carrier of newly born higher law is itself non-exact?

Answer is not automatically "another arity." Cokernel failure changes carrier doctrine while
preserving pentagon. Kernel ambiguity can then create next arity. Arity promotion and carrier
promotion are therefore independent axes.
