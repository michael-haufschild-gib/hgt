## 1. Why this programme exists

ULF-FFM-2 identifies exact recovery fibres with gauge torsors and proves that adjoint reversal swaps

The unresolved field question is mechanistic:

> Which composition laws force the work, and which source law decides whether it is globally

## 2. Formation laws

The programme proposes the following ordered mechanism.

1. **Exact split recovery.** Semantic and lineage legs multiply to one.
2. **Reciprocal reflection.** Functional reversal swaps the two legs.
3. **Direct/reflected fusion.** Independent formations may be composed both directly and after
   reflection. Their work must obey the resulting four-point fusion identity.
4. **Complexity grading.** Every formation coordinate carries source-generated complexity.
5. **Global topology.** Total work is the complexity-weighted sum of forced local works.
6. **Selection.** Compact work sublevels exclude adaptive escape; bounded amplified history then
   forces neutral formation.

ULF-RFTM-1 closes Laws 1–6 for a countable diagonal recovery field and classifies the exact
complexity threshold. ULF-RFTM-2 closes the noncommutative local fusion gate: quadratic congruence
produces an exact Jordan-radial term, a double-commutator history term, and an exact associator.

## 6. Falsifiers

The programme fails as a field advance if:

1. every noncommutative extension is merely a known symmetric-space metric with no source-generated
   formation law;
2. complexity weights must be fitted from a target route;
3. compactness cannot be characterized without imposing the desired selection conclusion;
4. the full work factors through the certificate-blind recovered carrier or a trace that erases
   `kappa_J`;
5. no source comparison realizes even the diagonal complexity grading before specialization.

## 7. Research boundary

The scalar fusion equation is classical d'Alembert theory, weighted compact embeddings are classical
sequence-space analysis, and positive restricted operator groups are established. The programme's
claim is their formation-law coupling and the sharp separation between fixed-ray positivity and
global adaptive compactness. The noncommutative formation law is now constructed. It remains a
Frankenstein synthesis until its joint radial/history ideal is globalized and a second literal
source realization is constructed.
