## 2. Seven promotion modes forced by certified results

### P1 -- strict relation promotion

Residual: an operation fails to descend or two operations fail to exchange.

Promotion: generate the least preserved congruence. The unary repair theorem and minimal exchange
strictification theorem prove existence, leastness, quotient executability, and universal
factorization.

Prediction: every strict observer retaining the laws must erase at least the generated congruence.

### P2 -- intervention promotion

Residual: passive data or a positive quotient identifies distinct mechanisms.

Promotion: add a source-fixed intervention family and retain its response.

Certified boundaries: passive equivalence can coexist with opposite intervention response;
equivariant interventions cannot break an observer-blind symmetry; a two-response coframe can
recover phase erased by modulus-square observation.

Prediction: adding more passive reporters inside the blind symmetry cannot select the law.

### P3 -- extension/history promotion

Residual: endpoint kernel/cokernel or defect data agree while composition histories differ.

Promotion: retain a nonsplit extension, carry, determinant line, or lifted history.

Certified boundary: split and nonsplit events can have identical endpoints and size but distinct
carry and recurrence holonomy.

Prediction: endpoint strictification cannot reconstruct lineage without an extension mark.

### P4 -- higher-arity promotion

Residual: every proper observer face is abelian or identical while a total interaction survives.

Promotion: adjoin an irreducible `n`-ary law or higher coherence rather than another unary/binary
constraint.

Certified boundary: nonzero laws of arbitrary first arity exist with every proper color face
trivial; unbounded interaction spectrum is possible.

Prediction: any observer bounded in joint intervention arity has a blind higher-law sector.

### P5 -- completion/admission promotion

Residual: all finite observers are effective but no admitted global realization exists, or a class
dies under same-carrier surjective evolution.

Promotion: change pro/Ind/Tate completion, domain, topology, or carrier.

Certified boundaries: coherent locally finite towers already have global sections; same-carrier
surjective erasure of a nonzero linear class forces infinite dimension; finite jet spaces cannot
continuously separate DA from HT laws.

Prediction: a genuine finite/global wall must identify noncoherence, nonproperness, nonclosed
admission, infinite carrier, or a doctrine not preserved by the limit.

### P6 -- directed-history promotion

Residual: recurrent or reversible endpoint data erase production history and cost.

Promotion: retain a directed monoid/action history rather than groupoid equivalence.

Certified boundary: every nonnegative additive groupoid cost vanishes; observer-created units can
give exact recurrent observation with injective open lift and linearly growing action.

Prediction: no nonzero production cost descends to the recurrent reversible quotient.

### P7 -- positive/thermodynamic promotion

Residual: hidden degrees are eliminated or irreversible semigroup evolution must be represented
positively.

Promotion: form a positive kernel/GNS/KMS realization with an explicit defect statistic.

Certified boundaries: eliminating hidden pure histories forces a PSD kernel; faithful invariant
positivity on a compact monoid forces reversibility, whereas marked KMS balance can retain a
one-sided defect.

Prediction: positivity alone neither creates nor destroys phase universally; the exact positive
construction determines what is retained.

## 3. Pairwise noncollapse laws

The corpus already forbids reducing all seven modes to one closure.

1. **P1 does not imply P3.** Minimal quotient strictification erases order; identical strict
   endpoints can support split and nonsplit histories.
2. **P2 does not reduce to passive refinement.** Complete passive equality can survive while one
   elementary intervention separates mechanisms.
3. **P4 does not reduce to pairwise exchange.** Every proper face can be trivial while total arity
   is nonzero; strict pairwise commuting idempotent formations have no three-way schedule defect.
   ULF-RLM-10 adds the sharp unary boundary: every additive three-step reversal is generated by
   contextual binary residuals, so a genuinely irreducible ternary law must leave that mechanism.
4. **P5 does not reduce to more finite stages.** Locally finite coherent towers already have ends,
   while finite jets remain unable to classify global differential phase.
5. **P6 does not reduce to endpoint metric.** Reversible additive positive cost is forced to zero.
6. **P7 does not reduce to phase quotient.** Positive local states can coexist with global
   contextual obstruction, and KMS positivity can retain one-sided defect.

Therefore the new field cannot identify one favoured promotion mode with the whole mechanism. Its
central problem is the **exchange law among promotion modes**.

## 4. Proposed master mechanism

For each exposed residual `r`, a legal promotion `P_r` changes the rulebook. Applying a second
promotion `P_s` before or after `P_r` produces comparison histories

\[
  P_sP_r
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  P_rP_s.
  \tag{OPA.2}
\]

The field must classify:

1. when both composites exist;
2. when they are strictly equal;
3. when they are equivalent only after a higher repair;
4. when one composite is inadmissible;
5. how source reporter charge moves between their residual sectors;
6. whether iterated promotion terminates, converges, or remains open.

This is not a request for a universal category. Categories, operads, rewrite systems, filtered
complexes, and topological vector spaces are possible charts. The invariant content is the law of
promotion and its exchange identities.
