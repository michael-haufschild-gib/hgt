# Counterfactual law science

- **ID:** ULF-CFL-0
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** CANDIDATE
- **Source:** universal-law-field speculative tournament
- **Statement:** Proof routes may be controlled interventions on a latent generative law, so route comparison is causal identification rather than coordinate change.

## 1. Primitive

A **law machine** consists of:

1. source variables or objects;
2. directed structural assignments producing theorem observables;
3. a category of legal interventions replacing selected assignments;
4. observer functors on every intervened system;
5. coherence for composing interventions.

The field studies what latent law machines are identifiable from families of theorem endpoints and
route interventions.

## 2. Reinterpretation

- one theorem endpoint is passive observational data;
- one proof route is a controlled intervention protocol;
- agreement of routes is interventional invariance;
- a no-go is a non-identifiability theorem or forbidden intervention;
- source-normality means interventions are defined before target success is known;
- positivity is one family of passive statistics, never automatically causally complete;
- recurrence can hide an open latent state behind periodic observations;
- hypertranscendence may be infinite causal depth or failure of finite intervention bases.

This is incompatible with the slogan that all routes are gauges. Some may be gauges, some passive
observers, and some interventions. The field must classify which is which.

## 3. Native survivor

`observationally-identical-law-systems-and-intervention-separation-law.md` proves that two opposite
directed law systems have identical complete passive joint distributions but are exactly separated by
one intervention. Endpoint abundance alone does not reconstruct law production.

## 4. Bold conjectures

### CFL-A — route intervention basis

There exists a finite or pro-finite source-normal family of elementary route interventions whose
joint response reconstructs the dependency skeleton of all theorem organs generated by the source.

### CFL-B — no-go localization

Minimal interventions that turn a failed theorem route into a successful one identify the causal
parents of its obstruction. Different minimal repair sets should form a matroid or antimatroid when
intervention composition is well behaved.

### CFL-C — hypertranscendental causal depth

No finite intervention depth identifies a hypertranscendental source: for every finite experiment
family there are distinct source extensions with identical responses through that depth.

### CFL-D — physical experiment bridge

If corpus recurrence and information-loss theorems have physical content, their correct comparison is
operational: same boundary statistics under different interventions, not a metaphorical geometric
similarity.

## 5. Fatal gate

The candidate dies if the corpus cannot supply at least three routes as legal interventions on one
shared pre-target source with typed composition. It also dies if intervention responses add no
information beyond the complete source observer nerve.
