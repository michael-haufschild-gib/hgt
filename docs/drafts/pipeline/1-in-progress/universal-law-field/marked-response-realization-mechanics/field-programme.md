The invariant law-state is the complete response to every legal intervention word. A matrix,
operator, kernel, probability law, differential equation, or proof route is only a presentation of

## 2. Core laws

### Law I — marked presentation invariance

Any two minimal linear presentations of one complete response law are uniquely isomorphic by a map
preserving the source mark, every legal intervention, and the output. This is the exact linear
meaning of “routes are gauges.” Carrier isomorphism is a theorem only after equality of complete
responses has been proved.

### Law II — doctrine-relative memory

For a subalphabet `B subset A`, let `R_B` be the state space reachable using only `B` and let `N_B`
be the part of `R_B` invisible to every future `B`-word. The minimal memory seen by the restricted
doctrine is

\[
 m_B=\dim(R_B/N_B).
\]

If the full `A`-presentation is minimal, then

\[
 \boxed{
 m_A-m_B=\operatorname{codim}_V R_B+\dim N_B.}
\]

This is the field's first presentation-invariant memory conservation law. New legal operations
create memory in exactly two ways:

1. **reachability birth:** new histories enter state directions unavailable to the old doctrine;
2. **observability repair:** new future contexts separate directions previously identified.

No third finite-linear mechanism exists.

### Law III — no-go localization

An old doctrine fails to reconstruct the full law exactly when one of the two terms above is
nonzero. Thus a no-go is not a generic “curvature.” It is typed as missing production, missing
testing, or both.

### Law IV — higher-slot composition

Parallel composition of response laws is pointwise multiplication. Tensoring their marked
presentations realizes that product in every arity. Its minimal memory is at most the product of the
individual memories, with equality exactly when the tensor presentation remains reachable and
observable. Minimal-presentation equivalences tensor coherently, so higher-slot response is
presentation-independent.

### Law V — complete positivity is a realization, not an erasure

For a positive `*-response`, the cyclic GNS space is the minimal marked realization of the complete
Gram response. Its dimension is the Hankel/response rank. Positivity loses phase only after an
additional scalarization; the complete positive kernel retains the whole cyclic response up to
unique marked unitary equivalence.

### Law VI — unbounded memory is doctrine growth

For increasing finite doctrines `B_n`, the ranks `m_(B_n)` are monotone. Bounded rank stabilizes;
unbounded rank is an intrinsic failure of every finite linear doctrine to present the complete
response. Hypertranscendence is not identified with this rank: it requires the stronger nonlinear
prolongation doctrine already separated by CPM. The law only supplies its linear-response shadow.
