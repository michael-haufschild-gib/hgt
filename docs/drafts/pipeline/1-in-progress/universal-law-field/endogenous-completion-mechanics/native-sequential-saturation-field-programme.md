## 1. Question forced by ULF-ECMPL-1

ULF-ECMPL-1 proves one recursive law:

\[
  \text{failed overlap}\to\text{conservative repair}
  \to\text{changed executable overlaps}\to\text{next failure}.
\]

The next field gate is not another rewrite system.  It asks whether one native mathematical
environment can make a repair output become legal input to a later failure test, while a coarser
semantic completion remains stationary.

The topological candidate is sequential saturation.  For `A` in a topological space `X`, let

\[
  S(A)=\{x:\text{some sequence in }A\text{ converges to }x\}.
  \tag{ST.1}
\]

Unlike ordinary closure, `S` need not be idempotent.  The topology itself supplies all convergence
tests; no stage-indexed challenge list is fitted.

## 3. External frontier

- [LITERATURE] Sequential closure and its transfinite iteration are standard; sequential order is
  the least ordinal at which iterated sequential closure reaches ordinary closure in a sequential
  space.  Baldovino surveys the hierarchy and compact constructions under additional set theory:
  [arXiv:1001.0908](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0908).
- [LITERATURE] Arens space `S_2` is sequential but not Fréchet--Urysohn.  Its native two-storey
  convergence pattern is explicit in Gruenhage--Tsaban--Zdomskyy:
  [arXiv:1005.5542](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.5542).

  need not collapse in countably many steps in general compact spaces:
  Borodulin-Nadzieja--Selim, [arXiv:1203.4270](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.4270).

No novelty claim is made for sequential order.  The field question is whether its native repair
mechanism is the same causal law as conservative critical completion.

## 4. Competing conjectures and hostile verdicts

### Conjecture A -- any extensive repair produces endogenous depth

**Verdict:** [FALSIFIED].  An idempotent closure stabilizes after one step.  The corpus fixed-action
defect theorem makes this failure exact: exposing every defect of one already supplied action gives
its orbit closure immediately.

**Positive survivor:** endogenous depth requires repair outputs to enlarge the legal input class of
the same repair, or to change the repair law itself.

### Conjecture B -- ordinary topological closure is the required native repair

**Verdict:** [FALSIFIED].  Ordinary closure is idempotent in every topological space.

**Stronger conjecture:** the operational repair is not semantic closure.  It is closure under
currently executable countable histories, namely `S`; ordinary closure is the stationary semantic
envelope.

### Conjecture C -- a single topology can source-generate repair heredity

There exists one topological space and `A` such that

\[
 A\subsetneq S(A)\subsetneq S^2(A)=\overline A,
 \qquad \overline{S(A)}=\overline A,
 \tag{ST.2}
\]

and every point in `S^2(A)\setminus S(A)` is witnessed by a sequence drawn entirely from the first
repair layer `S(A)\setminus A`.

**Preliminary verdict:** [SURVIVES PRELIMINARY ATTACK], with Arens space `S_2` as an exact
dependency-closed laboratory.

### Conjecture D -- this proves hypertranscendence is topological noncompactness

**Verdict:** [FALSIFIED].  Sequential rank, compactness, differential transcendence, and dynamical
escape are independent invariants.  The theorem can only provide native repair heredity and a
formation-depth coordinate.

## 5. Exact Arens laboratory

Use points

\[
 X=\{\infty\}\cup\{b_n:n\in\mathbb N\}
   \cup\{a_{n,m}:n,m\in\mathbb N\}.
\]

Declare `U` open exactly when:

1. `b_n in U` implies `a_(n,m) in U` for all sufficiently large `m`;
2. `infinity in U` implies `b_n in U` for all sufficiently large `n`.

Leaves `a_(n,m)` are isolated.  Put `A={a_(n,m)}` and `B={b_n}`.  The target theorem is

\[
 S(A)=A\cup B,
 \qquad S(A\cup B)=X,
 \qquad \overline A=X,
 \tag{ST.3}
\]

with `b_n` witnessed by the `n`th leaf row and `infinity` witnessed by the sequence `(b_n)`.
No sequence of leaves converges to `infinity`.
