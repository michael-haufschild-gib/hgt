## 1. Frontier forcing

The current RH frontier is receiver-rich and producer-poor.  Its five wall classes already possess
many exact tests, carriers, positivity forms, continuation devices, and RH endpoints.  They stall
where a failed test would have to manufacture a new *legal* test without importing the desired
answer.

Existing Universal Law Field results close three easier substitutes.

1. A diagonal reporter is representationally new but may be operationally inert.
2. A fixed challenge promoter gives classical observability refinement; one plateau is permanent.
3. Arbitrary state-dependent challenge production realizes every transition graph and predicts
   nothing.

The missing causal arrow is

\[
 \text{failure}_n
 \longmapsto \text{least conservative repair}_n
 \longmapsto \text{changed executable overlaps}
 \longmapsto \text{failure}_{n+1}.
 \tag{ECMPL.1}
\]

No proof route, zero, divisor, positivity form, or endpoint enters this programme.

## 2. Competing conjectures frozen before proof

### Conjecture A -- self-representation drives open execution

Iterated anti-diagonal representation should generate an open operational doctrine.

**Hostile verdict:** false in general.  ULF-REE-1 proves representational novelty and operational
effectivity are independent.  The canonical strict-order diagonal tower has injective current
semantics, so its next anti-diagonal is unrepresented but cannot split an old invisible
intervention.

**Survivor:** source-normal challenge production and operational law revision must be coupled by an
execution theorem.

### Conjecture B -- a fixed promoter supplies the coupling

Given a source operator `D` and reporter `q`, adjoin `q D^n` successively.

**Hostile verdict:** insufficient.  This is Krylov/observability mechanics.  Its challenge spaces
and data-processing law are exact, but `D` is already the complete future generator.  In finite
dimension one plateau is permanent.  The rule producing later challenges does not itself change.

**Survivor:** the repair must alter the future challenge generator, not merely reveal another
iterate of a fixed generator.

### Conjecture C -- critical completion is the minimal feedback law

An unresolved overlap determines a joining equation.  Orienting that equation changes the rewrite
system and therefore changes its future overlap language.  If the new rule overlaps an old
persistent rule, the first repair source-generates the next challenge.

**Status:** survives the first attack.  A two-letter length-decreasing system gives an exact open
completion chain while its generated equational theory remains fixed.

### Conjecture D -- an exact-couple or spectral-page package is already the mechanism

**Hostile verdict:** false as a foundation.  Exact couples and spectral sequences describe how
classes survive a supplied filtration.  They do not source-generate the filtration or its next
differential.  They remain the correct receiver for completion history after the causal repair law

## 3. Minimal word laboratory

Let the alphabet be `{a,b}`.  For every integer `n>=1`, define

\[
 \ell_n=a b^n a,
 \qquad r_n=b^n a,
 \qquad \rho_n:\ell_n\longrightarrow r_n.
 \tag{ECMPL.2}
\]

At finite stage `N>=1`, the legal rules are

\[
 \mathcal R_N=\{\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_N\}.
 \tag{ECMPL.3}
\]

The persistent seed `rho_1` and newest repair `rho_N` overlap on

\[
 w_N=a b a b^N a.
 \tag{ECMPL.4}
\]

They generate two reductions:

\[
 w_N\to b a b^N a\to b^{N+1}a=r_{N+1},
 \tag{ECMPL.5}
\]

\[
 w_N\to a b^{N+1}a=\ell_{N+1}.
 \tag{ECMPL.6}
\]

Both endpoints are normal for `R_N` and distinct.  The unique repair inside the declared schema is
therefore `rho_(N+1)`.  Adding it joins the marked peak, but its overlap with `rho_1` is exactly
`w_(N+1)`, which generates the next peak.

Every rule deletes one `a`, so object-level rewriting terminates.  Yet no finite completion stage
is confluent.  The completion history is open although execution is terminating.

The repair is conservative: equations `ell_(N+1)=r_(N+1)` already follow from the two old paths out
of `w_N`.  Thus adjoining `rho_(N+1)` changes executable normal-form behaviour without changing the
equational congruence generated by the seed.  A quotient observer sees a stationary theory while
the oriented completion history remains strictly open.

## 4. Candidate mechanism laws

### Law I -- challenge heredity

A legal repair is endogenous only when the next challenge is computed from overlaps involving the
new repair.  A prewritten challenge list is not law genesis.

### Law II -- conservative operational change

A repair may leave denotational or quotient semantics unchanged while changing executability,
normal forms, admission, or future challenge production.  Representation and execution therefore
remain independent under revision, not only at one stage.

### Law III -- two termination notions

Termination of every execution does not imply termination of rulebook completion.  A decreasing
object measure and an increasing formation resource can coexist.

### Law IV -- formation resource accounting

In the word laboratory, alphabet size and denotational theory stay fixed, but required overlap
length and rule index grow without bound.  This is the named resource escaping finite monotone
genesis termination.

### Law V -- observer-relative recurrence

An observer forgetting rule orientation sees the same generated congruence at every stage.  The
lifted legal history is nevertheless strict.  Recurrent or stationary observation and open
completion are different laws.

### Law VI -- higher history receiver

Critical peaks, joining rules, and subsequent overlaps form a law-history complex.  Exact-couple,
polygraphic, or homotopical machinery may receive this history only after the source-generated
completion law fixes its cells.

## 6. Cross-organ acceptance gate

The word theorem is one calibration, not the field.  A general completion law must predict the
same separation in at least three nonisomorphic laboratories:

1. term or polygraphic rewriting: terminating execution, nonterminating completion;

The source data must be fixed before the first failure.  A later mapping that chooses its next test
