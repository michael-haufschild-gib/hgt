## 1. Why REG was not high enough

Realization End Geometry correctly identifies finite extensions, ends, and nonproper boundaries,
but it is kinematic. It does not force positivity, phase, cost, or time from its extension tree.
Adding those as independent labels would reproduce the independent-slot no-prediction failure.

The Lean-certified history-polarization theorem forces a stronger primitive. A reversible groupoid
cannot support nonzero nonnegative additive cost, while a directed noninvertible history monoid can.
Therefore reversible memory and irreversible production cannot be two coordinates on one groupoid;
they must be two compositional regimes of a larger directed realization object.

## 2. Primitive

A **polarized realization process** is source-generated data

\[
  (\mathscr H,\,\{Q_a:\mathscr H\to\mathscr O_a\}_{a\in A}),
\tag{PRD.1}
\]

where:

1. \(\mathscr H\) is a category, higher category, or process equipment of partial laws and admitted
   realization histories;
2. its maximal subgroupoid \(\mathscr H^\times\) is the reversible core;
3. noninvertible arrows are genuine production, erasure, extension, or proof histories;
4. \(Q_a\) are source-defined observer/localization functors, allowed to send nonunits to units;
5. prolongation and completion act on this same process rather than creating separate route spaces.

The polarization is intrinsic: units versus nonunits are determined by composition. Phase
characters and holonomy live on the core. Positive additive action can live only on directed
history. Observer recurrence occurs when a nonunit becomes a unit after applying \(Q_a\).

**PRD-A — prolongation non-Noetherianity.** Genuine hypertranscendence is equivalent, under a

or action properness.

**PRD-D — quantum recurrence.** In a quantum realization, recurrence is a unitary core return while
complexity is directed nonunit charge. A true bridge to Brown--Susskind must identify one
source-derived history class carrying both projections.

**PRD-E — arithmetic source test.** One pre-zero arithmetic process must produce at least three
nonisomorphic observer localizations and one shared unitization class before divisor specialization.
