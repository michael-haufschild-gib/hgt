## 1. Thesis

Proof systems may be boundary conditions of a deeper law algebra rather than observers of one
classical object. Their common content is not a hidden coordinate but the action of bulk operators
and defects.

In this picture, a theorem endpoint is boundary data. A universal source relation is a bulk class
whose action is seen differently on inequivalent boundaries.

## 2. Primitive

A serious version requires:

1. a monoidal or higher bulk category \(\mathcal B\);
2. a category of boundary conditions \(\mathcal M\) acted on by \(\mathcal B\);
3. defect fusion and duality;
4. positive/reflection structure;
5. a source-native bulk object and boundary functors.

Proof routes are boundary modules. Route comparison is fusion with a bulk defect or passage through
a boundary-changing bimodule.

## 3. Reinterpretation

- many proof routes: inequivalent boundary conditions;
- common theorem: common bulk operator has constrained boundary action;
- no-go: boundary anomaly or failure to extend a boundary action to the bulk;
- positivity: reflection-positive boundary trace;
- phase: central bulk character erased by scalar boundary norm;
- recurrence: finite or compact defect-fusion orbit;
- open history: lift to the fusion word or universal grading group;
- hypertranscendence: boundary tower requiring unbounded bulk sectors;
- source obstruction: no canonical bulk algebra/action extracted from the source.

## 4. Native survivor

endomorphism dimension, and positive character norm. The bulk generator separates them by phase.
Fusion with one character cycles the boundaries with period three while the marked fusion count
remains open.
