# Polyscopic Law Geometry

- **Stage:** 1 — candidate higher synthesis
- **Working name:** **Polyscopic Law Geometry (PLG)**
- **Status:** [CANDIDATE UNIVERSAL FIELD] [TWO INDEPENDENT NATIVE REALIZATIONS]
  [MATCHING MAP PRIMITIVE] [DYNAMICS/TOPOLOGY/PHYSICS OPEN]
  [NOT RH] [NO LEAN]

## 1. Thesis

Mathematical laws are not reconstructed from one observer. They are reconstructed, obstructed, or
underdetermined relative to a family of overlapping observers.

PLG studies the canonical map

\[
 \text{total laws}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{compatible families of proper observer shadows}.
\]

The geometry lies in its realization fibres.

## 2. Why this is higher than the current forks

The same matching map has two opposite failures:

1. **contextual failure:** some compatible shadows have no total realization;
2. **interaction failure:** some shadows have several or higher-homotopic total realizations.

Nomological contextuality and operadic law genesis are therefore boundary phases of one theory.
NHG supplies differential geometry inside an inhabited realization fibre but cannot describe an
empty fibre. PLG can.

## 3. Primitive

A PLG object consists provisionally of:

1. a source-defined observer indexing category \(\mathcal I\);
2. a law-valued presheaf or higher diagram \(X\) on observer faces;
3. total-to-matching maps for finite observer covers;
4. a refinement/cofinality doctrine;
5. optional enrichments—cyclic, positive, topological, differential, resource, or dynamical—that
   must be functorial on the same matching diagram.

The un-enriched matching map is kinematic. A new field begins only when the enrichments interact by
theorems rather than coexist as labels.

## 4. First field invariant

For each finite observer cover \(I\), define the realization profile

\[
 \mathfrak R_I(X):
 m\longmapsto
 \operatorname{fib}_m\left(
 X_I\to\lim_{S\subsetneq I}X_S
 \right).
\]

This profile distinguishes:

- empty fibres;
- contractible fibres;
- discrete multiplicity;
- higher homotopy;
- noncompact/pro fibres after enrichment.

No scalar rank or determinant can recover the full profile.

## 5. Candidate meanings of the requested phenomena

- **Proof routes as local gauges:** equivalences between face presentations inside one observer
  cover.
- **No-gos:** empty or non-effective realization fibres.
- **Hidden source relation:** coordinate on a noncontractible total-law fibre, or an operation
  changing an empty fibre to an inhabited one.
- **Hypertranscendence:** unbounded/nonstabilizing realization profiles over the jet tower.
- **Positivity:** a positive-state enrichment that may exist on matching data without a
  deterministic total point.
- **Recurrence:** a loop in matching space with monodromy on total-law fibres.
- **Open history:** noncompact or infinite-action lift of that loop.
- **Physical interior:** information living in the total-law fibre but absent from every proper
  observer face.

These are conjectural interpretations, not yet universal theorems.

## 6. Research programme

1. Define source-native observer indexing and refinement.
2. Prove a matching-tower invariance theorem under cofinal observer changes.
3. Build a cyclic-positive matching model and compute its deterministic and state realization
   fibres separately.
4. Build a dynamical matching model with recurrent base loop and nontrivial fibre monodromy.
5. Relate unbounded Kolchin growth to nonstabilization of matching profiles.
6. Determine whether operadic Koszul duality supplies a canonical total-law object.
7. Only after these gates, test external proof systems.

## 7. Falsifier

PLG fails if observer covers cannot be selected independently of target results, or if refinement
changes realization profiles without a canonical comparison. It also fails as a universal field
if positivity, dynamics, and prolongation cannot be constructed functorially from the same matching
diagram.
