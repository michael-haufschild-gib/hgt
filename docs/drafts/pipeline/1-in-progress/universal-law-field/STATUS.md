`mark -> form -> ghost -> dualize -> admit -> saturate -> conserve`.

Eight verified laws currently survive all route languages.

1. **Finite freedom.** Finite positive zeta-valuation windows are differentially free; finite
   tail relations are only explicit pivot relations. No finite hidden arithmetic syzygy can select
   critical line.
2. **Completion birth.** Finite-depth formation commutes with completion. Off-line horizontal
   charge appears exactly in formation--completion boundary
   `closure(Sat(S)) \ Sat(closure(S))`, equivalently in pre-divisor Roos `lim^1` and strict
   Gamma/Sonin transport (`ArithmeticFormationCompletionBoundaryFusion`).
3. **Gauge invariance.** Mutually bounded generator dictionaries preserve that boundary; injective
   equivariant observers preserve it. This is proved coordinate-free invariant. Heterogeneous route
   charts differ only where transition maps become singular or erase a pointed class.
4. **One charge.** Every multiplicity-labelled Xi copy carries
   `a = 2 Re(q) - 1`; functional-equation reflection sends `a` to `-a`. Gamma/co-Poisson,
   prime-tail, Green, Gaussian, Bockstein, curvature, entropy, recurrence, and formation readers
   recover `a`, `a^2`, or `1_(a != 0)`.
5. **Neutral transport.** Common recovery and functional-equation lift are information- and
   formation-capital neutral. They may transport charge, form a two-cycle, or cancel its signed
   scalar, but cannot select `a = 0`.
6. **Quotient blindness.** Positive, reduced, traced, stable, compact, and observer-closed
   quotients can forget sign, tangent, provenance, or open history while lifted charge survives.
   Closed recurrence downstairs is compatible with open lifted history; faithful compact descent
   fails exactly when completion class is born (`ArithmeticFormationRecurrenceDescentFusion`).
7. **Conserved escape.** Hypertranscendence produces unbounded formation depth/noncompact escape,
   not annihilation. Singular completion conserves visible cancellation as exceptional capacity:
   incoming source energy is `4`, outgoing minimal budget is `4 + |d|^2`, and an opposite
   controller exports `2|d|^2` to a hidden port rather than destroying it.
8. **Selection.** Universal absence of completion-born class is exactly RH. Equivalent receivers
   include whole-source positivity, fixed-source flatness, finite action, shell-recovery closability,
   compact recurrence descent, reversible same-fibre KMS descent, global properness, and
   source-capacity `4`. None is yet produced independently.

## What this means

- Corpus is not missing another detector, packet, completion, positivity condition, spectral
  model, or route fusion. Those exist.
- Off-line zero is not locally inconsistent. It is a legal irreversible completion sector carrying
  a conserved signed class and a positive hidden resource.
- All major no-gos are anomaly-conservation theorems: information-neutral operations cannot erase
  class; nonfaithful operations erase source data; compensators move it to another port.
- RH therefore asks for a **no-ex-nihilo law for arithmetic completion**: actual prime--Gamma
  formation must be unable to create extra orthogonal capacity represented by `a`.
- Calling this law compactness, unitarity, positivity, passivity, cyclicity, properness, or
  self-adjointness does no work. Each word is already an RH-equivalent receiver unless derived from
  an executable source operation before divisor inspection.

  all-order formation, and all-order prime--arch recurrence already exist; endpoint is again
  RH-equivalent positivity.

  missing receiver.

  Finite energy is precisely missing.

  while remaining unrelated to Xi zero placement. Zeros are not phase coexistence.
- Idempotent formation/additive charge: idempotence holds at every grade; fixed-source
  self-adjointness is missing selector.
- Properly-infinite projection versus finite trace: prime branching is Toeplitz/KMS, not tracial;
  modular weights absorb replication. Tracial two-sided descent is existing reversible-KMS
  receiver.
- Catalytic passivity: visible cancellation pays controller debt and exports conserved hidden
  energy. Contradiction requires independently constructed closed source cycle returning source and
  controller unchanged; none is known.
- Generic autopoiesis, games, contextuality, operads, RG, anomaly inflow, cobordism, stable
  invariants, KMS/Cuntz systems, and presentation-free gauge language supply kinematics or classify
  escape. They do not produce arithmetic extinction.

   by `2|d|^2`. Exact conservation would force `d = 0`. Without closed-cycle theorem this is merely
   passivity wording and is rejected.
