## 1. Pivot

Every current field candidate fails at the same place.

- A product of relation, positivity, topology, history, and cost slots has only constant intrinsic
  invariants under independent relabelling.
- CEM correctly types interventions and doctrine changes but does not produce them.
- PCDG/Chu polarity produces closures from a pairing but does not produce the pairing or the law.
- Interfinity Geometry produces exactness topology from stable solvability but cannot manufacture
  the missing source relation.
- Nomological Homotopy Geometry produces higher laws from a coderivation but still needs a chosen
  realization to turn the law into observation, positivity, or completion.

The higher move is to stop treating source syntax and observable behaviour as independently chosen.
One source-marked endofunctor should generate both.

## 2. Primitive

Let \(P:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C\) be an accessible, source-marked endofunctor on a category in
which the required fixed points exist. Its three constitutive strata are

\[
 \mu P \longrightarrow \rho P \longrightarrow \nu P.
 \tag{NFD.1}
\]

- \(\mu P\): initial \(P\)-algebra, finite or well-founded law construction;
- \(\rho P\): rational or locally finite fixed point, behaviour generated by finitely presentable
  law systems;
- \(\nu P\): final \(P\)-coalgebra, complete possibly infinite behaviour/history.

Under standard hypotheses, Lambek inversion turns \(\mu P\) into a coalgebra and finality gives its
canonical behaviour map to \(\nu P\). For proper functors the rational fixed point embeds into the
final coalgebra. Failure of that embedding is therefore a typed admission or separation failure,
not a scalar curvature.

The primitive is not the tuple \((\mu P,\rho P,\nu P)\). All three and their comparison maps must be
generated from the same marked \(P\).

## 3. Reinterpretation of the corpus

| Corpus organ | Nomogenic fixed-point reading |
|---|---|
| finite proof, relation, or presentation | element/algebra in \(\mu P\) |
| cofinal finite aperture | stage in the construction of \(\mu P\), \(\rho P\), or the final sequence |
| finite recurrence or automaton | orbit inside \(\rho P\) |
| open lifted history | preimage/prefix history approaching a point of \(\nu P\) |
| completion boundary | \(\nu P\) beyond the image of the well-founded/rational strata |
| non-Hausdorff or nonclosed-range no-go | failure of the comparison to be monic, strict, or proper in the chosen base |
| route | algebra, coalgebra, realization, or producer morphism derived from the same \(P\) |
| positivity | a source-declared morphism of producers to an ordered/probabilistic producer |
| phase loss | nontrivial kernel pair of that producer morphism on fixed-point semantics |
| hypertranscendence | conjecturally, escape from every finitely presentable differential-behaviour coalgebra |

This changes the central question. The missing source-normal relation need not be another class
inside a fixed source complex. It may be a missing **law-producer morphism** whose existence is
required before two fixed-point semantics can be compared at all.

## 4. Why bialgebraic operational semantics is not yet enough

Turi--Plotkin style bialgebraic semantics couples a syntax functor and a behaviour functor by a
distributive law. This supplies the correct compositionality test: behavioural equivalence is a
congruence only when the interaction law exists.

But two functors remain independent inputs. NFD first tests the stronger one-producer regime in
which least and greatest semantics arise from the same \(P\). When distinct syntax and behaviour
functors are unavoidable, a distributive law is the required replacement for the missing
one-producer comparison; absence of that law is a first-class field obstruction.

## 5. Smallest exact calibration target

Take the polynomial producer

\[
 P_A(X)=1+A\times X.
 \tag{NFD.2}
\]

Its least fixed point is finite \(A\)-words. Its greatest fixed point is finite-or-infinite
\(A\)-words. Its finite-state behaviours are finite or eventually periodic words.

For \(A=\mathbb Z/4\), the periodic history

\[
 0,1,2,3,0,1,2,3,\ldots
 \tag{NFD.3}
\]

lies in the rational stratum. Reduction \(q:\mathbb Z/4\to\mathbb Z/2\) produces the recurrent
observer history \(0,1,0,1,\ldots\), while finite prefixes in the least stratum have unbounded
length. The nonsplit extension

\[
0\to\mathbb Z/2\to\mathbb Z/4\to\mathbb Z/2\to0
\tag{NFD.4}
\]

retains the carry/holonomy erased by the observer. The algebraic split-versus-nonsplit carry theorem
is already Lean-certified in
`GaloisForLFunctions.HTGeometry.ConstitutiveExtensionCarry`; the fixed-point interpretation and
finite-state classification are not yet formalized and therefore do not count as proved.

This is the first proposed calibration because one producer plus one declared quotient forces:

1. finite syntax;
2. finite-state recurrence;
3. open prefix history;
4. quotient phase/carry loss.

It does not yet force positivity, differential hypertranscendence, or physical cost.

## 6. Field laws proposed

### NFD-I — three-stratum law

A valid law producer must distinguish well-founded construction, finitely generated behaviour, and
completed behaviour. Identifying these strata requires a theorem, not notation.

### NFD-II — compositional-semantics law

Two routes are gauges of one law only when their semantics are induced by the same producer or by a
typed distributive law between their producers. Endpoint agreement is insufficient.

### NFD-III — irreversible observer law

A positivity, completion, or scalarization map is constitutive only if it is induced by a producer
morphism and therefore transports fixed-point semantics. Its kernel pair is the exact lost phase or
history. An arbitrary post hoc quotient is not a field operation.

### NFD-IV — rational recurrence/open completion law

Recurrence belongs naturally to rational/final behaviour. Open history belongs to the well-founded
approximants or a marked lift. Their coexistence is controlled by the fixed-point comparison, not by
endpoint distance.

### NFD-V — differential nonrationality law `[CONJECTURE]`

For a source-derived differential-behaviour producer, hypertranscendence should imply that the
behaviour lies in no finitely presentable differential subcoalgebra. This is intentionally stronger
and more typed than `HT = noncompactness`. It requires a literal differential coalgebra theorem and
is currently open.

## 7. Route-independent predictions

1. **Comparison invariant.** Routes claimed to realize one producer must agree on the well-founded
   part, rational behaviour, and kernel of the rational-to-final comparison. Disagreement kills the
   common-producer claim before endpoint analysis.
2. **Positivity compatibility.** A positive shadow can erase phase only through a producer morphism
   compatible with construction and behaviour. If no such morphism exists, positivity is an
   external observer, not a law gauge.
3. **No semantic repair after loss.** Once a producer morphism identifies two source behaviours,
   later semantic quotients cannot reconstruct their complete source nerve without an added mark.
4. **Recurrence/complexity split.** Periodic or almost-periodic final behaviour can coexist with
   unbounded construction depth. Complexity should live on the approximation/lift, not on the
   recurrent endpoint.
5. **HT gate.** Finite-dimensional covariance rank, noncompact topology, or infinite support alone
   cannot certify HT. A valid bridge must rule out every finitely presentable coalgebra in the
   source-derived differential behaviour category.
6. **Source-normal obstruction.** Repeated failure to find a source-native boundary inside one route
   predicts that the missing datum may be a producer comparison/distributive law, not another
   boundary representative.

## 8. Hostile conjecture loops

### Loop A — one functor automatically explains every organ

**False.** A plain set endofunctor supplies no positivity, cost, involution, topology, or physical
clock. Choosing a huge \(P\) that stores them all reproduces the independent-slot theatre.

**Survivor.** Only structures functorially induced by a source-marked producer or a typed producer
morphism count. Every claimed organ needs a construction theorem.

### Loop B — every rational fixed point embeds in the final coalgebra

**False in general algebraic bases.** Properness or related separation hypotheses are load-bearing.

**Survivor.** Noninjectivity of \(\rho P\to\nu P\) is a native finite-state semantic-collapse
invariant and a precise analogue of admission/completion failure.

### Loop C — hypertranscendence is behaviour outside \(\rho P\)

**False.** Many non-finite-state behaviours are differential-algebraic; irrational or nonperiodic
streams do not imply HT.

**Survivor.** Use a differential-behaviour category whose finitely presentable coalgebras encode
finite differential law, and prove the implication there.

### Loop D — recurrent observation forces open history

**False.** The identity lift of a recurrent compact system is compact.

**Survivor.** Open history requires a nonproper fixed-point comparison, prefix tower, or marked
cover. It is constitutive data, not a consequence of recurrence alone.

### Loop E — positivity is always phase quotient

**False.** A faithful positive identity channel loses nothing.

**Survivor.** Phase loss is the kernel pair of a specific ordered/probabilistic producer morphism.

### Loop F — fixed-point theory itself is the new field

**False.** Initial algebras, terminal coalgebras, rational fixed points, completions, and
bialgebraic semantics are established mathematics.

**Survivor.** Candidate field content is the corpus-wide interpretation plus new source-normal
producer constructions and cross-organ comparison theorems. Without those, NFD is another
container and must be rejected.

## 9. Parent mathematics and priority boundary

The categorical machinery is occupied:

- Adamek--Milius--Moss--Sousa identify initial algebras as final well-founded coalgebras and
  describe final and iterative fixed points: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0576>.
- Urbat--Wissmann give a 2025 coalgebraic Konig lemma and construct initial algebras from
  finitely presentable well-founded/recursive coalgebras: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18539>.
- Balan--Kurz extend the completion of initial algebras to final coalgebras over algebraic bases:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.0938>.
- Proper functors and rational fixed-point embedding are established:
  <https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CALCO.2017.18>.
- Turi--Plotkin bialgebraic operational semantics is the parent compositionality theory; current
  work extends it to trace and higher-order semantics:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18285> and <https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18295>.

No priority claim is made for those ingredients. NFD is deliberately a Frankenstein interpretation.
Its acceptance depends only on producing new comparison theorems from literal HGT source objects.

## 10. Anti-theatre gate

NFD is accepted as the universal field only after all of the following.

1. Construct one literal pre-target HGT producer \(P_{\mathrm{HGT}}\) before Xi-zero or route
   specialization.
2. Identify its least, rational/locally finite, and final semantics without inserting endpoint data.
3. Realize at least three nonisomorphic corpus organs through producer morphisms or distributive
   laws.
4. Prove one fixed-point comparison identity shared by those organs.
5. Derive one withheld theorem or impossibility from the comparison.
6. Formalize every significant dependency-closed theorem in Lean under the ULF umbrella.

Current status is below Gate 1. The \(P_A\) word model is a calibration, not an HGT producer.

## 11. Next attack

Three incompatible source candidates should be tested.

1. **Euler-shell polynomial producer:** constructors are prime-power/shell operations; behaviour is
   coefficient/Dirichlet continuation. Test whether the exact ramification law defines both
   initial and final semantics without route fitting.
2. **Differential-prolongation producer:** constructors adjoin derivatives/relations; behaviour is
   the complete differential-response coalgebra. Test NFD-V on a proved HT shell family.
3. **Source-normal boundary producer:** constructors are literal rational-adelic/Kummer operations;

   absence of a distributive law with the analytic completion producer.

Any candidate that merely renames an existing route is rejected. The first target is the common
fixed-point comparison, not an RH endpoint.
