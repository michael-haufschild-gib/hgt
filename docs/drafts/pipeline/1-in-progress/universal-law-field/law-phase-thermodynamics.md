## 1. Higher pivot

This candidate does not treat curvature, manifolds, or one RH route as primitive. Its primitive is a
source-indexed family of **ways mathematics counts as exact**.

Let (\mathfrak D) be a space or higher category of constitutive doctrines: coefficient systems,
weak equivalences, completions, observer classes, and cost conventions. Over it place a fibration

\[
\pi:\mathfrak L\longrightarrow\mathfrak D
\tag{LPT.1}
\]

whose fibre (\mathfrak L_d) is an exact/stable higher category of law cells, proof fillings, and
their transformations under doctrine (d). Equip admissible exact histories with one multiplicative
metric-line amplitude

\[
\mathcal A_d:\operatorname{Hist}(\mathfrak L_d)
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Pic}^{\mathrm{met}}_{\mathbb C}.
\tag{LPT.2}
\]

The complex line is not cosmetic. Its zero/nonzero status, phase, norm, logarithmic lift, and
uniformity under cofinal passage are different mathematical shadows.

This is a Frankenstein of stability conditions, Waldhausen (K)-theory, determinant functors,
analytic torsion, Lawvere cost, wall crossing, and higher descent. The proposed new interpretation is
that these are not auxiliary invariants of a fixed theory. Together they describe **phase changes of
what the theory accepts as a law and a proof**.

## 4. Proposed field laws

### Law I — five-way exactness separation

Any adequate law object must retain separately:

1. effectivity/nonemptiness;
2. stable factorization structure;
3. metric filling cost;
4. determinant phase/history;
5. cofinal strictness.

The metric-phase theorem proves no functor through the derived endpoint or positive Gram shadow can
reconstruct all five.

### Law II — constitutive wall crossing

Changing doctrine may change the exact or stable law category even when the underlying generator and
its algebraic charge are fixed. Such change is genuine field evolution only when comparison functors
are source-derived and satisfy gluing coherence.

### Law III — modulus is not selection

The positive quotient can erase phase, reverse chamber information, or change coefficient topology.
Therefore positivity alone cannot be assumed to select a physical/source chamber. A Real structure,
orientation, or stability order is independent constitutive data unless derived from source.

### Law IV — recurrent shadows can carry monodromy

A loop in coarse doctrine space may return every endpoint and positive observable while acting
nontrivially on determinant lines, proof fillings, or the law category. The correct invariant is then
monodromy/autoequivalence, not endpoint displacement.

## 5. Bold conjectures

### LPT-A — route scattering

Whenever several independent proof routes reach one endpoint from one source, their stable
factorizations should be related by a wall-crossing/scattering automorphism on a common completed Hall
or (K)-theoretic law algebra. The endpoint is its total charge; route-specific no-gos are failures of
factorization in particular chambers.

**Falsifier:** three route realizations with a common source and endpoint but no source-natural common
charge object or comparison composition law.

### LPT-B — hypertranscendental wall proliferation

For a genuinely hypertranscendental source, no finite collection of stability walls and stable law
charges should control all jet orders. New stable factors or walls must appear at unbounded
prolongation depth.

This replaces the false slogan "hypertranscendence is noncompactness." Compact observed dynamics can
coexist with unbounded law-factor spectrum.

**Falsifier:** a source-faithful finite stability datum reconstructing every differential-algebraic
prolongation.

### LPT-C — closed observer, categorical pump

A recurrent observer loop can pump a nontrivial determinant winding or autoequivalence even when all
scalar endpoint observables recur. Quantized pumping should be the stable memory of proof history.

**Falsifier:** every source-native observer loop has trivial action on the law-memory spectrum.

### LPT-D — law phase transition at cofinal strictness loss

When finite exact laws approach a nonclosed-range cofinal limit, inverse filling cost and determinant
free energy must diverge along some source cofinal subsystem. The algebraic endpoint may remain
unchanged until the limit.

**Falsifier:** a uniformly finite-cost source realization whose cofinal map nevertheless has dense
nonclosed range.
