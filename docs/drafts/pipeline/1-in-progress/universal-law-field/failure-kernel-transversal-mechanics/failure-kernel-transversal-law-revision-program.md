## 1. Pivot

The corpus should not be read as hundreds of attempts to build the same missing object.  A stronger
interpretation is possible:

> Each route proves that a different minimal collection of assumptions, observers, completions, or
> signs can sustain the same failure.  The corpus is therefore measuring the failure's minimal
> support hypergraph.  The unknown higher mathematics is the dynamics of its transversals.

This changes the research question from “what source-native object closes Route X?” to:

> Which mechanisms generate, transport, contract, relax, and cofinally complete the family of
> minimal failure kernels, and what laws govern the successor tests forced by that family?

## 2. Candidate laws

1. **Kernel genesis.**  A failed conclusion is decomposed into inclusion-minimal derivation
   supports.  These supports generate successor-test coordinates and arities.
2. **Transversal exactness.**  A law revision is exact iff it intersects every live kernel.
3. **Core retention.**  Every premise removed by a minimal repair uniquely blocks at least one
   kernel relative to that repair.
4. **Observer saturation.**  Quotient observers transport existence of repairs but pull them back
   only as fibre-saturated removals.
5. **Equivariant selection.**  A symmetric failure can forbid a deterministic minimal transversal;
   orientation, multivalued choice, or convex relaxation is then forced.
6. **Positive relaxation.**  Positive weights form the fractional transversal polytope.  Exact law
   birth requires an integrality/effectivity theorem; integrality gaps measure relaxation loss.
7. **Kernel exchange — corrected.**  Static kernel families are arbitrary clutters, even for
   extensive idempotent consequence operators, so no universal matroid/antimatroid exchange law
   exists.  Nontrivial structure first appears in typed transport.  Positive observer aggregation
   has a lax-overlap defect with an exact composition law.
8. **Cofinal openness.**  Birth of independent kernels forces unbounded transversal support/cost;
   every finite stage may close while global law history remains open.
9. **History holonomy.**  Different sequences of minimal contractions and expansions may return to
   the same endpoint doctrine with different retained provenance and future kernel families.
10. **Complexity law.**  Weighted minimum transversals measure repair cost; proof-route difficulty
    becomes a property of kernel overlap, not length of one derivation.

## 3. Why this is not another route

No arithmetic source, Xi specialization, Stokes contact, recurrence Hamiltonian, or route endpoint
enters the primitive laws.  Routes appear only later as independent presentations of kernel
families.  A route-local theorem matters to this programme only if it proves one of:

- a kernel is genuinely minimal;
- two routes encode distinct or overlapping kernels;
- an observer map transports kernels faithfully or saturates them;
- a proposed repair is or is not a transversal;
- cofinal kernel birth forces diverging exact cost.

## 4. Exact finite calibration

The companion Stage-2 card ULF-FKT-1 proves:

- success iff every minimal kernel is hit;
- exact core-retainment witnesses for minimal repairs;
- observer pushforward/pullback and fibre saturation;
- symmetric exact-selector failure with fractional positive repair;
- finite-stage but no finite global repair for cofinal singleton kernels.

These results establish kinematics.  They do not yet establish field dynamics.

## 5. Hostile gates

The programme fails as a new field mechanism if any of these occurs.

1. **No invariant kernels.**  Minimal proof supports depend entirely on presentation and no
   source-marked provenance equivalence preserves them.
2. **No semantic transport.**  Kernel hypergraphs can be listed, but a repair never changes a later
   legal test or admission rule.
3. **Product packaging.**  The five frontier classes merely contribute unrelated hypergraphs with
   no common transport or exchange law.
4. **All repairs are fitted.**  A transversal is selected only after looking at an RH endpoint or
   inserting the desired sign.
5. **Fractional theatre.**  positivity is called relaxation without an explicit integral/fractional
   comparison map and a proved gap/effectivity theorem.
6. **No route-independent prediction.**  kernel overlap, saturation, or cofinal rank predicts
   nothing not already encoded by the separate route statements.

## 6. Next research questions

1. What is the correct presentation-invariant equivalence of proof kernels: minimal premise sets,
   prime implicants, provenance monomials, or simple objects of a proof category?  Circuits cannot
   be universal: arbitrary clutters occur as exact kernel families.
2. When do two proof routes represent the same kernel under a change of doctrine?
3. Which source operations transport kernel profiles, and which lattice operations do they preserve
   strictly or only laxly?  ULF-FKT-OD-1 proves the first exact lax-meet chain law.
4. What is the exact commutator of two contractions?  Can it be read as a new mixed kernel rather
   than stored as an arbitrary history tag?
5. Which compactness/Helly hypotheses turn all finite transversals into a coherent global one?
6. When is the fractional transversal polytope integral?  Do total unimodularity, matroidality, or
   balancedness classify the positivity-to-exactness frontier?
7. Can hypertranscendence be characterized as unbounded independent kernel grade in a literal
   differential realization, rather than equated with topological noncompactness?
8. Can recurrence be characterized as periodic observed blocker data with nonperiodic lifted
   transversal lineage?
9. What source invariant fixes a transversal selection without using a target zero statement?
10. What theorem would falsify kernel--transversal mechanics across all five frontier classes?

## 7. First cross-corpus programme

Do not work route by route.  Instead:

1. define a neutral notion of marked minimal failure support;
2. take one theorem from each of the five frontier classes and test whether it produces that notion;
3. compute pairwise overlap/observer maps between those five kernels;
4. attempt one transversal prediction before reading the target route's proposed repair;
5. reject the programme if the prediction is only a renamed restatement.
