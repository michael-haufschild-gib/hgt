## 1. Pivot from arbitrary obstruction families

Static minimal failure kernels are arbitrary clutters.  No universal exchange law can be extracted
from them without inserting extra hypotheses.  Typed observation supplies the first non-arbitrary
mechanism.

Represent a source failure by a nonnegative profile.  Its lattice meet with another profile is the
amount of genuinely shared source support.  A positive observer aggregates source coordinates.  It
preserves addition exactly but can turn distinct source coordinates into common observed mass.

The founding question is:

> Which laws govern commonality manufactured by doctrine transport, and how does that manufactured
> commonality compose through a chain of observers?

## 2. Closed finite mechanism

The companion theorem ULF-FKT-OD-1 proves that finite deterministic observers carry a canonical
nonnegative defect

\[
  \Omega_f(u,v)=f_!u\wedge f_!v-f_!(u\wedge v).
\]

It satisfies:

1. **lax overlap:** `Omega_f >= 0`;
2. **exact chain law:**
   \[
   \Omega_{gf}(u,v)=\Omega_g(f_!u,f_!v)+g_!\Omega_f(u,v);
   \]
3. **distinguishability budget:** total defect is exactly total-variation loss;
4. **strictness:** zero defect for every pair is equivalent to injectivity of the observer;
5. **false shared repair:** two disjoint singleton kernels collapsed to one observer point acquire
   unit overlap and make exact repair cost appear to fall from two to one.

This is a process law: it assigns a response to observer composition.  No special RH source, target,
or endpoint is part of the theorem.

## 3. Candidate higher laws

### A. Multiway overlap

For a finite family `(u_i)`, define

\[
 \Omega_f^{(r)}(u_1,\ldots,u_r)
 =\bigwedge_i f_!u_i-f_!\!\left(\bigwedge_i u_i\right).
\]

The scalar nonnegative Möbius conjecture is false.  For `r` disjoint Dirac profiles collapsed by a
constant observer, the top Boolean Möbius coefficient is

\[
  (-1)^r(r-1),
\]

so it is negative at every odd arity.  The exact failed-route card is
`nonnegative-mobius-irreducible-overlap.md`.

**Positive survivor:** positivity lives in a witness enrichment, not an alternating scalar.
For nested finite observers, one coupling simultaneously maximizes agreement at every level.  The
simultaneous maximizers form an exposed consensus face, and the successive positive strata have
masses `A_j-A_{j-1}`.  Incomparable observers can have disjoint maximal faces.  This is proved in
`nested-observer-hierarchical-maximal-coupling-face-law.md`.

### B. Completion boundary

For positive maps between measure lattices or Banach lattices, the same lax-meet defect is defined
when meets and pushforwards are admitted.  Conjecture: a cofinal observer tower has a continuous
defect measure exactly when its accumulated total-variation budget is tight; otherwise a singular
boundary defect survives outside the Hausdorff observer limit.

**Open core:** distinguish actual lack of tightness from mere change of ambient norm.  No topology
is supplied by the finite theorem.

### C. Counterfactual repair response

If profiles are minimal failure kernels, nonzero `Omega_f` certifies that an observed common repair
is not source-common.  Conjecture: in an intervention category, the family of defects for all pairs
is sufficient to reconstruct the observer partition up to relabeling on the active source support.

The finite all-profile theorem supports this: zero defect for all pairs characterizes injectivity.
Full partition reconstruction from a restricted corpus profile family remains open.

### D. Positive recurrence ceiling

Normalized positive profiles have total variation at most one, so accumulated **unweighted**
overlap loss is bounded.  This does not bound depth-weighted history.  The explicit law

\[
  d_j=\frac1{j(j+1)}
\]

has unit total collision mass and vanishing observer tail, but collision depth has infinite first
moment.  Therefore a normalized positive defect chain can generate unbounded lifted cost without
radial growth, signs, or increasing arity.  What is independent is the scale/cost law and uniform
integrability.  See `cofinal-collision-depth-nonintegrable-history-law.md`.

## 4. Hostile gates

1. **Data-processing rediscovery.** Total-variation contraction and equality/sufficiency theory are
   classical.  Progress requires a new cross-doctrine consequence, not a renamed divergence.
2. **Pairwise insufficiency.** Pair defects need not determine higher interaction or transversal
   structure.
3. **Signed failure.** Positivity is load-bearing.  Signed cancellation needs an ordered double or
   Jordan data; it cannot be silently absorbed into `Omega`.
4. **Fitted profiles.** Route endpoints may not be declared profiles after the fact.  A realization
   must derive them before the target theorem.
5. **Cost typing.** Normalized overlap loss cannot itself be relabeled as action, complexity, or
   length.  An unbounded cost is possible only after an independently marked scale weights the
collision-depth measure; finiteness is then a uniform-integrability question.

### E. Conflict width versus admission

For a finite family of profile-pair tasks, put an edge between source atoms whose signed task
differences have opposite signs for at least one task.  The exact observer-resource theorem is:

> a deterministic observer preserves every declared total variation exactly iff its fibres are
> independent sets of this conflict graph.

Thus lossless observers are proper colorings and minimal observer range is chromatic number.
Every coloring failure carries a task-labelled quantitative loss witness.  Conversely every
finite simple graph occurs from positive Dirac profile pairs, so positivity supplies no special
graph class.

This produces a sharp higher split.  Unbounded chromatic number is a finite-width obstruction.
If finite chromatic width is uniformly bounded, de Bruijn--Erdos compactness removes any further
bare combinatorial obstruction; failure of a global observer must then come from topology,
measurability, equivariance, completion, or cross-stage coherence.  See
`total-variation-conflict-coloring-and-observer-width-law.md` and the failed card
`positive-conflict-graph-universal-geometry.md`.
