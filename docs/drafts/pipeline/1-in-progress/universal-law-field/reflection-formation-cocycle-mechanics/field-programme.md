## 1. Founding mechanism

The current corpus has many exact carriers and many exact no-go theorems. The missing operation is
often described as orientation, source selection, positive closure, cancellation, or singular
descent. Reflection--Formation Cocycle Mechanics asks one higher question:

> When two independently legal source operations are executed in opposite orders, what generated
> law measures their exchange, and can that law be removed without changing source formation?

The primitive is a schedule, not an object:

\[
 \textsf{mark operations}
 \to\textsf{execute both orders}
 \to\textsf{extract exchange cocycle}
 \to\textsf{test compensation}
 \to\textsf{admit globally}
 \to\textsf{measure proper work}.
 \tag{RFCM.1}
\]

This combines affine/nonabelian cocycles, renormalization semigroups, functional-equation duality,
Markov recovery, proper actions, recurrence covers, and singular completion. These parent subjects
are established. The programme stands or falls on source-generated exchange theorems and cross-
doctrine predictions.

## 2. Candidate laws

### Law I -- both parents are source-marked

An exchange counts only when duality and formation are fixed before the target failure is read.
Arbitrary post hoc transformations make every obstruction a commutator and are excluded.

### Law II -- exchange is law-valued

For legal operations (F,G), retain the typed residue comparing (FG) and (GF). It may be a
translation, central extension, higher homotopy, domain change, or nonclosed graph. The word
`curvature` is not assumed.

### Law III -- composition imposes a cocycle law

Residues for composite formation operations must satisfy the associativity identity forced by
schedule composition. A fitted family of unrelated defects is not a field law.

### Law IV -- compensation is classification, not erasure

A defect is removable only when a source-admitted change of formation trivializes it coherently at
every depth. The required compensator and its uniqueness are mathematical data.

### Law V -- recovery conserves generated distinctions

A common sufficient recovery kernel may relocate the exchange to a hidden fibre, but exact data-
processing equality prevents it from erasing the distinction.

### Law VI -- properness turns exchange into selection

If repeated exchange generates an open source orbit, every predeclared proper work diverges.
Bounded full-orbit work therefore forces the cocycle to vanish. This becomes a selector only after
the physical source supplies the bounded-work comparison.

### Law VII -- recurrent phenotype may hide open lineage

A compact quotient can make exchange history periodic or recurrent while its marked lift escapes.
The cover/history map is constitutive data, not a universal consequence of recurrence.
