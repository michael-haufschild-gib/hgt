## 1. One-sentence field definition

**Constitutive Process Mathematics (CPM)** studies mathematical laws as compositional processes that
accept interventions and produce structured responses, and studies proof methods as partial
instrument systems that reconstruct, distort, or fail to see those processes.

The field is deliberately a Frankenstein of known mathematics. Its claim is a new unit of analysis
and a new research programme, not newness of every ingredient.

## 2. Ontological shift

Ordinary mathematics usually places one of these at the centre:

- an object;
- a space of objects;
- an equation;
- an invariant;
- a proof;
- a category of transformations.

CPM instead places the **interventional law process** at the centre. A law is what answers all legal
counterfactual probes, not merely the passive object observed at one endpoint.

In a finite linear chart, the process is

\[
W\in(E_1\otimes\cdots\otimes E_k)^*.
\]

In nonlinear, analytic, operator, or higher-categorical charts, the tensor is replaced by a functor,
operadic algebra, process tensor, polynomial law, jet process, correspondence, or higher natural
transformation. These are charts of one doctrine only when explicit comparison maps exist.

## 3. Coordinate-free core

For each source object (s\), let \(\mathsf I_s\) be its category of legal interventions and let
\(\mathsf O_s\) be a response category. A constitutive process is a suitably enriched functor

\[
W_s:\mathsf I_s\longrightarrow\mathsf O_s.
\tag{CPM.1}
\]

Source arrows transport intervention categories and responses. A proof route is an instrument chart

\[
j_a:\mathsf B_a\longrightarrow\mathsf I_s.
\]

It sees only (j_a^*W_s). Its canonical reconstruction attempt is a Kan extension

\[
\operatorname{Lan}_{j_a}(j_a^*W_s)\longrightarrow W_s
\tag{CPM.2}
\]

or the right-Kan analogue, according to variance. The comparison in (CPM.2), not an arbitrarily
named curvature, is the fundamental typed defect.

- If (CPM.2) is an equivalence, the chart is sufficient for that process.
- If it is not, its fibre/cofibre or noninvertibility locus is the unseen process sector.
- Several routes are gauges of one process only if their restrictions reconstruct the same (W_s).
- Several lossy routes can be complementary when their joint chart is sufficient.
- A withheld theorem is a response at an instrument outside the training chart, forced by the
  reconstructed (W_s).

In finite vector spaces, this reduces exactly to the annihilator theorem

\[
\ker(\operatorname{res}_U)=U^\perp.
\]

## 5. Five exact laws already established

### Law I — process tomography

An intervention family reconstructs a finite multilinear process exactly iff its joint tensors span
the process domain. The blind sector is the annihilator of that span. Interior positivity does not
remove it.

### Law II — passive law is weaker than mechanism

Opposite causal mechanisms can have identical complete passive joint law and all positive passive
statistics while one elementary intervention separates them.

### Law III — observer loss is quantified by sufficiency

For a path law and reversal, every observer contracts forward/reverse relative entropy. Equality is
exactly sufficiency; the entropy gap is hidden law history.

### Law IV — finite nonlinear instruments cannot see HT

Every finite analytic jet has both a differentially algebraic and a hypertranscendental realization.
In formally smooth charts, unbounded Kolchin growth creates a nonlocally compact pro-Spencer tail
invisible to finite-order observers.

### Law V — reconstruction must predict

For a cyclic (r)-state unitary process, the first (2r) moments reconstruct the process and predict
all withheld later moments. The same process produces analytic transfer, positive Gram rank,
recurrent observer dynamics, and open lifted phase history.

### Law VI — process memory is doctrine-relative

Finite Hankel rank, finite rational-coefficient linear state, finite nonlinear differential state,
and hypertranscendence are four distinct phases. The same process can have infinite memory for a
constant controller and one-dimensional memory for a base-aware controller. The invariant is a
memory spectrum indexed by admitted intervention algebras, not one scalar rank.

### Law VII — one infinite process can carry all major shadows

For \(0<q<1\), Jackson's \(q\)-Gamma response is simultaneously an explicit positive discrete
law, a positive-definite GNS kernel, a recurrent pure-point unitary observation with open marked
history, a maximal-HT jet process, and an exact exponential-tilt/entropy intervention. Its
functional equation predicts the tilted response from the untilted one.

These laws give CPM nontrivial content before any RH application.

## 7. Fundamental conjectures

### CPM-A — common-process conjecture

A substantial fraction of apparently unrelated theorem families arise as restrictions of one
source-generated higher process, after changing instrument charts but before endpoint
specialization.

### CPM-B — complementarity theorem

There exist three unlike instrument families that are individually insufficient but jointly
sufficient for a nontrivial source-process sector. Their joint reconstruction predicts a fourth
withheld response.

### CPM-C — Kolchin/process-memory correspondence

For a source-generated analytic process, the doctrine-relative memory spectrum extends from
constant and rational-linear processors to algebraic jet processors. Kolchin dimension growth is
the algebraic-memory coordinate, while pro-Spencer topology controls which observers can remain
continuous.

### CPM-D — recurrence/dilation separation

Repeated observer processes are classified by peripheral/compact dynamics, while minimal dilations
carry a functorial open-history invariant not determined by recurrence data.

### CPM-E — physical realization

When a physical system supplies clock, cost, and intervention algebra, Brown--Susskind-type
complexity/recurrence separation and horizon observer loss become process-theoretic theorems rather
than analogies. No such equality is assumed.

## 8. Acceptance and death criteria

CPM becomes a real new field programme only if it achieves all of:

1. a coordinate-free intervention/process category;
2. two or more nonisomorphic source realizations;
3. a common-process theorem across at least three unlike mathematical doctrines;
4. one withheld cross-doctrine prediction;
5. one infinite/HT process-memory theorem;
6. presentation/Morita invariance of the claimed law state;
7. a physical realization with independently sourced clock and cost.

It dies as theatre if:

- (W_s) is fitted after seeing route outputs;
- legal interventions are chosen to force desired comparisons;
- every route needs an unrelated coefficient category with no common process;
- “process” means only an arbitrary tuple of existing objects;
- reconstruction never predicts an unobserved response;
- HT, positivity, recurrence, or physics are identified by definition rather than theorem.

realization, and independently physical clock/cost remain open. No RH route is being used to define
the field.
