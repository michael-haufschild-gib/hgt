## 1. Primitive

Start with a history category or groupoid \(\mathcal H\), a reversal operation \(\Theta\), and a
quasi-invariant constitutive path law (P). Observers are measurable, algebraic, analytic, or
categorical statistics (q:\mathcal H\to\mathcal O_q). The primitive field is not an object and
not a curvature tensor. It is the polar comparison between forward and reversed law:

\[
\mathfrak m
=\frac{dP}{d\Theta_*P}
\quad\text{and, when present,}\quad
\mathfrak a:\mathcal H\to U(1).
\]

Their combination

\[
\mathfrak z=\mathfrak m^{1/2}\mathfrak a\in\mathbb C^\times
\]

is a candidate universal history weight. Its modulus is statistical time asymmetry; its argument is
oriented phase. Composition should be multiplication, and reversal should invert \(\mathfrak z\).

## 4. Bold conjecture — polar modular realization

There exists a source-generated class of enriched history groupoids for which a single canonical
\(\mathbb C^\times\)-valued modular amplitude simultaneously determines:

1. exact relation transport by its support and isotropy;
2. oriented phase by its (U(1)) part;
3. positive response and cost by its modular absolute value;
4. recurrence by compact observer characters;
5. open lifted history by nontrivial modular periods;
6. finite-experiment failure by nonsufficiency of every bounded observer stage;
7. hypertranscendence by unbounded minimal sufficient-statistic/prolongation depth.

Items 1--6 have separate exact models. Item 7 is deliberately conjectural: ordinary entropy growth
does not imply differential hypertranscendence.

## 5. First hostile gates

1. **Arbitrary-measure gate.** Construct the history law from algebraic source operations, not by
   fitting a probability after seeing observers.
2. **Phase-genesis gate.** Derive the (U(1)) cocycle from the same producer as the modular ratio.
3. **Intervention gate.** Show source interventions act by absolutely continuous path-law changes
   and obey a chain rule not reducible to ordinary covariance bookkeeping.
4. **Presentation gate.** Prove Morita or measured-groupoid equivalence invariance.
5. **HT gate.** Produce one analytic family where differential-algebraic rank equals a genuine
   sufficiency-depth invariant; a generic infinite path space does not count.
6. **Non-equilibrium gate.** Find one theorem involving three observer doctrines whose joint
   information-loss identity is forced by the same path law.

Failure of Gates 1 or 2 kills this as a universal ontology. Passing only the entropy theorems leaves
classical stochastic thermodynamics under new names.
