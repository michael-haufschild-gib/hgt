## 1. Why this candidate exists

Three independent calibrations survive:

1. polyscopic matching detects missing or multiple total realizations;
2. filling/torsion geometry detects histories erased even when endpoints agree;
3. dynamic equivalence treats the weak-equivalence doctrine itself as variable.

Ordinary space-valued homotopy theory cannot retain all three. Acyclic based complexes are zero in
the derived category but retain determinant torsion; topological disks have one homotopy type but
arbitrarily large cellular filling area.

## 2. Candidate primitive

A **Waldhausen lawstack** is provisionally:

1. a source-defined observer site \(\mathsf O\);
2. a presheaf \(\mathcal W:\mathsf O^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathsf{Wald}_\infty\) of structured
   infinity-categories;
3. cofibrations encoding admissible births of relations, generators, or proof cells;
4. weak equivalences encoding the current doctrine of mathematical sameness;
5. descent maps comparing total and local law categories;
6. determinant/Picard and quantitative weight functors compatible with cofibration sequences.

This is one structured object, not a list of unrelated invariants:

- changing weak equivalences gives dynamic equivalence;
- the \(S_\bullet\)-construction organizes fillings and exact histories;
- algebraic \(K\)-theory records stable law memory;
- object-level descent retains contextual effectivity;
- determinant and hermitian refinements carry phase, orientation, and positive shadows;
- weighted enrichment carries proof cost.

## 3. Provisional law-memory spectrum

The spectrum

\[
 \mathbf K_{\mathrm{law}}(U):=K(\mathcal W(U))
\tag{WL.1}
\]

is not the whole field. It is the stable memory shadow of the lawstack.

Suggested readings of its low layers:

- \(\pi_0\): stable law-resource classes;
- \(\pi_1\): determinant/torsion histories invisible to endpoint exactness;
- higher \(\pi_j\): coherent higher proof transformations;
- descent failure of \(\mathcal W\): contextual non-effectivity not recoverable from group
  completion alone.

The distinction is load-bearing: \(K\)-theory may remember torsion while erasing the existence of
individual objects. Object-level stack and memory spectrum must coexist.

## 4. Why this might unify without reducing

### PLG

The total-to-proper-face matching map is promoted from spaces of laws to structured categories of
law presentations. Empty fibres and hidden interaction remain visible before group completion.

### Filling/torsion

Cofibration sequences and the \(S_\bullet\)-construction retain proof-cell attachment. Determinant
and \(K_1\) record exact histories erased by derived localization.

### Dynamic equivalence

A change of weak-equivalence class changes the Waldhausen structure itself. “Same law” is no
longer background.

### Positivity

Positivity is not assumed to be phase quotient universally. It should arise from a hermitian or
Real refinement and a comparison from oriented \(K\)-memory to a positive Witt/Grothendieck-Witt
shadow.

### Recurrence

Closed observer histories may represent nontrivial loops in a law-memory space or boundaries with
growing weighted fillings. Open history is not forced to mean holonomy of a connection.

## 5. Bold conjectures

### WL-A — law-memory migration

When a source relation becomes trivial after an admissible localization, its information either:

1. vanishes under the declared weak equivalence; or
2. migrates to a higher homotopy class of the law-memory spectrum.

The determinant complex \(C(u)\) realizes the second case: object-level exactness is zero while
determinant history survives in a loop-like multiplicative sector.

### WL-B — theorem endpoint spectrum

Large families of equivalent theorem endpoints should carry nontrivial higher law-memory even when
all scalar, homological, and positive endpoint invariants agree.

### WL-C — hypertranscendence as noncompact generation

Hypertranscendence may correspond not to curvature but to failure of the observer-indexed
Waldhausen lawstack to be generated by any finite stage under cofibrations, weak equivalences, and
prolongations. This must be proved in a native differential-algebraic model or abandoned.

### WL-D — physical recurrence

Quantum recurrence and complexity should arise when a compact positive/hermitian observer is a
quotient of a law-memory object whose weighted \(S_\bullet\)-fillings or universal lift are
unbounded.

## 6. Fatal tests

1. If observer restrictions do not define exact/Waldhausen functors, the stack fails.
2. If admissible proof moves do not determine source-native cofibrations and weak equivalences, the
   structure is fitted.
3. If \(K\)-theory erases the decisive contextual and quantitative data with no canonical
   structured lift, it is only another lossy observer.
4. If hypertranscendence has no relation to compact generation or higher law memory in a native
   model, WL-C dies.
5. If hermitian refinement does not reproduce any genuine positivity theorem, positivity remains
   external.
6. If a simpler doctrine simultaneously captures matching, filling, torsion, and dynamic
   equivalence, this candidate should be replaced.

Waldhausen, \(K\)-theoretic, hermitian, and enriched-category ideas under a new interpretation,
exactly as permitted by the research brief.

## 8. Literature collision and sharpened gate

The abstract ingredients are established:

- sheaf/cohomological contextuality and its incomplete obstruction hierarchy:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.3620 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00656;
- Real \(K\)-theory of Waldhausen infinity-categories with genuine duality:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11682;

  https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.07223;
- conditional and periodically localized algebraic \(K\)-theory descent:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03328.

The last parent is a warning: algebraic \(K\)-theory does not satisfy unrestricted descent.
Therefore one may not apply \(K\)-theory to a lawstack and assume the memory spectrum still glues.
The next theorem must prove either:

1. descent for the literal source-generated Waldhausen diagram under explicit hypotheses; or
2. a nonzero descent defect that itself becomes the law-memory anomaly.

This descent defect, not the existence of Waldhausen or hermitian \(K\)-theory, is the live field
gate.

## 9. Two-dimensional gate passed: coefficient-relative topology

The finite Moore-law calculation
even-moore-law-cell-effectivity-memory-and-positive-topology-collapse-law.md proves a stronger
non-product phenomenon.

For even \(n\), the single boundary operator

\[
 [n]:\mathbb R^\times\to\mathbb R^\times,\qquad x\mapsto x^n
\]

has both:

\[
 H_2\cong\{\pm1\}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 H_1\cong\{\pm1\}.
\]

The first is filling ambiguity; the second is fillability obstruction. Base change along
\(|\cdot|:\mathbb R^\times\to\mathbb R_{>0}\) kills both, because \(n\)-th power is an
automorphism on positive reals.

This forces a correction to the candidate field:

> Positivity is not generally a passive shadow of fixed law topology. It may be a coefficient
> doctrine that changes realization topology and law memory.

The emerging primitive is therefore closer to a coefficient-relative structured lawstack than a
fixed Waldhausen stack with one positive functional.

## 10. Live-source stress test: naïve base change fails

The first arithmetic stress test is closed in
coefficient-relative-source-dagger-stress-test.md.

The inversion/Cartier square over \(k[q,q^{-1}]\) commutes strictly, so its derived and
\(K\)-theoretic Beck--Chevalley defect is zero. Its odd Tor/conormal line survives, but the
lawstack does not manufacture the missing analytic dagger comparison. Positive Moore fillability
also fails because every radial gain \(p^{2a}\) is already a positive square.

Therefore the Waldhausen candidate gains no live arithmetic leverage from ordinary algebraic
base change. A viable successor must study a relative descent defect between inequivalent
doctrines—most plausibly algebraic versus analytic/bornological completion—not add more structure
to the strict square.
