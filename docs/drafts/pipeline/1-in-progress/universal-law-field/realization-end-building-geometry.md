## 1. Pivot

A single realization morphism still hides how finite laws extend. Replace it by its canonical tower
of extension data.

Let \(A\) be a directed aperture category. For each \(a\in A\), let \(\mathcal R_a\) be the
groupoid or space of laws realizing all experiments visible at aperture \(a\). Refinement
\(a\le b\) gives restriction

\[
\rho_{ba}:\mathcal R_b\longrightarrow\mathcal R_a.
\tag{REG.1}
\]

The Grothendieck construction of this diagram is the **extension complex**. Its vertices are finite
partial laws, its edges are source-admitted extensions, its higher cells are coherent simultaneous
extensions, and its ends are compatible infinite realization histories.

This is not assumed to be a Bruhat--Tits building. “Building” is used only when the extension
complex later satisfies genuine apartment and retraction axioms.

## 2. Coordinate-free dictionary

- global law: cartesian section/end of (REG.1);
- proof route: a local section, gallery, or chosen extension history;
- route gauge: change of local trivialization preserving the same end;
- finite no-go: empty vertex or nonfillable horn;
- global no-go: absent admitted end;
- hidden law memory: multiple ends over one observer profile;
- recurrence: periodic orbit after a finite or folded projection;
- open lifted history: translating/nonprecompact gallery above that orbit;
- positivity: a source-produced folding/retraction, not a label on vertices;
- hypertranscendence: an end whose differential-transcendence rank grows at every prolongation level,
  not merely an infinite ray;
- physical interior: extension data carried by the end/galleries but erased by boundary observers.

## 3. Canonical end geometry

For a countable tower, two ends are close when their restrictions agree to large aperture. Any
strictly decreasing scale \(\epsilon_n\to0\) gives the observer ultrametric

\[
d(x,y)=\epsilon_{\sup\{n:\rho_nx=\rho_ny\}}.
\tag{REG.2}
\]

Changing to a cofinal aperture subsequence changes coordinates but preserves the induced
pro-uniformity. This pro-uniformity, not one numerical metric, is the candidate coordinate-free
geometry.

Finite quotients may fold huge galleries into periodic cycles. Proper source action is then measured
upstairs by gallery length or another source-derived exhaustion. Brown--Susskind-style complexity is
therefore a possible realization of end displacement, not an axiom of the field.

## 4. Exact compactness wall

The companion theorem
`locally-finite-realization-tower-global-section-and-nonproperness-necessity-law.md` proves:

> every inverse tower of nonempty finite realization types with coherent total restriction maps has
> a compatible global section.

Therefore a persistent “finite realization but no global law” cannot occur in a locally finite
proper extension tree. At least one of the following must be load-bearing:

1. a finite compatibility locus is already empty;
2. restriction is partial/noncoherent or a higher horn fails;
3. a stage or transition fibre is infinite/noncompact;
4. the set-theoretic end exists but violates effectivity, topology, domain, positivity, or another
   admission doctrine not closed under inverse limits.

This is the first theorem that constrains the ontology rather than illustrating it.

The Lean-certified surjective-erasure theorem adds a second constraint. A same-carrier surjective
law evolution that kills a nonzero class cannot be finite-dimensional. Applied to the literal
fixed-corona Gamma response, it proves that the Bockstein lifetime/death transition requires an
infinite carrier. Thus REG must distinguish two independent failure axes: locally finite coherent
towers automatically acquire ends, while surjective class-death dynamics cannot live on finite
carriers. The viable boundary is necessarily nonproper, infinite-dimensional, or doctrine-changing.

## 5. Corpus reinterpretation without route reduction

The theorem forces a split among corpus obstructions.

- Boolean adaptive profiles: set-theoretic end exists; experimental *effectivity* excludes it.
- finite analytic jets: ends of incompatible global doctrines pass through every finite cylinder;
  the obstruction is nonseparation/admission, not finite emptiness.
- recurrence: finite folds can be periodic while an end-gallery translates indefinitely.
- completion radicals: failure is nonclosedness of the admitted end space.
- source-normal Stokes/Bockstein landing: any all-finite-stage construction that claims global
  annihilation must expose a noncompact/coframe-anomalous transition or a nonclosed admission wall;
  a locally finite exact tower cannot supply the disappearance.

The last item is a restriction on viable mechanisms, not an RH proof.

## 6. Higher conjectures

### REG-A — source extension complex

Construct one pre-target arithmetic source whose finite apertures generate an extension complex
carrying at least three nonisomorphic observer doctrines. No endpoint-fitted edges are permitted.

### REG-B — apartment theorem

Identify maximal doctrine families on which extensions compose uniquely. Prove these families obey
an apartment/retraction law. Proof routes become gauges only after this theorem.

### REG-C — differential end rank

Equip the extension complex with a Spencer/Kolchin rank functor. Prove hypertranscendence is exactly
unbounded rank along every cofinal end presentation, invariant under cofinal chart changes.

### REG-D — folding/action exchange

Derive a source-positive folding and a source cost on the same extension complex. Prove a response
law comparing folded recurrence with end displacement. This must predict a quantity not used to
define either structure.

### REG-E — arithmetic boundary transgression

Construct a source-native extension cell whose failure of coframe continuity defines the physical
Stokes/Bockstein dagger class before Xi specialization. The locally finite theorem says the cell
must cross a nonproper, nonclosed, or non-effective boundary.

## 7. Anti-theatre gate

Reject Realization End Geometry if:

1. the extension complex is built from known route endpoints rather than source operations;
2. every route receives an unrelated tree or metric;
3. “apartment” means only a chosen coordinate chart with no building axiom;
4. HT is represented by any infinite ray without differential rank;
5. positivity and cost are separately attached rather than derived from one extension law;
6. compactness classification never computes or forbids a live source mechanism.

## 8. Established parents

Inverse limits, Kőnig compactness, profinite/prodiscrete uniformities, trees and end spaces,

prevents finite toy towers from impersonating genuine corpus obstructions.
