## 1. One-sentence field claim

Ordinary mathematics fixes what counts as an object, equality, proof, completion, and admissible
operation; **constitutive experimental mathematics** studies those choices themselves as mathematical
state, changed by interventions and inferred from their response.

This is the current answer to what the corpus may be a shadow of. It is not differential geometry and
does not require curvature language.

## 2. Smallest primitive

The primitive is a **constitutive experiment**, not a space:

\[
(S,\mathsf I,\mathsf D,\mathsf R).
\tag{CEM.1}
\]

- (S): a marked generative source;
- (\mathsf I): legal interventions changing specified source laws;
- (\mathsf D): observer/equivalence/coefficient/completion doctrines;
- (\mathsf R(i,d)): the resulting higher category of law objects, proof fillings, and histories.

Interventions compose horizontally. Doctrine changes compose vertically. Mixed squares compare
"intervene then observe" with "transport the observer then intervene." These comparisons may be
equivalences, absent, lossy, or singular. No scalar defect is postulated.

Exact histories may additionally carry a multiplicative metric-line amplitude. That refinement records
phase, positive cost, and determinant memory, but it is not part of the bare primitive unless source
produces it.

## 3. Why this is higher than a route atlas

A route atlas asks how one fixed law looks in different coordinates. CEM distinguishes four operations
that route-level work repeatedly conflated:

1. **passive observation** — forget or measure a fixed source;
2. **intervention** — replace a source relation;
3. **doctrine change** — change equivalence, positivity, completion, or admissibility;
4. **history refinement** — retain a lift, filling, cost, or determinant memory.

Calling all four "gauge" or "curvature" destroys information. Their typed interaction is the field.

## 5. Proposed constitutive laws

### CEM-I — relative gauge law

A transformation is gauge only relative to a declared experiment family. Adding marks or interventions
shrinks the gauge relation to the subgroup preserving their full response. Therefore "route gauges"
must be proved, never asserted globally.

### CEM-II — interventional completeness law

Passive source nerves reconstruct only passive law. Directed constitutive structure requires an
intervention family. A proposed universal observer is complete only if it is jointly faithful over both
source objects and legal interventions.

### CEM-III — doctrine phase law

Changing coefficients, positivity, stability, completion, or weak equivalence can alter effectivity,
proof multiplicity, and stable factorization. Doctrine change is mathematical evolution, not
bookkeeping.

### CEM-IV — conserved response / migrating memory law

Under a legal constitutive change, source information need not stay in one degree or invariant. It may
migrate among endpoint, filling torsor, determinant line, topology radical, and history lift. Any
non-erasure claim must name a jointly faithful total response object.

### CEM-V — compact-response/open-experiment law

Recurrence belongs to a quotient response. Open history belongs to a marked lift or intervention
protocol. Neither implies the other without a constitutive covering map and cost.

## 6. Field-level predictions

### Prediction 1 — route type separation

Every serious proof route should admit a source-normal classification as passive observation,
intervention, doctrine change, history refinement, or a typed composite. Many current no-gos should
disappear as category errors once these types are separated.

### Prediction 2 — minimal separating experiment

For any finite aperture, there should be a smallest intervention/observer family separating all
source-law states visible at that aperture. Its rank is a new invariant: **constitutive response rank**.
It is not ordinary matrix rank because experiments may change the law category.

### Prediction 3 — hypertranscendence as inexhaustible response rank

Hypertranscendence should imply that constitutive response rank does not stabilize with jet depth:
every finite experiment family admits distinct source prolongations with identical current responses.
This is falsifiable and avoids equating hypertranscendence with topological noncompactness.

### Prediction 4 — positivity kernel predicts unselectability

If two source states lie in one fibre of a positive doctrine but have different intervention response,
no positive-only proof route can select between them. Selection requires a mark, Real/phase datum, or
new intervention not factoring through the positive quotient.

### Prediction 5 — recurrence pumps constitutive memory

A closed observer orbit may induce nontrivial monodromy on proof fillings or determinant histories.
Complexity should measure the lifted experiment, not distance between recurrent endpoints. This is the
mathematical bridge to quantum recurrence and Brown--Susskind-type intuition without identifying them.

### Prediction 6 — theorem families obey response identities

If multiple theorem endpoints truly arise from one source, their intervention derivatives and doctrine
changes must satisfy common composition/exchange identities. Mere endpoint agreement is insufficient.
Finding such identities is the acceptance test for a universal field.

## 7. Hypertranscendence, positivity, and recurrence

The requested slogans now become precise hypotheses rather than metaphors:

- **hypertranscendence:** failure of finite interventional/prolongation identifiability;
- **positivity:** a doctrine map that can change topology and erase phase;
- **recurrence:** return in a coarse response object;
- **open history:** nonreturn in a marked lift, filling category, or intervention protocol.

None is defined in terms of curvature.

## 8. Anti-theatre acceptance gate

CEM becomes more than architecture only after all of the following:

1. construct one literal pre-target source (S);
2. type at least three nonisomorphic proof organs as legal experiments on (S);
3. prove their serial/parallel intervention exchange laws;

5. derive one withheld theorem or impossibility shared by those organs;
6. prove presentation/marking dependence exactly rather than hiding it as gauge.

The field is currently below this gate.
