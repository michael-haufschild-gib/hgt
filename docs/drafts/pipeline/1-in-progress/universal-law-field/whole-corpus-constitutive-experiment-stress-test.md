## 1. Mechanical evidence

The current whole-corpus summary reports:

- HTGeometry: 3,248 modules and 36,472 declarations;
- RHTower: 3,934 modules and 49,953 declarations;
- total: 7,182 modules and 86,425 declarations.

Its primary mechanisms are not one family. HTGeometry alone separates algebraic incidence, analytic
boundary spaces, moments/operators, differential hypertranscendence, special functions,
projective-horizon objects, prime sources, and quantum-clock observers. RHTower separately contains
Xi/Zeta foundations, prime arithmetic, positivity/moments, growth/density, operator-spectral systems,
archimedean factors, Sonin/Fourier, Deninger geometry, and quantum dynamics.

The largest deterministic families repeatedly carry the flags

\[
\text{equivalence, no-go, observer, orientation, positivity, singularity, HT, RH}.
\tag{1}
\]

This is evidence against one scalar obstruction and against a one-route field core.

## 2. CEM classification test

Constitutive experimental mathematics predicts four distinct operation types. At mechanism-family
level the corpus contains candidates for each:

| CEM type | corpus-scale manifestations |
|---|---|
| passive observation | moment, spectral, explicit-formula, boundary-value, and finite-window reporters |
| intervention | coefficient perturbation, source shift, ramification, aperture change, and relation insertion |
| doctrine change | positivity, reflection/Real structure, completion, quotient, localization, and analytic continuation |
| history refinement | winding, recurrence lift, determinant/torsion, filtration, cofinal tower, and proof cost |

This table is a typing hypothesis, not a proof that every module has been classified.

## 3. What the census falsifies

The census directly falsifies three weak field proposals:

1. **all routes are local gauges** — many modules change source relations, domains, or completion;
2. **all no-gos are curvature** — nonadmission, sign input, arithmetic cancellation, dense radicals, and
   quantitative bounds are not one defect type;
3. **one positive invariant controls the corpus** — orientation, source acquisition, analytic
   continuation, and recurrence survive as separate required capabilities.

## 5. Hard failure of current field claim

CEM has not yet constructed:

- one literal source experiment spanning all primary mechanism families;
- typed intervention composition for the formal corpus;
- a jointly faithful total response functor;
- a three-organ source-normal exchange identity;
- a withheld arithmetic or physical prediction.

Therefore the statement "all 86,425 declarations are shadows of CEM" is presently unsupported.
The honest statement is narrower:

> CEM is the first candidate in this programme whose primitive has enough distinct operation types not
> to collapse the corpus by definition.

## 6. Next discriminating target

Do not refine another endpoint. Construct one **constitutive response square** from a pre-target source
where:

1. one horizontal arrow is a genuine source intervention;
2. one vertical arrow is a doctrine change;
3. both composites exist;
4. their comparison produces a nonzero response class;
5. passive endpoint data cannot reconstruct that class;
6. the same class controls at least three mechanism families.

Failure to construct this square kills CEM as a unifying field rather than sending work back into a
route.

fixed additive interventions have a non-descending exchange response, while two intervention

remains open.

A first literal source law is now recorded in
