1. Build the literal zero-independent Möbius--theta/prime--Gamma source experiment and its admitted
   effect calculus.
2. Prove that a completed Xi error at horizontal displacement `a` forms arbitrarily many copies of
   one finite target experiment `P_a`, with `P_a` neutral exactly when `a=0`.
3. Predeclare all gauges and final detectors from source structure. Prove from source estimates
   that the literal complete formation--detector action has one depth-independent bound;
   equivalently, prove a positive or too-slowly-decaying Hahn-label width for the full arithmetic
   effect dictionary. A compact effect hull, uniform modulus, fixed normal-family bound, or proper
   source graph-domain theorem may supply it, but bare Feller continuity or one conveniently gauged
   kernel norm cannot.
4. Apply resolution-price properness, or its compact-effect corollary, to obtain `P_a` neutral and
   hence `a=0`.
5. Preserve the internal multiplicity/conormal channel through the comparison, so the theorem
   excludes only transverse displacement and does not erase legitimate Xi zeros.

Steps 2--3 are open. Step 3 is the global formation/properness theorem demanded by the frontier in
source-specific form; the abstract theorem proving its selection consequence is closed.

There is now a second, weaker-regulation branch. Instead of a depth-independent continuous path
action, construct one divisor-independent prime-circle potential with finite Haar `L1` work and
exact affine covariance. Law XII then annihilates the modular character. This branch replaces
uniform boundary continuity by measurable finite-information trivialization; constructing that
potential remains open.

Law XIII exposes the broader compensated branch. A compact phase model may exist off wall if it
contains lattice carry. Here the correct source theorem is not global current zero—FE already
supplies opposite-current cancellation—but componentwise detailed balance or a source-predeclared
one-sided formation law forcing mean carry zero. Once proved, `(TAFM.9)` selects neutral modular
drift. This is strictly more informative than asking again for an uncompensated potential: it
classifies every integrable compact realization and names its only surviving off-neutral channel.

Law XIV weakens this branch again. Pointwise detailed balance is unnecessary. It suffices to prove
that the divisor-independent source construction assigns uniformly bounded absolute carry traffic
to every source word returning to one fixed prime-phase neighborhood. Adaptive gauges and different
probability fibres do not evade the theorem. The RH-bearing producer is now a local family
properness estimate, while failure predicts a concrete linear traffic explosion rather than an
unspecified obstruction.

Law XV explains how the Law XIV properness estimate can be produced. Build a local
divisor-independent formation complex whose vertex divergence is the recovered modular grade.
Exact Stokes converts all bulk current into cut flux. A uniform source bound on elementary edge
currents and an observer-closed Følner exhaustion then make boundary traffic sublinear and select
neutrality. Failure is informative: it must appear as positive formation Cheeger density,
inverse-isoperimetric edge blow-up, nonlocal production, or rank growing at packet-width scale.

Law XVI removes a separate observer-closure assumption at fixed rank. Product compact recurrence
automatically supplies arbitrarily large common closing widths for every finite palette. The source
comparison must therefore decide only whether legal local boxes and a uniform elementary-current
bound exist. If they do, neutral grade follows; if not, the failed clause identifies the precise
rank, capacity, locality, or packet fracture carrying open history.

Law XVII opens a strictly broader branch. Instead of bounding each elementary edge current, build
the source intervention grammar and fill repeated observer-return histories by proved rewrite
cells. Functional-equation-neutral faces may have zero curvature action even when their individual
edges are large. It suffices that complete curvature action plus unresolved residual action is
sublinear in winding. Failure has a sharp meaning: linear integrated curvature, linear residual
current, absence of cofinal legal fillings, or failure of bounded route representation.

Law XVIII fixes the flat observer quotient but also exposes why quotient-first universality is too
small. Primitive interventions modulo rewrite boundaries classify exactly the readouts that erase
all curvature. A general route needs maps on both history generators and rewrite generators, with
boundary and observer squares commuting. This correction prevents a flat observer theorem from
being relabeled as universality for all routes.

Law XIX supplies the missing chain-level property. Once a literal signature is declared, each route
must specify target witnesses for primitive rewrites and pass only two primitive square tests. The
entire route then exists uniquely, and all finite composite defects reconstruct from primitive
defects. Therefore literal extraction no longer needs a vague search for global coherence: it needs
an explicit generator/relation table and target witnesses. After this algebraic gate, only kernel,
topology/completion, norm, and sublinear filling action can carry the obstruction.

Law XX supplies the first literal dynamical relation shared by heterogeneous route organs. Static
source equalities become one scale connection: checkpoint curvature is its acceleration, Route-RG
is an additive gauge split, completed Abel is corrected connection current, and Gamma/Xi are
quadratic readouts. Its exact kernel theorem proves which information the current loses, while
harmonic no-trapping identifies a sharp selection mechanism. Remaining producer is no longer
another source equality: prove global source properness for the formation potential and sufficient
control of the retained correction, or prove that an off-line divisor forces persistent oriented
current and hence forbidden harmonic escape.

## 8. Why this is not regression into one route

The research target is not “find a source-native Route-R map.” It is to construct a doctrine in
which all legal formations obey `(TAFM.2)` and a common bounded-action law. Route R contributes the
tail counterexample; prime Poisson contributes a harmonic singular laboratory; Gamma-six
contributes a Borel--Cantelli laboratory; analytic routes receive the derivative-escape prediction;
RG supplies an independent moving-test stress test.

Work counts only when it proves a doctrine law or a typed comparison into one of these organs.
Another equivalent wall receiver does not count.

## 9. Hostile gates

Retire TAFM as field core if any survives:

1. literal arithmetic completion effects are not Feller in any source-defined compact topology;
2. their effect family is necessarily noncompact even at neutral grade;
3. Xi completion supplies no exact or asymptotically exact tensor-formation interpretation;
4. compact-effect admission on the actual source is equivalent to RH with no independent modulus
   or properness estimate;
5. preserving effect compactness necessarily erases prime labels or internal multiplicity;
6. the RG moving-width family cannot be represented in the same admitted effect calculus;
7. only tables, not source functors, connect the heterogeneous calibrations.
8. the actual source formation exists only with intrinsically unbounded resolution price even at
   neutral grade, so bounded-price properness cannot distinguish the wall.
9. every proposed numerical price bound depends on a metric reverse-engineered from the desired
   selector rather than a source-generated uniform gauge.
10. divergence can always be transferred into an unconstrained final detector or increasing path
    length, so no endpoint-fixed arithmetic action bound exists independently of RH.

## 10. External boundary

Parents: Blackwell comparison, Markov categories, Feller kernels, Le Cam testing, integral
probability metrics, Wasserstein/Dobrushin contraction, Kakutani product dichotomy,
Arzela--Ascoli, normal families, and causal/finitary factors. Recent universal
characterizations of measurable Markov categories emphasize why admission doctrine matters; they do
not produce the compact-effect arithmetic theorem.

Skip novelty as a promotion criterion. Judge the field by route-independent theorem production,
literal source comparison, and whether Steps 1--5 close without importing RH.
