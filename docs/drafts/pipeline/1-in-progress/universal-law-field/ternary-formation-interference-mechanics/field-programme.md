## 1. Question

Pairwise formation laws can all look exact while a third formation creates a new relation and a new
reporter obstruction.  For three source-generated relation subspaces `U,W,Z` of a vector space `V`,
the first exact primitive is not a scalar commutator but symmetric formation homology whose
one-leg chart is the distributivity quotient

\[
 \mathcal T(U;W,Z)
 =\frac{U\cap(W+Z)}{(U\cap W)+(U\cap Z)}.
 \tag{TFIM.1}
\]

It consists of `U`-relations generated jointly by `W` and `Z` but by neither separately.  The
proved chain model shows that the three choices of marked leg are canonically isomorphic.  The
charge is invisible on every pair and born only after fusion without intrinsically privileging
`U`.

## 2. Proved local laws and open global law

1. **Ternary genesis -- proved.** `T=0` exactly when intersection with `U` distributes over the fusion
   `W+Z`.
2. **Kernel-excess law -- proved.** `T` is the excess kernel of the map obtained by quotienting `W` and `Z`
   separately before fusing them.
3. **Reporter duality -- proved.** In finite dimension, `T*` is the dual distributivity quotient
   \[
   \frac{(U^\perp+W^\perp)\cap(U^\perp+Z^\perp)}
        {U^\perp+(W^\perp\cap Z^\perp)}.
   \tag{TFIM.2}
   \]
   Its nonzero classes are reporters legal on both pairwise overlaps but not on the fused relation.
4. **Positive carrier -- proved.** In a finite-dimensional Hilbert chart, if `E=(U∩W)+(U∩Z)` and
   `D=U∩(W+Z)`, then `P_D-P_E` is a positive projection of rank `dim T`.
5. **Equivariant neutrality -- proved.** Every invertible source action transporting the triple transports
   `T`, its perfect pairing, and the positive carrier isometrically.  Symmetry moves ternary charge;
   it does not annihilate it.
6. **Fisher prediction -- proved.** Fused Gram nullity equals local nullities minus overlap nullity
   plus `dim T`; locally identifiable Gaussian models can fuse into a model with exactly this
   nonidentifiability rank.
7. **Cofinal properness gate -- proved abstractly, arithmetic realization open.** A nonzero charge
   transported noncontractingly through a coherent cofinal tower has divergent weighted action.
   Finite-action admission forces zero.  What remains open is construction of the literal
   arithmetic formation complex and proof that its physical action is this cofinal action.
8. **Serial erasure--generation law -- proved.** For observers `L` then `M`, a five-term exact
   sequence gives `composite charge = upstream charge - erased charge + newly generated charge`.
   A lift is information-neutral exactly when it erases no upstream class and creates no downstream
   class.  Arbitrary serial chains obey an exact telescoping ledger.

## 6. Higher-arity field pivot

The proved theorem identifies the correct generalization.  For a finite formation family
`(W_i)`, form the augmented incidence complex

\[
 C_p(W_\bullet)=\bigoplus_{|I|=p+1}\bigcap_{i\in I}W_i,
 \qquad C_{-1}=\sum_iW_i,
\]

with alternating inclusion boundary.  For an observer `L`, form the separate complex obtained by
replacing every intersection with `L(intersection)` and the augmentation with `L(sum)`.  Its
homology measures relations created by observer-image formation at every arity.  The binary
Mayer--Vietoris case is now proved exactly: its middle homology is the symmetric ternary charge
with the kernel leg `ker L`.
