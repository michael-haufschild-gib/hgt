## 1. Thesis

General relativity promoted geometry from background to dynamical object. An analogous move here
may be to promote **mathematical equivalence itself** from background convention to field
variable.

A proof route does not merely observe one law object. It chooses which distinctions count, which
relations are inverted, which histories are identified, and which completions become equal. The
space of these doctrines can be nondistributive and scale-dependent.

## 2. Primitive

A candidate field consists of:

1. a source category \(\mathcal S\);
2. an admissible higher lattice \(\mathfrak E\) of congruences, localizations, or reflective
   doctrines on \(\mathcal S\);
3. dynamics transporting both source objects and doctrines;
4. a separation topology recording which cofinal doctrine families recover source distinctions;
5. optional determinant, cost, and positivity enrichments on quotient doctrines.

A theorem endpoint is an incidence statement

\[
 x\sim_E y
\tag{DE.1}
\]

for a particular doctrine \(E\), not an absolute equality.

## 3. Reinterpretation

- proof route: trajectory in doctrine space;
- equivalent endpoints: equality after different localizations;
- no-go: doctrines lack common refinement or combine nondistributively;
- source obstruction: no source-normal choice of equivalence doctrine;
- positivity: phase-erasing doctrine;
- recurrence: periodicity after localization;
- open history: nonperiodic source lift;
- hypertranscendence: no finite doctrine family separates the prolongation tower;
- completion: adjoining limits that change the doctrine lattice.

## 4. Why this is distinct

PLG fixes an observer diagram and asks whether face data realize globally. Dynamic equivalence asks
which face distinctions exist in the first place and how the equivalence doctrine changes.

Filling geometry fixes admissible proof cells and measures fillings. Dynamic equivalence asks when
two fillings or endpoints are declared the same.

These are logically independent:

- a reconstructible matching problem can still use a nondistributive doctrine lattice;
- a unique filling can become recurrent only after a coarse doctrine;
- large filling cost may survive or disappear under different equivalence fields.

## 5. Native survivor

The finite equivalence lattice on three points is already nondistributive. On \(\mathbb Z\), every
finite congruence doctrine sees periodic translation, while the cofinal family of all congruence
doctrines separates the open lift exactly.

## 6. Hard research questions

1. What replaces an arbitrary lattice of all equivalence relations with a source-generated,
   presentation-invariant doctrine field?
2. Is there a native action or entropy measuring the cost of changing equivalence doctrine?
3. When do two localization paths have the same composite doctrine but different determinant or
   filling history?
4. Can hypertranscendence be characterized as failure of compact generation in a doctrine
   spectrum?
5. Is there a Galois duality between admissible equivalence doctrines and source invariants?
6. Can physical complementarity be recovered as nondistributivity of operational equivalence,
   without assuming quantum mechanics?

## 7. Fatal test

If all decisive proof observers live in one distributive, finitely generated, source-faithful
localization lattice, dynamic equivalence adds no explanatory force.

If admissible doctrines cannot be defined before inspecting endpoint theorems, the proposal is
pure theatre.

## 8. Current verdict

Alive, but extremely preliminary. Its bold claim is not that routes are gauges of one geometry.
It is that the meaning of “same mathematical law” is itself the object undergoing dynamics.
