## 1. Primitive

Let \(\mathcal H\) be a source-generated history category or higher groupoid, equipped with reversal
\(\Theta\). A polar law is a multiplicative Hermitian line or rank-one correspondence over
\(\mathcal H\), together with a projective complex amplitude

\[
\mathcal Z(\gamma)\in\mathbb C^\times
\]

such that composition multiplies amplitudes and reversal applies the dagger/inverse operation.
Observers do not receive the whole amplitude. They receive pushforwards, matrix elements, positive
squares, conditional laws, or coarse path statistics.

The finite chart is not a tuple of probability and phase. The theorem
`projective-amplitude-fisher-phase-and-hidden-history-law.md` shows they are the polar coordinates of
one projective amplitude, with Fubini--Study metric

\[
g_{FS}=\tfrac14g_{Fisher}+g_{phase\ variance}.
\]

## 2. Proposed shadows

From one amplitude/history object:

- exact laws are support, annihilator, and interference relations;
- positive laws are squared norms or positive functionals;
- phase/orientation is relative argument before positive projection;
- response is the derivative of amplitude under source interventions;
- no-gos are failures of an observer channel to be sufficient or reversible;
- recurrence is closure in projective/positive observer space;
- open history is winding, modular production, or lift length upstairs;
- proof cost is a chosen Finsler/action functional on amplitude histories;
- hypertranscendence is conjecturally failure of every finite prolongation statistic to be
  sufficient for the analytic amplitude family.

Only the first seven have exact finite or measured calibrations. Cost and HT remain independent
until derived from the source amplitude.

## 3. Why this is not the discarded complex-action card

The complex Gaussian action was one local oscillatory integral. Polar history geometry instead
places projective amplitude, observer channel, and history composition at the primitive level. An
action functional is only one way to produce such amplitude. Transfer operators, determinants,
path products, categorical traces, and operator colligations are other possible charts.

The field cannot be accepted by showing that each chart has magnitude and phase. It must prove that
source changes transport all charts through the same multiplicative amplitude law.

## 6. Hostile gates

1. **Arbitrariness:** a freely chosen complex amplitude can fit any finite data.
2. **Basis/context dependence:** probability/phase splitting depends on a marked observer context.
3. **Mixed states:** coarse-graining generally leaves rank-one amplitudes and requires positive
   kernels or operator systems.
4. **Composition:** summing over intermediate histories can destroy naive multiplicativity.
5. **Source genesis:** no universal canonical amplitude exists on an arbitrary category.
6. **HT:** infinite Hilbert dimension and infinite sufficient-statistic depth are not automatically
   differential hypertranscendence.
7. **Physics:** Fubini--Study or Berry quantities are not automatically physical complexity,
   action, entropy, or wormhole length.

Failure of Gates 3 or 4 forces a pivot from rank-one amplitudes to positive decoherence kernels or
operator correspondences. Failure of Gate 5 kills universality and leaves only a useful receiver.

Gate 3 fails exactly. `hidden-history-elimination-forces-positive-kernel-and-purification-gauge-law.md`
shows that discarding one factor of an entangled pure amplitude produces a higher-rank positive
kernel. Rank-one amplitudes are closed only in the separable case. The forced successor is developed
in `constitutive-kernel-geometry.md`.
