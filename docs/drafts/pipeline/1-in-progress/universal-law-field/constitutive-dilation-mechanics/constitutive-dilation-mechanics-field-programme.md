## 1. Pivot

Constitutive Process Mathematics correctly replaces passive endpoints by intervention response,
but a process alone still permits positivity, completion, memory, and cost to be attached as
independent slots.  The full frontier says more: every legal observation either preserves source
work or transfers a measurable part of it into hidden history.  Composition determines how those
transfers add.  This suggests a smaller mechanism than a universal geometry and a stronger one than
process tomography.

**Constitutive Dilation Mechanics (CDM)** studies the laws by which an interventional process is
realized through passive observations, reconstructed through legal dilations, and split into two
different histories:

1. a **dissipative defect current**, measured by loss of a source-generated positive work;
2. a **conservative memory current**, invisible to that work but capable of phase, winding,
   recurrence, and open lifted history.

These currents must not be identified.  A positive defect cannot circulate around a closed passive
cycle.  A conservative lift can.

## 2. Primitive laws, not a primitive object

The field begins with a family of legal process transitions rather than one universal carrier.
Each transition $f:X\to Y$ is equipped with source work functions
$W_X:X\to\mathbb R$ and $W_Y:Y\to\mathbb R$.  Its defect current at $x$ is

\[
 J_f(x)=W_X(x)-W_Y(fx).
 \tag{CDM.1}
\]

The proposed field laws are:

1. **Passivity.** Legal positive observations satisfy $J_f\ge 0$.
2. **Serial conservation.** $J_{g\circ f}(x)=J_f(x)+J_g(fx)$.
3. **Dilation reconstruction.** A terminal state plus the ordered defect ledger reconstructs the
   initial work exactly.
4. **Split-cycle rigidity.** If legal passive reconstruction $r$ satisfies $r(fx)=x$, then
   $J_f(x)=J_r(fx)=0$.
5. **Conservative polarization.** Recurrent/open history with zero total defect belongs to a
   marked conservative lift, not to the positive defect current.
6. **Completion.** Infinite histories are admitted precisely in a completion where their defect
   ledger and conservative memory remain meaningful; changing completion can erase either.
7. **Prolongation.** Minimal dilation-memory growth under deeper legal interventions is the
   operational rank profile.  Hypertranscendence is a candidate unbounded-prolongation phase, not
   an orbit or curvature slogan.
8. **Strict selection.** If every nonneutral source formation forces positive defect on at least
   one leg of a source-generated closed passive cycle, the only admitted formation is neutral.

The Stage-2 companion card proves Laws 2--4 and the strict selection schema without an RH premise.

## 4. Why the corpus separates into these two histories

The corpus already proves both halves but usually in separate modules.

- Process tomography and source-coherent synchronization describe visible reconstruction and
  blind sectors.
- Observer-fibre chirality and conditional KL describe nonnegative hidden defect current.
- Norming-face fusion describes exact loss under contraction and fusion.
- Additive-positive groupoid triviality proves that nonzero positive cost cannot live on the
  reversible core.
- Observer unitization proves that recurrent observation can hide an open positive-cost directed
  history only by collapsing a nonunit to an observer unit.
- Reciprocal-character and formation-properness laws show how nonneutral source scale can force
  positive work to escape.
- Wold, recurrence, determinant, and Pluecker histories exhibit conservative memory with closed
  observation and open lift.

CDM's first route-independent prediction is therefore a separation theorem:

> A recurrent observer with open lifted history is not, merely from recurrence, evidence of hidden
> dissipation.  If the return is realized as a closed passive cycle in one work doctrine, every
> dissipative leg vanishes.  The remaining open history is conservative memory or records a change
> of doctrine/completion.

This prediction rules out a common ambiguity in the corpus: positive no-go current and phase
history cannot be silently counted as the same hidden coordinate.

## 5. Three structurally different realizations

The laws have exact parents in three unlike organs.

1. **Information organ.** For a statistical observer, source work is a directed divergence;
   conditional divergence is the defect ledger and the chain rule is serial conservation.
2. **Positive Hilbert/Gram organ.** For a contractive compression, source work is norm or norm
   square; the defect operator/current measures lost energy and a passive dilation restores it.
3. **Process/history organ.** For repeated process composition, the ordered defect ledger is the
   dissipative memory while a unitary/minimal dilation carries conservative recurrence and phase.

The fourth-organ prediction concerns completion: an exact recurrent return with positive local
defect cannot survive in one Hausdorff passive completion.  Any corpus realization claiming both
must exhibit the precise quotient, metric change, domain failure, or conservative lift that
separates them.

## 6. Selection mechanism and RH-facing target

The current RH frontier has many positive receivers with exact critical radical.  Their common
failure is not lack of another receiver; it is lack of one pre-zero reconstruction leg.

CDM converts that wall into a single constructive target:

1. build a source-generated positive work on the marked reflection packet;
2. build a passive compression whose defect is the existing Hodge/formation current;
3. build a legal passive reconstruction from the compressed source statistic;
4. prove the source laws make reconstruction a left inverse before Xi-zero specialization.

Then split-cycle rigidity forces the defect current to vanish.  Where the corpus has already proved
that current's critical radical, selection follows.

This target is stronger than endpoint equality and weaker than reconstructing every source
coefficient.  It asks for one **contractive sufficient statistic** on the actual source process.
It is also falsifiable: if every source-natural reconstruction is expansive, partial, or defined
only after divisor specialization, this field mechanism does not close RH.

## 7. Hostile boundaries

1. **A chosen norm proves nothing.** Work must be generated before the endpoint and transported by
   the source process.
2. **Passivity is not automatic.** Analytic continuation, indefinite forms, and singular
   completions can violate it.  Such failure is typed data, not permission to assume positivity.
3. **A left inverse alone is insufficient.** An expansive decoder can reconstruct information
   after arbitrary compression.  Both legs must be passive in the same source work doctrine.
4. **Zero defect does not imply zero memory.** Unitary phase, determinant winding, and deck history
   can remain.
5. **Recurrence does not imply return in source work.** Observer unitization may create a false
   cycle.
6. **Hypertranscendence is not proved equivalent to dilation growth.** Only a source-normal
   prolongation comparison could establish that bridge.
7. **The finite theorem is classical.** Contractive dilation and energy balance are established
   mathematics.  The field content is the requirement that one source process generate work,
   compression, reconstruction, conservative memory, and the cross-organ comparison.
