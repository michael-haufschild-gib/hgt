## 2. Exact finite theorem

The dependency-closed theorem is proved in the Stage-2 companion
`adelic-plucker-northcott-rigidity-and-static-gram-open-history-law.md`.

Let `K` be a number field, let `T` be an `r × n` matrix of rank `r`, and let `p_B` be its nonzero
maximal minors.  For `u=(u_i) in (K^×)^n`, set

\[
 T_m=T\operatorname{diag}(u_1^m,\ldots,u_n^m),
 \qquad p_B(m)=p_B\Bigl(\prod_{i\in B}u_i\Bigr)^m.
 \tag{APH.2}
\]

For an adjacent basis exchange `B' = B-i+j`, the normalized Plücker response is

\[
 \mathcal R_{B,i,j}(m)
 =\frac{p_{B'}(m)p_B(0)}{p_B(m)p_{B'}(0)}
 =\left(\frac{u_j}{u_i}\right)^m.
 \tag{APH.3}
\]

It obeys the exact all-place conservation law

\[
 \sum_v n_v\log|\mathcal R_{B,i,j}(m)|_v=0.
 \tag{APH.4}
\]

The first response reconstructs every relative intervention character on the element-exchange
graph.  Its complete relative height obeys the exact law `mathcal H_b(m)=m mathcal H_b(1)` and is
bounded exactly when the normalized character packet is periodic.  Bounded standard projective
height of the complete Plücker orbit is likewise equivalent to periodicity and to torsion of every
exchange ratio `u_j/u_i`.

\[

\[

\[
 \left|p^{2\rho-1}\right|=p^{2\operatorname{Re}(\rho)-1}

one falsifiable construction problem with a forced output.  Any proposed final RH mechanism must
build this formation law, or replace height properness by another proper global history invariant
and prove the corresponding reconstruction theorem.
