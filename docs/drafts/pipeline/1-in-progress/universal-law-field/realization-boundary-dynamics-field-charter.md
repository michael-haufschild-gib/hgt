## 1. Ontological pivot

Constitutive Process Mathematics places an intervention functor at the centre. That remains too
permissive: an arbitrary tuple of route outputs can always be packaged as one functor. The stronger
primitive is the **realization morphism** from source-effective laws to their complete observer
profiles,

\[
  \mathfrak R:\mathcal L_{\mathrm{eff}}
  \longrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal O}_{\mathrm{exp}},
\tag{RBD.1}
\]

together with source dynamics acting before observation. The field studies geometry and dynamics of
this one morphism: its essential image, self-fibre product, failure of properness, and boundary in the
experimental completion. These are not independently supplied organs.

The basic phenomenon is the noncommutation

\[
 \operatorname{EssIm}(\mathfrak R)
 \longrightarrow
 \varprojlim_i\operatorname{EssIm}(\mathfrak R_i).
\tag{RBD.2}
\]

A compatible finite profile may lie on the right without coming from one effective source law on
the left. This is not merely the slogan `forall finite, exists` versus `exists, forall`: RBD retains
the realization fibres, their topology, source dynamics, and escape rate.

## 2. One morphism, four failure modes

For a source-to-observer realization morphism, four corpus organs become properties of the same map.

1. **Existence:** failure of essential surjectivity is contextuality or global noneffectivity.
2. **Identifiability:** the kernel pair
   \(\mathcal L\times_{\widehat{\mathcal O}}\mathcal L\) is phase, gauge, or hidden law memory.
3. **Properness:** compact observer motion with escaping source lifts is recurrence with open history.
4. **Finite-type effectivity:** unbounded complexity of stagewise realizers is the exact place where
   hypertranscendental/prolongation phenomena can enter.

Positivity is not identified with phase loss. It is one observer factorization of (RBD.1), and phase
loss is its nontrivial kernel pair. Hypertranscendence is not identified with ordinary noncompactness.
It can enter only through an explicit finite-type realization doctrine whose bounded-complexity
sublevels fail to contain a global jet realization.

## 3. Compactness-escape law

The foundational conjectural principle has a classical topological core. Let \(C_n\) be nested
closed finite-stage realization loci in a source space. If every \(C_n\) is nonempty but
\(\bigcap_nC_n=\varnothing\), then no compact source region meets every \(C_n\). Consequently every
choice \(x_n\in C_n\) eventually escapes each compact region. If a source action has compact
sublevels, its cost diverges.

The topological lemma is classical. Field content begins only when:

- one source construction produces the observer tower and realization loci;
- different proof doctrines become restrictions of that tower;
- the same escaping history controls at least three unlike shadows;
- a withheld response is computed before its observer is consulted.

## 6. Fundamental conjectures

### RBD-A — source realization conjecture

There is a literal pre-target HGT source category and a realization morphism (RBD.1) whose declared
observer subdiagrams contain at least three independent corpus organs and whose kernel pair or
essential-image boundary predicts a fourth withheld response.

### RBD-B — properness/HT conjecture

For one genuine hypertranscendental source family, bounded differential-process complexity defines
compact or sequentially compact realization sublevels. Longer finite-jet realizers then escape every
such sublevel, making HT an exact finite-type realization-boundary phenomenon rather than a slogan.

### RBD-C — recurrent lift conjecture

For every source-generated recurrent observer process, the minimal effective realization history is
classified by properness of the realization morphism. Compact source fibres give closed lifted
history; nonproper fibres admit recurrent projection with escaping lift. Physical action requires an
independently sourced proper exhaustion.

### RBD-D — arithmetic persistence conjecture

The Gamma response lifetime is one coordinate of a larger source-normal realization boundary. A
coframe-conservative exactification exists only if the valuation reporter tower extends across that
boundary; otherwise the failure defines a nontrivial persistence class before Xi specialization.

## 7. Anti-theatre gate

RBD is rejected as universal field if any of the following persists:

1. realization morphisms are fitted after route endpoints are known;
2. observer towers differ by unrelated source categories with no common refinement;
3. compactness escape is only restated and never computes a withheld response;
4. HT is represented only by an elementary infinite chain with no differential realization;
5. positivity, phase, recurrence, and cost are stored as independent labels;
6. source-equivalent presentations change the claimed boundary invariant without a typed comparison.

## 8. External boundary

Codensity/pro-object compactness, phantom maps, descent, properness, persistence, and realization
spaces already occupy the machinery. Relevant parents include Leinster on the ultrafilter codensity
monad, Di Liberti on codensity/pro-objects/compactness, and Christensen on phantom maps as derived
inverse-limit classes. RBD makes no novelty claim for those mechanisms. Its open content is a
source-normal cross-doctrine realization morphism and route-independent predictions from its
boundary dynamics.
