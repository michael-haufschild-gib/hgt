## 4. Field laws

### Law I -- refinement precedes reduction

Positive legs compose before signed quotient. Reducing each event and then composing erases reserve
birth.

### Law II -- cancellation generates, not destroys

Opposite visible currents generate a positive fibre coordinate. Algebraic cancellation and
formation annihilation are different operations.

### Law III -- functional equation is polarity exchange

Functional equation sends `(P,N)` to `(N,P)`. It reverses visible deformation and preserves reserve.
Therefore it is information-neutral but not formation-neutral.

### Law IV -- causal support is a valuation of formation

convolution. In the scalar drift chart these endpoints reconstruct the two positive legs after
delay and window width are marked.

### Law V -- endpoint information is a quotient invariant

Exponential-family observers and every parameter-independent common recovery kernel depend only on
signed endpoint `D`. Their full divergence geometry is constant on unbounded reserve fibres.

### Law VI -- recurrence can close only after reduction

A source word with `D=0` returns the observer exactly while retaining reserve
`R=(1/2) sum |delta_i|`. Closed observer execution can therefore be an open positive formation.

### Law VII -- selection is a properness/passivity theorem

No symmetry of the signed quotient can select the neutral law. Selection requires a source theorem
that bounds, dissipates, forbids, or makes proper the positive reserve before reduction.
