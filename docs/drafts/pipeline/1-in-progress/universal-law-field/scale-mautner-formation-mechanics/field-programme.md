## 1. Pivot

The corpus repeatedly tries to retain four properties in one observer space:

1. a nontrivial one-parameter formation `V_t`;
2. an exact source dilation `U V_t U^{-1}=V_(q t)` with `q>1`;
3. recurrent or pure-point source evolution `U`;
4. a compact-resolvent clock generating `V_t`.

The first Scale--Mautner theorem proves that this is impossible.  It is not a route obstruction and
not a curvature analogy.  It is a formation law:

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{exact dilation transport}
 +\text{same-fibre recurrence or compact spectral admission}
 \Longrightarrow\text{formation collapse}.}
\]

The positive replacement is cross-fibre scale formation.  Exact dilation may transport a
compact-resolvent clock from `H_s` to `H_(q s)`; it cannot close that transport as a nontrivial
unitary loop in one fibre.
