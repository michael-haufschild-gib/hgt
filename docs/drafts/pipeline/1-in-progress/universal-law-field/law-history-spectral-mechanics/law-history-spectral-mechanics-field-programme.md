## 1. Fields-medalist pivot

The corpus already has mechanisms for:

- producing one challenge from one marked failure;
- diagonal challenge birth and grammar extension;
- prospective separation, permanent plateaux, and completion-born histories;
- primary, secondary, and three-stage obstruction filtrations;
- coefficient-lift transgression and closure-born homology.

Three tempting “higher theories” therefore fail immediately.

1. **Mutual law/challenge production** is arbitrary dynamics unless production is source-normal.
   The strict source-normal case is already contextual challenge transduction.
2. **A prospective ultrametric** is already ULF-PCG and classical automata/coalgebra geometry.
3. **One universal obstruction or curvature** is contradicted by the corpus's independent kernel,
   cokernel, completion, higher-arity, history, and positive sectors.

The missing mechanism is not another carrier. It is the law governing what happens *between*
successive failures:

> A local defect may be cancelled by a later stage, transported to a different grade, split by a
> refactorization, or survive as global law birth. Which laws govern this transmutation?

## 2. Primitive mechanism

A **law history** is a coherently composable sequence of admitted transformations

\[
 X_0\longrightarrow X_1\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow X_n.
 \tag{LHSM.1}
\]

The primitive operation is not “take a defect object.” It is

\[
 \text{history}
 \longmapsto
 \text{local failure pages}
 \longmapsto
 \text{transgressions}
 \longmapsto
 \text{survivors plus extension data}.
 \tag{LHSM.2}
\]

The history must be fixed before a target endpoint is inspected. Arbitrary factor insertion,
reporter fitting, or post-hoc page selection is illegal.

## 2A. Fields-medalist discovery protocol

Mechanism discovery is not allowed to begin with a named RH route. It begins with collisions among
theorems that live in different subjects and asks what operation changes all of them in the same
way.

### Questions

1. Which distinctions recur after every change of notation: birth versus survival, execution
   versus observation, algebraic exactness versus admitted exactness, local repair versus global
   formation?
2. Which operations fail to commute, and what typed residue measures that failure before any
   endpoint is specialized?
3. Which quantities are conserved under every legal revision? Which are monotone? Which can migrate
   between grades but cannot disappear?
4. What is the smallest laboratory in which two histories have the same endpoints and different
   law-forming behaviour?
5. Which factorization changes the intermediate spectrum without changing the total transformation?
6. When does a local failure die later, when does it transgress into a different type, and when does
   it force enlargement of the legal language?
7. Which observer operations merely forget history, and which genuinely change what formation is
   admitted?
8. What finite-resource theorem forces termination? Which named resource must diverge in every open
   genesis history?
9. Can one source-normal mechanism make one withheld prediction in three structurally different
   non-route laboratories?
10. Which part survives Lean formalization after all metaphorical language is removed?

### Research loop

1. Build a periodic table of **mechanism collisions**, not a catalogue of routes.
2. Construct minimal bifurcation cells with identical endpoints and different histories.
3. Extract exact conservation, transgression, refinement, and termination laws.
4. Attack each law with representation change, observer loss, refactorization, completion, and
   adversarial admission.
5. Formalize every surviving significant theorem in Lean before calling it proved.
6. Only then instantiate the mechanism in late laboratories; reject any mapping requiring fitted
   endpoint data.

### Mandatory pivot rule

Stop a line immediately when its next question becomes “find the source-native object that kills
this route-specific class.” Return to the mechanism level and ask instead:

> Which law would make an entire class of local failures die, migrate, or survive, and what other
> mathematical laboratories would obey the same law?

This rule is part of the field methodology. It prevents one hard endpoint from masquerading as the
foundational problem.

## 3. Proposed mechanism laws

### Law I -- local birth is provisional

A nonzero kernel, cokernel, relative fibre, or coherence defect at one stage is page-one data. It is
not yet global law birth. A later stage may cancel it.

### Law II -- future-tail survival

A local class survives only modulo the kernel of its complete remaining future. ULF-DKO-1 proves
this through three stages. ULF-LHSM-1 proves it for every finite linear history.

### Law III -- cancellation requires a transgression

When two nonzero local defects cancel in an exact total history, their relation is not scalar
subtraction. A typed connecting map pairs the outgoing defect of one stage with the incoming defect
of the next. In the split inclusion--retraction cell this transgression is a canonical isomorphism.

### Law IV -- global birth is survival, not accumulation

The final obstruction carries a filtration by survival depth. Its associated grades record which
local failures remain after every later cancellation. Dimensions alone do not retain the extension
data joining the grades.

### Law V -- refactorization refines, not preserves, the spectrum

Replacing one transformation by two composable transformations leaves the total endpoint map
unchanged but inserts one filtration layer. The old grade becomes an exact extension of the two new
grades. Thus total obstruction is factorization-invariant while its history spectrum is not.

### Law VI -- exact observers transport the mechanism

An exact realization should transport cofibres, connecting maps, and the survival filtration.
Nonexact observation, completion, scalarization, or domain restriction may erase a differential or
create derived excess. Such failure is a typed observer-exchange defect, not generic curvature.

### Law VII -- completion changes pages before it changes endpoints

When closure or completion is not exact, source and target homology enter between algebraic grades.
The correct infinite-history law therefore needs derived limit/completion pages, not a naive limit
of finite obstruction scalars.

### Law VIII -- open genesis is nontermination of survival revision

Repeated law birth remains open only if new legal stages keep changing the survival mechanism:
unbounded page depth, grammar growth, admission change, nonstationary refactorization, or a
completion-born differential. Repeating a fixed finite exact history cannot do this.

## 4. First exact theorem and decisive cell

ULF-LHSM-1 proves two finite-history laws.

1. For a finite linear history, the total cokernel has a canonical image filtration. Its `i`-th
   grade is

   \[
   \frac{X_i}{\operatorname{im}(d_i)+\ker(T_i)},
   \tag{LHSM.3}
   \]

   where `T_i` is the complete future tail from `X_i` to the endpoint.
2. Refactoring `d_i=h g` inserts a canonical intermediate grade and an exact sequence whose two
   new grades reconstruct the old one.

The split cell is load-bearing. For `g f=id`, both `coker f` and `ker g` may be nonzero although
the total history is exact. They are paired by

\[
 [y]\longmapsto y-f(g y).
 \tag{LHSM.4}
\]

This kills the idea that local defect counts, signs, or energies can simply accumulate.

ULF-LHSM-2 now constructs the general local differential behind this special cell. For composable
linear laws `X -f-> Y -g-> Z`,

\[
 \partial_{f,g}:\ker g\longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}f,
 \qquad y\longmapsto[y],
 \tag{LHSM.4a}
\]

and the canonical kernel--cokernel sequence is exact. Its kernel is the new composite kernel modulo
the old kernel; its cokernel is exactly the secondary-birth quotient
`Y/(im f + ker g)`. Hence the first page differential, rather than only the final filtration, is
now explicit. The composite is invertible exactly when `f` is injective, `g` is surjective, and
`partial_(f,g)` is an equivalence.

ULF-LHSM-3 closes the iteration law.  For an incoming law `a`, an interval transport `P`, and a
later law `q`, a later-kernel class in `ker q cap im P` has the canonical higher transgression

\[
 d_P:\ker q\cap\operatorname{im}P
 \longrightarrow
 Y/(\operatorname{im}a+\ker P),
 \qquad Py\longmapsto[y].
 \tag{LHSM.4b}
\]

Its kernel is the next kernel survivor and its cokernel is the next cokernel survivor.  Iterating
therefore gives a bounded two-row spectral machine whose stable page is the associated graded of
both the total kernel and total cokernel.  This is the first exact fusion of local transgression,
future-tail survival, and refactorization mechanics.

ULF-LHSM-4 supplies the fourth, topological organ.  For closed-range Hilbert laws,

\[
 \operatorname{ran}(gf)\text{ closed}
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \operatorname{ran}f+\ker g\text{ closed}
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \operatorname{ran}\partial_{f,g}\text{ closed}.
 \tag{LHSM.4c}
\]

Thus completion failure occurs at a definite transgression page.  A finite history is globally
formation-proper exactly when every prefix transgression is strict.  Final closedness alone is too
weak: a later zero law can hide a nonclosed earlier prefix.

ULF-LHSM-5 closes the universal formation/selection mechanism.  The Hilbert page machine splits
into orthogonal death layers and one stable sector.  The assembled page response has kernel exactly
the stable page and is globally proper exactly when all page gaps have a uniform positive lower
bound.  Any added response factoring through old page formation vanishes on the stable sector.  A
new organ unlocks selection exactly when it supplies a response bounded below there.  Its finite
diagonal Hodge normal form is Lean-certified; abstract countable assembly remains paper-proven.

## 5. Hostile conjecture loops

### Loop A -- sum all local obstructions

`[FALSIFIED]`. A split inclusion followed by its retraction has nonzero local cokernel and kernel
but identity composite.

**Survivor:** local defects must be joined by transgressions; global birth is their surviving
homology/filtration.

### Loop B -- total composite determines the law history

`[FALSIFIED]`. A one-step identity and a split inclusion--retraction have the same total map and
different local failure pages.

**Survivor:** total obstruction and factorization spectrum are complementary data.

### Loop C -- refinement adds an independent direct summand

`[FALSIFIED]`. The canonical statement is a short exact sequence. Even over a field, a splitting is
not supplied by the history and need not be natural.

**Survivor:** refactorization acts by exact refinement of grades.

### Loop D -- every filtration is already a spectral mechanism

`[FALSIFIED]`. A declared filtration has no source-normal stages, connecting maps, or admission
law. It can encode arbitrary target data.

**Survivor:** only filtrations generated functorially from an admitted history count.

### Loop E -- spectral-sequence machinery itself is the new field

`[REDISCOVERED]`. Filtered derived categories, exact couples, spectral sequences, octahedral
triangles, persistence, and Waldhausen/decomposition constructions are established mathematics.

**Survivor:** the new interpretation is a constitutive mechanics: stages are law-forming acts,
page differentials are cancellation/promotion laws, and source generation plus observer exactness
decide which spectral histories are legal.

challenge births, or observer/admission doctrine. No priority claim is made for the machinery.

## 7. Risky predictions

1. Any proposed route-independent scalar obtained by adding local defects will fail on an exact
   cancellation cell unless it factors through the survival spectrum.
2. Two histories with identical total maps can make different predictions under interventions
   that expose intermediate stages.
3. A pure postobserver can erase a born class or its transgression but cannot create a
   source-effective differential if the observer is exact on the history.
4. Every genuine finite-to-cofinal law birth must exhibit a nonstationary survival page, a derived
   completion term, or unbounded legal factorization depth.
5. Hypertranscendence, recurrence, positivity, and topology are not definitions of one another;
   each may alter different pages only after a literal constitutive history is constructed.
