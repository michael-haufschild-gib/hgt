## 1. Pivot

The corpus should not be modeled as many routes seeking one missing map.  Its recurring event is
more primitive:

> Forming one law changes which distinctions later laws are allowed to erase or required to retain.

Every law event has two possible effects:

1. **identification pressure:** some states must become equivalent for the law to execute;
2. **distinction pressure:** some states must remain separated for a reporter, intervention, or
   continuation law to remain faithful.

The new mathematics sought here is the calculus of how these pressures are generated, composed,
transported, and exactified.  Equivalence relations, conflicts, positive overlap, topology,
prolongation, and history are gauges of this mechanism, not independent fields stored in one
state object.

## 2. Exact finite seed

ULF-LSM-5 is Lean-certified.  Given mandatory relation `R` and TV conflict relation `C`:

- identification formation saturates `R` to `EqvGen(R)`;
- a faithful observer exists exactly when `EqvGen(R)` contains no `C`-edge;
- every failure has a finite critical-path certificate;
- every critical path forces strict loss in a reporter fixed before formation;
- compatible residual observer width is quotient-conflict chromatic width;
- two identification doctrines can each be compatible with fidelity while their join is not.

This is the first exact critical triple with a nonmanufactured quantitative response.  It remains a
finite supplied-relation theorem.  It does not yet explain how source laws generate both polarities.

## 3. Proposed mechanism laws

### Law A — endogenous polarity

A primitive law event must emit identification and distinction consequences together.  Neither leg
may be fitted after inspecting a target obstruction.  Formally, seek a source operation `a` whose
formation semantics produces

\[
a\longmapsto (I_a,D_a),
\tag{1}
\]

with a proof that both legs are natural under source morphisms.  A theory supplying arbitrary
independent `I_a` and `D_a` slots fails this gate.

**Finite operation-generated sector closed.**  ULF-LSM-6 takes source operations as the only
primitive.  Unitizing an operation generates its identification relation; two competing schedules
generate the canonical distinction `delta_(fgx)` versus `delta_(gfx)`.  Joint unitization collapses
the whole formation-word orbit and exactly changes this reporter's TV from one to zero whenever
the endpoints differ.  Two idempotent retractions on three states give the sharp pairwise-compatible
but jointly impossible critical triple.  The remaining Law-A problem is source generation in
analytic, ordered, or topological admission doctrines, not another supplied finite relation.

### Law B — opposite saturation

Identification consequences compose covariantly by generated join:

\[
I_{ab}=\operatorname{EqvGen}(I_a\cup I_b\cup I_{a,b}^{\mathrm{new}}).
\tag{2}
\]

Distinction consequences compose by retained tests plus any newly born mixed conflicts:

\[
D_{ab}=D_a\cup D_b\cup D_{a,b}^{\mathrm{new}}.
\tag{3}

The mixed terms are the possible new science.  If they are always zero or freely specified, the
programme collapses to classical union--find plus coloring.

### Law C — constitutive interference

Executability is an orthogonality law:

\[
I\perp D
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
I\cap D=\varnothing.
\tag{4}

Failure is not an extra object.  It is a critical circuit: an identification derivation whose
endpoints are required distinct.  Minimal circuits, their composition, and their transport are the
primary invariants.

### Law D — reporter balance

Every declared distinction pressure must be witnessed by a reporter fixed before the conflicting
formation.  When a critical circuit closes, reporter change must obey an exact balance identity,
not merely a sign.  In the finite TV gauge this is the positive-overlap identity and its serial law.
Other gauges must produce their own forced reporters from the same source event.

### Law E — history production

For a directed formation history `a_1,...,a_n`, let `I_k` be the generated identifications after
stage `k`.  The first `k` at which `I_k` meets the retained distinction relation is a **law-birth
time**.  A valid theory must prove:

1. stagewise reporter losses are nonnegative;
2. they add to endpoint loss;
3. order changes the birth profile precisely when formation exchange fails;
4. endpoint-equivalent schedules may retain distinct critical-path histories.

### Law F — exactification trilemma

Once a critical circuit exists, only three typed resolutions are allowed:

1. retract an identification law;
2. relax/transport the distinction reporter and pay quantified loss;
3. enlarge the history/source presentation so the apparently identified states lift apart.

Calling all three “gauge choices” erases the mechanism.  The source category must say which
resolution is legal and what it costs.

### Law G — compactness/admission separation

For finitary relations and a fixed finite observer width, de Bruijn--Erdos compactness says that
finite compatibility already gives a global set-theoretic observer after quotient.  Therefore a
corpus obstruction surviving every finite subsystem cannot be blamed on bare coloring.  It must be
located in admission: continuity, closed range, positivity, analytic continuation, uniform cost,
or coherence of the global lift.

This law prevents endless rediscovery of a “global combinatorial obstruction” that compactness
forbids.

## 4. Field-level research questions

A mathematician looking for the higher theory should ask these questions before touching another
endpoint.

1. Which source operations intrinsically emit both identification and distinction consequences?
2. What are the universal composition laws for their mixed terms `I_new,D_new`?
3. Which critical circuits are irreducible, and do they admit deletion--contraction, exchange, or
   factorization laws?
4. Is there a completeness theorem reconstructing formation semantics from all critical circuits
   and reporter responses?
5. When do pairwise-compatible formations obey a Helly law, and what extra structure is exactly
   needed when they do not?
6. Which reporter balance laws are invariant under change of observer gauge?
7. How do finite critical paths converge to nonclosed, nonproper, or infinite-cost global histories?
8. What replaces graph chromatic width for analytic, topological, ordered, and differential
   observers?
9. Can hypertranscendence be characterized as unbounded endogenous distinction birth under
   prolongation, rather than as generic noncompactness?
10. Can recurrence be characterized as repeated closure in an observer quotient while critical
    history remains unbounded in the lifted formation word?
11. Can positive scalarization be classified by exactly which distinction relations it forgets,
    instead of the false slogan that positivity always quotients phase?
12. Is there one source-generated critical circuit whose reporter has faithful realizations in
    three mathematically nonisomorphic doctrine gauges?

Questions 9--12 are conjectural reinterpretations, not field laws.  Each must be killed if a small
countermodel separates the proposed notions.

## 6. Falsifiers

The programme fails as a higher field if any of the following persists after a serious search:

1. every proposed bipolar source map is just two independent user-supplied relations;
2. all mixed terms vanish, reducing composition to classical congruence join and graph union;
3. reporters are chosen after critical circuits are known;
4. no theorem transfers between two nonisomorphic doctrine gauges;
5. all global examples reduce to set-theoretic compactness with no named admission failure;
6. the framework classifies old no-gos but predicts no withheld consequence.

Targets 2 and 3 are closed at their stated mechanism cores in paper and Lean.  The asymptotic
reporter of Target 3 is generated by completion of formation history rather than being the finite
schedule reporter of Target 2.  A single-source transfer through a second nonisomorphic admission doctrine therefore
remains load-bearing before any universal-field claim.  Until then, **Bipolar Law Formation** is a
serious mechanism programme, not the claimed universal field.
