rounds and can stabilize only at a limit ordinal. This is true but not a new mechanism:

- semigroups generated by closure operators are classical;
- Hecke--Kiselman semigroups encode noncommuting idempotent updates;
- alternation of closure operators already generates strict language hierarchies;
- abstract interpretation computes least common fixed points by closure iteration.

Consequently the statement

> noncommuting closures generate long history

cannot found the field by itself.

Require every closure to be **derived from operation descent through an observer quotient**. For a
source operation family \(F\) and observer kernel \(E\), the closure is not chosen arbitrarily:

\[
 E\longmapsto \operatorname{Cg}_F(E),
\]

the least equivalence through which all operations in \(F\) descend. This supplies three extra
claims absent from bare closure iteration:

1. a universal minimality property: every executable repair factors through the closure;
2. a typed sacrifice theorem: retaining the observer instead forces removal of all operations not
   in \(\operatorname{Pol}(E)\);
3. a measurable loss: newly merged source pairs are exact predictions of law admission.

finite/cofinal saturation horizon.
