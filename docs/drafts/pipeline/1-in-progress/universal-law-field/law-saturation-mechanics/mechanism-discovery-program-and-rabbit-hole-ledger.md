## 1. The change of question

Decades of individually strong theorems ending at different walls suggest that missing mathematics
is not another invariant attached to finished route objects. The higher question is:

> By which laws does imposing one mathematical doctrine change which other laws remain legal,
> observable, composable, and finitely realizable?

The state being evolved is the **rulebook**: admissible operations, observable distinctions, legal
interventions, and allowed completions. A formation changes future formation possibilities. This is
second-order mathematical dynamics.

## 2. What a top mathematician would do

### Step A — classify walls, not proofs

Erase route names. Record each stopping event only by transformation type:

- loss of descent;
- failed exchange;
- non-effective inverse limit;
- nonclosed admission domain;
- extension/carry invisible at endpoints;
- positive scalarization losing interventions;
- recurrent quotient with nonrecurrent lift;
- finite reporter stabilization with unbounded production depth.

Then determine which walls are equivalent under change of presentation and which remain logically
independent. The independent-slot theorem is the first anti-theatre test.

### Step B — search for the smallest countermodel preserving everything believed relevant

Do not generalize successful examples first. Construct two tiny systems agreeing on every proposed
invariant but differing in the claimed outcome. The missing datum is whatever distinguishes the two.
The corpus already supplies this pattern through passive/interventional separation,
split/nonsplit carry, and serial scalar-radius failure.

### Step C — formulate a mechanism theorem with a converse

A useful law has three parts:

1. a positive construction;
2. a rigidity or classification converse;
3. a countermodel at every omitted hypothesis.

Examples now closed are executable-descent iff kernel preservation, commute iff both two-step
schedules equal joint repair, and nontrivial formation iff some separating reporter responds.

### Step D — leave RH completely

Test the mechanism in laboratories where the source and answer are known:

- finite contextuality covers;
- rewriting systems and weak distributive laws;
- algebraic effects and handlers;
- differential-algebraic realization spaces;
- persistence/effectivity towers;
- group extensions and carry;
- positive/GNS quotients with intervention tomography.

A mechanism surviving only because RH is unresolved is empty.

### Step E — demand a withheld prediction

Construct formations from source data, calculate their exchange, and predict a third observable
before inspecting its target theory. Retrospective relabelling does not count.

### Step F — formalize immediately

Every significant survivor becomes a closed Lean theorem before it is used as a field law. Lean is
not cleanup; it is pressure against smuggled assumptions, type conflation, and metaphorical
composition.

## 3. Candidate laws of Law-Formation Mechanics

### Law 0 — noninteraction means nonprediction

Independently relabelled doctrine slots admit no nonconstant invariant coupling. Every prediction
must come from a source-generated interaction law.

### Law 1 — formation

A doctrine acts by a universal law-forming process: it changes the rulebook so the doctrine becomes
executable. Idempotent closures are only one sector.

### Law 2 — exchange

Order is irrelevant exactly when formations admit coherent exchange. Strict commuting closures are
Lean-proved to have no two- or three-way schedule defect. Higher content must therefore use weak,
noninvertible, partial, or extension-valued exchange.

### Law 3 — response and conservation

Primitive-natural reporters are conserved along finite formation histories. Every nonidentity
formation responds in a separating coframe. Reporter change at saturation certifies either broken
naturality or a genuinely infinite horizon.

### Law 4 — critical-pair transport

When exchange fails, the residue must be transported through later formations with a typed
composition law. It may be a quotient, carry, domain loss, or relative class; it is not called
curvature by default.

### Law 5 — saturation and effectivity

Repeated formations seek a common executable rulebook. Finite reporters may stabilize while
formation history remains open. Coherent locally finite towers do have ends, so a missing global law
must use nonproperness, noncoherence, admission, or a doctrine not preserved by limits.

### Law 6 — directed work

Cost measures formation history, not reversible endpoint equivalence. Nonnegative additive cost on
a groupoid vanishes; irreversible work requires a directed or subadditive mechanism.

### Law 7 — intervention completeness

Passive state equality does not identify a law-forming mechanism. A complete experiment includes
legal interventions and response composition. Any claimed observer equivalence must be tested
counterfactually.

## 4. Weird but serious rabbit holes

### R1. Mathematical doctrines as algebraic effects

Primitive source laws are effects; proof methods are handlers; a stuck proof is an unhandled or
non-distributive effect combination. Formation exchange becomes handler composition.

**Falsifier:** effect signatures and handlers can be chosen arbitrarily after seeing endpoints, or
the calculus predicts no response beyond existing distributive-law theory.

### R2. Self-modifying Pol--Inv dynamics

Operations and observable relations coevolve through preservation. Adding an operation changes
legal observers; changing observers changes executable operations. The dynamical variable is the
Galois-closed rulebook, not either side alone.

**Falsifier:** all corpus doctrines embed only as independent fixed coordinates rather than
nontrivial updates of the same legality relation.

### R3. Noncommutative thermodynamics of doctrine formation

Distinction loss is entropy, minimal formation-word cost is work, conserved reporter value is
charge, and failed exchange is order-dependent dissipation. This is useful only if exact variational
or fluctuation laws emerge.

**Falsifier:** every proposed energy is inserted from a route metric or can be changed without
altering formation laws.

### R4. Sheaf of experiments with non-effective global sections

Finite observers form a site; legal experiment transcripts are local sections; global laws require
effectivity, not merely compatible gluing. Hypertranscendence may appear as failure of finite
experimental generation rather than geometric noncompactness.

**Falsifier:** the topology/site is chosen to encode the desired DA/HT split, or locally finite
proper towers still lack sections contrary to the certified König boundary.

### R5. Developmental rather than geometric mathematics

Law formations resemble a developmental programme: early operations alter which later operations
can exist. Equivalent endpoints can retain different lineage through carry or extension data.

**Falsifier:** lineage never predicts an intervention response once endpoint and extension class are
fixed.

### R6. Game semantics of proof discovery

Observer chooses finite experiments; source answers with a finite mimic; cost measures minimum
support needed to survive the experiment. Global effectivity is winning against all adaptive
strategies, not possession of one static object.

**Falsifier:** adaptive and nonadaptive experiments always have identical exact complexity after
source constraints are imposed.

### R7. Singular exchange at limit doctrines

Two formations may exchange on every finite stage but fail after completion because exchange is not
continuous, strict, or admitted. The residue is a limit-exchange defect.

**Falsifier:** source-natural exchange extends continuously to every admitted completion, forcing
the proposed defect to vanish.

## 5. Killer questions

1. What operation changes the future space of legal operations?
2. Which doctrine pairs commute strictly, weakly, or not at all?
3. What is the minimal critical pair, and what later processes transport it?
4. Which reporters are fixed before formation and remain natural through every finite step?
5. Which limit changes reporter value, and exactly which continuity/admission law fails there?
6. What is the classification of systems with identical passive states but distinct intervention
   semantics?
7. Which finite/global gaps are forbidden by compactness or König-type theorems?
8. What source-derived cost is forced by formation composition rather than selected afterward?
9. Can three formations produce a residue that every pair misses?
10. Can the mechanism predict a theorem in a domain not used to construct it?
11. What is the smallest countermodel killing the entire proposed field?

## 6. Current hard gate

Generic weak distributive laws, higher rewriting, critical pairs, and effect handlers are occupied
machinery. Current work includes weak distributing retractions, monotone weak laws for probability
and nondeterminism, substructural distributive laws, and higher rewriting modulo. The field is not
the claim that these subjects exist.

The live gate is source generation: one pre-target signature must force three formations, a
nonidentity exchange or critical triple, a conserved reporter law, and a withheld consequence. If
that cannot be done, Law-Formation Mechanics remains a disciplined research methodology rather than
a new mathematical field.
