## 1. Law zero: independent coexistence predicts nothing

The Lean-certified independent-slot theorem proves that if the proposed organs may be relabelled
independently and transitively, every invariant phase and every invariant observable is trivial.
Consequently a list of state fields cannot be the new theory. Adding relation, positivity,
topology, recurrence, cost, and differential rank as independent coordinates is formally
nonpredictive.

The new field must begin with **interactions among law-forming processes**. Its primitive questions
are:

1. what distinctions does one formation erase or create;
2. which interventions remain legal after formation;
3. whether two formations exchange;
4. which repair is forced when they do not exchange;
5. which charge survives or migrates through repair;
6. whether repeated repair terminates, recurs after observation, or crosses an effectivity wall.

No hidden universal object is postulated.

## 2. Mechanism laws already forced by certified theorems

### M1. Observer--operation executability

An operation descends through an observer exactly when it preserves the observer kernel. The least
repair retaining a family of operations is its generated congruence. This is certified by
`ConstitutiveUnaryLawRepair`.

**Forced prediction.** Every proposed quotient retaining specified operations must identify at
least the canonical generated congruence. A smaller claimed loss is impossible.

### M2. Formation exchange and joint saturation

Law formations are extensive, monotone, idempotent transformations on distinctions. Their unique
least common repair exists. Every finite schedule lies below it. Two formations commute exactly
when both two-step orders equal the least common repair. Failed exchange is therefore genuine
ordered history, while commuting formations carry no order memory.

The dependency-closed theorem is in
`formation-exchange-joint-repair-and-ordered-history-law.md`; its full mechanism is Lean-certified.
The alternating unary theorem supplies sharp infinite-history behaviour: finite reporters stabilize
although no finite repair word reaches the common fixed point.

### M3. Endpoint data do not determine formation history

The split/nonsplit extension theorem gives identical kernel, cokernel, and size with different carry
and lifted recurrence. Thus M1--M2 cannot be reduced to endpoint equivalence relations. Exchange
data must retain extension or critical-pair memory when present.

**Forced prediction.** A scalar defect, rank, or endpoint pair cannot classify a law event unless a
separate theorem proves the relevant extension class vanishes.

### M4. Passive observation does not determine intervention law

The intervention-separation theorem gives two systems with identical complete passive laws and
opposite responses to marked interventions. The positive-response theorem further proves that the
positive quotient (z\mapsto |z|^2) does not support nonzero fixed additive interventions, while a
two-response coframe reconstructs the erased phase.

**Forced prediction.** A doctrine is not identified by its state space or passive measure. It must
specify legal interventions and their exchange with observation.

### M5. Directed cost is incompatible with reversible history

Every nonnegative additive cost on a groupoid vanishes; a directed monoid admits nonzero additive
cost. Adaptive mimicry then gives exact minimal directed support and coercive infinite-action
boundaries.

**Forced prediction.** Recurrence and action cannot be one additive groupoid invariant. Reversible
phase memory and irreversible production cost are distinct sides of one directed history law.

### M6. Finite observation and global effectivity separate

Adaptive procompletion, prefix effectivity, and finite-jet realization prove that all finite
experiments may stabilize or be exactly mimicked while a global doctrine remains non-effective or
topologically inseparable. The locally finite realization theorem supplies the converse boundary:
a coherent locally finite tower of nonempty realization fibres has a global section.

**Forced prediction.** A finite/global obstruction must be located in noncoherence, nonproperness,
admission, or a global doctrine not preserved by the inverse limit. “More finite stages” alone
cannot explain it.

### M7. Positivity is compatible with global obstruction

The connected parity-context theorem constructs compatible positive local states on every context
while a one-bit total charge forbids any global classical law, and every proper face remains
satisfiable.

**Forced prediction.** Positivity is not a universal phase quotient and does not classicalize.
Only a specified positive scalarization erases specified orbit data. Global obstruction may survive
all positive local marginals.

### M8. Serial loss is relational, not scalar

The serial reconstruction theorem disproves monotonicity of a plausible reconstruction radius under
coarsening. The exact survivor is the hidden-pair relation and its metric lower bound.

**Forced prediction.** Later observers cannot repair earlier loss. Any serial information law must
transport the kernel pair or richer relation before scalarization.

### M9. Exact erasure can force infinite carriers

The surjective-erasure theorem proves that a same-carrier surjective linear law killing a nonzero
class cannot occur in finite dimension. Gamma-response lifetime gives a concrete persistence
filtration realizing this mechanism.

**Forced prediction.** A finite exactification must either cease to be surjective, change carrier,
or change admission/topology at a genuine class-death event.

## 3. What these laws do not yet explain

The certified laws are not yet one universal mechanism. Four separations remain hard:

1. no single source-generated formation currently produces positive completion, differential
   prolongation, and topological completion together;
2. no source-generated three-way exchange has been proved, so higher critical-pair coherence is
   still a programme;
3. reporter/charge survival is proved in several linear charts but no schedule-independent general
   conservation theorem yet couples all formation types;
4. hypertranscendence is certified as finite-observer nonseparation, not yet as an intrinsic
   formation-horizon law.

Therefore it is false, at present, that hypertranscendence universally causes noncompactness, that
positivity universally quotients phase, or that recurrence universally means open lift. Each occurs
in exact coupled examples, but none is yet forced by the general mechanism.

## 4. Acceptance gate for the field

Law-Saturation Mechanics becomes more than a rigorous research doctrine only after one theorem
passes all six conditions:

1. **one source signature:** primitive generators are fixed before any target doctrine;
2. **three formations:** the source canonically produces three structurally distinct law-forming
   processes;
3. **nonidentity exchange:** at least one exchange or critical-pair map is computed, not declared;
4. **withheld consequence:** the exchange calculation predicts a response, obstruction, or forced
   repair not used to define the formations;
5. **independent validation:** the prediction is realized in a second corpus organ without fitted
   parameters;
6. **Lean closure:** the significant theorem and its sharp no-go boundary are formally certified.

Until then the correct status is a mechanism-first Stage-1 field programme, not a discovered
universal geometry.
