## 1. Founding question

Do not ask which object lies behind all routes. Ask:

> Which laws generate admissible operations, forced identifications, observer loss, ordered
> history, and finite-to-cofinal failure from one feedback mechanism?

The proposed answer is **law saturation**. A source supplies primitive operations and primitive
distinctions. An observer declares some states equivalent. Every operation that is to remain legal
after observation must preserve that equivalence. When it does not, the system must either remove
the operation or erase further distinctions. Repeating this repair under several law families
produces a history of closures. Its common fixed points are executable mathematical worlds.

This is a Frankenstein synthesis of universal algebra, Pol--Inv Galois theory, closure semigroups,
abstract interpretation, dynamic logic, Lawvere metrics, and pro-completion. The programme does not
claim those ingredients as new. Its proposed new unit of explanation is the **feedback law** between
operations and distinctions.

## 2. Six generative laws

### Law I — operation--distinction polarity

For operations \(F\) and relations \(R\), preservation gives an antitone correspondence

\[
  F\longmapsto \operatorname{Inv}(F),
  \qquad
  R\longmapsto \operatorname{Pol}(R).
\]

Adding legal operations can only reduce invariant distinctions. Adding mandatory distinctions can
only reduce legal operations. Closed operation doctrines and closed relation doctrines are the two
fixed-point faces of the same feedback law.

### Law II — executable descent

An observer quotient \(q:A\to B\) supports a source operation \(f\) exactly when
\(\ker q\) is preserved by \(f\). Equivalently, there is a unique descended operation
\(\bar f\) satisfying

\[
  qf=\bar f q.
\]

Thus observability is not passive. It decides which source laws remain executable.

### Law III — minimal repair

If \(F\not\subseteq\operatorname{Pol}(\ker q)\), no reformulation preserving both all source
operations and all observer distinctions exists. There are exactly two order-theoretic repairs:

1. **operation sacrifice:** replace \(F\) by a subdoctrine of
   \(\operatorname{Pol}(\ker q)\);
2. **distinction sacrifice:** replace \(\ker q\) by the least \(F\)-congruence
   \(\operatorname{Cg}_F(\ker q)\).

The second is universal: every quotient retaining \(F\) and identifying the original kernel factors
through \(A/\operatorname{Cg}_F(\ker q)\). This computes the forced loss instead of naming it.

### Law IV — noncommuting saturation

Different law families give different closure operators. They need not commute. Their generated
closure semigroup records the order in which laws are admitted. The joint executable doctrine is
the least common fixed point above the source state, not in general the result of one pass.

Noncommutation is history without invoking curvature: two legal repair schedules can have the same
eventual fixed world while producing different intermediate distinctions and different costs.

### Law V — irreversible loss and no promotion

Once a distinction is erased by a quotient, later observers cannot reconstruct it without a marked
lift or a new intervention. Dually, saturation under a fixed operation clone propagates existing
laws but cannot manufacture a primitive operation outside that clone. Therefore a stuck route cannot
repair itself merely by iterating its existing closure machinery.

This is the mechanism-level meaning of the corpus's serial source-loss and fixed-transformation
no-promotion theorems.

### Law VI — finite/cofinal horizon

Every finite reporter family may stabilize while the global saturation history remains strictly
open. The invariant is closure depth or closure ordinal, not noncompactness. Compact observer spaces
can contain convergent images of histories whose source saturation never terminates at finite time.

This law separates three notions that earlier field proposals conflated:

- observer recurrence or convergence;
- boundedness/compactness of the observer state;
- termination of source-law generation.

## 3. Geometry generated by the laws

Geometry is derived, not postulated. If \(\mathcal C\) is a set of primitive repair closures acting
on a distinction lattice, define the directed law distance

\[
 d_{\mathcal C}(x,y)
 =\inf\{\lvert w\rvert:y\leq w(x),\ w\in\mathcal C^*\}.
\]

Concatenation gives the triangle inequality. The distance is generally asymmetric: laws erase
distinctions but do not recreate them. A compatible observer commuting with every primitive closure
is nonexpansive. Closure ordinals, stabilizers, minimal repair words, and common-fixed-point strata
are consequently geometric outputs of the mechanism.

This is not a claim that every route is a gauge. A route is a gauge only inside a common fixed-point
stratum where its comparison maps are invertible. Outside that stratum it is a lossy saturation
schedule.

## 5. First exact calibration

The companion paper card
`alternating-unary-law-saturation-horizon-and-finite-observer-stabilization-law.md` constructs two
unary source operations. Each induced repair is an idempotent closure. They do not commute, and from
one primitive identification their alternating joint repair:

- needs arbitrarily many rounds in finite truncations;
- reaches its least common fixed point only at stage \(\omega\) in the infinite system;
- makes every finite reporter eventually constant;
- has exact directed repair cost equal to propagation depth.

This proves that ordered law history, finite/cofinal separation, and directed geometry can be
generated by the operation--distinction mechanism alone. It does not yet establish whole-corpus
universality.

## 7. Anti-theatre gates

Reject Law-Saturation Mechanics as the universal field if any of these persists:

1. routes are mapped only by renaming their existing obstruction as a closure;
2. no source-native operation family computes a previously unknown forced repair;
3. positivity, recurrence, and hypertranscendence remain independent annotations rather than
   consequences of one closure history;
4. the only theorems are generic lattice facts already available from closure theory;
5. no comparison across at least three structurally different corpus organs predicts a new
   invariant or rules out a live proposal.
