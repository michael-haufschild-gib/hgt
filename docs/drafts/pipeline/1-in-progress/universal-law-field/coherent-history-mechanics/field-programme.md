## 1. Field question

The frontier does not lack another object.  It lacks a calculus explaining how one source law can
generate signed or complex histories, how different proof doctrines collapse those histories, and
where the information lost by that collapse must go.

**Coherent History Mechanics (CHM)** studies the following mechanism:

1. a source intervention changes generators or their weights;
2. legal generators compose into marked histories;
3. coherent execution multiplies along a history and adds across alternative histories;
4. the positive variation execution replaces every local weight by its modulus before adding;
5. an observer forgets intermediate histories and retains only the coherent endpoint response;
6. the difference between positive history execution and positive endpoint execution is retained as
   interference memory;
7. admission, coercivity, carrier existence, and recurrence are properties of the coupled coherent
   and variation executions, not of either execution alone.

This is a mechanism layer inside Constitutive Process Mathematics.  It is narrower than the CPM
charter and stronger than a route atlas: it asserts exact equations connecting interventions,
composition, positivity, phase, and history loss.

## 3. Primitive laws, not primitive object

CHM is specified by laws.

### CHM-I — source-marked generation

History decompositions must be generated before endpoint observation.  Arbitrary decompositions of
an already known scalar or operator are inadmissible: they can fit any answer and change variation
cost at will.

### CHM-II — coherent/variation double execution

For a history weight `a_h`, coherent execution uses `sum_h a_h`; variation execution uses
`sum_h |a_h|`.  Composition is exact on marked histories.  After forgetting intermediate history,
coherent execution remains exact while variation execution becomes lax.

### CHM-III — minimal-majorant law

The entrywise modulus of a finite coherent operator is its least positive operator majorant.  A
larger source-marked history envelope measures genuine retained provenance, not a different norm on
the same collapsed operator.

### CHM-IV — interference conservation

Positive history mass is neither destroyed nor mysteriously converted into cancellation.  It splits
exactly into visible coherent response plus phase-dispersion memory.  Under serial composition the
location of the memory can migrate, but its two parenthesizations satisfy an exact cocycle identity.

### CHM-V — polar reconstruction

Positive history weights and their rank-one phase Gram packet reconstruct the coherent history law
up to one global phase.  An anchor fixes that phase.  Positivity alone and endpoint coherence alone
are separately insufficient.

### CHM-VI — admission before selection

Finite variation, tightness, uniform coercivity, or a bounded positive carrier is extra structure.
It is not inherited from exact coherent execution.  Selection theorems may use variation data only
after source admission has been proved.

### CHM-VII — recurrent shadow/open envelope

A coherent evolution can recur exactly while its canonical positive history envelope grows
exponentially.  Recurrence is therefore a quotient phenomenon; open history can be forced by the
same generated process without choosing an arbitrary universal cover.

## 5. Three failed stronger conjectures

### Failure A — every route is a regular linear operator

False.  The frontier contains nonlinear prolongation, adaptive null flags, partial relations,
completion changes, and higher-arity source operations.  Finite regular operators are a chart, not
the field.

**Survivor:** every finite generated history chart has a coherent/variation double execution, and
free hypergraph or rewriting histories supply the higher-arity successor.

### Failure B — the endpoint modulus is the complete positive history

False.  Two-stage cancellation can make `|BA|` strictly smaller than `|B||A|`.  The endpoint
modulus is the least majorant after forgetting the intermediate state; it is not the source history
envelope.

**Survivor:** the nonnegative defect `|B||A|-|BA|` is exactly the forgotten-intermediate-history
budget and obeys the associativity identity in the Stage-2 theorem.

### Failure C — positivity plus phase always selects the correct source law

False.  A source may admit many history presentations, and arbitrary splitting can increase the
variation envelope without changing coherent response.

**Survivor:** source marking is load-bearing.  CHM predicts consequences only after a generation
functor from the literal source has been constructed.
