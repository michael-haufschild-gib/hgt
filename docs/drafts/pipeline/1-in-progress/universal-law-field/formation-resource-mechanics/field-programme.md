## 1. Pivot from objects to a production law

The relevant primitive is not another carrier, curvature, quotient, or route endpoint.  It is a
production judgement

\[
  \mathcal E\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal F^{\otimes d},
  \tag{FRM.1}
\]

where `E` is one marked source experiment, `F` is one target experiment, and the arrow is a single
parameter-independent Markov formation.  The integer `d` is independent packet depth.  A legal
formation must work for every marked source law at once; allowing the kernel to know the hidden law
makes production vacuous.

The field asks for laws of:

1. **capital:** how much distinguishability the source owns;
2. **formation:** how independent depth consumes it;
3. **recovery:** when a macropacket stores the same capital exactly;
4. **symmetry:** how functional-equation actions transport capital;
5. **properness:** why fixed capital cannot finance unbounded nonneutral depth;
6. **species:** whether formed capital stays in the same analytic family or exits to a larger one.

These are mechanisms.  They do not presume any RH route object.

## 2. Frozen conjecture and exact failure

### Conjecture A — infinite divisibility is the missing producer

> A packet can generate arbitrarily many independent descendants without information loss exactly
> when its law is infinitely divisible.

**Verdict: false.**  Infinite divisibility is load-bearing only when the macropacket and all pieces
must remain in one convolution species under addition.  Generalized data thinning starts with a
sufficient statistic of an independent sample and reconstructs the sample by a conditional kernel
independent of the parameter.  The sufficient statistic can have an unnamed distribution and need
not lie in a convolution-closed one-particle family.

The corpus supplies a literal counterexample.  The normalized theta cusp likelihood `P_B` is an
exponential tilt and is formally proved not infinitely divisible by its square-gap atom support.
Nevertheless, the sum of `d` independent `P_B` packets is sufficient for `B`; conditioning the
product on its sum reconstructs all `d` packets through a `B`-independent kernel.  Nondivisibility
says the sum law exits the one-particle theta species.  It does not erase formation.

### Positive survivor

The invariant is not divisibility but **source capital**.  If one fixed binary experiment
`E=(r_0,r_1)` forms `d` independent copies of `F=(p_0,p_1)`, every additive data-processing
information satisfies

\[
  \mathcal C(E)\ \ge d\,\mathcal C(F).
  \tag{FRM.2}
\]

Hellinger affinity gives a theorem without absolute-continuity assumptions.  The full classical
Rényi family gives an additive **formation-capital spectrum**; KL, Jeffreys, and Fisher information
give further limiting/local faces when defined.  A sufficient macropacket realizes equality in
every face because statistic and common recovery make it Blackwell-equivalent to the independent
sample.

The dependency-closed theorem is in
`fixed-source-formation-capital-sufficient-macro-saturation-and-theta-species-exit-law.md`.

A second dependency-closed theorem operationalizes source-coherent quotient--fusion genesis as a
canonical Gaussian experiment. On that experiment chain the capital bound is complete: source
signal budget `b` forms `d` genesis packets of squared norm `Q` exactly when `b>=dQ`. Cofinal
formation therefore forces synchronization, and a trivial synchronization equalizer selects
neutral displacement. See
`gaussian-quotient-fusion-capital-exact-depth-and-testing-properness-law.md`.

## 3. Proposed field laws

### FRM-I — fixed-source capital law

No Markov formation increases any data-processing distinguishability.  Independent tensor depth is
additive for the Rényi capital spectrum, relative entropy, Jeffreys work, and Fisher information.
Hence one fixed overlapping source has finite nonneutral formation depth.

### FRM-II — sufficient saturation law

A macropacket is an exact capital store when a deterministic statistic maps independent descendants
to the macro and one common recovery kernel reconstructs the descendants.  Every common-recovery
information grade is then conserved exactly.

### FRM-III — functional-equation neutrality law

A reversible functional-equation action is an automorphism of the experiment preorder.  Its
coordinatewise tensor action and its induced macro action commute with sufficient formation.  It
permutes capital but does not create, destroy, or select it.

### FRM-IV — species-exit law

Failure of infinite divisibility means exact macro formation cannot stay inside the demanded
one-particle convolution species.  It does not mean capital disappeared.  The new atoms, sectors,
or history coordinates of the macro law record formed depth.

### FRM-V — cofinal formation properness

If a fixed source with overlapping marked laws forms arbitrarily large independent target depth,
the target experiment is neutral.  The only alternative is a singular source that already reveals
the hidden mark perfectly.

### FRM-VI — operational genesis law

Whenever source coherence supplies a Hilbert response packet, quotient--fusion genesis is the
independent Gaussian signal discarded by local quotienting. Its squared norm simultaneously is
Jeffreys work, eight times Bhattacharyya capital, and the Gaussian testing exponent. A fixed finite
source budget therefore admits an exact maximum coherent depth.

### FRM-VII — intensive capital-density law

For a growing source array, total information is not the selection variable. Exact formation of
`d_n` independent descendants forces target capital below source capital divided by `d_n`.
Vanishing density selects a neutral target; Gaussian quotient--fusion realizes the bound exactly.
This is the varying-source global properness law.

## 4. Conjecture/proof/failure ledger

### Loop B — fresh Markov randomness defeats the capital bound

**Conjecture.**  Since a Markov kernel may use fresh random coins, it can always prepare independent
targets.

**Failure.**  Fresh coins do not identify which of two overlapping source laws was used.  Hellinger
data processing gives `A(source) <= A(target)^d`; the right side tends to zero for a nonneutral
target, contradicting fixed positive source overlap.

**Survivor.**  External randomness is free; hidden-law distinguishability is the resource.

### Loop C — one affinity scalar classifies finite formation

**Conjecture.**  The Hellinger capital inequality is sufficient for a finite-depth formation.

**Failure.**  A source likelihood ratio confined to `[1/9,9]` cannot produce a target atom of
likelihood ratio `10`, although target Hellinger affinity can exceed source affinity.  Exact finite
convertibility requires the full Blackwell/likelihood-ratio order, not one scalar.

**Survivor.**  One affinity already proves cofinal impossibility.  The complete field coordinate is a
capital spectrum of Markov monotones; no single determinant or energy can replace it.

### Loop D — common recovery creates extra capital

**Conjecture.**  Adding a latent history through a common kernel can finance independent formation.

**Failure.**  Retaining the base coordinate makes split recovery an exact Hellinger and KL isometry.

**Survivor.**  Recovery relocates capital between macro and micro descriptions but cannot create it.

### Loop E — functional equation supplies the missing budget

**Conjecture.**  Reflection doubles a packet and therefore finances two independent sheets.

**Failure.**  Reflection is an invertible relabelling.  Affinity, KL, Jeffreys work, tensor depth,
and the clone fracture are invariant.

**Survivor.**  Functional equation is a capital-neutral action; any selection theorem needs a
source-produced formation/properness premise in addition to symmetry.

### Loop F — hypertranscendence is infinite information capital

**Conjecture.**  Differential hypertranscendence itself supplies arbitrary independent packet depth.

**Failure boundary.**  Differential independence gives unbounded response rank, not a Markov
formation kernel and not independent copies.  No source theorem currently converts jet rank into
experiment capital.

**Live successor.**  Construct a source-normal comparison from a hypertranscendental prolongation
tower to a sufficient macropacket tower and test whether its capital grows with depth.  Without that
map, the slogan is ill-typed.

### Loop G — positive quotient--fusion genesis already selects neutrality

**Conjecture.** Since genesis is nonnegative and vanishes exactly at synchronized local gauges,
positivity alone is a selection law.

**Failure.** Every `Q>0` Gaussian genesis packet is formable at each finite depth
`d<=floor(b/Q)` from a sufficiently informative finite source `G_b`. Nothing contradicts a
nonzero finite packet.

**Survivor.** Exact producer is cofinal formation: one fixed finite source cannot form unbounded
independent genesis depth unless `Q=0`. Selection is properness of a production map, not positivity
of one target value.

### Loop H — unbounded total source information prevents selection

**Conjecture.** If source capital diverges with aperture, no fixed-budget properness theorem can
apply and information theory loses selection leverage.

**Failure.** Formation spends capital per independent copy. Even `C(E_n)->infinity` selects
neutrality when `C(E_n)/d_n->0` and exact depth `d_n` is formed.

**Survivor.** Capital density, not total capital, is intensive selection coordinate. See
`cofinal-capital-density-selection-and-gaussian-rate-region-law.md`.
