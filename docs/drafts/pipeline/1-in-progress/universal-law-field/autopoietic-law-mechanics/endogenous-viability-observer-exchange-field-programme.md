## 1. Exact pivot forced by the enlarged Lean frontier

The corpus no longer mainly lacks mathematical objects. It already has exact carriers, quotient
complexes, moment forms, spectral determinants, compact realizations, source landings, finite
relations, multiplicity transport, recurrence models, and many RH-equivalent receivers. The five
first-open-premise classes are instead failures of promotion:

1. singular source descent;
2. all-prime oscillatory transport;
3. adaptive spectral orientation;
4. whole-space coercivity;
5. positive carrier existence.

In every class, local or finite executions succeed while one source-coherent global execution
remains unavailable. The field question is therefore not “which universal object do the routes
share?” It is:

> Which laws govern promotion of finite winning executions to one coherent infinite source
> strategy when execution itself generates the next legal challenge?

The candidate field is **Endogenous Viability Mechanics** (EVM), a mechanism layer inside
Autopoietic Law Mechanics. Its primitive verbs are

```text
admit -> execute -> generate challenge -> repair -> change legality -> prolong -> observe.
```

A route is a local gauge only after its source construction realizes these verbs. No route object,
RH endpoint, divisor, or target positivity is part of the field definition.

## 3. Proposed mechanism laws

### Law I -- challenge endogeneity

For current law state `x`, legal repair/control `u`, and current execution reporter, the next
challenge set must be generated by partial execution in a marked source context. Arbitrary
state-indexed challenge oracles are excluded.

The current Lean theorem `ConstitutiveContextualChallengeTransduction` is the one-round
calibration. EVM requires the law state itself to include the rulebook, so a repair changes which
challenges are legal at the next step.

### Law II -- predecessor formation

For candidate future law set `A`, define

\[
 \Phi(A)=\{x\in S:\exists u\in U(x)\;\forall c\in C(x,u),\;
                       T(x,u,c)\in A\}.
\tag{EVM.1}
\]

`Phi(A)` is not a receiver. It asks whether one currently legal repair keeps every challenge
generated by that repair inside the next viable law set. The lawbook is a state coordinate, so
`T` may update rules, domains, reporters, and future challenge grammar.

### Law III -- finite-horizon law filtration

Set `W_0=S` and `W_(n+1)=Phi(W_n)`. These are laws surviving `n` endogenous challenge rounds. A
single exact plateau is permanent. The global law is

\[
 W_\infty=\bigcap_n W_n.
\tag{EVM.2}
\]

When legal repairs are compact and admission is closed, predecessor formation commutes with this
decreasing intersection and `W_infinity` is the greatest viable fixed point.

### Law IV -- observer/cofinal exchange

For observer `q`, finite observed survival is `intersection_n q(W_n)`, whereas observation of
genuine global survival is `q(intersection_n W_n)`. Inclusion from the second set to the first is
automatic. Equality is a theorem only under compact source fibres and closed coherent horizons.
Their difference is **false observed immortality**: every finite observed challenge can be
survived, but not by one source-coherent law.

### Law V -- escape/coercivity duality

If false observed immortality occurs while horizons are closed and nested, source witnesses escape
every compact subset of the observer fibre. Every predeclared proper execution cost diverges along
the horizon. Nonattainment, noncompactness, and coercivity failure are three views of one mechanism.

### Law VI -- promotion trilemma

If every finite observed horizon succeeds but no global source law exists, at least one of these
load-bearing laws fails:

1. **closed admission:** limits of legal source strategies remain legal;
2. **proper source fibre:** bounded observed behavior and bounded source cost have compact lifts;
3. **coherent challenge generation:** all horizons iterate one monotone endogenous predecessor
   rather than horizon-fitted games.

The trilemma is exact. It does not identify its branches.

### Law VII -- hypertranscendence is nontermination of challenge rank

HT is not topological noncompactness. In a typed differential realization, HT means no finite jet
horizon exhausts algebraically independent source challenges. In EVM language it forbids a finite
plateau of the prolongation challenge filtration. Compact resolvent, recurrent observer orbit, and
unbounded challenge rank may coexist.

### Law VIII -- positivity and recurrence are observer laws

Positivity becomes a phase quotient only for a specified polar/Gram observer. Recurrence becomes
open lifted history only for a specified cover, extension, or accumulated cost. Neither slogan is
universal without reporter map and fibre data.
