## 1. Mechanism

Let `e_0,e_1,...` be law generators. A sequential repair does not merely adjoin `e_n`; it supplies
a certificate expressing its old semantic content. In the stationary scalar laboratory the
certificate is

\[
 e_n \sim \alpha e_{n-1}\qquad(n\ge1).
 \tag{CRGM.1}
\]

The certificate-bearing change of presentation is the causal unitriangular operator

\[
 G_\alpha=I-\alpha S^*,
 \tag{CRGM.2}
\]

where `S` is the unilateral forward shift. Finite truncations are always invertible. The completed
operator is not: its law type changes at `alpha=1`.

This distinguishes three pieces that earlier field candidates often collapsed:

1. **semantic conservation:** the new generator represents old content;
2. **genealogical formation:** the certificate history changes the presentation kernel;
3. **global properness:** completion decides whether that history is reconstructible, merely
   approximable, or genuinely invisible.

## 2. Proposed field laws

### Law I -- certified promotion creates lineage

Adjoining a semantically dependent generator with a chosen certificate preserves semantic range
and creates a distinguished syzygy. Forgetting the certificate retains equivalence but loses the
lineage needed to generate the next legal challenge.

### Law II -- finite conservative equivalence is not cofinal equivalence

Every finite unitriangular genealogy is an exact change of presentation. A cofinal genealogy is an
equivalence only when its certificate operator is a homeomorphism in the admitted completion.
Algebraic Tietze equivalence therefore does not imply topological law equivalence.

### Law III -- the critical phase is nonseparation, not carrier birth

For the stationary genealogy, the critical wall has injective dense nonclosed formation: no actual
hidden lineage exists, but arbitrarily accurate finite pseudo-lineages do. Above the wall a genuine
one-dimensional hidden lineage is born. These are different defects and require different repairs.

### Law IV -- compact repair has a sharp trichotomy

A compact local observer is unnecessary in the stable phase, cannot repair the critical
nonclosed-range phase, and repairs the supercritical phase exactly when it detects the born finite
kernel. This is the first coupling of conservative repair with the compact--cofinal properness
classification.

### Law V -- reciprocal formation carries a boundary port

Coefficient inversion does not identify the two genealogy phases by a common-kernel isometry. It
factors the supercritical genealogy through the adjoint subcritical genealogy and one coisometric
boundary map. The associated index jump is the information that a finite terminal projector sends
to infinity.

### Law VI -- source selection must act on genealogy, not only endpoints

An endpoint symmetry or packet information isometry transports a law already formed. It cannot
decide whether its repair genealogy is invertible, nonclosed, or has hidden kernel. Any final
selection mechanism must constrain the source certificate operator or its properness class.

## 5. Hostile boundary

The stationary scalar theorem is classical Toeplitz/weighted-shift mathematics. Higher rewriting
already treats coherent presentations, critical branchings, syzygies, and homotopical completion.
The proposed field content is the coupling of certificate genesis, admitted completion, and global
properness; no priority claim is made for the operator identities.

The field is not accepted until it survives all of the following attacks.

1. **Presentation attack.** Determine the largest class of bounded triangular changes of generators
   preserving the phase invariant. Arbitrary unbounded Tietze changes must not be called gauge.
2. **Nonstationary attack.** Classify `e_n ~ alpha_n e_(n-1)` without reducing everything to one
   scalar spectral radius.
3. **Higher-syzygy attack.** Prove how a born first syzygy generates later critical branchings;
   merely recording kernel dimension is insufficient.
4. **Positivity attack.** Separate positive feasible lineage from complex cancellation and from
   Hilbert-space kernel.
5. **Source attack.** Produce certificates from a pre-target arithmetic source rather than fitting
   `alpha_n` to a desired wall.
6. **Selection attack.** Show why the source selects one admissible genealogy phase. The present
   theorem proves that compact formation actually fails at the critical wall, so criticality alone
   cannot be the selection law.

## 7. Next conjecture

Let `C_n` be bounded certificate operators and form the block lower-bidiagonal genealogy

\[
 (Gx)_n=x_n-C_{n+1}x_{n+1}.
 \tag{CRGM.3}
\]

**CRGM-A (higher certificate-generation conjecture).** There is a source-normal branching
certificate complex in which the lineage packet born in degree `r` is the input generating the
degree `r+1` critical-branching differential. Its normalized semantic and lineage packet maps obey
a chain-level fusion identity whose degree-zero face is
`B_N Q_N=(N+1)I` and whose Euler characteristic records net repair work.

The conjecture will be rejected if the higher differential must be chosen independently of the
lower failure witnesses, if arbitrary presentations change its invariant, or if it reduces to a
standard resolution with no new formation/properness prediction.
