## 1. Higher pivot

The repeated question “which object is missing?” is too static. A source may not contain the final
repair as an already existing class. It may contain a law for manufacturing the next legal test
from the place where current execution failed.

The proposed mechanism is

\[
 \text{composed execution}
 \longrightarrow \text{marked failure site}
 \longrightarrow \text{one-hole context}
 \longrightarrow \text{plugged repair}
 \longrightarrow \text{new executable law}.
 \tag{DLG.1}
\]

This is not ordinary differential calculus on a parameter space. “Derivative” means the
one-hole-context derivative of compositional law syntax. The parents are derivatives of containers
and species, polynomial functors, zippers, operadic substitution, rewriting contexts, functor
calculus, and Faà-di-Bruno partition laws. Proposed field content is their reinterpretation as a
calculus of failure-generated challenge grammar.

The primitive question is therefore:

> Which laws make a marked execution failure produce its legal context, transport that context
> through composition, combine several failures, and decide whether observers retain the ancestry?

## 2. Proposed mechanism laws

### DLG Law I — source-marked focus

A failure can generate a challenge only when it has a source-marked site in an executing law. The
site is provenance, not a coordinate chosen after observing a target result.

**Falsifier:** the “failed site” changes under a source-equivalent presentation with no transported
comparison.

### DLG Law II — context production

Marking one input of a polynomial law produces the surrounding one-hole context. Plugging the
original input restores the execution. Plugging a replacement changes only the marked dependency
cone.

This is the smallest exact sector closed by ULF-DLG-1.

### DLG Law III — nested repair chain

A failure inside a composite law has outer and inner ancestry. Repair must plug through the inner
context and then through the outer context. The order is forced by composition, not chosen by a
route.

### DLG Law IV — interaction partition

Several failures are grouped by common ancestors in the syntax tree. Two failures either occur
inside one outer branch or across distinct branches. At higher arity, the partition of failure sites
by outer ancestor should be the law-genesis analogue of Faà di Bruno.

### DLG Law V — observer ancestry contraction

An observer may preserve failure count or value while forgetting ancestry. Such an observer cannot
decide whether a successor law is an inner repair or a cross-branch interaction. Equality of coarse
residuals is therefore weaker than equality of generated challenge grammar.

### DLG Law VI — semantic effectivity

A generated context counts as a new test only if adjoining it makes an old invisible intervention
illegal or splits an old reporter fibre. Context storage without future semantic contraction is
bookkeeping.

### DLG Law VII — higher coherence

Different sequences of context extraction and plugging must satisfy substitution coherence. Strict
polynomial syntax gives strict laws; quotient, homotopy, partial, or admission-sensitive syntax may
produce higher comparison data. “Curvature” is optional language, not the definition.

### DLG Law VIII — repair-selection independence

Context production determines where a repair belongs. It does not determine which replacement is
correct. Selection requires an independent source resource, admission, conserved reporter, or
strategy law. Hiding this choice inside the context would restore arbitrary endogenous revision.

## 3. Why this is above route repair

No arithmetic source, spectral endpoint, zero set, positivity form, Stokes contact, recurrence
clock, or physical interpretation occurs in Laws I–VIII. Routes may later be tested only as
realizations of:

1. source-marked composition;
2. failure-site extraction;
3. context plugging;
4. ancestry-preserving or ancestry-forgetting observation;
5. future semantic contraction.

A route-specific missing relation is no longer the primary target. The target is the rule by which
failure exposes a legal context and changes future composition.

## 4. Exact base cell

ULF-DLG-1 proves for set-level polynomial law signatures:

1. every marked execution canonically produces a one-hole context;
2. plugging the original value restores the execution;
3. a marked leaf of a composite law canonically produces nested outer/inner contexts;
4. nested plugging restores the composite and leaves every other outer branch unchanged;
5. every ordered failure pair has exactly one of two ancestry types—same branch or split branch;
6. the smallest two-branch example has a same-branch pair and a split-branch pair identified by an
   ancestry-blind observer.

This is a source-normal challenge-type producer in one mechanism class. It does not yet establish a
universal field.

## 5. Hostile boundaries

1. **Classical parent machinery.** Container derivatives and one-hole contexts are established.
   The claim is a new field interpretation and synthesis, not priority for the calculus.
2. **Presentation risk.** Arbitrary syntax can encode arbitrary transitions. A genuine realization
   needs a source-marked polynomial/operadic presentation and invariance under its accepted
   equivalences.
3. **No repair selector.** Context identifies location and dependency, not correct replacement.
4. **No automatic semantic birth.** A nonempty context space may be operationally inert.
5. **Polynomial scope.** Quotients, symmetries, partial operations, analytic domains, and higher
   coherences require groupoid, operadic, or homotopical extensions.
6. **No HT slogan.** An infinite derivative tower is not hypertranscendence without a literal
   differential-algebraic realization theorem.
7. **No positivity slogan.** Positivity may forget orientation or preserve it; only a typed
   observer map decides.
8. **No recurrence slogan.** Recurrent coarse execution does not imply open context history.

## 6. Research programme

1. **Closed:** formalize the polynomial context-production theorem in Lean.
2. Prove the full finite Faà-di-Bruno ancestry partition for an arbitrary finite set of failures.
3. **Closed in the total set-level polynomial chart:** define semantic effectivity of a context by
   contraction of invisible interventions and prove nonlinear postobserver data processing.
4. Classify context production under source-equivalent container, species, and operadic
   presentations.
5. Add partial/admission-sensitive plugging and locate its exact obstruction to strict execution.
6. Couple context birth to adversarial challenge selection: responses choose sites, while source
   composition constrains which contexts can be born.
7. Prove one withheld prediction in three non-RH laboratories before any proof-route application.

## 7. Acceptance gate

Promote Differential Law Genesis as field core only if one fixed mechanism proves:

1. **non-arbitrariness:** contexts are derived from source execution, not installed by an update
   oracle;
2. **semantic effect:** born contexts change future legal interventions;
3. **composition:** chain and higher partition laws survive accepted presentation changes;
4. **open genesis:** one dependency-closed source produces unbounded effective context birth;
5. **cross-domain prediction:** one law computes a consequence in three structurally different
   laboratories before their target results are read.
