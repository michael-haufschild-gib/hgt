## 1. Live mechanism question

ULF-DLG-1 proves that a marked failure produces a one-hole context.  That does not yet make the
context a test.  The missing operation is semantic:

\[
 \text{source reporter}
 \;\xrightarrow{\text{partial evaluation in a failure-born context}}\;
 \text{replacement challenge}.
 \tag{CTP.1}
\]

The programme asks for laws governing this transduction, not for a new carrier.  A legal successor
challenge must be derived from an already executing reporter and a source-marked context.  It may
not be installed by an arbitrary update oracle after a target failure is observed.

## 2. Frozen mechanism laws

### Law I -- contextual transduction

For a polynomial law container `C`, reporter `R : Ext_C(X) -> Y`, and one-hole context `c`, the
born challenge is forced:

\[
 \chi_{R,c}(x)=R(\operatorname{plug}(c,x)).
 \tag{CTP.2}
\]

### Law II -- operational effectivity

Relative to an old replacement reporter `a : X -> A`, the born challenge is effective exactly
when it separates two replacements in one old reporter fibre.  Equivalently, adjoining it strictly
contracts the old invisible-intervention doctrine.

### Law III -- nested chain

A challenge born at a leaf of a composite execution factors by first partially evaluating the
outer reporter and then partially evaluating the resulting reporter in the inner context.

### Law IV -- observer data processing

Post-observation commutes with challenge birth.  It can identify source-separated replacement
responses and erase an effective challenge.  It cannot make an inert source challenge effective.

### Law V -- selection independence

The born challenge partitions replacement candidates by reported consequence.  A non-singleton
fibre remains a genuine repair torsor.  Contextual transduction does not choose a point in it.

### Law VI -- recursive promotion

When a response changes executable syntax, newly marked failures must be differentiated again.
The full field target is the least source-closed challenge language generated by repeated
contextual transduction, together with admission and lineage laws for its strategies.

## 3. Conjecture/failure loops

**Positive survivor:** effectivity is exactly failure of determination by the old reporter, hence

**Positive survivor:** postprocessing obeys a one-way data-processing law.  Observed effectivity

**Verdict:** `[FALSIFIED]` in strict polynomial syntax.

contextual derivative.  The chain is strict and carries source ancestry.

**Positive survivor:** irreducible higher challenge birth must leave strict polynomial
partial-evaluation mechanics, for example through quotient, partial, homotopy-coherent, or
admission-sensitive syntax.

> A quotient, modulus, completion reporter, positive scalarization, or other pure postobserver
