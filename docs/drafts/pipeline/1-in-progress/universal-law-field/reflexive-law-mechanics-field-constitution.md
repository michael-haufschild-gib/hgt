## 1. Founding shift

Do not ask:

> What hidden object has all proof routes as coordinate charts?

Ask:

> What laws govern the birth, rejection, composition, and termination of new laws when the current
> rulebook fails?

The primitive scientific episode is

\[
 \mathcal L_n
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{execute}}
 \rho_n
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{select/promote}}
 \mathcal L_{n+1},
 \tag{RLM.1}
\]

where `L_n` is the current legal calculus and `rho_n` is a typed failure of an executable schedule.
The next failure is computed using `L_(n+1)`, not the old calculus. The theory is therefore
reflexive and path-dependent.

Objects, categories, complexes, topologies, and operators are laboratories in which this mechanism
may run. None is the universal primitive.

## 2. The twenty-seven field laws

### RLM Law I -- typed exposure

Executing two legal schedules produces a residual with a type determined before interpretation:
critical pair, associator, failed descent, missing primitive, nonclosed range, intervention response,
or limit defect. Calling every residual curvature destroys information.

### RLM Law II -- least-required / greatest-permitted corridor

Every proposed strict promotion has two boundaries:

- least relation required to make the requested laws executable;
- greatest relation permitted by conserved reporters under every future legal execution.

Strict promotion exists exactly when the first lies inside the second. ULF-OPM-5 proves this and a
delayed-detection countermodel in Lean.

### RLM Law III -- future conservation

A reporter is conserved through promotion only if it remains constant after every future law word,
not merely at the current endpoint. Present blindness may become future detection. Therefore
selection is intrinsically dynamical even when every carrier is finite.

### RLM Law IV -- conservation meet

Combining reporter doctrines intersects their future envelopes. Promotion admissibility composes by
conjunction; one exact conserved charge vetoes strict quotienting. No scalar average or majority
vote replaces this law. ULF-OPM-8 proves it in Lean.

### RLM Law V -- promotion differential

A compensator is tested by executing it in competing schedules. Its residual is the input to the
next promotion:

\[
 \kappa\xmapsto{\delta}\mathfrak a_\kappa.
 \tag{RLM.2}
\]

Vanishing closes the current promotion. Nonvanishing births a higher law. ULF-OPM-6 proves binary
central closure iff `delta kappa=0` and proves a nonzero ternary birth in Lean.

### RLM Law VI -- coherence is generated, not postulated

The next residual obeys an identity forced by the same schedule calculus. In the certified binary
case, every born associator satisfies the pentagon law because `delta^2=0`. Higher coherence is the
conservation law of repeated promotion.

### RLM Law VII -- exactness is admission-relative

An algebraic primitive repairs a residual only if it is legal in the current doctrine. The exact
mode trichotomy is:

1. admitted-exact: internal repair is possible;
2. algebraically exact but not admitted-exact: singular/admission promotion is forced;
3. algebraically nonexact: extension or higher promotion is required.

ULF-OPM-7 proves a residual that is algebraically exact and finitely admitted at every horizon but
has no admitted global primitive.

### RLM Law VIII -- feedback and directed work

Promotion changes the future residual calculus, reporter envelope, and admission doctrine. Its cost
belongs to directed formation histories. Reversible equivalence cannot carry nonzero nonnegative
additive formation cost. Termination, recurrence of observed rulebooks, and openness of lifted law
history are distinct questions.

### RLM Law IX -- contextual generation and arity birth

Order failure must be tested after other promotions have changed its input.  In unary additive
mechanics every three-step reversal is generated by contextual binary residuals:

\[
 PQR-RQP=[P,Q]R+Q[P,R]+[Q,R]P.
 \tag{RLM.3}
\]

Nested residuals obey Jacobi.  Therefore a nonzero triple schedule defect is not evidence of an
irreducible ternary law until every transported binary generator has been excluded.  Pairwise
exchange at the initial state is insufficient, and no universal finite context depth certifies
global exchange.  ULF-RLM-10 proves all four assertions in Lean.

### RLM Law X -- promotion/admission exchange

A coherent promotion does not automatically survive a change of admissibility.  For an additive
admission reporter (q) and history compensator (kappa), the following are equivalent:

1. every compensator value lies in (ker q);
2. the admitted promoted carrier is closed;
3. the quotient residual (q kappa) vanishes.

When these conditions hold, the admitted promoted operation exists uniquely and inherits ambient
associativity.  When they fail, (q kappa) is an exact singular-admission residual even if the
ambient associator is zero.  Thus coherence birth and admission birth are different mechanism
responses.  ULF-RLM-11 proves the equivalence, uniqueness, descent, and sharp associative mod-two
countermodel in Lean.

### RLM Law XI -- fixed-polarity collapse and endogenous genesis

Exact mutual generation of rules and tests under one fixed compatibility law cannot produce open
law history. Serial revision stabilizes after one full round. Synchronous revision satisfies
`P^3=P`, hence becomes fixed or period two after one round. Therefore prolonged genesis requires a
typed change in future compatibility: generated test kinds, changed admission, resource-filtered
polars, nonexact transport, or retained lineage. ULF-RLM-12 proves the complete dynamics in Lean.

### RLM Law XII -- unrestricted endogeneity is vacuous

Changing compatibility is necessary for open exact-polarity history, but arbitrary change is not
a mechanism. For any state set and any prescribed transition relation, one singleton test and a
state-indexed compatibility law realize that transition relation exactly as a polar. Thus
"compatibility depends on state" can encode every dynamics and predicts none. Law genesis must be
constrained by source-produced witnesses, locality, minimality, certificate conservation, repair
exchange, and explicit resource accounting. ULF-ECM-1 proves the universality no-go in Lean.

### RLM Law XIII -- finite monotone genesis terminates

Strictly adding compatibility facts cannot sustain an infinite active history when the vocabulary
of possible facts is finite.  The powerset of that vocabulary is finite, while strict monotonicity
makes the history injective.  Hence predictive open genesis must grow vocabulary, withdraw old
facts with conservation records, change admission, or retain lineage outside endpoint rulebooks.
ULF-GTM-1 proves the termination boundary in Lean.

### RLM Law XIV -- exact repair has no intrinsic least-work origin

For a split repair problem, every repair selector differs from every other by a unique vertical
shear. Nonzero shears fix no selector. Worse, every selector becomes the unique minimum on every
fibre for a positive squared-norm cost manufactured around it. Hence "choose the least-work repair"
is not a constitutive law until source interventions derive the work increments independently. The
intrinsic datum is a repair torsor; selection requires a source-produced symmetry reduction or a
source-revealed integrability law. ULF-DDE-1 proves the full boundary in Lean.

### RLM Law XV -- certificate-bearing mutation is predictive endogeneity

There is a strict middle regime between fixed-polarity collapse and unrestricted endogeneity. A
single local rank-two exchange changes visible compatibility with period two while generating an
unbounded positive semantic lineage. One transported certificate forces exact linearization and
reconstructs the nonlinear exchange, so future responses are predictions rather than entries in a
state-indexed transition table. ULF-SEGM-1 proves this mechanism in Lean.

The general live variable is therefore not arbitrary changing compatibility. It is a local
mutation together with a transported semantic certificate. Classifying which such pairs terminate,
recur observably with open lineage, cross an admission wall, or change formation class is now the
structured-endogeneity problem.

### RLM Law XVI -- source isotropy constrains successor doctrine

An unoriented failed pair plus fixed background cannot generate a deterministic binary successor
without breaking its symmetry. Every equivariant deterministic separator needs three distinct
output values. Exactly three minimal repairs are certified: raise deterministic arity to ternary,
add orientation and break the pair-swap, or convexify the binary doctrine so the background becomes
the unique symmetric mixture. ULF-EAG-1 proves this law in Lean.

Thus observer arity and doctrine are possible outputs of law genesis rather than fixed containers.
The next classification problem is composition of orbit-type constraints under successive failures.

### RLM Law XVII -- composed isotropy births mixed interaction channels

Composition is not multiplicative copying of the one-failure ternary reporter.  For `n` independent
inactive/unoriented failures, contextwise resolution forces distinct support strata and at least one
two-point response orbit on every nonempty support.  The minimum attained deterministic vocabulary is
`2^(n+1)-1`, achieved by the canonical support/parity chart.  Across blocks `A,B`, the
new mixed orbit count is `(2^|A|-1)(2^|B|-1)`.

ULF-EAG-2 certifies in Lean the support-separation theorem, sharp lower bound, exact canonical
attainment, canonical parity fibres, and mixed-channel product.  This is a law of finite interaction
vocabulary birth.  It does not cross the finitary-formation barrier and does not yet show that a
born mixed channel changes later promotion or admission.

### RLM Law XVIII -- serial birth has primary and secondary obstruction sectors

For successive carriers `E --S--> K --T--> A`, outer cokernel decides whether a residual has any
lift. Once it does, end-to-end birth is obstructed by the lift class modulo
`im S + ker T`, not modulo `im S` alone. Thus outer-kernel freedom can cancel an apparent inner
failure without altering the observed law. The primary, composite, and secondary sectors form one
canonical short exact sequence. ULF-PSB-1 proves lift independence, exactness, the exclusive
three-phase classification, a kernel-cancellation laboratory, and a genuine secondary-failure
laboratory in Lean.

This is the first certified composition law for two carrier engines. The next unresolved exchange
is topological: characterize when closure preserves this exact sequence and when
completion produces a new nonstrict composition defect.

### RLM Law XIX -- completion produces two independent serial homology defects

For a serial carrier under any enlargement doctrine, completion failure is not one scalar. The
source defect is the excess preimage `T⁻¹P/R`: a secondary law survives inner separation but dies
after composite observation. The target defect is `Q/(im T + P)`: a limit law exists in the closed
outer layer but is unattained by an actual carrier modulo the completed composite layer. The
separated serial sequence is short exact exactly when both defects vanish.

ULF-CHM-1 certifies the two converse identities, their logical independence, and a genuine
dense-proper-range target defect. The next mechanism question is how these two homology sectors
propagate through three or more stages; that requires a filtration or spectral-sequence law, not a
larger list of route obstructions.

### RLM Law XX -- obstruction has prospective survival depth

For `D -> E -> K -> A`, total birth is filtered by the images of future tails. The first grade is
`E/(im R + ker(TS))`, the second is `K/(im S + ker T)`, and the final grade is `coker T`. A local
failure is therefore measured relative to past attainment and future erasure simultaneously.
These grades form canonical short exact sequences; they need not split.

ULF-DKO-1 certifies the nested filtration and exact middle-layer transition. This replaces the
false expectation of one universal obstruction scalar by an obstruction spectrum with extension

### RLM Law XXI -- adaptive stress is independent of fixed-face complexity

A law doctrine is not characterized by tests selected before its responses are known.  Quantifier
order is constitutive: a source-admitted challenge can depend on the current response while still
retaining one globally consistent source law for the complete transcript.  Consequently uniformly
small fixed-face complexity does not bound repair depth.

ULF-ALG-2 certifies the sharp base cell.  Prefix cylinders are laminar and have static shattering
dimension one, but for every depth `d` a legal response-dependent challenge forces every
deterministic policy to fail on all `d` rounds.  Every answer remains realized by one final branch.
Fractional response changes the exact minimax damage from `d` to `d/2` but leaves the same
arbitrarily deep legal challenge mechanism intact.

This is not yet the field dynamics.  It proves that adaptivity is a new independent axis which the
existing residual/obstruction filtration does not contain.  Open gate: challenges must be allowed

### RLM Law XXII -- observer-made closure is a hidden promoted challenge

Let a source promoter `D` generate the challenge lineage `v,Dv,D²v,...`, and let an observer `q`
intertwine it with an observed promoter `E`.  The observer maps every finite source challenge span

ULF-ALG-3 certifies this transport, rank contraction, recurrence/kernel equivalence, and the exact
false-closure witness: if a source packet is independent while its observed packet is dependent,
some nonzero promoted source challenge lies in the observer kernel.  An observer injective on the

This turns observer contraction from metaphor into a typed mechanism.  It still leaves the central
formation question open: which exposed failure generates `D`, and when must a surviving hidden
residual be promoted into the next legal reporter?

### RLM Law XXVI -- genuine grammar birth is failure of old-quotient descent

Adjoin one proposed legal operation `h` to a fixed context grammar. Let `K` be the old future

\[
 h\text{ preserves }K

### RLM Law XXVII -- observation renormalizes functions into relations and execution becomes lax

Every deterministic source law has a canonical relational image under every observer. It remains
deterministic exactly when the source law preserves the observer kernel. For serial execution and
serial observation,

\[
\mathcal R_{pq}(gf)
\subseteq p_*(\mathcal R_q(f);\mathcal R_q(g))
\subseteq (p_*\mathcal R_q(f));(p_*\mathcal R_q(g)).
\tag{RLM.5}
\]

The first gap is source-execution splice; the second is observer-midpoint splice. They are
independent. The first vanishes under executable descent of the later law. The second vanishes for
all relations exactly when the further observer is injective. Hence observer renormalization has a
typed defect filtration, not one scalar curvature.

ULF-RLR-1 Lean-certifies relational survival, descent classification, lax execution, strict serial
pushforward, exact injectivity converse, selector no-go, and two unconditional four-state cells
isolating the defects. This closes set-level renormalization kinematics. Topological closure,
convex/probabilistic promotion, cofinal limits, and source-normal determinization remain open.

### RLM Law XXVIII -- remembering and forgetting are oppositely absorbing repairs

Fix a family of unary source laws and an observer equivalence `E`.  There are two extremal ways to
restore deterministic execution.  The least invariant coarsening `C(E)` forgets only distinctions
forced by the laws.  The greatest invariant refinement `I(E)` retains only identifications stable
under every finite future law word.  They obey

\[
I(E)\subseteq E\subseteq C(E),
\qquad
C(I(E))=I(E),
\qquad
I(C(E))=C(E).
\tag{RLM.6}
\]

Hence opposite repair schedules agree exactly when every law already preserves `E`.  Otherwise
remember-first and forget-first land at opposite executable envelopes: repair order is exact
history, not a gauge of one endpoint.  A three-state cell makes `I(E)` equality and `C(E)` the
universal relation while the unrepaired observed law is genuinely multivalued.

ULF-RLR-2 Lean-certifies the generic interior, both universal extremal laws, opposite absorption,
exchange iff executability, the exact three-state cell, and its observer-induced nondeterminism.
This joins future testing, law saturation, and relational renormalization into one mechanism.  It
still supplies no source selector between the two directions; topology, positivity, and recursive
failure-to-law genesis remain the next field layer.

## 3. Certified promotion decision mechanism

Given a typed residual and conserved reporter doctrine:

```text
future reporter corridor fits?
├─ yes: strict promotion permitted, not forced
└─ no: quotient forbidden
   └─ proposed compensator κ
      ├─ δκ ≠ 0: binary repair fails; ternary/higher law is born
      └─ δκ = 0: algebraic central closure exists
         ├─ admitted primitive/compensator: central or gauge promotion permitted
         └─ no admitted primitive: singular/admission promotion forced
```

This is not yet a deterministic algorithm. Several permitted promotions can coexist. Further source
reporters, directed cost, or a universal minimality law must choose among permitted modes. The
formerly independent promotion classes now have several exact transition laws.

## 6. Predictions already forced

1. **Delayed visibility:** endpoint-blind residual can be detected after later legal execution.
2. **Conservation veto:** one future-stable charge blocks quotienting despite arbitrarily many blind
   reporters.
3. **Coherence birth:** failed associative compensation produces a pentagon-coherent ternary law.
4. **Gauge invariance:** unary recalibration cannot change the central-versus-higher verdict.
5. **Finite-horizon mirage:** every finite stage can admit exact repair while no admitted global
   repair exists.
6. **Mode noncollapse:** strict, central, higher, and singular promotions are selected by different
   exact tests and cannot be one scalar obstruction.
7. **False closure:** lossy observation can make a nonassociative promoted source law exactly
   associative by killing its associator; faithful observation cannot.
8. **Arity boundary:** unary additive mechanisms cannot produce an irreducible ternary schedule
   defect; every such defect has a contextual binary generator.
9. **No finite exchange audit:** passing every exchange test through a fixed finite depth never
   certifies unrestricted exchange without a source-derived horizon bound.
10. **Coherence/admission bifurcation:** a promoted law can be perfectly associative yet fail every
    restriction to a declared admitted carrier; the quotient-valued compensator, not its
    associator, detects this singular failure.
11. **Exact-polarity collapse:** exact reciprocal rule--test generation has no open history; serial
    update closes in one round and synchronous update is post-first two-periodic.
12. **Endogeneity vacuity:** state-dependent compatibility alone imposes no law; one test realizes
    every transition system, so predictive revision must constrain compatibility birth.
13. **Finite monotone termination:** bounded compatibility vocabulary and strict monotone active
    repair cannot sustain open history; any infinite genesis must spend another named resource.
14. **Two-defect renormalization:** source-execution splice and observer-midpoint splice are
    independently nonzero; no scalar total defect determines where effective-law loss entered.

## 7. Fields-medalist research programme

### 7.1 Build a periodic table of residual types

For every theorem wall, record only

\[
 (\text{legal schedules},\ \text{typed residual},\ \text{conserved reporters},\
 \text{admission},\ \text{successful promotion}).
\]

Cluster walls by identical transition law, not by subject or proof route.

### 7.2 Search for conservation laws of law change

Ask:

1. Which reporters survive every admitted promotion?
2. Which residual differential is nilpotent, and at what level?
3. When do two promotion orders agree, braid, or require another higher law?
4. Which formation quantities are monotone under every promotion?
5. Which local repairs have a compactness theorem giving global admission?

### 7.3 Construct minimal bifurcation laboratories

Find smallest systems with the same exposed residual but different legal promotion modes. Vary one
mechanism condition at a time: reporter, differential, admission, or cost. Demand exact converses.

### 7.4 Develop promotion renormalization

Coarse observation can make multiple promotion histories appear identical. Study which effective
rulebook flows have fixed points, cycles, universality classes, and relevant/irrelevant reporter
directions. This is a plausible mechanism-level analogue of renormalization, not geometric GR.

### 7.5 Prove termination or open-genesis theorems

Classify when iterated promotion:

- terminates in a saturated rulebook;
- becomes periodic after observation but remains open in lifted history;
- converges only after singular completion;
- produces unbounded arity or coherence depth.

### 7.6 Only then return to the corpus

Each proof route becomes a late laboratory. A valid application must identify all entries of the
mechanism tuple before its endpoint and predict one theorem not used to define the mapping.

2. **Structured compatibility genesis:** ULF-SEGM-1 proves that certificate-bearing local mutation

3. **Revealed-work integrability:** derive directed work from source interventions; positive work
   cycles must falsify every scalar action rather than trigger a fitted metric change.
4. **Infinite coherence:** convergence criterion for an unbounded promotion differential tower.
5. **Higher renormalization:** extend certified set-level relational flow to closure, completion,
   convexification, probability kernels, and cofinal observers; derive nontrivial fixed points and
   exchange laws rather than fitting deterministic selectors.
6. **Whole-corpus realization:** one source-normal mechanism instance spanning at least three
   nonisomorphic mathematical organs.

## 9. Death criteria

Retire RLM as theatre if:

- transition laws must be independently fitted in every laboratory;
