## 1. The extracted grammar

The common structure is not a common target object and not a scalar obstruction.  It is a sequence
of operations:

\[
 \boxed{
 \textsf{mark}\to\textsf{form}\to\textsf{ghost}\to\textsf{dualize}
 \to\textsf{admit}\to\textsf{saturate}\to\textsf{conserve}.}
 \tag{WFG.1}
\]

1. **Mark.** A source operation selects a legal deformation, place, clock, relation, or challenge
   before divisor specialization.
2. **Form.** Local marked responses are assembled into words, configurations, relations, packets,
   moments, or histories.  This is where new arity and interaction vocabulary can be born.
3. **Ghost.** A logarithmic derivative, cross-effect, boundary, cumulant, or primitive projection
   linearizes formation and exposes the connected current.
4. **Dualize.** Inversion, reflection, parity, dagger, exterior formation, or an opposite port
   produces the signed/oriented companion erased by a positive scalar shadow.
5. **Admit.** A domain, topology, completion, compact carrier, or proper cost decides whether the
   generated family exists cofinally as one source law.
6. **Saturate.** Source-generated tests must separate the admitted response without an arbitrary
   state-dependent oracle.  Fixed finite tests are insufficient; unrestricted endogenous tests are
   vacuous.
7. **Conserve.** Unlike charts count as one mechanism only when a typed identity transports the
   same generated response among them and predicts a response not used to define the comparison.

The verbs are load-bearing.  Replacing them by a tuple of relation, positivity, topology, and
history objects recreates the independent-slot nonprediction theorem.
