## 1. Higher pivot

The corpus should not be unified by declaring one more universal object. The recurring missing mechanism is **formation**:

> A source algebra does not directly give a probability law, Hilbert vector, relation quotient, or history. It supplies native operations that generate a formation species. Route objects are local gauges of that formation, and route no-gos record information lost by changing species, normalizing, completing, or forgetting marks.

The primitive is therefore a law-governed formation doctrine, not a point in a cone and not a scalar obstruction.

## 2. First exact chamber

The additive chamber is closed by ULF-SRFM-1:

- independent superposition;
- Bernoulli thinning;
- vacuum;
- mean calibration.

These laws uniquely force Poisson formation. Common stochastic marking is an exact information-neutral lift through recovery. Applied to the Gamma-six source packet, the functional equation exchanges two extensive formations that have identical normalized shape but are globally mutually singular off-centre.

This is a genuine law-to-formation theorem. It is not yet a fusion theorem across species.

## 3. Ontology

A formation chart must contain at least:

1. **source ledger** \(C\): coefficients, labels, grades, multiplication, involutions, and intervention operations fixed before specialization;
2. **native formation laws** \(\mathcal L\): superposition, exclusion, substitution, carry, branching, fusion, or history composition;
3. **formation functor** \(\mathfrak F_{\mathcal L}(C)\): extensive state before normalization;
4. **observer exits**: normalization, quotient, completion, phase erasure, finite aperture, and endpoint projection;
5. **recovery kernels**: marked lifts or refinements whose projection reconstructs the pre-exit state;
6. **comparison cells** between formation species;
7. **properness law** controlling infinite formation and preventing observer equivalence from hiding source escape.

Normalized probability is a projective shadow. Positivity is not itself phase quotient. Recurrence is not itself open lifted history. Hypertranscendence is not itself noncompactness. Each requires a stated formation law and comparison.

## 5. Frozen field conjecture

### Conjecture SRFM-A (species-relative constitutive formation)

The literal pre-zero HGT coefficient source carries a finite set of native formation laws whose generated doctrines include additive, exterior, Witt/Bose, history, jet, and pro/Tate charts. There exists a coherent comparison system satisfying:

1. common source interventions act before chart formation;
2. every comparison preserves a declared joint recovery ledger;
3. the combined comparisons reconstruct that ledger;
4. formation energy/information obeys an exact conservation identity under recovery;
5. infinite formation is proper relative to source history;
6. functional-equation action lifts to the comparison system and is fixed-label quasi-invariant exactly at the neutral locus.

Only clause 2 is proved in the additive Poisson chamber, and only for common marking/recovery. The conjecture as a whole is open.

## 6. Research programme and acceptance gates

Work must follow this order.

### Gate I — extract native generation grammar

For every major corpus organ, record operations on the pre-specialization source rather than endpoint invariants. Required entries include addition, multiplication, thinning/restriction, exclusion, carry, substitution, involution, completion, and intervention.

Falsifier: two alleged gauges have no common pre-exit source operation.

### Gate II — construct a second uniqueness chamber

Prove a source-law characterization of exterior/hard-core formation, analogous to Theorem 2.1 for Poisson formation. It must derive exclusion from source operations rather than assume a Fock functor.

Falsifier: same stated native laws admit nonisomorphic exterior and symmetric formations.

### Gate III — first genuine fusion law

Construct a nonidentity comparison between at least three structurally different organs—for example additive Poisson formation, exterior Boolean formation, and group/history formation. Prove joint reconstruction or an exact conservation identity.

The comparison cannot be a shared scalar statistic. It must transport source interventions and recover a common ledger.

### Gate IV — fourth-organ prediction

Use the fusion identity to predict a theorem in a fourth organ not used to define the comparison. Prove it independently. Until this happens, the framework is organizational theatre.

### Gate V — formation/properness theorem

Prove that bounded observer formation plus bounded action has compact source-lift set, or equivalently that every new completion-boundary history has infinite relaxed action. This must be source-derived, not imposed by a chosen metric.

### Gate VI — selection

Only after Gates I--V, prove that the lifted functional-equation action is fixed-label quasi-invariant or proper precisely at its neutral locus. ULF-SRFM-1 already proves that this implication would select the centre in its Gamma-six Poisson chart; it does not produce the premise.

## 8. Why this may explain route stagnation

Many routes preserve one observer exit while silently changing formation species:

- normalization equates packets with different extensive mass;
- positive Gram formation forgets orientation lift;
- finite relation quotients forget completion reporters;
- recurrent phase projection forgets lifted history;
- Boolean support forgets carry digits;
- scalar traces forget multi-label intensity and intervention location.

These are not one curvature. They are failures of a formation comparison to be conservative, proper, or jointly reconstructive.
