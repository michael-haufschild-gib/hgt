## 1. Primitive

A constitutive process is not a state, endpoint, path probability, or static kernel. It is an object
that accepts legal interventions and returns states, amplitudes, probabilities, relations, or other
processes. In finite linear charts it is a multilinear functional

\[
W:E_1\otimes\cdots\otimes E_k\longrightarrow\mathbb F.
\]

In positive/noncommutative charts it is a process tensor, comb, completely positive higher map, or
operator correspondence. In algebraic charts it may be a polynomial law or higher natural
transformation. What makes these charts one field is not shared vocabulary but a represented common
process and compatible intervention calculus.
