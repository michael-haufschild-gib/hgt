## 3. Seven mechanism laws

### Law I — orbit formation

A positive source generator `L` and seed `v` create a continuous reporter packet
`t -> exp(-tL)v`. Integrating its rank-one responses produces the formation Gramian `G`.

### Law II — boundary conservation

The Lyapunov identity

\[
 LG+GL=|v\rangle\langle v|
 \tag{OGM.3}
\]

says that source dissipation on both packet legs equals the injected marked seed. It is the exact
fusion law, not an analogy between organs.

### Law III — observation/cost polarity

`G` measures how visible a displacement is under the whole orbit. `G^(-1)` measures the minimum
control energy needed to synthesize it. Compact weak observation and unbounded proper cost are not
competing topologies; they are spectral inverses.

### Law IV — least-capital formation

Let `W` be the reachability map generated by the orbit. A positive defect born as

\[
 X=WB,
 \qquad T=XX^*,
 \tag{OGM.4}
\]

has endogenous energy equal to the minimum of `||B||_HS^2` over all such factorizations. Finite
energy is equivalent to existence of a Hilbert--Schmidt source history. This law types
“source-produced defect”: the defect must factor through the same formation kernel, not merely live
on the same Hilbert space.

### Law V — normal/corona selection

If the compact source reporters have scalar common commutant, every normalized approximately
invariant normal defect escapes all compact charts. The inverse Gramian charges that escape
infinitely. Bounded formation capital and persistent normal mass are incompatible.

### Law VI — complex-time bifurcation

For arithmetic spectral labels, the same complex orbit `exp(-zL)v` has:

- real time: positive heat/Gibbs generation;
- imaginary time: phase and recurrence;
- derivatives: moment and HT prolongation;
- time integral: formation Gramian;
- inverse: global properness.

This reverses a false corpus slogan. HT is not the cause of noncompact observed motion. HT belongs
to the infinite prolongation of the complex orbit; noncompactness belongs to its inverse formation
cost or unwrapped history.

### Law VII — coordinate and clock covariance

Unitary source equivalence conjugates every object. Rescaling source time multiplies `G` and
`G^(-1)` by reciprocal constants. Selection depends on finite versus infinite formation capital,
not on chosen coordinates or time units.

### Law VIII — recovery data processing

Postcomposing source formation and defect by the same recovery map can only lower least formation
capital. It preserves capital exactly for every injective recovery, hence for every common split
recovery and every invertible functional-equation transport. A strict decrease is exactly a
nonzero residual erased by the recovery kernel. Recovery cannot finance selection without losing
information.

### Law IX — atomic refinement costs infinity

The source seed itself is an impulsive boundary datum, not a finite-capital orbit formation.
Positive-width source averages are reachable, but their least capital diverges when they refine to
the exact atom. Ambient convergence and observer recurrence therefore coexist with a properness
wall generated by the same source. Injective recovery and functional-equation transport preserve
that wall exactly. On the Euler shell, prime harmonic divergence makes the spectral sampling set a
Hardy uniqueness set, so the normalized width-`T` average has exact least capital `1/T`.

### Law X — source admission is a trace-space condition

For diagonal sources, finite formation capital is equivalent to Hardy interpolation on the source
spectrum. The Euler shell is prime-log complete, so source histories are unique. Its radial defect
`n^(2a)-1` has finite capital exactly at `a=0`; functional equation and common recovery preserve
this classification.

### Law XI — positive aggregation has no regularity cancellation

Endogenous energy is additive on positive defect sums. A functional-equation pair, divisor average,
or completed positive packet has finite capital only when every positive-weight component does.
Phase cancellation can regularize only after abandoning positivity or retaining oriented lift data.

### Law XII — drift consumes causal delay

Width-`T` radial drift `delta` is formable exactly after delay `b>=max(0,delta)`, with work
`2 min(|delta|,T)/T^2`. Functional-equation pairing requires common entrance latency `|delta|`.
Common extra delay is gauge. Work has a linear cusp while Jeffreys information is quadratic, so
smooth information cannot finance source formation. Zero entrance latency plus reflection would
