## 1. Frontier-forced pivot

The current RH frontier has five unlike open-premise classes, but their common logical shape is
now sharper than “one missing invariant”:

1. every declared local, finite, or positive test may admit a repair;
2. no source-coherent global execution of those repairs has been produced;
3. completion or scalar observation can erase the missing execution history;
4. adding an arbitrary witness, selector, or transition oracle is vacuous.

This suggests a mechanism rather than another receiver.  Given a failure-kernel family
`H`, distinguish two graded notions for a nonnegative integral weight `a`:

\[
 \operatorname{Loc}_m(H)
 =\left\{a:\sum_{i\in K}a_i\ge m\ \text{for every }K\in H\right\},
 \tag{SELM.1}
\]

and

\[
 \operatorname{Exec}_m(H)
 =\left\{a:\text{at least }m\text{ grade-one legal repairs fit coordinatewise under }a\right\}.
 \tag{SELM.2}
\]

`Loc` is observerwise or symbolic admission.  `Exec` is actual finite composition.  Every
executable law is locally admitted, but equality is an extra integrality/effectivity theorem.
The defect

\[
 \mathfrak D_m(H)=\operatorname{Loc}_m(H)\setminus\operatorname{Exec}_m(H)
 \tag{SELM.3}
\]

is a law accepted everywhere by the current test grammar but not generated by current execution.
It is therefore a candidate source of genuinely new legal context.

## 3. Candidate laws

### Law I -- failure kernelization

A failed conclusion must first produce its inclusion-minimal derivation kernels.  These—not a
chosen target theorem—are the source-native local tests.  ULF-FKT-1 already Lean-certifies the
kernel--transversal equivalence in the finite setting.

### Law II -- symbolic admission

Grade `m` means every failure kernel is covered with multiplicity at least `m`.  Symbolic
admission is local and conjunctive; it does not assert one decomposition into legal executions.

### Law III -- executable composition

Ordinary grade `m` consists of sums/products of `m` grade-one repairs, with arbitrary nonnegative
surplus.  This retains lineage: each unit of grade has an actual parent repair.

### Law IV -- defect genesis

A nonzero class in `Loc_m/Exec_m` is an effective candidate for law birth only when its failure of
ordinary decomposition produces a new legal context that separates a previously invisible
execution fibre.  Mere symbolic nonmembership is insufficient; ULF-REE-1 still supplies the
effectivity gate.

### Law V -- positive relaxation is not effectivity

Positive or convex weights naturally live in the rational cone cut out by `(SELM.1)`.  Clearing
denominators gives an integral symbolic law, not necessarily an executable one.  Thus positivity
is sometimes a symbolic relaxation of execution—not universally a phase quotient and not an
automatic carrier theorem.

### Law VI -- graded history

Minimal generator degrees of the symbolic law algebra measure primitive repair arities.  A defect
repeated to arbitrarily high grade may still come from one finite primitive generator; open
challenge genesis requires unbounded primitive degrees or a cofinal/non-Noetherian source tower.
This distinction blocks the false identification “unbounded grade = hypertranscendence.”

### Law VII -- observer recurrence versus lifted execution

An observer may retain only normalized weight and grade modulo a period.  It can therefore be
constant or recurrent while exact execution grade diverges.  The marked grade and decomposition
lineage are the open lift.  Recurrence alone never supplies this lift.

### Law VIII -- symbolic--ordinary equality is a rigidity law

Equality `Loc_m=Exec_m` for all grades says every locally admitted weighted repair decomposes into
legal grade-one executions.  In the classical finite graph parent, standard gradedness is tied to
bipartiteness; for hypergraphs it is controlled by stronger unimodularity/MFMC conditions.  In the
field interpretation, equality is absence of collective law birth.

### Law IX -- reflexive update

Primitive symbolic defects must be fed back as new tests or generators.  The update is legal only
if it is functorial in the marked failure family, survives presentation changes, and passes the
prospective fibre-splitting criterion.  This feedback step is open and load-bearing.

## 4. First exact calibration

For the triangle failure family

\[
 H_\triangle=\{\{0,1\},\{1,2\},\{0,2\}\},
\]

the weight `a_n=(n,n,n)` is locally admitted at grade `2n`: every edge has weight `2n`.
For `n>0` it cannot contain `2n` grade-one repairs.  Every grade-one repair has total weight at
least two, so `2n` executions require total weight at least `4n`, while `a_n` has total weight
`3n`.  Hence

\[
 a_n\in\operatorname{Loc}_{2n}(H_\triangle)
 \setminus\operatorname{Exec}_{2n}(H_\triangle)
 \qquad(n>0).
 \tag{SELM.4}
\]

After normalization by grade, every `a_n/(2n)` is the same positive profile
`(1/2,1/2,1/2)`, and every grade is zero modulo two, while the exact grade tends to infinity.
This one mechanism therefore gives:

- local admission without execution;
- positive relaxation without an effective integral carrier;
- closed observed profile with open graded history;
- persistent defects in unbounded grades.

It does **not** give unbounded primitive generator degree: the classical triangle vertex-cover
algebra is generated in degree two.  Therefore it is not a hypertranscendence model.

## 6. Hostile loops

1. **Every symbolic defect is new physics.** False.  Symbolic defects are standard algebra and may
   be generated by one low-degree class.  Survivor: require prospective effectivity and a withheld
   cross-doctrine prediction.
2. **Persistent defects imply hypertranscendence.** False by the triangle: all persistent gaps are
   generated at degree two.  Survivor: test unbounded primitive generator degrees in a cofinal or
   differential symbolic law algebra.
3. **Positive relaxation supplies an actual repair.** False by `(SELM.4)`.  Survivor: prove an
   integer-decomposition, unimodularity, tightness, or compactness theorem from source.
4. **Ordinary/symbolic equality is automatic in finite systems.** False on odd cycles.  Survivor:
   equality becomes a genuine rigidity/effectivity property.
5. **Blowing up the defect solves the original theorem.** False.  Rees/blow-up geometry organizes
   direction and multiplicity but supplies no endpoint annihilation, sign, or arithmetic source
   map by itself.
6. **The finite squarefree symbolic algebra is non-Noetherian.** False: the parent theorem proves
   finite generation.  Any HT/open-history claim must use a cofinal, analytic, or differential
   extension and prove failure of bounded primitive generation there.
7. **One combinatorial calibration establishes the field.** False.  Need three nonisomorphic
   source realizations and one new theorem linking their defect composition.

## 7. Acceptance gate

SELM becomes a field core only after all are met:

1. exact triangle mechanism is paper-proved and Lean-certified;
2. defect composition and observer transport are formalized without assuming polynomial rings;
3. three structurally distinct corpus mechanisms admit literal pre-endpoint `Loc/Exec` maps;
4. one source-derived rigidity theorem forces symbolic-to-ordinary effectivity in at least two of
   those mechanisms;
5. the reflexive update from primitive defect to new legal context passes ULF-PCG prospective
   effectivity and excludes arbitrary-oracle encoding;
6. HT is tied to unbounded primitive generation, not mere persistent grade;
7. all significant dependency-closed theorems are Lean-certified under the Universal Law Field
   umbrella.

## 9. Higher pivot after the static parent collision

The surviving field mechanism is not the clutter or its semigroup.  It is the **effectivity
history** of a law under a chain of presentation reductions and observer exits:

\[
  (H,a,m)\longmapsto
  \bigl(\operatorname{Local},\operatorname{Exec},
        \operatorname{Primitive},\operatorname{Gap}\bigr)
  \longmapsto f_!(H,a,m).
\]

ULF-SELM-7 proves that `Exec` is covariant while `Gap` is only contravariantly detectable.  Hence
gap survival along an observer chain must be prefix-closed: once a transported law becomes
executable, every later positive observer keeps it executable.  This predicts an obstruction-death
filtration, with possible finite death, eternal survival, or death only at a non-strict completion.

This mechanism gives route-independent, falsifiable demands on the sharpened frontier:

1. **singular source descent:** construct a source execution or a reflection theorem; target
   nullity after a lossy landing is insufficient;
2. **all-prime oscillatory transport:** prove the observer chain retains the primitive spectrum
   uniformly, or identify its exact death stage;
3. **adaptive spectral orientation:** show positive scalarization is injective on the oriented
   execution grammar, not only on its norm/Gram image;
4. **whole-space coercivity:** show completion reflects nonexecution instead of closing a dense
   algebraic gap observationally;
5. **positive carrier existence:** lift a fractional/positive local law to source execution, or
   exhibit the primitive that the carrier quotient erased;
6. **hypertranscendence:** prove unbounded primitive degree after semantic quotient, rather than
   infer it from persistent powers or noncompactness;
7. **recurrence:** treat recurrent observed execution and open source primitive lineage as distinct
   invariants.

ULF-SELM-8 now proves exact no-revival and death-time composition.  Positive observer transport is
strictly functorial on kernels, failure families, and weights.  Gaps pull backward along observer
chains, so their alive stages form an initial segment; the triangle-collapse witness has exact
death time one and remains dead after every later positive observer.  Paper proof and Lean
certification pass.

The next theorem target is a completion law separating finite persistence from death at infinity.
That is a mechanism problem, not an RH-route calculation.
