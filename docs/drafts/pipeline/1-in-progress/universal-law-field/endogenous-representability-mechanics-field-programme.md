- **ID:** ULF-ERM-0
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress

## 1. Founding question

Do not ask for an object containing all proof routes. Ask:

> When a legal calculus produces a challenge it cannot express, which laws govern creation of the
> least new expressive power, transport of old proofs, and exposure of the next failure?

The primitive mechanism is

\[
 \mathcal L
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{execute}}
 \chi
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{effectivity\ test}}
 \widehat{\mathcal L}
 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{certificate\ transport/repair}}
 \mathcal L^+ .
 \tag{ERM.1}
\]

Here `chi` is generated by the current calculus, not supplied by an endpoint oracle.
`widehat L` is a least legal extension representing `chi`, when such an extension exists.  The
final step may preserve the old doctrine, force a finer or coarser repair, change admission, or
cross a formation/completion boundary.  The next execution uses `L+`, so the process is reflexive.

The word *representable* is deliberately broad but typed in every laboratory.  It can mean
represented by a state, operation, reporter, relation, primitive, positive measure, admitted
vector, or coherent global history.  The programme does not identify these receivers.  It proposes
one set of laws for how failures create and test their representers.

## 2. The mechanism laws

### ERM Law I -- endogenous challenge

Only a failure extracted from legal execution may trigger revision.  An arbitrary state-indexed
challenge realizes arbitrary dynamics and has no predictive content.

### ERM Law II -- effective novelty

A supplied operation or reporter is genuinely new exactly when it breaks descent through the old
future-behaviour quotient.  Syntactic novelty alone is insufficient; an old-word-definable update
is inert.

### ERM Law III -- least conservative representation

When an effective challenge is representable, revision first adjoins the least expressive resource
that realizes it while preserving every old evaluation.  Minimality is relative to a declared
conservation doctrine, not cardinality or cost chosen after the fact.

ULF-DJG-1 is the first source-normal calibration: Boolean self-evaluation produces the absent
anti-diagonal challenge, and one new reporter is conservative, cardinal-minimal, and relabelling
natural.

ULF-REE-1 gives the exact load-bearing correction. Representer birth alone is not semantic law
birth. The anti-diagonal can be absent while already determined by complete row semantics. A valid
genesis claim must separately prove row-fibre splitting, equivalently name an old-invisible
intervention excluded by the challenge.

### ERM Law IV -- proof and certificate transport

Every old proof, reporter, and semantic certificate must state how it transports across genesis.
Failure of transport is not erased.  It is typed as a new coherence, admission, observer, or
formation defect.

### ERM Law V -- bipolar repair response

After genesis, deterministic execution has two extremal repairs: retain every identification stable
under future execution, or forget every distinction forced by execution.  They are not two gauges
of one endpoint.  Their order and their change under carrier growth are law history.

### ERM Law VI -- admission-relative representation

An algebraic representer outside the admitted doctrine does not close the failure.  It forces an
admission transition or a singular promotion.  Finite-horizon admission does not imply a global
admitted representer.

### ERM Law VII -- formation/limit debt

Representability at every finite horizon does not imply a source-realized global representer.
Applying genesis before a cofinal formation can differ from applying it after the limit.  The
difference is retained as completion history, not declared a new source state.

### ERM Law VIII -- observer data processing

Observation can delay or erase an effective source challenge and can turn deterministic execution
into a relation.  It cannot create earlier source separation.  Apparent closure after observation
must carry the hidden source residual as certificate.

### ERM Law IX -- genesis expenditure

Open predictive history must spend at least one named resource: symbols, admission, withdrawn
certificates, retained lineage, or formation class.  Fixed exact revision collapses; arbitrary
endogeneity is vacuous; finite monotone vocabulary terminates; finitary joint saturation has finite
ancestry.

## 4. Distant conjecture

### Conjecture ERM-A -- reflexive representability factorization

Every predictive, reporter-detectable revision generated by a legal failure factors, up to complete
future-reporter equivalence, through the following five stages:

1. endogenous challenge exposure;
2. independent operational-effectivity test and least conservative representer genesis;
3. bipolar deterministic repair;
4. admission/formation correction;
5. transported-certificate update.

Moreover, two such factorizations define the same law revision exactly when they induce the same
prospective separation filtration and isomorphic transported-certificate history.

This is not a theorem assembled from the present laws.  It is the proposed unifying mechanism.

**Falsifiers.** Any one of the following kills ERM-A:

1. a predictive strict revision with no endogenous challenge;
2. two non-equivalent revisions with identical complete future filtration and certificate history;
3. a source-generated failure requiring a sixth transition mode not reducible to representation,
   repair, admission/formation, or certificate transport;
4. unavoidable nonuniqueness of the least conservative stage after all source reporters are fixed;
5. inability to instantiate one factorization law in three structurally different mathematical
   organs.

## 5. First risky prediction: asymmetric change of carrier

Let a conservative carrier extension preserve every old law word.  ERM predicts:

1. the future-retaining repair restricts exactly to the old carrier;
2. the future-forgetting repair only satisfies a one-sided base-change inclusion;
3. strictness occurs when new states create a mediated return path between old states;
4. a compatible semantic retraction removes the defect.

ULF-ERM-1 proves these statements at set level and supplies a three-state strict cell.  This is the
first exact interaction between source-normal grammar growth and bipolar repair.  Generic
Beck--Chevalley theory is the parent language; the direction forced by remember/forget repair is the
mechanism-level content.

## 6. Parent collision boundary

Known mathematics owns the static ingredients:

- presheaf constructions and free cocompletions add specified classes of representers;
- exact and elementary quotient completions freely add effective quotients and extensionality;
- fibred doctrines, indexed monads, and Beck--Chevalley conditions govern change of base;
- CEGAR and active learning refine a fixed abstraction or hypothesis language using
  counterexamples;
- institution theory and abstract algebraic logic transport theories across fixed signature maps;
- Yoneda density and representability reconstruct objects from declared probes.

Primary anchors include Maietti--Rosolini, *Elementary quotient completion*
(arXiv:1206.0162); Bloomfield--Maruyama, *Fibered Universal Algebra for First-Order Logics*
(arXiv:2205.05657); Cavallo, *Stable factorization from a fibred algebraic weak factorization
system* (arXiv:1910.03121); Orchard--Wadler--Eades, *Unifying graded and parameterised monads*
(arXiv:2001.10274); and Arkor--McDermott, *Presheaves and cocompletions in formal category theory*
(arXiv:2604.22370).

These parents do not by themselves make the current calculus produce its next weight/signature,
choose an admitted representer, or conserve the corpus's source certificates.  ERM survives only if
that coupled reflexive production law yields withheld cross-organ theorems.  Otherwise it is a
reinterpretation of free completion plus counterexample refinement.

## 7. Research programme

1. **Carrier-genesis exchange.** Classify exact and lax base change of fine/coarse repair under
   conservative extensions; then add admission and topology.
2. **Source-normal effective production.** Generalize anti-diagonal birth beyond Boolean
   self-evaluation while separately proving which old semantic fibre is split and which legal
   intervention becomes impossible.
3. **Certificate functoriality.** Determine which certificates survive genesis, which transgress,
   and which force withdrawal of old law facts.
4. **Cofinal representability.** Characterize when finite representers globalize and when the
   successor/formation commutator is unavoidable.
5. **Adaptive genesis.** Let failure-born contexts alter later legal grammar and prove a
   nontermination or recurrence theorem.
6. **Three-organ withheld test.** Use one predeclared mechanism to predict a theorem in three
   nonisomorphic corpus organs without changing definitions.

## 8. Death gate

Retire ERM as universal field if every instance requires fitting the challenge, representer, or
certificate after inspecting a route endpoint; if ULF-ERM-1 remains only ordinary base-change
calculus with no recursive consequence; or if the five frontier classes require unrelated genesis
laws after source typing.
