## 1. Frontier-forced pivot

The current RH frontier has eleven independent lettered routes plus CCM-FU. Their first open
premises fall into five classes:

1. singular source descent;
2. all-prime oscillatory transport;
3. adaptive spectral orientation;
4. whole-space coercivity;
5. positive carrier existence.

No scalar defect, curvature metaphor, common carrier, or route gauge can identify these classes.
The repeated fact is different: each route already possesses many valid objects and receivers, but
its currently admitted transformations cannot **produce the next required law**.

This programme therefore asks for a mechanism, not a universal object:

\[
\boxed{
\text{admission}
\to\text{execution}
\to\text{endogenous challenge}
\to\text{failure}
\to\text{repair synthesis}
\to\text{new admission}
\to\text{new challenge}.}
\tag{ALM.1}
\]

The loop is autopoietic when its outputs become ingredients or catalysts for future instances of
the same loop. The field question is which laws govern this feedback, when it closes, when it
remains open, and what observers erase its causal depth.

## 2. Frankenstein parentage

The intended theory combines established mechanisms but changes their interpretation.

| Parent mathematics | Imported mechanism | ALM reinterpretation |
|---|---|---|
| critical-pair and proof completion | failure generates conservative inference | failed proof manufactures future legality |
| RAF/autocatalytic networks | products catalyze reactions that reproduce the network | admitted laws catalyze future law formation |
| chemical organization theory | closure and self-maintenance are independent | deductive closure does not imply viable proof dynamics |
| operads, rewriting, proof theory | contextual plugging and normalization | challenge and repair are higher-order composable processes |
| sheaves, descent, obstruction theory | local compatibility need not globalize | locally legal proofs may lack globally admitted execution |
| abstract interpretation and closure theory | observers are idempotent reflectors | semantic completion can erase operational formation depth |
| convexity, positivity, Krein theory | cones and signs define viable chambers | positivity tests survival; it does not manufacture selection |

| topology and dynamical systems | sequential order, recurrence, lifted history | closed observation may hide open production lineage |
| differential algebra and HT | unbounded prolongation freedom | no bounded law metabolism exhausts future challenges |

RAF theory formalizes reflexively autocatalytic, food-generated reaction sets; chemical
organization theory separates closure from mass maintenance. Those are parents, not names pasted
onto proofs. ALM must add changing admission, semantic observers, challenge generation, and proof
lineage. Primary entry points include
[Hordijk--Steel on self-generating autocatalytic networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11286130/)
and
[Dittrich--Speroni di Fenizio on chemical organization theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/q-bio/0501016).

## 3. Proposed laws

These are programme laws. Laws III--V have two exact calibrations; Law VI has a third exact
Lean-certified calibration.

### Law I -- source food is not target truth

Every production history begins with a declared food layer: source operations, marks, coefficient
streams, domains, and legal interventions available without using the desired conclusion. A target
receiver, fitted orientation, or post-zero object is not food. Changing the food layer changes the
mechanism and must be recorded.

### Law II -- challenges are endogenous

The relevant failures are generated by compositions currently admitted by the system. An arbitrary
external counterexample is diagnostic, not constitutive. A live repair theory must prove which
challenge appears from its own execution language.

### Law III -- conservative repair can change execution without changing semantics

A failed challenge may determine a repair already valid in an older semantic envelope but not yet
executable. Admitting it can leave represented equivalence or ordinary closure unchanged while
strictly enlarging legal histories.

### Law IV -- repair internality makes failure hereditary

An admitted repair becomes a legal term, rule, limit, context, or catalyst in future histories. A
new failure is hereditary when its complete witness uses a newly admitted repair output. Repair is
then causal, not bookkeeping.

### Law V -- formation rank is independent information

An idempotent semantic observer can be stationary while native operational repair has positive,
unbounded, or transfinite generation depth. Any quotient that remembers only terminal closure
forgets how laws became executable.

### Law VI -- viability is independent of closure

A collection of laws may be deductively closed yet fail to be self-maintaining under its cost,
domain, positivity, or compactness constraints. Viability requires a resource law ensuring that
execution does not consume unavailable domain, sign, mass, capacity, or regularity.

Positivity is therefore not universally a phase quotient. In ALM it is one possible viability
doctrine. A faithful positive channel can erase nothing; scalar norm or Gram reduction loses phase
only because that particular observer was chosen.

### Law VII -- selection needs a catalyst not supplied by failure

Failure can constrain possible repairs without choosing one. Selection requires a source mark,
conserved reporter, directed cost, asymmetry, resource gradient, or other independently produced
catalyst. Declaring the desired repair minimal after seeing the target is circular.

### Law VIII -- recurrence concerns phenotype; lineage concerns production

Return of an observed state says nothing by itself about its lifted history. Open history requires
a marked cover, extension, filtration, or accumulated cost. Recurrence becomes constitutive only
when the mechanism constructs that lift and proves how repairs act on it.

### Law IX -- hypertranscendence is challenge inexhaustibility, not noncompactness

HT does not imply a noncompact observer orbit: the corpus contains HT sources with compact
resolvent and precompact recurrent GNS orbit. The plausible ALM role of HT is different. Unbounded
differential-prolongation rank prevents any bounded-arity, bounded-depth law metabolism from
exhausting all future challenges. This requires a typed theorem and is not yet proved universally.

### Law X -- five frontier classes are five metabolic deficits

| Frontier class | Missing production mechanism |
|---|---|
| singular descent | admitted transport/catalyst across doctrine boundary |
| oscillatory transport | collective phase-coherent reaction unavailable from local absolute rules |
| adaptive orientation | symmetry-breaking selector tied to challenge that chose the face |
| whole-space coercivity | resource-maintenance law controlling every escape history |
| positive carrier existence | nucleation/gluing process producing viable carrier, not merely determining it |

This table is a research typing, not a theorem that one formalism already realizes all five.

## 4. Exact certified calibrations

### 4.1 Rewriting metabolism -- ULF-ECMPL-1

For rules `a b^n a -> b^n a`, every finite execution terminates but every nonempty finite rulebook
is nonconfluent. The missing joining rule is already in old semantic equivalence. Admitting it is a
strict executable change with stationary semantics, and the repair overlaps the persistent seed to
generate the next failure.

Lean: `HTGeometry.ConstitutiveCriticalCompletionMechanics`.

### 4.2 Topological metabolism -- ULF-ECMPL-TOPO-1

In the Arens two-storey topology, leaf rows converge to branch points, branch points converge to the
root, and no leaf-only sequence converges directly to the root. Native sequential closure has two
strict generations while ordinary closure is already the whole space at generation zero. The
second witness consists entirely of first-generation outputs.

Lean: `HTGeometry.ConstitutiveSequentialRepairHeredity`.

### 4.3 What these prove and do not prove

They prove Laws III--V occur in two nonisomorphic mathematical settings, one with rule birth and one
with a fixed native topology. They do not prove that every frontier stall is autocatalytic, that a
selected repair exists, that viable completion follows, or that one category encodes the corpus.

### 4.4 Viability--coercivity metabolism -- ULF-ALM-1

For a linear execution boundary `B`, adjoining the dependent repair `r=-B(c)` leaves `im B`
unchanged but splits one new kernel/history direction. When the old rules are a basis and `c` has
strictly positive coordinates, the repair creates a unique normalized full-support nonnegative
circulation. Simultaneously, the old strict progress functional ceases to exist on the enlarged
support. Repetition of the circulation gives arbitrarily long positive-cost histories with zero
semantic endpoint, so the repair itself manufactures the next challenge.

Paper theorem:
`conservative-repair-viability-coercivity-transmutation-and-open-history-law.md`.
Lean: `HTGeometry.ConstitutiveViabilityCoercivityTransmutation`.

## 5. Hostile countermodels

1. **Idempotent repair.** Ordinary closure satisfies `C(C(A))=C(A)` and has no hereditary layer by
   itself. Extensivity alone does not create law depth.
2. **Arbitrary repair oracle.** Declaring one new operation at each stage can realize any history.
   Without endogenous challenge and source-certified synthesis, ALM is theatre.
3. **Selection collapse.** The same failure may admit quotient, central, singular, or history
   repairs. Failure heredity does not choose among them.
4. **Positive identity.** A faithful positive identity channel preserves every phase. Positivity
   does not universally imply phase erasure.
5. **Compact HT orbit.** HT can coexist with compact resolvent and precompact recurrence. HT is not
   topological noncompactness.
6. **Recurrent compact lift.** An irrational rotation observed through the identity has a compact
   recurrent lift; through the universal cover it has unbounded lift. Observation does not choose
   lineage.
7. **Semantic equivalence without viable execution.** Rewriting completion can preserve equations
   while failing to terminate as a completion process. Closure is not self-maintenance.

Any universal statement contradicted by one of these is rejected, not repaired by extra nouns.

## 6. Research programme a field-builder should run

### Programme A -- abstract the feedback without trivial encoding

Find axioms characterizing endogenous challenge, certified repair, admission, and hereditary
generation. Prove a representation theorem only if it preserves formation rank and excludes the
oracle encoding of arbitrary transition systems.

### Programme B -- generalize the certified viability laboratory

The finite linear laboratory now proves that a conservative repair can become self-maintaining
while destroying strict progress. The next task is to determine which part survives in nonlinear,
infinite, or changing-admission systems, and whether resource law can select the repair before its
consequences are observed. It must not be an RH route.

Candidate parents: reaction networks with changing catalysts, domain-sensitive operator closure,
or positive measures whose support is generated by earlier weak limits.

### Programme C -- prove repair/observer exchange laws

For a native repair `S` and observer `Q`, classify:

\[
 Q S = Q,
 \qquad
 Q S = \bar S Q,
 \qquad
 Q S \ne \bar S Q,
 \tag{ALM.2}
\]

as semantic stationarity, faithful descent, and observer-induced false closure. Determine which
formation ranks survive each case.

### Programme D -- derive resource selection rather than postulate it

Attach costs or viability cones before repair choice. Ask whether the failure-generated candidate
is the unique repair preserving source food, lineage, and self-maintenance. A successful theorem
must state its falsifier and show a competing repair violates one predeclared law.

### Programme E -- test transfinite law metabolism

Replace finite repair stages by ordinal iteration. Compare sequential order, completion ordinals,
operadic depth, differential prolongation rank, and proof-theoretic ordinal. Determine which notions
are invariant under semantic observers and which are presentation artefacts.

### Programme F -- return to corpus only after mechanism predicts

No route-specific work counts here until an ALM law predicts one previously unproved structural
fact in at least three mechanism families. Routes are laboratories after prediction, not sources of
the field vocabulary.

## 7. Fields-medalist questions

1. What is produced: truths, proofs, rules, domains, observers, or future challenges?
2. Which products become catalysts for the same production mechanism?
3. What is freely available source food, and what has been smuggled in from target?
4. Which conservation laws survive repair, and which resources are consumed?
5. Is semantic closure self-maintaining, or merely extensionally complete?
6. What is minimal repair relative to a predeclared order, and is that order source-derived?
7. Does local termination coexist with open meta-completion?
8. What ordinal measures formation depth, and can an observer recover it?
9. Which failures are absence of catalyst, absence of resource, absence of selector, or absence of
   a viable carrier?
10. Can one force a phase transition from non-generative to autocatalytic proof dynamics?
11. Can different proof routes be shown to be distinct reaction pathways with a common conserved
   law charge, rather than merely renamed endpoints?
12. What theorem would falsify the entire autopoietic interpretation?

## 8. Acceptance gate

ALM becomes more than an organizing metaphor only after all of the following:

1. abstract feedback laws exclude arbitrary transition-oracle encoding;
2. a third nonisomorphic laboratory couples repair to viability or coercivity
   (**Lean-certified by ULF-ALM-1**);
3. one theorem predicts a withheld fact in at least three corpus mechanism families;
4. selection is derived from predeclared source resource, not fitted target;
5. significant dependency-closed theorems are formalized in Lean under the Universal Law Field
   umbrella.

Until then, honest status is a mechanism-first research programme with three certified
calibrations and open anti-theatre gates.
