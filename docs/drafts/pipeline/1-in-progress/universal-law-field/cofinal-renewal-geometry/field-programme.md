## 1. Pivot

The corpus does not lack another obstruction object.  It repeatedly forms a correct local observer
and then loses it through one of three asymptotically equivalent mechanisms:

1. admitted source mass tends to zero;
2. inverse conditioning or reporter cost tends to infinity;
3. normalization transports information into a completion boundary.

Treating these as unrelated route failures is the wrong level.  The proposed field studies laws of
**formation under cofinal renewal**: how much source-law change is required to keep an observer
admitted while its native aperture evacuates.

The geometry is not built from points and a metric.  Its local charts are probability laws, its
vertical directions are Radon--Nikodym interventions, its length is relative entropy, and its
completion boundary consists of histories with infinite accumulated renewal action.  This is a
Frankenstein of information geometry, large deviations, source formation, pro-topology, and
proper-action mechanics.  Each ingredient is established mathematics; the new interpretation is
their use as one formation/selection mechanism across the corpus.

## 2. Primitive grammar

```text

A_n        admitted aperture

W_n        observer work inside A_n
```

Derived quantities are


The field is governed by mechanisms, not a universal carrier.

## 3. Laws

### Law I — formation disintegration

Every renewal splits exactly into aperture creation and conditional internal transport:

```text
D(nu || mu)
 = d_B(alpha || epsilon)
   + alpha D(nu_A || mu_A)
   + (1-alpha) D(nu_Ac || mu_Ac).
```

This is the exact conservation law.  It prevents moving information between admission and internal
work without paying a typed term.

### Law II — minimal renewal

For fixed renewed admission `alpha`, the unique least-action intervention preserves both
conditional laws and changes only the event odds.  Thus the binary information projection is the
normal direction to the aperture stratum.

### Law III — cofinal action

Local costs do not disappear under refinement.  They add:

```text
A = sum_n D(nu_n || mu_n).
```

Finite-action histories are the admitted interior.  Infinite-action histories are completion
boundary points, even when every finite projection exists.

### Law IV — evacuation/work reciprocity

For any source gauge `phi(n)` whose exponential partition function is finite, finite global KL
forces `sum phi(n) alpha_n<infinity`.  Persistent current therefore requires

```text
sum phi(n)/W_n < infinity.
```

For stretched-exponential admission `exp(-beta n^gamma)` and polynomial work `O(n^d)`, the exact
critical surface is

```text
d = gamma+1.
```

Ordinary exponential evacuation has `gamma=1`, so degree two is the wall and cubic is the first
integer entrance.  Gaussian evacuation has `gamma=2`, so quartic is first.

### Law V — properness

On the product schedule space, total binary renewal action is lower semicontinuous with compact
sublevels.  Selection is exclusion from every finite-action sublevel, not a fitted scalar equation.

### Law VI — source naturality

A candidate arithmetic renewal is valid only if `nu_n` is constructed from pre-zero coefficient
operations, respects prime labels and multiplicities, commutes with the functional equation before
specialization, and does not use the target Xi zero.  This law is a gate, not yet a theorem.

### Law VII — global entropy-height properness

When cofinal apertures are disjoint events of one source law, one global renewal with finite KL
action satisfies

```text
lambda E_nu N <= D(nu||mu)+log E_mu exp(lambda N).
```

An exponentially evacuated source has a finite height partition function, hence finite KL forces
finite first aperture moment.  This one-law theorem recovers the cubic entrance wall without
summing unrelated scale-wise divergences.

## 4. First theorem and new leverage

The first Stage-2 anchor proves Laws I--V and the sharpness of Law IV.  Its source-horizontal corollary
says that any renewal maintaining nonzero adaptive information in the exact canonical abelian
aperture pays asymptotically at least `c(2-a)/(2a)` KL per forward scale and `c/a` per reverse
scale.  Both total actions are infinite.

The global Stage-2 anchor proves Laws IV and VII in coordinate-free form.  It replaces independent scale-wise interventions by one
source law `mu`, one renewal `nu`, and one KL action.  Finite action forces
`sum n nu(A_n)<infinity`; persistent `O(n^d)` current is impossible for `d<=2`, and one explicit
global renewal shows sharpness for `d>2`.  More generally it proves the exact
`work degree = evacuation degree + 1` phase diagram for stretched-exponential gauges.  It also
proves functional-equation and common-recovery neutrality of this properness law.

This changes the frontier:

- another fixed finite observer cannot work;
- another linear information amplifier cannot work;
- a quadratic metric/capacity amplifier cannot work;
- a finite-action compensator must be at least cubic, nonpolynomial, or non-probability-preserving;
- source selection can be unlocked by proving a finite-action theorem, rather than guessing a
  vanishing relation directly.

## 5. Frozen source conjecture

### CRG-A — source-generated finite-action renewal

The completed pre-zero arithmetic coefficient source canonically determines one global source law
`mu`, a pairwise-disjoint cofinal aperture tower `(A_n)`, and one renewed law `nu` satisfying all of
the following before Xi specialization:

1. coefficient fidelity, prime labels, and divisor multiplicities are retained;
2. functional-equation reversal transports the renewal history without changing total action;
3. global KL action `D(nu||mu)` is finite;
4. the admitted current is the same horizontal source current read by the abelian, Gamma/Stokes,
   and projective/Route-U organs.

If the common current is uniformly positive and its work is at most quadratic, the global Stage-2
theorem contradicts item 3.  Therefore CRG-A plus a source-current comparison would force current
neutrality.

This conjecture is deliberately stronger than “find a relation.”  It asks for an intervention law,
a conservation identity, and a global properness certificate.

### Falsifiers

- `nu_n` depends on a chosen Xi zero or on the statement `Re rho=1/2`;
- global action finiteness is postulated rather than derived from source coefficients;
- apertures are declared disjoint without a source-native formation map;
- functional-equation compatibility holds only after scalar specialization;
- the common current is obtained by matching formulas rather than a natural comparison;
- a source-derived counterexample has finite action and nonzero linear/quadratic current.

## 6. Research programme

### Gate A — construct source renewal

Search coefficient operations that genuinely change aperture mass: normalized convolution,
prime-infinity transfer, signed/source-star transport, or a non-mass-preserving cobordism.  Fixed
flags, ordinary Gamma-six multiplication, and Big-Witt relocation are already ruled out.

### Gate B — build one disjoint source tower

Embed all cofinal apertures as pairwise-disjoint events of one source probability law and prove the
exact exponential height-moment bound.  This is stronger and cleaner than declaring a sum of
independent local actions.  If disjointness is impossible, replace it by a stopping-time or marked
path-space construction and prove the corresponding occupation formula.

### Gate C — identify one current in three organs

Construct natural maps from the same source renewal into:

1. abelian aperture admission;
2. projective/curvature work;
3. Stokes or spectral boundary discharge.

Prove one exact balance, not three numerical analogies.

### Gate D — predict and verify a fourth organ

The current theorem predicts a cubic entrance wall.  Test whether a genuinely source-derived cubic
prolongation carries finite action; do not infer this from the existence of a cubic observable.

### Gate E — selection

Only after Gates A--D: show off-center source current is persistent and at most quadratic, while
source action is finite.  The properness theorem then excludes the off-center history.

This is a research interpretation, not a theorem about Xi.  Its value is falsifiability: construct
the source renewal and measure its action, or kill the programme.
