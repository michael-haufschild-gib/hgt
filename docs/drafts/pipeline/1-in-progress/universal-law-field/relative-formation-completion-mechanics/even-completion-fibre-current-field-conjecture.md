## 2. Ontological reversal

The familiar completed functional equation is usually treated as an intrinsic symmetry of one
function.  This programme instead treats it as a quotient operation

\[
  \text{directed source formation}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{reflection-even completion}.
  \tag{RFCM.1}
\]

The primitive is not a zero, a curvature tensor, a route endpoint, or a chosen norm.  It is the
**formation/completion exchange law**: a directed source experiment has a positive symmetric work
and an orientation current; even completion preserves the first and transfers the second into a
relative fibre.

For a differentiable source potential (A), center (c), and half-displacement (a), put

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 S_A(c,a)&=\frac{A'(c+a)-A'(c-a)}2,\\
 H_A(c,a)&=a\frac{A'(c+a)+A'(c-a)}2
          -\frac{A(c+a)-A(c-a)}2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{RFCM.2}
\]

The frozen conjecture is:

> **Relative formation-completion conjecture.**  The literal pre-zero arithmetic completion admits
> a source-normal relative realization in which (S_A) is the completed positive work and (H_A)
> is the complete formation current lost by reflection evenization.  The global source law forces
> this relative current to be both retained and null in the completed physical fibre.  Positive
> source chronology then permits this only at zero transverse displacement.

The conjecture does not postulate a new annihilating class.  It predicts a mechanism: form,
even-complete, record the lost current, and impose the source law before specialization.

directed source works must annihilate (H_A).  Therefore a positive-jump source with strict

This is falsified by one explicit off-neutral realization that is simultaneously:
