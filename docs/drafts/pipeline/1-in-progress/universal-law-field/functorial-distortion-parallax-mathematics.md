## 1. Primitive

Take an enriched source category \(\mathcal C\), an observer category \(\mathcal D\), and a lossy
functor

\[
F:\mathcal C\longrightarrow\mathcal D.
\tag{1}
\]

Do not assume \(\mathcal C\) is a hidden reality reconstructed by \(\mathcal D\). The primitive is
the **parallax profile of \(F\)**:

- sizes, topology, and higher structure of fibres;
- minimal lifting cost and its growth;
- failure of sections to be natural, continuous, coarse, or exact;
- monodromy or hysteresis of lifted observer cycles;
- kernels born under completion or doctrine change;
- response rank under interventions transverse to the fibres.

Objects are secondary. The field studies what mathematical information a transition destroys and
how that loss composes.

## 2. First exact law

`unbounded-observer-fibre-uniform-reconstruction-and-serial-irreversibility-law.md` proves

\[
\inf_s\sup_x d(x,sF(x))
=\sup_y\operatorname{rad}(F^{-1}(y)),
\tag{2}
\]

for surjective metric observers. Serial observation can only increase this obstruction. This one law
simultaneously covers finite recurrence/open integer lift, stable cancellation of positive defects,
closed phase/open logarithm, and infinite hidden Hilbert fibres.

## 3. Reinterpretation

- proof route: observer functor or reconstruction protocol;
- no-go: infinite reconstruction radius, nontrivial extension fibre, or absent coherent section;
- positivity: a particular lossy functor, not a universal ontology;
- phase quotient: compact fibre or covering transition;
- recurrence/open history: periodic image with unbounded lifting profile;
- hypertranscendence: conjecturally, infinite response/realization rank of the observer's transverse
  jet profile;
- completion wall: discontinuous change in fibre topology or radius;
- physical interior/complexity: minimal cost of lifting a recurrent coarse history.

## 4. Why this passes the independent-slot no-go

Fibre topology, cost, monodromy, and response are not freely chosen coordinates if all are derived
from one enriched functor. Their exchange laws come from composition, base change, and lifting.

However, declaring arbitrary enrichments on \(F\) would recreate the product-container failure.
The source must determine the enrichment and admissible reconstruction class.

## 5. Bold conjecture — parallax completeness

There exists a finite list of functorial constructions on a suitably enriched observer functor
\(F\)—fibre object, lifting-energy filtration, cyclic transition memory, and transverse response
complex—whose joint equivalence class is complete for the source distinctions relevant to every
legal experiment, while each individual construction is incomplete.

This is falsifiable. It fails if two legal source systems have identical full parallax profiles but
different intervention responses or proof costs.

### Verdict for passive profiles

`[FALSIFIED]`. `observationally-identical-law-systems-and-intervention-separation-law.md` gives two
oppositely directed causal law machines with identical complete passive joint law—and therefore
identical passive statistics—while one elementary intervention separates them. A passive enriched

### Stronger replacement

Adjoin a **generated legal experiment family**, not all possible responses. The exact theorem
`passive-fibre-size-hidden-dimension-and-minimal-experiment-law.md` couples passive fibre size to
minimum experiment capacity and proves in linear systems

\[
\operatorname{edim}(q_0)=\dim\ker q_0.
\]

This passes the previous killing mechanism because intervention response is no longer inferred from
passive fibres; its minimum required size is constrained by them.

## 6. Hostile attacks

1. **Metric arbitrariness.** A bounded remetrization can turn infinite fibre diameter finite. Only a
   source-selected coarse structure can carry meaning.
2. **Extension blindness.** Bounded fibres can still support split and nonsplit laws.
3. **Tautological completeness.** Including “all legal responses” makes the profile Yoneda-complete
   by definition and predicts nothing.
4. **Known parents.** Coarse quotient maps, enriched categories, metric fibrations, persistence,
   descent, and system identification occupy the components.
5. **No common object.** Some shadows may not arise from one \(F\) at all; then the non-objectal
   version must use transition data among shadows.

## 7. Stronger acceptance gate

The candidate advances only after one source-independent theorem couples three *different levels* of
the parallax profile—for example, fibre growth forcing a response-rank bound and a recurrence-lift
lower bound. Merely collecting those invariants fails.
