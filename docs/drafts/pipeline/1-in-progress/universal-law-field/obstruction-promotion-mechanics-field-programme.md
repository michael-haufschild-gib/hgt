- **ID:** ULF-OPM-1
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** NEW-FIELD MECHANISM / FIELDS-MEDALIST PROGRAMME
- **Claim:** Mathematical failure is not terminal data attached to an object. A typed failure can
  become the generator of the next law. The universal problem is to classify and control that
  promotion process.
- **Status:** [MECHANISM NOT OBJECT] [FRANKENSTEIN SYNTHESIS] [ROUTES EXCLUDED FROM FOUNDATIONS]

## 1. Founding question

Do not ask for the object behind all proof routes. Ask:

> Which laws turn a failed mathematical compatibility into a new legal operation, relation,
> coherence, intervention, or completion, and when does that self-revision terminate?

This changes the unit of research. A no-go is not primarily an invariant. It is a **production
signal**. The proposed field studies the cycle

\[
  \text{execute laws}
  \longrightarrow \text{expose residual}
  \longrightarrow \text{promote residual}
  \longrightarrow \text{change legal laws}
  \longrightarrow \text{execute again}.
  \tag{OPM.1}
\]

Law-Saturation Mechanics is the strict idempotent sector of this cycle. Homotopy transfer,
completion procedures, obstruction towers, renormalization, and counterexample-guided refinement
are other sectors. None is declared universal in isolation.

## 2. Eight proposed laws

### Law 0 -- independence produces no prediction

If doctrine slots can be relabelled independently, every invariant coupling is trivial. A field
cannot be a tuple of relation, positivity, topology, recurrence, cost, and differential rank. A
prediction requires a production law linking them.

### Law 1 -- exposure

Executing currently legal laws against a declared intervention or doctrine produces a **typed
residual** whenever the expected compatibility fails. Examples of types include a critical pair,
failed descent, extension class, missing domain, nonclosed range, higher operation, or divergent
counterterm. The residual type is part of the theorem; the word "curvature" is not a default.

### Law 2 -- promotion

A residual can be promoted only by a universal operation appropriate to its type:

- add a relation or rewrite rule;
- attach a cell or nullhomotopy;
- adjoin a higher-arity operation;
- refine an observer or intervention family;
- enlarge a domain or change completion;
- introduce a compensating sector.

Promotion is successful only if it cancels the residual family it was constructed from and makes a
withheld prediction. Merely storing the residual as another coordinate does not count.

### Law 3 -- feedback

Promotion changes which later operations descend, which distinctions remain observable, and which
new residuals can be formed. Therefore law genesis is nonlinear and order-sensitive. The next
residual is computed in the revised rulebook, not in the original one.

### Law 4 -- coherent higher birth

Failure of a strict law may be promoted to a higher law. Failure of its coherence is promoted one
level higher. In homotopy-algebraic charts the entire hierarchy obeys one square-zero or
Maurer--Cartan master identity; in rewriting charts it is higher critical-pair coherence. Pairwise
compatibility does not imply total compatibility unless the relevant higher identity is proved.

### Law 5 -- charge migration, not magical erasure

A source reporter natural for every admitted promotion is conserved through every finite promotion
history. A charged obstruction can disappear only by:

1. migration to a compensating layer;
2. failure of reporter extension, continuity, or admission;
3. abandonment of a stated law.

Observation can hide a residual. Hiding is not promotion and is not resolution.

### Law 6 -- saturation and open genesis

Iterated fair promotion seeks a rulebook with no exposed promotable residuals. Its invariants are
promotion depth, minimal intervention arity, critical-pair spectrum, closure ordinal, and surviving
charges. Every finite observer may stabilize while the promotion tower remains open. A compact or
recurrent shadow therefore says nothing by itself about termination.

### Law 7 -- directed work

Promotion has directed cost because it creates legal structure. Reversible equivalence carries no
nonnegative additive production cost. Cost belongs to histories of law formation, not endpoint
distance, and must be derived from source generators rather than fitted after success.

### Law 8 -- withheld prediction

A proposed mechanism is accepted only when a promoted law predicts a response not used to define
the promotion and the prediction survives in a second, structurally different laboratory. This is
the operational distinction between theory and redescription.

## 3. Three universality classes to classify

### Class A -- strict completion

Critical failures are promoted to new equations or rewrite rules. Buchberger completion,
Knuth--Bendix completion, congruence generation, and closure saturation are parents. Main questions:
termination, confluence, fairness, minimal generators, and closure ordinal.

### Class B -- homotopy-coherent promotion

Strict descent is impossible or undesirable. A failed identity becomes a higher operation, and the
next coherence failure becomes a still higher one. Homological perturbation, transferred
`A_infinity`/`L_infinity` laws, Postnikov towers, and Massey products are parents. Main questions:
minimality, truncation, gauge independence, cyclic/Real transfer, and convergence.

### Class C -- singular promotion

The missing law cannot be added inside the current admitted category. Promotion changes topology,
domain, bornology, completion, or coefficient doctrine. Main questions: strictness, continuity,
coframe-extension anomaly, limit exchange, and whether the promoted law is source-canonical.

These classes may interact. The field problem is to find laws controlling their exchange, not to
select one favourite carrier.

## 4. Exact evidence already available

1. **Strict exposure and minimal repair.** Unary observer descent fails exactly when the observer
   kernel is not operation-invariant; the least congruence repair is universal and Lean-certified.
2. **Order-sensitive saturation.** Two source formations can require infinitely many alternating
   repairs while every finite reporter stabilizes; exact word depth is Lean-certified.
3. **No higher defect in the strict commuting sector.** Pairwise commuting idempotent formations
   have identical three-fold schedules. Genuine higher birth therefore requires weak, partial,
   non-idempotent, or extension-valued promotion.
4. **Higher obstruction promotion.** The certified filtered Maurer--Cartan and cyclic
   `A_infinity` corpus proves exact extension-by-next-law criteria, higher obstruction spectra, and
   one master square-zero identity. Those are calibrations of Class B, not the universal field.
5. **Observer hiding versus mechanism change.** Passive/interventional separation, positive
   response coframes, split/nonsplit carry, and completion-port theorems prove that endpoint erasure
   does not determine promotion.

## 5. What this reinterpretation says about a stuck proof programme

A route reaches a wall when its current rulebook has saturated: every operation it can legally
compose remains inside the same closure class. Searching harder for another object inside that class
cannot create the missing law. There are only four serious moves:

1. expose a residual that the route had quotiented away;
2. promote it to a higher operation or coherence;
3. change the admission doctrine in a source-controlled way;
4. prove a no-promotion theorem and abandon the route.

The repeated request for a "source-native missing object" can therefore be structurally wrong. The
source may instead supply a **promotion rule** saying how an already visible failure changes the
rulebook.

## 6. Fields-medalist discovery protocol

1. Remove route names and catalogue every wall as `(current laws, residual type, attempted
   promotion, survivor)`.
2. Build a genealogy of residuals: which failures repeatedly produce which next laws?
3. Find minimal countermodels agreeing on all endpoint invariants but differing in promotion.
4. Identify universal identities among promotion histories: chain rules, braid/coherence laws,
   conservation laws, and termination criteria.
5. Test three competing mechanisms in solved laboratories before touching RH.
6. Demand a converse and a sharp countermodel for every proposed law.
7. Formalize every significant paper survivor in Lean before using it as field law.
8. Reject the programme if promotion rules must be fitted independently in every laboratory.

## 7. Killer questions

1. What exact failure is the input to law genesis?
2. What universal operation turns that failure into a new law?
3. Which future failures does the promoted law forbid or predict?
4. Does promotion commute with observation, completion, and change of coefficients?
5. Is order memory strict, extension-valued, or higher-coherent?
6. What reporter charge migrates when the visible obstruction disappears?
7. What is the least intervention arity detecting the promoted law?
8. Does the promotion tower truncate, converge only after completion, or remain open?
9. Which different promotion histories yield equivalent effective law, and by what theorem?
10. What finite countermodel would kill the claimed universality?

## 8. Immediate research gates

1. Recast the certified strict and filtered-homotopy examples as instances of Laws 1--6 without
   importing their route interpretations.
2. Prove a mechanism theorem for **residual promotion under observation**: exact conditions under
   which promotion descends, is hidden, or forces observer refinement.
3. Construct one finite system in which strict completion, higher-law promotion, and singular
   admission are three responses to the same exposed residual.
4. Derive a withheld prediction distinguishing those responses before examining a corpus target.
5. Only then test the mechanism against arithmetic, spectral, recurrence, or physical organs.

## 9. Death criteria

Retire Obstruction--Promotion Mechanics if:

- every residual-to-law map is arbitrary auxiliary input;
- the proposed master identities are only inherited names from unrelated parent theories;
- no promotion rule predicts a withheld response;
- source marking does not distinguish genuine promotion from observer erasure;
- whole-corpus walls require independent mechanisms with no exchange law.
