## 1. Question

The fixed-polarity collapse theorem shows that repeated exact rule--test feedback cannot sustain open
law genesis while the compatibility relation remains fixed. The immediate repair is to let the
compatibility relation change with the current state.

That repair is too permissive. The next-state law can be hidden directly in compatibility. The word
"endogenous" then explains nothing.

The research target is therefore not an enlarged state object. It is a set of laws governing the
production, transport, and interaction of revisions.

## 2. Killed conjecture

> A state-dependent rule--test compatibility relation is by itself a mathematical mechanism for
> open law genesis.

False. Let `S` be any set of law states and let `N(s) subset S` be any prescribed transition
relation. Use one test `*`, and at state `s` declare

\[
  x\perp_s * \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x\in N(s).
\]

The exact polar of the universal one-test doctrine is then precisely `N(s)`. Thus every deterministic
map, nondeterministic graph, cycle, chaotic-looking orbit, or terminating process is representable.
The representation makes no prediction because compatibility already contains the answer.

The dependency-closed paper theorem and Lean certification are recorded in Stage 2/3 as
ULF-ECM-1.

## 3. Positive survivor: revision must have constitutive laws

A live theory must forbid arbitrary rewriting of compatibility. Candidate revisions

\[
  (\perp_n,\mathcal A_n,\mathcal T_n)\longmapsto
  (\perp_{n+1},\mathcal A_{n+1},\mathcal T_{n+1})
\]

must be generated from exposed failure witnesses and obey at least the following mechanism laws.

### Law A -- witness locality

A failure may change only compatibility pairs in its source-generated dependency cone. Two states
with isomorphic marked failure neighborhoods must have isomorphic local repairs.

**Falsifier:** a repair changes an unrelated compatibility pair with no transported dependency path.

### Law B -- minimal effective repair

Among admitted local changes that remove the marked failure, revision chooses a minimal change in a
declared resource order. If no unique minimum exists, the unresolved choice must remain as marked
branch history rather than being silently quotiented.

**Falsifier:** two incomparable minimal repairs are collapsed without a new mark, cost, or symmetry
breaking datum.

### Law C -- certification conservation

Previously certified consequences survive whenever their proofs transport through the revision.
Lost consequences require a typed broken transport cell. Revision may not erase knowledge merely by
changing presentation.

**Falsifier:** a certified consequence disappears although its entire proof diagram remains admitted
and commutative.

### Law D -- repair exchange

For independent witnesses, the two repair orders commute up to a specified comparison. For
interacting witnesses, the comparison residue is retained and changes the next admissible repair
problem. The residue is not called curvature by default; it is an explicit failure of repair-order
exchange.

**Falsifier:** order dependence is asserted without two typed composites, or independent repairs fail
to exchange.

### Law E -- genesis accounting

Every strict revision must spend or create a named resource: a new test kind, a new rule symbol, a
larger admission horizon, a broken certification, or retained branch history. Open genesis with
bounded signature, bounded resources, monotone compatibility, and no retained history is forbidden.

**Falsifier:** an infinite strict revision history occurs while every named resource and every
compatibility pair has stabilized.

## 4. Competing mechanism programs

### Program 1 -- failure-generated language growth

A witness creates the least new test/rule constructor capable of distinguishing the failed states.
This resembles counterexample-guided refinement and attribute exploration, but the open problem is
to make constructor birth, not merely candidate elimination, functorial and source-normalized.

### Program 2 -- resource-filtered polarity

Compatibility is fixed globally, but only bounded-cost tests are currently expressible. Revision
raises the horizon by the minimal amount exposed by failure. This can produce long histories without
arbitrary state-dependent relations. The danger is that an arbitrary filtration merely re-encodes
the desired dynamics.

### Program 3 -- marked symmetry-breaking repair

Unmarked equivariant repair must add an entire failure orbit. A marked witness can reduce the
stabilizer and thereby change future repair orbits. The danger is collision with the already-proved
independent-slot transitivity no-go; dynamic content exists only if the mark changes future
dependencies, not just names a representative.

### Program 4 -- conservative nonmonotone revision

Some old compatibility pairs may be withdrawn, but every withdrawal must carry a certificate of
which former proof transport broke. This is the only current candidate that permits open histories
on a bounded carrier without arbitrary rewriting. Its falsifier is a revision loop with zero net
broken-transport record.

## 5. Acceptance gate for the field mechanism

A proposed law system is accepted only if it proves all four:

1. **non-universality:** it cannot realize every transition graph by definition;
2. **non-collapse:** it admits a dependency-closed open or unbounded revision history;
3. **exchange prediction:** it decides when repair order commutes and computes a retained residue
   when it does not;
4. **cross-organ realization:** one source-normalized mechanism, fixed before route specialization,
   realizes at least three structurally different corpus organs.

Until these gates close, "universal law field" is a research program, not an established field.

## 6. Parent mechanisms and exact scope

Counterexample-guided refinement, formal concept/attribute exploration, reflective rewriting,
performative prediction, and automated theory repair all provide important parent mechanisms. None
by itself supplies the five laws above across heterogeneous mathematical proof doctrines. This card
does not claim priority for adaptive logic or learning. It uses their existence to prevent a
category-theoretic renaming exercise and to demand a sharper constitutive mechanism.

No RH route, endpoint, positivity form, topology, or physical interpretation is used here. Routes
enter only after the mechanism laws survive their abstract acceptance gate.
