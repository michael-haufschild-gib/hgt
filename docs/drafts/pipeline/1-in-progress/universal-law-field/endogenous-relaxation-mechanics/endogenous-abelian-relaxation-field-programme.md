## 1. Why this programme exists

The corpus has two exact laboratories in which a repair changes what can fail next while a coarser
semantic observer remains stationary.

1. Critical-pair completion: a conservative joining rule changes future executable overlaps.
2. Arens sequential repair: a newly admitted limit becomes a legal term of a later convergent
   sequence although ordinary closure was already complete.

Neither laboratory supplies all of the following at once:

- a source-generated legal move rather than a prewritten test list;
- schedule-independent completion;
- an exact conservation identity retaining the repair lineage;
- a positive source-derived work functional;
- recurrent finite observation with an open lifted history;
- a properness theorem turning bounded work into neutral selection.

Endogenous Abelian Relaxation Mechanics studies that conjunction.  Its primitive is the process

\[
 \boxed{
 \textsf{violation}\longrightarrow\textsf{legal local repair}
 \longrightarrow\textsf{redistributed violation}
 \longrightarrow\textsf{least global lineage}
 \longrightarrow\textsf{stable quotient}.}
 \tag{EARM.1}
\]

The first exact calibration is dissipative chip firing on a finite graph with a sink.  Sandpile
theory is established mathematics; the field claim is not priority over it.  The role of the
calibration is to expose one causal package that the existing ULF laboratories separated.

**Verdict:** `[FALSIFIED AS FIELD PIVOT]`.  The complete family of likelihood ratios is already a

**Survivor:** the current state must determine which repairs are legal, and executing one repair
must change the legality of later repairs.

### Conjecture B -- arbitrary local relaxation is enough

Let every failed test choose an arbitrary correction.

**Verdict:** `[FALSIFIED]`.  Arbitrary corrections can realize every transition graph and have no
withheld consequences.

**Survivor:** legality, transport, and cost must all come from one source operator.

### Conjecture C -- failure-controlled abelian relaxation closes the causal cycle

Let a dissipative source operator determine local instability, legal repair, conservation modulo
its image, and positive Green work.  Then:

1. every execution terminates;
2. least action makes the odometer independent of repair schedule;
3. stabilization is a conservative normal form modulo the repair lattice;
4. repeated source formation has a recurrent finite quotient and an open odometer lift;
5. every closed observer cycle has an exactly reconstructible positive work;
6. bounded cofinal work selects zero source formation.

**Verdict:** `[SURVIVES]`.  ULF-EARM-1 proves all six clauses for every finite connected undirected
multigraph with a sink.

**Verdict:** `[FALSIFIED]`.  Critical-pair completion is generally nonabelian, and the Arens repair

## 3. Candidate field laws

### Law I -- endogenous legality

The source state determines the currently legal local repairs.  A repair may create new legal
repairs by transporting violation to neighbouring sites.  A stage-indexed oracle is forbidden.

### Law II -- conservative exactification

Every completed execution must satisfy

\[
 \boxed{\text{input}=\text{stable output}+\partial(\text{repair lineage}).}
 \tag{EARM.2}
\]

The observer quotient by repair boundaries is stationary even when the oriented lineage is open.

### Law III -- least lineage

Among all nonnegative repair histories producing an admissible endpoint, the legal execution is
coordinatewise least.  This is stronger than endpoint confluence: it canonically reconstructs how
much of every primitive repair occurred.

### Law IV -- positive Green work

When the boundary operator is a positive dissipative Laplacian, its inverse converts source load
into a positive lineage metric.  Work is derived from the same operator that determines legality;
it is not appended after observing an escape.

### Law V -- recurrent quotient, open lineage

A finite quotient may recur while the least repair lineage grows without bound.  The decisive
datum is not merely that a lift exists, but that its holonomy and cost are reconstructible from the
source boundary operator.

### Law VI -- properness selection

If every nonzero nonnegative source formation has positive Green capacity, its repeated lineage
work diverges quadratically.  A uniform full-history work bound therefore forces the source
formation to vanish.
