## 1. Pivot

The previous contextual-transduction theorem closes only one round:

```text
marked failure -> one-hole context -> partially evaluated reporter -> challenge.
```

It does not explain repeated challenge birth. Saturating a fixed reporter under a fixed context
grammar produces the classical future reporter kernel of ULF-OPM-5. Once that saturation is
stable, adding another challenge already definable in the same grammar cannot restart genesis.

The correct higher question is therefore:

> How far into legal future execution must two presently indistinguishable states travel before a
> reporter separates them, and which events change that prospective separation law?

The answer is a mechanism-derived geometry. It is not declared independently.

## 2. Prospective filtration

Fix legal context generators, their finite words, and reporter `r`. For horizon `n`, write

\[
 x\equiv_n y
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 r(wx)=r(wy)\text{ for every legal word }w\text{ of length }<n.
 \tag{PCG.1}
\]

The relations `equiv_n` are nested equivalences. Their classes are the balls of a
non-Archimedean prospective geometry. The first word separating a pair is its challenge-birth
depth. The intersection over all horizons is the future reporter kernel.

ULF-PCG-1 proves the exact set-level laws:

1. context execution shifts the filtration by one level;
2. one exact plateau is permanent and equals the full future kernel;
3. postobservation can only delay or erase pairwise separation;
4. a coarse observer may nevertheless display a later apparent birth of information already
   present at the source;
5. finite-horizon realizability does not imply realization of the coherent infinite signature.

## 3. Proposed field laws

### Law A -- prospective valuation

The primitive quantitative datum is earliest legal future separation, not a norm chosen on the
state space. Exponentiating separation depth yields the usual prefix ultrametric when a numerical
gauge is wanted. The filtration is prior to that gauge.

### Law B -- context expansion

Adding legal context constructors can only refine prospective equivalence. A new constructor is
genuinely generative exactly when it fails to descend to the old prospective quotient after some
old outer context. Merely recomposing old contexts is conservative.

### Law C -- observer delay

Postobservation coarsens every prospective equivalence. It cannot make a pair distinguishable
earlier than the source reporter. Apparent observed birth is delayed release of an already-present
source distinction, not creation ex nihilo.

### Law D -- exact plateau rigidity

Under a fixed total context grammar, equality of two consecutive prospective equivalences forces
all later equivalences to be equal. Any exact late reappearance of an obstruction therefore proves
that context grammar, admission, reporter, or carrier changed.

### Law E -- completion birth

A coherent family of finite-horizon reports is a completion history. It is a source law only when
one source state realizes the whole family. Completion birth is the failure of this realization,
not another late finite challenge.

### Law F -- reflexive grammar

Open law genesis requires the current failure to change the legal context grammar itself. The
resulting geometries form a directed history

\[
 (\equiv_n^{(0)})_n \rightsquigarrow
 (\equiv_n^{(1)})_n \rightsquigarrow\cdots,
 \tag{PCG.2}
\]

with expansion from source grammar and contraction from observation. Classifying which grammar
changes are source-generated, conservative, admission-changing, or completion-born is the live
field problem.

## 4. Hostile conjecture loops

content is the coupled grammar-expansion / observer-contraction / completion-realization law.

### Loop 2 -- observation only erases challenge birth

`[FALSIFIED]` as a stagewise statement. A source reporter may distinguish two states immediately,
while a coarse observer identifies them until one future context exposes the hidden difference.

**Survivor:** first source separation never occurs later than first observed separation. Observer
birth is exact delay, with the hidden source pair as certificate.

### Loop 3 -- one stable finite horizon may reopen later

`[FALSIFIED]` for a fixed total grammar. Consecutive equality makes the current equivalence
invariant under every generator; maximality of the future reporter kernel then forces permanent
stability.

**Survivor:** late reappearance is a typed certificate of grammar, admission, reporter, or carrier
change.

### Loop 4 -- every coherent finite profile is a source state

`[FALSIFIED]`. Finite Boolean lists realize every finite prefix of the all-true stream, while no
finite list realizes that stream globally.

**Survivor:** completion-born histories are exactly coherent finite profiles outside the source
signature image.

### Loop 5 -- hypertranscendence is prospective noncompactness

`[FALSIFIED]` as a universal identification. A hypertranscendental family may have compact observed
dynamics, and noncompact state spaces need no differential transcendence.

**Survivor:** HT may instantiate unbounded prospective refinement only after a source-derived
prolongation grammar is constructed. It is not the definition.

### Loop 6 -- positivity is always a phase quotient

`[FALSIFIED]`. Faithful positive reporters need lose no information; scalar norms can lose phase.

**Survivor:** a positive realization is a contraction only after its exact reporter map is typed.

## 5. All-route leverage gate

Every proposed proof-route obstruction must now answer three route-independent questions:

1. Which finite legal context first separates the claimed pair?
2. If no finite context does, which coherent completion signature is not source-realized?
3. If a distinction disappears or reappears, which grammar, admission, carrier, or observer changed?

Consequences:

- singular descent must identify the partial/admission change invalidating total-context plateau;
- cofinal/all-prime mechanisms must exhibit unbounded separation depth or a non-realized signature;
- adaptive orientation must expose the grammar change selected by prior responses;
- whole-space coercivity must show nonproperness of bounded prospective-depth sublevels;
- positive scalarization must name the contracted reporter pairs;
- recurrence must distinguish compact observer returns from the source separation history;
- HT must provide a prolongation grammar whose prospective filtration does not stabilize.

This is diagnostic leverage, not a claim that those mechanisms are isomorphic.
