## 1. Higher question

The finite fusion theorem produces multiplication/formation, exterior inversion, ghost
differentiation, Adams intervention, and receiver evaluation from one marked packet.  The current
completion gate asks that all of them survive in one Hausdorff multiplicative topology.

That wording may still be wrong.  Differentiation is normally unbounded on a Banach algebra of
analytic functions, Adams substitution changes convergence radii, and the purely formal topology
admits arbitrary coefficient growth but almost no ordinary complex evaluations.  The candidate
field law is therefore not a preferred completed object.  It is a mechanism assigning a change of
completion scale to every legal operation:

\[
 \boxed{\text{operation order}\longleftrightarrow
        \text{loss or transport of receiver scale}.}
 \tag{FSR.1}
\]

Routes are excluded from the foundations.  They may later instantiate scale fibres or transition
maps only after the abstract laws are proved.

## 2. Corpus forcing

The full-frontier grammar already separates `form`, `ghost`, `dualize`, `admit`, and `conserve`.
The finite Witt theorem proves they are algebraically generated shadows, while conservative inverse
completion proves exact inversion survives joint convergence.  Three corpus walls remain:

1. formal or coefficientwise completion conserves the algebra but may erase analytic receivers;
2. positive/Hilbert completion admits receivers but can lose exterior or oriented directions;
3. cofinal source operations are often unbounded or domain-changing in every fixed norm.

Compact finite-depth exchange further proves that completion birth is forced into unbounded
formation depth.  These facts suggest a scale law rather than another completion object.

## 3. Competing conjectures

### Conjecture A — one faithful Banach algebra carries the full grammar

There is a unital Banach algebra containing a faithful polynomial formation coordinate `T`, with a
bounded derivation `D(T)=1`, bounded Adams substitutions, continuous evaluations, and conservative
exterior inversion.

**Attack target:** prove this impossible before using arithmetic.  If `D` is bounded, Leibniz gives
`D(T^n)=nT^(n-1)`; multiplication by `T` should force an operator-norm contradiction as `n` grows.

### Conjecture B — formal completion solves the admission problem

The `T`-adic completion `C[[T]]` preserves every formal inverse and therefore should receive the
analytic point evaluations needed by the proof organs.

**Attack target:** classify continuous complex characters of the `T`-adic completion.  Arbitrary
coefficient growth is expected to force every continuous character to send `T` to zero.

### Conjecture C — operations generate a scale of completions

For `R>0`, let

\[
 \mathcal W_R=\left\{f(T)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nT^n:
             \|f\|_R=\sum_{n\ge0}|a_n|R^n<\infty\right\}.
 \tag{FSR.2}
\]

The family `(W_R)`, not any one fibre, should carry the exact fusion:

1. multiplication is contractive within each fibre;
2. evaluation at `|z|<=R` is continuous and every continuous complex character is such an
   evaluation;
3. differentiation maps `W_R -> W_r` for `0<r<R`, with exact collar-loss estimate;
4. Adams substitution `Psi_q f(T)=f(T^q)` maps `W_R -> W_r` whenever `r^q<=R`;
5. the exchange laws

   \[
   D\Psi_q=qT^{q-1}\Psi_qD,
   \qquad \operatorname{ev}_z\Psi_q=\operatorname{ev}_{z^q}
   \tag{FSR.3}
   \]

   hold on every typed overlap;
6. formation/exterior conservation remains fibrewise exact whenever both sides are admitted.

This is a mechanism: each operation transports legal receiver resolution.  No state-dependent
topology is chosen after seeing a zero.

## 4. Withheld prediction

If Conjecture C is correct, the receiver organ is forced rather than appended:

> the continuous receiver spectrum of `W_R` is exactly the closed disc `|z|<=R`, and Adams
> intervention transports a receiver by `z -> z^q` while differentiation necessarily consumes a
> positive analytic collar.

Thus a claimed cofinal law that keeps ghost differentiation bounded in one faithful Banach fibre is
impossible.  A claimed Adams law that does not transform receiver aperture violates `(FSR.3)`.
This predicts exact admission failures in any fourth organ before its special functions enter.

m-convex analytification, and the Hadamard topology on rational Witt rings simultaneously refines
