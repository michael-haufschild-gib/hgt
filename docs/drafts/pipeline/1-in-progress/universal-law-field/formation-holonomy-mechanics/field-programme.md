# Formation Holonomy Mechanics

- **ID:** ULF-FHM-FIELD
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** FIELD PROGRAMME
- **Statement:** Proof-route organs are split formations over partially recovered source data; all comparisons factoring only through a common recovered base are flat in the split-idempotent corner, while every genuine fusion law, obstruction, and selection current is vertical transport or vertical composition defect.

## 1. Why this field is needed

The corpus repeatedly constructs:

1. a rich source or history object;
2. an observer/recovery map;
3. a section, reconstruction kernel, or packet formation;
4. an exact symmetry on recovered data; and
5. a failure to extend that symmetry into the information erased by recovery.

The repeated stall is not accidental. A common recovery split makes canonical inter-organ
comparisons algebraically flat. More endpoint identities, common quotients, or regenerated packets
cannot produce the missing law because they all factor through the same recovered base.

Formation Holonomy Mechanics studies the part that does **not** factor through that base.

## 2. Primitive grammar

An atlas consists of a source/recovered object \(X\) and formations

\[
 X\xrightarrow{f_i}Z_i\xrightarrow{p_i}X,
 \qquad p_if_i=1_X.
 \tag{FHM.1}
\]

The idempotent

\[
 e_i=f_ip_i
 \tag{FHM.2}
\]

is the regenerate-after-recovery operation. The canonical comparison between two formations is

\[
 T_{ji}=f_jp_i.
 \tag{FHM.3}
\]

It remembers only recovered data. In an additive chart the vertical formation space is

\[
 V_i=\ker p_i,
 \qquad Z_i\cong X\oplus V_i.
 \tag{FHM.4}
\]

A comparison \(C_{ji}:Z_i\to Z_j\) is first required to preserve recovered source:

\[
 p_jC_{ji}=p_i.
 \tag{FHM.5}
\]

Its independent content is an affine formation current and a vertical transport

\[
 a_{ji}=C_{ji}f_i-f_j:X\to V_j,
 \qquad
 L_{ji}:V_i\to V_j.
 \tag{FHM.6}
\]

The stricter equation \(C_{ji}f_i=f_j\) is exactly \(a_{ji}=0\).

## 3. Field laws

### Law I -- split formation

Formation is not assumed invertible. It is a split section/recovery pair. Regeneration is the
idempotent \(e_i\), not the ambient identity of \(Z_i\).

### Law II -- common-recovery flatness

Canonical comparisons obey

\[
 T_{kj}T_{ji}=T_{ki},
 \qquad T_{ij}T_{ji}=e_i.
 \tag{FHM.7}
\]

Thus they form a pair groupoid only after passing to the Karoubi corner where \(e_i\) is the local
identity. Common recovery alone has no holonomy.

### Law III -- affine vertical normal form

Every comparison satisfying (FHM.5) is exactly

\[
 C_{ji}=\begin{pmatrix}1_X&0\\a_{ji}&L_{ji}\end{pmatrix}
 \tag{FHM.8}
\]

in the split chart. Base motion is fixed; all law content is vertical. Composition is semidirect:

\[
 (a_{kj},L_{kj})\star(a_{ji},L_{ji})
 =\bigl(a_{kj}+L_{kj}a_{ji},L_{kj}L_{ji}\bigr).
\]

### Law IV -- vertical composition defect

For three organs define

\[
 \Omega_{kji}=C_{kj}C_{ji}-C_{ki}.
 \tag{FHM.9}
\]

Then

\[
 \Omega_{kji}=
 \begin{pmatrix}0&0\\
 a_{kj}+L_{kj}a_{ji}-a_{ki}&L_{kj}L_{ji}-L_{ki}
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 p_k\Omega_{kji}=0.
 \tag{FHM.10}
\]

No reporter factoring through \(p_k\) can see this defect.

### Law V -- four-organ associativity conservation

For four composable organs,

\[
 \boxed{
 C_{\ell k}\Omega_{kji}+\Omega_{\ell ki}
 =\Omega_{\ell kj}C_{ji}+\Omega_{\ell ji}.}
 \tag{FHM.11}
\]

This is forced by associativity, not postulated geometry. Its affine component contains the mixed
term “transport defect acting on generated current.” It is the minimal route-independent fusion
law: three-organ defects cannot be assigned independently once a fourth organ is added.

### Law VI -- positive vertical work

A positive doctrine supplies a work \(W_i\) on \(V_i\), never merely on recovered \(X\). A strict
selection theorem requires either:

- passive vertical transport around a split loop;
- a proper vertical work along cofinal formation; or
- a positive defect whose vanishing is source-proved.

Positivity on \(X\) alone cannot constrain vertical holonomy because the base block is already flat.

### Law VII -- recurrence/history separation

A base observer may close after a finite symmetry orbit while vertical transport remains nontrivial
or accumulates an open history. Closed observer recurrence is therefore the base projection of a
possibly open vertical loop.

### Law VIII -- hypertranscendental non-finite generation

In differential/prolongation charts, hypertranscendence is not declared to be topological
noncompactness. It is failure of the vertical transport system to be generated by any finite jet
fibre compatible with all comparison laws. This is an explicit future theorem target, not yet a
field axiom proved by the finite theorem.

## 6. First prediction

Suppose three route organs are compared through one claimed common source observer. If every
comparison is constructed solely as

\[
 \text{form target}\circ\text{recover source},
\]

then every triangle defect is zero in the corner and every loop is the local idempotent. Therefore
no RH selection law can arise from that comparison system. A successful comparison must construct
and retain at least one nonzero vertical transport \(L_{ji}\), and a fourth organ must satisfy
(FHM.11).

This prediction is falsifiable by exhibiting a common-recovery-only comparison with nontrivial
corner holonomy. The finite theorem proves that no such example exists.

## 7. Functional-equation correction

The already constructed packet lift is a canonical corner action. It is information-neutral because
it acts through split formation. The source-natural functional-equation history alternates the two
recovery kernels; shifting history transports one phase to the other for every parameter. Hence a
frozen stationary-history entropy mismatch is not source-natural holonomy.

Its correct classification is:

- the entropy difference is a vertical scalar coboundary and cancels on the complete reflection
  orbit;
- the symmetric recovery-change work can remain positive and accumulate;
- selection requires a source law controlling that vertical work, not endpoint symmetry.

## 8. RH-facing producer gate

An RH-producing realization must construct, before zero specialization:

1. at least three literal formations over one source-generated recovered base;
2. at least one vertical comparison not factoring through recovery;

6. a strict theorem identifying zero vertical work with the critical formation.

Only then may an existing RH receiver be applied.
