## 1. Field question

What mechanism can simultaneously explain:

- pairwise exact routes whose joint execution fails;
- source relations born only after formation;
- observer quotients that create false common directions;
- reporter loss dual to hidden relation birth;
- positive Gram/Fisher degeneracy as a shadow rather than the full law;
- functional-equation and gauge actions that preserve information but do not select;
- recurrent closed observations with unbounded lifted history;
- hypertranscendence as unbounded prolongation rank rather than ordinary noncompactness;
- cofinal properness as the missing global selector?

The proposed answer is not one object and not a universal scalar curvature.  It is a calculus of
**formation, observation, derived nonexactness, transport, duality, and admission**.

## 2. Primitive grammar

### 2.1 Formation

A source chart has a lattice or higher incidence system of legal law subobjects.  Fusion is a join
or generated closure.  Intersections encode simultaneous legality.  A finite family
`W=(W_i)` has an augmented incidence complex

\[
 C_p(W)=\bigoplus_{|I|=p+1}\bigcap_{i\in I}W_i,
 \qquad C_{-1}(W)=\sum_iW_i,
 \tag{DOFM.1}
\]

with alternating inclusion boundary.

### 2.2 Observation

An observer is not assumed exact on this formation lattice.  In the linear base theorem a map
`L:V->Y` induces

\[
 F_L(W)=L(W).
 \tag{DOFM.2}
\]

It preserves joins exactly,

\[
 F_L(W+Z)=F_L(W)+F_L(Z),
 \tag{DOFM.3}
\]

but preserves meets only laxly,

\[
 F_L(W\cap Z)\subseteq F_L(W)\cap F_L(Z).
 \tag{DOFM.4}
\]

This one asymmetry is generative.

### 2.3 Derived formation charge

Apply the observer-formation operation degreewise to the incidence resolution.  The homology

\[
 \mathsf H_p(L;W):=H_p(F_LC_\bullet(W))
 \tag{DOFM.5}
\]

is the law created by failure of observation to commute with local-to-global formation.

The first proved case is two legs.  The exact source Mayer--Vietoris sequence becomes nonexact
after image formation, and

\[
 \mathsf H_{\rm mid}(L;W,Z)
 =\frac{L(W)\cap L(Z)}{L(W\cap Z)}
 \simeq
 \mathsf H(\ker L,W,Z).
 \tag{DOFM.6}
\]

The right side is a symmetric three-formation homology.  Thus kernel, two legal formation legs,
and observer-created overlap are one charge in different gauges.

## 3. Proved laws

### Law I -- symmetric genesis

Three subspaces have a permutation-invariant first homology.  Any marked-leg distributivity
quotient is canonically this same homology.  Pairwise exactness does not imply joint exactness.

### Law II -- observer-overlap realization

For `U=ker L`,

\[
 \frac{U\cap(W+Z)}{(U\cap W)+(U\cap Z)}
 \simeq
 \frac{L(W)\cap L(Z)}{L(W\cap Z)}.
 \tag{DOFM.7}
\]

Observation can manufacture a common target direction from source directions with no common
preimage.

### Law III -- recovery conservation

Reducing `W,Z` separately by the common recovery kernel and then fusing produces a complex whose
only excess homology is `(DOFM.7)`.  Joint kernel equals local kernels minus their common part plus
derived formation charge.

### Law IV -- reporter duality

In finite dimension, relation charge and reporter-legality defect pair perfectly.  Relation birth
and reporter loss are not two independent obstacles.

### Law V -- rank-slack realization

The charge rank is exactly the submodularity slack of observer rank and the supermodularity slack
of observer nullity.  It is also the exact missing rank of the fused Gram/Fisher operator.

### Law VI -- positive Hodge shadow

Finite formation complexes have positive Hodge Laplacians.  Harmonic projection represents the
charge, but metric choice changes its representative, not its rank, dual pairing, or zero locus.
Positivity is therefore a shadow/quotient of formation homology, not its source.

### Law VII -- equivariant neutrality

Invertible source actions transport formation homology.  Unitary actions conjugate Hodge carriers.
Functional equations, gauge changes, and exact recovery lifts can reverse or permute charge but do
not annihilate it.

### Law VIII -- serial erasure--generation

For `V -L-> Y -M-> Z`, the canonical sequence

\[
 0\to E_M\to O_L\to O_{ML}\to O_M\to0
 \tag{DOFM.8}
\]

is exact.  In finite dimension

\[
 \dim O_{ML}=\dim O_L-\dim E_M+\dim O_M.
 \tag{DOFM.9}
\]

Every observer stage separates retained old charge, erased old charge, and newly generated charge.
Arbitrary chains telescope.

### Law IX -- neutral lift classification

A lift is information-neutral exactly when it kills no upstream formation class and creates no
new downstream overlap.  Invertibility is sufficient but not necessary.

### Law X -- cofinal properness selection

If nonzero formation charge transports coherently and noncontractingly through cofinal apertures,
then every positive action with divergent tail weights diverges.  Finite-action admission forces
the persistent charge to vanish.  This is the first route-independent selection mechanism in the
programme.

### Law XI -- higher serial kernel/retention exactness

For every finite formation family, two serial observers determine two canonical short exact
sequences of augmented complexes.  The first isolates kernel-valued erasure; the second isolates
intersection information retained after the first observer but absent from direct composite
formation.  Their long exact homology sequences recover Law VIII on two legs and extend its
old-minus-erased-plus-new budget to every homological degree.

### Law XII -- coefficient-dominated presentation invariance

Adding a formation leg contained in an existing leg adds a contractible coefficient-incidence
sector.  Deleting it preserves all formation, erasure, and retention charges and both serial
connecting maps.  Equality of total joins is insufficient: a collectively generated but
non-dominated leg can create new homology.

### Law XIII -- source-edge Hodge/properness fusion

The literal Möbius prime-exponent edge family has observer-formation homology equal to the
zero-sum edge-packet sector.  Coarse endpoint plus harmonic class reconstructs the complete packet;
refinement excess is exactly its Hodge norm; functional-equation reversal is isometric on it;
parity recurrence is endpoint closure with open harmonic history; bilateral cofinal Hodge action is
finite exactly at zero radial drift.

## 6. Higher-arity research programme

### Conjecture A -- higher observer-formation homology

For every finite formation family, `(DOFM.5)` is presentation-independent under legal refinement
and admits a permutation-natural comparison with a kernel-augmented symmetric incidence complex.

ULF-DOFM-2 proves presentation independence for coefficient-dominated refinements and gives a
sharp countermodel to invariance under arbitrary join-preserving refinement.  What remains is to
classify broader legal refinement zigzags and construct the kernel-augmented symmetric comparison.

**Falsifier:** two legally equivalent formation presentations have nonisomorphic homology without a
canonical refinement zigzag.

### Theorem B in the finite linear chart -- serial hyperhomology sequence

For serial linear observers `L,M` and every finite linear formation family, higher charges fit into
two natural long exact sequences.  One is generated by the kernel-erasure complex; the other by the
retention-comparison quotient.  Their two-leg edge is `(DOFM.8)`.  This is proved by ULF-DOFM-1.

Nonlinear, topological, derived-completion, and source-analytic extensions remain conjectural.
Their falsifier is a legal formation doctrine in which no replacement for the two short exact
complexes exists.

### Conjecture C -- source prolongation law

For analytic/differential formation towers, hypertranscendence is equivalent to unbounded ranks of
source-faithful formation homology across prolongation order, not to noncompactness of any one
observer orbit.

**Falsifier:** a source-faithful HT family with uniformly bounded formation-homology rank in every
legal prolongation presentation.

ULF-DOFM-3 proves one literal positive case: the depth-12 Möbius source is differentially
independent and its finite character-observer formation charges have ranks `r-1`.  The universal
equivalence and source-faithful classification remain open.

### Conjecture D -- formation action properness

Literal source operations define a lower-semicontinuous action on coherent formation homology;
nonzero persistent charge has infinite action while exact charges have finite action.

**Falsifier:** a nonzero persistent arithmetic charge with finite source-derived action, or a zero
charge forced to infinite action.

ULF-DOFM-3 proves the conjectured selector for the literal prime-radial edge chart: bilateral
cofinal Hodge action is finite exactly at zero drift.  What remains is transport and unconditional
admission of that class on the complete multiplicity-resolved Xi source.

### Conjecture E -- arithmetic selection

The pre-zero Euler--Gamma--theta source generates one coherent formation complex whose route
realizations cover at least three structurally different live organs.  Its persistent charge is
the common recovery class that current routes retain but cannot annihilate.  Source properness,
not symmetry or positivity alone, forces its vanishing.

**Falsifier:** all candidate cross-route realizations factor through mutually nonisomorphic source
charges, or the only common charge is defined after Xi specialization.
