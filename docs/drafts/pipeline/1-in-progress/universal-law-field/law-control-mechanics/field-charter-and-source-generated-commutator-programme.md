## 3. Proposed laws

### Law I — source marking precedes generation

A derived intervention counts only when its parent transformations and legal schedules are fixed
before the target response is inspected.  Adding arbitrary state-indexed compatibility can realize
every transition graph and is therefore not a genesis law.

### Law II — exchange generates new law directions

Nonidentity schedule exchange is positive mathematical content.  A derived direction may be absent
from the primitive signature while forced by composition.  Strict quotient, central promotion,
higher promotion, or admission change are distinct responses to that direction.

### Law III — observers contract reachable law history

An observer maps legal words to observed words.  It may identify a nontrivial derived operation
with identity, but cannot create a source distinction.  A no-go therefore specifies which generated
direction was killed and which reporter stopped extending.

### Law IV — response, not endpoint, carries the mechanism

For a derived word `w`, retain its full response under real, imaginary, analytic, differential, and
completion charts when those charts exist.  Positivity, phase, divisor, recurrence, and
hypertranscendence are not identified by definition; they count as one mechanism only after a
reconstruction theorem proves they are restrictions of the same generated response.

### Law V — completion changes accessibility

Finite words, closure of the finite-word orbit, and completed reachability are different.  A
cofinal law can be born only through an infinitary operation, an admission/completion transition,
or a compactness theorem reducing it to finite ancestry.  This is the certified finitary-formation
barrier read as control accessibility.

### Law VI — cost must be generated by exchange

A cost is constitutive only when derived from source response of a schedule defect.  Information
divergence, minimal filling energy, or physical action inserted after the fact does not count.
