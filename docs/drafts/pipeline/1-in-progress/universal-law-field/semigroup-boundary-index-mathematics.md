## 1. Founding primitive

Start with a positive law semigroup \(P\), its reversible completion \(G\), and the boundary extension
generated by an isometric representation of \(P\):

\[
0\longrightarrow J_P\longrightarrow\mathcal T(P)
\longrightarrow\mathcal B(G)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{1}
\]

The semigroup is not an approximation to the group. It is the carrier of irreversible theorem
history. The boundary group is the recurrent observer shadow. The ideal \(J_P\) stores histories
erased by reversible completion. Connecting morphisms measure failure of a boundary law to lift to a
reversible source law.

The name “boundary” here is algebraic/topological, not spacetime language. No curvature primitive is
assumed.

## 2. Why this is stronger than the earlier field tuples

The reversible and irreversible parts cannot be chosen independently:

- additive nonnegative cost on a groupoid is forced to vanish;

- quotienting the defect creates a unitary phase;
- the connecting map reconstructs the erased defect from boundary winding.

The coupling is therefore constitutive, not bookkeeping.

## 6. Hostile attacks already visible

### Attack A — zero-index histories

Many nontrivial histories can have zero Fredholm/K-theory index. If all higher connecting invariants
also vanish while source cost differs, boundary-index reconstruction is false.

### Attack B — completion dependence

Full, reduced, and exotic semigroup completions can have different ideals and boundary maps. If the
source does not select a completion, the geometry is doctrine-relative rather than universal.

### Attack C — no semigroup presentation

Some corpus organs are affine, indefinite, or differential and may not arise from a positive monoid
without fitting. Failure for one primary organ prevents a whole-corpus claim.

### Attack D — hypertranscendence

Toeplitz dynamics alone does not imply differential hypertranscendence. Algebraic zeta functions can
come from infinite semigroups, while hypertranscendental functions may share simple local monodromy.
The proposed identification may be false.
