# Primary--secondary law-birth composition mechanics

- **ID:** ULF-PSB-PROGRAM
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** MECHANISM PROGRAMME
- **Statement:** Serial constitutive carriers generate primary and secondary birth obstructions;
  intermediate kernel freedom can cancel an apparent inner obstruction.

## Pivot

Do not ask which geometric object contains every proof route. Ask what laws govern repeated
attempts to produce a new law through several lossy doctrines.

For composable carriers

\[
E\xrightarrow{S}K\xrightarrow{T}A,
\]

an ambient residual \(a\in A\) first asks whether it lifts through \(T\). If it does, choose
\(k\in K\) with \(Tk=a\). The second question is not whether \(k\in\operatorname{im}S\).
That condition depends on the chosen outer lift. The invariant condition is

\[
k\in\operatorname{im}S+\ker T.
\]

The outer kernel is therefore active constitutive freedom: it can absorb failure of the inner
carrier without changing the observed law.

## Candidate laws

1. **Primary birth law.** The class of \(a\) in
   \(A/\operatorname{im}T\) is the obstruction to any outer lift.
2. **Secondary birth law.** Once an outer lift exists, its class in
   \(K/(\operatorname{im}S+\ker T)\) is independent of the lift and obstructs a composite lift.
3. **Kernel-cancellation law.** An outer lift outside \(\operatorname{im}S\) may still be
   composite-carried when its transverse component lies in \(\ker T\).
4. **Exact-sequence law.** The primary and secondary sectors must assemble as

   \[
   0\longrightarrow
   \frac{K}{\operatorname{im}S+\ker T}
   \longrightarrow
   \frac{A}{\operatorname{im}(TS)}
   \longrightarrow
   \frac{A}{\operatorname{im}T}
   \longrightarrow0.
   \]

5. **Irreversibility law.** Later observation cannot repair a primary failure. It can only erase
   or cancel an earlier secondary defect through its kernel.

## Why this is higher-level

The variables are not RH routes, spectra, primes, or particular operator algebras. They are
successive law-producing mechanisms. Quotients encode failed production; kernels encode
unobservable compensations; composition determines which failures are genuine. The same laws can
be instantiated in algebraic, analytic, categorical, probabilistic, or physical doctrines.

## Sharp boundary

Algebraic exactness does not automatically survive Hausdorff completion. Replacing ranges by
closed ranges yields a second interchange problem: inverse image and closure need not commute.
That topological composition law is intentionally left open until strictness hypotheses or an
explicit counterexample classify the failure.
