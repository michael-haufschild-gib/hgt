## 1. Thesis

Perhaps proof routes are neither coordinate charts nor observer contexts. They may be **fillings**
of theorem boundaries. Their hidden invariant is not curvature but the topology, volume, and
torsion of admissible proof histories.

Two exact calibrations support this possibility:

1. acyclic based complexes have identical homology but different determinant histories;
2. null words can have identical endpoint value and unbounded minimal filling area.

This fork treats theorem endpoints as codimension-one data and proofs as higher-dimensional bulk
objects.

## 2. Candidate primitive

A source-marked higher category \(\mathsf{FillLaw}\) with:

1. theorem boundaries as objects;
2. proofs as one-higher morphisms filling those boundaries;
3. proof transformations as still higher morphisms;
4. a determinant or \(K\)-theory orientation on exact fillings;
5. a positive filling-volume functional;
6. a completion admitting infinite fillings and ends.

The natural equivalence is not equality of endpoints. It is stable equivalence of fillings under
declared elementary expansions and cancellations.

## 4. Why this is not PLG

Polyscopic Law Geometry studies the matching map from total laws to proper observer faces. Filling
geometry studies the category of witnesses after a boundary has already been declared realizable.

A matching fibre can be contractible while its canonical fillings have large cost. Conversely, a
boundary can have many cheap fillings. Reconstruction ambiguity and proof-filling complexity are
independent axes.

## 5. Required next theorems

1. Define a presentation-stable filling invariant for a source-generated law category.
2. Couple determinant torsion and filling volume in one nontrivial family.
3. Build an infinite tower whose finite boundaries close while higher filling functions do not
   stabilize.
4. Determine whether a positive filling state can retain torsion phase through a noncommutative
   Gram object.
5. Compare filling categories under observer restriction without choosing target endpoints.

## 6. Fatal test

If every decisive proof route admits canonical normal forms whose higher filling categories are
contractible with uniformly bounded cost, filling/torsion geometry cannot explain the corpus.

If all candidate invariants change arbitrarily under harmless presentation moves, the fork is
presentation theatre.

## 7. Current verdict

Alive. It already produces one phenomenon PLG does not: unique/closed endpoints with arbitrarily
large or topologically nontrivial proof history. It remains only a fork until source marking and
stable presentation invariance are constructed.
