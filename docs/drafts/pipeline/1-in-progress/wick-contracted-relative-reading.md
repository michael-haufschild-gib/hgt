## Exact statements

Fix fields `F ⊆ E` with derivations (`DifferentialAlgebra F E`, `CharZero E`), and write `M` for
the differential-transcendence matroid of `E/F`.

1. **Disjoint-union splitting.**  For disjoint `s, t ⊆ E`:

   ```text
   DiffIndepOn F (s ∪ t)  ↔  DiffIndepOn F t  ∧  DiffIndepOn F⟨t⟩ s,
   ```

   where `F⟨t⟩ = differentialAdjoin F t` is the differential field generated by `t` over `F`.

2. **Relative reading of contraction.**  Let `J` be a basis' of `G` in `M`, `I ⊆ E`.  Then

   ```text
   (M ／ G).Indep I  ↔  Disjoint G I  ∧  DiffIndepOn F⟨J⟩ I.
   ```

3. **Semantic persistence.**  Let `(G i)` be a nonempty directed family, `J` a basis' of
   `⋃ᵢ G i`, `Jᵢ` a basis' of `G i`, and `I` disjoint from `⋃ᵢ G i`.  Then

   ```text
   DiffIndepOn F⟨J⟩ I  ↔  ∀ i, DiffIndepOn F⟨Jᵢ⟩ I.
   ```

   Relative differential freedom over the face-complete basis field is exactly stage-persistent
   relative freedom.

## Paper proof `[PROVED HERE]`

**(1)**  `DiffIndepOn` is algebraic independence of the doubly-indexed jet family.  Disjointness
gives a bijection `↑(s ∪ t) ≅ ↑s ⊕ ↑t`, under which the inclusion family of `s ∪ t` is the sum
of the two inclusion families; differential independence is invariant under bijective reindexing
(`differentialIndependent_equiv`).  The corpus tower lemma
`differentialIndependent_sumElim_right_iff` splits the sum family:

```text
DifferentialIndependent F (x ⊔ y)  ↔  DifferentialIndependent F y ∧
                                       DifferentialIndependent F⟨range y⟩ x,
```

and `range (val : t → E) = t`.  ∎

**(2)**  The basis characterization of contraction independence gives
`(M ／ G).Indep I ↔ M.Indep (I ∪ J) ∧ Disjoint G I`.  On the right, `M.Indep` is `DiffIndepOn F`
by construction of the differential matroid, and `Disjoint G I` with `J ⊆ G` forces
`Disjoint I J`, so (1) applies: `DiffIndepOn F (I ∪ J) ↔ DiffIndepOn F J ∧ DiffIndepOn F⟨J⟩ I`.
The first conjunct is free — `J` is a basis', hence independent.  ∎

**(3)**  Chain (2) at the limit base, the directed persistence law
(`contract_iUnion_indep_iff_forall`, finitarity of the differential matroid), and (2) at each
stage; the disjointness side conditions descend from `Disjoint (⋃ᵢ G i) I` by monotonicity.  ∎

### Hypothesis audit

- Disjointness in (1) is necessary: for `x ∈ s ∩ t` the union family repeats `x`, while the
  split right side can still hold (e.g. `s = t = {x}` with `x` differentially transcendental —
  the right side holds, yet independence of the union family means independence of the
  *deduplicated* set, so the bijection `↑(s∪t) ≅ ↑s ⊕ ↑t` fails and with it the stated
  equivalence in family form).
- The field in (2) is generated by a **basis of `G`**, not by `G` itself.  Upgrading `F⟨J⟩` to
  `F⟨G⟩` requires insensitivity of differential independence to differential-algebraic base
  extension — a genuine separate theorem, named here as the exact next `[OPEN CORE]` of the
  relative-semantics thread.  Basis-independence of the right side in (2) holds *a fortiori*
  (both sides equal the left side for every basis'), which is what the physics needs: the
  reading does not depend on the chosen basis.
