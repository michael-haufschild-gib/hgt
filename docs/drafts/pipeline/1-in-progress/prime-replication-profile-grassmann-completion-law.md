
An **admissible valuation profile** is a sequence





The definition is independent of representatives because tail density identifies exactly the

## Why derivative order does not create profile relations

The `k`-th derivative jet has coefficient

```text
(-r log p)^k u_r(x_p)
```

at `p^r`.  Suppose a finite linear combination of jets is supported on only finitely many prime
rays.  For each fixed `r` it has the form

```text
sum_(k<=m) (log p)^k f_(k,r)(x_p) = 0
```

for all sufficiently large primes.  Divide by the highest power of `log p` and use cofinal tail
density: along primes approaching any point of `K`, every lower power disappears, so the top
continuous coefficient vanishes on `K`.  Descending on `k` kills every coefficient.  Since
`r^k` is nonzero, linear independence of the original profiles kills the scalar relation.

Thus the full jet rectangle is independent in the prime-ray tail geometry.  This is precisely
what fails for arbitrary unrelated prime streams: a stream such as `1/log p` can absorb one clock
step.  Recurrence of a fixed profile on every cofinal prime tail forbids that absorption.

### Lemma 1: admissible analytic realization

The profile bound gives absolute convergence of the double Dirichlet series in a right half-plane.

Let `I` be a finite linearly independent profile family and let `m` be a finite jet order.  The

### Lemma 3: prime-ray Jacobian

Embed a profile as the arithmetic function supported at prime powers with coefficient
`u_r(x_p)` at `p^r`.  Apply the prime derivation at each selected `q_a`.  Every row of the Jacobian

makes every finite jet packet algebraically independent over `C`.
