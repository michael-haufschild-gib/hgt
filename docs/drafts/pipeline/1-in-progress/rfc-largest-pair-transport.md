# Transport of the two largest primes

- **Stage** 1-in-progress
- **Source** [Signed cancellation](../../../reports/review-1811/obstacles-summary.md#211-mathematics-for-a-proof-by-signed-cancellation-alone)
- **Question** Can one disjoint exchange cover the whole balanced-triple family witnessing empty ordered fibres?

For \(cqr\) keep \(c\) and merge the two largest primes into one prime in the same quotient interval. For fixed aspect ratio, the balanced pairs occupy \(O(T/\log^2 T)\) positions at scale \(T=X/c\); the dyadic prime supply is of order \(T/\log T\). Decode the target cofactor by removing its largest prime. Establish a uniform theorem on the actual dyadic relation and identify the boundary for partial intervals. The signed sum on the complementary vertices remains open.
