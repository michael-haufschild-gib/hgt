# No Dirichlet polynomial is a factor of ξ (two-sided constant terms)

- **ID:** IRP-DP
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Source:** IRP README §12, row "A factor with a Dirichlet series" (open case: several bases, no
  constant term, lowest frequency not dividing the others)
- **Statement:** every Dirichlet polynomial `D(s) = Σ_{n ∈ S} a_n n^{−s}` with at least two terms has
  a zero that is not a zero of `ξ`.

## Goal
Closes Q2 of IRP for Dirichlet polynomials with no hypothesis on the support. The mechanism does
not use the carrier rigidity (`RHTower.AtomDomination.eq_zero_of_herglotz_dirichlet_criticalCarrier`,
which rests on Hamburger's theorem for integer exponents and does not reach the rational
frequencies `log(n/n₁)`). Lean goes to `formal/GaloisForLFunctions/InfiniteRankPurityTheory/`.

## Approach

Notation: `n₁ = min S`, `N₁ = max S`, `Λ = D'/D`, `{u}` the fractional part,
`mellin f z = ∫_0^∞ u^{z−1} f(u) du`; `∫_0^∞ {u} u^{−s−1} du = −ζ(s)/s` for `0 < Re s < 1`.

### K. Kernel theorem (the two constant terms agree)
Let `m_R, m_L : (0,∞) → ℂ` vanish off `[1,∞)`, with `Σ_r ‖m_R(r)‖ r^{−σ} < ∞` for `σ ∈ (½, 1]` and
`Σ_r ‖m_L(r)‖ r^{σ} < ∞` for `σ ∈ [0, ½)`. Let `F` be holomorphic on `0 < Re s < 1`, bounded on
`¼ ≤ Re s ≤ ¾`, with
`F(s) = −(ζ(s)/s) Σ_r m_R(r) r^{−s}` on `½ < Re s < 1`, `F(s) = −(ζ(s)/s) Σ_r m_L(r) r^{s}` on
`0 < Re s < ½`. Then `m_L(1) = m_R(1)`.

1. `g₊(u) = Σ_r m_R(r){u/r}`, `g_L(u) = Σ_r m_L(r){ur}`. Since `{y} ≤ min(1, y) ≤ y^σ`:
   `‖g₊(u)‖ ≤ C_σ u^σ` (`σ ∈ (½,1]`), `‖g_L(u)‖ ≤ C'_σ u^σ` (`σ ∈ [0,½)`). By scaling of the Mellin
   transform and Fubini, `mellin g₊ (−s) = F(s)` on `½ < Re s < 1`, `mellin g_L (−s) = F(s)` on
   `0 < Re s < ½`.
2. `v = g₊ − g_L`, `P(s) = ∫_1^∞ v u^{−s−1}`, holomorphic on `Re s > ½`, bounded on `Re s ≥ ¾`,
   `P(x) → 0` as `x → +∞`; `Q(s) = ∫_0^1 v u^{−s−1}`, holomorphic on `Re s < ½`, bounded on
   `Re s ≤ ¼`. With `R₋(s) = ∫_0^1 g₊ u^{−s−1}` (holomorphic on `Re s < 1`) and
   `S₊(s) = ∫_1^∞ g_L u^{−s−1}` (holomorphic on `Re s > 0`): `E₁ = F − R₋ − S₊` on the strip equals
   `P` right of the line and `−Q` left of it. So `P, E₁, −Q` glue to an entire `E`, bounded
   (the hypothesis on `F` bounds `E₁` on `¼ ≤ Re s ≤ ¾`). Liouville and `P(x) → 0`: `E ≡ 0`.
3. Mellin inversion with transform `0`: `v(u) = 0` at every continuity point `u ≠ 1`. `v` is
   continuous off the countable set `{mr} ∪ {m/r}`, so continuity points accumulate at every point
   from both sides.
4. Jumps at `u₀ = 1/k`, `k ≥ 1` (termwise, the series converge uniformly near `u₀`): `g₊` jumps by
   `−m_R(1)` at `1` and is continuous at `1/k`, `k ≥ 2`; `g_L` jumps by `−Σ_{m ≥ 1} m_L(km)`.
   By 3: `Σ_{m ≥ 1} m_L(km) = [k = 1] m_R(1)` for every `k ≥ 1`.
5. Möbius inversion (`Σ_j ‖m_L(j)‖ d(j) < ∞`): `m_L(1) = Σ_k μ(k) Σ_m m_L(km) = m_R(1)`.

### A. Lower bounds by the lowest term
If `D` has no zero in `Re s > θ`, then for `σ₀ > θ` there is `δ > 0` with
`‖D(s)‖ ≥ δ n₁^{−Re s}` on `Re s ≥ σ₀` (compactness of the twists and Hurwitz, as P26.2, with the
far bound `‖D(s)‖ ≥ ½‖a_{n₁}‖ n₁^{−Re s}`). Hence `Λ` is bounded on `Re s ≥ σ₀`.

### B. Expansion of `Λ` up to the zeros
`D = a_{n₁} n₁^{−s}(1 + U)`, `U = Σ_{n > n₁} c_n (n/n₁)^{−s}`. For `Re s ≥ c` (`‖U‖ < 1`):
`Λ = −log n₁ + U' Σ_k (−U)^k`, a family `Σ_j b_j r_j^{−s}`, `r_j ≥ 1` in the multiplicative monoid `M`
generated by the `n/n₁`, absolutely convergent at `c`; merged: `Λ(s) = Σ_r m_R(r) r^{−s}`,
`m_R(1) = −log n₁`.
1. *Coefficient bound.* `m_R(r) = lim (2T)^{−1} ∫_{−T}^{T} Λ(c + it) r^{c+it} dt`; by Cauchy on the
   rectangle `[σ₀, c] × [−T, T]` the line `c` may be replaced by `σ₀`: `‖m_R(r)‖ ≤ B_{σ₀} r^{σ₀}`.
2. *Sparsity.* `Σ_{r ∈ M} r^{−ε} ≤ Π_n (1 − (n/n₁)^{−ε})^{−1} < ∞`.
3. So `Σ_r ‖m_R(r)‖ r^{−σ} < ∞` for every `σ > θ`, and `Λ(s) = Σ m_R(r) r^{−s}` on `Re s > θ`.
Left side: `D^†(s) = Σ a_n (L/n)^{−s}`, `L = lcm S`, is a Dirichlet polynomial with
`D(s) = L^{−s} D^†(−s)`, `Λ_D(s) = −log L − Λ_{D^†}(−s)`; its lowest frequency is `L/N₁`. So
`Λ(s) = Σ m_L(r) r^{s}` on `Re s < θ'`, `m_L(1) = −log N₁`.

### C. Conclusion
If every zero of `D` is a zero of `ξ`: zeros on `Re s = ½` (P25), `ζ` vanishes at them, so
`F = −Λ ζ/s` is holomorphic on the strip (`Λ` has simple poles). Bounded on `¼ ≤ Re s ≤ ¾`: on the
two lines by A; in between by the maximum principle on rectangles between heights at which
`‖D‖` is bounded below across the strip (almost periodicity, as P27.2), using
`‖ζ(s)/s‖ ≤ 1/‖s − 1‖ + 1/Re s`. By K and B: `−log N₁ = −log n₁`, so `D` has one term.
