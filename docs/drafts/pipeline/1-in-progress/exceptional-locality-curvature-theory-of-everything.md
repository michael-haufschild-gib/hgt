## Jump-first research protocol

The programme adopts the explicit meta-constraint:

> **Jump first; falsify second.**

The programme uses the following strict definition:

> **A conjecture is a statement that, based on everything presently known, we have a hunch is
> physically true even though we cannot presently tell how it could be proved.**

Provability is not a property used to generate, select, weaken, strengthen, rank, or reject the
conjecture. It enters only after the statement has been frozen. “Jump” means proposing such an
ontology or physical equivalence without obtaining it by interpolation between neighbouring
published models or reverse-engineering it from a visible proof route. It does not mean relaxing
proof, source, or empirical standards after the conjecture has been frozen. Each research cycle
therefore has two deliberately separated phases:

1. **Abductive jump.** Synthesize everything presently known; state the resulting physical hunch as
   a declarative sentence; freeze and timestamp it before asking how to prove it. Lean tractability,
   available machinery, literature proximity, and likely publishability are forbidden generation
   or pruning criteria in this phase. A candidate does not qualify as the primary conjecture if its
   author can already sketch the mechanism of proof that materially shaped it.
2. **Hostile descent.** Derive consequences; collide them with no-go theorems, established data,
   known limits, and competing literature; label failures; retain the strongest positive survivor;
   formalize every surviving mathematical claim in Lean.

A statement reverse-engineered from a known theorem or an available proof is a **derived theorem
candidate** or **bridge**, not a jump conjecture. Failure to find a proof is not evidence against a
conjecture; conflict with observation, internal incoherence, or failed novel predictions is.
Conjecture, literature, theorem, and physical evidence labels remain mandatory.

## Why this route is not another collage

\[

\]

finite-dimensional formally real Jordan algebra. It is not chosen from a catalogue of gauge groups.
Inside \(J\), choose a nested pair of Jordan subalgebras

\[
  X\subset B\subset J,\qquad
  X\cong H_2(\mathbb C),\qquad B\cong H_3(\mathbb C).                 \tag{1}
\]

The same \(X\) has two established readings:

- \(H_2(\mathbb C)\) is the observable algebra of a qubit;

\[
  \operatorname{Stab}_{F_4}(X)\cap

selects the \(\mathbb Z_6\)-quotiented Standard Model group without placing
\(U(1)\times SU(2)\times SU(3)\) into the definition.

The new route is to determine whether the *same nested pair*, globalized and made dynamical, forces

## Same-\(X\) weak--rotation overlap no-go

The canonical formulas in Baez--Schwahn make the obstruction explicit. For the standard nested
pair, the first \(SU(3)\) in
\(\operatorname{Stab}(B)_0\cong(SU(3)\times SU(3))/\mathbb Z_3\) acts on
\(B=H_3(\mathbb C)\) by

\[
  Y\longmapsto gYg^\dagger.                                         \tag{6a}
\]

Its subgroup preserving the upper \(H_2(\mathbb C)\) corner is the electroweak
\(S(U(2)\times U(1))\). In particular, its \(SU(2)\) factor acts on

\[
  x=x^0 I+\sum_{j=1}^3x^j\sigma_j\in H_2(\mathbb C)
\]

by \(x\mapsto uxu^\dagger\). This fixes \(x^0\) and applies the usual \(SO(3)\) rotation to
\((x^1,x^2,x^3)\). It is exactly the compact spatial-rotation action inside the Lorentz action

\[
  x\longmapsto g x g^\dagger,\qquad g\in SL(2,\mathbb C).            \tag{6b}
\]

Therefore the weak \(SU(2)\) selected by the nested-pair theorem acts nontrivially on the proposed
spacetime \(X\). It cannot lie in the kernel of the restriction map to Lorentz transformations.
Consequently, the same copy of \(X\) cannot simultaneously supply ordinary 4D spacetime and an
independent internal weak \(SU(2)\) through `(6)`.

This is a short corollary of `[LITERATURE]` action formulas, not a novelty claim. It closes the
naive slogan “the nested qubit is directly spacetime and its full stabilizer is internal”.

The strongest positive survivor is a sharper fork:

2. **Weak-is-rotation route.** Identify weak \(SU(2)\) with a chiral spacetime-rotation connection

## Why anomaly cancellation cannot select the theory

“Quantum consistency on every spacetime uniquely forces the Standard Model” is false without a
stronger algebra-selection principle.

For an Abelian chiral charge list, local gauge and mixed gravitational anomalies include odd charge
moments such as

\[
  \sum_i q_i,\qquad \sum_i q_i^3.                                  \tag{11}
\]

Appending a vectorlike pair \(q,-q\) leaves both expressions unchanged. More generally,
representation plus conjugate representation cancels its chiral anomaly contribution. A decoupled
pure Yang--Mills factor also leaves the original matter anomalies untouched. Hence anomaly freedom
does not bound the number of gauge factors and cannot prove “there can only be four forces”.

The positive survivor is exact:

> Any uniqueness theorem must derive the observable algebra and its notion of composition before
> applying anomaly cancellation; anomaly cancellation is a quantum admissibility gate, not the
> selector.

This is why \(J\) and `(1)` are load-bearing. If the nested pair must itself be inserted to recover
observed physics, the route has only repackaged the Standard Model.

## Hypertranscendence: permitted role

Hypertranscendence does not select \(H_3(\mathbb O)\), the Lorentz group, or the Standard Model
group. It must not be inserted as decoration.

It can serve three legitimate roles:

1. **Finite-model falsifier.** A fixed finite matrix/finite spectral triple has rational resolvent
   entries by the adjugate identity, hence differentially algebraic response. A calibrated
   hypertranscendental resolvent response excludes every fixed finite realization unless the
   reporter already contains an HT source.
2. **Continuum-phase witness.** In a sequence or ensemble of finite exceptional frames, HT can only
   appear after a genuinely non-finite limit or through an HT input. A source-pure HT observable
   would therefore distinguish the continuum phase from every fixed fuzzy truncation.
3. **Local-composition falsifier.** The existing calibrated inverse-response residual theorem can
   reject local single-metric tensor-product models. It does not identify the exceptional
   alternative; `(10)` must predict the residual pattern.

Any claim that “HT causes \(E_6\)”, “HT proves octonions”, or “HT alone proves quantum gravity” is
forbidden.

## Matter, chirality, generations, and Higgs gates

The group alone is not the Standard Model.

1. **Fermions.** Construct a positive physical state space carrying exactly the observed chiral
   \(G_{\rm SM}\) representations after symmetry reduction.
2. **No mirrors/exotics.** An \(E_6\) fundamental representation naturally contains more than one
   Standard Model generation's light content. A mass mechanism must remove unwanted states without
   fine-tuned projectors.
3. **Anomalies.** Prove perturbative and global gauge/gravitational anomaly cancellation for the
   actual representation and for every admitted spacetime topology.
4. **Three generations.** Triality and the three matrix slots suggest “three”, but suggestion is
   not a generation theorem. Derive three inequivalent chiral copies and their mixing, or leave
   generation count explicitly open.
5. **Higgs/Yukawa.** Derive the electroweak Higgs representation, symmetry-breaking vacuum,
   Yukawa maps, and neutrino sector from `(9)`. Do not import the Standard Model Lagrangian.
6. **Couplings.** Derive a boundary condition or relation for gauge, Higgs, Newton, and cosmological
   couplings and show renormalization does not contradict measured values.

Failure of any item prevents a theory-of-everything claim.
