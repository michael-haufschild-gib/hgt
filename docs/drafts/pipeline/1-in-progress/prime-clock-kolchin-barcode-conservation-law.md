## 2. Finite-jet rank and innovation

Let `Q` be the prime-power profile quotient, let `T : Q -> Q` be the logarithmic prime clock, let

```text

kappa_W(r) = dim_C S_W(r),
iota_W(0) = kappa_W(0),
iota_W(r+1) = kappa_W(r+1) - kappa_W(r).
```

The exact relative prime-profile theorem has the following finite-packet strengthening.

> **Relative algebraic rank law.**  For every finite family of positive-prime-power sources
> `h_i`, the carriers `x_(h_i)` are algebraically independent over `Phi(W)` if and only if their
> classes are linearly independent in `Q/W`.  Consequently the transcendence rank of any finite
> packet over `Phi(W)` is the complex dimension of the span of its quotient classes.

To prove the forward implication, a quotient-linear relation lifts to a linear combination whose
class lies in `W`; exact source-field membership turns that combination into an element of
`Phi(W)`, contradicting algebraic independence.  Conversely, choose positive-prime-power
representatives of a basis of `W`.  Quotient independence of the new classes and independence of
the old basis imply independence of the combined source classes.  The absolute prime-profile
matroid theorem makes the combined carriers algebraically independent over the finite-prime
field.  Splitting algebraic independence across the old and new summands makes the new carriers
algebraically independent over the field generated by the old carriers, which is `Phi(W)`.

For the numerical statement, choose a maximal linearly independent subpacket.  The equivalence
just proved makes it a maximal algebraically independent subpacket, while every remaining class
lies in its linear span and hence every remaining carrier lies in the corresponding generated
field.  Both ranks therefore equal the cardinality of that subpacket.

Apply this law to the finite source family

```text

```

Thus `kappa_W` is the finite-jet Kolchin rank profile in this carrier sector, not a proxy for it.

## 3. Theorem A: Kolchin--Krylov spectral law

For every finite packet:

```text
m >= iota_W(0) >= iota_W(1) >= iota_W(2) >= ... >= 0.        (1)
```

Consequently there is a unique nonincreasing lifetime vector

```text
nu_W = (nu_1,...,nu_m),       nu_j in N union {infinity},
```

where zero lifetimes are allowed, such that for every `r >= 0`

```text
kappa_W(r) = sum_(j=1)^m min(r+1, nu_j).                     (2)
```

Here `min(r+1,infinity)=r+1`.  The multiplicity of lifetime `t` is

```text
#{j : nu_j=t} = iota_W(t-1)-iota_W(t),                       (3)
```

with `iota_W(-1)=m`, and the multiplicity at infinity is the eventual value of `iota_W`.

The spectrum is complete for the finite-jet differential matroid: it reconstructs every rank
`kappa_W(r)`, the eventual Kolchin slope, the bounded DA memory, and the number of genuinely free
HT channels.  For one source, `(2)` reduces to the exact truncated cone

```text
kappa_W(r)=min(r+1, nu),                                     (4)
```

where `nu` is the least clock-annihilator order and `nu=infinity` exactly in the HT case.

### Proof

Write `S(r)=S_W(r)` and `S(-1)=0`.  The induced clock on `Q/W` maps the new layer

```text
S_W(r)/S_W(r-1) -> S_W(r+1)/S_W(r)
```

surjectively: modulo `S(r)`, the space `S(r+1)` is generated by the classes of
`T^(r+1)q_j`, and each is the image of `T^r q_j` modulo `S(r-1)`.  Therefore

```text
dim S(r+1)/S(r) <= dim S(r)/S(r-1),
```

which is `(1)`.  A bounded monotone integer sequence is eventually constant; call its eventual
value `d`.  Set `iota(-1)=m`.  Put `lambda_t=iota(t-1)-iota(t)` for `t>=0`, and put
`lambda_infinity=d`.  Telescoping gives

```text
sum_(t in N) lambda_t + lambda_infinity = m,
iota(k) = lambda_infinity + sum_(t>k) lambda_t.               (3a)
```

Only finitely many finite `lambda_t` are nonzero because `iota` stabilizes.  Summing `(3a)` for
`0<=k<=r` and interchanging two finite sums gives

```text
kappa_W(r)
 = sum_(k=0)^r iota(k)
 = lambda_infinity(r+1) + sum_t lambda_t min(r+1,t).          (3b)
```

List the value `t` with multiplicity `lambda_t` and infinity with multiplicity
`lambda_infinity`; this is `nu_W` and proves `(2)`.  Conversely,

```text
kappa_W(r)-kappa_W(r-1)=#{j:nu_j>r},                          (3c)
```

so every lifetime multiplicity, including the eventual infinite multiplicity, is recovered from
`kappa_W`.  Sorting makes the vector unique.  The nonlinear interpretation follows from the
relative algebraic rank law above, not from an assumed linear differential equation.

## 4. Theorem B: spectral realization and completeness

Every nonincreasing vector



Their joint complete clock family is linearly independent in `Q`.  Equivalently, the valuation
profiles in `(6)` are the coordinate vectors of the function space `C^N` and are linearly
independent; the scalar-replication clock theorem transfers this independence to all clock
iterates.  Directly, restricting a finite relation to the `j`th valuation layer gives a polynomial
in `log p` which vanishes for cofinitely many primes and therefore is zero.  Define

```text
W_nu = span_C {T^k [u_j] : k >= nu_j and nu_j < infinity}.    (7)
```

Then `W_nu` is invariant.  Let `x_(j,k)=T^k[u_j]`.  The full family `x` is linearly independent,
and `W_nu` is the span of the subset indexed by pairs `(j,k)` with `k>=nu_j` and finite `nu_j`.
Modulo that span, the prefix vectors with `k<nu_j` remain linearly independent, while those with
`k>=nu_j` vanish.  This follows from disjointness of the spans of disjoint subsets of a linearly
independent family.  The number of surviving indices through order `r` is exactly
`sum_j min(r+1,nu_j)`, proving `(2)`.  Thus `(1)` is the only constraint on a finite-source
prime-clock Kolchin rank profile.

This is the positive universality statement: finite-source differential complexity in the

## 5. Theorem C: inverse-limit conservation and spectral escape

Let

```text
W_0 >= W_1 >= W_2 >= ...,
W_infinity = intersection_n W_n,
```

be a descending chain of invariant source spaces.  For every fixed jet depth `r`,

```text
kappa_(W_infinity)(r) = sup_n kappa_(W_n)(r),                (8)
```

and the supremum is attained: some finite stage already has the exact limiting rank at that
resolution.  Equivalently, every capped spectral moment is conserved continuously at the inverse
limit:

```text
sum_j min(r+1,nu_infinity,j)
  = sup_n sum_j min(r+1,nu_n,j).                              (9)
```

Define the number of infinite-lifetime channels by

```text
d(W) = lim_(r->infinity) kappa_W(r)/(r+1)
     = #{j : nu_W,j=infinity}.                               (10)
```

Then

```text
sup_n d(W_n) <= d(W_infinity),                               (11)
```

and the integer defect

```text
E = d(W_infinity)-sup_n d(W_n)                               (12)
```

is the **Kolchin emergence index**: the exact number of HT channels created only at the inverse
limit.  It is equivalently the order-of-limits defect

```text
sup_n lim_r kappa_(W_n)(r)/(r+1)
  versus
lim_r sup_n kappa_(W_n)(r)/(r+1).                            (13)
```

If every stage is differentially algebraic as an `m`-packet but the limit packet is jointly
differentially independent, then all `m` finite lifetime atoms escape:

```text
for every R, eventually every nu_n,j > R,                    (14)
```

and the two iterated limits in `(13)` are respectively `0` and `m`.

### Proof

For fixed `r`, the unquotiented jet span `U_r` is finite-dimensional and

```text
U_r intersect W_infinity = intersection_n (U_r intersect W_n).
```

The descending chain on the right stabilizes inside `U_r`, so for some `N(r)` its intersection is
`U_r intersect W_(N(r))`.  The quotient-dimension identity

```text

```

then gives `(8)`, including attainment.  Equation `(9)` is `(8)` rewritten using Theorem A.
Dividing by `r+1` in `(2)` and letting `r` tend to infinity proves `(10)`.  Monotonicity of the
finite ranks gives `(11)`, and substituting `(8)` yields the two sides of `(13)`.

For `(14)`, joint differential independence at the limit gives
`kappa_(W_infinity)(R)=m(R+1)`.  By attainment in `(8)`, the same equality holds at every
sufficiently late stage.  In `(2)` every summand is at most `R+1`, so equality of their sum with
`m(R+1)` forces every lifetime to exceed `R`.  If every stage has zero eventual slope, the first
iterated limit in `(13)` is zero; the second is `m`.  This is stronger than annihilator-order
escape: it counts every independent channel and determines the entire crossover surface.
