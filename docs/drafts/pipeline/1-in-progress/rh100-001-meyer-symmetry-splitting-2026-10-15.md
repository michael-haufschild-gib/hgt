## Obstacle

The existing global actual-source complement preserves the generator and every
finite Hedenmalm causal chain, but its Baer extension is not known to commute
with Paugam reflection or arithmetic conjugation. Transporting an abstract
involution through comparison would not prove compatibility with the original
actual-source involutions required by symmetric metric constructions.

## Conjecture loop

1. Merely transfer the involutions through the existing comparison isomorphism:
   insufficient, since it need not equal the original source involution.
2. Prove the invisible kernel and finite causal span stable under the actual
   involutions. Average the invisible retraction over their finite symmetry
   group. An average of retractions with common kernel target is still a
   retraction; scalar-polynomial covariance may survive the affine and
   conjugate-affine generator transformations. This must be proved against the
   actual source definitions, including canonical chain covariance.
