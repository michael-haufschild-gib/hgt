## Conjecture C2

Let `Z_n(x)` be the full matched oriented phase symbol and `a_n` the nonzero projective gauge.
Define the Hermitian Dirac block

```text
H(Z) = [[0, 0, -conj Z],
        [0, 0,        1],
        [-Z, 1,       0]].
```

Its kernel is exactly the line generated by `v=(a_n,a_n Z,0)`. Let

```text
B_re = [[0, 1, 0], [1, 0, 0], [0,0,0]],
B_im = [[0,-i, 0], [i, 0, 0], [0,0,0]].
```

Then

```text
v* B_re v = 2 a_n² Re Z,
v* B_im v = 2 a_n² Im Z,
det(H + t B_re + u B_im) = -2(t Re Z + u Im Z).
```

For positive atom weight and `x != 0`, the even component of the matched profile is strictly
positive, hence `Z_n(x) != 0`. At `x=0`, `Z_n(0)=0` and `(Im Z_n)'(0)=-M_n<0` at every prime-power
wall. Thus the two-shear crossing gradient is globally nonzero off center and has a simple
transverse center crossing. A nonempty finite label-preserving packet inherits positive joint
crossing energy `sum a_n^4 |Z_n(x)|^2` for every `x != 0`.
