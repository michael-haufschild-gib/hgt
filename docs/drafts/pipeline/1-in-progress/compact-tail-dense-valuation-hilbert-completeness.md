# Compact tail-dense valuation--Hilbert completeness

- **ID:** `compact-tail-dense-valuation-hilbert-completeness`
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** `[CONJECTURE] [MASTER THEOREM] [GENERAL HT LAW]`
- **Scientific status:** `[NOVELTY UNVERIFIED] [SIGNIFICANCE CANDIDATE]`
- **Source:** compact prime tail constraints, continuous-observable detection, prime exponential--
  polynomial rigidity, and valuation--Hilbert tomography
- **Statement:** A prime profile that stays in a compact constraint space and visits every
  neighbourhood of every constraint point after every finite deletion transports the complete
  reduced constraint ideal into its valuation-graded differential relation ideal; distinct
  Dirichlet shifts and all logarithmic jets introduce no further relations.

## Goal

Replace deliberately repeated coefficient fibres by an intrinsic distribution law. Let `K` be a
compact Hausdorff space, let

```text
x : Primes -> K,
```

and assume every `v : K` is a cluster point of `x` for the cofinite filter on the primes. Equivalently,
every neighbourhood of every point of `K` is hit after deleting any finite set of primes. This is
the topological statement that every prime tail is dense in `K`.

The intended theorem is about hypertranscendental prime-supported systems in general. Natural
equidistributed local-parameter families are the motivating applications. RH is only a downstream
pressure test.

## Master theorem

Let `f_j : K -> C` be a finite family of continuous observables, and let `alpha_a` be a finite
palette of complex shifts with pairwise distinct real parts.

1. **Compact observable kernel.** For every continuous `f : K -> C`, the prime column
   `p |-> f(x(p))` is nonzero at only finitely many primes if and only if `f` vanishes identically
   on `K`.
2. **Constraint detection.** If `K` is a compact subset of a finite-dimensional complex affine
   space, a polynomial evaluation column has finite support exactly when the polynomial vanishes
   on `K`. In every homogeneous degree, the prime-tail quotient is canonically the image of
   restriction to `K`.
3. **Compact shift--clock separation.** If the restrictions `f_j|K` are linearly independent,
   then, for every finite jet order `m`, all columns

   ```text
   p |-> f_j(x(p)) p^(-alpha_a) (log p)^k,    0 <= k <= m,
   ```

   are independent modulo finite prime support.
4. **Valuation--Hilbert completeness.** Applied degree by degree to homogeneous polynomial
   observables, the valuation-graded arithmetic differential field has rank spectrum equal to the
   Hilbert function of the reduced homogeneous coordinate algebra of `K`; its complete labelled
   differential relation system is generated by the homogeneous vanishing ideal, its
   prolongations, and finite-prime base corrections.
5. **Naturality.** A homogeneous polynomial map of degree `q` sends the valuation grading
   `r |-> q r` and the order-`k` logarithmic jet clock by the exact factor `q^k`. The construction
   therefore defines a covariant constraint realization rather than an isolated encoding.

The positive law is:

```text
compact tail density prevents local arithmetic data from encoding the logarithmic clock;
prime valuation is therefore forced to linearize the complete reduced constraint geometry.
```

## Why this is stronger than cofinal recurrence

Cofinal recurrence separates jets by assigning every relevant coefficient value an infinite exact
prime fibre. That construction can be attacked as arbitrary coding. Compact tail density requires
no repeated values at all. A profile may be injective, equidistributed, or generated by an
arithmetic dynamical law. Its topology, not a chosen colouring, supplies the coefficient
separation.

The theorem would include finite-range and cofinally recurrent realizations as discrete special
cases, while admitting positive-dimensional compact constraint spaces and naturally distributed
arithmetic parameters.

## Proof architecture

1. Use cluster-point functoriality and uniqueness of limits to show that a continuous observable
   tending to zero along the cofinite prime filter vanishes at every point of `K`.
2. Convert finite support into eventual zero and apply the first step. The converse uses that the
   whole profile is valued in `K`.
3. For a finite shifted-jet relation, choose the shift of least real part and its highest nonzero
   logarithmic degree. Divide by that leading weight. Compactness uniformly bounds every
   coefficient observable; all lower clock powers and all faster-decaying shifts tend to zero.
4. The surviving continuous coefficient function tends to zero along the whole prime tail. Step 1
   makes it vanish on `K`; linear independence of the `f_j` kills its coefficients. Descend through
   clock degrees and shifts.
5. Feed the resulting tail-independent rectangles into the existing prime-power valuation,
   differential-independence, pivot-elimination, and homogeneous-relation machinery.
6. Combine with homogeneous-map valuation functoriality to obtain the naturality statement.

## Antagonistic attacks

1. **Compact-hull attack.** Hulls, cluster sets, and continuous-function algebras are classical.
   Novelty can only lie in the exact arithmetic valuation--Hilbert and all-jet differential
   completeness theorem, not in compactness itself.
2. **Asymptotic-scale attack.** Dominance of exponential polynomials is classical. The candidate
   survives only if compact tail density genuinely promotes it from scalar constants to a complete
   continuous coefficient algebra without a rate-of-equidistribution hypothesis.
3. **Encoding attack.** The theorem must apply to injective and naturally equidistributed profiles;
   no construction by repeated fibres may occur in the main statement or proof.
4. **Closure attack.** Point evaluations recover only the reduced vanishing ideal and its Zariski
   closure. Nilpotent scheme structure is not claimed.
5. **Algebraic-independence attack.** Linear tail independence is not itself hypertranscendence.
   The paper-facing result must include the unconditional arithmetic-function Jacobian and
   differential-field transfer already present in the formal corpus.
6. **Priority attack.** Search compact coefficient hulls of Dirichlet series, Bohr almost-periodic
   methods, arithmetic-function Jacobian criteria, differential algebra of Dirichlet series, and
   Hilbert-series reconstruction before any novelty claim.

## RH pressure test

For the scalar Euler factor of zeta, the compact local-parameter space is a point, so every finite
valuation degree has rank one and no hidden finite-window differential relation remains. More
generally, naturally distributed Satake-parameter families can contribute exactly their reduced
local constraint geometry and nothing beyond it. Any RH selector must therefore use an infinite
completion, an archimedean reflection coupling, or another structure not visible in finite
valuation--jet windows.

This is a positive architectural constraint, not an RH theorem.
