## Frozen public conjecture

> **Time is not the line along which the universe changes. Time is the
> completed space of changes an observer can distinguish.**

> **Different observers can therefore inhabit different dimensions of time,
> even while using the same one-dimensional clock reading.**

> **Interaction creates new dimensions of completed time. A horizon hides
> them.**

The one-parameter label \(t\) is only an ordering device. A single orbit can
be dense in a high- or infinite-dimensional completed temporal carrier, just
as one irrational line can be dense in a torus. The conjectured physical
object is that minimal carrier, not the redundant parameterization of it.

## The Einstein-style reversal

Conventional dynamics begins with a time set and asks how observables change
on it:
\[
  \text{time parameter}\longrightarrow\text{observable histories}.
\]
The conjecture reverses this dependency:
\[
  \boxed{
  \text{all operationally distinguishable histories}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{observer's time}.
  }
\]

Two parameter times are the same physical instant for an observer exactly
when no admissible experiment distinguishes their histories. Missing limit
points are then added. The resulting completion is the observer's intrinsic
time. Coordinate time is any one-dimensional path that generates it.

The invariant replacing an observer-independent time line is the system of
quotient maps between the completed times of all observer families.

## Frozen operational core

Let a physical process have a parameter semigroup \(P\), an admissible family
of preparations, interventions and reporters, and no presupposed physical
metric on \(P\). For an observer family \(\mathcal O\), let
\[
  \Phi_{\mathcal O}:P\longrightarrow
  \prod_{e\in\mathcal O} X_e
\]
be the complete vector of outcome laws of all experiments in
\(\mathcal O\). Give \(P\) the weakest uniformity making
\(\Phi_{\mathcal O}\) uniformly continuous, quotient parameter points with
identical outcome laws, and complete:
\[
  \mathbb T_{\mathcal O}
  :=
  \widehat{P/{\sim_{\mathcal O}}}.
  \tag{1}
\]

The conjecture is that \(\mathbb T_{\mathcal O}\), together with its
composition law and maps under observer refinement, is the unique minimal
physical time seen by \(\mathcal O\):

1. every observable history factors uniquely through
   \(\mathbb T_{\mathcal O}\);
2. every other complete carrier with that property maps onto
   \(\mathbb T_{\mathcal O}\);
3. if \(\mathcal O\subseteq\mathcal O'\), forgetting experiments induces a
   canonical quotient
   \[
     \mathbb T_{\mathcal O'}\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb T_{\mathcal O};
     \tag{2}
   \]
4. observer-dependent temporal dimension is the intrinsic topological,
   character, or scale-dependent metric dimension of this minimal carrier,
   not the dimension of \(P\);
5. compact directions are recurrent completed time; noncompact or transient
   directions are irreversible completed time.

The hard content is not the formal existence of a completion. It is the
claim that one construction simultaneously and minimally determines every
operational clock, every return law, observer dependence, interaction
composition and the recurrent/transient boundary.

## Frozen finite quantum specialization

For a finite closed quantum system, let the observer access a declared family
of energy-basis coherences. Join energy labels \(j,k\) when some admissible
experiment detects their relative phase. The observable history map contains
the characters
\[
  t\longmapsto e^{-it(\lambda_j-\lambda_k)}.
\]

The conjecture specializes to the following exact law:

1. \(\mathbb T_{\mathcal O}\) is the compact closure of the visible relative
   phase orbit;
2. its character module is generated by exactly the visible energy gaps;
3. its dimension is their rational rank after quotienting the independent
   phase choice on every connected component of the observer coherence
   graph;
4. every continuous observer return statistic is a function on this one
   carrier, and its complete long-time law is Haar pushforward;
5. two observers have the same physical time exactly when their completed
   carriers and all labelled return functions are isomorphic.

Thus a one-dimensional parameter orbit may generate zero, one, finitely many,
or infinitely many observer-visible dimensions of time.

## Frozen interaction law as a corollary, not the principle

For arbitrary finite tensor factors on an undirected interaction graph, put
\[
  \tau(C)=\dim\mathbb T_C.
\]
For generic local Hamiltonians the conjecture predicts
\[
  \tau(C)+1=\prod_{v\in C} \dim\mathcal H_v
  \tag{3}
\]
on every connected component \(C\). If one interaction joins components
\(A\) and \(B\), then
\[
  \boxed{
  \tau(A\Join B)
  =
  \tau(A)+\tau(B)+\tau(A)\tau(B).
  }
  \tag{4}
\]

Equation `(4)` says that interaction creates the cross-directions of
completed time. The previously separate statements

- generic local nonresonance,
- loss of exact recurrence,
- exponential recurrence scarcity,
- sensitivity to arbitrarily weak coupling,
- and divergence of finite-volume recurrence rank

would all be shadows of one compositional law for physical time.

## Frozen spatial law

For disjoint operational regions define temporal interaction curvature
\[
  \kappa(A,B)
  :=
  \dim\mathbb T_{A\cup B}
  -\dim\mathbb T_A
  -\dim\mathbb T_B.
  \tag{5}
\]

The conjecture is that, after minimizing over source-complete observer
families:

1. \(\kappa(A,B)=0\) exactly when \(A\) and \(B\) remain dynamically
   separable;
2. \(\kappa(A,B)>0\) detects physical adjacency or a minimal irreducible
   interaction hyperedge;
3. the birth order of nonzero conditional temporal curvature gives
   round-trip distance;
4. the complete multiscale curvature functor reconstructs the minimal
   spatial/causal interaction geometry up to operational gauge.

Space is then not an additional background reconstructed beside time. Space
is the pattern describing where completed time fails to compose additively.

## Frozen timeless-universe clause

A globally constrained or stationary state can have a trivial external orbit
while conditional observer histories have nontrivial completed carriers.
The conjecture is:

> **A universe may be globally timeless and internally temporal. Its
> observable time is the completed carrier of relational change, and its
> temporal dimension depends on which correlations an internal observer can
> access.**

For every finite relational-clock realization, the conditional completed time
is conjectured to be exactly the character completion of the energy gaps in
the clock-system correlation support. Coupling independent relational clocks
obeys `(4)` whenever it generically connects their correlation supports.

This clause is meant to answer the problem-of-time question, not evade it by
inserting an unobservable external clock.

## Frozen horizon and black-hole conjecture

An operational horizon for an exterior observer is a proper quotient
\[
  \pi_{\rm ext}:
  \mathbb T_{\rm full}\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb T_{\rm ext}
  \tag{6}
\]
whose kernel contains completed temporal directions accessible to global
experiments but to no exterior experiment.

The deliberately high-risk conjecture is:

> **Black-hole entropy counts the information capacity of time hidden by the
> horizon.**

More exactly, in any ultraviolet-complete finite-resolution quantum-gravity
model admitting full and exterior return atlases, the
Bekenstein--Hawking/generalized entropy is conjectured to equal the
renormalized logarithmic covering growth of the fibres of `(6)`, with the
same quantity governing exterior recurrence suppression and the multiplicity
of globally distinct histories compatible with one exterior completed time.

This is not presently a theorem, and no choice of covering functional is
frozen beyond the required properties below. A successful law must derive,
not assume:

1. regulator independence after the model's physical renormalization;
2. the area rather than volume scaling;
3. agreement with generalized entropy including exterior fields;
4. monotonicity under loss of exterior access;
5. a recurrence or spectroscopy consequence different from ordinary
   microstate counting.

If no non-arbitrary fibre invariant has all five properties, the black-hole
clause dies. The finite completed-time law may survive.

## Why this is a larger jump

This conjecture does not rename one spectral rank. It proposes one new
primitive and demands that current categories become projections:

| Familiar object | Conjectured status |
|---|---|
| coordinate time \(t\) | a generating path, not physical time itself |
| clock | a coordinate chart on an observer's completed time |
| temporal dimension | dimension of distinguishable history completion |
| recurrence | compactness and return geometry of completed time |
| arrow of time | transient/noncompact completed directions |
| interaction | creator of cross-directions of time |
| space | nonadditivity pattern of temporal composition |
| horizon | observer quotient hiding temporal directions |
| black-hole entropy | capacity of the hidden temporal fibre |

The paper succeeds only if several nontrivial rows follow from one exact
construction. A philosophical relabelling with separately attached theorems
fails.

## Hard falsifiers

Retire or split the conjecture if any occurs:

1. operational completion depends on arbitrary experiment coordinates rather
   than only distinguishability;
2. observer refinement does not induce canonical temporal quotients;
3. finite quantum completion fails to reproduce the exact visible-gap
   character group and all observer return statistics;
4. two operationally inequivalent histories have the same completed-time
   object;
5. interaction law `(4)` fails on a connected finite local model under the
   declared genericity;
6. temporal interaction curvature cannot distinguish interaction from
   common cause, indirect mediation, or observer choice;
7. globally stationary relational models require an external time to define
   their claimed internal carrier;
8. reversible continuous-spectrum dynamics is falsely classified as
   intrinsically irreversible;
9. a known theory already gives the same minimal observer-completed time,
   interaction multiplication law and spatial-curvature reconstruction;
10. horizon fibre capacity has no unique physical definition or cannot
    derive an area law and a new exterior prediction.

## First hostile attack after the freeze — 2026-07-30

### What is already occupied

The ontology cannot claim novelty merely from “time emerges,” “time is
observer-dependent,” “a stationary universe contains relational evolution,”
or “incommensurate frequencies form a higher-dimensional temporal torus.”
Primary parents already occupy those statements:

1. Page and Wootters derive dynamics relative to internal clock readings from
   stationary global observables:
   <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885>.
2. Connes and Rovelli identify physical time with a state-dependent modular
   automorphism flow and apply the proposal to Unruh and Hawking physics:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9406019>.
3. Quantum-clock and quantum-reference-frame work treats time as operational
   and observer-relative, including interacting and gravitationally
   interacting clocks:
   <https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2019-07-08-160/> and
   <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16013-1>.
4. Gemsheim and Rost derive interacting conditional Schrödinger dynamics from
   a static global state:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05159>.
5. Quasiperiodic-drive theory uses one irrational parameter orbit through a
   higher-dimensional frequency torus, with one synthetic dimension per
   incommensurate drive:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.04834>.
6. Bohr compactification, almost-periodic hulls and topological-dynamical
   orbit closures already supply the mathematical completion and its
   universal property.

Therefore equations `(1)`--`(2)`, the timeless-universe sentence, and the
finite phase-torus description are conceptual parents or mathematical
infrastructure. They do not by themselves support a novelty claim.

### What remains potentially new

No exact collision was found in the first search for the conjunction:

\[
  \boxed{
  \begin{gathered}
  \text{endogenous observer-completed temporal dimension}\\
  +\ \text{generic local-interaction multiplication `(4)`}\\
  +\ \text{spatial reconstruction from temporal curvature `(5)`}.
  \end{gathered}}
\]

The distinction from quasiperiodic synthetic time is sharp: its torus
dimension is externally supplied by the number of drive tones. Here the
conjectured dimensions are generated endogenously by the spectrum of one
static locally interacting system, and a single weak bridge creates the exact
cross term \(\tau(A)\tau(B)\).

This residue is only a candidate. A complete priority audit is still needed.

### Immediate truth failures and repairs

1. **Raw observational equivalence need not be a congruence.** Equality of
   present outcome vectors need not survive future translation, so
   \(P/{\sim_{\mathcal O}}\) may have no well-defined composition. The core
   must use the full translated-history atlas, not one-time outcomes.
2. **Non-precompact is not irreversible.** Reversible unitary
   continuous-spectrum dynamics can have a non-precompact norm orbit or
   decaying weak correlations. Clause 5 of the operational core is false if
   it identifies every noncompact direction with intrinsic irreversibility.
   Reversibility, dispersive escape and contractive loss require separate
   invariants.
3. **Temporal curvature can confuse direct and mediated interaction.** Ports
   \(A\) and \(B\) coupled only through an unobserved \(C\) may acquire a
   nonadditive joint completed carrier. Pair curvature alone cannot certify a
   direct edge. Exact spatial reconstruction needs conditional curvature over
   all port subsets or labelled return birth data.
4. **Completion alone is nearly tautological.** Calling a standard history
   hull “physical time” becomes science only if one new theorem forces a
   result not already present in the parameterized dynamics. Equations
   `(3)`--`(5)` are presently the only such candidate spine.
5. **Black-hole fibre capacity is underdefined.** Different coverings,
   observable algebras and regulators can manufacture different numbers. The
   horizon clause is not manuscript material unless gravity uniquely selects
   the invariant and the area law follows.

### Antagonist verdict

The larger ontology survives as a research conjecture but not yet as a paper
claim. Its generic mathematical shell is known. The nontrivial possible
discovery is narrower in statement but deeper in interpretation:

> **Spatial interaction is exactly the positive curvature of
> observer-completed temporal dimension, and connecting quantum worlds
> creates the cross-directions of time.**

This must be proved as one theorem, not assembled from an orbit-closure
definition plus a graph theorem. If conditional temporal curvature does not
recover interaction geometry, completed-time relativity reduces to a
philosophical relabelling and must be rejected.
