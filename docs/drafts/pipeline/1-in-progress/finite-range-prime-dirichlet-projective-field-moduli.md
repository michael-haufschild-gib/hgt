# Finite-range prime-Dirichlet projective field moduli

Status: in progress

Novelty status: [NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]

## Claim

Let \(c_j:\mathbb P\to\mathbb C\) be a finite family whose joint coefficient pattern has finite range, and let
\[
  P_{c_j}(s)=\sum_p c_j(p)p^{-s}.
\]
Over the finite-prime rational coefficient field, the complete family of natural translates
\(P_{c_j}(s+n)\), together with all derivatives, is algebraically independent exactly when the
tail classes \([c_j]\in \mathbb C^{\mathbb P}/\mathbb C^{(\mathbb P)}\) are linearly independent.

For two finite-range sources \(c,d\), the embedded differential fields generated by their complete
natural-shift palettes are equal exactly when
\[
  [c]=\lambda[d]
\]
for some \(\lambda\in\mathbb C^\times\). Thus their field moduli are the zero orbit together with
the projective space of nonzero finite-range tail classes. The signed-prime quotient by eventual
equality or opposition is the unit-sign corollary.

## Sharpness

Finite range is essential for the collapse from shift--jet rank to scalar tail rank. Outside it,
\(c_1(p)=1\) and \(c_2(p)=p^{-a}\) can be scalar-tail independent while their Dirichlet sources
satisfy \(P_{c_2}(s)=P_{c_1}(s+a)\).

## Formal target

1. Prove the exact finite shifted-palette criterion for bounded finite-range coefficients.
2. Pass to the complete natural-shift palette by finite-support restriction.
3. Prove invariance of the generated differential field under nonzero complex rescaling.
4. Identify finite-support scalar residuals with base-field jet corrections.
5. Prove the exact projective field-equality criterion, including the zero-tail orbit.
