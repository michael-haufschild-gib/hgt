### Universalization attempt A: sharp angular law

`[CONJECTURE]` For nonzero `z ∈ ℂ` and `n ≥ 1`, positivity of `Re(z⁻ⁿ)` is exactly membership of
`arg z` in the union of the open sectors on which `cos(n arg z) > 0`.

This strictly subsumes the five local `...re_pos_of_sectorRatio` lemmas after their rational
inequalities are mapped into the appropriate angular sectors.

`[REDISCOVERED]` The identity `Re(z⁻ⁿ) = |z|⁻ⁿ cos(n arg z)` proves and classifies the statement.
It is elementary polar form, not a new law.

### Universalization attempt B: symmetry-amplified moment exclusion

`[CONJECTURE]` Let a finite group `G` act on a countable atomic support `X`. Let `w : X → ℝ≥0` be
constant on `G`-orbits and summable, with total budget `Σ_x w(x) ≤ B`. Then every atom satisfies

`|G · x| w(x) ≤ B`.

Consequently, any geometric bad set on which `w(x) > B / |G · x|` is disjoint from the support.
For the five Xi theorems, `G` is the functional-equation/Schwarz Klein four action, `w` is the
nonnegative fourth reciprocal-trace atom, and each edge-corner lower bound exceeds one quarter of
the source budget. This law therefore subsumes all five corner-source exclusions and, composed with
attempt A, all five final reciprocal-power positivity theorems.

`[REDISCOVERED]` Summing `w(gx)=w(x)` over distinct orbit points gives the displayed inequality in
one line. It is the atomic support form of the pigeonhole/Markov bound. Parameterizing Xi's source
data does not create new mathematics.

### Universalization attempt C: optimal multi-moment exclusion

`[CONJECTURE]` Replace the single fourth-moment witness by all available reciprocal moments and
characterize the largest excludable bad sector by a polynomial dual certificate nonnegative on the
candidate support.

`[REDISCOVERED]` In finite degree this is a truncated semialgebraic moment problem: infeasible
support constraints are separated by a positive polynomial, with sum-of-squares/Positivstellensatz
certificates under standard compactness hypotheses. This is stronger than attempt B but belongs to
established moment-cone duality. The corpus's finite power-sum route independently records that its
purported new endpoint converges to standard Li/Bombieri--Lagarias machinery.

### Universalization attempt D: order-free reciprocal positivity

`[CONJECTURE]` A fixed finite source-moment budget plus FE--Schwarz symmetry forces positivity of
all sufficiently high reciprocal powers, extending orders `15`--`19` uniformly.

`[FALSIFIED]` If `μ ≠ 0` is not positive real, then `Re(μⁿ) ≤ 0` for infinitely many positive
integers `n`: write `μ = |μ| exp(iθ)`; rational `θ/(2π)` gives a nontrivial finite cyclic orbit
meeting the closed left half-plane, while irrational `θ/(2π)` gives a dense orbit. Thus eventual
positivity of every reciprocal power already forces the reciprocal coordinate to be positive real.
For the Xi squared coordinate this is the critical-line condition, so the proposed order-free law
is RH-strength rather than a consequence of the fixed fourth-moment budget.

The corpus's dependency-closed finite-degree blindness theorem supplies the complementary source
obstruction: every prescribed finite shifted-reciprocal Hankel audit remains positive after adding
a sufficiently small nonreal conjugate pair. Hence no finite collection of moment/sector tests can
upgrade the five protected orders to a uniform all-order theorem.

## No-go closure

- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:** attempt A is polar form; attempt B is orbit summation;
  attempt C is truncated moment-cone separation/Positivstellensatz; attempt D is the cyclic/dense
  power-angle classification together with finite-degree reciprocal blindness.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** `[BRIDGE]` For any finite symmetry group and any nonnegative
  summable orbit-invariant atom weight, `|G·x| w(x) ≤ Σ_y w(y)`. Composed with a geometric lower
  bound on a bad sector, this is a reusable source-budget localization lemma and exactly explains
  the five Xi corner exclusions. Its proof is elementary and it supplies no new consequence beyond
  the already-proved specializations, so it is not promoted or formalized.
- **New insight:** the order-`15`--`19` cluster is one bounded-order, source-certified positivity
  window. It is not evidence for an order-free reciprocal-power law. Crossing order `19` requires
  genuinely new source information uniform in degree/height; abstraction of the existing fourth
  moment cannot provide it.
