
The parent card proves that `S_(q,C)` is a regular factorizing scattering function, reconstructs



Some sources write the exponent with `-i`; their deformation parameters are `-alpha_s` relative

For `(1)`, the pole chart is exactly


## Claim A -- exact local higher-spin germ

For real rapidity in the germ `|sinh(theta)| < C`, choose the continuous phase

```text
P_(q,C)(theta) = exp(i Sigma_(q,C)(theta)).
```

The geometric sum and the Taylor series of `arctan` give

```text
Sigma_(q,C)(theta)
 = 4 sum_(n>=0)
     (-1)^n sinh(theta)^(2n+1)
     / ((2n+1) C^(2n+1) (1-q^(2n+1))).                     (5)
```

For `m>=0`, the elementary identity

```text
sinh(theta)^(2n+1)
 = 2^(-2n) sum_(j=0)^n
     (-1)^j binom(2n+1,j) sinh((2n+1-2j)theta)              (6)
```

turns `(5)` into the local-current chart

```text
Sigma_(q,C)(theta) = sum_(m>=0) alpha_(2m+1) sinh((2m+1)theta),

alpha_(2m+1)
 = 4 (-1)^m sum_(n>=m)
     binom(2n+1,n-m)
     / (2^(2n) (2n+1) C^(2n+1) (1-q^(2n+1))).              (7)
```

The rearrangement is absolutely convergent in a neighborhood of zero.  A sufficient estimate is
obtained by expanding `(6)`, bounding each harmonic by `exp((2n+1)|theta|)`, and taking
`exp(|theta|)<C`.  For every `m`, the unsigned series in `(7)` is convergent and strictly positive.
Consequently

```text
sign(alpha_(2m+1)) = (-1)^m,       alpha_(2m+1) != 0.       (8)
```

Every positive odd higher-spin direction is therefore present.  No finite set of local
current--current couplings represents the exact germ.

There is a sharper pole--coupling formula.  Write

```text
C q^(-k) = cosh(eta_k),
r_k = exp(-eta_k)
    = C q^(-k) - sqrt(C^2 q^(-2k)-1),       0 < r_k < 1.    (9)
```

The elementary strip expansion

```text
4 arctan(sinh(theta)/cosh(eta))
 = 8 sum_(m>=0)
     (-1)^m exp(-(2m+1)eta)/(2m+1)
     sinh((2m+1)theta)                                      (10)
```

gives the equivalent exact coefficient law

```text
alpha_(2m+1)
 = 8 (-1)^m/(2m+1) sum_(k>=0) r_k^(2m+1).                 (11)
```

Thus the sign-corrected higher-spin couplings

```text
b_m = (-1)^m (2m+1) alpha_(2m+1)/8
    = sum_(k>=0) r_k^(2m+1)                                (12)
```

are a Hausdorff moment sequence.  More precisely, for the finite positive measure

```text
nu = sum_(k>=0) r_k delta_(r_k^2),                          (13)
```

one has `b_m = integral y^m d nu(y)`.  Consequently every Hankel form

```text
sum_(i,j=0)^N conjugate(c_i) c_j b_(i+j) > 0               (14)
```

for every nonzero finite coefficient vector `c`: a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on the
infinite support of `nu`.  In particular, every finite Hankel determinant is strictly positive.
Since the Hausdorff moment problem on a compact interval is determinate, the full odd higher-spin
coupling vector uniquely recovers `nu`, hence all `r_k`, `eta_k`, and the CDD divisor `(4)`.  The
local irrelevant couplings and the global resonance divisor are therefore two lossless coordinates
for this pole-only completion.  The source papers state the coordinate equivalence; the explicit
strict moment reconstruction is a formula-level consequence whose literature novelty remains
unverified.

This gives an exact low-energy certificate for an infinite resonance tower.  A pole-only CDD
factor with `K` distinct pole radii gives a moment matrix of rank at most `K`, so every Hankel
determinant of size greater than `K` vanishes.  For the geometric tower, every finite Hankel
determinant is strictly positive.  The distinction between finitely and infinitely many resonance
scales is therefore visible in the complete local higher-spin coupling germ without first
performing its global meromorphic continuation.

## Claim B -- finite local truncations are DA

For odd `s`, there is a polynomial `H_s in Z[t]` satisfying

```text
H_s(sinh(theta)) = sinh(s theta).                           (15)
```

For a finite odd-spin set `F`, define

```text
Phi_F(t) = exp(i sum_(s in F) alpha_s H_s(t)).              (16)
```

Then `Phi_F` satisfies the first-order differential equation

```text
Phi_F'(t)
 = i (sum_(s in F) alpha_s H_s'(t)) Phi_F(t).              (17)
```

Hence every finite generalized-`T bar T`/higher-spin truncation is DA over `C(t)`.  It is also
entire and nonvanishing as a function of `t`, so it cannot reproduce the infinite divisor in
`(1)`.

By contrast, the global completion

```text
P_q(t) = product_(k>=0) ((1-q^k t)/(1+q^k t))^2            (18)
```

satisfies the rational difference constraint

```text
P_q(t) = ((1-t)/(1+t))^2 P_q(qt)                           (19)
```

and has infinitely many zeros and poles.  The Adamczewski--Dreyfus--Hardouin dichotomy therefore
puts `P_q` on the HT, rather than rational, branch.  The differential-algebraic class changes only
after the whole tower is globally resummed:

```text
every finite local-current truncation:  DA,
unique geometric meromorphic completion: HT.               (20)
```

## Claim C -- the physical meaning of the fracture

The measurable logarithmic-energy phase response is

```text

```


The parent ultraviolet-boundary card proves that, after logarithmic recentering, `(21)` tends to


This is an observable energy-scale connection, not yet a theorem identifying rapidity with a
Wilsonian RG time.

The completion fracture also has an exact exchange-geometric form.  Every finite CDD truncation


## What is proved and what is not

### Proved on paper

1. The pole-coordinate identification `(4)`.
2. The convergent local expansions `(5)`--`(12)`, moment positivity, determinacy, and
   nonvanishing of every odd-spin coefficient.
3. Differential algebraicity of every finite higher-spin truncation.
4. Hypertranscendence of the exact global completion, conditional only on the cited published
   difference-Galois theorem already audited at source level.
5. Existence of the corresponding local interacting algebraic QFT through the audited regular
   inverse-scattering theorem.
6. Every nonzero compact-local vacuum-to-two-particle form factor is HT, these form factors are
   dense by Reeh--Schlieder, and `sigma_loc,2 >= sigma_scatt`.
7. The infinite completion is not DA-exchange-gauge-equivalent to any finite CDD truncation.

### Not proved

1. An action-level construction obtained by integrating all current--current flows at once.
2. Existence or regularity of the generalized GGE/TBA for the infinite coupling vector `(7)`.
3. A Wilsonian ultraviolet fixed point or a conformal scaling limit.
4. The exact Hagedorn critical radius, branch topology, and density-of-states coefficient.  The
   child card now proves analytically that the real finite-free-energy TBA has a large-radius
   primary solution and no real solution below an explicit positive radius; it does not prove the
   numerically expected unique square-root turning point.
5. That HT characterizes Hagedorn behavior or cyclic RG.  Known elliptic DA models already show
   those phenomena.
