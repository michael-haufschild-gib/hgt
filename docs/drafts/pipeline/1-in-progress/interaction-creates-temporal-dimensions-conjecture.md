# Interaction creates temporal dimensions

- **ID:** `interaction-creates-temporal-dimensions`
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** FROZEN FOUNDATIONAL CONJECTURE / UNIVERSAL RECURRENCE LAW
- **Statement:** Interaction converts independently factorized temporal
  constraints into new joint temporal directions while preserving total
  observer-relative temporal capacity.

## Conjecture discipline

This truth-hunch is frozen before its open, infinite or continuum forms are
tested for proofability. Independent composition is an explicit physical
input. The conjecture does not derive space from temporal data and makes no
claim that a Hamiltonian selects its own tensor decomposition.

## Public law

> **Interactions do not merely happen in time. They create the joint
> dimensions of time in which interacting systems can change.**

Shortest form:

> **Every interaction makes time larger.**

“Larger” means more independent observer-distinguishable temporal
directions, not a faster clock and not an extra coordinate in a supplied
spacetime manifold.

## Ontological reversal

Conventional physics supplies one time parameter and lets interactions alter
states along it. The conjecture reverses that dependency:

1. an isolated system has a completed space of distinguishable temporal
   phases;
2. independent systems have factorized completed time;
3. interaction creates joint phase distinctions that neither system
   possesses separately;
4. the scalar clock parameter is only a path through the resulting completed
   temporal object.

The missing physical variable is therefore not another clock reading. It is
the number and organization of independent temporal distinctions created,
hidden or made transient by composition.

## Universal capacity law

For an observer \(\mathcal O\), let \(\mathbb T_{\mathcal O}(A)\) be the
completion of histories of system \(A\) after identifying exactly what
\(\mathcal O\) cannot distinguish. Let \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal O}(A)\) denote
the independent exact temporal constraints and
\(\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}(A)\) the transient directions that no longer
belong to the recurrent completion.

The conjecture is that every physically admissible composition has a
canonical capacity object
\[
  \mathcal C_{\mathcal O}(A)
\]
with a presentation-independent decomposition
\[
  \boxed{
  \mathcal C_{\mathcal O}(A)
  =
  \mathbb T_{\mathcal O}(A)
  \oplus
  \mathcal L_{\mathcal O}(A)
  \oplus
  \mathcal X_{\mathcal O}(A),
  }
  \tag{U1}
\]
interpreted as an exact sequence, dimension identity or generalized
dimension decomposition according to the physical category.

Interaction does not create capacity from nothing. It transfers capacity:

- destroyed exact constraints become new recurrent temporal directions;
- dissipation transfers recurrent directions into transient ones;
- observer coarse-graining transfers visible directions into hidden
  constraints;
- removal of an interaction reverses the transfer when the limit is
  dynamically resolvable.

The total capacity is functorial under physical equivalence and additive or
multiplicative under declared independent composition. A direct-sum
dimension formula is only its finite linear shadow.

## Exact finite closed shadow

For independently composed finite quantum systems \(A,B\), write
\(\tau_A,\tau_B\) for their complete projective temporal dimensions. A
generic interaction joining them is predicted to obey
\[
  \boxed{
  1+\tau_{A\Join B}
  =
  (1+\tau_A)(1+\tau_B),
  }
  \tag{U2}
\]
or equivalently
\[
  \boxed{
  \Delta\tau
  =
  \tau_A\tau_B.
  }
  \tag{U3}
\]

If \(\rho\) is exact projective spectral-relation rank, total finite capacity
is
\[
  \boxed{\tau+\rho=D-1.}
  \tag{U4}
\]
Thus `(U3)` is simultaneously
\[
  \Delta\tau=-\Delta\rho=\tau_A\tau_B:
\]
interaction converts exactly the cross-system recurrence constraints into
new dimensions of completed time.

For a finite interaction graph \(G\) with local dimensions \(q_v\), the
predicted all-graph form is
\[
  \boxed{
  \tau(G)
  =
  \sum_{C\in\pi_0(G)}
  \left(\prod_{v\in C}q_v-1\right)
  }
  \tag{U5}
\]
for generic local Hermitian dynamics. This formula is relative to the
declared physical factors; it does not reconstruct those factors from a bare
Hamiltonian.

## Frozen risky predictions

1. **Integer weak-coupling jump.** For every pair of finite systems with
   generic internal spectra, every sufficiently generic nonzero coupling,
   however weak, changes the asymptotic projective return exponent by exactly
   \(\tau_A\tau_B\).
2. **Noncommuting limits.** As coupling strength \(g\to0\), bounded-time
   dynamics varies continuously while infinite-time recurrence dimension
   jumps at \(g=0\). The observation time needed to resolve the new
   directions diverges.
3. **Network law.** Every finite local interaction graph obeys `(U5)`
   generically and all induced subsystem restrictions can obey it
   simultaneously.
4. **Exact return-density change.** For tolerance \(0<\varepsilon<2\), joining
   two components multiplies small-target return density by the universal
   additional factor
   \[
     \left(\frac{2\arcsin(\varepsilon/2)}{\pi}\right)^{\tau_A\tau_B}
   \]
   in the maximal-rank phase regime.
5. **Open-system ledger.** For every finite contractive quantum evolution,
   loss of peripheral recurrent capacity appears exactly as transient or
   observer-hidden capacity in `(U1)`; it cannot disappear from the complete
   capacity object.
6. **Infinite connected growth.** Along every locally finite connected
   exhaustion with nontrivial local factors, generic recurrent temporal
   dimension is unbounded even though each finite subsystem has finite
   capacity.
7. **Operational invariance.** Similarity, basis, energy-origin and clock
   coordinate changes leave the complete capacity decomposition invariant.

## Parameter-free finite experiment

Use two programmable finite quantum registers with independently calibrated
local dimensions and generic internal Hamiltonians. Measure projective
return density as a function of return tolerance before and after switching
on a tunable generic bridge interaction.

The predicted exponent change is the integer
\[
  \tau_A\tau_B,
\]
independent of nonzero bridge strength. Bridge strength controls the time
needed to reach the asymptotic regime, not the asymptotic exponent. Varying
strength therefore separates an integer structural jump from an ordinary
continuous rate change.

## What would make this a discovery

The paper must prove from one physical interaction theorem, rather than
assemble rank-nullity identities, that:

1. arbitrary local interaction graphs generically realize `(U5)`;
2. arbitrarily weak bridges realize the exact conversion `(U3)`;
3. the same \(\tau\) is the exponent of an exact recurrence-density law;
4. finite induced subsystems satisfy the law simultaneously;
5. the open and infinite clauses use the same capacity object;
6. at least one feasible protocol distinguishes the integer jump from
   finite-time crossover.

Without items 1--3, `(U4)` is only dressed-up rank--nullity. Without items
4--6, “universal” and the physical interpretation are not earned.

## Hard falsifiers

Retire or split the conjecture if any survives serious repair:

1. generic local Hamiltonians on one connected finite graph retain
   nontrivial rational projective spectral relations;
2. the all-graph rank depends on graph details beyond connected components;
3. the weak-coupling exponent change varies continuously with nonzero
   coupling strength;
4. return-density exponent is not the completed temporal dimension;
5. open-system loss cannot be represented canonically as transient or hidden
   capacity;
6. infinite limits have no compatible capacity object;
7. `(U1)` depends on presentation or imported observer coordinates;
8. the exact interaction-conversion and return-density law is already in
   external literature;
9. no finite experiment can distinguish the law from ordinary spectral
   dephasing;
10. the only universal content is rank--nullity.
