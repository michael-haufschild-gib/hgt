# Borel–Weil (and Borel–Weil–Bott) in Lean 4

- **ID:** borel-weil-bott
- **Stage:** 1-in-progress
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Source:** user goal, 2026-08-21
- **Statement:** For `G = SL(N,k)` with `k` an infinite field, `B` the standard Borel, and a weight
  `λ`, the space `H⁰(λ) = { f ∈ k[G] : f(gb) = χ_λ(b) f(g) }` of global sections of the associated
  line bundle on `G/B` is nonzero exactly for dominant `λ`, is finite dimensional, and (in
  characteristic zero) is the irreducible `G`-module of highest weight `λ`.

## Goal
Mathlib has no Borel–Weil, no flag varieties, no algebraic groups, no highest weight theory and no
sheaf cohomology of line bundles.  The corpus has none either (checked 2026-08-21 by
`scripts/formal_tools.py research` and greps over `formal/`, `docs/`).  So the whole development is
built here from scratch.

Formulation: the *associated-bundle* description of the sections of `L_λ = G ×^B k_λ` on `G/B`,
i.e. `ind_B^G λ`.  This is the standard algebraic definition (Jantzen, *Representations of
Algebraic Groups*, II.2) and avoids scheme-theoretic sheaf cohomology entirely.  Regular functions
on `SL(N,k)` are realized concretely as polynomial functions of the matrix entries, which is
faithful because `k` is infinite.

## Approach
1. Non-vanishing: `Δ_λ = ∏_j (leading j×j minor)^{m_j}` is a section, `Δ_λ(1) = 1`.  DONE.
2. Vanishing: rank-one Bruhat + polynomial degree bookkeeping.  DONE.
3. Finite dimensionality: a section is determined by its restriction to the opposite unipotent
   `U⁻` (density of the big cell `U⁻ B`), and that restriction has bounded degree.
4. Irreducibility: `dim H⁰(λ)^{U⁻} = 1` plus complete reducibility in characteristic zero.
5. Bott: rank-one higher cohomology with the `ρ`-shifted dot action.

## Log
- 2026-08-21 — created; layers 1–11 formalized and green under `lake env lean`.
- 2026-08-21 — polynomial model added.  Proved, unconditionally and with only the three standard
  Lean axioms: homogeneity + finite dimensionality of `H⁰(λ)`; the LU/big-cell theorem; uniqueness
  of the highest weight vector (`finrank (H⁰(λ)^{U⁻}) = 1`); the row-weight filtration replacing
  Kolchin's theorem; and irreducibility of `bwSocle λ`, the `GL(N,k)`-module generated by the minor
  product.  All valid over any infinite field, in every characteristic.
  Remaining at that point: complete reducibility in characteristic zero, the group-model
  comparison, and Bott.
- 2026-08-21 — **Borel–Weil closed.**  Complete reducibility was obtained without Weyl's theorem:
  the apolarity pairing is contravariant for the transpose (proved by two nested inductions — the
  chain rule for `pderiv` under a linear substitution, and multiplication-adjoint-to-differentiation),
  and the self-pairing of the minor product is a positive integer because its coefficients are
  integral.  Hence the radical of the form on `H⁰(λ)` vanishes and a rank count forces
  `H⁰(λ) = L(λ)`: **`H⁰(λ)` is an irreducible `GL(N,k)`-module of highest weight `λ` in
  characteristic zero**, nonzero exactly for dominant `λ`.
- 2026-08-21 — **Bott, rank one and projective space.**  `H^i(ℙ¹, O(d))` computed in full with the
  `ρ`-shifted dot action `s · d = -d - 2`, the singular-weight total vanishing at `d = -1`, and the
  Euler characteristic `d + 1`.  For `ℙⁿ` the Čech complex of the standard cover is treated
  multidegree-wise: the contracting homotopy of `CechHomotopy` gives middle-degree vanishing for
  `1 ≤ p` and `p + 1 < #T`, `H⁰` is exactly the space of degree-`d` forms, and the top-degree
  coboundaries are exactly the exponents that are nonnegative in some coordinate.
  Still open at that point: the surjectivity half of the `GL`/`SL` model comparison, and Bott for a
  general flag variety `G/B`.
- 2026-08-21 — **Model comparison closed.**  The divisibility that the classical account extracts
  from irreducibility of the determinant polynomial is instead obtained from Borel–Weil itself:
  `detᶜ · H⁰(λ)` is a nonzero `GL`-stable subspace of `H⁰(λ+c)`, so irreducibility forces equality,
  giving `exists_eq_detPow_mul`.  Extending a group-model section then only needs to divide one
  column by the determinant (`ColumnScaling`), which produces a polynomial section of weight
  `λ + R·1`; the divisibility removes the `detᴿ` again.  Hence `restrictToSL` is a **bijection**
  `polySections λ ≃ bwSections λ` (`restrictToSL_bijective`), and no determinant-irreducibility
  result is needed anywhere.
- 2026-08-21 — **Bott for a general `G/B`: foundation only.**  The Čech machinery was abstracted
  away from projective space (`CechSupport`): for an arbitrary cover index `T`, basis index `A`
  and support function `supp : A → Finset T`, a positive-degree cocycle all of whose basis vectors
  have `supp a ≠ univ` is a coboundary.  This is the shape the Bruhat cover of `G/B` takes, with
  `T` the Weyl group and `A` the torus weights.
  **Open core.**  A closed proof of Bott for a general `G/B` still needs (i) the Bruhat/Schubert
  cover of `G/B` and the sections of `L_λ` over each cell, (ii) the torus-weight decomposition of
  that Čech complex, and (iii) the concentration theorem: for each weight the reduced cohomology of
  the "bad set" subcomplex on the Weyl group is concentrated in degree `ℓ(w)`.  Unlike the
  projective-space case, (iii) genuinely produces intermediate degrees — that is exactly the
  `ρ`-shifted dot action — so the vanishing mechanism above does not by itself finish it.  Neither
  mathlib nor this corpus has any of (i)–(iii); they are the next multi-module build.
