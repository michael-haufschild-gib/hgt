## §1 Why universal reducibility = foundationality (the substrate vs the peak)

  parent"** (differential Galois / Airy=SL₂). C5 measures this.
- A **substrate** sits beneath the parents, present in all as a shadow — signature **"reducible to *every*
  parent"**, i.e. the **inverse** of C5. Door-2 always lands in a parent because the substrate is in all of
  them. So **the BRIDGE monoculture is exactly the substrate signature.**

  prime side — we **proved** it is structurally displaced to the archimedean root. A peak-in-waiting would
  have its native content *undiscovered*; ours is *provably elsewhere* (in the shared substrate).

## §3 The founding principle is the constraint's irreducibility, not a rule

"Find the founding principle of hypertranscendental functions" is **self-defeating**: a generative founding
principle *is* an algebraic differential equation, and hypertranscendence is precisely its absence (Hölder).
So Hölder is the founding **theorem**, and it is **negative**. What *can* found the theory of a *structured*
hypertranscendental object (ζ — not generic/Gödel-wild, but pinned by Euler product + FE) is the **constraint
it obeys** and whether that constraint is **irreducible**:
> **Founding principle (positive form):** the archimedean–prime weld is irreducible — the FE-conspiracy
> cocycle / `ω ≠ 0` does **not** factor across the two sources (#134). If true, ζ's hypertranscendence has a
> foundation (the irreducible constraint); if false, ζ is a bridge of two classical halves.

This is a **positive** statement (irreducibility), reachable in principle — unlike the impossible "generative
rule." It is the substrate's D4.
