## Goal
Over Beurling generalized primes, a system whose FE-crystalline measure is real-rooted (Lee–Yang) type
has its RH-analogue forced by construction. The arithmetic `ζ` is the degenerate point where the forcing
fails. A sibling family (not an RH-restatement) that isolates the localization axis and ties RH to the
missing real-rootedness input (Weil positivity). Source-pure: built from a prime measure, not the zeros.
