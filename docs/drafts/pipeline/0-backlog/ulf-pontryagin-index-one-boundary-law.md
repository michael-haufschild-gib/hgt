## Mechanism (why the law would be true)

Over `𝔽_q` the unique timelike direction is a *count*: `NS(C×C)/primitive` has rank two (the two
rulings, one hyperbolic plane), forced by Riemann–Roch plus "a nonzero section has at most `deg L`
zeros". For `ℕ` the analogous boundary is the completion boundary the corpus already locates the anomaly
in. Its additive part is the sawtooth's Fourier lattice `2πiℤ`; Riemann's first proof (Hankel contour on
`1/(e^t − 1)`, corpus `BoseMellinCoordinate`, `ZetaSawtoothTail`) resums those modes exactly into the
Gamma factor and `ζ(1−s)` — no additive boundary survives. Its multiplicative part is the simple pole
(density one, bounded discrepancy). So `∂ = (pole plane) ⊕ (Gamma modes)`, one positive square. Both
countermodels enlarge `∂`: `ζ·G` by a triple pole, the Beurling system by unresummed additive
discrepancy. This is the narrowing of the pre-divisor literal-source model class that MUST-40 requires:
the class "joint formations whose completion boundary is one hyperbolic plane ⊕ Gamma modes" contains
`ζ`, excludes both countermodels, and is defined before any zero.

## Missing mathematical object

A **source-generated Pontryagin completion of the ax+b semigroup action** `ℕ ⋊ ℕ^×` on the log-line,
