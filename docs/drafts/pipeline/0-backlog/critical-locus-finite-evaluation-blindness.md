## Statement (target)

**Admissible class 𝒜̂.** `F` holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed substrip
`|σ − 1/2| ≤ θ`, with `F ∘ σ = F` (σ: s ↦ 1−s), `F^κ = F` (κ: s ↦ s̄), `F` nonconstant, and
`F(σ+iτ) → 0` as `|τ| → ∞` uniformly on `|σ−1/2| ≤ θ`.

> **Θ4.** Let `F₁,…,F_k ∈ 𝒜̂` with line restrictions `u_i = Re F_i(1/2 + i·)` linearly independent
> modulo constants, let `Z₀` be an RH configuration with infinite support, and fix
> `0 < t* < θ`. Then there is a configuration `Z*` containing an off-line quadruple of
> displacement exactly `t*` with `Φ_{F_i}(Z*; Z₀) = 0` for every `i ≤ k`.

**Corollary (target).** No finite family of spectral-type positivity tests implies RH.

## Move calculus (settled)

`Z*` differs from `Z₀` by finitely many R-orbits. The available moves and their value vectors in
`ℝ^k` are

- `P(β) = 2u(β)` — insert a conjugate line pair at a free ordinate `β`; **unbounded multiplicity**;
- `V(γ,t) = 4 Re F(1/2+t+iγ)` — insert an off-line quadruple; **unbounded multiplicity**;
- `D(α) = −2u(α)` for `α ∈ supp Z₀` — delete a line pair; **budget-limited** by the multiplicity of
  `α`, though infinitely many `α` are available.

A *slide* (insert at `β`, delete at `α`) has value `2(u(β) − u(α))` and consumes deletion budget.

## New reduction (2026-08-21)

**(A) Bounded-rounding assembly.** Let `m_1,…,m_M` be a *repeatable* move set that positively spans
`ℝ^k`, and let a block family give image `⊇ B(b, r)` with `r > 0`. For any target `T`, write
`−T − Nb = Σ_j λ_j m_j` with `λ_j ≥ 0` and take `n_j = ⌊λ_j⌋`; the rounding error is at most
`C = Σ_j |m_j|`, **independent of `N`**, while `N` blocks give radius `Nr`. Taking `N > C/r` hits
`T` exactly. So exact hitting follows from: *the repeatable moves positively span `ℝ^k`*.

This removes the "IFT-patch bases race against their radii" obstruction whenever no deletion is
needed: the base is cancelled by an integer cone combination, not by shrinking.

**(B) Repeatable positive spanning fails exactly for positive-type families.**
`cone{P(β), V(γ,t)} ≠ ℝ^k` iff there is `w ≠ 0` with `U_w := Re Σ w_i F_i ≥ 0` on the whole
substrip `|σ−1/2| ≤ t*`. Such `F` exist inside `𝒜̂` (e.g. `F(s) = exp((s−1/2)²)` has
`u(β) = e^{-β²} > 0`, and Poisson integrals of nonnegative boundary bumps give strip-positive
examples), so deletions are genuinely needed in general and their budget
`B = 2 Σ_{α ∈ supp Z₀} u(α)` may be finite.

**(C) Second-derivative cone fullness (new, elementary, replaces the Phragmén–Lindelöf step).**
If `G ∈ 𝒜̂` and `Re G''(1/2+iγ) ≥ 0` for all `γ ∈ ℝ`, then `v(γ) := Re G(1/2+iγ)` satisfies
`v'' = −Re G''(1/2+i·) ≤ 0`, i.e. `v` is **concave** on `ℝ` and `v → 0` at `±∞`. A concave function
on `ℝ` with a point of nonzero slope is unbounded below along the tangent line, contradicting
`v → 0`; hence `v' ≡ 0` and `v ≡ 0`. Applying this to `G = Σ w_i F_i` and using linear independence
of the `u_i` modulo constants gives

> the closed convex cone generated by `{(Re F_1''(1/2+iγ), …, Re F_k''(1/2+iγ)) : γ ∈ ℝ}`
> is all of `ℝ^k`.

This is the sharpened Step A: no strip maximum principle, no line-independence lemma, no
Phragmén–Lindelöf. It is the cone of *splitting weights* `W(γ,t) ≈ 2t² Re F''(1/2+iγ)`, i.e. of the
moves "split a double line pair at `γ` into a quadruple of displacement `t`", which are free of
insertion cost **when `γ ∈ supp Z₀` has multiplicity ≥ 2**.

## The remaining open point

Exact hitting when deletions are essential cone directions and the deletion budget is finite:
the positive-type adversary (`u > 0` on the line and `Re F ≥ 0` on the substrip) with a sparse,
fast-decaying, multiplicity-one `Z₀` bounds the total achievable negativity, so the assembly must
succeed with `N` bounded, i.e. it needs a regular block tuple with `|b| < r` — a genuine
log-derivative estimate on `u` that no hypothesis in `𝒜̂` supplies.

Candidate levers (unchanged from the 2026-08-13 log, plus the new ones):
1. **Multiplicity-2 hypothesis.** If `Z₀` has infinitely many ordinates of multiplicity ≥ 2, the
   splitting moves `W(γ,t)` are free, they are repeatable, and by (C) they positively span `ℝ^k`;
   then (A) closes Θ4 outright. *This is a fully proved special case and should be formalized as
   such.*
2. Two-parameter extra-quadruple family `V(γ,t′)` with its `t′`-jets as weight vectors.
3. Far-field direction spread of `u(β)` versus the deletion capacity of `Z₀`.
4. A log-derivative lower bound `|u'|/|u| ≥ c` in the far field for `F ∈ 𝒜̂`.
