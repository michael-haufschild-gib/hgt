## Mechanism to audit

The corpus already proves |xi(s)|<=1/2 throughout the closed critical strip.
For nonnegative a, |exp(-a*s)|<=1 there, hence every d_a lies in the punctured
unit disk and avoids-1. That disk's fundamental group is Z, with winding a
complete invariant. All coordinate windings agree because exponential
factors have zero winding. Positive real connecting paths select the same
positive generator. An actual simple critical-line Xi zero, available
unconditionally, gives a small circle inside every such strip and supplies
primitive winding. Countable zero-set complement in a convex open strip
should provide the source connecting path.

This extends the existing quadratic strip collapse to the full discrete
marked target image, if every basepoint and domain step checks. It suggests
that these image subgroups are unchanged as central strips shrink, even
without RH; it does not assert the existence of an off-line Xi zero.
