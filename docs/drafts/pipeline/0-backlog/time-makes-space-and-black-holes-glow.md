## Exact north-star law

For a future-directed family of causally complete regions `D(T)`, define:

- `A(T)`: generalized screen area;
- `I(T;Delta,epsilon,E)`: maximum accessible information in a fixed-energy clock record that
  distinguishes ages in `[0,T]` with resolution `Delta` and error at most `epsilon`;
- `Phi(T)`: cumulative accessible information exported across an inaccessible null boundary.

The desired **temporal-capacity balance law** is

```text
unbounded faithful relational time
  -> limsup_T A(T)=infinity
     or limsup_T Phi(T)=infinity
     or fixed-resolution age fidelity fails.                  (TC)
```

The physical strengthening sought is a local balance or inequality

```text
d I_retained <= d(A/(4 G hbar)) + d Phi,                       (TC-local)
```

with the retained innovation derived from a source-selected HT clock rather than postulated.

Proposed readings:

1. A homogeneous causally closed cosmology with no future information outlet takes the area-growth
   branch. A successful derivation must still prove that this is growth of the cosmological scale
   factor, not merely growth of ordinary causal diamonds in static spacetime.
2. A stationary bounded-area horizon takes the flux branch. Under a separately proved local KMS
   or horizon thermality theorem, exported temporal information is observed as heat.
3. An extremal horizon is the singular boundary: ordinary thermal flux disappears, so retained
   formation memory, a nonthermal soft channel, area growth, or clock failure must replace it.

Fano plus Holevo gives, for an `(N,epsilon)` absolute-age code,

```text

```

If a gravitational screen independently obeys `chi <= A/(4 G hbar)`, then


This is valid for diffuse carriers when its operational premises hold. It does not show that HT
creates the required age code.

### 3. Warm-horizon phase-to-heat map

coefficient. The corpus proves:

```text

opposite-frequency intensity                = DA Planck sector.
```

Thus an exactly soluble horizon channel already realizes loss of an HT causal phase into a thermal
intensity. The result is a structural example, not yet a universal information-flux theorem.

### 4. Extremal boundary warning

Extremal Reissner-Nordstrom collapse replaces exponential peeling by an inverse-plus-logarithmic
relation. Its exact all-time coefficient contains

```text
K_(1+4 i M omega)(4 M sqrt(omega (omega+omega'))).
```

The fixed-order `K_1` law occurs only in a late-time/high-redshift asymptotic or after retaining the
dominant inverse term. Therefore:


is not established for the exact amplitude. Proving the differential type of the variable-order

survivor may be an observability transition: warm horizons hide causal complexity in phase, while
extremal horizons move it into a nonthermal intensity or memory channel.

## Fatal gaps

1. **HT is not operational information.** Hypertranscendence alone implies neither entropy growth,
   distinguishability, algorithmic randomness, nor a stable clock record.
2. **Finite area is not finite Hilbert dimension.** Type-II gravitational algebras support HT
   correlations at finite trace.
3. **Ordinary causal diamonds grow in flat spacetime.** Unbounded screen area is not by itself
   cosmological expansion.
4. **Flux is not heat.** Causal openness includes an environment, omitted modes, singular boundary
   data, or nonthermal radiation. KMS structure must be derived.
5. **Horizon Gamma phase is not automatically exterior-accessible.** Spontaneous Hawking intensity
   can erase it; a calibrated channel, partner correlation, or phase-sensitive experiment is
   required.
6. **Extremal formulas are not a clean DA endpoint.** The exact variable-order Bessel coefficient
   blocks the tempting zero-temperature classification.
7. **No Einstein equation follows yet.** Without a local focusing/entropy-balance derivation, the
   word “gravity” in the headline is unauthorized.
