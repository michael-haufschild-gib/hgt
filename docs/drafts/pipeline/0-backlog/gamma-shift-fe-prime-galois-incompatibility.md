## Conjectural Core

[CONJECTURE] Let `K_Gamma = C(s)<Gamma>` with the additive shift `sigma_infty: s -> s+1` and derivation `partial_s`. The parameterized Galois geometry generated by the Gamma shift equation

`Gamma(s+1) = s Gamma(s)`

does not contain an object with all three of the following:

1. zeta-like `sigma_infty` wildness, as in G1;
2. the completed functional-equation involution `iota: s -> 1-s`;
3. a strip-confined divisor whose residual law after the Gamma phase is prime-explicit.

Equivalently as a moonshot: zeta's FE-prime symmetry is not internal to the Gamma shift differential field. It requires adjoining an arithmetic generator.

## Why This Is A Leap

This conjecture is not verified. It is deliberately stronger than the currently proven local controls.

Known support:

1. The rational/algebraic part of `K_Gamma` has a vertical-strip divisor obstruction: finite Gamma-field expressions cannot encode the nontrivial zeta divisor. This is source-adjacent to Lu/Li-Ye and likely not novel globally.
2. Gamma c-points can have `T log T` leading density, but they escape fixed vertical strips. Density alone is not the invariant.
3. G1 records zeta's single-shift difference wildness.
4. The FE-prime structure is not a pure Gamma phenomenon: the Gamma factor supplies the archimedean phase, while the residual `S(T)` is prime/arithmetic.

Open leap:

The obstruction must survive beyond the rational Gamma field into the relevant finite differential-algebraic or parameterized PV extensions. This is exactly where naive strip-counting arguments fail.

## Falsification Tests

1. Construct a finite expression in `C(s)(Gamma,...,Gamma^(n))` whose denominator has infinitely many zeros in a fixed vertical strip. This kills the rational-part strip theorem.
2. Construct an element differentially algebraic over `K_Gamma` with a strip-confined `T log T` divisor and a non-smooth residual law. This kills the differential-closure version.
3. Show the FE involution `iota` is already internal to the Gamma shift parameterized Galois category. This kills the claimed symmetry incompatibility.
4. Show the prime residual `S(T)` is not a legitimate invariant under the chosen category. This kills the measurable shadow.
5. Source-kill the whole framework by identifying an existing theorem that states the same incompatibility in standard Nevanlinna or parameterized Galois language.

## Counterexample To The Overbroad DA Version

- 2026-06-23 - Professor found a counterexample showing that the conjecture cannot quantify over arbitrary DA extensions of `K_Gamma`.

Let `y=sin(s exp(s))`. This is single-valued entire and differentially algebraic over `C(s)`: with `u=s exp(s)`, `a=1+1/s`, and `E=(1/a)D_s`, we have `E u=u`, so

`E y = u cos u`,

`E^2 y = E y - u^2 y`,

hence `q=(E y-E^2 y)/y=u^2` and `E q=2q`. Clearing denominators gives an ADE over `C(s)`.

Its zeros satisfy `s exp(s)=n*pi`, so `s=W_k(n*pi)`. In any fixed vertical strip `a<=Re(s)<=b`, the asymptotic condition is `C_a |k| <= n <= C_b |k|`, giving `asymp T^2` zeros up to height `T` in the strip.

Impact: arbitrary differential-algebraic closure can create dense strip-confined divisors. The moonshot must restrict to a parameterized Picard-Vessiot / Gamma-shift-compatible category, not all DA-over-`K_Gamma` functions. Canonical counterexample card: `docs/drafts/pipeline/0-backlog/da-strip-dense-counterexample.md`.
