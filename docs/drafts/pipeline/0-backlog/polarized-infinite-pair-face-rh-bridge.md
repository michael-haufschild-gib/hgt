## Goal

Construct, or decisively refute, the missing map between two already rigorous structures:

1. the complete finite pair-face invariant for mixed-difference hypertranscendence; and
2. the archimedean Sonin/Herglotz polarization problem whose negative index detects off-line zeros.

The conjecture is deliberately stronger than an analogy. It asks for a functorial object, an
operator realization, and an index identity. Failure of any one is a clean no-go result.

## Prime specialization

For each prime `p`, put

```text
tau_p = i log(p)/(2 pi).
```

Every finite set `1,tau_(p_1),...,tau_(p_d)` is linearly independent over `Q`: a rational relation
among the imaginary parts would give a multiplicative relation among distinct primes. Therefore the
finite pair-face theorem applies to every finite prime subsystem.

For finite `S` of primes, write `Comp_S` for its compatible pair-divisor module. The algebraic
finite-stage system is not expected to create a generic `lim^1` phantom: prior infinite-base
cohomology work already showed that surjective restriction systems are Mittag--Leffler. Any new
obstruction here must come from a proved failure of surjectivity or, more plausibly, from the
Hermitian/normed completion and its corona at infinity.

## Conjectural object

Seek a prime-indexed family of local polarization potentials

```text
a_p in V,
```

where `V` is a Hilbert completion of a real form of the orbit-residue module, together with:

1. an involution induced by the completed functional equation;
2. functorial maps from every finite compatible pair-face family to pair differences
   `a_p-a_q` in `V`;
3. consistency under enlargement of the finite prime set;
4. an archimedean anchor that removes the common-potential mode; and
5. a Sonin boundary representation of the pair differences on a two-particle carrier.

The finite pair-face realization theorem is the local gluing input. The infinite completion is new:
finite-support charges lie in every Hilbert class and therefore cannot see an RH divisor change.
The bridge must retain an infinite tail.

## Target bridge theorem `[CONJECTURE]`

There should be a canonically defined functional-equation self-adjoint pair defect `Omega_xi` and a
closed Hermitian form `Pi_Sonin(Omega_xi)` such that:

```text
kappa_-(Pi_Sonin(Omega_xi)) = kappa_-(m_xi),                (B1)
```

and the right side is zero exactly under RH. At finite prime level, the noncompact part of the form
should factor through the pair-potential operator

```text
sum_(p<q) <a_p-a_q, e> (C_p tensor C_q)                    (B2)
```

for scalar probes `e` of `V`, or an equivalent Hilbert-valued amplification.

The theorem `sonin-two-particle-prime-coherence` would then give the decisive infinite step:
boundedness of every scalar probe of (B2) forces all `a_p` to coincide. The archimedean anchor forces
that common value to be zero. What remains is the local-to-global identity (B1).

## Why a direct quotient map is impossible

For distinct primes `p,q`, the subgroup generated by `tau_p,tau_q` is dense on the imaginary axis
because `log p/log q` is irrational. A strongly continuous representation of complex translations
that factors through

```text
C/(Z+Z tau_p+Z tau_q)
```

must therefore kill every imaginary translation. Mellin dilation does not: translation by
`i log p/(2 pi)` corresponds to a nontrivial prime scale. Hence the orbit quotient itself cannot act
equivariantly as the Sonin/Mellin dilation representation.

A viable realization must lift the pair-face divisor before quotienting the period action. Natural
homes include a transformation groupoid or crossed-product module in which the dense period group
acts nontrivially. This is a structural requirement, not a choice of presentation.

## Why the one-particle route is impossible

The positive Euler corners obey

```text
dist(sum_(p in F)c_p C_p, Compact)
  >= (sum_(p in F)|c_p|^2/p)^(1/2).
```

For the canonical coefficients `c_p=1`, reciprocal-prime divergence makes the finite sections
unbounded modulo compacts. Complex phases do not help. Therefore `Omega_xi` cannot be a bounded
one-particle additive Euler sum plus compact correction.

The conjectural pair realization is not cosmetic: pair differences are the first structure for
which the same critical weight becomes rigid through coherence rather than summability.

## Research directions opened for general hypertranscendence

### 1. Polarized pair-face modules

Add a real involution and a Hilbert or Pontryagin form to the algebraic module `Comp_d`. Classify not
only vanishing of HT charge but its negative-square index. The finite theory currently has no order
or positivity; this enrichment is independently meaningful for any constrained meromorphic
function with a reflection symmetry.

### 2. Infinite simultaneous divisor lifting

The finite simultaneous lifting lemma is an algebraic Chinese-remainder theorem on orbit divisors.
Determine whether a compatible prime family with finite pair energy has one global lift in a
Hilbert completion. This is an analytic infinite-rank extension of the all-depth normal form, not an
algebraic `lim^1` claim.

### 3. Difference-reflection obstruction theory

Replace the abelian translation group by the semidirect product generated by translations and the
functional-equation reflection. Classify mixed-difference solutions modulo DA in this dihedral
setting and determine whether the reflection supplies the canonical involution missing from
`Comp_d`.

### 4. Prime-parameter elliptic-gamma pro-object

Use `q_p=exp(2 pi i tau_p)=1/p` to ask whether the finite multiple-elliptic-gamma unit-charge
carriers form a regularizable infinite prime object. Its failure to globalize would itself define an
archimedean regularization anomaly; successful globalization would provide a candidate source for
the lifted pair cocycle.

These are general HGT problems even if no RH realization exists: they ask for infinite-rank,
polarized, and reflection-equivariant extensions of the finite constraint classification.

## Falsifiers

The bridge must be retired or sharply revised if any of the following occurs:

1. compatible pair-face data do not admit potential differences after any natural crossed-product
   lift;
2. every such potential construction depends on a discontinuous choice of orbit representatives;
3. the functional equation fails to give a well-defined involution on the lifted charge module;
4. the two-particle boundary form is unbounded for structural reasons stronger than the
   one-particle obstruction;
5. its Calkin or form index has no theorem-grade relation to `kappa_-(m_xi)`;
6. a located parent theory already contains the same polarized infinite lifting theorem.
