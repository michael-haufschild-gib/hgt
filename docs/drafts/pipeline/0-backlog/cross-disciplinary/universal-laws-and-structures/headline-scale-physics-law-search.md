### Positive survivor

recover spectral dimension. These are known ingredients. Their conjunction is supporting

## Candidate B: the slightest mass prevents exact return

### Public sentence

> **The slightest mass stops a closed quantum world from ever repeating exactly.**

### Source model

```text

```

```text

```

on a suitable infinite independent subset has no exact nonzero return, although discrete-spectrum
quantum recurrence still gives arbitrarily close returns.

The canonical positive-Euclidean-time return response is simultaneously expected to cross from
differentially algebraic at `m=0` to hypertranscendental at every `m>0`.

### Why it could matter

This is an exact recurrence phase transition at zero mass:

```text

massive:  approximate recurrence without exact return for full-support states.
```

It turns the arithmetic theorem into a statement about whether history can ever repeat exactly.

### Hostile attacks

1. Perfect revival iff occupied energy gaps are commensurate is known.
2. Generic incommensurate spectra already lack exact revival; novelty cannot be that observation.
3. The circle is a special compact space, not all closed worlds.
4. Finite-band states and finite experiments cannot certify eternal non-return.
5. Decoherence destroys exact revival for ordinary reasons.
6. State support matters: eigenstates and special finite-support states trivially recur.
7. “The slightest mass” is licensed only for exact relativistic dispersion on the circle.
8. Hypertranscendence must produce a new quantitative recurrence law or robustness result beyond
   rational independence.
9. A macroscopic waveguide analogue would establish classical wave physics, not quantum
   recurrence.
10. No cosmological claim follows without a legitimate closed-system Hilbert space and observable.

