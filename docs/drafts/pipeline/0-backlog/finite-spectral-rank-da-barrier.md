# Finite spectral-rank DA barrier

- **ID:** `finite-spectral-rank-da-barrier`
- **Stage:** 0-backlog
- **Type:** `[CONJECTURE]` / GENERAL HT LANDSCAPE
- **Statement:** An order-`<=1`, single-valued meromorphic function that is DA over the
  archimedean Gamma field and whose strip divisor has a pure-point almost-periodic residual should
  have a finitely generated residual frequency group after removal of the Gamma/Stirling component.

## Precise target to define

Let `f` be meromorphic of order at most one, with its divisor contained in a finite-width vertical
strip. Form the height pushforward

```text
nu_f=(Im)_* Div(f).                                         (1)
```

Choose an archimedean reference distribution `nu_Gamma(f)` determined only by the finite Gamma-field
part of the defining differential field. Assume the residual

```text
mu_f=nu_f-nu_Gamma(f)                                       (2)
```

is tempered and has pure-point Fourier transform. The conjectural conclusion is that the additive
group generated over `Q` by

```text
supp(Fourier(mu_f))                                         (3)
```

has finite rank.

The definition of `nu_Gamma(f)` must be canonical and stable under the allowed DA field operations;
otherwise `(2)` is presentation-dependent and the conjecture is not a theorem statement.

## Why the HT question remains live

The RH-selector use of spectral rank is retired separately. That does not settle this general
classification problem.

Classical adjacent cases support the conjecture:

1. exponential polynomials have finitely many generating exponential frequencies;
2. solutions of finite linear ODE systems have finitely many formal exponential parts;
3. first-order algebraic ODEs of finite order fall into classical Malmquist--Yosida-type classes;
4. the elementary DA relocation envelopes have rank-one periodic divisor spectra;
5. `almost-periodic-autonomous-ade-finite-frequency-law.md` settles every autonomous scalar ADE,
   including singular implicit equations with nonunique initial-value problems, for holomorphic
   uniformly almost-periodic solutions. The minimal-separant finite-sample atlas gives finite
   function and divisor frequency rank. The regular solved case is the classical
   Cartwright--O'Brien bridge recorded separately in
   `autonomous-ade-almost-periodic-frequency-rank.md`.

None of these parents proves the stated any-order nonlinear DA claim.

## Falsification targets

1. A single-valued meromorphic DA function over `C(s)` of order at most one whose strip divisor has
   a pure-point Fourier spectrum of infinite `Q`-rank.
2. The same over `C(s)<Gamma>` after every canonical Gamma/Stirling subtraction.
3. A proof that no canonical subtraction `(2)` can be stable under the DA field operations.
4. A source theorem showing that any such pure-point residual is already an exponential-polynomial
   divisor in disguise.

Painleve transcendents of order greater than one and infinite-order compositions such as
`sin(s exp(s))` do not settle the conjecture. Ordinary Dirichlet source spectra also do not settle
it: Ostrowski classifies their differential algebraicity, whereas `(1)`--`(3)` concern a divisor
distribution.

## Scientific gate

`[OPEN CORE]` Define the canonical archimedean residual and handle nonautonomous Gamma-field
equations, where translation does not preserve one autonomous differential polynomial. The entire
autonomous implicit class is closed by the finite-sample separant theorem.

No RH conclusion belongs in this card. If the landscape theorem is proved, its downstream meaning
is only that infinite-rank divisor spectra cannot be generated by the stated finite algebraic-flow
class. Transverse zero localization requires additional divisor-sensitive information.
