## 2026-06-24 entropy audit — likely protocol/bridge, not standalone novelty

Haiku drew C21 by `scripts/rng` while trying to escape the finite-jet route. Normal-form audit:
if a finite-dimensional family is given by a universal function differentially algebraic over `C(z,t_1,...,t_n)`
with the parameters `t_i` constant, then every specialization away from coefficient poles is differentially
algebraic over `C(z)`. Thus such a family contains no HT fibers at all. This is useful vocabulary for
"finite DA moduli cannot represent HT," but as stated it is close to definitional and overlaps C30/base-enrichment
resistance and standard differential-algebra closure.

To become a real theorem rather than protocol hygiene, C21 must specify a broader representation class where the
map from finite moduli to functions is not already DA, and then prove that the representation still bounds the
differential field generated by the fiber. Otherwise the card should remain bridge/protocol, not a new theorem.
