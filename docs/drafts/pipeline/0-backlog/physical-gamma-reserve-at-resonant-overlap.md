# Gamma reserve in physical square mass at resonant overlap

- **ID:** physical-gamma-reserve-at-resonant-overlap
- **Stage:** 0-backlog
- **Type:** CONJECTURE
- **Statement:** For every nonzero real `C²` profile `φ` supported in `[0,1]` and fixed `c>0`, there are `a>0,b∈ℝ` such that, eventually for every `N` and every real coefficient vector,

`archForm(F_N) ≥ (a log(log N)−b) ∫F_N²`,

where `F_N=atomicTest N x (atomDilate φ (c/N))`.

## Motivation and open input

The exact local Gram law and two-copy resonance invalidate a logarithmic floor by coefficient mass. They preserve the physical Gram, suggesting a lower bound in the actual synthesized norm.

Possible mechanism: real Fourier zeros have finite order; ordinary alias zeros in a compact positive overlap interval form a finite set. A quantitative localization argument would have to confine low-Gamma-energy sources near these resonances and then use uncertainty on the shrinking windows.

No such localization estimate or reserve is certified. Extending this to whole Weil positivity also requires control of prime and pole terms in the same physical norm. This conjecture alone does not resolve AAL.
