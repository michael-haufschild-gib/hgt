## §1 The base `R` and the proven generators `[cited — #136/#140/#141, not re-derived]`
`R=ℂ[x_p^{±1}]`, monomials `e_m=∏x_p^{m_p}=n_m^{−s}` (`n_m=∏p^{m_p}`, finite support). On the `e_m` basis,
**already proven/ledgered:**
- `σ_p e_m = p^{−m_p} e_m` (#136; Lean `bohrCharacter_scaleCoordinate` #141) — **constant** eigenvalue;
- `θ_p e_m = m_p e_m`, and `∂_s := −Σ_p(log p)θ_p` gives `∂_s e_m = −(Σ_p m_p\log p)e_m = −(\log n_m)e_m = −(m·ℓ)e_m`
  (#136; Lean `primeLogFrequency_*` #140) — and `∂_s x_p = −(\log p)x_p`, i.e. `∂_s = d/ds` (consistent with
  `x_p=p^{−s}`). `∂_s` is Connes' scaling operator `D` (support-log §7).

So `σ_p` and `∂_s` are **simultaneously diagonal** on `\{e_m\}`. Pairwise commutation among `\{σ_p\}` and with
`∂_s` is then immediate (diagonal operators commute): `[∂_s,σ_p]=0` because `∂_s σ_p e_m = p^{−m_p}(−(m·ℓ))e_m
= σ_p ∂_s e_m`. `[verified]`

## §2 The Γ-symbol `g` and the archimedean shift `σ_∞` `[recurrence/connection cited #133/#134; verified]`
Adjoin a transcendental `g` standing for `Γ_ℝ(s)=π^{−s/2}Γ(s/2)`. Its two structures (the archimedean analogue
of `σ_p`'s constant multiplier and of `∂_s`):
- **Difference (the Source-A polynomial multiplier).** `σ_∞:s↦s+2`,
  `   σ_∞ g = (s/2π)\,g`     since `Γ_ℝ(s+2)=π^{−1}π^{−s/2}(s/2)Γ(s/2)=(s/2π)Γ_ℝ(s)`  `[verified |diff|≤2.5e−32]`.
  The multiplier `s/2π` is **polynomial in `s`** (degree 1) — non-CC, Source A (#134); contrast the constant
  `p^{−1}` of `σ_p`.
- **Connection.** `∂_s g = g·(−½\logπ+½ψ(s/2))`, `ψ=Γ'/Γ`  `[verified, exact]`. The digamma term is the
  non-axis Source-A datum (#136); it is prime-free.

`σ_∞` acts on `R` (not just on `g`) through `x_p=p^{−s}`: `σ_∞ x_p = p^{−(s+2)} = p^{−2}x_p`, so on `e_m`,
`σ_∞ e_m = n_m^{−2} e_m`.

## §3 The ring `R⁺=R[g;σ_∞]` is well-defined: the three commutations `[PROVED HERE + verified]`
`R⁺` is the difference–differential ring generated by `R`, `g`, with `σ_p,σ_∞` (commuting automorphisms) and
the derivation `∂_s`. The non-trivial axioms:
- **`[σ_∞,σ_p]=0`.** Both diagonal on `e_m` (`σ_∞↦n_m^{−2}`, `σ_p↦p^{−m_p}`) and `σ_∞ g`,`σ_p g(=g)`; eigenvalues
  multiply commutatively. ✓
- **`[∂_s,σ_p]=0`** on `R` (§1) and on `g` (`σ_p g=g`, `∂_s g` prime-free, so trivially). ✓
- **`[∂_s,σ_∞]=0`.** On `R`: both diagonal (`∂_s↦−\log n_m`, `σ_∞↦n_m^{−2}`). On `g` — the one identity that
  must hold, and does, **because of the digamma recurrence**:
  ```
     ∂_s(σ_∞ g) = ∂_s((s/2π)g) = (1/2π)g + (s/2π)∂_s g,
     σ_∞(∂_s g) = (s/2π)g·(−½logπ+½ψ(s/2+1)) = (s/2π)∂_s g + (s/2π)·(1/s)g    [ψ(s/2+1)=ψ(s/2)+2/s]
                = (s/2π)∂_s g + (1/2π)g.
  ```
  Equal. ✓ `[ψ-recurrence verified |diff|≤2.3e−32]` So `[∂_s,σ_∞]=0` holds **iff** the digamma satisfies
  `ψ(s/2+1)=ψ(s/2)+2/s` — which is exactly the log-derivative of the Γ-recurrence `σ_∞ g=(s/2π)g`. The ring is
  consistent by construction: difference and differential structures are compatible because the connection is
  the log-derivative of the multiplier (`∂_s log(σ_∞ g/g)=∂_s log(s/2π)=1/s`, matching `½[ψ(s/2+1)−ψ(s/2)]`).

**`(R⁺,\{σ_p\},σ_∞,∂_s)` is therefore a well-defined difference–differential ring.** ∎

## §4 `σ_∞ = ∏_p σ_p²` — the product formula in operator form `[the new structural fact]`
On `R`, `σ_∞ e_m = n_m^{−2}e_m = (∏_p p^{m_p})^{−2}e_m = ∏_p p^{−2m_p}e_m = (∏_p σ_p²)e_m`. So
```
   σ_∞|_R = ∏_p σ_p²       (well-defined: on each finite-support e_m only finitely many factors act).   (4.1)
```
**The archimedean shift `s↦s+2` is the simultaneous square of all prime dilations.** This is the
**product-formula relation** `∏_v|x|_v=1` in operator form (the `#128` corank-collapse bet, made concrete on
the base): there is exactly **one** archimedean coordinate `s`, shared by all primes via `x_p=p^{−s}`, and its
shift is the diagonal of the `σ_p`. The new datum that `σ_∞` carries beyond `∏σ_p²` is its action on `g` (the
`Γ`-recurrence) — i.e. the archimedean place adds **only** the `Γ`-factor `g`, nothing on the prime
coordinates. This pins, at the base level, "the archimedean lane = the prime-diagonal + the `Γ`-symbol."
