Prime-square residual Hankel test from the theta/Xi Taylor source
Date: 2026-07-13

Target
------
A(u) is defined source-first by XiC(z)/XiC(0)=A(-z^2).  Its logarithmic power sums are

  c_n=(-1)^(n-1) n [u^n] log A(u).

The scalar prime-square tensor proposal requires

  q_n=c_n/P(2n)

to be the power-moment tower of a positive archimedean operator.

Method
------
1. Evaluate the completed Xi function directly from

     Xi(t)=1/2 s(s-1) pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2) zeta(s),  s=1/2+i t.

2. Compute its even Taylor series at t=0 by Cauchy quadrature, through order 28.
3. Convert the coefficients to A(u), then compute log A recursively from A'=A(log A)'.
4. Compute the prime zeta values by Mobius inversion

     P(s)=sum_(k>=1) mu(k) log zeta(k s)/k.

5. Form the shifted Hankel matrices (q_(N+i+j)).

This calculation uses no Xi zero table.  Two independent runs changed the Cauchy radius and
working precision:

  run 1: 85 decimal digits, radius 0.20, Mobius cutoff 80;
  run 2: 110 decimal digits, radius 0.15, Mobius cutoff 100.

Verdict
-------
The signs and leading digits are stable under independent precision, radius, and Mobius-cutoff
changes.  Therefore the canonical scalar residual tower fails the necessary Hankel condition for
a positive measure or positive compact-operator spectrum.  This is a high-precision source
calculation, not an interval-arithmetic proof.  The formal positive no-go is the general exact
two-atom quotient theorem in `formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/MomentTowerQuotientGhost.lean`.
