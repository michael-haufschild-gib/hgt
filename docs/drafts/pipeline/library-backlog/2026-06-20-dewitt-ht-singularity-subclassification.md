## RETRACTION
My earlier claim — that the prime zeta P(s)=Σ_p p^{−s} is hypertranscendental *because* it has a
natural boundary at Re(s)=0 — is WITHDRAWN. Two independent refutations:

1. **The probe cannot certify HT (Wheeler, id=678).** The nonlinear-ADE probe is ONE-SIDED: it CATCHES
   low-order DA (e.g. E2 via the order-3 Chazy equation) but "resists up to (R,D)" is INCONCLUSIVE —
   E4 is provably DA yet resists at low order (its ADE is higher-order). So P(s) "resisting up to (2,4)"
   is no evidence of HT.
2. **Natural boundary ⇏ HT (decisive counterexample).** The Eisenstein series **E2 has a natural
   boundary (∂ of the upper half-plane) AND is differentially algebraic** — it satisfies the Chazy
   equation y''' = 2yy'' − 3(y')² (Ramanujan system; Wheeler's probe CAUGHT σ1↔E2 at (3,2)=2.6e-42).
   The Chazy equation is the textbook nonlinear ADE whose general solution has a *movable natural
   boundary*. So "ADE ⟹ fixed finite/algebraic singular locus" holds only for LINEAR/holonomic ODEs;
   nonlinear ADEs can have natural boundaries. My singularity argument was therefore invalid.

- **P(s) HT-status is OPEN.** Natural boundary does not settle it; the probe cannot settle it. Resolving

- **General lesson:** HT-certification is NOT reducible to singularity/analytic structure — the

  HT can only be established by differential-transcendence (Hölder-type) arguments, never by "it has a
