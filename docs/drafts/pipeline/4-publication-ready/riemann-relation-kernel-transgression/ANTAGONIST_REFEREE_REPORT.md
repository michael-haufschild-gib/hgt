# Antagonist referee report

## Recommendation

**Major revision as a theorem-and-route paper. Reject if submitted or advertised as a proof of RH.**

Paper is materially stronger and cleaner than a collage of RH equivalences. It contributes one central unconditional theorem, an actual nonzero arithmetic realization, and a falsifiable chain-level route. It also identifies one load-bearing open theorem. That last theorem is still open, so paper does not meet standard for an RH proof.

## What is genuinely accomplished

1. Relation-kernel transgression theorem is stated with exact hypotheses and proved.
2. Alternating rational specialization gives a sharp zero criterion, not merely a one-way obstruction.
3. Calderón–Witt cup supplies a concrete vector-valued nonzero class with zero radical and finite-refinement stability.
4. Free-rational exactification converts that actual class into a canonical nonzero relation-kernel character. This is a genuine arithmetic object, not a decorative RH equivalent.
5. Paper accurately consolidates source, completion, harmonic primitive, adaptive comparison, Green transport, and resolved provenance as closed infrastructure.
6. Paper identifies the atomic horizontal coefficient `D_ρ = 2(Re ρ - 1/2)` and explains why codiagonal, positive, trace, bounded-completion, source-only Koszul, and fixed-carrier mechanisms do not force it to vanish.
7. Conditional capstone is logically exact: comparison identity plus literal boundary annihilation plus nonzero calibrator implies `D_ρ = 0` copy by copy.

## Strongest objection

Equation

```text
Trans(completed Green provenance at ρ) = D_ρ τ_p
```

is not constructed. This is not a cosmetic gap. It is precisely where prime–Gamma analytic boundary data, Calderón–Witt arithmetic cup data, free-rational relation data, and Xi-divisor provenance must meet. Until a chain map with this coefficient computation and boundary naturality exists, route remains a research program rather than zero exclusion.

Paper handles this honestly. It never promotes detector theorem `g_G = 0 iff RH` into proof and never treats boundary exactness on one complex as annihilation on another without the missing comparison.

## General-journal demands

A demanding referee would require at least:

1. **Positioning audit.** Compare relation-kernel theorem with standard inflation–restriction/transgression machinery. Paper currently makes no novelty claim, which is honest, but a journal still needs exact relation to known cohomology.
2. **Standalone source definitions.** Current `Q_p`, `P_p`, `S_p`, Calderón coordinate, affine rational-Witt packet, and first suspension are described functionally, not constructed from first principles. Either add a technical appendix or designate this as a synthesis paper with explicit companion references.
3. **Finite-stage comparison evidence.** Construct comparison on a nontrivial finite rational-Witt/finite-prime subcomplex and prove coefficient fidelity there. Without this, existence of global comparison remains plausible but untested.
4. **Boundary-domain proof.** Show compared Cauchy/Green representative lies in actual domain of secondary Meyer–Poisson regulator. Equality of terminal vectors is insufficient.
5. **Naturality diagram.** Provide one commutative diagram whose faces are cover pullback, cup differential, Gamma/co-Poisson reflection, Green transport, and relation-kernel restriction. Current C1–C4 specify this verbally.
6. **Normalization audit.** Prove comparison coefficient is exactly `D_ρ`, or a fixed nonzero scalar times it, with no prime-, exhaustion-, or divisor-dependent gauge.
7. **Literature comparison.** Structural comparison to classical routes is useful but not a novelty audit. This can be deferred during internal research, not at submission.

## Classical-route assessment

Paper succeeds in separating route from Hilbert–Pólya. No self-adjoint operator, spectral determinant, or positivity premise supplies first arrow. Deninger/Hodge language appears only as transport infrastructure. Connes/trace, Weil positivity, Sonin/Calkin, and quotient-spectral routes are downstream consumers of information whose unsquared provenance must already have survived.

Paper does **not** prove Hilbert–Pólya impossible in general, and correctly avoids that overclaim.
