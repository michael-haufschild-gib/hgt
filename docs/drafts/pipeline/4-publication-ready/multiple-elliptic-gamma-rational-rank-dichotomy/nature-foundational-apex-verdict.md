# Hostile Nature verdict: foundational-time apex

## Decision

**Reject as a Nature Article in its present scientific form.**

The corpus now supports a strong universal mathematical classification, but it does not support a
discovery about the physical origin of time, the Big Bang, a black-hole universe, or a multiverse.
No editorial reframing can repair that missing physical identification.

## Strongest unconditional result

For every nontrivial group-valued clock theory and every finite or infinite family of internal
clocks:

1. complete anchored relative readings classify configurations exactly up to one common clock
   origin;
2. no internally origin-invariant reporter can also report that common origin covariantly;
3. every covariant conditional history is equivalent to a globally stationary history under the
   diagonal clock--state action;
4. once physical observable labels are retained, generator ambiguity is exactly their commutant,
   and for a complete finite matrix algebra it is only a scalar energy-origin term;
5. for every differentially independent additive clock family, the erased common mode is
   hypertranscendental over the differential field generated by all pairwise readings;
6. every derivation-closed state field containing that mode has transcendence degree at least
   \(\aleph_0\) over the complete relational field, so no finite algebraic state model can contain
   it.

Items 5--6 remain true for arbitrary index cardinality. Adding infinitely many internal clocks
does not recover the common mode or reduce its exact state cost.

## Why this still fails Nature

### 1. No physical identification theorem

The differentially independent family in items 5--6 is an abstract differential-field object.
The corpus contains unconditional independent Gamma, elliptic-Gamma, and completed-Xi families,
but their variables are frequency, spectral, arithmetic, mass-probe, or chemical-potential
coordinates. None is a physical external time coordinate. Substituting the word “time” for those
variables would be false.

### 2. Parent theories already contain the conceptual core

The diagonal-origin quotient is standard gauge/torsor structure. Evolution from stationary
conditional histories is the Page--Wootters mechanism. Clock switching is established quantum
reference-frame theory. Retaining physically labelled observables as the cure for maximal clock
ambiguity is already explicit in O. C. Stoica, *The clock ambiguity problem: extended or
extinguished?* (2026), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21805>. The commutant and scalar-center
refinements are exact, but not by themselves a broad physical discovery.

### 3. No changed prediction

The result supplies no observable distinguishing:

- relational from external time;
- a universe with or without a pre-Big-Bang phase;
- ordinary cosmology from a black-hole-interior cosmology;
- one quantum-gravity theory from another;
- a single universe from a multiverse.

An invariant absence of clock origin is operationally invisible by construction. Nature would ask
what observation, calculation, or existing anomaly changes after the theorem. Present answer:
none.

### 4. No quantum-gravity dynamics

The stationary group-action theorem is not a Wheeler--DeWitt solution, physical Hilbert space,
constraint algebra, clock subsystem, conditional probability rule, semiclassical limit, or
cosmological perturbation spectrum. The project corpus itself previously classified those
transfers as blocked.

### 5. No Big Bang or black-hole-universe result

“What was before time?” becomes ill-posed after quotienting by clock origin, but that is a
statement about one relational description, not evidence about early-universe reality. Likewise,
the exact Gamma phase/intensity laws concern solvable scattering, de Sitter, thermal-CFT, and
moving-mirror channels. They do not prove that our universe is a black hole or derive an
astrophysical black-hole \(S\)-matrix.

### 6. Mathematical novelty is not physical significance

The new \(\aleph_0\) state-cost capstone is potentially publishable as differential algebra or
mathematical physics after specialist novelty review. Formal verification contributes zero to
novelty or physical significance. A general scientific journal would not accept an abstract
state-complexity theorem as a solution to the problem of time without a concrete physical
instantiation.

## Questions a hostile referee would require answered

1. Which physical clock observables instantiate the differentially independent family?
2. Which established theory proves that their erased additive mode is cosmic time?
3. Which measured quantity depends on the \(\aleph_0\) lower bound?
4. How is the physical Hilbert space and constraint algebra constructed?
5. How does the theorem select one clock-world factorization while preserving spacetime
   symmetries?
6. What differs experimentally from Page--Wootters and quantum-reference-frame results?
7. What prediction concerns the Big Bang, black holes, or cosmological data?
8. Why is the result not standard gauge reduction plus a differential-algebraic complexity
   corollary?

The current manuscript cannot answer questions 1--3, 4, 6, or 7.

## Exact missing scientific input

A Nature-scale claim requires at least one of:

1. a physically derived clock family in a concrete gravitational or QFT model, proved to satisfy
   the independence hypotheses, together with a measurable consequence of the infinite-state
   bound;
2. a solved constraint/Hilbert-space construction showing that the classified common mode is
   precisely the disputed external time in a realistic quantum-gravity model;
3. a new cosmological or black-hole prediction, quantitatively distinct from existing theory and
   tied deductively to the theorem;
4. observational evidence selecting that prediction.

None is present. Mathematics cannot manufacture this missing empirical or model-defining premise.

## Anti-theatre rule

Do not use any of these claims:

- “time is proved to emerge from infinity”;
- “there was no before the Big Bang”;
- “the universe is timeless”;
- “we live inside a black hole”;
- “the multiverse has no external clock”;
- “quantum gravity is solved.”

Permitted statement:

> In every model satisfying the stated relational and differential-independence hypotheses,
> complete internal pairwise clock data omit exactly one common mode; that mode has no finite
> algebraic state realization over the relational data.

That statement is strong and exact. It is not yet a claim about our universe.
