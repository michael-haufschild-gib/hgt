# Antagonistic mathematical-physics referee report

## Recommendation

**Major revision; do not submit as a universal-law paper yet.**

Correctness and scope appear disciplined. Main concern is whether theorem is
new mathematics or a compact composition of classical facts, followed by
several technically separate refinements.

## Strongest theorem

The exact defensible core is:

\[
\nu_1 * \widetilde{\nu_1}
=\nu_2 * \widetilde{\nu_2}
\iff
\text{all complete survival-return laws agree under all clocks},
\]

and

\[
\frac{\rho_1+\widetilde{\rho_1}}2
=\frac{\rho_2+\widetilde{\rho_2}}2
\iff
\text{all complete survival-return laws agree for all spectra}.
\]

The converses are attained by deterministic clocks and balanced two-level
systems. The new minimal-probe corollary makes the information boundary
especially clear: full universal equivalence is decided at first moment.

## Mathematical attacks

### “Maximal quotient” may be tautological after choosing all probes

The system converse says its survival curve is the characteristic function
of the gap measure. The clock converse says two-level survival reads the
cosine transform. Once all real times or all real gaps are allowed,
characteristic-function uniqueness finishes the proof. Explain why this is
more than defining the quotient by the available measurements.

### Return-law completeness adds little to quotient maximality

For one fixed pair, compact moment determinacy upgrades all moments to a
distribution. For universal equivalence, however, first moments already
suffice. The paper should distinguish:

- full-law reconstruction for a fixed system–clock pair;
- first-moment sufficiency for universal equivalence.

Otherwise “all-order law” sounds deeper than what drives the classification.

### Homometry is established inverse-problem language

Energy-gap equality is homometry/Patterson autocorrelation. The paper must
engage phase-retrieval and diffraction literature directly, not only
quantum-survival sources. A referee will ask whether the theorem is simply
the homometry equivalence transported to time.

### Clock orientation is observable-relative

The manuscript now states this correctly. Preserve that qualification in
title, abstract, figures, cover letter, and publicity. “Quantum recurrence
cannot orient its own signed clock coordinate” is defensible. “Quantum time
has no orientation” is false.

### Pure-point and local sections need a dependency proof

Clock alias rank follows naturally from restricting characteristic
functions to projective characters. The analytic valuation and local
Steiner/replica cone are more remote. The paper needs a proposition showing
that each object is functorially derived from the same restricted
Fourier-pairing data. Verbal “shadow” language is not enough.

### Infinite section mixes two notions of dimension

Finite/countable pure-point temporal rank and cardinal rank of an arbitrary
gap-support span are not automatically one metric notion. Define the
relationship precisely and avoid implying that cardinality alone controls a
finite-resolution experiment.

### Physical realization is underdeveloped

Balanced two-level tomography is plausible, but the theorem assumes a
tunable exact gap and an ensemble of signed stopping times. Discuss what
constitutes a clock sample, how negative readings are represented, and which
data are known versus inferred. Without this, the clock side may be seen as
probability theory with quantum notation.

## Required revisions

1. Center the paper on double maximality and minimal-probe collapse.
2. Move the six-level benchmark and most valuation/local machinery to a
   supplement unless one main-text physical prediction requires them.
3. Add a phase-retrieval/homometry related-work subsection.
4. State exact novelty against cosine-transform clock tomography.
5. Add quantitative finite-band/finite-sample stability, or state clearly
   that exact measure reconstruction is asymptotic and ill-conditioned.
6. Replace black-hole motivation with a concrete implication, or remove it.
7. Do not call the manuscript Nature-ready until local-interaction rank
   explosion is fully closed and integrated.

## Questions for authors

1. Is every symmetric probability measure with nonnegative characteristic
   function an energy-gap law? If not, can the image of the autocorrelation
   map be characterized?
2. Can two non-translation/non-reflection finite spectra be exhibited with
   the same gap law and hence identical universal return data?
3. Does a countable dense set of deterministic times and two-level gaps
   suffice for the two maximality tests?
4. Can any finite probe set be universally complete?
5. What stability metric on quotient measures is controlled by a bounded
   gap scan with noisy first moments?
6. Which result changes for non-survival transition probabilities or
   phase-sensitive interferometry?

These are not manuscript theorem gaps if omitted, but their answers determine
whether the work is a foundational classification or a concise observation.

## Verdict

As mathematics, double maximality is clean and useful. As a broad universal
law, it is not yet insulated from the charge of being classical phase
retrieval plus characteristic-function uniqueness. Strongest route is to
make it the exact kinematic layer beneath the corpus’s generic local
interaction theorem. That addition would transform “what information return
forgets” into “how locality and arbitrarily weak interaction universally
create the temporal complexity return can reveal.”
