Please consider the manuscript “Differential Algebraicity of Multiple Elliptic Gamma Functions: Nome-Rank Classification and Translation-Coset Matroids” for publication in *Advances in Mathematics*.

Kato proved differential algebraicity for rational period vectors in 2021 and explicitly left the non-rational case open. This manuscript gives a complete answer for every nonempty finite upper-half-plane period tuple. The exact invariant is the rational rank of the periods together with the unit period: rank two is precisely the differentially algebraic phase, while every rank at least three is hypertranscendental. Equivalently, differential algebraicity occurs exactly when the multiplicative group generated by the nomes has torsion-free rank one. This identifies all exceptional non-rational tuples and leaves no residual parameter cases.

For the rationally independent elliptic-gamma seed, the manuscript determines substantially more than hypertranscendence. If $P=\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\sigma+\mathbb Z\tau$, then every finite family of additive logarithmic defects and multiplicative shift quotients has differential transcendence degree equal to the number of distinct nonzero shift classes in $\mathbb C/P$. This holds over both the paired elliptic compositum and $\mathbb C(z)$. Thus the complete shift differential matroid is the pointed partition matroid of period classes, and the elliptic gamma function remains hypertranscendental over its natural elliptic coefficient field.

The proof uses a finite-family common-trace form of Ostrowski descent, specialization from a product of elliptic curves, and an orbital pole obstruction. The manuscript positions these mechanisms against Hardouin–Singer, Dreyfus–Hardouin–Roques–Singer, Arreche–Babbitt, and Felder–Varchenko, and claims novelty for the two complete classifications rather than for multiplication or orbital summability themselves. Nishizawa's face equations propagate the rank-three obstruction through every higher order.

The manuscript is self-contained apart from standard differential algebra and Kato's published positive theorem. It gives full proofs of the simultaneous descent, field disjointness, specialization, and pole-orbit steps.
