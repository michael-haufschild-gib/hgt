Please consider the manuscript “Differential Algebraicity of Nishizawa's Multiple Elliptic Gamma Functions: An Exact Rational-Rank Dichotomy” for publication in *Advances in Mathematics*.

Kato proved differential algebraicity for rational period vectors in 2021 and explicitly left the non-rational case open. This manuscript gives a complete answer for every nonempty finite upper-half-plane period tuple. The exact invariant is the rational rank of the periods together with the unit period: rank two is precisely the differentially algebraic phase, while every rank at least three is hypertranscendental. The result includes partially dependent and non-rational tuples, so it is not a generic-parameter theorem or a classification with residual cases.

The proof also establishes an exact finite-family Ostrowski criterion of independent use. For additive primitives under an arbitrary family of compatible automorphisms, differential dependence is equivalent to the existence of one simultaneous base-field telescoper for a nonzero family of constant-coefficient differential operators. The argument uses Kähler differentials and field trace and does not require commuting automorphisms, differential closure, or Picard–Vessiot existence. In the elliptic-gamma application, the criterion reduces the problem to an elliptic pole-orbit obstruction; Nishizawa's face equations then propagate the rank-three obstruction through the full hierarchy.

The manuscript is self-contained apart from Kato's published positive theorem, states the exact scope of the classification, and compares the translation mechanism with recent work on elliptic-curve isogeny equations and elliptic hypergeometric functions.
