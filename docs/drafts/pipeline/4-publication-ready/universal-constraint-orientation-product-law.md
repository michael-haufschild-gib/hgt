## The fixed analytic source

There is one bounded real gauge `u_*` for which

```text
g_*(z)=sum_(p prime) 2^(-p) exp(u_*(p))/(log p-z)             (1)
```

has nonnegative imaginary part on the upper half-plane and whose translates over the fixed
universal palette

```text
U=Fin(N) x (N ->_0 Z)                                       (2)
```

are differentially algebraically independent over `C(z)`.  The palette contains, as an injective
subpalette, the complete facet--Laurent orbit of every finite labelled simplicial complex.

This source and palette are chosen once.  No later simplicial complex, chamber, structural family,
or residue index changes them.

## The oriented residue cube

Put

```text
a_n=-(n+1),   n>=0.                                         (3)
```

These points are real, pairwise distinct, and disjoint from the positive prime-log pole support.
For finite sets `J subset S subset N`, define

```text
epsilon_J(n) = -1  if n in J,
                1  if n notin J,

g_(S,J)(z)=g_*(z)+sum_(n in S) epsilon_J(n)/(a_n-z).          (4)
```

The chamber set is the powerset of `S`, hence has `2^(#S)` vertices.  Define

```text
kappa_res(S,J)=#(S cap J).                                   (5)
```

When `J subset S`, this equals `#J`, the graph distance from the all-positive vertex in the
standard cube.

## Exact Herglotz chamber theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

For arbitrary finite `S,J`,

```text
Im g_(S,J)(z)>=0 for every Im z>0
  iff S and J are disjoint
  iff kappa_res(S,J)=0.                                     (6)
```

In particular, inside the cube `J subset S`, the representative is Herglotz exactly when `J` is
empty.

### Proof

If `S` and `J` are disjoint, every active residue is positive.  The imaginary part of each Cauchy
atom is

```text
Im 1/(a-z)=Im(z)/|a-z|^2>=0,                                (7)
```

and the fixed background `g_*` is Herglotz, proving the forward sign.

Conversely, suppose `n in S cap J`.  Remove the `n`th atom and write

```text
g_(S,J)(z)=G_n(z)-1/(a_n-z).                                (8)
```

The background `G_n` is continuous at `a_n`: the prime-log source is holomorphic there, and every
remaining finite atom has a different pole.  Choose `delta>0` so that

```text
|Im G_n(a_n+i y)|<M                                         (9)
```

for `0<y<delta`.  Taking also `y<1/M` gives

```text
Im g_(S,J)(a_n+i y)
  =Im G_n(a_n+i y)-1/y<0.                                  (10)
```

Thus every chamber with an active negative residue leaves the Herglotz cone.  Equations (5)--(6)
follow. `QED`

## Rational-gauge field invariance `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `K_*` be the embedded differential field over `C(z)` generated by all derivatives of the
universal translated palette.  The translated correction attached to (4) is rational:

```text
r_(S,J,h)(z)
  =sum_(n in S) epsilon_J(n)/(a_n-(z+h)) in C(z).            (11)
```

Every derivative of every `r_(S,J,h)` is again rational.  Coordinatewise addition of such base
elements is an automorphism of the differential-independence problem: on every finite jet packet it
is induced by the polynomial automorphism

```text
X_(i,m) |-> X_(i,m)+D^m r_i.                                (12)
```

Its inverse subtracts the same rational jets.  Therefore the gauged palette remains
differentially algebraically independent and

```text
C(z)<D^m T_h g_(S,J)> = K_*                                 (13)
```

as an embedded differential field for every finite chamber.  This is equality inside the same
upper-end meromorphic field, not an abstract isomorphism. `QED`

## Universal constraint--orientation product theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `Delta` be any finite labelled simplicial complex with facet family `F`, let `d` be any common
translation denominator, and let

```text
N_i in C[D][x_v^(+-1):v in V]/I_Delta,   i in I,             (14)
```

be any finite localized structural family.  Construct the facet-sewn analytic outputs from the
corresponding subpalette of translates of `g_(S,J)`.  Then, simultaneously for every finite
orientation chamber,

```text
the analytic outputs are differentially algebraically independent over C
  iff the classes N_i are linearly independent over C[D].   (15)
```

The same chamber also satisfies (6), (13), and (5).  Hence the realization map covers the exact
Cartesian product

```text
{all finite localized Stanley--Reisner nonlinear differential matroids}
  x {all finite residue cubes}.                              (16)
```

The first coordinate is unchanged when the second changes; the second is unchanged when the first
changes.

Equivalently, for every `k>=0` there is one explicit representative in the same field such that

```text
kappa_res=k,
g is Herglotz iff k=0,
and g realizes every finite constraint geometry.             (17)
```

### Proof

The rational-gauge theorem gives differential independence of the full gauged palette and the
field equality (13).  Restrict it along the injective embedding of the finite facet--Laurent orbit
of `Delta`.  The coefficient-uniform differential-operator theorem identifies independence of the
analytic outputs with row independence.  The exact facet-coordinate theorem identifies row
independence with independence of the classes (14), yielding (15).  The Herglotz and cubical
coordinates are (5)--(6) and do not enter the sewing.  Taking `S=J={0,...,k-1}` gives (17). `QED`

## Positive pointing law

The theorem identifies a new geometric distinction:

```text
unpointed HT field + complete finite constraint geometry
  does not determine
the Herglotz orientation.                                   (18)
```

This is not a statement that positivity is absent.  Positivity is present as the unique base
chamber of every finite residue cube.  It is additional pointed data: a coherent orientation of
the rational residue directions.

The law is stronger than finite-constraint universality.  The same field now contains, over every
finite constraint geometry, an entire finite polarization cube with an exact analytic selector.
Neither coordinate is a function of the other.
