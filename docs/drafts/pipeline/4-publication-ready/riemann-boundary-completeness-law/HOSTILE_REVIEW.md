# Hostile review — general scientific journal standard

## Verdict

**Major revision / not yet publication-ready.** Mathematical claims checked in the internal corpus
are coherent, and the new headline is genuinely more universal than the previous Hilbert-centered
draft. The paper does **not** yet satisfy the original terminal goal: it neither proves RH nor
produces the orientation-complete reporter, and external novelty has intentionally not been audited.

## Strongest contribution

The strongest concrete theorem is not the elementary kernel-pair factorization by itself. It is
the combined prime-normal nonlocal--singular law:

1. finite-depth source embeddings remain injective and divisor-faithful;
2. fixed spectral points collapse to one cofinal source atom;
3. every informative closed countable-prime completion needs topology strictly finer than product
   observation; and
4. exact reflected valuation sewing has norm growth beyond every finite Wold--Rees order.

This is a precise, route-specific frontier theorem. It excludes a broad class of regular,
finite-coordinate, finite-Rees completions without pretending to exclude differential, Stokes,
cohomological, or other non-Hilbert routes.

## What the universal law genuinely adds

The polarized conservativity theorem identifies a route-independent invariant: the kernel pair of
an observation. A target charge is recoverable exactly when its kernel pair contains the
observation kernel pair. Serial processing is irreversible; parallel tests intersect radicals;
reflection-even observations cannot recover a nonzero reflection-odd charge. This cleanly explains
why route-specific missing objects are all factors `d` in `χ = dπ`.

That synthesis is mathematically correct. Its elementary abstract proof means significance must
come from the concrete RH applications, not from presenting quotient factorization as deep by
itself.

## Blocking criticisms

1. **No RH proof or zero exclusion.** The Riemann closure theorem assumes a reporter whose source
   identity forces its charge to vanish. That producer property is exactly the missing mathematics.
   The manuscript labels it honestly, but cannot be sold as a proof.

2. **Novelty unknown.** No external literature audit has been run under current instruction. No
   claim of novelty, priority, or journal significance is supportable yet.

3. **No bibliography or positioning.** A general journal will demand comparison with classical
   factorization/quotient theory, rigged Hilbert and closed-relation theory, Gamma
   hypertranscendence, co-Poisson/Sonin work, Hedenmalm's program, and existing RH equivalences.

4. **Risk of collage remains.** Green complexes, group-scheme jets, Gamma canonical products,
   Bohr tomography, Hilbert defects, and Wold--Rees asymptotics occupy one manuscript. The new law
   provides a real organizing spine, but a reviewer may still ask that the nonlocal--singular RH
   theorem become the main application and that secondary examples move to appendices.

5. **Self-containedness is incomplete.** The Calderón--Witt source and complete prime-normal
   packet are summarized rather than constructed in full. A journal reader cannot independently
   verify every source assertion from this manuscript alone.

6. **Gamma section is explanatory, not yet a formal corollary of conservativity.** The contrast
   between exponent one and even exponents is exact and interesting, but the paper must define the
   target charge that fails to factor through the even fold if it wants this to be a theorem-level
   instance rather than an analogy.

7. **Route atlas mixes proved impossibilities with requested structures.** Rows must be visibly
   marked `constructed`, `classified singular`, or `not yet produced`; otherwise readers may infer
   more closure than proved.

8. **“Universal” needs disciplined scope.** The kernel-pair theorem is universal for observations
   of a fixed source and target. It does not establish that every conceivable RH proof must pass
   through this source model. Current wording now limits route-specific claims, but this limit must
   remain explicit throughout.

## Required revision for a credible submission

1. Center the paper on the polarized conservativity theorem plus the prime-normal
   nonlocal--singular application.
2. State all packet and Hellinger definitions explicitly and prove the combined theorem
   dependency-closed in the paper.
3. Recast Green, cyclic orientation, Gamma folding, jets, and tomography as short certified
   corollaries or appendices.
4. Add a status column to every route table.
5. Run a serious literature and priority audit only when authorized.
6. Remove any residual sentence suggesting that the manuscript already constructs the decisive
   Riemann reporter.
