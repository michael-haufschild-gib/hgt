# Antagonist referee report

Manuscript: *No Positive-Definite Metric on the Schwartz Source Core of the Hedenmalm Theta Pair*

Review standard: reputable broad mathematical-sciences journal. This assessment gives no novelty,
significance, or publication credit for mechanized or computer-assisted verification.

## Recommendation

**Accept as a research note after routine editorial copyediting. No mathematical revision
required.**

The paper proves a sharp and current theorem about Hedenmalm's June 2026 spectral proposal. It
does not prove RH and does not pretend to rule out Hilbert–Pólya in general. It completely answers
the source-core metric problem it states:

- all continuous positive pair-symmetric pullbacks are classified by positive temperate measures
  on the real `Xi` divisor;
- every nonzero such pullback has dense proper nonclosed ordinary-`L²` radical;
- every such form has a fixed-energy ordinary-`L²` null sequence and is nonclosable;
- the radical strictly exceeds `ker L`, so no positive-definite metric exists on the full
  algebraic range `L S(R)` under the stated source continuity;
- the exact surviving object is a degenerate divisor-sampling rigging, with a closed
  eigenprofile conservativity criterion equivalent to RH plus simplicity and positive fiber
  mass.

This is genuine progress on a newly proposed RH route: it closes its natural regular
source-extension branch and identifies the only surviving metric geometry.

## Hostile checks

### 1. Is this secretly conditional on RH?

No. The measure is supported only on real zeros because the measure lives on the real Fourier
axis and theta nullity forces `|Xi|²=0` on its support. No assertion that every zero is real is
used. The eigenprofile corollary states an equivalence: positive definiteness on all zero
profiles would force RH and simplicity. It does not assert that such conservativity has been
constructed.

### 2. Does the title overclaim a no-go for Hilbert–Pólya?

No. It names the Hedenmalm pair and the Schwartz source core. The paper explicitly says that a
form confined to Hedenmalm's finite zero-eigenfunction span and refusing source-core extension
is outside the theorem. Other Hilbert–Pólya, de Branges, trace-formula, and noncommutative
geometric architectures are expressly excluded from the claim.

### 3. Is `L S(R)` being smuggled into Schwartz space or ordinary `L²`?

No. The revised text states that `L S(R)` is only an algebraic target image; `L` is not assumed
to preserve Schwartz space or ordinary `L²`. The sole regularity hypothesis is joint Schwartz
continuity of the pullback. This disposes of the strongest domain objection.

### 4. Is the positive-definite no-go merely the `L²` nonclosability result restated?

No. It is strictly stronger and algebraic. Positive definiteness on `L S(R)` would make the
pullback radical exactly `ker L = Ck`. Pair symmetry and theta nullity instead put every
translation of `k` in the radical; Wiener cyclicity makes its ordinary-`L²` image dense. A
one-dimensional subspace cannot be dense in ordinary `L²`. No target topology or completion is
used in this contradiction.

### 5. Does the classification omit non-atomic measures or impose atomicity by hand?

No. Bochner–Schwartz first gives an arbitrary positive temperate measure. The identity
`Q(k,k)=∫|Xi|² dμ=0` forces support on the real zero set. Since `Xi` is a nonzero entire
function, that real set is discrete; atomicity follows. The converse includes every positive
temperate choice of divisor weights.

### 6. Is failure of closability proved, or only failure of bounded representation?

It is proved. Density of the radical produces `z_n -> 0` in ordinary `L²` with constant
nonzero form energy and zero difference energy. This violates the standard sequential
closability criterion directly and also rules out restriction from a closed nonnegative
ordinary-`L²` form.

### 7. Is the normalized source asserted rather than derived?

No. The paper gives the polynomial-Gaussian seed, the theta series, its Mellin transform, the
Jacobi symmetry and decay argument, and the exact identity `hat k = Xi`. It also identifies the
source with twice Hedenmalm's theta derivative after logarithmic change of variables.

### 8. Are classical ingredients being sold as new?

No. Bochner–Schwartz and Wiener are identified as classical, cited, and denied novelty credit.
The novelty claim is limited to the exact Hedenmalm-source classification, the collision of
divisor support with source cyclicity, strict source degeneracy, and the resulting no-go theorem.
A targeted current-literature audit found no collision. Because the direct parent appeared only
in June 2026, an editor should still ask a specialist to confirm priority; this is normal
editorial diligence, not a mathematical gap.

### 9. Does the manuscript leave an open question in place of an answer?

No. It poses no research question and defers no theorem clause. The category under study is
completely classified. The target-only finite-span regime is an explicit scope boundary, not an
unproved claim needed by the paper.

## Publication-threshold judgment

The theorem is too specialized for a flagship multidisciplinary venue whose criterion is broad
public reach, but it meets the research and exposition threshold of a reputable general
mathematics journal and should interest analysts, spectral theorists, and researchers following
RH. Its strongest feature is not a claim to solve RH; it is a complete, exact obstruction to a
natural Hilbert realization of a prominent new spectral formulation, together with the singular
replacement law.

No major correctness, novelty-boundary, significance, or exposition objection survives review.
