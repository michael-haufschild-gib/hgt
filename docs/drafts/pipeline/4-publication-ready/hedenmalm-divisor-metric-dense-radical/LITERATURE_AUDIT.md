# Literature audit

Cutoff: 27 July 2026.

## Direct parent

H. Hedenmalm, *Spectral interpretation of Riemann zeta zeros*, arXiv:2606.17494
(16 June 2026), constructs the theta differential pair, its zero-eigenfunction
characterization, the finite eigenfunction domain, and the self-adjoint-pair implication to RH.
Its Section 4 asks for related sesquilinear forms and a Hilbert-space inner product. It does not:

- extend the pullback to the translation-stable Schwartz source core;
- classify jointly continuous positive source-core forms;
- derive support of their spectral measure on the real `Xi` divisor;
- prove dense radical, strict source degeneracy, or ordinary-`L²` nonclosability.

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17494>

## Classical ingredients and novelty boundary

- L. Schwartz, *Théorie des distributions*, Hermann, 1966: positive-definite tempered
  distributions and positive temperate measures.
- N. Wiener, “Tauberian theorems,” *Annals of Mathematics* 33 (1932), 1–100: translation
  cyclicity from almost-everywhere nonvanishing Fourier transform.
- J. C. Lagarias, “The Riemann hypothesis: arithmetic and geometry,” *Clay Mathematics
  Proceedings* 6 (2006), 127–139: spectral interpretation alone does not prove RH; positivity is
  decisive.
- A. Connes, *The Riemann Hypothesis: Past, Present and a Letter Through Time*,
  arXiv:2602.04022 (2026): current broad survey of RH approaches.

No novelty is assigned to Bochner–Schwartz, Wiener cyclicity, or elementary finite-dimensional
closedness. Novelty claim is limited to their exact assembly for Hedenmalm's theta source:

1. complete divisor-supported classification;
2. dense proper radical and fixed-energy null sequences;
3. impossibility of a positive-definite metric on the full algebraic range with continuous
   Schwartz pullback;
4. exact singular Fourier-rigging replacement and eigenprofile sampling law.
