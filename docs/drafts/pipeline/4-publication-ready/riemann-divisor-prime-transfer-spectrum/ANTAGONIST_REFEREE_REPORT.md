# Antagonist referee report

Manuscript: **A Universal Asymptotic-Type Superselection Law:
Prime-Valuation Reflection and the Riemann Boundary**

Review posture: skeptical referee for a renowned broad scientific or general mathematics journal.

Date: 2026-07-30.

## Recommendation

**Potentially publishable after specialist review in a strong broad mathematics journal.
Reject in present form for Nature, Science, or an equivalent top general-science venue.**

This is now a substantive arithmetic information-geometry paper, not a decorative RH
equivalence. Its strongest theorem is the exact conjunction of:

1. a reflected Hellinger critical-window law;
2. exact Hellinger--Wick reciprocity;
3. a half-normal/unit/all-orders-flat outer type fracture;
4. an arithmetic-independent essential-sewing theorem in arbitrary normed spaces;
5. an odd pointed Fisher--Ward carrier that retains orientation before divisor scalarization.

That package appears new on the present literature audit. It is a credible novel RH proof
architecture. It still does not exclude a zero or prove that zeta reflection must act in the
type-preserving category.

## What is genuinely strong

### 1. Exact source object and nonlinear scaling law

The paper works with actual geometric prime-valuation product states, before zeros. It proves
\[
\mathcal R_N(c)^2\to e^{-\Phi(c)},\qquad
\Phi(c)=4\int_0^1\frac{\sinh^2(cu)}u\,du,
\]
from an exact local Hellinger defect. This is a theorem about a genuine probability source, not a
renaming of the Riemann hypothesis.

### 2. Hellinger--Wick reciprocity is the best novelty claim

The identity
\[
\mathcal R_N(c)^2e^{K_N(2c/\log N)}\to1
\]
identifies reflected information loss and prime-log Poisson fluctuation as two representations
of one action. The weighted Mertens test-function limit is standard and the manuscript now says
so. Significance rests on the exact Hellinger realization and reciprocity, not on rediscovering a
known prime-sum limit.

### 3. Off-line reflection changes asymptotic species

The fixed-displacement result is substantially stronger than “different Rees grades”:
\[
c<0:\ A_N(c)=\Theta(a_N^{1/2}),\qquad
c>0:\ A(c)\in\mathfrak F_\infty(a).
\]
One face is finite/half-normal; the other is flat beyond every finite Rees order. Kakutani
singularity maps both to the same yes/no answer and therefore erases orientation. This is a
memorable refinement of a classical dichotomy.

### 4. Universal essential sewing closes dimension, matrix, and arithmetic loopholes

Every bounded operator family with
\(\|R_N\|=O(a_N^{-K})\) preserves \(\mathfrak F_\infty(a)\), without a
finite-dimensionality assumption. Finite matrices, larger packets, infinite rank, and bounded
dilations do not cross the fracture unless the full operator norm leaves every finite Rees
order. The revised theorem no longer assumes primes: for any asymptotic scale, all-orders-flat
source germ, and nonzero finite-grade target germ in arbitrary real normed spaces, exact sewing
forces
\[
a_N^{-K}=o(\|R_N\|)
\qquad\text{for every fixed }K.
\]
Thus the escape is an essential scale singularity, not unspecified unboundedness. Prime
reflection is now a concrete arithmetic realization of a genuine universal law. The proof is
elementary after the invariant is found; discovery of the invariant and its sharp arithmetic
realization carry the significance.

### 5. Orientation is no longer merely asserted to be missing

The even profile \(\Phi\) cannot carry side. The pointed Fisher connection is odd under tangent
reversal, and its Ward trace equals a double-cutoff curvature pairing. This directly addresses
the earlier objection that squaring the Julia coordinate destroyed the sign needed to orient a
zero.

### 6. Positive escape and exact analytic consumer are both identified

The Weyl block shows how reflection can cross by modular retuning rather than contradicting the
no-sewing theorem. The singular Möbius--Gamma graph shows how divisor grade forces an unbounded
internal category. The four-unit Ward theorem then identifies the exact RH-complete boundary
estimate and gives a one-node witness for every strict-right zero.

## Fatal issue for a top general-science journal

The manuscript does not prove a source-to-zero transport theorem. A nontrivial zero
\(\rho=\tfrac12+c+i\gamma\) is not shown to induce:

- the reflected prime-valuation pair used in the Hellinger theorem;
- a pointed Fisher tangent with the required sign;
- a morphism through the singular Möbius--Gamma graph;
- a finite-Rees-moderate or otherwise type-controlled boundary operator;
- the four-unit Ward bound at zero aperture.

The functional equation pairs scalar zeros, but it does not supply this lifted source diagram.
Consequently the paper proves:

> every regular finite-Rees source lift preserves the all-orders-flat radical and cannot sew the
> two off-line valuation sectors;

not:

> every off-line zeta-zero pair would require such a forbidden lift.

The exact surviving singular graph explains why the latter implication is nontrivial. It also
means the no-sewing theorem cannot itself force \(c=0\).

## Major mathematical criticisms

### 1. The universal theorem is genuine, but the RH interaction is still a synthesis

The essential-sewing theorem is now genuinely universal: its hypotheses and conclusion do not
mention primes, zeta, dimension, or matrix rank.  But the even action, odd Fisher polarization,
depth--support filtration, and Rees type are not yet carried by one functorial object. The
source-type conservation theorem composes separate results; it does not prove a new compatibility
identity between hypertranscendental fusion, Hellinger type, and Ward curvature.

This is acceptable for a synthesis paper if stated plainly. It is not enough to claim a final
universal mechanism for RH.

### 2. Signed Fisher--Ward transgression is algebraically elementary

Rank-two trace cyclicity and oddness in the tangent are straightforward. Its value is conceptual:
it identifies where orientation can live before scalarization. A referee will demand that
significance be argued through the source-to-divisor chain, not through difficulty of the trace
identity.

### 3. Abstract negligibility preservation has classical precedent

Colombeau theory already treats all-orders negligible nets as an ideal under moderate nets and
records functoriality under continuous linear maps.  The manuscript now cites this directly.
Accordingly, the arithmetic-independent operator theorem is useful organizing language and its
essential lower bound is a sharp contrapositive, but neither should be sold as difficult or
priority-bearing in isolation.  Novelty must rest on the exact prime Hellinger realization,
reflection chirality, and source-to-boundary synthesis.

### 4. “Universal” must remain scoped

The revised text properly restricts universality to cutoff scaling and the two representations
inside the rational-prime valuation source. It must not drift in abstract, title, or publicity
into a claim about arbitrary Euler products, nonlinear maps, unbounded closed operators,
distributional boundaries, or all RH architectures.

### 5. Priority remains plausible, not certified

No formula-level collision was found for the reflected local defect, the exact
Hellinger--Wick reciprocal limit, or the half-normal/all-orders-flat classification. Search
silence does not prove priority. A specialist in product experiments, arithmetic statistical
mechanics, and mod-Poisson convergence should check this before submission.

### 6. RH significance is architectural

The paper identifies a new source invariant, shows what regular transport cannot do, supplies a
singular survivor, and locates the missing boundary estimate. That is legitimate progress in
proof architecture. It is not a bound on zeros, a zero-density theorem, a defect-index vanishing
theorem, or a proof of RH.
