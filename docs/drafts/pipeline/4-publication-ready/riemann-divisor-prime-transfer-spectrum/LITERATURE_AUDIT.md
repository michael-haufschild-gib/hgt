# Literature and novelty audit

Audit date: 2026-07-30.

## Candidate new theorem package

No priority is claimed for any ingredient in isolation. Candidate novelty is exact conjunction:

1. For reflected geometric prime-valuation laws at exponents
   \(1\mp 2c/\log N\), prove exact local Hellinger defect
   \[
   \frac{4p^{-1}\sinh^2(c\log p/\log N)}{(1-p^{-1})^2}
   \]
   and critical-window affinity
   \[
   \mathcal R_N(c)^2\to
   \exp\!\left[-4\int_0^1\frac{\sinh^2(cu)}u\,du\right].
   \]
2. Identify exact Hellinger--Wick reciprocity: the same action is the limiting symmetric
   prime-log Poisson cumulant and
   \(\mathcal R_N(c)^2e^{K_N(2c/\log N)}\to1\).
3. Resolve fixed-displacement Hellinger affinity against canonical Wold volume
   \(a_N=\prod_{p<N}(1-p^{-1})\), and prove the exact Euler-partition curvature identity
   \[
   A_N(c)^2=\frac{Z_N(1-c)^2}{Z_N(1)Z_N(1-2c)}
   \]
   and the sharp finite-face normalization
   \[
   \frac{A_N(c)^2}{a_N}\longrightarrow
   \frac{\zeta(1-c)^2}{\zeta(1-2c)}
   \qquad(c<0).
   \]
4. Prove exact three-phase law
   \[
   c<0:\ A_N(c)=\Theta(\sqrt{a_N}),\qquad
   c=0:\ A_N(c)=1,\qquad
   c>0:\ A(c)\in\mathfrak F_\infty(a).
   \]
5. Interpret Kakutani singularity as quotient that erases this reflection orientation: both
   off-critical faces are singular, but rates have different categories.
6. Prove an arithmetic-independent, arbitrary-dimensional asymptotic superselection theorem:
   fixed finite-Rees operator families preserve the all-orders-flat radical.
7. Quantify the universal escape.  For any asymptotic scale, all-orders-flat source germ, and
   nonzero finite-grade target germ, every exact bounded linear sewing family has norm beyond
   every inverse scale power.  The reflected prime multiplier is a concrete realization, not
   an assumption in the theorem.
8. Show how standard reflection escapes this obstruction: the second eigenvalue of the
   rank-one level-changing scattering block is unitary exactly on the zero-drift locus and is
   strictly expansive on the outward radial face. This is a classical block with a new role in
   the source-type classification, not a new representation-theoretic formula.
9. Identify source origin through diagonal \(\zeta(s)/\zeta(2s)\) activation:
   prime-harmonic occupancy, summable positive depth fibres, prime-harmonic Fisher-whitened Gamma
   fibre.
10. Prove a complementary signed orientation law: pointed Fisher connection is odd under tangent
    reversal and its cutoff Ward trace transgresses exactly to double-cutoff curvature.
11. Couple asymptotic obstruction with independently proved depth × support HT superselection and
    divisor grade drop, while keeping asymptotic type distinct from finite divisor grade.
12. Exhibit exact singular Möbius–Gamma overlap graph reconstructing completed squarefree and
   continued \(\Xi\)-currents.
13. Identify the exact analytic consumer: finite-aperture transport is four-unit nonexpansive,
    its boundary derivative is the Ward response, and the Euler-edge derivative bound is
    equivalent to zero exclusion with a one-node witness for every strict-right zero.

The novelty claim is this critical-window reciprocity/type-superselection/signed-transgression
synthesis. It is not a novelty claim for geometric prime valuations, Hellinger affinity,
Kakutani dichotomy, weighted Mertens test-function limits, Rees filtrations,
hypertranscendence of Dirichlet series, finite-rank trace cyclicity, or logarithmic
differentiation.

## Antagonist significance boundary

The local Hellinger sum and finite-order ideal invariance are elementary after correct object is
identified. Publication case rests on discovery that:

- Hellinger curvature is an exact midpoint ratio of finite Euler partitions, with a zeta-valued
  normalization constant;
- actual reflected zeta exponents fall into different asymptotic categories;
- classical Kakutani singularity erases exactly this category distinction;
- distinction survives arbitrary-dimensional finite-order operator transport;
- a standard Weyl block escapes precisely by changing modular class and is unitary only on its
  zero-retuning locus;
- activation and Gamma whitening identify source of singular scale;
- divisor differentiation forces same singular category;
- an exact source graph reaches continued \(\Xi\)-current in that category;
- the next analytic target is not unspecified positivity: it is the boundary derivative of an
  already contractive four-unit family, and every strict-right zero has an explicit one-node
  violation.

This is now a coherent new proof architecture rather than an isolated no-go: exact source
orientation, exact obstruction, exact positive escape, exact singular internal graph, and exact
RH-complete consumer. It is plausibly a strong broad mathematics-journal article if priority
survives expert review. It is not honestly a proof of RH or an unconditional zero-exclusion
theorem. A top general-science or Nature/Science case still requires the source-uniform
boundary-tangent estimate itself.
