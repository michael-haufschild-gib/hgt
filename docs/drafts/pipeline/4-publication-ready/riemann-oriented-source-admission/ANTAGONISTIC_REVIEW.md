## Hostile objections that remain

### 1. No zero exclusion follows

The source-to-Xi landing is unconditional, but source-derived landing in the sharp Euler--Hodge
admission domain is not. The Hedenmalm--Euler theorem proves that a regular bridge cannot work;
it does not construct the required singular bridge or show that any Xi zero fibre belongs to its
domain. No zero-free region, density estimate, spacing theorem, or critical-line theorem follows.

### 2. Parent theorem is elementary; significance rests on arithmetic instantiations

Relative quotient naturality is short linear algebra. A reviewer will not credit the abstract
theorem alone as a universal law of zeta sources. Scientific weight comes from the explicit
Hedenmalm/Euler mismatch, Deninger incidence realization, and physical Möbius--theta landing.
Those are genuine, but only the first currently yields a new cross-route theorem, and that theorem
is a necessary-singularity result rather than positive control of zeros.

### 3. “All no-gos are shadows” remains broader than the proved transfer theorem

The manuscript rigorously instantiates three carriers. Dense radicals, maximal verticality,
finite-Rees sewing, Ward positivity, Calkin consumers, hypertranscendence, and recurrence are
explained by the same admission language, but explicit chain squares and quotient maps are not
constructed for every named route. Those paragraphs remain disciplined synthesis, not one formal
corollary covering every row.

### 4. External novelty and priority are unknown

Literature review was intentionally deferred. The paper has no bibliography or comparison with
relative cohomology, homological perturbation, boundary triples, rigged Hilbert spaces,
Connes--Meyer/Burnol source spaces, Hedenmalm's program, or existing singular-intertwiner no-gos.
General-journal novelty cannot be judged without this work. “New” currently means new to the
internal research body, not established new to mathematics.

### 5. Decisive producer remains open

Equation (8.3) is much sharper than the old arbitrary closed-set criterion, but it is still a
conjectural construction. The manuscript explicitly says it is unavailable. This violates the
requested condition that the paper answer rather than leave its decisive questions open.

### 6. No classic route is ruled out in full

The paper proves that regular Hilbert transport cannot merge two named carriers and that several
finite/bounded/product/Rees mechanisms fail. It correctly does not infer that Hedenmalm,
Calderón--Witt, Connes/CCM, Hilbert--Pólya, or another broad route can never prove RH.
