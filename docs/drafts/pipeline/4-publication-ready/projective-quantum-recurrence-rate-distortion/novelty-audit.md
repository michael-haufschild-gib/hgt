# Novelty and significance audit

Date: 2026-07-28  
Status: candidate new result after exact-object search; absence from literature
cannot be proved by search.

## Exact novelty object

The central candidate is not “quantum systems recur,” reverse water filling,
Haar union bounds, tail truncation, or simultaneous Diophantine approximation
separately. It is the joined finite theorem

```text
two-sided exact return-target information for every fixed weight vector
  + deterministic deadline for every compatible eigenphase packet
  + equality with one Gaussian rate–distortion functional,
```

where:

1. a constructive product box proves positive target volume and the upper
   exponential envelope uses the same pivot-minimized water entropy;
2. negative log exact target volume and log least minimax deadline share that
   entropy with explicit finite errors;
3. the theorem proves a finite relative-error inequality, rather than hiding
   support-size dependence in asymptotic notation;
4. the full-support allocation functional is exactly the finite
   parallel-Gaussian reverse-water-filling program after a change of
   variables;
5. uniformity upgrades the positive near-hit deadline to a return after every
   prescribed delay `M`, by time `(M+1)T`; if exit occurred by `M`, the hit is
   genuinely post-exit.

The selected-support product and finite-horizon Haar union law are useful
corollaries, not the novelty definition. The former does not claim joint
optimality over discrete selections; the latter remains one-sided because
orbit hits are correlated.

The second candidate is the observer-family theorem:

```text
exact total matrix return distance = observer-union graph phase energy;
visible phase capacity = sectors − graph components;
deadline ≤ (Q+1)^(visible phase capacity).
```

This now includes transfer to every Hilbert–Schmidt-normalized self-adjoint
expectation probe, with additive calibration-error radii. A third extension is
genuine recurrence of every nonnegative summable countable pure-point phase
packet through a finite-head/summable-tail law.

## Closest primary literature

### Kotowski and Oszmaniec, 2026

“Tight bounds on recurrence time in closed quantum systems,” accepted in
*Physical Review Letters* on 23 June 2026,
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10409>.

They prove deterministic pure-state recurrence bounds, generic random
Hamiltonian lower bounds, inverse-speed results, and a hard effective-support
refinement. Their conclusion explicitly identifies mixed-state and
expectation-value bounds as open extensions. Tail truncation and
generic-spectrum lower bounds are therefore parent methods, not novelty here.
Their results do not give the pivot-resolved fixed-weight Haar budget, optimize
anisotropic coordinate resolutions after paying twice the discarded
probability mass, or identify the resulting reverse-water allocation.

### Gupta and Short, 2026

“Recurrence Time for Finite Quantum Systems,” *Physical Review A* 114,
012208 (2026), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14995>.

They prove simultaneous recurrence bounds for all states of a fixed finite
unitary system, including mixed states by convexity, using approximation of
energy differences. This kills any novelty claim of the form “mixed states
recur” or “all finite states return together.” Their system-uniform problem
does not contain weight-adaptive water entropy, an observer-union graph, or an
observer-rank exponent.

### Anand et al., 2026

“Quantum recurrences and the arithmetic of Floquet dynamics,” *Quantum* 10,
2074 (2026), <https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-04-20-2074>.

They classify exact, state-independent recurrences for fixed arithmetic
Floquet spectra. The present object is approximate, state-weighted, and
minimax over all compatible spectra. Neither theorem contains the other.

### Campos Venuti, 2015

“The recurrence time in quantum mechanics,”
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.04352>.

This gives generic recurrence estimates in terms of energy scale and effective
dimension. It does not give the finite fixed-weight minimax water law.

### Kac, 1943

“On the distribution of values of trigonometric sums with linearly
independent frequencies,” *American Journal of Mathematics* 65, 609–615.

This is a classical parent for value distributions of trigonometric sums.
It makes phase-space distribution non-novel. The present target-volume object
is used without rational-independence assumptions and is joined to a
spectrum-uniform minimax deadline; that joined law is the novelty candidate.

### Gimeno and Sotoca, 2017

“Upper bounds for the Poincaré recurrence time in quantum mixed states,”
*Journal of Physics A* 50, 185302,
<https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/aa67fe>.

They already prove recurrence bounds for mixed states with finite and
countably discrete spectra, including exit-conditioned recurrence. Therefore
neither “mixed states recur” nor qualitative countable recurrence is new. The
countable phase-energy theorem in the manuscript is a scope-closure result;
the candidate new observer content is the exact union-graph energy, capacity,
deadline and probe transfer.

### Rauer et al., 2018

“Recurrences in an isolated quantum many-body system,” *Science* 360,
307–310 (2018), <https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08231>.

Experimentally demonstrates that recurrence is observable-dependent in a
many-body system. It motivates the graph theorem but does not provide its
exact matrix identity, capacity, or deadline.

### Karimi et al., 2026

“Blueprint for experiments exploring the quantum recurrence theorem on a
coupled multiqubit system,” *Physical Review Research* 8, L012062 (2026),
<https://doi.org/10.1103/wv9h-x14y>.

Provides a current experimental setting and exponential size-scaling
prediction. It does not vary observer graph rank or derive the water
functional.

### Local-Hamiltonian ensemble boundary

The manuscript now gives an exact, explicit transfer: independently uniform
onsite phases in a Hermitian, particle-number-preserving local spin
Hamiltonian realize the phase torus in the single-excitation block. This
removes the claim that Haar geometry has no physical local realization. The
construction is engineered and noninteracting; it is not itself a novelty
claim about chaotic many-body spectra.

Cui, Schuster, Mao, Huang and Brandão, “Random unitaries from Hamiltonian
dynamics,” <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08434>, prove that ensembles of
constant-local time-independent Hamiltonian evolutions cannot form unitary
2-designs with the stated errors, even at arbitrarily long times. Their
object is a full unitary ensemble rather than an eigenphase marginal, so it
does not contradict the explicit onsite-field transfer. It does rule out
using a blanket “local dynamics become Haar” slogan to justify generic
interacting transfer.

### Gaussian lossy-compression boundary

Theis, Salimans, Hoffman and Mentzer, “Lossy Compression with Gaussian
Diffusion,” <https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08889>, use the classical
multivariate-Gaussian rate–distortion function as an operational benchmark
for diffusion compression. Their object has a random Gaussian source,
transmitted noise, reconstruction rule and, in the perfect-realism result,
shared randomness. It confirms that the same reverse-water allocation is
active in a modern compression system; it does not contain a phase-target
volume, spectrum-uniform recurrence deadline, or projective pivot.

Qian et al., “Rate-Distortion-Perception Tradeoff for Gaussian Vector
Sources,” *IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Information Theory* 6, 1–17
(2025), <https://doi.org/10.1109/JSAIT.2024.3509420>, prove that active
perception constraints replace classical reverse water filling by a
generalized allocation with positive component rates and unequal water
levels. This is a useful universality boundary: the manuscript's exact
identity is with the unconstrained parallel-Gaussian program, not every
Gaussian compression problem.

Kostina and Verdú, “Fixed-length lossy compression in the finite blocklength
regime,” *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 58, 3309–3338 (2012),
<https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2012.2186786>, derive rate-dispersion corrections
depending on coding blocklength and allowed excess-distortion probability.
Those variables have no counterpart in the deterministic target-volume or
minimax recurrence objects. Their sublinear coding correction therefore
cannot replace the manuscript's support-linear geometric remainder.

## Collision tests

Novelty is defeated if a prior source contains, up to notation and universal
constants:

1. the two-sided bound
   `(√2/π)^(r−1)e^(−H) ≤ μ_w(η)
   ≤ r e^(π(r−1)/4−H)` for the true target volume;
2. the same water functional controlling the least universal deadline with
   explicit logarithmic constants;
3. the same projective Gaussian rate–distortion variational identity attached
   to quantum recurrence;
4. the same finite observer-union graph identity, phase capacity,
   graph-rank guarantee and normalized-probe transfer.

Finding any parent ingredient alone is not a collision.

## Significance assessment

Positive case:

- answers “what information sets a quantum return deadline?” with one finite
  law rather than another dimension bound;
- connects quantum dynamics, information theory, graph topology, and
  Diophantine approximation;
- directly refines the hard-support conclusion of an accepted 2026 PRL;
- supplies a deterministic answer for finite families of mixed states and
  observables, a direction named open in that PRL;
- yields a falsifiable protocol in current multiqubit and observable-selective
  recurrence settings;
- includes an exact infinite pure-point boundary rather than pretending a
  continuous-spectrum theorem.

Negative case:

- no experimental data accompany the theory;
- the exact target-information remainder is still linear in support size in
  absolute terms, although its relative size is now explicitly controlled;
- no matching lower theorem for every fixed Hamiltonian is possible from
  weights alone;
- the graph deadline lacks a matching adversarial lower bound for every graph;
- Haar spectra model an ensemble, not every natural local Hamiltonian;
- “same as rate–distortion” is a convex-program identity, not a coding
  protocol.

These limitations must remain explicit. The Nature case rests on the joined
universal classification and immediate cross-disciplinary consequence, not
on cosmological rhetoric.
