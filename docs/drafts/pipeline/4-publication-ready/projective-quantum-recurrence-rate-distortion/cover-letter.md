# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

We submit “An information law for quantum recurrence” for consideration as an
Article in *Nature*.

The paper answers a basic quantitative question: what information must a
closed quantum system synchronize in order to return? Existing bounds count
Hilbert-space dimension or a hard effective support. We obtain a different
universal law. For every finite pure-state spectral probability vector and
every accuracy, we bound the full Haar fraction of compatible Floquet spectra
that return through every finite horizon, while retaining a deterministic
deadline for every spectrum. The Haar scale is controlled by a
pivot-optimized reverse-water-filling functional. The constructive theorem can
discard a chosen low-weight tail: discarded probability pays fidelity error,
whereas only retained coordinates pay clock dimension. The earlier
full-support-linear band is therefore a coarse corollary, not the refined law.
This is the finite convex program underlying parallel Gaussian
rate–distortion, now governing deterministic and distributional quantum phase
alignment.

We also solve the observer-dependent finite problem. For any collection of
mixed states or observables, total Hilbert–Schmidt return distance is exactly a
weighted phase energy on the union of their coherence graphs. Observable phase
capacity is the number of energy sectors minus the number of connected
components, and the same rank is the exponent in a deterministic recurrence
deadline. The exact identity extends to arbitrary summable pure-point spectra.

The result is timely. A June 2026 *Physical Review Letters* paper established
tight pure-state bounds and named mixed-state and observable dynamics as open
directions. A March 2026 *Physical Review Research* paper proposed a
superconducting multiqubit recurrence experiment, while earlier *Science*
experiments demonstrated observable-selective many-body recurrence. Our
theorems supply a weight-sensitive and observer-sensitive law for those
questions.

The immediate implication is experimentally discriminating: changing only the
prepared spectral weights changes the water allocation, while changing only
the probe family changes the coherence graph and its guaranteed exponent.
For random or programmable eigenphase ensembles, the Haar theorem gives a
finite-horizon recurrence quantile rather than one adversarial example.
The far-reaching implication is conceptual: recurrence is controlled by
distributed relational coherence, not by dimension, entropy, state spectrum,
initial speed, or quantum Fisher information alone.

We have deliberately excluded claims about cosmology, black holes, information
loss, or an operational communication code. They do not follow from the
hypotheses. The manuscript reports a finite mathematical law, its exact
infinite pure-point boundary, an explicit equal-information qutrit separation,
and a direct experimental protocol.

The work is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. No datasets
were generated. The author declares no competing interests. Generative

preparation; the human author takes responsibility for all claims, arguments,
citations, and conclusions.
