# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

We submit “A universal information law for quantum return” for consideration as an
Article in *Nature*.

The paper answers a basic quantitative question: what information must a
closed quantum system synchronize in order to return? Existing bounds count
Hilbert-space dimension or a hard effective support. We obtain a different
universal law. For every finite pure-state spectral probability vector and
every accuracy, one pivot-optimized reverse-water entropy controls three
quantities: exact negative log return-target volume, logarithm of the least
deadline valid for every compatible spectrum, and the parallel-Gaussian
rate–distortion functional. We prove both sides of the target-volume law by
constructing a positive-volume inner box and a matching exponential envelope.
All dimension terms are explicit, and the relative error is bounded by their
ratio to the water entropy. Haar volume is invariant phase-space geometry, not
a postulated physical Hamiltonian ensemble. A separate selected-tail
construction gives an exact certificate but is not presented as the joint
optimizer of the rounded clock product. The uniform law also guarantees a
return after any prescribed delay \(M\), by time \((M+1)T\); hence it supplies
a genuine post-exit recurrence whenever exit has occurred by \(M\).
The phase ensemble is not merely abstract: independently tunable on-site
fields in a nearest-neighbour number-preserving spin chain realize it exactly
in the one-excitation sector at one chosen sampling time. We do not extrapolate
that engineered transfer to generic interacting Hamiltonian ensembles.

We also solve the observer-dependent finite problem. For any collection of
mixed states or observables, total Hilbert–Schmidt return distance is exactly a
weighted phase energy on the union of their coherence graphs. Observable phase
capacity is the number of energy sectors minus the number of connected
components, and the same rank is the exponent in a deterministic recurrence
guarantee. Every Hilbert–Schmidt-normalized self-adjoint probe inherits the
bound, including two additive calibration-error radii. A standard
operator-norm-contractive probe inherits the corresponding bound with the
unavoidable general matrix-conversion factor \(\sqrt d\); the factor is
attained by the identity pairing. The observer identity extends to arbitrary
summable pure-point spectra. In addition, every
nonnegative summable countable pure-point phase packet recurs below every
positive phase-energy resolution.

The result is timely. A June 2026 *Physical Review Letters* paper established
tight pure-state bounds and named sharper mixed-state and observable dynamics
as open directions; earlier work had already established mixed-state recurrence
bounds for discrete spectra. A March 2026 *Physical Review Research* paper proposed a
superconducting multiqubit recurrence experiment, while earlier *Science*
experiments demonstrated observable-selective many-body recurrence. Our
theorems supply a weight-sensitive and observer-sensitive law for those
questions.

The immediate implication is experimentally discriminating: changing only the
prepared spectral weights changes the water allocation, while changing only
the probe family changes the coherence graph and its guaranteed exponent.
Randomized on-site fields give a one-step target-volume experiment without a
long coherence requirement. For a fixed Hamiltonian, the theorem also gives
an exact feasibility certificate:
\((Q+1)^{d-c(G)}\Delta t\leq\tau_{\mathrm{coh}}\).
Thus a proposed device either lies inside the proven observation window or it
does not; hardware parameters are not hidden in an asymptotic claim. The
deterministic graph and both normalized-probe inequalities remain falsifiable
within the calibrated closed-unitary model.
The far-reaching implication is conceptual: recurrence is controlled by
distributed relational coherence, not by dimension, entropy, state spectrum,
initial speed, or quantum Fisher information alone.

We have deliberately excluded claims about cosmology, black holes, information
loss, or an operational communication code. They do not follow from the
hypotheses. The manuscript reports a finite mathematical law, genuine
summable countable recurrence, an exact pure-point observer boundary, an
explicit equal-information qutrit separation and a direct experimental
protocol.
