# Antagonist referee report

Paper: *The orientation-carrier boundary of the Riemann theta source*

Review standard: skeptical referee at a reputable broad mathematics journal, with separate
top-tier/general-science significance check.

## Recommendation

**Broad mathematics journal: publishable after the revisions recorded below.**

**Nature/Science or top-four mathematics journal: reject on significance, not correctness.**

The paper does not prove RH and does not rule out Hilbert–Pólya, de Branges, CCM, or Hedenmalm's
finite zero-eigenfunction construction in full generality. Its real result is narrower and
defensible: it completely classifies a natural source-core carrier hierarchy for the new
Hedenmalm theta program, proves that every nonzero regular ordinary-`L²` carrier in that class
collapses, and shows that the canonical unsquared Julia alternative has the wrong polarity for
zero exclusion.

That is timely, nontrivial structural progress. It is not a headline solution of RH.

## Strongest reason to publish

The source-specific collision is clean and surprising:

1. The unsquared theta normal current has exactly the desired zero-excluding polarity and measures
   the full horizontal divisor width.
2. Every positive, momentum-symmetric, Schwartz-continuous source-core form annihilating the theta
   source is forced onto the real Xi divisor.
3. The same source is cyclic under real translations in ordinary `L²`, so every nonzero such form
   has dense proper nonclosed radical and is nonclosable.
4. Therefore no form in this complete regular source-core class can be the positive-definite
   Hilbert metric sought for the full theta-operator image.
5. Unsquared Julia–Ward positivity does survive, but with defect-counting polarity: an off-line
   quartet creates positive derivative and `8π` mass.
6. Hypertranscendental aperture geometry is maximally ranked yet cannot see arbitrary quartet
   multiplicity; only the singular noncompact marked boundary recovers it.

The abstract cyclic-null collapse proposition makes the topological obstruction universal. The
Riemann theta theorem is a sharp specialization because `Fourier(k)=Xi` simultaneously forces
divisor support and ordinary-`L²` cyclicity.

## Principal objections and dispositions

### 1. “The phase law is only another RH equivalence.”

**Objection sustained in part.** Horizontal xi monotonicity is prior art. The paper now gives it no
standalone novelty credit. The contribution is the exact quantitative width law for every
aperture, its realization as the actual theta autocorrelation current, and its role in the
complete carrier classification.

### 2. “The Julia normal derivative finally supplies the missing sign.”

**Objection rejected mathematically.** The paper computes the unsquared derivative before divisor
squaring. Its sign is positive for every inserted off-line quartet. It therefore counts the
unwanted defect rather than excluding it. This directly answers the motivating question; no
vanishing theorem is insinuated.

### 3. “The `8π` index still has no upper bound.”

**Objection accepted and converted into a theorem.** No upper bound or vanishing is claimed.
Instead, exact symmetric surgeries show arbitrary charge `8πN`, identical continuum aperture
matroid for every `N`, and bounded-window escape at fixed total mass. Thus the paper proves why the
index cannot become a zero-exclusion mechanism through the tested compact/local/differential
carriers.

### 4. “The Hilbert no-go is overclaimed.”

**Resolved.** Scope is exact: jointly Schwartz-continuous, nonnegative, momentum-symmetric forms on
the translation-stable source core, and their ordinary-`L²` closability. Forms defined only on a
finite zero-eigenfunction span, de Branges spaces, global trace formulas, and other singular
nonlocal constructions remain outside the theorem. The manuscript does not claim a universal
Hilbert–Pólya no-go.

### 5. “Hypertranscendence is decorative.”

**Resolved sufficiently, but still the weakest narrative layer.** The paper uses the
hypertranscendental family for one precise negative result: even the maximal pointed-collision
differential matroid of all real apertures is invariant under arbitrary off-line divisor charge.
This is not presented as evidence for RH. Its role is to prove that differential richness is
orthogonal to the missing orientation charge.

### 6. “Polynomial quartet surgery and compact-open instability are old.”

**Resolved.** Gauthier–Xarles receives explicit priority. No novelty is assigned to symmetric
polynomial insertion or all-jet compact-open convergence. Novelty rests on coupling that surgery
to the real-aperture matroid, exact Ward mass, polarity, and source-core carrier collapse.

### 7. “Proofs are sketches rather than journal proofs.”

**Resolved to submission standard.** The manuscript now states the prime-tail Ostrowski criterion,
gives its coefficient mechanism and primary citations, makes the archimedean subtraction explicit,
gives a complete cyclic-null proof, and supplies the weighted polynomial-density argument for the
Hankel converse. A specialist editor may still request an appendix expanding the standard
Ostrowski criterion, but no central inference is left as an unexplained slogan.

## Final editorial judgment

The paper is strongest when read as an **orientation-carrier classification**, not as an RH
criterion paper. The width theorem shows the desired sign exists before scalarization. The
cyclic-null theorem proves why the natural regular Hilbert realization fails. Julia–Ward analysis
proves why the obvious unsquared replacement has the opposite polarity. Hypertranscendental
geometry then proves that maximal local/differential information still misses the charge, while
the noncompact marked boundary retains it.

That narrative is surprising, closed, and useful to researchers evaluating the new theta-spectral
route. Any abstract, title, or cover letter claiming more should be rejected.
