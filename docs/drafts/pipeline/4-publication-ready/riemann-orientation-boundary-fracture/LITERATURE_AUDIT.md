# Literature and novelty audit

Audit date: 2026-07-28

Scope: theta/Hilbert--Pólya orientation, horizontal xi monotonicity, functional-equation-preserving
off-line perturbations, differential algebra of Dirichlet series, logarithmic-difference rigidity,
finite spectral factorization, and Ward/divisor charge.

## Result

Narrow defensible novelty:

> One orientation-carrier classification for the actual Riemann theta source: (i) quantitative
> theta-current/Hankel threshold equal to horizontal xi-divisor width; (ii) impossibility of every
> entire diagonal carrier and every finite constant-coefficient local autocorrelation Gram frame;
> (iii) complete classification of all nonnegative momentum-symmetric Schwartz source-core forms
> as positive real-divisor measures, with dense nonclosed radical and forced nonclosability;
> (iv) canonical unsquared Julia positivity has defect-counting, not zero-excluding, polarity; and
> (v) the full continuum real-aperture differential matroid is blind to arbitrary inserted quartet
> charge, while a source-marked noncompact Ward boundary recovers that charge and exhibits escape.

No checked source states this theorem package. No “first” claim is made in the paper.

## Closest prior work and exact overlap

### Hedenmalm, 2026

H. Hedenmalm, *Spectral interpretation of Riemann zeta zeros*,
[arXiv:2606.17494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17494).

- Constructs the theta differential eigenproblem.
- Explicitly says the eigenvalue interpretation is not yet a single self-adjoint operator and
  searches for a suitable sesquilinear form.
- Conditional self-adjointness plus a Hilbert-space inner product would force all Xi roots real.
- Does not state an aperture-current width phase law.
- Does not classify entire diagonal multipliers or finite differential autocorrelation frames.
- Does not contain quartet-matroid blindness or Ward escape.

Paper contribution relative to Hedenmalm: answers a carrier question adjacent to his missing
sesquilinear form. It proves a finite local/entire carrier impossible; classifies every positive
translation-stable Schwartz source-core extension; proves every nonzero member is nonclosable and
cannot have exactly the theta source line as radical; and identifies the divisor-singular
noncompact mode that survives.

### Bochner--Schwartz, Wiener, and cyclic-null collapse

L. Schwartz, *Théorie des distributions*, Hermann, 1966.

N. Wiener, *Tauberian theorems*, Ann. of Math. 33 (1932), 1--100.

- Positive translation-invariant tempered kernels are classically represented by positive
  temperate Fourier measures.
- A vector with almost-everywhere nonvanishing Fourier transform is classically cyclic for real
  translations in ordinary `L²`.
- The abstract cyclic-null singular-sequence argument is elementary and receives no standalone
  priority claim.
- The source-specific result not found in checked literature is the collision of these facts at
  Hedenmalm's actual theta source: nullity forces spectral support onto the real Xi divisor, while
  the same divisor is Lebesgue-null and therefore makes the source translation-cyclic. This yields
  the exact measure classification, dense proper radical, nonclosability, and source-line-radical
  obstruction.

### Gauthier--Xarles, 2009

P. M. Gauthier and X. Xarles, *Perturbations of L-functions with or without non-trivial zeros off
the critical line*, [arXiv:0911.5135](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.5135).

Strong overlap:

- RH-failing functions are open dense in broad FE-preserving function spaces.
- Their proof uses a symmetric polynomial
  `p(s) conjugate(p(conjugate s)) p(1-s) conjugate(p(1-conjugate s))`
  to insert an off-line orbit while approximating on a compact set.
- They also preserve real symmetry when present.
- Hence symmetric polynomial quartet insertion and compact-open instability are old and receive no
  novelty credit here.

What remains outside their result:

- every holomorphic jet along one explicit normalized escaping family;
- exact Ward density, `8 pi N` charge, and bounded-window escape;
- exact continuum real-aperture differential matroid collision for every `N`;
- no matroid charge classifier;
- marked source boundary injectivity and common-mode augmentation kernel;
- coupling to the actual theta-current carrier obstruction.

The first item alone is a standard consequence of holomorphic compact-open convergence and also
receives no standalone novelty credit.

### Julia/Ward polarity

- the theta normal current has zero-excluding polarity and an exact divisor-width threshold;
- the canonical unsquared Julia boundary defect is already nonnegative for each inserted off-line

The paper therefore does not treat unsquaring as a proof of RH. Its contribution is the exact

## Honest significance boundary

The theorem package is stronger than any one constituent:

- phase law gives a quantitative object rather than a decorative RH endpoint;
- local-carrier theorem closes a concrete class in Hedenmalm's theta program;
- global source-core theorem closes every nonnegative momentum-symmetric Schwartz extension and
  proves why the surviving divisor-sampling forms cannot be ordinary-Hilbert metrics;
- polarity theorem answers the unsquared-Julia question: its positive sign counts the unwanted
  defect rather than excluding it;
- continuum matroid collision proves maximal differential richness can still be charge-blind;
- marked Ward boundary gives the positive replacement and exact escape mechanism.

Remaining risk: a specialist may regard the phase law as a repackaging of horizontal monotonicity,
the local obstruction as an elementary residue argument, the global collapse as a short collision
of classical harmonic-analysis theorems, and the matroid/Ward layer as too synthetic. Publication
case therefore rests on the completeness and direct timeliness of the carrier classification, not
on any individual identity.

Honest venue boundary: this is a plausible paper for a reputable broad mathematics journal because
it resolves a concrete structural question in a new theta-spectral program and supplies an exact
universal obstruction. It is not an RH proof, not a famous-route impossibility theorem, and not
credible as a Nature/Science or top-four mathematics claim on present evidence.
