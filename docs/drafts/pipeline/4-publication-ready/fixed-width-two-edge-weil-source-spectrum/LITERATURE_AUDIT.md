# Current literature and novelty audit

Finite-Factor Orbits and One-Sequence Xi-Divisor Tomography**

Audit date: 2026-07-27

Standard: exact-result collision search, not keyword novelty. Formal verification receives zero

## Claimed new package

Priority is limited to the conjunction of:

1. a carrier-independent cofinite completeness theorem for reflected derivative-Lambert
   frequencies under translated sector-Blaschke divergence;
2. a dense nonlinear orbit generated only by arbitrarily small pole-free reflected local-factor
   modifications;
3. triviality of the continuous Hausdorff quotient on the analytic odd Schwarz-real germ fibre;
4. prime-log specialization after arbitrary finite prime deletion;
5. incompatibility of that quotient with an RH-equivalent source-Hankel sign;
6. one predetermined positive-width two-edge profile whose logarithmic integer samples recover
   every off-critical Xi zero and analytic multiplicity;
7. exact finite prime--Gamma evaluation of every admissible sample;
8. exact false-RH two-sided power, negative-abscissa, and cumulative negative-mass spectra;
9. individual completeness for every profile in a dense residual smooth class.

No novelty is claimed for Blaschke's condition, Möbius inversion, Weil positivity, Guinand--Weil
explicit formulae, Landau's theorem, Baire category, Hankel criteria, or shifted-zeta poles in
isolation.

## Current-source verification

- Suzuki 2026 explicitly presents a unified screw-function framework and a conjectural
  self-adjoint operator limit; the paper states its results are unconditional.
- CCM explicitly describes rigorous finite-prime self-adjoint operators and says convergence of
  their spectra/determinants would establish RH.
- Groskin explicitly proves a finite Galerkin dictionary and an archimedean tail certificate,
  with numerical verification separate from the exact theorem.
- Poltoratski's Annals paper concerns exponential type of a measure and pure exponential
  completeness.

These descriptions match the boundaries stated in the manuscript.

## Priority-risk assessment

- **Local reflected-Lambert theorem:** medium risk. Ingredients are classical and the proof is
  short enough that an equivalent theorem may exist under different terminology.
- **Fixed-width polar tomography:** medium risk. Shifted-zeta carrier is elementary once found,
  but the exact one-profile/multiplicity/cofinal-oscillation package was not located.
- **Combined local-to-global dichotomy:** lower collision risk. No source found pairing a
  cofinite local-factor quotient collapse with an explicit one-sequence global survivor.

The manuscript therefore uses “to the best of our knowledge” boundaries and claims only the exact
package above.
