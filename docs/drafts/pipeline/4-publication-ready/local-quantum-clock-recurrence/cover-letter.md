Dear Editors,

Please consider the Article “One quantum return record permits every future
return probability.”

The manuscript determines an exact limit on forecasting quantum return from
one finite-duration record. For every positive target time, every strictly
shorter continuous observation window, every positive precision and every
finite energy-moment order, one fixed pure state and fixed return projector
admit an isospectral family of finite nonnegative Hamiltonians whose return
histories all fit one record. At the target, probabilities fill the entire
interval from exact orthogonality to perfect revival. The fixed state also
has identical prescribed energy moments. A Vandermonde converse proves
minimum spectral support, and every forecasting rule incurs terminal error
at least one half on one compatible endpoint. Every terminal probability
has an exact nearest-neighbour one-particle representative, so dense-matrix
freedom is not needed. At perfect return, the representative is an active
Jacobi block plus an uncoupled diagonal spectator block. A converse rigidity
theorem proves that every tridiagonal fixed-protocol realization of that
endpoint must have at least one zero path coupling, while every
less-than-perfect terminal probability has a connected representative.
It embeds as the one-excitation block of a particle-number-preserving
nearest-neighbour occupation Hamiltonian. Canonical second quantization
then proves one complete common subset-sum spectrum in every fermion-number
sector. In the dual varying-preparation protocol, the complete ambiguity is
realized by one fixed uniform onsite qubit field, invariant under every
permutation of the qubits, and symmetric pure preparations.

The closest work is Manos, Weilenmann and Navascués, *Timed demolition
measurements* (arXiv:2507.06912), which establishes severe extrapolation
instability and “fog-bank” datasets for general unknown quantum measurements.
We do not claim generic extrapolation impossibility as new. Distinction here
is a complete and sharp law for survival probability under a fixed
preparation and measurement: continuous full-window agreement, one exactly
common spectrum, arbitrary finite fixed-state moment matching, every exact
terminal return probability, nearest-neighbour realizations throughout the
closed interval, all-sector free-fermion isospectrality, and minimum
spectral support. A dual representation uses one Hamiltonian and different
pure preparations; in fact, that Hamiltonian is a uniform onsite qubit
field and the preparations are permutation-invariant.

This boundary is relevant to recurrence experiments, survival-amplitude
zeros, dynamical quantum phase-transition diagnostics and certified quantum
forecasting. Manuscript distinguishes a finite survival zero from a
thermodynamic transition and makes no cosmological or indeterministic claim.

Work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has no associated
datasets, funding conflict or competing interest.
