# Hostile Nature editorial and referee review

Date: 28 July 2026  
Recommended decision: **Decline without external review**

## Editorial assessment

Nature asks whether a result is original, of outstanding scientific
importance, interesting across disciplines, arresting, and likely to have
immediate and far-reaching implications. Mathematical correctness is not
the limiting issue. The revised result is substantially stronger than the
original submission: preparation and projector are fixed, all candidate
Hamiltonians have one exact spectrum, and every terminal probability in
the closed interval has a nearest-neighbour-chain representative. Perfect
return uses an active Jacobi block plus an uncoupled diagonal spectator
block, and a converse proves that every tridiagonal fixed-protocol
realization of that endpoint must contain a zero path coupling, while every
strictly subunit probability has a connected Krylov representative. The
construction embeds in a particle-number-preserving occupation
model, and canonical second quantization proves a common complete spectrum
in every fermion sector. A dual construction puts the entire ambiguity into
symmetric preparations under one fixed uniform onsite qubit field invariant
under every permutation of sites. The central sentence is genuinely
arresting. The work still falls below Nature's general-interest significance
threshold.

## What now survives hostile technical review

1. Quantifiers cover every \(T>0\), every strict horizon \(H<T\), every
   \(\delta>0\), every finite moment order and every terminal probability.
2. One normalized state and one return projector are fixed across all
   candidates.
3. Hamiltonians are finite, nonnegative, self-adjoint, unitarily equivalent
   and have the same spectrum including multiplicities.
4. Agreement is uniform on the complete interval, not only at sample times.
5. Every \(p\in[0,1]\) has an exact tridiagonal nearest-neighbour
   one-particle realization. For \(p<1\), a connected representative exists.
   At \(p=1\), active and spectator blocks are disconnected, and every
   tridiagonal fixed-protocol realization at that endpoint is necessarily
   disconnected.
6. The tridiagonal generator is an exact one-excitation block of a
   Hermitian particle-number-preserving nearest-neighbour occupation matrix.
7. Intrinsic second quantization has a complete Slater eigenbasis and one
   common subset-sum spectrum at every fermion number, including \(p=1\).
8. Sine-power hiding, moment matching, the one-half minimax consequence and
   the common-support lower bound are exact.
9. In the dual protocol, one fixed uniform onsite qubit field, invariant
   under every site permutation, and symmetric pure states realize every
   terminal probability.
10. The manuscript separates its theorem from full tomography,
   indeterminism, thermodynamic phase transitions and cosmology.

The former fatal objection—candidate-dependent preparation and
measurement—has been closed.

## Remaining rejection grounds

### 1. Closest work still absorbs broad conceptual priority

*Timed demolition measurements* already establishes extreme quantum
extrapolation instability, complete future unpredictability through fog
banks, continua of future distributions, and binomial/power hard
instances. Fixed protocol, identical spectrum, survival probability,
continuous-window agreement and nearest-neighbour representatives are
meaningful new constraints. An editor can nevertheless reasonably view
them as a sharp specialization and refinement, not a field-changing
discovery.

### 2. The fixed-preparation extension remains free and inhomogeneous

The former one-particle-only gap is partly closed: an explicit
particle-number-preserving occupation embedding exists, and intrinsic
second quantization proves common complete spectra in every fermion sector.
The recurrence experiment itself remains in the one-excitation sector. The
fixed-preparation models are noninteracting and inhomogeneous; couplings
depend on target probability through a Krylov/Jacobi construction. The dual
protocol proves that inhomogeneity is not intrinsic if preparation may vary:
one fixed uniform onsite field and symmetric states suffice. This shifts
rather than removes the engineering burden. Nothing is proved for an
interacting family, a quantum field theory or a thermodynamic limit. A
many-body referee can still view both extensions as lifts of finite
inverse-spectral data rather than a new collective phenomenon.

### 3. The endpoint boundary is sharp but narrow

The former missing endpoint is closed: \(p=1\) has an exact tridiagonal
representative. It is a direct sum of an active Jacobi chain and diagonal
spectator sites, with a zero interface coupling. This satisfies
nearest-neighbour locality but not connected-chain locality. The converse
answers the natural objection: because an irreducible tridiagonal path has
a cyclic endpoint while the parity state omits every even spectral mode, at
least one path coupling must vanish in every tridiagonal endpoint
realization. This is a complete sharp boundary for the stated endpoint, not
a remaining mathematical gap. The positive side is sharp as well: every
\(p<1\) has an irreducible Krylov path. The dichotomy does not by itself
create an experimental or collective phenomenon.

### 4. Adversarial resources diverge

The construction uses dimension \(n+1\) and bandwidth \(\pi n/T\).
Required \(n\) grows when the error shrinks or the observation horizon
approaches the target. The theorem proves lack of a uniform estimator over
an expanding model class. It does not prove instability at fixed dimension,
fixed bandwidth, fixed coupling precision or fixed experimental resources.

### 5. No experimental or predictive consequence

No device realizes two members of the family; no synthesis tolerances,
shots, noise, decoherence or readout analysis are supplied. No existing
Loschmidt-zero, recurrence or forecasting experiment is shown to cross the
derived ambiguity boundary. The paper predicts no new observable value.
Nature generally needs more than an exact adversarial construction for an
immediate-impact claim.

### 6. Mathematical mechanism may be judged too elementary

Alternating binomial cancellation, convex interpolation, unitary transport,
Krylov tridiagonalization and Vandermonde separation are classical
ingredients. Their conjunction is elegant and appears new, but a hostile
referee may judge the intellectual step insufficient for a general
scientific journal unless it unlocks a concrete physical consequence.

### 7. Sharpness is narrower than the main headline

The \(n+1\)-level result is optimal for matching \(n\) moments on a common
set of distinct nodes. It is not an optimal
dimension--bandwidth--precision law for full-window forecasting and does
not lower-bound synthesis or measurement cost.

## Questions a referee would demand answered

1. Does exact complete ambiguity survive with one fixed preparation in a
   translationally invariant, fixed-coupling or interacting \(k\)-local
   Hamiltonian family?
2. Can the sharp endpoint disconnection be measured as a finite-resource
   diagnostic rather than imposed as exact algebraic structure?
3. What is the optimal resource tradeoff among horizon, precision,
   dimension and bandwidth?
4. Which real experiment or published forecast changes conclusion because
   of this theorem?
5. Which necessary and sufficient prior restores stable survival
   forecasting?
6. What proposition is unavailable from the 2026 fog-bank framework even
   after specializing its observable and model?
7. Are the engineered couplings robust to finite preparation, Hamiltonian
   and measurement errors?
8. In the uniform-field dual protocol, what resources and tolerances are
   required to prepare the symmetric states?

## What would justify reconsideration

At least one major scientific advance is still needed:

1. an exact interacting realization or a homogeneous fixed-preparation
   realization;
2. a matched optimal resource law at fixed physically meaningful
   constraints;
3. experimental observation of two indistinguishable early histories with
   separated later returns, including a noise and shot analysis;
4. a concrete reanalysis that invalidates or materially changes a published
   recurrence or dynamical-transition inference; or
5. a necessary-and-sufficient stability theorem that clearly exceeds the
   fog-bank framework.

Stronger cosmological language, black-hole analogies or a more dramatic
title would not close these gaps.

## Final judgment

Paper is mathematically strong, operationally cleaner than the closest
known collision, and now has genuine all-sector free-fermion and homogeneous
uniform-field statements in two distinct protocols. It is plausibly
suitable for a specialist high-level mathematical-physics or
quantum-information venue after independent priority review. It is not
presently ready for Nature. Honest antagonistic review therefore does
**not** confirm completion of requested goal.
