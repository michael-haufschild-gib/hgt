## Novelty boundary

Not novel and not claimed as novel:

- qualitative finite quantum recurrence;
- Kronecker--Weyl equidistribution;
- Dirichlet simultaneous approximation;
- Kleinbock--Margulis extremality for nondegenerate analytic manifolds;
- multinomial replica expansions and exact moment identities under independence assumptions;
- cyclotomic classification of exact recurrences in Floquet systems;
- random-Hamiltonian generic recurrence saturation;
- nondegenerate gaps almost everywhere in local families;
- rationally independent single-particle spectra in local free-fermion models.

Defensible novelty residue:

> In the full deterministic traceless onsite-plus-nearest-neighbour Pauli family on an open qubit path, every connected open simple-spectrum chart is locally generic for one same-Hamiltonian conjunction: maximal projective spectral rank, sharp `D-1` stroboscopic and `D-2` clock-normalized first-hit exponents, and all-order positive-weight collision/moment floors. The proof is driven by real affine spectral escape of the exact bounded-degree local family.

Broader significance:

- closes locality-versus-recurrence-codimension question for a canonical static interacting 1D family;
- shows linearly many local coefficients can generate the full `q^n-1` rational projective phase rank;
- converts same invariant into sharp returns, all-order resonances, exact completed density, and thermodynamic extinction without adding unrelated subjects.

## Claims intentionally excluded

- unrestricted continuous-time first-return distribution;
- quantitative finite-time convergence rate to completed density;
- infinite-volume global orbit recurrence;
- circuit complexity, loss, black holes, channels, finite-record inference, randomization, or chaos;
- claim that every local Hamiltonian is maximally projective;
- claim that component tools themselves are new.
