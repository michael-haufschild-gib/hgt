## Remaining limitations a reviewer may reasonably press

- Exact exponent is at fixed finite size as tolerance tends to zero. Onset constants are not controlled uniformly in system size, observer weights or Hamiltonian.
- Full operator displacement is stronger information than one measured expectation. A theorem for an arbitrary fixed local scalar probe would require additional work.
- The local family is the full real Hermitian span. Restricted Hamiltonian classes require their own nondegeneracy theorem.
- The physical witness proof is technical. The manuscript gives the seed, response packet, simultaneous polynomial avoidance, probe inheritance and locality transport; the separate exact claim trace provides the detailed formal dependencies.
- The stationary scaling/interval result is close to classical Wiener and compact-group theory. It improves conceptual coverage but is not advertised as a stand-alone breakthrough.
- No exact equivalent was found in the searched literature. This is not an independent priority certification or a guarantee of acceptance.
