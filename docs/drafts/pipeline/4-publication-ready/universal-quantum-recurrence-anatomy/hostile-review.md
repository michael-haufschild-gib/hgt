# Hostile editorial and referee review

Manuscript: **A universal law of quantum recurrence**  
Review date: 29 July 2026  
Standard: Nature editorial criteria plus a hostile mathematical-physics
referee.

## Executive verdict

**Mathematical validity:** pass, subject to independent checking.  
**Scientific coherence:** pass.  
**Specialist-journal threshold:** clear pass.  
**Nature send-to-review threshold:** warranted, but acceptance cannot be
certified.

Combining the two source papers is materially better. The former
rate--distortion paper answered how long finite recurrent packets can take,
but its significance was limited by worst-spectrum framing, finite-packet
scope and an additive dimension-linear remainder. The former
global-completion paper answered whether recurrence is locally decidable, but
without the quantitative clock it looked thin and predominantly negative.

The master paper now proves one hierarchy:

```text
persistent spectral mass       -> whether and how much can return
surviving spectral weights     -> when a finite packet must return
observer coherence graph       -> what return is visible
global gap collisions          -> every long-time return statistic
global spectral completion     -> what local/perturbative data cannot decide
```

This is a stronger scientific object than either manuscript. The collision
hierarchy is the positive completion of the non-identifiability theorem, and
the finite clock is the operational specialization of the global law. The
paper therefore no longer reads as a no-go theorem or a collection of
recurrence estimates.

## Principal attacks

### 1. “This is a collage of several old papers”

This is the most dangerous objection.

**Attack.** Finite contractions, finite pure-state packets, observer matrix
families and arbitrary probability measures are different mathematical
categories. A paper that lists theorems for each may be broad but not unified.

**Disposition.** The reduction is exact. Every finite contraction decomposes
into a transient plus a persistent finite phase packet. The packet's weights
enter the water clock. An observer quotients its relative phases through a
coherence graph. The completed spectral measure produces the gap law whose
collision hierarchy determines the long-time distribution. The
moment-indeterminacy theorem pinpoints where polynomial local information
stops in precisely this chain. Figure 1 and Theorem 1 make the dependency
structure explicit.

**Remaining risk.** The arbitrary-measure theorem is a completion of the
finite chain, not a finite-deadline theorem for continuous spectra. The
manuscript must retain this distinction.

### 2. “Universal” is overstated

**Attack.** The paper has no finite recurrence deadline for arbitrary
continuous spectra, nonlinear gain models, thermodynamic limits or gravity.

**Disposition.** The title refers to a universal classification hierarchy,
not one formula with one domain. Every finite contraction is classified by
the threshold law; every finite packet by the clock; every observer family by
the graph; every probability spectrum by the long-time collision law.
Continuous spectra are not omitted: atomless spectra receive a sharp
zero-density phase theorem.

**Rejection condition.** Reject if the abstract or publicity text says that
every quantum system returns in finite time or that one universal recurrence
time formula covers continuous spectra.

### 3. The timing theorem remains minimax

**Attack.** \(T_w(\eta)\) is the least deadline valid for every compatible
phase spectrum, not the first recurrence time of a given Hamiltonian.
Arithmetic spectra may return much earlier.

**Disposition.** Correct and explicit. Minimax is the only spectrum-uniform
question determined by weights alone. The lower bound has deterministic
avoiding spectra, so the clock is not merely a loose construction.

**Remaining risk.** An editor may prefer typical many-body scaling. The paper
does not provide it.

### 4. The dimension-linear remainder is not removed

**Attack.** When \(\mathcal H_{\rm proj}=O(r)\), the additive \(O(r)\)
constants can match the leading term. The phrase “rate--distortion law” may
sound sharper than the theorem.

**Disposition.** All constants are displayed. The paper states the
constant-free consequence only when
\(\mathcal H_{\rm proj}/r\to\infty\). The exact target volume is also bounded
on both sides by the same water functional. No equality or
dimension-independent remainder is claimed.

**Remaining risk.** A sharp circular large-deviation formula at moderate
information would be a major improvement but is not needed for validity.

### 5. Gaussian rate--distortion is only a formal analogy

**Attack.** There is no random source, encoder, mutual information or
reconstruction channel.

**Disposition.** The manuscript claims equality of finite convex programs,
not an operational coding theorem. The optimizer is useful because the same
allocation controls exact phase-target volume and minimax recurrence.

**Rejection condition.** Reject if source-coding performance, compression
rates or diffusion-code conclusions are attributed to the theorem.

### 6. “What an observer sees” uses Hilbert--Schmidt geometry

**Attack.** Hilbert--Schmidt distance may not match an experimental
measurement norm, and conversion can scale with dimension.

**Disposition.** Both Hilbert--Schmidt-normalized and operator-normalized
self-adjoint expectation probes are bounded. The latter pays an explicit
\(\sqrt d\) factor, which is attained for unrestricted matrix pairings. The
paper does not hide this cost.

### 7. Countably pure-point versus atomless is not a complete Lebesgue
classification

**Attack.** Mixed atomic/continuous and singular-continuous measures are not
assigned one simple phase label.

**Disposition.** They are covered by the arbitrary-spectrum collision
hierarchy, which is stronger than a binary label. The paper gives clean
corollaries for pure-point and atomless extremes without asserting that they
exhaust all measures.

### 8. The local-data theorem may be misunderstood

**Attack.** “Complete local dynamics” could be read as the complete local
observable algebra or all possible probes. The theorem concerns the
polynomial Krylov Gram and energy-multiplication tensors of one cyclic rule.

**Disposition.** The manuscript consistently says “complete polynomial local
dynamics” and identifies inequivalent global Hilbert-space completions. It
does not call the atomless measure an ordinary self-adjoint extension on the
same minimal Jacobi closure.

**Rejection condition.** Reject if this qualification is removed.

### 9. Moment indeterminacy and Wiener’s theorem are classical

**Attack.** The ingredients are classical; the result may be synthesis rather
than a discovery.

**Disposition.** Classical parents are cited. Novelty is not moment
indeterminacy, one-power Wiener averaging, graph coboundary rank or
reverse-water filling separately. The candidate new result is their joined
recurrence anatomy, the arbitrary-classifier separation at complete local
depth, the all-order collision closure, and the exact compact/nonperturbative
boundary.

**Remaining risk.** Only independent literature experts can settle priority.
The paper must not claim that database search proves novelty.

### 10. Black-hole relevance may be inflated

**Attack.** A fixed spectral-measure theorem does not derive a black-hole
spectrum, solve information loss or prove a gravitational effect.

**Disposition.** The manuscript makes one exact inference claim:
all-orders perturbative SFF data cannot universally certify a positive exact
plateau. This matters because black-hole models use SFFs as late-time
diagnostics. The paper explicitly says it is not a black-hole solution.

**Rejection condition.** Reject if publicity says the information paradox is
solved or that real black holes realize the constructed pair.

### 11. No experimental data

**Attack.** Nature may desk-reject a theorem without data, especially when
device horizons can be exponentially long.

**Disposition.** Nature publishes exceptional foundational theory, but this
is editorial rather than logical. The paper gives an exact one-tick
onsite-field ensemble transfer, fixed-device falsification inequalities and
a coherence-window certificate. It does not pretend these replace an
experiment.

**Remaining risk.** This is now the largest non-mathematical acceptance risk.

## Claim-by-claim rejection triggers

Reject if any of the following occurs:

1. a mathematical statement lacks an exact proof trace;
2. the finite deadline is described as typical or as a fixed Hamiltonian's
   first return;
3. the \(O(r)\) timing or target-information remainder is hidden;
4. Gaussian rate--distortion is promoted to an operational code;
5. Haar measure is called a universal physical spectrum ensemble;
6. every completely positive trace-preserving channel is claimed to be
   Hilbert--Schmidt contractive;
7. atomless zero-density return is called absence of every rare return;
8. the pure-point and atomless corollaries are claimed to exhaust arbitrary
   spectral measures;
9. polynomial local Krylov data are called the complete local observable
   algebra;
10. perturbative SFF non-identifiability is called a solution of black-hole
    information loss;
11. formal verification is used as novelty or significance evidence;
12. literature search is represented as proof of priority.

The current manuscript avoids all twelve.

## Final hostile conclusion

The merger is scientifically justified and substantially stronger than the
two source papers. It converts one paper's quantitative but narrow clock and
the other's broad but thin no-go into a positive universal classification:
threshold, deadline, observer quotient, collision distribution and
determinacy boundary.

An honest hostile reviewer would recommend external review. A specialist
referee should not reject it for correctness, scope concealment or lack of a
single organizing theorem. Nature rejection could still occur because the
work is theory-only, because an editor regards the synthesis of classical
parents as insufficiently transformative, or because independent priority
search finds an equivalent joined theorem. Those risks cannot be eliminated
by wording. They are now the real risks rather than defects hidden inside the
manuscript.
