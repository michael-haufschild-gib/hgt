# Hostile review: autonomous quantum complexity programme

**Review date:** 4 August 2026  
**Target:** Nature editorial screen plus hostile mathematical-physics review  
**Current verdict:** reject as a paper; continue as a research programme

## What has now been proved

For every finite nonempty connected qubit interaction graph, the nine literal
weight-two collective-Pauli commutator squares on each edge act exactly on the
global sitewise identity/swap sector. Their common physical kernel is exactly
the span of global identity and global replica swap. The coefficient model is
embedded injectively in the physical two-replica matrix algebra by an explicit
left inverse, so the result is not a surrogate or dimension-count inference.

This closes a genuine algebraic gap in the two-replica cavity route. It does
not close the paper's scientific question.

## Editorial verdict

The proposed headline—ordinary autonomous local quantum matter cannot
computationally skip its future—would be arresting and broadly important if
proved. The present results do not prove it. A Nature editor would reject any
manuscript assembled now because its main physical claims remain conjectural:

1. no matching lower bound on robust state or operator circuit complexity;
2. no proof of linear growth up to exponential saturation;
3. no proof that maximal complexity occupies asymptotic density one;
4. no matching doubly exponential first return to low complexity;
5. no completed circuit-union anti-concentration theorem for a static local
   Hamiltonian ensemble;
6. no theorem transferring the algebraic cavity result to a local-Hamiltonian
   ensemble with explicit probability and time quantifiers;
7. no black-hole, holographic or gravitational model to which the result has
   been transferred.

The old weak-coupling recurrence manuscript must not absorb this result. That
would produce a collage of correct theorems without one causal spine.

## Strongest technical attack

The connected-graph common-kernel theorem is weaker than Hall expansion. A
positive Casimir with trivial common kernel can still collapse a plane to a
line under its generating family. Therefore the new theorem cannot be used to
claim a full matrix pencil, operator scaling, semicircle regularity or
anti-concentration.

The exact next algebraic theorem must establish the full mixed-subspace Hall
inequality for the weight-at-most-two collective Pauli family. Existing
results separately control nonzero paired-Pauli characters and the
identity/swap sector. The missing bridge is:

- product-Clifford reducing decomposition of the canonical greatest Hall
  obstruction;
- an injective visible quotient detector for the zero-twirl anisotropic
  sector modulo occupied nonzero characters;
- injective quadratic recovery on the hidden identity/swap kernel using the
  now-literal connected edge Casimir.

Until this theorem is proved, all claims of matrix-semicircle fullness are
premature. Even after it is proved at two replicas, the growing-replica Hall
law, planar resummation, circuit selector and circuit union remain open.

## Novelty attack

No novelty credit should be assigned yet to the physical edge-Casimir theorem.
Its exact global arbitrary-register formulation and explicit coefficient
recovery may be new, but finite-group commutants, swap algebras and connected
ferromagnetic synchronization are established themes. External priority must
compare the exact quantified theorem, not keywords. The theorem currently
earns correctness credit only and serves as internal infrastructure.

## Nature rejection triggers

Reject immediately if a manuscript:

1. calls the connected common-kernel theorem a complexity-growth theorem;
2. substitutes Casimir positivity for the Hall quantifier;
3. calls product-Clifford covariance Haar randomness;
4. infers circuit anti-concentration from a two-replica calculation;
5. extrapolates finite-register algebra to thermodynamic limits;
6. claims Brown--Susskind growth, black-hole interior growth or holography;
7. describes formal verification as novelty or significance;
8. hides unresolved probability, circuit model, time-window or approximation
   quantifiers in prose.

## Honest acceptance condition

A defensible Nature submission requires one dependency-closed theorem for an
ordinary time-independent fixed-locality Hamiltonian ensemble proving, on one
explicit high-probability event, complexity growth, plateau measure, low-
complexity recurrence scale and permanent non-Haar locality distinction. The
current theorem is one algebraic lemma on that route, not the result itself.

## 14 August 2026 hostile addendum

The new selector-partition observer-channel law is a real frontier correction, not a Nature result.
It proves that the programme's later proposed scalar recurrence-energy closure was impossible:
fresh target channels saturate the exact positive inequality through diagonal norm even when all
off-diagonal recurrence disappears. The target-specific two-memory capstone removes uniform target
averaging and inverse-dictionary attenuation, while retaining only same-channel stationary pairs.

This improves mathematical honesty and prevents a false proof architecture. It does not satisfy
the acceptance condition above. A hostile reviewer still demands a quenched growing-system theorem
for one ordinary time-independent local-Hamiltonian ensemble that produces occupied observer-
resolution channels beyond complete shallow-circuit capacity, controls same-channel reuse, and
derives the claimed growth, plateau-measure and low-complexity recurrence scales on one event.
Without that theorem, the manuscript remains rejected and must not claim black-hole interior or
holographic consequences.
