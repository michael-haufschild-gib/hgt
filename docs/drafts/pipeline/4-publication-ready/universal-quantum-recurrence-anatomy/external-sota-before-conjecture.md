# External SOTA before conjecture

Cutoff: **1 August 2026**  
Status: **working priority dossier; no primary conjecture frozen**

## Rule

Proof effort is forbidden until an exact candidate has been compared theorem by
theorem with current primary literature, its citation ancestry and descendancy,
and its significance after all known pieces are removed. Search absence is not
priority evidence. A surviving exact-wording niche is not enough.

## Current decision

1. Interaction-graph first recurrence is retained only as a **bridge**.
2. No black-hole, holographic, arrow-of-time, or complexity claim follows from
   that bridge.
3. No primary conjecture is frozen.
4. Before any new proof work, a candidate must introduce a genuinely new
   primitive, make at least three occupied results into explicit shadows, and
   force one risky physical consequence not supplied by the parent theories.
5. If no candidate passes this gate, honest outcome is **no Nature paper from
   present theorem set**, not another narrower theorem.

## Collision result: return-to-simplicity codimension

Status: **KILLED AS A PRIMARY LAW; NO PROOF WORK AUTHORIZED.**

The proposed principle that first return to a low-complexity set is controlled
by its codimension inside the spectral torus is not an unoccupied mathematical
mechanism.

- Galatolo, [arXiv:0906.3416](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.3416), proves for
  rapidly mixing systems that hitting time is asymptotic on the logarithmic
  scale to inverse target measure for regular shrinking targets. Thus target
  dimension/codimension already controls hitting exponents in established
  shrinking-target theory when sufficient mixing is present.
- Linear torus flows are not covered by that mixing theorem. Their hitting and
  filling rates are instead controlled by Diophantine properties of the flow
  direction; see Kim--Marchese--Marmi,
  [arXiv:1705.03328](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03328), and Dumas--Fischler,
  [arXiv:2112.11786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.11786). A universal exponent
  depending only on target codimension is therefore false without arithmetic
  control.
- Kotowski--Oszmaniec--Horodecki,
  [JHEP 01 (2025) 116](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17538), already formulate
  static random-Hamiltonian evolution as motion on a random maximal torus,
  prove that such tori avoid neighbourhoods of low-complexity unitaries, and
  explicitly propose torus ergodic methods for recurrence. Their Appendix E
  already obtains an $\varepsilon^{d-1}$ small-target probability for diagonal
  random Hamiltonians.
- Oszmaniec--Kotowski--Horodecki--Hunter-Jones,
  [Phys. Rev. X 14, 041068 (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09734), already
  prove saturation and recurrence of approximate circuit complexity for random
  circuits and stochastic local Hamiltonian evolution, including inverse-volume
  recurrence scales.

After subtraction, only a genuine open problem remains: prove tight,
fixed-error complexity recurrence for a *single static, physically local,
noncommuting Hamiltonian*. Both the 2024 PRX paper and the 2025 JHEP paper
identify static local Hamiltonian dynamics as missing. Kotowski--Oszmaniec,
[arXiv:2601.10409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10409), separately ask for tight
first-state-recurrence bounds for physical local noncommuting Hamiltonians.
This is an externally documented open problem, not yet a Nature-level
conjecture: solving it by importing known shrinking-target geometry would be a
new application, not automatically a new universal law.

### Priority gate forced by this collision

Before any proof begins, a surviving candidate must specify all three items:

1. a theorem about static local noncommuting dynamics that is absent from the
   four results above;
2. a new invariant or mechanism beyond target volume, torus dimension, and
   Diophantine type;
3. a physical consequence derived inside a stated microscopic model, rather
   than inferred from black-hole analogy.

Failure of any item kills the candidate before paper proof or formalization.

## Ambitious-law search: randomness of autonomous dynamics

The master target is no longer a missing local-recurrence exponent. Recurrence
may be a consequence, but cannot be the organising primitive.

### Candidate A: autonomous temporal pseudorandomness

Proposed claim: after symmetry resolution and scrambling, generic static local
Hamiltonian evolution is computationally indistinguishable from Haar-random
unitary evolution for every efficient observer.

Status: **FALSE; KILLED BEFORE PROOF.**

Cui--Schuster--Mao--Huang--Brandao,
[arXiv:2510.08434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08434), prove that every ensemble
of constant-local Hamiltonian evolutions, at arbitrary evolution times, is
efficiently distinguishable from Haar randomness with two queries. Such
ensembles cannot form sufficiently accurate unitary 2-designs or pseudorandom
unitaries. The distinguisher detects correlations forced by the local conserved
generator. Increasing locality to polylogarithmic scale can evade the no-go,
but constant-local physical dynamics cannot.

This collision is decisive: a universal law of autonomous quantum time cannot
identify physical chaos with Haar randomness.

### Occupied positive replacements

- Mark et al.,
  [Phys. Rev. X 14, 041051 (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11970), formulate
  a maximum-entropy law for temporal state ensembles subject to energy and
  effective thermal constraints.
- Fava--Kurchan--Pappalardi,
  [Phys. Rev. X 15, 011031 (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.06200), replace
  full unitary designs by $k$-freeness on the ETH class of physical
  observables.
- Li--Zheng--Liu,
  [arXiv:2411.04893](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04893), construct efficient
  symmetry-resolved pseudorandomness under conservation laws.
- Ghosh--Hunter-Jones--Rodriguez-Nieva,
  [arXiv:2512.25074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.25074), give theory and numerical
  evidence that state ensembles under non-random chaotic Hamiltonians reproduce
  Haar moments for broad observable classes on polynomial, sometimes linear,
  timescales. This is state/observable randomization, not unitary-channel
  pseudorandomness.

### Candidate B: source-preserving randomness

Working conjecture, not priority-cleared:

> Autonomous quantum dynamics can become maximally random only after quotienting
> out information carried by its generator. For every observer class and every
> replica/query budget, the exact obstruction to randomization is the part of a
> conserved *source algebra* visible to that observer. All late-time deviation
> from the appropriate maximum-entropy reference ensemble is exhausted by this
> visible source; no additional dynamical memory survives.

Desired law would supply an exact observer/source decomposition, not merely a
new randomness diagnostic. It would explain simultaneously why state-only
observers can see Haar statistics, why two channel queries detect local origin
forever, why ETH applies only to restricted observables, and why recurrence
retains microscopic spectral information.

Status: **AMBITIOUS CONJECTURE; EXACT NOVELTY UNVERIFIED; NO PROOF AUTHORIZED.**

Main collision risk is high. Time twirls are projections onto resonance
commutants; maximum entropy under conserved constraints, symmetry-resolved
designs, $k$-freeness, and conditional expectations are established theories.
Candidate B survives only if it yields a new necessary-and-sufficient theorem
whose content is not ordinary commutant projection or maximum entropy in new
language, and if it forces a new physical prediction distinguishing observer
access models.
