# External SOTA before conjecture

Cutoff: **1 August 2026**  
Status: **working priority dossier; no primary conjecture frozen**

## Rule

Proof effort is forbidden until an exact candidate has been compared theorem by
theorem with current primary literature, its citation ancestry and descendancy,
and its significance after all known pieces are removed. Search absence is not
priority evidence. A surviving exact-wording niche is not enough.

## Live external questions and honest fit

| External question | Evidence it is live | Does current recurrence corpus answer it? |
|---|---|---|
| Tight first-recurrence bounds for physical local noncommuting Hamiltonians | Explicitly asked by Kotowski--Oszmaniec | **Possibly as a bridge.** The interaction-graph spectral-rank route could give generic sharpness, but most recurrence mathematics is inherited from classical Diophantine approximation. Current Nature significance: insufficient. |
| Superpolynomial/exponential circuit-complexity growth for a typical static local Hamiltonian | Brown--Susskind conjecture; 2025 complexity review identifies the barrier | **No.** Phase recurrence neither lower-bounds circuit complexity between returns nor supplies a gate-set-sensitive invariant. |
| Nonperturbative late-time bulk interpretation beyond special low-dimensional models | 2025 complexity review | **No.** A gravitational dictionary or model is absent. |
| Recurrence bounds for open channels and infinite-dimensional systems | Gupta--Short discussion; Kotowski--Oszmaniec conclusion | **No complete answer.** Contractivity and continuous spectrum change the problem; finite torus recurrence does not transfer. |
| Distinguishing chaos from integrability in continuum QFT by complexity methods | 2025 complexity review | **No.** Finite spectral arithmetic is neither a QFT regulator nor a chaos diagnostic. |

## Current decision

1. Interaction-graph first recurrence is retained only as a **bridge**.
2. No black-hole, holographic, arrow-of-time, or complexity claim follows from
   that bridge.
3. No primary conjecture is frozen.
4. Before any new proof work, a candidate must introduce a genuinely new
   primitive, make at least three occupied results into explicit shadows, and
   force one risky physical consequence not supplied by the parent theories.
5. If no candidate passes this gate, honest outcome is **no Nature paper from
   present theorem set**, not another narrower theorem.
