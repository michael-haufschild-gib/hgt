## Candidate universal law

Let \(P\) be the rational projective character space of a finite phase
carrier. For an observer and clock scaling, let \(L_{\mathcal O,T}\subseteq
P\) be the span of characters with zero dimensionless observer action, and
let \(\mathcal T_{\mathcal O,T}\) be the exposed temporal quotient. The
exact sequence
\[
  0\longrightarrow L_{\mathcal O,T}
   \longrightarrow P
   \longrightarrow \mathcal T_{\mathcal O,T}
   \longrightarrow0
\]
gives
\[
  \boxed{\dim\mathcal T_{\mathcal O,T}
    +\operatorname{rank}L_{\mathcal O,T}
    =\operatorname{rank}P.}
\]
Between any two valid observer-action laws on the same carrier,
\[
  \tau_0+\rho_0=\tau_1+\rho_1.
\]
Whenever \(\tau_0\le\tau_1\),
\[
  \boxed{\tau_1-\tau_0=\rho_0-\rho_1.}
\]

This is the universal law. Interaction is not added as a second principle:
it is a physical mechanism that changes the zero-action kernel. Observer
restriction and clock scaling change the same kernel. Dissipation first
removes transient directions and leaves a peripheral phase carrier to which
the same law applies.

For a countable pure-point carrier with balanced relation lattice \(L\), the
exact projective orbit closure is
\[
  K_L=\{z:z^k=1\ \text{for every }k\in L\}.
\]
Spectral weights pull the ambient phase metric back to \(K_L\), and the
weighted covering number bounds an actual first return. Thus relation
kernel, recurrence hull and return clock are successive parts of one
carrier law.

## Related no-gos now explained

### Finite observation cannot determine completed recurrence

Fixed-time histories depend continuously on Hamiltonian norm, whereas the
zero-action kernel is arithmetic and discontinuous. The exact operator bound
\[
  \|\alpha_t^{H_1}(O)-\alpha_t^{H_0}(O)\|
  \le 2|t|\,\|H_1-H_0\|\,\|O\|
\]
coexists with complete kernel deletion under arbitrarily small interaction.
This is one singular-limit law, not a contradiction.

### No finite replica cutoff is complete

Replica order bounds coefficient height. The complete relation lattice can
contain its first distinguishing relation above every prescribed finite
height. Replica moments are finite shadows of \(L\), never a replacement for
it.

### Complete local or perturbative data need not decide recurrence

Local jets do not determine global spectral completion. Two completions can
share all supplied local data while having different relation carriers or
different spectral types. A correct universal law must take the complete
carrier as input; no local classifier can replace it without an additional
global determinacy theorem.

### Exact rank is not finite-resolution capacity

Rational rank is an arithmetic invariant of the completed carrier.
Finite-resolution distinguishability is metric-statistical and depends on
weights, probes, noise and clock duration. The observer-action profile and
weighted hull metric keep these notions separate.

### Recurrence does not reconstruct locality

Interaction support and temporal curvature are meaningful relative to a
physically supplied factorization or control algebra. Invertible source
mixing changes coordinate sparsity. The law may compare supplied physical
parts; it cannot derive physical locality from phase relations alone.

### Pure-point and atomless spectra have opposite return phases

Countable pure-point carriers are compact in the weighted phase metric and
recur at every positive resolution. Atomless spectral laws have continuum
observer dimension and zero-density positive-fidelity returns. Spectral
type is part of the carrier and cannot be inferred from finite local data.

- arbitrary noncontractive open dynamics;

- any theorem about astrophysical black holes, cosmological time or gravity.

These are not “future-work questions” to advertise as if almost solved.
They are scope boundaries. The paper answers the complete question inside
its stated carrier classes and does not use the excluded claims for
significance.
