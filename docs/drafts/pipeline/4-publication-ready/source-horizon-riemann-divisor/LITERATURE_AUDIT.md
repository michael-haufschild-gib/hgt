> The paper proves an exact source--analytic classifier: differential
> independence of finite-abscissa prime-power Dirichlet families over all
> finite-prime Euler germs is equivalent to clock-orbit independence modulo
> finite prime rays, with an arbitrary relative quotient theorem.  Its Riemann
> realization first gives a partition of the positive integers into disjoint
> positive \(0/1\)-coefficient subseries, every member of which has exactly
> the zeta zero divisor on \(\operatorname{Re}s>1/2\).  The associated
> injective mass-one source has shell fields forming a faithful complete
> Boolean algebra relative to their common-zeta bottom and full-shell top:
> arbitrary nonempty intersections, composita, complements, inclusion,
> equality, and private tensor independence reproduce the corresponding
> source-set operations exactly, while every field observable has finite
> shell support.
> Its common zeta differential field is exactly the intersection of every shell-test
> field, whose logarithmic connection is irreducible over the complete
> transverse shell frame, and whose von Mangoldt/Xi primitive survives complete local
> residue--valuation fusion.  The same unsquared modular charge is transported
> from the cofinal prime tail through a Poisson packet to the centered Xi
> residue; bounded label-blind and positive consumers respectively erase or
> square it.  The finite-stage embeddings are nevertheless cofinally
> norm-collapsing, so every uniformly Lipschitz nonlinear consumer also loses
> spectral separation and inverse conditioning necessarily diverges; every
> exact reflected valuation sewing is singular beyond all finite Rees orders.

cannot rest on either deduction alone.  Strongest priority candidates are

regularity boundary: linearity and label blindness are no longer needed, because every uniformly

A referee may regard the generic free-field, boost/Fisher, and abstract
