### Defensible novelty statement

> The paper constructs an explicit positive coefficient-disjoint analytic
> resolution of unity whose mass-one \(\ell^1\) source map is injective and
> whose leaves share the Riemann divisor while their transverse quotients and
> common zeta mode are differentially irreducible in both directions. It
> classifies every bounded label-blind observer as a mass-only factor, proving
> that each such observer annihilates the nonzero off-line reflection
> displacement. It then identifies the labeled cofinal orientation and its
> positive Fisher, Weil, and Cauchy curvature shadow.

Not defensible:

- that largest-exponent densities, \(m\)-free series, the abstract
  \(\ell^1\) symmetry factorization, reciprocal boosts, Fisher information, Cauchy
  kernels, or Weil positivity are individually new;
- that generic differential algebra proves the Riemann hypothesis;
- that every Hilbert--Pólya, Connes--Consani, Hedenmalm, Sonin, or Ward
  construction is impossible;
- that source orientation itself excludes zeros;
- that the manuscript proves RH.

### Residual priority and significance risk

The reciprocal common-divisor theorem is a short consequence once joint
differential independence of \(\{\zeta\}\cup\{H_m\}\) is known. Novelty
cannot be assigned to that deduction alone.  Likewise, label-blind
factorization on abstract \(\ell^1\) is elementary.  Novelty rests on the
explicit positive isodivisorial mass-one embedding, its injectivity and
reflection displacement, the exact observer-blindness corollary on that
source, the countable joint-independence theorem, the cofinal two-atom
connection, and the reflection-information identification as one object.

A referee may regard the generic boost/Fisher algebra as elementary. The
manuscript must therefore lead with the arithmetic construction and its
two-sided separation, not with \(2\times2\) classification. It also must
state plainly that the missing source-derived isometry is not proved. On
current evidence, the work is a plausible strong specialist/general
mathematics paper; “Nature-level” or RH-proof claims are not supportable.
