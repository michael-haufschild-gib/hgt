## Internal predecessor boundary

The project post “False RH cannot hide in a sparse sign set” already contains a Poisson-square/Bargmann energy, moving derivative orders, exterior determinants, and ridge reconstruction. Those ingredients are not claimed as new here.

The present manuscript adds:

1. arbitrary nonzero generator \(b\), rather than only a Riemann Cayley mode;
2. uniqueness of the Poisson-normalized clock;
3. forced conjugate-reciprocal duality;
4. exact factorization of Fock growth into drift and dual orientation;
5. universal neutral line–shifted unit circle equivalence;
6. pullback to arbitrary source and multipolar horizons;
7. finite-carrier differential-algebraic localization and explicit annihilator;
8. combined compact-open/Kolchin/Ward escape theorem.

## Defensible novelty statement

Defensible:

> The paper proves a Poisson-normalized generator-duality classification and shows that meromorphic source evolution pulls its universal neutral horizon into divisor space. In the Riemann case this simultaneously identifies the critical line, inverse Li unit circle, coherent growth neutrality, and a finite differential-algebraic carrier for escaped divisor data.

Not defensible:

- “first use of coherent states for RH”;
- “first Poisson formulation of Li coefficients”;
- “first compact-open instability result”;
- “proof of RH”;
- “a zero-exclusion theorem”;
- “a no-go theorem for Hilbert–Pólya, Connes–Consani, or Hedenmalm.”

## Residual novelty risk

The universal identities are short enough that an uncatalogued prior occurrence is plausible. Significance cannot rest on algebraic difficulty. It must rest on explanatory compression plus the collision theorem: one forced carrier accounts for analytic escape, differential-matroid collision, Ward mass escape, Li dynamics, and exact height renormalization. Any submission should make that conjunction—not elementary coherent-state algebra—the lead contribution.
