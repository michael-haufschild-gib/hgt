# Literature and collision audit

Audit date: 2026-07-28.

## Question audited

1. actual-prime Hardy logarithmic clock with maximally dense graph after every finite trace;
2. Gamma-sewn scalar contraction whose positive Julia normal derivative is `log p`;
3. packetwise bridge forcing every bounded realization to diverge in operator norm;
4. one squared normal-coordinate Mellin orbit with von Mangoldt Euler and critical faces;
5. hypertranscendence of Euler face relative to differential field generated by every prime-zeta
   valuation layer;
6. fixed-width critical-face scalarization reconstructing every off-critical Xi zero and analytic

No exact collision was located.

## Primary neighboring literatures

### Julia operators and dilation

- P. R. Halmos, “Normal dilations and extensions of operators,” *Summa Brasil. Math.* 2
  (1950), 125–134.
- B. Sz.-Nagy, C. Foias, H. Bercovici, L. Kérchy, *Harmonic Analysis of Operators on Hilbert
  Space*, 2nd ed., Springer, 2010,
  [doi:10.1007/978-1-4419-6094-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4419-6094-8).

Known: positive defect operator, Julia block operator, minimal unitary dilation theory.

Not found: arithmetic Gamma-sewn contraction, normal derivative `log p`, Hardy packet bridge,
Mellin orbit, or Riemann-divisor transgression.

Novelty not claimed: abstract 2-by-2 Julia matrix or positive square-root defect.

### Spectral, CCM, and semilocal RH programs

- A. Connes, “Trace formula in noncommutative geometry and the zeros of the Riemann zeta
  function,” *Selecta Math.* 5 (1999), 29–106.
- A. Connes and C. Consani, “Weil positivity and trace formula, the archimedean place,”
  *Selecta Math.* 27 (2021), article 77.
- A. Connes and C. Consani, “Spectral triples and zeta-cycles,” *Enseign. Math.* 69 (2023),
  93–148.
- A. Connes, C. Consani, H. Moscovici, “Zeta spectral triples,”
  [arXiv:2511.22755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22755) (2025).

Current CCM work constructs finite-prime self-adjoint rank-one perturbations whose observed
spectra approach low zeta zeros; rigorous convergence would prove RH.

No collision: manuscript’s “one extra channel” is fiberwise per prime cell, and global theorem
is packetwise convergence plus unavoidable operator-norm blow-up. It neither reproduces nor
claims convergence of CCM spectral triples.
