# Literature and novelty audit

Audit date: 2026-07-31.  
Posture: skeptical priority check, not proof of priority.

## Candidate contribution

Paper's candidate novelty is not any component alone. It is the following
closed conjunction:

1. bounded prime-exponent source has three compatible faces: zeta bulk,
   reciprocal exceptional divisor, and centered logarithmic current;
2. positive exact-exponent shells force cofinal characters
   \(2^{1/2-s}\) and \(3^{1/2-s}\), with the pair recovering the whole
   complex spectral coordinate;
3. those shell directions are jointly differentially independent even
   after adjoining zeta and every zeta derivative;
4. compact BV prime-window residues and Hurwitz monodromy recover the same
   horizontal character;
5. diagonal squarefree activation is exactly the depth-one member
   \(A_1=\zeta(s)/\zeta(2s)\) of the same exponent source, and the valuation
   law is its conditional geometric fibre rather than an auxiliary model;
6. the positive atoms are Fisher-damped Gamma valuation coordinates;
   canonical Gamma sewing preserves prime summability exactly, but its
   boundary symbols have arbitrarily large weighted-Vandermonde Rees rank;
7. reflected valuation realizations of a nonzero horizontal charge are
   not two finite grades: one is all-orders flat and the other half-normal;
8. every finite-Rees bounded operator preserves the flat radical, while
   exact reflection sewing has norm beyond every inverse Rees power;
9. compact jets, bounded Ward windows, and reciprocal-square energies can
   all lose a fixed escaping charge while the character retains it.

No checked source contains this package or the finite-versus-flat
superselection theorem in this RH setting. This is qualified
non-collision evidence, not a claim that exhaustive worldwide priority has
been established.

## Honest significance assessment

Strongest defensible pitch:

> A source-side information law for the Riemann divisor: the same horizontal
> character is generated by positive exponent shells, survives normal-cone
> divisor formation, is independently readable by prime windows and Hurwitz
> monodromy, is retained by Gamma only as an infinite-rank Rees resolution,
> and becomes a finite-versus-flat superselection type that no bounded
> finite-Rees transport can cross.

This is stronger than a decorative RH equivalence and broader than a
single-route obstruction. It supplies a reusable operator theorem, a new
arithmetic realization, and an exact classification of information loss.

It is not a proof of RH, a new zero-free region, or a proof that all
Hilbert–Pólya/Connes–Consani approaches fail. Those claims would be false or
unsupported. Without zero exclusion, Nature/Science-level significance is
not established. Submission to a strong broad mathematics or mathematical
physics journal is defensible, subject to external specialist priority
review.
