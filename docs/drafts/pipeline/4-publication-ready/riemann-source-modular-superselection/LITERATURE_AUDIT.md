## Candidate contribution after corpus-wide revision

Paper’s candidate novelty is the **pointed-observability law** and its strict
positive hierarchy, not any ingredient alone:

1. arithmetic source data has independent valuation-depth and prime-normal
   breadth; two transverse views recover joint incidence, while no additive
   nonnegative scalar grade can;
2. finite positive Xi source metrics represent one rooted collision matroid,
   even after adjoining every scalar valuation layer;
3. positive source marking survives only as lift data above signed
   differential-field quotient: every projective-tail fibre is dense, but
   positive definiteness selects literal all-positive Euler source;
4. a nonnegative Mellin source with unit residue may have same exponential
   critical clock as squarefree source while carrying a prescribed conjugate
   zero pair anywhere in \(1/2<\Re s<1\), with Schwarz-pair Cauchy energy
   exceeding eight;
5. actual completed Xi source nevertheless gives unconditional self-localized
   global estimate
   \[
     0\le \mathcal E_\perp
     \le 4\left(\frac{C_\perp}{4-C_\perp}\right)^3
     <\frac1{150000},
   \]
   and \(C_\perp=\mathcal E_\perp=0\) exactly on RH fibre;
6. every hypothetical off-line Xi atom has real affine polynomial that kills
   positive diagonal Hilbert response while leaving transverse charge
   positive, so even atom-dependent finite diagonal domination cannot orient
   divisor;
7. positivity on physical reflection line need not have finite positive
   separated-cylinder lift;
8. global common-test rank-one Weil resolvent detects every off-axis zero by a
   negative diagonal value;
9. source orientation before divisor formation is odd prime-log Julia/Ward
   jet; squaring retains logarithmic current and hypertranscendence but loses
   orientation;
10. singular acquisition turns divisor into ramified character
    \((m_\rho,\chi_\rho)\), whose continuous rate and dyadic deck tower govern
    Wold, Sonin/Calkin, Ward, Rees, density, monodromy, and mass consumers.

The universal claim is therefore a strict information order:

\[
\text{source pointing}
\to\text{finite collision geometry}
\to\text{global defect magnitude}
\not\to\text{atomwise orientation control},
\]

whereas a shared global resolvent test sees orientation. This explains at once
why positive source models can be faithful, why their scalar clocks and local
Grams are insufficient, why global Xi source moments still impose a strong
defect budget, and why final zero selection must preserve pointing and couple
all divisor copies before diagonal/even/cylinder quotienting.

## Honest significance assessment

Strongest defensible pitch:

> Positive information about Riemann divisor is strictly stratified. Source
> pointing determines finite collision geometry and sharp global defect
> budget, but neither critical clock nor any atomwise diagonal Hilbert
> comparison determines transverse orientation. Orientation reappears only in
> odd source jet and global common-test resolvent coupling. Ramified character
> records what survives every later descent.

This is stronger than decorative RH equivalence, route-specific obstruction,
or catalogue of positive criteria. Most distinctive exact conjunction is:

- projective positive source pointing above dense signed field quotient;
- finite collision matroid/current-observability separation;
- explicit same-clock positive Mellin sources with prescribed right-strip
  zeros;
- unconditional Xi self-localized cubic transverse-energy budget with exact
  RH zero locus;
- impossibility of every atomwise finite diagonal comparison at an off-line
  copy;
- failure of finite separated sum-of-squares lifting;
- success of one shared rank-one Weil resolvent test;
- odd-source/even-current/ramified-character descent that explains Hedenmalm,
  Wold, Sonin/Calkin, Ward, and Rees shadows.

Novelty risk remains high because several individual proofs are elementary:
one added Mellin atom, finite-dimensional interpolation, cylinder
counterexample, and local pole asymptotics. Contribution must be judged as
exact universal conjunction and architecture, not depth of each lemma.

Cubic bound is genuine unconditional progress: it supplies source-derived
quantitative control of total transverse Xi defect. It does **not** supply zero
exclusion. Its height floor is far weaker than Platt--Trudgian verification and
must not be advertised as numerical advance. Paper also does not prove death
of Hilbert--Pólya/CCM; it excludes audited scalar, diagonal, finite-Rees, and
finite-cylinder realizations while identifying global source-pointed
correspondence as surviving route.

Honest editorial conclusion: coherent broad-mathematics candidate after major
compression and independent priority review. Flagship general-science
significance is not established because global common-test source
transgression still has no unconditional positivity/vanishing theorem.
