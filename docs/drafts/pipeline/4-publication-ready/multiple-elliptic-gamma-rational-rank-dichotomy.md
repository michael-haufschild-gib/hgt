## Result

Let `I` be finite and let every `tau_i` lie in the upper half-plane. If some two distinct periods,
together with `1`, are linearly independent over `Q`, then the genuine multiple elliptic gamma
function and its logarithmic derivative are hypertranscendental over `C(z)`. Arbitrary rational
dependencies among every other period are allowed. The same conclusion holds on every deletion
face whose surviving family contains such a pair.

Under the stronger hypothesis

```text
1, tau_i (i in I) are linearly independent over Q.
```

For a deletion face `J`, let `Psi_J` be the logarithmic derivative of the genuine multiple elliptic
gamma function on the surviving periods `I \ J`. Let `P_J` be its packet of translates by the
integer lattice generated by deleted periods.

Then:

1. the raw-function and logarithmic-derivative packet unions over every face retaining at least
   two periods are each jointly differentially algebraically independent over `C(z)`;
2. the two naturally labelled hierarchies have exactly the same complete differential matroid;
3. a raw or logarithmic packet element is differentially algebraic over `C(z)` iff its face
   retains fewer than two periods;
4. packets of arbitrary distinct non-boundary labels are disjoint, including incomparable faces;
5. for distinct selected packet coordinates `x_k` from either hierarchy and finite rows
   `L_a in C[delta]^N`,

```text
dtrdeg_(C(z)) C(z)<sum_k L_ak(delta) x_k : a in A>
  = dim_(C(delta)) span{L_a : a in A}.
```

Thus two surviving periods form the exact hierarchy-wide differential phase boundary, and every
finite differential-linear observation has nonlinear rank equal to ordinary operator-row rank.
