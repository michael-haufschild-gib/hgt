# Degree rotation preserves both integral loop vertices

- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Parent:** [Restricted elliptic rotation](../1-in-progress/degree-rotation-preserves-the-restricted-elliptic-loop-group.md).
- **Statement:** Rotation by a coefficient-field valuation unit preserves
  the entire loop group generated by the two coefficient-integral vertices.

## Proof

Power-series rotation multiplies coefficient `n` by `qⁿ`. When `v(q)=1`,
this preserves coefficient integrality. Constant diagonal rescaling multiplies
only the two off-diagonal entries, by `q` and `q⁻¹`, so preserves it as well.

Rotation sends the Laurent unit `X` to `qX`. On the shifted upper and lower
root parameters, it therefore sends `X⁻¹p` to `X⁻¹q⁻¹ rot(p)` and `Xp` to
`Xq rot(p)`. Four-root generation extends these identities to the actual
positive central lift. Thus rotation of the adjacent-vertex lift is its
shift applied to the rotated positive matrix, followed by constant
rescaling by `q⁻¹`. The central factor is fixed.

Both integral vertices are preserved, hence so is their generated subgroup.
The inverse scalar has valuation one and gives the reverse inclusion.
