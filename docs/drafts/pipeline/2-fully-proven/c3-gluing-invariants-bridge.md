- **Statement.** For infinite-base multi-`q` modules:
  **(a)** the *joint* isomorphism class is **strictly finer** than the tuple of single-prime
  (per-prime) classes — there exist modules isomorphic on every prime yet inequivalent jointly;
  **(b)** every *gauge-combinatorial* joint invariant detecting this (the curvature `[A_p,A_q]`, the

  — a coordinate on the GIT character variety of the underlying representation — so it **fails Door-2 and
  Door-3**;
  **(c)** the arithmetic of the primes (`Λ`, Baker's bound on `m·ℓ`) enters the classification **only**

  the combination-mode object of **Conjecture C5★**. Hence **C3-native ⟺ C5★**.

## Setup
Fix the prime base `P`. A **constant** rank-`n` infinite-base multi-`q` module is a tuple
`(A_p)_{p∈P} ∈ GL_n(ℂ)^{(P)}` (all but finitely many `A_p = I`), with operators `Φ_p = A_p σ_p`. These
are the cleanest **non-flat** (non-holonomic) objects: no commutation among the `A_p` is imposed.
Morphisms (gauge) are `g ∈ GL_n(ℂ)`, acting `A_p ↦ g A_p σ_p(g)^{-1} = g A_p g^{-1}` (constants are
`σ`-fixed). Two notions of equivalence:
- **per-prime (parent) data:** for each `p`, the single-`σ_p`-module `(V, A_pσ_p)` up to its own gauge —
  i.e. the conjugacy class `[A_p]`;
- **joint (native) class:** the whole tuple `(A_p)` up to **one** simultaneous conjugation.

## Theorem

### (a) The joint class is strictly finer (gluing is real)
**Witness** (`n=2`, primes `{2,3}`):
```
A_2 = [[1,1],[0,1]],   A_3 = [[1,0],[1,1]];      A_2 A_3 = [[2,1],[1,1]],  tr(A_2A_3) = 3.
B_2 = A_2,             B_3 = h A_3 h^{-1},  h = diag(2,1)  ⟹  B_3 = [[1,0],[1/2,1]];
                                                       A_2 B_3 = [[3/2,1],[1/2,1]],  tr(A_2B_3) = 5/2.
```
- **Per-prime equal:** `[B_2]=[A_2]` (identical); `[B_3]=[A_3]` (conjugate via the constant `h`;
  both have `tr=2, det=1`, the regular class).
- **Jointly distinct:** if `g A_2 g^{-1}=B_2=A_2` and `g A_3 g^{-1}=B_3` then
  `tr(A_2A_3)=tr(gA_2A_3g^{-1})=tr(B_2B_3)`; but `3 ≠ 5/2`. So no simultaneous gauge exists. ∎(a)

Thus the per-prime data does **not** determine the module: a joint invariant (here `tr(A_pA_q)`) is
needed. This is the structural content C3 demanded — "two objects with identical parent-data differ in
it."

### (b) But every such invariant is BRIDGE
With constant matrices the `σ_p` are **inert** (they fix `ℂ`), so the joint classification is exactly
```
{ constant rank-n multi-q modules } / joint iso  =  Hom(F_S, GL_n(ℂ)) / GL_n(ℂ),
```
the representation variety of the **free** group `F_S` on the (finite) support `S` (no flatness ⟹ no
relations), modulo simultaneous conjugation. By **Procesi (1976) / Artin**, the ring of simultaneous-
conjugacy invariants is generated by the **traces of words** `tr(A_{p_1}\cdots A_{p_k})`; the curvature
`[A_p,A_q]` and the joint conjugacy class are functions of these. 

**Door-2 (fails).** Strip the words "multi-`q` / `σ_p`": what remains is a tuple of matrices modulo
conjugation — a representation of a free group and its **character variety**. The invariant theory of
`GL_n` (Procesi), a parent, computes every joint invariant; the frequencies `(log p)` never appear. A
parent proves it intact ⟹ **BRIDGE**.
**Door-3 (fails).** The joint invariants are word-traces and the closure dimension (`corank`, already
graded BRIDGE in #23) — renamed classical (character-variety / dimension) invariants, not native ones.
∎(b)

### (c) The arithmetic enters only via C5★
Drop the constancy: let `A_p = A_p(x)` depend on the `𝕋^∞` coordinates, so `σ_p` acts nontrivially. The
gauge equation `B_p = g A_p σ_p(g)^{-1}` is now a genuine cocycle equation over the `𝕋^∞`-action, solved
mode-by-mode by dividing by small divisors (`λ_p(m)-1` for a single shift, or `m·ℓ` for the diagonal
flow). It splits into:
- a **qualitative** layer — solvability up to exact resonance, governed by `Λ = {0}` (unique
  factorization). This is the §59/§60 content: already **BRIDGE**.
- a **quantitative** layer — convergence/regularity of the transfer function, governed by the *size* of
  `|m·ℓ|`, i.e. **Baker's** lower bound on linear forms in `{log p}`. On combination modes this is
  precisely the cohomological equation of **Conjecture C5★**
  (`../1-in-progress/combination-mode-baker-reducibility.md`, Lemma R).

So the frequencies of the primes touch the module classification **only** through the quantitative
layer, and that layer **is** C5★. The gauge-combinatorial layer is parent (a,b); the only
Door-3-surviving refinement is the arithmetic regularity class. Therefore the native invariant is **not
independent of** the native theorem:

> **Reduction (C3 ⟺ C5★).** A Door-3-surviving native invariant of multi-`q` modules exists iff the
> combination-mode arithmetic layer is nontrivial in the irremovable infinite-base-uniform sense — which
> is exactly Conjecture C5★. C3 cannot be closed below C5.

(a) and (b) are complete proofs; (c) is the reduction, resting on the already-proven axis/combination-
mode criterion (`../2-fully-proven/erosion-cocycle-reducibility.md`) and Lemma R, with C5★ itself open.

## Door-2/Door-3 grade: BRIDGE
The proven content (a,b) is character-variety / invariant theory — a parent proves it. Its worth is the
**dividing line**: it shows definitively that no *cheap* (gauge-combinatorial) native invariant exists,

verdict and of the axis/combination-mode criterion.

and the native invariant coincides with the C5 theorem (c). Consequence for the branchhood milestone:

toward **one** hard problem (C5) plus the bridge (C6). C3 stays formally PARTIAL but with a sharp reason:
it closes exactly when C5★ is proven.
