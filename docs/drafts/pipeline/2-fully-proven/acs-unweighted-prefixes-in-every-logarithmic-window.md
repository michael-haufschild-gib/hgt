# Unweighted square-generator prefixes in every logarithmic window

Let $P_d=\prod_{j=1}^d d_{\rm ord}(jD_j)$. There is an integer $B>0$ such that every sufficiently late $T$ has $n>0$ with $|2\log n-T|\le10\log(T+2)$ and $B^{-d^3}\le P_d\le B^{d^3}$ for every integer $n\le d\le n+n^{1/4}$. Every conductor through $d$ occurs with multiplicity one. The [sharp prefix criterion](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheSignedProductOverSquareConductors.lean) needs the same bound at every endpoint.

## Proof

Write $L(t)=4h(t)-2h(t+\log4)$ for the long trace. Its positive theta mean is zero at every translate and $|L(t)|\le A e^{|t|/2}$. Set $F(t)=\int_0^1r^2L(t+2\log r)\,dr$. For $0<r\le1$, the integrand has absolute value at most $Ar e^{|t|/2}$. Dominated convergence gives continuity and the same strip envelope for $F$. Fubini gives $\int\Phi(x)F(t+x)\,dx=0$ since each inner translate has zero mean. Absolute domination follows from $Ar e^{|t|/2}\Phi(x)e^{|x|/2}$. [Theta annihilation](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheThetaMeanOfTheSquareTrace.lean)

On $|u-T|\le8\log(T+2)$, $F>1$ everywhere is impossible for late $T$. Apply the positive kernel leakage bound to $F-1$ with zero local floor. Its theta integral is $-\int\Phi<0$, whereas its lower bound tends to zero. Applying the same argument to $-F-1$ excludes $F<-1$ everywhere. Continuity on the interval supplies one point with $|F(u)|\le1$. [Logarithmic leakage](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SurfaceOfSpecZ/ScaleLocality/PositiveKernelLeakage.lean)

Let $X=e^{u/2}$, $H(v)=v^2 L(2\log v)$ and $I(y)=\int_0^y H(v)\,dv$. Substitution gives $I(X)=X^3F(u)$. On each unit cell $j-1<v\le j$, $j\ge2$, the trace changes by $O(v^{-1/4})$, $j^2-v^2\le2j$, and $|L(2\log v)|\ll v$. Thus $|H(j)-H(v)|\ll j^2$. The strip bound gives $|H(v)|\ll v$ on the first cell. The actual log generator at $j$ differs from $-H(j)$ by $O(j^2)$, using the signed degree at the upper square and the ordinary-contact estimate. Summing gives $|\log P_d+I(d)|\ll d^3$ for each integer $d$. [Root displacement and sampling](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheSignedDegreeAtTheNearestUpperSquare.lean) · [Ordinary contact](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/OneMultiplicityPerSquareConductor.lean)

Take $n=\lceil X\rceil$. It moves $2\log X$ by at most two. The strip estimate at width $3/8$ gives $|H(v)|\ll v^{11/4}$ for $v\ge1$. If $n\le d\le n+n^{1/4}$ then $0\le d-X\le 2d^{1/4}$, so $|I(d)-I(X)|\ll d^3$. Since $|I(X)|\le X^3$, all those literal prefixes have $|\log P_d|\ll d^3$. Exponentiation gives the same integer base throughout all blocks.
