# Exact Prüfer monodromy of disconnected quadratic Böttcher coordinates

- **ID:** bottcher-exact-pruefer-monodromy
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / concrete C11-C20 structure theorem
- **Source:** strengthens `bottcher-dyadic-monodromy-finite-type-absence`

## Statement

Let `f_c(z)=z^2+c` with `c notin M`, and let `phi_c` be the normalized direct
Böttcher coordinate germ at infinity:

`phi_c(f_c(z))=phi_c(z)^2`, `phi_c(z)/z -> 1`.

Let `Omega_c=C\K_c`. The scalar monodromy image of analytic continuation of
`phi_c` on `Omega_c` is exactly

`mu_{2^infty} = { exp(2*pi*i*r) : r in Z[1/2] }`,

the Prüfer `2`-group of all dyadic roots of unity.

Equivalently, the period group of `d log phi_c` is exactly

`2*pi*i Z[1/2]`.

## Proof

Let `mu_c` be the equilibrium measure of `K_c`. Since `c notin M`, `K_c=J_c`
is a Cantor set and `f_c|K_c` is conjugate to the one-sided full two-shift.
Under this coding, `mu_c` is the fair Bernoulli measure.

The complex Green potential gives, on the chosen branch near infinity,

`d log phi_c = int dz/(z-zeta) dmu_c(zeta)`.

For any based loop `gamma` in `Omega_c`, Fubini and the argument principle give

`int_gamma d log phi_c
 = 2*pi*i int_K Ind(gamma,zeta) dmu_c(zeta)`.

The function `zeta -> Ind(gamma,zeta)` is integer-valued and continuous on
`K_c`: the loop is compact and disjoint from `K_c`, so the winding number is
locally constant near each point of `K_c`. A continuous integer-valued function
on the full-shift Cantor set depends on only finitely many symbols. Therefore,
for some `N`, it is constant on every depth-`N` cylinder. Since each depth-`N`
cylinder has `mu_c`-mass `2^-N`,

`int_K Ind(gamma,zeta) dmu_c(zeta) in 2^-N Z subset Z[1/2]`.

Thus every period lies in `2*pi*i Z[1/2]`, and every scalar monodromy multiplier
lies in `mu_{2^infty}`.

Conversely, for any depth-`N` cylinder `C_w`, choose a loop or finite product of
based loops in `Omega_c` whose index is `1` on `C_w` and `0` on `K_c\C_w`.
Such a loop exists because `C_w` is clopen in the zero-dimensional compactum
`K_c`. The period of this loop is

`2*pi*i mu_c(C_w) = 2*pi*i/2^N`.

Products and powers of these loops realize every element of `2*pi*i Z[1/2]`.
Therefore the period group is exactly `2*pi*i Z[1/2]`, and the scalar monodromy
image is exactly `mu_{2^infty}`. ∎

## Consequences

1. **Finite monodromy impossible.** The continuation has infinitely many scalar
   branches at a generic basepoint, with orders `2^N` unbounded.
2. **Finite covers cannot kill it.** Since the image is the Prüfer `2`-group,
   every finite-index subgroup of `pi_1(Omega_c)` still maps onto the same
   Prüfer group. This is the content used by
   `bottcher-pruefer-monodromy-no-finite-cover.md`.
3. **The obstruction is topological, not local branch-point algebra.** Escaping
   precritical points are not the load-bearing local branch points of the direct
   Böttcher coordinate; the monodromy is the period of `d log phi_c` around
   clopen Cantor-boundary pieces.

## Significance

This theorem identifies the exact continuation group behind the disconnected
quadratic Böttcher hypertranscendence obstruction. The finite-handle failure is
not merely "infinite monodromy"; it is a precise divisible dyadic monodromy group
generated by harmonic measure of symbolic cylinders.

The result is still bridge-grade until source audit proves it is not standard in
complex dynamics. Its HGT value is structural: a concrete hypertranscendental
family carries a continuation group that no finite algebraic cover or finite
monodromy controller can absorb.
