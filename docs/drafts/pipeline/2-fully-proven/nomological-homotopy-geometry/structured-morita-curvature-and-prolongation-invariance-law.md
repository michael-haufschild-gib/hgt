# Structured Morita curvature and prolongation invariance law

- **ID:** `nhg-structured-morita-invariance`
- **Stage:** 2 — fully proven on paper
- **Status:** `[PAPER-PROVED] [UNIVERSAL FIELD THEOREM] [STRICT COVER-MORITA SECTOR]`

## 1. Structured fields

For `i=1,2`, let `M_i` be a smooth manifold and let

\[
 \mathcal F_i=(E_i,Q_i,\rho_i,F_i^\bullet,
 \langle-,-\rangle_i,\nabla_i,\alpha_i)
\tag{SM.1}
\]

be a finite-rank strict dg-Lie-algebroid chart of a source-anchored cyclic filtered law field. Thus
`E_i` is a graded dg Lie algebroid, `Q_i` is homological, `rho_i:E_i->TM_i` is the anchor, the
filtration is complete in the finite chart, the pairing is fixed-degree and perfect, `alpha_i` is
sourced, and `nabla_i` preserves the displayed structure. The higher `L_infinity` extension is not
claimed here.

Write

\[
 R_i=\nabla_i^2
 \in\Omega^2(M_i;\operatorname{Der}(E_i,Q_i))
\tag{SM.2}
\]

for relative connection curvature.

## 2. Structured cover-Morita equivalence

### Definition 2.1

The fields `mathcal F_1` and `mathcal F_2` are **strictly structured cover-Morita equivalent** if
there exist a manifold `Z`, surjective submersions

\[
 p_i:Z\longrightarrow M_i,
\tag{SM.3}
\]

and an isomorphism of transverse pullback algebroids

\[
 \Phi:p_1^!E_1\xrightarrow{\sim}p_2^!E_2
\tag{SM.4}
\]

that intertwines the pulled-back `Q`, anchor action, filtration, pairing, source section, and
connection:

\[
 \Phi\,p_1^!\nabla_1=p_2^!\nabla_2\,\Phi.
\tag{SM.5}
\]

This is deliberately stricter than equivalence of unbased minimal `L_infinity` fibres.

## 3. Curvature invariance

### Theorem 3.1 — structured curvature descent

Under Definition 2.1,

\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi\,p_1^*R_1=p_2^*R_2\,\Phi.}
\tag{SM.6}
\]

For every `k>=0`, the covariant curvature jets also agree:

\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi\,p_1^*(\nabla_1^kR_1)
 =p_2^*(\nabla_2^kR_2)\,\Phi.}
\tag{SM.7}
\]

Consequently vanishing, pointwise rank, filtration depth, cyclic contractions, and the Lie algebra
generated by the full curvature-jet tower are invariant on the common refinement.

#### Proof

Here `p^!E={(v,e) in TZ times p^*E:dp(v)=rho(e)}`; surjective submersions are transverse to every
anchor, so this pullback exists. Square `(SM.5)`. Since `Phi` has form degree zero,

\[
 \Phi(p_1^!\nabla_1)^2=(p_2^!\nabla_2)^2\Phi,
\]

which is `(SM.6)`. Apply the induced connections on endomorphism-valued forms repeatedly to obtain
`(SM.7)`. `Phi` is fibrewise invertible and preserves filtration and pairing, so it preserves all
listed algebraic invariants and sends the generated Lie algebra isomorphically to its counterpart.
QED.

### Lemma 3.2 — descent detects vanishing

If `p:Z->M` is a surjective submersion, pullback of vector-bundle-valued differential forms is
injective. Hence

\[
 p^*R=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R=0.
\tag{SM.8}
\]

#### Proof

For `x in M`, choose `z in Z` above `x`. Surjectivity of `d p_z` lets every tuple of tangent vectors
at `x` be lifted to `z`. If the pullback form vanishes on all lifted tuples, the original form
vanishes at `x`. QED.

Thus a nonzero relative curvature cannot be erased by passing to a structured cover.

## 4. Spencer invariance

Assume the prolongation tower is the principal-parts/Spencer tower and commutes with the smooth
base changes `(SM.3)`. Then `Phi` prolongs to isomorphisms

\[
 J^n\Phi:p_1^!J^nE_1\xrightarrow{\sim}p_2^!J^nE_2
\tag{SM.9}
\]

intertwining Spencer differentials.

### Theorem 4.1 — structured prolongation invariance

Under this base-change hypothesis, the ranks of every Spencer symbol, kernel, image, and cohomology
bundle agree on `Z`. Eventual dimension polynomials agree whenever defined.

#### Proof

The functorial principal-parts construction sends `(SM.4)` to `(SM.9)`. Intertwining the Spencer
differentials gives isomorphisms of the resulting complexes. Isomorphic finite-rank complexes have
equal ranks and isomorphic cohomology; equality of every sufficiently large rank value gives equality
of eventual dimension polynomials. QED.

## 5. Integrated transport

Assume both algebroids integrate and that `(p_i,Phi)` integrates to an equivalence of the pulled-back
law groupoids preserving connection transport. Then corresponding paths have conjugate holonomy,
and corresponding higher transport cells agree under `Phi`.

This conclusion is an explicit hypothesis about integration. It does not follow from fibrewise
quasi-isomorphism alone.

## 6. Failure tests

1. An isomorphism of graded cohomology bundles need not preserve `R_i`.
2. A quasi-isomorphism after forgetting the base algebra can erase a relative Atiyah class.
3. An arbitrary filtration can change prolongation ranks; principal-parts base-change is load-bearing.
4. Equality of scalar characteristic classes does not imply `(SM.6)`.
5. Morita equivalence of unstructured groupoids does not select a connection or cyclic pairing.
6. Completion or properness is not covered unless the equivalence also identifies the analytic
   structures.

## 7. Acceptance rule

Any proposed NHG invariant must either:

- descend under structured cover-Morita equivalence; or
- explicitly declare which additional marking prevents that descent.

Agreement only after forgetting anchor, connection, filtration, or pairing is not evidence that two
presentations are gauges of one law field.
