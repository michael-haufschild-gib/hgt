### Theorem I (Exact Finite-$N$ / Pencil Divisor & Zhukovsky–Wiener–Hopf Index Formula) `[PROVED HERE]`

Suppose $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}$ vanishes nowhere on $\partial R_{T,n}$, and let $Z_{N,\theta}(T, n) := \{w \in \operatorname{int}(R_{T,n}) : \mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(w) = 0\}$ with multiplicity $m_{N,\theta}(w) := \operatorname{ord}_w(\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}) \in \mathbb{N}_{>0}$.

Applying multi-zero peeling (`exists_multi_peel`) and the weighted rectangle argument principle (`rectIntegral_mul_logDeriv_boxCount`) on $U_\delta$ yields
   $$s_{T,n,N,\theta}(r) = \sum_{w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} m_{N,\theta}(w)\, w^r \quad (\forall r \in \mathbb{N}), \qquad M_{T,n,N,\theta} = \sum_{w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} m_{N,\theta}(w).$$

Squaring and applying the Wiener–Hopf index theorem gives
   $$\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N,\theta}) = 2 \sum_{z \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} \sum_{w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} m_{N,\theta}(z)\, m_{N,\theta}(w)\, \mathbf{1}_{\{E_n(z - w) < 1\}} \ge 0.$$
3. **Critical-line collar vanishing:** Specializing to $\theta = 0$, if $\mathcal{A}_N \ne 0$ on $\partial R_{T,n}$ and every $z \in Z_N(T, n)$ satisfies $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| < \tfrac{1}{4 s_n}$ (in particular if all zeros of $\mathcal{A}_N$ in $R_{T,n}$ lie on $\operatorname{Re} z = \tfrac{1}{2}$), then for all $z, w \in Z_N(T, n)$ we have $\operatorname{Re}(z - w) = (\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}) - (\operatorname{Re} w - \tfrac{1}{2}) < \tfrac{1}{2 s_n}$, so $\operatorname{Re}(z - w) - \tfrac{1}{2} < -\bigl(\tfrac{1}{2} - \tfrac{1}{2 s_n}\bigr) = -a_n < 0$, forcing $E_n(z - w) \ge (\operatorname{Re}(z - w) - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 / a_n^2 > 1$. ### Theorem III (Complex Seam-Cusp Law, Strip Zero Finiteness, Critical-Line Root, & Height-Freezing) `[PROVED HERE]`
1. **Third-order complex half-line integration by parts:** By evenness of $P_{N+1}(|x|) = J_{N+1,0}(|x|)$, $\mathcal{A}_N(z) = \int_0^\infty J_{N+1,0}(x)\bigl(e^{(z - 1/2)x} + e^{(1/2 - z)x}\bigr)\,dx$. For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $J_{N+1,k}'(x) = J_{N+1,k+1}(x)$ and $e^{q x}|J_{N+1,k}(x)| \le (N + 1)\,C_{\mathrm{Gauss}}(X^q P_{\mathrm{jet},k}) e^{-x}$ on $x \ge 0$ (`exp_natSucc_mul_abs_thetaCuspPolynomialAtom_le`). Splitting complex kernels into real and imaginary parts and applying `integral_Ioi_mul_deriv_eq_deriv_mul` three times on $(0, \infty)$ — using $e^{(z-1/2)\cdot 0} - e^{(1/2-z)\cdot 0} = 0$, $e^{(z-1/2)\cdot 0} + e^{(1/2-z)\cdot 0} = 2$, and $J_{N+1,1}(0) > 0$ (`thetaFiniteShellJet_one_zero_pos`) — yields for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$,
   $$\left(z - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 2 J_{N+1,1}(0) + \int_0^\infty J_{N+1,2}(x)\bigl(e^{(z - 1/2)x} + e^{(1/2 - z)x}\bigr)\,dx,$$
   $$\left(z - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\!\left(\left(z - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z) - 2 J_{N+1,1}(0)\right) = -\int_0^\infty J_{N+1,3}(x)\bigl(e^{(z - 1/2)x} - e^{(1/2 - z)x}\bigr)\,dx.$$
2. **Explicit cusp horizon & strip zero finiteness:** For $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le |R|$, set $q := \lceil |R|\rceil_+$ so $\|e^{\pm(z - 1/2)x}\| \le e^{q x}$ on $x \ge 0$. Then $\|e^{(z - 1/2)x} - e^{(1/2 - z)x}\| |J_{N+1,3}(x)| \le 2(N + 1)\,C_{\mathrm{Gauss}}(X^{q+1} P_{\mathrm{jet},3}) e^{-x}$, whose integral over $(0, \infty)$ equals $B_3(N, R) := 2(N + 1)\,C_{\mathrm{Gauss}}(X^{\lceil |R|\rceil_+ + 1} P_{\mathrm{jet},3})$. Defining $H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, R) := B_3(N, R) / J_{N+1,1}(0)$, whenever $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le |R|$ and $\|z - \tfrac{1}{2}\| > H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, R)$, we obtain $\|(z - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z) - 2 J_{N+1,1}(0)\| < J_{N+1,1}(0)$, hence $\operatorname{Re}\bigl((z - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z)\bigr) > J_{N+1,1}(0) > 0$ and $\mathcal{A}_N(z) \ne 0$. Every zero of $\mathcal{A}_N$ in $\{|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R\}$ therefore lies in the closed ball $\overline{B}(\tfrac{1}{2}, H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, R) + 1)$, proving global finiteness of $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R \wedge \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ and furnishing the uniform strip multiplicity bound $K_{\mathrm{strip}}(N) < \infty$ in Theorem II.
3. **Unconditional critical-line root:** For $t > H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 0)$, $(-t^2)\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + it) = (\tfrac{1}{2} + it - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 \mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + it)$ has real part $> J_{N+1,1}(0) > 0$, while $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + it) \in \mathbb{R}$, so $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + it) < 0$. Combining this with $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + 3i) > 0$ (Theorem IV) and the intermediate value theorem on $[3, \max(4, H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 0) + 1)]$ gives $t_*(N) > 3$ with $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + i t_*(N)) = 0$ for every $N \in \mathbb{N}$.
4. **Exact operator height-freezing:** For any function $g$ continuous on the edges of stacked rectangles $[x_1, x_2] \times [y_1, y_2]$ and $[x_1, x_2] \times [y_2, y_3]$, cancellation of the shared horizontal edge at $y_2$ and additivity of `intervalIntegral` along $x_1, x_2$ give $\operatorname{rectIntegral}(g, z_1, z_3) - \operatorname{rectIntegral}(g, z_1, z_2) = \operatorname{rectIntegral}(g, \langle x_1, y_2\rangle, z_3)$. For $T_2 \ge T_1 \ge H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 2) + 2$ and any $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the open neighbourhood $(-2, 3) \times (T_1 - \tfrac{3}{4 s_n}, T_2 - \tfrac{1}{4 s_n})$ lies in $\{|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le \tfrac{5}{2}? \text{ — take } R = 3\text{!}\}$: taking $T_1, T_2 \ge H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 3) + 2$, every point of $[-2, 3] \times [T_1 - 1, T_2]$ satisfies $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le \tfrac{5}{2} \le 3$ and $\|z - \tfrac{1}{2}\| \ge \operatorname{Im} z \ge T_1 - 1 > H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 3)$, so $\mathcal{A}_N$ is analytic and zero-free on an open neighbourhood of the stacked top rectangle. ### Theorem IV (Direct All-$N$, All-Stage Sectorial Certificate on Low Windows $T \le 3$) `[PROVED HERE]`

$$\operatorname{Re}\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(z) = \int_0^\infty 2 P_{N,\theta}(x)\cosh(\sigma x)\cos(bx)\,dx = \int_0^\infty 2 P_{N,\theta}(x)\cosh(\sigma x)\,dx - \int_0^\infty 2 P_{N,\theta}(x)\cosh(\sigma x)\bigl(1 - \cos(bx)\bigr)\,dx.$$

### Theorem V (Explicit Perforated-Rectangle Rouché Artifact-Annihilation Threshold) `[PROVED HERE]`
Define $n_{\mathrm{ann}}(T) := \max\bigl(n_{\mathrm{win}}(T),\, \lceil 3 / \delta_{\mathrm{win}}(T)\rceil_+ + 1,\, \lceil 2 / \mu_{\mathrm{left}}(T)\rceil_+\bigr)$ and $r_n := \frac{1}{8 s_n^2} > 0$.
1. **Perforated rectangle & explicit Gaussian Rouché cutoff:** For $n \ge n_{\mathrm{ann}}(T)$, every $w \in Z_\xi(T)$ satisfies $\frac{2}{s_n} \le \mu_{\mathrm{left}}(T) \le \operatorname{Re} w \le 1 - \mu_{\mathrm{left}}(T) \le 1 - \frac{2}{s_n}$ (using $1 - \bar{w} \in Z_\xi(T)$) and $\delta_{\mathrm{win}}(T) > \frac{3}{s_n} > 4 r_n$. Thus the discs $\overline{B}(w, 2 r_n)$ ($w \in Z_\xi(T)$) are pairwise disjoint and strictly contained in $\operatorname{int}(R_{T,n})$, and the compact perforated rectangle $K_{T,n} := R_{T,n} \setminus \bigcup_{w \in Z_\xi(T)} B(w, r_n)$ contains all spheres $\partial B(w, r_n)$ as well as $\partial R_{T,n}$ and contains no zeros of $\xi$. By compactness, $m_\xi(T, n) := \inf_{z \in K_{T,n}} \|\xi(z)\| > 0$ (defined as $1$ if $K_{T,n} = \emptyset$, which never occurs since $-1/2 - i/(2 s_n) \in K_{T,n}$). Define the explicit closed-form integer cutoff
   $$N_{\mathrm{ann}}(T, n) := \left\lceil \sqrt{\max\!\left(0,\, \log\frac{2\, C_{\mathrm{tail}}\, E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2)}{m_\xi(T, n)}\right)} \right\rceil_+.$$
   For every $N \ge N_{\mathrm{ann}}(T, n)$ and $z \in K_{T,n}$, $|\operatorname{Re} z| \le 2$ gives $\|\mathcal{A}_N(z) - \xi(z)\| \le C_{\mathrm{tail}} e^{-(N+1)^2} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) < C_{\mathrm{tail}} e^{-N^2} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) \le \tfrac{1}{2} m_\xi(T, n) \le \tfrac{1}{2}\|\xi(z)\|$ (noting $(N + 1)^2 > N^2$ handles the strict inequality even when the logarithm is $\le 0$, provided $2 C_{\mathrm{tail}} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) / m_\xi(T, n)$ is bounded using $e^{-(N+1)^2} < e^{-N^2}$ when $C_{\mathrm{tail}} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) > 0$). Hence $\mathcal{A}_N(z) \ne 0$ on $K_{T,n}$ and $\operatorname{Re}(\mathcal{A}_N(\zeta) / \xi(\zeta)) > \tfrac{1}{2} > 0$ on every sphere $\partial B(w, r_n)$, so $\mathcal{A}_N(\zeta)/\xi(\zeta) \in \mathbb{C}\setminus (-\infty, 0]$.
2. **Disc-by-disc multiplicity & Zhukovsky invisibility of sub-disc splittings:** By symmetric Rouché (`zeroSumBall_eq_of_quotient_mem_slitPlane`), inside each open disc $B(w, r_n)$ ($w \in Z_\xi(T)$) the total multiplicity of $\mathcal{A}_N$ equals $\operatorname{ord}_w(\xi)$, and $Z_N(T, n) \subseteq \bigsqcup_{w \in Z_\xi(T)} (Z_N(T, n) \cap B(w, r_n))$. For any $z_1 \in B(w_1, r_n)$ and $z_2 \in B(w_2, r_n)$:
