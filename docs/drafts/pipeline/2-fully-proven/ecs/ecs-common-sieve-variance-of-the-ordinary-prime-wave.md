# Ordinary-prime variance under a common sieve


Let $D=N-Ae^{t/2}$ be the [subcritical integer quadrature](../3-formalized/ecs-subcritical-exponential-mean-square-integer-quadrature.md). For a finite prime set $P$, the translated error recursion costs at most $\prod_{p\in P}(1+p^{\lambda-1/2})/(1-p^{-1})\le 4^{|P|}$ in weighted $L^2$. Hence the cost is $e^{o(T)}$ uniformly over every family with $|P|=o(T)$, at arbitrary prime sizes. This includes all subsets of the primes up to $T$ by Chebyshev's upper bound. Let $E$ be the ordinary-prime wave minus its polar term. For $d>\Theta-1/2$, $e^{-dt}E\in L^2(0,\infty)$: take the empty set of removed modes in the [ordinary-prime square-mean theorem](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Modes/UnheatedZeroModesBeyondTheWall.lean), using $-\Re z_c\le\Theta-1/2$ for every centered zero. Write $E_P$ for the ordinary-prime wave with $P$ removed, minus the normalized integer reference sieved by the same $P$, and $G=E-E_P$. Each deleted prime $p$ contributes a translate of $e^{\lambda u}\Phi(u)$ with coefficient $(\log p)p^{\lambda-1/2}=O_\lambda(1)$. Consequently $\|e^{\lambda t}G\|_2\ll_\lambda 4^{|P|}+|P|$. For $0\le r<1-\Theta$ choose $\Theta-1/2<d<\lambda-r$ and $\lambda<1/2$. Cauchy–Schwarz on the positive ray gives
\[
 \int_0^\infty e^{rt}|E_P^2-E^2|
 \le 2\|e^{-dt}E\|_2\|e^{\lambda t}G\|_2+\|e^{\lambda t}G\|_2^2
 \ll_r 16^{|P|}.
\]
Thus the cumulative absolute variance error on $[U,\infty)$ is $O_r(16^{|P|}e^{-rU})$. For every family of $o(T)$ removed primes of arbitrary size, and $\eta>0$, it vanishes exponentially even on the entire ray $[\eta T,\infty)$. In particular the unsieved integer-centered and polar-centered squared prime waves differ by an exponentially integrable function. The positive width follows from the [unconditional zero-abscissa bound](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Decay/TheLeftTraceUpToTheRateOfTheZeros.lean). A stronger simultaneous bound sums the absolute variance errors of **all** subsets of a pool $Q$: the right side is $M_re^{-rU}\sum_{P\subseteq Q}16^{|P|}=M_re^{-rU}17^{|Q|}$. Thus one may also select a different subset at each observation time from any pool of $o(T)$ primes.

**Moving moduli.** For $s>1$ the sum over all finite prime sets of $16^{|P|}(\prod_{p\in P}p)^{-s}$ is the convergent product $\prod_p(1+16p^{-s})$. Tonelli therefore bounds the exponentially weighted total absolute variance discrepancy of every common sieve at once, with weight $(\prod_{p\in P}p)^{-s}$. If the prime set may be chosen separately at each time and $\prod_{p\in P(t)}p\le e^{\gamma t}$, its variance error is exponentially integrable with every weight $e^{bt}$, $0\le b<1-\Theta-\gamma$. Indeed choose $s>1$ and $r<1-\Theta$ with $b+s\gamma\le r$, then dominate the selected term by the full positive sum. This permits sieving by coprimality to any measurable integer modulus $1\le q(t)\le e^{\gamma t}$, including a choice based on the observed prime wave. For the squared wave perturbation $|E-E_P|^2$ itself the available moment range is $r<1$, since $r=2\lambda$. The identical positive-sum argument therefore gives an integrable common envelope for $e^{bt}|E-E_P|^2$ for every $b+\gamma<1$, uniformly over all admissible moduli at each time.

For the larger sieve $p\le T^2$, choose $\lambda=0$. Its $L^2$ multiplier is at most the same $\prod_{p\in P}(1-p^{-1/2})^{-1}=e^{o(T)}$ as in the [quadratic-horizon sieve](../3-formalized/ecs-growing-sieve-for-integer-centering.md). The reverse triangle inequality therefore bounds the difference of the two **square roots of the integrated variances** by $e^{o(T)}$. On every scale where either $L^2(0,T)$ norm exceeds $e^{\delta T}$ for a fixed $\delta>0$, the ratio of the squared integrals tends to one, uniformly in the sieve. Deleting the true primes in this larger set contributes only $O(T^2)$ to the $L^2$ norm by the single-prime estimate.
