## Theorem

On \(V=\mathbb C^2\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 I(z,w)&=(iz,-iw),\\
 K(z,w)&=(-w,z),\\
 J(z,w)&=(-\bar w,\bar z),\\
 h(z,w)&=|z|^2+|w|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Then
\[
 I^2=K^2=-1,\qquad IK=-KI,\qquad IJ=JI,\qquad KJ=JK,
\]
and \(I,K,J\) preserve \(h\).  Moreover, there is no anti-linear map
\(C:V\to V\) satisfying
\[
 CI=IC,\qquad CK=KC,\qquad C^2=1.
\]

## Proof of no real reduction

Let \(e_1=(1,0)\), \(e_2=(0,1)\), and write
\(C(e_1)=(a,b)\).  From anti-linearity and \(CI=IC\),
\[
 -i(a,b)=(ia,-ib),
\]
so \(a=0\).  From \(K(e_1)=e_2\) and \(CK=KC\),
\[
 C(e_2)=K(0,b)=(-b,0).
\]
Therefore
\[
 C^2(e_1)
 =C(0,b)
 =\bar b\,C(e_2)
 =(-|b|^2,0),
\]
which cannot equal \(e_1\).  This proves the claim without continuity,
spectral coordinates, or RH.

The remaining identities follow directly from the displayed formulas.

representation of \(Q_8\).  Its commuting anti-linear structure has
quaternionic square \(-1\), not real square \(+1\).
