# Cancellation across the square-root product interval

**PVC · Paper theorem.** At each divided horizon $X$, set $D=\lfloor1+\log X\rfloor$, $Y=\min(X,(D+1)\lfloor\sqrt X\rfloor)$, $U=\lfloor X^{1/2+10^{-9}}\rfloor$, and $F=\max(2(D+1),\lfloor X^{1/200}\rfloor)$. In the two-width approximation, retain only ordinary first primes $p>F$ with $(p,v)=1$ and $\max(Y,U)<pv\le X$. The error over the divided horizons is $O(\|b_V\|_1(1+\log N)^6\sqrt N)$.

**Proof.** On each horizon the [coprime ordinary-prime row](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/CoprimeOrdinaryPrimeFactorsAtBothWidths.lean) approximates $\psi(X)-X$. [Small first primes](../3-formalized/pvc-cancellation-for-small-first-primes-at-all-products.md) cost $O((1+\log X)^6\sqrt X)$. For $p>F$, removing coprimality at either product endpoint costs $O((D+1)D\sqrt X(1+\log X))$ by the signed [shared-prime slice bound](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/CancellationOnRepeatedPrimeFactors.lean) and Mertens.

Raising the lower product endpoint from $Y$ to $\max(Y,U)$ removes precisely $Y<pv\le U$. The [short complementary factors](pvc-short-complements-through-the-square-root-product-window.md) $v\le X^{10^{-6}}$ in this interval cost $O((D+1)X^{0.499701})$. For the other factors apply the [factorial product-window estimate](pvc-centered-fractional-parts-across-a-product-window.md) with $H=\lfloor X^{1/5000000}\rfloor$. Both factor lower endpoints exceed $X^{10^{-6}}$, so $H^4$ fits below the first and $H$ below the second. For all sufficiently large $X$, write $r=X^{1/25000000}$. Then $H\ge r^4$, $128(128D^2+1)\le r$, $128(128D^2+1)U^2\le HX$, and $16X\le HY^2$. Also $U/H^{1/4}\le\sqrt X$. The stencil mass, prime weight and complementary weight each cost $O(1+\log X)$ and the two dyadic subdivisions cost two further logarithms. Finitely many smaller horizons are absorbed uniformly by their total absolute weights. Restoring coprimality above the new endpoint and applying the finite cofactor majorant gives the stated bound.
