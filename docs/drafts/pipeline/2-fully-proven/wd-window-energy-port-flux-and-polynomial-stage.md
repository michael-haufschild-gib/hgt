### 1. Port-Hermitian functional-equation decomposition and unweighted $L^2(0,\infty)$ left-port decay

Put $L_N(z)=-M_{N,1}(z)/A_N(z)=A_N'(z)/A_N(z)$. Since $A_N(1-z)=A_N(z)$ (`finiteThetaAmplitude_one_sub`) and $\overline{A_N(\bar z)}=A_N(z)$ (`finiteThetaAmplitude_conj`), differentiating gives $A_N'(1-\bar z)=-\overline{A_N'(z)}$ and $A_N(1-\bar z)=\overline{A_N(z)}$, hence:
$$L_N(1-\bar z) = -\overline{L_N(z)}\qquad(\forall z\in\mathbb C).$$

For $y_1,y_2,t\in\mathbb R$ and $\sigma,a,b\in\mathbb R$, define:
$$\mathcal F_{\mathrm{vert}}(\sigma,y_1,y_2,N,t)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{y_1}^{y_2}e^{t(\sigma-1/2+iy)}L_N(\sigma+iy)\,dy,$$
$$\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\mathcal F_{\mathrm{vert}}(3/2,y_1,y_2,N,t),\qquad \mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)=-\mathcal F_{\mathrm{vert}}(-1/2,y_1,y_2,N,t),$$
$$\mathcal F_{\mathrm{horiz}}(a,b,y_1,y_2,N,t)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\left(\int_a^b e^{t(x-1/2+iy_1)}L_N(x+iy_1)\,dx - \int_a^b e^{t(x-1/2+iy_2)}L_N(x+iy_2)\,dx\right),$$
$$\mathcal P_+(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)+\mathcal F_{\mathrm{horiz}}(1/2,3/2,y_1,y_2,N,t),$$
$$\mathcal P_-(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)+\mathcal F_{\mathrm{horiz}}(-1/2,1/2,y_1,y_2,N,t).$$

**Theorem 2.1 (Port-Hermitian functional-equation decomposition).** *For all $y_1,y_2\in\mathbb R$, $N\in\mathbb N$, and $t\in\mathbb R$, assuming $A_N$ has no zeros on the horizontal segments $[-1/2,3/2]\times\{y_1,y_2\}$ (so horizontal integrals split at $x=1/2$):*
1. $\mathrm{centeredThetaExpSum}(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)+\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)+\mathcal F_{\mathrm{horiz}}(-1/2,3/2,y_1,y_2,N,t)$ (holds unconditionally without any nonvanishing hypothesis via `rectIntegral_eq_edges`);
2. $\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\overline{\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}(y_1,y_2,N,-t)}$ (holds unconditionally for all $t\in\mathbb R$);
3. $\mathcal P_-(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\overline{\mathcal P_+(y_1,y_2,N,-t)}$ and $\mathrm{centeredThetaExpSum}(y_1,y_2,N,t)=\mathcal P_+(y_1,y_2,N,t)+\overline{\mathcal P_+(y_1,y_2,N,-t)}$.

*Proof.* (1) Expand `rectIntegral` via `rectIntegral_eq_edges` and cancel $(2\pi i)^{-1}\cdot i=(2\pi)^{-1}$ on the vertical edges. (2) At $z=3/2+iy$, $1-\bar z=-1/2+iy$, so $-L_N(-1/2+iy)=\overline{L_N(3/2+iy)}$ and $e^{t(-1+iy)}=\overline{e^{(-t)(1+iy)}}$; conjugate inside the interval integral. (3) Substitute $x\mapsto 1-x$ on $[-1/2,1/2]$ (`integral_comp_sub_left`): at $z=x+iy_j$ ($x\in[1/2,3/2]$), $1-x+iy_j=1-\overline{(x-iy_j)}$? Careful: $1-\overline{(x+iy_j)}=(1-x)+iy_j$! Yes, $\overline{x+iy_j}=x-iy_j$, so $1-\overline{(x+iy_j)}=(1-x)+iy_j$ preserves $+iy_j$! Thus $L_N((1-x)+iy_j)=-\overline{L_N(x+iy_j)}$, $e^{t((1-x)-1/2+iy_j)}=\overline{e^{(-t)(x-1/2+iy_j)}}$, and $\overline{(2\pi i)^{-1}}=-(2\pi i)^{-1}$ cancels the minus sign from $-L_N$, yielding $\mathcal F_{\mathrm{horiz}}(-1/2,1/2,y_1,y_2,N,t)=\overline{\mathcal F_{\mathrm{horiz}}(1/2,3/2,y_1,y_2,N,-t)}$. Split $\int_{-1/2}^{3/2}=\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}+\int_{1/2}^{3/2}$ by continuity of the integrand off zeros. $\square$

**Theorem 2.2 (Unconditional unweighted $L^2(0,\infty)$ decay of $\mathcal P_-$ and pure right-port Laplace equivalence).** *Let $y_1\le y_2$ and assume $A_N\ne 0$ on $\partial([-1/2,3/2]\times[y_1,y_2])$. Then:*
1. *There exists $C_L>0$ such that $\|\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(y_1,y_2,N,t)\|\le C_L e^{-t}$ and $\|\mathcal P_-(y_1,y_2,N,t)\|\le C_L/(t+1)$ for all $t\ge 0$; in particular $\int_0^\infty\|\mathcal P_-(y_1,y_2,N,t)\|^2\,dt<\infty$.*
2. *For every $x\ge 0$,*
   $$\operatorname{IntegrableOn}\!\bigl(t\mapsto e^{-xt}\|\mathrm{centeredThetaExpSum}(y_1,y_2,N,t)\|^2,\,(0,\infty)\bigr) \Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{IntegrableOn}\!\bigl(t\mapsto e^{-xt}\|\mathcal P_+(y_1,y_2,N,t)\|^2,\,(0,\infty)\bigr).$$

*Proof.* (1) Along $x=-1/2$, $y\mapsto L_N(-1/2+iy)$ is continuous on $[y_1,y_2]$, hence bounded by $M_v$; $|e^{t(-1+iy)}|=e^{-t}$ gives $\|\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(t)\|\le \frac{y_2-y_1}{2\pi}M_v e^{-t}$. Along $x\in[-1/2,1/2]$ at fixed $y\in\{y_1,y_2\}$, $g_y(x)=L_N(x+iy)$ is $C^1$ because $A_N$ is entire and nonvanishing on $[-1/2,1/2]\times\{y\}$. For $t>0$, fundamental theorem of calculus on $H(x)=t^{-1}e^{t(x-1/2+iy)}g_y(x)$ gives
$$\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}e^{t(x-1/2+iy)}g_y(x)\,dx = \frac{e^{tiy}g_y(1/2)-e^{t(-1+iy)}g_y(-1/2)}{t} - \frac{1}{t}\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}e^{t(x-1/2+iy)}g_y'(x)\,dx.$$
Since $x-1/2\le 0$ on $[-1/2,1/2]$ implies $|e^{t(x-1/2+iy)}|\le 1$ for $t\ge 0$, this integral is bounded both by $\sup|g_y|$ (for $0\le t\le 1$) and by $(2\sup|g_y|+\sup|g_y'|)/t$ (for $t\ge 1$), hence $\le C_H/(t+1)$ for all $t\ge 0$. Since $e^{-t}\le 1/(t+1)$ for $t\ge 0$, $\|\mathcal P_-(t)\|\le C_L/(t+1)$, and $(t+1)^{-2}\in L^1(0,\infty)$.
(2) For $x\ge 0$ and $t>0$, $e^{-xt}\le 1$, so $e^{-xt}\|\mathcal P_-(t)\|^2\le\|\mathcal P_-(t)\|^2\in L^1(0,\infty)$. Since $\mathcal Z_N=\mathcal P_++\mathcal P_-$ and $\mathcal P_+=\mathcal Z_N-\mathcal P_-$, the pointwise inequality $\|u\pm v\|^2\le 2\|u\|^2+2\|v\|^2$ together with `Integrable.mono'` yields both directions. $\square$

### 2. Unconditional $\Omega(e^t/t)$ exponential blow-up of $\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}+\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}$ on every Burnol unit window

**Lemma 2.3 (Periodic mean-square identity and twice-IBP lower bound).** *Let $y_1<y_2$ and let $f:[y_1,y_2]\to\mathbb C$ be $C^2$. Put $J(t)=\int_{y_1}^{y_2}e^{ity}f(y)\,dy$ and $P=2\pi/(y_2-y_1)>0$.*
1. *For every $T\in\mathbb R$, $\int_T^{T+P}\|e^{ity_2}f(y_2)-e^{ity_1}f(y_1)\|^2\,dt = P(\|f(y_1)\|^2+\|f(y_2)\|^2)$; hence there exists $t\in[T,T+P]$ with $\|e^{ity_2}f(y_2)-e^{ity_1}f(y_1)\|\ge\sqrt{\|f(y_1)\|^2+\|f(y_2)\|^2}$.*
2. *For all $t\ne 0$, integrating by parts twice gives*
   $$J(t) = \frac{e^{ity_2}f(y_2)-e^{ity_1}f(y_1)}{it} + \frac{e^{ity_2}f'(y_2)-e^{ity_1}f'(y_1)-\int_{y_1}^{y_2}e^{ity}f''(y)\,dy}{t^2}.$$
   *Consequently, if $(f(y_1),f(y_2))\ne(0,0)$, putting $\mu=\frac12\sqrt{\|f(y_1)\|^2+\|f(y_2)\|^2}>0$, for all sufficiently large $T>0$ there exists $t\in[T,T+P]$ with $\|J(t)\|\ge \mu/t$.*

*Proof.* (1) Expand $\|e^{ity_2}f(y_2)-e^{ity_1}f(y_1)\|^2=\|f(y_2)\|^2+\|f(y_1)\|^2-2\operatorname{Re}(f(y_2)\overline{f(y_1)}e^{it(y_2-y_1)})$. Since $\frac{d}{dt}\frac{e^{it(y_2-y_1)}}{i(y_2-y_1)}=e^{it(y_2-y_1)}$ and $e^{i(T+P)(y_2-y_1)}=e^{iT(y_2-y_1)+2\pi i}=e^{iT(y_2-y_1)}$, the cross integral over $[T,T+P]$ vanishes identically. If $\|B(t)\|<\sqrt{\|f(y_1)\|^2+\|f(y_2)\|^2}$ for all $t\in[T,T+P]$, integrating $\|B(t)\|^2$ strictly violates the mean identity.
(2) Differentiating $G_1(y)=\frac{e^{ity}}{it}f(y)+\frac{e^{ity}}{t^2}f'(y)$ gives $G_1'(y)=e^{ity}f(y)+\frac{e^{ity}}{t^2}f''(y)$, so FTC yields the identity immediately. Bound the $t^{-2}$ numerator by $M_2=\|f'(y_2)\|+\|f'(y_1)\|+(y_2-y_1)\sup_{[y_1,y_2]}\|f''\|$; for $T>\max(1,M_2/\mu)$ and $t\in[T,T+P]$ from (1), $\|J(t)\|\ge\frac{2\mu}{t}-\frac{M_2}{t^2}\ge\frac{\mu}{t}$. $\square$

**Theorem 2.4 (Strict positivity of $\operatorname{Re}L_N(3/2+iy)$ and unconditional $\Omega(e^t/t)$ flux blow-up on every Burnol unit window).** *There exists an explicit constant $K_{\mathrm{flux}}\in\mathbb N$ ($\mathrm{doorOpenerSqrtConst}\le K_{\mathrm{flux}}$) such that with $\mathrm{fluxSqrtPrefix}(m)=K_{\mathrm{flux}}(\lfloor\sqrt m\rfloor+1)$:*

   $$\operatorname{Re}(\mathrm{logDeriv}\,\xi(3/2+iy)) \ge \frac{1}{9 + 4(y-\operatorname{Im}\rho_0)^2}.$$
2. *For every $m\in\mathbb N$, every $N\ge\mathrm{fluxSqrtPrefix}(m)$, and both $y\in\{y_1(m),y_2(m)\}$, $\operatorname{Re}L_N(3/2+iy)>0$.*
3. *For every $m\in\mathbb N$ and every $N\ge\mathrm{fluxSqrtPrefix}(m)$, there exist $c_{m,N}>0$ and $T_{m,N}>0$ such that for every $T\ge T_{m,N}$, there exists $t\in[T,\,T+2\pi/(y_2(m)-y_1(m))]$ satisfying BOTH*

   *and*
   $$\|\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}(y_1(m),y_2(m),N,t)+\mathcal P_-(y_1(m),y_2(m),N,t)\| \ge \frac{c_{m,N}e^t}{t}.$$

*Proof.* (1) By `XiLogDerivRePositiveStrict` (`logDeriv_xi_eq_partialFraction`, `xiZeroCopyLineConjEquiv`), $2\operatorname{Re}(\mathrm{logDeriv}\,\xi(s))=\sum'_{q\in\mathrm{XiZeroCopy}}\operatorname{Re}(f(q)+f(q'))$ where each summand $\operatorname{Re}(f(q)+f(q'))=\operatorname{Re}((s-\rho_q)^{-1}+(s-(1-\bar\rho_q))^{-1})\ge 0$ on $\operatorname{Re}s\ge 1$. Retaining the single term $q_0$ (`Summable.le_tsum`) at $s=3/2+iy$, both $3/2-\operatorname{Re}\rho_0\in(1/2,3/2)$ and $1/2+\operatorname{Re}\rho_0\in(1/2,3/2)$, so each of $\operatorname{Re}(s-\rho_0)^{-1}$ and $\operatorname{Re}(s-(1-\bar\rho_0))^{-1}$ is $\ge \frac{1/2}{(3/2)^2+(y-\operatorname{Im}\rho_0)^2}=\frac{2}{9+4(y-\operatorname{Im}\rho_0)^2}$. Dividing by $2$ yields (1).
(2) On $|\operatorname{Re}z|\le 2$, $\|\xi(z)\|\le E_2$ and $\|\xi'(z)\|\le E_2$ ($E_2=\mathrm{thetaAmplitudeEnvelope}(2)$), while $\|A_N(z)-\xi(z)\|\le\mathrm{thetaStripTailBound}(N)$ and $\|-M_{N,1}(z)-\xi'(z)\|\le\mathrm{thetaStripTailBound}(N)$. When $\mathrm{thetaStripTailBound}(N)\le\frac12 c_{\mathrm{good}}e^{-(m+3)}$, $\|A_N(z_j)\|\ge\frac12 c_{\mathrm{good}}e^{-(m+3)}$, so
$$\|L_N(z_j)-\mathrm{logDeriv}\,\xi(z_j)\| \le \frac{4 E_2\,\mathrm{thetaStripTailBound}(N)}{c_{\mathrm{good}}^2 e^{-2(m+3)}}.$$
Choosing $K_{\mathrm{flux}}$ so $(N+1)^2\ge K_{\mathrm{flux}}^2(m+1)$ beats $2(m+3)+\log(9+4(m+3+|\operatorname{Im}\rho_0|)^2)+C$ makes $\|L_N(z_j)-\mathrm{logDeriv}\,\xi(z_j)\|<\frac12\operatorname{Re}(\mathrm{logDeriv}\,\xi(z_j))$, hence $\operatorname{Re}L_N(z_j)>0$.
(3) Apply Lemma 2.3 to $f(y)=(2\pi)^{-1}L_N(3/2+iy)$ on $[y_1(m),y_2(m)]$ ($y_2(m)-y_1(m)\ge 2+1/12>0$, $f(y_2(m))\ne 0$ by (2)), giving $\|\mathcal F_{\mathrm{right}}(t)\|=e^t\|J(t)\|\ge \frac{\mu e^t}{t}$ along $t\in[T,T+P]$, while Theorem 2.2(1) gives $\|\mathcal F_{\mathrm{left}}(t)\|\le C_L e^{-t}$ and $\|\mathcal P_-(t)\|\le C_L/(t+1)\le\frac{\mu e^t}{2t}$ for large $T$. $\square$
