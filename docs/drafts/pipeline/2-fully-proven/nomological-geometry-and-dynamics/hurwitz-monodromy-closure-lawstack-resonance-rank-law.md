## 1. Marked monodromy object

Let `I` be finite, let `s_j in C` satisfy `Re(s_j)>1`, and put

\[
 q_j=e^{-2\pi i s_j},
 \qquad
 T=(\mathbb C^\times)^I,
 \qquad
 q=(q_j)_{j\in I}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let `Z` act on `T` by translation `m*x=q^m x`. Define:

\[
 \mathfrak M_q=[T/\mathbb Z],
 \qquad
 H_q=\overline{\langle q\rangle}^{\rm Zar}\subset T,
\tag{2}
\]

and the resonance lattice

\[
 R_q={u\in\mathbb Z^I:q^u=1\}.
\tag{3}
\]

The affine character observer is

\[
 A_q=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C[T]^{\mathbb Z}.
\tag{4}
\]

This uses the actual common-loop action of the Hurwitz branch monodromy. It is not an arbitrary
product packaging of exponent coordinates.

## 2. Algebraic lawstack theorem

### Theorem 2.1 — orbit/character rank complementarity

The following hold:

1. invariant Laurent monomials are exactly those indexed by `R_q`, hence

   \[
    \mathbb C[T]^{\mathbb Z}=\mathbb C[R_q],
    \qquad
    A_q\cong\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C[R_q];
   \tag{5}
   \]

2. `R_q` is the annihilator character lattice of `H_q`, and

   \[
    \dim H_q+\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}R_q=|I|;
   \tag{6}
   \]

3. the published monodromy-character separation condition is equivalent to each of

   \[
    R_q=0,
    \qquad H_q=T,
    \qquad A_q=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C;
   \tag{7}
   \]

4. for `L>=1`, the Zariski closure of the independent branch-loop grid

   \[
    \{(q^{m_0},\ldots,q^{m_{L-1}}):m_n\in\mathbb N\}
   \]

   is `H_q^L`.

#### Proof

The Laurent coordinate ring of `T` has basis `{X^u:u in Z^I}`. Translation by `q` sends
`X^u` to `q^u X^u`, so a Laurent polynomial is invariant exactly when every monomial in its support
has `q^u=1`. This proves `(5)`.

A character `X^u` is trivial on the cyclic subgroup generated by `q` exactly when `q^u=1`; by
Zariski continuity, this is equivalent to being trivial on `H_q`. Hence `R_q` is the character
lattice of the quotient `T/H_q`. The standard character-lattice exact sequence gives

\[
 0\longrightarrow R_q\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^I
 \longrightarrow X^*(H_q)\longrightarrow0
\]

at the level of ranks, proving `(6)`.

The monodromy-character condition says that `alpha->q^alpha` is injective on `N^I`. If it fails,
`q^alpha=q^beta` gives the nonzero resonance `alpha-beta in R_q`. Conversely, write any nonzero
`u in R_q` as `u=u_+-u_-` with disjoint `u_+,u_- in N^I`; then
`q^(u_+)=q^(u_-)`, so separation fails. Thus separation is equivalent to `R_q=0`, which by `(6)`
is equivalent to `H_q=T`; `(5)` gives the last equivalence in `(7)`.

Finally the nonnegative cyclic orbit `{q^m:m in N}` has the same Zariski closure as the cyclic
subgroup: every Laurent polynomial vanishing for all nonnegative powers gives a finite linear
recurrence in distinct exponential characters and therefore vanishes on all integer powers. The
closure is `H_q`. Independent branch-loop counts give the Cartesian product of these dense sets,
whose closure is `H_q^L`. ∎

## 3. Differential realization theorem

For jet height `N`, let `L=N+1` and write the Hurwitz ladder

\[
 \mathcal L_N(s)=
 \bigl(\zeta(s_j+k,a)\bigr)_{j\in I,0\le k\le N}.
\tag{8}
\]

The published simultaneous monodromy theorem constructs the branch deformation

\[
 F_{j,k}^{c}(a)=\zeta(s_j+k,a)
 +\sum_{n=0}^{N}(c_{j,n}-1)(a+n)^{-s_j-k}
\tag{9}
\]

and proves that any polynomial relation on the original ladder vanishes on the entire loop grid
`c_(j,n)=q_j^(m_n)`. At fixed `a`, the deformation-to-ladder linear map is block diagonal with
invertible Vandermonde blocks.

### Theorem 3.1 — full monodromy closure forces joint differential freedom

If `R_q=0`, equivalently `H_q=T`, then for every `N` the entries of `(8)` are algebraically
independent over `C(a)`. Equivalently, the functions `a->zeta(s_j,a)` are differentially
algebraically independent over `C(a)`.

#### Proof

By Theorem 2.1, the loop grid is Zariski dense in `T^(N+1)`, hence in the ambient affine parameter
space after taking its closure. The branch-deformation theorem transports any polynomial relation
to that grid. The block-Vandermonde map in `(9)` is invertible, so the transported relation
vanishes on a Zariski-dense subset of the full ladder-value space and is zero. Canonical
denominator clearing upgrades `C[a]` coefficients to `C(a)`. Finally

\[
 \partial_a^k\zeta(s,a)=(-1)^k(s)_k\zeta(s+k,a)
\]

is an invertible diagonal change because `Re(s)>1`, proving differential algebraic independence.
This is exactly the published monodromy-character proof. ∎

### Resonant boundary

If `R_q` has positive rank, Theorem 2.1 gives

\[
 \dim H_q=|I|-\operatorname{rank}R_q<|I|,
\tag{10}
\]

so cyclic monodromy explores only the proper deformation variety `H_q^(N+1)`, while `A_q` retains
the residual character torus of rank `rank R_q`. This is a sharp boundary of the proof, **not a
converse**: resonance does not by itself produce a differential relation among Hurwitz functions.
