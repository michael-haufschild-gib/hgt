# The full prime ideal at diagonal scale

Let $P_*(a)$ be the sum of the integrated contacts against $\Lambda(k)$ over the original window of length $L$, and $V(a)$ the ordinary prime floor loss. For every signed integer spline divisor,
\[
 |\log d_{\rm full}(a)+V(a)-P_*(a)|\le C\sqrt L.
\]
The constant is absolute. In particular, on $mD_n$ the discrepancy is $O(n)$ uniformly in $m$. This compares the full generator with the full signed prime term while retaining only the ordinary floors.

Indeed, adding back every floor gives the sum of original signed readings. The absolute quadrature error is bounded by $C\sum_{k\le L}\Lambda(k)/\sqrt{k}\le34C\sqrt L$. The remaining proper-power floors have total weight at most $\psi(L)-\theta(L)\ll\sqrt L$, since each fractional part lies in $[0,1)$. The integrated contact scales exactly by $m^2$, and the square-conductor horizon satisfies $L\ll n^2$.

On $mD_n$ the positive pole vanishes and the sampled pole product costs $O(m)$. Hence the full logarithmic section surplus differs from the ordinary floor loss plus the jet radius minus $m^2P_*(D_n)$ by $O(n+m)$.
