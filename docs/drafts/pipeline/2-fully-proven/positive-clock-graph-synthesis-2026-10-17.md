## Revised theorem and paper proof, 2026-10-17

The distribution-dual/H1 attack above is superseded by positive smoothing.
No differentiability at an arithmetic wall is assumed.

Write `Y = criticalAbelLogProfile`, and let `G` be the original complete real
CHMD source graph. Fix a compact C2 cutoff chi before selecting clocks or
primes. Put `q_t(x) = chi(x) Y(t-x)`, viewed in G. For every real T, the closed
positive cone generated by `{q_t : t > T}` contains every compact C2 source
v satisfying `chi * v = v`. In particular it contains both signs of each such
v. The cutoff can be chosen nonnegative and equal to one on any prescribed
compact source support. Together with compact-core density, this controls
every vector of G by these literal complete-source clocks, with a cutoff
chosen before the large prime scale.

For each such fixed chi and v, every positive error tolerance, and every late
clock threshold, there is a scale N0 such that for every N >= N0 a finite set
of distinct actual primes synthesizes v in the G norm via the atoms
`(sqrt N / p) q_(log(N/p))`; the sum of squared G norms of the selected atoms
is smaller than the same tolerance. The weights remain the literal common-N
prime weights, not independently adjustable weights. This also controls the
continuous full signed Weil form, including both polar moments.

1. **Literal admission.** Extend the actual velocity by zero on the negative
   half-line. The primitive identity in
   `MobiusDivisorCriticalLogProfilePrimitive` and the exact zero identity in
   `MobiusDivisorCriticalCorrelationRegularity` give
   `Y(t) = integral_(0..t) g` for this globally integrable g. For every ordered
   finite partition, the sum of absolute increments of Y is at most the
   integral of abs g: bound each primitive increment, telescope the adjacent
   integrals of abs g, and enlarge to the whole line. Thus Y has finite global
   variation. Translation/reflection preserves this variation. The product
   partition identity gives
   `Var(chi Y_t) <= sup(abs chi) Var(Y) + sup(abs Y) Var(chi)`.
   A compact C2 cutoff has finite variation by its integrable derivative.
   `ThetaBVArithmeticAdmission.bvLift` therefore admits every q_t into G.

2. **The needed topology.** For a BV function f with sup norm at most B and
   variation V, the variation primitive w gives
   `abs(f(x+h)-f(x)) <= w(x+h)-w(x)` for h >= 0. Multiplying by the bound 2B
   and applying the existing monotone translation integral yields
   `integral (f(x+h)-f(x))^2 <= 4 B V h`.
   Let M be the finite total mass of massMeasure, P the finite total mass of
   the full prime-edge measure, C a global theta bound, and
   J the finite first Levy moment supplied by ThetaBVArithmeticAdmission.
   The literal graph definition then gives
   `norm_G(f)^2 <= (M + 4 P) B^2 + 4 C^2 J B V`.
   Consequently uniform convergence with a common variation bound implies
   graph convergence. Differences have variation bounded by the sum of the
   two variation bounds. This proves graph continuity of t -> q_t: on the
   compact support of chi, ordinary continuity of Y gives locally uniform
   parameter convergence, and step 1 gives a common variation bound.
   This estimate retains the entire prime measure. Independently,
   `CHMDStationaryGroundDomain.memLp_full_difference_iff_stationary` confirms
   that there is no additional hidden prime-domain condition.

3. **Bounded positive regularization.** Fix a nonnegative smooth compact
   mollifier rho of integral one and radius delta. Choose a compact interval K
   containing supp chi plus that radius. Extend f in C(K) by zero and set
   `S_rho(f) = chi * (rho * f)` as an actual source function. It is compact C2.
   Its sup norm is bounded by `sup(abs chi) norm(f)`. Its variation is bounded
   by
   `(Var(chi) + norm(chi)_1 norm(rho')_1) norm(f)`.
   These follow directly from the product derivative, differentiation of the
   smooth convolution kernel, and Tonelli. Step 2 makes S_rho a bounded
   linear map C(K) -> G. Zero extension causes no boundary difficulty: only
   the enlarged interval K is sampled where chi is nonzero.

4. **Preserving the positive cone.** Let C_T be the closed positive cone in
   the theorem. For t > T+delta, the exact identity in G is
   `S_rho(Y_t) = integral rho(s) q_(t+s) ds`.
   Step 2 ensures this is a genuine compactly supported continuous Bochner
   integral. Its source coordinate has the displayed convolution value by
   Fubini; injectivity of the source projection identifies the graph vector.
   Positive integration preserves a closed convex cone (equivalently use
   positive quadrature and graph continuity). Hence S_rho(Y_t) belongs to C_T.
   The existing positive clock-cone density in C(K), transported from [0,a]
   to K by a coordinate translation, and boundedness of S_rho show that
   S_rho(f) belongs to C_T for every f in C(K).

5. **Removing regularization.** For the compact C2 target v with chi*v=v,
   `chi*(rho_delta*v)` converges uniformly to v as delta decreases to zero.
   Its variation is bounded uniformly in delta by
   `sup(abs v) Var(chi) + sup(abs chi) norm(v')_1`;
   use integral-one positivity, differentiating v rather than the mollifier.
   Step 2 gives convergence in G. Closedness of C_T proves the theorem.
   This does not assume the desired graph approximation as a premise.

6. **Actual primes.** The map r -> r^(-1) q_(-log r) is norm-continuous for
   r>0 by step 2. Choose a finite positive clock approximation from step 5,
   and apply the already verified Banach-valued
   `PrimePreparation.eventually_exists_prime_vector_synthesis`.
   Its distinct-prime sampling and diagonal-cost conclusion apply to G
   itself. Fix the target, cutoff, finite clocks and tolerance before N.
   No further analytic or arithmetic assumption is introduced.
