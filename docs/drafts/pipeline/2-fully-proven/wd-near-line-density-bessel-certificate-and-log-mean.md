### 1. All-heights Littlewood identity and the $8$-way equivalence for $(L)$ and $\mathbb E\log|\zeta M_S|=o(1)$

**Lemma 1.1 (Two-sided all-heights Littlewood edge budget).** *There exist $C,K>0$ such that for every real $T\ge 21$,*
$$\left|\int_T^{2T}\log|\zeta(1/2+it)|\,dt - 2\pi\sum_{\substack{\rho\in\mathrm{shellZeros}(T,2T)\\ \operatorname{Re}\rho>1/2}} m_\rho\left(\operatorname{Re}\rho-\frac12\right)\right| \le C + K\log(2T).$$

*Proof.* Fix $T\ge 21$ and let $F=\mathrm{stripFinsetPos}(2T+1)$. Since $F$ is finite, the set of positive distances $\{|\operatorname{Im}z - T| : z\in F,\,\operatorname{Im}z\ne T\}\cup\{|\operatorname{Im}z - 2T| : z\in F,\,\operatorname{Im}z\ne 2T\}$ has a positive minimum (or is empty), giving $\delta_0\in(0,1/2)$ such that $F$ has no element with $\operatorname{Im}z\in[T-\delta_0,T)\cup(T,T+\delta_0]\cup[2T-\delta_0,2T)\cup(2T,2T+\delta_0]$. For every $\delta\in(0,\delta_0]$, put $T_1=T-\delta\ge 20$ and $T_2=2T+\delta$. Because $\zeta(s)\ne 0$ for $\operatorname{Re}s\ge 1$ and every strip zero with $\operatorname{Im}z\in(0,2T+1]$ belongs to $F$, $\zeta(x+iT_1)\ne 0$ and $\zeta(x+iT_2)\ne 0$ for all $x\in[1/2,15/2]$, while the off-line zeros of $\zeta$ in $[1/2,15/2]\times[T_1,T_2]$ coincide for every $\delta\in(0,\delta_0]$ with $F_T=\{z\in\mathrm{stripFinsetPos}(2T) : T\le\operatorname{Im}z\text{ and }\operatorname{Re}z>1/2\}$. Applying `exists_zetaLine_littlewood_edge_budget` on $[T_1,T_2]$ and letting $\delta\to 0^+$ by continuity of $x\mapsto\int_0^x\log|\zeta(1/2+it)|\,dt$ (`intervalIntegrable_log_norm_riemannZeta_line`) bounds the difference against $\sum_{z\in F_T}m_z(\operatorname{Re}z-1/2)$ by $C_0+K_0\log(2T)$. The slice $\operatorname{Im}z=T$ lies in $\mathrm{shellZeros}(T-1/2,T+1/2)$, whose total multiplicity is $\le 16(|c_2|+\log(T+5))$ (`shortShell_orderSum_le_log`) with $\operatorname{Re}z-1/2<1/2$, absorbing into $C+K\log(2T)$. $\square$

**Lemma 1.2 (Dyadic exponential off-line tail moment).** *There exist $B,K\ge 0$ such that eventually for $T\to\infty$, for all $c\ge 2$,*
$$\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathrm{shellZeros}(T,2T)\\ \operatorname{Re}z\ge 1/2+c/\log(4T)}} m_z\left(\operatorname{Re}z-\frac12\right) \le B e^{-c/28} T + K\log^2(4T).$$

*Proof.* Apply `exists_offLineTailMoment_window_le_exp` at $U=4T$, giving $T_1\in(T,T+1)$ and $T_2\in(2T-1,2T)$ on which the off-line sum in $[T_1,T_2]$ is $\le B e^{-c/28}(T_2-T_1)+K_0\log^2(4T)\le B e^{-c/28}T+K_0\log^2(4T)$. The two short shells $\mathrm{shellZeros}(T,T_1)$ and $\mathrm{shellZeros}(T_2,2T)$ each have length $\le 1$, hence total multiplicity $O(\log(4T))$ (`shortShell_orderSum_le_log`) and horizontal offset $<1/2$, contributing $O(\log(4T))\le O(\log^2(4T))$. $\square$

**Lemma 1.3 (Truncated Euler-product outer mollifier log-mean).** *For any finite set $S\subset\mathbb N$, put $M_S(s)=\prod_{p\in S,\,p\text{ prime}}(1-p^{-s})$. Then $M_S(s)\ne 0$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$, and $|\int_T^{2T}\log|M_S(1/2+it)|\,dt|\le C_S$ uniformly for $T\ge 1$; in particular $T^{-1}\int_T^{2T}\log|M_S(1/2+it)|\,dt\to 0$.*

*Proof.* For each prime $p\ge 2$ and $\operatorname{Re}s=1/2$, $|p^{-s}|=p^{-1/2}\le 2^{-1/2}<1$. By the Mercator–Taylor expansion $-\log(1-w)=\sum_{k\ge 1}w^k/k$ on $|w|\le 2^{-1/2}$, $\log|1-p^{-(1/2+it)}|=\operatorname{Re}\log(1-p^{-(1/2+it)})=-\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{p^{-k/2}}{k}\cos(tk\log p)$ uniformly in $t\in\mathbb R$. Integrating term-by-term on $[T,2T]$ gives $|\int_T^{2T}\cos(tk\log p)\,dt|\le \frac{2}{k\log p}$, hence $|\int_T^{2T}\log|1-p^{-(1/2+it)}|\,dt|\le \frac{2}{\log p}\sum_{k=1}^\infty\frac{p^{-k/2}}{k^2}<\infty$. Summing over the finitely many primes $p\in S$ yields a $T$-independent bound $C_S$. $\square$

**Theorem 1.4 ($8$-way unconditional equivalence for $(L)$, $\mathbb E\log|\zeta M_S|=o(1)$, $(Clust)$, and $o(\log U)$ Hecke/theta means).** *For every $0<c<1/2$, integer $M>1$, and finite $S\subset\mathbb N$, the following $8$ statements are unconditionally equivalent:*

6. $((1/U)\int_U^{2U}\mathrm{unitWindowDepthCharge}(T,c\log T)\,dT)/\log U\to 0$ as $U\to\infty$;

*Proof.*

- $(1)\Leftrightarrow(4)$: Immediate from Lemma 1.1 since $(C+K\log(2T))/T\to 0$.
- $(5)\Leftrightarrow(6)\Leftrightarrow(7)\Leftrightarrow(8)$: `dyadicMean_corridor_and_polynomialTheta_littleO_log_up_to_half_tfae`.

Partition $\{z\in\mathrm{shellZeros}(T,2T):\operatorname{Re}z>1/2\}$ at $\frac12+\frac{\kappa}{\log(2T)}$ and $\frac12+\frac{R}{\log(4T)}$:

  - Moderate band ($\frac{\kappa}{\log(2T)}\le\operatorname{Re}z-\frac12<\frac{R}{\log(4T)}$): every zero lies in $\mathrm{stripFinsetPos}(2T)$ with $\operatorname{Re}z\ge\frac12+\frac{\kappa}{\log(2T)}$, so the count is $\le\mathrm{offLineCountBeyond}(\kappa/\log(2T),2T)$. ### 2. Exact window-certificate equivalence, Cauchy–Schwarz bridge, and bidirectional countermodels

Let:
- $(D_{\mathrm{zero}})$: $\exists\varepsilon:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ with $\lim_{A\to+\infty}\varepsilon(A)=0$ such that $\forall A>0$, eventually $\sum_{z\in\mathrm{shellZeros}(V,2V),\,|\operatorname{Re}z-1/2|\ge A/\log V}m_z\le\varepsilon(A)V$.
- $(D_{\mathrm{win}})$: $\exists\delta:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ with $\lim_{A\to+\infty}\delta(A)=0$ such that $\forall A>0$, eventually $\#\{m\in[\lceil V\rceil,\lfloor 2V\rfloor]\cap\mathbb N : \exists\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m),\,|\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2|\ge A/\log V\}\le\delta(A)V$.
- $(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})$: $\exists\eta:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ with $\lim_{c\to 0^+}\eta(c)=0$ such that $\forall c>0$, eventually on all except $\le\eta(c)V$ indices $m\in[\lceil V\rceil,\lfloor 2V\rfloor]\cap\mathbb N$, for all $N\ge\mathrm{doorSqrtPrefix}(m)$ and all $K$ with $2D_{m,N}^2(1-\mu_{c,m})^{K+1}/\mu_{c,m}<\pi/2$ ($\mu_{c,m}=1/(4\,\mathrm{logDoorStage}(c,m))$), $\operatorname{Re}(\mathrm{besselSzegoCertificate}\cdot\mathrm{centeredThetaLaurentLoop})>0$ on $\mathbb T$.

**Theorem 1.5 ($(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})\Leftrightarrow(D_{\mathrm{win}})$, $(D_{\mathrm{zero}})\Rightarrow(D_{\mathrm{win}})\wedge(L)$, and Cauchy–Schwarz converse).**
1. *Unconditionally, $(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})\Longleftrightarrow(D_{\mathrm{win}})$.*
2. *Unconditionally, $(D_{\mathrm{zero}})$ implies both $(D_{\mathrm{win}})$ (hence $(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})$) and condition $(5)$ of Theorem 1.4 (hence all $8$ $(L)$/mollifier/Hecke equivalents).*
3. *Conversely, if $(D_{\mathrm{win}})$ holds and for each $A\ge 1$ there exists $M_2(A)$ with $\limsup_{V\to\infty}V^{-1}\sum_{m=\lceil V\rceil}^{\lfloor 2V\rfloor}N_m(A,V)^2\le M_2$ uniformly in $A$ (with $M_2$ independent of $A$, e.g. at $A=1$ since $N_m(A,V)\le N_m(1,V)$ for $A\ge 1$!), then $(D_{\mathrm{zero}})$ holds.*

*Proof.*
1. ($(D_{\mathrm{win}})\Rightarrow(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})$): Given $c>0$, put $A=1/(64c)$ (for $c\le 1/64$; for $c>1/64$, monotonicity from $c'=1/64$ suffices or choose $A=1/(64\min(c,1/64))$). For large $V$ and any $m\in[\lceil V\rceil,\lfloor 2V\rfloor]$ that has no $\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ with $|\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2|\ge A/\log V$, every $\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ satisfies $|\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2|<A/\log V\le 2A/\log(m+5)$. By `exists_logStage_tail_lt` and `exists_xiZero_near_thetaZero`, at $n=\mathrm{logDoorStage}(c,m)\le c\log(m+5)+7$ and every $N\ge\mathrm{doorSqrtPrefix}(m)$, every enclosed zero $z$ of $A_N$ lies within $1/(16n)$ of some $\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$, giving $\operatorname{Re}z-1/2<\frac{2A}{\log(m+5)}+\frac{1}{16n}\le\frac{1}{8n}=\frac{1}{2}-a_n-\mu_{c,m}$, so `besselSzegoCertificate_mul_loop_pos` certifies $m$. Thus bad certificate windows are a subset of $A$-bad occupancy windows, giving $\eta(c)=\delta(1/(64\min(c,1/64)))\to 0$ as $c\to 0^+$.
   ($(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})\Rightarrow(D_{\mathrm{win}})$): Given $A>0$, set $c=\min(1/8,\,1/(8A))>0$. Suppose $m\in[\lceil V\rceil,\lfloor 2V\rfloor]$ ($V$ large) has $\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ with $|\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2|\ge A/\log V$. Replacing $\rho$ by $1-\bar\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ if needed, $\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2\ge A/\log V$. Since $\operatorname{Im}\rho\in(m-3,m+3)$, $\rho\in\mathrm{xiUnitWindowZeros}(m')$ for some integer $m'$ with $|m'-m|\le 3$. At $N_0=\mathrm{doorSqrtPrefix}(m')$ and $n'=\mathrm{logDoorStage}(c,m')\ge c\log(m'+5)$, `exists_prefix_zero_in_ball_of_xi_zero` produces a zero $z_0$ of $A_{N_0}$ within $r=1/(16n')$ of $\rho$. Since $\frac{A}{\log V}\ge\frac{1}{8c\log V}>\frac{3}{8n'}$ for large $V$, $2(\operatorname{Re}z_0-1/2)>\frac{1}{2n'}=\frac12-a_{n'}$ while $2(\operatorname{Re}z_0-1/2)<1-\frac{1}{2n'}=\frac12+a_{n'}$, so `two_le_fredholmIndex_of_gap_lt_two_mul_depth` forces $\mathrm{fredholmIndex}\ge 2$ on window $m'$, violating $(\mathrm{Cert}_{\mathrm{alm}})$ at $m'$. Each failed $m'\in[\lceil V\rceil-3,\lfloor 2V\rfloor+3]$ accounts for at most $7$ indices $m$, so $\#\{\text{bad }m\}\le 7(\eta(c)(V+6)+O(1))\le(7\eta(c)+\text{const}\cdot\eta(c))V$.
2. For $A>0$, any $m\in[\lceil V\rceil,\lfloor 2V\rfloor]$ with a deep $\rho\in\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ has $\operatorname{Im}\rho\in(V-3,2V+3)$. Moreover, for fixed $\kappa>0$, telescoping $K$ dyadic shells $[V/2^{j+1},V/2^j]$ under $(D_{\mathrm{zero}})$ bounds $\mathrm{offLineCountBeyond}(\kappa/\log V,V)\le O_\kappa(V)+(\varepsilon/2)V\log V\le\varepsilon V\log V$ for large $V$, giving Theorem 1.4(5).

### 3. Unconditional positive window theorems for $\zeta$: Planck windows and double-logarithmic unit-window Bessel–Szegő certificates

**Theorem 1.7 (Unconditional critical-scale Planck-window certificate for $\zeta$).** *For every $0<r<1$ and $B>0$, there exists $C_{r}>0$ (independent of $B$ and $A$) such that for every $A\ge 2$ and $B>0$, eventually as $V\to\infty$,*
$$\operatorname{vol}\left\{t\in[V,2V] : \exists\rho\in\mathbb C,\;\xi(\rho)=0,\;t\le\operatorname{Im}\rho\le t+\frac{B}{\log V},\;\left|\operatorname{Re}\rho-\frac12\right|\ge\frac{A}{\log V}\right\} \le C_r B \frac{e^{-rA}}{A} V.$$

*Proof.* Choose $r'\in(r,1)$ ($r'=(r+1)/2$) and $\varepsilon=(r'-r)/(2r)>0$ so $r'/(1+\varepsilon)\ge r$, and apply `exists_selberg_arbitraryRate_nearLine_zeroDensity` with $(r',\varepsilon)$ at $S=\frac32 V$ (giving $T_1\in(V,V+1)$, $T_2\in(2V-1,2V)$). Every zero $\rho$ of $\xi$ with $\operatorname{Im}\rho\in[V,2V+B/\log V]$ and $|\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2|\ge A/\log V$ either lies in the two end-caps $[V,T_1]\cup[T_2,2V+B/\log V]$ ($O(\log V)$ zeros total by `shortShell_orderSum_le_log`) or in $(T_1,T_2]$ ($2\cdot\#\{\operatorname{Re}\rho\ge 1/2+A/\log V\}\le C'_{r'}(e^{-rA}/A)V\log V$ for $A\le c_0\log V$, and $0$ for $A\ge\log V/2$ since $|\operatorname{Re}\rho-1/2|<1/2$). Each bad zero $\rho$ hits $\{t\in[V,2V]:\operatorname{Im}\rho\in[t,t+B/\log V]\}$ only on $[\operatorname{Im}\rho-B/\log V,\operatorname{Im}\rho]$, of Lebesgue measure $B/\log V$. Subadditivity multiplies $O_{r'}((e^{-rA}/A)V\log V+O(\log V))$ by $B/\log V$, cancelling $\log V$ identically to give $\le C_r B(e^{-rA}/A)V$. $\square$
