## Proof

Write $P_{N+1}(x) := \sum_{m=1}^{N+1} \Phi_m(x)$ (`x ∈ ℝ`, where $\Phi_m(x) = 2\pi e^{x/2}(2\pi m^4 e^{4x} - 3 m^2 e^{2x})e^{-\pi m^2 e^{2x}}$), so the canonical reflected finite-theta source is $P_{N+1}(|x|) = \text{reflectedFiniteThetaSource } (N + 1)\ x$ with bilateral Laplace transform $\mathcal{A}_N(z) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} P_{N+1}(|x|) e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx$, and let $E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) := C_{\mathrm{tail}} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) e^{-(N+1)^2}$.

### 1. Infinitude of off-line zeros of $\mathcal{A}_N$ and Gaussian seam-mollification gauge invariance `[PROVED HERE]`

1. **Subquadratic order-$\tfrac{3}{2}$ growth of $\mathcal{A}_N$ on $\mathbb{C}$:** For $u \ge 0$, $e^{2u} \ge 1 + 2u + 2u^2 + \tfrac{4}{3}u^3 \ge u^3$, so $\Phi_m(u) \le 4\pi^2 m^4 e^{\frac{9}{2}u - \pi m^2 u^3} \le C_{N}' e^{-2 u^3}$ ($1 \le m \le N + 1$), giving $P_{N+1}(|x|) \le B_N e^{-|x|^3 - |x|}$ on $\mathbb{R}$ for an explicit constant $B_N \ge 1$. For any $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, set $s := \sqrt{1 + \|z\|} \ge 1$; the polynomial identity $|x|^3 + 2 s^3 - 3 s^2 |x| = (|x| - s)^2(|x| + 2 s) \ge 0$ gives $(1 + \|z\|)|x| - |x|^3 \le \tfrac{2}{3}(1 + \|z\|)^{3/2} \le (1 + \|z\|)^{3/2}$. Since $\operatorname{Re}((\tfrac{1}{2} - z)x) \le (\tfrac{1}{2} + \|z\|)|x| \le (1 + \|z\|)|x|$, integrating against $e^{-|x|}$ yields $\|\mathcal{A}_N(z)\| \le 2 B_N \exp((1 + \|z\|)^{3/2}) \le \exp(C_N (1 + \|z\|)^{3/2})$ with exponent $a = \tfrac{3}{2} < 2$.
2. **Super-exponential real-axis lower bound and complex zero infinitude:** For every $x_0 > 0$, $P_{N+1}(u) \ge \Phi_1(u) > 0$ on $[x_0, x_0 + 1]$. For real $\sigma \ge \tfrac{1}{2}$, positivity of the bilateral integrand gives
   $$\mathcal{A}_N(\sigma) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} P_{N+1}(|x|) e^{(1/2 - \sigma)x}\,dx \ge \int_{-(x_0 + 1)}^{-x_0} \Phi_1(-x) e^{(1/2 - \sigma)x}\,dx = \int_{x_0}^{x_0 + 1} \Phi_1(u) e^{(\sigma - 1/2)u}\,du \ge \kappa(x_0)\, e^{x_0 \sigma},$$
   where $\kappa(x_0) := \int_{x_0}^{x_0 + 1} \Phi_1(u) e^{-u/2}\,du > 0$. If $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ were finite, subquadratic Hadamard rigidity (`exists_finiteDivisorFactor_mul_exp_of_finite_zeroSet` with $a = \tfrac{3}{2} < 2$) would express $\mathcal{A}_N(z) = (\prod_{w \in t}(z - w)^{m(w)}) c e^{B z}$ ($c \ne 0$), which satisfies $\|\mathcal{A}_N(\sigma)\| \le C_P (1 + \sigma)^D e^{\|B\|\sigma}$ for $\sigma \ge 1$; choosing $x_0 := \|B\| + D + 2$ yields $\kappa(x_0) e^{(D + 2)\sigma} \le C_P (1 + \sigma)^D$, contradicting $(1 + \sigma)^D e^{-(D + 2)\sigma} \to 0$. Hence $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ is infinite.
3. **Infinitely many off-line zeros outside every vertical strip:** Since $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R \wedge \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ is finite (`finiteThetaAmplitude_strip_zeros_finite`), set difference yields $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0 \wedge |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| > R\}$ infinite for every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $R \ge 0$. At $R = 0$, $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + iz)$ has infinitely many nonreal zeros while $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2}) > 0$, so `z ↦ A_N(1 / 2 + I * z)` is not in the Laguerre–Pólya class (`im_eq_zero_of_laguerrePolya`).
4. **Gaussian seam-mollification ($P_{N+1}(|\cdot|) * G_a$, $a > 0$):** Define $\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(x) := \int_{\mathbb{R}} P_{N+1}(|u|)\, G_a(x - u)\,du$ with $G_a(y) = (2\pi a)^{-1/2} e^{-y^2/(2a)}$. Because $P_{N+1}(|\cdot|) \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$ is strictly positive and even while $G_a \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ is strictly positive and even with $G_a^{(m)}(y) = (2\pi a)^{-1/2} \text{gaussDeriv}(1/(2a), m, y)$, parametric differentiation under the integral establishes $\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}} \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$, strict positivity, evenness $\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(-x) = \widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(x)$, and $\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}\big)^{(2k+1)}(0) = 0$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Applying Fubini/convolution to $e^{(1/2 - z)x} = e^{(1/2 - z)u} e^{(1/2 - z)(x - u)}$ and $\int_{\mathbb{R}} G_a(y) e^{wy}\,dy = e^{\frac{a}{2}w^2}$ (completing the square via `integral_cexp_quadratic`) gives the exact identity
   $$\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(z) := \int_{\mathbb{R}} \widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(x)\, e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx = e^{\frac{a}{2}(z - 1/2)^2}\, \mathcal{A}_N(z) \qquad (\forall z \in \mathbb{C}).$$
   On $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$, $\|e^{\frac{a}{2}(z - 1/2)^2}\| = e^{\frac{a}{2}(\operatorname{Re} z - 1/2)^2 - \frac{a}{2}(\operatorname{Im} z)^2} \le e^{\frac{a}{2} R^2 - \frac{a}{2}(\operatorname{Im} z)^2}$ restores Gaussian super-polynomial strip decay $\|z - \tfrac{1}{2}\|^K \|\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(z)\| \to 0$ ($\forall K \in \mathbb{N}$). Because $e^{\frac{a}{2}(z - 1/2)^2} \ne 0$, $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}$ has the identical zero set and analytic orders as $\mathcal{A}_N$ (hence infinitely many off-line zeros outside every vertical strip and non-Laguerre–Pólya pullback). Moreover, whenever $\mathcal{A}_N$ avoids $\partial R_{T,n}$, $-\frac{(\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}})'(z)}{\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(z)} = -\frac{\mathcal{A}_N'(z)}{\mathcal{A}_N(z)} - a(z - \tfrac{1}{2})$; by `rectIntegral_eq_zero_of_analyticOnNhd` applied to the entire polynomial $z \mapsto -a z^r (z - \tfrac{1}{2})$, every window contour power sum, window degree, Newton difference polynomial, Zhukovsky loop, and Wiener–Hopf Fredholm operator of $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}$ is identically equal to that of $\mathcal{A}_N$.

### 2. Non-multiplicative $C^\infty$-even Poisson–Jacobi partition prefixes `[PROVED HERE]`

For $\kappa > 0$, define $\sigma_\kappa(x) := \sqrt{\kappa / \pi}\int_0^x e^{-\kappa u^2}\,du$ and $\chi_\kappa(x) := \tfrac{1}{2}(1 + \sigma_\kappa(x))$, and form the **partition-symmetrized Poisson–Jacobi prefix**
$$\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x) := \chi_\kappa(x)\, P_{N+1}(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x)\, P_{N+1}(-x) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(P_{N+1}(x) + P_{N+1}(-x)\bigr) + \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma_\kappa(x)\bigl(P_{N+1}(x) - P_{N+1}(-x)\bigr).$$
1. **$C^\infty$-evenness and seam-jet annihilation:** Since $\sigma_\kappa'(x) = \sqrt{\kappa/\pi} e^{-\kappa x^2} \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ and $P_{N+1} \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ (`thetaCuspSourceTerm` contains no $|x|$), $\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}} \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$. Oddness $\sigma_\kappa(-x) = -\sigma_\kappa(x)$ gives $\chi_\kappa(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x) = 1$ and $\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(-x) = \widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x)$, whence $\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(2k+1)}(0) = 0$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$.
2. **Complementary Gaussian tail bound and all-order super-polynomial strip decay:** From $\sqrt{\kappa/\pi}\int_0^\infty e^{-\kappa u^2}\,du = \tfrac{1}{2}$, for $x \ge 0$ substituting $u = x + v$ ($v \ge 0$, $(x + v)^2 \ge x^2 + v^2$) yields
   $$0 < \chi_\kappa(-x) = \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\kappa/\pi}\int_x^\infty e^{-\kappa u^2}\,du \le \tfrac{1}{2} e^{-\kappa x^2}\sqrt{\kappa/\pi}\int_0^\infty e^{-\kappa v^2}\,dv = \tfrac{1}{4} e^{-\kappa x^2}.$$
   For $y \le 0$, $e^y \le 1$ and $e^{-\pi m^2 e^{2y}} \le 1$ imply every derivative $P_{N+1}^{(r)}(y)$ is bounded on $(-\infty, 0]$, while on $[0, \infty)$, $P_{N+1}^{(r)}(x)$ decays double-exponentially; combined with $\chi_\kappa^{(j)}(\pm x) = Q_{\kappa,j}(x) e^{-\kappa x^2}$ ($j \ge 1$), Leibniz's rule gives $\bigl|\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(K)}(x)\bigr| \le C_{N,\kappa,K} e^{-\frac{\kappa}{2} x^2}$ on $\mathbb{R}$ for every $K \in \mathbb{N}$. Hence $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z) := \int_{\mathbb{R}} \widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x) e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx$ is entire, satisfies $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(1 - z) = \widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z)$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(\bar{z}) = \overline{\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z)}$, and $K$-fold integration by parts on $\mathbb{R}$ with zero boundary/seam terms yields
   $$(z - \tfrac{1}{2})^K \widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(K)}(x)\, e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx \qquad (\forall K \in \mathbb{N},\ z \in \mathbb{C}),$$
   bounding $\|z - \tfrac{1}{2}\|^K \|\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z)\| \le \int_{\mathbb{R}} \bigl|\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(K)}(x)\bigr| e^{R|x|}\,dx < \infty$ uniformly on $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$.
3. **Exact fixed-$\kappa$ convergence to $\xi$ and collision charge $O(T)$ as $N \to \infty$:** Because $\Phi(-x) = \Phi(x)$ (`thetaCuspFullSource_even`) and $\chi_\kappa(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x) = 1$, $\chi_\kappa(x)\Phi(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x)\Phi(-x) = \Phi(x)$ identically for every $\kappa > 0$. On $x \ge 0$, $\chi_\kappa(x)|\Phi(x) - P_{N+1}(x)| \le C_{\mathrm{tail}} e^{-(N+1)^2}\Phi(x)$, while $\chi_\kappa(-x)|\Phi(-x) - P_{N+1}(-x)| \le \tfrac{1}{4} e^{-\kappa x^2} \sum_{m=N+2}^\infty |\Phi_m(-x)|$. Since $(2\pi u^4 + 3u^2)e^{-\frac{\pi}{2}u^2} \le 20$ for $u \ge 0$, $|\Phi_m(-x)| \le 40\pi e^{-x/2} e^{-\frac{\pi}{2} m e^{-2x}}$ ($m \ge 1$), summing over $m \ge 1$ is dominated by $C_1 e^{2x}$, and $(1 + |x|) e^{-\kappa x^2 + (R + 5/2)x}$ is integrable on $[0, \infty)$; dominated convergence gives $\sup_{|\operatorname{Re} z| \le R} \|\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z) - \xi(z)\| \to 0$ and $\sup_{|\operatorname{Re} z| \le R} \|-(\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}})'(z) - \xi'(z)\| \to 0$ as $N \to \infty$ at fixed $\kappa > 0$, so the associated collision operator $\widetilde{W}_{T,n,N,\kappa}$ stabilizes to $O(T)$ on every window $(T, n)$ ($n \ge n_{\mathrm{win}}(T)$).
4. **Persistence of off-line zeros at large $\kappa$:** For fixed $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \ge 0$, $|\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x) - P_{N+1}(x)| = \chi_\kappa(-x)|P_{N+1}(-x) - P_{N+1}(x)| \le \tfrac{1}{4} M_N e^{-\kappa x^2}$, so $\sup_{|\operatorname{Re} z| \le R} \|\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z) - \mathcal{A}_N(z)\| \to 0$ as $\kappa \to \infty$. Given any $R_0 \ge 0$ and $M \in \mathbb{N}$, choosing $M$ distinct zeros $w_1, \dots, w_M$ of $\mathcal{A}_N$ with $|\operatorname{Re} w_j - \tfrac{1}{2}| > R_0$ (from §1.3) and isolating them by disjoint closed discs $\overline{B}(w_j, \rho_j) \subset \{z : |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| > R_0\}$ on whose boundaries $\mathcal{A}_N \ne 0$, Rouché's theorem produces $\kappa_0 > 0$ such that for every $\kappa \ge \kappa_0$, $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}$ has at least one zero in each $B(w_j, \rho_j)$, hence at least $M$ distinct off-line zeros outside $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R_0$, and `z ↦ Ã_{N,κ}^PJ(1 / 2 + I * z)` is not Laguerre–Pólya.

### 3. Purely source-intrinsic stopping certificates for $N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n)$ `[PROVED HERE]`

When $n \ge n_{\mathrm{win}}(T)$, taking $M \ge N_{\mathrm{thresh}}(T, n)$ yields $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N}) = O(T)$ and $N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n) \le N$.
   - *Completeness on simple-zero windows:* Assume $\forall w \in \text{stripZeros } T,\ \operatorname{ord}_w(\xi) = 1$, and take $n \ge n_{\mathrm{simple}}(T)$ ensuring $\frac{4}{s_n} < \text{collisionWindowGap } T$ and $\frac{3}{s_n} \le \text{windowStripLeftMargin } T$.
