# Beyond-the-wall prime-pole continuation excludes the critical state

- **ID:** ecs-q8-beyond-the-wall-prime-continuation
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Target:** ECS Q8; the arithmetic property that excludes the critical state, its state-level exclusion mechanism, corollaries, and unconditional refutation on the prime-two deformations and positive Euler twins.
- **Statement:** Every critical half-line ground state sees at least one left off-critical zero of $\zeta$ ($0 < \operatorname{Re}\rho < \tfrac12$), while the positive-charge-cancelled archimedean resolvent tail is holomorphic on $\operatorname{Re}p < \tfrac52$; consequently holomorphic continuation across $-\tfrac12 \le \operatorname{Re}p < 0$ of the beyond-the-wall prime-pole balance $\Phi_F(p) = \hat SF(p) + \mathcal LF(-\tfrac12)/(p+\tfrac12)$ (or of the bare prime head $\hat SG(p)$ on $\mathcal LG(-\tfrac12)=0$, or damped $L^2(0,\infty)$ membership of the right-of-wall prime wave for all $\delta \in (0,\tfrac12]$) excludes the critical state directly, is sharp at $\operatorname{Re}p = 0$, and fails unconditionally on both the prime-two deformations and the positive Euler twins.

## Objects

For an energy-domain profile $F \in \mathcal H$ (`CriticalHalfLineEnergyDomain F`) and a logarithmic Euler sequence $c : \mathbb N \to \mathbb C$ (with $c = \text{eulerLogDirichletCoeff}$ for $\zeta$, so $c(n)\log n = \Lambda(n)$, and $c = \text{eulerLogCoeff } p_0 \, \alpha \, \beta$ for finite reciprocal Euler modifications):

1. **Charge-cancelled archimedean wall tail:**
   $$J_{\ge 1}F(p) = \sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{\mathcal LF(2(k+1)+\tfrac12)}{2(k+1)+\tfrac12 - p}.$$
2. **Beyond-the-wall prime-pole balance:**
   $$\Phi_c(F,p) = \sum_{n=2}^\infty \frac{c(n)\log n}{\sqrt n}\,n^p \int_{-\log n}^0 F(u)e^{pu}\,du + \frac{\mathcal LF(-\tfrac12)}{p + \tfrac12}, \qquad \operatorname{Re}p < -\tfrac12.$$
   For $\zeta$, $\Phi_\zeta(F,p) = \hat SF(p) + \mathcal LF(-\tfrac12)/(p+\tfrac12)$, which on $\operatorname{Re}p < -\tfrac12$ is the Laplace transform $\int_0^\infty e^{pv}W_F(v)\,dv$ of the right-of-wall prime-remainder wave $W_F(v) = \sum_{n > e^v}\Lambda(n)n^{-1/2}F(v-\log n) - \mathcal LF(-\tfrac12)e^{v/2}$.
3. **Named arithmetic property (`ModelBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation c` / `ZetaBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation`):**
   For every $F \in \mathcal H$, $\Phi_c(F,\cdot)$ admits a holomorphic continuation from $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < -\tfrac12\}$ to $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < 0\}$. On the pole-neutral subspace $\mathcal LG(-\tfrac12)=0$, the polar term vanishes identically and the property reduces to holomorphic continuation of the bare prime-head series $\hat SG(p)$ alone (`PurePrimeHeadContinuation`).

## Dependency-closed paper proof

### 1. Strip-wide holomorphy of the archimedean wall tail (`[PROVED HERE]`)
For $F \in \mathcal H$ and $r > 0$, Cauchy–Schwarz on $(-\infty,0)$ gives $|\mathcal LF(r)| \le \|F\|_2/\sqrt{2r}$. On $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < \sigma\}$ with $\sigma < \tfrac52$, $|2(k+1)+\tfrac12 - p| \ge 2k + \tfrac52 - \sigma > 0$, so the $k$-th summand of $J_{\ge 1}F(p)$ is majorized by $C_\sigma (k+1)^{-3/2}$, which is summable. Weierstrass convergence gives holomorphy of $J_{\ge 1}F$ on $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < \tfrac52\}$, and `leftRemainder_eq_resolvents` gives $\text{globalRemainder }F\,p = J_{\ge 1}F(p) + \Phi_\zeta(F,p)$ on $\operatorname{Re}p < -\tfrac12$.

### 2. Every critical state sees a left off-critical zero (`[PROVED HERE]`)
Let $(W_0,F)$ be a critical half-line ground state ($W_0 > 0$). By `criticalHalfLineGroundState_exponential_bounds`, $\int F(u)^2 e^{-12u}\,du < \infty$ and $|F(u)| \le C_1 e^u$ a.e. on $u < 0$, so $\mathcal LF$ is holomorphic on $\operatorname{Re}w > -6$ and satisfies $|\mathcal LF(\sigma)| \le C_1/(\sigma+1)$ for $\sigma > 0$. Along $w(t) = 4 + it$, continuity from $\operatorname{Re}w > -4$ gives $\text{globalRemainder }F\,(4+it) = -a(W_0)\mathcal LF(-4+it)$, while $\mathcal LF(-4-it) = \overline{\mathcal LF(-4+it)}$. Since $\mathcal LF(\tfrac12) \ne 0$, the holomorphic function $\mathcal LF$ cannot vanish identically on $\operatorname{Re}w = -4$, so for every $N \ge 20$,
$$L_1(N) := -\frac{a(W_0)}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{N^2}{N^2+t^2}\,|\mathcal LF(-4+it)|^2\,dt \le L_1(20) < 0.$$
Multiplying `globalRemainder_sub_eq_tsum` at $p = 4+it$ and $q = -4+it$ by $\frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{N^2}{N^2+t^2}\mathcal LF(-4-it)$, interchanging the absolutely convergent `(XiZeroCopy × ℝ)` integral (majorized via `summable_inv_sq_centredZero`), and shifting the pole-subtracted `dslope` remainders on vertical strips to $\operatorname{Re}s = -M \to -\infty$ via `integral_vertical_line_shift_of_decay` yields for every $N > 20$ the exact identity
$$L_1(N) = \frac{N}{2}\mathcal LF(N-4)\,\text{globalRemainder }F\,(-N+4) + \sum_{c : \text{XiZeroCopy}} \frac{N^2}{N^2 - (z_c - 4)^2}\,\mathcal LF(z_c)\,\mathcal LF(-z_c),$$
because the $-N-4$ boundary pole cancels between the left-line integral and `globalRemainder_sub_eq_tsum` at $(-N+4,-N-4)$. As $N \to +\infty$, $\frac{N}{2}|\mathcal LF(N-4)| \le C_1$ while $\text{globalRemainder }F\,(-N+4) \to 0$ (`tendsto_globalRemainder_left`), so for large $N$ the real part of the zero sum is strictly negative. At every critical-line zero $z_c = i\gamma$ ($\gamma \in \mathbb R$), $\mathcal LF(i\gamma)\mathcal LF(-i\gamma) = |\mathcal LF(i\gamma)|^2 \ge 0$ and $\operatorname{Re}(N^2/(N^2 - (i\gamma - 4)^2)) = N^2(N^2 - 16 + \gamma^2)/((N^2 - 16 + \gamma^2)^2 + 64\gamma^2) > 0$. Hence some off-line zero $z_c$ ($\operatorname{Re}z_c \ne 0$) has $\mathcal LF(z_c)\mathcal LF(-z_c) \ne 0$. Reflecting $c$ if $\operatorname{Re}z_c > 0$ gives $c_-$ with $-\tfrac12 < (\text{centredZero }c_-).\text{re} < 0$ and $\mathcal LF(\text{centredZero }c_-) \ne 0$.

### 3. Direct single-state exclusion, pole-neutral form, and boundary sharpness (`[PROVED HERE]`)
If $\Phi_\zeta(F,\cdot)$ had a holomorphic extension $H$ to $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < 0\}$ for a critical state $F$, then $G(p) = \xi(\tfrac12+p)(J_{\ge 1}F(p) + H(p))$ would be analytic on the convex open set $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < 0\}$ and agree on $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < -\tfrac12\}$ with the entire function `clearedRemainder F`. By analytic continuation, `clearedRemainder F = G` near the seen left zero $z_0 = \text{centredZero }c_- \in \{-\tfrac12 < \operatorname{Re}p < 0\}$. Then $\text{analyticOrderAt }\xi\,(\tfrac12+z_0) \le \text{analyticOrderAt }G\,z_0 = \text{analyticOrderAt }(\text{clearedRemainder }F)\,z_0$, contradicting `analyticOrderAt_clearedRemainder_add_one`. Thus $\neg\text{HasBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation }F$ for every critical state $F$, whence `ZetaBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation` implies no critical state exists (and implies `RiemannHypothesis`). Conversely, under `RiemannHypothesis`, $\xi(\tfrac12+p) \ne 0$ on $\operatorname{Re}p < 0$, so $\text{globalRemainder }F\,p - J_{\ge 1}F(p)$ holomorphically extends $\Phi_\zeta(F,p)$ to $\operatorname{Re}p < 0$ for every $F \in \mathcal H$. For `PurePrimeHeadContinuation`, if $\mathcal LF(-\tfrac12)=0$ take $G=F$; otherwise $G(u) = F(u+1) - e^{1/2}F(u) \in \mathcal H$ satisfies $\mathcal LG(-\tfrac12)=0$ and $\mathcal LG(z_0) = (e^{-z_0}-e^{1/2})\mathcal LF(z_0) \ne 0$ since $|e^{-z_0}| = e^{-\operatorname{Re}z_0} < e^{1/2}$. Finally, for every $\delta > 0$, choosing any zero copy $c_0$ (`Infinite.nonempty XiZeroCopy`) gives $z_1 \in \{z_{c_0}, -z_{c_0}\}$ with $\operatorname{Re}z_1 \le 0 < \delta$ and $\mathcal L(\text{probe }5)(z_1) = (5+z_1)^{-1} \ne 0$, refuting continuation of $\Phi_\zeta(\text{probe }5,\cdot)$ to $\{p \mid \operatorname{Re}p < \delta\}$.

### 4. Unconditional refutation on prime-two deformations and positive Euler twins (`[PROVED HERE]`)
For $c = \text{eulerLogCoeff }p_0\,\alpha\,\beta$ with $p_0$ prime and $|\alpha|,|\beta| < p_0$, on $-4 < \operatorname{Re}p < -\tfrac12$ the probe evaluation at $F = \text{probe }5 \in \mathcal H$ splits into $\Phi_\zeta(\text{probe }5,p)$ minus $\frac{\log p_0}{5+p}\bigl(\frac{\alpha p_0^{p-1/2}}{1-\alpha p_0^{p-1/2}} + \frac{\beta p_0^{p-1/2}}{1-\beta p_0^{p-1/2}} - C_5\bigr)$. For prime-two deformations ($\frac12 < \theta < 1$, $p_0=2$, $\alpha=2^\theta$, $\beta=2^{1-\theta}$) set $p_* = \tfrac12 - \theta \in (-\tfrac12,0)$; for positive Euler twins ($\frac12 < \sigma < 1$, $p_0 > N$ prime, $\alpha = -p_0^\sigma$, $\beta = -p_0^{1-\sigma}$) set $p_* = \tfrac12 - \sigma + i\pi/\log p_0 \in \{-\tfrac12 < \operatorname{Re}p < 0\}$. In both cases $1 - \alpha p_0^{p_*-1/2} = 0$ with simple derivative $-\log p_0 \ne 0$, while $1 - \beta p_0^{p_*-1/2} = 1 - p_0^{1-2\sigma} \ne 0$. Clearing denominators by $(5+p)\,\text{zetaEntire}(\tfrac12-p)\,(1-\alpha p_0^{p-1/2})\,(1-\beta p_0^{p-1/2})$, any holomorphic continuation $H$ on $\operatorname{Re}p < 0$ produces vanishing order $\ge m + 1$ at $p_*$ where $m = \text{analyticOrderNatAt }(\text{fun }p \Rightarrow \text{zetaEntire}(\tfrac12-p))\,p_*$, whereas the explicit log-derivative jet has order exactly $m$ with nonzero coefficient $(m+1) \ge 1$, a contradiction unconditionally without any assumption on the zeros of $\zeta$.
