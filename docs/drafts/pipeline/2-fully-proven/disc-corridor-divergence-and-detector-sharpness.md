# Sharp V/log V Zero-Detection Phase Transition and Subcritical Mollifier Disc-Corridor Divergence

- **Statement:**
  1. **Sharp $V/\log V$ Disc-Detection Sufficiency (`occupiedBurnolWindows_le_of_shiftedDefect_div_log`, `logFreeNearLineWindowOccupancy_of_shiftedDefect_div_log`):** Let $V \ge e^2$, $0 < A \le \log V$, $C \ge 0$, and $r > 0$. For every holomorphic zero-detector $F : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ on $[\tfrac{1}{2}, 2] \times_{\mathbb{C}} [V - 5, 2V + 5]$ vanishing at every $\rho \in \operatorname{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ ($m \in [\lceil V\rceil, \lfloor 2V\rfloor]$) with $\operatorname{Re}\rho \ge \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{\log V}$ and satisfying
     $$\int_{V - 5}^{2V + 5} \left\|F(\sigma + it) - 1\right\|^2 dt \;\le\; C\, e^{-r(\sigma - 1/2)\log V} \frac{V}{\log V} \qquad \forall \sigma \in \left[\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{2\log V},\, 1 + \tfrac{A}{2\log V}\right],$$
     the number of occupied Burnol unit windows $m \in [\lceil V\rceil, \lfloor 2V\rfloor]$ containing a $\xi$-zero with $|\operatorname{Re}\rho - \tfrac{1}{2}| \ge \tfrac{A}{\log V}$ is at most $\frac{32 C}{\pi r} \frac{e^{-r A / 2}}{A^2} V$. Consequently, existence of such zero-detectors with uniform $(C, r)$ eventually in $V$ implies `LogFreeNearLineWindowOccupancy` (`D_win`).
  2. **Sharp $V/\log V$ Impossibility Across All $L^p$ ($1 \le p < \infty$) and $\log^+$ (`fejerDirichletDetector`):** For every $0 < \theta < 1$ and $A > 0$, the normalized Fejér–Dirichlet polynomial $F_V(s) = 1 - D_V(s)$ of length $\le \lfloor V^\theta\rfloor$ ($b_1 = 1$), where $\sigma_* = \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{\log V}$, $M = \lfloor \frac{\theta \log V}{2\log 2}\rfloor$, and $D_V(s) = 2^{-(s - \sigma_*)} (M^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{M-1} 2^{-j(s - \sigma_*)})^2$:
     - vanishes identically at $\operatorname{Re} s = \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{\log V}$ inside at least $\tfrac{1}{12} V$ distinct Burnol unit windows `(burnolUnitLower m, burnolUnitUpper m)` ($m \in [\lceil V\rceil, \lfloor 2V\rfloor]$) eventually in $V$;
     - satisfies for **every** $p \ge 1$ and **every** $\sigma \ge \tfrac{1}{2}$:
       $$\int_{V - 5}^{2V + 5} \left\|F_V(\sigma + it) - 1\right\|^p dt \;\le\; C(p, \theta, A) \frac{V}{\log V}, \qquad \int_{V - 5}^{2V + 5} \max\!\big(0, \log\|F_V(\sigma + it)\|\big)\,dt \;\le\; C(1, \theta, A) \frac{V}{\log V}.$$
  3. **Log-Free Barban–Vehov Shifted $L^2$ Defect at Scale $V$ (`exists_twoLength_defect_normSq_arbitrary_rate`):** Unconditionally for $\zeta$, for every $0 < r < 1$ and $r/2 < \theta_Y < \theta_X < 1/2$, there exists $A_0 > 0$ such that eventually in $S$, on admissible blocks $[T_1, T_2] \subset (2S/3, 4S/3)$ with $Y = \operatorname{molLen}(\theta_Y, S)$ and $X = \operatorname{molLen}(\theta_X, S) \le S^{\theta_X}$,
     $$\int_{T_1}^{T_2} \left\|\zeta(\sigma_0 + it) M_{X,Y}(\sigma_0 + it) - 1\right\|^2 dt \;\le\; (T_2 - T_1) A_0 e^{-r(\sigma_0 - 1/2)\log S} + 240 S^{\theta_X + 1/2} \qquad \forall \sigma_0 \in [\tfrac{1}{2}, 1].$$
  4. **Universal Subcritical Mollifier Defect Floor and $V\log V$ Disc-Corridor Divergence (`eventually_integral_normSq_zetaMollified_sub_one_ge`, `eventually_discCorridorBound_zetaMollified_ge_mul_log`):** For every $0 < \theta < 1/2$, $a \ge 0$, and $\varepsilon > 0$, eventually in $V$, every normalized Dirichlet polynomial $\Psi(s) = \sum_{d=1}^y b_d d^{-s}$ ($1 \le y \le V^\theta$, $b_1 = 1$, $\|b_d\| \le 1$, including Selberg, Möbius, and two-length Barban–Vehov $M_{X,Y}$) satisfies
     $$\int_V^{2V} \left\|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log V} + it\right)\Psi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log V} + it\right) - 1\right\|^2 dt \;\ge\; \left(\max\!\left(\log\tfrac{1}{\theta},\, \frac{c_{\mathrm{rough}}}{\theta}\right) e^{-2a} - \varepsilon\right) V,$$
     and its 2D disc-corridor integral over $[\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{2\log V}, \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{\log V}] \times [V, 2V]$ divided by $\pi (A / (2\log V))^2$ is eventually at least $\frac{\max(\log(1/\theta), c_{\mathrm{rough}}/\theta)\, e^{-2A}}{2\pi A} V \log V$.
  5. **Sharpness of Gallagher's Discrete Dirichlet Large-Values $\log N$ Factor (`exists_separated_dirPoly_sum_normSq_ge_mul_log`):** For every $N \ge 4$ and $V \ge 40$, there exist $> 9$-separated ordinates $\lambda_0 < \dots < \lambda_R$ in $[V, 2V]$ and nonzero coefficients $a : \operatorname{Fin} N \to \mathbb{C}$ such that
     $$\sum_{r=0}^R \left\|\operatorname{dirPoly}\, N\, a\, (\lambda_r)\right\|^2 \;\ge\; \frac{1}{40}\, V\, (\log N) \sum_{m : \operatorname{Fin} N} \|a_m\|^2.$$

## Proof `[PROVED HERE]`
1. **Sufficiency below $V/\log V$:** By `one_sub_star_mem_burnolUnitXiZeros`, every occupied Burnol unit window $m \in \mathcal{B} \subseteq [\lceil V\rceil, \lfloor 2V\rfloor]$ contains $\rho_m \in \operatorname{burnolUnitXiZeros}(m)$ with $\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{\log V} \le \operatorname{Re}\rho_m < 1$. Partition $\mathcal{B}$ into the $8$ residue classes modulo $8$ and select a largest class $\mathcal{B}_{j_*}$ ($|\mathcal{B}| \le 8|\mathcal{B}_{j_*}|$). By `burnolUnit_coords`, $\operatorname{burnolUnitLower}(m) \ge \operatorname{burnolUnitUpper}(m) - 5 \ge m - 3$ and $\operatorname{burnolUnitUpper}(m) \le m + 3$, so for distinct $m_1 < m_2$ in $\mathcal{B}_{j_*}$ ($m_2 \ge m_1 + 8$), $\operatorname{Im}\rho_{m_2} - \operatorname{Im}\rho_{m_1} > (m_2 - 3) - (m_1 + 3) \ge 2$. With $\delta_R = \frac{A}{2\log V} \le \frac{1}{2} < 1$, the open discs $B(\rho_m, \delta_R)$ ($m \in \mathcal{B}_{j_*}$) are pairwise disjoint and contained in $\mathcal{R}_A = \operatorname{Ioc}(\tfrac{1}{2} + \delta_R, 1 + \delta_R) \times_{\mathbb{C}} \operatorname{Ioc}(V - 5, 2V + 5)$. Applying `norm_sq_value_mul_area_le_integral_ball_local` to $F - 1$ at $\rho_m$ ($F(\rho_m) = 0$) gives $\pi\delta_R^2 \le \int_{B(\rho_m,\delta_R)} \|F(z)-1\|^2\,dA(z)$. Summing over $m \in \mathcal{B}_{j_*}$, applying `Complex.volume_preserving_equivRealProd` and Fubini on $\mathcal{R}_A$, and integrating $e^{-r(\sigma - 1/2)\log V}$ over $\sigma \in [\tfrac{1}{2} + \delta_R, 1 + \delta_R]$ yields $|\mathcal{B}_{j_*}| \pi \frac{A^2}{4(\log V)^2} \le \frac{C e^{-r A/2}}{r (\log V)^2} V$, i.e. $|\mathcal{B}| \le \frac{32 C}{\pi r} \frac{e^{-r A/2}}{A^2} V$.

2. **Impossibility at $V/\log V$ across all $L^p$ and $\log^+$:** Expanding $D_V(s) = M^{-2} \sum_{j_1, j_2 < M} 2^{-(1 + j_1 + j_2)(s - \sigma_*)}$ writes $F_V = 1 - D_V$ as `edPoly b (Icc 1 ⌊V^θ⌋₊)` with $b_1 = 1$ since $2^{2M - 1} < 2^{2M} \le V^\theta$. At $t_k = \frac{2\pi k}{\log 2}$, $2^{-n(\sigma_* + i t_k - \sigma_*)} = e^{-2\pi i n k} = 1$, so $D_V(\sigma_* + i t_k) = 1$ and $F_V(\sigma_* + i t_k) = 0$. Since $\frac{2\pi}{\log 2} > 8$, the floors $m_k = \lfloor t_k\rfloor$ for $t_k \in [V + 1, 2V - 1]$ are pairwise distinct integers in $[\lceil V\rceil, \lfloor 2V\rfloor]$ with $\operatorname{burnolUnitLower}(m_k) \le m_k - \frac{1}{12} < t_k < m_k + 1 < m_k + 2 \le \operatorname{burnolUnitUpper}(m_k)$, producing $\ge \frac{1}{12} V$ occupied windows eventually. For any $\sigma \ge \frac{1}{2}$, put $\rho = 2^{\sigma_* - \sigma}$. Since $(2M - 1)(\sigma_* - \sigma)\log 2 \le \theta A$ and $(\sigma_* - \sigma)\log 2 \le A$ (for $\log V \ge 1$), $\|D_V(\sigma + it)\| \le e^{(1+\theta)A}$ pointwise. Expanding $\|D_V(\sigma + it)\| = \frac{\rho}{M^2}\sum_{j_1, j_2 < M} \rho^{j_1 + j_2}\cos((j_1 - j_2)(\log 2) t)$ and integrating over one period $[t_0, t_0 + \frac{2\pi}{\log 2}]$ kills all $j_1 \ne j_2$ terms and leaves $\frac{2\pi \rho}{M^2 \log 2}\sum_{j=0}^{M-1} \rho^{2j} \le \frac{2\pi e^{(1+\theta)A}}{M \log 2}$. Covering $[V - 5, 2V + 5]$ by $\le V$ periods gives $\int_{V-5}^{2V+5} \|D_V(\sigma + it)\|\,dt \le C_1(\theta, A)\frac{V}{\log V}$, and multiplying by $\|D_V\|^{p-1} \le e^{(p-1)(1+\theta)A}$ (resp. using $\max(0, \log\|1 - D_V\|) \le \|D_V\|$) gives the $L^p$ and $\log^+$ bounds.

3. **Barban–Vehov shifted $L^2$ defect:** Combine `exists_twoLength_mollified_meanSquare_arbitrary_exponents` with $\|\zeta M_{X,Y} - 1\|^2 = \|\zeta M_{X,Y}\|^2 - 1 - 2\operatorname{Re}(\zeta M_{X,Y} - 1)$, `twoLengthCoeff_one`, `norm_twoLengthCoeff_le`, and `norm_integral_zeta_mul_edPoly_sub_one_block_le` ($T = 2S/3$, error $\le 4 + 2 S^{\theta_X}(45\sqrt{2S/3} + 8) \le 120 S^{\theta_X + 1/2}$ eventually).

4. **Universal subcritical mollifier defect floor & $V\log V$ corridor divergence:** Combine `eventually_integral_normSq_zetaMollified_ge_one_add_log_inv_theta` and `eventually_integral_normSq_zetaMollified_ge_roughConst_div_theta` on $[V, 2V]$ with `norm_integral_zeta_mul_edPoly_sub_one_block_le` ($T_1 = V$, $T_2 = 2V$, first-moment error $\le 4 + 2 V^\theta(45\sqrt{V} + 8) = o(V)$ since $\theta < 1/2$). Integrating the resulting lower bound over $\sigma \in [\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{2\log V}, \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{A}{\log V}]$ (where $e^{-2(\sigma - 1/2)\log V} \ge e^{-2A}$) and dividing by $\pi(A/(2\log V))^2$ yields $\ge \frac{\max(\log(1/\theta), c_{\mathrm{rough}}/\theta) e^{-2A}}{2\pi A} V \log V$.

5. **Sharpness of Gallagher's discrete MVT:** Put $K = \lfloor \frac{\log N}{\log 2}\rfloor \ge 2$ ($2^{K-1} < N$) and set $a_{2^j - 1} = 1$ for $0 \le j < K$ and $0$ otherwise ($\sum_m \|a_m\|^2 = K$). At $\lambda_r = \frac{2\pi}{\log 2}(\lceil \frac{V\log 2}{2\pi}\rceil + 1 + r)$, $\exp(i \lambda_r \log(2^j)) = \exp(2\pi i j (\dots)) = 1$, so $\operatorname{dirPoly} N a (\lambda_r) = K$. Since $\frac{2\pi}{\log 2} \in (9, 10)$, there are $R + 1 \ge \frac{V}{12}$ such $> 9$-separated ordinates in $[V, 2V]$, giving $\sum_{r=0}^R \|\operatorname{dirPoly} N a (\lambda_r)\|^2 = (R + 1) K^2 \ge \frac{1}{40} V (\log N) \sum_m \|a_m\|^2$.
