# Generic time change restores the lost differential coordinate

- **ID:** `generic-time-change-jet-birationality`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED HERE] [INFRASTRUCTURE]`
- **Statement:** Let `(E,D)` be a differential field extension of a field `K` of characteristic
  zero.  Let `w,a,f₁,…,f_k∈E`, assume `Dw=1`, and fix `m∈ℕ`.  If

  \[
    w,\quad D^ra\ (0\le r<m),\quad D^rf_j\ (1\le j\le k,\ 0\le r\le m)
  \]

  are algebraically independent over `K`, then, for the time-changed derivation `δ=aD`,

  \[
    \delta^rw\ (0\le r\le m),\qquad
    \delta^rf_j\ (1\le j\le k,\ 0\le r\le m)
  \]

  are algebraically independent over `K`.

## Role

At a Gamma pole, the first positive iterate becomes the new independent variable `w`, apparently
losing one differential coordinate.  A generic inverse branch contributes the time-change factor
`a=dw/dz`.  This theorem shows that the jets of `a` restore exactly the coordinate lost in the
depth shift, at every finite jet order simultaneously.

## Proof

Set

\[
  \mathcal S=
  \{w\}\cup\{D^ra:0\le r<m\}\cup
  \{D^rf_j:1\le j\le k,\ 0\le r\le m\}
\]

and

\[
  \mathcal T=
  \{\delta^rw:0\le r\le m\}\cup
  \{\delta^rf_j:1\le j\le k,\ 0\le r\le m\}.
\]

Both families have `1+m+k(m+1)=(k+1)(m+1)` elements.  Since `Dw=1`,

\[
  w=\delta^0w,\qquad a=\delta w.
\]

Algebraic independence of `\mathcal S` implies `a≠0`.  On the fraction field generated by
`\mathcal S`, therefore,

\[
  D=a^{-1}\delta.
\]

It follows recursively that

\[
  D^ra=(a^{-1}\delta)^r(\delta w)
\]

is rational in `δw,…,δ^(r+1)w`, and

\[
  D^rf_j=(a^{-1}\delta)^rf_j

\]

is rational in `δw,…,δ^rw` and `f_j,δf_j,…,δ^rf_j`.  Thus every member of `\mathcal S` belongs to
`K(\mathcal T)`.  The reverse inclusion `K(\mathcal T)⊆K(\mathcal S)` follows directly from
`δ=aD` and induction.  Hence

\[
  K(\mathcal S)=K(\mathcal T).
\]

The left field has transcendence degree `|\mathcal S|` over `K`; the right field is generated by
exactly `|\mathcal T|=|\mathcal S|` elements.  Therefore `\mathcal T` is algebraically independent.

Equivalently, the finite jet transformation is birational.  In polynomial coordinates its
triangular diagonal consists of powers of `a`; localization at `a` gives the displayed rational
inverse.

## Boundary

The theorem does not assert that a particular analytic time-change factor has independent jets.
For Gamma that is supplied by inverse-pole-branch coefficient freeness and scalar-extension
disjointness from the lower-iterate jet frame.
