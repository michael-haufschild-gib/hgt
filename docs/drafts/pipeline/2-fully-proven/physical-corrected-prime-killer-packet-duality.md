## Exact theorem

For an absolutely summable family `f : α → ℂ`, Lean proves the finite-core convergence law

```text
Re(tsum f) < B
  -> exists finite t, for every finite u containing t,
       Re(sum_(x in u) f(x)) < B.                            (1)
```

For fixed physical parameters, call `t : Finset XiZeroCopy` a corrected-prime killer packet at
scale `a` and floor `B` when every admissible pole-crossing rectangle `R` containing `t` satisfies

```text
Re(CorrectedPrimeContour(L,A,c,a;R)) < B.                   (2)
```

If complete physical theta--Abel Weil energy at scale `a` is below `B`, its zero-square family is
absolutely summable. Equation `(1)` supplies a finite core `t`. Every finite rectangle-zero packet
containing `t` remains below `B`; the exact residue and corrected-prime identities therefore give
`(2)` for every admissible enclosing rectangle.

For every `L,A,c>0`, Lean proves

```text
[for every B and N0, some n>=N0 with L<log(n)/2 has
 a corrected-prime killer packet at scale log(n)/2 and floor B]
    <-> not RH.                                               (3)
```

A premise-free unit-parameter specialization is included.

## Proof

Let `S=tsum f` and choose `ε=(B-Re(S))/2>0`. Unordered finite sums converge to `S` along the
`Finset α` inclusion filter, so eventually every partial sum lies in `ball S ε`. The real-part
norm bound then places every such sum strictly below `B`, proving `(1)`.

Apply `(1)` to squares of the physical theta--Abel two-edge transform evaluated at copied zeta
zeros. Absolute summability is unconditional for `A,c>0`. The closed-rectangle residue theorem
identifies the normalized source contour with the finite zero-square sum, and the pole-crossing
identity identifies that normalized source with the explicitly corrected literal prime contour.
Thus the core packet persists under every admissible enlargement.

If RH is false, the existing complete Weil energy theorem supplies arbitrarily late scales below
every real floor, so the preceding construction gives cofinal killer packets. Conversely, under
RH the existing packet-stable eventual lower bound says that, for every proposed packet at every
sufficiently late scale, an admissible enclosing rectangle has corrected-prime value at least a
fixed floor. A killer packet at that same floor contradicts it. This proves `(3)`.

## Failed conjecture and positive survivor

The first conjecture proposed an unconditional positive physical energy plus a bounded remainder
for the corrected-prime contour. It is impossible: the corpus already proves that false RH makes
complete physical theta--Abel Weil energy cofinally unbounded below while its remainder is
uniformly summable.

Strongest positive survivor is persistent finite-core duality `(1)--(3)`. Failure of RH is not
merely witnessed by adaptively chosen low rectangles: at arbitrarily late scales and below every
floor, a finite copied-zero core forces all admissible enclosing corrected-prime contours low.
Hence rectangle selection cannot evade the obstruction. Remaining RH burden is exactly exclusion
of these prime-contour killer packets, equivalently a uniform packet-stable lower floor.
