# Full original sections at every multiplicity on common conductors

There are $c>0$ and arbitrarily large conductors $n$ such that $S(n,m)\ge c(n^2+m^2)$ for *every* positive $m$, where $S$ is the surplus of the original radius over the full log generator. There is a common positive lower bound on $Q(D_n)$ on arbitrarily late conductors. The ordinary floors pay $n^2$ at $m\le n$. Above $n$ a positive degree pays $m^2$ uniformly, since the original resolution is at least $m$.

Off RH the positive degrees are unbounded on cofinal conductors. Under RH let $v_d$ denote the atom at conductor $d$ in the positive definite Weil form. The upper pair has squared norm $5\|v_1\|^2-4h(\log((2n+1)^2/n^2))$, where $h$ is the real spline trace. Its limit is $\|v_1\|^2+2\|v_1-v_4\|^2>\|v_1\|^2$, by strict positivity on the nonzero integer vector $e_1-e_4$. The reverse triangle inequality gives a uniform positive lower bound for the full three-atom degree under RH, independently of its long-range phase. The reverse triangle estimate also has an integer proof. Write $F=\|v_1\|^2$ and $H_n=\|2v_{n^2}-v_{(2n+1)^2}\|^2$. For every positive integer $k$, positivity on $kD_n+e_1$ gives $k(k+1)Q(D_n)\ge k(H_n-F)-F$. As $H_n-F$ has a strictly positive limit under RH, one fixed $k$ makes the right side at least one eventually.

Choose cofinal conductors with $Q(D_n)\ge\delta>0$. For $m\le n$ the full surplus is at least $m^2Q(D_n)+cn^2$. For $m>n$ the resolution is at least $m$ and the joint jet discrepancy is uniformly less than $\delta/4$ for large $n$. The ordinary floors are nonnegative, and the remaining $O(n+m)$ is less than $\delta m^2/4$. Thus the surplus is at least $\delta m^2/2$ in this range.
