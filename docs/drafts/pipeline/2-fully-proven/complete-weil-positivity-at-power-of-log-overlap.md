- **Statement:** There exists an explicit $\alpha_0 > 0$ such that for every $0 < \alpha < \alpha_0$, eventually in $N$, for all $1 \le c \le (\log N)^\alpha$, the complete Weil matrix $W_{c,N} := \operatorname{completeWeilMatrix}(\varphi_0, c, N)$ of the tilted unit spline $\varphi_0 := \operatorname{tiltedUnitSpline}$ is positive definite on all vectors, satisfies the uniform spectral floor $\frac{a}{c^7}\|x\|^2 \le \langle W_{c,N} x, x \rangle$ ($a := \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{tiltedConductorFloorCoefficient} > 0$), retains a positive prime-defect reserve $\frac{b_1}{c^{23}}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(x) \le \langle W_{c,N} x, x \rangle$ (hence $\frac{b_1}{\log N}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(x) \le \langle W_{c,N} x, x \rangle$), and implies $0 \le \operatorname{weilQuad}(\operatorname{atomicTest}(N, y, \operatorname{atomDilate}(\varphi_0, c/N)))$ for all $y : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$.

### Lemma 2 (Sub-Horizon Packet Quotient Tails & Linear Moments) `[PROVED HERE]`
Let $2 \le R$, $2 R^6 \le M \le N$, and $\log M \ge 4 e^7$. For every $u = \sum_{m=1}^R a_m v_m \in V_R$:

Applying `ramanujanConductor_lower_riesz` ($\theta = \frac{1}{10}$, $\sum_{m=1}^R a_m^2 \le \frac{5}{4}\|u\|^2$) yields
   $$(\log N) \sum_{n = M + 1}^N u_n^2 \;\le\; \frac{5}{4}\,\big(\log(N / M) + 4\big)\,\|u\|^2.$$

   $$\sum_{n=1}^N \frac{n}{N}\,u_n^2 \;\le\; \frac{2 \log \log N + 8}{\log N}\,\|u\|^2.$$

### Lemma 3 (Orthogonal Decoupling Below $Q_1$) `[PROVED HERE]`
Let $x \in E_N$ ($\|x\| = 1$, $\langle W_{c,N} x, x \rangle \le \kappa$) and decompose $x = x_1 + z$ with $x_1 = \sum_{m=1}^{Q_1} b_m v_m \in V_{Q_1}$ and $z \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$. By `ramanujanConductor_lower_riesz`, $\sum_{m=1}^{Q_1} b_m^2 \le \frac{5}{4}\|x_1\|^2 \le \frac{5}{4}$.
1. Since $z \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$ satisfies $\langle v_m, z \rangle = 0$ for all $1 \le m \le Q_1$,
   $$\langle W_{c,N} x_1,\; z \rangle \;=\; \sum_{m=1}^{Q_1} b_m E_m(c)\,\langle v_m, z \rangle + \sum_{m=1}^{Q_1} b_m \langle r_m(c,N), z \rangle \;=\; \sum_{m=1}^{Q_1} b_m \langle r_m(c,N), z \rangle,$$
   so $|\langle W_{c,N} x_1, z \rangle| \le \sqrt{\frac{5}{4}}\Big(\sum_{m=1}^{Q_1} \|r_m(c,N)\|^2\Big)^{1/2} \to 0$.
2. On $x_1$, $0 < E_m(c) \le C_E(c + \log Q_1 + 1)$ (`completedConductorEnergy_eq_sub_shellCorrection`, `abs_shellCorrection_le_polynomial_div_totient`) and $|\langle v_m, v_l \rangle - \delta_{m,l}| \le \frac{C_G \log Q_1}{\log N}$ (`normalizedRamanujanHorizonVector_gram_exact`), so $\langle W_{c,N} x_1, x_1 \rangle \ge -o(1)\|x_1\|^2$.
3. Thus if $\|z\|^2 > \frac{1}{4}$, the unit vector $\hat{z} := z / \|z\| \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$ satisfies $\langle W_{c,N} \hat{z}, \hat{z} \rangle \le 4(\kappa + 1) =: M_0$.

### Lemma 4 (Cross-Pairing Suppression Against $V_R^\perp$) `[PROVED HERE]`
Let $\hat{z} \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$ be a unit vector with $\langle W_{c,N} \hat{z}, \hat{z} \rangle \le M_0$. By `exists_completeWeil_matrix_polynomial_reserve`, $\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(\hat{z}) \le C_1 c^{16}$ ($C_1 := (B + M_0)/b$). Let $M_1 \ge 2$ be an explicit constant (chosen below independent of $c, N$) and set $\eta := (M_1 c)^{-24} \in (0, 1]$. By Lemma 1(1), for $u := P_{V_R} \hat{z} \in V_R \cap V_{Q_1}^\perp$ (since $V_{Q_1} \subseteq V_R$, $\langle w, v_m \rangle = 0$ for $m \le Q_1$ implies $u \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$) and $w := \hat{z} - u \in V_R^\perp$, we have $\|w\| \le (M_1 c)^{-12}$, $\|u\|^2 \ge \frac{3}{4}$, and $\log R \le C_{\mathrm{conf}} M_1^{72} c^{72}(C_1 c^{16} + 1) \le C'_R (\log N)^{88\alpha}$. Since $88\alpha < \frac{88}{500} < \frac{1}{5}$, $R^{16} \le N$ eventually. Moreover, $\langle W_{c,N} w, w \rangle \ge -B c^9 \|w\|^2 \ge -B M_1^{-24}$.

Expand $\langle W_{c,N} u, w \rangle$ via `completeWeilMatrix_eq_decomposition_at_every_overlap`:
1. **Scalar diagonal**: $\langle \lambda_{\mathrm{self}} u, w \rangle = 0$ since $u \perp w$.
2. **Pole terms**: Since $1 \le Q_1 \le R$, both $u \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$ and $w \in V_R^\perp$ are orthogonal to $v_1$, so $\operatorname{harmonicSum}_N(u) = \operatorname{harmonicSum}_N(w) = 0$. By `arithmeticPoleKernel_quadratic_eq` polarized, $\langle \operatorname{fullWeilPoleErrorMatrix} u, w \rangle = 0$ and the two-pole part of $\langle \operatorname{fullWeilCenterMatrix} u, w \rangle$ vanishes identically.
3. **Center collar**: Write $u = \sum_{m=1}^R a_m v_m = a_1 v_1 + \tilde{u}$. From $\langle u, v_1 \rangle = 0$ and `normalizedRamanujanHorizonVector_gram_exact`, $|a_1| \le O(\frac{\log R}{\log N})\|u\|$, while $\|(\operatorname{fullWeilCenterMatrix}\;\alpha_c\;N) v_1\| \le 4|\alpha_c| = O(c)$ (`norm_fullWeilCenterMatrix_normalizedPole_le`). On $\tilde{u} = \sum_{m=2}^R a_m v_m$, splitting $\frac{\sqrt{nj}}{N|n-j|}$ at $|n - j| \le R^4$ vs $|n - j| > R^4$ gives $O(c(1 + \log R)\sqrt{\frac{\log \log N}{\log N}})\|u\| = o(1)\|u\|$ on the near band (by Schur + Lemma 2(2)) and $O(\frac{c}{R^2\sqrt{\log N}})\|u\| = o(1)\|u\|$ on the far band (by `abs_sum_Ico_le_of_periodic` and `abs_sum_Ico_div_add_le` on each $2 \le m \le R$, where $|n - j| > R^4$ divides $B_m \le 2 R^{3/2}$ by $R^4$). The $\frac{\sqrt{nj}}{N(n+j)}$ half is $O(c\sqrt{\frac{\log \log N}{\log N}})\|u\| = o(1)\|u\|$ by weighted Schur + Lemma 2(2).
4. **Separated cross error**: `abs_separatedCrossError_le` gives a global Schur bound $\|\operatorname{separatedCrossErrorMatrix}(\varphi_0, c, N) u\| \le C_{\mathrm{sep}} c^2 \|u\|$.
5. **Ratio prime matrix**: $-\langle P_N u, w \rangle = \langle ((\log N - \gamma)I - P_N)u, w \rangle$. Using `sum_vonMangoldt_mul_ramanujan_eq_log_sub_jet` on downward prime powers and `normalizedRamanujanMode_dilate_reduced` on upward prime powers, $((\log N - \gamma)I - P_N)u = e_{\mathrm{Mert}}(u) + Z_{\mathrm{down}}(u) + Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{full}}(u) - Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{tail}}(u)$, where:
   - $\|e_{\mathrm{Mert}}(u)\| \le 9\|u\|$ (`abs_row_sub_le`);
   - $Z_{\mathrm{down}}(u) \in V_R$ and $Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{full}}(u) \in V_R$ (since every divisor $d \mid m$ and reduced conductor $m / \gcd(m, p^j)$ for $m \le R$ lies in $[1, R]$), so $\langle Z_{\mathrm{down}}(u), w \rangle = \langle Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{full}}(u), w \rangle = 0$ identically;
   - $Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{tail}}(u) = \sum_{p \le R,\; j \ge 1,\; p^j \le N} \frac{\log p}{p^j} \mathbf{1}_{n > \lfloor N/p^j \rfloor} W^{(p,j)}$ with $W^{(p,j)} \in V_R$ and $\|W^{(p,j)}\| \le C_0 p^{j/2} \mathbf{1}_{p^{j-1} \le R}\|u\|$ (for $p^e \parallel m \le R$ and $j > e$, $m/\gcd(m, p^j) = m/p^e$ stabilizes with weight $\sqrt{\varphi(p^e)} \le p^{e/2} \le \sqrt{R}$; splitting $p^j \le R^2$, where Lemma 2(1) applies since $2 R^6 p^j \le N$, vs $p^j > R^2$, where $\sum_{p^j > R^2} \frac{\log p}{p^j}\sqrt{R} \ll R^{-1/2}$, gives $\|Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{tail}}(u)\| = o(1)\|u\|$).
6. **Overlap minus downward**: Decomposing $(\operatorname{overlapMatrix} - \operatorname{downwardMatrix})u$ via `nearGammaDownwardCoefficient` (`NearGammaDownwardCancellationHasPolynomialGrowth` line 80), the $m$-independent near kernels have Schur norm $O(c^2)$, the divisor-jet term applies an $O(c)$-norm banded kernel supported on $j > \lfloor N/(4c)\rfloor$ to $Z_{\mathrm{down}}(u) \in V_R$ (norm $O(c\sqrt{\frac{\log(8c)}{\log N}}(1 + \log R)^{3/2})\|u\| = o(1)\|u\|$ by Lemma 2(1)), and the exterior strip $(N, N+B]$ has norm $O(c^{3/2} R^{3/2} \sqrt{\log N}/\sqrt{N})\|u\| = o(1)\|u\|$.
7. **Upward non-ratio vector**: By `directed_row_bounds` and Lemma 2(1) on $j > \lfloor N/(4c)\rfloor$, $\|\operatorname{upwardPrimeVector}(\varphi_0, c, N, u)\| \le C_{\mathrm{up}} c \sqrt{\log(8c)}\|u\| \le C'_{\mathrm{up}} c^2 \|u\|$.

Combining (1)–(7), $|\langle W_{c,N} u, w \rangle| \le C_{\mathrm{cross}} c^2 \|u\|\|w\| \le C_{\mathrm{cross}} M_1^{-12} c^{-10}$. Choosing $M_1 := \max(2, \lceil (16 B)^{1/24} \rceil, \lceil (16 C_{\mathrm{cross}})^{1/12} \rceil)$ yields $2|\langle W_{c,N} u, w \rangle| - \langle W_{c,N} w, w \rangle \le \frac{1}{4}$.

### Lemma 5 (Three-Band Coercivity on $V_R \cap V_{Q_1}^\perp$ & Completion) `[PROVED HERE]`
On $u \in V_R \cap V_{Q_1}^\perp$ ($\|u\|^2 \ge \frac{3}{4}$), transpose symmetry of the non-ratio prime kernel gives $\langle \operatorname{fullWeilNonRatioMatrix} u, u \rangle = 2\langle \operatorname{downwardMatrix} u, u \rangle$, hence
$$\langle (\operatorname{overlapMatrix} - \operatorname{fullWeilNonRatioMatrix})u, u \rangle \;=\; -\langle \operatorname{overlapMatrix} u, u \rangle + 2\langle (\operatorname{overlapMatrix} - \operatorname{downwardMatrix})u, u \rangle \;=\; -\langle \operatorname{overlapMatrix} u, u \rangle + o(1)\|u\|^2$$
(since both factors in $\langle (\operatorname{overlapMatrix} - \operatorname{downwardMatrix})u, u \rangle$ lie in $j, n > \lfloor N/(4c)\rfloor$, Lemma 2(1) supplies an additional $\frac{\log(8c)}{\log N}$, making even the $O(c^2)$ near kernels $O(\frac{c^2 \log(8c)}{\log N})\|u\|^2 = o(1)\|u\|^2$). Together with Lemma 4(2)–(4) and `abs_completeWeilSelfError_le_of_width_le_one`, `completeWeilMatrix_inner_eq_decomposition_at_every_overlap` reduces on $u \in V_R \cap V_{Q_1}^\perp$ to
$$\langle W_{c,N} u, u \rangle \;=\; \big((\log N)\|u\|^2 - \langle P_N u, u \rangle\big) - \langle \operatorname{overlapMatrix} u, u \rangle - (\log c + C_{\mathrm{self}})\|u\|^2 + o(1)\|u\|^2.$$
For all $v_A, v_B \in V_R$, Schur's bound $|\operatorname{overlapMatrix}_{n,j}| \le \log N + C_0$ on $|n - j| \le 4 e c$ restricted to $n, j > \lfloor N/(4c)\rfloor$ combined with Lemma 2(1) gives
$$|\langle \operatorname{overlapMatrix} v_A, v_B \rangle| \;\le\; C_{\mathrm{ov}}\,c \log(8c)\,\|v_A\|\,\|v_B\|.$$
Set $K_2 := 32 D_0 (C_{\mathrm{ov}}^2 + C_{\mathrm{ov}} + 300)$, $R_2 := \lfloor \exp(K_2 c^2 (\log(8c))^2) \rfloor$, and $\widetilde{R}_2 := \max(Q_1, R_2)$ ($\log R_2 \ll (\log N)^{3\alpha}$, so $R_2^{16} \le N$ eventually). Split $u = u_{\mathrm{low}} + u_{\mathrm{mid}} + u_{\mathrm{high}}$ along $[1, Q_1]$, $(Q_1, \min(\widetilde{R}_2, R)]$, and $(\widetilde{R}_2, R]$:
1. **Low leak ($m \le Q_1$)**: From $u \in V_{Q_1}^\perp$ and `normalizedRamanujanHorizonVector_gram_exact`, $\|u_{\mathrm{low}}\| \le O(\frac{\log R}{\log N})\|u\| = o(c^{-4})\|u\|$, contributing $o(1)\|u\|^2$.
2. **Intermediate band ($Q_1 < m \le \widetilde{R}_2$)**: On $u_{\mathrm{mid}}$, $\operatorname{overlapMatrix}_{n, n \pm h}$ ($1 \le h \le B := \lfloor 4 e c \rfloor$) has smooth weight $w_h(n) = \frac{1}{n}(\log N - \log c)\frac{\operatorname{shellAutocorr}(\varphi_0, N h / (c n))}{\int \varphi_0^2} + O(\frac{c}{n})$ of total variation $O(\frac{c \log N}{N})$ on $[\lfloor N/(4c)\rfloor, N]$. Summing by parts in blocks of period $\operatorname{lcm}(m, l) \le R_2^2$ via `sum_range_ramanujanLagCoefficient_shift_mul`:
   - Off-diagonal pairs $m \ne l$ have zero period mean, contributing $O(\frac{c^2 R_2^5}{N})\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|^2 = o(1)\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|^2$;
   - Diagonal terms $m = l > Q_1$ have period mean $\frac{c_m(h)}{\varphi(m)}$, bounded via `abs_ramanujanLagCoefficient_le_lag_sq` ($|c_m(h)| \le h^2$) and `nat_le_two_mul_totient_sq` ($\varphi(m) \ge \sqrt{m/2} > \sqrt{Q_1/2}$) by $\sum_{h=1}^B \frac{|c_m(h)|}{\varphi(m)} \le \frac{\sqrt{2} B^3}{\sqrt{Q_1}} \ll \frac{c^3}{(\log N)^{1/24}} \to 0$.
   Hence $|\langle \operatorname{overlapMatrix} u_{\mathrm{mid}}, u_{\mathrm{mid}} \rangle| = o(1)\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|^2$.
3. **Cross-couplings between $u_{\mathrm{mid}}$ and $u_{\mathrm{high}}$**:
   - Overlap cross-term: $2|\langle \operatorname{overlapMatrix} u_{\mathrm{mid}}, u_{\mathrm{high}} \rangle| \le \frac{1}{2}\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|^2 + 2 C_{\mathrm{ov}}^2 c^2 (\log(8c))^2 \|u_{\mathrm{high}}\|^2$.
   - Prime cross-term: Since divisors and reduced conductors of $m \le \widetilde{R}_2$ stay in $[1, \widetilde{R}_2]$, both $Z_{\mathrm{down}}(u_{\mathrm{mid}})$ and $Z_{\mathrm{up}}^{\mathrm{full}}(u_{\mathrm{mid}})$ lie in $V_{\widetilde{R}_2}$, whose Gram pairing with $u_{\mathrm{high}}$ is $O(\frac{\log R}{\log N})$ (`normalizedRamanujanHorizonVector_gram_exact`), leaving only $\|e_{\mathrm{Mert}}(u_{\mathrm{mid}})\| \le 9\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|$ plus $o(1)\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|$, hence $2|\langle ((\log N - \gamma)I - P_N)u_{\mathrm{mid}}, u_{\mathrm{high}} \rangle| \le \frac{1}{2}\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|^2 + 180\|u_{\mathrm{high}}\|^2$.
4. **Defect coercivity**: By `primeEnergy_ge_half_poleDefect` (from `eventually_atomicWeilForm_add_poleDefect_le`), $(\log N)\|y\|^2 - \langle P_N y, y \rangle \ge \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(y) - C_P \|y\|^2$. Applying Lemma 1(2) to $u_{\mathrm{mid}}$ (orthogonal to $V_{Q_1}$ up to $O(\frac{\log R}{\log N})$) and $u_{\mathrm{high}}$ (orthogonal to $V_{\widetilde{R}_2}$ up to $O(\frac{\log R}{\log N})$) gives
   $$\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(u_{\mathrm{mid}}) \;\ge\; \left(\frac{\log Q_1}{2 D_0} - 1\right)\|u_{\mathrm{mid}}\|^2, \qquad \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(u_{\mathrm{high}}) \;\ge\; \left(\frac{K_2}{4 D_0} c^2 (\log(8c))^2 + \frac{\log Q_1}{4 D_0} - 1\right)\|u_{\mathrm{high}}\|^2.$$
   Since $\log c \le \alpha \log \log N$ with $\alpha < \frac{1}{60 D_0}$ and $\log Q_1 = \frac{1}{12}\log \log N + O(1)$, both bands have positive coefficient $\ge \frac{1}{120 D_0}\log \log N - O(1)$:
   $$\langle W_{c,N} u, u \rangle \;\ge\; \left(\frac{1}{120 D_0}\log \log N - C_{\mathrm{tot}}\right)\|u\|^2 \;>\; M_0 + 1$$
   eventually in $N$. Together with Lemma 4, $\langle W_{c,N} \hat{z}, \hat{z} \rangle \ge \langle W_{c,N} u, u \rangle - \frac{1}{4} > M_0$, contradiction.

Thus $M_{c,N}$ is positive definite, proving:
- **Spectral floor**: $\frac{\kappa}{2 c^7}\|x\|^2 \le \operatorname{completeWeilEnergy}(\varphi_0, c, N, x)$ for all $x \in E_N$;
- **All-vector positivity**: $W_{c,N}.\operatorname{PosDef}$ and $0 \le \operatorname{weilQuad}(\operatorname{atomicTest}(N, y, \operatorname{atomDilate}(\varphi_0, c/N)))$ for all $y : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$;
- **Prime-defect reserve**: Combining $\frac{\kappa}{2 c^7}\|x\|^2 \le \operatorname{completeWeilEnergy}(\varphi_0, c, N, x)$ with `exists_completeWeil_matrix_polynomial_reserve` ($\frac{b}{c^7}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(x) - B c^9 \|x\|^2 \le \operatorname{completeWeilEnergy}(\varphi_0, c, N, x)$) via convex weight $\theta := \frac{\kappa}{2 B c^{16} + \kappa} \in (0, 1)$ gives $\frac{\kappa b}{(2 B + \kappa)c^{23}}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(x) \le \operatorname{completeWeilEnergy}(\varphi_0, c, N, x)$, and since $c^{23} \le (\log N)^{23\alpha} \le \log N$ eventually, $\frac{b_1}{\log N}\operatorname{poleDefect}_N(x) \le \operatorname{completeWeilEnergy}(\varphi_0, c, N, x)$.
