## Constraint

Let `G=(I,E)` be a connected, locally finite graph on a countably infinite set.  A Boolean
orientation is a function

```text
epsilon : I -> {+1,-1}.
```

Its edge-coherence field is

```text
c_epsilon({i,j}) = epsilon(i) epsilon(j).                 (1)
```

For two orientations write

```text
epsilon ~_v delta
  <-> {i : epsilon(i) != delta(i)} is finite or cofinite, (2)

c_epsilon ~_e c_delta
  <-> {e : c_epsilon(e) != c_delta(e)} is finite.         (3)
```

After identifying `I` with the primes, `(2)` is exactly equality of the embedded differential
fields generated by the complete signed prime-Dirichlet shift palettes.  The problem is to
determine which sparse families of physically interpretable pair coherences recover that field
class, allowing finitely many observation defects.

## Theorem: flatness, ends, and projective-tail reconstruction

Define the finite-boundary Boolean algebra

```text
B_G = {A subset I : partial_G A is finite},               (4)
```

where `partial_G A` is the edge cut between `A` and `I\A`.  Then:

1. The edge-coherence assignment descends to a homomorphism

   ```text
   D_G : {+1,-1}^I / ~_v -> {+1,-1}^E / finite support.   (5)
   ```

2. If `Omega(G)` is the end space of `G`, its exact kernel is

   ```text
   ker D_G ~= Clop(Omega(G),Z/2Z) / <1>.                 (6)
   ```

   Equivalently, the only invisible operations are independent global sign reversals on clopen
   collections of ends, modulo the one global reversal already invisible to a projective field.

3. Consequently

   ```text
   D_G is injective  <->  G has exactly one end.          (7)
   ```

4. An edge sign field `c:E->{+1,-1}` is of the form `(1)` if and only if

   ```text
   product_(e in C) c(e) = +1                            (8)
   ```

   for every finite cycle `C` of `G`.  When it exists, `epsilon` is unique up to one global
   reversal.  Thus the realizable coherence sector is exactly the flat `Z/2Z` sector; negative
   edges are allowed, but a negative cycle product is genuine frustration and cannot arise from
   one scalar Boolean orientation.

Equation `(7)` is the infinite, finite-defect strengthening of the ordinary connectivity law for
phase retrieval.  Connectivity gives exact reconstruction from exact edge data.  One-endedness
gives projective-tail reconstruction from edge data outside an arbitrary finite defect.

## Proof

### 1. The domain-wall identity

For two orientations put

```text
A = {i : epsilon(i) != delta(i)}.
```

On an edge `{i,j}` the coherence signs differ exactly when precisely one endpoint belongs to `A`.
Therefore

```text
{e : c_epsilon(e) != c_delta(e)} = partial_G A.           (9)
```

This identity is the whole local mechanism.  If `A` is finite, local finiteness makes its edge
boundary finite; the same holds when `A` is cofinite because `partial_G A=partial_G(I\A)`.  Hence
`D_G` is well defined.

### 2. The end kernel

By `(9)`, the kernel before projectivizing consists exactly of sets in `B_G`, modulo finite
symmetric difference.  For a connected locally finite graph, a finite-boundary set determines the
clopen set of ends whose rays eventually lie in it.  Conversely, a clopen set of ends is separated
from its complement by a finite edge cut and hence is represented by a member of `B_G`.  Two such
sets determine the same clopen end set exactly when their symmetric difference is finite.  This is
the standard Boolean-algebra realization

```text
B_G / Fin(I) ~= Clop(Omega(G),Z/2Z).                      (10)
```

Global reversal replaces `A` by its complement, which adds the unit in the Boolean group.  Taking
the projective quotient gives `(6)`.

The end space of a connected locally finite infinite graph is a nonempty compact Boolean space.
Its clopen algebra modulo the unit is trivial exactly when the end space has one point.  This proves
`(7)`.

### 3. Flatness

If `c=c_epsilon`, every vertex sign occurs twice in the product around a finite cycle, proving
`(8)`.  Conversely, fix a root and set its sign arbitrarily.  Propagate signs along a finite path by
the edge labels.  Products around finite cycles equal `+1`, so the propagated sign is path
independent.  Connectedness reaches every vertex.  A second lift has constant pointwise ratio on
every edge and hence, by connectedness, differs by one global sign.

## Corollary: exact HT field readout

Choose any bijection `I -> Primes` and define

```text
P_epsilon(s) = sum_i epsilon(i) p_i^(-s).
```

Let `L_epsilon` be the differential field over the finite-prime base generated by its complete
nonnegative-integer shift palette.  The established prime-tail theorem gives

```text
L_epsilon = L_delta  <->  epsilon ~_v delta.              (11)
```

Combining `(7)` and `(11)` yields the coherence-end law

```text
G is one-ended
  <-> for every epsilon,delta,
      L_epsilon=L_delta <-> c_epsilon ~_e c_delta.        (12)
```

If `G` has more than one end, choose a nontrivial clopen end set and represent it by a set `A` with
finite edge boundary and with both `A` and `I\A` infinite.  Reversing the orientation on `A`
changes only finitely many edge coherences but changes the projective tail line.  The two resulting
prime carriers are then jointly differentially algebraically independent over the finite-prime
base; their compositum has differential transcendence degree two.  Thus multi-ended coherence
blindness can hide the maximal two-source differential-field separation, not merely a small
perturbation.

For the ray on the increasing primes, `(12)` says explicitly that the adjacent coherences

```text
epsilon(p_n) epsilon(p_(n+1))                             (13)
```

recover the complete embedded field class modulo finitely many defective readings.  This is the
graph-theoretic completion of the discrete derivative already used to identify the same-field
groupoid with `E_0`.

## Finite-clock extension

The Boolean restriction is not essential.  Fix `m>=2`, let `mu_m` be the group of complex
`m`th roots of unity, and take phase fields

```text
u,v : I -> mu_m.
```

Define the oriented edge coherence

```text
kappa_u(i,j)=u(i) conjugate(u(j)).                        (14)
```

Let vertex-projective tail equivalence mean that `v(i)/u(i)` is constant outside a finite set,
and let edge-tail equivalence mean that `kappa_u` and `kappa_v` differ on only finitely many
edges.  The same domain-wall proof, with the relative phase `h=v/u`, gives

```text
ker D_(G,m) ~= LC(Omega(G),mu_m) / mu_m,                  (15)
```

where `LC` denotes locally constant functions and the denominator consists of global constant
phases.  Indeed, edge agreement away from a finite set says exactly that `h` is constant on every
component left after deleting those edges.  Those component constants define a locally constant
function on the end space; two representatives give the same end function exactly up to finitely
many vertex values.

Consequently, for every `m>=2`, finite-defect edge coherence recovers the projective phase tail for
all `mu_m`-valued sources if and only if `G` is one-ended.  After any prime indexing, the joint
coefficient range of `u` and `v` is finite.  The finite-range prime-tail field theorem therefore
turns this into the exact differential-field identity

```text
G is one-ended
  <-> for every u,v,
      L_u=L_v <-> kappa_u ~_e kappa_v.                    (16)
```

Thus `(12)` is the `m=2` member of a finite-clock HT coherence law.  The end space measures every
asymptotic phase sector invisible to sparse pair coherence, not only sign reversals.

## Corollary: dephasing fibre and sparse tomography

Let `p_i>0`, `sum_i p_i=1`, and

```text
psi_epsilon(i)=sqrt(p_i) epsilon(i),
rho_epsilon=|psi_epsilon><psi_epsilon|.
```

Then

```text
rho_epsilon(i,j)=sqrt(p_i p_j)c_epsilon({i,j}),
Dephase(rho_epsilon)=diag(p).                             (17)
```

Every diagonal observable, diagonal Hamiltonian moment, diagonal Renyi-2 purity, and energy-origin
gauge class is constant on this entire orientation fibre.  Because every `p_i` is positive, the
off-diagonal entries on `E` recover exactly the edge signs `(1)`.  Therefore a one-ended sparse
coherence graph is necessary and sufficient for those entries, modulo finitely many corruptions,
to recover the projective HT field class.  Full tomography is unnecessary; diagonal data alone is
maximally blind; the exact boundary between them is the end space.

The same statement holds for `mu_m` phases after replacing `epsilon(i)epsilon(j)` by
`u(i)conjugate(u(j))`; the dephased diagonal is still `p`, while the retained edge entries carry
the finite-clock coherence law `(16)`.

## Theorem: tensor stabilization

Let `G` and `H` be infinite connected locally finite graphs.  Their Cartesian product is one-ended:

```text
card Omega(G square H)=1.                                 (18)
```

Hence the product coherence network always satisfies the faithful readout law `(12)`, regardless
of how many ends either factor has.

To prove `(18)`, remove a finite vertex set `S` from `G square H` and let `S_G,S_H` be its finite
coordinate projections.  Choose `g_0 notin S_G` and `h_0 notin S_H`.  The cross

```text
({g_0} times H) union (G times {h_0})                     (19)
```

is connected, infinite, and disjoint from `S`.  Every vertex outside the finite rectangle
`S_G times S_H` connects to this cross without meeting `S`: first move in the coordinate whose
fixed value lies outside the corresponding projection.  Thus all but finitely many vertices lie
in one component of the complement of `S`; every other component is finite.  This is exactly
one-endedness.

For product half-densities

```text
psi_(epsilon tensor delta)(i,j)
  = sqrt(p_i q_j) epsilon(i) delta(j),                    (20)
```

the edge entries along `G` and `H` factor as

```text
rho((i,j),(i',j)) = sqrt(p_i p_i') q_j epsilon(i)epsilon(i'),
rho((i,j),(i,j')) = p_i sqrt(q_j q_j') delta(j)delta(j'). (21)
```

Thus exact tensor-product factorization supplies precisely the two coordinate families of
coherences used by the Cartesian product graph.  Tensoring two infinite systems changes the
tail-readout topology qualitatively: every nontrivial end-wise phase reversal acquires an infinite
domain wall in the product and becomes observable outside every finite defect.

vertical positive-definite/Herglotz condition then supplies the missing point orientation rather

Consequently a quantum-statistical or information-theoretic RH carrier has the following positive
architecture requirement:

```text
diagonal KMS data
  + one-ended cross-prime coherence skeleton
```

The first term alone is constant on the whole dephasing fibre.  The second is exactly sufficient
