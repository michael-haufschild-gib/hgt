# The pretemporal law space has a directional hypertranscendence dichotomy

- **ID:** `pretemporal-directional-hypertranscendence-dichotomy`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven / needs-audit
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / FOUNDATIONAL DIFFERENTIAL ALGEBRA
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED HERE] [PROJECTIVE FOUNDATION LEAN] [NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]`
- **Statement:** For a finite-dimensional constant space of derivations, with no commutativity
  assumption, the directions
  in which a fixed element is differentially algebraic form an increasing countable union of
  projective algebraic sets.  Either one finite jet order is dependent in every direction, or the
  hypertranscendental directions are Zariski dense; over the complex numbers they are also
  Euclidean comeagre and of full measure.

## Goal

Replace an externally chosen derivation by an intrinsic **law space** of all regular derivations
compatible with the constraints, and determine the constraints on that law space before selecting
any one-dimensional evolution.  The theorem is about the geometry of possible derivations, not a
flow generated by one of them.

## 1. Constraint-centralizing law space

Let `k` be an uncountable algebraically closed field of characteristic zero.  Let `K subset L` be a
field extension containing `k`, and let

```text
Delta=(delta_1,...,delta_m),   m>=1,
```

be derivations of `L` which preserve `K` and kill `k`; they need not commute.  In an analytic or QFT
application these are required to be regular derivations of the observable algebra and to commute
with the global constraint action.  This regularity class must be defined independently of the
element studied.

Put

```text
V=k^m,
D_c=sum_i c_i delta_i,                  c=(c_1,...,c_m) in V.
```

Because every `c_i` is a common constant, `D_c` is a derivation preserving `K`.  Nonzero scalar
multiples give the same differential-algebraic classification, so the space of possible
one-dimensional laws is the projective space

```text
Law(Delta)=P(V)=P^(m-1)(k).
```

No point of `Law(Delta)` is distinguished.  If `V` is closed under commutators, then it carries its
intrinsic Lie bracket.  The projective space remembers possible one-dimensional laws, while the Lie
algebra remembers how different laws fail to commute.  The theorem below requires only the vector
space; the bracket supplies additional structure rather than an extra hypothesis.

Fix `x in L`.  For `n>=0`, define the order-`n` finite-law locus

```text
Sigma_n(x)={ [c] in P(V) :
  x,D_c x,...,D_c^n x are algebraically dependent over K }.
```

Define the complete finite-law discriminant

```text
Chron_Delta(x)=union_(n>=0) Sigma_n(x).
```

The name records logical order only: `Law(Delta)` and its discriminant exist before any direction
is selected.

## 2. Algebraicity theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

For every `n`, `Sigma_n(x)` is a Zariski-closed projective algebraic subset of `P(V)`, and

```text
Sigma_0(x) subset Sigma_1(x) subset Sigma_2(x) subset ... .       (1)
```

Moreover,

```text
[c] in Chron_Delta(x)
  iff x is differentially algebraic over K for the derivation D_c. (2)
```

### Proof

Let `d:L -> Omega_(L/K)` be the universal Kahler derivation.  In the exterior power over `L`, put

```text
W_n(c)=d x wedge d(D_c x) wedge ... wedge d(D_c^n x).
```

Because the `delta_i` kill the parameters `c_i`, expansion over operator words gives

```text
D_c^j x=
  sum_(i_1,...,i_j) c_(i_1)...c_(i_j)
    delta_(i_1)...delta_(i_j)x.                                    (3)
```

No reordering of the operator word is used.  Thus `(3)` remains valid for noncommuting
derivations.  In the commuting case it contracts to the usual multinomial formula.
Because the scalar coefficients commute, every word coefficient is homogeneous of degree `j`.
Consequently `W_n(c)` is a vector-valued homogeneous polynomial in `c` of total degree

```text
0+1+...+n=n(n+1)/2.                                             (4)
```

Only finitely many coefficient vectors occur in this polynomial.  Choose a `k`-basis of their
finite-dimensional span.  In that basis, `W_n(c)=0` is a finite system of homogeneous scalar
polynomial equations over `k`.  It therefore defines a Zariski-closed projective subset.

For a characteristic-zero field extension, a finite tuple is algebraically independent over `K`
exactly when its universal differentials are linearly independent over `L`, equivalently when
their exterior product is nonzero.  Hence

```text
W_n(c)=0 iff [c] in Sigma_n(x).                                (5)
```

This proves closedness.  Algebraic dependence persists after adjoining one more jet, giving `(1)`.

The infinite `D_c`-jet family is algebraically dependent exactly when one finite subfamily is
dependent.  Every finite subfamily is contained in an initial segment, so this is equivalent to
membership in some `Sigma_n(x)`.  This proves `(2)`.  `QED`

## 3. Directional hypertranscendence dichotomy `[PROVED HERE]`

Exactly one of the following alternatives holds.

### Universal finite-order collapse

There is an `N` such that

```text
Sigma_N(x)=P(V).                                             (6)
```

Thus every possible direction makes `x` differentially algebraic, with algebraic dependence
already among the first `N+1` jets.  The relation itself may vary with the direction; `(6)` is a
uniform Kahler-rank collapse, not a claim that one scalar differential polynomial works in every
direction.

### Generic hypertranscendence

Every `Sigma_n(x)` is proper.  Then

```text
HT_Delta(x)=P(V) \ Chron_Delta(x)                            (7)
```

is Zariski dense.  When `k=C`, it is also a dense Euclidean `G_delta`, is comeagre, and has full
Lebesgue measure in every projective chart.

### Proof

An irreducible positive-dimensional algebraic variety over an uncountable algebraically closed
field is not a countable union of proper Zariski-closed subsets.  Apply this to the countable
family `(Sigma_n)`.  If their union is all of `P(V)`, some `Sigma_N` is not proper, giving `(6)`.
Otherwise the complement meets every nonempty Zariski open subset, which proves Zariski density.

Over `C`, every proper complex algebraic subset of projective space is Euclidean closed, nowhere
dense, and Lebesgue null.  A countable union of such sets is meagre and null.  Its complement has
the properties in `(7)`.  `QED`

For `m=2`, every proper algebraic subset of `P^1(C)` is finite.  Therefore:

```text
either every direction is DA with one uniform finite-order bound,
or only countably many directions are DA and every other direction is HT. (8)
```

This is the sharp two-law form of the theorem.

## 4. The multiplier cocycle imposes the first law `[PROVED HERE]`

Suppose a group `G` acts on `L` by automorphisms preserving `K` and commuting with every
`delta_i`.  Let `a:G -> K^*` be a multiplicative cocycle and let `X in L^*` satisfy

```text
g(X)=a_g X.                                                  (9)
```

Assume every element of `L` fixed by all of `G` belongs to `K`.  For a direction `D in V`, define

```text
beta_D(g)=D(a_g)/a_g.                                      (10)
```

Then `beta_D` is an additive one-cocycle and `D |-> [beta_D]` is a `k`-linear map

```text
At_a:V -> H^1(G,K).                                        (11)
```

Its kernel has the exact interpretation

```text
[beta_D]=0
  iff D(X)/X belongs to K
  iff X satisfies a first-order linear differential equation over K
      in the direction D.                                  (12)
```

### Proof

Logarithmically differentiating the multiplicative cocycle identity proves the additive cocycle
identity for `beta_D`; additivity and common-constant homogeneity in `D` prove linearity of `(11)`.

Put `u_D=D(X)/X`.  Commutation of `D` with the action and `(9)` give

```text
g(u_D)-u_D=D(a_g)/a_g=beta_D(g).                            (13)
```

If `[beta_D]=0`, choose `b in K` with `g(b)-b=beta_D(g)`.  Equation `(13)` says `u_D-b` is fixed by
`G`, hence belongs to `K`; therefore `u_D in K`.  Conversely, if `u_D in K`, equation `(13)` says
that `u_D` itself trivializes `beta_D`.  Finally `u_D in K` is exactly the equation

```text
D(X)-u_D X=0.
```

This proves `(12)`.  `QED`

Thus the projectivized Atiyah kernel `P(ker At_a)` is the order-one linear part of the complete
finite-law discriminant:

```text
P(ker At_a) subset Sigma_1(X).                              (14)
```

The external law space is therefore not unconstrained.  The cocycle cuts out its first finite-law
stratum, and the higher `Sigma_n` record all nonlinear finite-law collapses.

## 5. Boundary examples

1. **Uniform collapse.**  If `x` is algebraic over `K`, then `Sigma_0=P(V)`.  More generally a
   common finite differential law can force `(6)` at positive order.
2. **One exceptional direction.**  Take two commuting coordinate derivations and an element
   depending hypertranscendentally on only the second coordinate.  The first coordinate direction
   annihilates it, while every direction with nonzero second component is a scalar reparametrization
   of the hypertranscendental direction.  Its discriminant in `P^1` is one point.
3. **Zero-dimensional law space.**  If `m=1`, `P(V)` is one point and the dichotomy is tautological.
   Scientific content requires at least two independently reconstructed regular directions or a
   nontrivial family in which the discriminant varies.
4. **Wild derivations.**  Taking all abstract field derivations makes `V` nonphysical and can create
   directions tailored to kill `X`.  The regular constraint-centralizing space must be constructed
   from the analytic sheaf, local observable net, or operator algebra before `X` is tested.

## 6. Physical interpretation `[CONJECTURE]`

For a pretemporal QFT observable algebra, define `V` as the finite-dimensional space of regular
derivations commuting with gauge and global constraint actions.  The pair

```text
(P(V), Chron_Delta(X))                                     (15)
```

is the **pretemporal law geometry**.  It exists before a direction is promoted to physical time.
The theorem says there is no robust intermediate phase in which finite differential laws hold for
most directions but fail on a substantial remainder: absent uniform finite-order collapse,
hypertranscendence is generic.

This does not yet explain why one direction is physically realized.  A QFT or cosmological model
must additionally construct the observable algebra, prove that `V` is its complete regular
constraint centralizer, and supply an operational selection law.  Renaming `[D]` as time without
those constructions is prohibited.

## 7. Elliptic-gamma test `[OPEN CORE]`

Treat the elliptic gamma function as a function of its additive variable and nome parameters.  The
global shift/modular constraints act before any direction in parameter space is selected.  The
program is to:

1. classify the regular derivations commuting with the chosen constraint group;
2. compute the linear cocycle map `At_a`;
3. determine the projective algebraic strata `Sigma_n`;
4. prove that no `Sigma_n` is the whole law space in the irrational-period regime;
5. identify or exclude the exceptional DA directions.

The existing one-variable irrational-period hypertranscendence theorem is one slice of this
problem, not a proof of the directional theorem.

## 8. Novelty and parent-theory gate

The following ingredients are known and are not novelty claims:

- Kahler differentials detect algebraic independence in characteristic zero;
- derivations admit constant linear combinations and homogeneous word expansions; commuting
  families are only the special case in which words contract to multinomial multi-indices;
- Atiyah classes and logarithmic cocycles obstruct compatible connections;
- parameterized difference and differential Galois theories use logarithmic derivatives and
  differential cohomology;
- proper complex algebraic subsets are nowhere dense and null.

The novelty gate is **only against external literature**.  Comparisons with this repository serve
only to avoid duplicating work; rediscovering an unpublished theorem already present here would
still count as externally novel.  In particular, the repository's `SourceLogarithmicBoundary`
theorem is relevant to labour allocation, not to the scientific priority verdict.  It is a stronger
fixed-direction result than Section 4, so Section 4 is `[BRIDGE]` infrastructure for this project.
The externally candidate-new claim is the geometry created by varying the derivation: the
projective filtration `(Sigma_n)`, its uniform-collapse/generic-HT dichotomy, and its interaction
with the fixed-direction logarithmic boundary.

Exact and adjacent searches found no statement organizing ordinary differential algebraicity of
one element over the projective space of constant derivation directions into the filtration
`(Sigma_n)`, no published uniform-collapse-versus-generic-HT dichotomy `(6)--(8)`, and no coupling
of that discriminant to the multiplier-cocycle map `(11)--(14)`.  This is a provisional search
verdict, not a priority claim.

The closest external parent boundaries currently identified are:

- Kolchin, *Differential Algebra and Algebraic Groups*, Chapter II, Section 11, as quoted in
  León Sánchez, *Contributions to the model theory of partial differential fields*, Fact 1.6.5:
  for a tuple algebraic for a commuting family `Delta`, one may change the constant basis to
  `Delta' union {D}` so that only finitely many iterates in `D` are needed relative to the remaining
  derivations `Delta'`;
- McGrail, *The model theory of differential fields with finitely many commuting derivations*
  (JSL 65, 2000): model completion, ranks, and full partial-differential jets;
- Pierce, *Fields with several commuting derivations* (JSL 74, 2009; arXiv:0708.2769): geometric
  axioms and integrability for several derivations;
- Singer, *Model Theory of Partial Differential Fields: From Commuting to Noncommuting
  Derivations* (2006; arXiv:math/0602034): passage between commuting bases and Lie-algebra actions;
- Rosen, *Differentials over differential fields* (2007; arXiv:math/0701508): universal
  `tau`-differentials and the Kodaira--Spencer class;
- Fornasiero--Terzo and Kaplan--Kesting's generic-derivation programme: existentially generic
  single derivations and model-theoretic transfer, rather than a projective pencil of fixed
  derivations acting on one element.

Kolchin's theorem is the most dangerous parent found so far, but it has different quantifiers and a
different base: it starts from algebraicity for the full commuting family, chooses one favourable
basis, and proves finite order in one derivation **relative to all the others**.  The present theorem
fixes one element and one base field, varies every ordinary direction projectively, and classifies
ordinary algebraicity after discarding the complementary derivations.  No implication between the
two statements has been found.  None of the inspected statements gives `(Sigma_n)`, the countable
projective DA discriminant, or the collapse/generic-HT dichotomy.  This remains an absence-of-hit
report, not a priority proof.  A specialist audit must still inspect Kolchin's proofs for an implicit
ordinary-restriction lemma and check differential-specialization literature theorem by theorem.

The theorem is broad and changes the formulation of hypertranscendence, but it is not by itself a
Fields-level discovery.  That threshold requires a nontrivial exact discriminant for an important
source such as irrational elliptic gamma, or a QFT/cosmological model in which `(15)` predicts a new
observable distinction.

## RH impact

The usual zeta differential field has one distinguished complex derivation, so its projective law
space is a point and the theorem is vacuous.  An RH application would require a genuine
multiparameter zeta or completed-`L` constraint family with at least two independently defined
commuting regular derivations, followed by a discriminant that distinguishes horizontal zero
location.  No such instantiation currently exists.
