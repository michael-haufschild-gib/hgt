## Physical setup

Work on an open real energy interval `U` contained in one scalar elastic channel, away from
thresholds.  Let


be analytic and nonzero.  The derivation is physical energy differentiation `D=d/dE`; this


It is real on `U`.  If `S(E)=exp(2i delta(E))`, then `Q(E)=2 delta'(E)` in this convention.

## The time-delay reporter has exactly the scattering HT class `[PROVED]`

Differential algebraic functions form a differential field, so

```text
S DA  =>  -i S^(-1) D S DA.                               (3)
```

Conversely, if `Q` is DA, equation `(2)` gives the first-order equation


```text
S is HT over C(E)  <=>  Q is HT over C(E).                (5)
```

This is not a statement that a large or irregular time delay is HT.  It is an exact equivalence
of differential-algebraic type.




