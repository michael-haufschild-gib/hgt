# Affine-exponential cascade depth equals minimal algebraic-differential order

- **ID:** `exponential-tower-exact-differential-order`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED] [SUBSUMED]` by `../not_new_scientific_knowledge/asymptotically-exponential-algebraic-cascade-unit-cost.md`
- **Lane:** general hypertranscendence; canonical DA boundary family
- **Statement:** Every `n`-fold composition of real affine-exponential maps `f_j(w)=a_j+b_j exp(c_j w)` with `b_j,c_j>0` has minimal ADE order and positive-lower-order composition depth exactly `n`.

## Goal

Measure the exact finite algebraic-differential cost of repeated transcendental composition. The result supplies an all-order sharp family for the conjectural law

`cd_+(F) <= ord_DA(F)`

and a concrete HT obstruction: an entire function with unbounded positive-lower-order composition depth cannot have any finite-order ADE if the general budget law holds.

This theorem itself does not prove the general budget law.

## Definitions

For a DA entire function `F`, `ord_DA(F)` is the least `r` for which

`F,F',...,F^(r)`

are algebraically dependent over `C(z)`.

The positive-lower-order composition depth `cd_+(F)` is the largest number of transcendental entire positive-lower-order factors in a decomposition of `F`; polynomial and affine factors do not count.

## Theorem

`[PROVED HERE] [SUBSUMED]` Let

`f_j(w)=a_j+b_j exp(c_j w)`,

where `a_j` is real and `b_j,c_j>0`, and put

`F_n=f_n o ... o f_1`.

Then, for every integer `n>=1`,

`ord_DA(F_n)=cd_+(F_n)=n`.

In particular, for `E_1(z)=exp z` and `E_(n+1)(z)=exp(E_n(z))`,

`ord_DA(E_n)=cd_+(E_n)=n`.

## Proof

### 1. Algebraic independence of the cascade

`[PROVED HERE]` The functions `F_1,...,F_n` are algebraically independent over `C(z)`.

After clearing denominators, restrict a putative polynomial relation to the positive real axis. The functions are eventually positive, and

`log F_k(r)=c_k F_(k-1)(r)+O(1)`.

Order the finitely many monomials lexicographically from `F_n` down to `F_1` and then `z`. A larger power of `F_k` dominates every fixed product of powers of `z,F_1,...,F_(k-1)`. Hence the lexicographically largest nonzero monomial strictly dominates the sum of the others for large positive `z`, a contradiction.

### 2. Triangular observability

Let `R_m=C[p_1,...,p_m]`, fix a nonzero constant `c`, and define a derivation by

`delta_m(p_k)=p_k(c+p_1+...+p_(k-1))`.

Put `q_m=p_1+...+p_m` and

`J_m=det partial(q_m,delta_m q_m,...,delta_m^(m-1)q_m)/partial(p_1,...,p_m)`.

`[PROVED HERE]` `J_m` is a nonzero polynomial for every `m>=1`.

The base case is `J_1=1`. For the induction step, write

`s_m=c+q_(m-1)`

and

`delta_m=delta_(m-1)+p_m s_m partial_(p_m)`.

If `f_r^(m)=delta_m^r(q_m)`, then

`f_r^(m)=f_r^(m-1)+p_m B_r`,

where `B_0=1` and

`B_(r+1)=(delta_(m-1)+s_m)B_r`.

Thus every Jacobian row is affine in `p_m`. The coefficient of `p_m^(m-1)` in `J_m` is, up to sign,

`det partial(B_1,...,B_(m-1))/partial(p_1,...,p_(m-1))`.

The recurrence is unitriangular in the jets of `s_m`:

`B_r=delta_(m-1)^(r-1)(s_m)+P_r(s_m,...,delta_(m-1)^(r-2)(s_m))`.

Therefore this coefficient equals `J_(m-1)` up to sign. It is nonzero by induction, so `J_m!=0`.

The Jacobian criterion now shows that

`q_m,delta_m q_m,...,delta_m^(m-1)q_m`

are algebraically independent. Since source and target fields both have transcendence degree `m`, `C(p_1,...,p_m)` is algebraic over the field generated by these `m` observables.

### 3. Realization by affine-exponential composition

Fix `n>=3`, put `y=F_n`, and set

`x_k=F_k-a_k=b_k exp(c_k F_(k-1))`,

where `F_0=z`. The chain rule gives

`F_k'=c_k x_k F_(k-1)'`.

For `1<=k<=n-1`, define the logarithmic velocities

`p_k=c_(k+1)F_k'`.

Since `F_1''/F_1'=c_1` and

`F_k''/F_k'=c_k F_(k-1)'+F_(k-1)''/F_(k-1)'`,

induction gives

`p_k'=p_k(c_1+p_1+...+p_(k-1))`.

Thus these velocities realize the triangular system with `c=c_1`. At the output,

`p_(n-1)=y'/(y-a_n)`

and

`p_(n-1)'/p_(n-1)=c_1+q_(n-2)`.

Consequently `C(z)(y,y',...,y^(n-1))` contains `p_(n-1)` and the `n-2` observables

`q_(n-2),q_(n-2)',...,q_(n-2)^(n-3)`.

By triangular observability, `p_1,...,p_(n-2)` are algebraic over the field generated by those observables. In fact the intermediate compositions are then recovered by

`F_1=a_1+p_1/(c_1 c_2)`


`F_k=a_k+p_k/(c_(k+1)p_(k-1))`, for `2<=k<=n-1`.

These identities follow from `F_k'=c_k(F_k-a_k)F_(k-1)'`. Hence the cascade field `C(z)(F_1,...,F_n)` is algebraic over `C(z)(y,y',...,y^(n-1))`.

The cascade field has transcendence degree `n` by Step 1. Hence

`y,y',...,y^(n-1)`

are algebraically independent. No ADE of order below `n` exists. On the other hand, the cascade field is stable under differentiation and has transcendence degree `n`, so its `n+1` elements

`y,y',...,y^(n)`

are algebraically dependent. Thus `ord_DA(F_n)=n`. The cases `n=1,2` are direct; for `n=2`, first-order pseudo-primality also supplies the lower bound.

### 4. Exact composition depth

The displayed cascade gives `cd_+(F_n)>=n`, since every affine-exponential factor has positive lower order. Direct maximum-modulus induction gives

`log M(r,F_n) <= exp_(n-1)(r^C)`

for some `C>0` after enlarging constants. If `F_n` had `n+1` positive-lower-order transcendental entire factors, the Polya growth-depth lemma would force its Nevanlinna characteristic to have exponential height at least `n`, a contradiction. Therefore `cd_+(F_n)<=n`, completing the proof.

## HT interpretation

Finite composition does not merely preserve the DA/HT Boolean bit. Throughout a nontrivial parameter family of entire universal-cover cascades, every positive-growth transcendental layer consumes one and only one derivative order. An infinite positive-growth composition tower therefore sits beyond every finite ADE budget if the finite-depth inequality extends from this sharp family to arbitrary entire functions.

This is a structural boundary statement about HT functions in general. It is not an RH theorem.

## RH pressure test

The completed xi function has finite order, whereas every nontrivial affine-exponential cascade has increasing iterated growth height. Hence xi cannot hide such a positive-growth covering cascade. Any compositional RH route must instead pass through zero-order outer factors or a non-compositional constraint. This is a negative discriminator only.

- `[SEARCH]` Liao--Yang discuss growth and pseudo-primality but do not state `ord_DA(E_n)=n`.
- `[SEARCH]` Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv metadata, and the project corpus were queried for iterated exponentials, nested exponentials, differential-algebraic order, and minimal differential equations. No explicit all-order minimality theorem or Jacobian induction was found.

It neither determines the minimal ADE order of exponential cascades nor proves the output-jet observability used here; it does not subsume this theorem.
