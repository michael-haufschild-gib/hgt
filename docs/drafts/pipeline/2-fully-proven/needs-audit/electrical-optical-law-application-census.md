## Reproducible coverage boundary

The phrase "every formula on Google" has no finite literal meaning: search results change and the
web contains arbitrarily many restatements.  This census therefore uses the largest reproducible
finite boundary available in the request:

1. every equation family and named effect on Wikipedia's current
   [List of electromagnetism equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_electromagnetism_equations),
   [List of optics equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_optics_equations), and the
   electrical/optical entries of the [List of equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_equations)
   and [List of theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_theorems);
2. every network/passivity/scattering family returned by repeated Google-style searches for
   electrical engineering, optical engineering, passive synthesis, scattering, reciprocity,
   Wigner--Smith delay, diffraction, polarization, and differential algebra;
3. the stronger practical-law census already recorded in
   `projective-immittance-inversion-horizon-folding.md`.

Algebraically identical variants are grouped in one row, but every formula appearing on those
source pages is named in a row below.  Units, symbol aliases, and the same definition written as a
frequency or wavelength density are not counted as different mathematical laws.

## Test applied to every row

Each law was tested against three questions:

1. Can it create a source-resolved positive, passive, contractive, or oriented object from the
   actual prime/gamma source, rather than merely preserve such a premise?
2. Can it discharge an existing `Prop`/hypothesis or identify an existing interface with a
   concrete algebraic law?
3. Can it bridge existing theorem surfaces while preserving differential-field and
   differential-matroid content, and does the resulting statement survive the parent-theory
   novelty attack?

Verdict codes used below:

- **D** -- direct discharge or existing compiled connector;
- **B** -- bridge/composer, useful but parent-grade;
- **C** -- downstream consumer or coordinate representation only;
- **N** -- no-go for the required source effectivity; a positive survivor is identified;
- **O** -- no mathematical contact with the current problem spaces without adding a new physical
  realization, material model, or source map.

## Exact surviving outputs

### 1. New local theorem bridge, not new scientific knowledge

Lorentz reciprocity survived all three tests.  For symmetric invertible scattering `S`,

```text
Q = -i S^-1 S',        T = S Q = -i S',
T^T = T,               Q^T S = S Q.
```

The unordered entries of `T` form a panel of size `N(N+1)/2`.  Over the differential field
generated by the entries of `S`, this panel generates exactly the field of all entries of `Q` and
has the same contracted differential-matroid rank.  No smaller universal linear reporter family
distinguishes every symmetric tangent.  The paper proof, hostile audit, and literature boundary
are in the child card; the exact unconditional Lean declarations are in
`MatrixWignerSmithReciprocity.lean`.

The literature search recovered the classical reciprocal/lossless Wigner--Smith setting of
[Patel--Michielssen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.06985) and the recent transpose-product generalized
Wigner--Smith factorization in
[Physical Review Research 7, 013299 (2025)](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.7.013299),
but no differential-field/Kolchin reporter theorem.  Because the proof is a short composition of
classical reciprocity and symmetric-matrix dimension, the output stays `[BRIDGE] [NOVELTY
UNVERIFIED]`.

### 3. Strongest no-go survivors

- A raw scalar prime delay is not a Herglotz/positive-real function throughout the upper
  half-plane.  The positive survivor is a coupled finite-aperture colligation, not a scalar delay.
- Positive apertures, Gaussian smoothing, diffraction intensities, and unitary screens do not
  orient a Fourier current or locate zeta zeros.  The positive survivor is finite-resolution
  observability/tomography, not an RH criterion.
- Foster/Cauer/Brune, KYP, Bode--Fano, Kramers--Kronig, Nyquist, small-gain, Redheffer, and Hilbert
  contraction are all downstream composers or consumers.  None produces the arithmetic source
  sign it assumes.
