## The theorem

Fix `H>0`.  Let `B=C/(H Z)` be the set of additive shift orbits.  Let `G_H` be the multiplicative
group generated by

```text
Gamma((s+b)/H),  b in C,
```

together with nonzero gauges

```text
c exp(beta s) R(s),
c in C^x, beta in C, R in C(s)^x.                          (1)
```

Every `G in G_H` has a common-scale expression

```text
G(s)=c exp(beta s)R(s) product_(j=1)^d Gamma((s+b_j)/H)^(m_j),
m_j in Z.                                                  (2)
```

For an orbit `omega in B`, define

```text
q_omega(G)=sum_(b_j in omega) m_j.                         (3)
```

Only finitely many entries are nonzero.  Write

```text
q(G)=(q_omega(G))_omega in directSum_(omega in B) Z.        (4)
```

Let `D_H` be the subgroup of differentially algebraic elements of `G_H`.  Then:

1. `q` is a surjective group homomorphism.

2. Its kernel is exactly `D_H`:

   ```text
   q(G)=0  iff  G is differentially algebraic.             (5)
   ```

3. Therefore `q` induces a canonical isomorphism

   ```text
   G_H / D_H  ~=  directSum_(omega in B) Z.                (6)
   ```

4. Every nonzero coset in `(6)` is represented only by hypertranscendental functions.  Every
   finitely supported integer vector occurs, represented for example by one Gamma factor of the
   specified signed multiplicity in each occupied orbit.

5. Locally on one orbit, a finite exponent datum `D:Z ->_0 A` has the exact normal form

   ```text
   D = q[D] at the origin + boundary(E),                   (7)
   ```

   and two data differ by a finite shift boundary exactly when they have the same total charge.
   Equivalently, the augmented cellular sequence of the integer orbit is exact:

   ```text
   C_1(Z;A) --boundary--> C_0(Z;A) --augmentation--> A -> 0. (8)
   ```

Thus the integer orbit charge is not merely an obstruction to telescoping.  It is the complete
topological superselection label for this Gamma family.

## Proof

### Exact topology of one translation orbit

Orient the edge indexed by `z` from `z` to `z-1`.  For a finite one-chain `E`, its boundary is

```text
(boundary E)(z)=E(z+1)-E(z).                               (9)
```

Summing `(9)` over the finite support gives zero, so augmentation kills every boundary.

Conversely, for a coefficient `a` at a site `z`, take the finite oriented path from `0` to `z`.
Its boundary is

```text
a[z]-a[0].                                                 (10)
```

Summing these path chains over the support of `D` gives an explicit finite primitive `E_D` with

```text
boundary(E_D)=D-q[D][0].                                   (11)
```

If `q[D]=0`, equation `(11)` makes `D` a boundary.  If two data have the same charge, apply this
to their difference.  This proves `(7)--(8)` and the completeness assertion.

### Passage to Gamma products

The recurrence

```text
Gamma((s+b+H)/H)=((s+b)/H) Gamma((s+b)/H)                 (12)
```

moves one unit exponent along an oriented edge of its shift orbit and changes the function only
by a rational gauge.  Hence finite cellular boundaries are precisely finite recurrence gauges,
and `(7)` reduces every product to one Gamma power per occupied orbit, modulo `(1)`.

Multiplication adds exponent data, so `q` is a homomorphism.  A single factor in each chosen orbit
proves surjectivity.  The signed-Gamma orbit-charge phase theorem proves the analytic dichotomy:

```text
all orbit charges zero  => recurrence telescopes (2) to (1) => DA;
some orbit charge nonzero => G is hypertranscendental.      (13)
```

Thus the DA subgroup is exactly the kernel.  The first isomorphism theorem gives `(6)`.  If a
nonzero coset had a DA representative, that representative would lie in the kernel, a
contradiction.  This proves all claims.

## What this says about the nature of hypertranscendence

Within this large and arithmetically central family, hypertranscendence is a topologically
protected phase of a discrete constraint, not an absence of all structure.  Gamma is fixed by a
first-order shift relation, but its differentiated defect carries a nonzero integer homology
class.  Finite rational-exponential gauge changes can move that defect along its orbit but cannot
annihilate its total charge.  Differential algebraicity is precisely the neutral sector.

The positive statement is stronger than a no-go:

```text
every finite integer charge configuration is realized;
every nonzero configuration is hypertranscendental;
charge addition is multiplication of HT sectors;
charge equality is exactly finite-gauge equivalence.       (14)
```

This is a classification of surviving objects, not only an exclusion of DA ones.

## Physical/topological meaning

The translation equation is a discrete constraint groupoid.  Rational recurrences are local
gauge moves on its one-dimensional orbit graph.  Equation `(8)` says that the only global datum
left after all local moves is the component charge.  Equation `(6)` turns those charges into
superselection sectors: local gauges do not mix them, and every non-vacuum sector is HT.

This is a literal cellular-homology statement and a literal integer sector decomposition.  It is
not evidence for quantum dynamics, a Hamiltonian, physical time, black-hole entropy, or a
Lorentzian horizon.  The physically useful analogy is topological protection under local gauge
deformation.
