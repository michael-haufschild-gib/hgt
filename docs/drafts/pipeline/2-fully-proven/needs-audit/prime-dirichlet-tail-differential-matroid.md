## Goal

Replace the Baire-selected Cauchy source and the compact-open completion collapse by an explicit,
raw-field theorem.  For an orientation `epsilon : Primes -> {+1,-1}`, put

```text
P_epsilon(s) = sum_p epsilon(p) p^(-s),       Re(s) > 1,
```

and let `E_fin` be the fraction field of finite-prime Dirichlet series over `C(s)`; equivalently it
is generated by all individual prime exponentials `p^(-s)`, with every individual element using
only finitely many primes.  For a finite family `epsilon_1,...,epsilon_n`, the target equivalence is

```text
{P_epsilon_j}_j is differentially algebraically independent over E_fin
  <->
{[epsilon_j]}_j is linearly independent in
    C^Primes / C^(Primes).
```

Here `C^(Primes)` denotes the finitely supported sequences.  The theorem gives:

1. the explicit canonical source `P_+(s)=sum_p p^(-s)` is hypertranscendental;
2. two complete signed prime-zeta shift palettes generate the same raw differential field exactly
   when their signs agree eventually or are opposite eventually;
   relative to the canonical all-positive source, this is the exact finite--cofinite reversal law;
3. otherwise the two complete palettes are jointly differentially algebraically independent on
   every finite subfamily; in particular, the two one-carrier fields have compositum of
   differential transcendence degree two;
4. the entire finitary differential matroid of the orientation family is the tail linear matroid;
5. vertical positive-definiteness selects the all-positive carrier uniquely;
6. a canonical nonnegative-integer shift palette gives every orientation an explicit complete
   Stanley--Reisner constraint carrier, with no Baire-selected source.

The RH use is a constraint, not a proof claim: any field-only positivity route that is invariant
under finite Euler gauges can see at most the tail class.  Selecting the zeta orientation requires
the positive-definite Euler marking; locating its continued singularities requires additional
functional-equation/divisor data.

## Approach

### Algebraic direction

If `sum_j c_j epsilon_j` has finite support, then

```text
sum_j c_j P_epsilon_j
```

is a finite Dirichlet polynomial and therefore lies in `E_fin`.  Hence the carriers are
differentially dependent.

### Differential-independence direction

For a shift `a` and derivative order `k`, the prime coefficient of
`P_epsilon_j^(k)(s+a)` is

```text
epsilon_j(p) p^(-a) (-log p)^k.
```

Tail linear independence is equivalent to the following finite-pattern condition: the sign
vectors that occur at infinitely many primes span `C^n`.  On each infinite pattern fibre the
numbers `log p` are distinct.  A finite-support relation among the jet columns

```text
(epsilon_j(p) (-log p)^k)_(j,k)
```

becomes a finite sum `sum_a p^(-a) Q_a(log p)`.  Peeling off the least shift makes every later term
decay exponentially relative to its polynomial log weight, so every `Q_a` vanishes.  The jet
columns therefore span every finite shift--jet space, even after deletion of an arbitrary finite
prime set.  The orientation-tail reduction is now proved unconditionally in the internal theorem
surface, parameterized only by this classical exponential-polynomial rigidity input.  The
Shapiro--Sparer arithmetic-function Jacobian criterion then makes every finite jet rectangle
algebraically independent over the kernel of suitable basic prime derivations.  Deleting the
finite prime support of any coefficient in `E_fin` proves independence over `E_fin`.  Injectivity
of convergent Dirichlet series transfers the formal arithmetic-function statement to analytic
functions on `Re(s)>1`, and polynomial-weight uniqueness upgrades the coefficient field to
`C(s)`.

### Positive orientation

For every `sigma>1`,

```text
t |-> P_epsilon(sigma+i t)
```

is the Fourier transform of the finite signed measure

```text
sum_p epsilon(p) p^(-sigma) delta_(log p).
```

By Bochner uniqueness it is positive definite exactly when every sign is positive.

## Attacks

1. **Compact-open completion is not significant.**  On the upper half-plane, the algebra generated
   by one Cauchy atom is already compact-open dense in all holomorphic functions after a Moebius
   change of variable and Runge approximation.  The earlier completed-algebra constancy is correct
   but cannot be the new theorem.
2. **Global sign is a necessary exception.**  `P_(-epsilon)=-P_epsilon`, so raw-field equality
   cannot be classified merely by finite symmetric difference.  The correct two-point criterion
   is eventual equality or eventual opposition.
3. **Parent-theory collision.**  The sufficiency proof is close to the Shapiro--Sparer Jacobian
   criterion and Pong's differential algebra of arithmetic functions.  Scientific novelty is not
   claimed for the coefficient extraction or Jacobian mechanism.  The literature gate must decide
   whether the exact tail-matroid equivalence, raw differential-field classification, and unique
   positive-definite pointing constitute a non-routine combined theorem.
4. **No RH upcast.**  Positive-definiteness on the convergence half-plane is unconditional and does
   not locate zeros after continuation.  Any RH consequence must explicitly add the continued
   divisor and functional equation.
