## Why this card (the reorientation)
The whole team converged "C5 ⟺ G5-positivity ⟺ apex (RH)". **Wrong axis.** C5 needs one theorem unobtainable
in any *single* parent (statement or proof) — it need **not** be RH-bearing (the Airy=SL₂ keystone of
differential Galois is a *transcendence* theorem, not the hardest ODE problem). The founding fact is
**hypertranscendence** (Hölder). So the keystone target is the **weld of the two hypertranscendence sources
on the completed Λ**, on the transcendence axis. Crucially, hypertranscendence is a **function-level**
property (Hölder's pole/order method) — **not** value-transcendence — so it is plausibly **sub-Schanuel**
(provable), unlike the G2/DSS lower bound.

## Statement (the C5 target)
> **The completed `Λ(s) = Γ_ℝ(s)·ζ(s)` (`Γ_ℝ(s)=π^{-s/2}Γ(s/2)`) satisfies no algebraic

This generalizes the two founding theorems to their completion in the **joint** sense: Hölder (Γ: no
*differential* equation, Source A) ⊕ G1 (ζ: no *difference* equation, Source B) ⟶ Λ: no *difference-
differential* equation.

## Structural reductions (done — the real content)
**(R1) The `σ_p`-action on Λ is rank-1 and Γ-blind.** `σ_p` shifts only the `p`-coordinate `x_p=p^{-s}↦
p^{-1}x_p`; `Γ_ℝ` depends on the global diagonal `s`, not on individual `x_p`, so **`σ_p Γ_ℝ = Γ_ℝ`**
(archimedean factor is `σ_p`-invariant). Hence
> `σ_p Λ / Λ = σ_p ζ / ζ = (1−p^{-s})/(1−p^{-(s+1)})` — a fixed **prime-rational** function (Source B, no Γ).
So every `σ_S`-shift of Λ is `Λ` times an explicit element of the prime-rational field
`K_B := ℂ(p^{-s} : p)`. **The difference structure of Λ collapses to rank-1 multiplicative coefficients.**

**(R2) Reduction to a differential statement over the prime field.** By (R1) a difference-differential
equation for Λ is a **differential** equation (in `∂_s`) for Λ with coefficients in `K_B`. Since `ζ ∈`
(diagonal of) `K_B`, this is equivalent to:
> **`Γ_ℝ` is differentially transcendental over the prime-difference field `K_B = ℂ(p^{-s} : p)`.**
The weld is now a single clean question: *does adjoining the entire prime-exponential field tame the
archimedean Γ?*

**(R3) CC/non-CC reading (DeWitt #131).** Under `σ_∞:s↦s+1`, each `p^{-s}` has **constant** multiplier
`p^{-1}` (CC); `Γ_ℝ` has the **polynomial** multiplier `s/2π` (non-CC). So the weld is exactly *"the unique
non-CC element (Γ) stays differentially transcendental over the field generated by the CC elements
(prime exponentials)"* — the *"couple the non-CC lane to the CC lanes"* C5 named in #131.

**(R4) `∂_s` log-derivative seam.** `∂_s log Λ = ½ψ(s/2) − ½logπ + ζ'/ζ(s)` — archimedean digamma (Source A)
+ prime von-Mangoldt sum (Source B); the only Γ-content enters through `∂_s`, confirming (R1).

## Proof direction (Hölder-style, function-level, sub-Schanuel)
Hölder rules out a differential equation for Γ over `ℂ(s)` by its **pole structure** (simple poles at all
`s∈ℤ_{≤0}`, regularly spaced) being incompatible with any algebraic differential equation over `ℂ(s)`. The
weld upgrade: rule out a differential equation for `Γ_ℝ` over `K_B`. Key growth/pole facts:
- **`K_B` elements are bounded on vertical lines** (ratios of Dirichlet polynomials `Σc_n n^{-s}`, `|·|≤
  Σ|c_n|n^{-σ}`) and have poles only on the zero-sets of Dirichlet polynomials (discrete, log-period-driven,
  generically **off** the negative-real axis).
- **`Γ_ℝ` decays exponentially on vertical lines** (Stirling: `|Γ(s/2)|∼e^{-(π/4)|Im s|}`) and has its pole
  sequence at `s=0,−2,−4,…` (regular, on the negative-real axis).
The contradiction to chase: a differential equation `F(s, y, y',…,y^{(n)})=0` with coefficients in `K_B`,
solved by `Γ_ℝ`, would force the coefficients' (controlled) pole/growth data to reproduce Γ_ℝ's regular
real-axis pole sequence and vertical exponential decay — Hölder's incompatibility, now relative to `K_B`.
This is **function-level** (poles + Stirling growth), **not** value-transcendence — so it does **not** hit
the Schanuel-for-prime-logs wall (the G2/DSS obstruction C). That is the reason this weld can be a *provable*
C5 while the G2 transcendence lower bound stays FIELDS-MEDAL.

## The Door-2 crux — RESOLVED: the diagonal weld is BRIDGE (proof below); C5 relocates off-diagonal
The win/lose question was: does the proof need the *independent* `σ_p`, or reduce to `(σ_∞,∂_s)` finite-base
(Hardouin–Singer ⟹ BRIDGE)? **It reduces — BRIDGE.** Clean proof of the theorem, and of its grade:

**Proof of the theorem (diagonal joint hypertranscendence).** By (R1)–(R2) every `∂_s^a σ_S Λ` lies in the
`K_F`-span of `{∂_s^j Λ}_j` (Leibniz: `∂_s^a(Λ·r_S)=Σ_j\binom{a}{j}(∂_s^{a-j}r_S)∂_s^jΛ`, `r_S∈K_B`), where
`K_F := ℂ(s, p^{-s}:p)`. So a difference-differential equation for Λ is a **differential** equation for Λ
over `K_F`; it suffices to show `Γ_ℝ` (equivalently Λ) is **differentially transcendental over `K_F`**. Clean
argument, using only Hölder + elementary differential algebra (no unverified parametrized-Galois criterion):
- **Hölder 1887:** `Γ_ℝ` is differentially transcendental over `ℂ(s)` (Source A).
- **The prime exponentials are D-finite over `ℂ(s)`:** each `p^{-s}=e^{-s\log p}` solves `y'=−(\log p)y`, so
  `K_F` is a differential extension of `ℂ(s)` by **differentially algebraic** elements:
  `\mathrm{difftrdeg}(K_F/ℂ(s)) = 0`.
- **Additivity of differential transcendence degree:** adjoining diff-algebraic elements cannot lower a
  diff-transcendental element's status; `Γ_ℝ` (difftrdeg 1 over `ℂ(s)`) stays **diff-transcendental over
  `K_F`**.
Hence Λ satisfies no differential equation over `K_F` ⟹ **no diagonal difference-differential equation.** ∎

*(Equivalent CC reading, #131: `Γ_ℝ`'s multiplier `s/2π` is non-CC over `K_F` — the `1/s=∂_s\log(s/2π)`
Hölder obstruction is not a `σ`-coboundary; the prime multipliers `p^{-1}` are ∂-flat/D-finite, **inert**.
Matches the D-finiteness argument: the ∂-flat primes cannot absorb the archimedean `1/s`.)*

**Door-2 grade — BRIDGE.** The proof uses only "`s/2π ∉ ℂ(p^{-s})`" — which already holds over **one** prime
