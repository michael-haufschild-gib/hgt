1. Since $0 \le \|M_p(p^{-2it})\| \le 1$ and their product is $1$, $\|M_p(p^{-2it})\| = 1$ for every $p \in S$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$; as $t \mapsto p^{-2it}$ surjects onto $S^1$, $\|M_p(v)\| = 1$ for all $|v| = 1$.
