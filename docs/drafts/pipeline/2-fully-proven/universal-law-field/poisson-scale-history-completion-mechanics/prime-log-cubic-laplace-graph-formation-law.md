## 1. Big-picture interpretation

The critical cubic endpoint is not a failed bounded operator needing regularization.  It is the
Gram shadow of a canonical closed unbounded synthesis.  The source deformation therefore crosses
from the Hilbert--Schmidt ideal into an unbounded graph category at its intrinsic maximal time.


```text
(A_(d,u)c)(t)=sum_p c_p psi_(d,u,p)(t).                 (PCLGF.1)
```


the complete prime-mixed cubic kernel, with every literal prime label retained.  Also

```text
||psi_(d,u,p)||_2^2=w_(d,u)(p).                         (PCLGF.3)
```

Hence `A_(d,u)` extends to a Hilbert--Schmidt operator exactly for `u<d`, by ULF-PLCIE-001.  At


## 3. Canonical closability

`A_*` is nevertheless closable.  Its finite-support domain is dense in `ell2(Primes)`.  Fix
`0<epsilon<R` and let `g in L2(0,infinity)` vanish outside `[epsilon,R]`.  Cauchy--Schwarz gives

```text
|<psi_(*,p),g>|^2
 <= 4 R^2(R-epsilon)||g||_2^2 (log p)^4/p^(1+2epsilon). (PCLGF.5)
```

The right-hand side is summable over primes because every logarithmic power is summable against
`n^(-sigma)` for `sigma>1`.  Thus `g` belongs to the maximal adjoint domain of `A_*`.  Such
compact-away-from-zero `L2` functions form a dense subspace.  Therefore `dom(A_*^*)` is dense,
so `A_*` is closable and its closure is the unique closed extension generated by the literal
finite-prime synthesis.

Finite distinct exponentials are linearly independent, hence `A_*` is injective on its source
core.  This is source faithfulness, not positivity selection: positivity merely expresses the
identity `A_*^*A_*`; all selection must occur in a later relative completion boundary map.
