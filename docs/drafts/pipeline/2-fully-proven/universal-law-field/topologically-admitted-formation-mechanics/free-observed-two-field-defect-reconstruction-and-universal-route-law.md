## 4. Main theorem

### Theorem ULF-TAFM-14 — primitive-defect reconstruction and universal route law

For every grammar `G`, target observed two-field `Q`, and generator data `(psi,phi,A₀)`:

1. **Boundary-defect reconstruction.** For every finite rewrite chain `a`,

   \[
   \Delta_\partial(a)=\sum_r a_r\delta_\partial(r).       \tag{FOTF.14}
   \]

2. **Observer-defect reconstruction.** For every finite history `x`,

   \[
   \Delta_q(x)=\sum_i x_i\delta_q(i).                     \tag{FOTF.15}
   \]

3. **Primitive/global equivalence.** The complete defects vanish exactly when all primitive
   defects vanish:

   \[
   \Delta_\partial=0\iff(\forall r,\delta_\partial(r)=0),
   \quad
   \Delta_q=0\iff(\forall i,\delta_q(i)=0).               \tag{FOTF.16}
   \]

4. **Universal route law.** There exists exactly one morphism

   \[
   A:F(G)\longrightarrow Q                                \tag{FOTF.17}
   \]

   satisfying

   \[
   A_2([r])=\psi(r),\quad A_1([i])=\phi(i),\quad
   A_0=A_0^{\rm given}                                    \tag{FOTF.18}
   \]

   if and only if

   \[
   \Delta_\partial=0\quad\text{and}\quad\Delta_q=0.      \tag{FOTF.19}
   \]

Equivalently, route existence and uniqueness are decided completely by the two primitive square
tests `(FOTF.10)`--`(FOTF.11)`.

### Proof

For a primitive rewrite `[r]` with coefficient `c`, linearity gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta_\partial(c[r])
 &=L_\phi(c\rho(r))-\partial_Q(c\psi(r))\\
 &=c\bigl(L_\phi(\rho(r))-\partial_Q\psi(r)\bigr)
 =c\delta_\partial(r).
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{FOTF.20}
\]

Every element of `k^(R)` is a finite sum of such generators, so linearity proves `(FOTF.14)`.
The identical argument on an intervention generator gives

\[
 \Delta_q(c[i])=c\delta_q(i),                              \tag{FOTF.21}
\]

and finite linear extension proves `(FOTF.15)`.

If a complete defect is zero, evaluation on coefficient-one generators makes every primitive
defect zero. Conversely, `(FOTF.14)`--`(FOTF.15)` show that zero primitive defects make the complete
defect maps zero. This proves `(FOTF.16)`.

Assume `(FOTF.19)`. Define

\[
 A_2:=L_\psi,qquad A_1:=L_\phi,qquad A_0:=A_0^{\rm given}. \tag{FOTF.22}
\]

The two zero-defect equations are exactly the two naturality equations `(FOTF.6)`, so `(FOTF.22)`
is a morphism. It has the required generator values. If `B` is another such morphism, linearity and
finite support imply `B₂=L_psi` and `B₁=L_phi`; its observer map is prescribed, hence `B=A`.

Conversely, suppose `A` satisfying `(FOTF.18)` exists. Linear maps from a free finitely supported
module are determined by coefficient-one generators, so `A₂=L_psi` and `A₁=L_phi`. Substitution
into the two naturality equations of `A` gives `(FOTF.19)`. This proves every clause. ∎
