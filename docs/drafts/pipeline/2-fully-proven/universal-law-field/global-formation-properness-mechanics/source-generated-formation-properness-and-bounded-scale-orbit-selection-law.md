### Theorem 2.1 — formation genesis and scale covariance

For every \(u\ge0\),

\[
 \boxed{S H_u=V_u H_uS.}
\tag{GFPM.5}
\]

On the ambient parameter plane, where \(D_d^{-1}\) is defined,

\[
 \boxed{D_dH_uD_d^{-1}=H_{du}.}
\tag{GFPM.6}
\]

Consequently the exchange at the \(n\)-th source scale generates

\[
 \boxed{S H_{d^nu}=V_{d^nu}H_{d^nu}S.}
\tag{GFPM.7}
\]

Thus \(u\mapsto d^nu\) is not an imposed penalty schedule. It is generated by iterating a literal
source renormalization before repeating the same marked exchange experiment.

#### Proof

Direct substitution gives

\[
 SH_u(\sigma,\tau)=(\sigma+u,\tau+\sigma+u)
 =V_uH_uS(\sigma,\tau).
\]

Likewise

\[
 D_dH_uD_d^{-1}(\sigma,\tau)
 =D_d(\sigma/d+u,\tau)
 =(\sigma+du,\tau)=H_{du}(\sigma,\tau).
\]

Iteration gives \(D_d^nH_uD_d^{-n}=H_{d^nu}\), and (GFPM.5) at parameter \(d^nu\) gives
(GFPM.7). QED.

## 3. Canonical Boolean reporter

Let \(m_2\) be the unique mode consisting of the least prime \(2\) at first depth. Under the gas
state at \(\theta=(\sigma,\tau)\), its occupation number is geometric with failure ratio

\[
 q=2^{-(\sigma+\tau)}\in(0,1).
\tag{GFPM.8}
\]

The one-bit reporter records only whether this mode is occupied. Its law is Bernoulli\((q)\). After
the generated fugacity formation \(V_u\),

\[
 q_u=q2^{-u}.
\tag{GFPM.9}
\]

This reporter is canonical: no prime or depth is fitted. Prime \(2\) is the least prime and first
depth is the least nonzero depth.

For \(0<r,q<1\), write

\[
 \operatorname{BerJ}(q,r)
 =D(\operatorname{Ber}(q)\Vert\operatorname{Ber}(r))
  +D(\operatorname{Ber}(r)\Vert\operatorname{Ber}(q)).
\tag{GFPM.10}
\]

### Lemma 3.1 — exact one-bit work

For \(u\ge0\),

\[
 \boxed{
 J_q(u):=\operatorname{BerJ}(q,q_u)
 =(q-q_u)\left[u\log2+\log\frac{1-q_u}{1-q}\right].}
\tag{GFPM.11}
\]

In particular

\[
 J_q(u)\ge (q-q_u)u\log2.
\tag{GFPM.12}
\]

If \(u\ge1\), then

\[
 \boxed{J_q(u)\ge \frac{q\log2}{2}\,u.}
\tag{GFPM.13}
\]

Equality \(J_q(u)=0\) holds exactly when \(u=0\).

#### Proof

The elementary Bernoulli identity

\[
 \operatorname{BerJ}(q,r)
 =(q-r)\left(\log\frac q{1-q}-\log\frac r{1-r}\right)
\]

with \(r=q2^{-u}\) gives (GFPM.11). Since \(r\le q\), one has
\((1-r)/(1-q)\ge1\); its logarithm is nonnegative, proving (GFPM.12). If \(u\ge1\), then
\(2^{-u}\le1/2\), so \(q-q_u\ge q/2\), proving (GFPM.13). Strictness follows because
\(0<q<1\) and \(q_u=q\) exactly at \(u=0\). QED.

## 4. Full-source work dominates the reporter

Let \(P_\theta\) be the full zeta-gas class law and put

\[
 \mathcal W_\theta(u)
 =D(P_\theta\Vert P_{V_u\theta})+D(P_{V_u\theta}\Vert P_\theta).
\tag{GFPM.14}
\]

### Theorem 4.1 — local witness forces global properness

For every admitted \(u\ge0\),

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal W_\theta(u)\ge J_q(u).}
\tag{GFPM.15}
\]

Consequently \(\mathcal W_\theta\) is coercive on the positive formation ray:

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal W_\theta(u)\longrightarrow+\infty\quad(u\to+\infty).}
\tag{GFPM.16}
\]

Every finite-work sublevel is bounded. If work is read in the real topology, lower
semicontinuity of relative entropy makes every sublevel closed, hence compact.

#### Proof

The least-mode occupation bit is a measurable observer of the full configuration. Its pushforward
under \(P_\theta\) is Bernoulli\((q)\), and under \(P_{V_u\theta}\) is
Bernoulli\((q_u)\). Relative-entropy data processing in both directions gives (GFPM.15).
Equation (GFPM.13) then gives, for \(u\ge1\),

\[
 \mathcal W_\theta(u)\ge \frac{q\log2}{2}u,
\]

which proves coercivity and boundedness of sublevels. Lower semicontinuity supplies closedness.
QED.

The proof is genuinely local-to-global: one canonical two-outcome quotient makes the entire source
formation proper. No estimate over all groups or all primes is needed.

## 5. Bounded-scale-orbit selection

### Theorem 5.1 — global formation/properness selection

Fix an integer \(d\ge2\) and \(u\ge0\). The following are equivalent:

1. \(u=0\);
2. the reporter works \(\{J_q(d^nu):n\ge0\}\) are bounded;
3. the full-source works \(\{\mathcal W_\theta(d^nu):n\ge0\}\) are bounded;
4. the source-generated scale orbit meets some compact work sublevel at every depth.

For the formal carrier, relative entropy takes values in \(\mathbb R_{\ge0}\cup\{+\infty\}\).
Accordingly, “bounded” in items 2--4 means that there is one
\(C<+\infty\) dominating every member of the sequence. No separate pointwise finiteness premise is
needed. A nonneutral scale may already have infinite work; that only strengthens properness.

Equivalently,

\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_n\mathcal W_\theta(d^nu)<\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u=0.}
\tag{GFPM.17}
\]

#### Proof

If \(u=0\), every generated formation is the identity and both works vanish. Conversely, if
\(u>0\), then \(d^nu\to+\infty\). Equations (GFPM.13) and (GFPM.15) give

\[
 \mathcal W_\theta(d^nu)\ge J_q(d^nu)
 \ge\frac{q\log2}{2}d^nu
\]

for all sufficiently large \(n\). Both work sequences are therefore unbounded and eventually leave
every compact sublevel. QED.

## 8. Hostile conjecture loops

1. **Strict local Fisher information implies selection. — False.** It gives only local
   distinguishability. The source dilation and global scale orbit are load-bearing.
2. **Properness alone selects zero. — False.** It only bounds a parameter. Selection occurs because
   a nonzero parameter has the unbounded source orbit \(d^nu\).
3. **Any observer proves global properness. — False.** A constant or phase-only observer can erase
   the radial formation. The least-mode occupation bit is nonconstant and source-canonical.
4. **Recurrence contradicts properness. — False.** A lossy compact observer can recur while the
   sufficient/full work escapes. The theorem classifies that recurrence as information loss.
5. **Clock rescaling removes the theorem. — Only after changing the marking.** Replacing
   \(H_{d^nu}\) by a manually compensated \(H_u\) changes the source schedule. Properness is a law
   of marked formations, not an unparameterized orbit set.
6. **The theorem proves RH. — False.** An RH application must still construct, before zero
   specialization, a source-natural comparison sending radial displacement to \(u\) and prove the
   uniform bound (GFPM.18). The theorem proves that those two tasks are sufficient and that exact
   vanishing is unnecessary.
