## 1. Big-picture interpretation

Addition and multiplication cannot be glued by imposing a complementary Steinberg relation in a
static quotient on which the logarithmic Kummer clock descends.  One complementary cell and its
first two normal clock jets already generate the unit.  Hence the only such differential quotient
is the zero ring.

The useful object is therefore not a quotient by source relations.  It is the moving relation
together with its normal-jet resolution and the size/naturality of its splittings.  Local algebraic
exactness is automatic here; cofinal bounded split exactness is the nontrivial completion datum.

## 3. The theorem

Let `k` be a field of characteristic different from `2`.  Let `A,B in k` be nonzero and put

`R = k[X,Y]`,

`D = A X d/dX + B Y d/dY`,

`c = (X-1)(Y-1)`.

### Theorem SKJS-1

The ideal generated by `c`, `D c`, and `D^2 c` is the unit ideal:

`(c, D c, D^2 c) = R`.

Consequently every `D`-stable ideal containing `c` equals `R`.

### Proof

Write `U=X-1` and `V=Y-1`.  Direct differentiation gives

`c = U V`,

`D c = (A+B)c + A V + B U`.

Set

`g = D c - (A+B)c = A V + B U`.

Then

`U g - A c = B U^2`,

so `U^2` belongs to `(c,Dc)` because `B` is invertible.

A second differentiation gives

`D^2 c = (A+B)^2 c + A(A+2B)V + B(2A+B)U + 2AB`.

Therefore

`h = D^2c - (A+B)^2c - (A+2B)g`

satisfies

`h = 2AB + B(A-B)U`.

Put `h' = 2AB - B(A-B)U`.  Both `h` and `U^2` lie in `(c,Dc,D^2c)`, hence so does

`h h' + B^2(A-B)^2 U^2 = (2AB)^2`.

The scalar `(2AB)^2` is invertible, so `1` belongs to the ideal.  This proves the first statement.
If `J` is `D`-stable and contains `c`, it contains `Dc` and `D^2c`, hence contains `1`.  QED.

## 4. Actual group-algebra corollary

Let `x,y` be positive rationals with `x+y=1`, and let the Kummer derivation on the rational
multiplicative group algebra satisfy

`D([q]) = log(q) [q]`.

Map `X` to `[x]`, `Y` to `[y]`, `A` to `log x`, and `B` to `log y`.  Since `0<x,y<1`, both
logarithms are nonzero.  The Euler derivation intertwines the group-algebra Kummer derivation.
SKJS-1 therefore maps to

`1 in (c,Dc,D^2c)`

inside the actual rational-adelic group algebra.  Any Kummer-stable ideal containing even one
positive complementary Steinberg cell is the whole algebra.

## 5. No-go closure and positive survivor

- **Exact killing mechanism:** static Steinberg/Milnor quotient plus descended Kummer derivation is
  zero.  Adding p-derivation, lambda-ring, or Frobenius vocabulary does not repair the ordinary
  Kummer quotient; Teichmuller Frobenius is vertical and its arithmetic delta vanishes on the
  group-like atoms.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** the three-term normal-jet map
  `(u0,u1,u2) |-> u0 c + u1 Dc + u2 D^2c`
  is surjective at every complementary cell.  Thus the invariant can only be failure of a
  compatible uniformly bounded source-native splitting across the cofinal family, not its local
  cokernel or homology.
- **New insight:** ordinary quotient theories erase precisely the normal escape already detected by
  the corpus.  A completion must retain moving relations and measure the conditioning of their
  nullhomotopies.

## 6. RH boundary

SKJS-1 does not prove RH.  It narrows the pre-divisor model class.  In the live completion route,
one must construct from literal integer/prime/Gamma formation a compatible bounded splitting of
the common moving-relation jet resolution, with bounded information-neutral recovery and
functional-equation equivariance.  Existing bounded-factorization and codiagonal-rigidity theorems
would then force every horizontal coefficient to vanish and hence imply RH.

The theorem shows why that missing statement is global and quantitative: every finite cell is
already algebraically exact.
