## 3. Exact theorem

Let `A` be the Route-R summable Hadamard corona and

`h=[(1/p_n)_n] in A`.

For horizontal grade `t`, let `C(t)` be the external tensor curvature and let `E(t)` be the literal
Gamma-six paired boundary curvature.

### Theorem

1. If `t != 0`, then `C(t)=h tensor h`.
2. Hence `C(s)=C(t)` for every two nonzero `s,t`.
3. At prime coordinates `p_n,p_m`, the representative coefficient of `h tensor h` is
   `1/(p_n p_m)`, exactly the extended Big-Witt physical pair atom.
4. `E(1)<E(2)`.
5. There is no function `F : A tensor A -> R` with `F(C(t))=E(t)` for every real `t`.
6. The marked profile `(h_t,E(t))`, where `h_t` is the oriented completion class, is injective in
   `t`.

### Proof

For `t>0`, the oriented wall class is `-h`; for `t<0`, it is `h`; for `t=0`, it is zero. Tensor
bilinearity gives `(-h) tensor (-h)=h tensor h`, proving 1 and 2. Coefficient multiplication gives
`(1/p_n)(1/p_m)=1/(p_n p_m)`, proving 3. The source curvature formula and strict monotonicity of
`cosh` on the nonnegative ray give 4. If `F` existed, 2 with `s=1,t=2` would imply `E(1)=E(2)`,
contradicting 4. QED.

For 6, equality of oriented classes first puts two nonzero grades on the same sign ray; `h` and
`-h` are distinct because `h != 0` over the complex scalars. Equality of costs then gives equality
of `cosh` values. Strict monotonicity on the nonnegative ray, applied directly or after negation,
recovers the grade. Zero is already separated by vanishing of the oriented class. QED.
