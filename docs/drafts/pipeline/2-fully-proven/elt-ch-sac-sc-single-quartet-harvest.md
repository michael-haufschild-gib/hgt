### 2. Hecke-Dilated Integer-Probe Isometry and Welch Correlation Floors
On `ZeroFreeProbe` (`deg 1 = deg 2 = 0`), define `heckeDilateAt q f` ($q \ge 1$) by placing $f(m)$ at $qm$ and $0$ off $q\mathbb{N}$.

Thus $\langle T_q f, T_q g\rangle_{\mathrm{Weil}} = \langle f, g\rangle_{\mathrm{Weil}}$ for all $q \ge 1$, and more generally $\langle T_{rq} f, T_{rq'} g\rangle = \langle T_q f, T_{q'} g\rangle$.

### 4. Additive Shift Stability of the Weil Comb vs Prime-Contact Destruction
For an integer-translate comb $c$ supported on $[N, N+L]$ ($N \ge 1$) and an additive shift $h \ge 1$:
- **Singular-jet cancellation and $O(h/N)$ budget stability:** For $0 < b < a$, $\operatorname{jetKernel}(a+h, b+h) - \operatorname{jetKernel}(a, b) = \log(1 + \frac{a-b}{b}) - \log(1 + \frac{a-b}{b+h}) + \frac{(a-b)h}{2(a+b)(a+b+2h)}$ equals $\int_b^{b+h} \frac{a-b}{t(t+a-b)}\,dt + \frac{(a-b)h}{2(a+b)(a+b+2h)} \in [0, \frac{5(a-b)h}{4b^2}]$. ### 5. Four-Factor Log-Positivity Dichotomy, Bernoulli–Möbius `L²` Convergence, and Composite Dipoles
- **Four-factor dichotomy:** For $b : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $n \ge 1$, $\operatorname{Re}(2b(n) + b(n)(n^{-i\gamma} + n^{i\gamma})) = 4\cos^2(\frac{\gamma\log n}{2})b(n)$. Conversely, on single-prime quadratic Euler modifications, four-factor positivity fails at a single prime power: `PositiveFiniteEuler` with roots $-r, -p/r$ ($r > 0$) at $\gamma_0 = \pi/\log p$ has $p^2$ weight $2(1 - r^2 - (p/r)^2) \le 2(1 - 2p) \le -6$, and positive-root factors with roots $p^\sigma, p^{1-\sigma}$ at $\gamma_0 = 2\pi/\log p$ have $p^1$ weight $4(1 - p^\sigma - p^{1-\sigma}) \le 4(1 - 2\sqrt{p}) < 0$.
