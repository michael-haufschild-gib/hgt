## Definitions

Let `(C^bullet,d)` be a cochain complex of vector spaces over a field.  Write

\[
 Z^n=\ker(d:C^n\to C^{n+1}),
 \qquad
 H^n(C)=Z^n/dC^{n-1}.
\]

Let `O` be a poset of observers.  For every `o in O` and `k>=0`, let
`S_o^k subset C^k` be the declared subspace of primitives which vanish cofinally on every source
face required by observer `o`.  Assume monotonicity:

\[
 o\le o'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad S_o^k\subseteq S_{o'}^k.
\tag{1}
\]

Define the degree-`n` stable-primitive group

\[
 \mathbb H^n_{\infty,o}(C)=Z^n/dS_o^{n-1}.
\tag{2}
\]

## Theorem

For every `n>=1`:

1. There is a canonical short exact sequence

   \[
   0\longrightarrow
   {C^{n-1}\over S_o^{n-1}+Z^{n-1}}
   \xrightarrow{\ \bar d\ }
   \mathbb H^n_{\infty,o}(C)
   \xrightarrow{\ p_o\ }
   H^n(C)
   \longrightarrow0.
   \tag{3}
   \]

2. An `n`-cochain `c` has a primitive in `S_o^(n-1)` if and only if the following gates vanish in
   order:

   \[
   dc=0,
   \qquad [c]_{H^n(C)}=0,
   \qquad [a]=0\text{ in }C^{n-1}/(S_o^{n-1}+Z^{n-1}),
   \tag{4}
   \]

   where `c=da` after the second gate.  The third condition is independent of the chosen
   primitive.
3. If `o<=o'`, quotienting by the larger stable-coboundary space gives a canonical surjection and
   short exact sequence

   \[
   0\longrightarrow {dS_{o'}^{n-1}\over dS_o^{n-1}}
   \longrightarrow\mathbb H^n_{\infty,o}(C)
   \xrightarrow{q_{oo'}^n}\mathbb H^n_{\infty,o'}(C)
   \longrightarrow0.
   \tag{5}
   \]

   Moreover `p_(o') q_(oo')^n=p_o`.  Hence every class with nonzero ordinary image is immortal:
   it cannot vanish under observer strengthening.
4. For an exact cochain `c=da`, its death region

   \[
   D_n(c)=\{o:[c]_o=0\}
   =\{o:a\in S_o^{n-1}+Z^{n-1}\}
   \tag{6}
   \]

   is primitive-independent and upward closed.  The topology generated by all `D_n(c)`, in one
   degree or jointly over all degrees, is contained in the upper Alexandrov topology of `O`.
5. Let `U:C^bullet -> C'^bullet` be a cochain isomorphism and `theta:O->O'` an order isomorphism
   such that `U(S_o^k)=S'_(theta(o))^k`.  Then `theta(D_n(c))=D'_n(Uc)` and `theta` is a
   homeomorphism for the stable-exactness topologies.
6. Put `B^n=dC^(n-1)`, `K_o^n=dS_o^(n-1)`, and order the subspace lattice `Sub(B^n)` by
   inclusion.  The map

   \[
   \kappa_n:O\to\operatorname {Sub}(B^n),
   \qquad o\mapsto K_o^n,
   \tag{7}
   \]

   realizes the exactness topology as a Scott pullback:

   \[
   \tau_{\rm ex}^n=\kappa_n^{-1}\tau_{\rm Scott}.
   \tag{8}
   \]

   Equivalently, it is the initial topology of the map into a power of Sierpinski space

   \[
   e_n:O\to\mathbb S^{B^n},
   \qquad e_n(o)(c)=1\Longleftrightarrow c\in K_o^n.
   \tag{9}
   \]

   Its all-degree `T_0` reflection identifies `o,o'` exactly when `K_o^n=K_(o')^n` for every
   `n`; its observable preorder is coordinatewise inclusion of these subspaces.

## Proof

The natural map

\[
 p_o:Z^n/dS_o^{n-1}\longrightarrow Z^n/dC^{n-1}
\]

is well-defined and surjective.  Its kernel is `dC^(n-1)/dS_o^(n-1)`.  Define

\[
 \bar d:C^{n-1}/(S_o^{n-1}+Z^{n-1})
 \longrightarrow dC^{n-1}/dS_o^{n-1},
 \qquad [a]\longmapsto[da].
\]

This is well-defined and surjective.  If `da=ds` for some `s in S_o^(n-1)`, then
`a-s in Z^(n-1)`, so `[a]=0` in its source.  Thus it is injective and `(3)` follows.

If `c=ds` for stable `s`, all gates in `(4)` vanish.  Conversely, the first two gates give
`c=da`; the third writes `a=s+z` with `s in S_o^(n-1)` and `z in Z^(n-1)`, hence `c=ds`.  Two
primitives differ by a cocycle, proving independence.

Monotonicity `(1)` gives `dS_o^(n-1) subset dS_(o')^(n-1)`.  The resulting quotient map is
surjective and has kernel `dS_(o')^(n-1)/dS_o^(n-1)`, proving `(5)`.  Both `p` maps send a cocycle
to the same ordinary class.  If a stable-primitive class dies, its ordinary image is therefore
zero; this proves immortality.

Finally, `[da]_o=0` exactly when `da=ds` for some stable `s`, equivalently
`a-s in Z^(n-1)`.  This is `(6)`.  It is independent of `a` and upward closed by `(1)`.  A topology
generated by upper sets is contained in the upper Alexandrov topology.  A filtered cochain
isomorphism preserves `d`, cocycles, stable subspaces, and the criterion `(6)`, which proves the
covariance statement.

For `c in B^n`, let

\[
 U_c=\{K\in\operatorname {Sub}(B^n):c\in K\}.
\]

This is upward closed.  If a directed union of subspaces contains `c`, one member contains `c`, so
`U_c` is Scott-open.  Finite intersections of these opens are the sets of subspaces containing a
fixed finite-dimensional subspace.  They form a Scott basis because every subspace is the directed
union of its finite-dimensional subspaces, and finite-dimensional subspaces are compact in the
inclusion lattice.  Since

\[
 D_n(c)=\kappa_n^{-1}(U_c),
\]

equation `(8)` follows.  Coordinate `{1}`-opens in the Sierpinski product pull back to the same
sets, proving `(9)`.  Equality of all membership predicates is equality of every `K_o^n`, while
implication of all predicates is coordinatewise inclusion.  This proves item 6. `QED`
