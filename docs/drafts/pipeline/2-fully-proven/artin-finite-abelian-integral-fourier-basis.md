# Finite-abelian integral Fourier basis for concrete Artin characters

- **ID:** `artin-finite-abelian-integral-fourier-basis`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Source:** Classical finite-group character theory; Brauer, *On Artin's L-Series with General
  Group Characters*, Ann. of Math. 48 (1947), 502–514, DOI 10.2307/1969183.
- **Statement:** If `G` is finite abelian, every concrete virtual (respectively genuine) complex
  character of `G` is a finite `ℤ`-linear (respectively `ℕ`-linear) combination of the concrete
  one-dimensional characters `G →* ℂˣ`.

## Goal

Close the exact integral bridge missing from `RHTower.deningerCharacter_span`.  That theorem gives
only a complex vector-space span and cannot establish a Brauer generator statement.  This card
must show that Fourier coordinates of concrete virtual characters are canonical integers and those
of concrete genuine characters are canonical naturals, then reconstruct the character from those
coordinates.  It is known literature infrastructure, not new scientific knowledge and not an
Artin-holomorphy proof.

## Exact target

For a finite abelian group `G`, let

```text
L_λ(g) = λ(g)  for λ : G → ℂˣ.
```

Construct concrete genuine and virtual characters `linGen(λ)` and `linVirt(λ)` whose underlying
function is `L_λ`.  Then prove, for every `θ : VirtualCharacter G`,

```text
θ(g) = Σ_λ (virtualMultiplicity G (linVirt λ) θ : ℂ) · L_λ(g),
```

and for every `θ : GenuineCharacter G`,

```text
θ(g) = Σ_λ (genuineMultiplicity G (linGen λ) θ : ℂ) · L_λ(g).
```

The sums are over the finite character group.  Consequently the concrete virtual-character group
equals the additive subgroup generated by the `linVirt(λ)`, and the genuine-character monoid
equals the additive submonoid generated by the `linGen(λ)`.

## Paper proof

1. `RHTower.deningerCharacter_linearIndependent` and
   `RHTower.deningerCharacter_span` show that the `L_λ` form a complex basis of `G → ℂ`.
2. Direct finite-character orthogonality gives
   `⟨L_λ, L_μ⟩ = 1` if `λ = μ`, and `0` otherwise, for the normalized pairing already used by
   `GaloisForLFunctions.characterPairing`.  This is independent of semisimplicity and has no
   Brauer-induction input.
3. Basis orthogonality yields Fourier reconstruction with coefficient
   `⟨θ, L_λ⟩`.
4. `linearCharacterRepresentation λ` is a genuine one-dimensional representation with character
   `L_λ`.  Thus `virtualMultiplicity_cast` identifies every coefficient of a concrete virtual
   character with an integer, and `genuineMultiplicity_cast` identifies every coefficient of a
   concrete genuine character with a natural number.
5. Substituting those coefficients in reconstruction proves the two closures.  Conversely each
   `L_λ` is itself a concrete genuine character, so the inclusions reverse.

No step assumes Artin holomorphy, automorphy, a factorization theorem, or a character-decomposition
theorem.  The remaining conversion from these same-group linear generators to literal
`Ind_G^G λ` monomial generators is a separate transport lemma and is not silently folded into this
card.
