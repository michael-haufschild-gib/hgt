## Main theorem

Let `d>=2`, let `1,tau_1,...,tau_d` be linearly independent over `Q`, and let `Q_d` and
the compatible pair-divisor module `Comp_d` be as in
`all-depth-pair-face-signature-completeness`. For `F_1,...,F_m in Q_d`, put

```text
D_j=PairFace_d(F_j) in Comp_d.
```

The rank formula is

```text
dtrdeg_(C(z)) C(z)<F_1,...,F_m>_delta
  = dim_(C(delta)) span{D_1,...,D_m}                     (1)
```

inside `C(delta) tensor_(C[delta]) Comp_d`.

Equivalently, every nonlinear differential dependence among arbitrary constrained functions is
already witnessed by one constant-coefficient differential-linear relation among their compatible
pair-face signatures.

For any fixed `F in Q_d` and arbitrary finite differential-translation operators
`P_1,...,P_m in C[delta][C]`, equivariance would give

```text
dtrdeg(P_1F,...,P_mF)
  = dim_(C(delta)) span{P_1 PairFace_d(F),...,P_m PairFace_d(F)}.  (2)
```

This strictly contains the structural Stanley--Reisner theorem: the operators in (2) may involve
arbitrary complex translations, and the ambient object is the universal compatible pair-face
module rather than the Laurent module generated by hierarchy parameters.


## What the parent does and does not prove

The parent proves an additive differential-translation module isomorphism

```text
Q_d/(Q_d intersect Mer_DA) ~= Comp_d.                    (5)
```

It follows from (5) that `D_j=0` exactly when `F_j` is DA and that every
constant-coefficient differential-linear DA relation is detected by the signatures. It does not
imply (1): an additive module quotient does not exclude nonlinear differential relations between
elements with linearly independent images. Formula (1) is therefore a new mathematical gate, not
a restatement of pair-face completeness.

## Surviving mechanism

The proof should be a rank-refined descent through the bottom-charge exact sequence


The nonformal point is the relative independence assertion:


A dependence in (5) would, by the several-automorphism Ostrowski theorem, produce one

## Adversarial attack

1. **Module-isomorphism fallacy.** Equation (3) alone is insufficient; no promotion is allowed
   without proving (5).
2. **Nonlinear closure test.** A counterexample would be `F,G in Q_d` with `G` differentially
   algebraic over `F` but with `PairFace_d(G)` not in the `C(delta)`-span of
   `PairFace_d(F)`. Rational functions such as `F^2` or `1/F` do not generically preserve the full
   mixed additive constraint, but this must be proved rather than assumed.
3. **Depth-two boundary.** At `d=2`, (1) is exactly the triptic divisor-matroid theorem, not merely
   triptic DA completeness. The all-depth statement inherits that stronger base theorem.
4. **Cross-face dependence.** Individual atomicity on each pair face does not imply joint
   independence across different elliptic curves. Triple compatibility creates genuine linear
   coupling; the proof must show it creates no additional nonlinear circuit.
5. **Dependent-period boundary.** Rational relations introduce finite orbits and extra constants,
   so (1) is not asserted outside the independent regime.
