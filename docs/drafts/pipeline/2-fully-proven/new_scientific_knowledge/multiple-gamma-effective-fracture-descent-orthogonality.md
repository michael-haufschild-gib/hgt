### Proof

First work in `C[x_0,...,x_r]`.  Coordinate-facet ideals satisfy

```text
J_F+J_G=J_(F intersection G)                              (5)
```

and the finite distributivity law

```text
J_F + intersection_G J_G
  = intersection_G (J_F+J_G).                             (6)
```

Equation `(6)` gives the pairwise-to-global assertion by induction, while `(3)` gives uniqueness.

For full exactness, split `(4)` by monomial exponent.  A monomial with support `S` survives
exactly on the facets containing `S`.  If `S` is a face, those facets form a nonempty full simplex,
and its augmented simplex complex is exact.  If `S` is not a face, the monomial is absent from
every term, including `C^(-1)`.  Taking the direct sum over monomials proves exactness.  Localizing
at the elements `1+x_j=t_j` preserves the exact complex and yields `(4)` over `R_tau`.
