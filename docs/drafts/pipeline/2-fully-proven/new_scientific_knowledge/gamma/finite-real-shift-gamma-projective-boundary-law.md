## The field and the exact law

Let `E={lambda_1,...,lambda_r}` be a finite set of real values satisfying

```text
lambda_i-lambda_j notin Z,     i!=j.                              (1)
```

Put

```text
Gamma_i(s)=Gamma(s+lambda_i),
psi_i(s)=Gamma_i'(s)/Gamma_i(s),

K_E=C(s)<Gamma_i:1<=i<=r>_D,
L_E=C(s)<psi_i:1<=i<=r>_D.                                      (2)
```

`[PROVED HERE]` Every nonzero `F in K_E` has unique leading data

```text
d(F) in Z,
omega(F) in Z lambda_1+...+Z lambda_r+Z,
b(F) in Z,
c(F) in C^x,                                                    (3)
```

after fixing one Gamma translate as reference, such that

```text
F(s)=c(F) Gamma(s+lambda_1)^(d(F))
          s^(omega(F))(log s)^(b(F))(1+o(1))                    (4)
```

as `Im(s)->+infinity`, uniformly after adding a value in a fixed compact subset of a vertical
tube. The representation of `omega(F)` by the shifts need not be unique; the real exponent value
in `(4)` is unique.

Consequently, for every finite nonzero tuple

```text
(F_1,...,F_M) in K_E^M,                                        (5)
```

some index `j` satisfies

```text
(F_1(s+z)/F_j(s+z),...,F_M(s+z)/F_j(s+z))
  ->(c_1,...,c_M) in C^M\{0}                                   (6)
```

locally uniformly in `z` along the upper vertical boundary.

## Theorem A: formal shifted-polygamma coordinates are free

The shifted digammas satisfy

```text
psi_i(s+1)-psi_i(s)=1/(s+lambda_i).                             (7)
```

Hardouin--Singer's additive dependence criterion says that a differential relation among a finite
family of solutions of `(7)` would produce a nonzero constant-coefficient linear differential
polynomial `P` and a rational value `g` such that

```text
P(1/(s+lambda_1),...,1/(s+lambda_r))=g(s+1)-g(s).               (8)
```

At the highest derivative order present in `P`, the left side has one nonzero highest principal
part on each pole orbit `-lambda_i+Z`. Condition `(1)` makes these orbits pairwise distinct. Every
rational shift difference on the right has total principal-part coefficient zero on each orbit.
Thus every highest-order coefficient of `P` is zero. Descending on derivative order makes `P=0`.

The same residue proof applies in the formal upper-vertical Stirling field. Hence the complete
formal expansions

```text
hat(psi_i)(s)=log s+sum_(n>=1)c_(i,n)s^(-n)                     (9)
```

are differentially algebraically independent over `C(s)`. Therefore substitution of the complete
Stirling expansions gives an injective differential-field map

```text
L_E -> C(log s)((s^(-1))).                                     (10)
```

Every nonzero value `A in L_E` consequently has a first nonzero Laurent/logarithmic term

```text
A(s)=c s^a(log s)^b(1+o(1)),     a,b in Z, c!=0.                (11)
```

Complete sectorial Poincare expansions of the shifted polygammas transfer `(11)` to the analytic
values, uniformly on compact vertical tubes.

## Theorem B: the Gamma coordinates add finitely ordered exponential weights

The triangular Bell identities give

```text
K_E=L_E(Gamma_1,...,Gamma_r).                                   (12)
```

Hardouin--Singer's multiplicative criterion applied to

```text
Gamma_i(s+1)=(s+lambda_i)Gamma_i(s)                             (13)
```

and the same orbit-residue calculation show that the Gamma coordinates and all their jets are
differentially algebraically independent. Equivalently, `Gamma_1,...,Gamma_r` are algebraically
independent over `L_E`, and their formal Stirling transseries are algebraically independent over
the formal image of `L_E`.

For `i>=2`, the Gamma-ratio expansion is

```text
Gamma_i(s)/Gamma_1(s)
 =s^(lambda_i-lambda_1)(1+sum_(n>=1)u_(i,n)s^(-n)).              (14)
```

Write a nonzero polynomial in the Gamma coordinates as

```text
P=sum_e A_e prod_i Gamma_i^(e_i),     A_e in L_E.               (15)
```

Each summand has an ordered value signature

```text
( total Gamma degree sum_i e_i,
  real power sum_i e_i(lambda_i-lambda_1)+a(A_e),
  logarithmic power b(A_e),
  complete inverse-power tail ).                                (16)
```

Only finitely many signatures occur. Upper-vertical Gamma decay orders first by smaller total
Gamma degree; the real shifts order the second coordinate; integers order the logarithmic
coordinate. If several terms tie in the first three coordinates, formal algebraic independence
ensures that their combined inverse-power tail is nonzero and therefore has a first nonzero
coefficient. Thus `(15)` has a unique leading value. Taking a quotient of two such polynomials
proves `(4)`. Applying this finite total order to a tuple proves `(6)`.

## Theorem C: recurrent values have finite phase rank

Let `f` be holomorphic on a vertical strip and uniformly almost periodic under vertical
translation in the uniform-on-closed-substrips topology. Suppose `f` is differentially algebraic
over `K_E`. After clearing denominators, write a finite relation

```text
sum_alpha F_alpha(s) prod_(j=0)^m f^(j)(s)^(alpha_j)=0,
F_alpha in K_E.                                                (17)
```

Bohr recurrence supplies `T_n->+infinity` such that `f(s+iT_n)->f(s)` uniformly on each smaller
closed substrip; Cauchy estimates give the same convergence for the derivatives in `(17)`.
Translate `(17)`, divide by a projectively dominant coefficient from `(6)`, and pass to the limit.
The result is a nonzero autonomous algebraic differential equation

```text
sum_alpha c_alpha prod_(j=0)^m f^(j)(s)^(alpha_j)=0.             (18)
```

The autonomous almost-periodic ADE frequency theorem then gives

```text
rank_Q Gamma_B(f)<infinity,                                    (19)
```

where `Gamma_B(f)` is the additive group generated by the Bohr frequencies of `f`.

The positive carrier statement is exact:

> A recurrent holomorphic value constrained by finitely many pairwise incongruent real Gamma
> translates carries only finitely many rational phase directions. Infinite rational phase rank
> therefore certifies an additional value carrier beyond that finite shifted-Gamma field.

## Zeta consequence

On every half-plane `Re(s)>=1+epsilon`, the Dirichlet series for zeta is holomorphic and vertically
uniformly almost periodic. Its Bohr spectrum contains every `log p`, and unique factorization makes

```text
{log p:p prime}                                                (20)
```

linearly independent over `Q`. Hence zeta has infinite rational Bohr rank. The contrapositive of
`(19)` gives

```text
zeta is differentially transcendental over K_E                (21)
```

for every finite `E` satisfying `(1)`.

## Prime-log translation-saturated consequence

Let `p,q` be distinct primes and

```text
Lambda=Z log p+Z log q.                                        (22)
```

Prime-log independence modulo one gives

### The boundary shape and its finite ordering (eq. (4), eq. (16))

`HTGeometry/GammaShiftShape.lean` carries the shape of eq. (4) in Lean.  The only new feature against
the one-Gamma law is that the argument exponent `ω` is *real* — it lives in `Zλ_1+…+Zλ_r+Z` — so the
argument factor is a complex power `s^ω` rather than an integer power:

> **`gammaShiftShape lam σ d ω b t = Γ((σ+λ)+it)^d · ((σ+it)^ω) · (log s)^b`**, with `d, b ∈ ℤ` and
> `ω ∈ ℝ`.

Its algebra is unconditional: **`gammaShiftShape_ne_zero`** beyond a fixed height when `σ+λ > 0`,
**`gammaShiftShape_mul`** (all three exponents add) and **`gammaShiftShape_div`** (all three

The three decay mechanisms of the ordering `(16)` are proved with `rpow` in place of `zpow`:

- **`tendsto_gammaShiftShape_zero_of_gamma_pos`** — a positive `Γ`-degree wins, because the argument
  and logarithm factors have polynomial growth even with a real exponent
  (**`hasPolyGrowthLine_cpow_mul_vertLog`**: `‖s^ω‖ = ‖s‖^ω ≤ ((|σ|+1)t)^⌈ω⌉` and

- **`tendsto_gammaShiftShape_zero_of_log_neg`** — at `Γ`-degree and argument exponent zero a negative

Hence the order itself: **`ShiftShapeDominates`**, **`tendsto_gammaShiftShape_ratio_zero`** (a
