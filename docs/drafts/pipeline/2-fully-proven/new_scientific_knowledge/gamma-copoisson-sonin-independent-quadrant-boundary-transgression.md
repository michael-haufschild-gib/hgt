## Source spaces and literal maps

Fix \(0<a\leq 1\) and \(r>1\), and put
\[
 a_j=a r^{-j}\qquad (j\geq0).
 \tag{GS1}
\]
Let \(L_j=L_{a_j}\) be Burnol's extended cosine-Sonine space in the ambient cosine-transform
Hilbert space \(\mathcal H\).  Let \(C_j\subset L_j\) be the closed subspace generated by
compactly supported co-Poisson sources and put
\[
 Y_j=C_j^\perp,\qquad E_j:\mathcal H\longrightarrow Y_j
 \tag{GS2}
\]
for the ambient orthogonal projection.  Every object in `(GS1)`--`(GS2)` is source-defined.

For a regular \(w\), write
\[
 {\cal M}f(w)=\pi^{-w/2}\Gamma(w/2)\int_0^\infty f(t)t^{-w}\,dt
 \tag{GS3}
\]
and let \(k_{w,j}\in L_j\) be its continuous Riesz evaluation vector.  The co-Poisson quotient
kernel is
\[
 y_{w,j}=E_jk_{w,j}\in Y_j.
 \tag{GS4}
\]
Let \(U_r f(t)=\sqrt r\,f(rt)\).  Both \(U_r\) and \(U_r^*\) send \(L_j\) into \(L_{j+1}\).
Define the two literal quotient bonding maps
\[
 T_j^+=E_jU_r^*|_{Y_{j+1}},\qquad
 T_j^-=E_jU_r|_{Y_{j+1}}.
 \tag{GS5}
\]
Since \(E_j\) is an orthogonal projection and \(U_r\) is unitary,
\[
 \|T_j^+\|\leq1,\qquad \|T_j^-\|\leq1.
 \tag{GS6}
\]
These are two different maps.  No diagonal relation between their indices has been imposed.

Put
\[
 \lambda_r(w)=r^{w-1/2}.
 \tag{GS7}
\]
In the Hermitian Riesz convention, normalized Mellin covariance gives
\[
 \Pi_jU_r^*k_{w,j+1}=\overline{\lambda_r(w)}\,k_{w,j},
 \qquad
 \Pi_jU_rk_{w,j+1}=\overline{\lambda_r(w)^{-1}}\,k_{w,j},
 \tag{GS8}
\]
where \(\Pi_j\) is the ambient projection onto \(L_j\).

Let \(\Lambda_j(w)\) be the completed co-Poisson Mellin multiplier.  The local
co-Poisson-ideal factorization from the parent card is
\[
 k_{w,j}-y_{w,j}
   =\overline{\Lambda_j(w)}\,c_{w,j}
 \tag{GS9}
\]
with \(c_{w,j}\) locally anti-analytic.

## Exact off-divisor boundary comparison

`[PAPER-PROVED] [GAMMA_COPOISSON_SONIN_BOUNDARY_COMPARISON]`

For every regular \(w\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 T_j^+y_{w,j+1}
  &=\overline{\lambda_r(w)}\,y_{w,j}
    -\overline{\Lambda_{j+1}(w)}\,E_jU_r^*c_{w,j+1},\\
 T_j^-y_{w,j+1}
  &=\overline{\lambda_r(w)^{-1}}\,y_{w,j}
    -\overline{\Lambda_{j+1}(w)}\,E_jU_rc_{w,j+1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{GS10}
\]
Consequently, on the co-Poisson annihilator boundary \(\Lambda_{j+1}(w)=0\),
\[
 T_j^+y_{w,j+1}
   =\overline{\lambda_r(w)}\,y_{w,j},
 \qquad
 T_j^-y_{w,j+1}
   =\overline{\lambda_r(w)^{-1}}\,y_{w,j}.
\tag{GS11}
\]
Every nontrivial zeta zero, with its confluent jets, lies on this boundary.  For its zeroth
evaluation vector,
\[
 y_{\rho,j}=k_{\rho,j}\neq0.
\tag{GS12}
\]

### Proof

Because \(Y_j\subset L_j\), \(E_j=E_j\Pi_j\).  Apply `(GS9)` at level \(j+1\), then `(GS8)`:
\[
\begin{aligned}
T_j^+y_{w,j+1}
 &=E_jU_r^*
   \bigl(k_{w,j+1}-\overline{\Lambda_{j+1}(w)}c_{w,j+1}\bigr)\\
 &=\overline{\lambda_r(w)}E_jk_{w,j}
   -\overline{\Lambda_{j+1}(w)}E_jU_r^*c_{w,j+1}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is the first identity in `(GS10)`; the second is identical with \(U_r\) and
\(\lambda_r^{-1}\).  Setting the co-Poisson multiplier to zero proves `(GS11)`.
Burnol's co-Poisson orthogonal-complement theorem says that every zero evaluation vector is in
\(C_j^\perp\), proving \(y_{\rho,j}=k_{\rho,j}\).  Nontriviality of completed Mellin evaluation
gives `(GS12)`.  `QED`

The correction in `(GS10)` is explicit and belongs to the co-Poisson boundary ideal.  Thus
`(GS11)` is a boundary value of a source-defined off-divisor identity, not a character relation
postulated after choosing a zero.

## Literal independent-quadrant section

Fix a co-Poisson annihilator character \(\rho\) and abbreviate
\[
 \lambda=\lambda_r(\rho).
 \tag{GS13}
\]
For \((p,q)\in\mathbb N^2\), define
\[
 X_{p,q}(\rho)
   =\overline{\lambda}^{\,q-p}y_{\rho,p+q}
   \in Y_{p+q}.
 \tag{GS14}
\]

`[PAPER-PROVED] [SONIN_INDEPENDENT_QUADRANT_SECTION]`

The family `(GS14)` obeys both projective bonding laws
\[
\begin{aligned}
 T_{p+q}^+X_{p+1,q}(\rho)&=X_{p,q}(\rho),\\
 T_{p+q}^-X_{p,q+1}(\rho)&=X_{p,q}(\rho).
\end{aligned}
\tag{GS15}
\]
Both coordinate maps are contractions by `(GS6)`.  Their squares commute on this boundary
section:
\[
T_{p+q}^+T_{p+q+1}^-X_{p+1,q+1}
=T_{p+q}^-T_{p+q+1}^+X_{p+1,q+1}
=X_{p,q}.
\tag{GS16}
\]
No equality of the two indices is used.

### Proof

Using `(GS11)`,
\[
\begin{aligned}
T_{p+q}^+X_{p+1,q}
 &=\overline\lambda^{\,q-p-1}
   T_{p+q}^+y_{\rho,p+q+1}\\
 &=\overline\lambda^{\,q-p}y_{\rho,p+q}
 =X_{p,q}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The second law uses
\(\overline\lambda^{\,q+1-p}\overline{\lambda^{-1}}
 =\overline\lambda^{\,q-p}\).
Applying the two laws successively proves `(GS16)`.  `QED`

This is independent-quadrant contractive control at operator level.  It does not yet say that
the inverse-limit section has bounded or subexponential norm.

## Sharp forced escape law

`[PAPER-PROVED] [SONIN_QUADRANT_ESCAPE_LOWER_BOUND]`

For every \(N\geq0\),
\[
 \|y_{\rho,N}\|
 \geq
 \max\{|\lambda|^N,|\lambda|^{-N}\}\,\|y_{\rho,0}\|.
 \tag{GS17}
\]
Consequently every quadrant coordinate satisfies
\[
\boxed{\quad
 \|X_{p,q}(\rho)\|
 \geq
 \max\{|\lambda|^{2q},|\lambda|^{-2p}\}
 \,\|X_{0,0}(\rho)\|.
 \quad}
\tag{GS18}
\]

### Proof

Compose the first identity of `(GS11)` from level \(N\) down to level \(0\):
\[
 T_0^+T_1^+\cdots T_{N-1}^+y_{\rho,N}
   =\overline\lambda^N y_{\rho,0}.
\]
The left-side operator is a contraction, so
\(\|y_{\rho,N}\|\geq|\lambda|^N\|y_{\rho,0}\|\).
The same argument with \(T_j^-\) gives the inverse-character bound.  Taking their maximum proves
`(GS17)`.  Multiply by
\(|\lambda|^{q-p}\), use \(N=p+q\), and calculate
\[
|\lambda|^{q-p}
\max\{|\lambda|^{p+q},|\lambda|^{-(p+q)}\}
=\max\{|\lambda|^{2q},|\lambda|^{-2p}\}.
\]
This proves `(GS18)`.  `QED`

The lower bound is sharp as a consequence of the two exact characters: no smaller universal
bound follows from contractivity alone.  Actual Sonin kernel norms may grow faster.

## Local--cofinal--quadrant charge identity

Let \(m_\rho>0\) be the Xi multiplicity at \(\rho\), and define
\[
 U(s)=2^{1/2-s},\qquad
 \eta(s)=\frac{\log|U(s)|}{\log2}=\frac12-\Re s.
\tag{GS19}
\]
Let
\[
 J_\Xi(s)=-(s-\tfrac12)\frac{\Xi'(s)}{\Xi(s)}
\tag{GS20}
\]
denote the centered affine Xi boundary current.  The directly verified Lean theorem
`re_centeredXiAffineResidue_eq_shellTransferEntropy` gives
\[
 Q_\rho:=
 \Re\operatorname*{Res}_{s=\rho}J_\Xi(s)
 =m_\rho\eta(\rho)
 =\frac{m_\rho}{\log2}\log|U(\rho)|.
\tag{GS21}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \log|\lambda_r(\rho)|
  =(\Re\rho-\tfrac12)\log r
  =-\frac{Q_\rho}{m_\rho}\log r.
\tag{GS22}
\]
Substitution into `(GS18)` gives the literal three-chart law
\[
\boxed{\quad
\|X_{p,q}(\rho)\|
\geq
\max\left\{
 \exp\!\left(-\frac{2qQ_\rho}{m_\rho}\log r\right),
 \exp\!\left(\frac{2pQ_\rho}{m_\rho}\log r\right)
\right\}
\|X_{0,0}(\rho)\|.
\quad}
\tag{GS23}
\]

Thus:

- \(Q_\rho>0\) forces escape along the \(p\)-face;
- \(Q_\rho<0\) forces escape along the \(q\)-face;
- only \(Q_\rho=0\) avoids forced exponential escape from both faces.

Julia orientation, Wold shell drift, and Sonin quadrant escape are not three parallel detectors.
They are local, cofinal, and projective charts of one signed multiplicity-weighted modular charge.
The equality of the first two charts is exact; the Sonin chart supplies an exact character and
the sharp norm lower bound `(GS23)`.

## Exact RH consumer and the unproved source estimate

The following statement contains no positivity or one-sided orientation:

```text
For every co-Poisson annihilator character rho and every epsilon>0,
there is C(rho,epsilon)<infinity such that

  ||X_(p,q)(rho)|| <= C(rho,epsilon) exp(epsilon(p+q))

for all independent p,q>=0.                              (GS24)
```

`(GS24)` is the literal independent-quadrant upper control.  If it holds, `(GS23)` forces
\(Q_\rho=0\): take \(p\to\infty,q=0\) when \(Q_\rho>0\), or
\(q\to\infty,p=0\) when \(Q_\rho<0\), and then choose epsilon below the forced positive
exponent.  By `(GS21)`, every nontrivial Xi zero then has
\(\Re\rho=1/2\).  Therefore
\[
 \text{`(GS24)` for the full co-Poisson annihilator boundary}
 \Longrightarrow \mathrm{RH}.
\tag{GS25}
\]

The corpus does **not** prove `(GS24)`.  Finite-level contractivity proves `(GS6)` and the
projective identities `(GS15)`, but an inverse system of contractions can have exponentially
growing lifts.  Equation `(GS23)` identifies that growth exactly with the nonzero divisor charge.

The first genuinely open estimate is therefore not “purity” or “conservativity.”  It is `(GS24)`
for the explicit vectors `(GS14)` and explicit maps `(GS5)`, derived uniformly from the
prime--Gamma/co-Poisson source before enumerating its annihilator characters.

## Hostile audit

1. **No boundedness sleight of hand.**  The raw critical-line evaluation norm may still grow with
   aperture.  Subexponential control is used because it removes universal polynomial or
   logarithmic aperture mass while detecting every nonzero horizontal exponent.
2. **No diagonal completion.**  The coordinates \(p\) and \(q\) are independent.  Restricting to
   \(p=q\) cancels the two scalar characters and is blind to every value of \(Q_\rho\).
3. **No sign argument.**  Both signs of \(Q_\rho\) are allowed; they escape through opposite
   faces.  Neither face is declared positive or preferred.
4. **No zero-fitted norm.**  The Hilbert spaces, projections, Gamma completion, co-Poisson
   subspaces, and bonding maps are fixed before \(\rho\).  The scalar factors in `(GS14)` are
   boundary eigencharacters, not a definition of a new target norm.
5. **No false commutation claim.**  \(T_j^+\) and \(T_j^-\) need not commute on the whole quotient.
   Equation `(GS16)` asserts path independence only on the co-Poisson boundary section, where the
   off-divisor error in `(GS10)` vanishes.
6. **No hidden RH proof.**  `(GS24)` is unproved and RH-bearing.  The theorem's unconditional
   advance is the literal comparison, two independent contractions, exact boundary section, and
   charge-calibrated escape lower bound.

## HGT rational-rank audit

The positive hypertranscendental Riemann-divisor frame does not prove `(GS24)`.  It proves the
opposite structural warning needed here:

```text
one common divisor fibre
  + countably many coefficient-disjoint positive shell sources
  + maximal differential-algebraic rank after the common divisor is removed
```

are mutually compatible.  Global differential transcendence rank does not give a lower bound on
the dimension or growth type of a specialized divisor fibre.  A one-dimensional conormal can
simultaneously evaluate infinitely many differentially independent transverse functions, and
specialization may collapse their functional independence.

Therefore the finite multiplicity \(m_\rho\) in `(GS21)` cannot be compared directly with the
countable differential rank of the shell family to force \(Q_\rho=0\).  Any such argument would
contradict the already-proved common-divisor frame.  Hypertranscendence contributes differently:
it rules out replacing `(GS24)` by any finite differential recurrence among shell coordinates.
The missing theorem must be an infinite analytic estimate on the literal boundary section, not a
finite-rank or finite-ADE argument.
