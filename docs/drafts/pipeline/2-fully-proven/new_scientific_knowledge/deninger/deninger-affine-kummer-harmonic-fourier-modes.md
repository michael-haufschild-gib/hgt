## 1. Finite phase stage

Let \(L\cong\mathbb Z^m\) be a finite character lattice and
\[
 T_L=\operatorname{Hom}(L,S^1)
\]
its compact phase torus.  Fix a real functional
\[
 \ell:L\longrightarrow\mathbb R
\]
with
\[
 \ell(a)\ne0\qquad(0\ne a\in L).                               \tag{1}
\]
Condition `(1)` is the finite-stage form of
`DAC_RESIDUAL_DENSE_KUMMER_LEAVES`.

Let \(Y_\ell\) be the constant Kronecker vector field on \(T_L\)
defined by
\[
 Y_\ell\chi_a=i\ell(a)\chi_a.                                  \tag{2}
\]
On
\[
 M_L=(0,\infty)_r\times T_L
\]
take the leaf distribution generated by
\(\partial_r,Y_\ell\).  Write \(dy\) for the formal leafwise
one-form dual to \(Y_\ell\), so
\[
 d_{\mathcal F}f
 =
 \partial_r f\,dr+Y_\ell f\,dy.                                \tag{3}
\]

The affine Kummer metric from
`DAC_HILBERT_KUMMER_AFFINE_LEAF_DISTRIBUTION` is conformal on each
leaf to
\[
 g_{c}
 =
 \frac{c}{r}(dr^2+dy^2),\qquad c>0.                             \tag{4}
\]
Indeed \(r=e^s\), \(A=r\partial_r\),
\(K=rY_\ell\), and the dual affine coframe is
\[
 \alpha=\frac{dr}{r},\qquad \beta=\frac{dy}{r}.
\]
Thus \(cr(\alpha^2+\beta^2)\) is `(4)`.

## 2. Measured leafwise Hodge equations

Use Haar probability measure \(d\eta\) on \(T_L\).  For leafwise
one-forms, conformal cancellation gives the longitudinal energy
\[
 \|\alpha\|_{\mathcal H^1}^2
 =
 \int_0^\infty\int_{T_L}
 \left(|a(r,\eta)|^2+|b(r,\eta)|^2\right)
 \,d\eta\,dr                                                    \tag{5}
\]
for \(\alpha=a\,dr+b\,dy\).  The Hodge star is
\[
 *dr=dy,\qquad *dy=-dr.                                        \tag{6}
\]

Closedness and coclosedness are respectively
\[
 \partial_r b-Y_\ell a=0,\qquad
 \partial_r a+Y_\ell b=0.                                     \tag{7}
\]

`[PROVED HERE] [DAC_AFFINE_KUMMER_HARMONIC_FOURIER_MODES]`

For every \(0\ne a\in L\), put
\[
 \mu_a=\ell(a),\qquad \varepsilon_a=\operatorname{sgn}(\mu_a)
\]
and
\[
 \alpha_a(r,\eta)
 =
 e^{-|\mu_a|r}\chi_a(\eta)
 \left(dr-i\varepsilon_a\,dy\right).                           \tag{8}
\]
Then:

1. \(\alpha_a\) is closed and coclosed;
2. it is square-integrable for `(5)`, with
   \[
    \|\alpha_a\|_{\mathcal H^1}^2=\frac1{|\mu_a|};              \tag{9}
   \]
3. it has definite chirality
   \[
    *\alpha_a=i\varepsilon_a\alpha_a;                           \tag{10}
   \]
4. the full space of square-integrable solutions of `(7)` on the
   open leaf cylinder is the orthogonal Hilbert sum of these
   one-dimensional mode spaces.

### Proof

On the \(a\)-th Fourier mode, `(7)` becomes
\[
 b'-i\mu_a a=0,\qquad a'+i\mu_a b=0.                            \tag{11}
\]
The coefficient of \(dr\) therefore satisfies
\[
 a''=\mu_a^2a.
\]
The only square-integrable solution at \(r=\infty\) is a scalar
multiple of \(e^{-|\mu_a|r}\), and `(11)` then gives
\[
 b=-i\varepsilon_a a.
\]
This proves `(8)` and completeness of the list for
\(\mu_a\ne0\).  If \(\mu_a=0\), both coefficients are constant and
no nonzero solution is in \(L^2(0,\infty)\).  Assumption `(1)` leaves
only the constant torus mode in this case, which also contributes
nothing.

Haar orthogonality separates distinct character modes.  Direct
integration gives
\[
 \int_0^\infty
 2e^{-2|\mu_a|r}\,dr
 =
 \frac1{|\mu_a|},
\]
proving `(9)`.  Formula `(6)` gives `(10)`.

## 3. Rational-power action

For a positive integer \(q\), the source power map is
\[
 F_q(r,\eta)=(qr,\eta^q).                                     \tag{12}
\]
Its pullback on phase characters is
\[
 F_q^*\chi_a=\chi_{qa},\qquad
 \mu_{qa}=q\mu_a.                                              \tag{13}
\]
Therefore
\[
 F_q^*\alpha_a=q\,\alpha_{qa}.                                \tag{14}
\]

Let
\[
 e_a=|\mu_a|^{1/2}\alpha_a .
\]
Then \(\|e_a\|=1\) and
\[
 F_q^*e_a=q^{1/2}e_{qa},\qquad
 q^{-1/2}F_q^*e_a=e_{qa}.                                     \tag{15}
\]
Thus the local harmonic carrier is a weighted
\(\ell^2\)-completion of the nonzero Kummer lattice on which the
centered power correspondences act by the arithmetic shift
\[
 a\longmapsto qa.                                              \tag{16}
\]
This is infinite-rank transverse data already present in Deninger's
unitary phase.  It is not an added spectral-divisor coefficient.

## 4. Cup and Hodge atom

On finite Fourier sums define the leafwise cup trace
\[
 B_L(\alpha,\beta)
 =
 \int_0^\infty\int_{T_L}\alpha\wedge\beta.                    \tag{17}
\]

`[PROVED HERE] [DAC_KUMMER_HARMONIC_CUP_ATOM]`

The mode pairing is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_L(\alpha_a,\alpha_b)&=0
 &&\text{if }b\ne-a,\\
 B_L(\alpha_a,\alpha_{-a})
 &=\frac{i}{\mu_a}.                                           \tag{18}
\end{aligned}
\]
It is alternating and nondegenerate on every finite
\(\{\pm a\}\)-stable mode packet.  Moreover
\[
 B_L(\alpha_a,*\overline{\alpha_a})
 =
 \frac1{|\mu_a|}>0.                                           \tag{19}
\]
Power pullback has weight one:
\[
 B_L(F_q^*\alpha,F_q^*\beta)
 =
 q\,B_L(\alpha,\beta),                                        \tag{20}
\]
and \(q^{-1/2}F_q^*\) preserves both `(18)` and `(19)`.

### Proof

Haar orthogonality makes the character product integrate to zero
unless \(a+b=0\).  For \(b=-a\), write
\(m=|\mu_a|\) and \(\varepsilon=\operatorname{sgn}(\mu_a)\).
Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &(dr-i\varepsilon\,dy)\wedge
  (dr+i\varepsilon\,dy)\\
 &\qquad=2i\varepsilon\,dr\wedge dy.
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
 B_L(\alpha_a,\alpha_{-a})
 =
 2i\varepsilon\int_0^\infty e^{-2mr}\,dr
 =
 \frac{i\varepsilon}{m}
 =
 \frac{i}{\mu_a}.
\]
Complex conjugation sends \(\alpha_a\) to
\(\alpha_{-a}\), and `(10)` gives
\[
 *\overline{\alpha_a}
 =
 -i\varepsilon\alpha_{-a}.
\]
Combining this with `(18)` proves `(19)`.  Finally `(14)` and
\(\mu_{qa}=q\mu_a\) give `(20)`.

This is a source Poincare/Hodge atom.  Equality of its smooth
suspension and unit-amalgamated transfer with Paugam's global pairing
is still the cup-isometry theorem.

## 5. Exact boundary of the theorem

The mode theorem is on the open positive-modulus leaf cylinder.
It does not decide the global domain at \(r=0\), where the original
affine frame loses rank and the unitary character locus remains.
Different relative, absolute, or rigged boundary conditions can
remove or retain `(8)`.

It also does not identify the quotient of the power-shift complex with
\(M_\zeta\).  That requires:
