## Prime-packet topology

Fix a rational prime \(p\), and set
\[
 U_p=\widehat{\mathbb Z}_{(p)}^\times
     =\prod_{\ell\ne p}\mathbb Z_\ell^\times,\qquad
 B_p=U_p/p^{\widehat{\mathbb Z}},                                  \tag{1}
\]
where \(p^{\widehat{\mathbb Z}}\) is the closed subgroup generated by
\(p\).  Let
\[
 S_p=\mathbb R/(\log p)\mathbb Z.                                  \tag{2}
\]

`[PROVED HERE] [DAC_DENINGER_PRIME_PACKET_HAAR_RETRACTION]`

For Deninger's rational-Witt suspension \(X_{\mathbb Z}\), the packet
\(\Gamma_p\) over the closed point \((p)\) is canonically, up to the
cyclotomic coordinate used to write `(1)`, homeomorphic and
\(\mathbb R\)-equivariant to
\[
 \Gamma_p\cong B_p\times S_p,                                      \tag{3}
\]
where the flow translates the second factor.

### Proof

Choose a geometric point over \(p\).  Its multiplicative residue field is
\[
 \overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times
 \cong \mu_{p'}
\]
and its injective complex characters are the automorphisms of
\(\mu_{p'}\).  With the topology of pointwise convergence this
automorphism group is \(U_p\) with its profinite topology.  The
decomposition group
\(\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb F}_p/\mathbb F_p)
\cong\widehat{\mathbb Z}\) acts by powers of Frobenius, hence through the
closed subgroup \(p^{\widehat{\mathbb Z}}\).  Deninger's quotient topology
on the injective characteristic-\(p\) fibre is therefore the compact
Hausdorff profinite group \(B_p\).

Frobenius \(F_p\) acts trivially on \(B_p\), because multiplication by
\(p\) has already been divided out in `(1)`.  Thus the mapping-torus term
in Deninger's Theorem 7.10 is
\[
 B_p\times_{p^{\mathbb Z}}\mathbb R_{>0}
 \cong
 B_p\times S_p.                                                     \tag{4}
\]
Theorem 7.10 gives a continuous bijection from `(4)` onto the
characteristic-\(p\) packet in \(X_{\mathbb Z}\).  Its source is compact.
Deninger's Corollary 7.9 makes the suspension for the affine arithmetic
scheme \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\) Hausdorff.  A continuous
bijection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a homeomorphism,
proving `(3)`.

Deninger's warning after Theorem 7.10 that the displayed global
continuous bijections need not be homeomorphisms is not contradicted:
his counterexample is the generic fibre, whose source has many connected
components while its target is connected.  Compactness and Hausdorffness
close the issue separately on each prime packet.

## Real Milnor collapse at the geometric prime

For every \(n>0\),
\[
 K_n^M(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R=0,
 \qquad
 K_0^M(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R
 \cong\mathbb R.                                                    \tag{5}
\]

Indeed, Milnor \(K_n\) is generated by pure symbols.  Every entry of a
symbol lies in the torsion group
\(\overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times\).  If the first entry has order \(m\),
then multilinearity gives
\[
 m\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}
 =\{a_1^m,a_2,\ldots,a_n\}=0.
\]
Every finite sum of symbols is consequently torsion.  Tensoring with
\(\mathbb R\) proves `(5)`.

Thus the real Tate--Milnor algebra on a geometric prime chart has only
its degree-zero unit.  No positive Milnor cell, Steinberg relation, or
higher syzygy survives on the prime side after realification.

## Prime coefficient and derived sections

On \(\Gamma_p=B_p\times S_p\), let
\(\mathscr R_p^\infty\) be the sheaf of real functions that are locally
constant in \(B_p\) and smooth in \(S_p\).  This is the smooth-transverse
version of Deninger's leafwise sheaf on the prime packet.

`[PROVED HERE] [DAC_PRIME_PACKET_PRO_STEIN_KUMMER_COMPARISON]`

The sheaf \(\mathscr R_p^\infty\) is fine and
\[
 R\Gamma(\Gamma_p,\mathscr R_p^\infty)
 \simeq
 C_{\mathrm{lc}}\!
   \left(B_p,C^\infty(S_p,\mathbb R)\right)[0].                    \tag{6}
\]
The real unit-amalgamated Tate--Milnor coefficient on the geometric
prime chart is equivalent to \(\mathscr R_p^\infty\), so it has strict
derived-section amplitude \([0,0]\), hence in particular \([0,1]\).

### Proof

The compact profinite space \(B_p\) has a clopen basis.  Given an open
cover of \(B_p\times S_p\), compactness gives a finite refinement by
sets \(C_j\times I_j\), with \(C_j\) clopen and \(I_j\) an interval in
the circle.  Refine the finitely many \(C_j\) to a finite clopen
partition \((D_\alpha)\) of \(B_p\).  For each \(D_\alpha\), the
intervals \(I_j\) for which \(D_\alpha\times I_j\) lies in the
refinement cover the circle \(S_p\); choose a smooth partition of unity
\((\psi_{\alpha j})_j\) subordinate to them.  The functions
\[
 \rho_j(b,t)
 =
 \sum_\alpha
 \mathbf 1_{D_\alpha}(b)\psi_{\alpha j}(t)
\]
form a partition of unity subordinate to the original cover and are
locally constant in \(b\), smooth in \(t\).  Thus
\(\mathscr R_p^\infty\) is fine and acyclic.  Its global sections are
the right side of `(6)`.

For \(F=\overline{\mathbb F}_p\), every unit is torsion, so the
non-torsion unit generator space is zero.  The free unit algebra is the
degree-zero unit, and `(5)` says that the real Milnor algebra is the
same unit.  The derived unit-amalgamation pushout therefore leaves
\(\mathscr R_p^\infty\) unchanged.  This proves the comparison and
amplitude claim.  No Cartan theorem B is needed: clopen refinement is
the characteristic-\(p\) analogue of the generic pro-Stein argument.

## Canonical packet average

Give \(B_p\) and \(S_p\) normalized Haar probability measures
\(\mu_{B_p}\) and \(\mu_{S_p}\).  On the smooth/locally constant packet
test space
\[
 \mathcal S_p
 =
 C_{\mathrm{lc}}\!
   \left(B_p,C^\infty(S_p,\mathbb R)\right)                         \tag{7}
\]
define
\[
 \operatorname{Av}_p(f)
 =
 \int_{B_p}\int_{S_p}f(b,t)\,
 d\mu_{S_p}(t)\,d\mu_{B_p}(b).                                    \tag{8}
\]
Let \(\iota_p:\mathbb R\to\mathcal S_p\) send a scalar to the constant
function.

Then
\[
 \operatorname{Av}_p\iota_p=\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb R},\qquad
 \mathcal S_p
 =
 \iota_p(\mathbb R)
 \mathbin{\widehat\oplus}
 \ker(\operatorname{Av}_p).                                       \tag{9}
\]
The splitting is strict in the natural LF topology of `(7)`.

### Proof

A locally constant function on compact profinite \(B_p\) factors through
a finite clopen quotient.  Hence `(7)` is the filtered strict inductive
limit of finite direct sums of the nuclear Fréchet space
\(C^\infty(S_p,\mathbb R)\).  At every finite stage `(8)` is a finite
convex average followed by circle integration, so it is continuous.
It commutes with the transition maps and is continuous on the strict
inductive limit.  Equations `(9)` follow from the continuous idempotent
\[
 P_p=\iota_p\operatorname{Av}_p.
\]
The image and kernel of a continuous projection are closed and give a
topological direct sum.

The same average extends contractively to both \(L^1(\Gamma_p)\) and
\(L^2(\Gamma_p)\):
\[
 |\operatorname{Av}_p(f)|\leq\|f\|_1,\qquad
 |\operatorname{Av}_p(f)|\leq\|f\|_2.                             \tag{10}
\]
Therefore it is a strict continuous projection for the mixed norm
\(\|f\|_1+\|f\|_2\) and for the associated Gelfand triple.

## Arithmetic meaning

The generalized-log Kummer residue of a generic unit is the packet-
constant function
\[
 a\longmapsto
 \operatorname{ord}_p(a)\,\mathbf 1_{\Gamma_p}.                   \tag{11}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{Av}_p
 \bigl(\operatorname{ord}_p(a)\mathbf 1_{\Gamma_p}\bigr)
 =
 \operatorname{ord}_p(a).                                        \tag{12}
\]
The normalization counts the entire compact packet once, exactly as the
single closed point \((p)\) occurs once in the Rost--Schmid divisor
complex.  Formula `(12)` is compatible with
`DAC_ADELIC_PLACE_AVERAGE_RESIDUE` and
`DAC_FINITE_PRIME_MILNOR_ENDPOINT_PAGE`.

Combining `(5)` and `(9)`, the scalar one-prime quotient of the
Tate--Milnor packet coefficient is strictly quasi-isomorphic to
\(\mathbb R\) in degree zero.  This proves the algebraic and strict
topological core of the one-prime Kummer comparison.

## No-go closure and positive survivor

The Haar projection is a canonical operation on derived global sections,
but it is not an ordinary local sheaf endomorphism: evaluating
\(\operatorname{Av}_p(f)\) uses values of \(f\) on the entire compact
packet.  More sharply, an ordinary sheaf on a disjoint clopen
decomposition satisfies the sheaf product axiom, so it cannot force
independent sections on the two clopen pieces to have one common scalar
without extra equivariance or a nonlocal operation.

Therefore the source coefficient retains
\[
 \ker(\operatorname{Av}_p)\subset H^0(\Gamma_p,\mathscr R_p^\infty)
                                                                    \tag{13}
\]
as genuine prime-packet modes.  Only the valuation image lies in the
closed scalar summand.  After the residue-fibre shift, `(13)` contributes
to arithmetic \(H^1\), not to the endpoint \(H^2\).  Removing it would
change ordinary coarse sheaf cohomology into an unannounced equivariant
or packet-quotient theory.

Thus `DAC_PRIME_PACKET_PRO_STEIN_KUMMER_COMPARISON` is closed by `(6)`.
The remaining prime-side gates are the analytic Kummer generator square
and the strict all-prime restricted sum, not one-prime amplitude.

### The real Milnor collapse `(5)` landed

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DeningerSymbolTorsion.lean`.  Axiom audit:
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

mathlib has no Milnor K-theory, so the theorem is formalized through the property the card's proof
actually uses: additivity of the symbol in each entry.  Every construction of `K^M_n` as a group
generated by multilinear symbols satisfies it, so the collapse applies to any of them.

- `DeningerSymbolAdditive` — additivity of a symbol in one entry.
- `deningerSymbol_one` — a symbol with a unit entry vanishes.
- `deningerSymbol_pow` — raising an entry to the power `k` multiplies the symbol by `k`, which is
  the card's `m{a₁,…,aₙ} = {a₁^m,a₂,…,aₙ}`.
- `deningerSymbol_nsmul`, `deningerSymbol_isTorsion` — hence the order of an entry annihilates the
  symbol: over a torsion group every symbol is torsion.
- `deningerSymbolGenerated_isTorsion` — **a group generated by such symbols is torsion**, because
  the torsion elements form a subgroup and therefore contain the subgroup the symbols generate.
- `deningerTorsion_tensor_real` — **a torsion group has vanishing realification**: a real scalar is
  divisible by the order of the group element, and the order moves across the tensor sign.  This is
  the `⊗ ℝ = 0` half of `(5)`.
- `deningerDegreeZero_tensor_real` — the degree-zero unit realifies to `ℝ`.

Over `F̄_p` every unit is a root of unity, which is the hypothesis `∀ g, IsOfFinOrder g`.

- `deningerAtom C T = {b | ∀ j, j ∈ T ↔ b ∈ C j}` — the atoms of the Boolean algebra generated by a
