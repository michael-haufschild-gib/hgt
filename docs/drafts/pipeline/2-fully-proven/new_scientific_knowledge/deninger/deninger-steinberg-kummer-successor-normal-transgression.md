## 1. Why the fixed Steinberg pair is insufficient

For the real horizontal spectral displacement \(\delta\in\mathbb R\), put

\[
                 c_\delta(u)=u^\delta-1
                 \qquad (u\in\mathbb Q_{>0}).
\]

The crossed additivity law

\[
 c_\delta(uv)=c_\delta(u)+c_\delta(v)+c_\delta(u)c_\delta(v)
\tag{1}
\]

is exact, but the ordinary Steinberg product does not vanish off the critical section.  For
\(x=3/5\) and \(y=2/5\),

\[
 c_\delta(x)c_\delta(y)=0

\tag{2}
\]

Raising this fixed pair to the depth \(m\) does not repair the problem: \(x^m+y^m\ne1\), so
\((x^m,y^m)\) is no longer a Steinberg pair.  A full-grade construction must change the rational
pair with the depth.

## 3. Cubic normal selector

\[

\tag{7}

\[
