## Source

For a characteristic-zero field \(F\) containing all roots of unity, put
\[
 V_F=(F^\times/\mu_\infty)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q.
\]
The generic multiplicative-character component is modeled by
\[
 Z_F=\operatorname{Hom}(V_F,\mathbb C^\times).
\]
Its compact unitary retraction is the Kucharczyk--Scholze pro-torus
\[
 Y_F=\operatorname{Hom}(V_F,S^1).
\]
Deninger's rational-Witt character space contains these generic
components, and at the end of §12 he states that his tentative sheaf
\(\mathcal O_{\mathcal F c}\) should acquire an analyticity condition along
the character-space, hence leaf, coordinate.

## Construction

Let \(\mathcal I_F\) be the filtered category of pairs \((W,L)\), where
\[
 W\subset V_F
\]
is finite-dimensional over \(\mathbb Q\) and \(L\subset W\) is a full
lattice.  Set
\[
 Z_{W,L}=\operatorname{Hom}(L,\mathbb C^\times)
 \cong(\mathbb C^\times)^{\operatorname{rk}L}.                       \tag{1}
\]
Finite-index lattice inclusions give finite étale power isogenies and
inclusions \(W\subset W'\) give coordinate projections.  The resulting
cofiltered pro-complex manifold has points \(Z_F\).

Define the cylinder-holomorphic sheaf and its de Rham complex by
\[
 \mathcal O_{\mathrm{cyl},F}
 :=\varinjlim_{(W,L)\in\mathcal I_F}
   \pi_{W,L}^{-1}\mathcal O_{Z_{W,L}},                               \tag{2}
\]
\[
 \Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm{cyl},F}
 :=\varinjlim_{(W,L)\in\mathcal I_F}
   \pi_{W,L}^{-1}\Omega^\bullet_{Z_{W,L}}.                           \tag{3}
\]
The colimit is taken in the pro-Ind analytic sheaf category.  Equivalently,
a function is cylinder holomorphic when locally it is a holomorphic
function of finitely many evaluation coordinates
\[
 P\longmapsto P(a_1),\ldots,P(a_r).
\]
This local definition proves the ordinary sheaf gluing condition directly.

On Deninger's suspension cover, tensor `(2)`--`(3)` with functions in the
transverse coordinate of the regularity class being used and impose his
local constancy condition when defining
\(\mathcal O_{\mathcal F c}^{\mathrm{an}}\).  Galois maps permute evaluation
coordinates.  Frobenius sends
\[
 P(a)\longmapsto P(a^q)=P(a)^q,                                     \tag{4}
\]
so `(2)`--`(3)` are stable under every power map and under
Verschiebung after passing to the rational-Witt colimit.

`[PROVED HERE] [DAC_PRO_STEIN_CYLINDER_ANALYTIC_LEAF]`

Formulas `(1)`--`(4)` define a functorial equivariant pro-Stein analytic
ringed refinement of every generic Deninger character component.

### Proof

Every \(Z_{W,L}\) is the finite-dimensional Stein manifold
\((\mathbb C^\times)^r\).  All transition maps are holomorphic.
Local cylinder representations are preserved by restriction, addition,
multiplication, exterior derivative, Galois permutation, and `(4)`.
If cylinder-holomorphic functions agree on overlaps, their glued function
is locally one of them and hence again cylinder holomorphic.  This proves
the sheaf and functoriality claims.  The same local argument applies to
differential forms.

## Cohomology and acyclicity

For every finite stage, holomorphic de Rham and singular cohomology give
\[
 \mathbb H^k(Z_{W,L},\Omega^\bullet_{Z_{W,L}})
 \cong
 H^k((\mathbb C^\times)^r,\mathbb C)
 \cong\Lambda^k(L\otimes\mathbb C),                                 \tag{5}
\]
generated by \(d\log P(a)\).  Passing through finite-index lattices
rationalizes the lattice, and passing through finite-dimensional
subspaces exhausts \(V_F\).  Therefore
\[
 \mathbb H^\bullet_{\mathrm{pro\text{-}Ind}}
   (Z_F,\Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm{cyl},F})
 \cong\Lambda^\bullet(V_F\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb C).              \tag{6}
\]
This is the analytic realization of
`DAC_CHARACTER_SHAPE_FORMALITY`.

`[PROVED HERE] [DAC_PRO_STEIN_RELATIVE_CELL_GLOBAL_SECTIONS]`

Let \(P\to Q\) be a Tate-graded relative Sullivan algebra presented as a
filtered union of finite cell stages, with every fixed bidegree having
finite rank at each stage.  Apply the same cells to
\(\Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm{cyl},F}\) in the pro-Ind analytic sheaf category.
Then derived global sections commute with the relative cell construction:
\[
 R\Gamma_{\mathrm{pro\text{-}Ind}}
 \left(
   \Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm{cyl},F}
   \mathop{\otimes}^{\mathbb L}_{P}Q
 \right)
 \simeq
 R\Gamma_{\mathrm{pro\text{-}Ind}}
   (\Omega^\bullet_{\mathrm{cyl},F})
   \mathop{\otimes}^{\mathbb L}_{P}Q.                               \tag{7}
\]

### Proof

At a finite character stage and finite cell stage, the semi-free
filtration has fixed-bidegree subquotients that are finite direct sums of
locally free coherent analytic sheaves.  Cartan theorem B makes every such
subquotient acyclic for global sections.  Induction over the finite cell
filtration therefore identifies the derived sheaf attachment with the
attachment of global sections.  The pro-Ind global-section functor is, by
definition, the filtered colimit of these finite-stage derived sections.
Filtered colimits of complex vector spaces are exact, and every fixed
bidegree occurs at a finite stage.  Taking the filtered colimit proves
`(7)`.

Applying `(7)` to the relative Milnor resolution gives the chartwise
comparison
\[
 R\Gamma_{\mathrm{pro\text{-}Ind}}
   (Z_F,\mathscr A^M_{F,\mathrm{an}})
 \simeq
 C^\bullet_{\mathrm{cyl}}(Z_F;\mathbb R)
 \mathop{\otimes}^{\mathbb L}_{\mathcal P_{F,\mathbb R}}
 \mathcal K^M_{F,\mathbb R}.                                       \tag{8}
\]
For algebraic cyclotomic \(F\), the right side has amplitude \([0,1]\)
by `DAC_ALGEBRAIC_CYCLOTOMIC_MILNOR_AMPLITUDE`.

## Hostile audit

- `(2)` is an analytic refinement, not a claim that Deninger's coarse
  infinite-dimensional topology is a complex manifold.
- Cartan theorem B is used only at finite Stein stages.  No partition of
  unity or finite covering dimension is asserted for the coarse
  rational-Witt suspension.
- `(7)` uses the pro-Ind global-section functor defined stagewise.  A
  comparison with ordinary derived global sections on the coarse
  suspension remains separate.
- The analytic chain action `(4)` is non-scalar.  Its action on local
  cohomology becomes the pure power action; therefore non-proper global
  descent and the analytic completion must not be replaced by pure scalar
  suspension.  `DAC_PURE_SUSPENSION_SCALAR_SPECTRUM_NO_GO` enforces this.
- The theorem constructs no trace-class completion, fixed-orbit formula,
  Hodge metric, or zeta determinant.

**Verdict:** `FULLY PROVEN ON PAPER; ANALYTIC LOCAL SHEAF AND CHARTWISE
GLOBAL-SECTION COMPARISON CLOSED ON THE PRO-STEIN REFINEMENT; COARSE
SUSPENSION DESCENT OPEN`.

### The descended primitive is holomorphic

- `deningerLogBranch_analyticAt`, `deningerLogBranchDeriv_analyticAt` — a branch of the logarithm

- `deningerDescendForm_holomorphic` — **the descent of a periodic complex differentiable form is a
  holomorphic stage form**: upstairs it is analytic by the several-variable analyticity above, and
  near a point of the stage it is its pullback along a branch of the logarithm.
- `deningerConstantStageForm_holomorphic` — the constant stage forms are holomorphic.
- `deningerStage_closed_form_decomposition_hol`,
  `deningerStage_closed_form_dlog_decomposition_hol` — **`(5)` at a finite stage, in every degree,
  inside the holomorphic de Rham complex**: every closed holomorphic form is a combination of the
  exterior products of the logarithmic differentials of the coordinates plus the exterior derivative
  of a *holomorphic* form.  With `deningerDlogWedge_not_exact` and
  `deningerConstantStageForm_unique` the comparison is an isomorphism.

### Exterior powers commute with the limit over the lattices

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/ExteriorPowerDirectLimit.lean`.  Axiom audit:
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

- `exteriorPower_map_injective` — the exterior power of an injective map of vector spaces is
  injective, because such a map splits.
- `exteriorPowerDirectLimitMap_bijective`, `exteriorPowerDirectLimitEquiv` — **for a directed system
  of vector spaces with injective transition maps, the exterior power of the direct limit is the
  direct limit of the exterior powers.**  Surjectivity is the spanning of the exterior power by the
  products of tuples together with the fact that finitely many elements of a direct limit come from
  a common stage; injectivity is the injectivity of a limit of injective maps.

  This is the passage to the limit of `(6)`: the lattices of `I_F` form a directed system with
  injective maps whose union is `V_F`, so the colimit of the `Λ^k(L ⊗ ℂ)` is `Λ^k(V_F ⊗ ℂ)`.

- `deningerDetForm_linearIndependent`, `deningerDetFormBasis` — the determinant forms of the

- `deningerCohomologyBasis` — **the classes of the exterior products of the logarithmic differentials

### `(6)` : the colimit of the stage cohomologies

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DeningerCohomologyColimit.lean`.  Axiom audit:
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

- `deningerMatrixCLM_transpose_comp`, `deningerCohomologyMap_comp`, `deningerCohomologyMap_id` —
  **the maps induced on cohomology compose according to the product of the exponent matrices and the
  identity matrix induces the identity**, so a directed system of exponent matrices gives a directed
  system of cohomologies.  The proof goes through the comparison with the exterior powers, where the
  induced maps are exterior powers of the transposed matrices and matrix multiplication is
  composition.
- `deningerCohomologyDirectLimitEquiv` — **the colimit of the cohomologies of a directed system of
  character stages is the exterior power of the colimit of the covering spaces**, that is
  `colim_k H^k(Z_{W,L}) ≅ Λ^k(V_F ⊗ ℂ)`.  This is `(6)`, obtained by transporting the stagewise
  comparison through the direct limit — legitimate because it is natural in the lattice — and then
  commuting the exterior power with the direct limit.

### Filtered colimits are exact, an ingredient of `(7)`

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/DirectLimitExact.lean`.  Axiom audit:
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

- `directLimit_map_exact` — **a stagewise exact sequence of directed systems of modules has exact
  direct limit.**  An element of the limit killed by the second map comes from a stage, dies there
  after moving far enough along the system, and is therefore in the image of the first map at that
  stage.  Mathlib has the direct limit and the fact that a component dying in the limit dies at some
  stage, but not this exactness.

  This is the ingredient of `(7)` that the proof invokes as "filtered colimits of complex vector
  spaces are exact"; the remaining ingredient is Cartan theorem B at the finite Stein stages.

### The exterior product of the de Rham complex `(3)`

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/MultilinearFiniteDimensional.lean` and
`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/ContinuousWedge.lean`.  Axiom audit:
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

- `multilinearMap_bound` — **a multilinear map whose arguments run over a finite coordinate space is

### Remaining

1. **`(6)`** is proved for an arbitrary directed system of character stages given by exponent
   matrices with injective covering maps, **and now for the index category `I_F` itself**; see the
   entry below.
2. **`(7)`**, the relative cell statement.  Of its two named ingredients, the exactness of filtered
   colimits is now proved; what is left is Cartan theorem B at the finite Stein stages, which needs
   coherent analytic sheaves and Dolbeault cohomology of a complex manifold.  Mathlib has neither, and
   neither exists anywhere in Lean: it is an area-sized gap, not a missing lemma.  Concretely the
   statement needed is `H^q(Z, O_Z) = 0` for `q ≥ 1` on `Z = (ℂ^×)^r`, since the sheaves of the cell
   filtration are free there; the path to it runs through the Cauchy–Pompeiu formula, the
   Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma, and sheaf cohomology on complex manifolds.
- 2026-08-07 — Formalized Cartan's theorem B, the second named ingredient of `(7)` and the one the
  card leans on at each finite character stage.  `RHTower.deningerHolomorphic_ext_eq_zero_punctured`
  gives `H^{q+1}((ℂ^×)^n, O) = 0` for every `q ≥ 0`, unconditionally, in the corpus's sheaf
  cohomology `Ext(deningerOpenFree U, F, n)`; `RHTower.deningerHolomorphic_ext_eq_zero` is the same
  statement for `ℂ^n`.  Both are assembled from a Dolbeault resolution built as actual sheaves —
  `deningerFormSheaf` and `deningerClosedFormSheaf` on the domain regarded as its own space — whose
  steps are short exact (`deningerFormStep_shortExact`: the closed forms are an explicit kernel of
  the differential, and the differential is an epimorphism by the local `∂̄`-lemma on a polydisc)
  and whose terms are fine hence acyclic (`deningerFine_acyclicOn` over the smooth multipliers, with
  smooth partitions of unity from `deningerHasPartitions_smooth`).  The analytic input is the global
  `∂̄`-lemma in every positive degree, `setDbar_exact_global` and `setDbar_exact_punctured`.  With
  the exactness of filtered colimits already banked, both named ingredients of `(7)` are now
  formalized.
- 2026-08-07 — Formalized the index category `I_F` itself, closing the last item of `(6)`.
  `formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DeningerLatticeIndex.lean`; axiom audit
  `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

  The card's pairs `(W, L)` carry no more information than `L`: the stage `Z_{W,L} = Hom(L, ℂ^×)`
  does not mention `W`, and `W` is the rational span of `L`.  So `RHTower.DeningerIndex V` is the
  poset of finitely generated subgroups of a rational vector space, and everything the colimit
  theorem asks for is proved of it:

  * `deningerRatTorsionFree` — a rational vector space is torsion free over `ℤ`, since a nonzero
    integer becomes invertible in `ℚ`.  With finite generation this gives `deningerIndexFree`: every
    stage is free of finite rank over the principal ideal domain `ℤ`, hence has a basis
    `deningerIndexBasis` and a rank `deningerIndexRank`.
  * `deningerIndexDirected` — **the index category is directed**, because the sum of two finitely
    generated subgroups is finitely generated.  This is the filteredness the card asserts.
  * `deningerIndexMatrix` — the exponent matrix of an inclusion, the coordinates of the smaller
    basis in the larger.  `deningerIndexMatrix_comp` and `_id` are the cocycle conditions; they come
    from `LinearMap.toMatrix_comp` and `toMatrix_id`, i.e. from uniqueness of coordinates, so no
    coherent choice of bases is needed — independent choices already compose.
  * `deningerIndexCovering_injective` — **the covering maps are injective.**  This is the one point
    with content.  The inclusion is injective over `ℤ`; a chosen basis is therefore `ℚ`-independent
    in the ambient space (`LinearIndependent.iff_fractionRing` — an integral independence inside a
    vector space over the fraction field is a rational one), so the exponent matrix is injective
    over `ℚ`, where an injection splits.  The splitting is a matrix of rational numbers, and the
    *same* matrix splits the map over `ℂ`.  That is what makes the complexified inclusion injective
    without any rank or minor argument.
  * `deningerIndexDirectedSystem`, `deningerIndexCohomologyDirectLimitEquiv` — the stages form a
    directed system and **the colimit of their cohomologies is the exterior power of the colimit of
    the covering spaces**, for the index category itself.

  With this, both named ingredients of `(7)` and both items of `(6)` are formalized.
