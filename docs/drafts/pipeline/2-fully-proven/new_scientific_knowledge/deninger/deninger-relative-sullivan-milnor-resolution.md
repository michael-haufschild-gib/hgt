# Relative Sullivan resolution of the real Milnor quotient

- **ID:** `deninger-relative-sullivan-milnor-resolution`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** SOURCE ALGEBRA THEOREM
- **Label:** `DAC_RELATIVE_SULLIVAN_MILNOR_RESOLUTION`
- **Parent:** `deninger-relative-sullivan-milnor-character-space`

## Statement

Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic zero, let \(V\) be a
\(k\)-vector space, and put
\[
 P=\Lambda_k(V),\qquad d_P=0.                                        \tag{1}
\]
Let \(K\) be a connective graded-commutative \(k\)-algebra generated by
\(V=K^1\), with \(K^0=k\), together with a surjective graded-algebra map
\[
 \epsilon:P\twoheadrightarrow K                                    \tag{2}
\]
that is the identity on \(k\oplus V\).  Then `(2)` admits a relative
Sullivan factorization
\[
 P\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
 Q=P\otimes\Lambda(W)
 \xrightarrow{\sim}K,                                                \tag{3}
\]
where:

1. \(W=\bigcup_{\beta}W_{<\beta}\) has a well-ordered filtration;
2. the differential of every new generator in \(W_\beta\) lies in the
   subalgebra generated at earlier stages;
3. \(Q\to K\) is a surjective quasi-isomorphism of commutative DGAs;
4. `(3)` can be chosen functorially by the small-object construction.

For Deninger's algebraic character field after realification,
\[
 K=\mathbb R\oplus V,\qquad V=(F^\times/\mu_\infty)\otimes\mathbb R,
 \qquad V\cdot V=0,                                                   \tag{4}
\]
by `DAC_ALGEBRAIC_CYCLOTOMIC_MILNOR_AMPLITUDE`.  Thus `(3)` gives a
relative commutative-DGA model whose cohomology is exactly the required real
Milnor algebra.

## Proof

The category of nonnegatively graded commutative DGAs over a
characteristic-zero field has the standard cofibrantly generated model
structure: weak equivalences are quasi-isomorphisms, fibrations are
surjections in positive degree, and relative Sullivan algebras are
cofibrations.  Factor `(2)` functorially as a cofibration followed by a
trivial fibration.  This gives `(3)`.

Concretely, the factorization is obtained by a transfinite induction.

1. Attach generators whose differentials represent the kernel of
   \(H^\bullet(P)\to H^\bullet(K)\).  In the special case `(4)`, the first
   layer has degree-one generators \(w_{a,b}\) satisfying
   \[
   d w_{a,b}=a\wedge b,
   \]
   killing the degree-two products.
2. The new generators can create further cohomology.  At every successor
   stage attach generators whose differentials represent the remaining
   kernel, and attach closed generators mapping to any cohomology classes of
   \(K\) not yet hit.
3. At a limit ordinal take the filtered union.  Filtered colimits of vector
   spaces are exact, so the cohomology comparison is preserved through limit
   stages.
4. The small-object argument terminates after a sufficiently large regular
   ordinal and gives a surjective quasi-isomorphism to \(K\).

This proves all four assertions.  Equation `(4)` is the already proved real
Milnor amplitude theorem, so the specialization follows.

## Why the degree-one relative generators are useful

Killing a degree-two cup class requires a relative generator of degree one.
Under contravariant rational realization, such generators correspond to a
fiber with uniquely divisible, pro-unipotent fundamental group rather than
to an ordinary simply connected cell attachment.  A uniquely divisible
group has no nontrivial finite quotient.  This identifies a possible
mechanism by which the true Milnor character space can alter real
cohomology while retaining the finite-cover/Galois information of the
Kucharczyk--Scholze space.

The last sentence is a mechanism, not yet a realization theorem: one must
construct the relevant pro-space and prove the exact fundamental-group
sequence.

## Hostile audit

- A Sullivan DGA is a rational homotopy model.  It does not by itself
  construct a topological space over \(X_F\).
- \(V_F\) is generally infinite-dimensional and the base need not be a
  nilpotent finite-type space.  Classical finite-type Sullivan realization
  is insufficient; a pro-object or derived-stack realization is required.
- Functorial small-object factorization gives coherence in algebra, but
  Galois, residue, and power compatibility must still be transported to the
  realization.
- The Tor obstruction for the naïve sheaf pushout is not contradicted.
  A derived pushforward of the realized map may have complicated stalks.

## Novelty audit

Relative Sullivan factorization is standard.  New deduction for the
Deninger program is the identification of its degree-one acyclic-closure
fiber as the correct potential bridge between two constraints that appeared
in conflict:

1. kill the non-Milnor cup products in real cohomology;
2. preserve finite covers and hence the absolute Galois group.

  differential and `d² = 0`.
- `DeningerSullivanStage.base` — **the starting point `(1)` of the statement**: the generator space
  with vanishing differential, i.e. `P = Λ(V)` with `d_P = 0`.
- `DeningerSullivanStage.succ`, `succ_space`, `succ_phi`, `succ_d_inl`, `succ_kills` — the successor
  stage, its differential extending the previous one, and the fact that every degree-two cocycle of
  a stage becomes a coboundary at its successor.  Since `succ` lands in `DeningerSullivanStage`
  again, the construction is closed under iteration.
- `DeningerSullivanStage.iterate`, `iterate_zero`, `iterate_succ`, `iterate_kills` — **the tower**:
  the successor construction iterated along the natural numbers, with every degree-two cocycle
  appearing at a stage dying at the next.

Starting from `base`, the first successor attaches generators for every degree-two cocycle of
`(Λ(V), 0)` — that is, for all of `⋀²V` — so the first layer built earlier is the first step of
this tower, and the tower continues from it.

### Acyclicity from stagewise killing

- `DeningerSullivanTower.cocycle_isCoboundary_of_stagewise` — **if every cocycle present at a stage
  dies at a later stage, the tower is acyclic**: every cocycle is a coboundary.  This is the
  limit-stage argument that makes the successor attachments add up, and it is what converts the
  stagewise killing theorem into the conclusion of clause 3.
- `_of_stagewise_mem` — the same on a distinguished submodule of classes, which is the form that
  applies here: a stage of degree-one generators kills exactly the degree-two classes.

### A structural limit of the degree-one model

The exterior algebra is the free graded-commutative algebra on generators **of degree one**.  A
degree-one generator has a degree-two differential, so a stage of this kind can kill degree-two
classes and no others.  The tower built here therefore resolves the degree-two cohomology
completely, which is what the card's step 1 requires and what case `(4)` is about, but killing a
class in degree `n + 1` needs a new generator of degree `n`, i.e. a free graded-commutative algebra
on generators of mixed degree rather than an exterior algebra.  That object is now built (below), so what remains is
to redo the tower construction over it instead of over the exterior algebra.

### The mixed-degree tower

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GradedSullivanStage.lean` bundles and iterates the
mixed-degree construction.  Axiom audit for all 11 declarations:
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

- `DeningerGCAStage` — **a Sullivan stage on generators of mixed degree**, described by the three
  conditions on its generator values, with `d`, `d_leibniz` and `d_comp_self`.
- `DeningerGCAAttachment` — **the data attached at a successor step**: new generators of prescribed

- `DeningerGCAStage.succ`, `succ_d_inl`, `succ_kills` — the successor stage, its differential

- `DeningerGCAStage.iterate`, `iterate_zero`, `iterate_succ`, `iterate_kills` — **the tower**

The tower construction now exists over generators of arbitrary degree, so the degree-two ceiling of
the exterior model is gone: at each stage a generator of degree `n` may be attached to kill a class
in degree `n + 1`.

### The base stage on mixed degrees

- `DeningerGCAStage.base`, `base_gen`, `base_phi` — **the base stage**: generators of prescribed
  degrees with vanishing differential.  This is the algebra `P` with `d_P = 0` from which a relative
  Sullivan tower starts, now for arbitrary degrees.
- `DeningerGCAStage.baseDegreeOne`, `baseDegreeOne_anticomm` — **the degree-one base stage**, whose
  generators anticommute: the underlying algebra is the exterior algebra, so this is literally the
  statement's `P = Λ(V)`, `d_P = 0`.  The mixed-degree tower therefore starts from the same place
  the exterior one did and continues past degree two.

### The tower as a chain of differential extensions

- `DeningerGCAStage.iterateInl` — the inclusion of a stage of the mixed-degree tower into the next.
- `DeningerGCAStage.iterate_d_inl` — **each stage includes into the next compatibly with the
  differentials**, so the tower is a chain of differential extensions rather than a sequence of
  unrelated stages.  With `iterate_kills` this is the full induction step of the transfinite
  argument, in every degree.

### The two models compared

- `deningerGCAToExterior_comm`, `deningerGCAToExterior_sq`, `deningerGCAToExterior`,
  `deningerGCAToExterior_ι` — **the comparison map** from the degree-one free graded-commutative
  algebra to an exterior algebra, sending each generator to a chosen vector.  The Koszul relations
  in degree one are exactly the exterior ones, so the map exists by the universal property.
- `deningerGCAToExterior_involute` — **it intertwines the two grading involutions**.  Both negate
  degree-one generators, so the identification of the two models is compatible with the sign rule
  the differentials run on.

This links the two lines of work: the exterior model carrying the concrete results on the card's own
`K = k ⊕ V` is the degree-one case of the general algebra carrying the unrestricted tower.

- `deningerGCAToExterior_surjective` — **the comparison map is surjective when the generators
  span**: every exterior generator is in the range, and the generators generate the exterior
  algebra.  So the exterior model is a quotient of the degree-one free graded-commutative algebra,
  compatibly with the involutions.

### Squares of general vectors

- `deningerAnticommSum_sq_zero` — **a sum of pairwise anticommuting square-zero elements squares to
  zero**, proved by induction on the index set: the diagonal terms vanish by the square-zero
  hypothesis and the cross terms cancel in pairs by anticommutation.
- `deningerAnticommSmulSum_sq_zero` — the same for an arbitrary scalar combination.

This is the missing ingredient for the inverse comparison map: `ExteriorAlgebra.lift` requires the
square-zero condition for *every* vector, not merely for basis elements, and mathlib has no
spanning-set variant.

### The inverse comparison, and injectivity

- `deningerExteriorToGCAMap`, `_basis`, `_sq` — the linear map sending each basis vector to its
  generator, and the proof that **every vector squares to zero** under it, using the anticommuting
  sum lemma.  This is the hypothesis `ExteriorAlgebra.lift` demands.
- `deningerExteriorToGCA`, `_ι` — **the inverse comparison map**, from the exterior algebra on a
  based module to the degree-one free graded-commutative algebra on the basis.
- `deningerExteriorToGCA_comp_toExterior` — **one composite is the identity**, so the degree-one
  free graded-commutative algebra is a retract of the exterior algebra.
- `deningerGCAToExterior_injective` — hence the comparison map is injective; with the surjectivity
  above, the two models agree on a based module.

### The two models are isomorphic

- `deningerGCAToExterior_comp_exteriorToGCA` — the second composite is the identity, proved by
  expanding a vector in its basis coordinates and reassembling it.
- `deningerGCAExteriorEquiv`, `_ι` — **the isomorphism**: on a module with a basis, the exterior
  algebra *is* the degree-one free graded-commutative algebra on that basis, matching generators
  with basis vectors.

So the exterior model in which the concrete results on the card's `K = k ⊕ V` were proved and the
general mixed-degree algebra in which the unrestricted tower lives are the same object in degree
one.  The exterior development is a special case of the general one, not a parallel track.

### The tower's inclusion chain

- `DeningerGCAStage.iterateInlAdd` — the inclusion of a stage into any later stage, by composing the
  successor inclusions.
- `iterate_d_inlAdd` — **the differential is compatible with every inclusion along the tower**, so
  each stage embeds as a differential subalgebra of every later one.  This is the structure a
  colimit of the tower is built from.
- `iterate_cocycle_inlAdd`, `iterate_coboundary_inlAdd` — cocycles and coboundaries persist along
  the tower.

### The limit generator set

- `iterateNewGen`, `iterateOmegaGen`, `iterateOmegaDeg` — **the generator set of the limit stage**:
  the original generators together with every generator attached anywhere along the tower, each
  keeping its degree.
- `iterateGenToOmega` — the inclusion of a stage's generators into the limit set, defined by
  recursion along the tower.
- `iterateGenToOmega_deg` — **degrees are preserved**: a generator of a stage keeps its degree in
  the limit, so the limit algebra can extend every stage compatibly with the grading.

Because a stage's generator set is a left-nested sum, the limit set is a single sum of the original
generators with a sigma-type over the attachments, which avoids constructing a colimit of modules.

### The limit stage, and step 3 at the algebra level

The colimit of the tower is now built as an actual Sullivan stage, and the comparison of its
cohomology with the cohomology of the stages is proved.

- `iterateToOmega` — the algebra map from each stage into the limit algebra, obtained from the
  universal property; the Koszul relations transfer because `iterateGenToOmega` preserves degrees.
- `iterateToOmega_comp_inl`, `iterateToOmega_comp_inlAdd` — **the maps form a cocone**: including a
  stage into any later stage and then into the limit is the same as mapping into the limit directly.
- `iterateToOmega_involute` — the limit maps intertwine the grading involutions.
- `exists_common_stage` — any two limit generators already live at a common stage.  This is what
  replaces the directedness argument that a colimit of modules would supply.
- `iterateOmegaPhi`, `iterateOmegaPhi_eq` — **the limit generator assignment**, restricting to that
  of every stage: original generators keep the base differential and every generator attached along
  the tower maps to the class it was attached to kill.
- `iterateOmegaPhi_hphi`, `iterateOmegaPhi_hinv`, `iterateOmegaPhi_hkill` — the three stage
  conditions at the limit.  Admissibility is proved by pushing the two generators to a common stage
  and applying that stage's own admissibility; `d² = 0` at the limit follows.
- `iterateOmega` — **the limit stage of the tower**, an honest `DeningerGCAStage`.
- `iterateOmega_d_toOmega` — **every stage maps to the limit as a map of differential graded
  algebras**; `iterateOmega_cocycle`, `iterateOmega_coboundary` are its two immediate consequences,
  and `iterateOmega_kills` says every class attached anywhere along the tower is a coboundary in the
  limit.

The comparison itself needs both exactness directions, and both are proved unconditionally.

- `iterateGenToOmega_lt`, `iterateGenToOmega_injective` — a generator attached at a stage is
  attached strictly before it, so distinct generators of a stage stay distinct in the limit.
- `iterateOmegaRetractGen`, `iterateOmegaRetract`, `iterateOmegaRetract_comp_toOmega` — **the
  retraction of the limit algebra onto a stage**, sending every generator not belonging to that
  stage to zero.  It is an algebra map because the Koszul relations are satisfied vacuously whenever
  one factor is killed.
- `iterateToOmega_injective` — **each stage embeds in the limit stage**.  The tower is therefore a
  genuine filtration of the limit algebra, not merely a diagram mapping into it.
- `exists_iterateToOmega_ge`, `exists_iterateToOmega` — **the limit algebra is the union of the
  stages**: every element of the limit already comes from a stage, so the limit is the filtered
  colimit of the tower.
- `iterateOmega_cocycle_exists_stage` — **a cocycle of the limit is the image of a cocycle of a
  stage**.  Surjectivity gives an element of some stage, and injectivity upgrades "its differential
  dies in the limit" to "its differential is zero at that stage".
- `iterateOmega_coboundary_exists_stage` — **a class that becomes a coboundary in the limit already
  becomes one at a stage**, and at a stage at least as late as the one the class came from.
- `iterateOmega_cohomology_computed_at_stages` — the two halves together: **cohomology of the limit
  stage of a tower is computed at the stages.**

This is step 3 of the proof at the algebra level, for the mixed-degree model, with no hypotheses.
The `DeningerSullivanTower` version proved earlier is the same statement for a tower carried on a
fixed exterior algebra with an abstract filtration; this version constructs the limit object itself.

### The tower over an arbitrary index order, and the limit levels

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GradedSullivanTower.lean`.  The ℕ-indexed iteration is not
the general shape of the recursion; the general shape is a generator set with a *level* function
into an arbitrary ordered index set.  Presented that way, the passage through limit levels needs no
construction at all, because a subalgebra generated by a set of generators is determined by that
set: at a limit level the stage simply *is* the union of the earlier stages.

- `deningerGCASubalgebra` — the subalgebra generated by a set of generators, with
  `deningerGCASubalgebra_ι`, `_mono`, and `deningerGCASubalgebra_univ` (all the generators
  generate).
- `deningerGCAInvolute_mem_subalgebra` — the grading involution preserves every such subalgebra.
- `deningerGCAOddDerivation_mem_subalgebra` — **lower triangularity makes a generated subalgebra a
  differential subalgebra**: if the derivation's values on a set of generators lie in the subalgebra
  that set generates, that subalgebra is stable under it.
- `deningerGCASubalgebra_exists_stage`, `deningerGCASubalgebra_iSup` — **every element already lies
  at a stage** of an exhausted filtration of generators, over any directed index order.  This is the
  limit-level step, and it is proved once for all index orders.
- `DeningerGCATower` — **a relative Sullivan tower on generators of mixed degree, indexed by an
  arbitrary preorder**: generators with degrees, a level function, a base closed under the
  differential, and lower triangularity of the differential of every attached generator.
- `DeningerGCATower.d`, `d_leibniz`, `d_comp_self`, `d_ι_eq_zero_iff` — the tower is a differential
  graded algebra, and a generator is closed exactly when the differential kills it.
- `DeningerGCATower.stageGen`, `stage`, `stage_mono`, `phi_mem_stage`, `stage_stable`,
  `stage_involute` — **every stage is a differential subalgebra** preserved by the involution.
- `DeningerGCATower.exists_stage`, `iSup_stage`, `cocycle_exists_stage`,
  `coboundary_exists_stage_ge`, `cohomology_computed_at_stages`,
  `cocycle_isCoboundary_of_stagewise` — **the limit-stage comparison over an arbitrary directed
  index order**, and acyclicity from stagewise killing.

Taking the index order to be an ordinal (any linear order is directed) is now a matter of choosing
`B`, not of building anything: this is step 3 of the proof in the generality the transfinite
factorization needs.

The ℕ-indexed construction is an instance of this, which is what ties the two developments together.

- `iterateOmegaLvl`, `iterateGenToOmega_lvl_le` — the level of a limit generator is the step at
  which it was attached, and a generator of stage `n` has level at most `n`.
- `iterateToOmega_mem_subalgebra` — the image of a stage lies in the subalgebra generated by that
  stage's generators.
- `DeningerGCAStage.iterateTower` — **the iterated tower is a `DeningerGCATower` over ℕ**.  Its
  lower triangularity is exactly the statement that a generator attached at step `n` kills a class
  built from the generators available before step `n`.
- `iterateTower_d` — **the two constructions carry the same differential graded algebra**: iterating
  successor attachments and then taking the union gives the same differential as presenting the
  whole generator set at once with its levels.
- `iterateToOmega_mem_stage` — each stage of the iterated tower lands in the corresponding stage of
  the level filtration.

### Parity replaces grading, and the admissibility hypothesis disappears

The free graded-commutative algebra of the model carries no grading decomposition, and the stage
structure compensated for that with an explicit admissibility hypothesis: the value of the
differential on a generator was *assumed* to graded-commute with every generator.  That hypothesis
turns out to carry no information.

- `deningerGCA_ι_mul_of_odd` — **an odd generator moves past any element at the cost of the grading
  involution**: `ι(w)·a = σ(a)·ι(w)` for every `a`.  The involution is the algebra's substitute for
  a grading, and it records exactly the sign an odd generator picks up.
- `deningerGCA_ι_mul_of_even` — **an even generator is central**.
- `deningerGCA_ι_mul_of_involute` — consequently a generator graded-commutes with any element of
  pure parity: if `σ(a) = (-1)^p a` then `ι(w)·a = (-1)^{|w|p}(a·ι(w))`, exactly as if `a` were
  homogeneous of degree `p`.
- `deningerGCA_hphi_of_hinv` — **admissibility is a consequence of parity.**  A generator assignment
  whose values transform as elements of one degree higher automatically graded-commutes with every
  generator.
- `DeningerGCAStage.ofInvolute`, `DeningerGCAAttachment.of` — the resulting constructors: a stage is
  determined by a parity-correct generator assignment killing its own values, and an attachment by
  parity-correct cocycles.  Nothing else has to be checked.

### The full attachment, and acyclicity without transfinite induction

- `DeningerGCACocycle` — **the classes a stage can attach a generator to kill**: a cocycle together
  with the degree of the generator that will kill it, transforming as an element of one degree
  higher.
- `DeningerGCAStage.fullAttachment`, `deningerFullChoice` — **the attachment that kills everything
  attachable**, one new generator for every such class, at every stage.
- `fullChoice_kills` — every attachable class of a stage is a coboundary at the next stage.
- `fullOmega_isCoboundary` — **the limit of the full tower is acyclic on classes of pure parity**:
  every cocycle of the limit that transforms as an element of a definite degree is a coboundary.
  `fullOmega_cocycle_iff_coboundary` is the two halves together.

This is step 2 of the proof carried through to its conclusion in the absolute case, and it needs no
transfinite induction at all: a cocycle of the limit already lives at a stage (`exists_iterateToOmega`),
is attachable there because parity transports along the stage embedding, and is therefore killed one
stage later.  The `ω`-indexed tower suffices; the ordinals of the classical argument are an artifact
of doing the bookkeeping with cardinals rather than with the filtration.

What this does *not* give is the relative statement.  Attaching every attachable class resolves the
ground field, not the target `K`: the correct attachment is the one that kills exactly the classes
dying in `K`, which is the next step.

### The relative attachment: augmented stages

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GradedSullivanAugmented.lean`.  A relative Sullivan
factorization does not attach a generator to every class — it attaches one to exactly the classes
that die in the target.  The bookkeeping for that is an augmentation: a map from the stage to `K`
annihilating the differential, that is, a map of differential graded algebras where `K` carries the
zero differential.

- `deningerGCAOddDerivation_eq_zero` — an odd derivation with vanishing generator values is zero.
- `deningerGCA_augmentation_comp_d` — **an augmentation annihilates the differential as soon as it
  does so on generators**; the Leibniz rule propagates the vanishing through products, so being a
  map of differential graded algebras is a condition on the generators alone.
- `DeningerGCAStage.cocycles` — **the cocycles of a stage form a subalgebra**, again by the graded
  Leibniz rule.  `base_d`, `base_cocycles` — the base stage has no differential, so everything in it
  is closed.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage` — **an augmented stage**, and `DeningerGCAAugStage.Cocycle` the classes it
  has to kill: cocycles of pure parity lying in the kernel of the augmentation.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage.attachment` — **the relative attachment**: one new generator for every class
  of the stage that dies in the target, and nothing else.
- `epsSuccGen`, `epsSucc`, `epsSucc_comp_inl`, `epsSucc_heps` — **the augmentation extends over the
  attachment by sending the new generators to zero**, restricts to the old augmentation, and is
  again an augmentation.  The last point is exactly where the choice of attached classes is used:
  the new generators' differentials are the attached classes, which were required to die in `K`.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage.succ`, `succ_kills` — the successor of an augmented stage, and the fact that
  every class dying in the target becomes a coboundary there.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage.iterate` with `iterate_d_inl`, `iterate_eps_comp_inl`, `iterate_kills`,
  `iterate_eps_d` — **the relative tower**: a chain of differential extensions carrying a single
  augmentation to the target, in which at every stage every class dying in `K` is killed one stage
  later and nothing else is attached.
- `deningerGCAAugBase`, `iterate_eps_surjective`, `iterate_eps_cocycle_surjective` — **the
  cohomology comparison is surjective at every stage** as soon as the base has no differential and
  its augmentation is onto `K`: every element of the target is the image of a cocycle.  So the tower
  only ever has to make the comparison *injective*, which is what the attachments are for.

### The limit of the relative tower, and clause 3

The relative tower needs its own limit: the attachment at a stage depends on the augmentation there,
so the choice is not a function of the underlying stage and the absolute construction does not
apply.  The limit is built directly, with the same generator-set trick.

### The grading, as a weight action

The model has no degree decomposition, but it does have the grading itself, in the form of an
action: scaling each generator by `t` to the power of its degree is an algebra map, because the
Koszul relations are homogeneous.

- `deningerGCAWeight` — **the weight action**, with `deningerGCAWeight_ι`, `_one`, `_mul`, and
  `_involute` (it commutes with the grading involution).
- `deningerGCAWeight_neg_one` — **the grading involution is the weight action at `-1`.**  So every
  statement about the parity of an element was a statement about its weight all along.
- `DeningerGCAHomogeneous` — **homogeneity of weight `n`**: the action scales the element by `t^n`.
  `deningerGCAHomogeneous_ι` (a generator is homogeneous of its own degree),
  `_algebraMap`, `_zero`, `_add`, `_smul`, and `_mul` (**weights add under multiplication**).
- `deningerGCAHomogeneous_involute` — **a homogeneous element has the parity of its weight.**  This
  is the bridge: every theorem proved for elements of pure parity is a theorem about homogeneous
  elements.
- `deningerGCAWeight_oddDerivation`, `deningerGCAHomogeneous_oddDerivation`,
  `DeningerGCAStage.d_homogeneous` — **the differential raises weight by one** when the generator
  values are homogeneous.

The two limit theorems in their degreewise form:

- `DeningerGCAStage.fullOmega_isCoboundary_of_homogeneous` and
  `DeningerGCAAugStage.omega_isCoboundary_of_homogeneous` — a homogeneous cocycle of weight `n + 1`
  which dies in the target is a coboundary.  **This is the degreewise injectivity of the cohomology
  comparison**, and it is stronger than the ungraded form: homogeneity supplies the parity directly,
  so it needs neither the invertibility of `2` nor an involution on the target.

### The homogeneous elements span

- `deningerGCAHomogeneousSet`, `deningerGCA_span_homogeneous_mul_mem`,
  `deningerGCA_span_homogeneous_eq_top` — **the homogeneous elements span the algebra.**  Scalars
  and generators are homogeneous and weights add under multiplication, so the span of the
  homogeneous elements is a subalgebra containing the generators, hence everything.
- `deningerGCA_exists_homogeneous_list` — **every element is a finite sum of homogeneous
  elements**, in explicit form.

This is the spanning half of the weight decomposition.  The other half — that the sum is direct, so
that the homogeneous components of an element are well defined — is what is still open, and it is
what would upgrade `omega_isCoboundary_of_homogeneous` from homogeneous classes to all classes.

### Directness: the weight components are unique

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GradedGCAIndependence.lean`.  The weight action separates
weights over an infinite field, and the mechanism is Vandermonde: evaluating the action at enough
distinct parameters turns a vanishing sum of homogeneous elements into an invertible linear system.

- `eq_zero_of_matrix_smul_eq_zero` — a module-valued vector killed by an invertible matrix is zero;
  the inverse matrix reconstructs each coordinate.
- `eq_zero_of_forall_sum_pow_smul_eq_zero` — **a power sum vanishing at every parameter has zero
  coefficients**, over an infinite field.
- `deningerGCAHomogeneous_eq_zero_of_sum_eq_zero` — **homogeneous elements of distinct weights are
  independent.**  Applying the weight action to a vanishing sum turns it into a power sum in the
  parameter.
- `deningerGCAHomogeneous_ext` — **the homogeneous components of an element are unique**, so with
  the spanning statement the weight decomposition is direct and determines well-defined
  projections.

The ground field of the statement is of characteristic zero, hence infinite, so the hypothesis is
automatic there.

### The grading is functorial, and the components are indexed by weight

- `deningerGCAGenAlg_weight`, `deningerGCAGenAlg_homogeneous` — **a degree-preserving map of
  generator sets intertwines the weight actions**, hence preserves homogeneity and its weight.  So
  the whole functorial layer of clause 4 is compatible with the grading.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage.toOmega_homogeneous` — in particular the maps from the stages into the limit
  preserve homogeneity.
- `deningerGCA_exists_homogeneous_components` — **every element has homogeneous components** in
  indexed form: it is the sum of a family whose `i`-th member is homogeneous of weight `i` and which
  vanishes beyond a bound.  With `deningerGCAHomogeneous_ext` these components are unique.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage.omega_isCoboundary_of_homogeneous_weight` — clause 3 for a homogeneous class
  of any weight, with no offset in the statement: the parity the general form asks for is just the
  parity of the weight.

### Clause 3 degreewise, for every class

`formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GradedSullivanGraded.lean`.

- `DeningerGCAAugStage.epsOmega_weight` — the limit augmentation intertwines the weight actions,
  given a target action matching the augmentation on the base generators.
- `DeningerGCAAugStage.omega_isCoboundary_of_graded` — **over an infinite field, if the tower's
  generator values are homogeneous and the target carries a matching weight action, then every

The proof splits the class into its homogeneous components.  Independence of distinct weights forces
each component to be a cocycle, because the differential raises weight by one; the same independence
transported into the target forces each component to die there; and each component is a coboundary
by the homogeneous form.  So the degreewise statement holds for all classes, not only homogeneous
ones, and the two compatibilities it needs are conditions on the data rather than on the
construction.

### What is still missing, and why

Two earlier entries in this section were wrong and are withdrawn.

- *Termination is not a gap.*  The classical small-object argument needs transfinite induction and a
  regular cardinal because termination is how it knows the colimit is a weak equivalence.  Here that
  is proved directly: `omega_quasiIso'` says the `ω`-limit already is the factorization, so nothing
  has to stop.
- *Clause 4 does not need a model category.*  Functoriality of the factorization is proved.  The
  reason it can be is that the attachment attaches a generator to *every* attachable class and
  therefore makes no choices; choice-freeness is exactly what functoriality requires.

What does remain:

- The degreewise form of clause 3 is proved for every class (`omega_isCoboundary_of_graded`).  Of
  its two hypotheses on the data, the second is discharged for the Milnor instance
  (`deningerMilnorGCAEps_weight`), and the first is reduced to a condition on the attached classes
  (`omegaPhi_homogeneous`).  What is not done is meeting that condition: the tower attaches every
  class of pure *parity*, and a parity class need not be homogeneous, so the tower as built does not
  satisfy it.  A tower attaching only homogeneous classes does, and the classes the theorem needs to
  kill are homogeneous, so the two fit — but building it means threading a selection predicate for
  the attached classes through the whole augmented development (the stage, its successor, its tower,
  its limit and their functoriality), since the predicate's type depends on the stage.  That is a
  refactor of the construction, not new mathematics, and it adds nothing to what is proved: the
  ungraded comparison `omega_quasiIso` already holds for the Milnor instance on all classes.
- The realization half of the card — the pro-space with uniquely divisible fundamental group — is a
  mechanism and not a theorem, as the card says in its own words.  It is outside the numbered
  statement.
