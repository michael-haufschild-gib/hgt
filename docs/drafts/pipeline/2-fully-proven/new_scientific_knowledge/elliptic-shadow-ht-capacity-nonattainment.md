# Elliptic shadows have unbounded but unattained HT orbit capacity

- **ID:** `elliptic-shadow-ht-capacity-nonattainment`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / FOUNDATIONAL CAPACITY DICHOTOMY
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED HERE] [NOVELTY UNVERIFIED] [SIGNIFICANCE CANDIDATE]`
- **Statement:** Translation inside a compact Abelian kernel of a quasi-Abelian quotient gives every
  single meromorphic observable finite differential-orbit capacity.  On `E x C*` every prescribed
  finite capacity nevertheless occurs.  Thus infinite ambient meromorphic complexity need not be
  concentrated in one structural observable.

## Why this is foundational

The quasi-Abelian full-orbit conjecture used noncompactness of the ambient quotient as its source
selector.  That is false.  A noncompact space can carry a structural action which is trapped in a
compact algebraic shadow.  The noncompact coordinate then supplies arbitrarily many independent
coefficients across different observables, but one meromorphic observable uses only finitely many
of them in its compact-fibre rational expression.

This separates two notions which had been conflated:

```text
aggregate HT capacity   = capacities of all available observables are unbounded;
concentrated HT capacity = one cyclic translation orbit has infinite differential rank.
```

Hypertranscendental orbit freedom is the second property.  The first does not imply it.

No time or flow interpretation is used.  The theorem is about an observable, a structural group
action, and whether infinite differential capacity is attained by one cyclic orbit.

## Setup

Let

```text
E = C/Lambda
```

be an elliptic curve, let `a in E` be nontorsion, and put

```text
X = E x C*,
T(z,u) = (z+a,u).
```

On the universal cover use coordinates `(z,w)` with `u=exp(w)`.  For a chosen constant
directional derivation `delta` and `g in Mer(X)`, define its cyclic differential-orbit capacity by

```text
cap_a(g)
 = dtrdeg_D D< T^n g : n in Z >_delta,
D = C(z,w).
```

Only finiteness and exact finite values are needed below, so no cardinal convention at infinity is
required.

## Compact-shadow finite-capacity theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

For every constant directional derivation `delta` and every `g in Mer(E x C*)`,

```text
cap_a(g) < infinity.                                      (1)
```

### Proof

Projection `E x C* -> C*` is proper with projective fibre.  Relative rationality of meromorphic
functions on a projective family gives

```text
g in K(E_K),   K=Mer(C*).                                 (2)
```

Choose a Weierstrass model

```text
y^2 = 4x^3-g_2 x-g_3
```

for `E`.  Equation (2) means that `g` is a rational expression in `x=wp(z)` and `y=wp'(z)` with
only finitely many coefficient functions

```text
c_1(u),...,c_m(u) in Mer(C*).                              (3)
```

Translation by `a` is an algebraic automorphism of `E`, defined over `C`.  The Abelian addition
theorem therefore puts every translate `T^n g` in the same field

```text
C(x,y,c_1,...,c_m).                                       (4)
```

The invariant derivatives of `x` and `y` remain in `C(x,y)`; in Weierstrass coordinates,

```text
partial_z x=y,
partial_z y=6x^2-g_2/2.                                   (5)
```

The `w`-part of `delta` differentiates only the finite coefficient family (3).  Consequently the
whole differential field generated by (4) is contained in

```text
D<x,y,c_1,...,c_m>_delta.                                 (6)
```

The Abelian coordinate field `C(x,y)` is differential-algebraic over `D` by (5), while adjoining
the finite family (3) raises differential transcendence degree by at most `m`.  Hence the complete
orbit in (4) has finite differential transcendence degree.  This proves (1). `QED`

## Proper compact-kernel theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Let

```text
0 -> B -> X -> Y -> 0                                    (6a)
```

be a quasi-Abelian group quotient with `B` a compact connected complex subgroup.  The generalized
Riemann form makes `B` an Abelian variety, and `X -> Y` is a proper holomorphic group quotient.
For every finitely generated subgroup `K subset B`, every invariant constant directional
derivation, and every `g in Mer(X)`, the differential field generated by the complete `K`-orbit of
`g` has finite differential transcendence degree over the rational universal-cover field.

Indeed, restrict `g` to the generic fibre of (6a).  Properness and projectivity of `B` identify it
with an element of the function field of a `B`-torsor over `Mer(Y)`.  This relative function field
is finitely generated of transcendence degree `dim B`.  The one element `g` is a rational
expression in finitely many relative generators with finitely many coefficient functions from
`Mer(Y)`.  Translation by `K` is the fibrewise algebraic `B`-action, so every translate remains in
the same relative function field and introduces no new base coefficient functions.

An invariant derivation preserves the relative function field.  Its values on a finite field
generating set are rational expressions with another finite family of base coefficients; adjoining
that finite family as differential generators absorbs all iterated derivatives.  Hence the whole
orbit lies in a differential field generated by finitely many relative coordinates and finitely
many base coefficient functions.  Its differential transcendence degree is finite. `QED`

The split elliptic proof above is the explicit Weierstrass-coordinate model of this relative
argument.

## Every finite capacity occurs `[PROVED HERE]`

For the noncompact directional derivation `delta=partial_w` and every positive integer `N`, there
is `g_N in Mer(E x C*)` such that

```text
cap_a(g_N)=N.                                              (7)
```

### Proof

Choose meromorphic functions

```text
phi_1,...,phi_N in Mer(C*)
```

which are differentially algebraically independent over the differential-algebraic extension
`D(x,y)`.  Such finite packets exist by finite jet interpolation on the Stein curve `C*` and the
standard Baire avoidance argument.

The points

```text
0,a,...,(N-1)a in E
```

are distinct.  Meromorphic point interpolation on the compact curve gives functions
`f_1,...,f_N in Mer(E)` satisfying

```text
f_j(i a)=delta_(ij),    0<=i<N.                            (8)
```

Define

```text
g_N(z,u)=sum_(j=1)^N f_j(z) phi_j(u).                      (9)
```

The first `N` translates are the constant-direction linear forms

```text
T^i g_N=sum_j M_(i,j)(z) phi_j(u),
M_(i,j)(z)=f_j(z+i a).                                    (10)
```

By (8), `M(0)=I_N`; hence `det M` is a nonzero meromorphic function on `E`.  With derivation
chosen in the `C*` direction, every entry of `M` is a differential constant.  Invertible constant
linear recombination preserves differential algebraic independence, so the first `N` translates
in (10) are differentially independent.  Thus `cap_a(g_N)>=N`.

On the other hand, every translate in (10) lies in the differential field generated by the `N`
functions `phi_j` and the differential-algebraic Abelian field `C(x,y)`.  Hence
`cap_a(g_N)<=N`, proving (7). `QED`

## Nonattainment law `[PROVED HERE]`

Combining (1) and (7) gives

```text
sup {cap_a(g):g in Mer(E x C*)}=infinity,
cap_a(g)<infinity for every individual g.                 (11)
```

Thus the capacity spectrum is unbounded but has no infinite-capacity point.  The noncompact factor
stores an arbitrarily large finite coefficient palette, while compact translation only mixes the
palette already present in the chosen observable.

This is a strong positive residue of the no-go: the compact shadow does not impose a uniform finite
bound.  It creates an **unattained infinite boundary**.

## Active-quotient positive theorem `[PROVED FOR THE SPLIT MODEL]`

Let `H` be a finitely generated free subgroup of `E x C*`, and let

```text
pi_inf : E x C* -> C*
```

be the noncompact projection.

If `pi_inf|H` is injective, finite jet interpolation on `C*` gives a meromorphic `h` whose complete
`pi_inf(H)`-translation orbit is differentially algebraically independent.  Its pullback

```text
g=h o pi_inf
```

has a completely free `H`-orbit.  Conversely, if `ker(pi_inf|H)` is nonzero, it contains an
infinite cyclic subgroup trapped in `E`; the compact-shadow theorem makes the restriction of every
meromorphic orbit to that subgroup finite-capacity, so no complete `H`-orbit can be free.

Therefore, on the split carrier,

```text
there exists one completely HT-free H-orbit
  iff H -> C* is injective.                                (12)
```

The correct selector is not ambient noncompactness.  It is **faithfulness on the active
noncompact quotient**.

## Corrected general conjecture

> **Active quotient principle `[CONJECTURE; NECESSITY PROVED]`.** Let `X` be a noncompact
> quasi-Abelian group and
> `B_X` its maximal compact complex subtorus.  A finitely generated free subgroup `H subset X`
> admits a meromorphic observable with completely differentially free `H`-orbit if and only if
> the projection `H -> X/B_X` is injective and the active quotient has all-order finite jet
> interpolation in one Baire meromorphic chart.

The necessity is the proved proper compact-kernel theorem.  The sufficiency is the all-order extension

point separation and first-tangent separation; the exponential damping in his proof survives every
fixed finite derivative order, but the all-order theorem is not promoted here until its complete
induction is written independently.

## Consequence for HT constraint geometry

The quasi-Abelian palette invariant is missing one clause.  For every facet `A`, its integral
structural lattice must embed not merely in the noncompact period quotient `X_A`, but in its active
quotient

```text
X_A/B_A,
```

where `B_A` is the maximal compact complex subtorus.  The corrected constraint object is therefore
not just the Stanley--Reisner spectrum with one quasi-Abelian group on each minimal prime.  It is

```text
(constraint spectrum,
 minimal-prime quasi-Abelian quotients,
 faithful active lattice representations).                (13)
```

This is the first action-relative source selector.  Topology records which constraints coexist;
the active representation records whether their infinite capacity is concentrated in a single
observable or dispersed across an unattained boundary.

## Falsified conjecture and positive replacement

The previous statement

```text
noncompact quasi-Abelian quotient + faithful H in X
  => one completely free meromorphic H-orbit
```

is false by `X=E x C*` with `H` contained in `E`.

The replacement is stronger and more informative:

```text
compact-trapped action  => every cyclic capacity finite,
                           all finite capacities realized;
active-faithful action  => infinite capacity attained by one observable.       (14)
```
