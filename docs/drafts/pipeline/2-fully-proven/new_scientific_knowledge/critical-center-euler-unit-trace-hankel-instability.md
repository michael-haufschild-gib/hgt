## 1. The critical-centre tangent system

Fix `0 < r < 1/2` and put

```text
D_r = {s in C : |s-1/2| < r},
R(s) = 1-s.
```

Let `O(D_r)` carry the compact-open topology and define its FE-odd subspace

```text
O^-(D_r) = {f in O(D_r) : f(1-s) = -f(s)}.
```

For every prime `p`, set

```text
w_p(s) = (log p) p^(-s)/(1-p^(-s))^2,
u_p(s) = w_p(s)-w_p(1-s).
```

The denominators do not vanish on `D_r`, because its closure lies in `Re s>0`.  Every `u_p` is
holomorphic and FE odd.

### Theorem A: cofinite fixed-locus completeness

For every finite prime set `S`, the complex span of

```text
{u_p : p prime, p notin S}
```

is dense in `O^-(D_r)`.

### Proof

Let `ell` be a continuous complex-linear functional on `O^-(D_r)` which annihilates every retained
`u_p`.  Define the continuous odd projection

```text
Pi^-(f)(s) = (f(s)-f(1-s))/2
```

and extend `ell` to `O(D_r)` by

```text
L(f) = ell(Pi^-(f)).
```

Then

```text
L(f o R) = -L(f).                                      (1)
```

Compact-open continuity gives a compact `K subset D_r` and a constant `C_K` such that

```text
|L(f)| <= C_K sup_(s in K) |f(s)|.
```

Define the Fourier--Borel transform

```text
F(z) = L(s |-> exp(-zs)).                              (2)
```

It is entire.  Since `K` is compact in `0<Re s<1`, there is a sector `|arg z|<theta` and a constant
`c>0` such that throughout that sector

```text
Re(zs) >= c|z|,
Re(z(1-s)) >= c|z|                                   (3)
```

for every `s in K`.

Put

```text
G(z) = F(z)-exp(-z)F(-z),
H(z) = sum_(m>=1) m G(mz).                            (4)
```

The estimates (3) make the series for `H` normally convergent in the sector and bounded on every
translated closed subsector.  The geometric-derivative expansion

```text
p^(-s)/(1-p^(-s))^2 = sum_(m>=1) m p^(-ms)
```

converges uniformly on `K`.  Therefore

```text
L(u_p) = (log p) H(log p).                            (5)
```

Thus `H(log p)=0` for every retained prime.

Map a translated subsector conformally to the right half-plane by a power map.  If `H` were not
identically zero, the half-plane Blaschke condition would force

```text
sum_(p notin S) 1/(log p)^alpha < infinity            (6)
```

for a fixed `alpha>0`. This series diverges for every `alpha` without the prime number theorem:
eventually `(log p)^alpha <= p`, so the terms dominate `1/p`, whose prime sum diverges by Euler,
even after deleting finitely many primes. Hence

```text
H=0.                                                   (7)
```

For sufficiently large positive `x`, the exponential bounds justify absolute regrouping in

```text
sum_(n>=1) mu(n)n H(nx)
  = sum_(n,m>=1) mu(n)n m G(mnx)
  = sum_(k>=1) k G(kx) sum_(n|k) mu(n)
  = G(x).                                              (8)
```

Equations (7)--(8) give `G(x)=0` on a positive ray, and therefore `G=0` by analyticity.

The anti-invariance (1), applied to the exponential in (2), gives

```text
exp(-z)F(-z) = L(s |-> exp(-z(1-s))) = -F(z).         (9)
```

Consequently `G(z)=2F(z)`.  Since `G=0`, also `F=0`.  Differentiating at zero gives

```text
L((-s)^j)=0
```

for every `j`.  Polynomials are dense in `O(D_r)`, so `L=0`, hence `ell=0`.  Hahn--Banach proves
the cofinite density.  QED.

## 2. The Schwarz-real fixed locus

Let

```text
O^-_R(D_r) = {f in O^-(D_r) : f(conj s)=conj(f(s))},
```

viewed as a real Frechet space.

### Theorem B: real cofinite completeness

For every finite prime set `S`, the real span of the retained `u_p` is dense in `O^-_R(D_r)`.

### Proof

The family `u_p` lies in the real form.  Every complex FE-odd germ has the unique decomposition

```text
f = (f+f#)/2 + i (f-f#)/(2i),
f#(s)=conj(f(conj s)),
```

with both summands in `O^-_R(D_r)`.  Thus `O^-(D_r)` is the complexification of
`O^-_R(D_r)`.  A continuous real-linear functional annihilating all `u_p` extends complex-linearly
to `O^-(D_r)` and is zero by Theorem A.  Real Hahn--Banach gives the result.  QED.

## 3. Nonlinear finite-unit orbit

For `a` near `1`, write the one-prime logarithmic flux

```text
phi_(p,a)(s) = (log p) a p^(-s)/(1-a p^(-s)).
```

For small `b`, define the FE-paired actual-source increment

```text
Delta_(p,b)^-(s)
  = [phi_(p,1+b)(s)-phi_(p,1)(s)]
    -[phi_(p,1+b)(1-s)-phi_(p,1)(1-s)].               (10)
```

It is a holomorphic FE-odd germ on `D_r`, and

```text
d/db Delta_(p,b)^-|_(b=0) = u_p.                      (11)
```

Let `G_r` be the additive group generated by all sufficiently small finite collections of these
increments.  For every scalar `c`,

```text
N Delta_(p,c/N)^- -> c u_p                            (12)
```

in the compact-open topology.  Each left side is the flux increment of a finite product of local
Euler units.  Theorems A and B now give

```text
closure(G_r) = O^-(D_r),
closure(G_(r,R)) = O^-_R(D_r)                         (13)
```

in the complex and real categories respectively.

For `N>1`, equation (12) repeats the same prime-local generator and therefore produces a
higher-power local unit. The density claim concerns the generated finite-unit group `G_r`, not the
unmultiplied degree-one Euler-parameter locus.

Thus the generated finite FE-paired Euler-unit orbit is dense in the entire critical-centre odd germ space,
not only on a quartet-disjoint domain to the right of the critical line.

## 5. The Hankel-positive cone is nowhere thick

Write a real even germ as

```text
g(z)=sum_(n>=0) m_n z^(2n).
```

Define its trace-Hankel quadratic form by

```text
Q_g(c) = sum_(i,j>=0) c_i c_j m_(i+j)                 (17)
```

for finitely supported real coefficient sequences `c`.  Let

```text
C_H = {g : Q_g(c)>=0 for every finitely supported real c}.  (18)
```

### Theorem C: closed empty-interior cone

The cone `C_H` is closed and has empty interior in the compact-open topology of real even germs.
Its complement is therefore open and dense.

### Proof

Every coefficient map `g |-> m_n` is continuous.  For fixed `c`, the expression (17) is a finite
continuous real-linear function of those coefficients.  Hence `C_H`, an intersection of closed
half-spaces, is closed.

Fix `g in C_H` and a compact-open neighbourhood of `g`.  After shrinking to a basic
neighbourhood, it is enough to control the supremum on `|z|<=r_0`, where `r_0` is strictly smaller
than the germ radius.  Choose `r_0<r_1` inside that radius and put

```text
B = sup_(|z|=r_1) |g(z)|.
```

Cauchy's estimate gives

```text
|m_(2N)| <= B r_1^(-4N).                              (19)
```

Since `g in C_H`, the monomial square test gives `m_(2N)>=0`.  Define

```text
g_N(z)=g(z)-(m_(2N)+1)z^(4N).                         (20)
```

Then

```text
sup_(|z|<=r_0)|g_N(z)-g(z)|
  <= B(r_0/r_1)^(4N)+r_0^(4N) -> 0.                  (21)
```

But the coefficient of `z^(4N)` in `g_N` is `-1`.  Taking the polynomial test `p(y)=y^N` gives

```text
Q_(g_N)(p)=-1<0.                                      (22)
```

Thus every neighbourhood of every point of `C_H` meets its complement.  The cone has empty
interior.  QED.

### Corollary: source-germ instability of trace-Hankel positivity

For every real trace-moment germ `g`, its finite FE--Schwarz Euler-unit orbit is dense.  Because
the complement of `C_H` is open and dense, every compact-open neighbourhood of `g` contains a
finite-unit orbit point whose trace-Hankel form fails a finite polynomial square test.

In particular, even if the actual Xi germ lies in `C_H`, that membership cannot be forced by a
compact-open continuous local invariant which survives finite Euler-unit changes.  The obstruction
uses the full germ space and is not a finite-jet warning.

## 6. Attacks and exact scope

1. **The theorem does not prove or disprove RH.**  The actual zeta coefficient field is one fixed
   point.  A theorem using its exact weighted all-prime tail may select that point discontinuously
   relative to compact-open continuation.
2. **The result does not call distinct Euler products identical.**  Finite Euler units change the
   arithmetic source.  Density says only that the locally continued germ forgets the weighted-tail
   distinction.
3. **All Taylor orders are included.**  The compact-open topology on holomorphic germs makes the
   quotient classification an all-jet theorem rather than a finite-truncation statement.
4. **The fixed locus is essential.**  The anti-invariant projection in Theorem A replaces the
   separated-carrier argument used on domains strictly to the right of `Re s=1/2`.
5. **No zero data enter the completeness proof.**  The prime-Lambert tangent family, FE reflection,
   prime-log uniqueness, and source-unit action are defined before any divisor is read.
6. **No support exhaustion is promoted.**  Cofinite density is a duality theorem.  Equation (12)
   concerns closure of a static group, not convergence of Euler cutoffs to a new carrier.
7. **The Hankel argument is a parent lemma.**  Empty interior of the positive moment cone follows
   from Cauchy estimates and high-order monomial square tests.  The candidate novelty is its exact
   coupling to critical-centre completeness of the actual Euler-unit Lambert family.
