## Why this is not contained in individual atomicity

For a fixed parameter pair, the order-one theorem makes all distinct external translates
differentially independent. It says nothing about two atoms having different structural elliptic
curves. Individual transcendence does not imply joint transcendence: two individually
hypertranscendental functions may satisfy a nonlinear differential relation.

At order three, the bottom faces occupy six different parameter pairs. They are also not a direct
sum of unrelated functions: all six arise as second structural differences of the same primitive.
The result below proves that this shared origin creates no nonlinear dependence.

## Free-block replication lemma `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `k` be a differential field, let `sigma` be an automorphism commuting with its derivation, and
let `K/k` be a differential field extension of finite differential transcendence degree `d`.
Assume

```text
sigma^n(K) is DA over K for every n in Z.                 (1)
```

Let `B={b_1,...,b_s}` be a finite family. Assume that, for every positive integer `M`, there are
integers `N_1,...,N_M` such that the translated blocks

```text
sigma^(N_1)B,...,sigma^(N_M)B
```

are pairwise disjoint and their union is differentially algebraically independent over `k`. Then
`B` is differentially algebraically independent over `K`.

### Proof

Suppose `B` is dependent over `K`. Translate this dependence by each `sigma^(N_j)`. The `j`-th
block is dependent over `sigma^(N_j)(K)`.

Let `K_*` be the compositum of `K` and these finitely many translated coefficient fields. By (1),
`K_*/K` is DA, so

```text
dtrdeg_k K_*=d.
```

Every block has differential transcendence degree at most `s-1` over `K_*`. Hence their union has
degree at most

```text
d+M(s-1)
```

over `k`. But the union consists of `Ms` differentially independent elements, so

```text
Ms<=d+M(s-1),
```

which gives `M<=d`. Taking `M>d` is a contradiction. `QED`

The lemma uses no linearization of the hypothetical differential polynomial. It converts finite
coefficient-field complexity directly into a contradiction with arbitrarily many free blocks.

## Graph orbit-gluing theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `V` be a finite set. Let `(sigma_v)_(v in V)` be commuting differential-field automorphisms of
a common overfield of `k`. For every edge `e={u,v}` of a simple graph on `V`, choose an element
`A_e` and define its two-direction orbit

```text
O_e={sigma_u^a sigma_v^b A_e:a,b in Z}.
```

Assume:

1. **edge atomicity:** every `O_e` is differentially algebraically independent over `k`;
2. **off-edge DA triviality:** if `q notin e` and `Y in O_e`, then

   ```text
   sigma_q^n(Y)-Y is DA over k for every n in Z.          (2)
   ```

Then the union of all edge orbits

```text
union_e O_e                                                   (3)
```

is differentially algebraically independent over `k`.

### Step 1: every vertex star is independent

Fix `q in V`. Consider the edges `{q,i}` incident to `q`. Take an arbitrary finite family from
their orbits and order the finitely many represented edges.

The first edge family is independent by edge atomicity. Suppose finite families from earlier edges
`{q,i_1},...,{q,i_{r-1}}` have already been adjoined, and let `B` be the chosen finite block from
the next edge `{q,i_r}`. Let `K` be the differential field generated by all earlier coordinates.

For every earlier edge, `i_r notin {q,i_j}`. Therefore (2) implies

```text
sigma_(i_r)^n(K) is DA over K for every n in Z.
```

Arbitrarily many widely separated `sigma_(i_r)`-translates of `B` are disjoint subsets of the
single independent orbit `O_{q,i_r}`. The free-block replication lemma makes `B` independent over
`K`. Induction proves that the full star

```text
Star(q)=union_(e contains q) O_e                            (4)
```

is differentially algebraically independent.

### Step 2: a minimal circuit cannot cross a star

Suppose (3) is dependent and choose a finite minimal dependent subfamily `C`. Choose an edge
represented in `C` and one of its vertices `q`. Partition

```text
B=C intersect Star(q),
C_0=C\B.
```

The block `B` is nonempty. Minimality makes `C_0` independent over `k`, and dependence of `C`
therefore makes `B` dependent over the differential field

```text
K=k<C_0>_delta.                                            (5)
```

Every edge represented in `C_0` avoids `q`. By (2),

```text
sigma_q^n(K) is DA over K for every n in Z.
```

Arbitrarily many separated `sigma_q`-translates of `B` are disjoint subsets of the independent
star (4). The free-block replication lemma contradicts dependence over (5). Hence no circuit
exists and (3) is independent. `QED`

The star is the essential combinatorial object. A sequential proof over all edges fails because a
late edge need not have a private direction outside every earlier edge. Minimal-circuit
partitioning restores a private direction globally: the star moves while its complementary graph
is DA-stationary.

## Attacks and boundaries

1. **Atomic parent.** The theorem is conditional on the generic order-one atomic theorem. The
   graph argument does not repair a failure there.
2. **Cross-triviality is essential.** Individual edge-orbit independence alone does not prevent an
   algebraic relation between two edge families. The proof specifically uses DA-stationarity in
   every off-edge direction.
3. **Rationally dependent periods.** Dependence can identify external orbit coordinates and destroy
   both edge atomicity and free block separation.
4. **Higher bottom faces.** At order `r>=4`, a bottom order-one face has `r-1>=3` external
   directions and is indexed by a hyperedge rather than a graph edge. The star proof above does not
   automatically order the incident hyperedges by private directions.
5. **Next face layer.** The result closes only bottom-face core A of the order-three matroid
   program. It does not prove external-orbit independence of the four order-two faces.

## Parent-theory and literature audit

The free-block lemma is finite differential-transcendence bookkeeping, and the graph theorem is an
abstract gluing bridge. Adamczewski--Dreyfus--Hardouin--Wibmer prove algebraic independence of a
solution to a linear equation for one operator from a solution to a linear equation for an
independent operator. Their theorem concerns two functions, algebraic rather than differential
independence, and fixed operator classes; it does not give the graph-star gluing statement or the
six elliptic-gamma face family.

Hardouin's diagonal-system work and Ogawara's triangular one-shift systems are stronger parents for
nonlinear-to-linear reduction inside one difference system. They do not compare six atoms whose
structural elliptic curves and external translation pairs vary with the edge.

Narukawa's multiple-elliptic-gamma work proves modular and integral identities. Kato proves DA for
rational period tuples and explicitly leaves generic non-rational DA versus HT open. Searches
through 2026 for independence of multiple elliptic gamma functions with different parameter pairs,
joint differential independence of elliptic gamma faces, graph-glued difference orbits, and the
star-replication mechanism located no equivalent or stronger theorem.

The abstract graph lemma is not promoted as new science. Conditional on acceptance of the atomic
parent, the six-face theorem changes the known classification level from one elliptic curve at a
time to simultaneous differential independence across the complete parameter-pair boundary of an
order-three constraint. Its novelty remains provisional until independent specialists audit both
the proof and the literature boundary.
