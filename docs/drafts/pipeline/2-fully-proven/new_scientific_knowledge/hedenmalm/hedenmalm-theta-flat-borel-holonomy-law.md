## Theorem 1: exact Borel-tail factorization

For every complex `alpha` with `XiC(alpha)=0` and every real `x`,

```text

```

Moreover,

```text

integral_0^infinity b_x(t)dt=integral_x^infinity k(y)dy. (5)
```

Thus the all-orders-flat theta tail is not zero in the essential chart.  It is a positive
source measure multiplied by one universal complex dilation character.

### Proof

In the right-tail integral in `(1)`, make the exact substitution

```text

dy=dt/(2(1+t)).                                          (6)
```

Then

```text

```

and `(4)` follows.  Positivity in `(5)` follows from positivity of `k`; its mass identity is the
same substitution with `alpha=0`. QED.

## Theorem 3: reflection, adjunction, and radial drift are different operations


