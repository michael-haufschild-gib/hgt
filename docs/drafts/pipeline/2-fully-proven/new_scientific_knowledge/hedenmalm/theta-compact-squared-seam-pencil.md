# Theta compact squared-seam pencil

## Status

Paper proof complete. Lean target: `HedenmalmThetaSquaredSeamPencil.lean`.

## Statement

Let `B` be the fixed compact theta source inverse on quantile `L²`. For every multiplicity copy
`q` of a Xi zero, put `z = xiSpectralPoint q`, `lambda = -z⁻¹`, and let `v_q` be the nonzero
theta divisor vector constructed in `HedenmalmThetaCompactDivisorEigenmode`. Then

1. `B v_q = lambda v_q`;
2. if `mu_q = xiSquaredSeamOneCoordinate q`, then
   `B² v_q = mu_q (v_q + B² v_q)`;
3. `v_q + B² v_q` is nonzero, so `mu_q` is the unique generalized eigenvalue of this pencil on
   the line generated by `v_q`.

## Proof

The compact divisor theorem gives (1), hence `B² v_q = lambda² v_q`. By definition,
`xiZeroCopyReciprocalSquare q = z⁻² = lambda²`. The shift-one seam identity gives

`mu_q = lambda² / (1 + lambda²)`.

The denominator is nonzero by the already-proved positive-real-part bound for
`1 + xiZeroCopyReciprocalSquare q`. Therefore

`mu_q (v_q + B² v_q) = mu_q (1 + lambda²) v_q = lambda² v_q = B² v_q`.

Nonvanishing follows from `(1 + lambda²) != 0` and `v_q != 0`. If another scalar `nu` satisfies
the same pencil equation, subtract the two equations and cancel the nonzero vector
`v_q + B² v_q`, giving `nu = mu_q`.

## Research consequence

The squared-seam dyadic RH endpoint is now a spectral statement about one fixed source-built
compact operator, not a divisor-indexed diagonal surrogate. Missing theorem is theta-specific:
prove the generalized eigenvalues of `(B², I + B²)` on its genuine divisor modes lie on the
nonnegative real ray. Compactness alone cannot imply this.
