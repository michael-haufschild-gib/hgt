## Setup

Let `(K,delta)` be a differentially closed field of characteristic zero with algebraically closed
constant field `C`.  Let `A/K` be an abelian variety of dimension `g`, not assumed constant or
isotrivial.  Let `a` be a differential-generic point of `A` over `K`, and write

`L=K<a>`

for its differential rational function field.  Fix an integer `m` with `|m|>1`.  Multiplication
by `m` induces an injective differential endomorphism `[m]*:L->L`.  Define

`Tail_m(A)=intersection_(n>=0) [m]^(n*)L.`                          (1)

Let `A^sharp` be the Manin kernel: the smallest Zariski-dense differential algebraic subgroup of
`A`, equivalently the smallest definable subgroup containing all torsion.  Let

`mu_A:A->A/A^sharp`

be the differential algebraic Manin map.  Intrinsically, if `A_tilde` is the universal vectorial
extension and `W_A` its vector subgroup, then

`mu_A:A -> Lie(A_tilde)/dlog_(A_tilde)(W_A)`

is induced by the logarithmic derivative on `A_tilde`.  Its kernel is exactly `A^sharp`.

## Theorem A: Manin-horizon theorem

**[PROVED HERE]**

`Tail_m(A)=L^(A^sharp)=K<mu_A(a)>.`                                (2)

The equality is an equality of embedded differential subfields of `L` after interpreting the
Manin quotient through its differential rational coordinate functions.

### Proof

For every `n`, `[m]^n:A->A` is a finite etale Galois quotient by the translation action of the
finite group `A[m^n]`.  On every jet level, the same finite deck group over `K` acts through its
prolonged translations and the quotient is the prolonged multiplication map.  Therefore

`[m]^(n*)L=L^(A[m^n]).`                                            (3)

Intersecting (3) over `n` gives the field fixed by the union

`T_(m,infinity)=union_n A[m^n]`.                                  (4)

Let `H` be the Kolchin closure of (4).  The `m`-primary torsion is Zariski dense in `A`, so `H`
is a Zariski-dense differential algebraic subgroup.  By the minimality of `A^sharp`,
`A^sharp subset H`.  Conversely `A^sharp` is Kolchin closed and contains all torsion, hence
`H subset A^sharp`.  Thus

`H=A^sharp.`                                                       (5)

Every element of `L` has finite differential order.  Invariance under (4) is consequently a
Kolchin-closed condition on the translation parameter, so it is equivalent to invariance under
its Kolchin closure.  Equations (3)--(5) prove

`Tail_m(A)=L^(A^sharp).`                                           (6)

Here the right side means differential rational invariants: `f(a+h)=f(a)` generically for every
`h in A^sharp`.  It need not mean that every non-`K` point of `A^sharp` acts as an automorphism of
the particular field generated by one chosen generic point.

The Manin map is a surjective differential algebraic group homomorphism with kernel `A^sharp`.
Its generic fibres are exactly the `A^sharp`-translation orbits.  The differential rational
quotient theorem therefore identifies the invariant field in (6) with `K<mu_A(a)>`, proving
(2).

The classical input `ker(mu_A)=A^sharp` for arbitrary abelian varieties, together with the
universal-vectorial-extension construction, is stated and proved in Bertrand--Pillay:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.02012>.  The new step is the identification of that quotient with the
stable intersection (1).

## Theorem B: universal abstract tail and scalar dynamics

**[PROVED HERE]** The differential algebraic quotient `A/A^sharp` is noncanonically isomorphic
over `K` to the full additive differential group `G_a^g`.  Hence

`Tail_m(A) isomorphic to K<v_1,...,v_g and all derivatives>`,       (7)

where the `v_i` are differentially independent over `K`.  Under this isomorphism,

`[m]^*(v_i)=m v_i` for every `i`.                                  (8)

### Proof

Buium's embedding of `A/A^sharp` into a vector group and Cassidy's classification give a
differential algebraic isomorphism `A/A^sharp isomorphic to G_a^g`; the dimension `g` statement
is the standard Manin-quotient theorem.  The image of a differential-generic `a` is therefore a
differential-generic vector, proving (7).  Since `mu_A` is a group homomorphism,

`mu_A([m]a)=m mu_A(a)`,

which proves (8).

## Corollary C: full abelian no-hair theorem

For fixed `g` and `m`, every `g`-dimensional abelian variety over every differentially closed
base of characteristic zero has the same abstract multiplication-tail dynamical field (7)--(8),
after base identification.  This includes:

- constant and nonconstant abelian varieties;
- isotrivial and nonisotrivial families;
- all polarizations, period data, endomorphism rings, and isogeny classes.

Those data can occur only in the **embedded** Manin map

`K<mu_A(a)> subset K<a>`,                                         (9)

not in the abstract differential field with scaling action.

## Corollary D: the correct closure changes with the base

For a constant abelian variety, `A^sharp=A(C)` and `mu_A` is the ordinary
Maurer--Cartan/logarithmic derivative.  The closure controlling the horizon can then be described
as an algebraic group of constant translations.

For a varying abelian variety, the correct symmetry is the differential algebraic Manin kernel
`A^sharp`, not a constant translation group and not merely the Zariski closure of torsion.  The
tail construction therefore detects a transition from algebraic topology to differential
algebraic topology while retaining the same universal abstract boundary field.
