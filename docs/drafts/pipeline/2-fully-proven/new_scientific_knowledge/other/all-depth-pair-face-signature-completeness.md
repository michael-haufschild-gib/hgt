# All-Depth Pair-Face Signature Completeness

- **ID:** `all-depth-pair-face-signature-completeness`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven / needs independent audit
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / ALL-DEPTH COMPLETE INVARIANT
- **Scientific status:** `[CANDIDATE NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]` — complete paper proof conditional only on the independently unaudited depth-two triptic DA-completeness theorem; no equivalent all-depth classification was located in the checked literature
- **Statement:** For a meromorphic `1`-periodic function with constant full mixed difference in any number of independent directions, differential algebraicity is classified by finitely many unpushed rank-three residues: one for every pair of directions, after differencing in all complementary directions. The pair residues realize exactly the finite families whose pushforwards agree on every triple. Thus the mixed-constraint quotient modulo DA is the space of compatible pair-divisor families.

## Setup

Let `d>=2`, let `tau_1,...,tau_d in H`, and assume

`1,tau_1,...,tau_d`

are linearly independent over `Q`. Write `Delta_i=Delta_(tau_i)` and define

`Q_d={F in Mer(C):F(z+1)=F(z) and product_(i=1)^d Delta_i F in C}`.

For each unordered pair `{i,j}`, put

`W_(ij)(F)=product_(k notin {i,j}) Delta_k F`.

The product is empty when `d=2`. Commutativity of translations gives

`W_(ij)(F) in Q_2(tau_i,tau_j)`.

Let

`S_(ij)(F)=R_2^(ij)(W_(ij)(F)) in C[delta][C/(Z+Z tau_i+Z tau_j)]`

be the complete triptic orbit-residue divisor of this two-parameter face.

Define the **pair-face signature**

`PairFace_d(F)=(S_(ij)(F))_(1<=i<j<=d)`.

The signature has `binom(d,2)` finite differential divisors. It retains every rank-three face before any push to the rank-`(d+1)` quotient.

## Main theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Assume the depth-two theorem

`U in Q_2(tau_i,tau_j) is DA iff R_2^(ij)(U)=0`

for every rationally independent triple `1,tau_i,tau_j`.

Then, for every `d>=2` and every `F in Q_d`,

`F is DA over C(z) iff PairFace_d(F)=0`.

This classifies the full mixed-constraint space `Q_d`, not only the cyclic module generated by a multiple elliptic gamma function.

## Exact image and universal pair-face normal form `[PROVED HERE]`

For every nonempty subset `S` of the parameter indices, put

`H_S=Z+sum_(i in S)Z tau_i`, `A_S=C[delta][C/H_S]`.

Whenever `S subset T`, write

`p_(S,T):A_S -> A_T`

for the finite-support pushforward.

Define the compatible pair-divisor space

`Comp_d subset direct_sum_(i<j) A_{ij}`

by the condition that, for every triple `i<j<k`,

`p_(ij,ijk)(D_(ij))=p_(ik,ijk)(D_(ik))=p_(jk,ijk)(D_(jk))`.

For `d=2` this condition is vacuous.

Then the pair-face map induces an isomorphism of differential-translation modules

`Q_d/(Q_d intersect Mer_DA) isomorphic to Comp_d`.

Injectivity is the main theorem. The pair signature of every actual `F` is compatible because, after applying all differences outside a triple, higher face reciprocity says that the three triptic pair residues have one common pushforward. It remains to prove that every compatible family is realized.

### Simultaneous finite-divisor lifting lemma

Fix `tau_d`. Suppose divisors

`D_i in A_{i,d}`, `1<=i<d`,

satisfy the pairwise compatibility conditions

`p_(i d, i j d)(D_i)=p_(j d, i j d)(D_j)`

for every `i<j<d`.

Then there is `B in A_d` such that

`q_i(B)=D_i`

for every `i<d`.

To prove this, decompose into the free `Z^(d-1)`-orbits generated by `tau_1,...,tau_(d-1)`. On one orbit the assertion is the exactness of

`R -> direct_sum_i R/(x_i)`

at the equalizer of the pairwise reductions to `R/(x_i,x_j)`, where

`R=C[delta][u_1^(+-1),...,u_(d-1)^(+-1)]`, `x_i=u_i-1`.

This has an elementary induction. Lift the first `n-1` components to `b`. Compatibility of the last component with each earlier one says that its difference from `b` modulo `x_n` lies in every ideal `(x_i)`, `i<n`. Their intersection is `(product_(i<n)x_i)`, so adding a multiple of that product to `b` matches the last component without changing the earlier ones. Finite orbitwise support gives the global lift.

### Realization proof

Proceed by induction on `d`. At `d=2`, surjectivity of `R_2` follows from the elliptic-gamma logarithmic derivative, whose normalized triptic residue is `[0]`.

Let `d>=3` and let `(D_(ij)) in Comp_d`. Apply the simultaneous lifting lemma to the components `D_(i,d)`. Obtain `B in A_d` with

`q_i(B)=D_(i,d)`.

The bottom-charge map `rho_d` is surjective: if `Psi_(d-1)` is the logarithmic derivative of the order-`(d-1)` multiple elliptic gamma function with parameters `tau_1,...,tau_d`, then

`rho_d(B Psi_(d-1))=B`.

Choose

`F_0 in Q_d`, `rho_d(F_0)=B`.

Its pair components involving `d` are exactly the prescribed `D_(i,d)`.

For `i<j<d`, define the error

`E_(ij)=D_(ij)-S_(ij)(F_0) in A_(ij)`.

Triple compatibility on `{i,j,d}` gives

`p_(ij,ijd)(E_(ij))=0`.

Finite telescoping along the infinite-order `tau_d` orbit yields a unique

`C_(ij) in A_(ij)`

such that

`E_(ij)=(t_(tau_d+H_(ij))-1)C_(ij)`.

The family `(C_(ij))_(i<j<d)` is compatible. Indeed, the errors have equal pushforwards on every triple below `d`, and after substituting the displayed formula their differences are killed by `t_(tau_d)-1`. That operator is injective on finite-support divisors because `tau_d` has infinite order on every such quotient.

By the induction hypothesis, choose

`F_1 in Q_(d-1)(tau_1,...,tau_(d-1))`

with pair signature `(C_(ij))`.

Viewed as an element of `Q_d`, `F_1` contributes zero to every pair `{i,d}`: the complementary differences leave a depth-one quasi-elliptic function, hence a DA function, so its triptic residue vanishes. On a pair `{i,j}` below `d`, translation equivariance gives the contribution

`(t_(tau_d+H_(ij))-1)C_(ij)=E_(ij)`.

Therefore

`PairFace_d(F_0+F_1)=(D_(ij))`.

Every compatible family is realized, completing the exact-image theorem.

## Multi-telescoping lemma `[PROVED HERE]`

Fix one direction `tau_d` and put

`Lambda_d=Z+Z tau_d`, `A_d=C[delta][C/Lambda_d]`.

For `1<=i<d`, let

`q_i:A_d -> C[delta][C/(Lambda_d+Z tau_i)]`

be the finite-support orbit-sum pushforward and put `x_i=t_(tau_i)-1`.

Then

`intersection_(i=1)^(d-1) ker q_i=(product_(i=1)^(d-1)x_i)A_d`.

### Proof

The subgroup generated by `tau_1,...,tau_(d-1)` acts freely on `C/Lambda_d`. Indeed, an integer relation among those classes would give a `Q`-linear relation among `1,tau_1,...,tau_d`.

Decompose a finite differential divisor into its finitely many `Z^(d-1)`-orbits. On one orbit, the coefficient module is the Laurent polynomial ring

`C[delta][u_1^(+-1),...,u_(d-1)^(+-1)]`.

The map `q_i` sets `u_i=1`, so its kernel is `(u_i-1)`. The elements `u_i-1` are pairwise nonassociate primes in this unique-factorization domain. Therefore

`intersection_i (u_i-1)=(product_i(u_i-1))`.

Summing the finitely many orbit components proves the identity.

Thus a finite divisor whose orbit sum vanishes independently in every transverse direction is already one full transverse coboundary. No analytic or Galois input enters this lemma.

This lemma is formalized unconditionally and axiom-clean in
`GaloisForLFunctions.TelescopeIdeal`. The per-orbit identity `⋂_i (u_i - 1) = (∏_i (u_i - 1))` is
`iInf_span_singleton_eq_span_prod` (for pairwise relatively prime elements) and
`iInf_span_singleton_eq_span_prod_of_primes` (for pairwise non-associate primes, the exact
Laurent-ring hypothesis), holding in any unique factorization domain. Summing the finitely many
orbit components is the remaining bookkeeping over the group algebra.

## Bottom charge

For `F in Q_d`, define its bottom charge in the surviving direction `tau_d` by

`rho_d(F)=R_1(product_(k=1)^(d-1)Delta_k F) in A_d`.

The function inside `R_1` is quasi-elliptic for the lattice `Lambda_d`.

The kernel is exact:

`ker rho_d=Q_(d-1)(tau_1,...,tau_(d-1))`.

Indeed, zero `R_1` means the quasi-elliptic function has no poles. Its derivative is then an entire elliptic function, hence constant, and period `1` forces that constant to be zero. The quasi-elliptic function is therefore constant.

For every `i<d`, the pair-face divisor on `{i,d}` is the orbit-sum pushforward of the bottom charge:

`S_(i,d)(F)=q_i(rho_d(F))`.

This equality is merely the definition of `R_2`: first take the principal-part charge on the `tau_d` elliptic curve, then sum it along the non-torsion `tau_i`-orbits.

## Proof of the main theorem

The proof is by induction on `d`.

### Base `d=2`

There is one pair and

`PairFace_2(F)=R_2(F)`.

The claim is exactly the assumed depth-two completeness theorem.

### Inductive step

Let `d>=3`, assume the theorem at depth `d-1`, and suppose

`PairFace_d(F)=0`.

Put

`D=rho_d(F) in A_d`.

For every `i<d`, the `{i,d}` component vanishes, so

`q_i(D)=S_(i,d)(F)=0`.

The multi-telescoping lemma gives

`D=(product_(i=1)^(d-1)x_i)C`

for some `C in A_d`.

Choose lifts of the finite support of `C`. Let `lambda_d` be a normalized theta logarithmic derivative for `Lambda_d`, so that

`R_1(lambda_d)=[0]`.

Put

`Z=C lambda_d`.

The function `Z` is DA. Translation equivariance of `R_1` gives

`rho_d(Z)=R_1(product_(i=1)^(d-1)Delta_i C lambda_d)`

`=(product_(i=1)^(d-1)x_i)C=D`.

Hence

`E=F-Z`

lies in `ker rho_d`, so

`E in Q_(d-1)(tau_1,...,tau_(d-1))`.

It remains to prove that the pair-face signature of `E` at depth `d-1` vanishes. Fix `1<=i<j<d` and set

`V_(ij)=product_(k<d, k notin {i,j}) Delta_k E`.

Then `V_(ij) in Q_2(tau_i,tau_j)`. The corresponding depth-`d` face is

`W_(ij)(E)=Delta_d V_(ij)`.

Since `Z` is DA, its pair-face divisors vanish. Therefore the hypothesis on `F` gives

`0=S_(ij)(E)=R_2^(ij)(Delta_d V_(ij))`.

By translation equivariance,

`R_2^(ij)(Delta_d V_(ij))`

`=(t_(tau_d+H_(ij))-1)R_2^(ij)(V_(ij))`,

where `H_(ij)=Z+Z tau_i+Z tau_j`.

The class of `tau_d` has infinite order modulo `H_(ij)`. A finite-support divisor invariant under an infinite-order translation is zero. Hence

`R_2^(ij)(V_(ij))=0`.

This holds for every pair `i<j<d`, so

`PairFace_(d-1)(E)=0`.

The induction hypothesis makes `E` DA. Since `Z` is DA, `F=E+Z` is DA.

The forward implication follows because translations, differences, and finite products of commuting differences preserve the DA field, and the depth-two residue vanishes on DA functions.

This completes the induction.

## Complete differential-translation module consequences `[PROVED HERE]`

The pair-face map is additive, commutes with `delta`, and is equivariant for every complex translation. The exact-image theorem identifies the induced map

`Q_d/(Q_d intersect Mer_DA) -> Comp_d`,

`[F] mapsto PairFace_d(F)`

as an isomorphism, not merely an injection.

Let

`B=C[delta][C]`

be the finite-support differential group algebra of all complex translations, and let

`pi_(ij):B -> C[delta][C/H_(ij)]`

be the quotient action on the `{i,j}` face. For every `P in B`, completeness and equivariance give the exact criterion

`P F is DA iff pi_(ij)(P)S_(ij)(F)=0 for every i<j`.

Consequently

`Ann_DA^B(F)=intersection_(i<j) pi_(ij)^(-1)(Ann(S_(ij)(F)))`.

The exact DA translation spectrum is

`Spec_DA(F)=intersection_(i<j) {h in C:t_(h+H_(ij))S_(ij)(F)=S_(ij)(F)}`.

Zero components impose no condition. Every nonzero finite-support component has a finite stabilizer in its quotient group. Thus the signature computes every constant-coefficient differential-translation cancellation and every DA shift directly from the pair faces.

For a multiplicative solution `Y`, the same statement classifies every finite integer product or quotient of arbitrary translates: its logarithmic derivative is obtained by multiplying each integer pair-face divisor by the corresponding group-algebra element.

## Terminal residue versus pair-face signature

The all-rank terminal charge `R_d(F)` is the common pushforward of every component `S_(ij)(F)` to

`C[delta][C/(Z+sum_i Z tau_i)]`.

At depth two there is no information loss. At every depth at least three, distinct nonzero pair-face configurations can push to zero; structural differences of higher multiple elliptic gamma functions provide HT examples with zero terminal charge.

The theorem identifies the exact repair:

`terminal charge` = conserved but incomplete total,

`all unpushed pair faces` = complete DA/HT invariant.

No higher raw residues are needed. The proposed full simplex of all face levels was overbuilt: depth-two residues on the `2`-skeleton, with equality on every triple, give the entire quotient normal form.

## Constraint filtration interpretation

The proof supplies a canonical reduction, not a flow. At one chosen surviving direction:

1. read the bottom principal-part charge;
2. use the pair-face vanishing conditions to prove that charge is a full transverse coboundary;
3. remove that coboundary by a DA theta layer;
4. descend from `Q_d` to `Q_(d-1)`;
5. repeat.

The function is fixed by the compatibility of its finite face charges. The multiple elliptic gamma hierarchy is not used to propagate the proof.

## Multiplicative theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `Y` be a nonzero `1`-periodic meromorphic function and assume the logarithmic derivative of its full `d`-direction multiplicative coboundary is constant. Then

`F=delta Y/Y in Q_d`.

For every pair `{i,j}`, take the complementary iterated multiplicative coboundary of `Y`, then compute its unpushed triptic integer divisor on the `{i,j}` face. Let the resulting collection be

`MPairFace_d(Y)`.

Conditional on depth-two completeness,

`Y is DA iff MPairFace_d(Y)=0`.

The components are precisely the pair-face residues of `delta Y/Y`. The conclusion follows from the additive theorem and the first-order equation `Y'=(delta Y/Y)Y`.

## Hypertranscendental consequence

For every `F in Q_d`, at least one pair-face divisor is nonzero if and only if `F` is HT. Thus every HT function in this constraint class has a finite, directly readable witness on some rank-three subconstraint, regardless of the ambient constraint depth.

This is stronger than the multiple-elliptic-gamma hierarchy theorem: it applies to every meromorphic solution of the bare constraint and shows that no genuinely higher-dimensional HT class can be invisible on all triptic faces.
