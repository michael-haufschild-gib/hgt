## Complete paper proof

### 1. Finite jet rectangles over `C(s)`

Fix finite depth `d` and derivative cutoff `m`.  Package a derivative cell as

```text
(r, z, k),  r < d, z in A, k <= m.
```

The `k`-th derivative of `F_(r,z)` is the analytic realization of the `r`-th prime-power
dilation of the rotated logarithmic derivative of the anchored prime source.  Tail completeness
and linear independence of the anchored basis imply tail coefficient independence for every
fixed depth.  The already-established prime-power packet theorem over the actual embedded
coordinate field therefore makes the packaged family algebraically independent over `C(s)`.
The derivative/dilation interchange introduces only the nonzero scalar `(r+1)^k`; coordinatewise
rescaling by nonzero constants preserves algebraic independence.  Hence every finite
depth-by-state-by-jet rectangle is algebraically independent over `C(s)`.

### 2. The entire countable family

Consider a finite subset of the full jet family

```text
{ partial^k F_(r,z) : r,k in N, z in A }.
```

Its depth coordinates and derivative coordinates have maxima.  The subset therefore embeds into
one finite rectangle from Step 1, so it is algebraically independent.  Algebraic independence has
finite character; consequently all jets of the family `(F_(r,z))_(r,z)` are algebraically
independent over `C(s)`.  This is precisely joint differential independence of the whole
countable carrier family.

Restricting to `r < d` gives a differentially independent family indexed by
`Fin d times product_i A_i`, of exact cardinality

```text
d * product_i |A_i|.
```

### 3. Exact frontier rank

Split the independent packet through depth `d` into the earlier layers `r < d` and the new layer
`r = d`.  Clearing denominators in a hypothetical differential relation over the differential
field generated by the earlier block would produce a relation over `C(s)` among the joint block.
Thus the new layer is differentially independent over `K_d`.  It is indexed by `A`, hence its
exact relative rank is `product_i |A_i|`; in particular adjoining it strictly enlarges `K_d`.

### 4. Fusion forces every layer

Let `1_A` be the all-basepoint state.  Its anchored observable is the source unit, its support is
empty, and `z union 1_A = z`.  The exact coefficient fusion law gives

```text
V_r(b_z) star V_0(1) = V_(r+1)(b_z).
```

If `L` contains every depth-zero carrier and is carrier-fusion complete, induction on `r` places
every `F_(r,z)` in `L`.  If `L` is also derivation-closed, it contains every jet and every field
operation on those jets, hence `K_infty <= L`.

### 5. Principal-filter classification

By construction, `K_infty` contains every carrier and all of its derivatives.  It is therefore
derivation-closed, contains depth zero, and contains the exact output of every carrier fusion.
Now let `L` be derivation-closed.

- If `L` contains depth zero and is fusion complete, Step 4 gives `K_infty <= L`.
- If `K_infty <= L`, then `L` contains depth zero.  Whenever two carrier inputs lie in `L`, the
  prescribed carrier output lies in `K_infty` independently of those membership assumptions, and
  therefore lies in `L`.  Thus `L` is fusion complete.

This proves the equivalence and identifies the completion class with the principal upper filter
above `K_infty`.

### 6. Actual primes

For pairwise-coprime positive moduli `q_i`, Chinese remaindering identifies simultaneous unit
residue states and Dirichlet recurrence makes the actual prime profile tail-complete.  Since
`|(Z/q_i Z)^x| = phi(q_i)`, the preceding formulas become

```text
finite-depth rank = d * product_i phi(q_i),
frontier rank = product_i phi(q_i).
```

This completes the proof. `QED`
