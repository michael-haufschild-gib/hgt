- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / GENERAL HYPERTRANSCENDENCE LANDSCAPE

- **Statement:** The differential field generated by any finite archimedean Gamma product with

  Thus finite-degree Langlands completion requires a separate explicit infinite arithmetic phase

## 1. Archimedean product and positive law

Fix

```text
d>=1,  alpha in R,
lambda_1,...,lambda_d>0,  mu_1,...,mu_d in C,               (1)
```

and assume the positive scales are commensurable: there is an `H>0` such that

```text
n_j=H lambda_j in N_(>0)  for every j.                      (2)
```

Put

```text
G(s)=exp(alpha s) product_(j=1)^d Gamma(lambda_j s+mu_j).    (3)
```

Repetitions and constant nonzero factors are allowed. This class contains every standard
finite-degree Langlands archimedean factor: `Gamma_R(s+mu)` has scale `1/2`, `Gamma_C(s+mu)` has
scale `1` or splits into two `Gamma_R` factors, and one may take `H=2`. Positive conductor powers
are absorbed by the real exponential `exp(alpha s)`.

Define

```text
K_G=C(s)<G>,
Psi=G'/G,
L=C(s)<Psi>.                                                (4)
```

### Archimedean monomial valuation theorem

`[PROVED HERE]` For every nonzero `F in K_G`, there are unique

```text
k(F),a(F),b(F) in Z,  c(F) in C^x                          (5)
```

such that, as `Im(s)->+infinity` with `Re(s)` in an arbitrary fixed compact interval,

```text
F(s)=c(F) G(s)^k(F) s^a(F) (log s)^b(F) (1+o(1)).          (6)
```

The convergence is uniform after adding a variable in any fixed compact subset of a vertical
tube. The logarithm is the principal branch in the upper half-plane.

Consequently, for every finite nonzero tuple

```text
A=(F_1,...,F_r) in K_G^r,                                  (7)
```

there is an index `j` for which

```text
(F_1(s+z)/F_j(s+z),...,F_r(s+z)/F_j(s+z))
  ->(d_1,...,d_r) in C^r\{0}                               (8)
```

locally uniformly in `z` as `Im(s)->+infinity`. Thus every finite coefficient tuple in the full
differential field of a completed archimedean product has one constant upper projective boundary
point.

### Recurrent finite-phase theorem

Let `V_(sigma,eta)={s:|Re(s)-sigma|<eta}`. Let `f` be holomorphic on this vertical strip and Bohr
uniformly almost periodic under vertical translation: its translate family is relatively compact
for uniform convergence on every whole closed substrip strictly contained in `V_(sigma,eta)`.

`[PROVED HERE]` If `f` is differentially algebraic over `K_G`, then

```text
f is differentially algebraic over C,
rank_Q Gamma_B(f)<infinity.                                 (9)
```

The same finite-rank conclusion holds for every continuous translation-equivariant observable of
the zero divisor of a nonzero `f`.

The positive law is:

> A recurrent object controlled by a finite-degree archimedean completion must carry a compact
> abelian phase object of finite covering dimension. Every infinite-rank arithmetic phase module
> must therefore be supplied by a separate infinite carrier and preserved through completion.

The finite carrier need not be a Lie torus; finite-rank character groups may dualize to solenoids.
The invariant is finite covering dimension, equivalently finite rational rank of the character
group.

## 2. The rational shift equation

Translation by the common step `H` and the integer Gamma recurrence give

```text
G(s+H)=C P(s)G(s),
C=exp(alpha H),
P(s)=product_(j=1)^d product_(ell=0)^(n_j-1)
       (lambda_j s+mu_j+ell).                              (10)
```

Therefore

```text
Psi(s+H)-Psi(s)=P'(s)/P(s).                                (11)
```

The constant multiplier `C` disappears after logarithmic differentiation. Put `Tau=Psi'`. Then

```text
Tau(s+H)-Tau(s)=r(s),
r(s)=(P'/P)'(s)
    =-sum_(j=1)^d sum_(ell=0)^(n_j-1)
       1/(s+(mu_j+ell)/lambda_j)^2.                        (12)
```

The crucial feature of `(12)` is not merely that `r` is rational. Its order-two pole mass is
strictly negative in every nonempty shift orbit.

## 3. The shift-orbit mass obstruction

`[PROVED HERE]` Equation

```text
y(s+H)-y(s)=r(s)                                           (13)
```

has no rational solution.

Group the poles of `r` into cosets of `H Z`. Fix one such coset and choose a representative `a`.
For a rational function `u`, let `c_n(u)` be the coefficient of `(s-a-nH)^(-2)` in the Laurent
expansion at `a+nH`; only finitely many `c_n(u)` are nonzero. If `u` has poles of order greater than
two in this orbit, the maximal-order coefficients of `u(s+H)-u(s)` have a finite-support endpoint
and cannot disappear, whereas `r` has no such poles. Thus a putative solution of `(13)` has order at
most two in the orbit.

At order two,

```text
c_n(u(s+H)-u(s))=c_(n+1)(u)-c_n(u).                        (14)
```

Summing `(14)` over all integers telescopes to zero. In contrast, the sum of the order-two
coefficients of `r` in this orbit is

```text
- #{(j,ell):-(mu_j+ell)/lambda_j in a+H Z},                (15)
```

counted with multiplicity, and is strictly negative. This contradiction proves the claim. Simple
poles and polynomial parts cannot repair an order-two coefficient mismatch.

This orbit sum is the order-two **discrete residue** from classical rational summability theory.
Abramov's rational summation problem and the later Chen--Singer residue formulation show generally
that a rational function is a shift difference exactly when all discrete residues vanish. The
elementary proof above is the specialization needed here. It survives collisions and integral
shifts among the `mu_j`; such collisions add positive multiplicity rather than permitting
cancellation.

## 4. Formal hypertranscendence of the logarithmic derivative

Stirling expansion in an upper sector gives a complete formal expansion

```text
hat(Psi) in C(log s)((s^(-1))),
hat(Tau)=D hat(Psi) in C((s^(-1))).                         (16)
```

Equations `(11)` and `(12)` hold formally. Let `rho(u)(s)=u(s+H)` and put

```text
q(s)=rho(r)(s)/r(s).                                       (17)
```

Since `(rho-1)hat(Tau)=r`, one has the homogeneous rational shift equation

```text
(rho-q)(rho-1)hat(Tau)=0.                                  (18)
```

Adamczewski--Dreyfus--Hardouin, Theorem 1.2 in Case `S_infinity`, applies to
`C((s^(-1)))` for every nonzero shift, in particular `H`: a formal Laurent solution of a
homogeneous rational linear shift equation belongs to `C(s)` or is hypertranscendental over
`C(s)`.

If `hat(Tau)` were rational, it would be a rational solution of `(13)`, contradicting Section 3.
Hence

```text
hat(Tau) is hypertranscendental over C(s),
hat(Psi) is hypertranscendental over C(s).                  (19)
```

The analytic `Psi` is also hypertranscendental. Indeed, an algebraic differential relation for
`Psi` over `C(s)` would pass term-by-term to its complete sectorial Poincare expansion and give the
same nonzero differential-polynomial relation for `hat(Psi)`, contradicting `(19)`.

Finally `G` is hypertranscendental: if `G` were differentially algebraic over `C(s)`, then the
logarithmic derivative `G'/G=Psi` would be differentially algebraic as well.

This proof does not assume differential independence of the individual shifted Gamma factors.
Their product is treated as the single object fixed by the rational shift constraint `(10)`.

## 5. No flat cancellation in the archimedean residue field

Both `Psi` and `hat(Psi)` are differential indeterminates over `C(s)`. Therefore

```text
Psi |-> hat(Psi)                                            (20)
```

extends to an isomorphism from the abstract differential rational field `L` onto its formal
Stirling image. No nonzero element of `L` can have zero formal expansion.

The formal image lies in `C(log s)((s^(-1)))`. A nonzero element has a first Laurent coefficient in
`C(log s)`, and a nonzero rational function of `log s` has a unique leading logarithmic monomial.
Consequently every nonzero `A in L` has unique

```text
a(A),b(A) in Z,  c(A) in C^x                              (21)
```

with

```text
A(s)=c(A)s^a(A)(log s)^b(A)(1+o(1))                        (22)
```

uniformly on compact vertical tubes.

Transfer from the formal field uses the complete sectorial expansions of the shifted digamma
functions and their termwise derivatives. Poincare expansions are closed under finite sums,
products, differentiation, and inversion when the formal leading term is nonzero. The isomorphism
`(20)` supplies exactly that nonvanishing for every nonzero differential rational expression.

## 6. The product contributes one exponential valuation direction

The Bell-polynomial identities

```text
G^(n)/G=Bell_n(Psi,Psi',...,Psi^(n-1))                     (23)
```

and the inverse logarithmic-derivative identities give

```text
K_G=L(G).                                                   (24)
```

Moreover `G` is transcendental over `L`. Since `G` is hypertranscendental, its derivatives are
algebraically independent over `C(s)`. For every `lambda in C^x`, simultaneous scaling

```text
G^(n) |-> lambda G^(n)                                     (25)
```

defines a differential automorphism of `C(s)<G>`. It fixes `Psi` and hence fixes `L`. Were `G`
algebraic over `L`, its orbit under `L`-automorphisms would be finite, contradicting `(25)`.

Thus every nonzero `F in K_G` can be written

```text
F=P_0(G)/Q_0(G),  P_0,Q_0 in L[X].                         (26)
```

Uniform Stirling asymptotics on fixed vertical tubes give

```text
|G(s)|=exp(-(pi/2)(sum_j lambda_j)Im(s)+O(log|s|)),        (27)
```

so `G(s)->0` exponentially as `Im(s)->+infinity`. Every quotient of nonzero `L` coefficients has
only algebraic-logarithmic size by `(22)`. If `u` and `v` are the least powers of `X` with nonzero
coefficients in `P_0` and `Q_0`, respectively, then

```text
P_0(G)~A_u G^u,
Q_0(G)~B_v G^v.                                            (28)
```

Applying `(22)` to `A_u/B_v` proves `(6)` with `k=u-v`. Uniqueness follows successively from
exponential separation of distinct `G` powers, power separation of distinct `s` exponents, and
logarithmic separation of distinct `log s` exponents.

Ordering the triples `(k,a,b)` by smaller `k`, then larger `a`, then larger `b`, and normalizing a
finite tuple by a component with dominant triple proves `(8)`.

## 7. Recurrence removes the archimedean coefficient state

Assume `f` is differentially algebraic over `K_G`. After clearing denominators, choose a finite
nonzero relation

```text
sum_(beta in E)
  F_beta(s) product_(j=0)^n (f^(j)(s))^(beta_j)=0.          (29)
```

Bohr uniform almost periodicity supplies positive return times `T_l->+infinity` such that

```text
f(s+iT_l)->f(s)                                             (30)
```

uniformly on every fixed smaller closed substrip; derivatives converge locally uniformly by
Cauchy's formula.

Translate `(29)` by `iT_l`, choose a dominant coefficient `F_gamma`, and divide pointwise by
`F_gamma(s+iT_l)`. Formula `(6)` makes the normalizing coefficient nonzero on each fixed compact
for all large `l`, and `(8)` gives a nonzero constant coefficient vector in the limit. Passing to
the limit yields a nontrivial autonomous ADE for `f`.

The paper theorem `almost-periodic-implicit-ade-relative-frequency-law.md`, specialized to constant
coefficients, now gives `(9)`, including singular implicit presentations and continuous
translation-equivariant divisor observables. No global initial-value uniqueness is used.

## 8. Hypertranscendence and completed-L consequences

The contrapositive is:

> Every holomorphic vertically uniformly almost-periodic function of infinite rational Bohr rank
> is hypertranscendental over the differential field of every finite archimedean Gamma product
> `(3)`.

In particular, let

```text
D(s)=sum_(n>=1) a_n exp(-omega_n s)                         (31)
```

have pairwise distinct real exponents `omega_n`, nonzero coefficients, uniform convergence on a
right half-plane, and exponent group of infinite rational rank. Uniqueness of the Dirichlet
expansion identifies the nonzero Bohr spectrum with the `omega_n`. Hence

```text
D is hypertranscendental over C(s)<G>.                      (32)
```

For an Euler source whose nonzero spectrum contains `log p` for infinitely many primes, unique
factorization gives infinite rational rank. Thus its arithmetic function and the entire finite
archimedean product are differentially algebraically independent over `C(s)`.

The statement does not require the Euler source to satisfy a functional equation. Conversely, the
functional equation does not generate the conclusion: it identifies the completion, while the
frequency rank distinguishes the arithmetic carrier from the completion field.

## 9. Positive requirements for RH architectures

For every finite-degree completed `L`-source, the theorem separates the two carriers:

```text
finite archimedean Gamma product -> one exponential valuation direction,
Euler/Dirichlet source          -> infinite arithmetic phase module.   (33)
```

Therefore a viable architecture must preserve the second carrier explicitly through the first.

1. **Connes / Hilbert--Polya / de Branges.** The operator, determinant, or canonical system must
   contain an infinite-rank arithmetic phase factor which survives insertion of the finite
   archimedean local factor.
2. **Nyman--Beurling / Weil positivity.** The cofinal test spaces must have unbounded arithmetic
   phase dimension; the Gamma completion can weight or reflect those modes but supplies no missing
   prime directions.
3. **Li-type criteria.** The unbounded coefficient hierarchy must retain a character module
   isomorphic to the Euler-frequency module, or replace it by another explicit infinite-rank
   carrier with a proved intertwining map.
4. **Universality and recurrence routes.** The distinguished limit law must remember the marked
   prime-log embedding. Finite archimedean differential data contributes no additional recurrent
   frequency rank.

This is not a categorical verdict on an RH-equivalent criterion. It is a positive implementation
law: every route must identify its infinite arithmetic carrier and prove that completion preserves
it.

At prime cutoff `N`, retaining every phase `log p` for `p<=N` forces carrier dimension at least
`pi(N)`. A `1/log N` radial crossover profile changes metric overlap but not this character-rank
lower bound. Thus any cofinal finite-prime realization must transport diverging phase dimension
through the archimedean boundary layer.

## 10. Attacks and exact boundaries

1. **Individual shifted Gamma independence is not assumed.** Integer-related shifts and
   multiplication-formula identities do not damage the proof because only the product shift
   equation `(10)` is used.
2. **Positive pole mass is load-bearing.** Allowing negative Gamma multiplicities can cancel the
   order-two shift-orbit mass in `(15)`. Ratios of Gamma products require a separate classification;
   the theorem is for genuine finite archimedean local factors with positive multiplicities.
3. **Commensurability is load-bearing.** Products with incommensurable positive scales do not satisfy
   one common polynomial shift equation. Standard finite-degree Langlands factors are
   commensurable after expressing them through `Gamma_R`; arbitrary extended-Selberg-class scales
   need a separate multi-valuation theorem.
4. **Real exponential normalization is load-bearing only for decay.** A complex `alpha` can change
   the upper-boundary orientation. Standard conductors give real `alpha`.
5. **Compact-open normality is insufficient.** The recurrence theorem requires Bohr uniform
   convergence on whole closed substrips, not merely Montel compactness on compact subsets.
6. **Meromorphic recurrent outputs are not included.** Moving poles require a spherical hull and a
   chart-independent derivative/divisor argument.
7. **Finite phase rank is not finite Fourier support.** Autonomous DA functions may have infinite
   support inside a finitely generated frequency group.
8. **Completion alone is not an RH locator.** The theorem identifies carrier provenance, not the
   transverse position of zeros.
