## Theorem

Let `m>=1`, `0<=k<m`, and let `p` be a rational prime. Define the Gauss component

```text
gamma_(m,k)(z)=Gamma(z/(2m)+k/m),
phi_(m,k)(z)=gamma_(m,k)(z)/gamma_(m,k)(1-z),
rho_infinity^(m,k)(z)
 =(pi/m)^(1/(2m)-z/m) phi_(m,k)(z).
```

Let `P` be the Hardy projection and put

```text
H_(m,k,p)=(1-P) M_(rho_infinity^(m,k) rho_p) P.
```

Then `|H_(m,k,p)|^(2m)` belongs to `L^(1,infinity)`, is measurable in the sense of
Connes, and

```text
Tr_omega(|H_(m,k,p)|^(2m))
 = 2 (p-1)^(2m) (p^m-1)^2 / (p^(2m) m^2 log p).    (A)
```

In particular, the critical residue is independent of the Gauss-component index `k`.

For `m=1`, formula (A) is

```text
2 (p-1)^4/(p^2 log p).
```

## 1. Source reduction

The proof of the one-prime decomposition in Connes--Consani applies with
`rho_infinity` replaced by `rho_infinity^(m,k)`. Its only non-trace-class term is

```text
E_(m,k,p)=(1-p)/p W_- C_(m,k,p) W_+*,
C_(m,k,p)=B_p D_(m,k,p) I B_p,
```

where `W_+` and `W_-` are isometries, `I e_n=e_(-n)`, and

```text
D_(m,k,p)e_0=0,
D_(m,k,p)e_n=d_n e_n,
d_n=rho_infinity^(m,k)(2 pi i n/log p).
```

Under Fourier series, `B_p` is multiplication by

```text
b_p(x)=p^((1-x)/2),  0<=x<1.
```

The omitted terms are rapidly smoothing or finite rank. Hence their contribution to the
`2m`-th positive power differs from that of `E_(m,k,p)` by a trace-class operator.

Write `L=log p`, `B=B_p`, `D=D_(m,k,p)`, and `C=C_(m,k,p)`. As in the `m=1`
case,

```text
G=C^2=p B D B^(-2) D* B.                            (1)
```

Thus `|C|^(2m)=G^m`.

## 2. Universal magnitude and phase

Stirling's formula applied to the two gamma factors gives, uniformly for fixed `m,k`,

```text
|rho_infinity^(m,k)(it)|
 =(2 pi/|t|)^(1/(2m)) (1+O(1/|t|)),                (2)
```

and

```text
arg rho_infinity^(m,k)(it)
 =(t/m)(log(t/(2 pi))-1)+c_(m,k)+O(1/t).           (3)
```

The leading terms are independent of `k`. At `t=2 pi n/L`, equations (2)--(3) become

```text
|d_n|^(2m)=L/|n| (1+o(1)),                          (4)

conj(d_(n+j))/conj(d_n)
 =exp(-2 pi i j delta_n)+o(1)                      (5)
```

for every fixed integer `j`, where

```text
delta_n=log(n/L)/(m L) mod 1.                       (6)
```

They also give the global bounds needed below:

```text
|d_n|^2 <= C (1+|n|)^(-1/m),
|d_n|^(-2) <= C |n|^(1/m).                         (7)
```

## 3. Moving-translation lemma

The Fourier coefficients of `b=b_p` satisfy

```text
b_hat(j)=(sqrt(p)-1)/(L/2+2 pi i j),
|b_hat(j)|<=C/(1+|j|).                              (8)
```

Equations (5), (7), and (8) imply

```text
(conj(d_n))^(-1) e_(-n) D* B e_n
 - b(.-delta_n) -> 0 in L^2(R/Z).                  (9)
```

For completeness, after fixing the modes `|j|<=J`, the squared tail is bounded by

```text
C n^(1/m) sum_(|j|>J) 1/(1+j^2)(1+|n+j|)^(-1/m).
```

On `|n+j|>=n/2` this is `O(1/J)`. On `0<|n+j|<n/2`, it is `O(1/n)` for
`m>1` and `O(log(n)/n)` for `m=1`. This proves (9), and the reality relation
`d_(-n)=conj(d_n)` proves the same statement at negative frequencies.

The same proof is uniform for bounded-variation amplitudes with uniformly bounded variation.
The amplitudes generated by finitely many products of translates of `b` and `b^(-1)` form such
a family.

## 4. Iterated principal action

Put `s_p(x)=b_p(x)^2=p^(1-x)` and

```text
a_delta(x)=s_p(x)/s_p(x+delta).
```

Apply (9) first to `D*` and its conjugate version to `D`. Equation (1) gives, for every
bounded-variation amplitude `f`,

```text
G(e_n f)
 =p |d_n|^2 e_n (a_(delta_n) f+o_(L^2)(1)).        (10)
```

The uniform bounded-variation form of the lemma permits iteration a fixed number of times.
Therefore

```text
<G^m e_(+/-n),e_(+/-n)>
 =p^m |d_n|^(2m)
   (integral_0^1 a_(delta_n)(x)^m dx+o(1)).         (11)
```

## 5. Exact two-variable average

The function

```text
A_m(delta)=integral_0^1 a_delta(x)^m dx
```

is continuous and one-periodic. Haar invariance and Fubini give

```text
integral_0^1 A_m(delta) ddelta
 =(integral_0^1 s_p(x)^m dx)
  (integral_0^1 s_p(x)^(-m) dx)
 =(p^m-1)^2/(p^m m^2 L^2).                         (12)
```

Harmonic logarithmic rotation gives, for every continuous one-periodic `f`,

```text
(1/log N) sum_(n<=N) f(delta_n)/n
 -> integral_0^1 f(delta) ddelta.                  (13)
```

This follows by trigonometric approximation: every nonzero Fourier mode reduces to
`sum_(n<=N)n^(-1+i alpha)=O(1)`.

Combining (4), (11), (12), and (13) yields the Fourier-diagonal limit

```text
lim_(N->infinity) (1/log N)
 sum_(0<|n|<=N) <G^m e_n,e_n>
 =2 (p^m-1)^2/(m^2 L).                             (14)
```

## 6. Weak modulation and measurability

Let `V_0=(1+|N|)^(-1)`. Equation (7) gives `|D| in L^(2m,infinity)`. Choose a
real exponent `r>2m` and put `u=1/r`. Every multiplication operator occurring in
the factorization of `G^m` is piecewise smooth with finitely many jumps, hence is bounded on
`H^u(R/Z)` because `u<1/2`. Diagonal phases commute with `V_0`.

Move `V_0^(-u)` leftward through those bounded factors. The resulting factorization has
`2m-1` copies of `|D| in L^(2m,infinity)` and one copy of

```text
|D| V_0^(-u) in L^(q,infinity),
1/q=1/(2m)-1/r.
```

Weak ideal Holder therefore gives

```text
G^m V_0^(-1/r) in L^(r/(r-1),infinity).
```

Thus `G^m` is weakly `V_0`-modulated. The eigenvalue comparison theorem for weakly
modulated operators turns (14) into

```text
lim_(N->infinity) (1/log N) sum_(j<=N) mu_j(G^m)
 =2 (p^m-1)^2/(m^2 L).                             (15)
```

This proves membership and measurability.

## 7. Restore the local-factor coefficient

The isometric source reduction gives

```text
|E_(m,k,p)|^(2m)
 ~ ((p-1)/p)^(2m) G^m
```

up to zero summands. The rapidly smoothing and finite-rank remainder changes the positive
`2m`-th power only by a trace-class operator. Multiplying (15) by `((p-1)/p)^(2m)` proves
formula (A).

## Exact scope

`[PROVED ON PAPER]` The exact critical weak-Schatten residue exists for every Gauss component,
not merely an upper order estimate.

`[PROVED ON PAPER]` The residue is independent of `k`, although the Gauss components themselves
are different functions.

`[NOT PROVED]` Formula (A) is for a single paired factor
`rho_infinity^(m,k) rho_p`, not yet the full product over `m` primes.

`[NOT PROVED]` It gives no zero exclusion or verified-range increase by itself.
