# Power canonical products have an exact moment-Borel power-lattice law

- **ID:** `power-canonical-product-exact-moment-borel-lattice-law`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM / positive survivor of a failed hypertranscendence route
- **Source:** the Barnes tail of `W_s`, the Riemann zeta functional equation, and
  the nonlinear-ODE resurgence audit
- **Statement:** The exact order-`s` moment-Borel transform of the inverse-power
  asymptotic tail of the logarithmic derivative of `W_s` is a normally convergent
  meromorphic Lambert sum.  Unless `s` is an even integer, its poles are precisely
  two power lattices.  Newton-compatible ramification straightens each power lattice
  to the ordinary lattice `2 pi N` and makes every residue constant.

## 1. Setup

For `s>0`, put

```text
g(s)=floor(1/s),
E_g(w)=(1-w) exp(sum_(r=1)^g w^r/r),
W_s(z)=product_(n>=1) E_g(-z/n^s),
H_s(x)=x W_s'(x)/W_s(x).
```

The Barnes expansion on the positive real axis has the form

```text
H_s(x)
 = (pi/s)csc(pi/s)x^(1/s)
   + sum_(j=1)^g (-1)^j zeta(sj)x^j
   - 1/2 + R_s(x),

R_s(x) ~ sum_(k>=1) b_k x^(-k),
b_k=(-1)^k zeta(-sk).                                      (1)
```

Only the formal tail is needed below.  Define its order-`s` moment-Borel transform by

```text
B_s(xi)=sum_(k>=1) b_k xi^k/Gamma(1+sk).                    (2)
```

This normalization is forced by the exact Gevrey order proved in
`irrational-power-canonical-products-hypertranscendental-by-gevrey-slope-rigidity.md`.

## 2. Exact transform

Set

```text
alpha=pi s/2,
A_n=(2 pi n)^s,
u_n=xi/A_n.
```

The zeta functional equation gives, for every positive integer `k`,

```text
zeta(-sk)/Gamma(1+sk)
 = -2 sin(k alpha) zeta(1+sk)/(2 pi)^(1+sk).
```

Consequently

```text
b_k/Gamma(1+sk)
 = (-1)^(k+1) sin(k alpha) zeta(1+sk)
   / (pi (2 pi)^(sk)).                                      (3)
```

For `|xi|<(2 pi)^s`, expand `zeta(1+sk)` by its absolutely convergent
Dirichlet series and interchange the two sums.  The elementary identity

```text
sum_(k>=1) (-1)^(k+1) sin(k alpha) u^k
 = u sin(alpha)/(1+2u cos(alpha)+u^2)                       (4)
```

then yields

```text
B_s(xi)
 = sin(alpha)/pi
   sum_(n>=1) (1/n) u_n/(1+2u_n cos(alpha)+u_n^2).           (5)
```

For `xi` in a fixed compact set, the `n`th term in `(5)` is
`O(n^(-1-s))`, uniformly after finitely many terms are removed.  Thus `(5)`
converges normally away from its displayed poles and meromorphically continues
`B_s` to the whole `xi`-plane.

## 3. Pole and residue classification

If `s` is an even integer, `sin(k alpha)=0` for every `k`; hence every `b_k`
vanishes and

```text
B_s=0.                                                       (6)
```

Suppose now that `s` is not an even integer.  The two roots of the denominator
in `(5)` are `-exp(i alpha)` and `-exp(-i alpha)`.  Distinct `n` have distinct
pole moduli.  Hence all poles are simple and are exactly

```text
xi_(n,+)=-A_n exp(i alpha),
xi_(n,-)=-A_n exp(-i alpha),       n>=1.                     (7)
```

Direct differentiation of the quadratic denominator gives

```text
Res_(xi=xi_(n,+)) B_s
  = A_n exp(i alpha)/(2 pi i n),

Res_(xi=xi_(n,-)) B_s
  = -A_n exp(-i alpha)/(2 pi i n).                           (8)
```

Now pull back each singular ray separately:

```text
xi=-exp(i alpha) eta^s       on the `+` ray,
xi=-exp(-i alpha) eta^s      on the `-` ray.                 (9)
```

At the positive points `eta_n=2 pi n`, the change-of-variable residue formula
and `(8)` give

```text
Res_(eta=2 pi n) B_s(-exp(i alpha)eta^s)  =  i/s,
Res_(eta=2 pi n) B_s(-exp(-i alpha)eta^s) = -i/s.            (10)
```

Thus both the locations and the residues become independent of `n`: the two
power lattices are ramified copies of the ordinary arithmetic lattice.

For reduced rational `s=p/q`, the relations in `(9)` are algebraic:

```text
(xi/(-exp(+-i alpha)))^q=eta^p.                              (11)
```

They are therefore exactly the finite ramification allowed by Newton-polygon
reduction of an analytic algebraic ODE.

## 4. The failed Borel-rank obstruction

A tempting argument for reduced `s=p/q` with `q>1` was:

1. the pole radii `(2 pi n)^(p/q)` appear to have infinite rational rank;
2. Borel singularities of a differentially algebraic formal solution should be
   generated by finitely many characteristic actions;
3. therefore `W_(p/q)` should be hypertranscendental.

The argument is invalid.  Kamimoto's resurgence theorem applies to a normalized
rank-one nonlinear system and places ordinary Borel singularities in the additive
closure of finitely many eigenvalues.  General algebraic ODEs first undergo the
Newton ramification and acceleration attached to their rational slopes.  Formula
`(11)` is precisely such a ramification, and `(10)` shows that the allegedly
infinite-rank pole set becomes two ordinary rank-one lattices in the normalized
coordinate.

Therefore rational rank of locations in the unnormalized moment-Borel plane is not
an invariant of differential algebraicity.  Multisummability or resurgence cannot
be used to contradict `W_(p/q)` on this basis.

## 5. Strongest positive survivor

The exact survivor is stronger than the negative audit:

> The Barnes zeta tail is not merely Gevrey-`s`.  Its natural moment-Borel transform
> is globally meromorphic, has exactly two simple power lattices, and each lattice
> has constant alien residue after its Newton-compatible ramification.

This explains simultaneously:

- the complete disappearance of the tail for even integer `s`;
- the rational Newton compatibility of every nonintegral rational `s`;
- why the irrational Gevrey-slope theorem proves hypertranscendence while the first
  resurgent singular layer supplies no further obstruction in the rational case.

It does **not** prove hypertranscendence for reduced `p/q` with even `p` and `q>1`.
Those cases remain the exact real-parameter open core.
