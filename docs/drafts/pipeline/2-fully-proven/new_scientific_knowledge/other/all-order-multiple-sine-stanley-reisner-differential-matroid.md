## Setup

Let `V={1,...,N}` and, for every nonempty `A subset V`, write

```text
S_A(z)=S_|A|(z;(omega_i)_(i in A)),
L_A(z)=d/dz log S_A(z),
T_i f(z)=f(z+omega_i),
x_i=T_i-1.
```

Put

```text
S=C[delta],
R_N=S[t_1^(+-1),...,t_N^(+-1)],
x_i=t_i-1,
I_(N-1)=(x_J : J subset V, |J|=N-1).
```

The standard Barnes-zeta recurrence gives the multiple-sine equation

```text
S_A(z+omega_j)=S_A(z)/S_(A\{j})(z),   j in A,          (1)
```

up to the reciprocal convention.  Logarithmic differentiation gives

```text
(T_j-1)L_A=-L_(A\{j}).                                  (2)
```

Only the harmless global sign changes under the other convention.

For `|A|=1`,

```text
S_A(z)=2 sin(pi z/omega_i),
L_A(z)=(pi/omega_i) cot(pi z/omega_i),                  (3)
```

so the order-one boundary is differentially algebraic.

## Face coordinates

For `J subset V`, define

```text
F_J=x_J L_V.
```

Iterating (2) gives, modulo the convention sign,

```text
F_J=(-1)^|J| L_(V\J).                                   (4)
```

Hence the non-DA faces are precisely

```text
Delta={J subset V : |J|<=N-2}.                          (5)
```

If `|J|=N-1`, equation (4) is the cotangent boundary (3), so every squarefree degree-`N-1`
monomial belongs to the DA annihilator:

```text
I_(N-1) subset Ann_DA(L_V).                             (6)
```

The face complex (5) is the `(N-3)`-skeleton of the `(N-1)`-simplex.  Its maximal faces have
cardinality `N-2`, and its minimal nonfaces have cardinality `N-1`.

## Atomic maximal faces

For a maximal face `J`, the complement is a pair

```text
V\J={i,j},
F_J=+/- L_{i,j}.
```

Its external structural orbit is

```text
O_J={T_J^a F_J : a in Z^J}.                              (7)
```

The DA period subgroup of `L_{i,j}` is

```text
H_ij=Z omega_i+Z omega_j.
```

Two external shifts `sum_(k in J)a_k omega_k` and `sum_(k in J)b_k omega_k` have the same class
modulo `H_ij` only if `a=b`, by rational independence of all `omega_k`.  The parent
double-sine defect-coset theorem and its full-orbit consequence therefore make every `O_J`
differentially algebraically independent.

If `q notin J`, then `q in {i,j}`.  Equation (2) gives

```text
(T_q-1)F_J=+/- L_{({i,j}\{q})},                          (8)
```

which is DA.  Finite telescoping shows that every `T_q^nY-Y`, with `Y in O_J`, is DA.  Thus each
maximal orbit is free in its external directions and DA-stationary in every off-face direction.

The nested-star/free-block argument now glues all maximal `O_J` jointly: inside a moving star one
external direction replicates arbitrarily many independent blocks, while every maximal face
outside that star is DA-stationary in that direction.  A minimal differential circuit would
therefore violate the free-block replication inequality.  Hence the union of all maximal-face
orbits is jointly differentially algebraically independent.

## Descent through all face layers

Let `D` be the differential field of DA meromorphic functions.  It is stable under every
translation, and it is differential algebraic over `C(z)`.

Descend through nonmaximal faces in decreasing cardinality.  Suppose all deeper face orbits have
already been adjoined freely and fix a current face `J`.  Since `|J|<=N-3`, choose distinct

```text
q,u in V\J
```

with both `J union {q}` and `J union {u}` in `Delta`.  The orbit `O_J` satisfies the compatible
primitive equations

```text
(T_q-1)T_J^aF_J=T_J^aF_(J union {q}),
(T_u-1)T_J^aF_J=T_J^aF_(J union {u}).                   (9)
```

The joint fixed meromorphic field of `T_q,T_u` is `C`: the irrational ratio
`omega_q/omega_u` makes `Z omega_q+Z omega_u` dense in the real line.

A hypothetical nonlinear differential dependence in a finite block of `O_J` therefore triggers
the simultaneous additive telescoping theorem.  It produces one nonzero constant-coefficient
differential-linear form and one element `G` of the deeper field satisfying both coboundary
equations.  Remove the target orbit `O_(J union {q})` from the deeper field.  The remainder is
`T_q`-stable, while the target is a free differential-rational shift orbit.  The right side of the
first coboundary equation occupies one nonzero `q`-slice of that free orbit.  The denominator-orbit
and top-degree argument for a rational function in free differential indeterminates says that such
a one-slice term has no rational `T_q`-primitive.  This contradiction makes `O_J` free over every
deeper layer.

Nested-star gluing within each cardinality layer and downward induction reach `J=empty`.  Thus

```text
union_(J in Delta) O_J                                  (10)
```

is differentially algebraically independent over `D`, hence also over `C(z)`.

## Reflection removes one face layer

For the Barnes multiple-gamma logarithmic derivative with the same `N` periods, the parent theorem
gives

```text
Ann_DA(Psi_N)=(x_1...x_N).                               (14)
```

Its face complex contains every proper subset of `V`; the only minimal nonface is `V` itself.
The multiple sine is the reflected quotient

```text
S_N(z)=Gamma_N(z)^(-1) Gamma_N(|omega|-z)^((-1)^N).
```

Equations (12) and (14) identify the induced structural quotient as the canonical surjection

```text
R_N/(x_1...x_N) -> R_N/I_(N-1).                          (15)
```

The kernel is `I_(N-1)/(x_1...x_N)`: exactly the span of the maximal proper faces.  Thus reflection
does not merely impose a parity grading and does not collapse every order to one class.  At every
`N`, it removes precisely one complete face layer.

For `N=2`, `I_1=(x_1,x_2)`, so (15) is the rank-one collapse proved in the double-sine parent.  For
`N=3`, the surviving quotient is

```text
R_3/(x_1x_2,x_1x_3,x_2x_3),
```

with a common top class and three free one-axis face families.  Higher orders continue the same
Stanley--Reisner pattern.

## Attacks and boundaries

1. **Atomic parent.** The all-order conclusion depends on the positive-real double-sine
   defect-coset theorem.  Ordinary HT of one `S_2` is insufficient.
2. **Rational periods.** For a commensurable period vector, Kurokawa--Wakayama prove DA and the
   claimed free matroid collapses.  Rational independence is structural, not cosmetic.
3. **Non-real period ratios.** Kato proves HT when a ratio is non-real, but that does not supply the
   positive-real defect matroid needed for the maximal-face orbits here.
4. **Constant differential coefficients.** The operator ring is `C[delta]`.  DA function
   coefficients require a different summability problem.
5. **Structural translations only.** Arbitrary translations are classified at the bottom
   double-sine faces, but the displayed all-order quotient concerns the structural period lattice.
6. **Formalization boundary.** The abstract face-complex layer is now complete at the gluing
   interface: `Rank.uniformFace_nestedStar_indep` handles arbitrary uniform face families, the

### Toward the atomic parent: the exact multiple-sine difference equation

The independence half needs the double-sine atomic theorem, whose hypothesis is the **exact**
difference equation `(T_1 - 1) L_2 = ε_1 a_2` with a cotangent right-hand side — not the
DA-modulo form.  The following are now Lean theorems, and they reduce that hypothesis to an
explicit rational normalization.

`HTGeometry.BarnesLadderDefectPolePart`

- `barnesSource_ladder_defect_eq` — the Barnes ladder defect **is** the embedded rational function
  `N_j/(X + ω_j)`, not merely a DA element.
- `barnesLadderNumerator_eval_neg_period` — the numerator is `-1` at `z = -ω_j`, so
- `barnesLadderRatio_eq` — `N_j/(X + ω_j) = P_j - 1/(X + ω_j)`: the entire pole part of the ladder
  defect is one simple pole at `-ω_j` with residue `-1`.

`HTGeometry.PolynomialShiftDifference`

- `fallPoly`, `shiftDiff_fallPoly` — the falling-factorial basis of a step, with
  `Δ_a F_{k+1} = (k+1) a F_k`.
- `surjective_shiftDiffPoly`, `exists_shiftDiff_eq` — **discrete integration**: for a nonzero step
  every polynomial is a shift difference of a polynomial.

`HTGeometry.MultipleSineLadderRationalDefect`

- `subframe_ladder_defect_eq`, `multipleSine_ladder_defect_eq` — equation `(2)` with its **exact**
  rational defect `R_k + ε_S · R_k(c_{S∖k} - ·)`.  The two summands have their poles at `-ω_k` and
  at `c_{S∖k} + ω_k`, one period apart, with opposite residues.

`HTGeometry.MultipleSineOrderOneSourceExact`

- `frameSourceMero_singleton_eq` — the order-one source **is** `periodCotMero` plus the explicit
  rational function `(2z - ω)/(z(ω - z))`.
- `addTranslationMeroEquiv_periodCotMero` — the period cotangent is invariant under its own period,
  i.e. `(T_i - 1) L_{i} = 0`.

`HTGeometry.MultipleSineRationalNormalization`

- `linInv`, `ratShiftBy_linInv`, `ratReflectBy_linInv` — simple poles in the coordinate field and
  how the translation and the reflection move them.
- `orderOneCorrection_eq`, `subframeLadderRat_eq` — both rational data in partial fractions: two
  simple poles at `0, ω_j`, and one simple pole at `-ω_k`.
- `normalizationRat c = 1/X - 1/(X - c)` and `multipleSine_two_normalized_defect` — **the
  normalized two-period defect is a polynomial**: the two shift-difference pole pairs of `ρ` are
  exactly the two pairs of the accumulated defect, so every pole cancels and the antisymmetrized

`HTGeometry.MultipleSineNormalizedSource`

- `addTranslationAnalyticEquiv_comm`, `addTranslationMeroEquiv_comm` — translations commute on the
  meromorphic field.
- `normalizedSource_shiftDiff` — `(T_k - 1)(L_S + ρ) = -(π/ω_j) cot(π z/ω_j) + Q_k` with `Q_k` a
  polynomial.

`HTGeometry.DoubleSineExactDifference` — **the exact double-sine difference equation**.

- `exists_exact_doubleSine_source` — for a two-period frame there is a rational correction `r` such
  that

  ```text
  (T_k - 1) (L_S + r) = -(π/ω_j) cot(π z/ω_j),
  (T_j - 1) (L_S + r) = -(π/ω_k) cot(π z/ω_k) + b,
  ```

  with `b` a constant.  Discrete integration kills the polynomial in the first direction; in the
  second the remainder is a polynomial annihilated by the first shift difference — because the
  translations commute and the cotangent of a period is invariant under that period — hence a
  constant, which is exactly what the cotangent-model character obstruction tolerates.

`HTGeometry.PeriodCotangentCylinderForm` — **the increment in cylinder coordinates**.

- `exists_doubleSine_source_imaginaryRay` — for periods `i a`, `i b` with `a, b > 0` (which satisfy

`HTGeometry.DoubleSineIncrementBaseForm` — **the increments inside the base field**.
