are incongruent modulo `Z`, and Wang--Yao applies to every finite subfamily. `QED`

There is a stronger all-jet version.  Let `F` be the field of one-periodic meromorphic functions.

> **Prime-log Gamma differential-coordinate law.** The family
> `{Gamma(s+lambda):lambda in Lambda}` is differentially algebraically independent over `F(s)`.
> Hence it is differentially algebraically independent over `C(s)`, and
> `K_(Gamma,Lambda)` is a differential rational function field on differential coordinates indexed
> by `Lambda`.

```text
Gamma(s+lambda+1)=(s+lambda) Gamma(s+lambda).               (10)
```

For a finite set `E subset Lambda`, differential dependence over `F(s)` would give a nonzero
constant-coefficient homogeneous linear differential polynomial `L` such that

```text
L((s+lambda)^(-1):lambda in E)=g(s+1)-g(s),   g in C(s).    (11)
```

Every derivative of `(s+lambda)^(-1)` has its sole pole at `-lambda`.  Distinct `lambda` lie in
distinct pole orbits modulo `Z`.  At the highest pole order occurring in `L`, the discrete residue
on each orbit is therefore one nonzero scalar coefficient.  A rational difference
`g(s+1)-g(s)` has zero discrete residue at every orbit and every pole order.  All highest-order
coefficients must vanish; descending on pole order makes `L=0`, a contradiction. `QED`

For the fourth relation, enumerate a finite set of distinct shifts as
`lambda_1,...,lambda_r`.  For an exponent vector `e in Z^r`, define its signed moments

```text
M_k(e)=sum_(i=1)^r e_i lambda_i^k,   0<=k<r.               (14)
```

> **Finite Stirling-signature separation.** The map
> `e |-> (M_0(e),...,M_(r-1)(e))` is injective.  Consequently two distinct Laurent monomials
> `product_i Gamma(s+lambda_i)^(e_i)` have distinct complete formal Stirling signatures.

top term is `lambda_i^k`; the triangular Bernoulli-to-power change of coordinates therefore turns
equality of complete signatures into equality of the moments (14). `QED`
