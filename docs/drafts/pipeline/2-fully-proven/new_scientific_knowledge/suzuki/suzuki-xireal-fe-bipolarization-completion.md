## 2. Bipolarization theorem

Let `V=R^2`, write `x=(x1,x2)`, and put

```text
Omega_theta(x,y)=theta(x1 y2-x2 y1),
J(x1,x2)=(-x2,x1),
R(x1,x2)=(-x1,x2).                                    (2)
```

The Xi-real and FE twists of the same weld area are

```text
P_theta(x,y)=Omega_theta(x,Jy)
            =theta(x1 y1+x2 y2),
K_theta(x,y)=Omega_theta(x,Ry)
            =theta(x1 y2+x2 y1).                      (3)
```

Define their half-sum and half-difference

```text
Q+_theta=(P_theta+K_theta)/2,
Q-_theta=(P_theta-K_theta)/2.                          (4)
```

Then, exactly,

```text
Q+_theta(x,y)=theta/2 (x1+x2)(y1+y2),
Q-_theta(x,y)=theta/2 (x1-x2)(y1-y2),                 (5)
P_theta=Q+_theta+Q-_theta,
K_theta=Q+_theta-Q-_theta.                            (6)
```

For `theta>=0`, both `Q+_theta` and `Q-_theta` are positive semidefinite rank-at-most-one forms.
For `theta>0`, their kernels are the negative and positive light lines respectively.  Therefore

```text
K_theta+Q-_theta=Q+_theta>=0.                          (7)
```

This is precisely the local law demanded abstractly by S43:

```text
Krein block + negative square = positive square.       (8)
```
