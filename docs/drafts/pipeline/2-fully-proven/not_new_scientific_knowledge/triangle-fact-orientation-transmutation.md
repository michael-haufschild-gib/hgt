## Result

Let \(V\) be an unlabeled three-element set and let
\[
P=\mathbb Z[V]
\]
be its permutation module. Define
\[
C_1=\bigwedge^2P,\qquad C_2=\bigwedge^3P
\]
with the usual augmented simplex boundary
\[
0\longrightarrow C_2
\xrightarrow{\partial_2}
C_1
\xrightarrow{\partial_1}
P
\xrightarrow{\varepsilon}
\mathbb Z
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{1}
\]
Then:

1. `(1)` is an exact \(S_3=\operatorname{Aut}(V)\)-equivariant sequence with ranks
   \[
   1\longrightarrow3\longrightarrow3\longrightarrow1.
   \tag{2}
   \]
2. For the empty triangle, obtained by deleting \(C_2\), the unique loop-disagreement is
   \[
   H_1(\partial\Delta^2;\mathbb Z)
   =\ker\partial_1
   \cong\mathbb Z_{\mathrm{sgn}}.
   \tag{3}
   \]
   Every vertex permutation acts on this rank-one group by its sign.
3. Consequently
   \[
   H_1(\partial\Delta^2;\mathbb Z)^{S_3}=0.
   \tag{4}
   \]
   An unlabeled triangle has no automorphism-invariant nonzero loop generator and hence no
   source-natural choice of orientation.
4. The two-element set of primitive generators
   \[
   \operatorname{Or}(V)=\{z,-z\}
   \tag{5}
   \]
   is nevertheless canonical. It is an \(S_3\)-equivariant orientation torsor.
5. The missing filler is source-natural only as a new transforming **type**:
   \[
   C_2\cong\mathbb Z_{\mathrm{sgn}}
   \xrightarrow[\cong]{\partial_2}
   \ker\partial_1.
   \tag{6}
   \]
   No orientation generator is chosen in `(6)`.

Over a characteristic-zero field \(F\), write
\[
P_F\cong\mathbf1\oplus\mathrm{Std}.
\]
Then
\[
\bigwedge^2P_F\cong\mathrm{Std}\oplus\mathrm{Sgn},
\tag{7}
\]
and `(1)` becomes
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathrm{Sgn}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{Std}\oplus\mathrm{Sgn}
\longrightarrow\mathbf1\oplus\mathrm{Std}
\longrightarrow\mathbf1
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{8}
\]
The common standard representation cancels through the differential. The two one-dimensional
endpoints do not have the same transformation law:

```text
terminal public fact       = trivial representation;
irreducible disagreement  = sign/orientation representation.
```

Thus exact self-witnessing does not return a second scalar fact. It transmutes a fact into an
orientation line.

## Proof

Choose a temporary ordering \(V=\{0,1,2\}\) only to write the maps. Let \(e_i\) be the corresponding
basis of \(P\). Put
\[
\varepsilon(e_i)=1,
\qquad
\partial_1(e_i\wedge e_j)=e_j-e_i,
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
\partial_2(e_0\wedge e_1\wedge e_2)
=e_1\wedge e_2-e_0\wedge e_2+e_0\wedge e_1.
\tag{10}
\]
Direct substitution gives
\[
\partial_1\partial_2(e_0\wedge e_1\wedge e_2)
=(e_2-e_1)-(e_2-e_0)+(e_1-e_0)=0
\tag{11}
\]
and \(\varepsilon\partial_1=0\).

The augmentation kernel is generated by \(e_1-e_0\) and \(e_2-e_0\). These are respectively
\(\partial_1(e_0\wedge e_1)\) and \(\partial_1(e_0\wedge e_2)\), so
\[
\operatorname{im}\partial_1=\ker\varepsilon.
\tag{12}
\]
If
\[
x=a(e_0\wedge e_1)+b(e_0\wedge e_2)+c(e_1\wedge e_2),
\]
then
\[
\partial_1x
=(-a-b)e_0+(a-c)e_1+(b+c)e_2.
\tag{13}
\]
The equation \(\partial_1x=0\) is therefore equivalent to
\[
b=-a,\qquad c=a.
\tag{14}
\]
Hence
\[
\ker\partial_1
=\mathbb Z\,
\bigl(e_0\wedge e_1-e_0\wedge e_2+e_1\wedge e_2\bigr)
=\operatorname{im}\partial_2.
\tag{15}
\]
The displayed generator is primitive, so \(\partial_2\) is injective. This proves exactness.

Every permutation of \(V\) acts on \(P\) by permuting the \(e_i\), hence on its exterior powers.
The definitions of exterior boundary and augmentation commute with that action, so `(1)` is
\(S_3\)-equivariant without depending on the temporary ordering.

Put
\[
t=e_0\wedge e_1\wedge e_2,\qquad z=\partial_2t.
\]
For every \(\sigma\in S_3\),
\[
\sigma t=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)t,
\]
and equivariance gives
\[
\sigma z
=\partial_2(\sigma t)
=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)z.
\tag{16}
\]
Deleting \(C_2\) leaves the empty triangle, so its first homology is
\(\ker\partial_1=\mathbb Zz\). Equation `(16)` proves `(3)`.

If \(x\in\mathbb Zz\) is fixed by every automorphism, it is fixed by a transposition \(\tau\).
But `(16)` gives \(\tau x=-x\), hence \(2x=0\). The group \(\mathbb Zz\) is torsion-free, so
\(x=0\). This proves `(4)`.

The primitive elements of \(\mathbb Zz\) are exactly \(z\) and \(-z\). Odd permutations exchange
them and even permutations preserve them, proving `(5)`. Equation `(6)` is the already proved
equivariant isomorphism from \(C_2\) to \(\ker\partial_1\).

Finally, over characteristic zero,
\[
P_F=\mathbf1\oplus\mathrm{Std}
\]
and exterior algebra gives
\[
\bigwedge^2P_F
\cong
\bigwedge^2\mathrm{Std}\oplus\mathrm{Std}.
\]
The determinant of the two-dimensional standard representation is the sign character, so
\(\bigwedge^2\mathrm{Std}\cong\mathrm{Sgn}\). Also
\(\bigwedge^3P_F\cong\mathrm{Sgn}\). This proves `(7)`--`(8)`. \(\square\)

## Exact no-go

Call a choice from an unlabeled source **source-natural** when every source automorphism fixes it.
Then `(4)` proves:

> **Static self-witness no-selection theorem.** An unlabeled empty triangle cannot source-naturally
> select a nonzero scalar loop generator, a clockwise direction, or an absolute temporal arrow.

Any construction that writes one preferred cycle
\[
e_0\wedge e_1-e_0\wedge e_2+e_1\wedge e_2
\]
and calls it physically canonical has silently imported a vertex ordering. That ordering is an
outside frame.

The characteristic-two boundary is exact and important: there \(-1=1\), so the sign and trivial
characters coincide. The orientation-transmutation distinction is erased. No characteristic-two
argument may be used to claim the characteristic-zero physical result.

## Strongest positive survivor

The no-go does not force an external symmetry breaking. It identifies a canonical source-only
repair:

> **Promote the ambiguity, not one of its values.**

The unlabeled source generates the orientation module and its equivariant boundary map. It does
not generate a preferred basis vector of that module. The filler is canonical as a typed

This is the first exact instance of the candidate no-outside rule:

> **No-outside equivariant promotion law.** When a closed source generates a residual class but
> its automorphisms forbid every nonzero invariant representative, the residual can enter the
> successor law only as its full equivariant type, never as an absolutely chosen value.

The theorem proves this law only for the triangle. Its general physical universality is a
conjecture.
