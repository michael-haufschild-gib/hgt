## Set-up and normalized lift

Let `X in P^(N-1)` be differential-generic over algebraically closed constants `C`, with projective
differential field `F`. Choose a homogeneous lift `v` and put

```text
Omega=det(v,delta v,...,delta^(N-1)v).                     (1)
```

Differential genericity gives `Omega != 0`. In the algebraic extension obtained by adjoining an
`N`-th root of `Omega^(-1)`, rescale `v` to a lift `v_tilde` satisfying

```text
det(v_tilde,delta v_tilde,...,delta^(N-1)v_tilde)=1.       (2)
```

There are unique coefficients `q_0,...,q_(N-2)` such that

```text
delta^N v_tilde=sum_(j=0)^(N-2) q_j delta^j v_tilde.      (3)
```

The missing `delta^(N-1)v_tilde` coefficient is zero because differentiating `(2)` makes it the
logarithmic derivative of the normalized Wronskian.

## Theorem

Let

```text
I=C<q_0,...,q_(N-2)>.                                     (4)
```

Then:

1. the `q_j` are projective differential invariants and are jointly differentially algebraically
   independent over `C`;
2. `I=F^(PGL_N(C))`, the full projective differential-invariant field;
3. the frame matrix

   ```text
   M=[v_tilde,delta v_tilde,...,delta^(N-1)v_tilde] in SL_N
   ```

   is a fundamental matrix for the companion first-order system determined by `(3)`;
4. the normalized-lift field over `I` is Picard--Vessiot with group `SL_N(C)`;
5. its center-fixed projective field is `F`, so `F/I` is the corresponding projective
   Picard--Vessiot torsor with group `PGL_N(C)`;
6. the deepest common projective horizon is

   ```text
   intersection_H mathcal H_H=I,                          (5)
   ```

   and every elementary horizon is the unipotent reduction

   ```text
   mathcal H_H=F^(U_H(C)).                                 (6)
   ```

For `N=2`, the sole coefficient in `(3)` is a convention-dependent nonzero scalar multiple of the
Schwarzian derivative, recovering the scalar projective invariant core.

## Proof

The columns in `M` form a basis by `(2)`, giving the unique relation `(3)`. Constant
`SL_N` transformations left-multiply `M` and do not change its companion coefficients, so the
`q_j` are projective invariants. Conversely, projective moving-frame normalization shows that every
rational differential invariant is generated by the normalized companion coefficients and their
derivatives; hence `(4)` is the full fixed field.

Equation `(3)` is equivalent to `delta M=M A(q)` for the companion matrix `A(q)`. The entries of
`M` generate the normalized-lift field, its determinant is one, and the source jets are generic,
so the Picard--Vessiot group is the full `SL_N`. Quotienting its scalar center gives the projective
field and group. Since a finite-dimensional constant Galois group does not lower differential
transcendence degree,

```text
dtrdeg(I/C)=dtrdeg(F/C)=N-1.
```

The `N-1` generators `q_j` are therefore jointly differentially independent. Equations `(5)--(6)`
are the fixed-field statements from the horizon-building theorem.

## Correct interpretation

The frame equation `(3)` is not a generative flow for the HT source. The curvature tuple
`(q_0,...,q_(N-2))` is itself differentially free. The equation only reconstructs a projective
frame after the unconstrained curvature data have been supplied. Hypertranscendence has moved into
the invariant curvature field; it has not been explained away by a linear equation.

This gives a useful decomposition:

```text
projective HT source = freely differential curvature + finite-dimensional projective gauge. (7)
```
