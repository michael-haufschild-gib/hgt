## Analytic category and source complex

Let

```text
A=O(C)
```

with its usual compact-open Frechet topology, and let `F` be a nonzero entire function.  Multiplication
by `F` is a continuous injective map on `A`.  Form the two-term complex

```text
C_F=[A --m_F--> A].                                    (1)
```

Its only cohomology is

```text
D_F=A/F A.                                             (2)
```

For a zero `rho` of order `m_rho`, division by a holomorphic unit identifies the local stalk of
(2) with

```text
C[z-rho]/(z-rho)^(m_rho).                              (3)
```

Consequently the local length is `m_rho`.  Conversely, a class whose germ vanishes to order at
least `m_rho` at every zero is divisible by `F`: the quotient is holomorphic across every zero and
away from the zeros division is ordinary.  Thus the local support of (2), with lengths, is exactly
the complete divisor of `F`.

For the Riemann theta source, take

```text
F(z)=XiC(z)=xi(1/2+i z).
```

The Fourier transform of the completed theta kernel is `XiC`, so (1) is a precise Fourier-side
analytic realization of theta convolution.  It is defined from the source before its zeros are
listed, and its one-by-one determinant is `XiC`.

## Translation action

For every real `a`, multiplication by the nowhere-zero function `exp(-i a z)` defines an
automorphism

```text
T_a g(z)=exp(-i a z)g(z)                               (4)
```

of `A`.  Since all multiplier operators commute,

```text
T_a m_F=m_F T_a,                                       (5)
```

so `T_a` descends canonically to `D_F`.  On the semisimple local fiber at `rho`, it acts by the
character

```text
a |-> exp(-i a rho).                                   (6)
```

Restricting `a` to the subgroup generated by the prime logarithms gives exactly the proposed prime
translation action.  No Euler-product property of `F` was used in (4)--(6).

The character (6) is unitary for all real `a`, or merely for one nonzero prime logarithm, exactly
when `rho` is real.  Hence the off-unitary Koszul contraction is valid, but it is a selector applied
*after* the divisor has appeared; it does not prevent a nonreal fiber from appearing in (2).

## Universality obstruction

The construction (1)--(6) works unchanged for every real entire function

```text
F(conj z)=conj(F(z)),                                  (7)
```

including functions with nonreal conjugate zero pairs.  Therefore none of the following properties
can imply real support:

```text
analytic multiplier complex,
source-first divisor realization,
real-translation or prime-logarithm action,
duality under complex conjugation,
off-unitary Koszul contractibility.                    (8)
```

They are divisor-universal structures.

Let the finite-support semisimple defect core be spanned by the local fibers `e_rho`.  If a Hermitian
form is invariant under (4), its coefficient between `e_rho` and `e_sigma` can be nonzero only when
`rho=conj(sigma)`.  A nonreal conjugate pair therefore has matrix

```text
[ 0       c ]
[ conj(c) 0 ],                                         (9)
```

which is either degenerate or has eigenvalues `+abs(c),-abs(c)`.  It follows that

```text
D_F has a faithful positive translation-invariant
Hilbert semisimplification
iff every zero of F is real.                            (10)
```

For `F=XiC`, the right side of (10) is RH.

## Route-A consequence

The analytic-category and divisor-realization parts of Route A are fully available; singular
category ambiguity is not the remaining obstruction.  The missing step is a theorem, special to
the theta-plus-prime source, that constructs a faithful positive invariant form on (2).  By (10),
that theorem is neither formal category theory nor inherited polarization.  It is precisely a proof
that the Xi divisor is real.

Any proposed Route-A continuation must therefore exhibit additional pre-divisor structure that
fails for generic `F` and prove that this structure forces (10).  Repackaging (1)--(6) in a more
singular category cannot supply that force.
