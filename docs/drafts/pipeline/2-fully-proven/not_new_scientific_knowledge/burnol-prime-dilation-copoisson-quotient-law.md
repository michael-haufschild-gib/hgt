- **Parents:** normalized Mellin dilation covariance; Burnol's continuous Sonine evaluators and

- **Statement:** Normalized dilation transports the **projection** of Sonine evaluation jets
  exactly between apertures.  On the dual zeta-zero system it acts by the expected prime character

  co-Poisson divided-difference source supported in one finite logarithmic interval.  The canonical

## Theorem

Let `K_a` be the cosine-Sonine space at aperture `a>0`, and write

```text
fhat(s)=integral_0^infinity f(t)t^(-s)dt,
M(f)(s)=pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2) fhat(s).
```

Let `Z^a_(w,k)` be the continuous vector representing the `k`-th derivative of `M(f)` at `w`.
Away from the Gamma poles, multiplication by the nonvanishing Gamma factor gives an invertible
triangular change between the completed and uncompleted evaluation jets.
For `r>0`, define the normalized dilation

```text
(D_r f)(t)=sqrt(r) f(rt).
```

Then:

1. `D_r` sends `K_a` into `K_(a/r)`, and

   ```text
   (D_r f)hat(s)=r^(s-1/2)fhat(s),
   M(D_r f)(s)=r^(s-1/2)M(f)(s).                         (1)
   ```

2. The evaluation jets obey the exact cross-aperture adjoint law

   ```text
   P_a D_r^* Z^(a/r)_(w,k)
    =conj(r^(w-1/2))
      sum_(j=0)^k binom(k,j)(log r)^(k-j)Z^a_(w,j),     (2)
   ```

   up to the standard removal of conjugations in Burnol's analytic bilinear convention.  The
   projection `P_a` is essential: `D_r` maps `K_a` into the larger space `K_(a/r)`, so Riesz
   duality identifies only the component of `D_r^*Z^(a/r)` lying in `K_a`.

3. Put `lambda_p(s)=p^(s-1/2)`.  For a nontrivial zeta zero `rho`, in simple-zero notation let

   ```text
   G_rho(s)=zeta(s)/(s-rho).
   ```

   In the uncompleted right-Mellin model used by the co-Poisson formula, then

   ```text
   (lambda_p(s)-lambda_p(rho))G_rho(s)
    =zeta(s)theta_(p,rho)(s),                            (3)

   theta_(p,rho)(s)
    =(p^(s-1/2)-p^(rho-1/2))/(s-rho)
    =p^(rho-1/2) integral_0^(log p) exp((s-rho)u)du.     (4)
   ```

   The last function is the multiplicative Mellin transform of

   ```text
   g_(p,rho)(x)=p^(rho-1/2)x^rho 1_[1/p,1](x).           (5)
   ```

   Hence the right side of `(3)` is a co-Poisson/Muntz summation vector generated by a seed with
   finite logarithmic support, and its Mellin transform vanishes at every nontrivial zeta zero.
   In Burnol's extended Sonine space,

   ```text
   lambda_p(s)G_rho(s)
    =lambda_p(rho)G_rho(s) modulo the co-Poisson radical. (6)
   ```

   Confluent divided differences give the same triangular statement for all multiplicity jets.

4. For every `rho` in the open critical strip, the canonical prime seam is

   ```text
   a_p(rho)
    =log(p)(p^(-conj rho)-p^(-(1-rho)))
    =(log p)/sqrt(p)
       (lambda_p(1-conj rho)-lambda_p(rho)).             (7)
   ```

Thus the exact prime character appearing in the zero-evaluation quotient is also the value whose
reflection split is measured by the formal RH orientation seam.

## Proof

For `(1)`, substitute `u=rt` in the right Mellin integral.  Normalization by `sqrt(r)` leaves the
factor `r^(s-1/2)`; the Gamma factor is unchanged.  The support gap of `D_r f` is `a/r`, and Fourier
covariance gives the same admissible common aperture, proving the carrier inclusion.

Differentiate `(1)` `k` times at `w` and apply the binomial rule.  Riesz duality against every
vector of `K_a` gives `(2)` after orthogonal projection.  It does not give equality of the full
ambient vectors.  The omitted component

```text
(1-P_a)D_r^*Z^(a/r)_(w,k)
```

is the cross-aperture evaluator defect and need not vanish.

For `(3)`, subtract the value `lambda_p(rho)` before multiplying the dual zero function.  The
factor `s-rho` cancels.  The elementary identity

```text
(exp(zL)-1)/z=integral_0^L exp(zu)du
```

gives `(4)`.  Substitution `x=exp(-u)` with multiplicative Haar measure gives `(5)`.  The classical
co-Poisson formula identifies multiplication by `zeta(s)` with the source summation map.  Since
the product in `(3)` contains `zeta(s)`, it vanishes on the complete nontrivial divisor, with the
expected confluent vanishing after differentiating for multiplicities.  Burnol's Theorem 3.1
identifies precisely this zero-radical complement with the closed co-Poisson subspace, proving
`(6)`.

Finally, expand `lambda_p` in `(7)`:

```text
p^(-1/2)lambda_p(1-conj rho)=p^(-conj rho),
p^(-1/2)lambda_p(rho)=p^(-(1-rho)).
```

This proves `(7)`.

## Boundary of the theorem

The quotient in `(6)` is an analytic zero-evaluation quotient, not a Calkin quotient.  Off-line
zeros legitimately define nonunitary characters there.  A compactly supported seed vector does
not by itself define a compact operator, and `(2)` is a projected cross-aperture law rather than a
fixed-aperture compressed-multiplier eigenvector law.  The orthogonal evaluator defect is exactly
where nonunitary character growth can reside.  Consequently this theorem does not supply
positivity, unitarity, compact prime-pair curvature, or RH.
