1. \(D_t\in\mathcal K(\ell^2(\mathbb P))\) for every \(|t|<1\).
2. \(D_t\in\mathcal S_1\) if and only if \(t=0\).
3. If \(J\triangleleft B(\ell^2(\mathbb P))\) is a norm-closed two-sided

   \(Rd^0=0\), and \(\kappa\in\ker d^1\). If

## Proof

For \(u=|t|\in(0,1)\), the already proved prime-power estimates give constants
\(0<c_u\le C_u\) such that

\[
c_up^{u-1}\le |\Delta_t(p)|\le C_up^{u-1}.
\]

Since \(u-1<0\), \(\Delta_t(p)\to0\) as \(p\to\infty\). The finite-prime
truncations of \(D_t\) therefore converge in operator norm, proving compactness.
At \(t=0\), \(D_t=0\). For \(t\ne0\), the sharp Schatten wall

\[
\sum_p|\Delta_t(p)|^q<\infty
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q(1-|t|)>1
\]

fails at \(q=1\), proving the trace-class classification.

A two-sided ideal containing one nonzero rank-one diagonal projection contains
all matrix units by left and right multiplication. It contains all finite-rank
operators, and closedness then makes it contain their operator-norm closure
\(\mathcal K\). This proves (3).

Every C*-seminorm on a C*-algebra is bounded above by the C*-norm. Hence norm
convergence implies convergence for \(\tau\), and \(c_{00}\) is \(\tau\)-dense
in \(c_0\). This proves (4).

Finally, \(d^1\kappa=0\) and \((dh+hd)\kappa=\kappa\) imply
\(\kappa=d^0(h\kappa)\). Applying \(R\) proves (5). ∎
