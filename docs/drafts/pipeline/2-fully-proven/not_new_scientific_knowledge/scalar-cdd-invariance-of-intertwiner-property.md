# Scalar CDD dressing does not change the all-particle intertwiner obstruction

- **ID:** `scalar-cdd-invariance-of-intertwiner-property`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven / not_new_scientific_knowledge
- **Type:** BRIDGE THEOREM / MATRIX FACTORIZING QFT / LOCALITY REDUCTION
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED AS A DIRECT COROLLARY] [REDISCOVERED BRIDGE]`
  `[NOT NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Scope:** regular finite-multiplet adjacent-braid scattering functions; scalar dressings that
  preserve the zero anchor; no RH or distinguished special-function input
- **Parents:** `identity-zero-componentwise-pauli-modular-nuclearity.md`,
  `kolchin-exchange-cocycle-obstruction.md`, and
  `projective-wigner-smith-nonabelian-ht-separation.md`
- **Statement:** Let `B` be a regular matrix-valued factorizing braid input and let `phi` be an
  anchor-preserving regular scalar unit with `phi(0)=1`, so `phi` and `phi^(-1)` have the scalar
  intertwiner construction on a common admissible zero-neighborhood. Then `B` has the Alazzawi--Lechner
  all-particle intertwiner property if and only if the scalar-dressed family `phi B` has it. The
  exponential norm constants change only multiplicatively. Hence an anchor-preserving scalar CDD
  factor, including an HT one, can neither remove nor create the Alazzawi--Lechner intertwiner
  obstruction within that regular-unit orbit. This transfers their sufficient nuclearity
  criterion; it is not an invariance theorem for modular nuclearity or for the resulting local
  nets themselves.

## Setup

For the adjacent transposition `tau_k`, write the exchange representation generated by `B` as

```text
(D_n^B(tau_k) Psi)(theta)
  =B_(k,k+1)(theta_(k+1)-theta_k) Psi(theta tau_k).             (1)
```

Assume that on the Hardy tube used in the modular-nuclearity theorem there are analytic
multipliers `I_n^B` satisfying

```text
I_n^B H_n^B = H_n^(B(0)),
||I_n^B|| <= gamma_B^n,
||(I_n^B)^(-1)|| <= gamma_tilde_B^n                         (2)
```

on the specified analytic domain and central boundary, respectively. This is the published
intertwiner property; a generator-wise intertwining identity is sufficient but is not assumed for
the matrix family `B`.

Let `phi` be regular, scalar, and anchor preserving:

```text
phi(theta) phi(-theta)=1,
phi(0)=1.                                                     (3)
```

The scalar intertwiner theorem supplies an analytic half-phase `y` and pair multiplier

```text
phi(t)=y(t)/y(-t),
J_n(theta)=product_(1<=l<r<=n) y(theta_l-theta_r),             (4)
```

with, after the standard `1/n` tube shrinkage,

```text
||J_n|| <= gamma_phi^n,
||J_n^(-1)|| <= gamma_tilde_phi^n.                            (5)
```

On the real central boundary `J_n` is unitary. These facts are precisely the scalar case of the
published intertwiner construction; no new scalar factorization theorem is asserted here.

## Theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Put

```text
B^phi(theta)=phi(theta) B(theta).                              (6)
```

Then `B^phi(0)=B(0)`. For an adjacent transposition, all pair factors in `J_n` cancel under the
swap except the `(k,k+1)` factor, and `(4)` gives

```text
J_n(theta) phi(theta_(k+1)-theta_k)
  =J_n(theta tau_k).                                          (7)
```

Because `J_n` is scalar on the internal tensor space, `(7)` implies

```text
J_n D_n^(B^phi)(tau_k)=D_n^B(tau_k) J_n.                      (8)
```

The adjacent transpositions generate the symmetric group, so `(8)` holds for the complete
exchange representations. In particular,

```text
J_n H_n^(B^phi)=H_n^B.                                      (9)
```

Compose with `(2)`:

```text
I_n^(B^phi):=I_n^B J_n,
I_n^(B^phi) H_n^(B^phi)=H_n^(B(0)).                          (10)
```

Analyticity is preserved, and `(2)`, `(5)` give the required quantitative bounds

```text
||I_n^(B^phi)|| <= (gamma_B gamma_phi)^n,
||(I_n^(B^phi))^(-1)||
  <= (gamma_tilde_phi gamma_tilde_B)^n.                       (11)
```

This proves the forward implication. Apply the scalar construction separately to the regular
inverse factor `phi^(-1)` and repeat the same composition to obtain the converse. Therefore

```text
B has the intertwiner property
  iff
phi B has the intertwiner property.                           (12)
```

The proof also applies to the identity-zero reference space: `phi(0)=1` leaves `B(0)=-I`
unchanged, and `J_n` acts only by scalar multiplication, so the rapidity-only antisymmetric
projection and its factorial trace estimate are untouched.

## Hypertranscendental consequence

The theorem separates two kinds of differential complexity that had to be kept distinct:

```text
central scalar HT CDD dressing
  = explicit HT pair-string in the intertwiner,
  but no change of the existence obstruction;

matrix analytic cocycle modulo regular scalar units
  = the residual sector in which the all-particle intertwiner
    obstruction can still live.                                              (13)
```

Thus multiplying a known matrix solution by a scalar HT factor can produce a physically real HT
delay or exchange string, but it cannot solve the open nonabelian locality problem. Conversely,
an HT scalar dressing cannot destroy modular nuclearity once the undressed matrix family has the
intertwiner property and the other published hypotheses hold.

The word **unit** is load-bearing. A common scalar factor with zeros cannot be divided out inside
the regular analytic scattering category, even when its projective class is well-defined. Thus
`(12)` proves invariance only along regular scalar-unit orbits; it does not prove that the bare
`PGL` field determines the intertwiner property.

The standard `O(N)` formula is **not** evidence for an intrinsic projective singularity at zero.
Although the conventional `sigma_2`-normalized rational representative has a pole, one has
`sigma_3(0)=-1`; normalizing by `sigma_3` gives the regular rational projective chart

```text
S(theta)/sigma_3(theta)
  =P + i(N-2)theta/(2pi) I + theta/(i pi-theta) K.           (13a)
```

The former pole is therefore a chart artifact. Any proposed divisor-framed refinement must be
defined globally and proved relevant to the analytic cocycle; it cannot be inferred from that
local normalization.

This is compatible with the Kolchin exchange theorem: if `phi` is HT, then its half-phase `y` and
the pair string `J_n` are HT, even though they remain bounded analytic intertwiners. Analytic
triviality and differential-algebraic triviality are different categories.

Scalar CDD directions are genuine physical deformations. Quotienting them here concerns only the
intertwiner predicate; it does not identify the dressed and undressed QFTs.

## Anti-theatre and novelty gate

1. **Composition of intertwiners advertised as a founding theorem.** Rejected. Equations
   `(7)`--`(11)` are an elementary composition of the published scalar and matrix intertwiner
   mechanisms. The result is valuable as an exact route-pruning theorem.
2. **Scalar HT called projective mixing.** Rejected. Scalar multiplication leaves all channel
   ratios and the traceless Wigner--Smith matrix unchanged.
3. **Arbitrary scalar normalization removed.** Rejected. The theorem concerns regular scalar
   scattering factors with `phi(0)=1`; it does not divide out a common factor that fails the
   scalar scattering axioms or changes the zero anchor.
4. **The open identity-zero intertwiner declared solved.** Rejected. Equation `(12)` removes only
   scalar CDD ambiguity. It leaves the undressed projective matrix cocycle untouched.

The parent literature proves the scalar pair-half-phase construction, its exponential bounds,
and the matrix intertwiner criterion. Focused searches found no explicit published statement of
the biconditional `(11)` for scalar CDD dressing of a matrix solution. But `(7)`--`(12)` are an
immediate cocycle composition, so textual absence does not make them new scientific knowledge.
Their value is diagnostic: central HT is rigorously orthogonal to the remaining matrix-locality
gate inside a regular scalar-unit orbit.

## Failed stronger conjecture and surviving insight

The stronger conjecture that positive-unitary factorization forces all HT into commuting
eigenphase sectors was rejected before promotion. It redresses existing corpus results on
flat finite-rank matrix-symbol towers and is not implied by `(11)`. A projectively HT,
intrinsically mixing solution may still exist; `(11)` says only that scalar CDD dressing cannot
create it or repair its analytic intertwiner.

The failed attempt to trivialize every matrix cocycle by scaling rapidities to zero identifies a
quantitative obstruction. Finite-group averaging formally kills the infinitesimal cocycle, but
differentiation of `B(t theta)` introduces unbounded real rapidity. A naive uniform loss larger
than one across `O(n^2)` matrix factors is fatal. The companion theorem
`unitary-pair-twist-hardy-exponential-bound.md` records the parent-theory fact that the pair count itself is
not fatal when each factor is bounded holomorphic and unitary on the real axis: shrinking-tube
interpolation reduces the total loss to `exp(O(n))`. The unsolved problem is therefore the
collision-regular relative factorization with zero-loss boundary normalization, not qualitative
group cohomology or pair count alone.
