- **Statement:** Let `Omega` be an open convex permutation-invariant domain in `C^n`, and let
  `B` generate a holomorphic invertible adjacent-braid `S_n` cocycle on `Omega`, with
  `B(0)=-I`.  Then for this fixed `n` there is a holomorphic invertible matrix `J_n` on
  `Omega` satisfying

  ```text
  J_n(z) B_k(z_(k+1)-z_k)=-J_n(z^tau_k).                    (1)
  ```

  Thus the identity-zero exchange cocycle has no ordinary holomorphic or topological
  obstruction.  The theorem gives no global norm, inverse-norm, real-boundary unitary, or
  particle-uniform estimate and therefore does **not** prove the Alazzawi--Lechner
  intertwiner property, modular nuclearity, or compact locality.

## The sign-corrected Coxeter cocycle

Let `V=K^(tensor n)`, and write `B_n^pi(z)` for the matrix cocycle generated by

```text
B_n^tau_k(z)=B_k(z_(k+1)-z_k).                              (2)
```

The adjacent Yang--Baxter equation gives the braid relation, analytic unitarity gives the
involution relation, and disjoint tensor legs commute.  Hence these generators define a genuine
holomorphic `GL(V)`-valued cocycle

```text
B_n^(pi sigma)(z)=B_n^pi(z) B_n^sigma(z_pi).                (3)
```

The word **genuine** is load-bearing.  A pointwise quotient of two noncommuting scattering
cocycles need not satisfy `(3)` and cannot be inserted into the proof below.

Twist `(3)` by the sign character:

```text
A_n^pi(z)=sgn(pi) B_n^pi(z).                                (4)
```

This is again a holomorphic cocycle.  At an adjacent collision,

```text
z_k=z_(k+1)  implies  A_n^tau_k(z)=-B_k(0)=I.               (5)
```

The sign twist is the exact reason the identity-zero convention is geometrically special.  The
untwisted exchange bundle generally has nontrivial collision isotropy and does not descend as an
ordinary vector bundle.

## Every stabilizer acts trivially `[PROVED]`

Let `G_z` be the stabilizer of `z` under coordinate permutations.  It is a product of symmetric
groups permuting indices whose coordinates have equal values, and is generated by transpositions
`t=(i j)` with `z_i=z_j`.

Choose `pi` and an adjacent transposition `tau_k` such that

```text
t=pi tau_k pi^(-1),
(z_pi)_k=(z_pi)_(k+1).                                     (6)
```

The cocycle identity, `(5)`, and the inverse cocycle identity give

```text
A_n^t(z)
  =A_n^pi(z) A_n^tau_k(z_pi) A_n^(pi^(-1))(z_(pi tau_k))
  =A_n^pi(z) I A_n^pi(z)^(-1)
  =I.                                                       (7)
```

Since the transpositions in `(6)` generate `G_z`, every element of every point stabilizer acts
trivially on its fiber.

## Analytic descent and Oka trivialization `[PROVED]`

Use `(4)` to define the right action on the trivial holomorphic bundle `Omega x V`

```text
(z,v) . pi = (z_pi,A_n^pi(z)^(-1)v).                        (8)
```

Equation `(3)` makes `(8)` an action.  By `(7)`, the analytic descent criterion for equivariant
vector bundles applies: the quotient

```text
E_n=(Omega x V)/S_n  ->  Q_n=Omega/S_n                     (9)
```

is a holomorphic vector bundle.

The quotient has the following exact geometry.

1. `Omega` is convex, hence Stein.
2. A finite analytic quotient of a Stein space is Stein, so `Q_n` is Stein.
3. Point stabilizers are products of symmetric groups acting by complex reflections.  The local
   Chevalley--Shephard--Todd theorem makes `Q_n` smooth.
4. Averaging any point of `Omega` over `S_n` gives a fixed point `p in Omega`.  The straight-line
   contraction `z |-> (1-t)z+t p` is equivariant, so `Q_n` is contractible.

By the Oka--Grauert principle, every finite-rank holomorphic vector bundle on the contractible
Stein manifold `Q_n` is holomorphically trivial.  Trivialize `(9)` and pull its frame back to
`Omega`.  This gives a holomorphic map

```text
J_n:Omega -> GL(V),
J_n(z_pi)=J_n(z) A_n^pi(z).                                 (10)
```

For `pi=tau_k`, equations `(2)`, `(4)`, and `(10)` are exactly `(1)`.  Multiplication by the
central-boundary value of `J_n` therefore intertwines the exchange representation generated by
`B` with the constant identity-zero representation, wherever it defines a bounded multiplier.

## Exact boundary of the theorem

For the Alazzawi--Lechner tubes

```text
Omega_n=R^n+i(lambda_(pi/2)+(-kappa/(2n),kappa/(2n))^n),    (11)
```

Definition 5.2 requires constants independent of `n` such that

```text
sup_(z in Omega_n) ||J_n(z)|| <= gamma^n,
sup_(theta in R^n) ||J_n(theta+i lambda_(pi/2))^(-1)||
  <= gamma_tilde^n.                                         (12)
```

Oka--Grauert supplies neither estimate.  Its frame can grow without bound along the unbounded
real rapidity directions, become arbitrarily ill-conditioned on the central slice, and need not
be unitary or compatible with the physical real structure.  Compact-open approximation on an
exhaustion does not imply `(12)`.

Coordinatewise strip-to-disc maps identify `(11)` equivariantly with the polydisc, whose quotient
is the symmetrized polydisc.  The missing theorem is therefore a boundary-conditioned equivariant
`H-infinity` intertwining theorem with an `exp(O(n))` norm and a lower singular-value bound on the
central real slice.  Invertibility on the complete complex tube would be stronger than the
Alazzawi--Lechner definition. Ordinary Oka theory, additive bounded-cohomology averaging, and
generic corona language do not supply the actual boundary-conditioned statement.

## Quantitative residue and attack surface

The `1/n` tube width already controls the upper size of the permutation cocycle.  Real-axis
unitarity and three-lines give a loss `M^(O(1/n))` per adjacent factor, while a longest
permutation word has `O(n^2)` factors.  Thus

```text
sup ||A_n^pi|| <= exp(O(n)).                                (13)
```

A normalized twisted Reynolds average can preserve holomorphy, exact equivariance, and the upper
scale `(13)`.  Its failure is sharp: averages of unitary matrices can be singular, and even a
pointwise invertible average can have its least singular value tend to zero at rapidity infinity.
The frontier is uniformly conditioned invertible selection, not cocycle coherence or an upper
norm count.

The surviving quantitative conjecture and the `O(n)`-layer collision-gluing attack are separated
into `../../1-in-progress/quantitative-coxeter-cartan-hardy-trivialization.md`.  The conjecture is
the existing `O(N)` Alazzawi--Lechner locality problem in a sharpened controlled-descent form, not
a new claim of priority.

## Consequence for hypertranscendental physics

The multiparticle exchange constraint separates into three inequivalent categories:

```text
ordinary holomorphic category:  fixed-n cocycle is always flat;
bounded Hardy category:         uniform conditioning is open;
differential-algebraic category: an HT exchange gauge can remain nontrivial.  (14)
```

Thus a hypertranscendental scattering constraint can be holomorphically trivial and still be
