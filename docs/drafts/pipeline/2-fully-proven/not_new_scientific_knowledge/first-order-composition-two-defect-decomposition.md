# Two-defect decomposition for first-order entire composition

- **ID:** `first-order-composition-two-defect-decomposition`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Scientific status:** `[BRIDGE] [PROVED HERE] [NOT NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Lane:** general hypertranscendence; differential-order geometry under composition
- **Statement:** The differential order of a first-order entire composition splits exactly into primitive visibility and derivative-shadow recovery.

## Goal

Separate the already understood primitive compression from the genuinely open loss of inner derivative-shadow states. This is a differential-field dimension decomposition and is not claimed as new scientific knowledge.

## Setup

Let `f` be a transcendental entire first-order DA function, let `g` be a transcendental entire DA function, and put

`h=f o g`.

Write

`B=C(z)`,

`K=B<g'>`,

`L=B<h>`,

`N=K L`.

Define the primitive defect

`pi(g)=trdeg_K K(g)=ord_DA(g)-ord_DA(g') in {0,1}`.

Define the autonomy bit

`aut(f)=1`

when `f'` is algebraic over `C(f)`, and `aut(f)=0` otherwise.

Finally define the **shadow-recovery defect**

`lambda_f(g)=trdeg_L N.`

It counts the inner derivative-shadow states still missing after all output derivatives have been adjoined.

## Relative visibility theorem

`[BRIDGE] [PROVED HERE]`

`trdeg_K N = 1+(1-aut(f)) pi(g).`

### Outer-state independence

Clunie composition growth and the Nevanlinna root estimate show that `h` is transcendental over every field generated by finitely many derivatives of `g`. Hence `h` is transcendental over `B<g>=K(g)`.

### Primitive-rigid inner input

If `pi(g)=0`, then `g` is algebraic over `K`. Every derivative of `h` lies in an algebraic extension of `K(h)`, so

`trdeg_K N=1`.

### Primitive-free inner input and autonomous outer constraint

If `pi(g)=1` and `aut(f)=1`, the autonomous relation makes `f'(g)` algebraic over `C(h)`. Since

`h'=f'(g)g'`,

the fields `N` and `K(h)` are algebraic over one another. Thus again

`trdeg_K N=1`.

The inner primitive state is replaced by the outer state.

### Primitive-free inner input and nonautonomous outer constraint

Assume `pi(g)=1` and `aut(f)=0`. Then `f` and `f'` are algebraically independent over `C`. A first-order ADE gives an algebraic relation among the independent variable `w`, `f(w)`, and `f'(w)`; therefore `w` is algebraic over `C(f(w),f'(w))`.

Inside `N`, the element

`a=f'(g)=h'/g'`

is present. Substitution in the preceding input-recovery relation makes `g` algebraic over `C(h,a)`, hence over `N`. Conversely all output derivatives are algebraic over `K(g,h)`. Therefore `N` and `K(g,h)` are algebraic over one another. Since `g` is transcendental over `K` and `h` is transcendental over `K(g)`,

`trdeg_K N=2`.

This proves the relative formula.

## Exact two-defect identity

By the tower formula,

`ord_DA(h)=trdeg_B L`

`=ord_DA(g')+1+(1-aut(f))pi(g)-lambda_f(g).`

Equivalently,

`ord_DA(f o g)=ord_DA(g)+1-aut(f)pi(g)-lambda_f(g).`

Thus there are exactly two logically distinct compression mechanisms:

1. **primitive compression** `aut(f)pi(g)`, completely classified by the autonomy bit and primitive defect;
2. **shadow loss** `lambda_f(g)`, the remaining algebraic observability defect.

The autonomous-cap theorem proves `lambda_f(g)=0` when `aut(f)=1`. The examples `f(w)=w exp(w)` with the Gaussian primitive and all first-order-inner cascades already proved in this project also have `lambda_f(g)=0`.

## Parent-theory verdict

The quantity `lambda_f(g)` is the generic dimension of the derivative-shadow part of the unobservable foliation of the composition realization. This is standard differential-algebraic observability in the sense of Wang--Sontag and nonlinear cascade matching in the sense of Isidori--Krener--Gori-Giorgi--Monaco.

The decomposition is useful because it isolates the entire-function question, but it is built from standard field dimensions and observability. It is therefore a bridge, not a scientific-novelty claim.
