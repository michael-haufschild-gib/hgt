# Exact rational RG generation dichotomy

- **ID:** `exact-rational-rg-generation-dichotomy`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / FOUNDATIONAL PHYSICS LANGUAGE
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED] [PARENT-DERIVED] [NOT NEW MATHEMATICS]`
  `[FOUNDATIONAL FOR RG-GENERATION HT]`
- **Statement:** For any one-coupling rational step-scaling map at a repelling fixed point, the
  exact beta field is differentially algebraic exactly when the Poincare function is
  differentially algebraic, equivalently exactly when the map preserves a rational Ritt
  differential cocycle.  Otherwise every locally invertible DA coupling scheme has an HT beta
  field, and every linearizing scheme is HT.

## Setup

Let `R in C(t)` have degree at least two, let `c` be a fixed point with multiplier
`lambda=R'(c)` and `|lambda|>1`, and let

```text
Phi(0)=c,       Phi'(0)=1,       R(Phi(z))=Phi(lambda z).          (1)
```

Write `kappa=Phi^(-1)` locally at `c`, and define the exact local flow and its beta field by

```text
F_s(t)=Phi(lambda^s kappa(t)),
beta(t)=(log lambda) kappa(t)/kappa'(t).                            (2)
```

Then

```text
beta(R(t))=R'(t)beta(t).                                          (3)
```

The analytic functional-conjugation construction `(2)` and beta constraint `(3)` are established
renormalization-group parent theory.

## The dichotomy

The following are equivalent.

1. `Phi` is differentially algebraic over `C(z)`.
2. `kappa` is differentially algebraic over `C(t)`.
3. `beta` is differentially algebraic over `C(t)`.
4. After translating `c` to zero, there exist integers `r != 0`, `j`, and a nonzero rational
   function `A` such that the normalized Poincare germ `sigma` satisfies

   ```text
   (sigma')^r=z^j A(sigma).                                       (4)
   ```

5. Equivalently, with `B(t)=A(t-c)`, the exact step map preserves the rational differential
   cocycle

   ```text
   R'(t)^r B(t)=lambda^(r+j) B(R(t)).                              (5)
   ```

## Proof

The equivalence `(1) <=> (4)` is Ritt's theorem in the Di Vizio--Fernandes form.  Differentially
algebraic locally invertible germs are closed under compositional inversion, proving `(1) <=> (2)`.
Equation `(2)` gives `(2) => (3)`.  Conversely, if `beta` is DA, then

```text
kappa'=((log lambda)/beta)kappa                                  (6)
```

makes `kappa` DA over the differential field generated by `beta`; transitivity gives `(3) => (2)`.

Differentiate the Poincare equation and substitute `(4)` at `z` and `lambda z`; this gives `(5)`.
Conversely, assume `(5)`.  The meromorphic germ

```text
H(z)=(sigma'(z))^r/A(sigma(z))                                   (7)
```

satisfies `H(lambda z)=lambda^j H(z)`.  Since `lambda` is not a root of unity, comparison of its
Laurent coefficients forces `H(z)=a z^j` for a nonzero constant `a`.  Rescaling `A` by `a` gives
`(4)`.  Thus `(4) <=> (5)`.

## Scheme theorem

For a locally invertible DA coupling chart `u=h(t)`, the transported beta field is

```text
beta_h(h(t))=h'(t)beta(t).                                        (8)
```

Closure of DA germs under local inversion, composition, differentiation, and division proves

```text
beta_h is DA  <=>  beta is DA.                                   (9)
```

Therefore failure of the rational cocycle `(5)` is precisely the obstruction to a finite
algebraic-differential exact beta field in every DA coupling scheme.

An arbitrary analytic scheme can still use `u=kappa(t)` and make the beta field linear.  In the HT
case the dichotomy proves that this linearizing chart is itself HT.  Hence the invariant statement
is not “all analytic coordinates display HT”; it is

```text
finite algebraic-differential schemes cannot eliminate the exact RG complexity.                (10)
```

## Physical discriminator

The theorem is not itself new mathematics; it joins known iteration theory, differential algebra,
and exact RG conjugation.  Its purpose is to turn a physical step-scaling map into a sharp test:

```text
find a rational cocycle (5)  -> exact beta is DA;
prove every such cocycle impossible -> exact beta is HT in the entire DA scheme class.           (11)
```

The exact diamond-Ising map passes the second branch by an explicit infinite-divisor argument; see
`../needs-audit/diamond-ising-da-scheme-rg-generation-hypertranscendence.md`.  Pure parallel Ising
coupling doubling passes the first branch and has a DA beta field.  These two physical maps are the
current positive/negative discriminator pair.

### Reduced to two base obstructions (2026-08-30)

The homogeneous branch of the base relation is *not* a separate case — it **is** Ritt's form.
`HTGeometry/RittHomogeneousBaseRelation.lean`,
**`exists_rittForm_of_homogeneous_base_relation`**: if the base carries `u ≠ 0` with


### The logarithmic derivative names the obstruction (2026-08-30)

`HTGeometry/RittBaseExponent.lean` and `HTGeometry/RittLogDerivativeObstruction.lean` close the last

| `k ≠ 0` | scaling by `−k` gives `ρ·ψ(a) = a − dK ρ/ρ` — the **rational affine-connection equation** |
| `k = 0` | `ρ·ψ(v) = v` — an **invariant rational vector field** |

| Statement | Name |

This makes the homogeneous branch consumable by `RationalAffineConnection.lean` directly, in its own
vocabulary, without going through Ritt's form — and it does so *without seeing the `q`-exponent*, so
the exponent cancellations are harmless for it.

**Remaining, precisely.**  Only the fully degenerate configuration is untouched: the descent's
scalar collapses to `q^0ρ^0 = 1` with zero inhomogeneity, leaving `ψ(u) = u`, whose logarithmic
derivative `v` satisfies `ρ·ψ(v) = v` and may be zero — the case where every coefficient of the

### A checkable criterion that kills every polynomial solution (2026-08-30)

`HTGeometry/RittTransportNoSolution.lean`,
**`no_polynomial_solution_of_natDegree_lt`**: for any `R` with `deg R ≥ 2`, any nonzero constant `β`,

1. **no constant solution** — `Cp ≠ α·(R′)^J − β·α` for every `α ≠ 0`, and
2. **low degree** — `deg Cp < deg R + J·(deg R − 1)`,

the polynomial identity

```text

```

has no nonzero solution `A`.  The proof is pure degree bookkeeping: with `deg A ≥ 1` the left side

by hypothesis 2, since `deg A·deg R ≥ deg R` — `deg Cp`, while the right side has degree at most the
maximum of those two; and `deg A = 0` is exactly hypothesis 1.

Both hypotheses are *arithmetic in the coefficients of the map*: no orbits, no critical points, no
valuations.  Combined with `exists_polynomial_of_ratOrder_nonneg` and
`polynomial_identity_of_isAffineTransport`, a solution of the transport equation that has no pole is
now excluded outright; only solutions *with* a pole survive, and those are what the orbit closers of
