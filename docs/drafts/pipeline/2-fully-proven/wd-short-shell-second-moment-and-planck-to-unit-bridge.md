### §1. Short-Shell × Selberg Per-Window $L^p$/$L^2$ Moment Bounds (`[PROVED HERE]`)

For $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $A, V \in \mathbb{R}$, set
$$N_m(A, V) := \sum_{\substack{\rho \in \mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}\,m \\ |\operatorname{Re}\rho - 1/2| \ge A / \log V}} \operatorname{ord}_{\xi}(\rho), \qquad I_V := [\lceil V \rceil, \lfloor 2V \rfloor] \cap \mathbb{N}.$$

**Theorem 1.1 (Pointwise Short-Shell Cap & Unconditional Per-Window $L^p$/$L^2$ Bounds).**
Let $C_{\mathrm{sh}} := 48(|\mathrm{xiLineTwoLogDerivConstant}| + 2) > 0$.
1. For every $V \ge 32$ with $\log V \ge 4$, every $m \in I_V$, and every $A \in \mathbb{R}$, $N_m(A, V) \le C_{\mathrm{sh}} \log V$.
2. For every $0 < r < 1$, with $C_d, \kappa_r > 0$ from `deepExtendedShell_orderSum_le`, eventually in $V$: for all $2 \le A \le \kappa_r \log V$ and all $p \ge 1$,
   $$\sum_{m \in I_V} N_m(A, V)^p \le 8 C_{\mathrm{sh}}^{p - 1} C_d \frac{e^{-r A}}{A} V (\log V)^p, \qquad \sum_{m \in I_V} N_m(A, V)^2 \le 8 C_{\mathrm{sh}} C_d \frac{e^{-r A}}{A} V (\log V)^2.$$
3. *(Double-logarithmic $o(V)$ second moment)* For every $0 < r < 1$, eventually in $V$: uniformly across $0 \le u \le \frac{\kappa_r}{2} \log V$, setting $A_V(r, u) := \frac{2}{r}\log\log V + u$,
   $$\sum_{m \in I_V} N_m(A_V(r, u), V)^2 \le \frac{4 r C_{\mathrm{sh}} C_d e^{-r u}}{\log\log V} V.$$

*Proof.*
(1) By `mem_burnolUnitXiZeros_iff`, `sub_three_le_burnolUnitLower`, and `burnolUnit_coords`, every $\rho \in \mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}\,m$ ($m \in I_V$, $V \ge 32$) satisfies $0 < \operatorname{Re}\rho < 1$ and $m - 3 < \operatorname{Im}\rho \le m + 3 \le 2V + 4$, so $\mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}\,m \subseteq \mathrm{shellZeros}(m - 3, m + 3)$ with $(m + 3) - (m - 3) = 6$ and $m - 3 \ge V - 3 > 0$. Applying `mediumCap_orderSum_le_logV` (three applications of `shortShell_orderSum_le_log`) gives $N_m(A, V) \le \sum_{z \in \mathrm{shellZeros}(m-3, m+3)} \operatorname{ord}_\xi(z) \le C_{\mathrm{sh}} \log V$.

(2) Set $B_{\mathrm{ext}} := \{z \in \mathrm{shellZeros}(V - 4, 2V + 4) : |\operatorname{Re} z - 1/2| \ge A / \log V\}$. For $m \in I_V$, every $\rho \in \mathrm{burnolUnitXiZeros}\,m$ has $V - 3 < \operatorname{Im}\rho < 2V + 3$, so $\rho \in B_{\mathrm{ext}}$ whenever $|\operatorname{Re}\rho - 1/2| \ge A / \log V$. Moreover, $m - 3 < \operatorname{Im}\rho < m + 3$ implies $\lfloor \operatorname{Im}\rho \rfloor - 2 \le m \le \lfloor \operatorname{Im}\rho \rfloor + 3$, a subset of $[\lfloor \operatorname{Im}\rho \rfloor - 3, \lfloor \operatorname{Im}\rho \rfloor + 4]$ of cardinality $\le 8$. Exchanging finite sums gives
$$\sum_{m \in I_V} N_m(A, V) \le 8 \sum_{z \in B_{\mathrm{ext}}} \operatorname{ord}_\xi(z) \le 8 C_d \frac{e^{-r A}}{A} V \log V.$$
For $p \ge 1$ and $N_m(A, V) \ge 0$, $N_m(A, V)^p = N_m(A, V)^{p-1} N_m(A, V) \le (C_{\mathrm{sh}}\log V)^{p-1} N_m(A, V)$ (`Real.rpow_le_rpow` for $p - 1 \ge 0$, trivial at $N_m = 0$). Summing over $m \in I_V$ yields the $L^p$ and $p = 2$ bounds.

(3) For $\log\log V \ge 2$ and $\frac{2}{r}\log\log V \le \frac{\kappa_r}{2}\log V$, $A_V(r, u) \in [2, \kappa_r \log V]$, $A_V(r, u) \ge \frac{2}{r}\log\log V$, and $e^{-r A_V(r, u)} = e^{-2\log\log V - r u} = (\log V)^{-2} e^{-r u}$. Substituting into (2) cancels $(\log V)^2$ identically and gives $8 C_{\mathrm{sh}} C_d \frac{e^{-r u}}{(2/r)\log\log V} V = \frac{4 r C_{\mathrm{sh}} C_d e^{-r u}}{\log\log V} V$. $\square$
