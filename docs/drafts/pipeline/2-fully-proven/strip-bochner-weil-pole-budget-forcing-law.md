### Part I: The Coupled Strip Bochner–Weil Form and Its Shadows (`StripBochnerShadows.lean`)

1. **Definition (`stripBochnerForm`).** Let $\iota$ be a countable index type, $w : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ nonnegative weights ($w_i \ge 0$), $\lambda : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ frequencies, and $K : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ a nonnegative scale aperture ($K_i \ge 0$) such that $\sum_i w_i K_i < \infty$. For any $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and channel configuration $x, c : \operatorname{Fin} n \to \mathbb{R}$, define the **Strip Bochner–Weil Form**:
   $$\mathcal{B}_{w, \lambda}(K; x, c) := \sum_{j=1}^n \sum_{k=1}^n c_j c_k \sum_{i \in \iota} w_i K_i \cos\bigl(\lambda_i (x_j - x_k)\bigr).$$
   By the trigonometric Gram identity $\sum_{j,k} c_j c_k \cos(\lambda_i(x_j - x_k)) = \bigl(\sum_j c_j \cos(\lambda_i x_j)\bigr)^2 + \bigl(\sum_j c_j \sin(\lambda_i x_j)\bigr)^2 \ge 0$ (`gram_cos_eq`) and termwise nonnegativity $w_i K_i \ge 0$, $\mathcal{B}_{w,\lambda}(K; x, c) \ge 0$ unconditionally for every aperture $K \ge 0$ and every channel tuple $(x, c)$.

2. **Boundary Shadow Specialization (`isEvenPosDef_of_stripBochner_limit`).** Whenever a family of strip kernels $T_\delta : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ ($\delta > 0$) satisfies $T_\delta(-u) = T_\delta(u)$, $\sum_{j,k} c_j c_k T_\delta(x_j - x_k) \ge 0$ for all finite channel tuples, and $\lim_{\delta \downarrow 0^+} \delta T_\delta(u) = D(u)$ pointwise on $\mathbb{R}$, passing to the limit along $\delta \downarrow 0^+$ yields `MultiplicativePositivityGeometry.IsEvenPosDef D`. Specializing $T_\delta(u) = \sum_i w_i(\delta) \cos(\lambda_i u)$ recovers `IsBoundaryDivisor.isEvenPosDef` directly as the $\delta \downarrow 0^+$ boundary shadow.

3. **Scaling-Surface $\kappa$-Polar Hodge Shadow (`polarSurfaceOfPoleBudget`).** Let $E$ be a real vector space equipped with a symmetric bilinear local pairing $L : E \to_{\mathbb{R}} E \to_{\mathbb{R}} \mathbb{R}$ and linear pole functionals $\ell_H, \ell_V : E \to_{\mathbb{R}} \mathbb{R}$ on $E$. Adjoining the two isotropic fibre directions on $E \oplus \mathbb{R}^2$ (orgiven a `PolarSurface E` $S$ and pole budget $\kappa > 0$), scaling the isotropic fibre generator $H_\kappa := \kappa^{-1} S.H$, $V_\kappa := S.V$ on the $\kappa$-graded pairing $\langle x, y \rangle_\kappa$ gives a `PolarSurface` whose Castelnuovo–Severi defect is proportional by $\kappa^{-1}$ to $\kappa \cdot S.\text{polar}(x, y) - L(x, y)$. Thus single-channel ($n = 1, x = 0$) positivity of the $\kappa$-polar minus strip-local form is equivalent to the Hodge index condition (`hodgeIndex_tfae`) on the $\kappa$-graded polar surface.
