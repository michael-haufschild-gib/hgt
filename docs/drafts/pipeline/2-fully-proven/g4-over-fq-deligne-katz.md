## §2 The weight/angle split `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `M` specialize to a sheaf **pure of weight `w`** (Deligne, Weil II). Frobenius eigenvalues factor
```
   α_{v,i} = q^{w·\deg v / 2} · u_{v,i},        |u_{v,i}| = 1,
```
the **modulus** `q^{w\deg v/2} ∈ ⟨q^{1/2}⟩` (rank-1) and the **angle** `u_{v,i}` on the unit circle. The
weight grading gives the exact sequence of Tannakian groups
```
   1 → G^{0}_{\mathrm{geom}} → G_L → W → 1,
```
with `W` the image of the **weight/Tate cocharacter** — generated by the single Frobenius weight `q` (the
Tate twist `ℚ_ℓ(1)` carries Frobenius `q^{-1}`), so `dim W = 1` — and `G^{0}_{\mathrm{geom}}` the
**reductive** geometric monodromy (the angles `u_{v,i}`; for `M_ζ^{ff}` trivial, for a general motive the
Sato–Tate group).

**Identification with corank.** The multiplier of `M_ζ^{ff}` is `g_v = q^{-\deg v}` — exactly the modulus
direction — and #121 computed `corank(Λ_ff) = 1` (`Λ_ff = \ker\deg`, all multipliers powers of one `q`).
Hence
```
   dim W = corank(Λ_ff) = 1.
```
**The corank-1 diagonal of #121 IS the weight torus `W`.** The corank invariant sees precisely the
weight/Tate direction and is **blind** to the reductive angle direction `G^{0}_{\mathrm{geom}}` (the
genuine Deligne–Katz content). This cleanly **localizes the two faces**: G5/RH (`|α| = q^{w\deg v/2}`,
Wheeler) lives in `W` (the modulus); G4/Sato–Tate (the angle distribution) lives in
`G^{0}_{\mathrm{geom}}`. ∎
