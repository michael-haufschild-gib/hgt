faces, discover exact identities, optimize constants, and validate finite realizations. It cannot
establish cofinal no-escape, an all-order common zero, or source-to-Xi landing from finite samples.
Known-zero checks cannot certify a source premise against close complex pairs. Every positive
numerical pattern remains `[CONJECTURE]`; only independently reconstructed paper algebra may enter
