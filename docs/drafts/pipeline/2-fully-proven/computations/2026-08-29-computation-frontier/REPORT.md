# RH computation decision frontier

Date: 2026-08-29  
Status: research triage; computations are diagnostic only and forbidden as Lean inputs

## What computation has already contributed

- **S6F/TCD:** selected direct native D1 over stronger projective drift; exact geometry exposed
  scale increase, clock-square concavity, seam/outer reduction, failure of higher alternating
  derivatives, and irreducibility of naive source/gap factorization. Refined-cone follow-up killed
  global wall and upper-wall clock monotonicity while isolating one univariate outer-face decrease.
- **CLG/TTF:** broad source scans and fixed-action Jacobian scans survived. Exact stationary-locus
  follow-up found the literal modular-center velocity positive at all 79,999 tested radii and no
  stationary root, selecting direct radial monotonicity as a stronger proof target.
- **T2:** killed present direct finite-shell exponent claim and generic `L2/L4` repairs. Surviving
  mechanism needs target-reciprocal localization plus a genuinely arithmetic selected-cell law.
- **C1:** current global-mean carrier misses its theta scalar target by a large stable margin.
  Computation selects an exact head/tail strict-separation proof to retire that carrier.
- **JAS/RFHM:** killed unitary integration of flat scalar insertion gains and reciprocal bilateral
  scalar completion. Finite packet independence survives, but diagonal Gamma weights cannot create
  missing coupling.

   `-d²D1/dw²`; separately factor the univariate outer-face derivative after translations at both

   sharpens a conjecture. Current corpus has definitions but no validated numerical renderer.
5. **O and F2 certified finite models.** First prove or numerically validate convergent Galerkin
   realizations of Gamma--Slepian head and Sonin projection. Only then compute extremal finite-head

6. **Off-wall quartet response atlas — route opening, not proof.** Insert a functional-equation
   compatible close quartet into synthetic entire functions and compare response of candidate
   nonlocal/singular receivers across aperture and scale. Purpose is to find first invariant not
   suppressed with finite wall jets. Any survivor is still a receiver until literal source landing
   is proved.
7. **Candidate-specific finite falsifiers for HB, JAS/RFHM, T2, U, E.** Run only after an exact
   balanced assembly, reflected sewing, selected-cell law, cross-degree law, or fixed Hall relation
   is stated. Data-fitting such a law after observing output is circular.

## Hard boundary

Computation can kill universal inequalities with one faithful counterexample, locate dangerous
faces, discover exact identities, optimize constants, and validate finite realizations. It cannot
establish cofinal no-escape, an all-order common zero, or source-to-Xi landing from finite samples.
Known-zero checks cannot certify a source premise against close complex pairs. Every positive
numerical pattern remains `[CONJECTURE]`; only independently reconstructed paper algebra may enter
Lean.
