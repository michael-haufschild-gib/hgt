# Complete moving-corner reality detector: computation report

The exact retained moving-characteristic corner ratio obeys the expected reflection law, but reality does not uniquely identify the critical line.

The parent run evaluated the coefficient vector `(W_R,W_Delta)` of `(RQ,R Delta Q)` after complementary-branch subtraction, retaining all source-polynomial terms and `D_star f33`. Analytic radial primitives agreed with direct quadrature to `6.2e-100`; finite-difference `D_star` errors fell from `6.15e-17` to `6.15e-25`. Numerically, `kappa(1-sigma)=conjugate(kappa(sigma))`, so `Im kappa=0` at `sigma=1/2`. At `L=2e`, however, extra reflected roots occur at heights 50 and 100: approximately `.114281/.885719` and `.159561/.840439`. Across the extended height set 20--200, every height except the first zeta ordinate had an extra pair at `L=2e`. Complete denominators were nonzero and candidates repeated at 120/180 digits. The frozen uniqueness gate failed.

Deleting `D_star f33` is unphysical and materially changes topology: it produced different false roots, including `.347387/.652613` at the first zeta height and `L=10`. Its broad scale scan was numerically unstable in central derivative refinement, so the reported 54 ablated “bifurcations” are rejected; direct root counts alone show highly scale-sensitive ablated topology.

The physical pitchfork challenge found no zero of the critical-line slope on `2e<=L<=40`, although the off-critical pair present at `L=2e` was gone by `L=6`. Thus the pair does not merge into the critical root. The denominator-safe follow-up solved `N=-Im(W_R conjugate(W_Delta))` over the complete half-strip. It found no interior double-root event and 17 non-polar strip-boundary crossings between `2e` and 6: one each at heights 20, 30, 50; three each at 75, 100, 150; five at 200. Examples are `L≈5.5295718256267683` at height 20 and crossings `5.7129723277, 5.7246006891, 5.7522120468, 5.7767877012, 5.7919830237` at height 200. Coefficient denominators range from `0.0354` to about `295`, never approach zero. Each event repeated at 130/190 digits and changed the half-strip root count across a `1e-30` scale perturbation. Both pods returned identical coordinates using the same frozen implementation. The simple one-exit-per-height gate failed; positive salvage is repeated boundary exit/re-entry driven by scale and height.

Consequence: even the complete derived corner component has off-critical reality points and oscillatory strip crossings. Corner reality alone cannot supply Identification or Selection, and choosing a convenient fixed scale requires a separate source law. This remains one exact boundary component, not assembled causal action: other faces, angular realization, onset/time-cap matching, reader remainder and independent SS/SV/VV sectors remain open.
