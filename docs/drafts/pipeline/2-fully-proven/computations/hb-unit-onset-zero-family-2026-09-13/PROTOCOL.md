# H6: zeros of the prescribed unit onset and shape controls

Frozen before computation. Factor-level investigation; not a complete-action calculation or proof.

Question: does the literal unit-duration smoothTransition derivative have off-imaginary-axis transform zeros in .1 <= |Re z| <= 3, .1 <= |Im z| <= 144? This differs from choosing a nearby duration. The current corpus proves real-exponent positivity, isolated zeros on duration rays, a duration avoiding all actual factors, and fixed-onset survival at sufficiently high overtones. None of these inspected statements determines the unit fundamental factor's zero locus in this box. Results about other durations/overtones are not discovery targets here.

The literal transition is chi(u)=g(u)/(g(u)+g(1-u)), g(u)=exp(-1/u) for u>0 and zero otherwise. Its centered transform is G(z)=exp(-z/2)*onsetPulseTransform(z)=integral_0^1 chi'(u) exp(z(u-1/2)) du. On 0<u<=1/2, q=exp(-1/u+1/(1-u)), chi'=q/(1+q)^2*(u^-2+(1-u)^-2). Symmetry gives G(z)=2 integral_0^(1/2) chi'(u) cosh(z(u-1/2)) du. The exponential normalization is nonzero and does not change zeros.

Competing explanations: genuine off-axis source blind spots; zeros confined to the imaginary axis in the tested band; or quadrature/contour artifacts. Positivity and evenness alone do not decide this specific entire function's zeros. No applicable factorization or zero-location theorem was found. The experiment tests a bridge question, not a new universal-law conjecture.
