METHOD + the spurious-artifact catch:
  - A naive mp.pslq(vec, maxcoeff=1e12) at dps=120 RETURNED a vector with coeffs ~1e11 and residual 8.4e-92.
    This is SPURIOUS: residual 8.4e-92 >> precision floor ~1e-120, and coeffs are huge. PSLQ on n=9 reals at

    NOT a true relation. (Lesson: always check residual vs the precision floor and coefficient height.)
  - Correct test: low maxcoeff (only genuine low-height relations) + dps-stability.

   dps=160: NO genuine low-height relation
   dps=220: NO genuine low-height relation
=> NO integer linear relation of height <= 1e6 among the 9 constants, stable across 100/160/220 digits.

rung 1, height <= 1e6. Supports the DSS-cross relative apex / corank^exp_1 full / regularization-freshness
