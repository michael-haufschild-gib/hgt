### Theorem 1 (`[PROVED HERE]` — Post–Widder Polar Weight Positivity and the Real-Anchor Law)
Let $S \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a finite set stable under conjugation ($\bar{z} \in S$ for $z \in S$) and let $c : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $c(\bar{z}) = c(z)$ for all $z \in S$. Define $G(s) := \sum_{z \in S} \frac{c(z)}{s - z}$. Suppose there exists $\sigma_1 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $\operatorname{Re} z \le \sigma_1$ for all $z \in S$ and for every $\sigma > \sigma_1$, the vertical section $u \mapsto \operatorname{Re} G(\sigma + iu)$ is positive definite (`IsPosDef`).

1. **Nonnegative scale weight (`posDef_polarSum_laplaceWeight_nonneg`):** For every $t > 0$,
   $$w_G(t) := \sum_{z \in S} c(z)\, e^{\operatorname{Re} z \cdot t} \cos(\operatorname{Im} z \cdot t) \ge 0.$$
   *Proof.* Set $a := \max(\sigma_1, 1)$. On $U := \{s \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Re} s > a\}$, every $z \in S$ satisfies $\operatorname{Re}(s - z) > a - \sigma_1 \ge 0$, so $s - z \neq 0$ and $G$ is holomorphic on $U$. For real $x > a$, conjugation invariance of $(S, c)$ gives $\overline{G(x)} = \sum_{z \in S} \frac{c(\bar{z})}{x - \bar{z}} = G(x)$, so $\operatorname{Im} G(x) = 0$. For $x \ge a + 1$, $|x - z| \ge x - \operatorname{Re} z \ge 1$, whence $\operatorname{Re} G(x) \le |G(x)| \le \sum_{z \in S} |c(z)| \le \bigl(\sum_{z \in S} |c(z)|\bigr)\sqrt{x}$ (as $\sqrt{x} \ge \sqrt{2} \ge 1$). By `re_iterate_deriv_two_mul_nonneg`, `hasDerivAt_re_iterate_deriv`, and `tower_odd_nonpos` (`PositiveDefiniteSectionsAreCompletelyMonotone`), $(-1)^n \operatorname{Re}(G^{(n)}(x)) \ge 0$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x > a$. By induction on $n$ on the open set $U$,
   $$G^{(n)}(s) = (-1)^n n! \sum_{z \in S} \frac{c(z)}{(s - z)^{n + 1}} \qquad (s \in U).$$
   Fix $t > 0$. For every integer $n > a t$, $x_n := n / t > a$, and multiplying $(-1)^n \operatorname{Re}(G^{(n)}(x_n)) \ge 0$ by $x_n^{n+1} / n! > 0$ yields
   $$0 \le \operatorname{Re} \sum_{z \in S} c(z) \bigl((1 - z t / n)^{-1}\bigr)^{n+1}.$$
   Since $(1 - z t / n)^n \to e^{-z t} \neq 0$ and $(1 - z t / n) \to 1$ as $n \to \infty$, $\bigl((1 - z t / n)^{-1}\bigr)^{n+1} \to e^{z t}$, and $\operatorname{Re}(c(z) e^{z t}) = c(z) e^{\operatorname{Re} z \cdot t}\cos(\operatorname{Im} z \cdot t)$. Passing to the limit $n \to \infty$ gives $w_G(t) \ge 0$. $\square$

2. **Multi-ordinate and single-ordinate real-anchor bounds (`real_anchor_of_posDef_polarSum`, `two_mul_le_real_anchor_of_single_ordinate`):** Suppose $\sigma_{\max} \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $\operatorname{Re} z \le \sigma_{\max}$ whenever $z \in S$ and $c(z) \neq 0$, and suppose $z_1 = \sigma_{\max} + i\gamma_1 \in S$ has $\gamma_1 \neq 0$ and $c(z_1) > 0$.
   - *(General multi-ordinate case)* For every $T > 0$ and $t \in [T, 2T]$, since $1 - \cos(\gamma_1 t) \ge 0$ and $e^{-\sigma_{\max} t} w_G(t) \ge 0$, we have
     $$0 \le \frac{1}{T} \int_T^{2T} e^{-\sigma_{\max} t} w_G(t)\bigl(1 - \cos(\gamma_1 t)\bigr)\,dt.$$
     Partition $S' := \{z \in S : c(z) \neq 0\}$ into $S_{<} := \{z \in S' : \operatorname{Re} z < \sigma_{\max}\}$ and $S_{=} := \{z \in S' : \operatorname{Re} z = \sigma_{\max}\}$. For $z \in S_{<}$, $\delta_z := \sigma_{\max} - \operatorname{Re} z > 0$, so on $[T, 2T]$ the integrand is bounded in absolute value by $2 |c(z)| e^{-\delta_z T} \to 0$ as $T \to \infty$. On $S_{=}$:
     - if $z = \sigma_{\max}$ ($\operatorname{Im} z = 0$), $\frac{1}{T}\int_T^{2T} (1 - \cos(\gamma_1 t))\,dt = 1 - \frac{\sin(2\gamma_1 T) - \sin(\gamma_1 T)}{\gamma_1 T} \to 1$;
     - for the distinct conjugate points $z_1 = \sigma_{\max} + i\gamma_1 \in S_{=}$ and $\bar{z}_1 = \sigma_{\max} - i\gamma_1 \in S_{=}$ (with $c(\bar{z}_1) = c(z_1)$), $\cos(\pm\gamma_1 t)(1 - \cos(\gamma_1 t)) = \cos(\gamma_1 t) - \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\cos(2\gamma_1 t)$, whose mean on $[T, 2T]$ tends to $-\frac{1}{2}$ each, contributing $-c(z_1)$;
     - for every other $z \in S_{=}$ ($\operatorname{Im} z \notin \{0, \gamma_1, -\gamma_1\}$), $\cos(\operatorname{Im} z \cdot t)(1 - \cos(\gamma_1 t)) = \cos(\operatorname{Im} z \cdot t) - \frac{1}{2}\cos((\operatorname{Im} z - \gamma_1)t) - \frac{1}{2}\cos((\operatorname{Im} z + \gamma_1)t)$ has three nonzero frequencies, so its mean on $[T, 2T]$ tends to $0$.
     Taking $T \to \infty$ yields $(\sigma_{\max} \in S \text{ ? } c(\sigma_{\max}) : 0) - c(z_1) \ge 0$, whence $\sigma_{\max} \in S$ and $c(\sigma_{\max}) \ge c(z_1) > 0$.
   - *(Single-ordinate case)* If additionally every $z \in S_{=}$ has $\operatorname{Im} z \in \{0, \gamma_1, -\gamma_1\}$, evaluating $e^{-\sigma_{\max} t_k} w_G(t_k) \ge 0$ along $t_k := \frac{(2k + 1)\pi}{|\gamma_1|} \to +\infty$ (where $\cos(\gamma_1 t_k) = -1$ and $e^{(\operatorname{Re} z - \sigma_{\max})t_k} \to 0$ for $z \in S_{<}$) yields $(\sigma_{\max} \in S \text{ ? } c(\sigma_{\max}) : 0) - 2 c(z_1) \ge 0$, whence $\sigma_{\max} \in S$ and $c(\sigma_{\max}) \ge 2 c(z_1)$.
   - *(Corollary for zero-tax quartet multipliers)* For any $0 < a < 1/2$ and $\gamma \neq 0$, no polynomial $Q_{\text{crit}}$ with roots on $\operatorname{Re} z = 1/2$ and polynomial $P_{\text{num}}$ with roots on $\operatorname{Re} z = 1/2$ can make $W(s) = P_{\text{num}}(s) / (\operatorname{quartetOffPoly}(a, \gamma; s) \cdot Q_{\text{crit}}(s))$ have positive-definite vertical $-\logDeriv$ sections on $\sigma > 1$, because on $\operatorname{Re} z \le 1/2 + a$ its polar sum has $c(1/2 + a + i\gamma) = 1 > 0$ and $c(1/2 + a) = 0$. $\square$

### Theorem 3 (`[PROVED HERE]` — Archimedean Exterior-Square Divisor and Zero-Tax Midpoint Contraction)
For a finite family $\rho : \iota \to \mathbb{C}$, define the centered scale trace $z_\rho(t) := \sum_{i \in \iota} e^{(\rho_i - 1/2)t}$, the exterior-square scale trace $\Lambda^2 z_\rho(t) := \sum_{S \in \binom{\iota}{2}} e^{\sum_{i \in S} (\rho_i - 1/2)t}$, and the exterior-square divisor polynomial
$$P_{\Lambda^2}(\rho; s) := \prod_{S \in \binom{\iota}{2}} \Bigl(s - \Bigl(\frac{1}{2} + \sum_{i \in S} (\rho_i - 1/2)\Bigr)\Bigr).$$
1. **Adams–Newton identity (`exteriorTwoScaleTrace_eq_newton`):** Expanding $z_\rho(t)^2 = \sum_i e^{2(\rho_i - 1/2)t} + \sum_{i \neq j} e^{((\rho_i - 1/2) + (\rho_j - 1/2))t}$ and fibers of $(i, j) \mapsto \{i, j\} \in \binom{\iota}{2}$ gives $2 \Lambda^2 z_\rho(t) = z_\rho(t)^2 - z_\rho(2t)$, cancelling the diagonal $2\rho_i - 1/2$ identically.

### Theorem 4 (`[PROVED HERE]` — Full-Strip Coupled Symmetric-Square Quartet Contraction Attaining the Sharp Real-Anchor Bound)
For $b, a, \gamma \in \mathbb{R}$, define the relative-degree-zero ($\Delta\kappa = 0$) rational multiplier
$$\operatorname{coupledQuartetSymSquare}(b, a, \gamma; s) := \operatorname{midpointSymSquare}(1/2 + b, s)^2 \cdot \frac{\operatorname{quartetCritPoly}(\gamma; s)}{\operatorname{quartetOffPoly}(a, \gamma; s)}.$$
1. **Full-strip Bochner positivity (`isEvenPosDef_coupledQuartetSymSquare`):** Suppose $0 < a \le b < 1/2$ and $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$. For every $\sigma > 1$, every zero and pole of `coupledQuartetSymSquare b a γ` has real part in $\{1/2, 1/2 \pm a, 1/2 \pm b\} \subset (0, 1)$, so Laplace cosine integration on $(0, \infty)$ gives
   $$\operatorname{Re}\bigl(-\operatorname{logDeriv}(\operatorname{coupledQuartetSymSquare}(b, a, \gamma))(\sigma + iu)\bigr) = \int_0^\infty e^{-\sigma t}\, w_{b,a,\gamma}(t)\cos(tu)\,dt,$$
   where
   $$w_{b,a,\gamma}(t) = 2 e^{t/2}\Bigl(\bigl(e^{bt/2} - e^{-bt/2}\bigr)^2 + \cos(\gamma t)\bigl(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2}\bigr)^2\Bigr).$$
   For $t \ge 0$ and $0 < a \le b$, $e^{at/2} \le e^{bt/2}$ and $e^{-bt/2} \le e^{-at/2}$, so $0 \le e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2} \le e^{bt/2} - e^{-bt/2}$. Since $\cos(\gamma t) \ge -1$, $w_{b,a,\gamma}(t) \ge 2 e^{t/2}(1 + \cos(\gamma t))(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2})^2 = 4 e^{t/2}\cos^2(\gamma t / 2)(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2})^2 \ge 0$. At $b = a$, $w_{a,a,\gamma}(t) = 4 e^{t/2}\cos^2(\gamma t / 2)(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2})^2$ attains Theorem 1's sharp real-anchor bound $c(1/2 + a) = 2 = 2 c(1/2 + a + i\gamma)$ across the entire strip $0 < a < 1/2$ with zero poles at $\{0, 1\}$. By Bochner integration against a nonnegative integrable kernel (`isEvenPosDef_quartet_integral`), the vertical section is `IsEvenPosDef` for every $\sigma > 1$.
2. **Single-system $\Delta\kappa = 0$ cross-channel contractions (`CompletedPositiveSystem.contractComplexQuartet_of_doubleRealAnchor`, `CompletedPositiveSystem.contractComplexQuartet_of_singleDeepRealAnchor`):**
   - If $(F, \Lambda) \in \operatorname{CompletedPositiveSystem}\;\kappa\;d$ satisfies $2 \le \operatorname{ord}_{1/2 + b}(\Lambda)$ and $\Lambda(1/2 + a + i\gamma) = 0$ ($0 < a \le b < 1/2, \gamma \neq 0$), then multiplying $F$ by `coupledQuartetSymSquare b a γ` produces an entire $\Lambda_c$ inside $\operatorname{CompletedPositiveSystem}\;\kappa\;d$ ($\Delta\kappa = 0$) contracting both real pairs at $1/2 \pm b$ and the complex quartet at $1/2 \pm a \pm i\gamma$ onto $\{1/2, 1/2 \pm i\gamma\}$.
   - Moreover, even a **simple** real zero $\Lambda(1/2 + b) = 0$ with $2a \le b < 1/2$ ($0 < a < 1/4, \gamma \neq 0$) suffices at $\Delta\kappa = 0$ via $\operatorname{midpointSymSquare}(1/2 + b, s) \cdot \operatorname{quartetCritPoly}(\gamma; s) / \operatorname{quartetOffPoly}(a, \gamma; s)$, because $e^{bt/2} - e^{-bt/2} \ge e^{at} - e^{-at} = (e^{at/2} + e^{-at/2})(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2}) \ge 2(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2})$, so $(e^{bt/2} - e^{-bt/2})^2 + 2\cos(\gamma t)(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2})^2 \ge (4 - 2)(e^{at/2} - e^{-at/2})^2 \ge 0$. $\square$
