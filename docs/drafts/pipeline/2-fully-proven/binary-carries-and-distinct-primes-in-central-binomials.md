### 2. Smooth-integer division trees and top-octave parity aliasing
Let $\Psi(M,y)$ count $y$-smooth integers in $[1,M]$ and $\Psi_{\mathrm{odd}}(M,y)$ count odd $y$-smooth integers in $[1,M]$. For $y\ge 2$, doubling $m\mapsto 2m$ bijects $y$-smooth integers in $[1,\lfloor M/2\rfloor]$ onto even $y$-smooth integers in $[1,M]$, so $\Psi(M,y)=\Psi(\lfloor M/2\rfloor,y)+\Psi_{\mathrm{odd}}(M,y)$. Setting $M=2n$ and $M=n$ yields for all $n\ge 0$, $y\ge 2$ (without requiring $2n<(y+1)^2$) $[PROVED HERE]$:
$$2\Psi(n,y)-\Psi(2n,y)=\sum_{\substack{1\le j\le 2n\\ P^+(j)\le y}}(-1)^j=\Psi(\lfloor n/2\rfloor,y)-\bigl(\Psi_{\mathrm{odd}}(2n,y)-\Psi_{\mathrm{odd}}(n,y)\bigr).$$
Grouping each $j=2^a u$ by its odd $y$-smooth part $u$ ($0\le a\le\lfloor\log_2(M/u)\rfloor$) gives $\sum_{a=0}^L(-1)^{2^a u}=L-1$, hence
$$\Phi(M,y):=\sum_{\substack{1\le j\le M\\ P^+(j)\le y}}(-1)^j=\sum_{\substack{1\le u\le M\\ 2\nmid u,\,P^+(u)\le y}}\bigl(\lfloor\log_2(M/u)\rfloor-1\bigr).$$
Thus the $2$-adic tree cancels identically on $u\in(n/2,n]$, assigns $-1$ on $(n,2n]$, and leaves strictly positive surplus $\lfloor\log_2(2n/u)\rfloor-1\ge 1$ on $u\le n/2$. Partitioning by $P^+(j)\in(z,y]$ for $2\le z\le y$ (every such prime is odd, preserving $(-1)^j$) yields the exact Buchstab tree law $\Phi(M,y)=\Phi(M,z)+\sum_{z<p\le y}\Phi(\lfloor M/p\rfloor,p)$ with base $\Phi(M,2)=\lfloor\log_2 M\rfloor-1$ ($M\ge 1$). Summing over $k<2N$ by induction on $N$ gives
$$\sum_{k=0}^{2N-1}\bigl(2\Psi(\lfloor k/2\rfloor,y)-\Psi(2\lfloor k/2\rfloor,y)\bigr)=\sum_{\substack{1\le j<2N\\ P^+(j)\le y}}(-1)^j(2N-j)+\Psi_{\mathrm{odd}}(2N-1,y).$$
While $\Psi(x,y)+\sum_{m=y+1}^B g(m)\lfloor x/m\rfloor=x$ on $y<x\le B<(y+1)^2$ uniquely forces $g(m)=\mathbf{1}_{m\text{ prime}}$ by triangular induction, the central-binomial carry $\lfloor 2n/m\rfloor\bmod 2$ satisfies $\lfloor 2n/(2r-1)\rfloor\bmod 2=\lfloor 2n/(2r)\rfloor\bmod 2=\mathbf{1}_{r\le n}$ for all $r\in(\lfloor N/2\rfloor,N]$ and $n\le N$, aliasing odd primes in $(N+1,2N]$ with adjacent even composites.
