## Setup

Let `k` be a field, `A` a unital associative `k`-algebra with an increasing filtration

\[
 k1=A_{\le0}\subseteq A_{\le1}\subseteq\cdots,
 \qquad A_{\le m}A_{\le n}\subseteq A_{\le m+n}.
\]

Let `M` be a left `A`-module and `V subset M` a finite-dimensional seed. Define

\[
 P_n=A_{\le n}\otimes_kV,
 \qquad \operatorname{ev}_n(a\otimes v)=av,
 \qquad R_n=\ker\operatorname{ev}_n,
 \qquad K_n=A_{\le n}V.
\]

Identify `P_n` with its natural subspace of `P_{n+1}` and set degree `-1` terms to zero.

## Master theorem

For every `n >= 0`:

1. `P_n/R_n` is naturally isomorphic to `K_n`;
2. `R_{n+1} cap P_n=R_n` and `K_n subseteq K_{n+1}`;
3. there is a natural short exact sequence
   \[
   0\longrightarrow R_n/R_{n-1}
   \longrightarrow P_n/P_{n-1}
   \longrightarrow K_n/K_{n-1}
   \longrightarrow0;
   \tag{1}
   \]
4. the killed-old-freedom term is zero:
   \[
   (P_{n-1}\cap R_n)/R_{n-1}=0.
   \tag{2}
   \]

When all pieces are finite-dimensional, `(1)` gives the exact budget

\[
 (\dim R_n-\dim R_{n-1})+(\dim K_n-\dim K_{n-1})
 =\dim(A_{\le n}/A_{\le n-1})\dim V.
 \tag{3}
\]

### Proof

The first isomorphism theorem identifies `P_n/R_n` with the image `K_n`. Evaluation on `P_n` is
the restriction of evaluation on `P_{n+1}`, so

\[
 R_{n+1}\cap P_n=R_n.
\]

Consequently the induced map `P_n/R_n -> P_{n+1}/R_{n+1}` is injective and agrees with
`K_n subseteq K_{n+1}`.

Consider the surjection

\[
 P_n/P_{n-1}\longrightarrow K_n/K_{n-1}.
\]

Its kernel consists of classes of `p in P_n` whose evaluation lies in `K_{n-1}`. Choose
`q in P_{n-1}` with the same evaluation. Then `p-q in R_n`. Hence the kernel equals

\[
 (R_n+P_{n-1})/P_{n-1}
 \cong R_n/(R_n\cap P_{n-1})
 =R_n/R_{n-1}.
\]

This proves `(1)`. Equation `(2)` is the same intersection identity. Taking dimensions proves
`(3)`. QED.

## Degree-one plateau theorem

Assume

\[
 A_{\le n+1}=A_{\le n}+A_{\le1}A_{\le n}.
 \tag{4}
\]

Then

\[
 K_{n+1}=K_n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad K_n=AV.
 \tag{5}
\]

Thus one plateau forces permanent stabilization. If `D=dim AV<infinity` and `r=dim V`,
stabilization occurs by depth at most `D-r`.

### Proof

If `K_{n+1}=K_n`, equation `(4)` implies `A_{\le1}K_n subseteq K_n`. Since `A` is generated by
`A_{\le1}`, `K_n` is `A`-stable and contains `V`, so `K_n=AV`. The converse is immediate. Before
stabilization dimension grows by at least one at each step, giving the bound. QED.

The degree-one hypothesis is necessary: for `A=k[x]` with weighted degree `deg x=2`, the regular
cyclic module has `K_0=K_1` but `K_2` is larger.

## Consequences

1. Longer words under one fixed action never perform selection. A nonzero killed-freedom term
   requires changing action, module, quotient, admission domain, or source law.
2. Word depth is not intrinsic to a bare algebra. It is invariant only under equivalences
   preserving generators, costs, action, and seed.
3. Finite-dimensional rank growth saturates. Long quantum complexity needs spectral weights,
   metric/path data, growing effective dimension, or a changing source frame.
4. In differential algebra, order alone leaves unbounded polynomial degree. A meaningful finite
   budget needs at least order--degree bifiltration; Kolchin growth is not linear Krylov growth.
