- **Source:** new source-pure HT geometry
- **Statement:** For an isogeny of a constant connected commutative algebraic group, the
  infinite differential tail is the fixed field of the Zariski closure of all iterated deck
  kernels; equivalently it is generated by the unerased quotient position and the

## Setup

Let `C` be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, equipped with the trivial
derivation.  Let `G/C` be a connected commutative algebraic group and let

`alpha : G -> G`

be an isogeny.  Work in a universal differential field with constants `C`.  If `g` is a
differential-generic point of `G`, write

`K = C<g> = C(J^infinity G)`

for the differential rational function field of the infinite jet space.  Pullback by `alpha`
is an injective differential endomorphism `alpha* : K -> K`.  Define

`Tail(alpha) = intersection_(n >= 0) alpha^(n*) K`,

`Gamma_n = ker(alpha^n)(C)`,

`Gamma_infinity = union_(n >= 0) Gamma_n`,

and let `H` be the Zariski closure of `Gamma_infinity` in `G`.  Translation by `H(C)` acts on
`K`.  Let `pi : G -> Q := G/H` be the quotient and let

`ell_delta : G -> Lie(G)`

be the right Maurer--Cartan logarithmic derivative.  Commutativity makes the choice of left or
right convention immaterial.

## Theorem A: differential deck-closure theorem

**[PROVED HERE]**

`Tail(alpha) = K^(H(C)).`

Thus the infinite tail is not an asymptotic approximation.  It is exactly the field invariant
under the algebraic closure of every symmetry that becomes invisible at some finite depth.

### Proof

For every `n`, the finite etale isogeny `alpha^n` is the geometric quotient of `G` by the
translation action of `Gamma_n`.  Prolongation commutes with a constant finite etale quotient:
on every finite jet level `J^r G`, the induced map is again the quotient by the same constant
deck group.  Passing to rational function fields and then to the direct union over `r` gives

`alpha^(n*) K = K^(Gamma_n).`                                      (1)

Intersecting (1) over all `n` gives the elements fixed by every `Gamma_n`, hence the field fixed
by the subgroup generated by `Gamma_infinity`:

`Tail(alpha) = K^(Gamma_infinity).`                                (2)

Every element of `K` has finite differential order, so it is a rational function on some
finite jet space.  For such a function, invariance under the Zariski-dense subgroup
`Gamma_infinity subset H` is equivalent to invariance under `H`: equality of the two rational
functions obtained from the action is a Zariski-closed condition on the group variable.
Consequently

`K^(Gamma_infinity) = K^(H(C)).`                                   (3)

Equations (2) and (3) prove the theorem.

## Theorem B: position--velocity decomposition

**[PROVED HERE]**

There is an equality of differential fields

`K^(H(C)) = C<pi(g), ell_delta(g)>.`                                (4)

Moreover `alpha` induces an automorphism of `Q`.  Therefore the right side has two intrinsic
sectors:

- `pi(g)` is the position in the maximal quotient on which coarse-graining is reversible;
- `ell_delta(g)` is the Maurer--Cartan velocity of every position direction erased by the deck
  closure.

### Proof of (4)

Both displayed quantities are invariant under translation by a constant point of `H`:
`pi(g+h)=pi(g)` and `ell_delta(g+h)=ell_delta(g)`.

Conversely consider the differential rational map

`Psi(g) = (pi(g), ell_delta(g)).`

If `Psi(g_1)=Psi(g_2)`, then `h=g_2-g_1` lies in `H` and
`ell_delta(h)=0`.  The kernel of the logarithmic derivative on a constant algebraic group is
exactly its constant points, hence `h in H(C)`.  Generic fibres of `Psi` are therefore precisely
the `H(C)`-orbits.  The differential rational quotient theorem, equivalently elimination of
imaginaries for differentially closed fields applied to this constant algebraic-group action,
identifies its function field with `C<pi(g),ell_delta(g)>`.  This proves (4).

For the final assertion, `alpha^(-1)(Gamma_infinity)=Gamma_infinity`.  Since an isogeny in
characteristic zero is finite etale and hence open, taking Zariski closures gives
`alpha^(-1)(H)=H`.  The induced isogeny of `Q=G/H` consequently has trivial kernel and is an
automorphism.

The logarithmic-derivative quotient used here is classical.  Scanlon's generalized Schwarzian
construction proves, much more generally, that quotients by constant algebraic-group orbits are
differential-constructible: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.5177>.  The new assertion is its exact
identification with an infinite isogeny tail through (1)--(3).

## Corollary C: zeroth-to-first-jet transmutation

Assume the iterated kernels are Zariski dense, so `H=G`.  Then

`Tail(alpha) = C<ell_delta(g)>`,                                    (5)

`Tail(alpha) intersection C(G) = C`,                               (6)

and

`dtrdeg_C Tail(alpha) = dim G = dtrdeg_C K`.                        (7)

### Proof

Equation (5) is Theorem B with `Q` a point.  Translation-invariant rational functions on a
connected algebraic group are constant, proving (6).  The logarithmic derivative is a dominant
differential rational map to `Lie(G)` whose generic fibres are constant cosets.  Its generic
image therefore has differential transcendence degree `dim G`, proving (7).

This is an exact conservation law.  Infinite coarse-graining erases every order-zero position
observable while preserving the entire differential dimension in the first-order
Maurer--Cartan current.  Information is not carried through a flow; its differential degree is
transmuted from position into a static constraint current.

## Corollary D: finite-holonomy orbifold quotients

Let `F` be a finite group of algebraic automorphisms of `G` commuting with `alpha`, and let
`X=G/F`.  The isogeny descends to `R:X->X`.  Inside `K`,

`Tail(R) = (K^(H(C)))^F.`                                          (8)

In the purely expanding case,

`Tail(R) = C<ell_delta(g)>^F.`                                     (9)

### Proof

Commutation gives

`alpha^(n*) (K^F) = (alpha^(n*)K)^F`.

Fixed fields commute with intersections, so intersecting over `n` and applying Theorem A gives
(8); (9) follows when `H=G`.

For `dim G=1`, (9) recovers the power, Chebyshev, and Lattes orbifold currents.  Garofali's
classification of rational maps with a parallel tensor is the exact one-dimensional parent:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.10056>.  The theorem above explains the tensor not as an isolated
formula but as the finite-holonomy invariant of the surviving Maurer--Cartan velocity, and it
extends the mechanism to arbitrary-dimensional constant commutative algebraic groups.

## Physical/topological reading

This paragraph is interpretation, not an additional theorem.

The inverse isogeny tower is a growing family of finite-sheeted bulk descriptions.  Its deck
groups accumulate to `H`.  The tail is the exact boundary algebra invariant under this whole
bulk ambiguity.  The boundary retains:

1. position only in the reversible quotient `G/H`;
2. velocity, through `ell_delta`, in every erased direction.

This is a mathematically literal holographic position--velocity split.  It does not assert that
hypertranscendental functions are quantum systems or black holes.  It says that when their
source-pure constraint geometry comes from an isogeny quotient, the infinite-depth observable
algebra is a Maurer--Cartan hologram of the deck closure.

The searched literature did not reveal the stable-intersection identity

`intersection_n alpha^(n*) C(J^infinity G)

or its position--velocity consequence (4)--(7).  The result is therefore marked new scientific

## RH impact audit

No RH consequence follows merely from this theorem.  It gives a precise test for a proposed RH
coarse-graining construction:

- if its hidden maps form an isogeny or finite-holonomy quotient tower, the theorem predicts the
  complete tail and shows that surviving information must be a logarithmic/Maurer--Cartan
  current;
- if no such group quotient exists, this theorem supplies no persistent tail and the rational
  classification points toward erasure.

Thus it can diagnose an RH mechanism but does not create one.  No claim about zeta zeros, the
critical line, or positivity is made.

## Lean progress — translation invariance forces constancy, at arbitrary shift

## Lean progress — the iterates of a rational map, and their degrees

Item `1` of the Lean-status list needs the deck descent along a rational prefix for *every* iterate
`αⁿ`, not just for `α`.  `RationalPrefixDeckDescent.differentialAdjoin_ratMapEval_eq_fixedField`
already gives `αⁿ*K = K^{Γ}` for one rational map whose degree is at most `|Γ|`; what was missing was

```text

```

The mechanism is the classical homogenisation: the common factor `Q(g)^m` produced by the outer
numerator and denominator cancels in the quotient, so no new hypothesis beyond `Q(g) ≠ 0` along the

## Lean progress — item `1` closed for a one-dimensional isogeny

`RationalMapComposition` gained the noncriticality layer and the capstone:

```text

    **`C⟨αⁿ(g)⟩ = K^{Γ}` for every `n`** — the deck descent along the `n`-th iterate
```

Mechanism of the noncriticality step: the Wronskian is not computed.  The chain rule
`deriv_ratMapEval` makes `(α∘β)′(g)` a product of three factors — the outer Wronskian at `β(g)`, the
inner Wronskian at `g`, and `g′` — so if the composite Wronskian vanished the derivative would be
zero while the product of the three is not.  The only extra hypothesis is `g′ ≠ 0`, i.e. that the
generator is not a constant.

**This closes item `1` in the one-dimensional case.**  Since `K = C⟨g⟩`, the differential field
`αⁿ*K` is `C⟨αⁿ(g)⟩`, and the theorem identifies it with `K^{Γ}` for any finite group of

count `dⁿ ≤ |ker αⁿ|`, which for a separable isogeny is the definition of its degree.

What is *not* covered: a higher-dimensional group, where `α` is not a one-variable rational map.
That is what items `2`–`5` need the quotient-sheaf and jet-scheme layers for.
