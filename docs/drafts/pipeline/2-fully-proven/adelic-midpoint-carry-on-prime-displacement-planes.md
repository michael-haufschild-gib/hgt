# Shared adelic midpoint encoding on disjoint-prime-support displacement planes

## 1. Rational section representatives and adelic skew rigidity

For an integer spline divisor $v\in\mathbb{Z}^N$, write $d(v)=\mathrm{generator}(v)\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0}$ and $A(v)=\mathrm{radius}(v)\in\mathbb{R}$. Each integer coordinate $z\in\mathbb{Z}$ indexes the rational section representative $\mathrm{sectionRep}(v,z)=d(v)z\in\mathbb{Q}$, with inverse $\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v,s)=(s/d(v)).\mathrm{num}$ on the fractional ideal $d(v)\mathbb{Z}=\{s\in\mathbb{Q}:(s/d(v)).\mathrm{den}=1\}$ (`inSectionLattice`). For $s\ne 0$, membership in $d(v)\mathbb{Z}$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{ratPrimeVal}_p(d(v))\le\mathrm{ratPrimeVal}_p(s)$ at every prime $p$ (`inSectionLattice_iff_primeValuations`). The finite small-section set $\mathrm{rationalSections}(v)$ consists of $s\in d(v)\mathbb{Z}$ with $|s|\le e^{A(v)}$ (`mem_rationalSections_iff`) and corresponds bijectively to $\mathrm{divisorSections}(v)$ (`rationalSectionEquiv`).

- **Midpoint-only rational-section rigidity.** At translations $(k,2m)$ and $(2k,m)$, the midpoint divisor $(2m)(kb)=m(2kb)$ and its rational section space $\mathrm{rationalSections}(2mkb)$ coincide identically. Whenever $Q(b)>0$ and $Q(b+a)\ne Q(b-a)$, no single map $\Phi:\mathbb{Q}^2\to\mathbb{Q}^2$ depending only on midpoint section representatives can map $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mkb)^2$ into $\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb+a))\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb-a))$ with $\log\mathrm{maxFibre}\le\varepsilon m^2$ across all large $k$ (`not_exists_midpointOnly_subquadratic_rationalSectionMap`).
- **Archimedean-versus-finite skew cancellation.** On $b_p=\mathrm{primePair}(p)$, bounded pole quadrature error and uniform finite jet debit give $\lim_{m\to\infty}A(m(kb_p))/m^2=k^2 R_\infty(b_p)$ with $R_\infty(b_p)\ge c_0\sqrt{p}-4J$ ($c_0=2I_{-1/2}I_{+1/2}>0$), while $(A(mb_p)-\log d(mb_p))/m^2\to Q(b_p)=o(\sqrt{p})$. Hence $\lim_{m\to\infty}\log d(m(kb_p))/m^2=k^2(R_\infty(b_p)-Q(b_p))\asymp k^2\sqrt{p}$. Along $(2mb_p,3mb_p,4mb_p)$, the finite-place generator skew $\frac{1}{2}(\log d(4mb_p)-\log d(2mb_p))$ exceeds $c_0\sqrt{p}\,m^2>7|Q(b_p)|m^2$ eventually for all sufficiently large primes $p$ (`eventually_primePair_finitePlace_skew_overshoots`), and any centered carry schedule exceeding $7Q(b)m^2$ overflows $\mathrm{divisorSections}(4mb)$ (`eventually_not_centeredCarry_of_skew_gt_seven`). Both finite-place valuations and the archimedean place must enter the carry order.

## 2. One explicit adelic ternary carry rule and valuation-paid decoder

Define the pre-cardinality Arakelov degree $\widehat{\deg}(v)=A(v)-\log d(v)$ (`arakelovDegree`), the outer adelic skew $\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)=\frac{1}{2}(\widehat{\deg}(v_1)-\widehat{\deg}(v_2))$ (`outerAdelicSkew`, independent of the midpoint $v_0$ and of all section cardinalities), the ternary carry order $\tau(v_1,v_2)=\lfloor|\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)|/\log 3\rfloor_+$ (`ternaryCarryOrder`), and half-modulus $K_\tau=(3^\tau-1)/2$ (`ternaryHalfMod`, $2K_\tau+1=3^\tau$).

- **Encoder and decoder.** Given $(s_1,s_2)\in\mathbb{Q}^2$, set $z_i=\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v_0,s_i)$. When $\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)\ge 0$, set $(w_1,w_2)=\mathrm{centeredCarry}_{K_\tau}(z_1,z_2)=(\mathrm{balRem}_{K_\tau}(z_1)+3^\tau z_2,\mathrm{balQuot}_{K_\tau}(z_1))$; when $\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)<0$, swap the two output coordinates (`orientedTernaryEncode`). The encoder $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}(v_0,v_1,v_2)$ outputs $(d(v_1)w_1,d(v_2)w_2)\in d(v_1)\mathbb{Z}\times d(v_2)\mathbb{Z}$, and $\mathrm{adelicSectionDecode}(v_0,v_1,v_2)$ applies the inverse swap and $\mathrm{centeredCarry}_{K_\tau}$ (`orientedTernaryDecode`) to $(\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v_1,t_1),\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v_2,t_2))$. They form exact mutual inverses between $(d(v_0)\mathbb{Z})^2$ and $d(v_1)\mathbb{Z}\times d(v_2)\mathbb{Z}$ for every $(v_0,v_1,v_2)$ (`adelicSectionDecode_encode`, `adelicSectionEncode_decode`).
- **Valuations paying for every ambiguous digit and comparison with two-place obstructions.** Write $w_{\mathrm{wide}},w_{\mathrm{narrow}}$ for the wide and narrow integer output coordinates and $t_{\mathrm{wide}}$ for the wide rational section representative:
  1. For fixed $z_2$ and narrow quotient $q$, the narrow slice $\{z_1\in\mathbb{Z}:w_{\mathrm{narrow}}(z_1,z_2)=q\}$ is $[3^\tau q-K_\tau,3^\tau q+K_\tau]$ of exact size $3^\tau$ (`narrowSlice_card_and_mem`). Two inputs share the same carry digit $\mathrm{balRem}_{K_\tau}(z_1)$ iff $w_{\mathrm{wide}}\equiv w'_{\mathrm{wide}}\pmod{3^\tau}$ (`carryDigit_eq_iff_wideCoord_modEq`), equivalently iff either $t_{\mathrm{wide}}=t'_{\mathrm{wide}}$ or $\mathrm{ratPrimeVal}_3(t'_{\mathrm{wide}}-t_{\mathrm{wide}})\ge\mathrm{ratPrimeVal}_3(d_{\mathrm{wide}})+\tau$ (`adelicCarryDigit_paid_iff_threeAdic_valuation`).
  2. Linear conservation $w_{\mathrm{wide}}+3^\tau w_{\mathrm{narrow}}=z_1+3^\tau z_2$ holds across all moduli (`orientedTernary_linear_conservation`). Once the single $3$-adic valuation on $t'_{\mathrm{wide}}-t_{\mathrm{wide}}$ pays for $\mathrm{balRem}_{K_\tau}(z_1)$, for every modulus $u\ge 1$ coprime to $3$ (all prime powers $q^r$ at all primes $q\ne 3$ simultaneously), $(w_1\bmod u,w_2\bmod u)$ preserves and recovers $(z_1\bmod u,z_2\bmod u)$ with zero $u$-adic precision loss (`orientedTernary_coprime_zeroLoss_recovery`), escaping the quadratic bill forced when either output stores two-place congruences independently (`adelicRayMap_precision_comparison`).
  3. At $q=3$, for every $r\ge 0$, $(w_{\mathrm{wide}}\bmod 3^{r+\tau},w_{\mathrm{narrow}}\bmod 3^{r-\min(r,\tau)})$ uniquely recovers $(z_1\bmod 3^r,z_2\bmod 3^r)$ with total ternary valuation budget $(r+\tau)+(r-\tau)=2r$ for $r\ge\tau$ (`orientedTernary_threeAdic_asymmetric_recovery`), whereas demanding symmetric precision $3^{r+k}$ on both outputs forces $k=\tau\sim 6Q(b)m^2/\log 3>\frac{5}{2}Q(b)m^2/\log 3$ (`adelicRayMap_precision_comparison`).
- **Asymptotic midpoint validity and global RH equivalence.** Since $\tau(mv_1,mv_2)\log 3/m^2\to\frac{1}{2}|Q(v_1)-Q(v_2)|$ (`tendsto_ternaryCarryScale`), whenever $v_1=v_2=v_0$ (`adelicSectionEncode_self_mapsRationalSections`) or $Q(v_0),Q(v_1),Q(v_2)>0$ with $2Q(v_0)<Q(v_1)+Q(v_2)$, $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}$ maps $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mv_0)^2$ injectively (`maxFibre = 1`) into $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mv_1)\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(mv_2)$ for all sufficiently large $m$ across all skew signs (`eventually_adelicSectionEncode_mapsRationalSections`, `eventually_adelicSectionEncode_midpoint_ray`). On the fixed two-atom positive background $b$, RH is equivalent to $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}$ succeeding on every displacement (`riemannHypothesis_iff_singleRule_adelicSectionEncode`, `exists_prime_background_singleRule_adelicSectionEncode`).

## 3. Disjoint-prime-support displacement planes and promotion equivalences

- **Unconditional solution on separated prime-supported planes and rank-$r$ subspaces.** Fix the two-atom positive prime background $(p,b)$ (`exists_prime_background_subquadraticMidpointTest`) and any separation factor $R\ge 2$. Let $k_0=\max(k(4R),1)$ from `positive_integer_window_moments (4R)`. By short-interval prime avoidance (`eventually_card_primes_Ioc_ge`), for sufficiently large $M>p+1$ both $(M,2M]$ and $(2RM,4RM]$ contain at least $k_0+1$ primes. Kernel vectors over $\mathbb{Q}$ of the first $k_0$ power-sum equations on those two prime shells clear denominators to linearly independent zero-sum signed divisors $a_1,a_2\in\mathbb{Z}^{4RM}$ supported solely on disjoint finite sets of primes $>p+1$ separated by factor $>R$ (`exists_primeShell_momentKernel_vector`, `exists_disjoint_primeSupported_signed_plane`). Every $(c_1,c_2)\ne(0,0)$ gives $Q(c_1a_1+c_2a_2)>0$. Consequently, for every $(c_1,c_2)\in\mathbb{Z}^2$, every sufficiently large translation $k$, and all sufficiently large $m$, the single rule $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}$ encodes $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mkb_{\mathrm{pad}})^2\hookrightarrow\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb_{\mathrm{pad}}+a))\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb_{\mathrm{pad}}-a))$ with `maxFibre = 1` ($\log\mathrm{maxFibre}=0\le\varepsilon m^2$) and forces $0\le\mathrm{midpointExcess}(kb_{\mathrm{pad}},a,m)$ eventually and $0\le Q(a)$ (`exists_prime_background_shared_adelicCarry_plane`). Partitioning $(M,2^rM]$ into $r$ dyadic prime shells extends the same construction to rank-$r$ lattices $\bigoplus_{s=1}^r\mathbb{Z}a_s$ for every $r\ge 1$ (`exists_disjoint_primeSupported_signed_subspace`).
- **Sharpness of plane promotion and full prime-support index.** If RH fails, positive zero width produces a prime peak $2\operatorname{Re}F(\log q)>2\operatorname{Re}F(0)+1$; approximating $(T,T+\log q)$ by distinct primes $(p_1,p_2)$ above any cutoff $P_0$ via `exists_log_prime_avoiding_small_finset` yields two disjoint two-prime differences $a_1=e_{p_1}-e_{p_2}$ and $a_2=e_{p_3}-e_{p_4}$ with $Q(a_1)<0$ (`exists_twoPrime_diff_neg_integerProbe_of_not_rh`). Thus promoting subquadratic-fibre midpoint encoding over the fixed background $(p,b)$ to all disjoint-prime-support signed planes — and already to two-prime-difference planes — is unconditionally equivalent to RH (`riemannHypothesis_iff_all_disjoint_primeSupported_signed_planes`). Moreover, prime-supported Gram submatrices carry the full off-line zero index `(rightZeros zetaZeroConfig).encard` (`rightZeros_encard_eq_iSup_primeSubmatrix`).
