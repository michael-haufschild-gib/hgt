# Shared adelic midpoint encoding on disjoint-prime-support displacement planes

## 1. Rational section representatives and adelic skew rigidity

For an integer spline divisor $v\in\mathbb{Z}^N$, write $d(v)=\mathrm{generator}(v)\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0}$ and $A(v)=\mathrm{radius}(v)\in\mathbb{R}$. Each integer coordinate $z\in\mathbb{Z}$ indexes the rational section representative $\mathrm{sectionRep}(v,z)=d(v)z\in\mathbb{Q}$, with inverse $\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v,s)=(s/d(v)).\mathrm{num}$ on the fractional ideal $d(v)\mathbb{Z}=\{s\in\mathbb{Q}:(s/d(v)).\mathrm{den}=1\}$. For $s\ne 0$, membership in $d(v)\mathbb{Z}$ is equivalent to $v_p(d(v))\le v_p(s)$ at every prime $p$. The finite small-section set $\mathrm{rationalSections}(v)$ consists of $s\in d(v)\mathbb{Z}$ with $|s|\le e^{A(v)}$ and corresponds bijectively to $\mathrm{divisorSections}(v)$.

- **Midpoint-only rational-section rigidity.** At translations $(k,2m)$ and $(2k,m)$, the midpoint divisor $(2m)(kb)=m(2kb)$ and its rational section space $\mathrm{rationalSections}(2mkb)$ coincide identically. Whenever $Q(b)>0$ and $Q(b+a)\ne Q(b-a)$, no single map $\Phi:\mathbb{Q}^2\to\mathbb{Q}^2$ depending only on midpoint section representatives can map $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mkb)^2$ into $\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb+a))\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb-a))$ with $\log\mathrm{maxFibre}\le\varepsilon m^2$ across all large $k$.
- **Archimedean-versus-finite skew cancellation.** On $b_p=\mathrm{primePair}(p)$, bounded pole quadrature error and uniform finite jet debit give $\liminf_{m\to\infty}A(mb_p)/m^2\ge c_0\sqrt{p}-C_0$ with $c_0=2I_{-1/2}I_{+1/2}>0$, while $(A(mb_p)-\log d(mb_p))/m^2\to Q(b_p)=o(\sqrt{p})$. Hence $\liminf_{m\to\infty}\log d(mb_p)/m^2\ge c_0\sqrt{p}-C_0-Q(b_p)\asymp\sqrt{p}$. Along $(2mb_p,3mb_p,4mb_p)$, any centered carry whose modulus scales with the finite-place generator skew $\frac{1}{2}\log(d(4mb_p)/d(2mb_p))\asymp 6c_0\sqrt{p}\,m^2$ overshoots the admissible carry window $(5Q(b_p),7Q(b_p))=o(\sqrt{p})$ for all sufficiently large primes $p$. Both finite-place valuations and the archimedean place must enter the carry order.

## 2. One explicit adelic ternary carry rule and valuation-paid decoder

Define the pre-rounding Arakelov degree $\widehat{\deg}(v)=A(v)-\log d(v)$, the outer adelic skew $\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)=\frac{1}{2}(\widehat{\deg}(v_1)-\widehat{\deg}(v_2))$ (independent of the midpoint $v_0$ and of all section cardinalities), the ternary carry order $\tau(v_1,v_2)=\lfloor|\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)|/\log 3\rfloor_+$, and half-modulus $K_\tau=(3^\tau-1)/2$ ($2K_\tau+1=3^\tau$).

- **Encoder and decoder.** Given $(s_1,s_2)\in\mathbb{Q}^2$, set $z_i=\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v_0,s_i)$. When $\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)\ge 0$, set $(w_1,w_2)=\mathrm{centeredCarry}_{K_\tau}(z_1,z_2)=(\mathrm{balRem}_{K_\tau}(z_1)+3^\tau z_2,\mathrm{balQuot}_{K_\tau}(z_1))$; when $\Delta_{\mathrm{skew}}(v_1,v_2)<0$, swap the two output coordinates. The encoder $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}(v_0,v_1,v_2)$ outputs $(d(v_1)w_1,d(v_2)w_2)\in d(v_1)\mathbb{Z}\times d(v_2)\mathbb{Z}$, and $\mathrm{adelicSectionDecode}(v_0,v_1,v_2)$ applies the inverse swap and $\mathrm{centeredCarry}_{K_\tau}$ to $(\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v_1,t_1),\mathrm{sectionCoord}(v_2,t_2))$. They form exact mutual inverses between $d(v_0)\mathbb{Z}\times d(v_0)\mathbb{Z}$ and $d(v_1)\mathbb{Z}\times d(v_2)\mathbb{Z}$ for every $(v_0,v_1,v_2)$.
- **Valuations paying for every ambiguous digit and comparison with two-place obstructions.** Write $w_{\mathrm{wide}},w_{\mathrm{narrow}}$ for the wide and narrow integer output coordinates:
  1. For fixed $z_2$ and narrow quotient $q$, the narrow slice $\{z_1\in\mathbb{Z}:w_{\mathrm{narrow}}(z_1,z_2)=q\}$ is $[3^\tau q-K_\tau,3^\tau q+K_\tau]$ of exact size $3^\tau$. Two inputs share the same carry remainder $\mathrm{balRem}_{K_\tau}(z_1)$ if and only if $w_{\mathrm{wide}}\equiv w'_{\mathrm{wide}}\pmod{3^\tau}$.
  2. Linear conservation $w_{\mathrm{wide}}+3^\tau w_{\mathrm{narrow}}=z_1+3^\tau z_2$ holds across all moduli. Once the single $3$-adic residue $w_{\mathrm{wide}}\bmod 3^\tau$ pays for $\mathrm{balRem}_{K_\tau}(z_1)$, for every modulus $u\ge 1$ coprime to $3$ (including all prime powers $q^r$ at arbitrarily many primes $q\ne 3$ simultaneously), $(w_1\bmod u,w_2\bmod u)$ uniquely determines $(z_1\bmod u,z_2\bmod u)$ with zero $u$-adic precision loss, bypassing the quadratic cost forced when either output stores two-place congruences independently.
  3. At $q=3$, for every $r\ge 0$, $(w_{\mathrm{wide}}\bmod 3^{r+\tau},w_{\mathrm{narrow}}\bmod 3^{r-\min(r,\tau)})$ uniquely determines $(z_1\bmod 3^r,z_2\bmod 3^r)$, with total $3$-adic valuation budget $(r+\tau)+(r-\tau)=2r$ for $r\ge\tau$ (zero net loss, contrasting with the $\frac{5}{2}Q(b)m^2$ loss under symmetric precision $(3^{r+k},3^{r+k})$).
- **Asymptotic midpoint validity and global RH equivalence.** Since $\tau(mv_1,mv_2)\log 3/m^2\to\frac{1}{2}|Q(v_1)-Q(v_2)|$, whenever $v_1=v_2=v_0$ or $Q(v_0),Q(v_1),Q(v_2)>0$ with $2Q(v_0)<Q(v_1)+Q(v_2)$, $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}$ maps $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mv_0)^2$ injectively (`maxFibre = 1`) into $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mv_1)\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(mv_2)$ for all sufficiently large $m$ (whether $Q(v_1)-Q(v_2)$ is positive, negative, or zero). On the fixed two-atom positive background $b$, RH is equivalent to $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}$ mapping $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mkb)^2$ into $\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb+a))\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb-a))$ cofinally in $m$ at cofinal translations $k$ for all $a$.

## 3. Disjoint-prime-support displacement planes and promotion equivalences

- **Unconditional solution on separated prime-supported planes (and rank-$r$ lattices).** Fix the two-atom positive prime background $(p,b)$ (`exists_prime_background_subquadraticMidpointTest`) and any separation factor $R\ge 2$. Let $k_0=\max(k(4R),1)$ from `positive_integer_window_moments (4R)`. By Chebyshev's $\theta$-bound, for sufficiently large $M>p+1$ both $(M,2M]$ and $(2RM,4RM]$ contain at least $k_0+1$ primes. Kernel vectors over $\mathbb{Q}$ of the first $k_0$ power-sum equations on those two prime sets clear denominators to linearly independent zero-sum signed divisors $a_1,a_2\in\mathbb{Z}^{4RM}$ supported solely on disjoint finite sets of primes $>p+1$ separated by factor $>R$. Every $(c_1,c_2)\ne(0,0)$ gives a zero-sum signed prime-supported combination $a=c_1a_1+c_2a_2$ with $Q(a)>0$. Consequently, for every $(c_1,c_2)\in\mathbb{Z}^2$, every sufficiently large translation $k$, and all sufficiently large $m$, the single rule $\mathrm{adelicSectionEncode}$ encodes $\mathrm{rationalSections}(mkb_{\mathrm{pad}})^2\hookrightarrow\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb_{\mathrm{pad}}+a))\times\mathrm{rationalSections}(m(kb_{\mathrm{pad}}-a))$ with `maxFibre = 1` ($\log\mathrm{maxFibre}=0\le\varepsilon m^2$) and forces $0\le\mathrm{midpointExcess}(kb_{\mathrm{pad}},a,m)$ eventually and $0\le Q(a)$. Partitioning $(M,2^rM]$ into $r$ dyadic prime shells extends the same construction to rank-$r$ lattices $\bigoplus_{s=1}^r\mathbb{Z}a_s$ for every $r\ge 1$.
- **Sharpness of plane promotion and full prime-support index.** Replacing each integer node $i+1$ of any nonzero $u\in\mathbb{Z}^M$ by two distinct primes $p_i,q_i>P_0$ with $|\log p_i-(T+\log(i+1))|,|\log q_i-(T+\log(i+1))|\to 0$ (`exists_log_prime_avoiding_small_finset`) and weights $+2u_i,-u_i$ produces a genuinely signed prime-supported vector $a_1$ with $Q(a_1)\to Q(u)$. Pairing $a_1$ with any signed prime-supported $a_2$ above $\mathrm{supp}(a_1)$ shows that subquadratic-fibre midpoint encoding across *all* disjoint-prime-support signed planes over the fixed background $b$ is unconditionally equivalent to RH. Moreover, prime-supported Gram submatrices carry the full negative index `(rightZeros zetaZeroConfig).encard`.
