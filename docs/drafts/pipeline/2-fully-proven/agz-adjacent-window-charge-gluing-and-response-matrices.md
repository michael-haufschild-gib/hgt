## Proofs (`[PROVED HERE]`)

### 1. Two-sided potential brackets and multi-charge response-matrix inertia
For symmetric matrices $A \in \mathbb{R}^{J_1 \times J_1}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{J_2 \times J_2}$, coupling $K \in \mathbb{R}^{J_1 \times J_2}$, and rank-$r$ charge matrices $U \in \mathbb{R}^{J_1 \times r}$, $V \in \mathbb{R}^{J_2 \times r}$ satisfying $\sum_{k=1}^r U_{i k} V_{j k} \le K_{i j}$, let $F_{i j} := K_{i j} - \sum_{k=1}^r U_{i k} V_{j k} \ge 0$, $d^L_i := \sum_j F_{i j}$, $d^R_j := \sum_i F_{i j}$. From $F_{i j}(x_i \mp y_j)^2 \ge 0$,
$$\operatorname{bilin}(\operatorname{gluedMatrix}(A - \operatorname{diag} d^L, B - \operatorname{diag} d^R, U, V), (x, y)) \le \operatorname{bilin}(\operatorname{coupled}(A, B, K), (x, y)) \le \operatorname{bilin}(\operatorname{gluedMatrix}(A + \operatorname{diag} d^L, B + \operatorname{diag} d^R, U, V), (x, y)),$$
hence $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{gluedMatrix}_{\text{high}}) \le \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{coupled}(A, B, K)) \le \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{gluedMatrix}_{\text{low}})$.
When $A$, $B$, and $\mathcal{A} := U^T A^{-1} U$ are invertible, applying Haynsworth inertia additivity twice to $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} \mathcal{A}^{-1} & V^T \\ V & B \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ and once to $\operatorname{gluedMatrix}(A, B, U, V)$ yields
$$\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{gluedMatrix}(A, B, U, V)) + \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\mathcal{A}) = \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(A) + \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(B) + \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\mathcal{A}^{-1} - \mathcal{B}), \qquad \mathcal{B} := V^T B^{-1} V.$$

### 2. Automatic sign elimination and exact single-block global maximum
- **Sign elimination:** If $A, B$ are symmetric, each takes a strictly negative value on some vector, $A \succeq 0$ on $\ker u$, $B \succeq 0$ on $\ker v$, $A a = u$, $B b = v$, and $1 \le (u \cdot a)(v \cdot b)$, then necessarily $u \cdot a < 0$ and $v \cdot b < 0$ (testing $A \succeq 0$ on $(u \cdot a) x_0 - (u \cdot x_0) a \in \ker u$ forces $u \cdot a \le 0$, and $1 \le (u \cdot a)(v \cdot b)$ excludes $0$ and forces equal negative signs), so $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{glued}(A, B, u, v)) = 1$.
- **Exact global maximum for $J_1 = 1$:** Suppose $A = (a_{00})$ with $a_{00} < 0$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{J_2 \times J_2}$ is symmetric, $K_0 \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^{J_2}$, $B b_0 = K_0$ with $b_{0, j} \le 0$ for all $j$, and $\alpha_0 \beta_0 := a_{00}^{-1}(K_0 \cdot b_0) \ge 1$. Rescaling $u_0 \ne 0$ to $u_0 = 1$ writes $v = K_0 - \delta$ with $\delta \ge 0$. For $\beta(\delta) = v \cdot \psi < 0$, testing $B_{\text{low}} \succeq 0$ on $\beta(\delta) b_0 - (v \cdot b_0)\psi \in \ker v$ gives $(-\beta(\delta))(B_0 + T_2) \le (B_0 - T_1)^2$ where $A_0 = -a_{00} > 0$, $B_0 = -(K_0 \cdot b_0) \ge A_0$, $S_1 = \sum \delta_j$, $T_1 = -\sum \delta_j b_{0, j} \ge 0$, $T_2 = \sum \delta_j b_{0, j}^2$. Since $T_1^2 \le S_1 T_2$ by Cauchy–Schwarz and $A_0 \le B_0$, $A_0(B_0 - T_1)^2 \le (A_0 + S_1)(B_0 + T_2)B_0$, yielding $\alpha(\delta)\beta(\delta) = \frac{-\beta(\delta)}{A_0 + S_1} \le \frac{B_0}{A_0} = \alpha_0 \beta_0$.

### 3. Certified narrow windows ($16 J \le R$): positive potential, zero bound states, and unbounded opposite-sign susceptibilities
- Using the midpoint enclosure $\log(n + \frac{1}{2}) + 1 - \frac{1}{(4n+1)(4n+3)} \le \Delta(n) \le \log(n + \frac{1}{2}) + 1$ ($n \ge 1$, and $\Delta(0) = 0 \le \log\frac{1}{2} + 1$) and $(j + \frac{1}{2}) + (J - 1 - j + \frac{1}{2}) = J$, AM–GM gives $\frac{1}{2}(\Delta(j) + \Delta(J - 1 - j)) \le \log J - \log 2 + 1$. For $1 \le J$ and $16 J \le R$, $\operatorname{blockPotential}(Q, R, J, j) > 1 > 0$ for all $j < J$. By `hermNegIndex_neutralWindow_le_card_wells`, $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{neutralWindow}(Q, R, J)) = 0$, and `bilin` is strictly positive on every nonzero real vector.
- For two narrow windows with $16(J_1 + J_2) \le R$, same-sign charges $0 \le u_i v_j \le K_{i j}$ keep $A_{\text{low}}, B_{\text{low}} \succ 0$, so $u \cdot a \ge 0$ and $v \cdot b \ge 0$ (no negative-susceptibility pairs exist). Conversely, opposite-sign spike charges $u = -p e_0$, $v = f_0$ ($p > 0$) satisfy $u_i v_j \le 0 < K_{i j}$, leave $\ker u = \{x_0 = 0\}$ and $\ker v = \{y_0 = 0\}$ strictly positive definite, and produce $\alpha(p) = \frac{p}{\hat{a}_0 - p} < 0$, $\beta(p) = \frac{p}{\hat{b}_0 - p} < 0$ with $\alpha(p)\beta(p) = \frac{p^2}{(p - \hat{a}_0)(p - \hat{b}_0)} \to +\infty$ as $p \to \max(\hat{a}_0, \hat{b}_0)^+$.

### 4. Ratio-four windows $(R, 2R]$ and $(2R, 4R]$ ($Q \ge 3$): complete answer
- **Cutoff stabilization:** For $a, b < 4R$ and $k \ge 4$, $\lfloor b / k \rfloor \le \lfloor (4R - 1)/4 \rfloor < R$, so $\operatorname{neutralBlock}(Q, a, b) = \operatorname{neutralBlock}(3, a, b) = \operatorname{neutralBlock}(4, a, b)$ for all $Q \ge 3$.
- **Raised model ($R \ge 1, Q \ge 3$):** At $u = v = 0$, $B_{\text{high}} \succeq B$ gives $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(B_{\text{high}}) \le 1$, and evaluating $B_{\text{high}}$ on $\mathbf{1}_{[2R, 4R)}$ gives $R(2 N_{11} + K_{12} + K_{13}) < 0$ by refinement, so $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(B_{\text{high}}) = 1$. On $A_{\text{high}}$, telescoping row sums and substituting $t = \frac{2i+1}{2R} \in (0, 1)$ in the midpoint enclosure cancels all discrete shifts, reducing $(A_{\text{high}}\mathbf{1})_i$ to $\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{4(1+t)^2(2+t)(5+t)(3-t)}{(3+t)^2 t(1-t)^2} + c_3(R, i) - \gamma - \log 2\pi - \text{err}(R) > 0$ via polynomial homogenization on $t \ge \frac{1}{3}$ ($3584/25$) and $t < \frac{1}{3}$ ($2 c_3 > \log 4$). Hence $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(A_{\text{high}}) = 0$ and $\operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{glued}_{\text{high}}) = 1$, recovering $1 \le \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{neutralWindow}(Q, R, 3R))$.
- **$R = 1$ ($Q \ge 3$):** Zero-excess charges $u = (1)$, $v = K_0$ satisfy $A_{\text{low}} = A$, $B_{\text{low}} = B \succeq 0$ on $\ker v$, $\alpha_0 \beta_0 = N_{11}^{-1}(K_0^T B^{-1} K_0) > \frac{8}{5} > 1$, and achieve the exact global maximum over all admissible pairs.
- **$R \ge 2$ ($Q \ge 3$):** Using $\operatorname{bilin}(\operatorname{glued}(A_{\text{low}}, B_{\text{low}}, u, v), (x, y)) = \operatorname{bilin}(G_0, (x, y)) + \sum_{i, j} u_i v_j (x_i - y_j)^2$:
  - For $R \ge 4$ (and every adjacent near-dyadic certified pair with $R \le J_1 \le \frac{53}{50} R$, $R + J_1 \le J_2$), choosing $c = \arg\max_i u^+_i$ bounds $\sum_{i,j} u^+_i v^+_j \le \sum_{i,j} \min(K_{c,j}, K_{i,j})$. Monotonicity of $\operatorname{blockJet}(a, b)$ in $a < b$ (`2c < R`) and jet decay (`2c ≥ R`) yield contact savings $> 0.27 R$, making the $2 \times 2$ restriction to $\operatorname{span}\{(\mathbf{1}_{[R, 2R)}, 0), (0, \mathbf{1}_{[2R, 4R)})\}$ strictly negative definite.
  - For $R = 3$, $\sum_{i, j < 3} \min(K_{c, j}, K_{i, j}) < 3$ for every $c \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, making the restriction to $\operatorname{span}\{(\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{1}, \mathbf{1}), (-\mathbf{1}, 2\mathbf{1}, 0)\}$ on the coarse $3 \times 3$ subspace strictly negative definite.
  - For $R = 2$, $u_0 u_1 \le 0$ is strictly negative definite on $\operatorname{span}\{(\mathbf{1}, 0), (0, \mathbf{1})\}$; for $u_0, u_1 > 0$, splitting at $4 u_0 \le 5 u_1$ vs $5 u_1 \le 4 u_0$ makes $\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,1,1,1,1), (-2,0,1,2,1,0)\}$ and $\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,1,1,1,1), (0,2,-1,-1,-1,0)\}$ strictly negative definite.
  Thus $2 \le \operatorname{hermNegIndex}(\operatorname{glued}(A_{\text{low}}, B_{\text{low}}, u, v))$ for all $R \ge 2$ and all $u_i v_j \le K_{i j}$. At $R = 2$, $u = (1, 1), v = (\frac{2}{3}, \frac{3}{10}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2})$ still yields $A_{\text{low}} \succ 0$ on $\ker u$, $B_{\text{low}} \succ 0$ on $\ker v$, and $\alpha \beta \in (0, 1)$.
