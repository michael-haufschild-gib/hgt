## Physical setup

On one ordered two-particle rapidity chamber, use center coordinate `u` and the canonical odd
crossing coordinate `t`.  The exact factorizing scattering operator acts by

```text
Psi_out(u,t)=S(t) Psi_in(u,t).                              (1)
```

Let

```text
K=C(u,t),       D=d/dt,       D(u)=0.                      (2)
```

Assume `S` is HT over `C(t)` and `Psi_in` is a nonzero DA germ over `K`.  The incoming packet may,
for example, be chosen from nonvanishing Gaussian or exponential-polynomial Schwartz profiles;
no compact-support analyticity is assumed.

## Maximal relative Kolchin production `[PROVED]`

Let

```text
B=K<Psi_in>
```

be the differential field generated by the complete incoming jet.  Since `Psi_in` is DA over `K`,
the extension `B/K` is differential algebraic.  If `S` were DA over `B`, differential-algebraic
transitivity would make `S` DA over `K`, contradicting the HT hypothesis.  Hence `S` remains HT
over `B`.

Equation `(1)` and nonvanishing of the incoming germ give

```text
B<Psi_out>=B<S>.                                           (3)
```

At finite jet height the Leibniz rule is triangular:

```text
D^j Psi_out
  = Psi_in D^j S
    + sum_(k<j) binom(j,k) D^(j-k)Psi_in D^k S.            (4)
```

All coefficients involving the incoming packet lie in `B`, and `Psi_in` is invertible in the germ
field.  Thus `(4)` and its triangular inverse identify the two finite jet fields.  For every
`r>=0`,

```text
trdeg_B B(Psi_out,D Psi_out,...,D^r Psi_out)
  = trdeg_B B(S,D S,...,D^r S)
  = r+1.                                                   (5)
```

Define the collision production slope relative to the full incoming differential field by

```text
Delta_sigma_collision(Psi_in)
  = lim_(r->infinity)
      trdeg_B B(Psi_out,...,D^r Psi_out)/(r+1).             (6)
```

Then

```text
S HT and Psi_in nonzero DA  =>  Delta_sigma_collision=1.   (7)
```

The gain is relative, not a subtraction of two potentially infinite absolute ranks.  It measures
new differential information that is absent from the complete incoming jet field.

## Probability invisibility `[PROVED]`

Elastic unitarity gives `|S(t)|=1` on the real scattering locus.  Therefore

```text
|Psi_out(u,t)|^2=|Psi_in(u,t)|^2.                          (8)
```

Every diagonal joint momentum probability is unchanged.  The maximal Kolchin gain `(7)` lies
entirely in the phase and is visible only to coherent reporters: interference, off-diagonal
density-matrix elements, local form factors, or phase-delay observables.

This separates HT quantum information from classical probability complexity.  A detector that
records only asymptotic momentum counts can be exactly blind to the new differential information.

## Infinite separation-rank consequence `[PROVED FROM PARENT TRANSLATION THEORY]`

Take a separable incoming packet

```text
Psi_in(theta_1,theta_2)=f(theta_1)g(theta_2)               (9)
```

with nonzero analytic `f,g`.  On subintervals where both are nonvanishing, finite two-variable
separation rank of `Psi_out` would imply finite separation rank of the difference kernel

```text
S(theta_1-theta_2).                                       (10)
```

The Anselone--Korevaar finite-translation theorem then makes `(10)` an exponential polynomial in
relative rapidity.  Composing such a finite exponential polynomial with the local inverse
rapidity map `v(t)` gives a DA germ in `t`: `v(t)` is DA, and each
`exp(lambda v(t))` satisfies a first-order equation with algebraic logarithmic derivative.
Therefore exponential-polynomial `(10)` would make `S(t)` DA, a contradiction.

Consequently

```text
S HT and Psi_in separable analytic nonzero
  => Psi_out has infinite two-variable separation rank.    (11)
```

Equation `(11)` is not characteristic of HT.  Some DA CDD phases also have infinite translation
rank.  The defining HT result is the maximal relative jet law `(5)`--`(7)`; infinite Schmidt rank
in a chosen labeled-packet or mode embedding is a weaker quantum-information shadow.

2. **Any phase called HT.**  False.  The proof needs the externally established HT theorem for
   `S(t)`.  A complicated or nonseparable DA phase has zero one-reporter Kolchin slope.
3. **Infinite separation rank called equivalent to HT.**  False, as recorded after `(11)`.
4. **Entanglement inferred from a probability distribution.**  The distribution is unchanged.
   Entanglement is carried by nonseparable phase coherence.
5. **Absolute rank subtraction.**  The production invariant is a relative transcendence degree
   over the complete incoming differential field `B`; no undefined infinity-minus-infinity is
   used.
6. **Incoming packet fine-tuned.**  Every nonzero DA incoming germ satisfies `(7)`.  The only
   cancellation packet `Psi_in proportional to S^(-1)` is itself HT and lies outside the stated
   DA source class.
7. **Rapidity-coordinate inflation.**  Kolchin growth is measured in the canonical crossing
   coordinate.  Rapidity enters the Schmidt-rank corollary only, where exponential-polynomial
   rapidity dependence would imply DA after the explicit DA inverse-coordinate substitution.
8. **Identical-particle entanglement left implicit.**  A one-species scalar theory has no canonical
   particle-label bipartition.  Equation `(11)` is unconditionally a separation-rank theorem on
   the ordered rapidity chamber.  Calling it Schmidt rank requires an explicit mode or labeled-
   wave-packet embedding and is not part of the bare QFT claim.
