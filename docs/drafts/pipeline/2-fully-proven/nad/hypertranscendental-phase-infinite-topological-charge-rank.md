- **Statement:** A Lorentz-invariant two-body dressing phase generated by a bilinear coupling of
  finitely many additive one-particle charges has finite translation rank, is an exponential
  polynomial, and is differentially algebraic.  Therefore a hypertranscendental scattering phase
  cannot be generated by any finite-charge double-current topological gauging.  Its microscopic
  source must activate infinitely many independent charge profiles or leave the bilinear
  charge-gauging class.

## Goal

Determine exactly what must become infinite in a non-propagating QFT source for an HT elastic
phase.  The one-species theorem already excludes particle proliferation as the answer.  The
flat-JT tail theorem shows that an ordinary two-charge topological sector is the tangent ray, but
not the full completion.

The result below identifies the missing infinity without guessing an action: it is the separation
rank of the rapidity-difference kernel, equivalently the dimension of the translation orbit of an
unwrapped phase.

## Translation rank and active charge rank

For a continuous function `Phi : R -> C`, let

```text
tau_a Phi(x)=Phi(x-a),
V_Phi=span_C {tau_a Phi : a in R},
rank_tr(Phi)=dim_C V_Phi.                                  (1)
```

Suppose a two-particle dressing is produced from `N` additive one-particle charge eigenvalue
profiles `q_1,...,q_N` by a fixed bilinear form `Omega`:

```text
K(x,y)=sum_(a,b=1)^N Omega_(a b) q_a(x) q_b(y).            (2)
```

Relativistic boost invariance requires the pair phase to depend only on the rapidity difference:

```text
K(x,y)=Phi(x-y).                                           (3)
```

The **active topological charge rank** of `Phi` is the least `N` for which a factorization
`(2)`--`(3)` exists.  It counts charge profiles that actually enter the dressing.  It is not the
total number of conserved quantities possessed by the undeformed integrable theory.

## Theorem A -- finite active charge rank forces finite translation rank `[PROVED]`

For each fixed `y`, equations `(2)`--`(3)` give

```text
tau_y Phi(x)
  =sum_a q_a(x) [sum_b Omega_(a b) q_b(y)].                (4)
```

Thus every translate of `Phi` lies in `span{q_1,...,q_N}`.  Consequently

```text
rank_tr(Phi) <= N.                                         (5)
```

No field equation, perturbation expansion, locality assumption, positivity assumption, or choice
of charge basis enters `(5)`.  It is the exact finite-rank content of a bilinear charge dressing.

If `Omega` or the charge profiles are redundant, `(5)` improves to the dimension of their active
span.  The theorem does not infer that every finite-rank kernel has a consistent topological QFT
realization; it gives a necessary condition only.

## Theorem B -- finite translation rank is exactly exponential-polynomial type `[PROVED]`

The space `V_Phi` in `(1)` is translation invariant.  The Anselone--Korevaar finite-translate
theorem states that every finite-dimensional translation-invariant subspace of `C(R)` is spanned
by exponential monomials.  Hence `(5)` implies

```text
Phi(x)=sum_(j=1)^r P_j(x) exp(lambda_j x)                  (6)
```

for complex polynomials `P_j` and complex frequencies `lambda_j`.  Conversely, all translates of
an exponential polynomial `(6)` lie in a finite-dimensional space.  Therefore

```text
rank_tr(Phi)<infinity
  iff Phi is a continuous exponential polynomial.         (7)
```

Every function in `(6)` is annihilated by a nonzero constant-coefficient linear differential
operator,

```text
product_j (D-lambda_j)^(1+deg P_j) Phi=0.                 (8)
```

In particular `Phi` is D-finite and differentially algebraic over `C(x)`.

## Theorem C -- HT scattering forces infinite active topological charge rank `[PROVED]`

Let an elastic scattering function have a continuous real-axis phase lift

```text
S(x)=epsilon exp(i Phi(x)),       epsilon in {+1,-1}.      (9)
```

Assume that `S` is hypertranscendental over `C(x)`.  If `rank_tr(Phi)` were finite, Theorem B
would make `Phi` differentially algebraic.  Over the differential field `C(x)<Phi>`, the function
`Y=exp(i Phi)` satisfies

```text
Y' - i Phi' Y=0.                                          (10)
```

Differential-algebraic extensions are transitive, so `(10)` would make `Y`, and hence `S`,
differentially algebraic over `C(x)`.  This contradicts hypertranscendence.  Therefore

```text
S hypertranscendental
  => rank_tr(Phi)=infinity
  => active topological charge rank=infinity.             (11)
```

The statement is independent of the choice of continuous phase lift: two lifts on the connected
real rapidity axis differ by a constant multiple of `2 pi`, which changes translation rank by at
most the constant direction and cannot turn an infinite-rank orbit into a finite-rank one.

## Exact examples

### Ordinary T bar T / flat JT

The gravitational dressing phase has the form

```text
Phi_JT(x)=kappa sinh(x)
         =(kappa/2) exp(x)-(kappa/2) exp(-x).              (12)
```

For `kappa != 0`, its translation rank is exactly two.  It is differentially algebraic, and a
finite topological gauging is possible.  This is the source-theoretic reason the flat-JT tangent
ray found in the geometric-tail theorem is not itself HT.

### Finite generalized T bar T dressing

For a finite nonzero spin set `F`,

```text
Phi_F(x)=sum_(s in F) alpha_s sinh(s x)                    (13)
```

has translation rank at most `2 |F|` and is annihilated by

```text
product_(s in F) (D^2-s^2).                               (14)
```

Distinct active spins give equality in the generic nondegenerate case.  No finite higher-spin
truncation can cross the HT boundary.

### Anti-converse: one elementary CDD shell already has infinite rank

For `B>1`, the single-shell phase

```text
Phi_B(x)=4 arctan(sinh(x)/B)                               (15)
```

is differentially algebraic but is not an exponential polynomial: its meromorphic derivative has
translated `sech` poles.  Theorem B therefore gives `rank_tr(Phi_B)=infinity` even though the
corresponding CDD factor is rational in `sinh(x)` and DA.

This destroys a tempting converse to Theorem C.  Infinite active charge rank does **not** detect
hypertranscendence; it detects departure from finite bilinear charge dressing.  The result is a
source no-go, not a classification of HT physics.

### The geometric CDD completion

For the geometric product, let `Sigma_(q,C)` be the continuous unwrapped real-axis phase defined by

```text
S_(q,C)(x)=-exp(i Sigma_(q,C)(x)).                         (16)
```

The published rational-or-HT theorem and the infinite divisor prove that `S_(q,C)` is HT.
Theorem C therefore gives

```text
rank_tr(Sigma_(q,C))=infinity.                             (17)
```

This conclusion is global and basis-free.  It is stronger than observing infinitely many nonzero
coefficients in one chosen higher-spin expansion: no nonlinear change among finitely many charge
profiles can reduce the dressing to finite bilinear rank.

## Physical law

The QFT interpretation of hypertranscendence now separates three ranks:

```text
asymptotic particle-species rank:        1,
finite flat-JT tangent charge rank:      2,
full HT phase translation/charge rank:  infinity.         (18)
```

The infinity in the exact interaction is not a hidden mass tower.  It is an irreducible
infinite-dimensional response of the constraint sector to relative rapidity.

For a positive microscopic construction inside the bilinear-charge framework, `(11)` leaves two
classes:

1. **infinite-dimensional topological gauging:** construct a topological path integral for a
   countable or measure-valued family of charges whose bilinear dressing converges directly to the
   global CDD product;
2. **non-bilinear source:** leave finite double-current gauging and produce an infinite-rank kernel
   through a genuinely nonlinear topological, defect, or constrained sector.

An ordinary finite set of hidden gauge fields cannot evade `(11)` merely by complicated
interactions if its exact two-body dressing still factorizes through finitely many additive charge
profiles.

## Relation to exact topological gauging

Dubovsky--Negro--Porrati construct double-current deformations by topologically gauging the
corresponding symmetries.  Their exact dressed in/out states and S-matrix acquire a phase bilinear
in the one-particle conserved charges.  For a finite gauged charge algebra this is precisely
`(2)`, so Theorems A--C classify the reach of that construction:

```text
finite topologically gauged charge data -> DA dressing,
HT dressing -> no finite-charge realization of that type. (19)
```

Their topological fields add no ordinary local propagating modes, which makes the framework
compatible with the one-species saturation theorem.  The obstruction is not to topological
gauging itself; it is to **finite-dimensional** topological gauging.

## Anti-theatre and novelty gate

Classical or published parents:

1. finite-dimensional translation-invariant function spaces are exponential-polynomial
   (Anselone--Korevaar, 1964);
2. exponential polynomials satisfy constant-coefficient linear ODEs;
3. double-current and generalized `T bar T` deformations dress factorized S-matrices by skew
   bilinears in conserved charges;
4. integrable QFTs can possess integer- and fractional-spin charge towers, as emphasized again in
   the 2026 graded-scattering construction.

None of these parent facts is claimed as new.  The candidate residue is the invariant source gate
`(11)` and its physical application `(18)`--`(19)`: HT of a one-species elastic law forces infinite
active topological charge rank even though it forces neither infinitely many particles nor local
propagating gauge modes.

Focused searches combining translation rank, finite-rank rapidity kernels, double-current
topological gauging, CDD phases, and hypertranscendence found no published statement of this gate.
The 2026 fractional-spin work supplies an adjacent infinite charge hierarchy but does not classify
HT phases or prove the necessity of infinite active dressing rank.  This is negative evidence,
not a priority proof.  Specialist review of integrable-deformation and finite-rank-kernel
literature remains required.

The elementary-shell anti-converse sharply limits significance: infinite active rank is common
outside HT and cannot be the founding invariant of the new field.  The significance gate is not
closed by this no-go.  Fields/Nobel-scale content requires an invariant separating the one-sided
HT completion from DA infinite-rank CDD shells, the positive construction of its source, and at
least one observable beyond the restatement of the exact S-matrix.

## What is not proved

1. No countably gauged or measure-valued topological path integral is constructed here.
2. Infinite translation rank is necessary for HT, not sufficient: many infinite-rank phases may
   still be differentially algebraic.
3. The theorem does not say that every QFT with infinitely many conserved charges has an HT
   S-matrix; only the charges active in the dressing count.
4. No ultraviolet completion, stress tensor, finite-volume spectrum, or off-shell correlator for
   the full geometric phase is produced.
5. No claim is made for a non-bilinear finite-field action whose exact elimination yields an
   infinite-rank kernel; that is the second surviving source class above.
