## The universal clock space

Let `P` be the primes and `N+={1,2,...}`.  Let `A` be the complex vector space of arrays

```text
a : P x N+ -> C
```

having finite abscissa:

```text
sum_p sum_(r>=1) |a(p,r)| p^(-r sigma) < infinity
```

for some real `sigma`.  Let `A_loc` be the subspace of arrays supported on only finitely many
prime rays:

```text
{p : exists r, a(p,r) != 0} is finite.
```

Put

```text
Q = A / A_loc.
```

Multiplication by the diagonal clock

```text
(T a)(p,r) = r log(p) a(p,r)
```

preserves finite abscissa and finite-prime support.  Hence `Q` is a module over `R=C[X]`, with
`X` acting as `T`.

The carrier of `a` is

```text
F_a(s) = sum_p sum_(r>=1) a(p,r) p^(-r s).
```

Let `B_loc` be the differential field over `C(s)` generated by all carriers of arrays in
`A_loc`.  It contains arbitrary finite-abscissa local functions `H_p(p^(-s))` on every finite set
of primes, not merely rational functions of the single-prime exponentials.

For a family `c : I -> A`, write

```text
M(c) = sum_i C[X] [c_i] <= Q,
K(c) = B_loc < F_(c_i) : i in I >_diff.
```

For an `R`-submodule `M <= Q`, define `Phi(M)` to be the differential field generated over
`B_loc` by carriers of representatives of all classes in `M`.  This is independent of the
representatives because changing a representative adds an element of `B_loc`.

## Master theorem: the prime-clock completion correspondence

**[PROVED HERE] Prime-clock differential-field classification.**

For arbitrary families of finite-abscissa prime-power arrays:

1. **Static tail matroid.**  A family `(F_(a_i))` is algebraically independent over `B_loc` iff
   the classes `([a_i])` are complex-linearly independent in `Q`.

2. **Clock torsion-freeness.**  The `C[X]`-module `Q` is torsion-free.  In particular every
   nonlocal prime-power carrier is hypertranscendental over `B_loc`.

3. **Relative differential matroid.**  For old sources `c` and new sources `g`, the family
   `(F_(g_h))` is differentially algebraically independent over `K(c)` iff

   ```text
   directSum_h C[X] -> Q / M(c),
   (P_h)_h |-> sum_h P_h(T)[g_h]
   ```

   is injective.

4. **Exact field order and recovery.**

   ```text
   K(c) <= K(d)  iff  M(c) <= M(d),
   K(c)  = K(d)  iff  M(c)  = M(d).
   ```

   More intrinsically, if

   ```text
   Psi(L) = {[a] in Q : F_a belongs to L},
   ```

   for an intermediate differential field `L`, then

   ```text
   Phi(M) <= L  iff  M <= Psi(L),
   Psi(Phi(M)) = M.
   ```

5. **Exact lattice law.**  For `R`-submodules `M,N <= Q`,

   ```text
   Phi(M + N) = compositum(Phi(M),Phi(N)),
   Phi(M intersect N) = Phi(M) intersect Phi(N).
   ```

6. **Complete relation ideal.**  Over `K(c)`, every differential relation among `(F_(g_h))`
   is generated by the affine linear relations attached to the kernel of the clock-module map in
   item 3.  Explicitly, a module identity

   ```text
   sum_h P_h(T)[g_h] = sum_i Q_i(T)[c_i] + [b],   b in A_loc,
   ```

   gives the affine differential relation obtained by replacing `T` by `-d/ds`; these relations
   and their prolongations generate the full differential ideal.  There are no additional
   nonlinear relations.

7. **Rank law.**  For a finite new family, the relative differential transcendence degree equals
   the `C[X]`-rank of `(M(c)+M(g))/M(c)`.  Torsion summands contribute their exact linear
   differential equations and no differential transcendence.

8. **Differential-algebraic closure law.**

   ```text
   F_a is differential-algebraic over Phi(M)  iff  [a] is torsion in Q/M.
   F_a is hypertranscendental over Phi(M)      iff  [a] is non-torsion in Q/M.
   ```

   Field membership is the sharper order-zero condition `[a] in M`.

## Proof of theorem A

### Lemma A0: faithful finite-abscissa coordinate realization

Let `D_fa` be the Dirichlet-convolution algebra of arithmetic functions whose Dirichlet series
converges absolutely in some right half-plane.  Uniqueness of Dirichlet series makes the map from
`D_fa` to upper-end analytic germs injective, and hence extends injectively from its fraction field
`L_fa` to upper-end meromorphic germs.

The coordinate function `s` is transcendental over `L_fa`.  Indeed, after clearing denominators,
a polynomial relation would have the form

```text
sum_(k=0)^d s^k sum_(n>=1) a_(k,n) n^(-s) = 0.            (A0.1)
```

If it is nonzero, choose the least `n_0` for which

```text
P_(n_0)(s) = sum_(k=0)^d a_(k,n_0) s^k
```

is a nonzero polynomial.  On the positive real axis, multiply (A0.1) by `n_0^s`.  Absolute
convergence at some `sigma_0` gives, for each `k`,

```text
|s^k sum_(n>n_0) a_(k,n) (n_0/n)^s|
  <= C_k s^k (n_0/(n_0+1))^s.
```

Thus `P_(n_0)(s)` is exponentially small as `s->+infinity`, which is impossible for a nonzero
polynomial.  Hence every coefficient in (A0.1) is zero.  It follows that evaluation at the actual
coordinate defines an injective field homomorphism

```text
L_fa(S) -> upper-end meromorphic germs,   S |-> s.         (A0.2)
```

Every element of `B_loc`, and every source carrier in the theorem, lies in the image of (A0.2).
Therefore a putative analytic relation lifts to a unique formal rational-coordinate relation.
This proves that the formal prime-derivation argument below loses neither coefficients nor
relations when it is transferred to analytic germs.

### Lemma A1: clock stability and torsion-freeness

For every `epsilon>0` and fixed `k`, the function

```text
t |-> t^k exp(-epsilon t)
```

is bounded on the nonnegative real axis.  With `t=r log p`, this shows that multiplication by
`(r log p)^k` only shifts the abscissa by `epsilon`; hence `T` preserves `A`.  It plainly
preserves `A_loc`.

Let `0 != P in C[X]` and suppose `P(T)[a]=0` in `Q`.  Outside a finite set of primes,

```text
P(r log p) a(p,r) = 0                 for every r>=1.
```

The map `(p,r) |-> r log p` is injective: equality gives `p^r=q^t`, so unique factorization gives
`p=q` and `r=t`.  A nonzero polynomial has finitely many roots, hence only finitely many pairs
`(p,r)` can satisfy `P(r log p)=0`.  After adding their prime coordinates to the exceptional set,
`a` vanishes on every remaining prime ray.  Thus `[a]=0`, proving torsion-freeness.

### Lemma A2: separated ray packets

Let `[a_1],...,[a_n]` be linearly independent in `Q`, and delete an arbitrary finite set `S` of
primes.  For `(p,r)` with `p notin S`, form the evaluation row

```text
v(p,r) = (a_1(p,r),...,a_n(p,r)) in C^n.
```

These rows span `C^n`.  Otherwise a nonzero linear functional annihilates every one of them, and
the corresponding combination of the `a_j` is supported on `S`, contradicting independence in
`Q`.

Rows with pairwise distinct prime coordinates can be selected inductively.  After choosing a
prime, delete that prime ray as well.  Independence in `Q` survives every finite deletion, so the
remaining rows still span `C^n`.  Consequently there exist distinct `q_1,...,q_n notin S` and
positive `r_1,...,r_n` such that

```text
det(a_j(q_i,r_i))_(i,j) != 0.
```

### Lemma A3: the dynamic prime-ray Jacobian

Regard `a_j` as the arithmetic function supported at `p^r` with value `a_j(p,r)`.  Apply the
prime derivation at `q_i`.  Its value at `q_i^(r_i-1)` is

```text
r_i a_j(q_i,r_i).
```

Every Jacobian row is supported on its own prime ray.  The coefficient of the determinant at

```text
product_i q_i^(r_i-1)
```

is `prod_i r_i` times the determinant from Lemma A2.  Unique factorization prevents cancellation,
so the arithmetic Jacobian is nonzero.

A relation over `B_loc` contains only finitely many coefficients, and each coefficient is built
from finitely many local carriers.  Their union has a finite prime activity set `S`.  Choose the
packet outside `S`; the selected prime derivations kill every coefficient.  The
Shapiro--Sparer Jacobian criterion gives algebraic independence over their common kernel.

To pass from formal arithmetic functions to actual meromorphic germs, use the faithful
finite-abscissa Dirichlet realization.  The upper-end coordinate `s` is transcendental over the
fraction field of finite-abscissa Dirichlet series, so adjoining `C(s)` introduces no relation.
Thus the formal relation obtained from a putative analytic relation is zero, proving the reverse
direction of item 1.  The forward direction is immediate: a nonzero linear combination lying in
`A_loc` gives a nonzero affine relation over `B_loc`.

### Lemma A4: relative jet criterion

Termwise differentiation gives

```text
(d/ds)^k F_a = (-1)^k F_(T^k a).
```

Consider a finite set of new jet variables.  If their clock classes are dependent modulo `M(c)`,
the dependence has a finite old-module plus local residual and therefore gives an affine relation
over `K(c)`.

Conversely, a putative polynomial relation uses finitely many coefficients of `K(c)`.  Those
coefficients involve only a finite-dimensional complex subspace `U` of `M(c)`.  Independence of
the selected new clock classes modulo `M(c)` implies that a basis of `U` together with the new
classes is linearly independent in `Q`.  Lemma A3 makes the corresponding carriers jointly
algebraically independent over `B_loc`; pure-transcendental base change then makes the new jets
algebraically independent over the field generated by `U`, contradicting the relation.  Finitary
independence proves item 3 for arbitrary families.

### Lemma A5: field and lattice reconstruction

If `[a] in M`, its carrier lies in `Phi(M)` by construction.  If `[a] notin M`, every expression
from `Phi(M)` uses only finitely many module classes spanning a finite-dimensional subspace
`U <= M`.  Then `[a]` is independent from `U`, so Lemma A3 makes `F_a` transcendental over the
field used by that expression.  Therefore

```text
F_a in Phi(M)  iff  [a] in M.
```

This proves source recovery, the Galois connection, and exact field order.

For the meet law, choose a basis of `M intersect N`, extend it separately to bases of `M` and
`N`, and observe that the three resulting blocks are jointly linearly independent in `Q`.
Lemma A3 turns them into three algebraically independent carrier blocks over `B_loc`.  The standard
intersection identity for rational function fields on independent blocks gives

```text
Phi(M) intersect Phi(N) = Phi(M intersect N).
```

The sum/compositum identity is immediate from the generators.

### Lemma A6: complete relations and rank

Let `J` be the complex vector space of all finite new jet combinations and let

```text
Lambda : J -> Q/M(c)
```

send the jet `(h,k)` to `T^k[g_h]`.  Every vector in `ker Lambda` gives the announced affine
linear relation.  Quotient the polynomial jet algebra by the ideal generated by these relations.
It is the symmetric algebra of `im Lambda`.  Choose a Hamel basis of `im Lambda`; every finite
subfamily lifts to classes independent modulo `M(c)`, so Lemma A4 makes its carriers algebraically
independent over `K(c)`.  The induced symmetric-algebra map is therefore injective.  Hence no
nonlinear relation remains, proving item 6.

For a finite source family, the image module over the PID `C[X]` is finitely generated and splits
as `C[X]^rho` plus torsion.  Lift the free basis to polynomial combinations of the carriers;
item 3 makes those lifts differentially independent.  Every torsion generator satisfies a
nonzero affine linear differential equation over the free field, and every original generator is
differential algebraic over it.  This proves item 7.  Applying item 3 to one source gives item 8.

## The free recurrent sector

Let `K` be a topological space and let

```text

```

be cofinally tail-dense: every neighbourhood of every point of `K` is hit after deletion of any


```text

```

has finite abscissa.  A convenient sufficient condition is a bound `|u_r(x_p)|<=M_r` with
`sum_r M_r 2^(-r sigma)<infinity` for some `sigma`.

**[PROVED HERE] Recurrent declocking lemma.**  The canonical map


is injective.

Consequently, for every source subspace `W <= E_x`, put

```text
Phi_x(W) = B_loc < F_(Rep_x(u)) : u in W >_diff.
```

Then:

1. replicated carriers are differentially independent over `B_loc` iff their profiles are
   complex-linearly independent;
2. new replicated carriers are differentially independent over `Phi_x(W)` iff their profile
   classes are linearly independent in `E_x/W`;
3. `Phi_x` preserves and reflects inclusion, equality, intersection, and sum/compositum;
4. the complete relation ideal is generated by profile-linear relations and their prolongations;
5. relative differential transcendence degree is ordinary profile quotient dimension; and
6. the replicated-profile part of the differential-algebraic closure is source-pure:

   ```text
   F_(Rep_x(u)) is differential-algebraic over Phi_x(W)  iff  u in W.
   ```

   Thus every omitted profile direction is hypertranscendental, not merely absent from the field.

The theorem is unchanged by altering the sampler or the profiles on finitely many prime rays,
because those changes lie in `B_loc`.  Hence it also applies to samplers that enter `K` after
finitely many transient primes.

## Proof of theorem B

Take a nonzero finite tensor and write it as

```text
sum_i P_i(X) tensor u_i
```

with the `u_i` linearly independent.  Let `m` be the largest degree among the `P_i`, and let
`v_m` be the nonzero profile formed from their degree-`m` coefficients.  Choose `r_0` and `z_0 in K`
with `(v_m)_(r_0)(z_0) != 0`.  For each lower degree `k`, let `v_k` be the analogous profile.

If the replicated tensor vanished modulo finitely many prime rays, then outside a finite set,

```text
sum_(k=0)^m (r_0 log p)^k (v_k)_(r_0)(x_p) = 0.             (*)
```

Continuity gives a neighbourhood `U` of `z_0`, a number `delta>0`, and finite bounds `C_k` such
that on `U`

```text
|(v_m)_(r_0)| >= delta,
|(v_k)_(r_0)| <= C_k                 (k<m).
```

Tail density supplies primes of arbitrarily large logarithm with `x_p in U`, outside the finite
exceptional set.  Divide `(*)` by `(r_0 log p)^m`.  The lower terms tend uniformly to zero while
the leading term has modulus at least `delta`, a contradiction.  This proves injectivity.

The six consequences now follow from theorem A because the image of a source subspace is exactly
the free module `C[X] tensor W`, and quotient independence over `C[X]` reduces to ordinary
complex-linear independence in `E_x/W`.

## Euler completion corollary

Take `K` to be one point.  An admissible profile is a scalar sequence `u=(u_r)`, with local
generating function

```text
H_u(z) = sum_(r>=1) u_r z^r,
```

and replicated carrier

```text

```

Let `E_fin` be the finite-support profile space and

```text
K_fin = B_loc < R(u) : u in E_fin >_diff.
```

The recurrent declocking theorem gives the exact completion law

```text
R(u) is differential-algebraic over K_fin  iff  u has finite support.
```

Therefore every admissible infinite profile produces a hypertranscendental extension of the full
finite-layer field, even though it may be a convergent limit of finite profiles.

For the coordinate profile `e_m`,

```text
R(e_m)(s) = P(m s),
```

where `P` is the prime zeta function.  Hence the countable family `(P(ms))_(m>=1)` is jointly
differentially independent over `B_loc`, and

```text
K_fin = B_loc < P(m s) : m>=1 >_diff.
```

The Euler logarithm has the infinite profile `lambda_r=1/r`, so on `Re(s)>1`,

```text
R(lambda)(s) = sum_(m>=1) P(m s)/m = log zeta(s).
```

Thus

```text
log zeta(s) is hypertranscendental over
B_loc < P(m s) : m>=1 >_diff.
```

This is stronger than externality from every finite window: it excludes every differential

## Hostile audit and falsifiers

1. **Plain-tail Grassmann law `[FALSIFIED PREDECESSOR]`.**  Let `c(p)=1/log p` and `d(p)=1`.
   Their ordinary tail classes are independent, but `T[c]=[d]`.  The clock module correctly
   records the differential relation.
2. **Tail-density-only restriction quotient `[FALSIFIED PREDECESSOR]`.**  With `K={0}`,
   `x_p=1/log p`, and `f(x)=x`, the sampler approaches `K` transversely; `f|K=0`, but one clock
   step turns its carrier into the constant prime source.  The recurrent theorem requires the
   sampler to live in `K` modulo finitely many primes.  The universal theorem still classifies the
   transverse source by its nontrivial clock module.
3. **Bare Euler-exponential base `[FALSIFIED PREDECESSOR]`.**  An infinite valuation profile at
   one transient prime gives an arbitrary local function `H(p^(-s))`, not necessarily an element
   of the rational single-exponential field.  The analytic base `B_loc` contains all such finite-
   prime local carriers.
4. **No passage of independence through limits.**  The completion theorem uses the exact source
   criterion and finite character of differential algebra, not closure of fields under analytic
   limits.
5. **Branch domain.**  The identity with `log zeta` is asserted only on the zero-free half-plane
   `Re(s)>1`; no continuation or zero statement is used.
6. **Nonlinear-collapse burden.**  Rank alone is insufficient.  Promotion requires the static
   iff theorem, dynamic coefficient descent, field intersection, and the complete affine relation
   ideal.
7. **Analytic lifting burden.**  The formal arithmetic theorem is not the analytic theorem.  The
   finite-abscissa fraction field with an independent coordinate must embed faithfully into the
   upper-end meromorphic field, and `B_loc` must be identified inside its image.
8. **Literature burden.**  The one-source HT consequences are classical and are not claimed as
   new.  The paper makes one novelty claim: the exact multi-source prime-clock correspondence,
   whose saturation law forces the differential-algebraic completion barrier.  Field recovery
   and the complete relation presentation are completeness clauses of that correspondence, not
   separate narratives.

not a proof claim.  The full countable ghost field still cannot differentially reconstruct the
Euler logarithm.  Therefore an RH-capable Euler-side mechanism must contain a genuine completion
operation external to differential algebra and must couple that completed valuation profile to
the archimedean reflection.  Finite differentiation, rational field operations, finite prime
gauge changes, and even adjoining all individual finite valuation layers cannot replace that
cofinal coupling.
