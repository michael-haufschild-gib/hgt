- **Statement:** For every finite-channel scattering matrix `S(E)`, its Wigner--Smith matrix

## Differential-field setup

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero differential field containing `i`, let `L` be a differential
field extension, and let

```text
S in GL_n(L),
Q=-i S^(-1) D S.                                           (1)
```

Write

```text
E_S=K<S_ab : 1<=a,b<=n>,
E_Q=K<Q_ab : 1<=a,b<=n>.                                  (2)
```

Since matrix inversion is rational in the entries and determinant inverse, `(1)` gives

```text
E_Q <= E_S.                                                (3)
```

No unitarity is needed for the algebraic theorem.

## Finite differential defect `[PROVED]`

Equation `(1)` is equivalent to the linear matrix system

```text
D S=i S Q.                                                 (4)
```

Define matrices over `E_Q` recursively by

```text
C_0=I,
C_(r+1)=i Q C_r+D C_r.                                    (5)
```

Repeated differentiation of `(4)` gives

```text
D^r S=S C_r.                                               (6)
```

Fix one entry `S_ab`.  Its first `n+1` derivatives have the form

```text
D^r S_ab = row_a(S) dot column_b(C_r),       0<=r<=n.       (7)
```

The `n+1` coefficient columns in `(7)` lie in the `n`-dimensional vector space `E_Q^n`, so they
have a nonzero linear relation over `E_Q`.  Dotting that relation with `row_a(S)` produces a
nonzero scalar linear differential equation of order at most `n` for `S_ab` over `E_Q`.

Thus every entry of `S` is DA over `E_Q`, and therefore

```text
E_S/E_Q is differential algebraic.                        (8)
```

Combining `(3)` and `(8)` proves the exact Kolchin-completeness law

```text
dtrdeg_K E_S = dtrdeg_K E_Q.                              (9)
```

The Wigner--Smith matrix loses at most a finite-dimensional initial-condition layer; it loses no
differential transcendence.

## Finite-jet comparison `[PROVED]`

The proof is quantitative before taking the differential-field union.

1. From `(1)`, every jet of `Q` through order `r` is rational in the jets of `S` through order
   `r+1` and `det(S)^(-1)`.
2. From `(6)`, every jet of `S` through order `r` lies in the field generated by the order-zero
   entries of `S` and the jets of `Q` through order `r-1`.

Consequently the two finite-jet transcendence ranks differ only by an order shift and at most
`n^2` initial coordinates.  In particular every normalized ordinary Kolchin growth slope of the
matrix tuples agrees:

```text
sigma(S)=sigma(Q).                                        (10)
```

This is stronger than the implication “HT somewhere in `S` implies HT somewhere in `Q`.”  It
states conservation of the entire differential-transcendence capacity of a finite channel.

## Physical real locus

On a real elastic energy interval suppose

```text
S(E)^* S(E)=I.                                             (11)
```

Then `S^(-1)=S^*` and differentiation of `(11)` shows that

```text
Q(E)=-i S(E)^* S'(E)                                      (12)
```

is Hermitian.  For a constant normalized incoming channel vector `v`, the mean delay is the real
scalar

```text
tau_v(E)=v^* Q(E) v.                                      (13)
```

This is the multichannel Smith lifetime-matrix observable.  The distinction between Smith delay,
angular delay, and other time-delay conventions is parent scattering theory and must not be
silently erased.

## A fixed `n^2`-reporter tomography panel `[PROVED]`

Let `e_j` be the channel basis.  Use the diagonal reporters

```text
tau_j=e_j^*Qe_j=Q_jj                                      (14)
```

and, for every `j<k`, the two unnormalized superposition reporters

```text
tau^+_(jk)=(e_j+e_k)^*Q(e_j+e_k),
tau^i_(jk)=(e_j+i e_k)^*Q(e_j+i e_k).                    (15)
```

Put

```text
A_jk=tau^+_(jk)-tau_j-tau_k=Q_jk+Q_kj,
B_jk=tau^i_(jk)-tau_j-tau_k=i Q_jk-i Q_kj.               (16)
```

Then

```text
Q_jk=(A_jk-i B_jk)/2,
Q_kj=(A_jk+i B_jk)/2.                                    (17)
```

There are exactly

```text
n+2 binom(n,2)=n^2                                        (18)
```

reporters, and `(14)`--`(17)` prove

```text
K<all panel delays>=E_Q.                                  (19)
```

Together with `(9)`, this gives

```text
dtrdeg_K K<all panel delays>=dtrdeg_K E_S.                (20)
```

If the matrix scattering field is not DA, at least one scalar reporter in this fixed finite panel
is HT.  Otherwise every panel member would be DA and their finitely generated joint field would be
DA, contradicting `(20)`.

Normalized physical superpositions merely multiply the reporters in `(15)` by the nonzero DA
constant `1/2`; the generated differential field is unchanged.

## Constant channel-gauge covariance `[PROVED]`

For energy-independent invertible channel changes `U,V` over the constant field,

```text
S -> U S V,
Q -> V^(-1) Q V.                                         (21)
```

Left output-basis changes disappear from `Q`; right input-basis changes conjugate it.  Both the
field `E_S` and the joint field `E_Q` are unchanged by these invertible constant linear
recombinations.  Hence `(9)`, `(10)`, and `(20)` are constant-channel-gauge invariants.

An energy-dependent basis change contributes its own connection term.  It is not an admissible
neutral gauge unless that connection is included in the DA base.

## Anti-theatre attacks

1. **Wigner--Smith matrix renamed.**  Equations `(11)`--`(13)` are parent physics.  The candidate
   new content is Kolchin completeness `(9)`--`(10)` and its finite physical tomography
   realization `(20)`--`(22)`.
2. **A matrix linear ODE declared transcendental.**  Solutions of a finite linear differential
   system are DA over its coefficient field.  The new differential complexity is already in `Q`,
   not manufactured by solving `(4)`.
3. **Trace alone claimed complete.**  False.  `tr(Q)` can be DA by cancellation while off-diagonal
   or traceless channels are HT.  The complete reporter is the `n^2` polarization panel.
4. **Basis-dependent entries called physical invariants.**  Individual reporters depend on the
   chosen channel basis; the joint differential field and its Kolchin rank are invariant under
   constant channel changes.
5. **Energy-dependent gauges ignored.**  They carry a connection term and can inject HT.  They are
   not neutral unless their connection belongs to the declared base.
6. **A nonabelian HT QFT claimed constructed.**  Not yet.  The theorem applies to any such finite
   channel once its scattering matrix is known HT.  The current geometric CDD example is the
   `n=1` case; direct sums are reducible controls, not the desired nonabelian existence theorem.
7. **Finite resolution equated with uniform stability.**  Equation `(22)` gives exact algebraic
   fidelity, but its differential inverse is unbounded on an unrestricted `L^2` frequency range.
   Stability and HT faithfulness are distinct axes.
