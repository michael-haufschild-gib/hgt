## Exact construction and paper proof `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `L` be a real-linearly independent pair of complex periods and `T = C / L`.
Translations have derivative the identity. Consequently holomorphic one-forms on `T` are
exactly holomorphic cotangent-valued maps `omega : C -> (C ->L[C] C)` invariant under `L`.
This is the covering-space definition of global one-forms; it imposes regularity everywhere,
including the poles of Weierstrass coordinates. Meromorphic functions on the same quotient
are the existing field `ellipticSubfield L`.

For such a form, the coefficient `f(z) = omega(z)(1)` is entire and lattice-periodic.
Its range is bounded, by a compact fundamental parallelogram. Liouville implies `f` is
constant. Complex linearity gives `omega(z)(v) = f(z) v`. Evaluation at `(0,1)` therefore
gives a linear equivalence from global holomorphic forms to `C`; the inverse is `c dz`.
Their dimension, hence the geometric genus, is exactly one. The constant form `dz` is nonzero
at every point, since it evaluates to one on the tangent vector one.

The coefficient map sends a form to the meromorphic germ of `z -> omega(z)(1)` in the same
elliptic field. It is injective, by the linearity identity above. Its image consists precisely
of field elements having an entire representative. Indeed any such representative inherits
lattice periodicity from its germ: punctured equality extends across the missing point by
continuity. Liouville makes it constant, and it is the coefficient of a constant multiple
of `dz`. This identifies regular field differentials with the entire cotangent sections,
without a chosen subspace omitting any regular forms.

The function field is finitely generated by `P,P'`. The former is transcendental because
it has a pole, while the latter satisfies `P'^2 = 4P^3 - g2 P - g3`. The field is algebraic
over `C(P)`, hence has transcendence degree one. Taking the real basis `1,i` discharges the
period independence condition and yields a concrete field with no hypotheses.
