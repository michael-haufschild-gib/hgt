# A sparse fixed set detecting the full Mertens exponent

- **Stage** 3-formalized
- **Target** For every density and displacement tolerance, construct one genuine short-exchange involution whose fixed signed prefix has exactly the same positive power bounds as the ordinary Möbius prefix. The selected map must work simultaneously for all exponents.

**Exact local difference.** Fix disjoint prime ranges and their product modulus \(Q\). Put \(U(X)=\sum_{a\le X,(a,Q)=1}\mu(a)\) and \(D_rG(X)=G(\lfloor X/r\rfloor)\). In each band mark one canonical pair \(p\leftrightarrow w=qr\). One may keep the exchange or omit it, testing the other pairs as usual. The two local fixed-divisor sums \(A,B\) differ by \(D_w-D_p\) because adding these two fixed divisors adds their opposite Möbius charges. Local divisor sums from disjoint bands multiply by unique factorization. Their product applied to \(U\) therefore gives the actual fixed signed prefix at each integer cutoff.

**Geometric inversion.** For \(0<a<b\) and \(G(0)=0\), a bound \(|(D_a-D_b)G(X)|\le C X^\theta\), \(\theta>0\), implies \(|G(n)|\le Ca^\theta n^\theta/(1-(a/b)^\theta)\). Evaluate at \(X=an\) and induct on \(n\), since \(\lfloor an/b\rfloor<n\). Conversely every finite linear combination of sharp dilations preserves each positive power bound.

**One choice for all exponents.** The sets of admissible positive exponents for two sequences are upward closed, hence nested, including their endpoint behavior. Both local fixed sums preserve every exponent of \(G\). If both transformed sequences satisfy a bound, their difference satisfies it, so \(G\) does too by inversion. Their two exponent sets thus intersect in exactly the set for \(G\). Keep the choice with smaller exponent set. Repeat once per band. After \(m\) stages one table retains every power-bound equivalence for \(U\), with at most two candidate extensions at each stage. The choice is existential and need not be computable from finite samples.

Deleting one prime uses the invertible difference \(D_1-D_p\), so \(U\) and \(M\) have the same positive power exponents. In a band with displacement at most \(p/J\), the marked prime \(p\) is at least \(J\), since it differs by a nonzero integer from its semiprime partner. Among integers detected at a single-prime endpoint, omitting one pair can newly fix at most \(X/J\) integers per band. The crossing strip contributes at most \(X/J+1\). Choose the number of bands to control missed residues, then the resolution \(J\) to control both additional terms.

**Lean.** `one_sparse_fixed_set_detects_every_mertens_power` gives one actual restricted involution for each tolerance. Its fixed and crossing population is eventually at most \(\varepsilon X\). Its fixed signed prefix alone satisfies exactly the positive power bounds of the full Möbius prefix, including their endpoint. The map also gives exact signed balance at every cutoff. The theorem uses only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
