## Paper proof of strict scalar/resolved separation

`[PROVED HERE]` Let `C=(0,1,4)` and `D=(0,10,30)`.  On the nine labelled
levels `(i,j)` define

\[
 E_{ij}=C_i+D_j,\qquad F_{ij}=C_i-D_j,
\]

and give every level weight `1/9`.  Write

\[
 A_C(t)=\frac13\sum_i e^{-itC_i},\qquad
 A_D(t)=\frac13\sum_j e^{-itD_j}.
\]

Finite distributivity gives

\[
 A_E(t)=A_C(t)A_D(t).
\]

Because `D_j,t` are real,

\[
 A_F(t)=A_C(t)\frac13\sum_j e^{itD_j}
       =A_C(t)\overline{A_D(t)}.
\]

Therefore

\[
 |A_E(t)|^2=|A_C(t)|^2|A_D(t)|^2=|A_F(t)|^2
\]

for every real `t`.  Pushing this pointwise equality through any probability
clock proves equality of the complete scalar survival-return laws, hence of
all scalar moments and all scalar threshold probabilities.

Now let `k` have coefficients `+1` at `(0,1)`, `-10` at `(1,0)`, `+9` at
`(0,0)`, and zero elsewhere.  Its coefficient sum is zero.  Its detunings are

\[
 \langle k,E\rangle=(0+10)-10(1+0)+9(0+0)=0,
\]

and

\[
 \langle k,F\rangle=(0-10)-10(1-0)+9(0-0)=-20\ne0.
\]

Thus `k` belongs to the projective spectral relation lattice of `E` but not
that of `F`; the lattices and completed phase ensembles differ.  Complete
resolved-replica tomography therefore supplies a finite-order character
detector that distinguishes them.  This proves strictness without numerical
search, genericity or an unproved existence premise.

The same factorization proves the general product/reflection statement for
arbitrary finite real packets and arbitrary product probability weights.
