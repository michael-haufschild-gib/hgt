# Two-gap rigidity of perfect-pairing forms

**Status:** paper proof and Lean formalization complete. `ThreeBlock`, `TwoGap`, and `Activation` passed individual final builds and all 53 declaration axiom audits. This is a structural continuation of chronological tensor geometry. Priority has not been established; the counting mechanism is standard invariant combinatorics.

## Statement

Fix an ordered set of 2d endpoints and two distinct gaps. Set all other gaps to zero and denote the two remaining increments by u,v. The endpoints split into three nonempty consecutive blocks A,B,C of sizes a,b,c, with a+b+c=2d. For each perfect pairing D, orient its chords by endpoint order and write `P_D=∏_(i,j∈D)(x_j−x_i)`.

Set `α=d−c`, `β=d−a`, `γ=d−b`, using natural-number subtraction. Let ε_D be 1 if D has no chord within a single block, and 0 otherwise. Then, for all real u,v,

\[
P_D(u,v)=\varepsilon_D\,u^\alpha v^\beta(u+v)^\gamma.
\]

Consequently every linear combination `F=Σ_D λ_D P_D` restricts to

\[
F(u,v)=\left(\sum_D\lambda_D\varepsilon_D\right)
        u^\alpha v^\beta(u+v)^\gamma,
\qquad
|F(u,v)|\le |F(|u|,|v|)|.
\]

Thus the restriction space has dimension at most one. Replacing both active increments by their absolute values preserves total variation and cannot decrease absolute response. No probe supported on at most two gaps can exceed the full monotone capacity of a perfect-pairing form.

## Proof

Endpoint positions in the three blocks are respectively 0,u,u+v, after translation. A chord within one block has zero difference, so any pairing containing one has zero response, proving its formula immediately.

Otherwise every chord joins different blocks. Let p,q,t count chords of types AB,BC,AC, respectively. Since each endpoint is used exactly once,

`a=p+t`, `b=p+q`, `c=q+t`, and `d=p+q+t`.

Subtracting yields `p=d−c=α`, `q=d−a=β`, `t=d−b=γ`. In this case a,b,c≤d, so these are ordinary nonnegative integer differences. Endpoint orientation gives chord factors u,v,u+v for AB,BC,AC. Multiplication proves the formula. If one block has more than d endpoints, every pairing necessarily has an intrablock chord; the already proved zero case covers it without any extra assumption.

Summing over D factors out the common monomial. Its absolute value is
`|Σ λ_D ε_D| |u|^α |v|^β |u+v|^γ`. Apply `|u+v|≤|u|+|v|` and monotonicity of natural powers. This gives exactly the response bound above. Both probes have variation `|u|+|v|`. A probe with only one active gap is already monotone after global sign reversal; homogeneity preserves absolute response. The zero probe is immediate.

For a variation-at-most-one signed probe with at most two active gaps, the absolute-increment probe is therefore an admissible monotone probe with at least as large an absolute response. This proves the capacity consequence.

## Sharp activation threshold and scope

The existing six-endpoint form `27ace−(a+b+c+d+e)(b+c+d)c` has monotone capacity 1. Its probe `(1/3,0,−1/3,0,1/3)` has variation 1, three active gaps, and response `−28/27`. These facts already have unconditional Lean proofs in the chronological modules. Therefore three active gaps are necessary in general and sufficient in this explicit example. `Activation.lean` constructs the two genuine endpoint enumerations, proves their actual interval-window response and monotone capacity, and proves that the displayed normalized witness has exactly three active gaps and magnitude 28/27. It also classifies every probe with at most two active gaps into a two-gap face. The general theorem `pairing_reversal_support_bound` states the resulting universal support lower bound for every nonempty perfect-pairing family.

“Three blocks” means consecutive blocks in endpoint chronology. This is not a claim about arbitrary probes that happen to visit only three spatial values: repeated visits can have more than two active gaps.

The two-gap homogenized Chebyshev extremizers are unrestricted finite-window forms. Their repeated endpoint use need not satisfy the perfect-pairing incidence constraints above. The rigidity theorem supplies a direct explanation for that distinction.
