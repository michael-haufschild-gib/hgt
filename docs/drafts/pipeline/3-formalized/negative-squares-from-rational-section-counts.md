# Negative squares from rational section counts

For each multiplicity $m$, subtract the dual section height from the ordinary height, divide by $m^2$, and polarize on each pair of integer conductor atoms. The resulting finite matrix is Hermitian. Its entries converge to the spline Gram entries. The supremum of the negative indices of the limiting matrices counts distinct zeta zeros right of the critical line, including infinite cardinality. It also gives the supremum of indices detected by the integer Pick tests.

Under RH the critical ordinates separate the conductor atoms, so the limiting Gram is positive definite at every fixed horizon. All sufficiently late finite matrices there have negative index zero. Outside RH the conjugate right zeros give index at least two at some finite horizon. The corresponding negative plane persists at every sufficiently large multiplicity. Thus a cofinal bound of one negative square at each horizon is equivalent to RH.

The same strict positivity removes the quadratic allowance from the least-section test. RH is equivalent to the integral contact modulus fitting inside the original real radius at arbitrarily large multiplicities for every prime pair $e_1+e_p$.
