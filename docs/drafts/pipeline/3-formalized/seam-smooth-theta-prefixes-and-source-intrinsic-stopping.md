## Proof

Write $P_{N+1}(x) := \sum_{m=1}^{N+1} \Phi_m(x)$ ($x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $\Phi_m(x) = 2\pi e^{x/2}(2\pi m^4 e^{4x} - 3 m^2 e^{2x})e^{-\pi m^2 e^{2x}}$), so the canonical reflected finite-theta source is $P_{N+1}(|x|) = \text{reflectedFiniteThetaSource } (N + 1)\ x$ with bilateral Laplace transform $\mathcal{A}_N(z) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} P_{N+1}(|x|) e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx$, and let $E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) := C_{\mathrm{tail}} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) e^{-(N+1)^2}$.

### 1. Infinitude of off-line zeros of $\mathcal{A}_N$ and Gaussian seam-mollification gauge invariance

1. **Subquadratic order-$\tfrac{3}{2}$ growth of $\mathcal{A}_N$ on $\mathbb{C}$:** For $u \ge 0$, $e^{2u} \ge 1 + 2u + 2u^2 + \tfrac{4}{3}u^3 \ge u^3$, so $\Phi_m(u) \le 4\pi^2 m^4 e^{\frac{9}{2}u - \pi m^2 u^3} \le C_{N}' e^{-2 u^3}$ ($1 \le m \le N + 1$), giving $P_{N+1}(|x|) \le B_N e^{-|x|^3 - |x|}$ on $\mathbb{R}$. For any $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, set $s := \sqrt{1 + \|z\|} \ge 1$; the identity $|x|^3 + 2 s^3 - 3 s^2 |x| = (|x| - s)^2(|x| + 2 s) \ge 0$ gives $(1 + \|z\|)|x| - |x|^3 \le (1 + \|z\|)^{3/2}$. Integrating against $e^{-|x|}$ yields $\|\mathcal{A}_N(z)\| \le 2 B_N \exp((1 + \|z\|)^{3/2})$ (`finiteThetaAmplitude_subquadratic_growth`) with exponent $a = \tfrac{3}{2} < 2$.
2. **Super-exponential real-axis lower bound and complex zero infinitude:** For every $x_0 \ge 1$, $P_{N+1}(u) \ge \Phi_1(u) > 0$ on $[x_0, x_0 + 1]$. For real $\sigma \ge \tfrac{1}{2}$, positivity of the bilateral integrand gives
   $$\mathcal{A}_N(\sigma) \ge \int_{x_0}^{x_0 + 1} \Phi_1(u) e^{(\sigma - 1/2)u}\,du \ge \kappa(x_0)\, e^{x_0 \sigma}, \qquad \kappa(x_0) := \int_{x_0}^{x_0 + 1} \Phi_1(u) e^{-u/2}\,du > 0.$$
   If $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ were finite, subquadratic Hadamard rigidity (`exists_finiteDivisorFactor_mul_exp_of_finite_zeroSet` with $a = \tfrac{3}{2} < 2$) would express $\mathcal{A}_N(z) = (\prod_{w \in t}(z - w)^{m(w)}) c e^{B z}$ ($c \ne 0$), contradicting $\log \mathcal{A}_N(\sigma) / \sigma \to \infty$ (`finiteThetaAmplitude_superlinear_real_ray`). Hence $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ is infinite (`finiteThetaAmplitude_zeros_infinite`).
3. **Infinitely many off-line zeros outside every vertical strip:** Since $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le \max(0, R) \wedge \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ is finite (`finiteThetaAmplitude_strip_zeros_finite`), set difference yields $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0 \wedge R < |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}|\}$ infinite for every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $R \in \mathbb{R}$ (`finiteThetaAmplitude_offline_zeros_outside_strip_infinite`). At $R = 0$, $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + iz)$ has nonreal zeros while $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2}) > 0$, so `z ↦ A_N(1 / 2 + I * z)` is not in `LaguerrePolya` (`not_laguerrePolya_finiteThetaAmplitude_criticalLine`).
4. **Gaussian seam-mollification ($P_{N+1}(|\cdot|) * G_a$, $a > 0$):** Setting $\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(x) := \int_{\mathbb{R}} P_{N+1}(|u|)\, G_a(x - u)\,du$ with $G_a(y) = (2\pi a)^{-1/2} e^{-y^2/(2a)}$, parametric differentiation against `gaussPolyFun` establishes $\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}} \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$, strict positivity, evenness, and $\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}\big)^{(2k+1)}(0) = 0$ ($\forall k \in \mathbb{N}$). Fubini/Tonelli and completing the square (`integral_cexp_quadratic`) give
   $$\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(z) := \int_{\mathbb{R}} \widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(x)\, e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx = e^{\frac{a}{2}(z - 1/2)^2}\, \mathcal{A}_N(z) \qquad (\forall z \in \mathbb{C}).$$
   On $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$, $\|e^{\frac{a}{2}(z - 1/2)^2}\| = e^{\frac{a}{2}(\operatorname{Re} z - 1/2)^2 - \frac{a}{2}(\operatorname{Im} z)^2}$ restores all-order super-polynomial strip decay while preserving the entire zero divisor of $\mathcal{A}_N$. Whenever $\mathcal{A}_N$ avoids $\partial R_{T,n}$, $-\frac{(\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}})'(z)}{\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}(z)} = -\frac{\mathcal{A}_N'(z)}{\mathcal{A}_N(z)} - a(z - \tfrac{1}{2})$; by `rectIntegral_eq_zero_of_analyticOnNhd` applied to $z \mapsto -a z^r (z - \tfrac{1}{2})$, every window contour power sum, window degree, Newton difference polynomial, Zhukovsky loop, and Wiener–Hopf operator of $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}$ equals that of $\mathcal{A}_N$ identically (`gaussMollifiedCollisionOperator_eq`).

### 2. Non-multiplicative $C^\infty$-even Poisson–Jacobi partition prefixes

For $\kappa > 0$, define $\chi_\kappa(x) := \tfrac{1}{2} + \sqrt{\kappa / \pi}\int_0^x e^{-\kappa u^2}\,du$ (`gaussianPartitionSwitch`) and
$$\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x) := \chi_\kappa(x)\, P_{N+1}(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x)\, P_{N+1}(-x) \qquad (\text{`partitionSymThetaSource`}).$$
1. **$C^\infty$-evenness and seam-jet annihilation:** Both $\chi_\kappa$ and $P_{N+1}$ (`thetaCuspFiniteShellPrefix (N + 1)`) belong to $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$, $\chi_\kappa(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x) = 1$, and $\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(-x) = \widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x)$, so $\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(2k+1)}(0) = 0$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$ (`iteratedDeriv_odd_partitionSymThetaSource_zero`).
2. **Complementary Gaussian tail bound and all-order super-polynomial strip decay:** From $\sqrt{\kappa/\pi}\int_0^\infty e^{-\kappa u^2}\,du = \tfrac{1}{2}$, substituting $u = v + x$ ($x, v \ge 0$, $(v + x)^2 \ge x^2 + v^2$) gives $0 < \chi_\kappa(-x) \le \tfrac{1}{2} e^{-\kappa x^2}$ (`gaussianPartitionSwitch_neg_bounds`). Boundedness of $P_{N+1}^{(r)}$ on $(-\infty, 0]$, double-exponential decay on $[0, \infty)$, and Leibniz's rule yield $\bigl|\big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(K)}(x)\bigr| \le C_{N,\kappa,K} e^{-\frac{\kappa}{2} x^2}$ on $\mathbb{R}$ (`abs_iteratedDeriv_partitionSymThetaSource_le_exp`). Hence $K$-fold bilateral integration by parts on $\mathbb{R}$ with zero seam/boundary terms gives
   $$(z - \tfrac{1}{2})^K \widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \big(\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}\big)^{(K)}(x)\, e^{(1/2 - z)x}\,dx \qquad (\forall K \in \mathbb{N},\ z \in \mathbb{C}),$$
   establishing uniform vertical-strip boundedness of $\|z - \tfrac{1}{2}\|^K \|\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z)\|$ (`partitionSymThetaAmplitude_superpolynomial_strip_decay`).
3. **Exact fixed-$\kappa$ convergence to $\xi$ and collision charge $O(T)$ as $N \to \infty$:** By Jacobi reflection $\Phi(-x) = \Phi(x)$ and $\chi_\kappa(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x) = 1$, $\chi_\kappa(x)\Phi(x) + \chi_\kappa(-x)\Phi(-x) = \Phi(x)$ identically for all $\kappa > 0$. Using $(2\pi u^4 + 3u^2)e^{-\frac{\pi}{2}u^2} \le 20$ ($u \ge 0$) to bound $\sum_{m=1}^\infty |\Phi_m(-x)| \le C_1 e^{2x}$ on $x \ge 0$ against $\chi_\kappa(-x) \le \tfrac{1}{2} e^{-\kappa x^2}$, uniform dominated convergence gives $\sup_{|\operatorname{Re} z| \le R} \|\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(z) - \xi(z)\| \to 0$ and $\sup_{|\operatorname{Re} z| \le R} \|-(\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}})'(z) - \xi'(z)\| \to 0$ as $N \to \infty$ (`tendsto_partitionSymThetaAmplitude_xi_strip`), stabilizing `partitionSymCollisionOperator T n N κ` to `xiHorizontalCollisionCharge T` for $n \ge n_{\mathrm{win}}(T)$ (`partitionSymCollisionOperator_eventually_eq_charge`).
4. **Persistence of off-line zeros at large $\kappa$:** At fixed $N$, $|\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}(x) - P_{N+1}(|x|)| = \chi_\kappa(-|x|)|P_{N+1}(-|x|) - P_{N+1}(|x|)| \le \tfrac{1}{2} M_N e^{-\kappa x^2}$ yields $O(\kappa^{-1/2})$ uniform strip convergence $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}} \to \mathcal{A}_N$ (`norm_partitionSymThetaAmplitude_sub_finite_le_strip`). Isolating $M$ distinct off-line zeros of $\mathcal{A}_N$ outside $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$ by disjoint Rouché discs transfers at least $M$ distinct off-line zeros to $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}$ for all $\kappa \ge \kappa_0(N, R, M)$ (`exists_kappa_offline_zeros_partitionSym`), refuting Laguerre–Pólya critical-line purity (`exists_kappa_not_laguerrePolya_partitionSym`).

### 3. Purely source-intrinsic stopping certificates for $N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n)$

Let $E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) := C_{\mathrm{tail}} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) e^{-(N+1)^2}$ (`thetaStripTailBound N`). For all $M \ge N$ and $|\operatorname{Re} z| \le 2$, $\|\mathcal{A}_M(z) - \mathcal{A}_N(z)\| \le 2 E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N)$ and $\|-\mathcal{M}_M(z) - (-\mathcal{M}_N(z))\| \le 2 E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N)$ with zero reference to $\xi$.

1. **Source-perforated window certificate (`IsSourceAlgebraicPerforatedCertified T n N`):** Let $Z_N := \text{prefixEnclosedZeros } T\ n\ N$, $r_n := \frac{1}{8 s_n^2}$, $K_{T,n,N}^{\mathrm{src}} := \text{windowClosedRect } T\ n \setminus \bigcup_{w \in Z_N} B(w, r_n)$, and $m_N^{\mathrm{src}}(T, n) := \min_{z \in K_{T,n,N}^{\mathrm{src}}} \|\mathcal{A}_N(z)\|$. At a single prefix $N$, `IsSourceAlgebraicPerforatedCertified T n N` checks: (i) $\frac{2}{s_n} \le \operatorname{Re} w \le 1 - \frac{2}{s_n}$ and $(\text{ll}_n).\text{im} + 2 r_n < \operatorname{Im} w < (\text{ur}_{T,n}).\text{im} - 2 r_n$ for all $w \in Z_N$; (ii) $4 r_n < \operatorname{dist}(w_1, w_2)$ for distinct $w_1 \ne w_2 \in Z_N$; (iii) for all $w_1, w_2 \in Z_N$, either $|\operatorname{Im}(w_1 - w_2)| < r_n \wedge (w_1 = w_2 \vee \frac{7}{2 s_n} < |\operatorname{Re}(w_1 - w_2)|)$ or $\frac{7}{2 s_n} < |\operatorname{Im}(w_1 - w_2)|$; and (iv) $4 E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) < m_N^{\mathrm{src}}(T, n)$.
   - *Soundness:* For every $M \ge N$, $\mathcal{A}_M$ is nonvanishing on $K_{T,n,N}^{\mathrm{src}} \supset \partial R_{T,n}$, symmetric Rouché preserves multiplicity inside each $B(w, r_n)$, and the polynomial gaps $(1 - \frac{1}{s_n})a_n^2 - (\frac{1}{2} - \frac{2}{s_n} + \frac{1}{4 s_n^2})^2 = \frac{1}{4 s_n}(3 - \frac{13}{s_n} + \frac{6}{s_n^2} - \frac{1}{4 s_n^3}) > 0$ ($s_n \ge 6$) and $\frac{1}{s_n}b_n^2 - (\frac{3}{8 s_n^2})^2 = \frac{1}{s_n^3}(1 - \frac{9}{64 s_n}) > 0$ classify $E_n(z_1 - z_2) < 1 \iff |\operatorname{Im}(w_1 - w_2)| < r_n \wedge \operatorname{Re} w_2 < \operatorname{Re} w_1$, locking $M_{T,n,M} = M_{T,n,N}$ and $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,M}) = \operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N})$ for all $M \ge N$ (`sourceAlgebraicPerforatedCertified_locks_all_ge`) and certifying $N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n) \le N$ when $n \ge n_{\mathrm{win}}(T)$ (`sourceAlgebraicPerforatedCertified_sound`).
   - *Completeness on simple-zero windows:* Under `HasSimpleWindowZeros T` and $n \ge \text{thetaSimpleZeroScale } T$, once $6 E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) < m_\xi^{(1/2)}(T, n)$ (`N ≥ simpleZeroStoppingPrefix T n`), Rouché capture on $B(\rho, r_n/2)$ ($\rho \in Z_\xi(T)$) has total multiplicity $\operatorname{ord}_\rho(\xi) = 1$, forcing $Z_N \cap B(\rho, r_n/2)$ to be a singleton $\{w_\rho\}$, whence $K_{T,n,N}^{\mathrm{src}} \subset K_{T,n}^{(1/2)}$, $m_N^{\mathrm{src}}(T, n) \ge m_\xi^{(1/2)}(T, n) - E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) > 4 E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N)$, and `sourcePerforatedStoppingPrefix T n ≤ simpleZeroStoppingPrefix T n` (`sourcePerforatedStoppingPrefix_complete_of_simpleZeros`).
2. **Root-free contour-loop certificate (`IsSourceLoopCertified T n N`):** From $\mu_N^{\partial}(T, n) := \min_{t \in [0, 1]} \|\mathcal{A}_N(\gamma_{T,n}(t))\|$ (`boundaryPrefixMin`) and $\lambda_N(T, n) := \min_{u \in S^1} \|\text{integerThetaBaseLoop } T\ n\ N\ u\|$ (`baseLoopCircleMin`), using $\|\gamma_{T,n}(t)\| \le B_T := \max(0, T) + 3$, $\|\gamma'_{T,n}(t)\| \le L_T := 4\max(0, T) + 9$, and the $(k + 1)^{-1}$-cancelling Newton–Girard bounds $\|e_k(d)\| \le D^k$ and $\|e_k(d_1) - e_k(d_2)\| \le k D^k \delta$, the three source inequalities $4 E_{\mathrm{tail}}(N) < \mu_N^{\partial}(T, n)$, $\delta_0(T, n, N) < \tfrac{1}{2}$, and $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{loop}}(T, n, N) < \lambda_N(T, n)$ lock $M_{T,n,M} = M_{T,n,N}$ and $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,M}) = \operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N})$ across all $M \ge N$ (`sourceLoopCertified_locks_all_ge`, `sourceLoopCertified_sound`), hold eventually in $N$ on *every* window $(T, n)$ ($n \ge n_{\mathrm{win}}(T)$) regardless of zero multiplicities (`eventually_isSourceLoopCertified`), and yield `isArtifactFreeFrom_sourceLoopStoppingPrefix` and `riemannHypothesis_iff_forall_nat_sourceLoopStoppingPrefix_index_eq_zero`.

## No-go closure
- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:**
  1. *Naive seam-smoothing critical-line purity hypothesis:* Eliminating the $x = 0$ cusp jump ($\widetilde{P}^{(2k+1)}(0) = 0$ for all $k$) and restoring super-polynomial vertical-strip decay at finite $N$ does **not** restore Schur–Cohn / Hermite–Biehler / Laguerre–Pólya critical-line purity: (a) subquadratic Hadamard rigidity + super-exponential real-axis Laplace growth + strip zero finiteness force every $\mathcal{A}_N$ ($N \in \mathbb{N}$) to have infinitely many off-line zeros outside every vertical strip $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$; (b) $x$-space Gaussian mollification $\widetilde{P}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}} = P_{N+1}(|\cdot|) * G_a$ multiplies $\mathcal{A}_N$ by $e^{\frac{a}{2}(z-1/2)^2}$, preserving all infinitely many off-line zeros and leaving $W_{T,n,N}$ gauge-invariant; (c) non-multiplicative $C^\infty$-even Poisson–Jacobi partition smoothing $\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}$ inherits $\ge M$ distinct off-line zeros outside $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$ for $\kappa \ge \kappa_0(N, R, M)$ (and exhibits critical-strip off-line quartets even at moderate smoothing, e.g. $0.983403224 + 34.491698792\,i$ at $N = 1, \kappa = (0.15)^{-2}$, and $0.680125913 + 3.437405457\,i$ at $N = 2$ in the pure two-sided symmetrization).
  2. *Fixed-radius single-root perforation on multiple-zero windows:* A multiple zero $\operatorname{ord}_\rho(\xi) \ge 2$ can split at finite $N$ into distinct roots separated by $o(r_n)$, violating fixed-radius $4 r_n$ pairwise root separation in `IsSourceAlgebraicPerforatedCertified`, whereas simple-zero windows force singleton Rouché capture; `IsSourceLoopCertified` bypasses root isolation altogether via contour moments + $S^1$ loop modulus.
- **Strongest positive survivor:**
  1. Exact fixed-$\kappa$ modular convergence of $C^\infty$-even super-polynomially strip-decaying Poisson–Jacobi partition prefixes $\widetilde{P}_{N,\kappa}^{\mathrm{PJ}}$ to $\xi$ and $O(T)$, alongside exact collision-operator gauge invariance under Gaussian seam mollification and unconditional infinitude of off-line zeros of $\mathcal{A}_N$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{A}}_{N,a}^{\mathrm{gauss}}$.
  2. Two 100% $\xi$-free single-prefix stopping certificates (`IsSourceAlgebraicPerforatedCertified` with explicit Gaussian completeness on simple-zero windows, and root-free `IsSourceLoopCertified` with universal completeness across arbitrary multiplicities and single-stopping-prefix `RiemannHypothesis` equivalence).
- **New insight:** The finite-prefix off-line zero obstruction is an arithmetic Hadamard tail phenomenon rather than a local $x = 0$ regularity defect: $x$-space seam smoothing removes the $(z - \tfrac{1}{2})^{-2}$ cusp amplitude without supplying the missing Euler–Hadamard tail, as proved starkly by the zero-preserving factor $e^{\frac{a}{2}(z-1/2)^2}$ of Gaussian convolution and by subquadratic Hadamard rigidity. Conversely, stopping certification on a fixed window needs no $\xi$-oracle: either source-perforated Rouché modulus (complete on simple-zero windows) or direct boundary-to-$S^1$ Newton–Girard Lipschitz propagation (universally complete) halts purely from integer-theta data at a single prefix $N$.
