## Proof

Write $s_n := \max(n, 6) \ge 6$, $a_n := \tfrac{1}{2} - \tfrac{1}{2 s_n} \in [\tfrac{5}{12}, \tfrac{1}{2})$, $b_n := \tfrac{1}{s_n} \le \tfrac{1}{6}$, $E_n(w) := (\operatorname{Re} w - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 / a_n^2 + (\operatorname{Im} w)^2 / b_n^2$, and $R_{T,n} := [-\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{3}{2}] \times [-\tfrac{1}{2 s_n}, \max(0, T) - \tfrac{1}{2 s_n}]$. For $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$, the Haglund–Jacobi shell pencil $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(z) := (1 - \theta)\mathcal{A}_N(z) + \theta \mathcal{A}_{N+1}(z)$ is entire with bilateral Laplace kernel $P_{N,\theta}(x) = \sum_{m=1}^{N+1} \Phi_m(|x|) + \theta \Phi_{N+2}(|x|)$, satisfies $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}'(z) = -\mathcal{M}_{N,\theta}(z)$, $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(1 - z) = \mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(z)$, $\overline{\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(\bar{z})} = \mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(z)$, and $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(\sigma) > 0$ for all $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\theta \in [0, 1]$, hence has finite analytic order everywhere and finitely many zeros in every closed ball.

### 1. Finite-$N$ divisor-index formula and critical-line collar vanishing

Whenever $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}$ vanishes nowhere on $\partial R_{T,n}$ ($\theta \in [0, 1]$), isolating the finite exterior zero set in $\overline{B}(0, \max(0, T) + 6)$ from $R_{T,n}$ produces an open $\delta$-fattened rectangle $U_\delta \supset R_{T,n}$ whose zeros of $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}$ are exactly $Z_{N,\theta}(T, n) := \{w \in \operatorname{int}(R_{T,n}) : \mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(w) = 0\}$ with multiplicity $m_{N,\theta}(w) := \operatorname{ord}_w(\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta})$. Multi-zero peeling and the weighted rectangle argument principle give
$$s_{T,n,N,\theta}(r) = \sum_{w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} m_{N,\theta}(w)\, w^r \quad (\forall r \in \mathbb{N}), \qquad M_{T,n,N,\theta} = \sum_{w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} m_{N,\theta}(w).$$
By Newton–Girard reconstruction and the Zhukovsky winding dichotomy, if $E_n(z - w) \ne 1$ for all $z, w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T, n)$, then $\Lambda_{T,n,N,\theta}$ is nonvanishing on $S^1$ and
$$\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N,\theta}) = 2 \sum_{z \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} \sum_{w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T,n)} m_{N,\theta}(z)\, m_{N,\theta}(w)\, \mathbf{1}_{\{E_n(z - w) < 1\}} \ge 0.$$
At $\theta = 0$, if every $z \in Z_N(T, n)$ lies in the critical-line collar $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| < \tfrac{1}{4 s_n}$ (allowing arbitrary multiplicities on $\operatorname{Re} z = \tfrac{1}{2}$), then $\operatorname{Re}(z - w) - \tfrac{1}{2} < \tfrac{1}{2 s_n} - \tfrac{1}{2} = -a_n < 0$, so $E_n(z - w) > 1$ for all $z, w \in Z_N(T, n)$ and $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N}) = 0$.

### 2. Shell-pencil double-wall transition law ($N \to N + 1$) and mandatory ingress

When $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}$ avoids $\partial R_{T,n}$ for all $\theta \in [0, 1]$, joint compactness on $[0, 1]^2$ makes $\theta \mapsto s_{T,n,N,\operatorname{clamp}(\theta)}(r)$ continuous for every $r \in \mathbb{N}$, locking the integer degree $M_{T,n,N,\theta} \equiv M_{T,n,N}$ across $\theta \in [0, 1]$ and making $(\sigma, u) \mapsto \Lambda_{T,n,N,\operatorname{clamp}(\sigma)}(u)$ jointly continuous on $\mathbb{R} \times S^1$. If no pair $z, w \in Z_{N,\theta}(T, n)$ crosses $E_n(z - w) = 1$, winding homotopy invariance gives $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N}) = \operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N+1})$. Contrapositively, any index jump $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N}) \ne \operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N+1})$ requires $\theta_* \in [0, 1]$ at which either $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta_*}$ vanishes on $\partial R_{T,n}$ (Wall A) or $\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta_*}$ avoids $\partial R_{T,n}$ and admits $z, w \in Z_{N,\theta_*}(T, n)$ with $E_n(z - w) = 1$ (Wall B).

### 3. Complex seam-cusp law, strip zero finiteness, critical-line root, and height-freezing

Integrating $\mathcal{A}_N(z) = \int_0^\infty J_{N+1,0}(x)(e^{(z - 1/2)x} + e^{(1/2 - z)x})\,dx$ by parts three times on $(0, \infty)$ against $J_{N+1,k}' = J_{N+1,k+1}$ gives
$$\left(z - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\!\left(\left(z - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z) - 2 J_{N+1,1}(0)\right) = -\int_0^\infty J_{N+1,3}(x)\bigl(e^{(z - 1/2)x} - e^{(1/2 - z)x}\bigr)\,dx,$$
where $J_{N+1,1}(0) > 0$. Setting $B_3(N, R) := 2(N + 1)\,C_{\mathrm{Gauss}}(X^{\lceil |R|\rceil_+ + 1} P_{\mathrm{jet},3})$ and $H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, R) := B_3(N, R) / J_{N+1,1}(0)$, every $z$ with $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R$ and $\|z - \tfrac{1}{2}\| > H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, R)$ satisfies $\|(z - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z) - 2 J_{N+1,1}(0)\| < J_{N+1,1}(0)$, hence $\operatorname{Re}((z - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 \mathcal{A}_N(z)) > J_{N+1,1}(0) > 0$ and $\mathcal{A}_N(z) \ne 0$. Thus:
1. $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le R \wedge \mathcal{A}_N(z) = 0\}$ is globally finite, bounding $M_{T,n,N} \le K_{\mathrm{strip}}(N) < \infty$ uniformly in $(T, n)$ at every boundary-regular prefix $N$.
2. On the critical line, $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + it) < 0$ for $|t| > H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 0)$; combined with $\mathcal{A}_N(\tfrac{1}{2} + 3i) > 0$, every prefix $N \in \mathbb{N}$ has a genuine critical-line zero $t_*(N) > 3$.
3. For $T_1, T_2 \ge H_{\mathrm{cusp}}(N, 3) + 2$ and all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathcal{A}_N$ is analytic and zero-free on an open neighbourhood of the stacked top rectangle between $T_1$ and $T_2$, so $s_{T_1,n,N}(r) = s_{T_2,n,N}(r)$ for all $r \in \mathbb{N}$ and $W_{T_1,n,N} = W_{T_2,n,N}$ identically.
4. By Hardy's theorem (`exists_odd_order_line_zero_gt`), $N_\xi(T) \to \infty$ as $T \to \infty$; hence for every $N_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ there exists $T > 0$ such that on every $n \ge n_{\mathrm{win}}(T)$, some pencil step $N \to N + 1$ ($N \ge N_0$) crosses $\partial R_{T,n}$.

### 4. Direct all-$N$, all-stage sectorial certificate on low windows ($T \le 3$)

For $|\operatorname{Re} z - \tfrac{1}{2}| \le \tfrac{9}{8}$, $|\operatorname{Im} z| \le \tfrac{61}{20}$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\theta \in [0, 1]$, writing $\sigma := \tfrac{1}{2} - \operatorname{Re} z$ and $b := -\operatorname{Im} z$ splits $\operatorname{Re}\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(z) = \int_0^\infty 2 P_{N,\theta}(x)\cosh(\sigma x)\,dx - \int_0^\infty 2 P_{N,\theta}(x)\cosh(\sigma x)(1 - \cos(bx))\,dx$. The Ward–Laplace half-density identity $\int_0^\infty e^{-x/2}\Phi_1(x)\,dx = (2\pi - 1)e^{-\pi}$ bounds the main term below by $10 e^{-\pi}$, while $1 - \cos(bx) \le \tfrac{1}{2}b^2 x^2$, $P_{N,\theta} \le \frac{11}{10}\Phi_1$, and the cubic Taylor sum-of-squares bound $(2x + 2x^2 + \tfrac{4}{3}x^3) - (\tfrac{106}{25}x - \tfrac{184}{375}) = (x - \tfrac{2}{5})^2(\tfrac{4}{3}x + \tfrac{46}{15}) \ge 0$ bound the oscillatory defect above by $\frac{704}{675} b^2 e^{-\pi}$, yielding $\operatorname{Re}\mathcal{A}_{N,\theta}(z) \ge \frac{5026}{16875}e^{-\pi} > 0$ (and $\operatorname{Re}\xi(z) \ge \frac{5026}{16875}e^{-\pi} > 0$). Consequently, for every $T \le 3$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $s_{T,n,N}(r) = 0$, $M_{T,n,N} = 0$, $\Lambda_{T,n,N} \equiv 1$, $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N}) = 0$, $O(T) = 0$, and $N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n) = 0$.

### 5. Explicit perforated-rectangle Rouché artifact-annihilation threshold

For $n \ge n_{\mathrm{ann}}(T)$ and $r_n := \frac{1}{8 s_n^2}$, the discs $\overline{B}(w, 2 r_n)$ ($w \in Z_\xi(T)$) are pairwise disjoint inside $\operatorname{int}(R_{T,n})$, and the compact perforated rectangle $K_{T,n} := R_{T,n} \setminus \bigcup_{w \in Z_\xi(T)} B(w, r_n)$ satisfies $m_\xi(T, n) := \inf_{z \in K_{T,n}} \|\xi(z)\| > 0$. For all $N \ge N_{\mathrm{ann}}(T, n) := \lceil \sqrt{\max(0, \log(2 C_{\mathrm{tail}} E_{\mathrm{amp}}(2) / m_\xi(T, n)))} \rceil_+$, Gaussian tail decay gives $\|\mathcal{A}_N(z) - \xi(z)\| < \tfrac{1}{2} m_\xi(T, n) \le \tfrac{1}{2}\|\xi(z)\|$ on $K_{T,n}$, expelling all spurious finite-prefix zeros from $K_{T,n}$ and placing $\mathcal{A}_N / \xi \in \mathbb{C} \setminus (-\infty, 0]$ on every sphere $\partial B(w, r_n)$. Symmetric Rouché equates the multiplicity of $\mathcal{A}_N$ in $B(w, r_n)$ with $\operatorname{ord}_w(\xi)$, while exact polynomial factorizations of $(1 - \frac{1}{s_n}) a_n^2 - (\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{s_n})^2 = \frac{1}{4 s_n}(1 - \frac{1}{s_n} - \frac{1}{s_n^2}) > 0$ and $\frac{1}{s_n} b_n^2 - \frac{1}{16 s_n^4} = \frac{1}{s_n^3}(1 - \frac{1}{16 s_n}) > 0$ show that $E_n(z_1 - z_2) < 1$ holds for $z_1 \in B(w_1, r_n), z_2 \in B(w_2, r_n)$ if and only if $\operatorname{Im} w_1 = \operatorname{Im} w_2 \wedge \operatorname{Re} w_2 < \operatorname{Re} w_1$ (with same-disc splittings $w_1 = w_2$ satisfying $E_n(z_1 - z_2) > 1$). Collapsing the double sum proves $\operatorname{ind}(W_{T,n,N}) = O(T)$ for all $N \ge N_{\mathrm{ann}}(T, n)$. Conversely, $N_\xi(T) > K_{\mathrm{strip}}(N_0)$ forces $N_0 < N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n)$, proving $N_{\mathrm{first}}(T, n)$ is unconditionally unbounded in $T$.
