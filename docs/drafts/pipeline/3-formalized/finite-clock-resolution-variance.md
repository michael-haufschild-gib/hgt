## Exact relation

Let `U` be isometric with period `d M`.  Write

```text
P_full = average of 1,U,...,U^(d M-1),
P_sub  = average of 1,U^d,...,(U^d)^(M-1).
```

Every full-clock stationary vector is subclock stationary, hence

```text
P_sub P_full = P_full.
```

Both averages are orthogonal projections, so for every vector `x`,

```text
||(1-P_full)x||^2
  = ||(1-P_sub)x||^2 + ||(P_sub-P_full)x||^2.             (1)
```

The observable conditional expectations form the corresponding invariant tower:

```text
Phi_sub(Phi_full(X)) = Phi_full(X).                        (2)
```

If `X` is self-adjoint and `U psi=psi`, the erased-information law turns `(1)` into a law of total
clock observability variance:

```text
Re <psi,D_full(X)psi>
  = Re <psi,D_sub(X)psi> + ||(P_sub-P_full)X psi||^2.      (3)
```

Thus the information inaccessible to the refined clock is the information already inaccessible to
the subclock plus one nonnegative, explicitly localized resolution layer.  No Hamiltonian or
temporal evolution occurs.
