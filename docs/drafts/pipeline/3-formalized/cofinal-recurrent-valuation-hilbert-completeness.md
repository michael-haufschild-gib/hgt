## Theorem

Let `sigma` be finite and let

```text
x : Primes -> C^sigma,
R_x = {v | {p | x(p)=v} is infinite}.
```

Assume the intrinsic cofinal-recurrence condition

```text
{p | x(p) notin R_x} is finite.                                      (CR)
```

For homogeneous degree `m`, let `I_x(m)` be the space of homogeneous polynomials vanishing on
`R_x`, and let

```text
H_x(m) = dim_C Sym^m(C^sigma) / I_x(m).
```

Then the following hold.

1. **Exact cofinal kernel.** For every observable `f` on the profile space,

   ```text
   {p | f(x(p)) != 0} is finite
       iff
   f vanishes on R_x.
   ```

   In particular, the degree-`m` valuation-tail relation space is exactly `I_x(m)`, and its
   quotient is canonically the recurrent evaluation image. Its dimension is `H_x(m)`.

2. **Recurring-pattern jet law.** Let a finite coefficient family have recurring joint values
   spanning its coefficient space. For every finite shift palette with pairwise distinct real
   parts and every finite jet cutoff, all shifted logarithmic jets remain independent modulo
   finite prime support. No finite-range assumption is required.

3. **Complete valuation basis.** Choose in each degree a basis of the recurrent homogeneous
   evaluation image. Put its coefficient columns in the matching prime-power valuation layer.
   Across any finite valuation window and finite shift palettes, all chosen carriers are jointly
   differentially independent over the intrinsic finite-prime rational coefficient field.

4. **Exact field and rank.** The chosen carriers form a differential transcendence basis for the
   field generated by all selected homogeneous monomial carriers. If the selected shifts in layer
   `m` form `A_m`, then

   ```text
   dtrdeg = sum_m card(A_m) H_x(m).
   ```

5. **Complete relation ideal.** Every differential relation among the full monomial palette is
   generated by the prolonged homogeneous coordinate reductions and their finite-prime base
   corrections. There are no additional nonlinear relations and no relations mixing distinct
   valuation degrees.

The positive structural law is therefore:

```text
cofinal recurrence forces every nonlinear profile constraint to reappear in matching
prime-valuation degree, and it forbids differential algebra from creating hidden cross-degree
constraints.
```

## Strict strengthening over finite-range tomography

Finite range implies `(CR)`, but `(CR)` allows infinitely many recurrent profile values. Hence the
theorem is no longer confined to reduced finite point sets or eventually constant Hilbert
functions. It provides the exact differential carrier theorem needed for positive-dimensional
recurrent constraint geometry.

The finite-range theorem is recovered verbatim. Degree one recovers the ordinary recurrent-profile
tail matroid. The genuinely new formal bridge is that recurring-pattern spanning, already known to
be the correct fibrewise jet condition, is enough to drive the complete valuation basis and
relation ideal without finite range.
