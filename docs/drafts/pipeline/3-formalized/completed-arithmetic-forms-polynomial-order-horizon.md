The relevant coefficient inequality is
$$P_k(q)\left(1-\frac{k(k-1)}{8q}\right)\le\gamma_kq^k.$$
Euler differentiation gives $\mathcal EP_k(q)\le kP_k(q)$ and
$\mathcal E^2P_k(q)\le k^2P_k(q)$ on the positive ray. With
$d_k=8q-k(k-1)$ and $A_k=8q(2k+1)+4k^2$, the exact payment is
$$8q(2k+1)d_k-A_kd_{k+1}=16qk+4k^3(k+1)\ge0.$$
It proves $d_kP_k(q)\le8q\gamma_kq^k$ by induction. At $q\ge k^2$,
$$7P_k(q)\le8\gamma_kq^k,\qquad
7q[P_k(q)-\gamma_kq^k]\le k^2\gamma_kq^k.$$
