### 1. Antiunitary generator–correlation dichotomy and cross-horizon Hankel law
Let `H` be a complex Hilbert space with antiunitary involution `J : AntiunitaryInvol H`.
1. **Commuting positive-energy collapse (`posGen_eq_zero_of_comm_antiunitaryInvol`).** Let `U : ℝ → (H →L[ℂ] H)` satisfy `J (U(a) (J x)) = U(a) x` for all `a ∈ ℝ`, `x ∈ H`, and let `P : H →L[ℂ] H` be self-adjoint (`star P = P`) with `0 ≤ Re ⟪x, P x⟫` and strong derivative `d/da|_{a=0} U(a) x = i P x` for all `x ∈ H`. Differentiating `J (U(a) (J x)) = U(a) x` at `a = 0` via real-linearity and continuity of `J` (`AntiunitaryInvol.toRealCLM`) gives `-i J(P(J x)) = i P x`, i.e. `P (J x) = -J (P x)`. Hence for every `x ∈ H`,
   `0 ≤ Re ⟪J x, P (J x)⟫ = -Re ⟪J x, J (P x)⟫ = -Re ⟪P x, x⟫ = -Re ⟪x, P x⟫ ≤ 0`,
   so `Re ⟪x, P x⟫ = 0` for all `x ∈ H`. Self-adjointness forces `Im ⟪x, P x⟫ = 0`, so `⟪x, P x⟫ = 0` for all `x ∈ H`, and polarization gives `P = 0`.
2. **Cross-horizon Hankel law (`StandardForm.naturalCone_partner_hankel`).** Let `(H, M, ms)` be a `StandardForm M`, `S` a commutative semigroup, and `U : S → (H →L[ℂ] H)` a multiplicative isometric representation (`U(s * t) = U(s) * U(t)`, `star (U s) * U s = 1`) fixing `ms.Ω` (`U s ms.Ω = ms.Ω`) and inverted by `ms.J` (`ms.J.conjOp (U s) = star (U s)`). Then `(star (U r)) ms.Ω = (star (U r) * U r) ms.Ω = ms.Ω`. Setting `covShift U s A := U s * A * star (U s)`, for all `A₀, B₀ : H →L[ℂ] H` and `s, t ∈ S`:
   `ms.J.conjOp (covShift U t B₀) ms.Ω = ms.J (U t (B₀ ((star (U t)) ms.Ω))) = ms.J.conjOp (U t) (ms.J (B₀ ms.Ω)) = (star (U t)) (ms.J (B₀ ms.Ω))`.
   Applying `covShift U s A₀` and using `star (U s) * star (U t) = star (U t * U s) = star (U (s * t))` together with `(star (U s)) ms.Ω = ms.Ω` gives
   `⟪ms.Ω, covShift U s A₀ (ms.J.conjOp (covShift U t B₀) ms.Ω)⟫ = ⟪(star A₀) ms.Ω, (star (U (s * t))) (ms.J (B₀ ms.Ω))⟫`,
   which depends on `(s, t)` solely through `s * t`.

### 2. Positive classification of Partner-Hankel `∩` Hecke-isometric kernels (Square-class survivor)
1. **Square-class equivalence on `ℕ+` (`isMulHankel_and_heckeInv_iff_sqClass`, `mulHankel_heckeInv_iff_factorsThrough_sqFreeCore`).** A kernel `K : ℕ+ → ℕ+ → α` is both multiplicatively Hankel (`∃ g, K m n = g (m * n)`) and Hecke-dilation invariant (`K (p * m) (p * n) = K m n`) iff `∃ g : ℕ+ → α, (∀ p k, g (p ^ 2 * k) = g k) ∧ ∀ m n, K m n = g (m * n)`. Writing `sqFreeCore (n : ℕ+) : ℕ+` for `∏ p ∈ (n : ℕ).primeFactors, p ^ ((n : ℕ).factorization p % 2)`, prime-factorization division `e_p = 2(e_p / 2) + e_p % 2` gives `n = (sqRootPart n) ^ 2 * sqFreeCore n` with `Squarefree (sqFreeCore n : ℕ)` and `sqFreeCore (p ^ 2 * k) = sqFreeCore k`. Hence `∀ p k, g (p ^ 2 * k) = g k` holds iff `g` factors through `sqFreeCore`.
2. **Nontrivial noncommutative `StandardForm` realization (`exists_nontrivial_vacuumFixing_partner_standardForm_heckeInv`).** On `H2 = EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin 2 × Fin 2)` with `mat2VN = (Set.range rightMul2)′`, `Ω2 = (1 / √2) (e_{00} + e_{11})`, and `J2(X)_{ij} = conj(X_{ji})`, `mat2StandardForm` is a `StandardForm mat2VN`. For `chi2(n) = (-1)^{(n : ℕ).factorization 2}` and `D2(n) = diag(1, chi2(n))`, conjugation `U(n) = leftMul2(D2 n) * rightMul2(D2 n)` is a multiplicative unitary representation fixing `Ω2` with `J2.conjOp (U n) = star (U n)`, `U(2) ∉ mat2VN`, `U(2) ∉ mat2VN.commutant`, and `covShift U n A₀ ∈ mat2VN` for `A₀ = leftMul2 !![1, 1; 0, 0]`. Its natural-cone kernel `K(m, n) = (1 + Re(chi2(m * n))) / 2` is positive semidefinite, Hecke-dilation invariant, and nonconstant (`K(1, 1) = 1 ≠ 0 = K(1, 2)`).
3. **Constant collapse (`const_of_mulHankel_eq_logToeplitz`, `const_of_sqClass_laplaceMeasure`).**
   - *Continuous log-Toeplitz:* If `Φ : ℝ → β` (`T2Space β`) is continuous and `Φ(log(m / n)) = g(m * n)`, then `(a ^ 2) * (b ^ 2) = (a * b) ^ 2` gives `Φ(2 log(a / b)) = Φ(0)`, and density of `{2 log(a / b) : a, b ∈ ℕ+}` in `ℝ` (`dense_mul_log_pnat_div`) forces `Φ(y) = Φ(0)` everywhere.
   - *Laplace positive energy:* For a finite positive measure `μ` on `ℝ` with `μ(Iio 0) = 0`, `laplaceProfile μ 4 = laplaceProfile μ 1` gives `∫ λ in Ici 0, (1 - exp(-λ log 4)) ∂μ = 0` with integrand strictly positive on `(0, ∞)`, forcing `μ(Ioi 0) = 0` and `laplaceProfile μ n = laplaceProfile μ 1` for all `n : ℕ+`.

### 3. Borchers `ax + b` spectral and Bohr-frequency rigidity
Let `B : BorchersPair H` (`Δ(s) U(a) Δ(-s) = U(e^{-2πs} a)`).
1. **Forced vacuum invariance and strict externality (`BorchersPair.fix_of_delta_fix`, `BorchersPair.mem_iff_eq_one`).** If `∀ s, B.Δ s Ω = Ω`, then `‖B.U a Ω - Ω‖ = ‖B.U (e^{-2πs} a) Ω - Ω‖ → 0` as `s → +∞`, so `B.U a Ω = Ω`, and in any `StandardForm M` separatingness of `ms.Ω` gives `B.U a ∈ M ↔ B.U a = 1` and `B.U a ∈ M.commutant ↔ B.U a = 1`.
2. **Absence of non-identity eigenvectors and triviality on finite-dimensional subspaces (`BorchersPair.eigenmode_eq_zero`, `BorchersPair.eigenvector_eq_zero_of_ne_one`, `BorchersPair.trivial_on_finiteDimensional`, `BorchersPair.no_nontrivial_borchers_on_quadraticAlgebra`).**
   - If `∀ a, B.U a v = exp(i ω a) • v` with `ω ≠ 0`, then for `s ≠ 0` and `a₀ := π / ((1 - e^{-2πs}) ω)`, isometry of `B.U a₀` on `⟪v, B.Δ (-s) v⟫` yields `⟪v, B.Δ (-s) v⟫ = -⟪v, B.Δ (-s) v⟫ = 0`, and `s → 0` gives `‖v‖² = 0`, hence `v = 0`.
   - Suppose `B.U a w = μ • w` with `a > 0` and `μ ≠ 1` (if `‖μ‖ ≠ 1` then isometry forces `w = 0`; if `a < 0`, apply to `-a > 0` with eigenvalue `conj μ ≠ 1`). Writing `μ = exp(i θ)` with `θ := μ.arg ≠ 0` and `ω_k := (θ + 2π k) / a ≠ 0`, each `F_k(t) := exp(-i ω_k t) • B.U t w` is `a`-periodic, so `w_k := ∫ t in 0..a, F_k(t)` satisfies `B.U b w_k = exp(i ω_k b) • w_k`, forcing `w_k = 0` for all `k ∈ ℤ`. Shifting `g₁(t) := F_0(t - a / 2)` shows `ψ(t) := Re ⟪w, g₁(t)⟫` has `∫ t in 0..a, exp(-i (2π k / a) t) ψ(t) = 0` for all `k ∈ ℤ`, hence `∫ t in 0..a, (sin(π t / a)^2)^m ψ(t) = 0` for all `m ∈ ℕ`, while `ψ(a / 2) = ‖w‖²` and `|ψ(t)| ≤ ‖w‖²`. Strict peaking of `sin(π t / a)^2` at `t = a / 2` (`peaked_integral_pow_zero_imp_le_zero`) forces `‖w‖² ≤ 0`, so `w = 0`.
   - On any finite-dimensional `B.U`-invariant subspace `V ≤ H`, for each `a ≠ 0` the finite-dimensional unitary `U_V(a)` decomposes `V` into generalized eigenspaces (`Module.End.exists_eigenvector_of_isAlgClosed` on `(ker(U_V(a) - 1))ᗮ`), every eigenvalue `μ ≠ 1` has no nonzero eigenvector by `eigenvector_eq_zero_of_ne_one`, so `U_V(a) = 1`. In particular, `B.U` acts trivially on every finite-dimensional carrier of `QuadraticAlgebra ℝ (-q) t`.
3. **Antiperiodic triviality and vanishing of alternating Cesàro Bohr averages (`BorchersPair.antiperiodic_eq_zero`, `BorchersPair.alternating_cesaro_tendsto_zero`).** Specializing `eigenvector_eq_zero_of_ne_one` to `μ = -1` gives `ker(-B.U τ - 1) = {0}` for all `τ ≠ 0`. For `γ ≠ 0` and `W := -B.U (π / γ)`, both `ker(W - 1)` and `ker(star W - 1) = ker(-B.U (-π / γ) - 1)` vanish, so `(LinearMap.range (W - 1))ᗮ = ⊥` and `range(W - 1)` is dense in `H`. Telescoping `∑_{k < K} W^k (W - 1) x = W^K x - x` on dense coboundaries and approximating within `ε / 2` yields `(1 / K) ∑_{k < K} (-1)^k B.U (k π / γ) ψ → 0` and `(1 / K) ∑_{k < K} (-1)^k ⟪ψ, B.U (k π / γ) ψ⟫ → 0`.

### 4. Unconditional resolution of B114 over `ℤ` and partner standard-form trichotomy
1. **Cross-horizon natural-cone impossibility (`no_vacuumFixing_partner_tateLimit_integerProbe`, `no_vacuumFixing_partner_bornRule_integerProbe`).** Degree-2 homogeneity (`eq_of_quadratic_tateLimit`), polarization on `ℤ^N` (`symmKernel_eq_splineCorr_of_integerProbe`), and Section 1.2 force `splineCorr(log(m / n)) = (g(m * n) : ℂ)`; by `const_of_mulHankel_eq_logToeplitz`, `splineCorr` is constant on `ℝ`, contradicting `exists_coprimeCrossDiff_ratCorr_ne_zero`.
2. **One-sided Borchers GNS / Lax–Phillips impossibility (`no_borchers_gns_tateLimit_integerProbe`, `no_borchers_gns_bornRule_integerProbe`).** Homogeneity forces `integerProbe N a = ‖∑_{i < N} a_i B.U(log(i + 1)) ψ₀‖² ≥ 0`, hence `RiemannHypothesis` (`riemannHypothesis_iff_integerProbes`), `Re ⟪ψ₀, B.U y ψ₀⟫ = Re (splineCorr y)` on `ℝ` by log-density, and `splineCorr y = ∑' j, (onLineWeight e j : ℂ) * exp(i (e j).im y)` with `onLineWeight e j ≥ 0`. Selecting `ρ₀ ∈ criticalSeen \ {1/2}` (`criticalSeen_infinite`) gives `γ₀ := ρ₀.im ≠ 0` and `onLineWeight e j₀ > 0`, so dominated convergence (`tendsto_tsum_of_dominated_convergence`) forces `(1 / K) ∑_{k < K} (-1)^k Re (splineCorr (k π / γ₀)) → ∑' j, (if z_j = 1 then onLineWeight e j else 0) ≥ onLineWeight e j₀ > 0`, contradicting `BorchersPair.alternating_cesaro_tendsto_zero`.
3. **Half-sided modular inclusion impossibility and trichotomy (`no_halfSided_modular_inclusion_carries_integerProbe`, `partner_standardForm_trichotomy`).** Combining `BorchersPair.fix_of_delta_fix` with (1) and (2) rules out both the cross-horizon natural-cone and one-sided GNS readings on any `HalfSidedPartnerInclusion` and establishes the three-way classification across internal central, external vacuum-fixing, and external Borchers standard-form architectures.
