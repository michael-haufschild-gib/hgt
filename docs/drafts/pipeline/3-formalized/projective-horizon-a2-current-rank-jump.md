## Statement

Work in `sl₃(K[t])` over a characteristic-zero field.  The existing two-horizon projective-plane
algebra contains every polynomial mode of

```text
E₀₁, E₁₀, E₂₀, E₂₁, H₀₁=E₀₀-E₁₁,
```

but this is only the parabolic current algebra
`sl₂(K[t]) semidirect (K[t]²)^*`.  Adjoin one constant opposite transverse shear

```text
W₀=E₀₂.
```

Then the generated Lie algebra contains, for every `k>=0`,

```text
t^k E_ij  for every i != j in {0,1,2},
t^k(E₀₀-E₁₁),
t^k(E₀₀-E₂₂).
```

Consequently it contains every Chevalley current mode of `sl₃(K[t])`.  In every Lie-module
representation, a vector annihilated by the eight old depth-two modes and by `W₀` is annihilated
by the full `A₂` current algebra.

## Proof

Write

```text
W_k=t^k E₀₂,
X_k=t^k E₁₂,
J_k=t^k(E₀₀-E₂₂).
```

The old algebra already contains `H₁=t(E₀₀-E₁₁)` and every mode of `F=E₁₀` and `U=E₂₀`.  Direct
matrix multiplication gives

```text
[H_i,W_j]=W_(i+j),
[F_i,W_j]=X_(i+j),
[W_i,U_j]=J_(i+j).                                      (1)
```

Starting from the adjoined `W₀`, the first bracket with `H₁` recursively generates every `W_k`.
The second bracket with `F₀` then generates every `X_k`, and the third bracket with `U_k` generates
every `J_k`.  Together with the five old mode families, these are the six root spaces and two
Cartan directions of `A₂` in every polynomial degree.

For a Lie-module vector `v`, its annihilator is a Lie subalgebra.  If it contains the nine
generators, it contains their Lie span and hence every mode above.

## Why the rank jump matters

Every obstruction family isolated so far is rank one: finite-tail flips, dyadic branch monodromy,
or a differential clock torsor.  Their natural transformation groupoids admit unbounded Hilbert
cocycles.  The old two-horizon algebra has only an `sl₂` Levi and therefore does not reach the
higher-rank rigidity boundary.

The single opposite shear is the exact algebraic transition from that persistent parabolic regime
to an irreducible rank-two root system.  For a finitely generated coefficient ring `R`, the parent
theorem of Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain--Kassabov gives property `(T)` to the corresponding elementary
`A₂` group.  The theorem here does **not** yet construct that group action from the completed HT
source; it proves that the current-algebra rank required by the rigidity mechanism is generated by
one additional horizon direction rather than postulated.
