# Conjugation of the zeros is the word of the turns around the real branch times

- **ID:** galois-zero-conjugation-word
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Source:** `docs/reports/review-1311/obstacles-summary.md` §2.19 question 4; programme card `gtz-galois-theory-of-zeros-programme.md`
- **Statement:** over a positive real time `t` off the branch times, `cr = λ_m ⋯ λ_1`, where `p_1 < … < p_m` are the real branch times above `t` and `λ_j` is the lasso at `t` whose head is a small turn around `p_j` and whose tail runs from `t` through the closed upper half-plane.

## Goal

`fibreConj t ∈ forwardMonodromy t` is a theorem; the element was not written in the generators.
The word makes the real-time criterion a statement on a list: RH ⇔ the list is empty at every `t > 0`.

## Notation

`c_t = fibreConj t` on `Fibre t`, `t` real. `U_γ : Fibre (γ 0) ≃ Fibre (γ 1)` the transport along
a forward path `γ`. `γ̄` the conjugate path. Products are compositions of maps.

## Paper proof

**Lemma 1 (conjugate path).** If `γ` is a forward path from a real time `t` to a real time `t'`,
then `U_γ̄ ∘ c_t = c_{t'} ∘ U_γ`.

*Proof.* `Transports γ z w` gives `Transports γ̄ z̄ w̄` (`Transports.conj`), and transports along a
forward path are unique. ∎

**Lemma 2 (real segment).** If the segment `[t, t']` of positive real times has no branch time,
its transport `S` satisfies `S ∘ c_t = c_{t'} ∘ S`. (Lemma 1 with `γ̄ = γ`.)

**Lemma 3 (turn law).** Let `p` be real, `r > 0`, the circle `T(s) = p + r² e^{2πis}` a forward
path, `A` its upper half from `p + r²` to `p − r²`. Then the lower half is the reversed
conjugate of `A`, so with `c_± = c_{p ± r²}` and `θ` the transport along the circle

`θ = U_{Ā}⁻¹ ∘ U_A = c_+ ∘ U_A⁻¹ ∘ c_- ∘ U_A`,  i.e.  `c_- = U_A ∘ (c_+ ∘ θ) ∘ U_A⁻¹`.

*Proof.* `T(1 − s) = conj T(s)`; cut the circle at `s = 1/2` (`fibreEquiv_cut`), apply Lemma 1
to `A` and reverse (`fibreEquiv_reverse`). ∎

**Theorem.** Let `t > 0` be real, not a branch time, `ε > 0`. Let `p_1 < … < p_m` be the real
branch times in `(t, ∞)` (all are `< 1/8`; finitely many). There are radii `r_j < ε` and forward
paths `δ_j` from `t` to `p_j + r_j²` with `0 ≤ Im δ_j ≤ ε²` such that, with
`λ_j = U_{δ_j}⁻¹ ∘ θ_{p_j, r_j} ∘ U_{δ_j}`,

`c_t = λ_m ∘ λ_{m−1} ∘ ⋯ ∘ λ_1`.

*Proof.* Induction on `m`. For `m = 0`: if `t ≥ 1/8` the zeros are real and `c_t = 1`; otherwise
the segment `[t, 1/8]` is a forward path and Lemma 2 gives `c_t = S⁻¹ c_{1/8} S = 1`.

Step. Choose `r < ε` with `t < p_1 − r²`, the closed disc of radius `r²` around `p_1` free of other
branch times (branch times are isolated in the forward half-plane), and the circle a forward
path. Put `s_1 = p_1 + r²`; the real branch times above `s_1` are `p_2 < … < p_m`. Let `η` be the
segment `[t, p_1 − r²]` followed by the reversed upper half circle, `W = U_η = U_A⁻¹ ∘ S`.
By Lemmas 2 and 3,

`c_t = S⁻¹ c_- S = S⁻¹ U_A (c_{s_1} θ) U_A⁻¹ S = W⁻¹ (c_{s_1} ∘ θ) W`.

By induction `c_{s_1} = λ'_m ⋯ λ'_2` with lassos `λ'_j` at `s_1`, tails `δ'_j`. Then

`c_t = (W⁻¹ λ'_m W) ⋯ (W⁻¹ λ'_2 W)(W⁻¹ θ W)`,

and `W⁻¹ λ'_j W` is the lasso at `t` with tail `η · δ'_j`, `W⁻¹ θ W` the lasso with tail `η`. ∎

**Corollaries.**
1. `c_t` lies in the subgroup generated by the turn lassos around the real branch times above `t`.
2. `c_+ ∘ θ` is an involution for every turn around a real point: `c_+ θ c_+ = θ⁻¹`.
3. The number of factors is the number of real branch times above `t`; RH ⇔ the word is empty
   at every `t > 0`.
