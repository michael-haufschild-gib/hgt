# The turns generate the forward monodromy group; only a collision off the centre moves a pair

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Question:** review-1311 §2.19 question 1 (B193: generators of `G₊`; the collision away
  from the centre as the only source of the permutation part).

`H_τ(z)` the heat family. A *forward path* is a continuous `γ : [0,1] → {Re τ > 0}` off the
branch times; `T_γ` its transport of zeros, a bijection of the end fibres; `G₊(b)` the group
of the transports along forward loops at `b`. The *turn* `ω_{p,r}` is the circle
`p + r²e^{2πis}`. A *lasso* at `b` is `δ · ω · δ⁻¹`, `δ` a forward path from `b` (the tail),
`ω` a forward loop at the end of `δ` (the head). `N` is a subgroup of the permutations of the
fibre over `b`.

## Theorems

1. (Functoriality.) `T_{γ₁γ₂} = T_{γ₂} ∘ T_{γ₁}`, `T_{γ⁻¹} = T_γ⁻¹`, and the transports along
   the parts of one path compose.
2. (Gluing.) Let `A₁`, `A₂` be open sets of times and `h` a base time. Suppose
   (i) every forward loop at `h` inside `A_i` has its transport in `N`;
   (ii) every forward time of `A_i` off the branch times is the end of a forward path from
   `h` inside `A_i`;
   (iii) every such time `x ∈ A₁ ∩ A₂` is the end of forward paths `η₁ ⊂ A₁`, `η₂ ⊂ A₂`
   from `h` with `T_{η₂}⁻¹ T_{η₁} ∈ N`.
   Then every forward loop at `h` inside `A₁ ∪ A₂` has its transport in `N`.
3. (Gluing of lassos.) The same for lassos with tails inside a set `D ⊇ A₁ ∪ A₂`, with the
   junction (iii) required after every tail from `b` to one common time `x₀ ∈ A₁ ∩ A₂`.
4. (One branch time.) Let `C ⊂ D` be convex and open, of forward times, with exactly one
   branch time `p`, and let the closed disc of radius `r²` around `p` lie in `C`. If every
   lasso `δ · ω_{p,r} · δ⁻¹` with tail inside `D` has its transport in `N`, then every lasso
   with tail inside `D` and head inside `C` has.
5. (Generation over a convex region.) Let `D` be a convex open set of forward times and `b ∈ D`
   off the branch times. Suppose that for every branch time `p ∈ D` there are arbitrarily small
   `r` such that every lasso `δ · ω_{p,r} · δ⁻¹` with tail inside `D` has its transport in
   `N`. Then every forward loop at `b` inside `D` has its transport in `N`.
6. (Generation.) `G₊(b)` is generated by the transports of the lassos `δ · ω_{p,r} · δ⁻¹`,
   `p` a forward branch time. For each `p` any set of radii that accumulates at `0` suffices.
7. (Central turn.) Let `Re τ₀ > 0` and let every multiple zero of `H_τ₀` be `0`. For small `r`
   the turn `ω_{τ₀,r}` carries every zero `z` to `z` or to `−z`.
8. (Local theorem.) Let `D` be a convex open set of forward times over which every multiple
   zero is `0`. Every forward loop inside `D` carries every zero `z` to `z` or to `−z`.
9. (Detector.) A forward loop inside a convex open set `D` of forward times that carries a
   zero `z` to a zero other than `±z` certifies a multiple zero `ζ ≠ 0` of `H_τ` for some
   `τ ∈ D`.
10. (Criterion.) Every element of `G₊(b)` carries every zero `z` to `±z` if and only if no
    `H_τ`, `Re τ > 0`, has a multiple zero `ζ ≠ 0`.
11. (The group without exchanges.) If no `H_τ`, `Re τ > 0`, has a multiple zero `ζ ≠ 0`, then
    `σ² = 1` for every `σ ∈ G₊(b)` and `G₊(b)` is commutative.

## Proof

1. A lift along `γ₁` followed by a lift along `γ₂` is a lift along `γ₁γ₂`; lifts are unique.
   A lift `z` along `γ` restricted to `[a, b]` and reparametrized is a lift along the part.
2. Lebesgue: the loop `γ` is cut at `k/n` into parts, each inside `A₁` or `A₂`. Invariant at
   the parameter `t`: for `B ∈ {A₁, A₂}` and every forward path `η ⊂ B` from `h` to `γ(t)`,
   `T_η⁻¹ T_{γ|[0,t]} ∈ N`. At `t = 0` this is (i). Step over a part in `B`: with paths
   `θ, ζ ⊂ B` from `h` to the ends of the part (by (ii)),
   `T_η⁻¹ T_{γ|[0,t']} = (T_η⁻¹ T_ζ)(T_ζ⁻¹ T_{part} T_θ)(T_θ⁻¹ T_{γ|[0,t]})`: the last factor is
   the invariant, the middle one a loop inside `B`, and the first a change of the return
   path, in `N` by (i) when `η ⊂ B` and by (iii) and (i) otherwise. At `t = 1` take the
   constant path.
3. For a head `γ` at the end `y` of the tail `δ`, choose `θ ⊂ A_i` from `x₀` to `y`. The lasso
   `δ γ δ⁻¹` equals the lasso with tail `δθ⁻¹` and head `θγθ⁻¹`, a loop at `x₀` inside
   `A₁ ∪ A₂`. Apply 2 at the base `x₀` to the subgroup `T_{δθ⁻¹} N T_{δθ⁻¹}⁻¹`.
4. Three open half-planes with `p` on the boundary, in the directions `π/3`, `π`, `−π/3`,
   cover `ℂ ∖ {p}`; their parts `S₁, S₂, S₃` in `C` are convex without branch times, so their
   loops have the trivial transport (homotopy invariance). The arcs `s ∈ [0,⅓], [⅓,⅔], [⅔,1]`
   of the turn lie in `S₁, S₂, S₃`. Glue `S₁` and `S₂` at the base `ω(⅓)`: the intersection is
   convex, one path serves both. Glue `S₁ ∪ S₂` and `S₃` at the base `ω(0)`: over a time of
   `S₁ ∩ S₃` one path serves both; over `x ∈ S₂ ∩ S₃` take `η₁ = ω|[0,⅔] · ξ` and
   `η₂ = (ω|[⅔,1])⁻¹ · ξ`, `ξ ⊂ S₂ ∩ S₃` from `ω(⅔)` to `x`; then `T_{η₂}⁻¹ T_{η₁}` is the
   transport of the turn, which is the hypothesis.
5. The loop lies in `C = D ∩ {Re τ > t₀} ∩ {‖τ‖ < T}`, convex, open, with finitely many branch
   times. Induction on their number `n` for convex open `C ⊂ D`. `n = 0`: trivial transport.
   `n = 1`: 4 with a small turn. `n ≥ 2`: for two branch times `p₁, p₂` and
   `φ(τ) = ⟨τ, p₂ − p₁⟩`, choose `c ∈ (φ(p₁), φ(p₂))` and `ε > 0` such that no branch time of
   `C` has `|φ − c| < ε`. `C₁ = C ∩ {φ < c + ε}` and `C₂ = C ∩ {φ > c − ε}` are convex with
   fewer branch times, `C₁ ∩ C₂` is convex without branch times, and a point of the segment
   `[p₁, p₂]` lies in it. Glue by 3. In convex open sets with finitely many branch times two
   times off them are joined by an arc `x + (y−x)(s + iηs(1−s))` for all but finitely many
   small `η`.
6. `⊆`: 5 with `D = {Re τ > 0}` and `N` the generated subgroup. `⊇`: a lasso is a forward
   loop.
7. By the theorem on the turn on the whole fibre, a zero that does not return is a sheet
   `Z_ρ(r)` of a collision of order `≥ 2` at `τ₀` and goes to `Z_{−ρ}(r)`. The collision is at
   the centre, and there the reflection law gives `Z_{−ρ} = −Z_ρ`, for all roots `ρ` of the
   profile at once.
8. The permutations that carry every `z` to `±z` form a subgroup `N`. Transport along a tail
   commutes with `z ↦ −z`, so by 7 the lassos of small turns are in `N`. Apply 5.
9. Contrapositive of 8.
10. `⇐`: 8 with `D = {Re τ > 0}`. `⇒`: a multiple zero `ζ ≠ 0` at a forward time gives an
    element that moves a pair (card `galois-zero-forward-monodromy-group-and-conjugation`).
11. Every element commutes with `z ↦ −z`: if `σz = −z` then `σ²z = −σz = z`. A group of
    involutions is commutative.
