## Exact structural result

For arbitrary characters chi_i of positive moduli and arbitrary complex
translations b_i, let `F_i(s)=completedLFunction chi_i (2*s+b_i)` and
`lambda_i=(b_i+parity(chi_i))/2`. Over the actual upper-end differential
field B generated by the coordinate s and **all** finite-abscissa absolutely
convergent ordinary Dirichlet series, the finite family F_i is differentially
independent **iff the lambda_i occupy distinct integer-translation orbits**.
The same iff holds for its logarithmic derivatives. When two labels share
an orbit, the actual quotient of the corresponding completed functions lies
in B. Every actual character yields arbitrarily large explicit independent
translated families; unshifted zeta and the conductor-four quadratic
L-function attain the two parity channels. This entire classification,
including its positive survivor and explicit attainments, counts once.

## Unshifted specialization and normalization

Let B be the actual upper-end meromorphic field generated by the Dirichlet

convergence, as in `HTGeometry/DigammaOverDirichletBase.lean`. Work in the
doubled argument: a nonzero Dirichlet character chi of positive modulus q,

`F_chi(s) = completedLFunction chi (2*s)`.

The corpus uses Mathlib's normalization, without the conductor power:

`pi^(-s-a/2) Gamma(s+a/2) L(chi,2s)`.
The natural parameter class is every Dirichlet character of positive modulus;
each has coefficient 1 at the integer 1, so no extra nonzero-character
premise is needed.
A finite family of these actual completed profiles is
differentially independent over B exactly when its parity labels are
injective. Thus the completed family has two possible independent
archimedean differential channels, regardless of how many Euler sources B
already contains. Each nonempty parity class contributes one channel.
The logarithmic-derivative family has the same classification.

   finite-abscissa series remain finite-abscissa; rational operations and
   the Dirichlet coordinate preserve that class. Existing differential
   realization machinery may supply this, but B has not yet been verified
   as a differential field in this exact representation.
2. Establish joint differential independence over B of the two actual
   shifted digammas psi(s) and psi(s+1/2). Their unit-shift defects have poles
   on distinct integer-translation orbits. The exact rational-defect
   inertness theorem says adjoining all Dirichlet series creates no new
   rational shift coboundaries. Combine it with the joint pole/jet descent,
   rather than assuming independent shifts over B from single-function
   hypertranscendence.
3. Check the literal logarithmic-derivative decomposition
   `D(F_chi)/F_chi = (-I)*psi(s+a/2) + I*log(pi) + D(ell_chi)/ell_chi`,
   with `D=d/dz`, `s=-I*z` and `ell_chi=L(chi,2s)`.
   The last two terms belong to B. The doubled L-series is an ordinary
   Dirichlet series supported on squares; nonvanishing on a right half-plane
   and all character admissibility facts must come from actual Lean sources.
4. Distinct parities inherit joint differential independence. Equal parities
   differ by a B-valued logarithmic term and are differentially dependent.
   Use the existing raw/logarithmic-coordinate matroid theorem to transport
   the exact classification to the completed functions themselves.

## Dependency-closed paper proof (2026-10-17)

Write E for the upper-end meromorphic field, s=-I*z, D=d/dz, and
sigma(f)(z)=f(z+I). Let B be `dirZetaBase` and R=C(s). The following
argument uses the actual germs in E throughout.

1. **Differential coefficient field.** D(s)=-I and D(c)=0. For each
   finite-abscissa source f, the corpus proves
   `D(realize f)=realize(finiteAbscissaRotatedDeriv f)` in
   `PrimeResidueValuationAnalyticSuperselection`. Logarithmic weighting
   preserves finite abscissa (`FiniteAbscissaDirichletUpperEnd`). The sum,
   product and inverse derivation rules therefore prove D(B) is contained
   in B by intermediate-field adjunction induction. B is sigma-stable and
   B^sigma=C by `DigammaOverDirichletBase`.

2. **Relative joint shifted-digamma freeness.** For any finite shifts
   lambda_i pairwise incongruent modulo Z, put y_i=psi(s+lambda_i).
   These are translations by I*lambda_i of the actual rotated digamma
   carrier. Translation commutes with D, and
   sigma(D^j y_i)=D^j y_i+(-I)^j j!(-1)^j/(s+lambda_i)^(j+1).
   A finite jet-polynomial relation over B generates a shift-stable
   annihilator. The minimal-degree/top-support elimination in
   `ShiftEliminationEngine.no_shift_stable_annihilatorGen` produces
   a nonzero finite constant charge matrix delta and w in B whose shift
   defect is the corresponding sum of those rational increments.
   This eliminator only requires B^sigma=C. It does NOT require E^sigma=C;
   that stronger condition is false because E has nonconstant periodic
   germs. `SourceLogarithmicBoundary`'s ambient-fixed-field criterion is
   consequently not applicable here.

   `DirichletDefectInertness.rational_defect_inert` replaces w by an element
   of R with exactly the same defect. Identify R with C(X) by X↦s.
   Absorb the nonzero factors (-I)^j into delta. Then
   `ShiftedDigammaFamilyIncrement.ratShift_sub_familyIncrement_combination`
   forces every charge to vanish: at the largest charged jet order, the
   pole on each selected integer orbit is isolated and has nonzero leading
   coefficient, whereas a rational shift coboundary cannot have one lone
   pole on an orbit. Descending the jet order kills the whole matrix.
   Contradiction. Thus all jets of the family are algebraically independent
   over B. No assertion about the ambient periodic field is used.

3. **Actual arithmetic sources.** Given chi modulo q>0, put f(0)=0 and
   f(n)=chi(n) for n>0. `DirichletCharacter.norm_le_one` and absolute
   convergence for Re(s)>1 put f in the finite-abscissa algebra. Its
   square-supported dilation g(n^2)=f(n), zero elsewhere, belongs to the
   same algebra by `finiteAbscissaPrimePowerDilation` at e=1. The literal
   dilation identity gives L(g,s)=L(chi,2s) on a right half-plane.
   Its germ ell_chi is nonzero because g(1)=1 and the L-series realization
   is injective. Hence D(ell_chi)/ell_chi belongs to B. This discharges
   arithmetic admission and nonvanishing without an Euler-product or RH
   premise.

4. **Literal completion.** Mathlib's `gammaFactor` equals GammaR(2s+a),
   where a=0 for even chi and a=1 for odd chi. Its definition is
   pi^(-s-a/2) Gamma(s+a/2). The theorem
   `LFunction_eq_completed_div_gammaFactor`, applied in Re(2s)>1, identifies
   this product with the actual completedLFunction germ. The Gamma factor
   and pi exponential are nonzero on that half-plane. Differentiating gives
   D(F_chi)/F_chi = (-I)*psi(s+a/2) + I*log(pi)
                    + D(ell_chi)/ell_chi.
   Thus each logarithmic completion coordinate is a fixed nonzero constant
   multiple of its parity digamma plus an explicitly B-valued term.

5. **Exact classification.** The shifts 0 and 1/2 are incongruent modulo Z.
   Step 2 makes their digammas jointly differentially independent over B.
   Multiplying coordinates by nonzero B-constants and adding B-elements
   preserves algebraic independence of all jets by an invertible triangular
   affine substitution. Any parity-injective subfamily is therefore
   differentially independent. If two parity labels coincide, subtracting
   their logarithmic completion coordinates lies in B, a nontrivial linear
   relation. Conversely, injective parity labels select at most these two
   free coordinates. This proves the iff, including empty families.
   The corpus's finite raw/logarithmic-coordinate differential-matroid
   equivalence transfers the iff to F_chi itself. Equal-parity raw ratios
   also lie in B: the common Gamma and pi^(-s) factors cancel and the
   remaining pi parity factor is constant. Modulus-1 trivial character and
   `quadraticCharacterFour` (proved odd in `SymmetricShiftDirichletExample`)
   realize both channels, so the upper bound two is attained.
