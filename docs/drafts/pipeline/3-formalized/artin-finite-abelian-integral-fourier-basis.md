# Finite-abelian integral Fourier basis for concrete Artin characters

- **ID:** `artin-finite-abelian-integral-fourier-basis`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM
- **Status:** `[LITERATURE] [PAPER-PROVED] [LEAN-PROVED] [BRIDGE] [NOT NEW]`
- **Source:** Classical finite-group character theory; Brauer, *On Artin's L-Series with General
  Group Characters*, Ann. of Math. 48 (1947), 502–514, DOI 10.2307/1969183.

## Exact claim

For every finite abelian group `G`, each concrete virtual complex character is a finite
`ℤ`-linear combination of the concrete one-dimensional characters `G →* ℂˣ`; each concrete
genuine character is a finite `ℕ`-linear combination of them.  More precisely, for
`λ : G →* ℂˣ`, let `linGen(λ)` and `linVirt(λ)` be the concrete character of the corresponding
one-dimensional scalar representation and its virtual lift.  Then

```text
θ(g) = Σ_λ (virtualMultiplicity G (linVirt λ) θ : ℂ) · λ(g)
θ(g) = Σ_λ (genuineMultiplicity G (linGen λ) θ : ℂ) · λ(g).
```

Consequently, the concrete virtual-character group equals the additive subgroup generated by the
`linVirt(λ)`, and the concrete genuine-character monoid equals the additive submonoid generated
by the `linGen(λ)`.

## Paper proof

1. `RHTower.deningerCharacter_linearIndependent` and `RHTower.deningerCharacter_span` give a
   complex basis of character functions.  This alone is insufficient: its coordinates could be
   arbitrary complex numbers.
2. Normalized orthogonality of the one-dimensional scalar representations makes this basis
   orthonormal for `characterPairing`; Fourier inversion follows by span induction.
3. Existing exact cast theorems identify the coordinate of `λ` with
   `virtualMultiplicity G (linVirt λ) θ : ℤ` or
   `genuineMultiplicity G (linGen λ) θ : ℕ`.
4. The resulting subtype equalities place every character in the indicated closure.  The reverse
   inclusions hold because each linear character is itself concrete genuine, hence concrete
   virtual.

No step assumes Artin holomorphy, automorphy, Brauer induction, a factorization theorem, or an
unproved character-decomposition interface.

## Boundary

infrastructure, not new mathematical knowledge and not an Artin-holomorphy proof.  The separate
transport from same-group linear generators to literal `Ind_G^G λ` monomial syntax remains open.
