- **Statement:** If the nonzero translation defects of a meromorphic function form the atomic
  differential matroid indexed by an infinite quotient group, then the base function is
  differentially algebraically independent from every finite defect family. Consequently the
  differential-algebraic matroid of all finite differential-translation superpositions is exactly
  the linear matroid of the localized differential group algebra. There are no hidden nonlinear
  differential relations.

## Goal

The atomic-defect theorem controls functions of the form

```text
Delta_a f = f(z+a)-f(z).
```

It does not formally control the undifferenced translates `f(z+a)` because all of them share the
unknown base value `f(z)`. Determine whether that common mode can carry an additional nonlinear
differential relation invisible to every defect.

The theorem shows that it cannot when the quotient of translation parameters is infinite. A new
translation can move a finite configuration to a disjoint cluster. A hypothetical relation would
then make the defect joining the two clusters differential-algebraic, while atomicity says that
joining edge raises differential transcendence degree by one.

## Setup

Let `f` be meromorphic on `C`, let `H` be an additive subgroup of `C`, and put

```text
G=C/H.
```

Assume `G` is infinite. Write `T_a f(z)=f(z+a)` and `Delta_a=T_a-1`.

Assume that `f` has the **`H`-atomic defect property**: for every finite list of shifts, the
differential transcendence degree over `C(z)` of their defects is the number of distinct nonzero
classes in `G`. Defects from the zero class are DA; defects in the same class differ by a DA
function.

Let

```text
A=C[delta][G]
```

be the finite-support differential group algebra, where `delta=d/dz`. For

```text
D=sum_g L_g(delta)[g] in A,
```

choose lifts `a_g` and put

```text
D f = sum_g L_g(delta) f(z+a_g).
```

Changing a lift changes this function by a DA term and therefore does not change any differential
transcendence degree considered below.

For a finite family `D_1,...,D_m`, define

```text
rank_delta(D_1,...,D_m)
  = dim_(C(delta)) span{D_1,...,D_m}
```

inside `C(delta) tensor_(C[delta]) A`. Here `C(delta)` is the fraction field of the constant
coefficient differential-operator ring `C[delta]`; `delta` in this expression is a formal operator,
not a new derivation on coefficients.

## Defect linearization lemma `[PROVED IN THE PARENT]`

Let `I(G)` be the augmentation ideal of `C[G]`. Associate to

```text
v=sum_g c_g[g] in I(G)
```

the defect combination

```text
ell_f(v)=sum_g c_g f(z+a_g).
```

For arbitrary finite `v_1,...,v_n in I(G)`, atomicity gives

```text
dtrdeg C(z)<ell_f(v_1),...,ell_f(v_n)>_delta
  = dim_C span{v_1,...,v_n}.                               (1)
```

Indeed, the basis vectors `[g]-[0]`, `g!=0`, map to distinct nonzero defects up to DA corrections.
Atomicity makes those coordinates differentially algebraically independent, and constant linear
changes preserve differential transcendence degree.

## Bridge-edge theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

For every finite set of distinct nonzero classes `g_1,...,g_n in G`, the family

```text
f, Delta_(g_1)f,...,Delta_(g_n)f                            (2)
```

is differentially algebraically independent over `C(z)`.

### Proof

Put

```text
d_i=Delta_(g_i)f.
```

The `d_i` are differentially algebraically independent by atomicity. Suppose, toward a
contradiction, that `f` is differentially algebraic over

```text
K=C(z)<d_1,...,d_n>_delta.                                  (3)
```

Choose `q in G` such that the finite sets

```text
S={0,g_1,...,g_n},
q+S={q,q+g_1,...,q+g_n}
```

are disjoint. This is possible because only the finite difference set `S-S` is forbidden and `G`
is infinite.

Translate (3) by `q`. Translation commutes with `delta` and preserves `C(z)` as a differential
field, so `T_q f` is differentially algebraic over

```text
K_q=C(z)<T_q d_1,...,T_q d_n>_delta.
```

Let `L` be the differential field generated by `K` and `K_q`. Both `f` and `T_q f` are
differentially algebraic over `L`. Hence their difference

```text
d_q=T_q f-f
```

is differentially algebraic over `L`.

In the augmentation ideal, the two defect clusters correspond to

```text
v_i=[g_i]-[0],
w_i=[q+g_i]-[q].
```

Their supports lie in disjoint clusters. The `2n` vectors `v_i,w_i` are linearly independent, and
every vector in their span has coefficient sum zero separately on each cluster. The joining edge

```text
e_q=[q]-[0]
```

does not have that property, so

```text
e_q notin span{v_i,w_i}.                                   (4)
```

Apply (1). The generators of `L` have differential transcendence degree `2n`, while adjoining
`d_q=ell_f(e_q)` raises it to `2n+1` by (4). Thus `d_q` is differentially transcendental over `L`,
contradicting the conclusion above.

When `n=0`, if `f` were DA then both `f` and `T_qf` would be DA, while their nonzero-class defect
would be differentially transcendental by atomicity. Thus the same conclusion holds. `QED`

## Differential-linear rank lemma `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `x_1,...,x_N` be differentially algebraically independent over a differential field `K`, and
let `P` be an `m by N` matrix over `C[delta]`. Put

```text
y=P(delta)x.
```

Then

```text
dtrdeg_K K<y_1,...,y_m>_delta = rank_(C(delta)) P.          (5)
```

### Proof

The Euclidean domain `C[delta]` admits Smith normal form. Unimodular row operations replace the
`y_i` by an invertible constant-coefficient differential-linear transformation and preserve their
generated differential field. Unimodular column operations replace the `x_j` by another
differentially independent family generating the same differential field.

It remains to treat a diagonal matrix with nonzero entries

```text
p_1(delta),...,p_r(delta).
```

For one differential indeterminate `x`, the derivatives of `p(delta)x` are triangular in the jet
variables of `x`: if `p` has order `s` and leading coefficient `c!=0`, then

```text
delta^k(p(delta)x)=c delta^(k+s)x + lower jets.
```

Therefore the family of all derivatives of `p(delta)x` is algebraically independent. Different
diagonal coordinates use disjoint differential-indeterminate jets, so the `r` nonzero outputs are
differentially algebraically independent. The zero rows contribute nothing. This proves (5). `QED`

## Full orbit-matroid theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

For every finite family `D_1,...,D_m in A`,

```text
dtrdeg_(C(z)) C(z)<D_1 f,...,D_m f>_delta
  = rank_delta(D_1,...,D_m).                               (6)
```

In particular, the functions `D_jf` are differentially algebraically independent if and only if
the operators `D_j` are linearly independent over `C(delta)`.

### Proof

Let `S subset G` be the finite union of the supports of the `D_j`, enlarged to contain `0`. For
every `g in S-{0}`, put

```text
d_g=T_gf-f.
```

The bridge-edge theorem says that

```text
f, (d_g)_(g in S-{0})                                     (7)
```

is differentially algebraically independent.

Write

```text
D_j=sum_(g in S)L_(j,g)(delta)[g],
epsilon(D_j)=sum_(g in S)L_(j,g)(delta).
```

Then, modulo DA lift corrections,

```text
D_j f = epsilon(D_j)f
        +sum_(g in S-{0}) L_(j,g)(delta)d_g.                (8)
```

On the free `C[delta]`-module supported on `S`, the coefficient change

```text
(L_(j,0),(L_(j,g))_(g!=0))
  mapsto (epsilon(D_j),(L_(j,g))_(g!=0))
```

is invertible. Hence the matrix in (8) has `C(delta)`-rank exactly
`rank_delta(D_1,...,D_m)`. Apply (5) to the independent coordinates (7). Adjoining the finitely
many DA lift corrections changes no differential transcendence degree. This proves (6). `QED`

## Consequences for arbitrary atomic-defect functions

### All distinct translates are independent `[PROVED HERE]`

For distinct classes `g_1,...,g_m in G`, the basis elements `[g_j]` are linearly independent over
`C(delta)`. Therefore

```text
T_(g_1)f,...,T_(g_m)f
```

are differentially algebraically independent over `C(z)`.

Thus the common base value does not create a hidden relation among translates. Atomic independence
of differences forces full differential independence of the entire translation orbit.

### Exact matroid of finite superpositions `[PROVED HERE]`

For finite group-algebra divisors `E_1,...,E_m in C[G]`,

```text
dtrdeg(f_(E_1),...,f_(E_m))
  = dim_C span{E_1,...,E_m}.                               (9)
```

Consequently every nonlinear differential-algebraic dependence among finite translate
superpositions is already forced by an ordinary constant linear dependence among their divisors.

### No hidden nonlinear relations `[PROVED HERE]`

For arbitrary differential-translation operators, differential dependence occurs exactly when a
nontrivial constant-coefficient differential-linear relation occurs after clearing denominators in
`C(delta)`. Nonlinear differential polynomials create no additional dependence locus.

## Triptic mixed-constraint class `[PROVED FROM THE PARENTS]`

Let `1,tau,sigma` be `Q`-linearly independent and let

```text
Q_(tau,sigma)={F meromorphic:
  Delta_1F=0 and Delta_tau Delta_sigma F in C}.
```

The triptic normal-form theorem assigns every `F in Q_(tau,sigma)` a unique constraint divisor

```text
D_F in C[delta][C/(Z+Z tau+Z sigma)]
```

such that

```text
F-D_F Psi is DA,
```

where `Psi` is the elliptic-gamma logarithmic derivative. Conditional on the parent atomic-defect
theorem, for arbitrary `F_1,...,F_m in Q_(tau,sigma)`,

```text
dtrdeg_(C(z)) C(z)<F_1,...,F_m>_delta
  = dim_(C(delta)) span{D_(F_1),...,D_(F_m)}.               (10)
```

This computes the complete differential-algebraic matroid of the whole bare mixed-constraint
class, not only the DA/HT status of one function. In particular:

- `F_j` are differentially algebraically independent exactly when their constraint divisors are
  `C(delta)`-linearly independent;
- every differential dependence in the class is generated, modulo DA terms, by a
  constant-coefficient differential-linear dependence;
- functions with the same nonzero divisor are not independent, but any family with independent
  divisors is independent regardless of the nonlinear differential polynomials allowed.

## Elliptic-gamma consequences `[PROVED FROM THE PARENT ATOMIC THEOREM]`

Let

```text
Psi(z)=delta log Gamma(z;tau,sigma),
H=Z+Z tau+Z sigma.
```

For pairwise distinct classes `a_1+H,...,a_m+H`,

```text
Psi(z+a_1),...,Psi(z+a_m)
```

are differentially algebraically independent over `C(z)`.

The same is true for the elliptic-gamma translates themselves:

```text
Gamma(z+a_1;tau,sigma),...,Gamma(z+a_m;tau,sigma).          (11)
```

Indeed, the differential field generated by the functions in (11) contains their logarithmic
derivatives, which are the independent `Psi` translates. A differential field generated by `m`
elements has differential transcendence degree at most `m`, so it has degree exactly `m`.

More generally, let `E_1,...,E_m in Z[G]` and form the finite products

```text
Gamma_(E_j)(z)=product_g Gamma(z+a_g;tau,sigma)^(n_(j,g)).
```

Then

```text
dtrdeg(Gamma_(E_1),...,Gamma_(E_m))
  = dim_C span{E_1,...,E_m}.                               (12)
```

The lower bound follows from the independent logarithmic derivatives `E_jPsi`. For the upper
bound, an integer matrix has the same rank over `Q` and `C`; integer relations among the divisors
give multiplicative monomial relations among the corresponding products.

## Sharp boundaries

1. **Infinite quotient is essential to the proof.** The bridge translation `q` must move a finite
   support to a disjoint cluster. For finite `G`, atomicity of the `|G|-1` defects alone does not
   determine whether the remaining common mode `f` is independent.
2. **Translation invariance of the base is essential.** The argument uses that translating a
   differential-algebraic relation over `C(z)` produces another relation over the same differential
   field.
3. **Atomicity is stronger than pairwise HT.** It is not enough that every nonzero defect be HT;
   the exact differential matroid of all defect combinations is used at the joining edge.
4. **The concrete elliptic-gamma conclusions inherit the unaudited atomic theorem.** Failure of
   that parent demotes every concrete consequence automatically.
5. **No all-depth family theorem is claimed.** At higher mixed-constraint depth the quotient has a
   compatible pair-face module rather than one cyclic atomic generator. Cross-face differential
   independence requires a separate theorem.

The infinite-quotient hypothesis is sharp in abstract difference-differential fields. If an
involution `sigma` fixes the base and sends a differential indeterminate `x` to `-x`, then the only
nonzero defect `sigma(x)-x=-2x` is differentially transcendental, but the base value `x` is already
linear over that defect. The disjoint-cluster move is unavailable for the quotient group of order
two.

## Parent-theory and preliminary literature attack

The Smith-normal-form rank lemma and the linear-matroid consequence are standard differential
algebra once the independent coordinates are known. The nonroutine step is the bridge-edge theorem:
atomic independence is stated only for differences, while the conclusion recovers the missing
common mode and excludes every nonlinear relation among undifferenced translates.

The strongest located parent is Charlotte Hardouin, *Hypertranscendance des systèmes aux
différences diagonaux* (Compositio Math. 144, 2008). Its Theorem 4.2 classifies
hyperalgebraic dependence among solutions of diagonal translation equations by linear dependence
of periodic divisors, and Corollary 4.4 proves hyperalgebraic independence for generic families of
classical gamma translates and dilates. It therefore establishes that the abstract pattern
"independent divisor data implies no nonlinear differential relations among translates" is known
in a major parent class. Hardouin--Singer's parameterized difference Galois theory is the general
framework behind such statements.

Adjacent orbit-independence theorems of Adamczewski--Dreyfus--Hardouin--Wibmer and their elliptic
analogue prove algebraic independence of iterates under a second independent automorphism for
solutions of linear difference equations. They do not state the present atomic-defect implication,
its exact `C(delta)[G]` matroid, or the mixed two-translation elliptic-gamma consequence.

For the concrete special function, Kato's 2021 theorem proves DA only for rational period tuples and
states that even DA versus HT for a non-rational multiple elliptic gamma function is unknown.
Narukawa, Winding, and the 2025--2026 geometric-family papers treat modular identities, cones,
coboundaries, and arithmetic cocycles rather than algebraic differential independence. Exact,
equivalent, and stronger searches located no result asserting differential algebraic independence
of generic elliptic-gamma translates or the exact family formula (10)--(12).

This audit forces a split grade:

- the abstract bridge-edge and Smith-normal-form package is `[BRIDGE]` relative to established
  diagonal difference-Galois theory;
- the concrete elliptic-gamma/triptic family matroid is `[NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]`, and can be promoted
  only together with an independently accepted proof of the atomic parent.
