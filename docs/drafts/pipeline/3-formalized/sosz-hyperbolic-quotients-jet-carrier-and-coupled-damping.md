- **Statement:** On hyperbolic three-space `ℍ³`, an element of `SL(2, ℂ)` preserves the off-diagonal archimedean Frobenius pairing along the vertical degree geodesic iff it is an elliptic rotation fixing the geodesic pointwise (`b = c = 0`, `‖a‖ = 1`); the free-space `ℍ³` Robin constant of `2π G_s` at `s = 1/2` is the degree-independent constant `1/4` (incompatible with the unbounded prime-orbit self-intersection `(p − 2)/(p − 1) · log p`); every discrete subgroup `Γ ≤ SL(2, ℂ)` of finite hyperbolic covolume satisfies a thin–thick dichotomy on any infinite set of positive integer degrees (forcing either infinitely many bounded-injectivity points modulo the finite axis stabilizer `Γ_ℓ` or arbitrarily small quotient distance at arbitrarily large degree ratios), while every non-zero parabolic cusp translation diverges harmonically at `s = 1/2` with meromorphic horospherical zero-mode `−min(t, u)^{1/2} max(t, u)^{3/2} / Λ_area`; the pure trivial-zero jet kernel `jetKernel m n = archKernel m n − min(m, n)` has nonsingular ordered Cauchy blocks and infinite rank on coprime primes, and is carried with the `𝔽₁`-cusp by the dilation-covariant causal monomial Hilbert bundle `f_{n, k}(x) = √(4k + 1) 1_{(0, n]}(x) (x / n)^{2k}` on `L²(0, ∞)`; finally, no counterterm-free positive-semidefinite damping of atomic `weilDeltaKernel` can have `o(n log n)` diagonal growth or decouple as `A_r + twistedEnergy r` (finite diagonal limits force asymptotic norm equality and unit inner-product correlation between the archimedean and torsion channels), whereas on smoothed cubic-spline Frobenius graphs a counterterm-free positive-semidefinite damping limit exists on and off the diagonal iff `RiemannHypothesis` holds, even though neither the smoothed prime channel nor the smoothed archimedean-plus-pole channel admits such a presentation on any non-zero nonnegative bump.

## Proof `[PROVED HERE]`

### 1. `SL(2, ℂ)` metric-measure geometry of `ℍ³`, geodesic stabilizer rigidity, and free-space Robin constant
1. **Upper half-space model, Hermitian matrix embedding, and triangle inequality.**
   Define `H3 := {(z, t) ∈ ℂ × ℝ : 0 < t}` with vertical geodesic points `degPoint t := (0, t)` (`t > 0`), `natPoint n := (0, n)` (`n ≥ 1`), chordal invariant `h3Cosh p q := 1 + (‖p.z − q.z‖² + (p.t − q.t)²) / (2 p.t q.t) ≥ 1`, and geodesic distance `h3Dist p q := arcosh(h3Cosh p q) ≥ 0`. Clearly `h3Dist p q = 0 ↔ h3Cosh p q = 1 ↔ p = q`, `h3Dist p q = h3Dist q p`, and `h3Dist (degPoint t) (degPoint u) = |log u − log t|`.
   Embed `p = (z, t) ∈ H3` into `M₂(ℂ)` by `toHermMat p := !![(‖z‖² + t²) / t, z / t; conj z / t, 1 / t]`. Entry `(1, 1)` determines `t = ((toHermMat p 1 1).re)⁻¹` and entry `(0, 1)` determines `z = (toHermMat p 0 1) * t`, so `toHermMat` is injective, `det(toHermMat p) = 1`, and `Re Tr(toHermMat p * adjugate(toHermMat q)) = 2 * h3Cosh p q`.
   For `g = !![a, b; c, d] ∈ SL(2, ℂ)`, `h3Denom g p := ‖c p.z + d‖² + ‖c‖² p.t² > 0` and
   `h3Act g p := (((a p.z + b) conj(c p.z + d) + a conj(c) p.t²) / h3Denom g p, p.t / h3Denom g p)`
   satisfies `toHermMat (h3Act g p) = g * toHermMat p * gᴴ`. Injectivity of `toHermMat` yields `h3Act 1 p = p` and `h3Act (g₁ * g₂) p = h3Act g₁ (h3Act g₂ p)`, while `adjugate(g M gᴴ) = adjugate(gᴴ) adjugate(M) adjugate(g)` and cyclicity of trace yield `h3Cosh (h3Act g p) (h3Act g q) = h3Cosh p q` and `h3Dist (h3Act g p) (h3Act g q) = h3Dist p q`.
   Translating `q` to `o := degPoint 1` by the upper-triangular element `borelToPoint q ∈ SL(2, ℂ)` reduces `h3Dist p r ≤ h3Dist p q + h3Dist q r` to `q = o`, where the hyperboloid coordinates `C(p) = h3Cosh p o` and `X(p) = (Re p.z / p.t, Im p.z / p.t, (‖p.z‖² + p.t² − 1) / (2 p.t))` satisfy `C(p)² − ∑_{i=1}^3 X_i(p)² = 1` and `h3Cosh p r = C(p) C(r) − ∑_{i=1}^3 X_i(p) X_i(r) ≤ cosh(h3Dist p o + h3Dist o r)` by Cauchy–Schwarz.

2. **`SL(2, ℂ)`-invariance of hyperbolic volume `h3Measure`.**
   Give `H3` the open-embedding topology and Borel `σ`-algebra induced by `h3Vec : H3 → Fin 3 → ℝ`, `p ↦ ![Re p.z, Im p.z, p.t]`, and set `h3Measure := (volume.comap h3Vec).withDensity (fun p => ENNReal.ofReal (p.t⁻¹ ^ 3))`. Every `g = !![a, b; c, d] ∈ SL(2, ℂ)` factors into Bruhat generators (`bruhat_sl2c`): `T(a b) * D(a)` when `c = 0`, and `T(a / c) * D(c⁻¹) * S * T(d / c)` when `c ≠ 0`. Parabolic translations `T(ω)` act by Euclidean translation preserving `volume` and `t`; diagonal matrices `D(a)` act by a linear map of determinant `‖a‖⁶` cancelling `(‖a‖² t)⁻³ = ‖a‖⁻⁶ t⁻³`; and `S = !![0, −1; 1, 0]` acts on `{w : 0 < w 2}` by `w ↦ (−w 0, w 1, w 2) / r²` (`r² = ∑_i w_i²`), whose `3 × 3` Fréchet Jacobian has determinant `r⁻⁶` cancelling `(w 2 / r²)⁻³ = r⁶ (w 2)⁻³` via `lintegral_image_eq_lintegral_abs_det_fderiv_mul`. Thus `h3Measure (g • s) = h3Measure s` for all `g ∈ SL(2, ℂ)` and measurable `s ⊆ H3`.

3. **Vertical geodesic stabilizer rigidity and free-space Robin mismatch.**
   Since `archProfile 0 = 0` and `archProfile` is strictly positive and strictly decreasing on `(0, ∞)`, `archProfile` is injective on `[0, ∞)`. Preserving `archProfile (h3Dist (natPoint m) (h3Act g (natPoint n))) = archProfile |log n − log m|` at `(m, n) ∈ {(1, 2), (4, 2), (1, 3), (9, 3)}` forces `h3Act g (natPoint 2) = natPoint 2` and `h3Act g (natPoint 3) = natPoint 3`. Comparing heights gives `‖d‖² + 4 ‖c‖² = 1` and `‖d‖² + 9 ‖c‖² = 1`, hence `c = 0`, `‖d‖ = 1`, `b = 0`, and `‖a‖ = 1` (`sl2_preserves_archFrobenius_iff`).
   For `d ≠ 0`, rewriting `2π G_s(d) − 1 / (2 d) = ((e^{−(s−1)d} − 1) / d − (sinh d − d) / d²) / (2 (sinh d / d))` and applying the mean-value bound `|sinh d − d| ≤ d² sinh |d|` (`(sinh d − d) / d² → 0`) gives `2π G_s(d) − 1 / (2 d) → (1 − s) / 2` as `d → 0, d ≠ 0` (`1 / 4` at `s = 1 / 2`), whereas `orbitIntersection p p / φ(p) = ((p − 2) / (p − 1)) log p → +∞` across primes `p`.

### 2. Finite-covolume thin–thick dichotomy and parabolic cusp divergence (No-go for B118)
1. **Scale-invariant hyperbolic ball volume.**
   For `ρ > 0` and `δ(ρ) := min(1 / 2, √((cosh ρ − 1) / 4)) > 0`, the Euclidean box `[−δ t, δ t]² × [(1 − δ) t, (1 + δ) t]` sits inside `{p ∈ H3 : h3Dist (degPoint t) p < ρ}` for every `t > 0`, yielding `h3Measure {p : h3Dist (degPoint t) p < ρ} ≥ (64 / 27) δ(ρ)³ > 0` independent of `t` (`h3Measure_ball_ge`).

2. **Thin–thick dichotomy on finite-covolume Kleinian quotients.**
   Let `Γ ≤ SL(2, ℂ)` be discrete of finite covolume (`HasFiniteCovolume Γ`). Its vertical-geodesic pointwise stabilizer `vertStab Γ` embeds into the compact circle `{!![a, 0; 0, a⁻¹] : ‖a‖ = 1}`, hence is finite of order `M ≥ 1` (`finite_vertStab`), while second-countability of `SL(2, ℂ)` makes `Γ` countable. If an infinite set `S ⊆ ℕ₊` simultaneously avoided thin collapse modulo `vertStab Γ` and thick ratio folding at scale `ε > 0`, choosing a lacunary sequence `n_{k+1} / n_k > R` in the thick tail would produce pairwise disjoint measurable projections `E_k := (⋃ γ : Γ, γ • B(natPoint n_k, ε / 2)) ∩ F ⊆ F` with `(64 / 27) δ(ε / 2)³ ≤ M * h3Measure E_k` (decomposing `Γ` into left cosets of `vertStab Γ`), contradicting `h3Measure F < ⊤` (`kleinian_thin_thick_dichotomy`). On primes, thick folding forces any `Γ`-invariant kernel bounded below by `c₁ / h3Dist − C₁` to blow up along prime pairs of arbitrarily large ratio `q / p → ∞` (`newtonian_quotient_unbounded_on_primes`).

3. **Parabolic cusp harmonic divergence and horospherical zero-mode.**
   For `ω ≠ 0` and `t, u > 0`, `cosh d_k = O(k²)` along `(parabolicSL2 ω)^k`, so `hyperbolicGreen (1 / 2) d_k ≥ c / k` diverges harmonically (`parabolic_poincare_not_summable_half`). Integrating horizontally across a cusp of covolume `Λ_area > 0` for `s > 1` yields `Λ_area⁻¹ ∫_{0}^∞ 2π r G_s(horoDist t u r) dr = min(t, u)^s max(t, u)^{2 − s} / (2 (s − 1) Λ_area)` (`integral_horospherical_green_eq`), whose analytic continuation at `s = 1 / 2` is `−min(t, u)^{1/2} max(t, u)^{3/2} / Λ_area` (`cuspZeroMode_at_half`), with diagonal `−n² / Λ_area` growing super-logarithmically (`tendsto_abs_cuspZeroMode_diag_div_log`).

### 3. Pure trivial-zero jet Cauchy determinant and causal Taylor-jet Hilbert carrier (B115)
1. **Pure jet decomposition and nonsingular Cauchy blocks.**
   Off the diagonal, `archKernel m n = min (m : ℝ) n + jetKernel m n` with `jetKernel m n := min(m, n)³ / (max(m, n)² − min(m, n)²)` (`archKernel_eq_min_add_jetKernel`). On any ordered block `m i < n j` with `0 < m i` and `m, n` injective on `Fin r`, `Matrix.of (fun i j => jetKernel (m i) (n j))` factors as `Matrix.diagonal (m³) * HTGeometry.cauchyMatrix (−m²) (n²)`, hence has non-zero determinant (`det_jetBlock_eq`, `det_jetBlock_ne_zero_of_lt`). Evaluating on disjoint blocks of primes `P(0..r−1)` and `P(r..2r−1)` rules out any finite-rank factorization of `jetKernel` or `frobeniusIntersection − min` on coprime primes (`not_finiteRank_jetKernel_on_coprime`, `not_finiteRank_frobeniusSubCusp_on_coprime`).

2. **Causal monomial Hilbert bundle on `L²(0, ∞)`.**
   The dilation-covariant family `causalJetWave a k x := 1_{(0, a]}(x) √(4 k + 1) (x / a)^{2k}` satisfies `∫_{0}^∞ causalJetWave a k x * causalJetWave b k x dx = min(a, b) (min(a, b) / max(a, b))^{2k}` (`integral_causalJetWave_mul`) with uniform diagonal norm `a` across all `k ∈ ℕ` (`integral_causalJetWave_sq`). Summing with weights `r^{2k}` (`0 ≤ r < 1`) realizes `dampedFrobenius r m n` (`k ≥ 0`) and the positive-semidefinite pure-jet kernel `dampedJet r m n` (`k ≥ 1`, `dampedJet_posSemidef`).

### 4. Counterterm-free damping walls, ultraviolet correlation locking, and smoothed RH equivalence (B88)
1. **Atomic super-Arakelov `N log N` diagonal wall.**
   Any family of PSD kernels `K_r` (`0 ≤ r < 1`) converging pointwise on `ℕ₊ × ℕ₊` to `K₁` with `K₁ m n = weilDeltaKernel m n` (`m ≠ n`) makes `K₁` PSD. Testing `K₁` on `Ico N (2 N)` with `x_n = √n` against the dyadic harmonic lower bound on `frobeniusIntersection` forces `∃ n ∈ Ico N (2 N), K₁ n n ≥ (N / 8)(log N − 17)` (`atomic_countertermFree_diag_lower_bound`), refuting every `O(log n)` diagonal (`not_atomic_countertermFree_log_diag`).

2. **Decoupled damping impossibility and ultraviolet correlation locking.**
   Since `twistedEnergy r n n + log|1 − r|` converges while off-diagonal entries have finite `r → 1⁻` limits, `A_r + twistedEnergy r` diverges to `+∞` on every non-zero finitely supported vector whenever `A_r` is PSD (`tendsto_add_twistedEnergy_atTop`). In any real inner-product space `H`, `‖v_r‖ → +∞` and `‖u_r − v_r‖² → D` force `‖u_r‖ / ‖v_r‖ → 1` and `⟪u_r, v_r⟫ / (‖u_r‖ ‖v_r‖) → 1` (`ultraviolet_correlation_locking`).

3. **Smoothed counterterm-free damping equivalence to RH and single-channel impossibility.**
   By finite induction on `S.card`, `min(A_i, A_j) * Q(i, j)` is PSD whenever `Q` is PSD and `A ≥ 0` (`schur_min_posSemidef`), whence `exp(−l |x_i − x_j|) * Q(i, j)` is PSD for `l ≥ 0` (`ornsteinUhlenbeck_schur_posSemidef`). Combining this Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Schur multiplier at `l = 1 − r` with `riemannHypothesis_iff_splineOrbit_hasseWeil`, `orientation_tfae`, and `riemannHypothesis_iff_hasseWeil_integerDegrees` yields `riemannHypothesis_iff_exists_damped_psd_limit`. By contrast, for every non-zero nonnegative `f ∈ realTests`, `smoothedPrimeKernel f` fails PSD on `{1, 2}` with weights `(1, 1)` (`not_psd_limit_smoothedPrime`), while `smoothedArchPoleKernel f` has bounded archimedean integral, constant diagonal, and unbounded pole `(√n + 1/√n) poleB(f) poleA(f)` as `n → ∞`, failing PSD on `{1, n}` with weights `(1, −1)` (`not_psd_limit_smoothedArchPole`).

## No-go closure (mandatory when falsified)
- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:**
  1. *B118 (`Γ \ ℍ³` Robin constant = arithmetic diagonal counterterm):* Off-diagonal preservation of `archProfile` restricts `SL(2, ℂ)` to pointwise vertical-axis rotations (`sl2_preserves_archFrobenius_iff`, infinite covolume, constant free-space Robin shift `1/4` against unbounded `(p − 2)/(p − 1) · log p` in `not_exists_const_robin_prime_selfIntersection`); any discrete finite-covolume `Γ ≤ SL(2, ℂ)` triggers `kleinian_thin_thick_dichotomy` and `newtonian_quotient_unbounded_on_primes`; and any parabolic cusp diverges harmonically at `s = 1/2` (`parabolic_poincare_not_summable_half`) with meromorphic zero-mode `−min(t, u)^{1/2} max(t, u)^{3/2} / Λ_area` (`cuspZeroMode_at_half`, `tendsto_abs_cuspZeroMode_diag_div_log`).
  2. *Decoupled / `O(log n)`-diagonal atomic B88:* Dyadic harmonic jet mass forces `∃ n ∈ [N, 2N), K₁(n, n) ≥ (N / 8)(log N − 17)` (`atomic_countertermFree_diag_lower_bound`, `not_atomic_countertermFree_log_diag`); additive decoupling `A_r + twistedEnergy r` diverges as `−log(1 − r) ∑ x_n² → +∞` (`tendsto_add_twistedEnergy_atTop`); and neither smoothed channel alone admits a PSD damping limit (`not_psd_limit_smoothedPrime`, `not_psd_limit_smoothedArchPole`).
- **Strongest positive survivor:**
  1. `SL(2, ℂ)` isometric metric-measure theory on `ℍ³` (`h3Dist_h3Act`, `h3Dist_triangle`, `h3Measure_h3Act`), geodesic stabilizer rigidity (`sl2_preserves_archFrobenius_iff`), and free-space Robin limit `(1 − s)/2` (`tendsto_freeSpace_robin`).
  2. Pure-jet Cauchy determinant law and infinite coprime-prime rank (`det_jetBlock_ne_zero_of_lt`, `not_finiteRank_jetKernel_on_coprime`, `not_finiteRank_frobeniusSubCusp_on_coprime`), plus the dilation-covariant causal monomial Hilbert carrier `causalJetWave` on `L²(0, ∞)` (`integral_causalJetWave_mul`, `dampedJet_posSemidef`).
  3. Hilbert-space ultraviolet correlation locking (`ultraviolet_correlation_locking`) and smoothed counterterm-free PSD damping equivalence to `RiemannHypothesis` (`riemannHypothesis_iff_exists_damped_psd_limit`).
- **New insight:**
  1. The `ℍ³` realization of `archKernel` is intrinsically an infinite-volume collar around the vertical dilation geodesic rather than a finite-volume quotient `Γ \ ℍ³`; its Hilbert-space carrier is the causal monomial bundle `L²(0, ∞) ⊗ ℓ²(ℕ)`.
  2. Counterterm-free positivity requires coherent difference cancellation `‖u_m^{(r)} − v_m^{(r)}‖²` locking the archimedean and torsion ultraviolet divergences to unit correlation; on atomic degrees the dyadic jet barrier still forces `Ω(N log N)` diagonal self-energy, whereas smoothing removes the ultraviolet barrier and makes counterterm-free PSD damping equivalent to `RiemannHypothesis`.
