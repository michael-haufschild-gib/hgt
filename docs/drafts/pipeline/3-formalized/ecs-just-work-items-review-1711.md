### E-J10 (plan)

### E-J14

For a wall test G (domain, carried by [−½,0]): ∫UG + ∫ν∫ΔUΔG = ∫UG + levyMixed(U,G) − ∫r∫ΔUΔG; the r-part is 2(∫r)∫UG minus two convolutions with r (bounded against bounded U); levyMixed(U,G) − levyMixed(F,χG) = ∫ω(x)∫(χ(u)−χ(u+x))(F(u+x)G(u) − F(u)G(u+x)) with |ω(x)(χ(u)−χ(u+x))| ≤ 14e^{−x/2}; against χG the weak equation is Γ(F,χG) = backward prime wave − pole − a·confinement (no forward translate meets the collar since ½ < log 2), and Σ Λ(n)n^{−½}F(y − log n) is bounded on the collar by |F(u)| ≤ Ce^{2u}.
