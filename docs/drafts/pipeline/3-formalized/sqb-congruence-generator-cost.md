# Original congruence generator comparison and its nonuniform word cost

Status: dependency-closed proof, formalized and axiom-audited. A positive
comparison is constructed at each actual level. Its quadratic word-cost
coefficient provably diverges with the level. The quarter comparison and RH
remain unproved.

## Finite comparison constructed from actual generators

For a finite group G generated by s,t, a cost certificate uses the identity
at cost zero, either generator at cost one, addition of costs under products,
and unchanged cost under inversion. Subgroup closure induction constructs a
certificate for every g. Let c(g) be its least certified cost and n=card G.
The definition uses mathematical least-number choice, not matrix enumeration.

For the complete regular unitary action U, set

```
d_g(v)=||U_g v-v||,
C=1+n⁻¹ sum_g c(g)².
```

Unitary multiplication gives d_gh(v)≤d_g(v)+d_h(v), and inversion preserves
d_g(v). Certificate induction therefore proves
d_g(v)≤c(g)(d_s(v)+d_t(v)). Squaring, using (a+b)²≤2(a²+b²), and applying
the exact Reynolds identity from the previous source construction gives

```
||v-Pv||² = (2n)⁻¹ sum_g d_g(v)²
          ≤ C (d_s(v)²+d_t(v)²),
C>0.
```

Both generator motions vanish exactly when P fixes v. No spectral-gap bound
is supplied as a premise: finiteness and the proved generation construct C.

Mathlib's actual `SpecialLinearGroup.SL2Z_generators` theorem descends through
the literal quotient homomorphism to prove that the classes of S and T
generate `Deck N`. This constructs a number C_N depending only on N.

For an actual Maass cusp eigenform at level Gamma(N+1), let F_q be its complete
original congruence family, m its average, a_q=F_q-m, and D=sum_q |F_q|².
Define the actual generator displacement energy

```
E_ST(z)=sum_q |F_q(Sz)-F_q(z)|²
       +sum_q |F_q(Tz)-F_q(z)|².
```

The original pullback identity identifies these terms with the two regular
unitary displacements. Thus

```
sum_q |a_q(z)|² ≤ C_N E_ST(z),
D(z) ≤ n|m(z)|²+C_N E_ST(z).
```

At a point where m(z)=0, this controls the entire original density. The source
definition supplies the actual form; neither a nonzero average nor a graph-gap
assumption is added.

## Normalized directions and original nodes

Original modular density invariance gives D(Az)=D(z). Using the already proved
reconstruction F_q=sqrt(D)u_q at every point, including common zeros, gives

```
sum_q |F_q(Az)-F_q(z)|²
  = D(z) * directionDistanceSq(z,Az).
```

This multiplied identity retains the original nodal set. At positive density
with m(z)=0, the finite comparison yields the quantitative two-move law

```
1 ≤ C_N [directionDistanceSq(z,Sz)+directionDistanceSq(z,Tz)].
```

No smoothness of the unregularized direction at common zeros is asserted.
This is a source displacement comparison, not yet an integrated curvature
comparison. Existing weighted path estimates retain an inverse-amplitude
resistance; they do not provide a regulator-independent constant through nodes.

## Counting law and actual level growth

Let A={1,s,t,s⁻¹,t⁻¹}. Every element of cost at most k belongs to A^k, using
identity padding. Since card A≤5, at most 5^k elements have cost at most k.
All remaining elements have cost greater than k. Splitting the complete finite
sum into these two sets proves

```
k² (n-5^k) ≤ sum_g c(g)².
```

Consequently, for k>0,

```
C≤k²/2  implies  n≤2*5^k.
```

For the actual quotient, the classes of T^j for 0≤j<N+1 are distinct.
Equality of two classes would put their difference power in Gamma(N+1).
Its upper-right matrix entry modulo N+1 then forces the two indices equal.
This gives a literal injection `Fin (N+1) -> Deck N`, hence card(Deck N)≥N+1.
Combining both proved bounds gives the explicit threshold

```
k>0 and 2*5^k<N+1  imply  C_N>k²/2.
```

Choosing k for any prescribed real bound proves C_N tends to positive infinity.
Furthermore, any other complete certified word-cost family b_N(g) satisfies
c_N(g)≤b_N(g), by least-cost minimality. Therefore

```
C_N ≤ 1+card(Deck N)⁻¹ sum_g b_N(g)².
```

No choice of complete word representatives can make this quadratic average
uniformly bounded over the actual levels. This includes the least-cost choice;
it is not an artifact of arbitrarily long representatives.

## Exact scope and surviving work

The retired shortcut is obtaining a uniform coefficient from this complete
quadratic word-cost recipe. The positive survivor is the constructed finite
comparison, its exact original-source and normalized-chord forms, its fixed
vector classification, and the explicit group-size/cost constraint.

The proof does not determine the optimal S/T graph or source comparison
constant. Divergence of C_N does not show that the optimal spectral gap tends
to zero. A sharper comparison could retain simultaneous generator correlations
or exploit source structure lost in the triangle and squared-word bounds.
Such an estimate must still treat the invariant mean, full pair and phase
currents, and nodal contribution before giving the required core-plus-cusp
quarter inequality. Independent full-Xi occurrence also remains necessary
for SQB's RH handoff.
