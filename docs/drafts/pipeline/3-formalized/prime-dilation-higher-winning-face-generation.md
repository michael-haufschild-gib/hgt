## Setup

Let \(x=(x_p)_p\) be a bounded supernatural prime-valuation vector and
\[
 M(x)=\max_p x_p.
\tag{1}
\]
Prime multiplication is
\[
 D_px=x+e_p.
\tag{2}
\]
For a function \(f\) on the source define its prime covariance defect
\[
 \Delta_pf=f\circ D_p-f.
\tag{3}
\]

For \(m\geq0\), let
\[
 a_m(x)=\mathbf1_{\{M(x)=m\}}.
\tag{4}
\]
For a nonempty finite prime set \(F\), let
\[
 b_{F,m}(x)
 =
 \mathbf1_{\{M(x)=m,\ x_p=m\ \forall p\in F\}}.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(b_{F,m}\) marks the radius-\(m\) face on which every prime in \(F\) is a winner; other
primes may also tie.

The formalized first-face law is
\[
 \Delta_pa_m=b_{\{p\},m-1}-b_{\{p\},m},
\qquad m\geq1.
\tag{6}
\]

## Face-extension lemma `[PAPER-PROVED]`

If \(F\ne\varnothing\) and \(p\notin F\), then
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \Delta_pb_{F,m}=-b_{F\cup\{p\},m}.
 \qquad}
\tag{7}
\]

### Proof

If \(x_p<M(x)\), then \(D_p\) leaves the radius unchanged. It also leaves every \(F\)-coordinate
unchanged. Hence \(b_{F,m}(D_px)=b_{F,m}(x)\). Since \(x_p<M(x)\),
\(b_{F\cup\{p\},m}(x)=0\), and both sides of `(7)` vanish.

If \(x_p=M(x)\), then \(D_p\) raises the radius by one and makes \(p\) the unique winner. Since
\(F\ne\varnothing\) and \(p\notin F\), no member of \(F\) wins after dilation, so
\[
 b_{F,m}(D_px)=0.
\]
Before dilation, \(p\) already wins. Therefore
\[
 b_{F\cup\{p\},m}(x)=b_{F,m}(x),
\]
and `(7)` follows.

## Higher winning-face generation theorem `[PAPER-PROVED]`

Let \(p_1,\ldots,p_k\) be pairwise distinct primes, with \(k\geq1\), and put
\[
 F=\{p_1,\ldots,p_k\}.
\]
Then for every \(m\geq1\),
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \Delta_{p_1}\cdots\Delta_{p_k}a_m
 =
 (-1)^{k-1}
 \left(b_{F,m-1}-b_{F,m}\right).
 \qquad}
\tag{8}
\]

### Proof

Induct on \(k\).

For \(k=1\), `(8)` is `(6)`.

Assume `(8)` for the nonempty set
\[
 F'=\{p_2,\ldots,p_k\}.
\]
Pairwise distinctness gives \(p_1\notin F'\). Apply \(\Delta_{p_1}\) to the inductive identity and
use `(7)` at radii \(m-1\) and \(m\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta_{p_1}\cdots\Delta_{p_k}a_m
 &=
 (-1)^{k-2}
 \left(
   \Delta_{p_1}b_{F',m-1}
   -
   \Delta_{p_1}b_{F',m}
 \right)\\
 &=
 (-1)^{k-2}
 \left(
   -b_{F,m-1}+b_{F,m}
 \right)\\
 &=
 (-1)^{k-1}
 \left(
   b_{F,m-1}-b_{F,m}
 \right).
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves `(8)`.
