# A zero of the heat family continues along a path of times until it escapes

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Question:** review-1211 §2.19 Q5 (the group generated by the monodromy); tool: monodromy
  of analytic covers.

`H_τ(z)` the heat family at complex time, `γ : [0,1] → ℂ` continuous. A *lift* on `[0,b]` is
a continuous `z` with `H_{γ(s)}(z(s)) = 0`.

## Theorems

1. (Chart.) If `(τ₀, z₀)` is a simple zero there are `δ, ρ > 0` and a continuous
   `ζ : B(τ₀, δ) → B(z₀, ρ)`, `ζ(τ₀) = z₀`, such that for `τ ∈ B(τ₀, δ)` the only zero of
   `H_τ` in `B(z₀, ρ)` is `ζ(τ)`.
2. (Staying on a branch.) If `γ([a,c]) ⊆ B(τ₀, δ)`, `w` is a continuous family of zeros over
   `[a,c]` and `w(a) ∈ B(z₀, ρ)`, then `w = ζ ∘ γ` on `[a,c]`.
3. (Uniqueness.) Two lifts on `[0,b]` through simple zeros with the same start agree.
4. (Injectivity of transport.) If `γ` avoids the branch times, two lifts that take the same
   value at one parameter have the same start.
5. (Alternative.) If `γ` avoids the branch times and `H_{γ(0)}(z₀) = 0`, then either a lift
   on `[0,1]` starts at `z₀`, or there are `b ∈ (0,1]` and a continuous family of zeros `z`
   on `[0,b)` with `z(0) = z₀` and `|z(s)| → ∞` as `s ↑ b` (`z₀` *escapes*). The two cases
   exclude each other.

## Proof

1. The analytic implicit function gives `ζ` with `H_τ(ζ(τ)) = 0` near `τ₀`. A disc around
   `z₀` contains exactly one zero of `H_τ` for `τ ≠ τ₀` near `τ₀`, and only `z₀` at `τ₀`
   (collision of order one). `ζ(τ)` lies in the disc for `τ` near `τ₀`.
2. `A = {s : w(s) = ζ(γ(s))}` is closed in `[a,c]` and contains `a` by 1. If `x ∈ A`, `x < c`,
   then `w(x) = ζ(γ(x)) ∈ B(z₀, ρ)`, so `w(s) ∈ B(z₀, ρ)` for `s` right of `x`, and
   `w(s) = ζ(γ(s))` by 1. A closed subset of an interval that contains the left end and is
   open to the right is the interval.
3. `A = {z₁ = z₂}` is closed and contains `0`. At `x ∈ A` take the chart at
   `(γ(x), z₁(x))`; on a right interval both lifts are `ζ ∘ γ` by 2.
4. 3 for the reversed path and the reversed lifts on `[0, s₁]`.
5. Let `S` be the set of `b ∈ [0,1]` with a lift on `[0,b]` from `z₀`; `0 ∈ S`. A lift on
   `[0,b]`, `b < 1`, extends by the chart at its end, so `sup S ∈ S` implies `sup S = 1`.
   Otherwise `b* = sup S ∉ S`, `b* > 0`, and by 3 the lifts on `[0,b]`, `b < b*`, glue to
   one family `Z` on `[0, b*)`. If `|Z|` does not tend to infinity, `Z` stays in a compact
   set along parameters accumulating at `b*`, and has a cluster value `z*`, a zero of
   `H_{γ(b*)}`, simple. In its chart, `Z(s₁) ∈ B(z*, ρ)` for some `s₁` close to `b*`, so
   `Z = ζ ∘ γ` on `[s₁, b*)` by 2, and `Z` extends continuously to `b*`: `b* ∈ S`.
   Exclusion: an escaping family agrees with the lift by 3, and the lift is bounded.
