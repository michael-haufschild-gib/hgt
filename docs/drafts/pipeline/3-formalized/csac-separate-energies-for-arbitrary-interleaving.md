# Power threshold, anchored norm and exhaustion of interleaving supports

For positive nondecreasing conductor weights $r$ with $1/r\in\ell^2$, a cutoff-independent bound on the weighted cells and integrated half-jet is equivalent to $w/r\in\ell^2$. [Sharp integral criterion](csac-separate-conductor-energies-on-arbitrary-interleavings.md)

For two pairs of truncations, bilinearity splits the difference into the lower tail against one upper section and the other lower section against the upper tail. The summable majorant for these differences gives convergence of both quotient variation and jet norm on $\ell^2_r\times\ell^2_r$. The rounded conductors converge in the weighted square norm after division by their common scale. Taking terminal differences before division gives integral endpoint jumps. Lean proves the square-norm limit, absolute incidence exhaustion and uniform jet limit in `normalizedConductor_energy_limit`, `integral_incidence_exhaustion` and `dominated_jet_exhaustion`. [Incidence and jet limits](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/AbsoluteConductorIncidencesInTheCompletion.lean) · [Weighted cell limit](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheCompletedWeilIntersectionOfTwoConductorSections.lean)

For the fixed lower atom the positive cells are $2v_1-v_2,v_2-v_3,\ldots,v_N$, with the first entry $2v_1$ when $N=1$. The exact real operator norm into weighted variation is $\sqrt{(2w(1)/r(1))^2+\sum_{k=2}^N((w(k)+w(k-1))/r(k))^2}$. The triangle bound is attained by alternating signs with magnitudes proportional to the corresponding weight divided by $r(k)^2$. Integer scalings and rounding give the same supremum. Lean proves `anchored_real_extremizer` and `anchored_optimal_constant`. [Exact norm and integral tests](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheSharpWeightTestOnOneConductor.lean)

For the de la Vallée Poussin price, a single integral family detects every $s<3/2$ without rounding. Take $u=\delta_1$ and $v_k=(-1)^k$ on $(K,2K]$, zero elsewhere. Then $|C_k|=2$ in the interior. Its variation is at least of order $K^2\exp(-c\sqrt{\log(2K)})$, while the product of the individual norms with $r(k)=k^s$ is at most of order $K^{s+1/2}$ for $s\ge0$. The ratio diverges for every $s<3/2$. Lean proves `alternating_dyadic_price_obstruction` and the endpoint criterion `critical_joint_power_iff`.
