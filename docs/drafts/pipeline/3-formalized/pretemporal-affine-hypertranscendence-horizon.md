## 1. The external object and its laws

Let `k subset K subset L` be fields of characteristic zero.  Let `T,E` be `k`-derivations of `L`
which preserve `K`.  Assume that for a fixed nonzero weight `w in k`,

```text
[E,T]=w T.                                                     (1)
```

The two-dimensional constant space

```text
g = k T + k E                                                  (2)
```

is not merely an externally supplied list of possible evolutions.  Equation `(1)` makes it the
two-dimensional affine Lie algebra.  Its bracket is the first law obeyed by the law space itself.
The possible one-dimensional laws form `P(g)=P^1(k)`.

Fix `x in L` satisfying the scale Ward identity

```text
E x = 0.                                                       (3)
```

For `[a:b] in P^1(k)`, put

```text
D_(a,b)=a T+b E.                                               (4)
```

Thus `[0:1]` is the pure scale law and every point with `a != 0` contains a translation component.

## 2. Affine jet normal form `[PROVED HERE]`

For all `j>=0`,

```text
E(T^j x)=j w T^j x.                                           (5)
```

Consequently

```text
D_(a,b)(T^j x)=a T^(j+1)x + b j w T^j x.                      (6)
```

For every `n>=0`, the complete normal form is

```text
D_(a,b)^n x =
  sum_(j=0)^n S(n,j) a^j (b w)^(n-j) T^j x,                   (7)
```

where `S(n,j)` is a Stirling number of the second kind.  In particular, the change from

```text
(x,Tx,...,T^n x)  to  (x,Dx,...,D^n x)                        (8)
```

is lower triangular with diagonal

```text
1,a,a^2,...,a^n.                                              (9)
```

### Proof

Equation `(5)` follows by induction.  The induction step is

```text
E T^(j+1)x
 = T(E T^j x)+[E,T]T^j x
 = j w T^(j+1)x+w T^(j+1)x.
```

Equations `(4)` and `(5)` give `(6)`.  Induct on `n` in `(7)`, apply `(6)` termwise, and use

```text
S(n+1,j)=j S(n,j)+S(n,j-1).
```

The coefficient of `T^n x` in the `n`-th row is `a^n`, proving `(9)`.  `QED`

The Stirling normal-ordering identity is classical operator combinatorics.  It is not the novelty
claim of this card.

## 3. Exact horizon theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Assume that the `T`-jet family

```text
x,Tx,T^2x,...                                                  (10)
```

is algebraically independent over `K`; equivalently, `x` is hypertranscendental over `K` for the
derivation `T`.  Then

```text
x is DA over K for D_(a,b)  iff  a=0.                          (11)
```

Equivalently, the complete differential-algebraic direction spectrum is the single point

```text
Chron_g(x) = { [E] } subset P(g).                              (12)
```

Every other projective direction is hypertranscendental.

### Proof

If `a=0`, then `D_(0,b)x=bEx=0`, so `x` satisfies an order-one linear differential equation.

Suppose `a != 0`.  At every order `n`, `(8)` is an invertible lower-triangular constant linear
change of variables by `(9)`.  Hence the first `n+1` `D_(a,b)`-jets are algebraically independent
over `K` because the first `n+1` `T`-jets are.  Every polynomial relation uses only finitely many
jets, so the full `D_(a,b)`-jet family is algebraically independent.  Thus `x` is
hypertranscendental for `D_(a,b)`.  `QED`

This is stronger than saying that hypertranscendence is generic: it computes the discriminant
exactly.

The coefficient field in this statement is genuinely `K`, not merely the derivation-constant
field `k`.  The formal theorem allows `k subset K subset L`, assumes the source jets are
algebraically independent over `K`, and base-changes the triangular matrix from `k` to `K`.  This
distinction is essential for applications over fields such as `C(z)`.

## 4. Why noncommutativity is essential

The law-space theorem does not require candidate derivations to commute.  Constant linear
combinations of arbitrary derivations remain derivations, and their `n`-jets are homogeneous degree
`n` polynomials in the direction coefficients by expansion over operator words.  The Lie bracket
then adds physical and geometric information.

Here `[E,T]=wT` is precisely what produces the triangular Ward law `(7)`.  Requiring an abelian law
space would delete the scale direction and make the elliptic-gamma application artificially
one-dimensional.

## 5. Constraint-centralizer reconstruction `[PROVED HERE]`

The affine law algebra above is forced by the period constraints rather than postulated.

Let the coordinate space be

```text
X=k_z direct_sum k^r_omega
```

and, for `1<=i<=r`, let the `i`-th period shear be

```text
S_i(z,omega)=(z+omega_i,omega).                                (13)
```

An affine-regular vector field has the form `V(q)=Aq+v`.  Require exact constraint compatibility

```text
V(S_i q)=S_i V(q)  for every q and i.                          (14)
```

Then there are unique `lambda,c in k` and `beta in k^r` such that

```text
V=(lambda z+c+sum_i beta_i omega_i) partial_z
    +lambda sum_i omega_i partial_(omega_i).                    (15)
```

Thus the full affine-regular constraint centralizer has dimension `r+2`.  It is spanned by

```text
T=partial_z,
P_i=omega_i partial_z,
E=z partial_z+sum_i omega_i partial_(omega_i),                  (16)
```

with bracket laws

```text
[E,T]=-T,   [E,P_i]=0,   [T,P_i]=[P_i,P_j]=0.                  (17)
```

### Proof

Write `S_i=I+N_i`, where `N_i` sends the `omega_i` coordinate to the `z` coordinate and kills the
other basis vectors.  Equation `(14)` is equivalent to

```text
A N_i=N_i A,   N_i v=0.                                       (18)
```

In block form relative to `k_z direct_sum k^r_omega`, write

```text
A=[[alpha,beta],[gamma,B]].
```

The lower block of `A N_i=N_i A` gives `gamma=0`.  Its upper block says that row `i` of `B` is
`alpha` times row `i` of the identity.  Hence `B=alpha I`, while `beta` is unrestricted.  The
equations `N_i v=0` kill every `omega` component of `v` and leave its `z` component unrestricted.
This is exactly `(15)`.  Direct commutator calculation gives `(17)`.  `QED`

This theorem identifies what the external thing is **after** choosing affine regularity.  It does
not prove that affine vector fields exhaust all physically admissible derivations.  Allowing all
meromorphic or arbitrary field derivations produces a much larger centralizer; a QFT application
must derive the correct regularity class from locality or its observable sheaf.

## 6. Full affine-centralizer horizon `[PROVED HERE]`

Work over the parameter field `K=k(omega_1,...,omega_r)`, with the `omega_i` algebraically
independent over `k`.  Let `x` obey `Ex=0` and be hypertranscendental for `T`.  For constants
`lambda,c,beta_i in k`, put

```text
f(omega)=c+sum_i beta_i omega_i,
D=f(omega)T+lambda E.                                         (19)
```

Then

```text
x is DA for D  iff  f=0  iff  c=beta_1=...=beta_r=0.           (20)
```

Consequently the complete DA spectrum in the projectivized `(r+2)`-dimensional affine constraint
centralizer is still the single dilation point:

```text
Chron_aff(x)={ [E] } subset P^(r+1)(k).                        (21)
```

### Proof

The pure dilation case is killed by `Ex=0`.  Suppose `f != 0`.  Since `T f=0`, induction using
`[E,T]=-T` shows

```text
D^n x = f^n T^n x + a K-linear combination of
        x,Tx,...,T^(n-1)x.                                    (22)
```

Indeed, applying `D` to the leading term produces `f^(n+1)T^(n+1)x`; derivatives of `f^n`, the
commutator contribution from `E T^n`, and the derivative of every lower jet remain in orders at
most `n`.  Thus the finite change from `T`-jets to `D`-jets is lower triangular with diagonal
`1,f,...,f^n`, which is invertible over `K`.  Hypertranscendence transfers exactly as in Section 3.
Algebraic independence of the `omega_i` makes `f=0` equivalent to vanishing of all its constant
coefficients.  `QED`

## 7. Elliptic-gamma instantiation `[OPEN ANALYTIC IDENTIFICATION]`

The standard additive elliptic gamma is written in normalized variables

```text
Gamma_e(zeta;tau_1,tau_2),
```

with an implicit base period `1`.  Scaling only `(zeta,tau_1,tau_2)` is **not** a symmetry.  The
missing external coordinate is the base period `omega_0`.  Define the honest homogeneous carrier

```text
Gamma_hom(z;omega_0,omega_1,omega_2)
  =Gamma_e(z/omega_0;omega_1/omega_0,omega_2/omega_0).          (23)
```

For every nonzero `c`, this has the exact simultaneous-scale symmetry

```text
Gamma_hom(c*z;c*omega_0,c*omega_1,c*omega_2)=Gamma_hom(z;omega). (24)
```

This identity is now unconditional in Lean for the repository's actual all-order multiple elliptic
gamma product.  It is false without `omega_0`; adjoining the base period is a mathematical
correction, not extra packaging.

On the homogeneous variables, take

```text
T = partial_z,
E = z partial_z + sum_(i=0)^2 omega_i partial_(omega_i).         (25)
```

Then

```text
[E,T]=-T.                                                       (26)
```

Differentiating `(24)` on a rigorously specified multivariable meromorphic function field should
give

```text
E Gamma_hom = 0.                                                (27)
```

Moreover, `partial_z^n Gamma_hom` is `omega_0^(-n)` times the normalized `zeta` derivative.  Thus
translation-jet algebraic independence transfers by a nonzero diagonal rescaling once both

the theorem gives

