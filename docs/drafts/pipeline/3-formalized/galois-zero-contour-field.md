# Contour traces recover the entire zero field

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** PROVED
- **Question resolved:** Fixing all local source traces forces pointwise fixation
  of every centered ξ zero.

## Exact statement

Write `w=(ρ−1/2)/i`. Over all admissible rational rectangles, let `K₀`, `K₁`
and `K` be generated over ℚ by zeroth moments, first moments, and all
nonnegative moments of the continued prime–archimedean source. Let `L` be
generated by the entire centered zero carrier. Then

```
K₀ = ℚ,      K₁ = K = L.
```

Every field automorphism of ℂ fixes all first moments exactly when it fixes
every centered zero. This also characterizes fixation of all higher moments.
No continuity, RH or simplicity hypothesis enters. No strict inequality
between ℚ and `L` is asserted.

## Proof

Each centered zero lies strictly inside `|Im w|<1/2`. Local finiteness gives
a positive distance to every other support point. Choose rational rectangle
edges sufficiently close to the point, inside that distance and inside the
open strip. Its boundary avoids the divisor and is source-admissible. The
rectangle contains exactly the multiplicity copies of the chosen point.

The exact source-residue identity gives `s₁=mw`, with integer `m>0`.
Consequently every zero belongs to `K₁`. Conversely every source moment is
a finite sum of powers of enclosed zeros, giving `K₁⊆K⊆L`. Zeroth moments
are integers, so their generated subfield is the prime field.

An automorphism fixing first moments fixes `mw` and the nonzero integer `m`,
and hence fixes `w`. The converse follows by applying the automorphism to
the finite residue sums. Every argument ranges over the entire divisor.

## Obstruction and positive survivor

All local traces cannot serve as a ground field for a nontrivial relative
action on the zero field. The positive survivor is exact global field
reconstruction, with the sharp zeroth-versus-first trace distinction and
an automorphism-stabilizer characterization. Both paper proof and Lean
formalization are complete.

Smaller aggregate contour families remain open. The source here is
analytically continued; the theorem gives no absolutely convergent prime-series
algorithm inside the critical strip.
