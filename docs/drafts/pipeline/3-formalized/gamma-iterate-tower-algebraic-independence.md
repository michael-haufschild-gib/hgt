# Algebraic independence of the complete Gamma-iterate tower

- **ID:** gamma-iterate-tower-algebraic-independence
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM · ALGEBRAIC INDEPENDENCE
- **Status:** `[PROVEN] [LEAN FORMALIZED] [CANDIDATE NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Statement:** The countable family of functions

  \[
    z,\Gamma(z),\Gamma^{\circ2}(z),\ldots
  \]

  is algebraically independent over `ℂ`.  Stronger, at every finite depth `k ≥ 1`, no nonzero
  polynomial relation among the first `k + 1` coordinates holds on any nonempty open subset of
  the depth-`k` regular set.

## Depth-lowering proof mechanism

Assume a nonzero relation `P(z,F₁(z),…,Fₖ(z))=0`, with `Fⱼ=Γ^{∘j}`.  View `P` as a polynomial in
the point variable `z`, with coefficients in `ℂ[Y₁,…,Yₖ]`, and retain its nonzero leading
coefficient `Q` in that variable.  At

\[
  z_n=-n+\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n!}x,
\]

divide the relation by `(n+1)^d`, where `d=deg_z P`.  The exact factorial microscope gives

\[
  \frac{z_n}{n+1}\to-1,
  \qquad F_j(z_n)\to F_{j-1}(-1/x).
\]

All lower point-degree terms vanish, while the top term converges to

\[
  (-1)^d Q\bigl(-1/x,F_1(-1/x),\ldots,F_{k-1}(-1/x)\bigr)=0.
\]

Because `Q ≠ 0`, Möbius inversion gives a nonzero local polynomial relation among
`z,F₁,…,Fₖ₋₁`, contradicting the induction hypothesis.  The base case is the local Hölder theorem
for Gamma.

The key distinction from differential independence is that the rational boundary coordinate
`-1/x` has no **algebraic** polynomial kernel over `ℂ`.  Its derivative jet does have a large
differential ideal, so this proof must not be upcast to differential independence.
