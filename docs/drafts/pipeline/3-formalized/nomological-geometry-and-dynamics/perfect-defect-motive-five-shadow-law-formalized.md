## 1. Perfect defect with holonomy

Let `k` be a field, let `C^bullet` be a bounded complex of finite-dimensional `k`-vector spaces,
and let `T:C^bullet->C^bullet` be a cochain automorphism. Define

\[
 \operatorname{str}(T;C)=\sum_i(-1)^i\operatorname{tr}(T_i),
\tag{1}
\]

and

\[
 \operatorname{sdet}(T;C)=\prod_i\det(T_i)^{(-1)^i}.
\tag{2}
\]

### Theorem 1.1 — five-shadow cancellation

For every integer `n>=1`,

\[
 [C]:=\sum_i(-1)^i[C^i]\in K_0(k),
\tag{2a}
\]

\[
 [C]=\sum_i(-1)^i[H^i(C)]\quad\text{in }K_0(k),
\tag{3}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{str}(T^n;C)
 =\sum_i(-1)^i\operatorname{tr}(T^n|H^i(C)),}
\tag{4}
\]

and

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{sdet}(T^n;C)
 =\prod_i\det(T^n|H^i(C))^{(-1)^i}.}
\tag{5}
\]

Over `C`,

\[
 \boxed{
 |\operatorname{sdet}(T^n;C)|^2
 =\prod_i|\det(T^n|H^i(C))|^{2(-1)^i}.}
\tag{6}
\]

### Proof

Use the `T`-equivariant exact sequences

\[
 0\to Z^i\to C^i\to B^{i+1}\to0,
 \qquad
 0\to B^i\to Z^i\to H^i(C)\to0.
\tag{7}
\]

Classes and traces are additive in short exact sequences; determinants are multiplicative. In the
alternating product/sum, every boundary contribution from `B^(i+1)` cancels the same contribution
in the next degree. This proves `(3)`--`(5)` for `T^n`. Taking modulus square proves `(6)`. QED.

## 2. Five readings and unitary recurrence

The same pair `(C,T)` has five exact shadows:

1. **additive/K-theoretic:** Euler class `(3)` in `K_0`;
2. **cohomological:** the objects `H^i(C)`;
3. **character/determinant:** all supertraces and superdeterminants of `T^n`;
4. **positive scalar modulus:** equation `(6)`;
5. **iterative automorphism:** the full sequence of powers `T^n`.

If every `T_i` is unitary, the cyclic subgroup generated by `T` has compact closure in the finite
unitary group. Hence every vector has arbitrarily late approximate returns. Equation `(6)` is then
identically `1`, while character readings may retain nontrivial phase. In the one-dimensional
complex concentrated in degree zero with `T=e^(i theta)`, the modulus is `1` while trace powers are
`e^(in theta)`. Even/odd supertrace cancellation can erase phase in larger complexes.

The identities are a finite-perfect conservation calibration: these readings separately factor
through cohomology by additivity or multiplicativity. They do not equal one another.

## 3. Why this is not yet the HT field equation

A bounded perfect complex has finite total cohomological dimension. A proposed all-jet HT
realization is expected to leave this perfect category, but that remains a research target;
nonperfectness alone is not HT.
An infinite direct sum of algebraically trivial objects is nonperfect without carrying any
polynomial jet-growth theorem.

The missing realization theorem must identify a named function's failure of every finite
prolongation observer to be conservative with a precise nonperfectness/nondualizability property
of its constraint motive. The five-shadow theorem supplies the finite local model, not that
equivalence.

## 4. Countermodels to scalar unification

1. **Decategorified shadows do not determine the object.** Over
   `R=k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2)`, let `epsilon` act trivially on `k direct-sum k`. The modules `R`
   and `k direct-sum k` have equal length and `G_0` class; with identity automorphisms they have
   equal `k`-linear trace, determinant, and powers but are nonisomorphic. This lies outside
   Theorem 1.1's field-vector-space category and tests a proposed generalization. Extension data
   matters.
2. **Positive shadow loses polarity.** Half-characters `u` and `-u` have the same `z=u^2` and
   `S^2` but opposite odd soldering.
3. **Finite doctrine misses completion boundary.** For
   `Z <-^(times2) Z <-^(times2) ...`, ordinary inverse limit is zero while
   `lim^1(Z <-^(times2) Z <- ...) = Z_2/Z` is nonzero.
4. **Motive does not determine cost.** Actions `A` and `2A` on the same `(C,T)` have identical
   five shadows and different history geometry.
5. **Flat does not mean globally trivial.** Zero local curvature can coexist with nontrivial flat
   holonomy.
