## Exact relations

Let `Phi` be the nonempty finite cyclic observable twirl generated by an isometric `U` with
`U^N=1`, and put `D = X - Phi(X)`.  Then

```text
Phi(X†X) - Phi(X)†Phi(X) = Phi(D†D).                    (1)
```

The orbit-energy formula is

```text
Re <psi, Sum_k U^k (D†D) U^(N-k) psi>
  = Sum_k ||D U^(N-k) psi||^2.                         (2)
```

Every summand in `(2)` is nonnegative and the `k=0` term is `||D psi||^2`.  Hence

```text
Phi(D†D)=0  iff  D=0.                                  (3)
```

Combining `(1)`--`(3)` with the already formalized fixed-point theorem gives

```text
starKadisonDefect_U(X)=0
  iff Phi(X)=X
  iff U X = X U.                                       (4)
```

No self-adjointness or positivity premise on `X`, and no selected stationary state, appears in
`(1)`--`(4)`.  The clock is used as an observability constraint, not as a time evolution.
