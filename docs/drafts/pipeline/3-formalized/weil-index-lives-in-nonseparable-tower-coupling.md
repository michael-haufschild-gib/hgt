### 1. Polar second-difference reduction and polar trichotomy on `ℤ`
For `k : Multiplicative ℤ → ℂ`, define the centered second difference
`Δ²k(m) = 2 k(m) − k(m + 1) − k(m − 1)` (`secondDiff`).
On any contiguous grid `y_a = L + a` (`0 ≤ a ≤ M`), the linear difference map
`diffMap M : (Fin M → ℂ) →ₗ[ℂ] (Fin (M + 1) → ℂ)`, `d ↦ Σ_{i < M} d_i (e_i − e_{i+1})`,
has left inverse `prefixSum M c i = Σ_{a ≤ i} c_a`, maps into `ker(totalMass)`, and hence is a linear isomorphism `Fin M → ℂ ≃ₗ[ℂ] ker(totalMass(Fin (M + 1)))` by rank-nullity. Bilinear expansion gives
`hermQuad (gram k y) (diffMap M d) = hermQuad (gram (Δ²k) y|_{Fin M}) d` (`hermQuad_gram_gridPts_diffMap`).
Embedding arbitrary finite families on `ℤ` into contiguous grids via `pushVec` yields the exact identity in `ℕ∞`:
`polarIndex k = negIndex (Δ²k)` (`polarIndex_eq_negIndex_secondDiff`).

If `k` is Hermitian and `Δ²k(n) → 0` as `n → +∞`, then `Δ²k` is Hermitian and `MixingKillsNegativeSquares.negIndex_eq_zero_or_top_of_tendsto_int` gives `polarIndex k ∈ {0, ⊤}` (`polarIndex_eq_zero_or_top_of_secondDiff_tendsto`).

For a local pole pair `pairKernel (valChar r) ω` (`r > 1`) plus a Hermitian kernel `g` with `Δ²g(n) → 0` as `n → +∞`, `Δ²(pairKernel (valChar r) ω) = pairKernel (valChar r) ((2 − r − r⁻¹) ω)` with `2 − r − r⁻¹ = −(r − 1)²/r ≠ 0`. Hence `polarIndex (pairKernel (valChar r) ω + g) = negIndex (Δ²g + pairKernel (valChar r) ω')` satisfies the **polar trichotomy** (`polarIndex_pairKernel_add_of_secondDiff_tendsto`):
1. If `¬ IsPosDefKernel (Δ²g)`, then `negIndex (Δ²g) = ⊤`; since `pairKernel` vanishes on `ker(charEval (valChar r))`, `hermNegIndex_le_add_one_of_eq_on_ker` forces `polarIndex = ⊤`.
2. If `IsPosDefKernel (Δ²g)` and `ω = 0`, then `polarIndex = 0`.
3. If `IsPosDefKernel (Δ²g)` and `ω ≠ 0`, then `ω' ≠ 0`, `‖valChar r (ofAdd 1)‖ = r > 1`, and `r ≠ r⁻¹`; `negIndex_add_sum_pairKernel_le` and `le_negIndex_of_separated_out` give `polarIndex = 1`.

### 2. Negative resolution for separable `S`-truncated explicit-formula assemblies
For any finite prime set `S` and tower-bounded arithmetic weight `lam`, restricting `lam` to prime powers `{p^k : p ∈ S, k ≥ 1}` produces an `ℓ¹` Dirichlet weight at `s = 1/2`:
`Σ_{n ≥ 2} |truncWeight S lam n| / √n = Σ_{p ∈ S} Σ_{k ≥ 1} |lam(p^k)| / (√p)^k < ∞` (`summable_truncWeight_div_sqrt`).
For `C²` compactly supported `g : ℝ → ℂ` and `ℓ > 0`, dominated convergence gives `towerPrime (truncWeight S lam) g ℓ (ofAdd n) → 0` as `n → +∞` (`tendsto_towerPrime_atTop`), while `tendsto_archPart_transl` and Hermitianness give `towerArch g ℓ (ofAdd n) → 0` (`tendsto_towerArch_atTop`). Every local counterterm `C + D·δ₁ + A·negAbsKernel` satisfies `Δ²(·)(n) = 0` for `n ≥ 2`.

Consequently, for every separable assembly `K_S = sTruncatedAssembly S lam g wPole wPrime wArch ct` with `c > 0`:
- **Zero-blindness of finite local polar indices (`negIndex_archGlue_sTruncatedAssembly`):** Either some `p ∈ S` has `¬ IsPosDefKernel (Δ²g_p^{(S)})` and `κ(archGlue(K_S, c)) = ⊤`, or every `Δ²g_p^{(S)}` is positive definite, `Σ_{p∈S} κ₀(k_p^{(S)}) = r_S := #{p ∈ S : wPole p · poleWeight g ≠ 0}`, and `κ(archGlue(K_S, c)) ∈ {r_S + 1, r_S + 2}`. - **Pole-retaining unconditional divergence (`tendsto_negIndex_archGlue_poleRetaining_atTop`):** For `g = cx cubicSpline`, `paperFT_cubicSpline_ne_zero` at `±I/2` gives `poleWeight (cx cubicSpline) ≠ 0`. ### 3. Obstructions to arbitrary / untruncated separable assemblies
- **Pointwise closure impossibility (`exists_coprimeCrossDiff_ratCorr_ne_zero`, `not_tendsto_towerAssembly_ratCorr`):** Every `towerAssembly S k` satisfies `coprimeCrossDiff K u v := K(uv) − K(u) − K(v) + K(1) = 0` whenever `u, v` have disjoint prime supports. Since `ratCorr` is continuous in `logPos` and satisfies `negIndex ratCorr = ⊤ ∨ posIndex ratCorr = ⊤`, no nontrivial net of `towerAssembly` kernels converges pointwise to `ratCorr`.
- **Axial replication blow-up (`tendsto_negIndex_archGlue_axialAssembly_ratCorr_atTop`):** On `S.Nonempty`, the unique `towerAssembly S k` matching `K` on all prime axes `p^ℤ` (`p ∈ S`) is `axialAssembly S K`. ## No-go closure (mandatory when falsified)
- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:** Centered second-difference identity `polarIndex k = negIndex (Δ²k)` (`polarIndex_eq_negIndex_secondDiff`) combined with `ℓ¹` summability `Σ_{p∈S, k≥1} |λ(p^k)| p^{−k/2} < ∞` (`summable_truncWeight_div_sqrt`), Riemann–Lebesgue decay of `archPart`, and the indefinite Howe–Moore mixing law forces `κ₀(k_p^{(S)}) ∈ {0, 1, ⊤}` (`polarIndex_sTruncatedLocal`, `negIndex_archGlue_sTruncatedAssembly`, prime- and Gamma-blind whenever finite); independent `{2, 3}` vs `{5, 7}` log-density blocks pointwise approximation of `ratCorr` by any separable `towerAssembly` (`not_tendsto_towerAssembly_ratCorr`).
- **Strongest positive survivor:** (1) Unconditional coupled `S`-valuation signature identity `negIndex (archGlue (coupledAssembly S ratCorr) c) = negIndex zetaKernel + 1` for every `S` with `2 ≤ S.card` and `c > 0` (`negIndex_archGlue_coupledAssembly_ratCorr`, plus `orbitCorr Z f` and primitive Dirichlet `L(s, χ)`); (2) exact zero-counterterm window-coupled exhaustion `gram (archGlue (sTruncatedCorr S) c) g = gram (weilArchState c) g` for `windowPrimes L ⊆ S` on `|logPos (slopeAbs (g i))| ≤ L` (`gram_archGlue_sTruncatedCorr_eq_weilArchState`), whose supremum over `L` equals `negIndex zetaKernel + 1` unconditionally (`negIndex_weilArchState_eq_iSup_windowPrimes`).
- **New insight:** Two distinct obstructions were conflated in Route SC: (a) additive separability across prime towers (`Σ_{p∈S} k_p(v_p)` vs `K(∏_{p∈S} p^{v_p})`), which destroys inter-prime cross-differences where off-line zero cancellation lives; and (b) non-commutativity of `sup_g` (unbounded slope height) with `S → ∞` in the explicit formula, governed by the transition from `Σ_{n} Λ_S(n)/√n < ∞` (mixing) to `Σ_{n} Λ(n)/√n = ∞`. Keeping inter-prime coupling on `ℤ^S` (`|S| ≥ 2`) or coupling `S` to the slope-height window `e^{2L+8}` eliminates both obstructions without running counterterms.
