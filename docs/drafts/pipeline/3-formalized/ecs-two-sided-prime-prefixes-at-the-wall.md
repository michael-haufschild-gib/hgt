# Two-sided centered prefixes on the original centers

Fix $0<\varepsilon<1$ and $1/6<\alpha<1$. Outside a set of measure $o(X)$ in $[X,2X]$, simultaneously for every $y\ge u$,
\[
 |\psi(y)-\psi(u)-(y-u)|\le \varepsilon(y-u)+4X^\alpha.
\]
In particular $\alpha=1/5$ supplies an upper prefix on increasing state weights and a lower prefix on decreasing weights. Integer centering adds at most one. For $z=u+\sqrt{u\log u}$ the same bound holds from $z$, after removing a further $o(X)$ centers and allowing the constant for the next octave. Thus any original favorable set of positive relative measure retains positive fractional-test mass. The estimate is uniform in the choice of the prescribed measurable set. [Lean theorem](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TwoSidedPrimePrefixesAtAlmostEveryCenter.lean).

Put $d=X^{\alpha-1}$, $\eta=\varepsilon/8$, $\rho=1+\eta$. Use every $r=d\rho^k\le 1/2$. There are $O_\varepsilon(1+\log X)$ such radii. At each, write $r=X^{\theta-1}$, so $\theta\ge\alpha$. The quantitative [two-sided exceptional-set estimate](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PrimeAlmostAllShortIntervalsAsymptotic.lean) bounds the relative exception by a common constant times $(2+\log X^2)^2 Z(X^2,c(X^2)^{1-\alpha})$, where $c=192/(5\eta)$ and $Z$ is the sum of the nonnegative short-window zero weights. Monotonicity in its second parameter gives uniformity in $k$. The large-height condition holds at $\alpha$ and hence at every $\theta\ge\alpha$. The [zero-density bound](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PrimeAlmostAllShortIntervals.lean) with $u_0=(1-\alpha)/2<5/12$ and three log powers makes the union $o(X)$.

For $d\le (y-u)/u\le 1/4$, consecutive good radii bracket the endpoint with ratio $\rho$. Monotonicity of $\psi$ bounds its relative increment between $(1-\eta)/\rho$ and $(1+\eta)\rho$. For smaller increments the first good radius bounds the total error by $(1+\eta)du\le4X^\alpha$. For $y-u\ge u/4$, the prime number theorem gives $|\psi(y)-\psi(u)-(y-u)|\le (\varepsilon/9)(y+u)\le\varepsilon(y-u)$ uniformly in $y$.

To retain the far edge, apply the same exceptional-set bound in the finitely many octaves meeting $[X,3X]$. The inverse of $u\mapsto u+\sqrt{u\log u}$ on $[X,2X]$ is 1-Lipschitz, since its added term is increasing. Removing both exceptional sets therefore costs $o(X)$ in length and $o(1)$ under $K(\log X-\log u)\,du/u$. At $y=u+h(u)$ and $\alpha=1/5$, the additive error divided by $h(u)$ vanishes uniformly on the octave. The [signed wall law](../3-formalized/ecs-signed-chebyshev-excess-at-the-critical-width.md) therefore makes the absolute first reading smaller than any fixed tolerance off a set of measure $o(X)$. These same centers carry both prefix bounds. [Signed reading and joint exceptional set](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/PrimeSupportAcrossLateCriticalWidthWindows.lean).
