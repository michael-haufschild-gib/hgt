  1. **Critical-line & right-shifted prime-log and $y$-rough $1/\theta$ floors ($\theta \in (0, 1)$):** For every $0 < \theta < 1$, $a \ge 0$, and $\varepsilon > 0$, eventually as $T \to \infty$, every mollifier $\Psi(s) = \sum_{d=1}^y b_d d^{-s}$ with $1 \le y \le T^\theta$, $b_1 = 1$, and $\|b_d\| \le 1$ satisfies both
     $$\int_T^{2T} \left\|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log T} + it\right)\Psi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log T} + it\right)\right\|^2 dt \;\ge\; \left(1 + e^{-2a}\log\!\left(\tfrac{1}{\theta}\right) - \varepsilon\right) T$$
     and, for an explicit absolute constant $c_{\mathrm{rough}} > 0$ (`roughMollifierConst`),
     $$\int_T^{2T} \left\|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log T} + it\right)\Psi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log T} + it\right)\right\|^2 dt \;\ge\; \left(\frac{c_{\mathrm{rough}}\, e^{-2a}}{\theta} - \varepsilon\right) T.$$
  2. **Unconditional vertical-flow credited-Jensen divergence:** Uniformly across all nonzero Dirichlet polynomials $\Psi(s) = \sum_{d=1}^y b_d d^{-s}$ with $1 \le y \le (\log T)^{1/8}$ ($\exists d \in [1, y],\, b_d \ne 0$, arbitrary complex coefficients and right-half zeros) — and hence for every fixed nonzero $\Psi$ — the homogeneous degree-zero credited Jensen budget on $[T, 2T]$ diverges to $+\infty$:
     $$\frac{1}{2}\log\!\left(\frac{1}{T}\int_T^{2T} \left\|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}+it\right)\Psi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}+it\right)\right\|^2 dt\right) - \frac{1}{T}\int_T^{2T} \log\left\|\Psi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}+it\right)\right\| dt \;\xrightarrow{T \to \infty}\; +\infty.$$
  3. **Gaussian Cahen–Mellin single-zero critical-line spike ($\theta > 1$, $\theta \to \infty$), Eventual Quasi-RH, and `RiemannHypothesis`:** Every off-line zero $\rho_0 = \tfrac{1}{2} + \delta_0 + i\gamma_0$ ($\delta_0 > 0$) of $\zeta$ forces a localized spike on $[\gamma_0 - R, \gamma_0 + R]$ of $\int_{\gamma_0 - R}^{\gamma_0 + R} \|\zeta(\tfrac{1}{2}+it)\Psi(\tfrac{1}{2}+it)\|^2\,dt \ge \tfrac{1}{25}\delta_0 e^{-2\alpha\delta_0^2} Y^{2\delta_0}$ for every mollifier with $b_1 = 1$, $\|b_d\| \le B$, and $\sum_{n=2}^{X_1} \|c_n\| n^{-(1/2+\delta_0)} \le \tfrac{1}{4}$ under explicit Gaussian localization bounds. Consequently:
     - Dyadic second-moment boundedness on $[T, 2T]$ at prefix-cancellation length $X_1 \ge T^{\theta_1}$ ($\theta_1 > 1/2$) forces eventual quasi-RH: for every $\varepsilon > 0$, only finitely many nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$ satisfy $|\operatorname{Re}\rho - \tfrac{1}{2}| \ge \tfrac{1}{2\theta_1} + \varepsilon$.
     - Cofinal second-moment boundedness on $[1, T]$ for prefix-exact mollifiers ($X \le T^\theta$, $X_1 \ge T^{\theta/2}$) across arbitrarily large $\theta > 0$ implies `RiemannHypothesis`.

## Proof `[PROVED HERE]`
1. **Gaussian floor at $\sigma = \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log T}$ ($a \ge 0$) with $\theta_1 \in (\theta, 1)$:** Given $0 < \theta < 1$, pick $\theta_1 \in (\theta, 1)$ close to $1$, so $y \le T^\theta \le T^{\theta_1}$, and set $\theta' = \theta_1 + (1 - \theta_1)/4 \in (\theta, 1)$ and $M = \lfloor T^{\theta'}\rfloor$. Applying `gaussFloor_window`, `gaussFloor_error_bounds`, `gaussFloor_scale`, and `integral_ge_of_gaussian_windows` at parameter $\theta_1$ and abscissa $\sigma = \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{a}{\log T} \in [\tfrac{1}{2}, 1)$ reduces the lower bound to $D = \sum_{n=1}^M \|c_n\|^2 n^{-2\sigma}$.
   - *Prime log floor:* Keeping $n = 1$ ($\|c_1\|^2 = 1$) and primes $p \in (y, M]$ ($\|c_p\|^2 = 1$, $p^{-2\sigma} \ge p^{-1} M^{-2a/\log T} \ge e^{-2a}/p$) together with `abs_mertensS_sub_logLog_sub_const_le` gives $\sum_{y < p \le M} p^{-1} \ge \log(\theta'/\theta) - o(1)$, yielding $1 + e^{-2a}\log(1/\theta) - \varepsilon$ as $\theta_1 \to 1^-$.
   - *$y$-rough $1/\theta$ floor:* Factor each $m \in [1, M]$ into its $y$-smooth part $u(m) = \prod_{p \mid m,\, p \le y} p^{v_p(m)} \in U \subseteq [1, M]$ and $y$-rough part $v(m) = \prod_{p \mid m,\, p > y} p^{v_p(m)} \in V \subseteq [1, M]$. Because $u(m) v(m) = m$, $m \mapsto (u(m), v(m))$ is injective and $\sum_{m=1}^M m^{-1} \le (\sum_{u \in U} u^{-1})(\sum_{v \in V} v^{-1})$. Every $u \in U$ has $v_p(u) \le M$, so $\sum_{u \in U} u^{-1} \le \prod_{p \le y} \sum_{e=0}^M p^{-e} \le \prod_{p \le y} \tfrac{p}{p-1} \le \exp(1 + \operatorname{mertensS}(y)) \le C_0 \max(1, \log y)$ by `abs_mertensS_sub_logLog_sub_const_le` and $\sum_{p \ge 2} \tfrac{1}{p(p-1)} \le \sum_{k=2}^y (\tfrac{1}{k-1} - \tfrac{1}{k}) \le 1$. Every $v \in V$ has all prime factors $> y$, so its only divisor in $[1, y]$ is $1$ and $c_v = b_1 = 1$. Hence $\sum_{v \in V} v^{-1} \ge \log(M+1)/\exp(1 + \operatorname{mertensS}(y)) \ge c_{\mathrm{rough}} \theta' / \theta$.

2. **Flow credited-Jensen divergence:** Normalize $\Psi$ by $\lambda = (\max_{1 \le d \le y} \|b_d\|)^{-1}$ (`flowCreditedJensenBudget_smul`). By Möbius inversion on $[1, y]$, $b_d = \sum_{e \mid d} \mu(d/e) c_e$, so there exists $e_* \in [1, y]$ with $\|c_{e_*}\| \ge 1/y$. For every $y$-rough $v$, $\gcd(d, v) = 1$ for all $d \le y$ gives $c_{e_* v} = c_{e_*}$, so $D(M) \ge \|c_{e_*}\|^2 e_*^{-1} \sum_{v \le \lfloor M/e_*\rfloor,\, P^-(v) > y} v^{-1} \ge c'_0 y^{-3} \frac{\log T}{\max(1, \log y)}$ (`M = \lfloor T^{5/8}\rfloor`). Meanwhile, Cauchy–Schwarz bounds $\|\Psi(\tfrac{1}{2}+it)\|^2 \le y \sum_{d=1}^y d^{-1} \le 2 y^2$, so $T^{-1}\int_T^{2T} \log\|\Psi(\tfrac{1}{2}+it)\|\,dt \le \tfrac{1}{2}\log(2 y^2)$. For $y \le (\log T)^{1/8}$, subtracting cancels the polynomial powers of $y$ against $\log T$: $\tfrac{1}{2}\log(c'_0 y^{-3} \log T / \log\log T) - \tfrac{1}{2}\log(2 y^2) \ge \tfrac{1}{4}\log\log T - O(1) \to +\infty$.

3. **Gaussian Cahen–Mellin single-zero spike and RH forcing:** For $\rho_0 = \beta_0 + i\gamma_0$ ($\beta_0 = \tfrac{1}{2} + \delta_0 \in (\tfrac{1}{2}, 1)$ by `riemannZeta_zero_re_lt_one`, $\gamma_0 \ge R + 1$), put $F(w) = \zeta(\rho_0 + w)\Psi(\rho_0 + w)\exp(\alpha w^2 + w\log Y)$. Since $\zeta(\rho_0) = 0$, $F(0) = 0$, so `dslope F 0` is holomorphic across $0$ (`Complex.differentiableOn_dslope`) on $\{w : \operatorname{Im}(\rho_0 + w) > 0\}$, and its boundary integral on $[-\delta_0, 3 - \beta_0] \times [-R, R]$ vanishes. On $\operatorname{Re} w = u_R = 3 - \beta_0$, expanding $\zeta(\rho_0 + w)\Psi(\rho_0 + w) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty c_n n^{-(\rho_0 + w)}$ gives $c_n n^{-\rho_0} \exp(\alpha w^2 + w\log(Y/n))/w$. For $u > 0$, residue/contour shifting across $0$ (`cahenMellinGauss_sub_neg_eq_one` + `cahenMellinGauss_eq_of_pos`) evaluates $J_\infty(\alpha, u, x) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}} \frac{\exp(\alpha(u+i\tau)^2 + x(u+i\tau))}{u+i\tau}\,d\tau$, yielding $\|J_\infty(\alpha, u, x)\| \le \frac{\sqrt{\alpha}}{|x|} e^{-x^2/(4\alpha)}$ for $x < 0$ and $\operatorname{Re} J_\infty(\alpha, u, \log Y) \ge \tfrac{3}{4}$ for $\log Y \ge 2\sqrt{\alpha}$. Bounding the $2 \le n \le X_1$ sum by $\tfrac{1}{4}$ (`norm_cahenMellinGauss_le_two`), the $n > X_1$ tail via $(\log(n/Y))^2 \ge A_0^2$, and the $|\tau| > R$ tail plus horizontal edges via `norm_riemannZeta_le_sqrt_norm_add_two_half` gives $\|\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-R}^R \zeta\Psi(\tfrac{1}{2} + i(\gamma_0+\tau)) \frac{\exp(\alpha(-\delta_0+i\tau)^2) Y^{-\delta_0+i\tau}}{-\delta_0+i\tau}\,d\tau\| \ge \tfrac{1}{10}$. Cauchy–Schwarz with $\int_{-R}^R \frac{d\tau}{\delta_0^2 + \tau^2} = \frac{2}{\delta_0}\arctan(R/\delta_0) \le \frac{\pi}{\delta_0}$ yields the spike. Setting $\alpha = 1$, $R \asymp \sqrt{\log T}$, $T = \tfrac{2}{3}\gamma_0$ gives eventual quasi-RH on $[T, 2T]$; setting $R = \tfrac{9}{2}$ (valid for every strip zero in $\operatorname{Im} s > 0$ since $\gamma_0^2 > 32 > (\tfrac{11}{2})^2$ by `riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_im_sq_le`), $\alpha = \tfrac{1}{10}\theta\log T$, and $Y = T^{\theta/5}$ forces `RiemannHypothesis` via `riemannHypothesis_iff_riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_mem_upper_strip`.
