### Part I: The Coupled Strip Bochner–Weil Form and Its Three Shadows (`StripBochnerShadows.lean`)

1. **Definition (`stripBochnerForm`).** Let $\iota$ be an index type, $w : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ nonnegative weights ($w_i \ge 0$), $\lambda : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ frequencies, and $K : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ a nonnegative scale aperture ($K_i \ge 0$) such that $\sum_i w_i K_i < \infty$. For any $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and channel configuration $x, c : \operatorname{Fin} n \to \mathbb{R}$, the **coupled strip Bochner–Weil form** is
   $$\mathcal{B}_{w, \lambda}(K; x, c) := \sum_{j=1}^n \sum_{k=1}^n c_j c_k \sum_{i \in \iota} w_i K_i \cos\bigl(\lambda_i (x_j - x_k)\bigr).$$
   By the trigonometric Gram identity $\sum_{j,k} c_j c_k \cos(\lambda_i(x_j - x_k)) = \bigl(\sum_j c_j \cos(\lambda_i x_j)\bigr)^2 + \bigl(\sum_j c_j \sin(\lambda_i x_j)\bigr)^2 \ge 0$ (`gram_cos_eq`) and termwise nonnegativity $w_i K_i \ge 0$, $\mathcal{B}_{w,\lambda}(K; x, c) \ge 0$ unconditionally across every finite channel configuration (`stripBochnerForm_nonneg`, `hasSum_stripBochnerForm`).

2. **Boundary Multiplicative Shadow (`isEvenPosDef_of_stripKernel_tendsto`, `stripKernel_recovers_boundaryDivisor`).** Along any family of nonnegative scale apertures $K_\delta$ ($\delta > 0$) whose normalized strip kernels $\delta \cdot \text{stripKernel}(w, \lambda, K_\delta, u)$ converge pointwise to $D(u)$ as $\delta \downarrow 0^+$, multi-channel strip positivity passes in the limit to `MultiplicativePositivityGeometry.IsEvenPosDef D`, recovering `IsBoundaryDivisor.isEvenPosDef` at unit weight $w = 1$.

3. **Scaling-Surface $\kappa$-Polar Hodge Shadow (`polarSurfaceOfPoleBudget`, `defect_polarSurfaceOfPoleBudget`, `hodgeIndex_polarSurfaceOfPoleBudget_iff`, `defect_mono_poleBudget`).** On any real vector space $E$ equipped with a symmetric bilinear local strip pairing $L : E \to_{\mathbb{R}} E \to_{\mathbb{R}} \mathbb{R}$ and linear pole functionals $\ell_H, \ell_V : E \to_{\mathbb{R}} \mathbb{R}$, adjoining two isotropic polar fibre classes $H = (0, 1, 0)$ and $V = (0, 0, 1)$ on $E \times \mathbb{R}^2$ with pole budget $\kappa \in \mathbb{R}$ defines `polarSurfaceOfPoleBudget L hL ℓH ℓV κ`, whose Castelnuovo–Severi defect is identically $\kappa (\ell_H(x)\ell_V(y) + \ell_V(x)\ell_H(y)) - L(x, y)$. Its Hodge index condition holds iff $L(x, x) \le 2\kappa\,\ell_H(x)\ell_V(x)$ for all $x \in E$, forces $L(x, x) = 0$ at $\kappa = 0$ when $L$ is positive semidefinite (`local_diag_eq_zero_of_hodgeIndex_pole_zero`), and increases monotonically in $\kappa$ on polar-aligned cycles (`defect_mono_poleBudget`).

### Part II: Collective Multi-Zero Interior Pole–Repulsion Forcing Laws (`CollectivePoleRepulsion.lean`)

1. **Completed Positive Systems (`CompletedPositiveSystem`, `CompletedPositiveSystem.ofFiniteOrder`).** For $\kappa \in \mathbb{N}$ and $d \ge 1$, $(F, \Lambda)$ forms a `CompletedPositiveSystem κ d F Λ` if $F$ is holomorphic and nonvanishing on $\{\operatorname{Re} s > 1\}$ with even positive-definite vertical log-derivative sections $u \mapsto \operatorname{Re}(-\operatorname{logDeriv} F)(\sigma + iu)$ for every $\sigma > 1$, and $\Lambda(s) = (s(s-1))^\kappa \Gamma_{\mathbb{R}}(s)^d F(s)$ extends to an entire function in `OrderOneEnvelope` satisfying $\Lambda(1-s) = \Lambda(s)$ and $\Lambda(\bar{s}) = \overline{\Lambda(s)}$. Any finite-order completion with $F$ bounded on $\{\operatorname{Re} s \ge 2\}$ upgrades automatically to `OrderOneEnvelope` via Phragmén–Lindelöf on $-1 \le \operatorname{Re} s \le 2$ and Stirling's envelope (`CompletedPositiveSystem.ofFiniteOrder`). Canonical instance: `completedPositiveSystem_riemannZeta` ($\kappa = 1, d = 1, \Lambda = 2\xi$).

2. **Dihedral Orbit Multiplicity Extraction (`sum_orbit_resolvent_le_re_logDeriv`).** Every zero of $\Lambda$ lies in $0 \le \operatorname{Re}\rho \le 1$, so every summand of `hasSum_logDeriv_zeroPairs` has nonnegative real part on $\operatorname{Re} s > 1$. For $r$ upper-half-plane zeros $\rho_1, \dots, \rho_r$ with pairwise distinct positive ordinates $\gamma_\ell = \operatorname{Im}\rho_\ell > 0$ and integer multiplicities $m_\ell \le (\operatorname{analyticOrderAt} \Lambda \rho_\ell).\text{toNat}$, the dihedral orbits $\{\rho_\ell, \bar{\rho}_\ell, 1 - \rho_\ell, 1 - \bar{\rho}_\ell\}$ are pairwise disjoint across $\ell$. Whether $\operatorname{Re}\rho_\ell = 1/2$ (orbit of size $2$, weight $2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 1$) or $\operatorname{Re}\rho_\ell \ne 1/2$ (orbit of size $4$, keeping $\rho_\ell, \bar{\rho}_\ell$ and dropping the nonnegative reflected terms), extracting $m_\ell$ copies in `ZeroCopy` per orbit point yields
   $$\sum_{\ell=1}^r m_\ell \left(\operatorname{Re}\frac{1}{s - \rho_\ell} + \operatorname{Re}\frac{1}{s - \bar{\rho}_\ell}\right) \le \operatorname{Re}(\operatorname{logDeriv} \Lambda s).$$

3. **Master Coupled Strip Pole–Zero Forcing Law (`coupled_strip_pole_zero_forcing`).** Evaluating multiplicative Gram positivity on any finite height configuration $x, c : \operatorname{Fin} n \to \mathbb{R}$ ($c_j \ge 0$) at $s_{j,k} = (1 + \delta) + i(x_j - x_k)$ ($\delta > 0$) yields
   $$\sum_{\ell=1}^r m_\ell \sum_{j,k} c_j c_k \left(\operatorname{Re}\frac{1}{s_{j,k} - \rho_\ell} + \operatorname{Re}\frac{1}{s_{j,k} - \bar{\rho}_\ell}\right) \le \kappa \sum_{j,k} c_j c_k \operatorname{Re}\left(\frac{1}{s_{j,k}} + \frac{1}{s_{j,k} - 1}\right) + d \sum_{j,k} c_j c_k \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{logDeriv}\Gamma_{\mathbb{R}}(s_{j,k}).$$

4. **All-Height Archimedean Bound & Restricted Width Extraction (`re_logDeriv_Gammaℝ_le_all_heights`, `width_ge_of_pole_growth_bound`).** Shifting $\psi(s/2)$ twice into $\operatorname{Re} \ge 5/2$ gives $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{logDeriv}\Gamma_{\mathbb{R}}(\sigma + it) \le \frac{1}{2}\log(3 + |t|) + \frac{1}{4}$ for all $\sigma \in [1, 2]$ and all $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Whenever $0 \le A < B$, $0 < G$, $A \le G$, $0 \le \eta$, and $B / (\delta + \eta) \le A / \delta + G$ for all $\delta \in (0, 1]$, geometric-mean optimization (`exclusion_at_geometric_mean` for $A > 0$, direct small-$\delta$ limit for $A = 0$ or $\eta = 0$) gives $\eta \ge \min\bigl(1, (\sqrt{B} - \sqrt{A})^2 / G\bigr)$.

5. **Sharp Collective Half-Pole Interior Repulsion (`collective_half_pole_interior_forcing`).** Let $M := \sum_{\ell=1}^r m_\ell$ satisfy $2 M > \kappa$. In the additive subgroup generated by $(\gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_r)$, the image Finsets $S_k := \{\sum_{\ell=1}^r v_\ell \gamma_\ell : 0 \le v_\ell \le k\} \subset \mathbb{R}$ have polynomial growth $|S_k| \le (k+1)^r$, so there exists $k \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|S_{k+1}| \le (1 + \frac{1}{4M})|S_k|$. Indexing $S_k$ injectively (`n = |S_k|`) with uniform weights $c_j = 1/\sqrt{n}$ eliminates all off-diagonal pole collisions ($x_j - x_{j'} \ne 0$ for $j \ne j'$) while retaining at least $(1 - \frac{1}{4M})n$ resonant pairs for both $+\gamma_\ell$ and $-\gamma_\ell$. This yields $\frac{2M - 1/2}{\delta + \eta} \le \frac{\kappa}{\delta} + G_k$ for all $\delta \in (0, 1]$ with $2M - 1/2 \ge \kappa + 1/2 > \kappa$, forcing $\max_\ell (1 - \operatorname{Re}\rho_\ell) \ge w > 0$ with zero linear-independence or separation hypotheses on $(\gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_r)$.

6. **Exact Sharpness of $2M > \kappa$ (`CompletedPositiveSystem.twistBoundaryZero`, `exists_completedPositiveSystem_boundary_zeros_at_half_pole`).** For any $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$ and $a > 0$, $\operatorname{Re}\bigl(\frac{2}{a + iu} - \frac{1}{a + i(u-\gamma)} - \frac{1}{a + i(u+\gamma)}\bigr) = \int_0^\infty 2 e^{-at}(1 - \cos(\gamma t))\cos(ut)\,dt$ is even positive definite on $\mathbb{R}$. Multiplying $F$ by $\frac{((s-1)^2+\gamma^2)(s^2+\gamma^2)}{s^2(s-1)^2}$ and $\Lambda$ by $((s-1)^2+\gamma^2)(s^2+\gamma^2)$ increases $\kappa$ by $2$, preserves multiplicative Gram positivity (`a = σ - 1 > 0` and `a = σ > 0`), and places a boundary zero at $1 + i\gamma$. Iterating $r$ times on $\zeta$ gives an explicit `CompletedPositiveSystem (2 * r + 1) 1 F Λ` vanishing at $1 + i\gamma_1, \dots, 1 + i\gamma_r$ on $\operatorname{Re} s = 1$ ($2r = \kappa - 1 \le \kappa$, zero interior depth).

7. **Uniform $(r+1)$-Channel Star Collar Exclusion & $\kappa^2$ Collar Sparsity (`collective_star_collar_forcing`, `card_separated_collar_zeros_le_sq_poleOrder`).** Coupling $0$ (weight $\sqrt{r}$) against $r$ $\Delta$-separated ordinates in $[\Delta, T]$ (unit weights) bounds off-diagonal $s = 1$ pole terms via `sum_inv_sq_le_of_separated` and yields $\frac{M/\sqrt{r}}{\delta + \eta} \le \frac{\kappa}{\delta} + \text{starCollarGrowth}(\kappa, d, r, \Delta, T)$. Whenever $M > \sqrt{r}\,\kappa$, at least one zero lies at depth $\ge \min\bigl(1, (\sqrt{M/\sqrt{r}} - \sqrt{\kappa})^2 / \text{starCollarGrowth}\bigr)$. Setting $r = \kappa^2 + 1$ proves at most $\kappa^2$ $\Delta$-separated zeros in $[\Delta, T]$ can enter the explicit $\asymp_{\kappa, \Delta} \frac{1}{d \log(3 + T)}$ collar (`0` for $\kappa = 0$, at most `1` for $\kappa = 1$).

### Part III: Infinite Multiplicative Gauge Collapse & Simple-Pole Refund Rigidity (`InfiniteGaugePoleRigidity.lean`)

1. **Infinite Multiplicative Gauge Collapse (`exists_monic_poly_of_completed_gauge`, `exists_monic_poly_of_laplace_exp_gauge`, `completed_gauge_zero_and_quartet_budget`).** Let $G$ be bounded on $\{\operatorname{Re} s \ge \sigma_0\}$ with $\lim_{\sigma \to +\infty} G(\sigma) = 1$, and suppose $E(s) = (s(s-1))^\kappa G(s)$ extends to an entire function of finite order satisfying $E(1-s) = \varepsilon E(s)$ ($\|\varepsilon\| = 1$). Applying `PhragmenLindelof.vertical_strip` to $E(z)/(z + N + 1)^{2\kappa}$ on $1 - N \le \operatorname{Re} z \le N$ ($N = \max(\max \sigma_0 2, 0) + 1$) gives $\|E(z)\| \le M_0 (\|z\| + 1)^{2\kappa}$ across $\mathbb{C}$, so $E(s) = P.\text{eval}(s)$ for a polynomial $P$ (`exists_polynomial_of_polyGrowth`). Since $P(\sigma)/\sigma^{2\kappa} = (1 - \sigma^{-1})^\kappa G(\sigma) \to 1$ as $\sigma \to +\infty$, $P$ is monic of exact degree $2\kappa$ (`monic_and_natDegree_of_tendsto_eval_div_pow`), and comparing $E(1 + \sigma)/\sigma^{2\kappa} = \varepsilon E(-\sigma)/\sigma^{2\kappa}$ as $\sigma \to +\infty$ forces $\varepsilon = 1$. Consequently, $\sum_{z \in S} (\operatorname{analyticOrderAt} E z).\text{toNat} \le 2\kappa$ on every finite set $S \subset \mathbb{C}$, and when $E(\bar{s}) = \overline{E(s)}$, $4$-element dihedral orbits force $2 \sum_{i=1}^q m_i \le \kappa$ for any $q$ upper-half-plane off-critical zeros ($\operatorname{Re}\rho_i \ne 1/2$).

2. **Simple-Pole ($\kappa = 1$) Critical-Refund Rigidity (`simple_pole_nonneg_laplace_refund_rigidity`).** Let $\nu$ be any nonnegative Borel measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with $\text{laplaceMass}\,\nu\,\sigma_1 \ne \top$, and let $E$ be any non-identically-zero entire function satisfying $E(1-s) = \varepsilon E(s)$ ($\varepsilon \ne 0$, with no finite-order assumption on $E$ and arbitrary critical-zero deletions from $\xi$ permitted) such that $E(\sigma) \ne 0$ and $-\operatorname{logDeriv} E(\sigma) + \operatorname{logDeriv}\xi(\sigma) = \text{laplaceReal}\,\nu\,\sigma$ for $\sigma > \sigma_1$.
   - If $\exists \sigma_0, \text{laplaceMass}\,\nu\,\sigma_0 = \top$, let $c$ be the finite abscissa (`exists_isAbscissa`). On $(c, \infty)$, $\xi(t) \ne 0$ (`xi_ne_zero_of_ofReal`) and `complexLaplace ν` is holomorphic (`differentiableOn_complexLaplace`), so the real-analytic linear ODE $E'(t) = (\operatorname{logDeriv}\xi(t) - \text{complexLaplace}\,\nu(t)) E(t)$ propagates from $(\max c \sigma_1, \infty)$ to all of $(c, \infty)$ with $E(t) \ne 0$ (`ne_zero_of_entire_linear_ode`). Nonnegativity of $\text{laplaceReal}\,\nu\,t$ on $(c, \infty)$ rules out $E(c) = 0$ (`ne_zero_at_endpoint_of_nonneg_logDeriv_diff`), making $-\operatorname{logDeriv} E + \operatorname{logDeriv}\xi$ analytic at $c$ in contradiction to Landau's real-axis theorem (`false_of_analyticAt_abscissa`).
   - Thus $\text{laplaceMass}\,\nu\,\sigma \ne \top$ for all $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$, so $\nu + \text{volume}$ has abscissa $0$ (`isAbscissa_add_volume_zero`) and `complexLaplace ν` is holomorphic on $\{\operatorname{Re} z > 0\}$. Propagating the ODE to $(0, \infty)$ and adding the identity at $\sigma = 3/4$ and $1 - \sigma = 1/4$ via reflection antisymmetry gives $\text{laplaceReal}\,\nu\,(3/4) + \text{laplaceReal}\,\nu\,(1/4) = 0$. Strict positivity of $e^{-u/4}$ on $(0, \infty)$ forces $\nu|_{(0,\infty)} = 0$, hence $(E/\xi)' = 0$ near $1$, $\varepsilon = 1$, and $E(s) = (E(1)/\xi(1))\,\xi(s)$ across $\mathbb{C}$.
