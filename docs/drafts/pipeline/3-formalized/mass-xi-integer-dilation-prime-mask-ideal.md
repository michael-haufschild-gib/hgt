### Every nonprimitive dilation is bounded

For every `r≥2`, the `r`-th prime-power layer is absolutely summable and uniformly bounded on
every positive vertical time line.

Therefore every horizontal dilation direction `D_r`, `r≥2`, is unconditionally uniformly bounded.


