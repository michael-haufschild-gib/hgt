# Absolute geometry of Spec ℤ: the arithmetic square, carries, and the surface

- **ID:** agz-absolute-geometry-of-spec-z
- **Stage:** 3-formalized (every theorem below is in Lean; open topics listed at the end)
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Source:** `docs/reports/review-0911/fable.md` §9; `docs/reports/review-1011/obstacles-summary.md` §2.14
- **Lean:** `formal/GaloisForLFunctions/AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ/` (umbrella `AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ.lean`)
- **Statement:** In Λ-geometry `Spec ℤ ×_{𝔽₁} Spec ℤ = Spec W(ℤ)` exists, is the union of the
  Frobenius graphs, and carries the prime part of Weil's off-diagonal exactly; neither Weil's nor
  Stepanov's argument runs on it, for reasons that are theorems.

## Notation

`𝒢` = integer sequences `(x_n)_{n≥1}` with `p^{v_p(n)+1} ∣ x_{pn} − x_n` for all primes `p`, `n ≥ 1`.
`(F_k x)_n = x_{kn}`. `(V_k x)_n = k x_{n/k}` if `k ∣ n`, else `0`. `e_d = V_d 1`. `w_n(x) = x_n`.
`J(m,n)` = ideal of `ℤ` generated by `{x_m − x_n : x ∈ 𝒢}`. `[a]_n = aⁿ`.

## Theorems

**A1 (ring).** `𝒢` is a subring of `ℤ^{ℕ₊}`; `F_k` is a ring endomorphism of `𝒢`, `V_k` an additive
endomorphism; `F_kF_l = F_{kl}`, `F_kV_k = k`,
`F_{gn}V_{gm} = g V_mF_n` for coprime `m, n`, `V_k(x)·y = V_k(x·F_k y)`.
*Proof.* Congruences are preserved by sums and by `xy − x'y' = x(y−y') + y'(x−x')`. For `F_k`:
`v_p(kn) ≥ v_p(n)`. For `V_k`: if `k ∣ n` the difference is `k(x_{pn/k} − x_{n/k})` and
`v_p(k) + v_p(n/k) + 1 = v_p(n) + 1`; if `k ∤ n`, `k ∣ pn` then `v_p(k) = v_p(n) + 1` and the term
`k x_{pn/k}` is divisible by `p^{v_p(n)+1}`; if `k ∤ pn` both terms vanish. The identities are
checked on components. ∎

**A2 (necklace decomposition).** `x ∈ 𝒢` iff there are integers `b_d` with
`x_n = Σ_{d∣n} d b_d` for all `n`; the `b_d` are unique. `e_de_{d'} = gcd(d,d') e_{lcm(d,d')}`.
*Proof.* (⇐) `x_{pn} − x_n = Σ_{d ∣ pn, d ∤ n} d b_d`, and such `d` have `v_p(d) = v_p(n)+1`.
(⇒) Define `b_n` by strong induction. With `y = x − Σ_{d∣n, d<n} b_d e_d ∈ 𝒢`, `y_k = 0` for all
proper divisors `k` of `n`; for each prime `p ∣ n`, `p^{v_p(n)} ∣ y_n − y_{n/p} = y_n`; hence
`n ∣ y_n`, and `b_n = y_n/n`. Uniqueness: triangularity. ∎

**A3 (cofreeness).** Let `A` be a commutative ring with ring endomorphisms `ψ_n` (`n ≥ 1`),
`ψ_1 = id`, `ψ_mψ_n = ψ_{mn}`, and `ψ_p(a) − a^p ∈ pA` for all primes `p`. For every ring
homomorphism `f : A → ℤ`, `f̂(a) = (f(ψ_n a))_n` is a ring homomorphism `A → 𝒢` with
`w_1 f̂ = f`, `f̂ψ_k = F_k f̂`, and it is the only one with these two properties.
*Proof.* Fix `p` and write `n = p^r m`, `c = ψ_m a`, `g_r = f ψ_{p^r}`. Claim: `p^{r+1} ∣
g_{r+1}(c) − g_r(c)` for all `c`. `r = 0`: `f(ψ_p c) = f(c)^p + p f(b) ≡ f(c)` (Fermat). Step:
`g_{r+1}(c) = g_r(ψ_p c) = g_r(c)^p + p g_r(b)` and `g_r(c) = g_{r−1}(c)^p + p g_{r−1}(b)`; by
induction `g_r ≡ g_{r−1} (mod p^r)` on all elements, so `g_r(c)^p ≡ g_{r−1}(c)^p (mod p^{r+1})` and
`p g_r(b) ≡ p g_{r−1}(b) (mod p^{r+1})`. Uniqueness: `h(a)_n = (F_n h(a))_1 = h(ψ_n a)_1 =
f(ψ_n a)`. ∎
`𝒢` itself is such a ring: `(x_{pn} − x_n^p)/p` satisfies the congruences (for `q ≠ p` divide by
`p`; for `q = p` use `x_{pn} ≡ x_n (mod p^{v+1}) ⇒ x_{pn}^p ≡ x_n^p (mod p^{v+2})`).

**A4 (graphs meet in the primes).** For `m ≠ n`,
`J(m,n) = (gcd{d : d divides exactly one of m, n})`. Explicitly: if `n = m p^j`, `j ≥ 1`, `p`
prime, then `J(m,n) = (p^{v_p(m)+1})`; if `m ∣ n` and `n/m` is not a prime power, or neither
divides the other, `J(m,n) = (1)`. In particular `#(ℤ/J(1,n)) = e^{Λ(n)}` for `n ≥ 2`.
*Proof.* `e_d` gives `d ∈ J` for `d` dividing exactly one. Conversely `x_m − x_n =
Σ_{d∣m, d∤n} d b_d − Σ_{d∣n, d∤m} d b_d` by A2. The gcd: if `n = m p^j`, the divisors of `n` not
dividing `m` are the `d` with `v_p(d) > v_p(m)`, all multiples of `p^{v_p(m)+1}`, which is one of
them. If two primes `p ≠ q` have `v_p(n) > v_p(m)`, `v_q(n) > v_q(m)` (or one each way), the
divisors `p^{v_p(m)+1}`, `q^{v_q(m)+1}` are coprime. ∎

**A5 (Weil's prime kernel).** For `m ≠ n`, `g = gcd(m,n)`:
`g · log #(ℤ/J(m/g, n/g)) = min(m,n)·Λ(max/min)` if `min ∣ max`, and `0` otherwise. Hence
`SurfaceOfSpecZ.frobeniusIntersection m n = min/(1 − (min/max)²) + g·log #(ℤ/J(m/g, n/g))`.
*Proof.* A4 with coprime arguments, and `frobeniusIntersection_eq`, `orbitIntersection_formula`. ∎

**A6 (constants).** `F_p x = x^p` for all primes `p` iff `x = [x_1]`. `[a] ∈ 𝒢`.
`[a][b] = [ab]`. The `[k]`, `k ≥ 1`, are linearly independent. For `q ∈ ℕ`, `[q]` is effective: its
components `qᵏ` count the periodic points of the full shift on `q` letters, so its necklace
coordinates are the numbers of primitive cyclic words of length `d` (for `q` a prime power these
are the numbers of monic irreducible polynomials of degree `d` over `𝔽_q` [C], not in Lean).

**A7 (Adams ring).** `T` = subring generated by the `[a]`. The ideal generated by
`{x_n − x_1 : x ∈ T}` is `(gcd_a(aⁿ − a)) = (Π_{p−1 ∣ n−1} p)` for `n ≥ 2`.
*Proof.* `p ∣ aⁿ − a` for all `a` iff `a^{n−1} = 1` on `𝔽_p^×` iff `p − 1 ∣ n − 1` (cyclic);
`p² ∤ pⁿ − p`. ∎

**A8 (δ).** `δ_p x = (F_p x − x^p)/p ∈ 𝒢`.
`p(δ_p(x+y) − δ_p x − δ_p y) = x^p + y^p − (x+y)^p`.
`δ_p(xy) = x^pδ_p y + y^pδ_p x + pδ_p xδ_p y`. `δ_p 1 = 0`, `δ_p[a] = 0`, `δ_pF_k = F_kδ_p`.
On `ℤ`, `v_p(δ_p a) = v_p(a) − 1` for `p ∣ a ≠ 0` (`SignFreePurityMechanisms`).

**A9 (fixed scheme).** If `A` has Frobenius lifts and `f : A → ℤ`, the ideal generated by
`f(a) − f(ψ_n a)` lies in `J(1, n)`; in particular `p ∣ f(a) − f(ψ_{p^k}a)`. On `𝒢` it equals
`J(1, n)`. The cost `m·θ(S) ≤ log|f(a)|` holds when the integer `f(a)` has order `m` at
every prime of `S`, by `SignFreePurityMechanisms.mul_sum_log_le_log_abs`.
Evaluated δ-jets give this integer order only with Frobenius compatibility of `f`.

**A10 (the restriction to the Frobenius graph).** `x_p = x_1^p + p(δ_p x)_1`. If `x_1 = 0` then
`(δ_p x)_1 = b_p(x)` and `p^m ∣ x_p` iff `p^{m−1} ∣ b_p(x)` (`m ≥ 1`): first order
at every prime is the one condition `x_1 = 0`; the conditions of higher order at different primes
are divisibility conditions on independent coordinates.

**B1 (carry).** `log binom(a+b, a) = Σ_{k≥1} Λ(k)(⌊(a+b)/k⌋ − ⌊a/k⌋ − ⌊b/k⌋)`;
`v_p binom(a+b,a)` = number of carries (Kummer, Mathlib).

**B2 (window).** For `q≥1`,
`Σ_{k≤N} Λ(k)⌊q{N/k}⌋ + Σ_{N<k≤qN} Λ(k)⌊qN/k⌋ = log((qN)!/(N!)^q)`.
For `q=2`, the first sum divided by `N` tends to `2 log 2−1>0`, while the second tends to `1`.
The exact identity and this limit are audited in `TheCarryIsTheDefectOfTheWindowCount.lean`.

**D1 (jets).** `τ = (n^{−2})_n ∈ ℚ^{ℕ₊}`, `𝒜 = ℚ[τ]`. `F_kτ^j = k^{−2j}τ^j`;
`Σ_{j≥0} n^{−2j} = 1 + 1/(n²−1)` for `n ≥ 2`. On a weight line (`F_n = n^z`), `F_nV_n = n` forces
`V_n = n^{1−z}`.

**D2 (gluing).** For `m < n`: `Σ_{j≥0} m^{1+2j}n^{−2j} = m/(1−(m/n)²)`, the trace of
`V_mF_n` on the weights `−2j`. With A5:
`frobeniusIntersection m n = g·log#(ℤ/J(m/g,n/g)) + Σ_j m^{1+2j}n^{−2j}`. For `k ≥ 2`:
`frobeniusIntersection m (mk) = m·N(k)`.

**D3 (support).** Let `R ⊂ ℚ^{ℕ₊}` be a subring containing `e_k`, and `V : R → R` additive with
`V(x·F_k y) = V(x)·y` whenever `x, y, F_k y ∈ R`. Then `(V y)_n = 0` for `k ∤ n`.
*Proof.* `F_k e_k = k`, so `kV(y) = V(y·F_ke_k) = V(y)e_k` and `V(y)(e_k − k) = 0`; `(e_k)_n = 0`
for `k ∤ n`. ∎
Witt's `V_k` on `𝒢` satisfies the projection formula. Hence no Verschiebung with the projection
formula gives a nonzero number at `(2,3)`, where Weil's is `18/5`; and `kF_k^{-1}` on `𝒜` has
`V(1) = k ≠ e_k`.

**E1 (lattice).** The Teichmüller points `[1],…,[K]` are linearly independent over `ℤ`; the lattice
`L(A,K) = {Σ_{k≤K} c_k[k] : |c_k| ≤ A}` has `(2A+1)^K` elements, and `|x_n| ≤ K·A·Kⁿ` on it.

**E2 (forced zeros).** For a commutative ring `R`, ring homomorphisms `r_1, r_n : R → ℤ`,
`I = ker r_1`: `r_n(I^ℓ) = r_n(I)^ℓ`. On `𝒢`: `r_n(I^ℓ) = J(1,n)^ℓ`, and `e_p^ℓ ∈ I^ℓ` has
`n`-th component `p^ℓ` for `n = p^j`.

**C1 (off-diagonal identity on the carrier).** For coprime `a < b`:
`frobeniusIntersection(a², b²) = (ab)²·b⁻²/(1−(a/b)⁴) + log#(ℤ/J(a², b²))`, where
`b⁻²/(1−(a/b)⁴)` is the coupled critical kernel of the adelic carrier
(`HyperbolicArakelovTheory.CoupledOrbit.term 2`) and the contact is `Λ(b)` if `a = 1`, else `0`.
*Proof.* D2 with `g = 1` on the coprime pair `(a², b²)`. ∎

**C2 (finite parts).** With `weilRow n = Σ_{a<n, (a,n)=1} frobeniusIntersection(a², n²)/(an)²`:
`geometricRow n = weilRow n − Λ(n)/n²`, and
`Σ_{n<N}(weilRow − collisionRow − densityRow) → orbitFinitePart + Σ Λ(n)/n²`.

**C3 (density constant).** `4c₀ = −2γ₀ − (log 4π + γ)`, `c₀ = regularMean`,
`γ₀ = gammaHardyPhaseRate 0` (`regularMean_eq`, `digamma_quarter`).

**C4 (rows).** `jetKernel(m,n) = ½(1/|m−n| + 1/(m+n))` equals `Γ_m·_∞Γ_n/(mn)` for `m ≠ n`. For
`1 ≤ n ≤ M`: `Σ_{m≤M, m≠n} jetKernel(m,n) = ½(H_{M−n} + H_{M+n}) − 3/(4n)`; minus `log M` it tends
to `γ − 3/(4n)`. `Σ_{2≤m≤M} N(m)/m = Σ_{m≤M}Λ(m)/m + H_M − 3/4 − 1/(2M(M+1))`, hence
`Σ_{2≤m≤M} N(m)/m − 2 log M → −3/4`.
*Proof.* `Σ_{m<n} 1/(n−m) = H_{n−1}`, `Σ_{n<m≤M} 1/(m−n) = H_{M−n}`,
`Σ_{m≤M} 1/(m+n) = H_{M+n} − H_n`; the term `m = n` of the last sum is `1/(2n)`. The row of `Γ_1`
by induction on `M` from `N(m) = Λ(m) + 1 + 1/(m²−1)`; the limit by Mertens
(`Σ_{m≤M}Λ(m)/m − log M → −γ`) and `H_M − log M → γ`. ∎

**C5 (the jets are the Lévy measure).** With `ω(x) = e^{−x/2}/(1−e^{−2x})` and
`K(m,n) = jetKernel(m,n)/√(mn)`: `K(m,n) = ω(|log(m/n)|)/(mn)` for `m ≠ n`. For a finite set `S`
of degree points and real `C`, with `w(m,n) = n·K(m,n)`:
`Σ_{m≠n∈S} C_mC_nK(m,n) = Σ_n (C_n²/n) Σ_{m∈S, m≠n} w(m,n) − ½Σ_{m≠n∈S}(C_m−C_n)²K(m,n)`.
*Proof.* For `m > n`, `x = log(m/n)`: `e^{−x/2} = √(n/m)`, `e^{−2x} = (n/m)²`, so
`ω(x)/m = √(n/m)·m/(m²−n²)`. The identity: `C_mC_n = ½(C_m²+C_n²) − ½(C_m−C_n)²` and symmetry of
`K`. ∎

**C6 (the diagonal on the degree points).** `U(n) = Σ_{m≥1, m≠n} w(m,n)` converges and
`U(n) = H_{2n} − 3/(4n) + L_n + T_n`, `L_n = Σ_{m<n} φ(m/n)/n`, `T_n = Σ_{m>n} ψ(m/n)/n`,
`φ(t) = 1/(√t(1+√t)(1+t))`, `ψ(t) = 1/((1+√t)(1+t))`. `L_n, T_n → ½ log 2 + π/4`. Hence
`U(n) − log(n/2π) → γ + π/2 + log 8π = −A(0)`, `A(0) = ψ(¼) − log π` the constant of Weil's
archimedean form (`EF.gammaBracket 0`, `gammaBracket_zero_eq`).
*Proof.* With `a = √m`, `b = √n`: for `m < n`, `w = jetKernel + b²/(a(a+b)(a²+b²))` and the second
term is `φ(m/n)/n`; for `m > n`, `w = n/(m²−n²) + b/((a+b)(a²+b²))`, the second term `ψ(m/n)/n`,
and `Σ_{m>n} n/(m²−n²) = ½H_{2n}` (telescoping). `Σ_{m<n} jetKernel = ½H_{2n} − 3/(4n)` (C4 with
`M = n`). `φ`, `ψ` decrease and have the primitives
`Φ = log(1+√t) − ½log(1+t) + arctan√t`, `Ψ = arctan√t − (log(1+√t) − ½log(1+t))`; by the mean
value theorem a Riemann sum of a decreasing function lies between two increments of its primitive:
`Φ(1) − Φ(1/n) ≤ L_n ≤ Φ(1)`, `π/2 − Ψ(1+1/n) ≤ T_n ≤ π/2 − Ψ(1)`; `Φ(1) = ½log2 + π/4`,
`Ψ(1) = π/4 − ½log2`, `Ψ(∞) = π/2`. `H_{2n} − log 2n → γ`. ∎

**C7 (the counterterm belongs to the comb).** `w(gm, gn) = w(m,n)/g`. There is no `δ : ℕ → ℝ` with
`U(k) − δ(k) → −A(0)` and `U(k) − δ(gk) → −A(0)` (`g ≥ 2`).
*Proof.* The first limit along `gk` and C6 give `δ(gk) − log(gk/2π) → 0`; the second and C6 give
`δ(gk) − log(k/2π) → 0`; the difference is `log g ≠ 0`. ∎
On the comb `gℕ` the point `gk` has mass `g` times its mass in `ℕ`, so its total rate is `U(k)`.

**C8 (the gluing index is the divergent part of the energy of the jets).**
`E(M) = Σ_{m≠n≤M} jetKernel(m,n) = (M+½)H_{2M} − (5/4)H_M − M`.
`(E(M) − log M!)/M → γ + log 2`; `(E(M) − Σ_{n≤M} log(n/2π))/M → log 4π + γ`.
*Proof.* Sum C4 over `n`: `Σ_{j<M}H_j = MH_M − M`,
`Σ_{n≤M}H_{M+n} = (2M+1)H_{2M} − (M+1)H_M − M`. Then E5 and `H_N − log N → γ`. ∎

**C9 (the density of the carrier is Weil's constant).** On the comb of the square degree points
(`n²` with mass `2/n`) the rate of `n² → m²` is `w₂(m,n) = 2n·jetKernel(m²,n²) =
(2/m)·ω(2|log(m/n)|)`. `geometricRow n = Σ_{a≤n, (a,n)=1} jetKernel(a²,n²)` (`n ≥ 2`).
`U₂(n) = Σ_{m≠n} w₂(m,n) = H_{2n} − 3/(4n) + Σ_{m≠n} n/(m²+n²)` and `U₂(n) − log(n/4π) → −A(0)`.
`Σ_{m<n} w₂(m,n) = ½H_{n−1} + (2/n)(smoothRow n − 3/8)`, within `9/(4n)` of `½H_{n−1} + 2c₀`.
`H_{n−1} + 4c₀ − (log(n/4π) − A(0)) → 0`; hence `U₂(n) − (H_{n−1} + 4c₀) → 0`.
*Proof.* For `m < n`: `w₂ = jetKernel(m,n) + n/(n²+m²)`; for `m > n`:
`w₂ = ½(1/(m−n) − 1/(m+n)) + n/(m²+n²)`. The sums of `n/(m²+n²)` over `m < n` and over `m > n` are
Riemann sums of `1/(1+t²)` with primitive `arctan`; both tend to `π/4` (as in C6). Half a row:
`2n·jetKernel(m²,n²) = (2/n)/(1−(m/n)⁴)` and `1/(1−r⁴) = 1/(4(1−r)) + regularProfile r`
(`profile_split`); `|smoothRow n − n c₀| ≤ 3/4` (`abs_rowError_le`). Constants:
`γ + 4c₀ + log 4π + A(0) = 0` by `regularMean_eq` and `gammaBracket_zero_eq`. ∎
So `−A(0) = 4c₀ + γ + log 4π`: density row, harmonic collision row, mesh of the comb of squares.

**C10 (flat packets).** `X(A,B) = Σ_{n≤A}Σ_{m≤B, m≠n} jetKernel(m,n)` is symmetric and, for
`A ≤ B`, `X(A,B) = ½(S(B) − S(B−A) + S(B+A+1) − S(B+1)) − (3/4)H_A`, `S(M) = M·H_M − M`. For
`1 ≤ a ≤ b`: `(X(aN,bN) − Σ_{n≤aN} log(n/2π))/N → W(a,b)`,
`W(a,b) = ½((b+a)log(b+a) − (b−a)log(b−a)) + a(γ + log 2π − log a)`; `W(a,a) = a(γ + log 4π)`. The
energy inside `(A,B]` is `X(B,B) − 2X(A,B) + X(A,A)`, with finite part
`W(b,b) − 2W(a,b) + W(a,a)` per degree point of the scale. `Σ_{m∣n, m<n} Λ(n/m) = log n`.
*Proof.* Sum C4 over `n ≤ A`: `Σ_{n≤A} H_{B−n} = S(B) − S(B−A)`,
`Σ_{n≤A} H_{B+n} = S(B+A+1) − S(B+1)`. `S(xN)/N − x log N → x(log x + γ − 1)`,
`H_{xN}/N → 0`, `log((aN)!)/N − a log N → a log a − a`; the terms in `log N` cancel. ∎
[C]: Weil's archimedean term of `1_{(0,α]}`, `1_{(0,β]}` is
`α(log 4π + γ) + α log((α+β)/2α) + ½(β−α) log((β+α)/(β−α)) = W(α,β)`.

**C11 (the invariant comb).** For `h > 0` the comb `e^{hℤ}` with mass `h` at each point has the
total jump rate `U(h) = Σ_{k≥1} 2h·ω(kh)` at every point, and `U(h) + log(2πh) → −A(0)` as
`h → 0⁺`.
*Proof.* `2ω(y) = 1/(e^{y/2} − e^{−y/2}) + 1/(e^{y/2} + e^{−y/2})`. The second part is a Riemann sum
of a decreasing function with primitive `2 arctan e^{y/2}`: its sum over `k` lies in
`[π − 2 arctan e^{h/2}, π/2]`. With `σ = e^{−h/2}` the first part is
`h Σ_k σ^k/(1−σ^{2k}) = h Σ_{i≥0} σ^{2i+1}/(1−σ^{2i+1})` (both are `Σ σ^{k(2i+1)}`), i.e.
`Σ_i h/(e^{y_i} − 1)`, `y_i = (i+½)h`, and `h/(e^{y_i}−1) = 1/(i+½) − hρ(y_i)`,
`ρ(y) = 1/y − 1/(e^y−1)`. `Σ_{i<I} 1/(i+½) = 2H_{2I} − H_I`. `ρ' ≤ 0` since
`y ≤ 2 sinh(y/2)`; `ρ ≤ 1`; `R = log y − log(1 − e^{−y})` is a primitive with `0 ≤ R(y) ≤ y`,
`log y ≤ R(y) ≤ log y + 1/y`. Hence `R(y_I) − R(y_0) ≤ Σ_{i<I} hρ(y_i)` and
`Σ_{i≤J} hρ(y_i) ≤ R(y_J) − R(y_0) + h`, and with `2H_{2(J+1)} − H_{J+1} − log(J+1) → γ + 2 log 2`
the first part lies in `[γ + 2log 2 − log h + R(h/2) − h, γ + 2 log 2 − log h + R(h/2)]`. ∎

**C12 (the twisted density of the carrier).** For `0 < r < 1`,
`cos(τ log r)/(1−r⁴) = 1/(4(1−r)) + P_τ(r)`, `P_τ(r) = regularProfile(r) − (1 − cos(τ log r))/(1−r⁴)`,
bounded on `(0,1]`. The half-row of the orbit twisted by `r^{iτ}` at height `n` is
`H_{n−1}/(4n)` plus the twisted smooth row over `n²`; the twisted smooth row over `n` tends to

the sections with the weights `0,…,E` (or `−J,…,E−J`) and `b` digits in `[−A,A]` number
`(((2A+1)^b)^{τ(M)})^{E+1}`.
*Proof.* `d ∣ kn ⇔ d/g ∣ n`. Independence: on `n = d(1+Ms)` the section is `d·Σ_e n^e P_{e,d}(qⁿ)`
after induction on `d`. If `Σ_e n^e P_e(qⁿ) = 0` for arbitrarily large `n` and `D` is the largest

beyond its roots, and the rest is at most `B n^E q^{(D−1)n}`; so `qⁿ ≤ B n^E`, false for large
`n`. Multiplication by `χ_{−1}^J` is injective. ∎

**E12 (the gluing count at every height).** For `T = 2π(n + ½ + θ)`, `0 ≤ θ < 1`,
`G(T) = log n! + θ log(n+1)`: `G(T) − N(T) + S(T) → ½ log 2π − 7/8`, and

*Proof.* E6 at `n`; `x log x − x` is convex, so its increment over `θ` lies between
`θ log(n+½)` and `θ log(n+3/2)`; `S` is unbounded (corpus). ∎

**F1 (the square of a Dedekind domain).** `R` Dedekind; families `(x_𝔞)` over the nonzero ideals
with `x_{𝔭𝔞} − x_𝔞 ∈ 𝔭^{k+1}` for `𝔭` prime, `𝔭^k ∣ 𝔞` form a subring, stable under

divisible by two primes; over a number ring `#(R/𝔭) = N𝔭`. (ii) `(a, b) = (x_1, x_𝔭)` for some
`x` iff `b − a ∈ 𝔭`. (iii) The values `x_𝔡` of the `x` vanishing at the proper divisors of `𝔡`
are exactly the elements of `𝔡`. (iv) Such an `x` with `(x_𝔡) = 𝔡` exists iff `𝔡` is principal.
*Proof.* In a cancellative monoid, `d ∣ pa`, `d ∤ a`, `p^k ∣ a`, `p` prime imply `p^{k+1} ∣ d`
