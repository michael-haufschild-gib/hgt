## Formal theorem

For finitely supported prime coefficients `a,b`, let

`O_a(z) = Σ_p (a_p/F_p) B_p(z) = Σ_p a_p (p^{-z} - p^z)`,

where `B_p` is actual holomorphic SC prime Bockstein and `F_p > 0` is actual Fisher factor,

and form the additive `2 × 2` pencil

`M_{a,b}(z) = [[1, O_a(z)], [-O_b(z), 1]]`.

Then `det M_{a,b}(z) = 1 + O_a(z) O_b(z)`. For every entire even `f` with
`f(0)=1`, there are sequences of finitely supported prime coefficients `a_n,b_n` for which
`det M_{a_n,b_n} → f` locally uniformly on `ℂ`. In particular this holds unconditionally for
`f(z)=XiC(z)/XiC(0)`.

## Conjecture loop — discharged

### C1 — odd Bockstein synthesis is compact-open dense — proved

The corpus proves cofinite prime exponentials `p^{-z}` have dense finite complex span in the compact-open holomorphic space. Apply the continuous odd projection `h(z) ↦ h(z)-h(-z)`. Nonzero Fisher factors can be absorbed into finite coefficients, so finite prime Bockstein sums should be dense in all odd entire functions.

### C2 — normalized even entire functions have a two-odd-factor presentation — strengthened

Corpus removable divided slope gives a shorter factorization without square-descent infrastructure:

`f(z)=1+u(z)v(z)`, `u(z)=z`, `v(z)=dslope(f,0)(z)`,

with both `u,v` odd entire.

### C3 — determinant universality — proved

Approximate `u` and `v` independently by finite prime Bockstein sums. Compact-open multiplication gives `1+O_{a_m}O_{b_m} → f`, and the exact `2 × 2` determinant identity realizes every approximant as an additive cross-prime pencil.
