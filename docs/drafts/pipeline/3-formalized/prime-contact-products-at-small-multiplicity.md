# Prime-contact products at small multiplicity

For every integer spline divisor of total variation $M$, the product $U$ of its positive ordinary-prime contact valuations satisfies $\log U\le C M^4$, with one constant for all conductor labels. The unrounded row at $q$ has size at most $AM^2/\sqrt q$. Its positive floor vanishes above $A^2M^4$. Summing the remaining rows uses $\sum_{q\le X}\Lambda(q)/\sqrt q\le 34\sqrt X$.

For $D_n=e_1+2e_{n^2}-e_{(2n+1)^2}$ there are $c>0$, an integer $B>0$, and a fixed finite set of primes such that, whenever $m\le c\sqrt n$, all positive valuations of $mD_n$ lie in this set and $d_{\rm ord}(mD_n)\le U(mD_n)\le B^{m^2}$. Indeed a nonzero row requires two occupied conductors with ratio comparable to the lag $q$. The three conductors have only bounded or quadratic ratios. In the latter range the unrounded row costs at most $C m^2/n<1$.
