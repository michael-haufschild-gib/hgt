# Exporting precision in midpoint section maps: carry dichotomy, fibre collisions, and mixed-family prototype

For a finite map $f:X\to Y$ write $F(f)=\max_{y\in Y}|f^{-1}(y)|$ (`maxFibre`). For integer spline divisors $b,a$ and $S(v)=\mathrm{divisorSections}(v)$, any map $f_m:S(mkb)^2\to S(m(kb+a))\times S(m(kb-a))$ satisfies $|S(mkb)|^2\le F(f_m)|S(m(kb+a))||S(m(kb-a))|$, hence $-\log F(f_m)/m^2\le\mathrm{midpointExcess}(kb,a,m)$ (`neg_log_maxFibre_le_midpointExcess`).

## 1. Subquadratic-fibre midpoint receiver and translation rigidity

- **Subquadratic-fibre RH equivalence.** Let $b$ satisfy $Q(b)>0$. Cofinal maps $f_m:S(mkb)^2\to S(m(kb+a))\times S(m(kb-a))$ with $\log F(f_m)\le\varepsilon m^2$ for every $\varepsilon>0$ along cofinal translations $k$ imply $Q(a)\ge0$ (`integerProbe_nonneg_of_subquadraticMidpointFibres`). Over any positive background $b$ — in particular over one unconditional two-atom prime background $b=e_p\pm e_1$ — the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to obtaining such subquadratic-fibre midpoint maps for every displacement $a$ and all sufficiently high translations $k$ (`riemannHypothesis_iff_subquadraticMidpointTest`, `exists_prime_background_subquadraticMidpointTest`).
- **Translation-uniformity obstruction.** Intersecting translations $k\ge2$ at multiplicity $2m$ and $2k$ at multiplicity $m$ at their shared midpoint $S(2mkb)^2$ places the image of any single coordinate map $\Psi:\mathbb{Z}^2\to\mathbb{Z}^2$ on a positive ray $a=b$ inside $S(m(2k+1)b)\times S(2m(k-1)b)$, forcing $\log F(\Psi|_{S(2mkb)^2})>\delta(2m)^2$ eventually for every $\delta<\frac{4k-5}{4}Q(b)$ (`eventually_doubling_translation_fibre_cost`, `exists_prime_ray_doubling_translation_obstruction`). More generally, whenever $Q(b+a)\ne Q(b-a)$ (nonzero mixed Weil pairing), intersecting $(k,2m)$ and $(2k,m)$ yields outer log-rate deficit $k|Q(b+a)-Q(b-a)|-5Q(a)\to+\infty$, ruling out any single translation-uniform map $\Psi:\mathbb{Z}^2\to\mathbb{Z}^2$ with subquadratic logarithmic fibres (`not_exists_translation_uniform_subquadratic_midpointMap`).

## 2. Two-place precision export, polynomial rounding with carry, and joint inverse loss

Write $n=|S(3mb)|$, $L=|S(4mb)|$, $s=|S(2mb)|$ on a ray $b$ with $d=Q(b)>0$.

1. **Two-place precision versus two-output collisions.** For any map $f:\mathrm{Fin}\,n^2\to\mathrm{Fin}\,L\times\mathrm{Fin}\,s$ (with unrestricted first coordinate $f_1$) having narrow-coordinate precision losses $(i,j)$ at coprime bases $p,q>1$, either $n^2\le F(f)L$ or $n<ps\,p^i+qs\,q^j$ (`two_place_fibre_or_precision_loss`). Along $(2mb,3mb,4mb)$, eventually $\delta_1 m^2<\log F(f)$ or $\delta_2 m^2<\max(i\log p,j\log q)$ for every $\delta_1<2d$ and $\delta_2<5d$ (`eventually_twoPlace_fibre_or_precision_cost`). Specializing to injections ($F=1$) sharpens the two-place precision cost from $d$ to $5d$ in $\max(i\log p,j\log q)$ (`eventually_sharpened_twoPlace_precision_cost`). Every map with $\log F(f)\le\delta m^2$ ($\delta<2d$) retains $F(f)L<n^2$, so every scalar $D>0$ clearing narrow congruences satisfies $\log\log D/m^2>9d-\varepsilon$ (`eventually_loglog_clearing_of_subquadratic_fibre`).
2. **Retirement of polynomial rounding with arbitrary first-output carry.** If $P\in\mathbb{Z}[X_1,X_2]$, $D\ge1$, $D\cdot f_2(x)+r(x)=P(x_1,x_2)$ with $0\le r(x)<D$, and $f_1(x)\in\mathrm{Fin}\,L$ is completely arbitrary (including any carry of $r(x)$), then $P$ takes values in $[0,Ds)$ and preserves congruences modulo the coprime pair $(Ds,Ds+1)$, forcing $n^2\le F(f)L$ or $n<2Ds+1$ (`polynomial_rounding_carry_fibre_or_denom`). Along $(2mb,3mb,4mb)$, eventually $\delta_1 m^2<\log F(f)$ or $\delta_2 m^2<\log D$ for all $\delta_1<2d$ and $\delta_2<5d$ (`eventually_polynomial_carry_quadratic_fibre`).
3. **Carry infection of the wide output.** Any map packing the Euclidean remainder into the wide output as $f_1(x_1,x_2)=(x_1\bmod M)+Mx_2$ ($M\ge2$, $n\ge2$) obeys $n(n-1)<L(p\,p^i+q\,q^j)$ for any first-output precision losses $(i,j)$ at coprime $p,q>1$ (`carryMap_firstPrecision_loss`), forcing $\max(i\log p,j\log q)>\delta m^2$ eventually for all $\delta<2d$ along $(2mb,3mb,4mb)$ (`eventually_carryMap_firstOutput_precision_cost`).
4. **Universal joint inverse loss at one place.** Even at a single base $p>1$, any map $f:\mathrm{Fin}\,n^2\to\mathrm{Fin}\,L\times\mathrm{Fin}\,s$ whose outputs modulo $p^{r+k}$ jointly determine both inputs modulo $p^r$ (`jointInversePrecisionLoss`) obeys $n<ps\,p^{2k}$ (`joint_inverse_precision_loss`), hence $k\log p>\delta m^2$ eventually for all $\delta<\frac{5}{2}d$ along $(2mb,3mb,4mb)$ (`eventually_joint_inverse_precision_cost`, `exists_prime_ray_twoOutput_carry_obstructions`).

## 3. Exact carry window and parameter-free reversible one-place prototype on mixed families

1. **Centered Euclidean carry involution and exact logarithmic window.** For $K\in\mathbb{N}$ and $M=2K+1$, the integral map `centeredCarry K` sending $(z_1,z_2)$ to $((z_1+K)\bmod M - K + M z_2,\lfloor(z_1+K)/M\rfloor)$ is an involution on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ (`centeredCarry_involutive`) satisfying $w_1+Mw_2=z_1+Mz_2$ (`centeredCarry_linear_conservation`), and maps $[-r_0,r_0]^2$ into $[-r_1,r_1]\times[-r_2,r_2]$ for $K\le r_0$ iff $r_0\le Mr_2+K$ and $Mr_0+K\le r_1$ (`centeredCarry_maps_Icc_iff`). An odd schedule $M_m=2K_m+1$ with $\log M_m/m^2\to\mu$ maps $S(mv_0)^2$ into $S(mv_1)\times S(mv_2)$ eventually whenever $Q(v_0)-Q(v_2)<\mu<Q(v_1)-Q(v_0)$ (`eventually_centeredCarry_sections_of_window`), while cofinal validity along $(m(kb-a),mkb,m(kb+a))$ forces $Q(a)\ge0$ (`integerProbe_nonneg_of_cofinal_centeredCarry`).
2. **Parameter-free base-$2$ block carry and genuinely mixed family.** Splitting $512=2^9$ blocks recursively into $32768\times 8=2^{15}\times 2^3$ blocks (`natBlockCarry`, `natBlockRecover`) and conjugating by `zigZagEquiv : ℤ ≃ ℕ` gives a single global parameter-free bijection `intBlockCarry : ℤ × ℤ ≃ ℤ × ℤ`, defined by integral arithmetic prior to computing any section cardinalities. It preserves base-$2$ block congruences modulo $512^t\mapsto 32768^t\times 8^t$ (`natBlockCarry_modEq`) and embeds $S(mv_0)^2\hookrightarrow S(mv_1)\times S(mv_2)$ eventually whenever $0<Q(v_0)$, $5Q(v_0)<3Q(v_1)$, and $Q(v_0)<3Q(v_2)$ (`eventually_intBlockCarry_sections`, `eventually_intBlockCarry_positive_ray`). For every separation ratio $p\ge2$ and all sufficiently large $N$, taking a nonzero integer vector $u$ in the half-window $(2p)$-moment kernel and setting $U=\mathrm{padDivisor}(u)$, $V=\mathrm{dilate}_p(u)\in\mathbb{Z}^{pN}$ yields a positive background $b=U+V$ and a genuinely mixed zero-sum displacement $a=3U-V$ with strictly positive and strictly negative coefficients separated in conductor by factor $>p/2$, $Q(3b+a)=16Q(b)+4Q(U-V)>15Q(b)$, and $Q(3b-a)=4Q(b)+4Q(U-V)>3Q(b)$ via `integerProbe_dilate`, on which the fixed bijection `intBlockCarry` restricts for all large $m$ to an explicit reversible injection $S(3mb)^2\hookrightarrow S(m(3b+a))\times S(m(3b-a))$ (`exists_mixed_family_reversible_blockCarry`).
