## Theorem

Put

\[
  s_n=\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n!},\qquad
  U_n(x)=\frac{\Gamma(1+s_nx)}{\prod_{k=1}^{n}(1-s_nx/k)}.
\]

The function `U_n` is analytic at zero, `U_n(0)=1`, and on a punctured neighbourhood of zero

\[
  \Gamma(-n+s_nx)=-\frac{U_n(x)}{x}.
\]

Let

\[
  \lambda_{j,n}=\left.\frac{d^j}{dx^j}\frac{U_n'(x)}{U_n(x)}\right|_{x=0},
  \qquad
  r_{j,n}=-\frac{U_n^{(j+1)}(0)}{(j+1)!}.
\]

Regard `n ↦ lambda_(j,n)` and `n ↦ r_(j,n)` as germs of complex sequences at infinity.  Then
both countable families

\[
  (\lambda_j)_{j\ge 0},\qquad (r_j)_{j\ge 0}
\]

are algebraically independent over `C`.  In particular, the coefficient field generated by the
regular Laurent coefficients of the complete factorially rescaled Gamma pole family has countably
infinite transcendence degree over `C`.

If `R(X)=sum_(j>=0) r_j X^j` is formed over the pole-index germ ring, then no nonzero finite-order
algebraic differential polynomial over `C[X]` vanishes on `R`.

\[

   =s_n\,\psi(1+s_nx)

\]

\[

   =s_n^{j+1}\left(\psi^{(j)}(1)+j!H_n^{(j+1)}\right).
