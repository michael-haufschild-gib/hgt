## Goal

Construct positive, source-pure geometry intrinsic to hypertranscendence. The object must use only
the simultaneous algebraic independence of the jet tower and projective changes of its generator;
it must not introduce a flow, carrier, evolution equation, probability law, or numerical model.

## Set-up

Let `C` be an infinite differential-constant field of characteristic zero, let `x` be
hypertranscendental over `C`, and put

```text
F = C<x> = C(x,x',x'',...).
```

For a projective point `p in P^1(C)`, choose `M_p in PGL_2(C)` sending `p` to infinity and define

```text
H_p = C((M_p x)', (M_p x)'', ...).                         (1)
```

The exact projective stabilizer theorem implies that `H_p` is independent of the choice of
`M_p`: two such coordinates differ by a constant-slope affine transformation, and every
positive-order jet tail is invariant under precisely those transformations.

## The candidate theorem

### A. Proper projective horizon family `[PROVED]`

For every `p`,

```text
C < H_p < F,                                               (2)
```

and

```text
H_p = H_q  iff  p=q.                                       (3)
```

Properness and distinctness are the strict first-jet horizon and exact stabilizer theorems.

### B. Pairwise two-jet reconstruction `[PROVED]`

For distinct `p,q`,

```text
H_p H_q = F.                                               (4)
```

It is enough to take `p=infinity`, `q=0`. Write `y=x^(-1)`. Then

```text
y'  = -x'/x^2,
y'' = 2(x')^2/x^3 - x''/x^2,

x''/x' - y''/y' = 2x'/x,

x = 2x' / (x''/x' - y''/y').                              (5)
```

The right side of `(5)` belongs to `H_infinity H_0`; its denominator is nonzero because HT gives
`x != 0` and `x' != 0`. Once `x` is recovered, every jet of `x` is already in `H_infinity`, so the
join is all of `F`. Conjugating by a constant Möbius transformation proves `(4)` for every
distinct pair.

First derivatives alone determine only `x^2=-x'/y'`; the second derivative removes the quadratic
ambiguity. Thus `(5)` is an explicit reconstruction law, not a dimension count.

### C. Schwarzian common curvature `[PROVED INCLUSION]`

Let

```text
S(x)=x'''/x'-(3/2)(x''/x')^2.
```

For every `p`, projective invariance gives

```text
C<S(x)> <= H_p.                                            (6)
```

The inclusion is nonconstant: the Schwarzian of an HT element cannot lie in `C`.

### D. Exact pairwise meet `[PROVED]`

For distinct `p,q`,

```text
H_p intersect H_q = C<S(x)>.                              (7)
```

The proof identifies `H_p` with the fixed field of the maximal unipotent subgroup
`U_p < PGL_2(C)` fixing `p`. Distinct `U_p,U_q` generate `PGL_2(C)`, so the left side of `(7)` is
the full projective differential-invariant field. The next section proves directly, in the
rational infinite-jet category, that this invariant field is `C<S(x)>`.

## Proof of the exact meet

Write `x_n=delta^n x` and first take `p=infinity`. The translation subgroup

```text
U_infinity : x_0 |-> x_0+t,   x_n |-> x_n for n>=1
```

fixes `H_infinity=C(x_1,x_2,...)`. Conversely, every element of `F` uses only finitely many jet
variables. A rational function in `x_0,...,x_N` fixed by every translation parameter `t in C`
is independent of `x_0`, because `C` is infinite. Hence

```text
F^(U_infinity)=H_infinity.                                 (8)
```

Conjugating `(8)` by a projective coordinate carrying `p` to infinity gives

```text
F^(U_p)=H_p                                                 (9)
```

for every `p`. Two distinct maximal unipotent subgroups of `PGL_2(C)` are conjugate to the upper
and lower unipotent subgroups. Those generate `PSL_2(C)=PGL_2(C)`. Therefore

```text
H_p intersect H_q = F^(PGL_2(C))                           (10)
```

when `p!=q`.

It remains to compute the right side without an appeal to an unverified infinite-jet invariant
theorem. Put

```text
I=C(S,S',S'',...),   S=S(x).
```

Projective invariance gives `I <= F^(PGL_2(C))`. The defining identity

```text
x_3=x_1 S+(3/2)x_2^2/x_1                                  (11)
```

and its successive derivatives have the triangular form

```text
delta^r S = x_(r+3)/x_1 + R_r(x_1,...,x_(r+2)).            (12)
```

Thus every higher jet is recovered recursively and

```text
F=I(x_0,x_1,x_2).                                          (13)
```

At every finite jet level the coordinate change

```text
(x_0,...,x_N) <-> (x_0,x_1,x_2,S,S',...,delta^(N-3)S)
```

is triangular birational, so `x_0,x_1,x_2` are algebraically independent over the corresponding
finite Schwarzian field.

Now let `R in F` be projectively invariant. It lies in a finite field
`I_N(x_0,x_1,x_2)`. Invariance under all complex projective matrices is a rational identity in
the matrix entries. Since the complex points are Zariski dense, the identity remains valid after
base change from `C` to `I_N`; this is the precise step that permits a rational moving frame whose
entries lie in the coefficient field.

On the open set `x_1!=0`, successively apply

```text
z |-> z-x_0,
z |-> z/x_1,
z |-> z/(1+(x_2/(2x_1))z).                                (14)
```

The prolonged two-jet action sends

```text
(x_0,x_1,x_2) |-> (0,1,0).                                (15)
```

Therefore `R(x_0,x_1,x_2)=R(0,1,0)` belongs to `I_N`. This proves

```text
F^(PGL_2(C))=I=C<S(x)>.                                    (16)
```

Combining `(10)` and `(16)` proves `(7)`.

## Geometric and physical interpretation

The fields `H_p` form a projective line inside the lattice of differential subfields of `F`:

```text
distinct meet  = projective curvature C<S(x)>,
distinct join  = complete source F.
```

Each horizon separately erases the projective position of `x`; any two different projective ends
reconstruct it from second-order boundary data. The Schwarzian is the information common to every
horizon. This is a static `(2,2)` algebraic reconstruction geometry, not a quantum-mechanical or
black-hole claim. Its closest physical analogy is complementarity: incompatible boundary cuts are
individually incomplete but jointly exact, with a common gauge-invariant curvature sector.

### Exact projective and quantum-logical corollary

Let `I=C<S(x)>` and let

```text
Q={I,F} union {H_p : p in P^1(C)}.
```

The meet/join theorem shows that `Q` is closed under intersection and compositum. The assignment

```text
0       |-> I,
C^2     |-> F,
line p  |-> H_p
```

is an isomorphism from the lattice of linear subspaces of `C^2` to `Q`. Consequently this HT
horizon lattice is atomic, modular, complemented, and non-distributive. For three distinct
projective ends `p,q,r`,

```text
H_p intersect (H_q H_r)=H_p,

(H_p intersect H_q)(H_p intersect H_r)=I.                 (17)
```

This is exactly the incidence lattice used as the algebraic skeleton of two-level quantum logic.
The theorem does **not** supply a Hermitian form, orthocomplement, probability measure, Born rule,
state space, or physical quantization. Without such extra data every other horizon is a complement
of `H_p`, and none is canonically its orthogonal complement. The rigorous physical statement is
therefore limited but sharp: every HT scalar carries a canonical qubit-shaped logic of projective
information contexts over its Schwarzian curvature field.
