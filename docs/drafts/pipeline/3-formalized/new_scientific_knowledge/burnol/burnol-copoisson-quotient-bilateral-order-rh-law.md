- **Statement:** A single parity-mixing value relation for the finite dilation holonomy compressed

## Source spaces

Let `L_a` be Burnol's extended cosine-Sonine space: both a vector and its cosine Fourier transform
are constant on `(0,a)`.  Let `Pi_a` be its ambient orthogonal projection.  For `0<a<=1`, let
`C_a` be the closed co-Poisson subspace generated by the explicit Duffin--Weinberger--Burnol
summation map applied to seeds supported in the finite multiplicative interval `[a,1/a]`.

Let `Y_a` be the closed span in `L_a` of the completed evaluation jets indexed by the full
nontrivial zeta divisor, with multiplicity.  Burnol's Theorem 3.1 gives the exact source
identification

```text
Y_a^perp=C_a,
Y_a=C_a^perp.                                             (1)
```

The first definition mentions the divisor; the right side of `(1)` does not.  It is the
orthogonal complement of a closed subspace generated directly by compactly supported source
functions and the co-Poisson summation law.

## Bilateral quotient curvature

Fix `r>1` and `a>0`, and put `b=a/r`.  Assume `b<=1`.  Let `U_r` be normalized dilation.  Both
`U_r` and `U_r^*` map `L_a` into `L_b`.  Define on the ambient Hilbert space

```text
H_(a,r)=U_r Pi_a U_r^*-U_r^* Pi_a U_r.                   (2)
```

This is a bounded self-adjoint difference of two orthogonal projections.  Let `E_b` be the
orthogonal projection of `L_b` onto the source quotient realization

```text
range(E_b)=C_b^perp.                                     (3)
```

The source-order hypothesis is

```text
E_b H_(a,r) E_b <= 0.                                    (4)
```

Equivalently, for every `f in L_b` orthogonal to every co-Poisson source vector,

```text
Re <f,H_(a,r)f> <= 0.                                    (5)
```

Every object in `(4)`--`(5)` is defined before inspecting a zeta zero.

## Theorem

For any one pair `(a,r)` with `r>1` and `0<a/r<=1`, the source-order hypothesis `(4)` implies the
Riemann hypothesis.

### Proof

Let `rho` be a nontrivial zeta zero and let `Y^b_rho` be its zeroth completed evaluation vector in
`L_b`.  By `(1)`,

```text
Y^b_rho in C_b^perp=range(E_b).                           (6)
```

The projected forward and inverse evaluator laws are

```text
Pi_a U_r^*Y^b_rho=conj(r^(rho-1/2))Y^a_rho,
Pi_a U_r  Y^b_rho=conj(r^(rho-1/2)^(-1))Y^a_rho.          (7)
```

Applying the bilateral Pythagorean identity gives

```text
Re <Y^b_rho,H_(a,r)Y^b_rho>
 =(r^(2 Re(rho)-1)-r^(1-2 Re(rho)))||Y^a_rho||^2.        (8)
```

Evaluation off the de Branges symmetry axis is nontrivial, so `Y^a_rho` is nonzero whenever
`Re(rho) != 1/2`.  If a zero satisfied `Re(rho)>1/2`, then `r>1` would make the right side of `(8)`
strictly positive.  This contradicts `(4)` using `(6)`.

Thus there is no right-half off-critical zero.  Schwarz reality and the functional equation send
every left-half off-critical zero to a right-half off-critical zero.  There are therefore no
off-critical zeros, which is RH.  `QED`

The equality

```text
E_b H_(a,r) E_b=0                                        (9)
```

also implies RH.  It is not actually stronger than the one-sided order once Fourier reflection is
used, as the next section proves.

## Reflection collapse to one parity block

Let `F` be the cosine Fourier involution.  The extended Sonin space and the closed co-Poisson
subspace are both `F`-invariant, so `F` commutes with `E_b`.  Dilation reflection gives

```text
F U_r F=U_r^*,
F H_(a,r) F=-H_(a,r).                                    (R1)
```

For `T=E_bH_(a,r)E_b`, therefore

```text
F T F=-T.                                                 (R2)
```

If `T<=0`, unitary conjugation by `F` gives `-T<=0`, hence also `T>=0`; consequently `T=0`.
The converse is immediate.  Thus

```text
T<=0 iff T=0 iff T>=0.                                   (R3)
```

In particular, the one-sided quotient order is not genuinely weaker than exact quotient
flatness.  This is a positive structural reduction: no estimate is needed if one can prove the
right source value relation.

Let

```text
E_b^+=(1+F)E_b/2,
E_b^-=(1-F)E_b/2                                         (R4)
```

be the Fourier-even and Fourier-odd quotient projections.  Equation `(R2)` kills the two diagonal
parity blocks, so `T` is determined by one off-diagonal block and its adjoint.  Hence

```text
T=0 iff E_b^+ H_(a,r) E_b^-=0.                           (R5)
```

The smallest source theorem exposed by this route is therefore the single constraint `(R5)`.

It has an exact cross-Gram form.  Let `F_a` be the Fourier involution restricted to `L_a` and put

```text
B_+=E_b^+ U_r iota_a,
B_-=E_b^- U_r iota_a.                                   (R6)
```

From `U_r^*=F U_r F` and `E_b^+F=E_b^+`, `E_b^-F=-E_b^-`,

```text
E_b^+ U_r^* iota_a= B_+ F_a,
E_b^- U_r^* iota_a=-B_- F_a.                            (R7)
```

Using `Pi_a=iota_a iota_a^*` in the two terms of `(2)` now gives

```text
E_b^+ H_(a,r) E_b^-=2 B_+ B_-^*.                        (R8)
```

Therefore `(R5)` is equivalent to the static source decorrelation

```text
B_+ B_-^*=0.                                             (R9)
```

This is the narrowest current **source-pure operator** target in this quotient route: prove that
the Fourier-even and Fourier-odd components of one forward-dilation channel are cross-Gram
orthogonal after quotienting by the explicit co-Poisson source subspace.  It is an exact value
relation, not a sign estimate.

## Off-diagonal strength and the weakening frontier

For two evaluation points `w,v`, the same projected transport calculation gives the full kernel
identity

```text
<Y^b_w,H_(a,r)Y^b_v>
 =(lambda_r(w)conj(lambda_r(v))
   -(lambda_r(w)conj(lambda_r(v)))^(-1))
    <Y^a_w,Y^a_v>.                                      (K1)
```

The diagonal `w=v` is exactly `(8)`.  For distinct critical-line points the scalar in `(K1)` need
not vanish.  Therefore quotient flatness and the decorrelation `(R9)` impose off-diagonal
conditions in addition to RH; this card proves they are sufficient for RH, not necessary under
RH.  The source-pure gain is purchased by a potentially strong cross-fiber constraint.

The exact weakening frontier is now visible.  A source-defined diagonal functional `D_b` on the
co-Poisson quotient satisfying

```text
D_b(T) evaluated at the character w
 =<Y^b_w,T Y^b_w>/||Y^a_w||^2                          (K2)
```

would discard the extra cross-fiber terms while retaining the signed orientation value `(8)`.
Nonpositivity of `D_b(H_(a,r))` on the source character spectrum would then be the diagonal
criterion.

This diagonal map is now discharged by
`burnol-copoisson-berezin-diagonal-reflection-law.md`.  If `k_w^b` is the continuous evaluation
vector of `L_b`, then `y_w^b=E_b k_w^b` is the reproducing kernel of the closed source-defined
quotient `C_b^perp`, and

```text
Ber_b(T)(w)=<y_w^b,T y_w^b>                              (K2a)
```

is its canonical Berezin diagonal.  At an actual zero `rho`, every co-Poisson source vanishes, so
`y_rho^b=k_rho^b` and `(K2a)` gives exactly `(K2)`.  The family is faithful on positive operators
because the quotient kernels have dense span.  Moreover Fourier reflection gives

```text
Ber_b(H_(a,r))(1-conj(w))=-Ber_b(H_(a,r))(w).            (K2b)
```

If `Lambda_b` is the completed co-Poisson Mellin multiplier, the same card proves the local source
factorization

```text
Ber_b(H_(a,r))(w)
 =2 sinh(2 log(r)(Re(w)-1/2))||k_w^a||^2+R_(a,r)(w),
R_(a,r) in (Lambda_b,conj(Lambda_b)).                    (K2c)
```

Thus the quotient correction is forced into the co-Poisson annihilator ideal and vanishes on
every zero fibre.  This is the exact constraint--orientation splitting of the scalar reader.

Thus the diagonalization problem itself is no longer open and requires neither zero enumeration
nor a biorthogonal divisor basis.  The remaining RH-bearing problem is the scalar sign of `(K2a)`
on the co-Poisson annihilator spectrum.  The Berezin construction supplies no such sign.

This is the Burnol-coordinate version of the existing ordinate-diagonal gap in
`../../1-in-progress/euler-horizontal-charge-separator.md`.  Indeed, writing `r=exp(t)` and
`x=Re(w)-1/2`, the normalized diagonal in `(8)` is

```text
exp(2tx)-exp(-2tx)=2sinh(2tx),                            (K3)
```

whose first scale derivative at `t=0` is `4x`.  The quotient Berezin construction now gives this
linear horizontal-charge reader from the infinitesimal projection phase polarization already
formalized in Lean.  It does not by itself give the reader's sign, the positive Cauchy layer
energy, or the absolute bound sought in the horizontal-leakage card; the exact comparison of those
two diagonal readers is another open value relation.

## Ambient supersymmetry is already formalized

Put

```text
P_+=U_r Pi_a U_r^*,
P_-=U_r^* Pi_a U_r,
B_(a,r)=1-P_+-P_-.
```

Both `P_+` and `P_-` are orthogonal projections and `H_(a,r)=P_+-P_-`.  The existing
`HTGeometry.TwoProjectionSupersymmetry` theorems therefore give, before co-Poisson compression,

```text
H_(a,r)^2+B_(a,r)^2=1,
H_(a,r)B_(a,r)+B_(a,r)H_(a,r)=0,
||H_(a,r)||<=1.                                          (S1)
```

Thus the finite holonomy is already a bounded static constraint with an exact supersymmetric
partner.  The strong operator problem is not its domain, closure, or norm; it is whether its one
Fourier-parity cross-block vanishes after the canonical co-Poisson quotient compression.  The
weaker diagonal route asks only for the sign of its canonical quotient Berezin value.

## Douglas factorization: a non-sign producer

Let `iota_a:L_a -> H` be the isometric inclusion and define the two source quotient channels

```text
A_+=E_b U_r   iota_a,
A_-=E_b U_r^* iota_a.                                   (10)
```

Since `Pi_a=iota_a iota_a^*`, equation `(2)` gives the exact factorization of the compressed
holonomy

```text
E_b H_(a,r) E_b=A_+A_+^*-A_-A_-^*.                     (11)
```

Consequently the open source order `(4)`, and hence by `(R3)` exact flatness `(9)`, is equivalent
to

```text
A_+A_+^*<=A_-A_-^*.                                    (12)
```

Douglas's range-factorization theorem converts `(12)` into a pure value relation:

```text
there exists a contraction C_r:L_a -> L_a
such that A_+=A_- C_r.                                  (13)
```

Thus one need not attack an operator sign directly.  It is enough to construct a canonical
co-Poisson quotient intertwiner `C_r` which makes the forward quotient channel factor
contractively through the inverse channel.  Reflection then forces the majorization to saturate
to equality.  On a zero-evaluator adjoint fiber, `(7)` forces

```text
C_r^* Y^a_rho=r^(2(rho-1/2))Y^a_rho                    (14)
```

up to the analytic-bilinear conjugation convention.  Contractivity then forbids every right-half
character.  Equation `(14)` is a consequence to verify after constructing `C_r`; using it to
define `C_r` from the divisor would be circular.

## Exact top-down chain

```text
source-defined contractive factorization A_+=A_-C_r
 -> exact co-Poisson quotient flatness by Fourier reflection
 -> parity-channel decorrelation B_+B_-^*=0
 -> every zero evaluator has nonpositive forward/inverse defect imbalance
 -> no right-half off-critical zero
 -> no off-critical zero by FE--Schwarz reflection
 -> RH.                                                  (15)
```

This chain bypasses the longer requirements

```text
co-Poisson/time-band Calkin lift
 -> pairwise prime-charge coherence
 -> cofinal vanishing of the common charge
 -> RH.
```

It also avoids a divisor-fed sign assumption.  The source quotient `C_b^perp` is selected by
orthogonality to all compactly supported co-Poisson sums, not by choosing or enumerating their
zero-evaluator annihilator.

## What remains open

The theorem does not prove `(4)`, `(9)`, or `(R5)`.  Ordinary projection positivity gives separate
nonnegative forward and inverse defect energies but does not compare them.  Fourier reflection
does prove that any comparison must saturate, but it does not make the parity-mixing block vanish.
Burnol's completeness theorem replaces the divisor span by a source quotient; it does not assert
that the quotient compression of `H_(a,r)` is flat.  Identity `(K1)` also shows that full flatness
contains cross-fiber information beyond the pointwise RH orientation.

The stronger operator target is any of the equivalent statements:

```text
prove E_b H_(a,r) E_b=0 for one nontrivial scale r>1;

prove the single parity value relation E_b^+ H_(a,r) E_b^-=0;

prove the cross-Gram decorrelation B_+B_-^*=0;

or construct a source-defined contraction C_r with A_+=A_-C_r.            (16)
```

Other acceptable producers include an explicit square factorization

```text
-E_b H_(a,r) E_b=A^*A,
```

a contractive source intertwiner between the two quotient maps, or a canonical co-Poisson
reflection identity which gives `(4)` after compression.  The strictly weaker target now available
is a source proof of

```text
Ber_b(H_(a,r))(rho)<=0
```

on every right-half co-Poisson annihilator character.  A proof that merely renames those
characters as zeta zeros and assumes the desired character modulus is circular.
