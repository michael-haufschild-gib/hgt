## Theorem

```text
E_k=t^k E_01,  F_k=t^k E_10,
U_k=t^k E_20,  V_k=t^k E_21,

```

The modes `F_k,U_k` are the radical of one standard projective-plane chart horizon, and
`E_k,V_k` are the radical of a distinct horizon. Let `L_2` be the Lie subalgebra generated by

```text

```

Over every characteristic-zero field `K`, for all `k>=0`,

```text

```

Moreover, in every Lie-module representation, a vector annihilated by the eight generators in
`(1)` is annihilated by every mode in `(2)`.

## Proof

The matrix brackets include

```text
[E_i,F_j]=H_(i+j),
[H_i,E_j]= 2E_(i+j),
[H_i,F_j]=-2F_(i+j),
[H_i,U_j]=-U_(i+j),
[H_i,V_j]= V_(i+j).                                      (3)
```

First `H_1=[E_0,F_1]`. Bracketing by `H_1` recursively raises the polynomial degree of all four
root/shear families; then `[E_0,F_k]=H_k`. This proves `(2)`. For the representation theorem, the
modes annihilating a fixed vector form a Lie subalgebra containing `(1)`, hence contain `L_2`.
