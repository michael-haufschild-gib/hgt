## The formal theorem chain

**The private-sector identity `(1)`.**  `TelescopeIdeal.iInf_span_singleton_eq_span_prod_of_primes`
identifies the intersection of the transverse push kernels with the ideal generated by the product
of the augmentation factors, on a free Laurent orbit.

**The nonzero witness `(2)` and the exact transverse order `d-2`.**
`privateSectorPolynomial_ne_zero`, `setVariableOne_privateSectorPolynomial_eq_zero`,
`privateSectorPolynomial_totalDegree`, `privateSector_card_le_totalDegree_of_dvd`,
`pairPrivateWitness_exact`, `pairPrivateWitness_coordinates`: a private charge is divisible by every
transverse augmentation factor, and the canonical witness realizes exactly that degree, so a
pair-private mode cannot be hidden with fewer than one difference in every transverse direction.

**The canonical carrier `(3)`.**  `iteratedTransverseDifferenceMero_face` is the relative coface
equation: applying the structural differences along a duplicate-free list of directions that the
face has not already deleted deletes exactly those parameters.  Hence
`pairFaceCarrier_eq_iteratedTransverseDifference`:

```text
F_e = ∏_{k ∉ e} Δ_{τ_k} Ψ
```

is the face coordinate whose surviving parameters are exactly the pair `e`.

**The target signature `(4)`.**  `not_differentialAlgebraicOver_pairFaceCarrier`: two surviving
directions whose periods are rationally independent together with `1` give a rank-three period
minor, so the carrier never reaches the differentially algebraic locus.  The order-one atomic input
this rests on is corpus-owned and unconditional
(`ellipticGammaExternalDefectFamily_differentialIndependent_daExtension`), and the source itself is
constructed rather than named: `ellipticGammaSource_periodic` and
`ellipticGammaSource_mixed_difference` prove `Δ_1 Ψ = 0` and `Δ_a Δ_b Ψ = -2πi`, i.e. `Ψ ∈ Q`.

**The off-target signatures `(5)`.**  `inter_subsingleton_of_card_two`: two distinct two-element
faces meet in at most one direction.  `differentialAlgebraicOver_pairFaceSignature_of_inter_subsingleton`:
a face with at most one surviving parameter has no rank-three minor — with none it is the terminal
constant — hence is differentially algebraic.  So pushing `F_e` toward any other pair kills it.

**Boundary noncompression.**  `exists_private_carrier_of_omitted_pair`: for any collection of pair
faces omitting `e`, the carrier `F_e` is hypertranscendental while every retained signature is
differentially algebraic.  No terminal residue, spanning tree, selected atlas, or other proper
deletion of the pair-face coordinates can classify differential algebraicity.

**The direct-sum private field.**  `card_pairFaceFamily`: there are exactly `choose(d, 2)` pair
faces.  `pairFaceStar_diffIndepOn` and `pairFaceStar_diffIndepOn_daExtension`: for an integrally
independent period family their packets are jointly differentially independent over the constants,
and over any differentially algebraic extension of them.  So the `choose(d, 2)` pair sectors are
independently addressable.

## What is proved and what is not

Proved unconditionally in Lean: every statement the card makes.  Two of them were carried on the
card as conditional and are not conditional in the formalization.

* The order-one elliptic-gamma atomic theorem, recorded on the card as an "independently unaudited"
  external parent, is a theorem of this tree with hypotheses that are the data of the class only.
* The direct-sum private field was recorded as conditional on the all-depth exact-image theorem.
  That parent is not used.  What "independently addressable pair sectors" asserts — that no
  pair-private mode is a differential function of the others — is proved directly from the
  bottom-layer star-gluing engine, and joint differential independence is strictly stronger than an
  injection of abstract charge groups.

Not claimed here: the all-depth exact-image theorem itself, which belongs to
`all-depth-pair-face-signature-completeness` and is not needed by anything on this card; and the
realization of a private charge as a principal part of a prescribed meromorphic function, which is
that same parent's statement.
