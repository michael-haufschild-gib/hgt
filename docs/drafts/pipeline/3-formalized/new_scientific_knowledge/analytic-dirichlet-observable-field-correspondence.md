# Analytic Dirichlet observable-field correspondence

- **ID:** `analytic-dirichlet-observable-field-correspondence`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** GENERAL HT MASTER THEOREM / ANALYTIC GRASSMANN-LATTICE RECONSTRUCTION
- **Scientific status:** `[UNCONDITIONALLY FORMALIZED] [NOVELTY PROVISIONAL: HIGH]`
- **Statement:** A cofinite-tail-dense prime sampler sends arbitrary independent bounded continuous
  source families to actual meromorphic differential fields over the genuine coordinate field
  `C(s)` and reconstructs their source spans exactly. One universal family canonically embeds its
  full powerset lattice into the analytic differential-field lattice, preserving intersections and
  composita. Source interaction creates a relatively HT sector exactly when it creates a new
  source direction.

## Theorem

Let `x : primes -> V` have every cofinite tail dense in `K`. For a bounded continuous observable
`f`, let `Q_x^a(f)` be the upper-end meromorphic germ of

```text
sum_p f(x_p) p^(-(s+a)),
```

and for an observable subspace `W` with an arbitrary independent bounded continuous basis let

```text
F_x^a(W) = C(s)<Q_x^a(f) : f in W>_diff.
```

The following laws hold without any finiteness assumption on the source index set.

1. **Coordinate disjointness.** The upper-end coordinate `s` is transcendental over the fraction
   field of all finite-abscissa Dirichlet coefficient germs. Hence coefficient independence over
   `C` survives analytic realization over the actual embedded field `C(s)`.
2. **Arbitrary differential freeness.** Every source-linearly-independent family gives an actually
   differentially independent germ family over `C(s)`. Finite character removes the earlier need
   to assemble finite chambers or choose an inverse system.
3. **Membership reconstruction.** `Q_x^a(g)` belongs to `F_x^a(W)` exactly when `g|_K` belongs to
   `W`.
4. **Grassmann reconstruction.** For arbitrary independent presentations, field inclusion and
   equality are equivalent to source-span inclusion and equality. Consequently the generated field
   depends only on the source subspace, not its basis.
5. **Exact source creation.** If `g|_K` escapes `W`, then `Q_x^a(g)` is differentially independent
   over the entire field `F_x^a(W)`.
6. **Interaction law.** For bounded continuous `u,v`, the product carrier `Q_x^a(uv)` is relatively
   HT over `F_x^a(W)` exactly when `(uv)|_K` escapes `W`.
7. **Arbitrary meet law.** For three jointly independent source blocks `A,B,C` of any cardinality,

   ```text
   F(A+B) intersect F(A+C) = F(A).
   ```

8. **Canonical lattice law.** Fix one arbitrary independent source family indexed by `J`. For
   `S subset J`, let `F(S)` be the field generated by the `S`-coordinates. Then

   ```text
   F(S) <= F(T) iff S subset T,
   F(S) intersect F(T) = F(S intersect T),
   compositum(F(S),F(T)) = F(S union T).
   ```

   Thus `S -> F(S)` is a canonical order embedding of the complete powerset order into actual
   analytic differential fields; its image preserves binary meets and joins even for infinite
   source sets.
9. **Constraint-Hilbert spectrum.** In every finite valuation window, homogeneous constraint
   Hilbert pivots become an actual `C(s)`-independent meromorphic spectrum with cardinality

   ```text
   sum_r Hilbert_K(r+1) * number_of_shifts(r).
   ```

The residue--valuation source-creation tower is a finite product-state specialization of these
laws rather than the master object.

## Geometric meaning

The Dirichlet carrier is a faithful differential-field encoding of source linear geometry, but it
does not preserve pointwise source multiplication. The field remembers every source subspace and
every inclusion between such subspaces; for a fixed independent frame it also remembers coordinate
unions and intersections exactly. The failure of multiplicative closure is therefore measurable
inside the same geometry: multiplication produces a new analytic HT sector if and only if it exits
the represented source span. This is a source-to-field lattice law, not merely a list of independent
Dirichlet-series examples.

## Novelty gate

Classical parents include uniqueness of convergent Dirichlet series, the Shapiro--Sparer
independence criterion, Pong's differential-algebraic refinements, the classical upgrade of
convergent Dirichlet independence from `C` to `C(s)`, Bohr-transform theory, pure transcendental
base extension, and ordinary Hilbert functions. None of those ingredients is claimed as new.

The candidate new result is not arbitrary-family algebraic independence by itself. Pong already
uses arbitrary indexed families in arithmetic-function independence results, and the abstract
intersection laws of purely transcendental fields are classical. The candidate new result is the
analytic coupling: tail-dense source geometry is reconstructed exactly by actual embedded
differential fields over `C(s)`, for arbitrary source cardinality, and yields a canonical infinite
powerset embedding with exact intersections and composita plus an exact interaction-creation law.
No located source states this coupled analytic Grassmann-lattice correspondence. Priority remains
provisional pending specialist review of Shapiro--Sparer, Pong, and modern Dirichlet-algebra and
Hardy-space literature.

## RH pressure test

The law does not address zeros. It imposes a positive architectural requirement on an Euler-side
RH route at arbitrary scale: an Euler observable field absorbs exactly the source directions in its
represented span, and intersections or composita of coordinate sectors cannot create hidden source
identifications. Therefore every relevant Euler interaction must either lie in the represented
source algebra or be identified through additional completed structure such as the archimedean
factor and functional-equation reflection. Enlarging a route through an infinite tower of Euler
coordinates does not evade this constraint; the canonical full field still reconstructs the full
source span exactly.

## Proof boundary

- The arbitrary source family may have any index type. Each observable must be continuous and
  individually uniformly bounded along the sampled primes; no common bound is required.
- Tail completeness is essential; without it distinct source observables can induce the same prime
  coefficient stream.
- The analytic objects are actual meromorphic germs at the complex upper end. No global
  continuation or functional-equation claim is made.
- The three-block meet theorem has arbitrary cardinality. For coordinate subspaces of one universal
  independent family, the full intersection and compositum laws are separately packaged.
- The powerset embedding treats coordinate subspaces of a fixed independent frame. The arbitrary
  order/equality theorems separately show basis independence for all subspaces admitting bounded
  continuous independent presentations; a single bundled Grassmannian object is not introduced.
- The exact quotient-rank formulation as a transcendence-degree identity is not separately
  packaged; relative differential independence and the exact finite Hilbert cardinalities are.
