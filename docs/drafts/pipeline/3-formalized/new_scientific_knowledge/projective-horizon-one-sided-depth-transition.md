## Complete heterogeneous-family classification

Let `(H_i,n_i)` be any nonempty finite family of projective horizons with positive depths, let
`d=max_i n_i`, and let `S` be the span of their normal covectors. Exactly one of the following
holds.

1. All `H_i` are one hyperplane `H`. Then

   ```text
   intersection_i mathcal H_(H_i,n_i)=mathcal H_(H,d).    (12)
   ```

2. At least two `H_i` are distinct and every `n_i=1`. Then

   ```text
   intersection_i mathcal H_(H_i,1)=F^(G_S).              (13)
   ```

3. At least two `H_i` are distinct and some `n_i>=2`. Then

   ```text
   intersection_i mathcal H_(H_i,n_i)=C.                  (14)
   ```

For `(14)`, choose a depth-at-least-two horizon and any distinct member; their pairwise meet is
`C` by `(2)`, so the full intersection is `C`. Equations `(12)--(13)` are respectively tail
nesting and the depth-one span theorem.

The join of the family is its single-label shallowest horizon in case 1 and is `F` in cases 2--3.
Thus `(12)--(14)` classify both meet and join for every finite mixture of projective observer labels
and derivative depths.
