## Statement

Let \(A\) be a commutative ring, let \(\varepsilon\in A\) satisfy
\[
 \varepsilon^2=1,
\]
and write \(h=1+\varepsilon\).  In any \(A\)-algebra in which the image of \(h\) is invertible,
\[
 \varepsilon=1.                                                   \tag{1}
\]
Consequently every quadratic integer
\[
 n_\varepsilon=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\varepsilon^j
\]
becomes the ordinary scalar degree:
\[
 n_\varepsilon=n\cdot1.                                          \tag{2}
\]

For the universal real Grothendieck--Witt algebra
\[
 A_{\mathrm{GW}}=\mathbb Z[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2-1),
\]
there is a canonical isomorphism
\[
 A_{\mathrm{GW}}[h^{-1}]\simeq\mathbb Z[1/2]                     \tag{3}
\]
carrying \(\varepsilon\) to \(1\).  Thus hyperbolic localization is precisely the rank channel;
it kills the signature component rather than choosing between two characters afterward.

## Proof of `(1)`--`(2)`

Factor
\[
 (1-\varepsilon)h
  =(1-\varepsilon)(1+\varepsilon)
  =1-\varepsilon^2
  =0.
\]
After \(h\) is inverted, multiplication by \(h\) is injective, so
\(1-\varepsilon=0\).  This proves `(1)`.  Substitution in the finite sum immediately gives
\[
 n_\varepsilon=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}1=n\cdot1,
\]
which is `(2)`.

## Proof of `(3)`

The homomorphism
\[
 \mathbb Z[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2-1)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z[1/2],
 \qquad \varepsilon\longmapsto1
\]
sends \(h\) to \(2\), hence extends through \(h\)-localization.  Conversely, `(1)` gives
\(\varepsilon=1\) in the localization, while \(h=2\) is invertible there.  Therefore the
localized algebra is generated by \(\mathbb Z\) and \(2^{-1}\), with no remaining relation beyond
those of \(\mathbb Z[1/2]\).  The two maps are inverse.

Equivalently, after rationalization,
\[
 A_{\mathrm{GW}}\otimes\mathbb Q
 \simeq \mathbb Q e_+\oplus\mathbb Q e_-,
 \qquad e_\pm=(1\pm\varepsilon)/2.
\]
Here \(h=2e_+\).  Localizing at \(h\) retains the rank summand \(e_+\) and annihilates the
signature summand \(e_-\).

## Exact Deninger consequence

Let \(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{MW}}\) be a finite Milnor--Witt correspondence complex with framed
power pullback and pushforward satisfying
\[
 F_nV_n=n_\varepsilon\,\mathrm{id}.
\]
Define its algebraic Gamma rank boundary by flat base change
\[
 C^\bullet_{\Gamma,\mathrm{rk}}
   :=C^\bullet_{\mathrm{MW}}\otimes_{GW}GW[h^{-1}].
\]
Localization is flat, so it preserves the cochain identity and commutes with cohomology.  On the
localized complex, `(2)` gives the exact ordinary degree law
\[
 F_nV_n=n\,\mathrm{id}.                                           \tag{4}
\]
Combining `(4)` with Poincare adjunction and a positive moving Hodge structure invokes
`deninger-polarized-transfer-half-density` and forces
\[
 \|V_nx\|^2=n\|x\|^2,\qquad U_n=n^{-1/2}V_n\ \text{isometric}.
\]

This constructs the scalar rank channel algebraically.  No positivity, Xi zero, spectral
coordinate, RH statement, or choice of real character is used.

## What remains open

Hyperbolic localization alone does not construct Deninger's positive Hodge metric.  The remaining
source theorem is a functorial Gamma boundary realization
\[
 \operatorname{Real}_\Gamma^{\mathrm{pol}}:
 C^\bullet_{\mathrm{MW}}[h^{-1}]
   \longrightarrow \text{positive odd Hilbert complexes}
\]
which:

1. realizes the localized framed power action and its Poincare adjunction;
2. equips finite correspondence packets with the rectangular/Gamma odd Hodge operator;
3. is compatible with the bar differential;
4. has cofinal harmonic limit and no lost source cohomology;
5. is defined before any comparison with the Xi divisor.

The signature sector may remain in the integral incidence complex, but it is annihilated at the
positive Gamma boundary.  A proof must show that this annihilation is compatible with cup product
and does not erase the source classes needed by determinant/Lefschetz comparison.

## Novelty audit

The algebraic localization calculation and plus/minus splitting are standard.  New content is the
use of hyperbolic localization as the exact constructive replacement for the previously
conjectural “Gamma rank collapse” in Deninger's source cohomology.  It strengthens character
rigidity from uniqueness to a canonical algebraic realization.

## Falsifiers

- Localizing at \(\eta\) or selecting the Witt/signature summand is the opposite operation and
  does not give `(4)`.
- Forgetting Milnor--Witt data before forming the incidence differential loses the Steinberg and
  quadratic-orientation information.
- Declaring an arbitrary Euclidean norm after localization does not construct the Gamma Hodge
  theory; positivity and flow naturality remain separate.
- If the localized comparison is defined using Xi zeros or assumes centered spectrum real, RH
  has been inserted.
