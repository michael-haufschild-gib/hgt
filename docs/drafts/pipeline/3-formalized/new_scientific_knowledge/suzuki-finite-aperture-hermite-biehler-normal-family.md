## 1. Abstract all-angle theorem

Let `A,E : C -> C` be continuous and suppose

```text
A(i)=0,                                                    (1)

A(z)+uE(z)!=0
whenever Im z!=0 and |u|=1.                               (2)
```

Then

```text
|A(z)|<|E(z)|                 for Im z>0.                  (3)
```

If `A` and `E` are entire, it follows that

```text
Theta(z)=A(z)/E(z)                                        (4)
```

is holomorphic on the upper half-plane and satisfies `|Theta(z)|<1` there.

### Proof

At `z=i`, (1) and (2) imply `E(i)!=0`, so

```text
|A(i)|-|E(i)|<0.                                          (5)
```

Fix `z` in the upper half-plane and join `i` to `z` by

```text
gamma(t)=(1-t)i+tz,       0<=t<=1.                        (6)
```

The segment stays in the upper half-plane.  If (3) failed at `z`, continuity and the intermediate
value theorem would give `t` such that

```text
|A(gamma(t))|=|E(gamma(t))|.                              (7)
```

If both sides of (7) vanish, then every characteristic combination vanishes at the nonreal point
`gamma(t)`, contradicting (2).  Otherwise `E(gamma(t))!=0` and

```text
u=-A(gamma(t))/E(gamma(t))
```

has modulus one and makes `A(gamma(t))+uE(gamma(t))=0`, again contradicting (2).  This proves (3).
In particular `E` has no upper-half-plane zero, so the quotient in (4) is holomorphic and (3)
gives its strict Schur bound.

This argument uses the **whole extension circle**.  Reality of the zeros for only one selected
angle is not enough.

## 2. Exact application to Suzuki's characteristic family

For fixed aperture `a>0` and a shift `lambda<lambda_a`, Suzuki defines deficiency vectors by

```text
T_(a,lambda) v_+ = exp(x),
T_(a,lambda) v_- = exp(-x),
T_(a,lambda)=A_a-lambda I>0,                              (8)
```

and proves that, for every extension angle,

```text
W(a,theta;z)
 =(z-i) integral_(-a)^a v_+(x)exp(izx)dx
  +exp(i theta)(z+i) integral_(-a)^a v_-(x)exp(izx)dx     (9)
```

is entire and has only real zeros.  Put

```text
A_(a,lambda)(z)
 =(z-i) integral_(-a)^a v_+(x)exp(izx)dx,

E_(a,lambda)(z)
 =(z+i) integral_(-a)^a v_-(x)exp(izx)dx.                (10)
```

Then (9) is exactly

```text
W(a,theta;z)=A_(a,lambda)(z)+exp(i theta)E_(a,lambda)(z). (11)
```

The exponential parametrization covers the unit circle, Theorem 1.5 supplies (2), and the factor
`z-i` supplies (1).  Hence

```text
Theta_(a,lambda)(z)=A_(a,lambda)(z)/E_(a,lambda)(z)       (12)
```

is unconditionally Schur on the upper half-plane.

Moreover `A(i)=0` gives the aperture- and shift-independent normalization

```text
Theta_(a,lambda)(i)=0,
m_(a,lambda)(z)=i(1+Theta_(a,lambda)(z))/(1-Theta_(a,lambda)(z)),
m_(a,lambda)(i)=i.                                        (12a)
```

The strict Schur bound makes `m_(a,lambda)` Herglotz.  Thus no extra affine Herglotz
normalization is needed before compactness, and a subsequential Schur limit can only degenerate to
the anchored constant `0` (equivalently the Herglotz constant `i`), not to an arbitrary boundary
constant.

Applying Schwarz's lemma after conjugating the upper half-plane to the disk gives the quantitative
aperture-uniform estimate

```text
|Theta_(a,lambda)(z)| <= |(z-i)/(z+i)|,       Im z>0.     (12b)
```

The right side contains no aperture, shift, or arithmetic constant.  This is stronger than the
pointwise strict disk bound and gives a single compact-open majorant for the entire finite-aperture
family.

The screw kernel is real and even, so `T_(a,lambda)` commutes with conjugation and parity.  The
uniqueness in (8) gives

```text
v_+ real,
v_-(x)=v_+(-x).                                          (13)
```

Consequently

```text
A_(a,lambda)=E_(a,lambda)^#,                              (14)
```

where `F^#(z)=conj(F(conj z))`.  Thus (3) is precisely the Hermite--Biehler inequality

```text
|E^#(z)|<|E(z)|,       Im z>0,                            (15)
```

and `Theta=E^#/E` is a meromorphic inner function.  The extension spectrum at angle `theta` is the
real boundary level set

```text
Theta(x)=-exp(i theta).                                   (16)
```

This packages all of Suzuki's self-adjoint extensions into one finite-aperture de Branges/Livsic
carrier.

## 3. Unconditional compactness of the corrected limit carrier

Let `(a_n,lambda_n)` be any sequence with `a_n>0` and `lambda_n<lambda_(a_n)`.  Every function
`Theta_n=Theta_(a_n,lambda_n)` is holomorphic and bounded by one on the upper half-plane.  Montel's
theorem therefore gives, on every closed ball compactly contained in the upper half-plane, a
subsequence converging uniformly on every smaller concentric closed ball to a holomorphic Schur
function.  A diagonal exhaustion gives upper-half-plane locally uniform subsequential compactness.

No estimate on the primes and no RH assumption enters this compactness statement.  The common
anchor `(12a)` eliminates arbitrary normalizing automorphisms.  The later negative-shift escape
theorem shows that the sole constant collapse `Theta=0` actually occurs along admissible cofinal
shift schedules, so independence over all shifts is false.  Arithmetic is needed instead to prove
anchored-jet survival and subsequential uniqueness in the canonical ground-centered projective
chart, then identify that limit with the global Xi/de Branges characteristic carrier.

## 4. Two-angle meromorphic quotient and gauge removal

For two distinct unit phases `u_0,u_1`, set

```text
W_j=A+u_j E,
Q_01=W_0/W_1.                                             (17)
```

Then, wherever the quotients are defined,

```text
Q_01=(Theta+u_0)/(Theta+u_1),
Theta=(u_0-u_1 Q_01)/(Q_01-1).                            (18)
```

Thus a quotient of two extension characteristic functions recovers the Livsic function by a fixed
Mobius transformation.  Any common nonvanishing entire normalization cancels from `Q_01` exactly.
This is the natural pole-carrying replacement for Corollary 1.6's impossible ordinary compact


The separately formalized projective free-boundary law proves why this restriction is necessary:

## Literature collision audit

The generic mechanism is classical, not new.  Martin's 2009 representation theorem proves that
regular simple symmetric operators with deficiency indices `(1,1)` admit de Branges models, and
Aleman--Martin--Ross (2012) develop the Livsic characteristic function for simple symmetric
operators with equal deficiency indices.  These sources prevent claiming the abstract
Hermite--Biehler/Livsic mechanism as new.

A targeted search of Suzuki's June 2026 paper and the directly related 2026 material found no
finite-aperture extraction of `E^#/E`, no two-angle correction of Corollary 1.6, and no normal-family
compactness theorem for the screw characteristic family.  Accordingly only the exact
Suzuki-specific extraction and corrected limit wall are labeled candidate new; specialist priority
review is still required.
