- **Statement:** If a nonconstant holomorphic uniformly almost-periodic function has minimal
  autonomous algebraic-differential order `n`, then its Bohr frequency group has rational rank at
  most `2n`, even for a singular implicit equation with a nonunique initial-value problem. The same
  bound holds for its translation-equivariant zero-divisor distribution.

## Setup

Let

```text
S_eta={z in C: |Im z|<eta},  eta>0.                         (1)
```

Let `f` be holomorphic on `S_eta` and uniformly almost periodic under real translations: for every
`0<eta'<eta`, the family

```text
{z |-> f(z+t):t in R}                                      (2)
```

is relatively compact in the uniform norm on `|Im z|<=eta'`. Let `Gamma(f)` be the additive group
generated by the Bohr frequencies of `f` on the real axis.

Assume `f` satisfies a nonzero autonomous differential polynomial

```text
P(f,f',...,f^(n))=0,
P in C[Y_0,...,Y_n].                                       (3)
```

No equation solved for the highest derivative and no initial-value uniqueness are assumed.

## The theorem

`[PAPER-PROVED]` Under `(1)`--`(3)`, if `n` is minimal as in Section 1, then

```text
rank_Q Gamma(f)<=2n<infinity.                              (4)
```

Moreover, the almost-periodic divisor distribution of `f` has frequency group of rational rank at
most `2n`.

If `f` is constant, the assertion is immediate. Assume henceforth that `f` is nonconstant.
Then the minimal order below satisfies `n>=1`, since a holomorphic function annihilated by a
nonzero polynomial in `Y_0` alone has connected image contained in a finite set and is constant.

## 1. Minimal autonomous equation and its separant

Among all autonomous differential polynomials annihilating `f`, choose `P` first with minimal order
`n` and then with minimal positive degree in `Y_n`. Replace it by an irreducible factor that still
annihilates `f`. Put

```text
Sep_P=partial P/partial Y_n.                               (5)
```

Then

```text
Sep_P(f,f',...,f^(n))                                      (6)
```

is not identically zero. Indeed, characteristic zero makes `(5)` a polynomial of strictly smaller
`Y_n`-degree than `P`. If `(6)` vanished identically, it would be an annihilating autonomous
differential polynomial of the same order and smaller leading degree, contradicting the choice of
`P`.

## 2. The entire hull cannot be singular

Let `X_f` be the translation hull of `f` in the compact-open substrip topology. Standard Bohr hull
theory makes `X_f` a compact connected abelian group with a dense real translation orbit; in
particular its translation flow is minimal.

Local uniform convergence of holomorphic functions implies local uniform convergence of every
derivative. Consequently every `g in X_f` satisfies

```text
P(g,g',...,g^(n))=0.                                      (7)
```

Consider

```text
Z={g in X_f: Sep_P(g,g',...,g^(n)) is identically zero}.   (8)
```

The set `Z` is closed and translation invariant. It does not contain `f` by `(6)`. Since `X_f` is
minimal, it has no nonempty proper closed invariant subset. Therefore

```text
Z=empty.                                                    (9)
```

Thus every hull member is regular at at least one real time, although no common regular time and no
globally unique initial-value formulation were assumed.

## 3. Compactness produces a finite regular-chart cover

For `t in R`, define the open subset

```text
U_t={g in X_f: Sep_P(g(t),g'(t),...,g^(n)(t))!=0}.          (10)
```

Equation `(9)` says that `(U_t)_(t in R)` covers `X_f`. Compactness supplies finitely many real
numbers

```text
t_1,...,t_m                                                (11)
```

such that

```text
X_f=U_(t_1) union ... union U_(t_m).                        (12)
```

For the qualitative finite-rank theorem it is enough to combine all samples into the observation
map

```text
J:X_f->C^(m(n+1)),
J(g)=(g^(k)(t_j))_(1<=j<=m,0<=k<=n).                       (13)
```

It is continuous. It is also injective.

To prove injectivity, suppose `J(g)=J(h)`. Choose `j` with `g in U_(t_j)`. The two full `n`-jets at
`t_j` agree, so the separant is nonzero at their common jet. The analytic implicit-function theorem
solves `(7)` near that jet as one explicit branch

```text
y^(n)=Phi(y,y',...,y^(n-1)).                               (14)
```

Both `g` and `h` remain on this branch near `t_j`, and their lower initial jets agree. Local
uniqueness for the explicit analytic equation `(14)` gives `g=h` near `t_j`. The identity theorem
then gives `g=h` throughout `S_eta`.

Since `X_f` is compact and the target of `(13)` is Hausdorff, `J` is a topological embedding. This
already proves finite covering dimension, but its bound depends on the number `m` of chosen samples.

The order-sharp local argument removes that dependence. Since `X_f` is compact Hausdorff, the finite
open cover `(12)` has a finite closed shrinking

```text
X_f=F_1 union ... union F_m,    F_j subset U_(t_j).          (15)
```

For each `j`, use only the single full-jet map

```text
E_j:F_j->C^(n+1),
E_j(g)=(g(t_j),g'(t_j),...,g^(n)(t_j)).                     (16)
```

The same implicit-function and uniqueness argument used above shows that `E_j` is injective: if
`g,h in F_j` have the same full jet, their common separant is nonzero, so they solve the same
explicit local branch with the same lower initial jet and hence agree on the strip. Compactness of
`F_j` makes `E_j` a topological embedding.

Its image lies in

```text
M_P={y in C^(n+1):P(y)=0 and Sep_P(y)!=0}.                  (17)
```

The nonzero `Y_n`-partial derivative makes `M_P` a complex `n`-manifold: locally it is the graph of
`Y_n` over `(Y_0,...,Y_(n-1))`. Therefore every subset of `M_P` has covering dimension at most
`2n`, and hence

```text
dim F_j<=2n.                                                (18)
```

The countable closed-sum theorem for covering dimension in metrizable spaces, applied to this
finite closed cover of the compact metric hull, now gives

```text
dim X_f<=2n.                                                (19)
```

The finite collection `(11)`, rather than a single initial time, is the mechanism that removes the
singular/nonunique obstruction. Passing to a closed shrinking and measuring each regular chart in
the smooth jet hypersurface is what recovers a bound depending only on the differential order.

## 4. Hull dimension equals frequency rank

The character group of the Bohr hull is the discrete additive group generated by the frequencies of
`f`. Pontryagin dimension theory for compact connected abelian groups gives

```text
rank_Q dual(X_f)=dim X_f.                                   (20)
```

Combining `(19)` and `(20)` proves `(4)`.

The theorem asserts finite rational rank, not finite generation as an abelian group. A finite-rank
hull may be a protorus rather than an ordinary torus.

## 5. Divisor-frequency law

For a nonzero holomorphic function `g`, its zero divisor is the Poincare--Lelong distribution

```text
Div(g)=(1/(2pi)) Delta log|g|.                              (21)
```

The hull of a nonzero almost-periodic function contains no zero function. Under local uniform
convergence of nonzero holomorphic functions, `(21)` converges in the distribution topology. Thus

```text
g |-> Div(g)                                                (22)
```

is a continuous translation-equivariant map from `X_f` onto the divisor hull. The latter is a
compact abelian factor of `X_f`; its dual character group embeds in `dual(X_f)`. Therefore

```text
rank_Q Gamma(Div(f))<=rank_Q Gamma(f)<=2n.                  (23)
```

Every continuous translation-equivariant divisor observable inherits the same finite-rank bound.

## Hypertranscendence consequence

Call a function autonomously DA if it satisfies an equation `(3)` with constant coefficients.
The contrapositive of `(4)` is:

> A holomorphic uniformly almost-periodic function with infinite rational Bohr-frequency rank is
> autonomously hypertranscendental.

This is weaker than hypertranscendence over `C(z)`: a nonautonomous differential equation with
explicit `z`-coefficients is not excluded. The distinction is essential and is not suppressed in
the claim.

For example, on every half-plane `Re(s)>1`, ordinary Dirichlet sources with nonzero coefficients at
infinitely many primes have frequency group containing the independent family `{log p}`. They cannot
be generated by any autonomous algebraic differential constraint, even an implicit singular one.
Ostrowski gives the stronger full-DA conclusion for ordinary Dirichlet series; the present theorem
applies to arbitrary holomorphic almost-periodic carriers and their divisors without a Dirichlet
expansion.

## RH-route consequence

The theorem closes every implementation of a Connes, Hilbert--Polya, de Branges,
Nyman--Beurling, Weil, or Li program whose proposed source generator is a fixed finite autonomous
algebraic differential state, including a singular implicit DAE used to evade ordinary ODE
uniqueness. Such a state cannot carry the infinite prime-log frequency module.

It does not exclude the complete named routes. They may use an infinite-dimensional operator, an
exact arithmetic constraint, a nonautonomous Gamma/Euler source, or the full divisor-sensitive
positivity criterion. The precise law is:

```text
infinite-rank prime spectrum
  => not any finite autonomous ADE constraint
  => no autonomous algebraic flow, even a singular one, can be the missing source producer. (24)
```

This is a constraint-side result: it proves that the source cannot be propagated by the proposed
finite flow. It does not infer zero location from hypertranscendence.

## Attacks and boundary cases

1. **Rubel universal DAEs.** Their smooth patchwork solutions need not be analytic or almost
   periodic. Analytic universal polynomial ODEs approximate arbitrary targets but, by the theorem,
   cannot have an exact infinite-rank almost-periodic orbit. There is no contradiction.
2. **Everywhere-singular branch.** Minimality of `P` prevents the original solution from lying in
   its separant. Minimality of the hull then proves `(9)` for every translate limit.
3. **No common regular time.** The finite subcover `(11)` is exactly why a common time is unnecessary.
4. **Two solutions with the same singular initial jet.** The observation map records jets at all
   finite atlas times; at least one is nonsingular for either solution, and equality of observations
   moves the comparison to that regular chart.
5. **Nonautonomous equations.** Translation no longer preserves `(3)`, so `(7)` fails. This is the
   exact open boundary, not a technical omission.
6. **Merely Besicovitch almost periodic functions.** Their translation hull need not be compact in
   the topology used above. The theorem is for uniform Bohr almost periodicity.

This is `(19)`, with the bound depending only on the differential order.  The tree's earlier

* Separation uses `carrier_eq_of_jet_eq_local` with the jets of orders `0 … n−1` only; the top jet

* Injectivity is needed only *on each closed piece*, which is what
  `exists_finite_closed_cover_subordinate` supplies, and that is exactly the hypothesis shape of
  `card_le_of_hull_charts`.  Without the closed cover the bound would carry the atlas size.

Combined with `(4)` — the identification of the hull's character group with the frequency group —
this is the card's finite-frequency law with the sharp constant.

### `(23)` — the divisor law

```text

  the same hypotheses, plus `f ≠ 0`,
  φ continuous with compact support inside the substrip
    ==>  rank_ℤ ⟨Bohr spectrum of  t ↦ ⟨Div (F(· + t·i)), φ⟩⟩ ≤ 2n
```

The divisor is the honest one: `divisorPairing f φ = ∑ᶠ a, (order of vanishing of f at a) · φ(a)`,
the Poincaré–Lelong measure of `f` tested against `φ`.  What had to be built is its *continuity*,
i.e. the classical statement that zeros move continuously under uniform convergence.  The route is
the argument principle, and it is now Lean-proved from the corpus's own factorization theory rather
than assumed:

```text

HTGeometry.zeroMult_of_factorization             the multiplicities of a factorized function are its
                                                 exponents (analyticOrderAt of a product of powers
                                                 of linear factors times a nonvanishing factor)

    ∮_{|z−c|=t} (f'/f) = 2πi · ∑ (multiplicities of the zeros inside),

                                                 function of the integrand, is locally constant
HTGeometry.exists_local_zeroSet                  around an isolated zero, a nearby function has its
                                                 zeros in the same small disc with the same total
                                                 multiplicity
HTGeometry.finite_zeros_of_isCompact             a nowhere locally trivial holomorphic function has
                                                 finitely many zeros on every compact set
HTGeometry.continuousAt_divisorPairing           ⟹ the pairing is continuous in the parameter
```

The estimate behind `exists_eps_norm_logDeriv_sub_le` is Cauchy's: on a disc of radius `(R−t)/2`
about a point of the circle, `‖h′ − f′‖ ≤ ‖h − f‖ · 2/(R−t)`, and with `‖f‖` bounded below on the
circle the quotient `h′/h − f′/f` is controlled linearly by `‖h − f‖`.

On the carrier, `CarrierDivisorObservable` supplies the two remaining facts.  No representative
vanishes identically (`not_eventually_rep_eq_zero`): it would vanish on the central line, so the
carrier evaluation would vanish on a dense orbit and hence everywhere, forcing `f = 0`.  And the
representatives converge uniformly on the whole substrip as the carrier point moves
(`continuous_divisorPairing_rep`), because they are the values of one continuous map into a sup-norm
space.  Finally `eqOn_rep_bohrIota` identifies the observable along the line with the divisor of the
vertical translates of `F`, so the observable form of the frequency law applies verbatim.

This is the card's own §5 argument, mechanised: `Div` is a continuous translation-equivariant map
from the carrier onto the divisor hull, so the divisor's character group embeds in the carrier's and
inherits the bound `2n`.  The general statement — every continuous translation-equivariant observable
of the carrier inherits the bound — is
`rank_bohrSpectrum_observable_le_of_strip_differential_equation`.
