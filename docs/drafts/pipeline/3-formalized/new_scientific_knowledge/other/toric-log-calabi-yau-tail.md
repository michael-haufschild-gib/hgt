## Setup

Let `C` be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero with trivial derivation.  Let
`X/C` be a smooth complete toric variety of dimension `r`, let `T=(G_m)^r` be its dense torus,
and let `D=X\T` be the reduced toric boundary.  Let

`alpha:T->T`

be an isogeny which extends to a finite toric endomorphism `f:X->X`.  For a
differential-generic point `x` write

`L=C<X>=C<T>`

for the differential rational function field, and define

`Tail_X(f)=intersection_(n>=0) f^(n*)L`.

Let `Gamma_n=ker(alpha^n)(C)`, let `H` be the Zariski closure of
`union_n Gamma_n`, and let `pi:T->T/H` be the quotient.

## Theorem A: toric compactification invariance

**[PROVED HERE]**

`Tail_X(f)=L^(H(C))=C<pi(x), ell_delta(x)>`.                       (1)

Here `ell_delta(x)` is the torus logarithmic derivative.  In character coordinates
`chi_1,...,chi_r`,

`ell_delta(x)=(delta chi_1(x)/chi_1(x),...,
               delta chi_r(x)/chi_r(x)).`

### Proof

The dense open immersion `T subset X` is birational, so `C(X)=C(T)`.  A
differential-generic point avoids every proper closed subset, and adjoining all derivatives of
either rational function field therefore gives the same differential field `L`.  The pullback
`f*` on `C(X)` is the pullback `alpha*` on `C(T)`, hence this remains true on every differential
iterate and

`Tail_X(f)=Tail_T(alpha)`.                                        (2)

The differential deck-closure theorem for constant connected commutative algebraic groups gives

`Tail_T(alpha)=L^(H(C))=C<pi(x),ell_delta(x)>`,                    (3)

which proves (1).  Notice that no morphism from the complete variety `X` to a semiabelian variety
is required.  The group geometry lives on the logarithmic open stratum `X\D`.

## Theorem B: pure multiplication is position-to-log-current transmutation

Let `alpha=[m]` with integer `m>1`.  Then the iterated torsion kernels are Zariski dense in `T`,
so `H=T`, and

`Tail_X([m])=C<u_1,...,u_r>`,                                    (4)

where

`u_i=delta chi_i(x)/chi_i(x)`.

Moreover

`Tail_X([m]) intersection C(X)=C`,                               (5)

`dtrdeg_C Tail_X([m])=r=dtrdeg_C L`,                              (6)

and

`[m]*u_i=m u_i`.                                                  (7)

### Proof

Every proper algebraic subgroup of a torus is cut out by a nonzero character relation.  The
union of all `m^n`-torsion points satisfies no such relation, so it is Zariski dense.  Equations
(4)--(6) are the pure-expanding case of (1).  Equation (7) is the logarithmic derivative of
`chi_i(x)^m`.

Thus compactification does not add a hidden persistent position sector.  It changes where the
current is geometrically represented: the current is a rational logarithmic one-form with poles
along `D`.

## Theorem C: character-lattice linearization

Let the isogeny be represented on characters by an integral matrix `A=(a_ij)`, so

`alpha*chi_i=product_j chi_j^(a_ij)`.

Then its logarithmic currents obey the exact matrix law

`alpha*u_i=sum_j a_ij u_j`.                                      (8)

### Proof

Leibniz and the inverse rule give

`delta(product_j chi_j^(a_ij)) / product_j chi_j^(a_ij)
  = sum_j a_ij delta(chi_j)/chi_j`.

## Log Calabi--Yau interpretation

For a smooth complete toric variety, `D` is a simple normal-crossings divisor,

`K_X+D=0`,

and `Omega^1_X(log D)` is the trivial bundle generated by the character forms
`dchi_i/chi_i`.  Equations (4)--(8) therefore identify the stable differential tail with the
differential field of the flat log-cotangent frame of the pair `(X,D)`.

This is a precise boundary statement rather than a physical analogy: infinite algebraic
coarse-graining erases bulk position but preserves the complete differential rank as logarithmic
boundary current.  If one uses physical language, the correct object is a static holographic
surface algebra on a log Calabi--Yau boundary pair, not a quantum time evolution.

## Counterexample and repaired classification boundary

This theorem kills the narrow conjecture that every persistent tail on a projective variety must
come from a projective semiabelian quotient, a one-dimensional parabolic orbifold, or an
automorphic factor.  A projective toric variety generally has no nonconstant morphism to a
semiabelian variety, yet its power endomorphism has the rank-`r` tail (4).

The missing object is the logarithmic pair.  Higher-dimensional toroidal or log-parabolic strata
must be admitted as primitive tail sources.  The new open classification problem is whether every
nontrivial expanding tail, after quasi-etale and birational modifications, comes from:

1. a Manin/semiabelian quotient;
2. a log-parallel or log-Calabi--Yau factor carrying a flat logarithmic tensor;
3. a reversible automorphic factor.

One-dimensional parabolic orbifolds become the rank-one finite-holonomy members of item 2.

The geometric structure of polarized log-Calabi--Yau pairs is not new here:
Moraga--Yanez--Yeong prove that, after the required modification and finite cover, such a pair is
a toric log-Calabi--Yau fibration over an abelian variety
(<https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18092>).  What remains specific to the tail program is deriving an
effective log-Calabi--Yau boundary from persistent differential observables rather than assuming
one.

## Novelty and significance gate

The toric facts `K_X+D=0`, the trivial logarithmic cotangent frame, and the toric/abelian structure
of polarized log-Calabi--Yau pairs are classical or supplied by the cited literature.  The field
calculation is a direct application of the new isogeny deck-closure theorem.  The exact
stable-tail identification (1), its compactification-invariance reading, and the use of that
calculation to falsify the projective-factor tail converse were not found in the audited tail
literature.  They are marked new knowledge provisionally.

This result is significant as a falsifying and repairing theorem for the candidate converse, but
it does **not** clear the Fields-Medal gate.  The medal-level target is now the repaired converse:
prove that log-parallel geometry is not merely sufficient but exhaustive.

## RH impact audit

There is no direct RH consequence.  The result does give a sharper diagnostic: a proposed
algebraic RH tail need not descend through a projective group quotient; it may live on a
logarithmic open stratum and should then expose an integral character lattice with a flat
`dchi/chi` frame.  No such toroidal factor has been proved for the completed-zeta source, so the
theorem neither advances a zero-free region nor supplies positivity.  It prevents an incorrect
no-go argument based only on the absence of projective semiabelian quotients.
