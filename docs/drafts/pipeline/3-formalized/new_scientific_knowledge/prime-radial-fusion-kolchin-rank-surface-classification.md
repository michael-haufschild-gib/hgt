## Master law

Let `P = t C[[t]]`.  A profile `u in P` defines the prime-radial arithmetic source `a_u` by

```text
a_u(p^n) = [t^n]u,
a_u(m) = 0 away from positive prime powers.
```

Let `E_fin` be the intrinsic field of arithmetic-function fractions detected by only finitely
many prime-coordinate derivations, and let

```text
K(W) = E_fin<a_u : u in W>_D
```

for a complex profile subspace `W <= P`.

The complete theorem is:

1. **Prime--Euler stability.**  For every prime `q`,

   ```text
   [partial_q,D] = i log(q) partial_q,
   activePrimes(Dx) <= activePrimes(x).
   ```

   Hence `E_fin` is an actual differential subfield.

2. **Exact source--field recovery.**  For arbitrary subspaces `U,W <= P` and `u in P`,

   ```text
   a_u in K(W)  iff  u in W,
   K(U) <= K(W) iff  U <= W,
   K(U) = K(W)  iff  U = W.
   ```

3. **Exact fusion classifier.**  Same-prime Cauchy fusion obeys

   ```text
   a_u boxdot a_v = a_(uv).
   ```

   Thus `K(W)` is closed under radial-carrier fusion iff `W` is multiplicatively closed, and
   nonunital subalgebras of `P` order-embed into embedded fusion fields.

4. **Complete-tail channel law.**  Let `pi_N` retain coefficients `1,...,N`, let
   `W_>N = W intersect ker(pi_N)`, and lift a basis of `pi_N(W)` to profiles `b_j in W`.  Then
   their actual carriers are jointly differentially independent over the field generated by the
   entire invisible tail `W_>N`.  Every channel-by-jet rectangle through order `r` is algebraically
   independent over that tail field.

5. **Exact order census and rank surface.**  With

   ```text
   Omega(W) = {ord_t(u) : 0 != u in W},
   H_W(N,r) = (r+1) dim_C pi_N(W),
   ```

   one has

   ```text
   dim_C pi_N(W) = # (Omega(W) intersect [1,N]),
   H_W(N,r) = (r+1) # (Omega(W) intersect [1,N]).
   ```

6. **Fusion order law.**  For a profile algebra `B`,

   ```text
   Gamma(B) = {0} union Omega(B)
   ```

   is an additive submonoid of `N`.  Its membership is exactly the discrete cutoff derivative of
   the rank surface: the jump at `N+1` is one entire column of height `r+1` iff
   `N+1 in Gamma(B)`.

7. **Conductor law.**  The additive-submonoid conductor theorem gives a cutoff `c` and modulus
   `d = gcd Gamma(B)` such that beyond `c` the rank jump is exactly `r+1` at multiples of `d` and
   zero at all other cutoffs.

8. **Inverse completeness.**  For every additive submonoid `S <= N`, the support algebra

   ```text
   B_S = {u in t C[[t]] : support(u) <= S}
   ```

   satisfies `Gamma(B_S)=S`.  Moreover,

   ```text
   H_B = H_C  iff  Gamma(B) = Gamma(C),
   K(B) = K(C) iff  B = C.
   ```

   Therefore equality classes of complete prime-radial fusion--Kolchin rank surfaces are exactly
   additive submonoids of `N`, while embedded fields retain the strictly finer full profile
