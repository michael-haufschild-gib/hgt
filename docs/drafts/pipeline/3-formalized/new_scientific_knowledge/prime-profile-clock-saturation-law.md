## Theorem

Let

```text
Q = complex prime-power profiles / finite-prime-ray profiles
```

and let `T : Q -> Q` be multiplication by the descended rotated logarithmic prime clock. For every
complex subspace `W <= Q`, let

```text
Sat_T(W) = intersection {U : W <= U and T(U) <= U}.
```

Let `c_W` be any positive-prime-power source family whose clock classes form a Hamel basis of
`W`. Then:

1. `Sat_T` is the closure operator onto the `T`-invariant Grassmannian: it is extensive,
   monotone, idempotent, and `Sat_T(W)=W` exactly when `W` is `T`-invariant.
2. The complete clock module generated by `c_W` is exactly `Sat_T(W)`.
3. If `D(W)` is the differential field generated by the carriers of `c_W`, and `Phi(U)` is the
   ordinary Grassmann field of a source subspace, then

   ```text
   D(W) = Phi(Sat_T(W)).
   ```

4. Consequently, for arbitrary source subspaces `U,W`,

   ```text
   D(U) <= D(W)  iff  Sat_T(U) <= Sat_T(W),
   D(U)  = D(W)  iff  Sat_T(U)  = Sat_T(W).
   ```

Thus differentiation introduces no unidentified analytic closure: on source geometry it is
exactly the least invariant closure under one explicit linear clock.

## Complete proof

The intersection defining `Sat_T(W)` contains `W`, because every subspace in the defining family
does. It is `T`-invariant, because an element of the intersection lies in every invariant member,
and applying `T` keeps it in every such member. Hence `Sat_T(W)` is the least invariant subspace
containing `W`. Extensivity and minimality give monotonicity and idempotence; the fixed-point law is
immediate.

Let `c_W` be the represented Hamel basis of `W`. Every clock jet of a basis class lies in
`Sat_T(W)` by invariance, so the full clock module of `c_W` is contained in `Sat_T(W)`. Conversely,
that clock module is invariant and contains every order-zero basis class, hence contains `W` and,
by minimality, contains `Sat_T(W)`. This proves equality.

The intrinsic prime-clock completion correspondence says that two positive-prime-power source
families generate the same differential field exactly when their complete clock modules agree.
Apply this to `c_W` and to a represented basis of `Sat_T(W)`. Their clock modules are both
`Sat_T(W)`. Since the latter subspace is invariant, its ordinary represented basis field is already
differentially closed. Therefore

```text
D(W) = Phi(Sat_T(W)).
```

The field order and equality laws now follow from the exact Grassmann order representation of
`Phi`. `QED`

## Why this is stronger than the existing invariant theorem

The previous theorem classified a subspace after invariance had already been assumed. The present
law starts from an arbitrary source subspace and calculates its differential closure exactly. It
turns the invariant Grassmannian from a selected locus into the fixed-point lattice of a canonical
closure operator, and classifies when different non-invariant presentations collapse to the same
differential field.

## Novelty boundary

Closure under a linear operator and invariant-subspace lattices are classical. Differential
adjunction and Kolchin closure are classical. The candidate new content is the exact arithmetic
coupling: for positive-prime-power Dirichlet carriers modulo finite prime rays, differential-field
generation is neither larger nor smaller than source clock saturation, and embedded field order
and equality are completely classified by the saturated subspaces. A specialist literature audit
is required before asserting priority.

## RH pressure test

Any Euler-side construction obtained from a source sector by finitely many differential-field
operations can see only its prime-clock saturation. A proposed RH mechanism that distinguishes two
sources with the same saturation cannot be encoded by this differential carrier field; it must use
extra analytic structure such as archimedean completion, reflection, positivity, or an infinite
limit. This is a route constraint, not a zero theorem.
