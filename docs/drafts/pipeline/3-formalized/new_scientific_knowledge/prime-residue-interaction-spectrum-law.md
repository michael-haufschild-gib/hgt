## Master theorem

Let `I` be a finite index set and let `A_i` be finite pointed sets.  On each one-factor function
space choose the identity-anchored basis

```text
b_1 = 1,
b_a = delta_a  for a != 1.
```

Their finite tensor products form a basis of the full joint observable space

```text
C^(product_i A_i).
```

The support of a basis vector is the set of coordinates whose local index is not the identity.
Define

```text
L_k = span{b_x : |support(x)| <= k},
H_k = span{b_x : |support(x)| = k}.
```

For every `k>0`, the source-space interaction law is

```text
L_k = L_(k-1) direct_sum H_k,
L_|I| = C^(product_i A_i),

dim H_k
  = sum_(S subset I, |S|=k) product_(i in S)(|A_i|-1).
```

The filtration is intrinsic even though the displayed complement is anchored.  If `I_S` denotes
the full space of observables depending only on coordinates in `S`, then

```text
L_k = join_(|S|<=k) I_S.
```

Consequently `L_k` is independent of all local basepoints and complements.  More strongly, every
tensor-product basis whose distinguished local vector is the constant function produces exactly
the same filtration.  After the classical finite Fourier reindexing, the filtration by support of
nontrivial tensor characters is therefore this same intrinsic tower; anchored differences and
characters are coordinate systems on one associated-graded geometry.

The tower is also multiplicative:

```text
L_k L_l <= L_(k+l).
```

Interaction support may fuse by union under nonlinear multiplication, but it cannot create a
coordinate absent from both inputs.

The formula is not merely a count.  Let `y` be any tail-complete prime source on the product, let
`F(U)` denote the embedded prime differential field generated by all observables in `U`, and put

```text
K_k = F(L_k).
```

Then a basis `h_1,...,h_r` of `H_k` satisfies

```text

  are differentially independent over K_(k-1),

K_(k-1)<carrier(h_1),...,carrier(h_r)>_diff = K_k,

r = sum_(|S|=k) product_(i in S)(|A_i|-1).
```

If `r>0`, then

```text

```

Thus interaction order is an exact relative-HT grading: no positive grade can be generated,
compressed, or simulated by all lower grades together.

   decomposition.  The load-bearing clauses are relative differential independence over the

2. **Dirichlet-independence attack.**  Known independence of `L`-functions does not classify an
   exhaustive Boolean tower of prime-residue observable fields or give the exact relative base at
   every support order.
3. **Rank-formula attack.**  The elementary-symmetric count alone is elementary.  Here each counted
   direction is realized as a necessary relative-HT generator and the generated field is proved to
   be exactly the next filtration stage.
4. **Artificial-source attack.**  The capstone uses the actual prime residue profile.  Tail
   completeness is discharged by finite CRT plus Dirichlet recurrence, not by a universal repeated
   profile.
5. **Pairwise-wrapper attack.**  The theorem is not an iteration of arbitrary binary complements.
   One tensor basis gives a symmetric support classification for every subset and every order, and
   the exact rank is proved globally.
6. **Coordinate-choice attack.**  The anchored basis is not the invariant.  It is proved to equal
   the intrinsic join of all at-most-`k` coordinate-observable spaces, and every
   constant-distinguished tensor basis yields the same tower.
7. **Nonlinear-collapse attack.**  The filtration is a filtered algebra.  Multiplication can fuse
   supports only by union and cannot bypass interaction order.
8. **Analytic-overclaim attack.**  The current carrier is the formal prime-supported differential
   carrier.  No analytic Dirichlet-germ, completed `L`-function, or zeta-zero theorem is claimed.
