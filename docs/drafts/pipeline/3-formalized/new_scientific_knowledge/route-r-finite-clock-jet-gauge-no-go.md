## Exact result

Let `D = diag(lambda_i)` be the finite prime-depth log clock, let `P_a` be actual reflected
joint-depth incidence at horizontal displacement `a`, and let

```text
G_a = diag(exp(a lambda_i)).
```

Existing source factorization gives `P_a = G_a^(-1) P_0 G_a`, while diagonality gives
`D = G_a^(-1) D G_a`.  For every finite noncommutative expression `E` generated from `D` and `P`
using addition, multiplication, and complex scalar multiplication, structural induction therefore
gives

```text
E(D,P_a) = G_a^(-1) E(D,P_0) G_a.
```

Consequently, for every finite packet and every `m : Nat`,

```text
charpoly(E(D,P_a)) = charpoly(E(D,P_0)),
det(E(D,P_a))      = det(E(D,P_0)),
trace(E(D,P_a)^m)  = trace(E(D,P_0)^m).
```

The syntax contains `[D,P]` and every iterated clock commutator.  Thus arbitrarily high finite
clock jets may retain off-diagonal prime/depth labels, but every similarity-invariant statistic of
those jets remains independent of horizontal divisor displacement.

## No-go closure

- **Exact killing theorem/mechanism:** `D` and `P_a` undergo simultaneous diagonal conjugation, and the full finite clock/incidence expression algebra is closed under this action.  Increasing packet size, polynomial degree, or commutator depth never breaks the gauge orbit.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** finite clock jets carry a complete gauge-covariant source algebra, including literal iterated clock commutators.  Any Route-R transverse selector must leave that algebra: couple divisor provenance before the vertex gauge, introduce an independent Euler--Gamma incidence not transformed by the same gauge, or use a genuinely singular/infinite source operation for which finite similarity closure is inapplicable.
- **New insight:** source rank at least two is not enough.  Even unbounded finite commutator depth is blind when all generators share the same vertex gauge.  Required new datum is gauge-incompatible incidence or singular infinite-level structure, not another finite rank upgrade.
- **Status:** paper proof dependency-closed and formalized unconditionally.  This theorem does not prove RH and does not supply the still-missing transverse arithmetic annihilator.
