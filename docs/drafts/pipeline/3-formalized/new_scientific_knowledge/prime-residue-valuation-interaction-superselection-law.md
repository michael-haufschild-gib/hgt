- **Statement:** A tail-complete finite product residue source generates a strict infinite tower
  of differential fields. At every depth the complete next residue layer is relatively
  hypertranscendental over every carrier at every previous depth, with exact rank equal to the

## Master object

Let `I` be finite, let `A_i` be finite pointed sets, and let

```text
y : primes -> product_i A_i
```

be tail-complete: every cofinite prime tail is dense in the finite product. Let `b_z` be the
anchored tensor basis of all functions on the product, indexed by `z in product_i A_i`, and write

```text
supp(z) = {i : z_i != 1}.
```

For a positive valuation window of depth `d`, define the carrier grid

```text
C_(r,z) = prime-power carrier in the p^(r+1) layer
          with coefficient b_z(y(p)),

0 <= r < d.
```

The two indices have different meanings:

```text
r       = prime-power valuation depth,
supp(z) = independent residue-interaction breadth.
```

## Rectangular differential spectrum

For every `d>0`, the complete family

```text
{C_(r,z) : 0 <= r < d, z in product_i A_i}
```

is jointly differentially independent over the intrinsic finite-prime rational coefficient
field.

This is stronger than independence of the rows or columns separately. For every predicate `P` on
the interaction labels, the selected sector

```text
{C_(r,z) : P(z)}
```

is differentially independent over the differential field generated by its complete complement

```text
{C_(r,z) : not P(z)}.
```

Thus every union of cells is an exact relative-HT sector. No unselected cell can be manufactured
from all selected cells by rational operations, derivation, or any finite combination of them.

### Exact support sectors

For `S subset I`, the exact-support sector has relative rank

```text
rank_HT(S; d) = d * product_(i in S) (|A_i|-1).
```

It is relatively HT over the field generated by every carrier whose support is not exactly `S`.
This includes every other valuation depth and every lower, equal-cardinality, or higher interaction
support.

### Exact interaction orders

The complete order-`k` sector is relatively HT over every other interaction order and has rank

```text
rank_HT(k; d)
  = d * sum_(S subset I, |S|=k) product_(i in S)(|A_i|-1).
```

Hence the one-axis interaction tower is the cumulative shadow of a stronger rectangular
superselection spectrum.

## Polynomial breadth conservation

Let `I_S` be the observables depending only on coordinates in `S`. If every input observable of a
multivariate polynomial lies in `I_S`, then its evaluation also lies in `I_S`, regardless of the
polynomial degree:

```text
f_j in I_S for every j
  => Q(f_1,...,f_n) in I_S for every polynomial Q.
```

Thus unbounded nonlinear degree on a fixed residue support cannot buy one new residue coordinate.
Valuation depth and interaction breadth are independent resources, not two presentations of the
same complexity.

## Fusion geometry

For observables `f in I_S` and `g in I_T`, prime-local valuation fusion satisfies

```text
V_e(f) star V_r(g) = V_(e+r+1)(f g),
f g in I_(S union T).
```

The valuation coordinate adds, while the interaction coordinate unions.

For anchored pure basis vectors on disjoint supports, the result is exact with no lower-support
leakage. Define the pointed union by taking the nonidentity entry from either factor. Then

```text
supp(z) disjoint supp(w)
  => b_z b_w = b_(z union w)
  => V_e(b_z) star V_r(b_w) = V_(e+r+1)(b_(z union w)).
```

For overlapping supports,

```text
|S union T| < |S| + |T|,
```

and the product lies in interaction filtration degree `|S union T|`. Therefore every overlap
vanishes from the principal product of degree `|S|+|T|`. This is the hard-core collision law of
the associated graded interaction algebra.

## Characteristic HT law: external fusion, internal non-generation

Take disjoint pure sectors `z,w` in valuation layers `e,r`, and suppose the finite window contains
the output layer `e+r+1`. Put

```text
input_1 = C_(e,z),
input_2 = C_(r,w),
output  = C_(e+r+1,z union w).
```

Then simultaneously

```text
V_e(b_z) star V_r(b_w) = V_(e+r+1)(b_(z union w)),

output notin DifferentialField<input_1,input_2>.
```

The first identity says fusion is exact. The second says no differential-rational formula in the
input carriers realizes it internally. The coefficient algebra knows how to fuse, but the
differential field must acquire the result as a genuinely new HT coordinate.

> **External-fusion law.** In a tail-complete prime-supported HT geometry, exact nonlinear source
> fusion is a creation operation, not an internal differential-field operation. Its output is
> externally prescribed and internally hypertranscendental over its inputs.

The pairwise statement is only the first shadow of the stronger law below.

## Master theorem: the strict source-creation tower

Let `K_n` be the differential field generated by **every** carrier in every residue sector at
valuation depths `0,...,n`. The next layer is not merely absent cellwise. Its complete residue
family is differentially independent over `K_n`, with exact relative rank

```text
rank_HT(layer n+1 over K_n) = product_i |A_i|.
```

Consequently

```text
K_0 proper-subfield K_1 proper-subfield K_2 proper-subfield ...
```

is a strict infinite tower. Now take disjoint residue labels `z,w`. The depth-`n` carrier of `z`
and the depth-zero carrier of `w` both belong to `K_n`, while

```text
V_n(b_z) star V_0(b_w) = V_(n+1)(b_(z union w)),

C_(n+1,z union w) notin K_n.
```

This proves finite-prefix nonclosure in its strongest finite form: the output remains new even
after the input field is enlarged from the two chosen carriers to **all** residue interactions at
**all** earlier depths.

> **Strict source-creation law.** A tail-complete prime source can obey a finitely described exact
> coefficient-fusion rule while its valuation lift has no finite differential-field closure under
> that rule. Each application at the depth frontier creates a complete relatively HT layer.

The coefficient algebra and differential field therefore have provably different generation
complexities. This is the load-bearing theorem. The rectangular spectrum is its horizontal state
geometry; pairwise externality is its smallest local witness.

### Unit-source complexity explosion

After prime-valuation lifting, repeated fusion advances through every valuation layer, but every

## Actual prime-residue realization

Let `q_i` be pairwise coprime positive moduli and

```text
A_i = (Z/q_i Z)^x,
y(p) = (p mod q_i)_i,
```

assigning the identity at the finitely many nonunit primes. Finite Chinese remainder theory and
Dirichlet recurrence make `y` tail-complete. Therefore

```text
rank_HT(S; d) = d * product_(i in S)(phi(q_i)-1),

rank_HT(k; d)
  = d * sum_(|S|=k) product_(i in S)(phi(q_i)-1),
```

and both sectors are relatively HT over their complete complements. This is an arithmetic theorem
about the actual simultaneous residue profile of rational primes, not an artificial repeated
source.

## Analytic realization

The complete source-creation law survives faithful realization as actual Dirichlet-series germs
in the meromorphic differential field at the complex upper end. If `K_n^an` is generated by every
analytic valuation--residue carrier through depth `n`, then

```text
K_n^an proper-subfield K_(n+1)^an,

rank_HT(layer n+1 over K_n^an) = product_i |A_i|.
```

Exact source fusion from two analytic prefix carriers lands in a next-layer analytic germ outside
`K_n^an`. For actual pairwise-coprime moduli the exact frontier rank is
`product_i phi(q_i)`. Thus external creation and internal non-generation are properties of actual
meromorphic germs, not artifacts of the arithmetic-function ambient field.

The analytic theorem is presently over complex constants. Relative independence over the larger
rational coordinate field `C(s)` remains open.

## Anti-theatre audit

1. **ANOVA attack.** Deleting every prime-valuation and differential-field clause leaves a
   classical support decomposition. The master theorem disappears.
2. **Generic-independence attack.** Joint independence alone gives sector separation, but not the
   actual-prime totient spectrum, exact fusion transport, overlap degree loss, or zeta vacuum
   identification.
3. **Fusion-is-just-multiplication attack.** Source fusion is coefficient multiplication, but the
   theorem proves its output is not in the differential field generated by the inputs. This is the
   precise nontrivial incompatibility.
4. **Rank-packaging attack.** The rank formulas are shadows. The load-bearing clause is relative HT
   of the entire next layer over the complete earlier prefix, followed by exact fusion escape from
   that prefix.
5. **Artificial-source attack.** The arithmetic capstones use actual rational primes and discharge
   tail completeness by CRT plus Dirichlet recurrence.
6. **Zeta-rhetoric attack.** Zeta appears only through an exact carrier equality with the vacuum
   spine. No analytic zeta or zero statement is inferred.
7. **Physics-vocabulary attack.** “Superselection,” “hard-core,” and “creation” can be deleted
   without changing a theorem. The literal mathematical content is relative differential
   independence and a field non-membership theorem.
8. **Hadamard-nonclosure attack.** Classical examples show that differentially algebraic classes
   can fail closure under coefficientwise products. Here the inputs and outputs form an explicit
   prime-supported HT family, every frontier has exact relative rank, the obstruction is relative
   to the complete earlier field rather than membership in a broad function class, and the fusion
   output is identified exactly. Removing those clauses destroys the theorem.
9. **Formal-carrier attack.** The strict tower, exact frontier ranks, and fusion escape have been
   transferred to actual meromorphic Dirichlet germs. The phenomenon is not created by the formal
   rational-coordinate ambient field.

## RH pressure test

The theorem does not prove RH and does not provide a zero selector. It forces a positive
architectural conclusion:

> An RH route based only on finite prime-power Euler algebra must supply a genuinely completed
> operation. Every finite complete analytic valuation field before completion has a rigorously
> identified next-layer
> defect: its own exact coefficient fusion exits the field into a relatively HT layer. No amount
> of differential-rational manipulation inside finitely many Euler valuation carriers can close
> this defect, and the zeta Euler source occupies only the empty residue-support spine.

Therefore an RH mechanism must introduce structure not generated by finite Euler fusion itself.
The natural candidates are the completed functional-equation reflection, its archimedean gamma
coupling, positivity tied to that reflection, or a genuinely infinite analytic completion. Adding
prime-power depth alone cannot substitute for residue breadth; adding either finite resource cannot
produce the missing global analytic selector internally.
