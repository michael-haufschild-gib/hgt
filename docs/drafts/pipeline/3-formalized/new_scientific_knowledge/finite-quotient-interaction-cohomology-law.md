## Master theorem

Let `W` be a nonempty discrete space, let `y : Primes -> W` be tail-complete, and let `F(U)` be the
embedded differential field generated by the prime carriers of every observable in a finite
dimensional subspace

```text
U <= C^W.
```

Then the source-space construction is an exact lattice embedding:

```text
F(U + V) = F(U) F(V),
F(U ∩ V) = F(U) ∩ F(V).
```

The first equality is unconditional for arbitrary observable subspaces; the second uses finite
dimension and tail completeness.  Equality of fields is equivalent to equality of source
subspaces.

Now let `r` and `s` be finite-index equivalence relations on `W`.  Write

```text
I_r = {observables constant on r-classes},
K_r = F(I_r).
```

The exact interaction laws are

```text
K_r ∩ K_s = K_(r ∨ s),

K_r K_s = F(I_r + I_s) <= K_(r ∧ s).
```

Define the canonical interaction cohomology

```text
H¹(r,s) = I_(r ∧ s) / (I_r + I_s).
```

Its dimension is

```text
δ(r,s)
  = |W/(r ∧ s)| - |W/r| - |W/s| + |W/(r ∨ s)|.
```

The subtraction is nonnegative because it is a quotient-space dimension.  The marginal fields
generate the full common-refinement field exactly when this cohomology vanishes:

```text
K_r K_s = K_(r ∧ s)  iff  H¹(r,s) = 0  iff  δ(r,s) = 0.
```

Finally, there is a complement `Q` of `I_r + I_s` in `I_(r ∧ s)` such that

```text
Q ≅ H¹(r,s),
dim Q = δ(r,s).
```

For any basis `q_1,...,q_δ` of `Q`, the corresponding prime carriers are differentially
independent over `K_r K_s`, and

```text
(K_r K_s) <carrier(q_1),...,carrier(q_δ)>_diff = K_(r ∧ s).
```

Thus the interaction cohomology is not merely a dimension count.  It is realized exactly as the
minimal relative hypertranscendental layer missing from the two marginal fields.

## Incidence-graph interpretation

The classes of `r` and `s` form the two vertex parts of a bipartite graph; a nonempty joint class is
an edge.  Its edge-function space is `I_(r ∧ s)`, and row-plus-column potentials form `I_r + I_s`.
Consequently the classical first cochain quotient of this incidence graph is the interaction
cohomology above.  The four quotient counts are respectively edges, left vertices, right vertices,
and connected components, so the displayed formula is the graph Euler number `E - V + c`.

This graph interpretation is classical.  The new claim is the exact realization of that quotient
as the relative differential-transcendence layer between embedded prime fields.

## Anti-theatre attack

1. **Euler-formula attack.** The dimension formula alone is elementary graph linear algebra.  The
   load-bearing result is that a basis of the quotient becomes relatively differentially
   independent over the marginal compositum and generates exactly the joint field.
2. **ANOVA attack.** Row/column effects and interaction terms are classical.  ANOVA does not supply
   an embedded differential-field meet/compositum theorem or a hypertranscendental realization of
   the interaction quotient.
3. **Gács--Körner attack.** Connected components explain the common deterministic factor, matching
   the exact field-intersection law.  They do not identify the cycle quotient as a relative
   differential-transcendence extension.
4. **Pure differential-algebra attack.** A differential transcendence basis can make the lattice
   identities formally plausible.  The theorem additionally realizes every finite quotient
   geometry through one prime-supported carrier construction and identifies the precise source
   quotient controlling the relative extension.
5. **Wrapper attack.** Removing the prime carrier theorem leaves classical finite-dimensional
   cohomology.  Removing the cohomology leaves the earlier field anti-embedding.  Neither half
   implies the exact relative-HT realization by itself; their equivalence is the new theorem.
6. **Canonicality attack.** The complement `Q` is choice-dependent, but the quotient `H¹(r,s)` is
   canonical.  Every chosen complement is formally linearly equivalent to it, and its field effect
   is invariant.

## RH pressure test

This theorem does not prove an RH criterion.  It gives a new necessary architecture for any route
that tries to assemble the zeta field from finitely coarse prime observables:

> Marginal prime quotients can recover a joint refinement without new input only when their
> incidence interaction cohomology vanishes.  When it does not vanish, every successful route must
> supply exactly the missing cohomology classes as genuinely new relative HT directions.

Therefore pairwise or separately symmetric prime statistics are structurally insufficient when
their quotient-incidence graph has cycles.  A positive RH route of this kind must exhibit an
explicit mechanism--for example reflection, order, signed archimedean coupling, or positivity--that
selects and controls the required interaction classes.  This is stronger than saying the marginal
route fails: it specifies the exact kind and number of new directions a successful refinement must
contain.

The present theorem remains finite-quotient and formal-Dirichlet.  An RH-significant next theorem
must identify a natural classical zeta observable whose missing interaction cohomology is forced to
vanish or whose cohomology basis is controlled by the functional equation and positivity.
