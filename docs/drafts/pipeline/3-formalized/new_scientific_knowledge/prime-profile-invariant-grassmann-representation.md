## Exact conjecture

Let

```text
Q = complex prime-power profiles / finite-prime-ray profiles
```

and let `T` be multiplication by the rotated logarithmic prime clock.  For every complex subspace
`W <= Q`, choose any Hamel basis, represent its vectors by positive-prime-power sources, and let
`Phi(W)` be the ordinary embedded field generated by their carriers over the intrinsic finite-prime
field.

**[CONJECTURE: invariant Grassmann representation]**

1. `Phi(W)` is independent of all basis and representative choices.
2. For arbitrary subspaces `U,W <= Q`,

   ```text
   Phi(U) <= Phi(W)  iff  U <= W,
   Phi(U ∩ W) = Phi(U) ∩ Phi(W),
   Phi(U + W) = compositum(Phi(U), Phi(W)).
   ```

3. Carrier pullback recovers `W` exactly.
4. A subspace `W` is `T`-invariant iff its basis field `Phi(W)` is the differential field generated
   by its represented basis.  Every `T`-invariant subspace is realized as the complete clock module
   of a positive-prime-power presentation, and every source-generated complete clock module is
   `T`-invariant.
5. Hence the source-generated embedded differential fields are precisely the image of the
   `T`-invariant Grassmannian.  On this locus, field order, intersection, and compositum are exactly
   invariant-subspace order, intersection, and sum.
6. For a source class `q`, differential algebraicity over `Phi(W)` is exactly polynomial-clock
   saturation: some nonzero `A in C[X]` sends `q` into `W`.

The intended paper narrative is therefore one theorem:

```text
prime-tail carrier fields represent the Grassmannian of Q;
differentiation cuts out its invariant-subspace geometry;
Kolchin closure becomes polynomial saturation there.
```

The Euler completion barrier is an application to the replicated valuation-profile sector, not a
separate main theorem.

## Hostile checks

- **Choice dependence:** neutralized by exact source-span field recovery; any two bases span the same
  subspace and therefore generate the same embedded field.
- **Meet failure:** requires the already-proved three-block rational-field intersection theorem.
  Decompose `U` and `W` into their common intersection and two disjoint complements; the static tail
  theorem makes the three carrier blocks jointly algebraically independent.
- **False essential surjectivity:** avoided by restricting the differential statement to
  `T`-invariant subspaces.  The represented basis presentation contains every basis vector at clock
  order zero, while invariance keeps every higher jet inside the subspace.
- **Parent-theory theatre:** ordinary basis fields and rational-function intersections are not the
  claimed novelty.  The candidate contribution is their exact arithmetic realization on the
  prime-tail quotient together with identification of the differential locus and saturation law.
- **Literature boundary:** Ostrowski, Shapiro--Sparer, Amou--Katsurada, Pong, and Lu--Jiang--Lu give
  independence or hypertranscendence results.  The checked sources have not yielded this
  arbitrary-subspace meet/join representation or its clock-invariant differential restriction.
  Status remains `NOVELTY UNVERIFIED` until a broader referee-style search is completed.
