# Simultaneous small sections on conductor thickenings

**Theorem.** On a fixed conductor window starting at $N=\lfloor\sqrt m\rfloor$, prescribe 1 on any set of graph thickenings and 0 on the others. One nonzero rational function $F(T,Y)/D(T)$ realizes all these jets. Uniformly over polynomial graphs $Y=y(T)$ in the closed unit bidisk, its mean positive boundary log modulus plus $\log\operatorname{content}(D)$ is $o(m^2)$. The order of vanishing covers both rounded multiplicities in the signed spline construction. Equivalently, each integer ray has a section within every positive quadratic height allowance at all sufficiently large dilations.

**Proof.** Write $g_i=G_i(T)$. In $(g_i-g_j)^{2h}$ expand $((Y-g_j)+(g_i-Y))^{2h}$. Terms with at least $h$ factors of $Y-g_j$ vanish to order $h$ on graph $j$. The complementary terms vanish to order $h$ on graph $i$. Multiplying these selectors and clearing the remaining row factors gives a polynomial equal to $D$ on the selected graph and zero on all other thickenings, for the same nonzero $D=\prod_{i\ne j}(g_i-g_j)^{2h}$ at every graph. Linear combinations lift arbitrary integral polynomial data. A further marked branch outside the prescribed window gives $F(T,g_i)=D(T)\ne0$, even when every requested jet vanishes.

For $i\le BN$, the divisor sum gives $|g_i(T)|\le i^2$ on the unit disk. The two sampled multiplicities are bounded by $h=1+\lceil Cm/\sqrt N\rceil$. The binomial theorem bounds each selector on the unit bidisk, so both $F$ for bounded data and $D$ have log norm $O_{B,C}((1+hN^2)\log(2+N))=o(m^2)$. If $L$ is the common log bound, then on the unit circle
$\log^+|F/D|\le L-\log|D|$ away from the zeros of $D$. Jensen's formula for the integer polynomial gives $\int\log|D|\ge\log|\operatorname{lc}(D)|\ge\log\operatorname{content}(D)$ for normalized circle measure. Both costs share the same allowance.

For all sufficiently large torsion orders $q$, $D$ is nonzero on every primitive $q$th root. Only finitely many root orders occur among its zeros. The norm product $\prod_{\zeta}|D(\zeta)|$ is the cardinality of $\mathbb Z[T]/(\Phi_q,D)$ by the integral resultant. Summing the same pointwise inequality over these roots shows that the logarithm of this actual finite cardinality plus the sum of the positive log moduli of the specialized quotient is at most $\varphi(q)L$. Thus the normalized bound also holds on closed cyclotomic fibres.
