## Main theorem

Let (F\colon\mathbb C\to\mathbb C) be square-summable on the multiplicity-exact centered Xi
divisor.  Write (v_F) for its evaluation vector in the Xi divisor Hilbert carrier.  Then:

1. (v_F) belongs to the Hilbert closure generated by regular Cauchy evaluations whose poles lie
   in the analytically continued prime--Gamma source domain.
2. There is a sequence (P_j) of finite pairwise-conjugate-separated continued Cauchy packets such
   that
   
   \[
   P_j^{\mathrm{eval}}\longrightarrow v_F.
   \]
3. Each packet has an exact finite continued prime--Gamma divided-difference energy
   (E_{\mathrm{src}}(P_j)), and
   
   \[
   E_{\mathrm{src}}(P_j)
   \longrightarrow
   \operatorname{Re}\mathcal W_\Xi(F,F).
   \]

The proof is multiplicity-safe.  It first decomposes (v_F) into complete analytic-multiplicity
fibres.  Normalized Cauchy pole exhaustion places every fibre in the continued source closure;
closedness and the (\ell^2) fibre sum give the universal statement.  Pairwise separation then
removes every conjugation denominator collision without changing the limit.

## Rectangular cofinal section

Fix (L>0) and let

\[
q_L(n)=\operatorname{Re}\mathcal W_\Xi
\left(F_{L,\frac12\log n},F_{L,\frac12\log n}\right).
\]

There exists one sequence of finite separated packets (P_n) satisfying

\[
\left|E_{\mathrm{src}}(P_n)-q_L(n)\right|<\frac1{n+1}
\qquad(n\ge0).
\]

For every packet section satisfying this estimate,

\[
\mathrm{RH}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists B\in\mathbb R\ \exists N_0\ \forall n\ge N_0,
\quad B\le E_{\mathrm{src}}(P_n).
\]

This is a positive carrier law: a successful CCM implementation must select a cofinal separated
source section whose energy tracks the fixed rectangular observable with vanishing error, and it
must prove one eventual finite lower floor on that section.

## Exact limitation

The packet section is selected through membership of the target Xi evaluation vector in a Hilbert
closure.  The theorem does not select the packet parameters from primes, Gamma factors, or the
semilocal Sonin operator alone.  Therefore it does not prove RH.  The remaining RH-bearing theorem
is precisely a source-only or semilocal selection and lower-floor theorem for such a cofinal
section.
