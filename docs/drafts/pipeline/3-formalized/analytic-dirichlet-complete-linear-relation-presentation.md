# Complete linear presentation of analytic Dirichlet differential relations

- **ID:** analytic-dirichlet-complete-linear-relation-presentation
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Source:** greenfield synthesis from the analytic Grassmann correspondence and the pivot relation-ideal theorem
- **Statement:** For an arbitrary source subspace and an arbitrary cardinality family of source vectors inside it, the full differential-polynomial relation ideal of the associated analytic Dirichlet carriers over the embedded coordinate field is generated by the jetwise prolongations of the ordinary source-linear relations.

## Goal

Upgrade source reconstruction from a classification of generated fields to a presentation of the
entire differential algebra.  After a basis of the source subspace is chosen, every additional
source has finitely supported basis coordinates.  The conjecture says that these coordinate
identities, repeated at every jet order, generate every algebraic relation among all jets.  Thus no
hidden nonlinear differential relation survives beyond the source-linear geometry.

The target is hypertranscendence-first.  The RH consequence is downstream: any use of a
prime-supported analytic palette must obtain genuinely new nonlinear information from outside its
source span, because the intrinsic differential-relation ideal contains no such information.

## Exact theorem

Let `x : Primes → V` have cofinite tail dense in `K`, and let `f : J → C(V)` restrict to a
linearly independent family on `K`, uniformly bounded along `x`.  For a subspace
`W ≤ J →₀ ℂ`, choose a basis `(b_q)_{q∈B}` of `W`.  Let `c : R → W` be arbitrary, with basis
coordinates `A_r : B →₀ ℂ`.  Write `F_q` and `G_r` for the corresponding shifted meromorphic
Dirichlet germs.

In the differential polynomial ring over the embedded coordinate field `ℂ(s)` on variables
indexed by `(B ⊕ R) × ℕ`, define

`L_{r,n} = X_{(r,n)} - Σ_q A_r(q) X_{(q,n)}`.

Then

`Rel(F ⊕ G) = (L_{r,n} : r ∈ R, n ≥ 0)`.

The statement is all-cardinality: `B` and `R` need not be finite.  Finiteness enters only through
the intrinsic finite support of each coordinate vector and each polynomial.

## Proof

1. `DifferentialFinsuppPivotRelationIdeal.lean` generalizes the finite pivot matrix
   `A : R → B → F` with `[Fintype B]` to finitely supported rows `A : R → B →₀ F`.
2. Elimination, normal form, kernel equality, evaluation factorization, and the complete
   relation-ideal theorem without any cardinality assumption on `B` or `R`.
3. `AnalyticDirichletCompleteRelationPresentation.lean` uses the vector-space basis of `W`; its
   coordinate map already lands in a `Finsupp` space.
4. The established tail-density theorem makes the basis carriers differentially independent
   over the actual embedded coordinate field.
5. Carrier linearity and its all-jet version identify every dependent carrier jet with the
   same finitely supported coordinate row.
6. The generalized relation theorem then gives the asserted ideal equality.

## Dual gate

- **Novelty risk:** the abstract elimination theorem is commutative-algebra infrastructure and is
  not itself new science.  The candidate novelty is the exact all-cardinality coupling to actual
  tail-dense analytic Dirichlet germs over embedded `ℂ(s)`.
- **Significance test:** the result must classify the full relation ideal, not only independence,
  field equality, transcendence degree, or selected finite palettes.
- **Literature status:** initial searches found differential-independence results for Dirichlet
  series and general free-differential-algebra presentation theory.  Shapiro--Sparer provide
  Dirichlet-series algebraic-independence criteria; Pong develops arithmetic-function independence
  through Ax's differential Schanuel theorem; Guo--Li study extension of presentations and
  Groebner--Shirshov bases to free differential algebras.  These are genuine parent theories.  No
  theorem was located that identifies the full relation ideal of this tail-dense analytic
  Dirichlet realization with the prolonged source-linear ideal, or identifies its relation
  quotient with the polynomial algebra on the independent source jets.  Specialist priority
  review remains required before a publication claim.

## Anti-theatre tests

- Erasing the analytic Dirichlet realization leaves a standard polynomial elimination theorem;

- Erasing the exact relation ideal leaves only the already-proved Grassmann field classification;
  that is insufficient.
- A successful theorem must cover arbitrary source families, arbitrary source subspaces, every jet
