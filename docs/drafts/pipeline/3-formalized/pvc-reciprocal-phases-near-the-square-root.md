# Ordinary primes near the square-root horizon

**Route** PVC. **Stage** 3-formalized.

For $\alpha=\eta=1/100000$, an explicit factorial weight with $R=\lfloor X^\alpha\rfloor$ carries $\psi(X)-X$ on ordinary primes $X^{1/2+\eta}<p\le X/R$ with $O(\sqrt X)$ error at the original horizon. Every surviving prime exceeds its complementary quotient $\lfloor X/p\rfloor$.

**Two moments.** Write $v_R$ for the [proper-power correction](pvc-cancellation-of-proper-powers.md), $J=128R^2$, and $g_a=h_a-64h_{64a}$ with $h_a(j)=\lfloor j/a\rfloor-2\lfloor j/(2a)\rfloor$. For $a=J+1$, $g_a$ vanishes on the entire square-root moment. Its constant term as a function of the real argument is $-63/2$. If $M_R$ is the constant term of $v_R$, then $v_R+(2M_R/63)g_a$ has both moments zero and is a sum of centered fractional parts with coefficient mass $O(R)$ and divisors $k\le128(J+1)$. Here $M_R=1+\tfrac12\operatorname{stencil}_R(1)+\tfrac{63}2\operatorname{momentRatio}(R)=O(R)$. The full Mangoldt pairing of $g_a$ costs $O(1+\log a+\log X)$, and its proper powers lie below $X/a$ with mass $O(\sqrt{X/a})$. After multiplication by $O(R)$ the total cost is $O(\sqrt X)$.

**Signed prime band.** Put $L=\lceil X^{1/2-\alpha}\rceil$ and $U=\lfloor X^{1/2+\eta}\rfloor+1$. Primes below $L$ cost $O(RL)=O(\sqrt X)$ by Chebyshev. For $L\le A\le B\le U$, $B\le2A$, and $1\le k\le128(J+1)$, let $Y=X/k$. For large $X$, $A^{1.998}\le Y\le A^{2.002}$: the lower bound follows from $1.998(1/2+\eta)+2\alpha<1$, while $2.002(1/2-\alpha)>1$ gives the upper bound. Apply the [centered reciprocal prime estimate](pvc-centered-reciprocal-prime-block.md) at each divisor. The sum over the full block is $O(RA^{999/1000})$. Dyadic subdivision costs $O(1+\log X)$ by `norm_sum_Ico_le_of_blocks`. Since $\alpha+(1/2+\eta)999/1000<1/2$, primes in $[L,U)$ cost $O(\sqrt X)$ in the signed sum. On bounded $X$ enlarge the absolute constant.

**Cofactor counts.** The corrected weight vanishes when $1\le j<R$. Thus at $X=\lfloor N/d\rfloor$ the surviving ordinary primes satisfy $X^{1/2+\eta}<p\le X/R$. Multiply the row error by $|b_V(d)|$ and sum, using $\sqrt X\le\sqrt N$. Both widths have total error $O(\sqrt N\sum_{d\le W}|b_V(d)|)$.
