## 1. Construction

Let `R` be a commutative ring, let `A` be a commutative `R`-algebra, let

\[
  \partial:A\longrightarrow A
\]

be an `R`-derivation, and fix an equation `a∈A`. Define the **equation Wronskian current**

\[
  W_a(f)=a\,\partial f-(\partial a)f.                    \tag{ODT.1}
\]

Let `I_a=(a)`, `Q_a=A/I_a`, and write `[f]` for the class of `f` in `Q_a`.

The word "on shell" means passage from `A` to the full quotient `Q_a`, not evaluation on the
reduced point set. This distinction retains nilpotent multiplicity directions.

## 2. Exact defect divisibility

### Theorem 2.1 — relative intertwiner law

For all `b,f∈A`,

\[
  W_a(bf)-bW_a(f)=a(\partial b)f.                       \tag{ODT.2}
\]

Consequently the failure of `W_a` to be `A`-linear lies exactly in `(a)`.

### Proof

By Leibniz,

\[
\begin{aligned}
W_a(bf)-bW_a(f)
 &=a((\partial b)f+b\partial f)-(\partial a)bf
   -b(a\partial f-(\partial a)f)\\
 &=a(\partial b)f.
\end{aligned}
\]

No zero, divisor point, or support assumption enters. ∎

### Theorem 2.2 — exact ideal preservation

For every `f∈A`,

\[
  W_a(af)=a^2\partial f.                                \tag{ODT.3}
\]

Hence `W_a(I_a)⊆I_a`.

### Proof

Expand `W_a(af)` with Leibniz. The two terms `a(∂a)f` cancel. Every element of the principal ideal
is `af` for some `f`, proving preservation. ∎

## 3. On-shell internalization

By Theorem 2.2, `W_a` descends to an additive endomorphism

\[
  \overline W_a:Q_a\longrightarrow Q_a.
\]

### Theorem 3.1 — quotient linearity, reconstruction, range, and square

The descended current is `Q_a`-linear and satisfies, for all `z∈Q_a`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \overline W_a(z)&=-[\partial a]z,                     \tag{ODT.4}\\
 \operatorname{im}(\overline W_a)&=Q_a[-\partial a],  \tag{ODT.5}\\
 \overline W_a=0&\Longleftrightarrow \partial a\in(a),\tag{ODT.6}\\
 \overline W_a^2(z)&=[\partial a]^2z.                 \tag{ODT.7}
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus one off-shell commutator defect is transmuted on shell into the principal conormal current.
The quotient does not merely say that the defect vanishes: it reconstructs the surviving operator
exactly from the conormal class `[∂a]`.

### Proof

Equation (ODT.2) becomes zero modulo `(a)`, so `overline W_a` is `Q_a`-linear. Evaluating (ODT.1)
modulo `(a)` gives (ODT.4). Its image is therefore the principal `Q_a`-submodule generated by
`-[∂a]`, proving (ODT.5). Formula (ODT.4) shows that vanishing of `[∂a]` implies vanishing of the
whole operator; conversely, evaluating a zero operator at `1` gives `-[∂a]=0`. This proves
(ODT.6). Applying (ODT.4) twice proves (ODT.7). ∎

## 4. Functional-equation parity

Let `τ:A≃A` be a ring automorphism satisfying

\[
  \tau(a)=a,
  \qquad
  \partial\tau=-\tau\partial.                          \tag{ODT.8}
\]

The first condition induces an automorphism `bar τ` of `Q_a`.

### Theorem 4.1 — reflection-odd on-shell current

\[
  W_a\tau=-\tau W_a,
  \qquad
  \overline W_a\,\overline\tau
    =-\overline\tau\,\overline W_a.                   \tag{ODT.9}
\]

### Proof

Applying (ODT.8) to `a` gives `τ(∂a)=-∂a`. Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
W_a(\tau f)
 &=a\partial(\tau f)-(\partial a)\tau f\\
 &=-a\tau(\partial f)-(\partial a)\tau f\\
 &=-\tau(a\partial f-(\partial a)f).
\end{aligned}
\]

Pass to the quotient. ∎

For a completed functional equation `a(1-s)=a(s)` and `τf(s)=f(1-s)`, ordinary differentiation
anti-commutes with `τ`; hence the theorem gives the exact parity demanded of a divisor current.

## 5. Multiplicity-phase classification

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero field, `A=K[X]`, `∂=d/dX`, and `a=X^m` with `m>0`. Put

\[
  Q_m=K[X]/(X^m).
\]

### Theorem 5.1 — every multiplicity produces a nonzero internal current

In `Q_m`,

\[
  \overline W_{X^m}(1)=-mX^{m-1}\ne0.                 \tag{ODT.10}
\]

Moreover:

1. if `m=1`, then `overline W_X=-id`;
2. if `m≥2`, then `overline W_{X^m}≠0` but
   `overline W_{X^m}^2=0`;
3. its range is the top multiplicity layer `K·X^{m-1}`, the socle of `Q_m`.

Thus the current distinguishes a reduced wall from every thickened wall without choosing a zero
or differentiating to a multiplicity-dependent order.

### Proof

Since `∂X^m=mX^{m-1}`, (ODT.4) gives (ODT.10). If this class were zero, `X^m` would divide
`mX^{m-1}`. Comparing the coefficient of degree `m-1` gives `m=0` in `K`, contradicting
characteristic zero and `m>0`.

For `m=1`, (ODT.4) gives multiplication by `-1`. For `m≥2`, equation (ODT.7) gives multiplication
by `m²X^{2m-2}`, and `2m-2≥m`, so the square vanishes in `Q_m`. Finally (ODT.5) says the range is
generated by `mX^{m-1}`. Since `m` is a unit in `K`, this equals `K·X^{m-1}`. It is annihilated by
`X`; conversely, if `Xf=0 mod X^m`, the coefficients of `f` below degree `m-1` vanish, so this is
exactly the socle. ∎
