\[

\tag{PH.1}
\]

## 2. Exact horizon theorem

### Theorem 2.1

The chain \((H_n)\) satisfies all of the following.

1. **Finite observer stabilization:**

   \[
   O_m(H_n)=H_{\min(m,n)},
   \qquad n\ge m\Longrightarrow O_m(H_n)=H_m.
   \tag{PH.4}
   \]

2. **Coherent non-effective limit:** the pointwise limit of \(\chi_{H_n}\) is the all-true profile,
   which is not the response profile of any finite effective law.

3. **Constant production:**

   \[
   H_{n+1}\setminus H_n=\{n\}.
   \tag{PH.5}
   \]

4. **Open source action:**

   \[
   A(H_n)=n.
   \tag{PH.6}
   \]

5. **Recurrent phase and positive erasure:**

   \[
   \phi(H_{n+2})=\phi(H_n),
   \qquad P(H_n)=1.
   \tag{PH.7}
   \]

6. **Simultaneous closed observation and open lift:** for every aperture \(m\) and action budget
   \(B\), there is even \(N\) such that

   \[
   O_m(H_N)=H_m,
   \qquad \phi(H_N)=1,
   \qquad A(H_N)>B.
   \tag{PH.8}
   \]

Thus one source history has eventually fixed finite observer projections and recurrent phase while
its effective action diverges toward a coherent non-effective boundary.

### Proof

For (1), membership in \(H_n\cap H_m\) is the conjunction \(k<n\) and \(k<m\), equivalent to
\(k<\min(m,n)\). If \(n\ge m\), the minimum is \(m\).

For (2), fix \(k\). Whenever \(n>k\), \(k\in H_n\), so the pointwise limit is the constant-one
profile. If a finite set \(F\) had this profile, any \(k\notin F\) would have response zero, a
contradiction.

For (3), \(k\in H_{n+1}\setminus H_n\) means \(k<n+1\) and not \(k<n\), hence \(k=n\).
For (4), the finite initial segment \(H_n\) has exactly \(n\) elements.

For (5),

\[
 \phi(H_{n+2})=(-1)^{n+2}=(-1)^n=\phi(H_n),
 \qquad P(H_n)=((-1)^n)^2=1.
\]

For (6), take

\[
 N=2(m+B+1).
\]

Then \(N\ge m\), \(N>B\), and \(N\) is even. Apply (1), (4), and (5). `QED`
