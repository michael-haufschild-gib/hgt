## 1. Big-picture interpretation

reflected proof histories remain distinguishable. Gamma-six assigns continuous formation cost.
External Route-R completion retains only occupied versus empty defect sector. These are not three
analogies: Gamma cost is the exact odds transform of proof distinguishability, while external
curvature is its support quantization.

For grade `a`, let

`p=exp(a)/(1+exp(a))`, `q=exp(-a)/(1+exp(-a))`,

`E=threeSlotExchangeDefect(p,q)=(p-q)^2/2`, and

`K=sourceHorizontalGammaSixUnitOppositeBoundaryCurvature(a)=2(cosh(a)-1)`.

`K(1-2E)=8E`, `1-2E>0`, hence `K=8E/(1-2E)`.

Thus proof exchange reconstructs full Gamma magnitude. External completion curvature has the same
zero locus but collapses every nonzero grade to one prime-harmonic occupation quantum.

## 3. Proof

Put `x=exp(a)>0`. Reflection gives `exp(-a)=x^-1`, so

`p-q=(x-1)/(x+1)` and `E=(x-1)^2/(2(x+1)^2)`.

Also

`K=x+x^-1-2=(x-1)^2/x`,

while

`1-2E=4x/(x+1)^2>0`.

Multiplication gives `K(1-2E)=8E`. The zero-locus statements follow from positivity and injectivity
of the logistic family.
