## 1. Why this is the next mechanism

The sharpened RH frontier no longer lacks finite constructions.  It repeatedly has correct local
inequalities, finite apertures, finite moments, finite null flags, finite positive carriers, or
finite cancellation mechanisms, but the required law must survive increasing aperture, contact
order, ground-space dimension, or all-prime completion.

Bare finite-to-cofinal quantifier exchange is already covered by ULF-GTM-2 and the coalitional and

system.  This card proves that source-local admission can force genuinely new primitive execution
degrees at every aperture.  No bounded grammar can absorb the family.

## 2. Complete-complement failure systems

Fix an integer `k>=2`.  Let the resource coordinates be

\[
 I_k=\{0,1,\ldots,k-1\}.
\]

For every `i in I_k`, declare the failure kernel

\[
 K_i=I_k\setminus\{i\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal H_k=\{K_i:i\in I_k\}.
 \tag{CC.1}
\]

Let `a_k(i)=1` for all `i`.  A weight `a` is locally admitted at grade `m` when

\[
 m\leq\sum_{j\in K}a(j)
 \quad\text{for every }K\in\mathcal H_k.
 \tag{CC.2}
\]

As in ULF-SELM-2, a positive locally admitted pair `(m,a)` is **primitive** when it admits no
positive dominated split

\[
 m=m_1+m_2,
 \qquad a_1+a_2\leq a,
 \tag{CC.3}
\]

with both `(m_1,a_1)` and `(m_2,a_2)` locally admitted.

## 3. Primitive-degree theorem

### Theorem 3.1 -- complete-complement primitivity

For every `k>=2`,

\[
 \boxed{(k-1,a_k)\text{ is primitive for }\mathcal H_k.}
 \tag{CC.4}
\]

### Proof

Every kernel `K_i` contains exactly `k-1` coordinates, so

\[
 \sum_{j\in K_i}a_k(j)=k-1.
\]

Thus `(k-1,a_k)` is locally admitted and has positive grade.

Assume a positive dominated split `(CC.3)`.  Write

\[
 A_r=\sum_{i\in I_k}a_r(i),\qquad r=1,2.
\]

Because `m_r>0` and `(m_r,a_r)` is locally admitted, `a_r` is nonzero.  Choose `i_r` with
`a_r(i_r)>0`.  Applying local admission to the complementary kernel `K_(i_r)` gives

\[
 m_r\leq\sum_{j\ne i_r}a_r(j)=A_r-a_r(i_r)\leq A_r-1,
\]

and hence

\[
 m_r+1\leq A_r.
 \tag{CC.5}
\]

The domination `a_1+a_2<=a_k` gives

\[
 A_1+A_2\leq k.
 \tag{CC.6}
\]

But `(CC.5)` and `m_1+m_2=k-1` imply

\[
 k+1=(m_1+1)+(m_2+1)\leq A_1+A_2\leq k,
\]

a contradiction.  Therefore no split exists. ∎

## 4. Cofinal grammar escape

### Theorem 4.1 -- every bounded grammar fails at a later aperture

For every finite primitive-degree bound `D`, set `k=D+2`.  Then

\[
 k-1=D+1>D,
\]

and Theorem 3.1 supplies a primitive locally admitted law of degree `D+1`.  By ULF-SELM-2, a
primitive law generated by a `D`-bounded primitive grammar must have degree at most `D`.
Consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 \neg\operatorname{BoundedGenerated}_{D}
   (\mathcal H_{D+2},D+1,a_{D+2}).
 }
 \tag{CC.7}
\]

Equivalently, the cofinal primitive spectrum contains every positive integer:

\[
 \{k-1:k\geq2\}=\mathbb N_{>0}.
 \tag{CC.8}
\]

This is stronger than an open execution history generated by one fixed atom.  ULF-SELM-3 has
unbounded grade with bounded primitive degree.  Here the degree of the necessary new grammar atom
itself diverges.

## 5. Constitutive law

### SELM Law IV -- cofinal obstruction can be grammar growth

A finite observer can fail globally for two different reasons:

1. one fixed primitive executes for unbounded time, while observer quotients close its history;
2. later horizons require primitive laws of genuinely larger degree, so no bounded execution
   language is complete.

The first is history escape.  The second is **grammar escape**.  Adding more compositions of old
bounded-degree laws cannot repair grammar escape; a new primitive law-former must be admitted.

This separates three notions often conflated in the frontier:

\[
 \text{unbounded execution grade},\qquad
 \text{unbounded interaction arity},\qquad
 \text{unbounded primitive degree}.
 \tag{CC.9}
\]

Only the third certifies failure of every bounded grammar.

## 7. Hostile scope

1. Each `H_k` is finite; the theorem concerns the cofinal family, not one infinite completion.
2. The family changes coordinate type with `k`.  A source realization must supply transition maps
   and prove primitive lineage across apertures.
3. Primitive degree is not automatically analytic complexity, oscillatory cancellation strength,
   physical cost, or differential transcendence degree.
4. The theorem supplies no compactness, convergence, positivity transfer, sign, source
   annihilation, carrier, zero statement, or RH implication.
5. Arbitrary relabeling preserves the construction, but a general presentation-invariance theorem
   for SELM primitive spectra remains open.
6. The complete-complement family is an exact calibration.  Claiming that every live route has
   this family would be theatre until literal maps are built.
