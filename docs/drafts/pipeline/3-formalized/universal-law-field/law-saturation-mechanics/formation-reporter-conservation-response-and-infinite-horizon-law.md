## Certified mechanism

Every reporter invariant under every primitive formation is conserved along every finite formation
word. Hence a different reporter value at canonical joint repair certifies that no finite legal word
reaches that repair. Separately, every nonidentity formation triggers a nonzero response in every
separating reporter coframe.

This is the exact conservation/response law for the mechanism. It says where a claimed erasure must
live: failure of primitive reporter invariance, failure of finite reachability, or an additional
limit-continuity failure. It does not call that failure curvature or anomaly by definition.
