## 1. Result

\[
 A=\mathbf Z[T,T^{-1}],\qquad d=T-1,qquad I=(d),
\]

and let \(N=d\partial_T\) be the formation-number connection.  The positive Rees algebra is the
subalgebra

\[
 A_+=\mathbf Z[d]\subset A.
\]

The following mechanism is canonical.

1. **Connection uniqueness.**  If \(\delta:A\to A\) is a \(\mathbf Z\)-derivation satisfying

   \[
     \delta(d)=d,
   \]

   then

   \[
     \delta|_{A_+}=N|_{A_+}.                         \tag{1}
   \]

   Thus formation energy on the open, positive history semigroup is forced by augmentation
   geometry.

2. **Joint grade spectrum.**  For every \(n\ge0\), one formation grade has five exact shadows:

   \[
   \begin{array}{c|c}
   \text{organ} & \text{grade-}n\text{ value}\\ \hline
   \text{formation connection} & n\\
   \text{functional-equation reflection} & (-1)^n\\
   \text{compact resolvent} & (n+1)^{-1}\\
   \text{thermal/KMS history mass} & (1-q)q^n\\
   \text{Toeplitz boundary defect rank} & n.
   \end{array}                                      \tag{2}
   \]

3. **Global formation properness.**  Complete the one-dimensional grades in

   \[
     \mathcal H_{\mathrm{form}}=\ell^2(\mathbf N_0).
   \]

   The bounded operator

   \[
     R=(N+1)^{-1},\qquad Re_n=(n+1)^{-1}e_n,          \tag{3}
   \]

   is compact.  Equivalently, the formation-number graph domain has compact embedding into
   \(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{form}}\).  Unbounded formation history is therefore compatible with a
   canonical properness mechanism.

4. **Reflection-compatible completion.**  Define

   \[
     \Gamma e_n=(-1)^n e_n
   \]

   and let \(V e_n=e_{n+1}\) be the unilateral creation shift.  Then

   \[
     \Gamma^2=1,\qquad \Gamma R=R\Gamma,
     \qquad \Gamma V=-V\Gamma.                       \tag{4}
   \]

   Reflection preserves formation energy but reverses one-step oriented history.

5. **Necessary one-sided curvature.**  With \(V^*\) the backward shift,

   \[
     V^*V=1,qquad VV^*=1-P_0,                        \tag{5}
   \]

   where \(P_0\) is the rank-one ground-grade projector.  The defect after \(n\) steps has rank
   \(n\).  At positive temperature \(0<q<1\), the canonical Gibbs state satisfies

   \[
     \varphi_q(P_n)=1-q^n,qquad
     \varphi_q(V^nP_0V^{*n})=(1-q)q^n.               \tag{6}
   \]

These are not five analogies.  Connection uniqueness forces one grade variable, and every other
quantity in (2) is a function of its intrinsic eigenvalue.

## 3. Proof of connection uniqueness

The formation number is a derivation.  Indeed, from the Laurent Leibniz law,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 N(PQ)
   &=d\partial_T(PQ)\\
   &=d\bigl((\partial_TP)Q+P(\partial_TQ)\bigr)\\
   &=N(P)Q+PN(Q).
\end{aligned}
\]

It is \(\mathbf Z\)-linear and satisfies \(N(1)=0\).  Also

\[
  N(d)=d\partial_Td=d.
\]

Let \(\delta\) be any other \(\mathbf Z\)-derivation with \(\delta(d)=d\).  Derivations agreeing on
a generating set agree on the subalgebra generated by that set: constants agree because every
derivation kills the coefficient ring; addition and multiplication preserve agreement.  Since
\(A_+=\mathbf Z[d]\) is generated by \(d\), \(\delta=N\) on \(A_+\).  This proves (1).

In particular,

\[
  N(d^n)=n d^n.                                      \tag{7}
\]

Formation depth is now an eigenvalue of a uniquely characterized connection, not a bookkeeping
index.

## 4. Hilbert completion and compact resolvent

Identify the completed grade \(I^n/I^{n+1}\otimes\mathbf C\) with the line \(\mathbf Ce_n\).  The
orthogonal completion is \(\ell^2(\mathbf N_0)\).  Define \(R\) by (3).  Let \(R_M\) be its
truncation to grades \(0,\ldots,M\).  Each \(R_M\) has finite rank, and

\[
  \|R-R_M\|
  =\sup_{n>M}\frac1{n+1}
  =\frac1{M+2}\longrightarrow0.                     \tag{8}
\]

Therefore \(R\) is an operator-norm limit of finite-rank operators and is compact.

For the graph interpretation, if \(x\) belongs to the domain of \(N\), put \(y=(N+1)x\).  Then
\(x=Ry\).  A graph-norm bounded family gives a bounded family of \(y\)'s; compactness of \(R\)
makes its image relatively compact in \(\ell^2\).  This is the exact global formation properness
law.  It does not compactify source history by quotienting it.

## 5. Reflection and orientation

On grade \(n\), Laurent inversion acts by \((-1)^n\) modulo the next grade.  Hence its completed
grade action is \(\Gamma e_n=(-1)^ne_n\).  Directly,

\[
  \Gamma^2e_n=(-1)^{2n}e_n=e_n.
\]

Both \(R\) and \(\Gamma\) are diagonal, so they commute.  For the creation shift,

\[
\begin{aligned}
  \Gamma Ve_n
    &=\Gamma e_{n+1}\\
    &=(-1)^{n+1}e_{n+1}\\
    &=-V\Gamma e_n.
\end{aligned}
\]

This proves (4).  Diagonal energy is reflection-even.  A one-step adjacent-grade correlation is
reflection-odd.  Positivity can therefore quotient phase without erasing all oriented information:
orientation survives precisely in the off-diagonal shift channel.

## 6. One-sided curvature, Hopf obstruction, and hypertranscendence

Equation (5) is the Toeplitz relation.  The forward operation is information-neutral as an
isometry, but its backward observer cannot recover the ground grade after the order is reversed.
The curvature is the finite-rank defect

\[
  P_0=1-VV^*.
\]

Killing \(P_0\) makes \(V\) invertible and extends the positive monoid representation to the
Laurent/Hopf envelope.  It also erases the open-history boundary.  Positive-temperature KMS balance
cannot descend through that quotient: invertibility forces \(q=1\), where the geometric history
distribution ceases to be normalizable.

ULF-TAFM-25 proves the differential counterpart.  A finite formation-stable state containing a
hypertranscendental source cannot be closed under inverse gauge.  The Toeplitz completion realizes
the minimal allowed failure: inverse gauge exists as a bounded backward observer, but only as a
one-sided inverse, with the exact rank-one boundary curvature.

Thus hypertranscendence, one-sided Toeplitz defect, and thermal normalizability fit without being
identified:

- hypertranscendence forces inverse-gauge escape from finite states;
- Toeplitz curvature records the escape under completion;
- Gibbs weights control infinitely many forward grades;
- compact resolvent supplies global properness;
- Hopf quotient restores reversibility only by erasing the defect and destroying positive-temperature
  normalization.

  \gamma_{n+1}=-\gamma_n.                            \tag{9}
\]

This is joint reconstruction, not equality of unrelated route endpoints.
