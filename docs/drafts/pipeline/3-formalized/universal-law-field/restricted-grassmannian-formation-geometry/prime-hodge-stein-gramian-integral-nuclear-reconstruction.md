```text

one-step failure of adjoint transport             -> defect Delta=I-C*C.
```

Their nuclear incidence

```text
Phi=Delta^(1/2) G Delta^(1/2)                       (PHSG.1)
```

not a normalization.  It follows from the Stein boundary equation

```text
G-C G C*=P_0                                        (PHSG.2)
```

by trace cyclicity.  The integer is therefore the rank of the marked source port.  Prime
formation then lifts `Phi` without destroying any cross-prime entry.

This makes `mathsf T` one object generated by a mechanism:

```text
marked port + open survival evolution
  -> trace-class reachability G
  x singular adjoint defect Delta
  -> positive integral nuclear history incidence Phi
  x literal prime-pair formation
  -> prime-mixed homotopy defect mathsf T.
```

## 3. Forced controllability Gramian

Let `H_hist=ell2(Nat)`, with basis `(e_n)`, and recall

```text
C e_n=sqrt(r_n)e_(n+1),
r_n=m_(n+1)/m_n=(2n+3)/(2n+9),
m_n=105/((2n+3)(2n+5)(2n+7)),
m_0=1.                                                (PHSG.3)
```

Let `P_0=|e_0><e_0|`.  Define

```text
G=sum_(j>=0) C^j P_0 C*^j.                           (PHSG.4)
```

**Theorem PHSG-A (source Gramian).**  The series `(PHSG.4)` converges in trace norm and

```text
G e_n=m_n e_n,
G>=0,
Tr G=||G||_1=sum_n m_n=7/4,
G-C G C*=P_0.                                        (PHSG.5)
```

Moreover `G` is the unique trace-class solution of the Stein equation in `(PHSG.5)`.

**Proof.**  Iterating `(PHSG.3)` gives

```text
C^j e_0=sqrt(m_j)e_j,
C^j P_0 C*^j=m_j P_j.                                (PHSG.6)
```

The summands are positive rank-one operators on mutually orthogonal lines.  ULF-PHRD-001 proves
`sum_jm_j=7/4`, so `(PHSG.4)` converges in trace norm to the diagonal operator in `(PHSG.5)`.
For `n=0`, `CGC*e_0=0`; for `n>=1`,

```text
C G C* e_n=r_(n-1)m_(n-1)e_n=m_n e_n.
```

This proves the Stein equation.

For uniqueness, let trace-class `X` satisfy `X-CXC*=P_0`.  Iteration gives

```text
X=sum_(j<N) C^jP_0C*^j+C^N X C*^N.                  (PHSG.7)
```

Both `C^N` and `C*^N` converge strongly to zero: this holds on finitely supported vectors by the
weighted-shift formulas and extends by uniform boundedness.  Approximate `X` in trace norm by a
finite-rank operator.  The last term in `(PHSG.7)` then tends to zero in trace norm, so `X=G`.
QED.

Thus the earlier mean `7/4` is the total reachability mass of the unique source Gramian.  It is not
a recurrent winding degree.

## 4. Nuclear incidence and integral trace

Put

```text
Delta=I-C*C.                                         (PHSG.8)
```

ULF-PHSF-001 gives

```text
Delta e_n=h_n e_n,
h_n=6/(2n+9),
m_n h_n=w_n.                                         (PHSG.9)
```

**Theorem PHSG-B (Stein--defect incidence).**  The operator

```text
Phi=Delta^(1/2) G Delta^(1/2)                        (PHSG.10)
```

is positive trace class and

```text
Phi e_n=w_n e_n,
Tr Phi=||Phi||_1=Tr P_0=1,
||Phi||=w_0=2/3.                                     (PHSG.11)
```

**Proof.**  `G` and `Delta` are positive diagonal in `(e_n)`, so `(PHSG.9)` gives the first
formula.  It already implies positivity, trace-class membership, and the norm.  More
intrinsically, trace cyclicity and `(PHSG.5)` give

```text
Tr Phi
 =Tr(G Delta)
 =Tr G-Tr(G C*C)
 =Tr G-Tr(C G C*)
 =Tr(P_0)=1.                                         (PHSG.12)
```

Positive trace-class operators have trace norm equal to trace. QED.

Equation `(PHSG.12)` is the integral law.  It remains valid for any trace-class Stein Gramian with
a positive finite-rank source port: the resulting incidence has trace equal to the port rank.  In
the present source, rank one is forced before any divisor exists.

## 5. Exact reconstruction of the prime-mixed defect

There are two already proved prime realizations.

### Separable prime-history realization

Let `A_form` be the positive trace-one Stokes cubic prime-formation operator of ULF-PHIN-001.  Its
joint defect is

```text
mathsf T=Phi tensor A_form
 = [Delta^(1/2)GDelta^(1/2)] tensor A_form.           (PHSG.13)
```

Thus every same-history cross-prime matrix entry comes from the literal `A_form` kernel, while
history weights are forced by the Stein incidence.  Equations `(PHSG.11)--(PHSG.13)` recover
positivity, nuclearity, prime mixing, and trace one without choosing `w_n` independently.  The
full norm is `||Phi|| ||A_form||` and therefore has the previously proved strict sub-unit bound;
only the history marginal itself has norm `2/3`.

### Nonseparable Cayley-collision realization

Let

```text
f_n=E_0 S^n Omega_H,
||f_n||^2=w_n
```

in the history-graded prime-pair record space of ULF-CCRHD-001.  Define the isometry from source
history into that record space by

```text
V e_n=f_n/sqrt(w_n).                                 (PHSG.14)
```

Strict positivity of every `w_n` makes this well-defined, and distinct history grades make the
images orthonormal.  Then

```text
mathsf T=V Phi V*
        =V Delta^(1/2)GDelta^(1/2) V*.               (PHSG.15)
```

Indeed, the right side is

```text
sum_n w_n |f_n/sqrt(w_n)><f_n/sqrt(w_n)|
 =sum_n |f_n><f_n|,
```

which is exactly the nonseparable prime-pair defect.  Hence the prime amplitude and history
incidence are not merely tensor factors: `V` is the literal formation embedding, and
`(PHSG.15)` reconstructs every grade-dependent prime-pair kernel.

## 7. RH leverage and limitation

The theorem removes one ambiguity from every proposed completion law: `mathsf T` may no longer be
treated as a freely selected trace-one density or a fitted source budget.  A valid completion must
transport the entire marked Stein system

```text
(C,P_0,G,Delta,Phi,V),                               (PHSG.16)
```

preserving `(PHSG.2)`, the positive sandwich `(PHSG.10)`, and the literal formation embedding
`V`.  Transporting only `Tr mathsf T`, only `Delta`, or only a response scalar does not preserve
the source mechanism.

This does not prove RH.  Classical system realization cannot force a Poisson/theta completion,
and normal trace-preserving maps can still send a mixed state to a rank-one state.  The exact open
edge remains the source-native oriented completion/exhaustion theorem of ULF-ARCFP-001.  Its target
horizon must be shown to arise as the same Stein incidence, not merely have the same trace.
