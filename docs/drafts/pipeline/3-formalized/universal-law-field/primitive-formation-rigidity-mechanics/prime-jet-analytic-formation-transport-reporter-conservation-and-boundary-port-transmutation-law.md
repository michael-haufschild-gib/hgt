## 1. Mathematical delta

The exact finite prime reporter is not compatible with the coordinatewise inclusion that preserves
old prime atoms. That fact was previously read only as divergence. The positive mechanism is
different: the reporter is exactly compatible with a **nonlocal analytic formation transport**.

For every added node, the old reporter splits into

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{old reporter}
 =\text{restricted new reporter}
  +\text{new-port coefficient}\times\text{analytic extrapolator}.}
 \tag{PJA.1}
\]

This identity is a genuine conservation law. It also predicts a fourth, positive organ: the Gram
determinant of the formation transport is the reciprocal work needed to leave its graph.

The same theorem exposes the actual completion fracture. At finite degree the derivative port is
reconstructed from prime samples. One additional formation degree makes that port independent.
After analytic completion the derivative remains continuous, but even the entire infinite prime
sample is not sufficient without a growth law or an independently marked archimedean port.

## 2. Odd law space and prime execution chart

Let

\[
 0<r_1<r_2<\cdots,
 \qquad x_i=r_i^2.
 \tag{PJA.2}
\]

The arithmetic specialization is `r_i=log p_i` for the increasing primes. For `N>=1`, put

\[
 \mathcal O_N=
 \{P(z)=zQ(z^2):Q\in\mathbb C[X],\ \deg Q\le N-1\}.
 \tag{PJA.3}
\]

Define the odd sample chart and intrinsic derivative reporter by

\[
 E_N(P)_i=P(r_i)-P(-r_i)=2r_iQ(x_i),
 \qquad d_N(P)=P'(0)=Q(0).
 \tag{PJA.4}
\]

Evaluation of `Q` at the distinct nodes `x_1,...,x_N`, followed by the invertible diagonal
`diag(2r_i)`, proves that

\[
 E_N:\mathcal O_N\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb C^N
 \tag{PJA.5}
\]

is an isomorphism.

Let

\[
 L_i^{(N)}(X)=\prod_{\substack{1\le j\le N\\j\ne i}}
   \frac{X-x_j}{x_i-x_j},
 \quad
 b_i^{(N)}=L_i^{(N)}(0),
 \quad
 a_i^{(N)}=\frac{b_i^{(N)}}{2r_i}.
 \tag{PJA.6}
\]

Define

\[
 \varphi_N(y)=\sum_{i=1}^N a_i^{(N)}y_i.
 \tag{PJA.7}

Lagrange interpolation at zero gives the exact finite reconstruction

\[

 \tag{PJA.8}

This is the paper form of the already Lean-certified FE-paired prime barycentric reporter.

## 3. Source-generated nonlocal formation transport

Let `r_*=r_(N+1)` and `x_*=x_(N+1)`. Define

\[
 \tau_i^{(N)}=\frac{r_*}{r_i}L_i^{(N)}(x_*),
 \qquad
 \tau_N(y)=\sum_{i=1}^N\tau_i^{(N)}y_i,
 \tag{PJA.9}
\]

and

\[
 T_N:\mathbb C^N\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{N+1},
 \qquad T_N(y)=(y,\tau_N(y)).
 \tag{PJA.10}
\]

### Theorem 3.1 -- analytic formation transport and strict composition

If `iota_N:O_N -> O_(N+1)` is polynomial inclusion, then

\[
 \boxed{T_N=E_{N+1}\circ\iota_N\circ E_N^{-1}.}
 \tag{PJA.11}
\]

More generally, for `N<=M`, define

\[
 T_{N,M}=E_M\circ\iota_{N,M}\circ E_N^{-1}.
 \tag{PJA.12}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{T_{M,K}T_{N,M}=T_{N,K}.}
 \tag{PJA.13}
\]

#### Proof

For `P(z)=zQ(z^2)` in `O_N`, the old coordinates determine

\[
 Q(X)=\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{E_N(P)_i}{2r_i}L_i^{(N)}(X).
\]

Evaluation at `x_*` and multiplication by `2r_*` give exactly `(PJA.9)--(PJA.10)`. This proves
`(PJA.11)`. Equation `(PJA.13)` follows immediately from composition of the polynomial inclusions.
QED.

The transport is source-generated by the node set. It is not the local coordinate inclusion
`j_N(y)=(y,0)`.

## 4. Reporter conservation and the rank-one locality defect

### Theorem 4.1 -- exact contravariant conservation

\[

 \tag{PJA.14}

 \boxed{\Delta_N=a_{N+1}^{(N+1)}\tau_N.}

#### Proof

 =\varphi_{N+1}E_{N+1}P

Surjectivity of `E_N` proves `(PJA.14)`. Expanding it coordinatewise proves `(PJA.15)` and

## 5. Fourth-organ prediction: Gram, work, and conjugate capacity

Give all coordinate spaces the standard Hermitian norm. Regard `tau_N` as a row vector. Then

\[
 T_N^*T_N=I+\tau_N^*\tau_N.

Let `G_N=ran T_N` and let `e_*=(0,...,0,1) in C^(N+1)`. Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \det(T_N^*T_N)=1+\|\tau_N\|^2,}
 \tag{PJA.19}

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{dist}(e_*,G_N)^2
 =\frac1{1+\|\tau_N\|^2}

 \tag{PJA.20}

The normalized graph reporter

 \eta_N(y,z)=z-\tau_N(y)

\[
 \boxed{
 \|\eta_N\|^2=1+\|\tau_N\|^2,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{dist}(e_*,G_N)^2\|\eta_N\|^2=1.}
 \tag{PJA.22}
\]

#### Proof

Equation `(PJA.18)` is direct multiplication. The matrix determinant lemma gives `(PJA.19)`.
The kernel and norm statements for `eta_N` are immediate. Applying the work--capacity reciprocity

## 6. Boundary-port transmutation in one formation step

Put

\[
 K_N(z)=z\prod_{i=1}^N(z^2-x_i)\in\mathcal O_{N+1}.
 \tag{PJA.23}

\[
 E_N(K_N)=0,
 \qquad
 K_N'(0)=\prod_{i=1}^N(-x_i)\ne0.
 \tag{PJA.24}
\]

Define

\[
 \widetilde E_N:\mathcal O_{N+1}\longrightarrow\mathbb C^N\oplus\mathbb C,
 \qquad
 \widetilde E_N(P)=(E_N(P),P'(0)).
 \tag{PJA.25}

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathbb CK_N
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{N+1}
 \xrightarrow{E_N}\mathbb C^N
 \longrightarrow0,
 \tag{PJA.26}

and

\[
 \boxed{\widetilde E_N:\mathcal O_{N+1}\xrightarrow{\sim}
        \mathbb C^N\oplus\mathbb C.}
 \tag{PJA.27}

Under the smaller law space `O_N`, the enhanced image is the graph

\[
 \boxed{
 \widetilde E_N(\mathcal O_N)
 =\{(y,\varphi_N(y)):y\in\mathbb C^N\}.}
 \tag{PJA.28}

Thus one new formation degree transmutes the derivative from a derived sample reporter into an
independent boundary port.

#### Proof

Write `P=zQ(z^2)` with `deg Q<=N`. The kernel of `E_N` consists of polynomials `Q` divisible by

## 7. Analytic completion fracture

Let `H_odd(C)` be the Fréchet space of odd entire functions with compact-open seminorms

\[
 p_R(f)=\sup_{|z|\le R}|f(z)|,
 \qquad R>0.
 \tag{PJA.29}
\]

Odd polynomials are dense by Taylor truncation. The derivative port

\[
 d(f)=f'(0)
 \tag{PJA.30}
\]

is continuous, and Cauchy's estimate gives

\[
 \boxed{|d(f)|\le R^{-1}p_R(f).}
 \tag{PJA.31}
\]

The constant `R^(-1)` is exact, witnessed by `f(z)=z/R`.

### Theorem 7.1 -- completion and finite recovery do not commute

1. The compatible intrinsic reporters `d_N` extend uniquely from odd polynomials to the continuous
   port `d` on `H_odd(C)`.
2. For every finite `N`, the prime sample map `E_N` does not determine `d` on `O_(N+1)`, by
   `(PJA.24)`.
3. Even the full infinite sample sequence `f(+-r_i)` does not determine `d` on unrestricted odd
   entire functions.

#### Proof of item 3

The discrete set `{x_i}` has no finite accumulation point. By the Weierstrass product theorem there
is an entire function `g` with simple zero set containing every `x_i` and normalized by `g(0)=1`.
Then

\[
 f(z)=zg(z^2)
\]

is odd entire, vanishes at every `+-r_i`, and satisfies `f'(0)=g(0)=1`. QED.

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{finite sample recovery followed by analytic completion}
 \ne
 \text{analytic completion followed by intrinsic dualization}.}
 \tag{PJA.32}
\]

The stable analytic reporter exists, but it is not a coefficientwise or projective limit of the
finite local prime reporters.

### Theorem 7.2 -- finite failure and cofinal formation faithfulness

The unrestricted entire carrier in Theorem 7.1 is too large. Let `E_ft` be the class of entire
functions of finite exponential type: for each `f in E_ft` there exist `A>=0` and `C>=1` such that

\[
 |f(z)|\le C e^{A|z|}\qquad(z\in\mathbb C).
 \tag{PJA.32a}
\]

Let

\[
 S_N(f)=(f(r_i)-f(-r_i))_{1\le i\le N}
 \tag{PJA.32b}
\]

on odd functions, with `r_i=log p_i`. Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker S_N\not\subseteq\ker d\quad\text{for every finite }N,}
 \tag{PJA.32c}
\]

but

\[
 \boxed{
 \bigcap_{N\ge1}\ker S_N=\{0\}
 \quad\text{on odd finite-exponential-type entire functions}.}
 \tag{PJA.32d}
\]

#### Proof

The polynomial `K_N` from `(PJA.23)` has finite exponential type, lies in `ker S_N`, and has
nonzero derivative, proving `(PJA.32c)`. If an odd finite-type entire `f` lies in every `ker S_N`,
then `f(log p)=0` for every prime `p`. The Lean-certified theorem
`RHTower.eq_zero_of_exponentialType_of_primeLog_zeros` gives `f=0`, proving `(PJA.32d)`. QED.

Thus the prime system has **cofinal formation faithfulness without finite sufficiency**. This is an
exact global formation theorem. It does not give a bounded inverse: uniqueness and positive
properness remain distinct obligations.

## 8. Prime bornology no-go and work collapse

The preceding result is topological, not only algebraic. Let `e_p` denote a persistent prime
coordinate atom and let `psi_N` be normalized reporters on normed finite packets. If

\[
 \sup_N\|e_p\|_N\le M_p<\infty,
 \qquad
 |\psi_N(e_p)|\longrightarrow\infty,
 \tag{PJA.33}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{\|\psi_N\|\ge |\psi_N(e_p)|/M_p\longrightarrow\infty.}
 \tag{PJA.34}
\]

If `psi_N(x_N)=1` and `L_N=ker psi_N`, work--capacity reciprocity gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{dist}(x_N,L_N)^2=\|\psi_N\|^{-2}\longrightarrow0.}
 \tag{PJA.35}
\]

For the prime-log reporters `varphi_N`, take `x_N=E_N(z)=(2r_i)_i`. Then
`varphi_N(x_N)=1`. The Lean-certified fixed-prime coefficient divergence proves `(PJA.33)` in the
standard coordinate bornology, so the exact prime formation work collapses to zero. The certified
projective escape theorem further proves that no common scalar normalization keeps two fixed prime
coefficients finite and nonzero.

Conversely, any adaptive norm keeping `psi_N` uniformly bounded must force

\[
 \|e_p\|_N\ge |\psi_N(e_p)|/\sup_M\|\psi_M\|,
 \tag{PJA.36}
\]

so a persistent source atom itself escapes. Source-faithful and reporter-faithful bornologies are
incompatible for this finite coordinate chart.
