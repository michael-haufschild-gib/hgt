## 4. The two-source noncollapse theorem

### Theorem 4.1

The following universal implications are false:

1. commuting source transformations imply absence of generated law;
2. every generated law is detected by a pure endpoint commutator;
3. every higher source anomaly is a transported unary-additive binary residual.

More precisely:

- the gas source has a nonidentity marked endpoint commutator `(GSG.3)` which generates an absent
  intervention;
- the elliptic-gamma source has trivial pure translation commutator and trivial pure two-shift
  Koszul defect, but the nonzero marked mixed transgression `(GSG.8)`;
- no isomorphism of marked execution systems preserving endpoint composition can identify these
  genesis mechanisms, because such an isomorphism preserves whether the marked commutator is the
  identity.

Therefore any mechanism containing both sources must retain at least two independent structures:

\[
 \boxed{\text{execution exchange}}
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \boxed{\text{coefficient/admission lift transgression}}.
\tag{GSG.9}
\]

### Proof

The gas calculations `(GSG.2)`--`(GSG.3)` are direct substitution.  For the elliptic-gamma
source, commutativity of translations gives the trivial pure Koszul coboundary, while the
group--Cech construction, explicit integral cocycle

\[
 \nu(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=-\beta_1\gamma_2\alpha_3,
\tag{GSG.10}
\]

and alternation detector prove that the Dixmier--Douady class is not a coboundary.  The
coefficient connecting map then gives `(GSG.7)`--`(GSG.8)`.  This is a generated law with
commuting endpoints, disproving implications 1 and 2.

ULF-RLM-10 proves that every ternary reversal defect of unary additive endomorphisms decomposes
into contextual binary commutators.  The elliptic class survives only after adding the Cech and
coefficient directions and is invisible in the pure translation complex.  It therefore lies
outside that unary-additive mechanism, disproving implication 3 at the claimed universal scope.

Finally, a marked execution isomorphism conjugates commutators and hence preserves identity versus
nonidentity.  It cannot send `(GSG.3)` to the elliptic pure translation pair.  QED.

## 5. Exact survivor: genesis height is a filtration, not one number

The failed conjecture has a stronger positive survivor.

For a marked source execution, inspect possible law birth in this order:

1. **endpoint exchange:** compare legal schedule endpoints;
2. **contextual exchange:** transport lower residuals through later legal operations;
3. **coefficient/admission lift:** test whether a coarse coherent history has a coherent lift;
4. **mixed descent:** test Cech, group, or other realization directions jointly;
5. **cofinal formation:** test whether compatible finite fillers define one admitted global filler.

The **genesis filtration** is the ordered record of which stages vanish and the first stage that
does not.  It is not a scalar invariant: different directions can first become nonzero in
different bidegrees, and later formation can transgress or cancel earlier failures.

The gas occupies the endpoint-exchange stratum.  Elliptic gamma occupies a mixed-descent stratum
after pure endpoint and pure group-cochain flatness.  Existing ULF finite-history and
coefficient-lift theorems prove the transition laws between these strata; cofinal convergence is
still open in general.
