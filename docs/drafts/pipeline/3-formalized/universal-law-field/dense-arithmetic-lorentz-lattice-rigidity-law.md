## Theorem

Let `Gamma` be the additive subgroup of `R` generated by `{log p : p prime}` and let

`B(u) = [[cosh u, sinh u], [sinh u, cosh u]]`.

For `a in R`, assume

`2 cosh(a x) in Z` for every `x in Gamma`.

Then `a=0`.

Consequently, if a full rank lattice `Lambda` in `R^2` is preserved by `B(a x)` for every
`x in Gamma`, then `a=0`.

## Proof [PROVED HERE]

Assume `a != 0`. Since `Gamma` is dense and multiplication by `a` is a homeomorphism, `a Gamma`
is dense in `R`. The function

`tau(u)=trace(B(u))=2 cosh(u)`

is continuous. By hypothesis `tau` is integer-valued on the dense subset `a Gamma`. The integer
lattice is closed in `R`, so continuity forces `tau(u) in Z` for every `u in R`: for any `u`, take
`u_n in a Gamma` with `u_n -> u`; then `tau(u_n) -> tau(u)`, and a convergent integer sequence has
integer limit.

At `u=log 2`,

`tau(log 2)=2 cosh(log 2)=2 * (2+1/2)/2=5/2`,

which is not an integer. Contradiction. Hence `a=0`.

For the lattice corollary choose a basis of `Lambda`. Every lattice-preserving boost is represented
in that basis by a matrix in `GL_2(Z)`, whose trace is integral. Trace is invariant under change of
basis, so the hypothesis of the theorem holds.
