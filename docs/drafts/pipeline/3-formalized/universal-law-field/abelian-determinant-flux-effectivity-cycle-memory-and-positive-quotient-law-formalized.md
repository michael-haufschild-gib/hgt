## Setup

An oriented finite graph is a pair of maps `t, h : E → V`; connectivity is the inductive relation
`Linked t h` generated by the edges in either direction.  The coefficient group `A` is an arbitrary
`CommGroup`.  `fluxBoundary t h f v` is the product of `f` over the edges with head `v` divided by
the product over the edges with tail `v`, and `totalFlux b = ∏_v b v`.

## Certified declarations

### Theorem 2.1 — the determinant-flux exact sequence

- `prod_fluxBoundary` — `Π ∘ ∂_A = 1`, proved by the fibrewise product identity: every edge value is
  counted once at its head and once at its tail;
- `exists_flux_of_linked` — a link from `u` to `v` realizes the endpoint charge `a` at `v` and
  `a⁻¹` at `u`; the single-edge fluxes `fluxBoundary_singleEdge_forward/backward` and the composition
  law `endpointCharge_step` do the work, so no spanning tree is needed;
- `exists_realization_iff` — a vertex law is globally realizable iff `Π(b) = 1`.  The nontrivial
  direction is a support induction: two vertices of nontrivial charge are joined by a link, whose
  flux cancels one of them, and the total charge stays trivial;
- `range_fluxBoundaryHom` — hence `im ∂_A = ker Π` as subgroups;
- `totalFluxHom_surjective` — `Π` is surjective, closing the four-term sequence
  `1 → ker ∂_A → A^E → A^V → A → 1`;
- `realizationEquivKernel` — every nonempty realization fibre is a torsor under `ker ∂_A`,
  exhibited by translation with any one realization.

### Corollary 3.1 — proper-face exactness

`exists_realization_on_of_ne_univ` proves that for every proper `W ⊊ V` and every charge, some edge
field realizes it on `W`: the charge is corrected at one vertex outside `W` so that the total charge
becomes trivial.  A nontrivial total anomaly is therefore invisible on every proper observer face.

### Theorem 5.1 — positive coefficient quotient

`totalFlux_comap` proves `Π(p ∘ b) = p(Π(b))` for every homomorphism `p : A →* P`, and
`totalFlux_comap_eq_one_iff` that the `P`-observer declares the anomaly trivial exactly when
`Π(b) ∈ ker p`.
