## 1. Setup

Let `M` be a connected real-analytic manifold. Let `G` be a connected, simply connected,
finite-dimensional nilpotent Lie group whose Lie algebra `g` has:

- an invariant nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form;
- a bracket-stable filtration preserved by the adjoint action.

Let `P->M` be an analytic principal `G`-bundle with analytic connection `A`. Its Atiyah algebroid
fits into

\[
 0\longrightarrow\operatorname{ad}(P)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{At}(P)\longrightarrow TM\longrightarrow0.
\tag{AS.1}
\]

The connection is a horizontal splitting `s_A:TM->At(P)`. Define its vertical splitting defect

\[
 \Omega_A(X,Y)=[s_AX,s_AY]-s_A[X,Y]
 \in\Gamma(\operatorname{ad}(P)).
\tag{AS.2}
\]

This is the strict binary chart of a source-anchored cyclic filtered law algebroid.

## 2. Strict generation theorem

### Theorem 2.1 — strict Ambrose--Singer--Spencer law

For every base point `x in M`:

1. `Omega_A` is the curvature of `A` under the standard identification of the vertical Atiyah
   bracket defect with `ad(P)`.
2. It satisfies the Bianchi identity

   \[
   d_{\nabla^A}\Omega_A=0.
   \tag{AS.3}
   \]

3. The restricted holonomy Lie algebra is

   \[
   \boxed{
   \mathfrak{hol}_x^0(A)=
   \operatorname{Lie}\left\langle
   \operatorname{Ad}_{\tau_\gamma^{-1}}
   \Omega_y(u,v):
   \gamma:x\to y, u,v\in T_yM
   \right\rangle .}
   \tag{AS.4}
   \]

4. Analyticity gives the equivalent Spencer-jet form

   \[
   \boxed{
   \mathfrak{hol}_x^0(A)=
   \operatorname{Lie}\left\langle
   (\nabla^k\Omega_A)_x(X_1,\ldots,X_k;u,v):k\geq0
   \right\rangle .}
   \tag{AS.5}
   \]

5. A finite, connection-dependent subset of the displayed jets generates
   `mathfrak hol_x^0(A)`.
6. Since `G` is simply connected nilpotent, `exp:g->G` is a diffeomorphism and

   \[
   \operatorname{Hol}_x^0(A)=\exp(\mathfrak{hol}_x^0(A)).
   \tag{AS.6}
   \]

7. Parallel transport preserves the filtration and invariant pairing, so the restricted holonomy
   algebra lies in their infinitesimal stabilizer.

#### Proof

Item 1 is the definition of curvature through the connection splitting of `(AS.1)`. For horizontal
lifts `s_AX,s_AY,s_AZ`, expand the Jacobi identity in `At(P)` and take its vertical component. The
horizontal part is the Jacobi identity for vector fields; the remaining part is exactly `(AS.3)`.

For a sufficiently small rectangle based at `y`, its holonomy has first nonzero term equal to its
oriented area times `Omega_y(u,v)`. Conjugating by parallel transport along `gamma:x->y` moves this
infinitesimal holonomy to `x`. Hence the right side of `(AS.4)` lies in the restricted holonomy
algebra. Conversely, subdivide any null-homotopic loop into small rectangles. The product expansion
of their transported holonomies, followed by differentiation at the identity, places every
restricted infinitesimal holonomy in the Lie algebra generated by those transported curvature
values. This proves `(AS.4)`.

In analytic radial gauge at `x`, transported curvature is determined by its convergent covariant
Taylor series. Therefore transported curvature values and all covariant jets generate the same Lie
algebra, proving `(AS.5)`. Finite dimensionality implies every generating set of a Lie subalgebra
contains a finite subset generating that subalgebra: add generators until the generated subalgebra
dimension stops increasing. The exponential statement is standard for connected simply connected
nilpotent groups, where BCH is finite and `exp` is a global diffeomorphism. Invariance of the pairing
and filtration under the adjoint action proves item 7. QED.

### Corollary 2.2 — restricted versus full history

If `M` is simply connected, full and restricted holonomy coincide. In general there is an exact
sequence

\[
 1\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hol}_x^0(A)
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Hol}_x(A)
 \longrightarrow\Gamma_A\longrightarrow1,
\tag{AS.7}
\]

and a natural surjection `pi_1(M,x)->Gamma_A`. Curvature and its local prolongations determine only
the restricted term.

#### Proof

Restricted holonomy consists of transports around null-homotopic loops and is the identity component
of the holonomy group in this finite-dimensional setting. Homotopic based loops have transports in
the same component, giving the quotient map from `pi_1(M,x)`. If `M` is simply connected, every loop
is null-homotopic. QED.

## 3. Full-holonomy countermodel

Let `h_3=<x,y,z | [x,y]=z>` be the Heisenberg Lie algebra and let

\[
 \mathfrak g=T^*\mathfrak h_3
 =\mathfrak h_3\ltimes\mathfrak h_3^*
\]

be its cotangent Lie algebra, with invariant pairing

\[
 \langle(u,\alpha),(v,\beta)\rangle=\alpha(v)+\beta(u).
\tag{AS.8}
\]

It is finite-dimensional, two-step nilpotent, and quadratic. Put `X=(x,0)` and `Z=(z,0)`; `Z` is
central. On the trivial bundle over

\[
 M=\mathbb R^2_{u,v}\times(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)_\theta
\]

define

\[
 A_\lambda=u\,dv\,X+\lambda\,d\theta\,Z.
\tag{AS.9}
\]

Then

\[
 \Omega_\lambda=du\wedge dv\,X,
 \qquad \nabla^k\Omega_\lambda=0\quad(k\geq1),
\tag{AS.10}
\]

independently of `lambda`, whereas

\[
 \operatorname{Hol}_x^0(A_\lambda)=\exp(\mathbb RX),
\qquad
 \operatorname{Hol}_x(A_\lambda)=
 \exp(\mathbb RX)\langle\exp(\lambda Z)\rangle.
\tag{AS.11}
\]

Thus identical bracket, cyclic form, filtration, local curvature, and all local prolongations can
have different full holonomy. Setting the first term in `(AS.9)` to zero gives a flat field with
nontrivial monodromy.

## 4. No uniform jet cutoff

On `R^2`, keep the same central `Z` and define

\[
 A_N={x^{N+1}\over N+1}\,dy\,Z.
\tag{AS.12}
\]

Then

\[
 \Omega_N=x^Ndx\wedge dy\,Z.
\tag{AS.13}
\]

At the origin, every curvature jet of order `<N` vanishes and the order-`N` jet is nonzero, while
small rectangles away from the zero set have nontrivial holonomy. Hence no uniform prolongation
cutoff follows from the dimension, nilpotence class, or binary bracket arity. Analyticity gives a
finite generating order for each fixed connection, not one common order for the class.

## 5. Higher-arity type obstruction

Let `q_r:S^r(sH)->sH` be an internal minimal `L_infinity` bracket. Ordinary loop holonomy is
infinitesimally governed by a vertical-valued two-form. For `r>2`, there is no natural
`GL(TM)xGL(H)`-equivariant map

\[
 q_r\longmapsto\Omega^2(M;\mathfrak g)
\]

without additional horizontal soldering, vertical projection, and `r-2` contractions or a typed
transgression. A splitting `s` instead produces the natural horizontal component

\[
 \Omega_r(X_1,\ldots,X_r)=
 \operatorname{pr}_V q_r(sX_1,\ldots,sX_r),
\tag{AS.14}
\]

which is an `r`-form and belongs to `(r-1)`-dimensional higher holonomy. Thus the original claim
that an arbitrary primary minimal operation generates loop holonomy was ill-typed.

Indeed, before choosing `s`, the tensor `q_r` has no base-cotangent input. Under the tangent scaling
`lambda id_TM`, any natural two-form output scales by `lambda^-2`, while `q_r` is unchanged. Naturality
for every nonzero `lambda` forces such an unsoldered output to vanish.

For a compatible connection, every parallel transport preserves `q_r`. Infinitesimally,

\[
 Dq_r=q_r\sum_{i=1}^r
 (1^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes D\otimes1^{\otimes(r-i)})
\tag{AS.15}
\]

with graded signs. Therefore the unconditional relation is only

\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak{hol}_x^0\subseteq
 \mathfrak{stab}_{\mathrm{fil,cyc}}(q_r).}
\tag{AS.16}
\]

The bracket constrains allowable holonomy; it does not generate it.

## 6. Positive survivor and open field conjecture

The exact survivor is:

> Binary bracket plus source-canonical splitting produces ordinary curvature, whose transported
> analytic Spencer tower generates restricted loop holonomy. Higher brackets require soldering and
> generate higher-dimensional holonomy. Full monodromy remains independent global data.

This forces the next conjecture to be the **Soldered Graded Ambrose--Singer--Spencer conjecture**:
for a source-anchored analytic cyclic filtered `L_infinity`-algebroid with source-canonical
splitting, effective Spencer transgression, and nilpotent higher-groupoid integration, the restricted
vertical higher-holonomy `L_infinity` algebra is the smallest filtered cyclic subalgebra generated by
parallel transports of all forms `(AS.14)`, their mixed vertical components, and their covariant
Spencer prolongations. Ordinary loop holonomy is exactly the binary sector; full holonomy is an
extension by independent fundamental-infinity-groupoid monodromy.

The strict binary theorem above is the proved base case. The higher statement remains Stage 1.
