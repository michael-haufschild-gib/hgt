# Valued loops have integral, torus and unipotent factors

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Parent:** [Integral loop vertices](../1-in-progress/valued-loop-iwasawa-and-integral-vertices.md).
- **Statement:** Over any field with valuation in `ℤᵐ⁰`, every element
  of the actual central loop group factors as `khu`, where `k` belongs
  to the subgroup generated by the two integral simple `SL₂` groups,
  `h` has the central and finite torus coordinates, and `u` is in the
  completed positive unipotent group.

## Proof

The two vertex parabolics generate the actual central loop group. Each
vertex is positive Borel times its simple `SL₂` subgroup, by constant
reduction and the congruence kernel. The valued rank-one factorization
writes each simple factor as an upper-triangular matrix times an integral
matrix. Both simple upper-triangular groups lie in positive Borel.
Thus each vertex lies in `BK`, where `K` is generated by the two integral
simple groups.

Left multiplication by a vertex preserves `BK`: for `x` in that vertex
and `bk∈BK`, the element `xb` remains in the same vertex because it
contains `B`. Reapply its `BK` factorization and multiply the two
integral factors. Vertex generation gives `G=BK`. Inversion gives
`G=KB`. The audited Borel coordinates split the latter factor into
central torus, finite torus and completed positive unipotent factors.
