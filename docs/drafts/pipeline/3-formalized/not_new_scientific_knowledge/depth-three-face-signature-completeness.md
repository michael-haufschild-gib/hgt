# Depth-Three Face-Signature Completeness

- **ID:** `depth-three-face-signature-completeness`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven / needs independent audit
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / COMPLETE HIGHER-FACE INVARIANT
- **Scientific status:** `[BRIDGE / SUPPORTING CASE]` — the theorem is the first nontrivial instance of, and is now subsumed by, `all-depth-pair-face-signature-completeness`; retain it because it isolates the double-telescoping mechanism transparently
- **Statement:** At the first depth where the common terminal residue ceases to classify differential algebraicity, the three unpushed codimension-one residues do classify it. For every meromorphic `1`-periodic function with constant triple mixed difference, the function is differentially algebraic exactly when all three codimension-one triptic residue divisors vanish.

## Setup

Let `tau_1,tau_2,tau_3 in H` and assume

`1,tau_1,tau_2,tau_3`

are linearly independent over `Q`. Write `Delta_i=Delta_(tau_i)` and

`H_(jk)=Z+Z tau_j+Z tau_k`, `G_(jk)=C/H_(jk)`.

Let

`Q_3={F in Mer(C):F(z+1)=F(z) and Delta_1Delta_2Delta_3F in C}`.

For `F in Q_3`, each codimension-one difference

`F_i=Delta_i F`

belongs to the depth-two class for the two remaining parameters. Let

`R_2^(hat i)(F_i) in C[delta][G_(jk)]`, `{i,j,k}={1,2,3}`,

be its complete triptic orbit-residue divisor from `triptic-orbit-residue-reciprocity`.

Define the **depth-three face signature**

`Face_3(F)=(R_2^(hat 1)(Delta_1F),R_2^(hat 2)(Delta_2F),R_2^(hat 3)(Delta_3F))`.

The three components are deliberately not pushed to the common rank-four quotient. Their common pushforward is the terminal residue `R_3(F)`, which is already known to have a hypertranscendental kernel.

## Main theorem `[PROVED HERE]`

Assume the depth-two completeness theorem

`E in Q_2(tau_j,tau_k) is DA iff R_2(E)=0`.

Then, for every `F in Q_3`,

`F is DA over C(z) iff Face_3(F)=0`.

Thus the terminal-residue failure at depth three is exactly repaired by retaining the three residues before their final pushforward. There is no additional invisible primitive class at this depth.

The forward implication is immediate from translation-closure of the DA field and depth-two completeness. The reverse implication is the content of the proof below.

## Bottom-face exact sequence `[PROVED HERE]`

Fix the surviving direction `tau_3` and put

`Lambda_3=Z+Z tau_3`, `A_3=C[delta][C/Lambda_3]`.

Define

`rho_3:Q_3 -> A_3`,

`rho_3(F)=R_1(Delta_1 Delta_2 F)`,

where the two differences on the right are those in the `tau_1` and `tau_2` directions and `R_1` is the full labelled principal-part divisor on `C/Lambda_3`.

Then

`0 -> Q_2(tau_1,tau_2) -> Q_3 --rho_3--> A_3 -> 0`

is exact.

### Kernel

The function

`V=Delta_1 Delta_2 F`

is `1`-periodic and has constant `tau_3`-difference. If `rho_3(F)=0`, then `V` has no poles. Its derivative is therefore an entire elliptic function for `Lambda_3`, hence constant; period `1` forces that constant to be zero. Thus `V` itself is constant, which is exactly

`F in Q_2(tau_1,tau_2)`.

The converse inclusion is immediate.

### Surjectivity

Let `Psi_2` be the logarithmic derivative of the order-two multiple elliptic gamma function with parameters `tau_1,tau_2,tau_3`. The hierarchy gives

`Delta_1 Delta_2 Psi_2=Psi_0(z;tau_3)`,

a theta logarithmic derivative whose normalized `R_1` charge is `[0]`. Translation and differential equivariance therefore give

`rho_3(D Psi_2)=D`

for every finite differential divisor `D in A_3`, after choosing arbitrary lifts of its support. Different choices differ by an element of the kernel and do not affect surjectivity.

This exact sequence is a constraint statement. It does not use differential Galois theory.

## Double-telescoping lemma `[PROVED HERE]`

Let

`q_1:A_3 -> C[delta][C/(Lambda_3+Z tau_1)]`,

`q_2:A_3 -> C[delta][C/(Lambda_3+Z tau_2)]`

be the finite-support orbit-sum pushforwards. Put `x_i=t_(tau_i)-1`.

Then

`ker q_1 intersect ker q_2=x_1 x_2 A_3`.

### Proof

The subgroup generated by `tau_1,tau_2` acts freely on `C/Lambda_3`: an equality

`n_1 tau_1+n_2 tau_2 in Lambda_3`

would contradict the `Q`-linear independence of `1,tau_1,tau_2,tau_3` unless `n_1=n_2=0`.

Decompose a finite divisor into its finitely many `Z^2`-orbits. On one orbit, `A_3` is a Laurent polynomial module over

`C[delta][u^(+-1),v^(+-1)]`,

and the two orbit-sum kernels are the principal ideals `(u-1)` and `(v-1)`. Since

`(u-1) intersect (v-1)=((u-1)(v-1))`,

their intersection on every orbit is the double coboundary. Summing the finitely many orbit components proves the lemma.

This is the nonformal step that prevents a residue from vanishing separately in two directions without already being a two-direction boundary.

## Proof of face-signature completeness

Assume

`Face_3(F)=0`.

Set

`D=rho_3(F) in A_3`.

The first face component is computed by taking the bottom charge `D` and pushing it along the `tau_2`-orbits:

`R_2^(hat 1)(Delta_1F)=q_2(D)`.

Likewise,

`R_2^(hat 2)(Delta_2F)=q_1(D)`.

Both vanish. The double-telescoping lemma therefore gives

`D=x_1x_2 C`

for some `C in A_3`.

Choose lifts of `C` and let `lambda_3=Psi_0(z;tau_3)` be a normalized theta logarithmic derivative. Put

`Z=C lambda_3`.

Since `R_1(lambda_3)=[0]`, translation equivariance gives

`rho_3(Z)=R_1(Delta_1Delta_2 C lambda_3)=x_1x_2C=D`.

The function `Z` is DA because `lambda_3` is DA and the DA field is closed under finite translations and derivatives. The exact sequence now shows that

`E=F-Z`

belongs to `Q_2(tau_1,tau_2)`.

It remains to use the third face. Since `Z` is DA,

`R_2^(hat 3)(Delta_3E)=R_2^(hat 3)(Delta_3F)=0`.

Let

`D'=R_2(E) in C[delta][G_(12)]`.

Translation equivariance gives

`R_2(Delta_3E)=(t_(tau_3+H_(12))-1)D'=0`.

The class of `tau_3` has infinite order in `G_(12)`. A nonzero finite-support divisor cannot be invariant under an infinite-order translation, so `D'=0`.

Depth-two completeness now implies that `E` is DA. Since `Z` is DA, `F=E+Z` is DA. This proves the reverse implication.

## What the theorem changes

The common terminal residue alone is incomplete at depth three: for example, a structural difference of `Psi_2` is hypertranscendental but has zero terminal charge. The theorem shows that this loss occurs only in the final pushforward. Before that push, one of the three face divisors necessarily remains nonzero.

Equivalently:

- `R_3(F)` is a conserved total charge but not a complete state;
- `Face_3(F)` is complete modulo DA;
- every depth-three HT class is visible on at least one codimension-one triptic face;
- there is no nonzero class in `Q_3/(Q_3 intersect Mer_DA)` whose three structural first differences are all DA.

The last formulation is a rank-four primitive-vanishing theorem inside the constant triple-mixed-constraint class. It is sharp against the elliptic-gamma depth-two case, where the elliptic-gamma logarithmic derivative is HT while both structural first differences are DA.

## Multiplicative form `[PROVED HERE]`

Let `Y` be a nonzero `1`-periodic meromorphic function whose full three-direction multiplicative coboundary has constant logarithmic derivative. Put

`F=delta Y/Y in Q_3`.

For each `i`, compute the unpushed triptic integer divisor of the quotient

`Y(z+tau_i)/Y(z)`

on the complementary two-parameter face. Collect the three divisors as `MFace_3(Y)`.

Then, conditional on depth-two completeness,

`Y is DA iff MFace_3(Y)=0`.

Indeed these integer divisors are exactly the three components of `Face_3(delta Y/Y)`, and a nonzero meromorphic function is DA exactly when its logarithmic derivative is DA over a DA extension.

## Parent-theory and novelty attack

### Components that are not new

1. Theta logarithmic derivatives realize arbitrary principal parts on one elliptic curve.
2. The multiple elliptic gamma hierarchy supplies a splitting of the bottom-face exact sequence.
3. One-curve orbital residues and their panorbital refinements classify elliptic summability.
4. Finite-support telescoping kernels and the Laurent-polynomial identity

   `(u-1) intersect (v-1)=((u-1)(v-1))`

   are elementary.

These components alone are `[BRIDGE]` material.

### Scientific residue

The constraint-level synthesis is the exact three-face signature for every meromorphic solution of a constant triple mixed difference, together with the proof that the only simultaneous two-orbit cancellation is a DA theta layer and that the remaining third face kills the lower triptic layer.

The theorem is not a statement about one named gamma function. Its completeness proof uses only theta principal parts, finite orbit telescoping, and the depth-two invariant; the order-two multiple elliptic gamma appears only as a convenient proof that the bottom-face map is surjective. However, the same mechanism now proves the stronger all-depth pair-face theorem. Scientific novelty is therefore assessed on that general theorem and on the depth-two cross-curve parent, not independently on this special case.

### Literature boundary checked provisionally

- Felder--Henriques--Rossi--Zhu construct the elliptic-gamma gerbe and pseudodivisor calculus on triptic curves. Their local-finiteness lemma is a serious conceptual parent for finite boundary divisors, but it does not state a DA-complete three-face signature for arbitrary meromorphic mixed-constraint functions.
- Morain's higher geometric elliptic gamma papers prove modular coboundary relations and higher cocycles, not differential-algebraic completeness of face data.
- Arreche--Babbitt classify summability on one elliptic curve by orbital and panorbital residues, not simultaneous residues on three complementary triptic faces.
- Parameterized difference Galois theory supplies the depth-two atomic input, but no located theorem performs the double push-kernel intersection and lower-face elimination above.

Exact, equivalent, stronger, mechanism, and parent-field searches through 2026 located no theorem of this form. Scientific priority remains provisional pending expert review.
