## Statement

Let `F` be a field embedded in a differential field `K`, and let `x,y : I -> K` be two nonempty
equally indexed palettes.  Call them projectively base-jet equivalent when one of the following
single global alternatives holds:

```text
for every i,n, jet_n(x_i - y_i) lies in F;
for every i,n, jet_n(x_i + y_i) lies in F.
```

Suppose that palettes outside this relation are jointly differentially algebraically independent
over `F`.  Then

```text
F<jets of x> = F<jets of y>
  iff
x and y are projectively base-jet equivalent.
```

The same equivalence holds for one-element palettes.  Thus the theorem covers both the raw
prime--Dirichlet carrier fields and the fields generated by the complete shifted palettes.

## Proof

A coordinatewise correction whose complete jet sequence lies in the base field does not change the
generated differential field.  Simultaneous global negation also leaves that field unchanged.
These facts prove the forward construction for the two projective alternatives.

For the converse, reindex the full jet family of the disjoint union by

```text
(I + J) x Nat  ~=  (I x Nat) + (J x Nat).
```

Joint differential independence and the algebraic-independence sum theorem imply that the complete
`x`-jet family is algebraically independent over the ordinary algebra generated by the `y` jets.
Algebraic independence persists from that algebra to its fraction field, which is the embedded
differential field generated by `y`.  Hence every `x_i` is transcendental over the `y` field.  If
the two generated fields were equal, `x_i` would belong to that base field and therefore be
algebraic over it, a contradiction.

## Scientific role

The abstract field argument is standard algebraic-independence technology and is not claimed as a
new result in isolation.  Its role is exact: it makes the manuscript's raw-field quotient a proved
consequence of the prime-tail differential matroid rather than an informal appeal to clearing a
differential-rational denominator.  Applied to prime sign sequences, projective base-jet
equivalence is eventual equality or eventual opposition; mixed tails are the jointly independent
complement.  Therefore finite and cofinite reversals are precisely the canonical-source field
stratum, while genuinely mixed infinite reversals occupy transverse field strata.

The intrinsic base of this stratification is also formalized.  The relation on Boolean orientation
sequences generated by finite changes and one global complement is proved to be an equivalence
relation, and its quotient `BooleanProjectiveTail` is constructed explicitly.  Equality of quotient
classes is exactly eventual equality or eventual complement.  Combined with the field theorem
above, this is the deductive core of the paper's canonical projective-tail field bundle.  For the
canonical product topology, every quotient fibre is dense and the induced quotient topology is
indiscrete.  Consequently every continuous class invariant into a `T0` space is constant.  Thus
the quotient is canonical as an equivalence relation and groupoid, but cannot admit a nonconstant
continuous separated coordinate system without additional source-sensitive structure.  In fact,
continuity at even one orientation forces a class invariant into a `T1` space to be globally
constant; equivalently, every nonconstant separated class invariant is nowhere continuous.
