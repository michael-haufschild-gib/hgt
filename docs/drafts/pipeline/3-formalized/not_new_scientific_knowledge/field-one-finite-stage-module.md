# Finite-stage idele-class module

- **ID:** `field-one-finite-stage-module`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM / PRODUCT-FORMULA CONSTRUCTION
- **Label:** `F1-FINITE-STAGE-MODULE-1`

- **Target:** construct the finite-stage norm module and prove it descends
  through the diagonal rational \(S\)-unit quotient.

## Theorem

For every finite set \(S_f\) of rational finite places, the formula
\[
  \delta_S(u_\infty,(u_v))
    = |u_\infty|\prod_{v\in S_f}|u_v|
\]
defines a multiplicative character
\[
  \delta_S:\mathbb A_S^\times\longrightarrow\mathbb R_{\geq0}.
\]
The diagonal sign has module one. If \(v\in S_f\) corresponds to the positive
prime \(p\), then \(p\) has norm \(p^{-1}\) at \(v\), norm one at every other
selected finite place, and real norm \(p\). Hence its diagonal module is one.
The subgroup generated by these elements lies in the kernel, as does its
diagonal image. Therefore \(\delta_S\) descends to
\[
  C_{\mathbb Q,S}
    =\mathbb Z_S^\times\backslash\mathbb A_S^\times.
\]

## Formal proof

`FieldOneFiniteStageModule.lean` constructs the character, identifies each
height-one ideal of \(\mathbb Z\) with the span of its associated prime,
proves the same-place and different-place norm formulas, proves generator
and closure kernel containment, and constructs the quotient character with
its representative formula.

## No-go closure

- **Exact killing mechanism:** open quotient topology alone does not supply
  an invariant quotient measure. Rational \(S\)-unit invariance requires a
  scaling-character theorem.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** the expected norm character and its
  product-formula descent are now unconditional Lean theorems.
- **New insight:** remaining measure gap is not the algebraic product formula.
  It is identification of this norm character with product additive Haar's
  distributive character, followed by a genuine measurable quotient.
- **Status:** module stage formalized. Haar scaling, quotient measure, `L²`,
  trace, sheaf restriction, Hodge positivity, and RH remain open.
