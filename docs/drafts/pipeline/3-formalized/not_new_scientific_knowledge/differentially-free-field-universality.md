## Statement

Let `F` be a differential field and let `x : I -> K` and `y : I -> L` be two equally indexed
differentially algebraically independent palettes in differential field extensions of `F`.  Their
generated differential fields admit a canonical `F`-algebra equivalence

```text
F<x_i : i in I>_D  ~=  F<y_i : i in I>_D
```

which sends every labelled jet `D^n x_i` to `D^n y_i`.  This equivalence commutes with the
derivations.  It is the unique derivation-preserving `F`-algebra equivalence sending every
order-zero generator `x_i` to `y_i`.

Equivalently, the labelled differential-field isomorphism type of a free differential palette
depends only on its index set and its differential coefficient field, not on the analytic
realization of its generators.

## Proof

The complete jet families

```text
(D^n x_i)_(i,n)     and     (D^n y_i)_(i,n)
```

are algebraically independent over `F`.  Algebraic independence identifies each ordinary field
generated by one jet family with the fraction field of the polynomial algebra
`F[X_(i,n)]`.  Composing one parametrization with the inverse of the other gives the canonical
labelled field equivalence and immediately proves its action on every jet.

To prove compatibility with the derivation, induct over the field generated by the jet family.
The generator step is the identity `D(D^n x_i)=D^(n+1)x_i`; the coefficient step uses the
differential-algebra structure over `F`; addition and multiplication use additivity and Leibniz;
inversion uses `D(a^(-1))=-a^(-2)D(a)`.  Therefore the equivalence commutes with `D` everywhere.

Finally, any other derivation-preserving equivalence that agrees on order-zero generators agrees
inductively on every labelled jet.  Field-adjoin induction then forces equality on the whole
generated field.

## Scientific role

This is standard free differential-field algebra and is not claimed as new in isolation.  It is
the exact structural bridge needed for the Helson route: once an analytic theorem proves complete
differential independence of a shift palette, every analytic realization has the same labelled
abstract differential-field type.  Any invariant that still varies among those realizations must
therefore live in an embedding, continuation, boundary, or gluing datum rather than in that
abstract differential field.
