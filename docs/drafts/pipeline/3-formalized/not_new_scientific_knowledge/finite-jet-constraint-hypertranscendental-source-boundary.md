## Statement

Let `F ⊆ E` be differential fields of characteristic zero. Let `S` be a derivation-closed
intermediate field and let `y ∈ E`. Suppose a nonzero polynomial `Q` over `S` annihilates a
finite jet of `y`:

```text
Q(y, y', ..., y^(n)) = 0.                                             (1)
```

If `trdeg_F(S) < aleph0`, then `y` is differentially algebraic over `F`. The positive
contrapositive is the cardinal lower bound

```text
HypertranscendentalOver F y and (1)
  -> aleph0 ≤ trdeg_F(S).                                             (2)
```

If `S = F⟨h⟩_D` is the differential field generated by one input `h`, the corresponding source
theorem is

```text
HypertranscendentalOver F y and (1) over F⟨h⟩_D
  -> HypertranscendentalOver F h.                                      (3)
```

Thus a finite algebraic jet law can constrain an HT observable only by importing an infinite
coefficient reservoir. Equation `(2)` is stronger than a binary no-go: it gives a cardinal
complexity lower bound on the field seen by the observation law.

## Proof

Equation `(1)` is exactly a finite-jet witness that `y` is differentially algebraic over `S`.
When `S` is derivation closed and has finite transcendence degree over `F`, the existing
finite-transcendence descent theorem makes `y` differentially algebraic over `F`. Contraposition
against `HypertranscendentalOver F y` proves `(2)`.

For `(3)`, if `h` were differentially algebraic over `F`, then `y` would be differentially
algebraic over `F⟨h⟩_D` by `(1)` and differential-algebraic transitivity would descend this to
`F`, contradicting the HT hypothesis.

The existing polynomial first-order activation theorem has been refactored to apply `(3)` to

```text
Q(Y0,Y1) = Y1 - P(Y0).
```

This removes its duplicated transitivity/finite-jet proof and demonstrates a direct corpus
simplification rather than adding an unused wrapper.

## Constraint-side clock interpretation

The theorem never solves an ODE and assumes no initial value, existence, uniqueness, Hamiltonian,
or time-evolution operator. A derivative is an observable channel: `(1)` says that finitely many
readings of a clock satisfy one algebraic constraint. Hypertranscendence says that no such finite
constraint exists over the base field.

Equation `(2)` sharpens the motif “hypertranscendence is about the observability structure of a
clock, not temporal evolution.” If a finite observation law is nevertheless imposed, the infinite
structure has not disappeared; it has moved into the coefficient field.

## Physics and quantum transfer

The theorem applies to exact finite-order algebraic differential reductions, not to physics
vocabulary by itself.

- Picard--Fuchs equations for Feynman integrals are finite-order differential constraints in an
  external variable. Whenever their exact coefficient field is derivation closed and has finite
  transcendence degree, the constrained integral lies on the DA side of the boundary. Therefore
  such a finite master-integral sector cannot independently generate an HT clock.
- A finite minisuperspace Wheeler--DeWitt equation is a static quantum constraint on a wavefunction.
  If it becomes a nonzero polynomial finite-jet relation over a finite-transcendence coefficient
  field, the wavefunction is DA over the base. This transfer matches the constraint-side reading
  more closely than interpreting the WDW equation as temporal propagation.
- A finite closure of a Ward, Schwinger--Dyson, BRST, effective-field, or holographic radial
  hierarchy falls under the theorem only after it supplies an exact nonzero algebraic relation in
  a finite jet. An infinite hierarchy, functional derivative, nonlocal kernel, delay, fractional
  derivative, or limiting construction is not silently collapsed into this class.
- The same boundary covers finite scalar-field, Higgs, inflaton, electromagnetic, weak, strong,
  and gravitational reductions when their selected observable obeys `(1)`. It does not assert
  that the full field theory has such a reduction.

The useful research route exposed by the no-go is therefore precise: an HT physical clock must
enter through an infinite-transcendence coefficient reservoir, an HT latent source, or a genuinely
infinite/nonlocal observation operation. The next physics search should test those source-bearing
interfaces rather than finite QFI, KMS, Berry, or clock reparametrizations already saturated in the
corpus.

## Genomics, genetics, ML, and AI transfer

Rational ODE models of biochemical and gene-regulatory networks admit differential-algebraic
input/output elimination. If an exact measured gene-expression clock satisfies a resulting
finite-jet identity over a finite-transcendence parameter/latent field, it is DA. If the measured
clock is HT, `(2)` forces an infinite-transcendence parameter reservoir and `(3)` forces a singleton
latent regulator to be HT. This is a structural source statement, not a claim about noisy samples
or parameter estimation.

Symbolic equation recovery, genetic-programming equation solvers, neural ODE identification, and
interpretable AI commonly return finite symbolic differential laws. For any exact returned law in
the class `(1)`, an HT target certifies that the learned coefficient/latent field carries infinite
differential complexity. Approximate fitting, finite samples, and numerical residuals do not
trigger the theorem.

This also distinguishes a genomic or epigenetic clock from biological time evolution. A finite
readout of methylation or expression features is an observation structure. If its exact output is
HT, the source theorem requires HT complexity in a biological/latent input rather than in fixed
learned weights or in the mere passage of time.

## Exact scope and positive residue

The law must be exact, algebraic in a finite ordinary differential jet, and nonzero. The
coefficient field must be derivation closed. The theorem does not cover approximate equations,
PDE jets with several derivations, stochastic equations as such, functional differential
equations, arbitrary integral kernels, infinite hierarchies, fractional derivatives, or limits of
finite models.

The positive result is not “those models fail.” It is the exact alternative `(2)`: any successful
finite constraint on an HT observable certifies an infinite-transcendence coefficient field. This
moves the project beyond the yes/no HT predicate toward cardinal measurement of the observation
reservoir. Equation `(3)` then localizes that complexity when the reservoir has one named source.

This does not prove RH, select a sign sequence, or orient the signed-prime source. It advances the

The surviving RH-relevant route is narrower and positive. A bridge must prove source-specific
arithmetic orientation while exhibiting either an infinite-transcendence coefficient reservoir or
a genuinely infinite/nonlocal operation. The theorem audits exactly where that source enters and
prevents a finite physical or AI law from redressing it as a new producer.
