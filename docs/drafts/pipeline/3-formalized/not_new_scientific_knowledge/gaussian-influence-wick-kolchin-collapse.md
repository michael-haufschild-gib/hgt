## Exact one-source theorem

Let `A` be a positive Gaussian Hessian with discrete eigenvalues `E_n` and multiplicities
`rho(n)`.  For finite cutoff `N`, put

```text
W_N(x) = (1/2) sum_(n=0)^N rho(n) log(x+E_n).              (1)
```

Assume that the second resolvent trace

```text
Q_2(x) = sum_(n>=0) rho(n)/(x+E_n)^2                       (2)
```

converges locally normally.  Then, after subtracting the cutoff-dependent affine part of `(1)`,
every derivative of order `r >= 2` has a cutoff-independent limit

```text
W^(r)(x)
  = (1/2)(-1)^(r-1)(r-1)! Q_r(x),

Q_r(x) = sum_(n>=0) rho(n)/(x+E_n)^r.                     (3)
```

Termwise differentiation of `(2)` gives

```text
Q_r(x)
  = (-1)^(r-2)/(r-1)! (d/dx)^(r-2) Q_2(x).                (4)
```

Therefore every finite packet of homogeneous mass-source vertices of order at least two belongs
to the differential field generated by `Q_2`.  Its relative transcendence degree over the
corresponding finite jet packet of `Q_2` is zero.

This conclusion is unchanged when `Q_2` is hypertranscendental.  Hypertranscendence of the base
generator measures spectral/infinite-shell complexity; it does not create new relative
differential content in higher induced vertices.

## Wick interpretation

The determinant `(1)` comes from integrating a Gaussian field.  Its source derivatives are
connected cumulants of the quadratic composite coupled to `x`.  They are nonzero at arbitrarily
high order, but Wick's theorem expresses them as cyclic products of the two-point covariance.
For a general spacetime-dependent source, the correct base contains every pair contraction pulled
back along every face of the higher configuration.  Once that face-complete base is used, every
finite Gaussian composite packet lies in its algebraic closure.

The existing finite-rank theorem
`finiteRelativeTranscendenceRank_eq_zero_of_mem_adjoin` then gives zero relative rank.  Thus

```text
nonpolynomial effective action
+ infinitely many nonzero induced vertices
+ hypertranscendental mass response
does not imply interaction-relative hypertranscendence.                  (5)
```

## Exact boundary

The theorem does not say that integrating out every Gaussian field is physically trivial.
Gaussian determinants renormalize couplings, generate nonlocal kernels for spacetime-dependent
sources, and can change the dynamics of the retained field.  The claim is narrower: those kernels
remain Wick-generated by the eliminated field's two-point covariance and therefore have zero
relative rank over a face-complete covariance base.

Nor does the one-source calculation prove that an arbitrary functional reporter has one ordinary
differential generator.  The general vanishing statement uses Wick-face closure, not the
one-variable derivative identity alone.

## Positive residue and next selector

A successful interaction-HT source must satisfy both conditions:

1. a higher connected sector is not in the differential-algebraic field generated by all
   face-pulled one- and two-point sectors;
2. the source supplies a nontrivial rational scale defect or another verified selector that forces
   the surviving sector to be hypertranscendental.

Rigorous non-Gaussian `p`-adic fixed-point fields already exist, so non-Gaussianity or anomalous
scaling alone would be rediscovery.  Their strict scale covariance also does not by itself imply
HT.  The next genuine target is a higher connected shell-defect cocycle in such a model.
