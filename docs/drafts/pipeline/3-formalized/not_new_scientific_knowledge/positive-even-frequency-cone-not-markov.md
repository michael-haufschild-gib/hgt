# Positive Even Frequency Cone Is Not Markov

- **ID:** positive-even-frequency-cone-not-markov
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / source-transfer guardrail
- **Source:** `single-even-frequency-not-stieltjes.md`;
  `seam-log-markov-pick-rh-equivalence.md`;
  `source-selector-repair-obligation-ledger.md`
- **Statement:** A nonzero positive mixture of FE-even exponential frequencies
  has alternating shifted seam moments. In particular its second shifted moment
  is negative, so it cannot be the moment sequence of a positive measure on
  `R_+`. The seam-log Markov target cannot be proved by positive summation over
  FE-even Euler frequencies.

## Setup

Let `mu` be a finite positive Borel measure on `(0,infty)` such that all even
moments used below are finite. Define the even local log-germ

`L_mu(z)=int_0^infty (cosh(lambda sqrt(z))-1) dmu(lambda)`.

Equivalently, near `z=0`,

`L_mu(z)=sum_{k>=1} (int lambda^(2k) dmu(lambda))/(2k)! z^k`.

As in the reciprocal seam coordinate, define

`T_k(mu)=-2k(-1)^k [z^k]L_mu(z)`, for `k>=1`.

## Theorem

For every `k>=1`,

`T_k(mu)=(-1)^(k+1) (int lambda^(2k) dmu(lambda))/(2k-1)!`.

If `mu` is nonzero, then

`T_(2j-1)(mu)>0` and `T_(2j)(mu)<0`

for every `j>=1` for which the relevant moment is finite. In particular,

`T_2(mu)=-(int lambda^4 dmu(lambda))/6 < 0`.

Therefore `(T_k(mu))_{k>=1}` is not the moment sequence of a positive measure on
`R_+`.

## Proof

The coefficient calculation is termwise:

`[z^k]L_mu(z)=(int lambda^(2k) dmu(lambda))/(2k)!`.

Thus

`T_k(mu)
 = -2k(-1)^k (int lambda^(2k) dmu(lambda))/(2k)!
 = (-1)^(k+1)(int lambda^(2k)dmu(lambda))/(2k-1)!`.

Since `lambda^(2k)>0` on `(0,infty)` and `mu` is positive and nonzero, every
finite even moment `int lambda^(2k)dmu(lambda)` is strictly positive. The signs
therefore alternate with `k`.

If a positive Borel measure `nu` on `R_+` had moments `T_k(mu)`, then

`T_2(mu)=int y^2 dnu(y)>=0`,

contradicting the strict negativity above. Hence no such positive Markov /
Stieltjes representation exists.

## Consequence For The Euler-Product Transfer

The one-frequency obstruction is not an artifact of isolating a single source
atom. The whole positive cone generated by FE-even exponential frequencies has
the wrong coefficient sign for the seam-square Markov target.

Therefore a proof cannot start with positive Euler amplitudes, symmetrize each
frequency by the functional equation, sum them with positive weights, and expect
a positive Markov/Stieltjes measure on `R_+`. The completed source theorem must
use an interaction outside this cone: archimedean cancellation, signed
counterterms, multiplicative correlations, the completed explicit formula, or a
closed boundary/index object.

This sharpens the live A3 -> A6/A7 transfer in
`source-selector-repair-obligation-ledger.md`: Euler positivity is source
orientation, not the repaired index.

## Boundary

This theorem does not describe the completed `xi` seam-log germ as a positive
mixture of these frequencies. It proves the opposite kind of routing fact: if a
proposed proof reduces the source theorem to such a positive frequency cone,
then it has chosen a cone disjoint from the Markov target except at zero.

## Novelty Status

The computation is elementary. Its significance is that it kills a whole family
of positive-frequency transfer scripts, not only the pointwise kernel script.

## Theatre Test

For any Euler-positive source proof:

1. Does the proof model the source as a positive measure over FE-even
   frequencies `cosh(lambda w)-1`?
2. Does it then identify the seam moments with a Markov/Stieltjes moment
   sequence on `R_+`?
3. If yes, the second shifted moment is negative unless the source measure is
   zero.
4. Which completed interaction moves the construction out of this alternating
   cone?

## 2026-06-24 finite signed atom upgrade

`finite-signed-even-frequency-atoms-not-markov.md` closes the finite signed real-atom escape. For a finite signed FE-even frequency sum, the largest remaining real frequency dominates the coefficient tail after equal frequencies are combined. The shifted moments therefore become negative on one tail parity.

Thus the obstruction is not only positivity of the frequency weights. Finite signed real-frequency counterterms still stay outside the Markov/Stieltjes target unless the whole sum is zero.

## 2026-06-24 finite-order local operators do not repair the cone

`finite-order-local-frequency-operators-not-markov.md` adds the coefficient-local variant. Multiplying the `z^k` coefficient of a positive FE-even frequency mixture by a nonzero real polynomial `q(k)` gives transformed shifted moments

`T_k^{q,mu}=(-1)^(k+1)q(k)M_k/(2k-1)!`, with `M_k>0`.

Since `q(k)` has a tail sign, one tail parity is eventually negative. Thus finite-order local differential or finite-part operators cannot repair the positive frequency cone into the Markov target.
