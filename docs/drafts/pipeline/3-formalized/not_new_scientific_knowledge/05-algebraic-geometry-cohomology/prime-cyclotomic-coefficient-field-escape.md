# Mahler coefficient-field confinement and prime-cyclotomic escape

- **ID:** `prime-cyclotomic-coefficient-field-escape`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM / BRIDGE / NOT NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED] [SEARCH] [NOT NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Statement:** If a power series with algebraic coefficients is differentially algebraic, then
  all of its coefficients lie in one number field. Consequently every positive-radius series whose
  coefficients introduce primitive roots of unity of pairwise distinct unbounded prime orders is
  hypertranscendental. In particular,

  ```text
  sum_(n>=0) zeta_(p_n) z^n/n!
  ```

  is an explicit entire HT function with algebraic coefficients and exactly the same Taylor
  coefficient moduli, Newton geometry, order, and type as `exp(z)`.

## Algebraic-coefficient confinement `[SEARCH]`

Let

```text
f(z)=sum_(n>=0) a_n z^n in Qbar[[z]]
```

be differentially algebraic over `C(z)`. Then there is a number field `K` such that

```text
a_n in K for every n>=0.                                  (1)
```

### Proof

A witnessing algebraic differential equation uses only finitely many complex constants. Every
constant is differentially algebraic over `Qbar(z)` because its derivative is zero. Transitivity
of differential algebraicity therefore makes `f` differentially algebraic over `Qbar(z)`. After
clearing denominators, `f` satisfies a nonzero algebraic differential equation

```text
Q(z,f,f',...,f^(r))=0,
Q in Qbar[z,Y_0,...,Y_r].                                  (2)
```

Mahler's coefficient theorem extracts from (2) an eventual nonlinear recurrence of fixed arity.
In the notation used by Krattenthaler--Rivoal, it has the form

```text
a_(n+1)
  = 1/M(n) sum_(sigma,k) sum_(j_1+...+j_k=n-sigma)
      P_(sigma,k)(n,j_1,...,j_k) a_(j_1)...a_(j_k),        (3)
```

where the ranges of `sigma` and `k` are fixed, `M` is one nonzero polynomial, and all coefficient
polynomials belong to one number field. The Sibuya--Sperber tail normalization removes negative
`sigma`; after a finite shift every `j_i` on the right of (3) is at most `n`. Since `M(n)` has only
finitely many integer zeros, (3) recursively places every sufficiently late coefficient in the
field generated by:

1. the finitely many algebraic constants occurring in the recurrence; and
2. the finitely many initial coefficients preceding the normalized recurrence.

Those generators are algebraic numbers, so their field is a number field. Enlarging it by the
finitely many omitted coefficients proves (1). `square`

This is the qualitative field-confinement consequence of the classical Mahler--Sibuya--Sperber
coefficient recurrence. No new coefficient-tail theorem is required.

## Prime-cyclotomic escape `[PROVED]`

Let `(p_n)` be pairwise distinct odd primes and let `u_n` be a primitive `p_n`-th root of unity.
Let `(r_n)` be any sequence of nonzero rational numbers for which

```text
F(z)=sum_(n>=0) r_n u_n z^n                               (4)
```

has positive radius of convergence. Then `F` is HT over `C(z)`.

### Proof

Suppose `F` were DA. Its coefficients are algebraic, so coefficient-field confinement would put
all `r_nu_n`, hence all `u_n`, in one number field `K`.

For every finite set `S` of the indices, coprimality of the conductors gives

```text
Q(u_n:n in S)=Q(zeta_(product_(n in S)p_n))
```

and therefore

```text
[Q(u_n:n in S):Q]=product_(n in S)(p_n-1).                (5)
```

These degrees are unbounded as `S` grows. A number field has finite degree over `Q`, so it cannot
contain all `u_n`. This contradicts confinement. Hence `F` is HT. `square`

## Explicit fixed-modulus consequences `[PROVED]`

Choose `p_n -> infinity` and primitive phases `u_n` as above.

1. The series

   ```text
   F_0(z)=sum_(n>=0) u_n z^n
   ```

   has radius one and is HT.

2. The series

   ```text
   F_1(z)=sum_(n>=0) u_n z^n/n!
   ```

   is entire and HT. Every coefficient is algebraic, and

   ```text
   |[z^n]F_1|=1/n!=|[z^n]exp(z)|.
   ```

   Thus `F_1` and the DA function `exp(z)` have identical Taylor moduli, complete logarithmic
   Newton geometry, order one, and exponential type one.

3. Every nonzero rational magnitude profile with positive convergence radius has an explicit
   algebraic cyclotomic HT phase realization.

## Exact route consequence

The theorem excludes a finite algebraic-differential state space from generating a coefficient
model which introduces genuinely new coprime cyclotomic fields indefinitely. It also proves that
Taylor magnitudes, Newton geometry, order, and type do not determine DA versus HT status.

It does not exclude Hilbert--Polya, Connes, de Branges, Nyman--Beurling, Weil positivity, or Li's
criterion. Those routes are affected only when a concrete implementation first replaces its
route-specific spectral, norm, positivity, Euler-product, or divisor data by the finite
coefficient-field or magnitude quotient classified here.

## Parent and scientific verdict

The confinement theorem is already contained in the nonlinear coefficient-recurrence theory of
Mahler and Sibuya--Sperber. The prime-cyclotomic corollary is an immediate application of
coprime-conductor cyclotomic degree multiplicativity. The explicit examples are useful, but their
proof introduces no mechanism beyond those parents and therefore fails the scientific-novelty
gate.
