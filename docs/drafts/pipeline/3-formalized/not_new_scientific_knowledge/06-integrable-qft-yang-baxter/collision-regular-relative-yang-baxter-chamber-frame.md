- **Statement:** For a regular factorizing scattering function with `S(0)=-F`, the standard
  relative chamber intertwiner between the scattering cocycle and its collision cocycle glues to

## Setup

Let `S_n^pi(theta)` be the `S_n` scattering cocycle and let `S(0)_n^pi` be the constant cocycle
obtained by replacing every adjacent generator by `S(0)`.  For the closed chamber

```text
Sigma_pi={theta : theta_(pi(1)) <= ... <= theta_(pi(n))},
```

define

```text
I_pi(theta)=S(0)_n^pi (S_n^pi(theta))^(-1).               (1)
```

Both cocycles use the same right-action convention.  Regularity means bounded analytic
continuation to an enlarged strip; in particular

```text
L=sup_(x in R) ||S'(x)|| < infinity.                       (2)
```

## Collision gluing theorem `[PROVED]`

If `theta in Sigma_pi intersection Sigma_sigma`, the permutations `pi` and `sigma` differ by an
element of the stabilizer of the sorted rapidity tuple.  That stabilizer is generated by adjacent
transpositions within blocks of equal coordinates.  On every such generator the scattering
cocycle is evaluated at rapidity difference zero and hence equals `S(0)`, exactly the target
generator.  The source and target cocycles therefore agree on the complete stabilizer.  Applying
the two cocycle laws to `(1)` gives

```text
I_pi(theta)=I_sigma(theta).                                (3)
```

The closed-chamber formulas consequently glue to a globally continuous frame `I_n` on `R^n`.
Every factor in `(1)` is unitary on the real locus, so

```text
I_n(theta)^* I_n(theta)=I                                  (4)
```

for every `theta`.  Thus the real frame has condition number one, including every collision
stratum.

## Global Lipschitz bound `[PROVED]`

On a fixed chamber, choose a reduced word for `pi`.  Its factors are indexed by the inversion set.
Differentiating the product in a direction `h`, using real-axis unitarity for all undifferentiated
factors and `(2)` for the differentiated factor, gives

```text
||D I_pi(theta)[h]||
  <= L sum_((i,j) in Inv(pi)) |h_i-h_j|
  <= L sqrt(n |Inv(pi)|) ||h||_2.                          (5)
```

The identity behind the last estimate is

```text
sum_(i<j) |h_i-h_j|^2 <= n ||h||_2^2.
```

Since `|Inv(pi)|<=n(n-1)/2`, every chamber has the common bound

```text
Lip_2(I_n) <= L n sqrt((n-1)/2).                           (6)
```

Subdivide a line segment at its finitely many chamber-wall crossings.  Continuity `(3)` and the
common chamber estimate `(6)` sum to the same global Lipschitz bound.

## Exact wall normal form and failure of `C^1`

For adjacent chambers with sorting permutations `p` and `p tau_k`, set

```text
u=(theta_p)_(k+1)-(theta_p)_k,
H_p(theta)=A_n^p(theta)^(-1),
```

in the identity-zero sign-twisted cocycle.  The cocycle law makes the two traces

```text
H_p(theta),
A_k(u)^(-1) H_p(theta).                                   (7)
```

They agree at the wall because `A_k(0)=I`.  Their normal-derivative jump is

```text
-A_k'(0) H_p(theta).                                      (8)
```

For `n=2`, one side is the identity and the other is `S(0)S(u)^(-1)`.  Hence the frame is not
`C^1` whenever `S'(0)` is nonzero.  The nonsmoothness is a genuine analytic seam, despite perfect
continuous conditioning.

## Literature correction and exact scope

The sentence following equation (3.169) of Alazzawi's thesis says that these chamber intertwiners
are in general not continuous.  For the relative target `S(0)` under the collision convention
`S(0)=-F`, equations `(3)`--`(4)` show that statement is false.  The later Alazzawi--Lechner paper
only says that the simple frame lacks the required analyticity; that statement is correct.

This theorem does **not** prove the intertwiner property.  Definition 5.2 requires a bounded
holomorphic frame on the shrinking complex tube, with exponential-in-`n` norm control and a
central inverse estimate.  Ordinary Oka theory and generic smoothing do not preserve those
estimates.  The theorem removes a qualitative obstruction and identifies the analytic seam; it
does not close it.
