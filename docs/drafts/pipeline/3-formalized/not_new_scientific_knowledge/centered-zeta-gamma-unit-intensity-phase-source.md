## Exact positive statement

Write

`R(w) = Gamma(1/4-w) / Gamma(1/4+w)`.

Then the genuine meromorphic frequency reflection is `R(-w)=R(w)^(-1)`.  Therefore

`R(w)R(-w)=1`

and

`R(w)/R(-w)=R(w)^2`.

The general four-positive-charge phase theorem proves the square is HT.  Thus the loss of all
Gamma information in intensity has a nontrivial positive complement: the entire centered
archimedean HT source survives in the orientation-sensitive phase, and no scalar-aware finite
differential circuit generated by rational clocks and unit intensity can reconstruct it.

Its logarithmic phase current `R'/R` also remains HT.  More precisely, it is the negative
Wigner--Smith current of the reciprocal frequency-reflected carrier.  This supplies a standard
derivative observation that retains the source after the modulus channel has collapsed.

The finite-generator obstruction is positive at both surviving channels.  Every finite rational
state field containing the phase, or containing the logarithmic current, has at least one chosen
generator whose derivative is outside that field.  No analogous conclusion is asserted for the
intensity: it is exactly `1` and therefore differentially algebraic.
