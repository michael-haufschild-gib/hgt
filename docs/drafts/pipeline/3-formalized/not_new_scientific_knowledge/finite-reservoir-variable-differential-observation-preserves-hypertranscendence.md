## Statement

Let `F -> E` be differential fields, let `S` be a derivation-closed intermediate field, and assume
that `trdeg_F(S) < aleph0`.  For an `F`-hypertranscendental element `y` and a nonzero finitely
supported coefficient packet `c_j in S`, Lean proves

```text
HypertranscendentalOver F y and c != 0
  -> HypertranscendentalOver F (sum_j c_j D^j y).             (1)
```

It also proves the finite-reservoir escape law

```text
sum_j c_j D^j y notin S.                                    (2)
```

The coefficients need not be constants: their derivatives may be nonzero.  What matters is that
the complete derivation-closed coefficient reservoir has finite transcendence degree over the
base.  This strictly extends the earlier constant-coefficient differential-operator theorem.

## Proof

Suppose the filtered output `z = sum_j c_j D^j y` were differentially algebraic over `F`.  It is
then differentially algebraic over `S`.  Adjoin `z` differentially to `S`, obtaining `T = S<z>_D`.
The nonzero coefficient packet embeds into `T`, and the defining equality for `z` becomes a
nonzero affine jet-linear differential relation on `y` over `T`.  Hence `y` is differentially
algebraic over `T`.  Differential-algebraic transitivity descends this first to `S`; the finite
transcendence-degree hypothesis on `S/F` then descends it to `F`, contradicting the assumed
hypertranscendence of `y`.  This proves `(1)`.

If the output belonged to `S`, derivation closure and finite transcendence degree would make it
differentially algebraic over `F`, contradicting `(1)`.  This proves `(2)`.

The proof is entirely constraint-based: it never solves or propagates the differential equation
encoded by the filter.

## Transfer from the added search fields

The common exact relation across systems biology, genomics, genetics, ML, and AI is not that their
signals merely resemble clocks.  It is that structural identifiability and model discovery encode
an observable by finite input--output differential equations whose coefficient field carries the
recoverable model information.

- Ovchinnikov--Pogudin--Thompson prove, for important linear ODE and compartment-model classes,
  that the identifiable field is generated by coefficients of input--output equations
  (arXiv:1910.03960).
- GenSSI and GenSSI 2.0 apply differential-algebraic structural-identifiability methods to systems
  biology models including gene regulation, signaling, metabolism, and an Arabidopsis circadian
  network (Bioinformatics 2011 and 2018).
- Loman--Browning--Baker extend structural identifiability from parameters to unknown functional
  components, including neural-ODE and biological model discovery, using differential-algebraic
  elimination (arXiv:2606.30289).
- Scholl--Bacho--Boche--Kutyniok isolate uniqueness of the governing differential equation as the
  identifiability problem in ML-based symbolic equation recovery (arXiv:2210.08342).

Equations `(1)` and `(2)` therefore give a useful exact boundary for these fields: if an exact
biological or learned observation uses a nonzero finite jet filter with coefficients in a
finite-transcendence derivation-closed model field, an HT latent clock remains HT after
observation and the result cannot be represented inside that model field.  This does not reject
finite approximation or finite noisy data analysis.  Positively, it identifies an entire class of
observation maps under which infinite differential structure is faithfully retained.

## Physics and quantum transfer

Picard--Fuchs and D-module equations for Feynman integrals are variable-coefficient differential
operators, as developed by Mueller-Stach--Weinzierl--Zayadeh (arXiv:1212.4389),
Lairez--Vanhove (arXiv:2209.10962), and later twisted-form treatments.  The same algebraic
interface appears in response equations and constraint reductions in quantum field theory and
quantum cosmology.  The theorem does not infer that a physical period is HT.  It says that once an
HT source has been established, no nonzero operator whose coefficients remain in a finite
differential-algebraic reservoir can erase that fact.  The positive statement surviving the
no-go is a larger certified class of HT observables, not merely a prohibition on finite models.
