# RG-tower differential incoherence obstructs a finite exact generator

- **ID:** `rg-tower-differential-incoherence-obstruction`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / GENERAL RG-HT REDUCTION

  `[NOT NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Statement:** For an arbitrary analytically linearizable one-coupling RG germ, differential
  algebraicity of its exact beta field forces all discrete RG iterates to satisfy one common
  autonomous algebraic differential equation.  Therefore differential incoherence of the RG tower
  proves RG-generation HT, in every DA coupling scheme.

## Setup

Let `f` be an analytic germ at a hyperbolic fixed point `c`, with multiplier `lambda`, and let
`Phi` be a locally invertible linearizing germ:

```text
Phi(0)=c,          Phi'(0)=1,
f(Phi(z))=Phi(lambda z),              0<|lambda| != 1.           (1)
```

Write `kappa=Phi^(-1)`.  After choosing a logarithm of `lambda`, define

```text
beta(t)=(log lambda) kappa(t)/kappa'(t).                          (2)
```

No rationality, algebraicity, meromorphic continuation, or finite-dimensional parent map is
assumed.

## Definition: differential coherence

A family of analytic germs `{h_a}` is **differentially coherent** if there exist an integer `r` and
a nonzero polynomial `P in C[X_0,...,X_r]`, independent of `a`, such that

```text
P(h_a,h_a',...,h_a^(r))=0                                       (3)
```

for every member of the family.  The equation is autonomous: its coefficients do not depend on
the coupling variable.

## Coherence obstruction theorem

If `beta` is differentially algebraic over `C(t)`, then the RG tower

```text
{f^n : n >= 0}                                                   (4)
```

is differentially coherent.  Consequently,

```text
{f^n} differentially incoherent  ==>  beta is HT.                (5)
```

### Proof

First, `beta` is DA if and only if `kappa` is DA.  One implication follows immediately from `(2)`.
For the other, if `beta` is DA then `kappa` satisfies the first-order differential equation

```text
beta kappa'-(log lambda)kappa=0.                                 (6)
```

Thus `kappa` is differentially algebraic over the differential field generated by `beta`, hence
over `C(t)` by transitivity.  Local compositional inversion preserves differential algebraicity, so
`kappa` is DA if and only if `Phi` is DA.

Iterating `(1)` gives

```text
f^n(t)=Phi(lambda^n kappa(t)).                                   (7)
```

The family of linear germs `{lambda^n z}` satisfies the common autonomous equation `y''=0`.
The Boshernitzan--Rubel closure theorem for autonomous algebraic differential equations says that
composition with fixed DA germs preserves coherence, with a resulting equation depending only on
the equations for the input families.  Apply it first to `{Phi(lambda^n z)}` and then compose on
the right with the fixed DA germ `kappa`.  Equation `(7)` then supplies one autonomous algebraic
differential equation shared by every `f^n`.  This proves `(5)`.

## DA-scheme invariance

Let `u=h(t)` be a locally invertible DA scheme with DA inverse and

```text
f_h=h o f o h^(-1).                                               (8)
```

Then

```text
f_h^n=h o f^n o h^(-1).                                          (9)
```

The same closure theorem, applied in both directions, proves

```text
{f^n} is coherent  <=>  {f_h^n} is coherent.                    (10)
```

Thus differential incoherence is an intrinsic obstruction throughout the physical DA scheme
category, not a coordinate artifact.

## Physical meaning

The exact generator is HT if the entire tower of block transformations cannot be placed inside one
finite autonomous algebraic differential law.  This gives RG-generation HT a direct operational
meaning:

```text
finite ADE generation fails uniformly across physical scale.    (11)
```

The theorem is deliberately one-way for a general analytic germ.  It does not claim that every
coherent analytic RG tower has a DA linearizer.  For rational maps, Ritt and
Becker--Bergweiler supply the sharper classification recorded in
`exact-rational-rg-generation-dichotomy.md`.

## Global sufficient conditions

The reduction imports two known global routes.

1. If `f` extends rationally, Ritt's exceptional families classify the coherent branch.
2. If `f` extends to a transcendental entire function, Bergweiler's negative solution of Rubel's
   problem says that its iterates cannot form a coherent family.  Hence any repelling Poincare
   function and the exact beta field are HT.

For a constructive QFT germ, proving either a nonexceptional rational continuation or a
transcendental-entire continuation therefore closes the HT problem.  If neither continuation is
available, the physical task is to prove incoherence directly from the all-scale source or shell
hierarchy.

## Relation to finite-jet blindness

`finite-rg-jet-blindness-to-hypertranscendence.md` proves that every finite jet of `(f,beta)` has an
exact algebraic completion.  There is no contradiction: coherence is a uniform statement about
**all** iterates and all derivative orders occurring in one fixed ADE.  It is precisely the
nonperturbative datum that no finite list of coefficients can see.

## Lean route (opened 2026-08-30) — replacing Boshernitzan–Rubel by a transcendence-degree argument

The card's proof routes the composition step through the Boshernitzan--Rubel closure theorem.  That
theorem is not needed: for **this** family the common autonomous equation can be produced directly.

### The mechanism

Write the tower in linearized form, `f^n = Phi o (lambda^n . kappa)` with `kappa = Phi^(-1)`.  By
Faa di Bruno there are **universal** polynomials `Xi_k` (independent of `n`) with

```text
(f^n)^(k)(t) = Xi_k( Phi(w), ..., Phi^(k)(w);  mu;  kappa'(t), ..., kappa^(k)(t) ),
      w = mu kappa(t),  mu = lambda^n.                                    (12)
```

Let `p` bound the transcendence degree of the field generated by the `Phi`-jets and `q` that of the
`kappa`-jets; both are finite exactly because `Phi` and `kappa` are differentially algebraic.  Set
`d = p + q + 1`.  In any ultraproduct of the germ field along an ultrafilter on the index set `n`,
the germs of the three groups in `(12)` generate a field of transcendence degree at most `d`
(subadditivity; each group is a homomorphic image of the corresponding jet field, and `mu` costs
one).  Hence the `d+1` germs of `(f^n)^(0), ..., (f^n)^(d)` are algebraically dependent **over the
constants**, giving a nonzero autonomous `Q` valid for `u`-almost every `n`.

A compactness step turns "almost every" into "every": the sets `A_Q = {n : Q annihilates the jets of
f^n}` cover the index set, since otherwise their complements have the finite intersection property
and generate an ultrafilter on which no `A_Q` lies.  Finitely many `A_Q` therefore cover, and the
product of the corresponding `Q`'s is one nonzero autonomous equation shared by the whole tower.

The parameter `mu` is what makes the equation `n`-uniform; a per-`n` transcendence count alone gives
only a per-`n` equation.  This replaces the Boshernitzan--Rubel closure theorem entirely.
