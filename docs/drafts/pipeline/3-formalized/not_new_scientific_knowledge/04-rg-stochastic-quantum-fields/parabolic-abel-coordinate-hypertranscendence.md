# Abel coordinates of an interior tree channel at the parabolic parameter are hypertranscendental

- **Stage:** 3-formalized / not new scientific knowledge (Becker–Bergweiler 1995)
- **Serves:** 04 cards `gadc-tree-population-rg-clock-hypertranscendence` and
  `noisy-binary-tree-channel-rg-clock-hypertranscendence`, regime II (`λ = 1`).
- **Statement.** Let `R` be an interior tree channel map (`−1 < v < u < 1`, `p ≥ 2`) with
  `p(u − v)/2 = 1`.  If `D` is open connected, `R(D) ⊆ D`, `A` analytic on `D`, `A∘R = A + 1` on `D`
  and `A` nonconstant, then `A` satisfies no algebraic differential equation on any nonempty open
  subset of `D` (hence neither does the sectorial beta `1/A'`).

## Route (Becker–Bergweiler through Boshernitzan–Rubel, made algebraic)

**P1 — one equation for infinitely many iterates.**  `V = A'` is DA of minimal order `m`
(`(z, V, …, V^{(m−1)})` algebraically independent over `ℂ`).  In the field `Ω` of meromorphic
functions on `D`, `V∘Rⁿ = V/(Rⁿ)'` and `V^{(j)}∘Rⁿ` is a universal rational expression in the
`V`-jets and `Rⁿ`-jets, linear in `(Rⁿ)^{(j+1)}` with coefficient `−V/((Rⁿ)')^{2j+1} ≠ 0`.
Precomposition `f ↦ f∘Rⁿ` is a field endomorphism of `Ω`, so the algebraic relations of the
`V`-jets transport with nonzero leading coefficients.  In the ultrapower `Ω* = Ω^ℕ/𝒰` (a field) the
diagonal `V`-jets and the sequences `[(Rⁿ)^{(i)}]` generate a field algebraic over the `2m + 2`
elements `z, V_{<m}, [Rⁿ]_{≤ m}`; so `z, [Rⁿ], …, [(Rⁿ)^{(2m+1)}]` are algebraically dependent:
one nonzero `T` with `T(z, Rⁿ, …, (Rⁿ)^{(2m+1)}) = 0` for `𝒰`-many (infinitely many) `n`.

**P2 — the formal flow.**  At the parabolic point the Taylor coefficients of `Rⁿ` are polynomials
`p_j(n)` (`c_j(n+1) − c_j(n)` is a polynomial in the lower coefficients).  Vanishing for infinitely
many `n` makes `T(z, R^t, ∂R^t, …) = 0` in `ℂ[t][[z]]`; the lowest `t`-order part gives
`T_d(X, X', …) = 0` for the formal generator `X = Σ p_j'(0) z^j = a z^{k+1} + … ≠ 0`, which satisfies
`X∘R = R'·X`.  So `v̂ = 1/X` is DA over `ℂ(z)` with `σv̂ = v̂/R'`.

**P3 — the formal frame.**  `L = ℂ((z))`, `σ` = substitution of the expansion of `R`, `d = d/dz`,
`ℂ(z) ↪ L`.  `fixed`: a Laurent series with `f(R) = f` is constant (lowest term: `m a z^{m+k} = 0`).
The normal form (`RittLogSeedNormalForm`) gives `v^M ∈ K`, an invariant affine connection or an
invariant projective connection; `ratTransport_of_invariant_connection` and
`exists_invariant_connection_of_invariant_projective` (no fixed point needed) give a cocycle,
which `treeRG_no_cocycle_interior` refutes.
