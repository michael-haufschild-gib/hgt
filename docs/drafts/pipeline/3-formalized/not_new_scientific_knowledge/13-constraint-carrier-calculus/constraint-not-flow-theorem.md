  > **(a)** The prime torus carries a continuous Kronecker flow. Its finite Fourier characters have
  > frequencies `sum_p m_p log p`, with no nonzero finite resonance, and every finite prime aperture

  > **(b)** No bounded continuous symbol on the compact prime torus, and no finite-measure average of

## §1 Canonical prime flow and its exact obstruction `[PROVED] [LEAN]`

Source B's canonical dynamics is the **Kronecker flow** on the boundary `𝕋^∞`: `φ_τ : x_p ↦ x_p e^{-iτ log p}`,
generator `∂_t` (translation of `t = Im s`). Its spectrum (the eigenvalues of `∂_t` on Fourier modes) is
`{m·ℓ = Σ m_p log p} = ` the **prime-power frequencies**. On axis modes this is exactly `{log p}`. By
unique factorization the finite-character flow is free, and every finite aperture is dense by
Kronecker--Weyl—an RH-blind statement.

The exact noncoincidence theorem available here concerns observables, not numerical spectra. Every
continuous observable on the compact prime torus is almost periodic along the flow. The completed
critical-line amplitude tends to zero at positive infinity but is nonzero at the centre. Therefore
no continuous prime-screen symbol, nor any finite-measure translated continuous observable, equals
`XiC(t)` for all real `t`. No unproved claim that `{γ_n}` is disjoint from
`{sum_p m_p log p}` is used. ∎

## §2 Hilbert--Pólya scope correction `[AUDITED; NOT A THEOREM HERE]`

The former argument “self-adjointness = positivity, therefore every zero-spectrum operator is
archimedean” was invalid: a self-adjoint operator need not be a positive operator, and “Source B”
was not defined as a formal operator category. Identifying the canonical prime-flow frequencies also
does not prove numerical disjointness from every zero ordinate. These claims are removed from the
theorem. The proved replacement is the compact-prime-screen observable obstruction in §1.

## §3 What `Λ` is fixed by: the FE constraint `[PROVED] [LEAN]`

The completed object satisfies `Λ(s) = Λ(1−s)` — i.e. `Λ` is **fixed by the involution** `ι : s↦1−s`. `ι` is:
- a **constraint** (a *relation* the object obeys: `Λ = Λ∘ι`), not a propagator;
- **order 2** (`ι² = id`): the subgroup generated by this distinguished symmetry is `ℤ/2`;
- **archimedean** (it is the FE, intertwined by `Γ` — orthogonality meta-theorem §1), with fixed locus
  `Fix(ι) = {1/2}`. The critical line is instead `Fix(ι∘conj) = {Re s = 1/2}`.

Thus the functional equation is a relation that the completed object obeys, not an evolution law
selected by the prime Kronecker dynamics. Order two alone is not a no-flow theorem: explicitly,
rotation about `1/2` by angle `pi*t` is an affine `R`-flow and its time-one map is `ι`. The exact
conclusion is that the FE supplies a distinguished constraint; no categorical absence of all flows
is claimed. ∎

## §4 The strong version, tested — and where it FAILS `[proof-or-fail]`

**Strong conjecture (tested):** *the zeros are no spectrum at all* — Hilbert–Pólya is not merely Source-B-impossible
but categorically impossible. **This FAILS.** **Connes (1999)** realizes `{γ_n}` as the **absorption spectrum**
of the idele-class-group action on the **adele class space** `𝔸_ℚ/ℚ^×`. So the zeros **are** a spectrum.

Connes' space contains the archimedean place and therefore lies outside the compact prime-screen
observable model excluded in §1. This is compatible with the corrected theorem, but does not prove
that every possible prime-built spectral model fails. The categorical "no spectrum" and universal
"no Source-B operator" claims remain rejected. ∎(fail → precise scope)

## §5 Consequence and scope

- **HP search implication.** A bounded continuous observable of the canonical compact prime torus
  cannot be the completed Xi amplitude. Any proposal in that category is closed.
- **Constraint implication.** The functional equation and its Schwarz composite give exact
  reflection constraints and fixed loci. They do not by themselves rule out operator flows.
- **Scope.** Proved unconditionally: finite-character prime frequencies and no resonance, finite-
  aperture Kronecker density, compact-prime-screen observable obstruction, completed FE, and exact
  fixed loci. Not proved: universal Hilbert--Pólya impossibility, zero-ordinate/prime-frequency
  disjointness, or positivity from self-adjointness.

## 2026-06-24 (Turing) — ERROR in §3: Fix(ι) is the POINT ½, not the critical line

§3 states "ι … with fixed locus Fix(ι) = {Re s = ½} = the critical line." This is mathematically wrong and
inconsistent with the card's own Lean anchors.

- ι : s ↦ 1−s. Fix(ι) = {s : s = 1−s} = {½} — a **single point**.
- The critical line {Re s = ½} is **Fix(ι∘conj)**, the fixed locus of s ↦ 1−s̄ (s = 1−s̄ ⟺ s+s̄ = 1 ⟺ Re s = ½).

The card cites (§2→3 gate) exactly these two Lean theorems, which state it correctly:
`sigma_fixed_iff_eq_half` : σ(s)=s ↔ s = ½  (the POINT), and
`sigmaConj_fixed_iff_critical` : (σ∘conj)(s)=s ↔ Re s = ½  (the LINE).
(SigmaOrbit.lean's own docstring: "σ fixes only the center point ½; the full critical line is the fixed
locus of σ composed with complex conjugation.") So §3 contradicts its formal anchor.

**Correction:** §3 should read — Λ is fixed by the order-2 FE-involution ι (Λ = Λ∘ι), whose fixed locus is
the single center point Fix(ι) = {½}; the **critical line** is Fix(ι∘conj), and **RH = the zeros lie on
Fix(ι∘conj)**. The "constraint not flow" thrust is unaffected (ι is still an order-2 ℤ/2 relation, not an ℝ
flow), but the constraint that pins the critical line is ι together with conjugation (the reflection across
Re=½), not ι alone. The G5 phrasing in §5 ("RH = ι unitary ⟺ zeros real ⟺ on the line") is consistent with
this once ι's own fixed locus is correctly stated as the point ½.
