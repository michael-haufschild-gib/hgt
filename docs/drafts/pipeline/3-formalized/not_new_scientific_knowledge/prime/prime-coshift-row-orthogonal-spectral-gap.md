# The all-prime co-shift row has a uniform orthogonal spectral gap

- **ID:** `prime-coshift-row-orthogonal-spectral-gap`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper); source-constraint coercivity
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED HERE] [BRIDGE; NOT NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE]`
- **Source:** `prime-coshift-eigenline-regularized-boundary-complex.md` and
  `prime-coshift-additive-trace-coercivity-conjecture.md`
- **Statement:** If `Re(s)=sigma>1/2`, the canonical all-prime row

  ```text
  R_s x=((V_p^*-p^(-s))x)_p
  ```

  has a spectral gap on the orthogonal complement of its arithmetic ground state
  `Psi_s=(n^(-s))`:

  ```text
  ||R_s x||^2
    >=(1-2^(-sigma))^2 ||x-P_s x||^2,                  (1)
  ```

  for every `x` in `Dom(R_s)`, where `P_s` is the orthogonal projection onto
  `C Psi_s`.  The constant is uniform when `s` ranges over a compact subset of
  `Re(s)>1/2`.

## 1. The one-prime reduced minimum modulus

Fix a prime `p` and put

```text
a=p^(-s),       |a|=p^(-sigma)<1,
T_p=V_p^*-aI.                                             (2)
```

Decompose the positive integers uniquely as

```text
n=p^k m,       k>=0,       p does not divide m.            (3)
```

On each fixed `p`-free ray `{m,pm,p^2m,...}`, `V_p` is the unilateral shift `S` on
`l2(N_0)`, and `T_p` is `S^*-aI`.  Its kernel is the geometric line generated by

```text
g_a=(1,a,a^2,...).                                        (4)
```

Let `P_p` be the orthogonal projection onto `ker(T_p)` on the full direct sum of
the rays `(3)`.

`[PROVED]` One has

```text
||T_p x|| >=(1-|a|)||x-P_p x||.                          (5)
```

Indeed,

```text
T_p^*=S-conj(a)I
```

on every ray, so the reverse triangle inequality and the isometry of `S` give

```text
||T_p^*y||>=||Sy||-|a|||y||=(1-|a|)||y||.                (6)
```

Thus `T_p T_p^*>=(1-|a|)^2 I`; in particular `T_p` is surjective and
`ran(T_p^*)=ker(T_p)^perp` is closed.  Applying the same lower spectral bound to
`T_p T_p^*` shows that `T_p` is bounded below by `1-|a|` on
`ker(T_p)^perp`, which is `(5)`.  Taking the Hilbert direct sum over all `p`-free
rays proves `(5)` for the multiplicative co-shift.

Since `p>=2`,

```text
1-|a|=1-p^(-sigma)>=1-2^(-sigma).                        (7)
```

## 2. Commuting prime ground projections

For distinct primes `p,q`, the decompositions by `v_p(n)` and `v_q(n)` are
independent.  Under the canonical factorization

```text
l2(N_(>=1))
  = tensor_(r prime) l2(N_0),                             (8)
```

`P_p` acts only on the `p`-adic exponent coordinate and `P_q` only on the
`q`-adic coordinate.  Hence

```text
P_p P_q=P_q P_p.                                         (9)
```

This can equivalently be checked directly from the geometric-ray projection
formula; the two absolutely convergent geometric sums commute.

For a finite prime set `F`, commuting orthogonal projections satisfy

```text
I-product_(p in F)P_p <=sum_(p in F)(I-P_p).             (10)
```

As `F` increases through the finite prime sets, the products in `(10)` decrease
strongly to the projection onto the intersection of all one-prime kernels.  The
common-eigenline theorem identifies that intersection as

```text
intersection_p ker(V_p^*-p^(-s)I)=C Psi_s.               (11)
```

Therefore

```text
product_p P_p=P_s                                       (12)
```

in the strong-limit sense.

## 3. Global row gap

For `x in Dom(R_s)`, sum the squares of `(5)` and use `(7)`:

```text
||R_s x||^2
 =sum_p ||T_p x||^2
 >=(1-2^(-sigma))^2 sum_p ||(I-P_p)x||^2.               (13)
```

Equations `(10)`--`(12)` give

```text
sum_p ||(I-P_p)x||^2>=||(I-P_s)x||^2.                   (14)
```

Combining `(13)` and `(14)` proves `(1)`.

If `K` is compact in `Re(s)>1/2` and

```text
sigma_K=inf_(s in K) Re(s)>1/2,
```

then `(1)` holds uniformly on `K` with the positive constant

```text
lambda_K=(1-2^(-sigma_K))^2.                            (15)
```

## 4. Consequence for the fringe RH chain

The two-block reduction in `prime-coshift-additive-trace-coercivity-conjecture.md`
had two proposed inputs.  The first is now unconditional:

```text
orthogonal prime-row gap.                                (16)
```

The spectral half of the proposed chain would have been

```text
canonical closed additive trace B_s on Dom(R_s)
  -> mixed Green/boundary estimate on C Psi_s plus its orthogonal complement
  -> |zeta(s)|^2
       >=c_K |Re(s)-1/2|^2 zeta(2Re(s))
  -> no zero with 1/2<Re(s)<1
  -> RH.                                                 (17)
```

The gap theorem is pre-divisor and phase-sensitive only through the specified
prime characters.  It does not itself involve `zeta(s)`, its continuation, or
its zeros.

The companion theorem
`prime-coshift-row-boundary-trace-nonclosable.md` refutes the next arrow in `(17)`: ordinary
finite-support additive evaluation is not closable in the row graph norm.  Thus `(1)` survives as
source-constraint information, but this exact scalar-trace RH chain is retired.  A successor must
add an independent analytic direction or an additional source-defined unbounded boundary
operator before scalar evaluation.

## Lean decision

The proof uses standard Hilbert-space ingredients, but the repository does not
yet contain the concrete `l2(N)` multiplicative shifts, their `p`-adic ray
decomposition, or the closed all-prime row.  Formalizing an abstract projection
inequality alone would be wrapper infrastructure.  Lean work should begin only
with the concrete co-shift model and should expose `(1)` as its endpoint.
