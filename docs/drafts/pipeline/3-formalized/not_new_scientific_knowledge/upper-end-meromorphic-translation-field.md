## Geometric object

For `H in R`, put

```text
U_H={z in C : Im z>H}.
```

The upper-end filter is generated by the directed family `(U_H)_H`.  Define `A_infty` to be germs
of complex functions at this filter which have an analytic representative on some `U_H`.  Two
representatives are equal exactly when they agree on some higher tail.  Define

```text
M_infty=Frac(A_infty).
```

The native translation law is

```text
(z |-> z+h)^(-1)(U_H)=U_(H-Im h).                           (1)
```

Hence every complex translation is an automorphism of the same upper end.  Differentiation also
preserves eventual analyticity and commutes with (1).

## Why this carrier is necessary

The full countable palette `Q(i)+sum_v Z omega_v` has imaginary parts unbounded in both directions.
No fixed upper half-plane contains the analytic domains of all translates.  A global meromorphic
quotient would require an unnecessary Weierstrass representation of the infinite prime-log pole
divisor.  The upper-end field retains exactly the common-domain information used by every finite
relation and identifies all sufficiently high choices canonically.

This is a constraint carrier, not an evolution: height is discarded by the germ equivalence and
does not propagate the source.

## Signed two-ended law and RH consequence

The single-ended translation geometry has a formally proved two-ended completion.  If `F_+` and
`F_-` are the upper- and lower-end filters and `R(z)=1-z`, then

```text
R : F_+ <-> F_-,                  R^2=1,
R D = -D R.                                                   (2)
```

The first law is exact filter geometry: reflection reverses imaginary height.  The second is
proved on the eventual-analytic domains and on their full meromorphic fraction fields.  In
contrast, every translation satisfies `T_h D=D T_h` on one end.  Thus the joint carrier has a
signed affine differential geometry: translations are degree `+1`, while critical reflection is
degree `-1` for the derivation.

This gives a positive RH-facing requirement without making the source theorem depend on RH:

> **Two-ended sewing requirement.** A zeta-sensitive HT carrier must add an intrinsic sewing of the
> upper and lower translation ends carrying the signed law (2), together with an arithmetic
> pointing that distinguishes the Euler-product source.  A one-ended generic translation field
> supplies HT capacity, but the functional-equation polarization necessarily lives in the sewing
> data between the two ends.

The statement does not prove RH.  It locates where an RH mechanism can live: not in finite
differential relations of a freely chosen upper-end source, but in a global compatibility between
the two end germs, the reflection involution, and the Euler marking.

## Attack

  zeros.  The analytic-subring restriction and fraction field are essential.
- Translation cannot be defined on one fixed `U_H` for all shifts; it becomes canonical only after
  passage to the directed upper end.
- The final independence statement must be proved by equality of field evaluations with the
  already-formalized cleared functional predicate.  Merely assuming that compatibility would be a
  wrapper and would not close the route.
