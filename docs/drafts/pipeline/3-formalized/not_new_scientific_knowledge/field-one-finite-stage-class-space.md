## Theorem

For every finite set \(S_f\) of finite places of \(\mathbb Q\), define
\[
  \mathbb A_S
    =\mathbb R\times\prod_{v\in S_f}\mathbb Q_v,
  \qquad
  \mathbb A_S^\times
    =\mathbb R^\times\times\prod_{v\in S_f}\mathbb Q_v^\times.
\]
The rational subgroup generated by \(-1\) and primes in \(S_f\) embeds
diagonally and injectively into \(\mathbb A_S^\times\). Its image acts
coordinatewise on \(\mathbb A_S\). The full idele action preserves its orbit
relation, and the diagonal subgroup lies in the action kernel on
\[
  \mathbb Z_S^\times\backslash\mathbb A_S.
\]
Consequently scaling factors through the semilocal idele class group
\[
  C_{\mathbb Q,S}
    =\mathbb Z_S^\times\backslash\mathbb A_S^\times.
\]

The orbit projection is an open quotient map. Every finite-stage idele and
every semilocal idele class acts on the orbit quotient by a homeomorphism.

## No-go closure

- **Exact killing mechanism:** the previously completed
  \(L^2(\mathbb R\times\mathbb A_f)\) has the wrong carrier and action for a
  finite-\(S\) trace fiber. It cannot be reused by renaming.
- **Strongest positive survivor:** finite-stage orbit and multiplicative
  class-action types now exist unconditionally and independently of the
  global additive Weyl representation.
- **New insight:** open quotient topology does not construct the trace
  measure. Child card `field-one-finite-stage-module` now formalizes the norm
  character and its rational \(S\)-unit product formula. Additive Haar
  invariance still requires identification of that norm with the local and
  finite-product Haar scaling characters; quotient measure then requires a
  fundamental-domain or disintegration construction.
- **Status:** algebraic, topological, and norm-module stages formalized.
  Haar-character identification, measure, `L²`, crossed product, compatible
  restriction maps, sheaf cohomology, Hodge positivity, and RH remain open.
