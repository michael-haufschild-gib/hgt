# Bigraded corank tower factorization

- **ID:** bigraded-corank-tower-factorization
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Priority:** FOUNDATIONAL
- **Source:** split from `../2-fully-proven/bigraded-corank-dss-cross.md` §G
- **Statement:** For two disjoint indexed families in a field extension, joint algebraic independence is equivalent to algebraic independence of the first family over the base field together with algebraic independence of the second family over the field generated by the first.

## Goal
This banks the exact tower-law algebra behind the DSS-cross factorization reduction. It does not formalize prime logarithms, Stieltjes constants, infinite corank, exponential motives, or the DSS-cross apex.

## Paper Proof
Let `x : I -> E` and `y : J -> E` be families in a field extension `E/F`. Mathlib represents their disjoint union as `Sum.elim y x : J ⊕ I -> E`.

If the union is algebraically independent over `F`, then any subfamily is algebraically independent, so `x` is independent over `F`. A polynomial relation among finitely many `y_j` with coefficients in `F(x)` can be cleared of denominators to give a nonzero polynomial relation over `F` among finitely many elements of `x` and `y`, contradicting joint independence.

Conversely, if `x` is algebraically independent over `F` and `y` is algebraically independent over `F(x)`, then the transcendence tower law gives algebraic independence of the disjoint union over `F`.
