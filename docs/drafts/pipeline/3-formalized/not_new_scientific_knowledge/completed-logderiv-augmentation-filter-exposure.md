## Goal

Identify an unconditional law saying that no nonzero finite shift filter can turn a mixed
completed logarithmic carrier into a differential-algebraic handle.  The law is about mixed
hypertranscendental carriers in general.  The xi specialization is the first exact instance
and repairs the false finite-prime-field reduction recorded in
`5-failed/flow-constraint-asymmetry.md` and
`0-backlog/lambda-difference-differential-hypertranscendence.md`.

The RH consequence is deliberately limited: this rules out every proposed implementation
whose decisive step is any nonzero finite constant-coefficient `s -> s+2` filter followed by
a DA or finite-state residual.  It does not
rule out the full Hilbert--Polya, Connes, de Branges, Nyman--Beurling, Weil, or Li criteria.

## General augmentation-exposure law

Let `K` be the differential field of meromorphic functions DA over `C(s)`, stable under the
translation `T_h f(s)=f(s+h)`.  Suppose

`u = a + b`,

where:

1. `T_h^k a-a` belongs to `K` for every nonnegative integer `k`;
2. on a right half-plane,

   `b(s) = sum_(n>=1) beta_n n^(-s)`;

3. for every nonzero polynomial `P` under consideration with `P(1)=0`, the filtered
   coefficient sequence `beta_n P(n^(-h))` has support whose indices involve infinitely
   many distinct prime divisors.

Then `P(T_h)u` is HT over `C(s)` for every such `P`.

### Proof `[PROVED HERE]`

Write `P(X)=sum_(k=0)^m c_k X^k`.  Since `P(1)=sum c_k=0`,

`P(T_h)a = sum_k c_k (T_h^k a-a)`

belongs to `K`.  Absolute convergence permits translation term by term, so

`P(T_h)b(s) = sum_(n>=1) beta_n P(n^(-h)) n^(-s)`.

By the infinite-prime-support hypothesis and Ostrowski's theorem for ordinary Dirichlet
series, this filtered series is HT.  If `P(T_h)u` were DA, subtracting the DA function
`P(T_h)a` would make `P(T_h)b` DA, a contradiction.  Thus `P(T_h)u` is HT.  QED.

The theorem is a constraint law, not a flow: `P(1)=0` is exactly the augmentation ideal of
the translation group ring, and every nonzero augmentation filter exposes an arithmetic
HT residual.

## Constraint-propagation bootstrap `[PROVED HERE]`

The augmentation conclusion automatically propagates to every nonzero shift polynomial.
Suppose `K` is stable under `T_h` and every nonzero polynomial `A` with `A(1)=0` satisfies
`A(T_h)u notin K`.  Let `0 != P in C[X]`.  If `P(T_h)u` belonged to `K`, then shift stability
and field closure would give

`(T_h-1)P(T_h)u in K`.

But `(X-1)P(X)` is nonzero and vanishes at `X=1`, contradicting augmentation exposure.
Therefore

`P(T_h)u notin K` for every nonzero `P`.

For `K=DA(C(s))`, every nonzero finite shift filter of `u` is HT.  In quotient-module
language, the cyclic `C[T_h]`-module generated by the class of `u` in `Mer(C)/K` is free of
rank one.  Equivalently, the translate family `{T_h^k u}` is linearly independent modulo
the entire DA field, not merely linearly independent over `C`.

## Riemann xi specialization

Put

`u(s)=xi'(s)/xi(s)`

and, on `Re(s)>1`, split

`u(s)=a(s)+z(s)`

with

`a(s)=1/s+1/(s-1)-log(pi)/2+digamma(s/2)/2`

and

`z(s)=zeta'(s)/zeta(s)=-sum_(n>=2) Lambda(n)n^(-s)`.

For every `k>=0`, the digamma recurrence gives

`digamma(s/2+k)=digamma(s/2)+2 sum_(j=0)^(k-1) 1/(s+2j)`.

Consequently `a(s+2k)-a(s)` is rational.  Hence `P(T)a` is rational whenever
`P(1)=0`.

For the prime component,

`P(T)z(s)=-sum_(n>=2) Lambda(n) P(n^(-2)) n^(-s)`.

At a prime `p`, its coefficient is

`-log(p) P(p^(-2))`.

The numbers `p^(-2)` are pairwise distinct and tend to zero.  A nonzero polynomial has
only finitely many roots, so this coefficient is nonzero for all but finitely many primes.
The support therefore involves infinitely many distinct prime divisors.  Ostrowski applies,
making `P(T)z` HT.  The general law now proves

`P(T)(xi'/xi) is HT`

for every nonzero `P` with `P(1)=0`.  The constraint-propagation bootstrap then removes the
augmentation restriction and proves the statement for **every** nonzero `P in C[X]`.

Taking `P=X-1` gives the explicit source-exposure identity

`u(s+2)-u(s) - (1/(s+2)+1/(s+1)-1/(s-1))`

`= sum_(n>=2) Lambda(n)(1-n^(-2))n^(-s)`.

The right side has a nonzero coefficient at every prime.  The bootstrap applied to `P=1`
shows in particular that `xi'/xi` is HT.  The standard logarithmic-derivative equivalence
then gives:

**Corollary.** `xi` is hypertranscendental over `C(s)`.

No assertion about the location of xi's zeros or RH is used.

## Exact route exclusions

The theorem rules out the following precise mechanism shared by otherwise different RH
programs:

1. take finitely many even translates of the completed logarithmic derivative;
2. combine them with any nontrivial constant coefficients;
3. claim that the resulting object is DA, holonomic, P-recursive after a standard
   generating transform, or the output of a fixed finite algebraic state system.

Step 3 is impossible for every nonzero filter, whether or not it cancels the common gamma mode.
This applies only when a concrete proposed
Hilbert--Polya, Connes, de Branges, Weil, Li, or Nyman--Beurling implementation contains this
finite-filter step.  The named infinite-dimensional or RH-equivalent routes themselves remain
live.
