## Statement

Let `F_a` be the finite real-characteristic mode space of the exact CCM aperture symbol and let
`H_a` be its infinite Gamma boundary space. Suppose the characteristic Evans operator has a
bounded block realization

```text
E_a=[A_a B_a]
    [C_a D_a] : F_a direct_sum H_a -> F_a direct_sum H_a,       (1)
```

where the boundary block `D_a` is boundedly invertible. Define

```text
S_a=A_a-B_a D_a^(-1) C_a.                                    (2)
```

Then projection to `F_a` gives an isomorphism

```text
ker E_a isomorphic_to ker S_a,                                (3)
```

with inverse

```text
x |-> (x,-D_a^(-1)C_a x).                                     (4)
```

Consequently, when `F_a` is finite-dimensional,

```text
ker E_a={0} iff det S_a != 0.                                 (5)
```

The determinant in `(5)` is finite in size but not finite in source information: every entry of
`S_a` contains the exact infinite boundary resolvent `D_a^(-1)`. It cannot be replaced by the raw
characteristic polynomial, fixed boundary jets, or a finite rational/logarithmic cancellation of
the transported prime-log Cauchy carrier.

## Proof

If `E_a(x,h)=0`, the second block row gives

```text
C_a x+D_a h=0,
h=-D_a^(-1)C_a x.                                            (6)
```

Substitution in the first row gives

```text
(A_a-B_aD_a^(-1)C_a)x=S_a x=0.                               (7)
```

Conversely, `(7)` and the choice `(4)` make both block rows vanish. These maps are inverse and
prove `(3)`. Finite-dimensional linear algebra gives `(5)`. `QED`
