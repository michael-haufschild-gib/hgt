## Statement

Let `F ⊆ E` be differential fields of characteristic zero and let `h,y ∈ E`. Set
`S = F⟨h⟩_D`, the differential field generated by `h`. If `h` is differentially algebraic over
`F` and there is a polynomial `P ∈ S[Y]` satisfying

```text
y' = P(y),
```

then `y` is differentially algebraic over `F`. Equivalently,

```text
HypertranscendentalOver F y and y' = P(y)
  -> HypertranscendentalOver F h.                                      (1)
```

The coefficients of `P` may use arbitrary derivatives and rational differential expressions in
`h`, because they lie in the whole differential field `F⟨h⟩_D`.

Three exact chain-law corollaries are formalized:

```text
y' = h' * y                         -> HT(y) -> HT(h)                    (exponential)
y' = h' * y * (1 - y)               -> HT(y) -> HT(h)                    (logistic/sigmoid)
y' = h' * (1 - y^2)                 -> HT(y) -> HT(h)                    (tanh)
```

For every signed-prime orientation `epsilon`, if its unshifted arithmetic source satisfies such a
polynomial first-order law over `C⟨h⟩_D`, then `h` is hypertranscendental over `C`. The canonical
all-positive source is a specialization.

## Proof

Over `S`, form the nonzero two-jet polynomial

```text
Q(Y0,Y1) = Y1 - P(Y0).
```

Nonzeroness is certified by evaluating `Y0` at zero and `Y1` at `P(0)+1`. The assumed identity
makes `Q(y,y') = 0`, so the finite-jet relation theorem gives `DifferentialAlgebraicOver S y`.
Differential-algebraic transitivity descends through the DA generator `h` and gives
`DifferentialAlgebraicOver F y`. Contraposition proves `(1)`.

For the three chain laws, `h'` is first placed in `F⟨h⟩_D` by derivation closure; the displayed
right-hand sides then become explicit polynomials in `y` over that coefficient field.

The signed-prime consumer restricts the already formalized differential independence of the full
signed shift palette to the unshifted source and applies `(1)`.

## Clock-observability interpretation

Although the constraint contains a derivative, the theorem neither solves nor propagates an ODE.
It audits a reported value against a fixed differential observation identity. The conclusion says
that a polynomial response channel cannot create the infinite observation structure of an HT
clock from a DA input clock. If the output is HT, the input channel is positively identified as an
HT source.

This is the precise form of “hypertranscendence is about the observability structure of a clock,
not about temporal evolution” for first-order polynomial reporters.

## Genomics, genetics, ML, and AI transfer

The logistic and tanh identities are the differential constraints of standard sigmoid activation
families after a preactivation `h`; the exponential identity covers exponential response units.
Therefore an exact finite learned channel using one of these laws cannot present an HT output while
hiding behind a DA preactivation. The theorem does not claim that arbitrary black-box activations
or limits of networks preserve differential algebraicity.

Gene-regulatory identification has the same structural issue: observed trajectories can fit while
the latent regulation is not identifiable. For polynomial first-order response laws, `(1)` gives a
positive source statement rather than a fit criterion: an HT observed response forces an HT latent
input. Rational Hill-type formulas are instead covered by the scalar-aware finite differential
circuit theorem in `FiniteDifferentialCircuitBoundary.lean`.

Epigenetic clocks make the distinction between time and clock especially concrete. Classical
clocks are finite weighted methylation reporters; deep models use finite multilayer readouts of
CpG features. These are observation architectures, not the biological evolution of age. The
finite-circuit and finite-reporter modules audit the finite architecture, while the present module
audits exact sigmoid/tanh/exponential differential response channels.

## Exact scope and no-go residue

The theorem assumes an exact first-order polynomial identity over a singleton differential field.
It does not cover approximation, arbitrary nonsmooth activations, infinite-width limits, delay
equations, fractional derivatives, or an output equation with coefficients not generated by `h`.

The failure to cover every neural or genomic model is not the result. The significant positive
statement is `(1)`: whenever this broad exact response law is present, HT complexity is localized
in the input channel. For models outside the class, the remaining research question is explicit:
prove that their primitive operation preserves DA, or exhibit where a genuinely infinite
differential source enters.

## RH impact

This is not an RH proof and is not an independent producer of the signed-prime source. It improves
the RH search in three concrete ways:

1. a latent neural, genomic, or physical variable satisfying a polynomial first-order source law
   cannot discharge the arithmetic source hypothesis if that latent variable is only DA;
2. for every signed orientation, the theorem turns such a model into an HT source detector and
   prevents a learned activation or scalar-field response from redressing an imported oracle;
3. a viable bridge must now provide arithmetic orientation in an HT input, or leave this finite
   polynomial-response class through a genuinely infinite operation whose source incidence is
   proved rather than named.
