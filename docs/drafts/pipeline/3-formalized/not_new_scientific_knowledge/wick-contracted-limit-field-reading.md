# Wick-contracted limit-field reading

- **ID:** WCDMF-LIMITFIELD
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper)
- **Source:** `wick-contracted-intrinsic-face-reading.md` (its recorded open core) ·
  `quantum-matroid-pro-observable-field.md` (Q1 semantics)
- **Statement:** Differential independence over the face field of an arbitrary base holds as
  soon as it holds over the face field of every finite sub-base; consequently the Wick
  contraction of the differential-transcendence matroid measures, for every base `G` whatever
  its cardinality, exactly relative differential independence over the single face field
  `F⟨G⟩`, and relative hypertranscendence over the face-complete field is exactly
  stage-persistent relative hypertranscendence.

## Exact statements

**D (directed-union coefficient lemma).**  For any base `G ⊆ E` and signal `I ⊆ E`:

```text
(∀ finite G₀ ⊆ G, DiffIndepOn F⟨G₀⟩ I)  →  DiffIndepOn F⟨G⟩ I.
```

**E (limit-field intrinsic reading).**  In the differential matroid `M` of `E/F`:

```text
(M ／ G).Indep I  ↔  Disjoint G I  ∧  DiffIndepOn F⟨G⟩ I,
```

for every `G`, with no finiteness or basis hypothesis.

**F (relative-HT persistence).**  For a single observable `x ∉ G`, the vertical jet fibre of
`x` is free over `F⟨G⟩` exactly when it is free over `F⟨G₀⟩` for every finite `G₀ ⊆ G`:
intrinsic hypertranscendence relative to the face-complete field is stage-persistent relative
hypertranscendence, and any collapse is witnessed at one finite face stage.

## Paper proof `[PROVED HERE]`

**D.**  Differential independence over `F⟨G⟩` is algebraic independence of the jet family of
`I` over the field generated by all jets of `G`.  Suppose a polynomial `p` over `F⟨G⟩`
annihilates the jet family.  `p` has finitely many coefficients; by the finite character of
field adjunction each coefficient lies in the subfield generated by a finite set of jets of
`G`, and each such jet is a jet of one element of `G`.  Collecting these elements gives a
finite `G₀ ⊆ G` with all coefficients of `p` in `F⟨G₀⟩`, so `p` descends to a polynomial `p₀`
over `F⟨G₀⟩` with the same evaluation (scalar-tower compatibility of polynomial evaluation).
Independence at the stage `G₀` forces `p₀ = 0`, hence `p = 0`.  ∎

**E.**  (⇒)  The finite-stage intrinsic reading gives disjointness and independence over every
finite-stage face field; D upgrades to `F⟨G⟩`.  (⇐)  Independence over `F⟨G⟩` restricts down
along `F⟨G₀⟩ ≤ F⟨G⟩` to every finite stage (independence descends to subfields), and the
finite-stage intrinsic reading applies.  ∎

**F.**  Instantiate E and the finite-stage reading with `I = {x}` and negate: `{x}` free in
`M ／ G` means exactly `x` hypertranscendental over `F⟨G⟩` (a one-element set is differentially
independent precisely when its full jet fibre is algebraically free), and the equivalence of E
with the finite-stage form is the persistence statement.  ∎
