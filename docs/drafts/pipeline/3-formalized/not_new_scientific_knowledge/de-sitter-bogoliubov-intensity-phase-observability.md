## Source relation

Sato--Suzuki equation (3.7) contains, up to frequency-independent and elementary factors,

`A(z) = Gamma(z+a) / Gamma(-z+b)`,

with `z=-i*omega/2`,

`a = 1/4 + branchMass/2 - ell/2`,

`b = 1/4 + branchMass/2 + ell/2`.

Hence `b=a+ell`.  Lean formalizes the meromorphic Gamma carrier and its reflected-frequency
observation algebra.  It does not formalize the full mode expansion or identify `A(-z)` with a
complex conjugate outside the physical real-parameter slice.

## Exact positive statement

For arbitrary shifts,

`A(z)A(-z) = [Gamma(z+a)Gamma(-z+a)] / [Gamma(z+b)Gamma(-z+b)]`.

Thus intensity is a quotient of two thermal-CFT reflection kernels already present in HTGeometry.
When `b=a+ell`, recurrence in both orientations cancels all Gamma factors and leaves the explicit
nonzero gauge

`G_a/G_b · Pochhammer(a,ell)^(-1) · Pochhammer(1-b,ell)^(-1)`,

where `G_h` is the Euler sine gauge.  This channel is DA.

By contrast,

`A(z)/A(-z)`

reflects to a nonzero DA gauge times four positive-charge Gamma clocks at centers
`a, 1-b, b, 1-a`.  Positive charges cannot cancel even when centers collide modulo integers, so
the phase is HT without a parameter hypothesis.  Therefore no scalar-aware finite differential
circuit generated by rational clocks and the complete intensity can recover the phase.  Together
the phase and intensity still recover `A(z)^2` exactly.
