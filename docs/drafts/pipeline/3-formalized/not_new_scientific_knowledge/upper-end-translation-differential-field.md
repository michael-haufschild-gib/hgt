- **Statement:** Complex functions analytic above a representative-dependent height form an
  integral domain modulo eventual equality at the upper end.  Its fraction field carries ordinary
  differentiation, every complex translation acts by a differential-field automorphism, the
  translations commute, `C(z)` embeds faithfully, and every bounded-gauge prime-log Herglotz
  source together with all its complex translates belongs to the field.

## Geometry

For `H in R`, let `U_H={z:Im z>H}` and let `F_infty` be the filter generated by the `U_H`.  Put

```text
A_infty={germ_F_infty(f): f is analytic on some U_H},
M_infty=Frac(A_infty).
```

The construction is forced by the exact height law

```text
(z |-> z+h)^(-1)(U_H)=U_(H-Im h).
```

No fixed upper half-plane is invariant under all complex translations, while the directed upper
end is invariant under every one of them.

## Formalized laws

1. `A_infty` is an integral domain.  Eventual product vanishing and both analyticity heights admit
   a common higher tail; the identity theorem on that connected tail forces one factor germ to be
   zero.
2. `M_infty` is a field.  Ordinary differentiation is well-defined on upper-end germs, preserves
   `A_infty`, and extends to `M_infty` by the quotient rule.
3. Every displacement `h in C` induces an automorphism `tau_h`; one has
   `tau_h tau_k=tau_(h+k)`, so all translations commute, and `tau_h D=D tau_h`.
4. Polynomial evaluation gives an injective map `C[z] -> A_infty`, hence an injective field map
   `C(z) -> M_infty`.
5. Every bounded-gauge prime-log Herglotz source is analytic on `U_0`; its translate by any complex
   displacement is the image under the corresponding `tau_h` in `M_infty`.
