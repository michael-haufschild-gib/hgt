## Theorem

Let `F_+` be generated by `U_H={Im z>H}` and `F_-` by `L_H={Im z<H}`.  Then

```text
R^(-1)(L_H)=U_(-H),
R^(-1)(U_H)=L_(-H),
R^2=1.
```

Precomposition therefore gives a ring equivalence between unrestricted end germs.  Transporting
the upper-end eventual-analytic subring through this equivalence produces the lower-end analytic
domain; the equivalence lifts to their fraction fields.  If the lower derivation is ordinary
differentiation transported through reflection, then the chain-rule sign holds first on analytic
germs and then, by the quotient rule, on every meromorphic fraction.

anti-commutes with `D`.  The combined carrier is therefore signed: the translation subgroup acts
with differential sign `+1`, while the functional-equation reflection acts with sign `-1`.
