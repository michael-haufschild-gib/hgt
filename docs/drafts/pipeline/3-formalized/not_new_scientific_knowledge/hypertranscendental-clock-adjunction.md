- **Statement:** Finite differential prolongation and finite internal observability are adjoint

## Setup

Let `(F, delta) subset (E, delta)` be an ordinary differential-field extension of characteristic
zero. Write `L_F(E)` for the complete lattice of intermediate fields. For `A,B in L_F(E)` and
`r in Nat`, define

```text
J_r(A) = F(delta^q a : a in A, 0 <= q <= r),
C_r(B) = {b in B : delta^q b in B for every 0 <= q <= r}.   (1)
```

`J_r` is finite clock prolongation: it adjoins every reading obtainable in at most `r` clock
applications. `C_r` is finite clock erosion: it retains exactly the observables whose readings
through order `r` remain visible in the same field. Neither operation introduces physical time or
a flow for the underlying function.

## The clock adjunction theorem `[PROVED ON PAPER]`

For all intermediate fields `A,B` and all `r`,

```text
J_r(A) <= B  iff  A <= C_r(B).                              (2)
```

Consequently `(J_r,C_r)` is a Galois connection on `L_F(E)`. Its unit and counit are

```text
A <= C_r(J_r(A)),
J_r(C_r(B)) <= B.                                          (3)
```

The composites `C_r J_r` and `J_r C_r` are therefore respectively an order-theoretic closure and
interior operator. The **counit-defect interval** at resolution `B` is

```text
[J_r(C_r(B)), B].                                          (4)
```

It is zero exactly when every visible observable is generated by an `r`-tick-internal core.

## Semigroup law `[PROVED ON PAPER]`

The operators satisfy

```text
C_(r+s) = C_s C_r = C_r C_s,
J_(r+s) = J_r J_s = J_s J_r.                               (5)
```

For the erosion law, `b in C_s(C_r(B))` says that
`delta^(p+q)b in B` for every `p<=s` and `q<=r`. These sums are exactly all orders at most `r+s`.
The prolongation law follows either directly from iterated derivatives or by uniqueness of the
left adjoint to `C_(r+s)` in `(2)`.

## Hypertranscendental exact orbit `[PROVED ON PAPER]`

For a hypertranscendental `x in E`, put

```text
P_N = F(x, delta x, ..., delta^(N-1)x),  P_0=F.             (6)
```

For `N>=1` and every `r`,

```text
J_r(P_N) = P_(N+r),
C_r(P_(N+r)) = P_N.                                       (7)
```

The first equality follows because the successive derivatives of the top generator of `P_N`
supply every new jet, while differentiating a rational expression through order `r` introduces no
jet beyond `N+r-1`. The second equality is the exact finite clock-erosion theorem formalized in
`HypertranscendentalClockBoundary.lean`.

Thus the adjunction unit and counit become equalities along the HT jet ray. Hypertranscendence is
therefore an exact infinite observable-capacity phenomenon: each finite panel has a one-layer
clock boundary, and the prolongation and erosion operators translate along the tower without bulk
loss.

This exactness also proves a decisive no-go: the counit-defect interval `(4)` is identically trivial
on the free HT tower, because `J_r(C_r(P_(N+r)))=J_r(P_N)=P_(N+r)`. It therefore cannot be the
common obstruction object or carry a nontrivial degeneracy spectrum. The nontrivial residue is the
one-layer boundary seam `C_1(B) <= B`, together with the induced clock symbol
`b |-> delta(b) mod B`. On `B=P_(N+1)` its kernel is exactly `P_N`. Any unified object must
decorate this boundary symbol by the external constraints selecting the particular HT function;
the bare adjunction forgets those constraints.

## What is and is not new

The order-theoretic fact that adjoining a family is left adjoint to retaining objects whose images
stay inside a target is not new. Calling an adjoint pair dilation/erosion also belongs to
mathematical morphology. Differential kernels and prolongation spaces already supply the finite
map from one jet packet into the next.

The candidate-new package was the use of this adjunction as the intrinsic geometry of finite
hypertranscendental observability. The exact-orbit calculation kills the stronger claim: the
adjunction is useful infrastructure but is blind to the identity of the constrained HT function
and has no HT obstruction interval. The surviving research question is whether the boundary
symbol, after adding source constraint, self-duality, and orientation data, has representations in
genuinely different obstruction chains.

## Strong no-go and positive successor

The raw cutoff ray is contractible, the abstract Galois connection is general order theory, and its
unit/counit defects vanish on the exact HT orbit. None can be sold as a new topology of
hypertranscendence.

The positive successor is source-pure and testable: attach to each finite observable field `B` the
boundary exact sequence cut out by `C_1(B) <= B` and `delta mod B`, then ask whether source
constraint, self-duality, and Hermitian orientation turn it into one object represented by several
analytic obstruction chains. A topology is justified only after such comparison data exist.
