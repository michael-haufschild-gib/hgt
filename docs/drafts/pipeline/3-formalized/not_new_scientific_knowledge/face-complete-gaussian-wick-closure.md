## Goal

Remove the false identification

```text
nonzero higher connected correlator = interaction.
```

A nonlinear Wick composite of a free Gaussian field has nonzero connected correlators above order
two.  A viable differential-algebraic obstruction must therefore condition on every lower
two-point face used by Wick contraction, not merely on an unrelated finite sample of the chosen
observable's own two-point function.

## Theorem

Let `phi` be a centered quasi-free scalar field with covariance `W`.  Let
`O_1,...,O_n` be finite polynomial Wick composites of `phi` and finitely many of its derivatives,
with coefficients in a differential base field `K`.  Fix:

1. a finite family of admissible source probes into the `n`-point configuration space;
2. a finite jet cutoff `r` along those probes;
3. every pair projection and derivative label that can occur in a contraction of the chosen Wick
   monomials after at most `r` probe derivatives.

Let `B_(n,r)` be the finite packet of the resulting pulled-back two-point contractions and their
required derivatives.  Let `C_(n,r)` be the finite packet of jets of the connected correlator

```text
<O_1(x_1) ... O_n(x_n)>_c.
```

Then

```text
C_(n,r) is contained in K[B_(n,r)]
```

inside any common response field.  Consequently

```text
trdeg_K K(B_(n,r), C_(n,r)) - trdeg_K K(B_(n,r)) = 0.       (1)
```

The same conclusion holds for a finite family of connected correlators and probes after taking
the union of their face packets.

## Proof

Expand each polynomial Wick composite into finitely many Wick monomials.  The quasi-free Wick
theorem writes the expectation of a product of these monomials as a finite sum over admissible
pairings with no contraction internal to a normal-ordered block.  Each pairing contributes a
finite monomial in two-point contractions of the form

```text
D_x^alpha D_y^beta W(x_i,x_j).
```

The moment--cumulant inversion expressing the connected correlator is a finite integer-polynomial
combination of such moments.  Equivalently, it retains exactly the connected contraction graphs.
Thus the connected correlator is a polynomial in pair-face contractions.

Apply at most `r` source derivatives.  Repeated Leibniz and chain rules produce another finite sum
of monomials in the finitely many differentiated pair-face contractions included in `B_(n,r)`.
Every element of `C_(n,r)` therefore lies in `K[B_(n,r)]`.

Adjoining elements already in the algebra generated by `B_(n,r)` does not raise algebraic-matroid
rank.  Equation `(1)` follows.  For finitely many correlators, the union of the finite pairing and
jet lists remains finite and the same proof applies.

## Discriminator and no-go

- A polynomial composite of a free field can have nonzero higher connected functions.
- Its face-complete relative profile is nevertheless zero.
- Therefore positivity of a profile based on mismatched one-parameter samples can be a reporter
  artifact.
- A positive face-complete relative profile certifies failure of finite polynomial Wick
  generation from the chosen quasi-free covariance.  It does **not** by itself certify an absolute,
  reference-independent notion of dynamical interaction.

The correct provisional name for a positive value is **non-Wick hypertranscendence relative to a
quasi-free reference**, not interaction hypertranscendence.
