# Complete source fibres and the infinite zero field

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Questions:** Source ground fields; structural centrality of conjugation.

## Construction and theorem

Write `w=(ρ−1/2)/i`. A complete-fibre source germ at `(t,k)` is a value `z`
satisfying

```
∃ 0<c₀<1/2, ∃ h₀>0, ∀ c₀<c<1/2, ∀ 0<h≤h₀,
  slabContourMoment k t h c = z.
```

These germs are precisely `Σ_{Re w=t} multiplicity(w)·w^k`. Let `K` be the
field generated by all germ values and `L=ℚ(all w)`. Then:

1. `K⊆ℝ`, and `K` equals the field of all complete fibre polynomial coefficients.
2. The source polynomial at `t` is monic, with degree equal to the analytic
   fibre mass; its root multiset is exactly the full fibre divisor.
3. `L/K` is algebraic and normal, with no height cutoff.
4. Every automorphism preserves each finite ordinate fibre. Conjugation is
   central; every critical-line zero is fixed individually.
5. No automorphism action over `K` is transitive on the infinite zero carrier.
6. `K=L` iff every centered zero is real.

## Proof

Local finiteness gives a full-strip slab gap. The finite fibre fits inside
some transverse band `c₀<1/2`. Every sufficiently narrow slab and every wider
band `c₀<c<1/2` therefore encloses exactly that fibre and has nonvanishing
boundary. The continued-source residue identity gives the uniform germ formula.

Multiplicity-preserving conjugation makes every full trace real. Newton
identities descend the complete root polynomial to `K`; reverse Newton
identities recover every trace from its coefficients. Empty fibres and repeated
roots are included. The entire zero field is the compositum of the resulting
splitting fields, so it is algebraic and normal.

The first trace of a nonempty fibre is `m·t`, where `m` is its positive integer
mass. Hence `t∈K`. Polynomial-root transport preserves that fibre under every
source-field automorphism. On it, `conj(w)=2t−w`, which proves centrality on all
generators and thus on `L`. Every orbit is finite, whereas the entire support
is infinite. Real zeros equal their ordinates and already lie in `K`.
Finally `K⊆L∩ℝ`, and if all zeros are real they all belong to `K`.

## Scope

Conjugation of `w` represents `ρ↦1−conj(ρ)`. Ordinary conjugation of `ρ` needs
the Gaussian extension. No algebraicity over ℚ, RH, simplicity, transitivity,
or absolutely convergent prime series inside the critical strip is assumed.
