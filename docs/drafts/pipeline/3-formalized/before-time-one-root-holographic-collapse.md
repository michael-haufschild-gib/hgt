## Goal

## Exact theorem

Let `K` be a field, `A` a commutative `K`-algebra, `M` an arbitrary `K`-vector space, and

```text
rho : A -> End_K(M)
```

a unital `K`-algebra representation.  Fix a root state `m in M`, a law vector space `V`, and a
linear principal-symbol map `L:V->A`.  Write

```text
Ann(m) = {a in A : rho(a)m=0},
q_m     : A -> A/Ann(m).
```

The evaluation map `a |-> rho(a)m` factors through an injective linear map

```text
A/Ann(m) -> M.                                             (1)
```

Consequently, for every law `v`,

```text
{rho(L(v))^n m : n>=0} is linearly independent
  iff q_m(L(v)) is transcendental over K.                  (2)
```

Extend `rho(L(v))` to a derivation `D_v` of the symmetric observable algebra `Sym_K(M)`.  Lean
proves both

```text
[D_v,D_w]=0                                                (3)

D_v^n iota(m) = iota(rho(L(v))^n m).                       (4)
```

The degree-one observables in a symmetric algebra are algebraically independent exactly when the
underlying states are linearly independent.  Therefore

```text
{v : the complete jet (D_v^n iota(m))_n is algebraically dependent}
  = (q_m o L)^(-1)({elements algebraic over K}).            (5)
```

Over a field the algebraic elements form the integral closure of `K`, hence a `K`-subalgebra.
The set in `(5)` is the carrier of a vector subspace of `V`.  There are no assumptions of finite
dimension, faithfulness, cyclicity of `M`, grading, reducedness, or finite generation.

## Representation-level phase boundary

Every finite homogeneous nonlinear projective cone already has an exact complete one-observable
horizon in the free noncommuting word field.  Equation `(5)` proves that the same cone cannot be
the horizon of one root in any autonomous commutative linear representation, irrespective of the
size of the hidden state space.  The paired theorem is

```text
nonlinearProjectiveHorizon_free_realizable_commutativeRepresentation_impossible.
```

Thus adding unobserved linear sectors or an infinite-dimensional many-worlds state space does not
change the one-root answer.  Every orbit visible from one root is intrinsically cyclic.

## What the external object must be

A nonlinear one-root horizon cannot be supplied by a larger autonomous commutative linear state
representation.  At least one of the following must fail:

1. one-root observability: a packet of coupled roots may carry a nontrivial syzygy module;
2. autonomous linear action: principal symbols may vary by jet layer or act nonlinearly;
3. commutativity: free or quantum operator words may remain distinguishable;
4. unprolonged state description: gauge, BRST, Spencer, or Koszul constraints may carry the missing
   compatibility data.

The first escape is real rather than rhetorical.  The universal weighted-shift field already
proves that a packet of roots realizes any finite or infinite central hyperplane arrangement as an
exact selected-law dependence spectrum.  The sharp dividing object is therefore not hidden-state
dimension but **observable module rank and its syzygies**.

## Physical interpretation `[CONDITIONAL THEOREM, NOT A COSMOLOGY CLAIM]`

Interpret `V` as the space of candidate laws before a propagation direction is selected, `A` as a
commutative principal-symbol algebra, and `M` as the complete hidden sector.  Then `(5)` says that
one observable can see only a superposition-closed exceptional set, even if `M` contains infinitely
many hidden worlds.  A nonlinear holographic horizon is a certificate that the physical model is
not a one-root autonomous commutative linear theory.

In QFT language the theorem separates a single free abelian observable sector from genuinely
multi-field, nonlinear, gauge-prolonged, or noncommutative structure.  It does not prove that the
universe realizes any one of these algebraic models.

## Novelty and anti-theatre gate

The algebraic ingredients are classical:

- the cyclic submodule generated by `m` is `A/Ann(m)`;
- powers of an operator on a vector are governed by the annihilator polynomial of its cyclic
  subspace;
- locally algebraic operators are classical, including Kaplansky-type local-to-global results;
- algebraic elements of a commutative algebra over a field form the integral closure.

Relevant parent literature includes standard module texts, R. Harte, *Variations on a Theme of
Kaplansky* (2002), and M. Chitayat, D. Daigle, A. Regeta,
[*Locally finite sets of derivations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08677) (2026).  These sources do
not supply a priority claim for `(5)`.

Targeted searches found no statement combining the arbitrary-representation cyclic reduction,
actual commuting symmetric-algebra derivations, the complete varying-law algebraic-dependence
horizon, and the free projective universality separation.  This is an absence-of-hit verdict, not
a claim of priority.

The theorem is stronger than the prior cyclic result and closes a genuine loophole, but its proof
is assembled from classical algebra.  It is not Fields-level by itself.  The significant open core
is the general commuting-derivation superposition problem:

```text
[CONJECTURE]
For pairwise commuting derivations in characteristic zero, the set of directions along which one
element is ordinarily differentially algebraic is a vector subspace.
```

The two-direction engine would be a finite mixed-jet rectangle theorem: ordinary differential
algebraicity for each of two commuting derivations should force finite transcendence degree of the
field generated by all mixed jets, and hence differential algebraicity for their sum.  A proof
would remove the linear-representation hypothesis entirely.  A counterexample would instead
identify the first genuinely nonlinear commuting horizon.

## RH impact

Any one-function completed-`L` model whose law directions act through an autonomous commutative
linear principal-symbol representation has a linear complete dependence horizon, regardless of
hidden-state dimension.  A nonlinear arithmetic horizon must expose a multi-observable syzygy,
layer-dependent curvature, nonlinear action, prolongation, or noncommutativity.  This is a strict
route filter; it neither supplies zero locations nor proves RH.
