## Medalist statement

The finite constraint-completeness theorem left open whether one fixed positive
hypertranscendental source can carry a genuinely infinite constraint geometry without introducing
an infinite analytic sum.  The exact answer for complete coherent facet observation is:

> After splitting off one global constant mode, point-finiteness is necessary and sufficient for
> every polynomial observation to have finite facet support.  On every countable nonempty
> point-finite facet geometry, the same fixed positive prime-log HT source realizes the exact
> nonlinear differential matroid of every finite structural family by finite differential
> operators.

The word “exact” has two independent meanings:

1. the coherent facet row kills exactly the Stanley--Reisner nonface ideal;
2. the resulting analytic outputs are differentially independent exactly when their quotient
   classes are linearly independent over `C[delta]`.

The boundary is for **complete coherent facet observation**.  An arbitrary enumeration of allowed
monomials can bypass point-finiteness, but it discards the facet geometry and is not this theorem.

## Setup

Let `V` be countable and let `A` be a nonempty family of finite subsets of `V`.  Antichain and
cover assumptions may be imposed when `A` is presented as the facet set of a simplicial complex,
but the theorem itself only needs nonemptiness and point-finiteness:

```text
for every v in V, only finitely many F in A contain v.
```

Let

```text
S = C[delta][x_v : v in V]
```

and let `I_A` be the ideal generated by monomials whose finite variable support is contained in no
member of `A`.  For a monomial exponent `d`, write

```text
Vis(d) = {F in A : support(d) subset F}.
```

The coherent row index is

```text
{global constant} union {(F,d) : F in A and d is nonconstant}.
```

The row of a polynomial stores its constant coefficient once, and copies every nonconstant
monomial coefficient to all `(F,d)` with `F in Vis(d)`.

## Theorem

### A. Exact finite-observation boundary

The following are equivalent:

1. `A` is point-finite;
2. `Vis(d)` is finite for every nonzero exponent `d`;
3. for every polynomial `P`, the set of facets on which a nonconstant monomial of `P` is visible
   is finite.

The implication `1 -> 3` is the finite-union argument.  For a nonzero exponent choose one vertex
in its support; every facet in `Vis(d)` lies in the finite star of that vertex.  Conversely, apply
`3` to the coordinate polynomial `x_v`: its visible-facet set is exactly the star of `v`.

Thus point-finiteness is not a convenient sufficient hypothesis.  It is the exact boundary at
degree one.

### B. Constant-split coherent row and exact kernel

Let `Q_A(P)` be the coherent row described above.  Point-finiteness makes `Q_A(P)` finitely
supported for every `P`.  Then

```text
Q_A(P)=0  iff  P in I_A.
```

Proof.  If a coefficient has face support, choose a facet containing that support.  The
corresponding coherent coordinate reads the coefficient unchanged; the constant coefficient is
read by the global mode.  Hence a zero row has only nonface monomials.  Conversely, every nonface
monomial is invisible on every facet, and has no global constant contribution.

Equivalently, `Q_A` induces an injective `C[delta]`-linear map from `S/I_A` into a finite-support
facet row space.  The complete product map also gives the standard algebra embedding

```text
S/I_A -> product_(F in A) S/p_F,
```

where `p_F` is generated by variables outside `F`.

### C. One fixed positive source

Let `g_*` be the previously constructed universal bounded positive prime-log gauge source.  Its
complete universal translated orbit is differentially independent.  Because `V` is countable,
the coherent row index is countable and injects into that single orbit palette.

For any finite index type `iota` and any family `P_i in S`, define

```text
T(P_i) = sum_q Q_A(P_i)(q)(D) g_*(z + shift(q)).
```

Every sum is finite.  The finite polynomial-operator row theorem and the exact kernel give

```text
{T(P_i)} differentially independent over C
  iff {Q_A(P_i)} linearly independent over C[delta]
  iff {[P_i] in S/I_A} linearly independent over C[delta].
```

The source `g_*` is independent of `V`, `A`, the finite family, and its coefficients.  No
completion and no infinite analytic sewing is used.

## What is known parent theory

The algebraic infinite-face-ring layer is not claimed as new.

- Brun--Bruns--Römer, Example 5.1, already treats a locally finite simplicial complex--defined by
  the same point-finite vertex incidence--and identifies its Stanley--Reisner ring with an inverse
  limit of face polynomial rings: [arXiv:math/0502517](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502517).
- D'Alì--Delucchi define face rings of finite-length, possibly infinite simplicial posets as rings
  of global sections, allowing unrestricted compatible facet tuples:
  [arXiv:1804.07366](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07366).
- Li Yu uses locally finite panel structures to define topological face rings in a cohomological
  setting: [arXiv:2103.04281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.04281).

Accordingly, the infinite Stanley--Reisner intersection/product theorem is `[KNOWN BRIDGE]`.
The constant-split iff is the exact finite-observation refinement needed for analysis.

## Boundary attacks

1. **Finite collapse.**  For finite `A` the theorem reduces to the existing finite carrier.  The
   new content is the single-source quantifier over genuinely infinite point-finite families.
2. **Infinite star.**  If one vertex belongs to infinitely many facets, the row of `x_v` already
   has infinite support.  The claimed finite-observation realization fails before differential
   algebra enters.
3. **Constant obstruction.**  Copying the constant to every facet would give infinite support for
   every infinite family.  The separate global mode is load-bearing.
4. **Arbitrary enumeration.**  Enumerating face monomials would realize a quotient row without
   point-finiteness, but destroys complete facet observation.  It is outside the theorem.
5. **Completion escape.**  A convergent infinite sum may treat non-point-finite families, but that
   is a topological completion theorem with new analytic obligations, not a counterexample.

## Examples

- Infinite disjoint edges are point-finite and genuinely infinite.
- Any locally finite infinite triangulated surface lies in the theorem.
- An infinite star is the sharp negative example at its centre.
- The empty face is the reason for the unique global constant mode.

## RH-impact audit

`[PROVED CONSEQUENCE FOR SELECTOR DESIGN]`

Locally finite countable relation geometry, even combined with positivity and the full prime-log
pole carrier of the fixed source, cannot by itself select zeta.  The same non-zeta HT source
carries every such exact differential matroid.  An RH-relevant selector must therefore use at
least one ingredient outside this theorem's invariant class, such as:

1. non-point-finite cofinal incidence;
2. genuinely convergent infinite sewing that cannot be reduced to finite-support observations;
3. compatibility with functional-equation polarization or the actual Euler source, rather than
   relation geometry alone.

This is an exclusion theorem for RH selector research, not an RH proof route.
