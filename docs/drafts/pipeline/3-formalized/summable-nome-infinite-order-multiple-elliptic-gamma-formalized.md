## What each layer contributes

1. **The occupation mass at every real exponent.**  `nomeOccupationMass_rpow_eq_iSup_finset` proves
   `(C1)` in `ℝ≥0∞` for arbitrary index type and arbitrary positive real exponent, so the whole
   Schatten spectrum is classified at once rather than at determinant orders only.
   `summable_nomeMonomial_rpow_iff` is `(3)`, and the upward-closure of the convergence set follows.

2. **The exact genus threshold.**  Sufficiency is normal convergence
   (`infiniteMultipleEllipticGamma_hasProdLocallyUniformlyOn`).  Necessity runs through the exact
   primary-factor logarithm `primaryFactor_eq_exp_neg_primaryLogTail` and the lower bound
   `primaryFactor_sub_one_norm_ge`; the singleton occupations then force `(g+1)`-summability of the
   nomes.  `summable_nome_pow_iff_exists_normal_majorant` is the resulting equivalence.

3. **Exhaustion, collapse, and the parity obstruction.**  The forward factor collapses
   (`tendsto_forwardNomeFactor_one`) and the parity-normalized sections converge
   (`tendsto_parityNormalizedSection`).  The obstruction is exhibited rather than asserted: the raw
   net converges along the even and the odd cofinal classes to reciprocal limits, so a raw limit
   would force `𝒢² = 1` at every point, and `exists_point_of_half` produces a point where `Re 𝒢`
   equals `1/2` by the intermediate value theorem — elementary, no Picard.

4. **The counterterm jet, uniformly.**  `norm_jetSeries_sub_head_le` bounds the jet series by a
   geometric tail whose ratio is the nome product times the coordinate.  Both error terms are
   monotone in the modulus of the coordinate, so `tendstoUniformlyOn_jetSeries` holds on every
   ball; reading the coordinate as the reciprocal of the point gives
   `tendstoUniformlyOn_countertermJet` on every region bounded away from the origin, hence
   `tendstoLocallyUniformlyOn_countertermJet` on the punctured plane, at every counterterm order.

5. **Scheme change and the differential phase.**  `infiniteMultipleEllipticGamma_scheme_change` is
   `(C4)`; `infiniteGenusCounterterm_eq_moment_sum` evaluates its exponent as the finite sum of
   occupation moments at the newly added powers.  Restricting to a finite nome set gives `(21)`,
   `finiteNomeSection_eq_zero_genus_mul_gauge`, whose gauge is a polynomial with no constant term,
   so `finiteNomeSection_eq_zero_iff_genus_zero`: raising the genus moves no divisor.

   The differential half rests on one new abstract law:
   `differentialAlgebraicOver_of_deriv_eq_mul` — an element whose logarithmic derivative is
   differentially algebraic is itself differentially algebraic.  It is proved by passing to the
   differential field generated by that logarithmic derivative, where the element satisfies a
   homogeneous first-order linear equation, and then applying transitivity of differential
   algebraicity.  **This does not need Ostrowski's theorem**: Ostrowski is required for the
   independence direction, not for this one.  The consequences
   (`differentialAlgebraicOver_mul_iff_of_expGauge`,
   `hypertranscendentalOver_mul_iff_of_expGauge`, and the two logarithmic-derivative versions) are
   the exact invariance the card's Lemma 8 asserts.

   `MeroExponentialGauge` instantiates this on the meromorphic differential field of the plane:
   `deriv_analyticMeroElement` identifies the field derivation with the ordinary derivative on
   entire elements, so `exp ∘ P` satisfies the first-order relation with coefficient `P′`.
   `FiniteSectionGaugeDifferentialPhase` reads a finite section in an exponential coordinate
   `t = b e^{cz}`, shows the gauge exponent is a polynomial in `e^{cz}` and hence differentially
   algebraic, and concludes that genus does not move the phase — for the section, for its
   logarithmic derivative, for the two-branch assembly (the scale `b` carries both branches), and
   for the reciprocal, since inversion preserves the phase.

6. **The operator layer.**  `DiagonalEllTwoIndexed` builds diagonal multiplication by a bounded
   family on `ℓ²` over an arbitrary index type, with its adjoint, composition, modulus square,
   Hilbert–Schmidt criterion and Hilbert–Schmidt norm.  `BosonicFockOperatorRealization` then
   constructs `Γ_s(P)` as an actual bounded operator on `lp (fun _ : ι →₀ ℕ => ℂ) 2`: the occupation
   vectors are a Hilbert basis, each is an eigenvector with eigenvalue the occupation monomial, the
   eigenvalue is the diagonal matrix element, the eigenvalues are multiplicative in the occupation,
   the vacuum is a unit eigenvector, and the operator norm is exactly one.

   The model's quantities are then theorems about that operator: the singular value at an
   occupation is the norm of the image of the occupation vector and the square root of the
   corresponding diagonal element of `Γ*Γ` (itself the diagonal operator of the squared moduli);
   Schatten membership is summability of those norms; at exponent two it is the coordinate-free
   Hilbert–Schmidt property, which holds exactly when the one-particle contraction is
   square-summable, and then the Hilbert–Schmidt norm is the occupation mass.  In that regime
   `BosonicFockOperatorTrace` identifies the operator with the corpus's Hilbert–Schmidt diagonal, so
   its power traces from the second power onward are genuine operator traces, and at every genus at
   least one the trace expansion `(24)` is an identity in operator traces.

## Design notes

- The occupation identities are proved in `ℝ≥0∞` first, so no summability side condition enters the
  mass law; the complex-valued Euler product is a separate, absolutely summable statement.
- Several hypotheses that looked necessary are not, and were dropped rather than carried:
  `tendsto_forwardNomeFactor_one` and `tendsto_parityNormalizedSection` hold at every point
  including the origin, and `exists_primaryFactor_sub_one_bound` holds at every radius.
- Local uniformity of the exhaustion was never needed: the moving-coordinate limit follows from
  dominated convergence for infinite products once the centred primary factor is bounded on an
  arbitrary disc.
- The moment form of `(8)` does not elaborate as a single theorem; split into a per-term majorant,
  a summability lemma, an occupation-sum lemma and a short assembly it compiles in seconds.
  Avoiding `field_simp` on the `Nat.cast (m + (g+1))` denominators was the other half of the fix.
- Mathlib supplies neither a Schatten class at a general exponent nor a regularized Fredholm
  determinant.  Both are taken in their defining forms — summability of singular values, and the
  primary-factor product over the eigenvalues — which is also how the card states them; at exponent
  two the Schatten statement is upgraded to the coordinate-free Hilbert–Schmidt property.
