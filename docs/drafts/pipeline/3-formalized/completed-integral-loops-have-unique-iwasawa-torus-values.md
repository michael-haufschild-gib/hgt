# Completed integral loops have unique Iwasawa torus values

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Parent:** [Integral loop vertices](../1-in-progress/valued-loop-iwasawa-and-integral-vertices.md).
- **Statement:** Let `K=⟨P₀,P₁⟩` be generated by both coefficient-integral completed
  vertices. Then `K∩B=B₀`, every actual loop admits a `KHU` decomposition, and
  both torus valuations are independent of the decomposition.

## Proof

The two integral vertices contain `B₀` and meet Borel exactly there.
The subgroup-join normal form writes any element either in `B₀` or as a
nonempty alternating word with every factor outside `B₀`. Projecting the
latter to Laurent matrices gives a reduced word in the full completed vertices.
The audited extreme-coefficient obstruction excludes Iwahori membership,
hence excludes Borel membership in the central extension. Thus `K∩B=B₀`.

The finite integral simple subgroup lies in `P₀` by its constant power-series
splitting. Lifted rescaling identifies its swapped counterpart with the actual
affine integral simple subgroup, placing that subgroup in `P₁`.
The audited simple-integral Iwasawa decomposition therefore gives `G=KHU`.

For `k₁b₁=k₂b₂`, the Borel difference `b₁b₂⁻¹=k₁⁻¹k₂` lies in `K∩B=B₀`.
Both intrinsic torus valuations equal one there. Multiplicativity of each
Borel valuation gives equality of the corresponding values of `b₁,b₂`.

For a positive unipotent, its central positive-splitting factor is one.
The intersection identity and injectivity of the matrix projection therefore
identify membership in `K` exactly with coefficient integrality. This gives
the uniform finite-place condition needed by the restricted unipotent carrier.
