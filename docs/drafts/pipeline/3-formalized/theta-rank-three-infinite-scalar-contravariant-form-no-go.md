## Dependency-closed paper proof

Let `A = C[U,B,Q]`, `b=B`, and `r=U+3B`. Write `L_E,L_F,L_H` for the exact source Lie
derivatives. Suppose `B : A x A -> C` is conjugate-scalar-linear on the left,
scalar-linear on the right, strictly positive on every nonzero diagonal, and

```text
B(L_E p,q) = B(p,c L_F q)
```

for a real scalar `c` and all `p,q`.

1. Lie derivatives kill constants, so `L_E 1=0`. The adapted-coordinate identity gives
   `L_F b=-1`. Contravariance at `(p,q)=(1,b)` gives
   `0=B(1,-c*1)=-c B(1,1)`. Taking real parts and using `Re B(1,1)>0` yields `c=0`.
2. The same adapted coordinates give `L_F r=0` and `L_H r=2r`. Applying `[E,F]=H` to `r`
   yields `L_F(L_E r)=-2r`.
3. `L_E r` is nonzero: evaluation at `U=B=Q=0` equals `6`.
4. Contravariance at `(p,q)=(r,L_E r)`, followed by `c=0`, gives
   `B(L_E r,L_E r)=B(r,0)=0`, contradicting strict positivity and Step 3.

Therefore no such scalar contravariant positive form exists, in finite or infinite dimension, on
the full exact source polynomial carrier.
