### §2 — state reconstruction (Theorem 2.1, Corollary 2.2)

4. `probeGraph`, `adj_probeGraph` — the undirected graph generated by an oriented probe family
   `E ⊆ V × V`.
5. `eq_of_adj_of_reachable` — a labelling constant along every probe edge is constant along every

6. **`ker_probe_eq_range`** — `(GCR.6)`: the kernel of the observation map is exactly the set of

7. **`probe_separates_iff_connected`** — Theorem 2.1: for any nontrivial value type, the probes
   separate the points of the phase fibre **iff** the probe graph is connected.  The disconnected
   direction is realized by the two-valued labelling of the reachability class of a vertex.
8. **`phase_reconstruction_iff_connected`** and **`winding_reconstruction_iff_connected`** — its
   instances at the circle group (state reconstruction) and at `ℤ` (Theorem 4.1, winding

   `componentPullback_injective`, `range_componentPullback`, `kerEdgeDiffEquiv` and
   **`finrank_ker_edgeDiff`** — the real linearization: the kernel of the edge-difference map is

10. **`card_le_card_components_add_card`** — Corollary 2.2, `(GCR.7)`: rank-nullity for the
    edge-difference map gives `d ≤ c(G) + m`, i.e. `m` probes leave at least `d - m` components and
    at least `d - m - 1` invisible relative-phase directions.
11. **`card_le_card_probes_add_one`** — Theorem 2.1, minimality: a connected probe family has at

13. `walkProd`, `walkProd_nil`, `walkProd_cons`, **`walkProd_telescope`**,

15. `walkSum`, `walkSum_nil`, `walkSum_cons`, **`walkSum_telescope`**,

16. `walkSum_append`, `walkSum_reverse`, **`exists_potential_of_cut_law`** — the image of `B_G` is

    closed walk vanishes is the cut winding of a vertex labelling.
17. **`probe_blind_to_winding`** — `(GCR.8)`: endpoint probes are blind to winding; a closed history
    returns every cross-coherence to its initial value whatever its winding class.

### §5 — action remains additional data

19. **`diagProj_add_const`** and **`minAction_diag_invariant`** — the minimum action depends only on

## Scope notes

`(GCR.2)` — that both boundary reductions are constant on the framed fibre — is quoted from the
parent physics card and is not re-proved here; what this card proves and the module formalizes is
that the *cross-coherence* probes do see the fibre, and exactly when they see all of it.

`(GCR.6)` is recorded as the explicit description of the kernel (the pullbacks of component
labellings) together with its real dimension `c(G)`, rather than as a homeomorphism onto a torus
`T^{c(G)-1}`.  The two are the same statement: the kernel is `Map(π₀ G, T)` and the quotient by the
constant diagonal drops the dimension by one; the dimension count is what `(GCR.7)` and Prediction 3
use, and it is proved exactly, via `finrank_ker_edgeDiff`.

`(GCR.11)` is likewise quoted from the parent; the module defines it and proves the property this
card claims for it — invariance under the diagonal lift, hence determination by the winding class.

The executed-action separation is formalized in the elementary piecewise-linear model
(`pathLength` on a finite list of points), which is enough to prove the card's claim that endpoints
and winding do not determine executed action; the Fubini–Study parametrization is the parent card's
layer.
