# Opposite ordinary-prime excursions on the third confined span

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Source:** [QIC](../../../reports/rh-current-frontier-map.md#qic)

Three confined states admit a nonzero real combination H with ℒH(−½) = ℒH(0) = 0. Let V(t) = ∑ₚ (log p)/√p · H(t − log p), summed over ordinary primes. For some b > 0, both signs of V have infinite mass against exp(−bt) on the positive ray. For every M > 0, V exceeds M exp(bt/2) and falls below −M exp(bt/2) arbitrarily far out. No restriction on the off-line divisor is needed. [Damped masses](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OppositeExcursionsOfTheThirdConfinedPrimeWave.lean#L138) · [Unconditional QIC alternative](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OppositeExcursionsOfTheThirdConfinedPrimeWave.lean#L202)

**Proof.** The two real moments have a common kernel on the three-dimensional span. Each nonzero coefficient vector gives bare energy −∑ᵢ cᵢ² a(Rᵢ)W₈(Φᵢ,Φᵢ) < 0. If every right centred zero z satisfied ℒH(z)ℒH(−z) = 0, reflection would kill every off-line term at every positive heat scale. The remaining on-line sum is nonnegative at each scale, contradicting its negative limit. Thus H detects both sides of one actual quartet. The evaluation on the left obstructs continuation of the prime head. Proper prime powers are holomorphic on Re p < 0, so the obstruction persists for ordinary primes. [Coupled evaluations and cancellation](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/TheOrdinaryPrimeWaveOnTheThirdConfinedSpan.lean#L115)

If exp(−bt)|V(t)| were integrable for every 0 < b ≤ 1, its Laplace integral would give the forbidden continuation. Fix b where this fails. Both charges vanish, so the signed gamma means gₙ = ∫ V(t)tⁿ exp(−t)/n! dt decay geometrically. Multiplying the first n Taylor terms of exp((1−b)t) by exp(−t) gives Pₙ(t) increasing to exp(−bt), with |∫ PₙV| ≤ ∑ₖ |gₖ|. Finite damped mass for either sign would bound ∫ Pₙ|V| uniformly. Fatou contradicts divergence. Local integrability gives both late excursions.
