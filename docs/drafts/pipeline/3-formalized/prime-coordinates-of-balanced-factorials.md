# Joint value and entropy at finite horizon

For a balanced factorial product at horizon $L$, let $q=\prod(j!)^{c_j}$ and $E=\prod j^{j c_j}$. Every positive rational has an exact zero-entropy presentation at sufficiently large horizon. [Proof](../3-formalized/zero-entropy-factorial-presentations.md)

**Finite-horizon classification.** At $L\ge10$, both $q$ and $E$ are positive rationals with prime factors at most $L$. Necessarily $p\mid v_p(E)$ for every prime $p$, since $p\mid j$ in every contribution to $v_p(E)$. If $L$ is prime, $v_L(E)=L v_L(q)$ since only $L!$ reaches $L$. They suffice. For each prime $p$ first give $p!$ exponent $v_p(E)/p$, balance at $1!$, and correct the remaining value by zero-entropy presentations of the smaller primes. At a composite horizon every prime at most $L$ is strictly smaller than $L$. At a prime horizon the prescribed top valuation cancels before correction.

`factorialJointImage_eq_prime_readings` gives the integral generators. `factorialJointImage_iff_prime_coordinates` gives the exact valuation criterion. [Prime generators](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/ThePrimeLatticeOfFactorialEntropy.lean) · [Prime coordinates](../../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/PrimeCoordinatesOfBalancedFactorialValues.lean)

The simultaneous absolute value of entropy and Stirling remainder has infimum $|\log q|/2$ at horizons at least six. At composite horizons $L\ge10$ the infimum is attained exactly when $2p\mid v_p(q)$ for every prime $p$. At prime horizons $L\ge11$ attainment also requires $v_L(q)=0$. Indeed the equality case is $2S=\log q$. The valuation of each interior prime in $q$ is then twice its entropy valuation. At a top prime the two relations force its valuation to vanish. Conversely the half-valuations produce the required entropy. `factorial_half_price_attainable_iff` proves both directions.
