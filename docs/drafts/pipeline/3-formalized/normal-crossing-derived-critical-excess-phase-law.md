
## 2. Classical support threshold `[PROVED]`

The squarefree membership theorem gives

```text

```

Indeed a monomial belongs to every pair ideal exactly when its support misses at most one
variable, equivalently when it is divisible by a squarefree monomial of degree `N-1`.

Consequently:

```text

N>=2: height(I_N)=2.                                    (4)
```

Thus the critical support becomes nonempty at `N=2` and remains a codimension-two union of
coordinate subspaces at every higher order.

The derived amplitude therefore grows without bound even though the classical support has fixed
codimension two.
