## Independently defined arithmetic sections

Let `A_N=ℤ^N/L_N`, with `L_N` generated by the actual non-pole divisor
columns. A section of a class is an integral representative. Its size is
`Σ_i v_i²`; `E_N(c)` is the minimum over all representatives. Define the
minimum-section count as the cardinality of the actual minimizing sections.
Neither definition uses a Möbius sum or the Weil form.

Integral inversion identifies `A_N≃ℤ`. Write the resulting charge as
`k=q a+r`, where `q=Q(N)>0` is the squarefree count and `0≤r<q`. Then

`E_N(c)=q a²+r(2a+1)=k²/q+r(q−r)/q`.

Every minimizer vanishes on non-squarefree coordinates. On squarefree
coordinates, after multiplication by the actual Möbius orientation sign,
its values are `a` or `a+1`, with exactly `r` upper values. An explicit
bijection `minimumDivisorSectionEquiv` with `r`-element subsets proves

`#MinimumSections(c)=binomial(q,r)`.

The proof uses nonnegativity of `(z−a)(z−a−1)` for integral `z`; equality
classifies every coordinate. Thus both the minimum and its actual finite
section count are established, including negative charges and `N=1`.

## Exact addition law and Tate limit

Put `ρ(c)=min(r,q−r)`. The actual quotient addition satisfies

`E(c+d)+E(c−d)−2E(c)−2E(d)=−2 min(ρ(c),ρ(d))`.

In particular the doubling defect lies in `[-q,0]`. The sequence
`4^(−j)E(2^j c)` decreases to the independently defined Tate limit

`Ĥ_N(c)=k²/q`,
`0≤4^(−j)E(2^j c)−Ĥ_N(c)≤(q/4)4^(−j)`.

`divisorSectionMinimum_parallelogram` proves the exact defect;
`divisorSectionTateSequence_error` and `divisorSectionTateSequence_antitone`
prove the quantitative limit and monotonicity. The height satisfies the
exact parallelogram and natural-number scaling laws.
The conductor-dependent error is explicit; it is not treated as uniform
in `N`.

## Original arithmetic gluing class

For the actual class of `b=−Σ_(j≥2)e_j`, integral inversion gives
`k=Σ_p index_p(N)=1−M(N)`. Its charge satisfies `|k|≤q`, and

`E_N([b])=Nat.card G_N=|Σ_p index_p(N)|`,
`Ĥ_N([b])=D_N(b)²`.

`divisorGluingClass_minimum_eq_count` and `divisorGluingClass_tateHeight`
prove these comparisons. Here `G_N` is the independently presented integer gluing quotient and
`D_N` is the proved Euclidean transverse distance. Zero charge gives
minimum zero and the infinite cyclic case of `G_N`; the minimum-section
set still has one element.

## Scope

The minimum size is distinct from the logarithm of the number of
minimizers. The latter count is positive and at most `2^q`; consequently
`4^(−j)log #MinimumSections(2^j c)→0`, formalized as
`tendsto_minimumDivisorSectionLogCount_zero`. That finite count does not produce
the nonzero Tate height. The positive survivor is the complete integral
classification and the section-size height with its exact defect.

This is an actual arithmetic quotient and section theory. It supplies
neither a full Hecke-correspondence height nor a product-formula identity
for `Ĥ_N`. The independent bound `Ĥ_N([b])=Oε(N^(2ε))`, hence signed
cancellation at RH scale, remains open. A nontrivial log-count height
requires a further construction, such as the full Gaussian section sum.
