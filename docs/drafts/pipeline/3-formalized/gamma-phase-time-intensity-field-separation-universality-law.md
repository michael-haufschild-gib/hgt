## Exact claim

For complex shifts \(a,b\), define the opposite-orientation Gamma channel
\[
 A_{a,b}(z)=\frac{\Gamma(z+a)}{\Gamma(b-z)},
\]
its reflected intensity
\[
 I_{a,b}(z)=A_{a,b}(z)A_{a,b}(-z),
\]
its orientation-sensitive phase channel
\[
 P_{a,b}(z)=\frac{A_{a,b}(z)}{A_{a,b}(-z)},
\]
and its logarithmic Wigner--Smith current
\[
 T_{a,b}(z)=\frac{A'_{a,b}(z)}{A_{a,b}(z)}.
\]

Put
\[
 \mathcal C(a,b)\iff
 \bigl(b-a\in\mathbb Z\bigr)
 \ \lor\
 \bigl(1-2a\in\mathbb Z\ \land\ 1-2b\in\mathbb Z\bigr).
\]

Let \(\iota\) be any index type, with shifts \(a_i,b_i\in\mathbb C\), and suppose
\(\mathcal C(a_i,b_i)\) for every \(i\). Let
\[
 K_I=\mathbb C(z)\langle I_{a_i,b_i}:i\in\iota\rangle_\partial
\]
be the differential field generated by the entire intensity atlas. Then, for every \(j\in\iota\),
\[
 P_{a_j,b_j}\ \text{and}\ T_{a_j,b_j}
\]
are hypertranscendental over \(K_I\). Equivalently, neither satisfies any algebraic differential
equation whose coefficients belong to the differential field generated by all collapsed
intensities.

No finiteness, countability, independence, or distinctness hypothesis is imposed on \(\iota\).

## Stronger full-amplitude law

Let \(g_i,\widetilde g_i\) be arbitrary nonzero meromorphic functions that are differentially
algebraic over \(\mathbb C(z)\), independently for every \(i\). Restore the full normalized
channels
\[
 \widehat A_i=g_iA_{a_i,b_i},\qquad
 \widehat A_i^-=\widetilde g_iA_{a_i,b_i}(-z),
\]
and form
\[
 \widehat I_i=\widehat A_i\widehat A_i^-,
 \quad
 \widehat P_i=\widehat A_i/\widehat A_i^-,
 \quad
 \widehat T_i=\widehat A_i'/\widehat A_i.
\]
Then:

1. \(\widehat I_i\) is differentially algebraic **if and only if**
   \(\mathcal C(a_i,b_i)\). Thus arbitrary nonzero DA prefactors do not enlarge or shrink the
   exact collapse locus.

   infinite, full-intensity atlas \(\{\widehat I_i:i\in\iota\}\).

This closes the physical-prefactor gap: powers, exponentials, normalizations, and other nonzero DA
meromorphic factors may vary by mode. The theorem does not pretend those factors are absent.

## Exact one-turn horizon-clock law

For \(x=\omega/\kappa\) and \(\rho=\omega'/\kappa>0\), define the phase-carrying part of the exact
Carlitz--Willey coefficient and its dimensionless logarithmic phase slope by
\[
 C_\rho(x)=e^{-ix\log\rho}\Gamma(ix),\qquad
 \tau_\rho(x)=\operatorname{Im}\frac{C_\rho'(x)}{C_\rho(x)}
             =\operatorname{Re}\psi(ix)-\log\rho .
\]
The formal certificate proves the carrier derivative and logarithmic-derivative identity exactly,
not by naming the right-hand side as the observable.

Using the recurrence \(\operatorname{Re}\psi(ix)=\operatorname{Re}\psi(1+ix)\), vertical-trigamma
orientation, and the sharp vertical digamma asymptotic, it proves:

1. \(\tau_\rho\) extends continuously to
   \(\tau_\rho(0)=-\gamma-\log\rho\).
2. \(\tau_\rho'(x)=-\operatorname{Im}\psi'(1+ix)>0\) for every \(x>0\).
3. \(\tau_\rho(x)\to+\infty\) as \(x\to+\infty\).
4. A positive stationary frequency exists uniquely if and only if
   \(e^{-\gamma}<\rho\).
5. When it exists, the slope is negative before the stationary point and positive after it.
6. If \(0<\rho\le e^{-\gamma}\), the slope is positive at every positive frequency.

Thus every physical channel has strictly positive Gamma group-delay dispersion and at most one
stationary frequency. A common linear phase (time-origin shift) adds a constant to the slope and
moves the threshold, but cannot alter strict monotonicity or create two stationary frequencies.
