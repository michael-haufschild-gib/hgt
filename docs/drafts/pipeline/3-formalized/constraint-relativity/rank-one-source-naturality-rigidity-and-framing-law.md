## Theorem 1: unframed rank-one naturality rigidity

Let `L` range over one-dimensional complex vector spaces. Suppose each `C direct-sum L` is given
commutative unital complex-algebra structure with unit `(1,0)`, naturally in line isomorphisms:
for every isomorphism `f:L->L'`, map

\[
 \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb C}\oplus f:
 \mathbb C\oplus L\longrightarrow\mathbb C\oplus L'
 \tag{1}
\]

is algebra isomorphism. Then uniquely

\[
 L^2=0.
 \tag{2}
\]

### Proof

For `x,y in L`, decompose product as

\[
 xy=\beta(x,y)1+\alpha(x,y),
 \tag{3}
\]

where

\[
 \beta:L\otimes L\to\mathbb C,
 \qquad
 \alpha:L\otimes L\to L
\]

are bilinear. Apply naturality to automorphism `f=lambda id_L`. Scalar component of `(1)` is
identity while line component scales by `lambda`, hence

\[
 \lambda^2\beta=\beta,
 \qquad
 \lambda^2\alpha=\lambda\alpha
 \tag{4}
\]

for every `lambda in C^times`. Taking `lambda=2` yields `beta=0` and `alpha=0`. Thus `(2)` is
forced. Conversely square-zero multiplication is natural under every line isomorphism. QED.

Therefore only unframed, basis-free algebra law naturally generated by one source line is
classical dual-number extension. It is non-Morita-trivial but does not select any richer successor.

## Theorem 2: framed quadratic classification

Choose frame `e in L`, `e!=0`. Every unital two-dimensional monogenic commutative complex algebra
has presentation

\[
 A_{a,b}=\mathbb C[e]/(e^2-ae-b).
 \tag{5}
\]

Its discriminant

\[
 \Delta=a^2+4b
 \tag{6}
\]

classifies two Morita types:

\[
 \Delta\ne0
 \Longrightarrow A_{a,b}\cong\mathbb C\times\mathbb C,
 \qquad
 \Delta=0
 \Longrightarrow A_{a,b}\cong
 \mathbb C[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2).
 \tag{7}
\]

### Proof

Products of `e` reduce uniquely to span of `1,e`, giving `(5)`. Put `y=e-a/2`; then

\[
 y^2=\Delta/4.
\]

If `Delta!=0`, polynomial has two distinct complex roots and Chinese remainder theorem gives
product `C times C`. If `Delta=0`, translate repeated root to obtain square-zero dual numbers.
Centers are algebras themselves and semisimplicity differs, so two cases are not Morita
equivalent. QED.

## Corollary: rank/no-surplus data do not select law

After choosing frame, laws `e^2=0` and `e^2=1` have same one generator and one relation but are
not Morita equivalent. Hence anomaly dimension, generator count, relation count, or
no-surplus rank alone cannot determine successor theory. A genuine source-law transgression must
supply at least one extra intrinsic datum: frame, polarity, discriminant, or equivalent operation.

This strengthens promotion-anomaly theorem. Its rank identifies minimal charge capacity but not
law multiplication on compensator.

## Nomogenesis consequence

Source-selected law event cannot be specified by slogan “obstruction becomes new generator.” For
one-dimensional obstruction, basis-free naturality forces square-zero extension; any other event
requires source to break scaling symmetry and produce discriminant. Thus make-or-break map must be
an equivalence of structured relative law objects, not only vector spaces of same dimension.
