The residual-fibre argument is built on top of existing corpus layers rather than re-derived:

  `maximalIdeal_quotientPow`, `maximalIdeal_quotientPow_pow`, `exists_adjoin_eq_top_of_algEquiv`;

`ProductMonogenic` was generalised in the course of this card: the `CharZero` hypothesis was moved
off the section variables onto the single theorem that actually needs it
(`exists_adjoin_pi_eq_top`), so that `adjoin_add_algebraMap` is available over an arbitrary field.

## New infrastructure

Mathlib has base change of Kähler differentials only for a *pushout* square
(`KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquiv`) and carries no instance recognising a quotient square as
a pushout.  That instance is built here:

- `isPushout_quotientMap : Algebra.IsPushout R (R ⧸ I) A (A ⧸ I·A)`, from
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.quotIdealMapEquivQuotTensor` through `IsBaseChange.of_equiv`.

Mathlib also has no comparison between the Kähler module of a local algebra and its cotangent
space.  The split conormal map is built here from scratch:

- `resEquiv`, `resSection` — the ring-theoretic section of the residue map available when the
  residue field is the base field;
- `resShift`, `resShiftLinear` — the `k`-linear deviation `c ↦ c − σ(c̄)` valued in `𝔪`;
- `residueDerivation` — that map is a `k`-derivation into `𝔪/𝔪²` (Leibniz holds because the error
  term is `−(a − σ(ā))(b − σ(b̄)) ∈ 𝔪²`);
- `surjective_liftKaehler` — its universal lift `Ω[C⁄k] → 𝔪/𝔪²` is onto.

## Formal theorem chain

All declarations live in namespace `HTGeometry.ConstraintRelativity`.

### Theorem 1 — trace–Kähler equality is exactly monogenicity

1. `kaehler_isPrincipal_of_monogenic` — **card `(3) ⇒ (2)`**: the Kähler module of a monogenic
   order is cyclic, generated by the class of `dθ`.
2. `traceQuotient_isPrincipal` — **card `(3)`, hence `(1) ⇒ (2)`**: an invertible trace dual over a
   semilocal ring is free of rank one, so `L = uA/A` is cyclic.
3. `mulDifferentEquiv`, `traceKaehlerEquiv_of_monogenic` — **card `(10)`–`(12)`, `(3) ⇒ (1)`**:
   for `A ≅ R[T]/(P)` multiplication by `P'(θ)` induces `A^♯/A ≅ A/(P'(θ)) ≅ Ω[A⁄R]`.
4. `isPushout_quotientMap`, `kaehlerQuotientBaseChange` — **card `(5)`**: base change of Kähler
   differentials along the reduction of the base, `Ω[(A/IA)⁄(R/I)] ≅ (A/IA) ⊗_A Ω[A⁄R]`.
5. `kaehler_cyclic_quotient`, `kaehler_cyclic_quotient_algebra` — the cyclicity actually consumed
   downstream, transported along the surjection `Ω[A⁄R] ↠ Ω[B⁄k]` and along quotients of `B`.
6. `residueDerivation`, `surjective_liftKaehler` — **card `(6)`**: the cotangent fibre `𝔪ᵢ/𝔪ᵢ²`
   is a quotient of `Ω[Bᵢ⁄k]`.
7. `isPrincipal_maximalIdeal_of_kaehler_cyclic` — **card `(6)`, conclusion**: a cyclic Kähler
   module forces `dim_k 𝔪ᵢ/𝔪ᵢ² ≤ 1`, hence `𝔪ᵢ` principal (Nakayama, via mathlib's
   `finrank_cotangentSpace_le_one_iff`).
8. `exists_sub_aeval_mem_pow`, `adjoin_eq_top_of_isPrincipal_nilpotent` — **card `(7)`**: a local
   algebra with principal nilpotent maximal ideal and residue field `k` is `k[tᵢ]`.
9. `isCoprime_shift_pow`, `piQuotAlgHom`, `piQuotAlgHom_surjective`,
   `exists_adjoin_pi_quotient_eq_top` — **card `(8)`**: distinct centres `λᵢ` make the local
   factors pairwise coprime, and the Chinese remainder theorem monogenerates the product.
10. `piAlgHom_surjective`, `exists_adjoin_pi_eq_top_of_local` — **card `(7)`–`(8)` combined**: the
    same argument run directly on abstract local factors, translating each generator to its own
    centre; needs only that `k` is infinite (no characteristic hypothesis).
11. `exists_sub_algebraMap_mem_of_isPrime` — **card `(4)`, residue fields**: a finite separating
    family of `R`-points `A ↪ R^N` forces every residue ring of `A` to be generated by the image
    of `R`; the proof is `⨅ᵢ ker φᵢ = ⊥ ≤ 𝔭` plus primeness.
12. `exists_adjoin_eq_top_of_kaehler_cyclic_fibre` — the residual fibre `B = A/𝔪A` is monogenic
    over `k`, assembled from 5–10 through mathlib's Artinian decomposition
    `IsArtinianRing.quotNilradicalPowEquivPi`.
13. `adjoin_eq_top_of_adjoin_residual` — **card `(9)`**: Nakayama lifts a generator of `B` to a
    generator of `A` (`A = R[θ] + 𝔪A` and `A` module-finite give `A = R[θ]`).
14. `exists_adjoin_eq_top_of_kaehler_cyclic` — **card `(2) ⇒ (3)`, equation `(9)`**: the full
    implication.

Items 1, 2, 3, 14 close the cycle `(1) ⇒ (2) ⇒ (3) ⇒ (1)`, which is the card's Theorem 1.

### Theorem 2 — corank two: equal determinant, unequal modules

15. `quadDifferentMatrix`, `quadDifferent_mul_left`, `smith_quadDifferent` — **card `(18)`**:
    multiplication by `d_x = 2x − a` in the basis `(1,x)` has Smith form `diag(1, a²)`.
16. `mul_kronecker_mul_smith`, `smith_kronecker_quadDifferent` — **card `(19)`**: the Kronecker
    square has Smith form `diag(1, a², a², a⁴)`, with unit determinants on both flanks.
17. `nonempty_cokerQuad`, `nonempty_cokerKronecker` — **card `(16)`**:
    `L = A^♯/A ≅ R/(a²) ⊕ R/(a²) ⊕ R/(a⁴)`.
18. `nonempty_cokerQuadTensorOne`, `nonempty_cokerOneTensorQuad`, `nonempty_omega_corank_two` —
    **card `(17)`, `(20)`**: `Ω[A⁄R] ≅ (R/(a²))⁴`, one summand per hypersurface relation.
19. `corank_two_length_eq_partition_ne` — the bookkeeping: both modules have length `8` over
    `R/(a⁴)`-support, but the exponent partitions `(2,2,4)` and `(2,2,2,2)` differ.  This
    declaration is the arithmetic of the two partitions only; it does not by itself separate the
    modules.
20. `cons_one_sq_dvd_sq`, `sq_smul_omegaModel_eq_zero`, `exists_sq_smul_traceModel_ne_zero` — the
    separating invariant: `a²` annihilates the cotangent model, because every one of its Smith
    entries divides `a²`, while the class of `1` in the `(a⁴)`-summand of the trace quotient
    survives multiplication by `a²` (over a domain, `a⁴ ∣ a²` with `a ≠ 0` forces `a` to be a
    unit).
21. `not_nonempty_linearEquiv_traceModel_omegaModel`,
    `not_nonempty_linearEquiv_coker_kronecker_omega` — **cards `(16)`–`(17)`, boxed, the actual
    punchline**: over a domain in which `a` is a nonzero nonunit the trace quotient and the
    cotangent module of the tensor-square order are *not isomorphic*.  Equal Kähler and Dedekind
    different determinants therefore do not force isomorphic modules.
22. `not_nonempty_linearEquiv_coker_kronecker_omega_powerSeries` — the same over the card's literal
    base `R = ℂ[[a]]` with `a = X`, with no hypotheses: `X ≠ 0`, `X` is not a unit (its constant
    coefficient is `0`), and `2` is a unit.

## Scope

The Lean statement of `(2) ⇒ (3)` replaces the card's "`R` a discrete valuation ring with
uniformizer `a`" by "`R` a local ring, reduction modulo `𝔪`", which is strictly more general and
specialises to the card by `𝔪 = (a)`.  The card's `A ⊂ O = R^N` of finite index enters as the
separating family `φ : ι → (A →ₐ[R] R)` of coordinate projections, which is exactly the data of an
`R`-algebra embedding `A ↪ R^N`; `Module.Finite R A` is the finite-index hypothesis.  The infinite
residue field is `[Infinite (R ⧸ maximalIdeal R)]`.

Theorem 2 is formalised over an arbitrary commutative ring in which `2` is a unit, which covers the
card's `R = ℂ[[a]]`; the two modules are exhibited by explicit Smith normal forms rather than by
appeal to a structure theorem.

The card's closing "exact structural consequence" section is interpretive commentary on the two
theorems and carries no separate mathematical claim.
