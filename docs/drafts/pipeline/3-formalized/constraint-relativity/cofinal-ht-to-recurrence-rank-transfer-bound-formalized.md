## New infrastructure

The corpus had the *equality* case (a spanning family of exactly `dtrdeg` members is a basis) but
neither of the two one-sided bounds needed here:

- `cardinalMk_le_differentialTranscendenceDegree` — an independent family has at most `dtrdeg`
  members, from `Matroid.Indep.cardinalMk_le_cRank`;
- `differentialTranscendenceDegree_le_of_adjoin_eq_top` — a generating family has at least
  `dtrdeg` members, by passing to a matroid basis of the generating set and showing it is a basis
  of the whole matroid;
- `differentialAlgebraicOver_of_mem` — an element of a derivation-closed subfield is differentially
  algebraic over it;
- `totient_prod_of_pairwise_coprime` — mathlib has `Nat.totient_mul` for two coprime factors but no
  finite-product version;
- `differentialJetSet_empty`, `differentialAdjoin_empty` (added to
  `HTGeometry.DifferentialIndependence`) — the differential field generated by the empty family is
  the coefficient field.

## Formal theorem chain

All declarations live in namespace `HTGeometry.ConstraintRelativity`.

1. `cardinalMk_le_differentialTranscendenceDegree` — the `≤` half of card `(1)`: layer
   independence bounds `dtrdeg` from below.
2. `differentialTranscendenceDegree_le_of_adjoin_eq_top` — the `≥` half: a carrier with `D − 1`
   phase coordinates has `dtrdeg ≤ D − 1`.
3. `rank_transfer` — **card `(2)`**: if a differentially independent family lands inside the
   differential field generated by a finite family of phase coordinates, then the coordinate family
   is at least as large.  This is monotonicity of differential transcendence degree along the
   no-loss embedding, in the exact form the card uses.
4. `carrier_level_bound` — **card `(2)`, boxed**: with `N·m` independent directions realized by
   `D` phase coordinates, `N·m + 1 ≤ D + 1`, i.e. `D_N ≥ N m + 1`.
5. `carrier_level_bound'` — item 4 without the nondegeneracy assumption on the carrier.  The
   empty carrier generates only the coefficient field (`differentialAdjoin_empty`), which contains
   no differentially independent element, so the bound holds there too.
6. `totient_prod_of_pairwise_coprime`, `card_units_prod_eq_prod_totient` — **card `(3)`**: the
   simultaneous-unit-residue state count is `m = ∏ φ(q_i)`.
7. `carrier_level_bound_prod_totient` — **card `(4)`**, stated as a theorem rather than left to
   substitution: with `N` layers of simultaneous unit residues modulo a pairwise coprime family
   embedded without loss into a carrier with `D` phase coordinates,
   `N ∏ φ(q_i) + 1 ≤ D + 1`.

## Scope

The card is explicitly a *conditional* differential-coordinate bound: its hypothesis is the no-loss
embedding, and the formalization takes exactly that hypothesis (`hembed`).  The "Consequence" and
"Hostile audit" sections are commentary on what the hypothesis does and does not assert; nothing
there is a further theorem.
