## Realization

The acting monoid `M` of unital `k`-algebra endomorphisms is `M : Submonoid (F →ₐ[k] F)`, whose
multiplication is composition; `A : Subalgebra k F` is the observable algebra.

| Card | Lean |
|---|---|
| `Def_M(A)`, card `(1)` | `defectClosure M A = Algebra.adjoin k ((A : Set F) ∪ defectSet M A)` |
| `Orb_M(A)`, card `(2)` | `orbitClosure M A = Algebra.adjoin k (orbitSet M A)` |

## Formal theorem chain

All declarations live in namespace `HTGeometry.ConstraintRelativity`.

1. `subset_orbitSet`, `le_orbitClosure`, `le_defectClosure` — `A` sits inside both closures.
2. `defectClosure_eq_orbitClosure` — **(3)** `Def_M(A) = Orb_M(A)`.
3. `orbitClosure_invariant` — `Orb_M(A)` is `M`-invariant.
4. `orbitClosure_le` — **minimality**: every `M`-invariant subalgebra containing `A` contains
   `Orb_M(A)`.
5. `orbitClosure_self_of_invariant` — an invariant subalgebra is its own orbit closure.
6. `defectClosure_orbitClosure` — **idempotence**: `Def_M(Orb_M(A)) = Orb_M(A)`.
7. `defectIter`, `defectIter_succ_eq` — the finite recursion `(4)` satisfies `A_{n+1} = Orb_M(A)`
   for every `n ≥ 0`, i.e. `A_1 = A_2 = ⋯`.
8. `eq_orbitClosure_of_defectRecursion` — **(5)** in full transfinite form: any family
   `chain : Ordinal → Subalgebra k F` with `chain 0 = A`, `chain (β+1) = Def_M(chain β)`, and
   `chain λ = ⨆_{γ<λ} chain γ` at limits satisfies `chain α = Orb_M(A)` for every `α ≥ 1`.
9. `defectClosure_topologicalClosure_eq` — the **closed-subspace variant**: in a topological
   algebra the closed subalgebras generated by the two families coincide, because the underlying
   subalgebras already do.

The card's corollary about the corpus recursion `(6)` is item 8 read at `M = 𝒯`; the five
obligations it lists for a genuine successor law are a research directive, not a mathematical
statement, and are recorded on the paper card.
