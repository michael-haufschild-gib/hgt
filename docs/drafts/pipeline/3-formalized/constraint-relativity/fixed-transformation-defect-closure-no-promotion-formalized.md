## Realization

The acting monoid `M` of unital `k`-algebra endomorphisms is `M : Submonoid (F →ₐ[k] F)`, whose
multiplication is composition; `A : Subalgebra k F` is the observable algebra.

| Card | Lean |
|---|---|
| `Def_M(A)`, card `(1)` | `defectClosure M A = Algebra.adjoin k ((A : Set F) ∪ defectSet M A)` |
| `Orb_M(A)`, card `(2)` | `orbitClosure M A = Algebra.adjoin k (orbitSet M A)` |

## Formal theorem chain

All declarations live in namespace `HTGeometry.ConstraintRelativity`.

1. `subset_orbitSet`, `le_orbitClosure`, `le_defectClosure` — `A` sits inside both closures.
2. `defectClosure_eq_orbitClosure` — **(3)** `Def_M(A) = Orb_M(A)`.
3. `orbitClosure_invariant` — `Orb_M(A)` is `M`-invariant.
4. `orbitClosure_le` — **minimality**: every `M`-invariant subalgebra containing `A` contains
   `Orb_M(A)`.
5. `orbitClosure_self_of_invariant` — an invariant subalgebra is its own orbit closure.
6. `defectClosure_orbitClosure` — **idempotence**: `Def_M(Orb_M(A)) = Orb_M(A)`.
7. `defectIter`, `defectIter_succ_eq` — the finite recursion `(4)` satisfies `A_{n+1} = Orb_M(A)`
   for every `n ≥ 0`, i.e. `A_1 = A_2 = ⋯`.
8. `eq_orbitClosure_of_defectRecursion` — **(5)** in full transfinite form: any family
   `chain : Ordinal → Subalgebra k F` with `chain 0 = A`, `chain (β+1) = Def_M(chain β)`, and
   `chain λ = ⨆_{γ<λ} chain γ` at limits satisfies `chain α = Orb_M(A)` for every `α ≥ 1`.
9. `defectClosure_topologicalClosure_eq` — the **closed-subspace variant**: in a topological
   algebra the closed subalgebras generated by the two families coincide, because the underlying
   subalgebras already do.

The card's corollary about the corpus recursion `(6)` is item 8 read at `M = 𝒯`; the five
obligations it lists for a genuine successor law are a research directive, not a mathematical
statement, and are recorded on the paper card.

## The prime-shell application

The card's §"Prime-shell application" is the literal instance, formalized in
`PrimeShellDefectClosure.lean` on the bounded prime-valuation source of
`RHTower.PrimeDilationTropicalBoundaryTomography` (which already owns the boundary identity
`(7)` as `tropicalShellIndicator_primeDilation_sub` and its iterated form
`iteratedPrimeDifference_tropicalShellIndicator`; neither is re-proved here).

10. `pullbackAlgHom`, `primeDilationPullback`, `primeDilationMonoid` — precomposition with a
    self-map of the source is a unital algebra endomorphism of the reporter algebra, so the
    supplied prime dilations generate an acting monoid `M` in exactly the sense of the generic
    theorem.
11. `shellAlgebra`, `shellOrbitAlgebra` — the observable algebra `A₀` generated by the radial
    shell reporters and its orbit closure.
12. `primeDifference_mem_shellOrbitAlgebra`,
    `iteratedPrimeDifference_mem_shellOrbitAlgebra` — the orbit algebra absorbs one and then any
    finite iteration of prime-dilation covariance defects; the first uses
    `orbitClosure_invariant`, the second is a list induction.
13. `facetDifference_mem_shellOrbitAlgebra` — **card `(7)`**: the prime-winning facet difference
    `b_{p,m-1} − b_{p,m}` is exactly `V_p^*a_m − a_m` and therefore lies in `Orb_M(A₀)`.
14. `faceDifference_mem_shellOrbitAlgebra` — the same for every simultaneous-winning face of a
    squarefree prime list: the whole finite face nerve is already inside `Orb_M(A₀)`.  The sign
    `(−1)^{k−1}` is removed by scaling, since it squares to one.
15. `shell_defectClosure_eq`, `shell_defectIter_eq`, `shell_defectRecursion_eq` — **cards `(3)`,
    `(5)`, and the prime-shell content of `(8)`**: adjoining every radial covariance defect gives
    exactly the orbit algebra of the supplied dilations, and the transfinite recursion is constant
    from stage one on.  So the radial experiment does produce new observable distinctions — the
    facets and the face nerve of item 14 — while producing no transformation outside the
    dilations that were supplied.

Boxed `(8)` as written on the card composes results from three different cards ("deeper
fixed-action words", "faithful re-encoding", and this one) into one sentence; it is a
cross-card summary, not a statement of this card.  Its content *within this card's scope* is
item 15 together with item 14, and that is what is formalized.
