## Setup

Let `R` be a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer `a`, fraction field `K`, and normalized
valuation

\[
 v:K^\times\to\mathbb Z,
 \qquad v(a)=1.
 \tag{1}
\]

Fix pairwise distinct points `x_1,...,x_N in R`. Put

\[
 P(T)=\prod_{i=1}^N(T-x_i),
 \qquad
 A=R[T]/(P),
 \tag{2}
\]

and define sheet-evaluation observer

\[
 J:A\longrightarrow R^N,
 \qquad
 g(T)\longmapsto(g(x_1),\ldots,g(x_N)).
 \tag{3}
\]

In monomial basis `1,T,...,T^(N-1)`, its matrix is Vandermonde matrix

\[
 V=(x_i^{j-1})_{1\le i,j\le N}.
 \tag{4}
\]

For nonempty `I subset {1,...,N}`, define ultrametric collision energy

\[
 \mathcal E(I)=\sum_{\{i,j\}\subset I}v(x_i-x_j),
 \tag{5}
\]

and, for `1<=k<=N`,

\[
 d_k=\min_{|I|=k}\mathcal E(I),
 \qquad d_0=d_1=0.
 \tag{6}
\]

All minima are finite because sheets are distinct.

## Theorem 1: determinantal divisors are minimum collision energies

Let `D_k(V)` be ideal generated by all `k`-by-`k` minors of `V`. Then

\[
 \boxed{D_k(V)=(a^{d_k}).}
 \tag{7}
\]

Consequently, if

\[
 V\sim\operatorname{diag}(a^{s_1},\ldots,a^{s_N}),
 \qquad 0\le s_1\le\cdots\le s_N,
 \tag{8}
\]

is Smith form, then

\[
 \boxed{s_k=d_k-d_{k-1}\quad(1\le k\le N).}
 \tag{9}
\]

Thus full observer barcode is discrete derivative of minimum ultrametric energy profile.

### Proof

Choose row set `I={i_1,...,i_k}` and column exponents

\[
 0\le m_1<\cdots<m_k\le N-1.
\]

Corresponding minor is alternant

\[
 M_{I,m}=\det(x_{i_\alpha}^{m_\beta})_{\alpha,\beta=1}^k.
 \tag{10}
\]

As polynomial in variables `(X_1,...,X_k)`, every alternant is divisible over `Z` by Vandermonde:

\[
 \det(X_\alpha^{m_\beta})
 =\prod_{\alpha<\beta}(X_\beta-X_\alpha)\,S_m(X_1,\ldots,X_k),
 \tag{11}
\]

where `S_m` is symmetric polynomial with integer coefficients (equivalently a Schur polynomial,
up to indexing/sign convention). Evaluating in `R` gives

\[
 v(M_{I,m})\ge
 \sum_{\alpha<\beta}v(x_{i_\beta}-x_{i_\alpha})
 =\mathcal E(I)\ge d_k.
 \tag{12}
\]

Zero minors have valuation infinity and satisfy same inequality. Conversely, for every row set `I`,
choosing first `k` columns `m=(0,1,...,k-1)` makes `S_m=1`; minor is exactly Vandermonde and has
valuation `\mathcal E(I)`. Choose energy-minimizing `I`. Hence smallest valuation among all `k`-minors
is exactly `d_k`, proving `(7)`.

Over DVR, valuation of `k`-th determinantal divisor is sum `s_1+...+s_k`. Therefore

\[
 d_k=s_1+\cdots+s_k.
\]

Subtract equation for `k-1`, proving `(9)`. QED.

## Corollary 2: barcode, orientation, and cotangent square

Observer is injective and

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{coker}J
 \cong\bigoplus_{k=1}^N R/(a^{d_k-d_{k-1}}),}
 \tag{13}
\]

where summand with exponent zero is zero. Moreover

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{ord}_a\det J=d_N,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{ord}_a\operatorname{Disc}(P)=2d_N.}
 \tag{14}
\]

Writing `C=coker J`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Fitt}_0(C)=(a^{d_N}),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Fitt}_0^R(\Omega_{A/R})
 =(a^{2d_N})
 =\operatorname{Fitt}_0(C)^2.}
 \tag{15}
\]

### Proof

Distinct roots make Vandermonde determinant nonzero, hence `J` injective. Equation `(13)` is Smith
classification and `(9)`. Root formulas

\[
 \det V=\prod_{i<j}(x_j-x_i),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Disc}(P)=\prod_{i<j}(x_j-x_i)^2
\]

give `(14)`. First identity in `(15)` is determinant presentation of `C`. Since

\[
 \Omega_{A/R}\cong A/(P')\,dT,
\]

its `R`-module presentation is multiplication by `P'` on finite free module `A`. Its determinant is
the norm of `P'`, associated to `\operatorname{Disc}(P)`. This proves `(15)`. QED.

Orientation therefore carries half total collision energy; scalar discriminant doubles it and
forgets sign/orientation.

## Corollary 3: ultrametrically separated mixed-order horizon

Assume every `x_i` is nonzero and reorder so

\[
 r_i=v(x_i),
 \qquad
 r_1\le\cdots\le r_N.
 \tag{16}
\]

Suppose

\[
 v(x_j-x_i)=r_i
 \qquad(i<j).
 \tag{17}
\]

Condition is automatic when `r_i<r_j`; for equal orders it says leading residues are distinct.
Then

\[
 \boxed{
 d_k=\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}(k-i)r_i,
 \qquad
 s_k=\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}r_i.}
 \tag{18}
\]

Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 J\sim\operatorname{diag}
 \left(1,a^{r_1},a^{r_1+r_2},\ldots,
 a^{r_1+\cdots+r_{N-1}}\right).}
 \tag{19}
\]

The underived ideal sees only shallowest order,

\[
 (x_1,\ldots,x_N)=(a^{r_1}),
 \tag{20}
\]

while maximal observer depth is

\[
 s_N=r_1+\cdots+r_{N-1}.
 \tag{21}
\]

Adding one further, deepest sheet satisfying `(16)`--`(17)` appends abstract persistence bar

\[
 R/(a^{r_1+\cdots+r_N});
 \tag{22}
\]

the new depth is independent of `r_{N+1}` itself.

### Proof

For subset `I={i_1<...<i_k}`, condition `(17)` gives

\[
 \mathcal E(I)=\sum_{\alpha=1}^{k-1}(k-\alpha)r_{i_\alpha}.
 \tag{23}
\]

Monotonicity of `(r_i)` shows minimum occurs at `I={1,...,k}`, proving formula for `d_k`.
Subtract consecutive values to obtain `s_k`. Equation `(20)` follows from DVR ideals. At ranks `N`
and `N+1`, first `N` Smith exponents agree and new exponent is sum of first `N` orders, proving
`(22)`. QED.

Equal common order `r_i=r` recovers simple horizon staircase

\[
 s_k=(k-1)r.
 \tag{24}
\]

## Constraint-horizon interpretation

The theorem produces exact four-face local law:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{array}{c}
 \text{ultrametric sheet geometry }\{v(x_i-x_j)\}\\
 \Updownarrow\\
 \text{minimum collision energies }(d_k)\\
 \Updownarrow\\
 \text{observer Smith/persistence spectrum }(s_k)\\
 \Downarrow\\
 \text{orientation, discriminant, and cotangent Fitting order}.
 \end{array}}
 \tag{25}
\]

This is stronger than determinant/discriminant alone: `d_N` records only total collision energy,
whereas all `d_k` recover observer depth distribution. Underived support can remain fixed while
barcode changes arbitrarily deeply. Constraint multiplicity is not erased at horizon; it is moved
from codimension into normal persistence.

Equation `(9)` also forces convexity

\[
 d_k-d_{k-1}\le d_{k+1}-d_k,
 \tag{26}
\]

a nontrivial restriction on minimum ultrametric energy sequence, because Smith exponents are ordered.

## Conjecture/failure loops extracted

1. **Determinant suffices.** False: different Smith partitions can have same sum `d_N`.
2. **Mixed orders destroy exact normal form.** False under `(17)`; formula `(19)` is exact.
3. **Deepest sheet controls new depth.** False: successor depth `(22)` is accumulated history of
   earlier sheets.
4. **More constraints strengthen underived locus.** False in mixed profile: ideal remains
   `(a^{r_1})`.
5. **Arbitrary collision geometry is invisible except through discriminant.** False: minimum energies
   `(d_1,...,d_N)` determine full observer barcode.
6. **Barcode selects physical horizon.** False: construction is observer law after roots are supplied.
7. **All observer data are metric-dependent.** False for invariant factors; analytic singular-value
   constants depend on metrics, valuation exponents do not.
8. **Scalar phase/determinant captures all transport.** False in prime phase model: traceless
   non-Abelian excess curvature can have zero determinant curvature.
