# Prime-source trace descent to the actual zero divisor

## Statement

At each absolute ordinate retain both complete fibres, with analytic
multiplicity, and the traces of every finite integer product of prime
translations. Let `B` be the field generated by all these mixed traces and
`E` the full prime spectral field. Then `E/B` is algebraic and normal.
The field `B` is also generated by paired full-width germs of the continued
prime–archimedean source, tested against the corresponding entire exponentials.

Every embedding `E→ℂ` over `B` transports a zero's full prime pattern to one
actual zero of the same absolute height, preserving the character divisor
with multiplicity. The Galois group acts faithfully on the entire zero support.
Ordinary conjugation is a nontrivial group element. Every arithmetic symmetry
commutes with functional reflection on zeros. Fixing `B` automatically implies
full prime-spectrum compatibility for an ambient scalar embedding.

The base field is real, transcendental over `ℚ`, and strictly smaller than `E`.

## Paper proof

A zero `ρ` defines the character `m↦exp(ρ Σ m_p log p)` of the free integer
prime group. Two prime coordinates distinguish arbitrary complex exponents.
For an embedding fixing `B`, equality of all mixed traces is equality of the
sums of these characters before and after the embedding. Dedekind independence
makes the corresponding finitely supported character measures equal.
Their integer coefficients recover multiplicities. Each image character is
one actual zero character in the same paired fibre, and every target character
occurs. Joint-prime injectivity makes the selected zero independent of the
containing packet. Composition gives a group action; fixing all zero patterns
fixes every prime spectral generator, proving faithfulness.

For each monomial and each paired fibre, traces at integer multiples of its
prime vector are all power sums of its values. Newton identities place the
coefficients of the complete root polynomial in `B`. All its roots lie in `E`.
Conversely these polynomials include every original prime eigenvalue, so their
splitting fields have compositum exactly `E`. This proves normality and
algebraicity for the full extension.

Conjugating copies exchanges the two ordinate fibres and conjugates every
prime value. Thus every trace and the entire base field are real. Conjugation
acts nontrivially because every actual zero is nonreal and its joint prime
pattern distinguishes it from its conjugate. The base is strictly smaller.
If the base were algebraic over `ℚ`, the algebraic extension `E/B` would make
`E` algebraic over `ℚ`, contradicting its proved transcendental prime value.

The prime pattern at `1−ρ` is the rational unit-weight prime product divided
by the pattern at `ρ`. Every arithmetic automorphism fixes that rational
product. Joint-prime injectivity therefore proves commutation with functional
reflection, without asserting that reflection lifts.

For a scalar embedding fixing `B`, the preceding character theorem transports
every original zero. Products and inverses extend transport over the entire
integer zero group, discharging full prime compatibility.

For the source identification, every entire weight has a stable full-fibre
contour germ. The full-width admissibility theorem and the discrete slab gap
supply admissible boundaries uniformly for narrow slabs and bands sufficiently
close to the strip edges. Weighted residues recover every multiplicity copy.
The weight `exp((1/2+iw) Σ m_p log p)` takes the desired prime monomial value at
the centered zero coordinate. Pairing the two germs gives exactly the mixed
trace. Hence the source-germ and spectral definitions generate the same field.

## Boundary
