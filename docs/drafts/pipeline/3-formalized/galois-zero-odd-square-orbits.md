# Source norms of squared zero widths

- **ID:** galois-zero-odd-square-orbits
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Questions:** Complete-fibre arithmetic; location of the central width involution.

Let `K` be the complete-fibre source field, `E` the even-width field,
`F` the signed-width field, and `ω∈E×/(E×)²` the global width class.
For a nonzero width put `d=δ_q²`, `D=K(d)⊆E`, and `n=[D:K]`.

## Theorem

The nonzero source norm gives the exact class identity

```
[N_(D/K)(d)] = ω^n   in E×/(E×)².
```

Thus any nonzero squared width of odd source degree gives a continuous
section of the entire restriction `Gal(F/K)→Gal(E/K)`.
Unconditionally, either this restriction splits continuously, or every
nonzero squared-width minimal polynomial over `K` has even degree, and
every nonzero signed-width minimal polynomial has degree divisible by four.

The norm uses the actual simple source extension generated by the squared
width. The splitting conclusion concerns the full infinite fields and their
Krull topologies.

## Proof

The extension `D/K` is finite and separable. Normality of `E/K` makes its
minimal polynomial split in `E`. Its norm is the product over all
`K`-embeddings `D→E`, and their number is the minimal-polynomial degree `n`.
Each embedding extends to an automorphism of the whole `E/K`. Invariance
of `ω` therefore makes each factor have class `ω`; taking square classes
of the norm product gives `ω^n`.

Square classes have exponent two. If `n` is odd, the norm supplies an actual
source representative of `ω`. The global descent theorem then supplies
a continuous section. Taking the contrapositive for every nonzero width
gives the even-degree alternative.

In the nonsplit case, `F≠E`. The anchor presentation then puts every
nonzero `δ_q` outside `E`, hence outside `D=K(δ_q²)`. Since its square
lies in `D`, the extension `K(δ_q)/D` is quadratic. The tower law gives
`[K(δ_q):K]=2[D:K]`, proving the divisibility by four.
