# The fibre layers over the prime traces are finite Galois and generate `E`

- **ID:** gtz-fibre-layers-over-the-prime-traces
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM
- **Source:** programme card `gtz-galois-theory-of-zeros-programme.md`, problem 1 (controlled finite subfields and composita; review-1411 §2.19).
- **Statement:** `Z_t` the zeros with `|Im ρ| = |t|`, `E_t = ⨆_{ρ ∈ Z_t} B(p^ρ : p)`.
  (A) `Z_t` is finite, `G` keeps it; `E_t/B` is finite and Galois;
  (B) `σ ∈ Gal(E/E_t)` iff `σ` fixes every zero of `Z_t`; `[E_t : B]` is the index of this
  pointwise stabilizer;
  (C) `[E_t : B]` is even when `Z_t ≠ ∅`;
  (D) `⨆_t E_t = E`.

## Goal

The finite layers through which infinite Galois theory over `B` runs: one finite Galois
extension per height, acted on through the permutations of the zeros of that height.

## Proof

(A) The copies of ordinate `±t` (a finite type) map onto `Z_t`; each `B(p^ρ) = B(2^ρ, 3^ρ)` is
finite, so is a finite compositum. `G` keeps `|Im|`. (B) `σ` fixes a compositum iff it fixes each
piece (Galois correspondence `L ≤ E^H ⇔ H ≤ Gal(E/L)` with `H = ⟨σ⟩`), and fixes `B(p^ρ)` iff
`σρ = ρ`. The pointwise stabilizer of a `G`-stable set is normal; in infinite Galois theory a
normal closed fixing subgroup means `E_t/B` Galois. Krull: degree = index. (C) `[F_ρ : B]` is even
and divides `[E_t : B]`. (D) `E` is generated by the `2^ρ, 3^ρ`, and `2^ρ ∈ E_{Im ρ}`. ∎
