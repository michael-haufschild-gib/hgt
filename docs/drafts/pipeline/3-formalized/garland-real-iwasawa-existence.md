## Dependency-closed algebraic proof

Put O=F[[X]], E=F((X)), and let I be the matrix Iwahori. Let
P₁ be SL₂(O) inside SL₂(E), and let

    j([[a,b],[c,d]]) = [[a,X⁻¹b],[Xc,d]],   P₀=j(SL₂(O)).

Both Pᵢ are subgroups. The map j is the previously constructed diagonal
rescaling, with inverse rescaling by X. The criterion c(0)=0 proves
I⊂P₀∩P₁. In the central extension take the full preimages of P₀,P₁;
both contain the actual positive Borel B and the full center.

Reduction of p∈SL₂(O) gives h∈SL₂(F). Then
q=p const(h)⁻¹ has reduction one. Both q and j(q) lie in I:
the only apparently negative entry of j(q) is X⁻¹q₀₁, and q₀₁(0)=0
makes it integral; its lower-left entry Xq₁₀ is strictly integral.
It follows that every element of the first central parabolic is B times
an element of the finite SL₂ subgroup, and every element of the second
is B times an element of the affine SL₂ subgroup. Central discrepancies
are absorbed into B using its exact kernel description.

Use the oriented affine embedding: its upper triangular subgroup lies
in B, just as for the finite embedding. Coordinate swap is an involution,
so the affine raw embedding and the oriented embedding have the same
range. Over the reals apply ordinary SL₂ Iwasawa in each source. If Kᵢ
is the image of the stabilizer of i, each central parabolic is BKᵢ.

These two parabolics generate the full central loop group. Indeed their
matrix images contain W(1), W(X), and therefore

    W(X)W(1)⁻¹ = diag(X⁻¹,X).

All integral powers of this diagonal and their products with W(1) are
the already proved affine Weyl representatives. Affine Bruhat expresses
every matrix using these representatives and two Iwahori factors.
Lifting this equality and absorbing the residual central element proves
generation of the central extension as well.

Let K be the subgroup generated by K₀,K₁ and central signs. Induct over
a word in the two parabolics, with invariant g=bk. For a generator
p∈Pᵢ, the product p b still lies in Pᵢ, since B⊂Pᵢ. Factor it as b'kᵢ;
then p g=b'(kᵢk)∈BK. The same argument handles inverse generators, because
each Pᵢ is a subgroup. The identity starts the induction. Thus G=BK.

## Exact scope

This proves existence only. It does not assert uniqueness of Borel torus
coordinates, compactness of an infinite-dimensional group, a spherical
character outside B, or any arithmetic convergence. Intersection control
is separately open in [its own card](../1-in-progress/garland-compact-borel-intersection.md).
The [determinant-norm route](../0-backlog/garland-determinant-metric-iwasawa.md) remains
an alternative for more general root data.
