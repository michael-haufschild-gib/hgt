# Forward tameness of the heat cover: no zero escapes over times with `Re τ > 0`

- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM (Lean)
- **Question:** review-1211 §2.19 Q5 (the group generated by the monodromy); tool: monodromy
  of analytic covers.

`H_τ(z) = ∫ Φ(x) e^{τx²} e^{izx} dx` the heat family at complex time; box
`K = {t₀ ≤ Re τ, ‖τ‖ ≤ T₀}`, `t₀ > 0`. Branch times: the times with a multiple zero.

## Theorems

1. (Slope of ξ.) `xiSlope(s) = ½ log(s/2π)`. For `Re s ≥ 5`:
   `‖ξ'/ξ(s) − xiSlope(s)‖ ≤ 4/‖s‖ + 2^{5−Re s}/12`. If `‖ξ'/ξ − a‖ ≤ η` on the segment from
   `p` to `q` (in `Re > 1`), then
   `‖ξ(q) − ξ(p)e^{(q−p)a}‖ ≤ ‖ξ(p)‖ e^{Re((q−p)a)} (e^{η‖q−p‖} − 1)`.
2. (Gaussian average.) For `Re τ > 0`: `H_τ(z) = 𝒜(τ, z) + 𝒜(τ, −z)`,
   `𝒜(τ, z) = ∫_0^∞ g_τ(u) ξ(½ + iz + u) du`, `g_τ(w) = (4πτ)^{-1/2} e^{−w²/(4τ)}`.
3. (Saddle value, R1.) For `τ ∈ K`, `Im z ≤ Y`, `‖z‖ ≥ R(t₀, T₀, Y, ε)`: with
   `s = ½ + iz`, `a = xiSlope(s + Y + 5)`, `M = ξ(s + 2τa) e^{−τa²}`:
   `M ≠ 0` and `‖𝒜(τ, z) − M‖ ≤ ε‖M‖`.
4. (Strip, D.) For `τ ∈ K`, `Im z ≤ −(12 + 21T₀)`, `‖z‖ ≥ R`: `‖𝒜(τ, −z)‖ ≤ ε‖M‖`. Hence
   `H_τ(z) ≠ 0` for `|Im z| ≥ 12 + 21T₀`, `‖z‖ ≥ R`.
5. (Local exponential.) For `τ₀, τ ∈ K`, `Im z₀, Im z ≤ Y`, `‖z − z₀‖ ≤ 1`, `‖z₀‖ ≥ R`:
   `‖𝒜(τ, z) − M₀ e^{ia₀(z−z₀) + a₀²(τ−τ₀)}‖ ≤ ε ‖M₀ e^{…}‖`, `M₀, a₀` the saddle value and
   slope at `(τ₀, z₀)`.
6. (Two exponentials.) For `τ₀, τ ∈ K`, `|Im z₀| ≤ 12 + 21T₀`, `‖z − z₀‖ ≤ 1`, `‖z₀‖ ≥ R`,
   with `a, b` the slopes at `z₀`, `−z₀` and `P, Q ≠ 0` the saddle values:
   `H_τ(z) = P e^{ia(z−z₀) + a²(τ−τ₀)} + Q e^{−ib(z−z₀) + b²(τ−τ₀)}` up to `ε` times the sum
   of the two moduli.
7. (Phase lock.) If `(τ₀, z₀)`, `(τ, z)` are zeros as in 6, then `‖e^Φ − 1‖ ≤ ε` for
   `Φ = (a + b)(i(z − z₀) + (a − b)(τ − τ₀))`. `‖a − b‖ ≤ 3`, `‖a + b‖ → ∞`.
8. (Simple.) For `τ ∈ K` every zero `z` of `H_τ` with `‖z‖ ≥ R` is simple.
9. (Trap.) If `γ` is a path in `K` within `1/12` of `γ(s₁)` and `w` a continuous family of
   zeros over it with `‖w(s₁)‖ ≥ R`, then `‖w(s) − w(s₁)‖ ≤ 1/2`.
10. (No escape.) Along a path of times in `{Re τ > 0}` no zero escapes.
11. (Lifting and transport.) Along a path in `{Re τ > 0}` that avoids the branch times every
    zero of `H_{γ(0)}` lifts, every zero of `H_{γ(1)}` is the end of a lift, and the transport
    is a bijection of the two fibres.
12. (Discreteness.) The multiple zeros form a closed subset of `ℂ²`; over `K` there are
    finitely many, so `K` contains finitely many branch times, and every forward time has a
    neighbourhood without another branch time.

## Proof

1. Four-factor formula for `ξ'/ξ`, Stirling for the digamma function,
   `Λ(n)n^{−σ} ≤ Λ(n)n^{−4}2^{5−σ}`. Two-point comparison: Grönwall for `ξ(p + θ(q−p))
   e^{−θ(q−p)a}`.
2. `H_τ` is the Gaussian average of `ξ` over the horizontal line; split at `u = 0`.
3. Corner shift of the ray to height `c = Im(2τa)` (Cauchy on rectangles); on the shifted ray
   `g_τ(u + ic) e^{ua} = e^{−ica+τa²} g_τ(u − w₀)`, `w₀ = Re(2τa)`, and the two-point comparison
   gives `ξ(s + ic + u) = ξ(p) e^{(u − w₀)a}(1 + o(1))` with `η → 0` near the saddle and
   `η ≤ C + λ|u − w₀|/2` on the ray; the Gaussian concentrates at `w₀`. The side and the
   initial piece are smaller by `e^{−c t₀ α²}`, `α = Re a`.
4. `𝒜(τ, −z) = conj 𝒜(τ̄, z̄)`. Deep below the strip no shift is needed; in the strip-like
   range the reflected ray is shifted from the corner `Re = 9/2`; the exponent is
   `−2α(y' − 2βφ) + O(y' + α)` relative to `M`, negative for `y' ≥ 12 + 21T₀`.
5. Both saddle points `p₀ = s₀ + 2τ₀a₀`, `p = s + 2τa` lie in a disc of radius `O(α₀)` around
   the base point, where the slope of `ξ` is `a₀` up to an error decaying exponentially in
   `α₀`; one two-point comparison from `p₀` to `p`, and
   `(p − p₀)a₀ − τa² + τ₀a₀² = a₀(s − s₀) + a₀²(τ − τ₀) − τ(a − a₀)²`, `a − a₀ = O(1/‖s₀‖)`.
6. 2, 4 (strip), 5 for `(z₀, z)` and `(−z₀, −z)`.
7. At the base zero `‖P + Q‖ ≤ ε(‖P‖ + ‖Q‖)`, at the second `‖Pu + Qv‖ ≤ ε(‖Pu‖ + ‖Qv‖)`;
   `P(u − v) = (Pu + Qv) − (P + Q)v` gives `‖u − v‖ ≤ 9ε‖v‖`, and `u/v = e^Φ`.
8. At a zero the derivative of the model is `i(a + b)P − ib(P + Q)`, of modulus
   `≥ (Re a + Re b)‖P‖ − 3ε‖b‖‖P‖`. Cauchy's estimate on the circle of radius
   `1/(1 + ‖a‖ + ‖b‖)` bounds the derivative of the error by `9ε(1 + ‖a‖ + ‖b‖)‖P‖`.
9. `‖w − w(s₁)‖ ≤ ‖Φ‖ + 3‖γ − γ(s₁)‖`. If `‖Φ‖ ≤ 1/2` the zero is within distance one, so
   `‖e^Φ − 1‖ ≤ 1/8` by 7, hence `‖Φ‖ ≤ 1/4` (`‖e^Φ − 1 − Φ‖ ≤ ‖Φ‖²`). `‖Φ‖` is continuous,
   starts at `0` and omits `(1/4, 1/2]`: it stays `≤ 1/4`.
10. The path lies in a box. Near the escape parameter `b` the path moves by less than `1/12`
    and the zero is far; by 9 it is bounded on `[s₁, b)`.
11. 10 and the alternative "lift or escape"; reversed path; uniqueness and injectivity of lifts.
12. `∂_z H_τ(z)` is a partial derivative of a function analytic in `(τ, z)`, hence continuous;
    the multiple zeros are closed, discrete (isolated branch points), and bounded over `K` by 8.

## Not done

- Exact separation of far zeros at one time (`z = z₀` or `‖z − z₀‖ ≥ c/log‖z₀‖`). The phase
  lock gives: two zeros within distance one have `Φ` within `2ε` of `2πiℤ`.
- Soft routes (barriers, counting, traces, normal families at a fixed scale) do not give
  non-escape: a far zero moves farther than the spacing, and the family varies on the time
  scale `1/log²`.
