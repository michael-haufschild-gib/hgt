## Actual lattice and arithmetic volume

For `N≥1`, let `L_N⊂ℤ^N` be generated by the divisor columns
`Σ_(d|j)e_d`, `2≤j≤N`. This definition uses divisibility alone.
Integral inversion proves

`L_N={v:Σ_j μ(j)v_j=0}`,

with actual integral basis `b_j=e_j−μ(j)e_1`, `2≤j≤N`.
Its Gram matrix is `I+u uᵀ`, `u_j=μ(j)`. Thus

`det Gram(L_N)=Q(N)=#{1≤j≤N:Squarefree j}`,
`V_N=√det Gram(L_N)=√Q(N)≤√N`.

Here `V_N` is the Gram volume of the constructed integral basis; no
measure-theoretic lattice-covolume comparison is asserted.
Rescaling basis vectors by integer units gives Gram entries
`δ_jk+1_Squarefree(j)1_Squarefree(k)`: a squarefree `I+11ᵀ` block and
orthogonal unit directions. This normalization removes Möbius signs from
the metric matrix, not from the gluing displacement.

## Actual Euclidean displacement

The real span of these basis vectors is exactly the kernel of
`v↦Σ μ(j)v_j`. Put `μ=(μ(1),…,μ(N))`. The explicitly constructed map

`P_N(v)=v−(Σ μ(j)v_j/Q(N))μ`

belongs to that span and satisfies, for every `y` in the span,

`‖v−y‖₂²=(Σ μ(j)v_j)²/Q(N)+‖P_N(v)−y‖₂²`.

Consequently `P_N(v)` minimizes squared Euclidean distance. The distance
`D_N(v)=‖v−P_N(v)‖₂` is defined from actual vectors and projection; its
comparison with the signed arithmetic sum is proved afterward.

For the independently defined gluing column `b=−Σ_(j≥2)e_j`,

`Nat.card G_N=|Σ_p index_p(N)|=V_N D_N(b)`.

When `G_N` is finite, its product-formula height splits exactly as

`H_N=½log Q(N)+log D_N(b)`.

At zero charge, `G_N≃ℤ`; the Lean theorem records this case separately
with the established `Nat.card` and logarithm conventions.

## Proof and remaining target

`divisorRelationCoordinates` and `divisorRelationBasis_coe` construct the
integral basis of the actual image lattice. The rank-one determinant and
`μ²=1_Squarefree` prove `divisorRelationGram_det_eq_squarefreeCount`.
`divisorUnsignedBasis_gram` proves the unit-normalized metric formula.
`mem_divisorRelationRealSpace_iff`, `divisorRelation_pythagorean` and
`divisorRelationProjection_minimizes` establish the actual nearest-point
comparison. `localIndices_volume_distance` and
`integerGluingHeight_volume_distance` retain the original arithmetic count.

The proved positive square-root volume leaves **transverse displacement
uncontrolled**. An independently proved `D_N(b)=Oε(N^ε)` would suffice for
RH-scale signed cancellation. No such bound, signed square-root estimate
or RH theorem follows from the Gram classification alone.
