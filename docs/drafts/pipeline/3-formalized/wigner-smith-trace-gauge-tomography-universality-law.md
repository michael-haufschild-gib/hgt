## Universal law

For every invertible symmetric finite scattering matrix, after adjoining the scattering entries,
the normalized full Wigner--Smith delay panel generates exactly the same differential field as the
unordered reciprocal projective tangent reporter together with the single scalar trace of the
full Wigner--Smith connection.  Thus passage to projective reciprocal tomography loses no matrix
information except the one trace-gauge mode.

The proof also establishes a base-relative matrix law: over an arbitrary auxiliary generator set,
all entries of any finite matrix generate exactly its traceless entries plus its trace.

## Paper proof

Normalized panel tomography first replaces the panel by all entries of the full connection `Q`.
The canonical identity
`Q = traceless(Q) + trace(Q)/(n+1) I` shows that, over any base set, the entry field of `Q` equals
the field generated by all traceless entries and `trace(Q)`; conversely the trace is a finite sum
of diagonal entries and every traceless entry is an entry of `Q` plus a rational trace correction.
Reciprocal projective tomography then replaces all traceless entries, relative to scattering
entries, by the unordered entries of `S * traceless(Q)`.  Lifting both field equalities through
unions gives the asserted exact decomposition.
