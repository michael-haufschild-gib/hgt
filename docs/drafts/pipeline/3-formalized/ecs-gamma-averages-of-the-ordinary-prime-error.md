# Gamma averages of the signed ordinary-prime error

For any energy-domain profile Φ, let P(t)=Σₚ(log p)p⁻¹ᐟ²Φ(t−log p), L=ℒΦ, and E(t)=P(t)−L(−½)eᵗᐟ². Its positive moving averages with density tⁿe⁻ᵗ/n! tend to −L(0)/2 with geometric error. These windows have mean n+1 and standard deviation √(n+1).

**Proof.** Write H for the Mangoldt head and Q for its proper-power subseries, using the convention ∫₀∞eᵖᵗP(t)dt. On −4<Re p<−½ the ordinary-prime transform is H−Q. Completing each head integral gives H=−L(p)(ζ′/ζ)(½−p)−T(p), with T holomorphic for Re p>−4. Thus H+L(−½)/(p+½) is regular wherever the entire unit (s−1)ζ(s) is nonzero at s=½−p. This includes the closed disk |p+1|≤1 by the low-height zero-free theorem. The quotient (L(p)−L(−½))/(p+½) has a removable singularity.

Q is holomorphic for Re p<0. At the only point of that disk on the imaginary axis, p=0, its continuation has an analytic numerator J(p)=pQ(p) with J(0)=−L(0)/2. Hence Q+L(0)/(2p) is the divided difference of J at zero near that point. Subtracting these regular functions continues the transform of E+L(0)/2 analytically around the closed disk. Compactness gives a larger disk, |p+1|≤R for some R>1.

On the original side, the ordinary-prime sum after damping by e⁻³ᵗᐟ⁴ converges absolutely in ray L² by the translated-profile bound and Σₚ(log p)p⁻⁵ᐟ⁴<∞. Pairing with an exponential gives the original transform. Its nth derivative at −1 is the integral against tⁿe⁻ᵗ by Cauchy–Schwarz and differentiation with an exponential margin. The elementary transforms for the two polar terms give the same physical formula for the corrected wave. Cauchy's estimate on the larger disk bounds its normalized moments by C R⁻ⁿ. This argument uses the Euler product, confinement and the low-height zero-free theorem, independently of the state equation. It gives no estimate for the signed error on an individual ray.
