## Mechanism under audit

The Lean-proved contracted-energy identity gives antitonicity of
`p*K(p,p)` for every actual common limit. Consider instead the explicit
real diagonal kernel

`Kodd(j,k) = if j=k and j odd then 1/j else 0`.

It is PSD by its diagonal quadratic form, has Kodd(1,1)=1 and the usual
radius. For odd p, simultaneous dilation scales the entire kernel by 1/p;
for even p, the dilated kernel vanishes. Thus it satisfies every structural
condition listed in the original common-limit compactness theorem. But
2*Kodd(2,2)=0 and 3*Kodd(3,3)=1. It cannot be any actual common limit.
This is a source-selective obstruction beyond the previously audited fixed
signed Gram table, which adds no independent coordinates.

The positive invariant is stronger than this example: if K(p,p)=0 for any
actual cofactor p, then K(q,q)=0 for every q>=p. The corresponding Gram
vectors vanish, and the cyclic Hilbert space is generated by the finitely
many vectors with q<p. Hence the actual module is finite dimensional with
dimension at most p-1. Infinite actual modules have strictly positive
energy at every fixed cofactor. The finite-generation step must use closed
span density and finite-dimensional closedness, not an algebraic span
surjectivity assumption.

## Closed paper proof and draft status

For the odd kernel, the quadratic form is exactly
`sum_(j odd) c_j^2/j`. For odd p, primality of two says `2 divides p*j`
iff `2 divides j`, giving exact scaling; for even p every dilated index is
deleted. Symmetry, the radius and unit mass follow immediately from the
existing white-noise kernel. The antitonicity violation at 2 and 3 is exact.
No existing source-selective numeric-order condition surfaced in the corpus
search; prior packet conditions compare multiplicative scales only.

For an actual kernel with K(p,p)=0, positivity and antitonicity give K(q,q)=0
for every q>=p. The Gram norm identity gives v_q=0. The span of the p-1
vectors v_1,...,v_(p-1) contains every cyclic vector. It is finite dimensional
and closed, and the existing dense-span theorem makes it the whole Hilbert
space. Its dimension is at most p-1. Thus an infinite actual module has
strictly positive diagonal at every cofactor. This is the positive invariant
exposed by the counterexample, not a restatement of the exclusion.
