## What closed last

The audit on the paper card recorded the exact obligation: *construct `Δ(2,3,7)` as a group,
produce torsion-free finite-index subgroups of unbounded index, and derive the fibre-count
hypotheses of `hurwitzTriangle_projectiveCapacity_exact` from that construction.*  All three are
now theorems.

The construction is the monodromy dictionary.  A connected unramified cover of the `(2,3,7)`
orbifold is a transitive pair of permutations `X`, `Y` with `X² = Y³ = (XY)⁷ = 1` and every
nontrivial power fixed-point-free; the marked fibres are the orbit sets, so freeness gives the
counts `2 f₂ = 3 f₃ = 7 f₇ = D` at once, and the lifted triangulation of the base sphere gives
`χ = f₂ + f₃ + f₇ − D = −D/42`.  Integrality of the genus is not automatic: it is the orientation
constraint `sign X · sign Y = sign(XY)`, which forces `84 ∣ D`.

Unbounded degree is the substantive step, because every finite quotient of `Δ(2,3,7)` is perfect,
so no abelian or elementary family exists.  The route used is the mod-`p` homology of the covering
surface: a `ZMod p`-voltage on a cover lifts it to a `p`-fold cover of the *same* orbifold exactly
when the cyclic sums of the voltage around the orbits of `X`, `Y` and `XY` vanish, and the lift is
connected exactly when the voltage is not a coboundary.  Each cyclic-sum constraint takes values in
the invariants of its monodromy, so the three constraints have rank at most `f₂ + f₃ + f₇`; the
coboundary map kills the constants, so its image has dimension at most `D − 1`.  The two counts
separate precisely when `f₂ + f₃ + f₇ < D`, i.e. when the covering surface is hyperbolic — which is
exactly the case for `(2,3,7)`.  Iterating the doubling gives connected unramified covers of degree
`168·2ⁿ`, hence subgroups of `Δ(2,3,7)` of unbounded index, hence also that `Δ(2,3,7)` is infinite.
