# Nonautonomous ADEs split coefficient and Kolchin-state frequency budgets

- **ID:** `nonautonomous-kolchin-frequency-budget-law`
- **Stage:** 2-fully-proven / needs-audit
- **Type:** THEOREM (paper) / GENERAL HYPERTRANSCENDENCE LANDSCAPE
- **Scientific status:** `[PROVED] [NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]`
- **Statement:** For a jointly Bohr-almost-periodic coefficient/solution pair, total rational
  frequency rank is the sum of coefficient-hull rank and relative solution-state rank. If the fixed
  coefficient fiber obeys a nondegenerate algebraic differential constraint, its relative Kolchin
  jet capacity bounds the state rank. Prime frequencies must therefore be paid exactly once, either
  by the coefficient module or by relative jet geometry.

## 1. Joint hull and coefficient projection

Let

```text
a=(a_1,...,a_q),
f                                                        (1)
```

be holomorphic functions on a horizontal strip such that the vector-valued function `(a,f)` is
uniformly Bohr almost periodic. Write

```text
X=X_(a,f),
B=X_a                                                    (2)
```

for the joint and coefficient translation hulls. They are compact connected metrizable abelian
groups. Projection of a joint translate to its coefficient translate extends to a continuous
surjective group homomorphism

```text
pi:X->B.                                                  (3)
```

Let

```text
K=ker pi.                                                 (4)
```

After choosing the original coefficient point as the identity of `B`, elements of `K` are exactly
the compatible solution states lying over that fixed coefficient function. Every fiber of `pi` is
a coset of `K`.

## 2. Exact frequency conservation

Pontryagin duality turns `(3)` into an exact sequence of discrete abelian groups

```text
0 -> dual(B) -> dual(X) -> dual(K) -> 0.                  (5)
```

Tensoring with `Q` is exact, so

```text
rank_Q dual(X)=rank_Q dual(B)+rank_Q dual(K).             (6)
```

The duals of `X` and `B` are the additive groups generated by the Bohr spectra of `(a,f)` and `a`.
Thus `(6)` is the **coefficient--state frequency conservation law**:

```text
joint frequency rank
 = coefficient-carried rank + relative state rank.       (7)
```

This is equality, not merely an upper bound. A nonautonomous coefficient is therefore a positive
carrier entry in the budget, not an exception to be hidden inside the equation.

## 3. Relative Kolchin jet capacity

Fix the identity coefficient point `b_0 in B`. For `m>=0`, let

```text
J_m^rel:K->C^(m+1),
J_m^rel(b_0,g)=(g(0),g'(0),...,g^(m)(0)),
Z_m^rel=ZariskiClosure_C(J_m^rel(K)).                     (8)
```

Define

```text
kappa_rel=sup_m dim_C Z_m^rel.                            (9)
```

Assume the fixed fiber is constrained by a nonzero algebraic differential equation over the
differential field `M_(b_0)` of meromorphic coefficient germs generated by `b_0`:

```text
0!=P_(b_0) in M_(b_0){Y},
P_(b_0)(g)=0 for every (b_0,g) in K.                      (10)
```

`[PROVED FROM RITT--KOLCHIN INITIAL-DATA THEORY]`

```text
kappa_rel<infinity.                                       (11)
```

### Proof

Choose a common ordinary basepoint away from the poles of the finitely many coefficients appearing
in `(10)`; translating the evaluation point does not change the hull or its dimension. The radical
differential ideal of relations common to the fiber contains `P_(b_0)` and is proper. Its finite
essential-prime decomposition has differential type zero in the one solution indeterminate.
Kolchin's dimension polynomial is therefore eventually constant on every prime component.

At the ordinary basepoint, the characteristic-set initial-data theorem identifies each truncated
analytic solution-jet set with a constructible subset of the corresponding prolonged algebraic
variety. Singular components are included as separate essential primes rather than removed by a
separant inversion. Their truncated dimensions are uniformly bounded, and finitely many components
and initial truncation levels give `(11)`.

If every specialization of the proposed equation vanished identically on the coefficient fiber,
then `(10)` would fail. That is not a counterexample: the proposed equation carries no relative
constraint and must be recorded as an infinite-capacity/degenerate state channel.

## 4. Kernel-dimension theorem

The full relative jet map

```text
J_infinity^rel:K->product_(m>=0) C                        (12)
```

is injective by the holomorphic identity theorem. Its finite images form a surjective inverse
system, and compact nested fibers show

```text
K homeomorphic inverse_limit_m J_m^rel(K).                (13)
```

Each finite image is compact inside `Z_m^rel`, so

```text
dim_cover J_m^rel(K)<=2 kappa_rel.                        (14)
```

Covering dimension does not increase under inverse limits of compact metric spaces with surjective
bonding maps. Hence

```text
dim_cover K<=2 kappa_rel.                                 (15)
```

For compact abelian groups, covering dimension is the rational rank of the Pontryagin dual. Thus

```text
rank_Q dual(K)<=2 kappa_rel.                              (16)
```

Combining `(6)` and `(16)` gives

```text
rank_Q Gamma(a,f)
 <=rank_Q Gamma(a)+2 kappa_rel.                           (17)
```

No vector field, implicit-function theorem, or unique initial-value evolution occurs in the proof.

## 5. Arithmetic-complement law

At prime cutoff `N`, let

```text
V_N=span_Q{log p:p<=N},
dim_Q V_N=pi(N).                                          (18)
```

Assume `V_N` is retained in the frequency group of the joint carrier or of a continuous
translation-equivariant compact observable. Let

```text
D_a=Gamma(a),
A_N=V_N/(V_N intersection D_a).                           (19)
```

The image of `V_N` in the quotient `dual(X)/dual(B)`, which injects into `dual(K)` by `(5)`, has
dimension exactly `dim_Q A_N`. Therefore

```text
dim_Q A_N<=rank_Q dual(K)<=2 kappa_rel,                   (20)
```

and hence

```text
kappa_rel>=ceil(dim_Q A_N/2).                             (21)
```

> **Nonautonomous Kolchin-budget law.** Every prime direction absent from the coefficient module is
> forced into the relative solution-state carrier. A successful cofinal realization must either
> grow the coefficient frequency module or grow relative Kolchin jet capacity; the same prime
> direction cannot disappear between them.

Equation `(20)` is the exact positive form of the arithmetic-complement obstruction.

## 6. Gamma-coefficient consequence

For a finite archimedean Gamma coefficient field whose recurrent boundary carrier has finite
rational phase rank `r_infinity`, one has

```text
dim_Q(V_N intersection D_a)<=r_infinity.                  (22)
```

Thus every nonautonomous ADE over that fixed completion satisfies

```text
kappa_rel>=ceil((pi(N)-r_infinity)/2).                    (23)
```

Arbitrary polynomial jet depth in the Gamma coefficients does not alter `(23)`: noncompact local
jets contribute no additional compact character rank. The relative solution variety, or another
explicit infinite-dimensional carrier, must pay the remaining prime budget.

## 7. Positive requirements for RH architectures

1. **Hilbert--Polya and Connes.** If arithmetic is placed in nonautonomous boundary/domain
   coefficients, their hull rank is counted by `D_a`; every remaining prime direction must appear
   in the operator state kernel with capacity `(21)`.
2. **de Branges.** Frequencies carried by the Hamiltonian/phase coefficients subtract from `V_N`
   exactly once. The nested-space or relative canonical-system state must carry `A_N`.
3. **Nyman--Beurling.** Dilation coefficients may carry part of the prime module, but the closure
   defect/relative approximation state must have capacity at least `(21)`.
4. **Weil positivity.** Frequencies explicitly present in the test-function coefficients belong to
   `D_a`; the relative quadratic-form state must separate the quotient `A_N`.
5. **Li criteria.** Nonautonomous recurrence coefficients may pay part of the budget. The residual
   coefficient-generator state or divisor hierarchy must carry the exact complement `(19)`.

These are carrier allocations, not categorical rejections of the complete infinite criteria.

## 8. Attacks and boundaries

1. **Coefficient degeneracy is load-bearing.** If the specialized relation in `(10)` is identically
   zero, no relative ADE has been supplied. The capacity is not silently declared finite.
2. **Meromorphic poles are harmless only after choosing an ordinary germ.** The basepoint must avoid
   the finite coefficient pole/initial set used by the characteristic decomposition.
3. **Joint almost periodicity is load-bearing.** Separate compact hulls without a compact joint hull
   do not produce the exact group extension `(5)`.
4. **The coefficient projection must be remembered.** Passing only to the function hull can merge
   coefficient and state characters and destroys the equality `(6)`.
5. **The relative equation may be implicit, singular, and reducible.** All essential differential
   prime components contribute to `kappa_rel`; no uniqueness hypothesis is restored.
6. **Infinite coefficient rank is a valid carrier.** Then `(20)` may leave a small complement. The
   theorem requires the route to exhibit that coefficient module rather than calling it finite
   state.
7. **Noncompact or non-equivariant observables are outside the factor statement.** They require a
   separate continuity/cofinal theorem.

## 9. Parent and novelty audit

The proof combines:

1. exactness of Pontryagin duality for compact abelian group extensions;
2. rational-rank additivity after tensoring with `Q`;
3. Ritt--Kolchin finite initial-data dimension for a differential-type-zero fiber;
4. covering dimension of inverse limits.

O'Brien's nonautonomous frequency theorem is the closest regular-flow parent: it bounds the
additional solution frequencies relative to coefficient frequencies under unique evolution. The
present theorem replaces that evolution by the relative differential-jet fiber and includes
singular implicit equations. Preliminary searches did not locate the exact equality `(6)` combined
with the singular relative bound `(16)` or the arithmetic-complement law `(20)`.

`[NOVELTY UNVERIFIED]` The proof and significance gates pass, but exact priority across
nonautonomous almost-periodic systems, differential algebraic families, and compact-group extension
theory remains to be checked independently.
