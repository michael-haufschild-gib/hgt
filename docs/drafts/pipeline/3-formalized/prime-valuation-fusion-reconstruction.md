## Master theorem

Let `sigma` be finite, let

```text
x : Primes -> C^sigma,
```

and let `K subset C^sigma` contain the range of `x`. Assume every cofinite tail of `x` is dense in
`K`. For a homogeneous polynomial `Q` of degree `m > 0`, define the valuation realization

```text
V_x,m(Q)(p^m) = Q(x(p)),
```

with zero values away from the `m`-th prime-power layer. For pure valuation layers define the
same-prime fusion product

```text
(A_m star A_n)(p^(m+n)) = A_m(p^m) A_n(p^n).
```

Let `I_m(K)` be the space of degree-`m` homogeneous polynomials vanishing on `K`, and let

```text
Hull_h(K) = {v : Q(v)=0 for every m and every Q in I_m(K)}.
```

Then:

1. **Differential-fusion algebra.** Fusion is complex-bilinear, commutative, and associative with
   valuation degrees adding. The rotated logarithmic derivation is a derivation for fusion.
   For complex shifts `a,b` and every jet order `k`,

   ```text
   J^k_(m+n,a+b)(Q R)
     = sum_(i=0)^k binom(k,i)
         (J^i_(m,a)(Q) star J^(k-i)_(n,b)(R)).
   ```

2. **Faithful coordinate realization.** For every degree,

   ```text
   ker(V_x,m) = I_m(K),
   ```

   so `V_x,m` induces a canonical linear equivalence from the degree-`m` homogeneous coordinate
   quotient onto the actual `m`-th prime-valuation image. Multiplication is represented literally:

   ```text
   V_x,m+n(Q R) = V_x,m(Q) star V_x,n(R).
   ```

3. **Object reconstruction.** Two tail-complete sources `x` on `K` and `y` on `L` have identical
   labelled homogeneous prime-tail relation systems in every degree if and only if

   ```text
   Hull_h(K) = Hull_h(L).
   ```

   Thus the full arithmetic relation object is a complete invariant of the homogeneous algebraic
   source geometry, not merely of its ranks.

4. **Morphism reconstruction.** Let `Phi` be a homogeneous polynomial map of degree `q`. Then

   ```text
   Phi(K) subset Hull_h(L)
   ```

   if and only if pullback by `Phi` sends every degree-`m` relation of the target arithmetic tail
   to a degree-`q m` relation of the source arithmetic tail. On realized jets the same pullback is
   the `q`-fold prime-valuation dilation and multiplies the order-`k` clock by exactly `q^k`.

5. **Classified shadows.** The valuation Hilbert function is the rank shadow of the faithful
   realization. The prolonged homogeneous reductions generate the complete finite-window
   differential relation ideal. At degree one, finite continuous observable subspaces embed as
   differential fields: membership, field inclusion, and field equality are exactly source-span
   membership, inclusion, and equality; independent three-block decompositions satisfy the exact
   Grassmann meet law.

The positive law is:

> A tail-complete hypertranscendental source is not generated by a finite differential flow, but
> its complete homogeneous constraint geometry and its homogeneous maps are recoverable from the
> prime-valuation differential-fusion object.

## What is scientifically new

The theorem's individual algebraic mechanisms are not new:

- coefficientwise Hadamard multiplication and exponent-adding Hadamard--Kong products of
  Dirichlet series are classical constructions;
- homogeneous coordinate rings, vanishing hulls, contravariant pullback, and fully faithful
  homogeneous-coordinate functors belong to established algebraic geometry;
- Witt ghost maps and ring structures on arithmetic functions are established;
- one-source shift and logarithmic-derivative algebraic independence is established in the
  Shapiro--Sparer--Pong line.

The candidate new content is their exact arithmetic coupling under one intrinsic hypothesis:

```text
cofinite tail density
  => exact homogeneous kernel
  => faithful prime-valuation fusion realization
  => complete source-hull reconstruction
  => exact homogeneous-map detection from arithmetic relations.
```

The literature audit located no theorem reconstructing a homogeneous source hull and its
homogeneous-map criterion from the complete prime-power tail relation system while simultaneously
carrying the logarithmic derivation and all shifted jets. The novelty claim is therefore
provisional but high-confidence; it is not attached to fusion, coordinate duality, Hilbert ranks,
or Leibniz identities separately.

## Why this is one theorem rather than a multilayer bundle

The load-bearing object is the faithful graded differential-fusion realization `V_x`. Its kernel
identifies the source coordinate quotient, its multiplication supplies the graded algebra, its
complete family of kernels reconstructs the source hull, and pullback of those kernels detects
morphisms. Every rank, field, and relation-ideal statement is obtained by forgetting structure:

```text
reconstruction object
  -> forget multiplication: Hilbert ranks
  -> retain degree one: observable-field Grassmann geometry
  -> retain kernels: complete relation system and source hull
  -> retain pullback: homogeneous-map functoriality
  -> retain the derivation: valuation-clock covariance.
```

No independent “layers” are needed in the main narrative.

## RH pressure test

For the scalar Euler parameter of zeta, every positive homogeneous source degree is rank one and
the entire finite prime-valuation fusion object is source-determined. The theorem therefore forces
a positive architectural conclusion:

> Any RH proof using this landscape must add a global structure not already contained in the
> tail-complete differential-fusion reconstruction object.

Candidates include functional-equation reflection, archimedean coupling, positivity tied to that
reflection, or an infinite analytic completion. Finite local prime-power algebra cannot supply an
independent selector because it has already been reconstructed completely from the scalar source
geometry. This is a constraint on proof architecture, not a proof or equivalent reformulation of
RH.
