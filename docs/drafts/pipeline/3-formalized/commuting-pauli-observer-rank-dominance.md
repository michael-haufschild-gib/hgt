# Commuting Pauli sectors cannot beat observer-rank recurrence

- **ID:** `commuting-pauli-observer-rank-dominance`
- **Stage:** 3-formalized
- **Type:** THEOREM / hostile-boundary theorem for autonomous recurrence
- **Status:** `[FORMALIZED] [BRIDGE]`
- **Formal module:** `formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/CommutingPauliObserverRankDominance.lean`
- **Parent:** `../1-in-progress/observer-rank-autonomous-no-shortcut-law.md`
- **Statement:** Every pairwise commuting sector of nonidentity Pauli words of
  weight at most `q` has recurrence codimension at least the product-input
  observer rank.

## Exact theorem

Let `A` be any set of distinct, pairwise commuting, nonidentity `n`-qubit
Pauli words, every one of weight at most `q`.  Put

\[
 r=|A|,\qquad
 m_{n,q}=\sum_{k=1}^q3^k{n\choose k},\qquad
 v_{n,q}=2\sum_{k=1}^q{n\choose k}.
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 r\leq\sum_{k=1}^q(2^k-1){n\choose k}
 }
 \tag{1}
\]

and consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 m_{n,q}-r
 \geq\sum_{k=1}^q(3^k-2^k+1){n\choose k}
 \geq v_{n,q}.
 }
 \tag{2}
\]

For `q>=2`, the first comparison with `v_(n,q)` is strictly stronger at
leading order:

\[
 m_{n,q}-r-v_{n,q}
 \geq
 \sum_{k=2}^q(3^k-2^k-1){n\choose k}.
 \tag{3}
\]

## Proof

Partition `A` by exact support.  Fix a support `S` of cardinality `k`.  Since
every word in this fibre is the identity outside `S`, restriction to `S`
preserves distinctness and pairwise commutativity.  Modulo scalar phase, the
`k`-qubit Pauli group is the binary symplectic vector space

\[
 \mathbb F_2^{2k}.
\]

The symplectic pairing vanishes on the span of a pairwise commuting family.
Thus that span is isotropic.  Nondegeneracy of the symplectic form gives

\[
 \dim W+\dim W^\perp=2k,
 \qquad W\subseteq W^\perp,
\]

so `dim W<=k`.  The span therefore contains at most `2^k` vectors.  Removing
the identity leaves at most `2^k-1` nonidentity Pauli words on the fixed
support.  There are `binom(n,k)` supports of size `k`; summing proves `(1)`.

Subtracting `(1)` from the exact count `m_(n,q)` proves the first inequality
in `(2)`.  For every `k>=1`,

\[
 3^k-2^k+1\geq2,
\]

with equality only at `k=1`.  This proves the second inequality and `(3)`.
\(\square\)

## Consequence for the active conjecture

The recurrent refinement in
`fast-forwardable-stratum-codimension-obstruction.md` gives a translated
period lattice generated by `r` commuting Pauli involutions shortcut mass of
order `t^(-(m-r))`.  Equations `(1)`--`(3)` show that **every** such Pauli
recurrence lattice has exponent at least `v_(n,q)`.  The earlier audit checked
only the diagonal `Z` sector.  Commuting refocusing in an arbitrary Pauli
frame therefore cannot falsify the leading observer-rank prediction.

This does not cover a mixed carrier whose continuous part is a genuinely
noncommuting subspace of the observer-stationary kernel and whose transverse
part is periodic.  That mixed stationary--periodic problem is now the first
unexcluded recurrence-component attack.

## Hostile audit

1. The fixed-support bound is classical stabilizer/symplectic linear algebra;
   this card is a `[BRIDGE]`, not new scientific knowledge.
2. The result covers sectors with a basis of pairwise commuting Pauli words.
   It does not classify all commuting linear subspaces whose commuting basis
   consists of non-Pauli linear combinations.
3. It does not prove the selector-aware upper shortcut law.  It removes one
   complete counterexample class.
4. The Gaussian probability statement remains on the parent obstruction
   card.  This card proves only the exact finite combinatorial exponent
   comparison.
