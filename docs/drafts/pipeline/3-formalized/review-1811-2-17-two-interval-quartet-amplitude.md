## Mathematical Proof

1. **Universal collapse of adjacent-cofactor endpoint substitution and common-middle-endpoint expansion.**
   For $u \in \mathbb{N}$ with $2u+1$ prime and $4 \mid u+1$, `firstOctaveCofactor (u+1) d - firstOctaveCofactor u d = -1_{d = 2u+1}` holds pointwise for all $d \in \mathbb{N}$. Hence for every $g : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $g(0) = 0$ and every $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the difference of first-octave convolutions at $(2u+1)N$ equals $-g(N)$ identically. At $s_0 = t, s_1 = t+h, s_2 = t+2h$, setting $D_u(s) = -\text{adjacentCountAt } u\ (e^s) = \psi(e^s) - e^s$, the common-middle-endpoint quadratic form $(1 + A^{-2})D_u(t+h)^2 - 2D_u(t)D_u(t+h) - 2A^{-2}D_u(t+h)D_u(t+2h) + D_u(t)^2 + A^{-2}D_u(t+2h)^2$ equals $(\Delta_h E_\psi(t))^2 + A^{-2}(\Delta_h E_\psi(t+h))^2$ identically, independent of $u$, and its square root differs from $\sqrt{(\Delta_h E(t))^2 + A^{-2}(\Delta_h E(t+h))^2}$ by at most $12(\log 4 + 4)e^{(t+2h)/2}$.

2. **$\{0,1\}$-coprime integer-set realization with $O_{\rho,m,Q}(1+t)$ radial error.**
