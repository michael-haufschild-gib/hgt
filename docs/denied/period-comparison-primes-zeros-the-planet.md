## 1. The period matrix is computed  [PROVED]

explicit formula**. Its matrix entries are the **secular moments**, and they are *explicitly known*:

> **The period-comparison identity (= §8a, PROVED, pod-confirmed).** For `k ≥ 2`,
> ```
>    P_k = Σ_ρ ρ^{-k}   =   1  −  (−1)^k ζ(k)/2^k   −   a_{k-1},      a_{k-1} = [s^{k-1}](ζ'/ζ)(0).
>          └─ ZERO side ─┘     └ ARCHIMEDEAN ┘        └ PRIME side ┘
> ```
> The left side is built from the **zeros** (the spectral corner); the right side is built from the
> **archimedean place** (`ζ(k)`, from the `Γ`-factor) and the **primes** (`a_j = [s^j](−Σ Λ(n)n^{-s})`
> continued to `0`). The identity *is* the comparison `zeros = archimedean ⊕ primes` — a literal
> change of basis between the two corners, with the `P_k` as the period entries.

So the field already possesses, in closed form, the object the motivic world calls the period matrix:
the secular moments, split by place. Unlike the motivic periods (which require a motive), these exist
for a **hypertranscendental** object with no motive — which is the whole point (Hölder; the empty
fourth corner, charter Part VIII).
