## 1. Operations live cleanly on the prime side, opaquely on the zero side `[REFRAME]`

A functorial operation acts on the **Satake parameters** `{α_{p,i}}` algebraically:
```
   π₁ ⊗ π₂ :  {α_{p,i} β_{p,j}}        (products)         — Rankin–Selberg
   Symᵏ π  :  {α_p^{a_1}···}          (symmetric powers)
   ∧ᵏ π    :  {products of distinct α} (exterior powers)
```
On the **prime/Euler side** this is a clean algebra (a **λ-ring**: `Symᵏ`, `∧ᵏ` are the λ-operations, ⊗ the product). On the **zero side** it is *opaque*: the zeros of `L(s,π₁⊗π₂)` are **not** a simple function of the zeros of `π₁, π₂` (already for degree 1, `L(χ₁)⊗L(χ₂)=L(χ₁χ₂)` has unrelated zeros). The prime↔zero duality again: operations are multiplicatively clean on primes, transcendentally opaque on zeros — mediated only by the explicit formula.

## 2. But read through the explicit formula, ⊗ IS pair correlation `[REFRAME → Montgomery]`

The secular/log-derivative data of `L(s,π⊗π̃)` is, on the prime side, the **second moment** of `π`'s Satake parameters:
```
   −L'/L(s, π⊗π̃)  ~  Σ_p |a_π(p)|² (log p) p^{-s} + ···
```
and the analytic behavior of `L(s,π⊗π̃)` near `s=1` (its pole = `π` self-dual) governs the **pair correlation of `π`'s zeros** (Montgomery's method: the two-point function is extracted from `Σ x^{iγ}` weighted by the Rankin–Selberg data). So:
```
   π ⊗ π̃   (operation)        ⟷    pair correlation R₂ of {γ_π}     (Montgomery)
   Symᵏ / k-fold products      ⟷    k-level correlation R_k          (Rudnick–Sarnak)
```
> **The operations calculus IS the correlation hierarchy.** The functorial λ-ring operations on `L`-functions, transported to the zero side by the explicit formula, are exactly the **n-point correlation functions** of the spectrum. ⊗ gives `R₂` (GUE pair correlation, Montgomery); the symmetric/exterior powers give the higher `R_k` (Rudnick–Sarnak n-level).
