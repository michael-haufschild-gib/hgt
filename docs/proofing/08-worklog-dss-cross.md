# DSS-Cross Proof Attempt: Gamma_n Algebraic Independence

Source: `docs/consolidated/08-conjectures-required.md` §4.

Claim status: `[CONJECTURE]`.

## Target Claim

Modulo a Grothendieck period conjecture, DSS is equivalent to algebraic
independence of `{gamma_n}` over the period field.

## Attempt

The intended shape is Tannakian:

```text
trdeg(period coordinates) = dimension of Galois orbit
```

as in Papanikolas-type function-field results. If the Stieltjes/zero constants
`gamma_n` are realized as coordinates of an exponential-period torsor, and if
the Grothendieck period conjecture is true for that category, then algebraic
independence is equivalent to the expected orbit dimension.

## Weakened Theorem

If a period realization functor is fully faithful on the relevant exponential
motivic category and maps the DSS Galois orbit to the field generated by
`{gamma_n}`, then DSS is equivalent to algebraic independence of those values
over the period field.

## Obstruction

The period realization and injectivity are exactly the open part. The
conjecture combines a Grothendieck-period assumption with a Stieltjes/zero
comparison theorem not yet built.

## Missing

Define the exponential-period object, prove realization injectivity, and
identify the DSS orbit coordinates with `{gamma_n}`.
