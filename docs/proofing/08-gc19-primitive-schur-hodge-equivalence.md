finite shadow of an F1/Hodge polarization.

## Attempt

signature. If these finite signatures are truncations of a Hodge intersection
form, then their limiting primitive signature could be the faithful index.

Hodge/Weil faithful form, then their stable negative index equals `kappa_-`.
