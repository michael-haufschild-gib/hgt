# G-C18 Proof Attempt: Faithful-Index Rigidity

Source: `docs/consolidated/08-conjectures-required.md` appendix.

Claim status: `[CONJECTURE]`.

## Target Claim

The correct completed carrier has a faithful integer index, and the source
construction forces it to be zero.

## Attempt

Integer rigidity suggests a Fredholm or finite-negative-square index. If the
carrier is a continuous family in such a class, the index is locally constant.
Thus source data could force zero by connecting the object to a trivial
positive model without leaving the faithful class.

## Weakened Theorem

If the completed carrier is connected through faithful Fredholm/negative-square
objects to a zero-index source model, then its index is zero.

## Obstruction

No-margin threatens exactly the required stability: the path may leave the
Fredholm class or pass through essential spectrum at zero. Measured traces do
not solve this because they are not dust-proof.

## Missing

Faithful topology, source homotopy, proof of no index jump, and exclusion of
dust perturbations invisible to source data.
