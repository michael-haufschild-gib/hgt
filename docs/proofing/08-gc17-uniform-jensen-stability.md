# G-C17 Proof Attempt: Uniform Jensen Stability

Source: `docs/consolidated/08-conjectures-required.md` appendix.

Claim status: `[CONJECTURE]`.

## Target Claim

`xi` satisfies a uniform Jensen-polynomial stability theorem strong enough to
imply Laguerre-Polya membership.

## Attempt

Fixed-degree Jensen hyperbolicity is not enough. The needed statement is
uniform across degree, height, and the transition between asymptotic and small
parameters. If all Jensen polynomials in the required family are hyperbolic,
then `xi` lies in Laguerre-Polya, hence RH.

## Weakened Theorem

All-level Jensen hyperbolicity implies RH through the Laguerre-Polya criterion.

## Obstruction

Modern fixed-degree/asymptotic Jensen results do not control the growing-degree
and low-height arithmetic-correction regimes where the corpus's no-margin
numerics show sparse resonances.

## Missing

A uniform theorem covering small `n`, growing degree, arithmetic corrections,
and fake-family separation.
