# Routine work, open problems, and the shape of the missing mathematics

Companions: [per-route assessment of 29 routes, 19 candidates and PM's PW-B branch](hard-obstacles.md); [walls ledger](walls-ledger.md) (seventeen walls). Evidence tags as there: [L] committed umbrella-imported Lean at checkpoint `9e90e2b19`, [S] working-tree Lean, [P] paper only.

## 1. Assessment

Every route in the route ledger ends in one unproved statement that implies RH or in one unconstructed object whose construction would be such a statement. None of these remaining steps is routine, and no routine task below moves any of them. What is routine is small: committing three umbrella imports and wiring four working-tree modules, two receiver converses, a handful of named constants, and the extension of certified gauge windows by finite calculus up to explicit limits that the certificates themselves exhibit.

The open steps fall into six families (§2.2). Two of them, square-root cancellation of a signed prime sum and positivity of the complete Weil form, are one problem in two coordinate systems and carry the others. The corpus states both at their sharpest: on the prime side, RH is one real inequality per dyadic window `(X/2, 2X)` for every large `X`, with no height parameter, and the best unconditional floor is of de la Vallée Poussin strength, `P_v ≥ −|φ(0)| − C_w exp(v/2 − √v/10)` [L]; on the operator side, RH is the statement that the restricted Weil ground energy `μ(A)`, which is `≥ 1/5000` through aperture `3/10` [S], `≥ 1/1000` through `29/100` [L], and tends to `0` doubly exponentially, never crosses zero in between [L]. The corpus also proves, in the receiver's own coordinates, the two classical barriers of analytic number theory as sharp theorems: below half damping no sign-blind data decide the Möbius receiver (Wall 16), and no `ℓ²` Type-II input reaches the receiver's exponent at one modulus (Wall 17). It proves that the identity layer, the positivity-of-pieces layer, the finite-data layer, the averaging layer and the pointwise-gauge layer are each insufficient, and it names the five mechanisms one of which any proof must supply. No existing field supplies one. §2.3 describes the field that would, and records that its first objects, units and theorems exist in the corpus.

## 2.2 Open mathematical obstacles

Six families. They are not proved independent; a theorem closing one would close others.

### O1. Square-root cancellation of a signed prime sum (Walls 1, 16, 17)

**Routes.** WL, MC, PM, CTF, PBM, RB, FU, RP, LI, SRT (minimal receiver), CHMD (discrepancy pairing); BVI, MSC, EGC, DR as candidates.

**Exact receivers** [L]. `ψ(x) − x = O(√x log² x)`; `M(x) = O_ε(x^{1/2+ε})`; the one-sided deficit primitive `±∫₀ᵀ(eᵗ − ψ(eᵗ))dt ≤ Be^{T/2}`; the linear band `∫₀^L Ẽ² = O(L)`; `d_N → 0`; the Liouville receiver bounded at arbitrarily small damping; the regularized prime series convergent at every `σ > 1/2`; Robin on colossally abundant integers; the window ceiling remainder `c(L) ≤ e^{L/2} + O(L)` or its floor mirror; and the sharpest prime-side form: for one generic width `0 < w < log 2/2`, the window functional `P_v` of the unmodulated difference test is nonnegative at every lag `v ≥ V`, one real inequality per dyadic window `(X/2, 2X)` whose pole and archimedean terms pay the main term exactly.

**What is known to be insufficient.** Divisor-sum (Type I) information gives the constant `2`, never `1`. Exponential-sum methods save `exp(−c(log x)^{3/5})`, never a power; the lag envelope stands at `exp(v/2 − √v/10)` and the pole-free window at `O(e^{v/2})`. Zero-density estimates count in strips. Unsigned majorants stop at absolute convergence, in receiver coordinates at `ε = 1/2`. The positive absolute envelope of the signed covariance grows at the wrong exponent. Lag grids lose exactly the non-attained tops; on the continuum family the attainment premise is replaced by a horizon condition, and the zeros beyond every finite horizon remain uncontrolled. Below half damping no function of Ramanujan amplitudes, no two-point translation data and, for squarefree rows, no translated moment of any degree decides the receiver: the parity pairs exist at every `ε ≤ 1/2`, and only first-order data at prime moduli or odd-degree additive moments separate them. No `ℓ²` Type-II class reaches `θ > 1` at one modulus; Type-I classes stop at `θ ≤ 3/2`; the receiver sits at `θ = 2`. Window extremes are twist-invariant, so coarser than `O(log X)` at scale `√X` they cannot distinguish the prime measure from its Liouville twist.

**What a mechanism must satisfy.** It must fail for Beurling systems; survive one character, modulus one, shift zero; carry information beyond polynomial length equal to height at square-root precision; distinguish `log 4` from `log 6`; read the signs of the coefficients at prime moduli; and bound the single-modulus low-frequency statistics `Σ_p|Λ_p(c')|/p` of the actual Möbius or prime row, the residue of every Type-II reduction. Additive tools meet the first and third only after averaging over a family; multiplicative tools are Beurling-invariant; every tool so far is parity-blind or Type-I.

### O2. Positivity of the complete Weil form (Walls 8, 11, 12, 13)

**Routes.** RW, EWU, OWU, CHMD, HF, ACT, SRT (height-localized receiver), TT, PA; JAS at level one; AIP as candidate.

**Exact equivalent forms** [L]. The odd capacity inequality `sinhPairing(g)² ≤ 2·markovEnergy(g)`; the single-shift inequality `D_a(u) ≤ 2S_a(u) + V_a(u)` at any one `a > 0`; the nonnegative-cone inequality with no signed term; the pure jump form `J_M(u) + V_μ(u) ≥ 0` in any smeared gauge with conductance `M(x)M(y)(dm(|x − y|) + 2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2))` per unordered pair; prime-blind positivity on the full integer-log grid; the even blind minimum nonnegative at every aperture; absence of sign-changing odd CHMD eigenstates above `1/2`; `μ(A) ≥ 0` for every aperture, equivalently `μ(A) → 0`, equivalently `μ₀ ≥ 0` at every aperture with no attainment premise.

**What is known.** The cone is the point `Λ`; node criticality has exact Euler-product prices (single `Λ(m)/√m`, additive across primes and on the full integer grid, interacting within a tower, whole towers at the local Euler factors, `log 6` free as a node and paid as a decoupling length); the form is critical in every term; near-null families exist under RH. The ground-energy path is pinned: floors through `3/10` [S] and `29/100` [L], the Suzuki main-term floor below `log 2/2`, the polar floor `μ₀(A) ≥ −(10 + 68e^A)` at every aperture, the doubly exponential upper decay, and `RH ⟺ μ(A) → 0`. The odd sector is positive past the first prime: `Ẑ(F) ≥ (1/1000)∫F²` on `[−353/1000, 353/1000]` with the atom `Λ(2)/√2` paid from the archimedean strip surplus and the sharp micro-fold paying every prime-power shift separately within one twelfth past `log n/2` [L]. The even sector is positive through `3/10` [S] and no further; past `log 2/2` the folded conductances across the first prime are negative on even tests. Positivity on restricted families (critical factorial sources on high blocks, polynomial windows, lacunary unions, Müntz-repaired tails) is nonselective and paid by an archimedean gain `log M`; the limiting critical prime channel is negative definite. Every pointwise supersolution with a nonnegative profile fails at both ends; one added jump repairs infinity iff `e^{−2a} + e^{−2ℓ} < 1` and never the origin; a supersolution, if one exists, is sign-changing with `∫M dm ≤ 0` and `∫₀^∞M cosh ≤ 0`; the hub–Schur step replaces a pointwise supersolution by a Schur-averaged potential and is the mechanism of every window past the plastic threshold. Every certificate of positivity is the critical-zero measure (Wall 12); the published CCM finite-aperture realization is formalized as an unconditional dichotomy whose passage to `Ξ` is the unproved premise. In every coordinate system the unpaid term is the signed prime−polar pairing `ΣΛ(n)n^{−1/2}h(log n) − ∫2cosh(x/2)h`, a Chebyshev-error gauge: O2 contains O1.

### O3. Uniformity through a critical parameter (Walls 3, 4)

**Routes.** TH, HCF (all degrees at one heat time), PA (all Hausdorff orders at one shift), LA, SZ, TT (the critical shift, time or axis), GTA (rank-uniform decay), XN (all discs), WD (all zero blocks).

**Shape.** Each route has a fixed-parameter theorem, eventual in another parameter, and needs the common-parameter theorem. Fixed rank `N` is blind above height `O(N√log N)`; the de Bruijn–Newman constant is pinned to `[0, 1/8]` with RH the equality `Λ = 0`; shifts, differences and heat flow are real-rooted exactly beyond the strip edge. The missing step is control at the critical parameter itself, which in each case is O1 or O2 again.

### O4. Complete signed history and transport (Walls 1, 10, 14)

**Routes.** FU, RP, LI, SZ (Wronskian), TG, EGC.

**Shape.** The quantity is a sum over a history (blocks, fibres, cutoffs, contour copies); the positive pieces are proved and the signed cross terms between pieces are not. Absolute payment fails (fibre variation diverges, trace masses stay positive, envelopes fail); strong convergence does not pay norm or trace costs; the receivers are one-sided bounds whose exponent is the zero abscissa.

### O5. Every height, not almost every (Walls 3, 7)

**Routes.** BAG (recurrence on a prescribed disc), JAS (exceptional vectors), LA (near-double pairs), WL/MC (exceptional lags); SRT in its height-localized receiver and in the zeros beyond every finite horizon.

**Shape.** Every unconditional statement about the zeros near the line is averaged. Around an off-line zero the recurrence set has density zero; recurrence is closed on every zero-free disc, so for BAG the open statement is literally "exclude a zero from the disc" with no intermediate estimate [L]. Finite prime polynomials attain every phase pattern at suitable heights, so pointwise positivity must come from the coupling, through the functional equation, between finitely many primes and the infinite tail. On the lag family a hypothetical off-line zero of depth `δ₀` and transform amplitude `η` is visible at an explicit lag `V₀ ≍ (log Γ₀)² log log Γ₀/δ₀` without attainment, and hiding from any finite lag range needs an approximate multiple zero; the lag tends to infinity as the depth tends to zero, and the residual barrier is the pair-correlation barrier (twins closer than `log log γ/V`).

### O6. Arithmetic realization (Walls 5, 12, 14, 15)

**Routes.** HF (cohomology), RN and TG (native completion, transgression); candidates APF, BSH, CDC, DR, EWJ, FEL, HJ, IRK, MSC, PCS, PTR, RFHM, SC, SEC, W1C, WBF, PW-B.

**Shape.** The object sought is a space, operator, form or geometry that produces the prime powers, carries the Gamma and polar data, has a trace recovering the explicit formula, and places the zero support on the line. Constraints proved: self-adjointness is cheap; unitary producers have unit-modulus Euler factors; the prime part is not trace class, finite rank or finite deficiency and is as large as `S(T)`, which is unbounded; compact models fail Weyl density; positive currents and positive-definite transforms are orientation-blind; zero-indexed constructions are circular until their decisive premise is proved from the source; CCM's finite-aperture logarithmic Dirac operator is real-rooted in the η-branch and converges to `Ξ` only under an unproved premise. This is the only family where a construction rather than an estimate is sought.

### How the families relate

O2 and O1 are one problem: the Chebyshev-error gauge identifies the unpaid term of every positivity route with the one-sided prime bound, and the lag family writes RH as one window inequality per scale on the prime side and as the no-crossing of `μ(A)` on the operator side. Walls 16 and 17 are the prime-side obstruction in the receiver's coordinates: the cancellation must read signs and must happen at one modulus. O3 and O4 are O1/O2 with a limiting parameter attached. O5 is the quantifier gap of every averaged method. O6 is the construction form of O2: by Wall 12 a certificate of positivity is a spectral realization of the zeros, so an arithmetic realization and a positivity proof are the same object seen from two sides.

## 2.3 Does closing these obstacles need a new field?

### The case

Nothing proves that a new field is necessary; one unexpected estimate inside existing analytic number theory would suffice. But the corpus proves, route by route, why each existing layer stops where it stops, and the stopping points share one description. Sieve and bilinear methods see the additive structure of `ℕ` but only after averaging over a family, divisor-sum information is parity-blind, and the parity barrier is now a theorem in the receiver's coordinates: below half damping, no sign-blind datum decides. Multiplicative, spectral and explicit-formula methods are Beurling-invariant: everything they prove holds for generalized integer systems with off-line zeros. Dirichlet-form and criticality methods give the right sign framework, but their identities hold for any even positive kernel; the arithmetic enters only through the equation of motion of `Θ`, every nonnegative gauge fails, and every sign-changing Schur certificate is window-bounded. Restricted-family positivity is paid by an archimedean gain of logarithmic size against a prime cost of bounded size; on the RH-complete cone that gain is absent and the limiting prime channel is negative definite. Arithmetic geometry has the template and no object over `ℤ`. Each field has one of the three properties a closing theorem must have at once: it must see both operations of `ℕ` at a single modulus; it must carry a sign structure (one negative square, the pole plane, the signs of the Möbius row); and it must be resolved at the zero spacing, that is at square-root precision at every scale. No existing field has its basic objects built with all three. In that sense the missing step lies in a field that does not yet exist as a whole; its content is fixed by the walls, and its first objects, units and theorems exist in the corpus.

### What the field is about

The sign of the critical arithmetic form as a property of the incidence structure of the integers: which integers are multiples of which, and which are consecutive, read at a window `(X/2, 2X)` with precision `√X`. Equivalently, on the prime side, the one-parameter window inequality `P_v ≥ 0` for every late lag; on the operator side, the no-crossing law for the ground-energy path `μ(A)`; on the sieve side, the signed single-modulus cancellation that the parity pairs cannot see.

### Objects and language it is built on

- **The window and its two structures.** A dyadic window of integers with multiplication (divisibility, prime powers, the node set `{log n}` and its criticality; inside one window the only divisor edges are doublings and triplings and the only crossing prime-power edges are consecutive powers of `2` or `3`) and addition (`gcd(n, n + 1) = 1`, the Bézout relation `pa − qb = h`, residue classes omitted modulo `p`, the Diophantine spacing of jumps of `{N/p}`). The corpus's Selberg coordinates, pronic Gram, valuation clocks, radical states and Bernoulli residue kernels are its finite coordinates.
- **The signed conductance form.** The jump form `J_M(u) + V_μ(u)` of an odd gauge `M`: conductance `M(x)M(y)(dm(|x − y|) + 2sinh(x/2)sinh(y/2))` per unordered pair, `dm` the archimedean density plus the prime atoms. Same-side pairs are positive; a crossing pair is negative below the threshold `s₀ ∈ (log 5, log 6)` of the crossing symbol `cosh(s/2) − ω(s)`, in axis collars beyond it, and on every prime anti-diagonal `|x| + y = log n`; the pole pays the archimedean density off the collars and the atom only away from the axes. RH is the nonnegativity of this form; its criticality is proved; its odd restriction is positive past the first prime and its full restriction through `3/10`.
- **The ground-energy path.** `μ(A)`, one real function of one variable, positive on `[0, 3/10]`, equal to Suzuki's main term plus a bounded correction below `log 2/2`, decaying to `0` doubly exponentially under RH and to `−∞` off it.
- **The lag family.** The difference tests `κ_w(t + v/2) − κ_w(t − v/2)` of one generic width and their window functionals `P_v`; the crossing of the first negative lag on a crystal at `v ∈ (L − 2w, L]`; the horizon `V₀(z)` of a zero; the visibility lags `V₀(w, η, δ₀)` and `V(a, σ)`; the unconditional envelope `exp(v/2 − √v/10)`.
- **The receiver and its parity pairs.** The pronic energy `E_T(c)` of the centred prefix of `c ∗ 1` on the damped class; the parity involution `w ↦ λ·w` on Selberg coordinates; the separating observables (von Mangoldt pairing, logarithmic moment hierarchy, cubic translated moments, pole values `K(1 − ε)`); the critical Wiener algebra of receiver-preserving kernels.
- **The pronic Gram and its cross-scale laws.** `𝒢^{ec}(p, q)` with its exact rank-two part, the Bernoulli kernels `𝔅`, `𝔅₃` in Kloosterman fractions, the residue discrepancy `Δ_p(c')`, the coherent blocks, and the dual norm `Σ_p|Λ_p(c')|/p`.
- **Tests and gauges.** Even profiles in a Doob gauge; prime-blind autocorrelations and their deletion prices; `μ_a`-mean-zero profiles; the critical factorial sources; curved and quartic window gauges; hubs and Schur steps; the configuration of zeros as a tempered configuration determined by the all-aperture form.
- **Scale language.** Height `T`, aperture `A`, lag `v`, bandwidth `λ = log X/log T`, depth `δ`, damping `ε`, Gram exponent `θ = 2 log P/log X`. Every statement names its aperture, lag, damping or exponent and the strongest unconditional value known.

### Mechanisms it must discover

1. **A prime-side inequality with no slack, at arbitrary support.** Payment of each anti-diagonal atom `Λ(n)n^{−1/2}` by same-side atoms and the variance, on all even profiles, at one shift. The smallest open instance is the even sector through the first prime window, between `3/10` and the regime past `log 2/2` where the conductances across `log 2` turn negative; on the odd sector the same problem is solved through `353/1000` by the strip surplus, the sharp micro-fold and Schur-absorbed potentials, and stops at the inner edge of the prime strip. The paying object cannot be a nonnegative function; the field needs a theory of sign-changing supersolutions or Schur-averaged potentials at arbitrary support, a ground-state representation for forms with negative conductances on an arithmetic set of pairs, and a Harnack-type propagation across the prime shifts `log 2, log 3, …` beyond the one-twelfth reach of the micro-fold.
2. **Single-modulus signed dispersion.** Cancellation in `Σ_{p∼P}Σ_{q∼Q}a_pb_q𝒢^{ec}(p, q)` for the actual Möbius or prime row at `θ = 2`, reduced to the single-modulus low-frequency statistics `Σ_a W_p(a)Σ_n(c'(n)/n²)e(an/p)` of an arithmetic row. The `ℓ²` classes stop at `θ = 1` by the coherent-block law, Type-I classes at `3/2`; the replacement hypotheses (block equidistribution at scale `o(P/log²P)`, twisted short-interval square-root cancellation) are proved sufficient; the twisted cancellation is itself O1, while block equidistribution holds for the centred prime row throughout `P^{1+δ} ≤ Q ≤ P^{12/7−δ}` by Huxley's theorem (`V:CentredPrimeRowPassesTheCoherentBlockObstruction` [S]), so for the prime row only the balanced regime `Q ≍ P` is O1.
3. **A sign-reading input below half damping.** The parity pairs exist at every `ε ≤ 1/2`, so any receiver bound must read the Möbius signs at prime moduli (`k ↦ pk`) or through odd-degree additive moments (`k ↦ k + v`); the logarithmic moments are a complete invariant and the pole value `K(1 − ε)` classifies finite and critical-Wiener repairs. The field needs the estimate that turns these separating observables into a bound on the complete signed trans-horizon energy with means, endpoints and covariance retained.
4. **A no-crossing law for the ground-energy path.** A proof that `μ(A)` stays nonnegative between its certified floor and its limit at infinity: the first contact aperture must not exist for `ζ`, and any argument excluding contact must see every prime at critical strength while losing nothing at the archimedean place. In the lag family the same law reads: the first negative lag, which exists on every crystal and every finite off-line set, does not exist for `ζ`.
5. **A definite spectral realization with a large prime part.** By Wall 12 the certificate of positivity is the zero measure; a proof must exhibit the Hilbert space and self-adjoint operator realizing it, with a prime modification of the archimedean reference that is not trace class and is as large as `S(T)`, with non-unit-modulus Euler factors, and with finite-aperture ground transforms converging to `Ξ`. The field's positivity theorem and its realization theorem are the same theorem.

### Criteria the field must meet

- **Selectivity.** Every theorem states what it says for `ζ(s − a)ζ(s + a)`, for a Beurling system with an off-line zero, and for the Liouville-twisted weight. Window positivity of `ζ` is selective against large shifts; the Beurling and Liouville audits are open.
- **No family average.** Statements hold for one character, modulus one, shift zero.
- **Square-root precision beyond the diagonal.** Prime sums of length at least the height, controlled beyond the mean-value regime; on the lag family one inequality per window at every scale; on the Gram, `θ = 2`.
- **Node criticality.** The mechanism distinguishes `log 4` from `log 6` and reads the Euler-product prices of the blind cone; sampling-density, Nyquist and generic uncertainty arguments are excluded in advance.
- **Signs read.** Below half damping the mechanism uses first-order data at prime moduli or odd-degree additive moments; magnitude, two-point and translated-moment data are blind.
- **Every scale, not almost every.** A statement about an exceptional set, a proportion or a typical member is infrastructure.
- **Complete expression, no margin.** Mean, diagonal, mixed terms, Gamma and polar charges, endpoints and inherited history are retained; the inequality to be proved is critical, so exact cancellation is the only form a proof can take.
- **No archimedean subsidy.** Positivity obtained from a gain of size `log(1/w)` or `log M` against a bounded prime cost is nonselective by construction.
- **Units.** Positivity is reported as the aperture `A` through which `μ(A) ≥ 0` is proved for all profiles (`3/10` [S], `29/100` [L]; odd sector `353/1000` [L]); cancellation as the exponent `θ` obtained (`3/2` for Type-I classes, `1` for `ℓ²` Type-II classes, receiver at `2`) or the lag `V` beyond which `P_v ≥ 0` is proved (none; envelope `exp(v/2 − √v/10)`); localization as the depth `δ(n, R)` excluded below height `T(n, R)` and the visibility lag `V₀(w, η, δ₀)`; damping as the smallest `ε` with a bounded receiver (`1/2`). Identities, finite classifications and nonselective positivity count as zero.
- **Calibration.** A method claiming a cofinal sign or cancellation reproduces the corresponding theorem over `F_q[T]` or says why it cannot.

### Questions it must answer

1. Is the odd arithmetic jump form critical or supercritical at arbitrary support, and what object certifies the answer? (RH is "not supercritical"; equivalently `μ(A)` never crosses zero.)
2. What pays the first prime window on even tests, where the folded conductances across `log 2` are negative, and what classifies the profiles on which an anti-diagonal atom is unpaid beyond the inner edge of its strip?
3. What in the one-parameter lag family replaces the attainment premise of grids beyond the horizon condition: why does the actual `ζ` configuration have no first negative lag when every crystal and every finite off-line set has one, and what bounds the zeros beyond every finite horizon?
4. Which additive observable of the Möbius row, read at one modulus and at square-root precision, bounds the complete signed trans-horizon energy below half damping, and what is its exact loss?
5. What is the size of the single-modulus statistics `Σ_p|Λ_p(c')|/p` for the Möbius and prime rows, that is, of their low-frequency exponential sums at the rationals `k/p`?
6. In which Schatten or deficiency class, on which Hilbert space, do the primes modify the archimedean operator to realize the critical-zero measure, and under what condition do CCM's finite-aperture ground transforms converge to `Ξ`?
7. What in the multiplicative incidence of the node set makes `log 4` indispensable and `log 6` redundant, beyond the deletion prices, and how is that read by a positivity argument at arbitrary support?
8. What is the exact tradeoff between aperture, height localization and arithmetic length needed to make a given off-line configuration visible, and what is the strongest estimate available at that length?

### Tools it aims to develop

- Ground-state representations in sign-changing gauges, hub–Schur certificates at arbitrary support, and a Harnack inequality for the arithmetic jump kernel across every prime shift.
- Localized explicit formulas at height `T` with the off-diagonal pair structure at bandwidth `λ > 1`, organized around the square-root threshold rather than the cutoff.
- Signed single-modulus dispersion on the pronic Gram through the Bernoulli–Kloosterman reciprocity, and estimates for the low-frequency statistics of arithmetic rows at the rationals `k/p`.
- A theory of the parity involution on the receiver: which signed additive observables bound the trans-horizon energy, and the exact loss of each.
- Profile decompositions in the complete-form norm reducing positivity on all tests to positivity on single-frequency profiles; a passage from the critical factorial family to compact tests without an archimedean subsidy.
- Conditioned-tail estimates for the deterministic zeta orbit on a prescribed disc, beating the generic `1/κ` loss; control of near-top zeros beyond a finite horizon.
- Quantitative selectivity units and a function-field calibration module.

### What it would look like in practice

Theorems come in pairs: every invisibility theorem with a visibility witness, every identity with the estimate it enables or the sharp boundary it proves, every obstruction with its replacement hypothesis proved sufficient. Every statement is calibrated by aperture, lag, damping or exponent and the strongest unconditional value. Positivity statements are checked against the shifted pair and against Beurling systems before they count. The first theorems within reach are falsifiable and not routine: the variation inequality for all profiles through the first prime window on even tests; existence or impossibility of a sign-changing folded supersolution satisfying both moment conditions; a signed additive observable bounding the trans-horizon energy at one fixed `ε < 1/2`; a bound on the single-modulus low-frequency statistics of the Möbius row; `P_v ≥ 0` beyond an explicit lag for one generic width, or `μ(A) ≥ 0` through an aperture past `log 2/2` on all profiles, which would be the first selective, cofinal, weight-specific estimate the corpus does not have. Each of these either produces such an estimate or sharpens the obstruction into a positive survivor. That, and not another equivalent formulation of RH, is what would constitute progress.
