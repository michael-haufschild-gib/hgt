## 1. Assessment

**No route has a remaining step that is routine.** Every live route ends in one unproved arithmetic statement that implies RH, and every candidate ends in one unconstructed object whose construction would be such a statement. What the corpus owns on each route is exact: the receiver (an implication to RH, usually an equivalence), the complete expression of the quantity to be signed or bounded with every term retained, the positive pieces, the sharp boundary of every shortcut that has been tried, and small-parameter or restricted-family positivity that holds whether or not RH does.

Five ledger-wide facts determine what counts as "just work" below.

1. **No graded relaxation of a surveyed route is easier than a zero-free half-plane.** A one-sided power bound `O(x^θ)` on a Chebyshev or Möbius quantity is the statement `Θ ≤ θ` (the `QuasiRH*` abscissa laws [L]); every `θ < 1` is an unsolved quasi-Riemann hypothesis, and the unconditional savings in the corpus are of Vinogradov–Korobov shape. For the damped PM/BVI receiver, a horizon-uniform energy bound at one fixed damping `ε ∈ (0, 1/2)` yields the zero-translation law `ζ(ρ) = 0, 1/2 + ε < Re ρ < 1 ⟹ ζ(ρ − ε) = 0` (`pmPowerDamped_zero_translation` [L]); no method produces even this, and the unsigned methods stop exactly at `ε = 1/2`, the absolute-convergence line.
2. **The positivity problem has no slack anywhere, and its ground-energy profile is known at both ends.** The full Weil cone is the single point `Λ` [L]; prime-blind positivity is node-critical (deleting `log 4` makes it false) [L]; in the arithmetic criticality coordinates the odd form admits no Hardy weight, every prime rate `Λ(n) n^{−1/2}` is the least admissible rate, the variance term is indispensable, and the capacity ratio tends to its critical value `1` as the aperture grows [L]. The restricted Weil ground energy `μ(A)` of the CCM operator at aperture `A` satisfies `μ(A) ≥ 1/30` for `A ≤ 21/200`, `μ(A) ≥ −log(2A) − 3A/2 − 2·archConstantReal − 4A³` for `2A < log 2`, `μ(A) < ε` for all large `A` unconditionally, `μ(A) ≤ K exp((11/2)A − π e^{2(A−2)})` for `A ≥ 3` unconditionally, and `RH ⟺ μ(A) → 0`, with RH giving `μ(A) ≥ 0` at every aperture and any negative value persisting under enlargement [L]. RH is therefore the statement that one real function of the aperture, positive near `0` and tending to `0` at infinity, never crosses zero. Near-null test families exist under RH, so no certificate with a margin can exist (Wall 12).
3. **The prime side of RH is one inequality per dyadic window, with no height parameter.** For all but countably many bump widths `0 < w < log 2/2`, RH holds iff the window functional of the unmodulated difference test `κ_w(t + v/2) − κ_w(t − v/2)` is nonnegative at every lag `v ≥ V` [L]: each test reads `Λ(n)` only on `|log n − v| ≤ 2w ⊂ (X/2, 2X)`, `X = e^v`, and the archimedean and pole terms are explicit. This is a one-sided bound at exponent zero on a smoothed, `√X`-normalized prime window at every scale (Wall 1); height-localized receivers are alternate forms of SRT, not the minimal one.
4. **A new mechanism must supply one of five things** (walls ledger): square-root cancellation of a prime sum (Wall 1), equality in Jensen for `log|ζ|` (Wall 2), a Frobenius-type fibration (Wall 5), a Bochner-cone positivity transfer compatible with blindness (Wall 13), or a definite spectral realization of the zeros whose prime part is as large as `S(T)` (Walls 12 and 14). Any proposed mechanism must fail for Beurling systems and for `ζ(s−a)ζ(s+a)`, must survive the degenerate point (one character, modulus one, no family average), must carry information beyond polynomial length equal to height at square-root precision, and must distinguish `log 4` from `log 6`.
5. **One normalization caveat.** The gauge-free kernel of the odd jump form is `dm(|x−y|) + 2 sinh(x/2) sinh(y/2)` per unordered pair, forced by the proved identity `Z = J_M + V_μ` (`J_M` over `t > 0`, `V_μ` over all ordered pairs). The ACT README and the docstring of `ZeroFormJumpKernelGaugeFreedom` state it without the factor `2`; the arithmetic incidence positivity programme (AIP) is proved for the doubled kernel throughout [S] (ten modules compiled with `lake env lean`, standard axioms, working tree). Sign statements quoted from AIP below are for the doubled kernel.

## 2. Live routes

### WL — Weil lag function

- **Receiver.** Exact prime data → one-sided bound on the lag function `Φ(log n)`, on prime lags, or on the integrated Chebyshev deficit → RH. By Landau, a one-sided bound `±∫₀ᵀ (eᵗ − ψ(eᵗ)) dt ≤ B e^{T/2}` already forces RH [L].
- **Established.** Either one-sided bounded-lag criterion is RH-equivalent; power-gap, prime-mesh, progression and thin-exception samplings transfer the bound. Packet laws: every nonzero compact `C²` source has prime-minus-polar packet values `> c` and `< −c` arbitrarily late (packets cannot converge); narrow probes have amplitude `> √(c t log(1/t))`; an explicit universal probe `U(u) = e^u (U₁^{*4})(u)` on `[−4, 4]` sees every zero, and RH iff its packet is eventually bounded above; eventual one-sided boundedness of every compact packet is RH-equivalent, and a one-sided bound at rate `e^{στ}` on one packet excludes every zero of depth `> σ` it sees; bounded sign gaps of bounded packets, the band law `∫_T^L P_f² = O((1+L)e^{2σL})` iff `f̂` vanishes at every zero deeper than `σ`, the multiplicity ceiling `E_crit(f) ≤ K W_crit(f)` iff every critical zero has multiplicity `≤ K`, and the pairing-recurrence and translated-pairing envelope laws are all [L]. The packet energy has the exact Plancherel form `∫|𝓕f|²(¼ + 4π²ξ²) S_N(ξ) dξ`; outside the band `|ξ| ≤ √(N+2)` the energy is bounded; a linear band `∫₀^L Ẽ² = O(L)` is RH in both directions, the band's exponential rate is the zero width, and its slope bounds `Σ m_ρ²/|ρ|²` [L]. Under RH, Cramér's mean square `∫_X^{2X}(ψ(x)−x)² ≤ CX²` holds and the band liminf is at least the full zero energy `Σ m_ρ²/|ρ|²` [L]. Unconditional Chebyshev and sawtooth errors carry Vinogradov–Korobov rates; Halász-type large-value bounds hold on the critical band [L].
- **(a) Just work.** None; the window sign-gap law, both directions of the band equivalence and the RH-conditional Cramér laws are [L].
- **(b) Open, B-RH.** A source-derived one-sided bound at exponent zero on the lag function or the Chebyshev-deficit primitive, equivalently the linear band bound. Littlewood's `Ω±(√x log log log x)` forbids an `O(√x)` target and either eventual fixed sign; the receiver tolerates a logarithmic loss. A power saving `x^θ`, `θ < 1`, is a zero-free half-plane; the band laws repackage the same estimate. This is Wall 1 in its original form.
- **Walls** 1, 7, 10. **Refuted shortcuts:** packet convergence to zero; thin lag sampling (single base, two bases, countable progressions miss a non-attained off-line top); sub-`√x` Mertens cancellation.

### RW — Restricted Weil ground energy

- **Receiver.** Complete prime−Gamma−polar form → nonnegative constrained ground energy at every aperture → RH; equivalently the sinh-capacity bound at every level, equivalently vanishing all-aperture negative index [L]; equivalently `μ(A) → 0` at infinite aperture [L].

The CCM ground-energy profile of fact 2 above: floor `1/30` through aperture `21/200`, explicit logarithmic window floor equal to Suzuki's main term for `2A < log 2`, unconditional decay to `0` at a doubly exponential rate, `RH ⟺ μ(A) → 0`, every contact aperture (first `μ ≤ 0`) exceeds `21/200` [L]. Positivity holds unconditionally on every test of aperture `≤ 1/100` with margin `‖f‖²/25` [L], by a plateau gauge on supports below `e^{−2}/2` with margin `(3/40)∫F²` [L], and by pole conductance on `[−21/200, 21/200]` with margin `(1/30)∫F²`, translated windows of length `21/100` and height-windowed ζ forms inheriting it [L]. The constrained even-polar form has a self-adjoint ground operator with compact resolvent. Factorial-tent patterns of unrestricted scales, translations and coefficients are RH-complete: RH iff the complete form is nonnegative on all of them; on each fixed pattern support sufficiently high translates are coercive with arbitrarily large constant while the limiting critical prime channel is negative definite, so fixed-support high-scale positivity is nonselective [L]. The corrected-factorial, Müntz-repaired and ramp–tent families all have eventually positive complete energy without RH. Canonical odd primitive cutoff differences converge in graph norm to a nonzero radical vector with complete energy `O((n+1)^{−2})`: no uniform positive gap exists in the original source-graph norm, and the Gaussian decay of `μ(A)` rules out a uniform positive gap at unbounded aperture [L]. The pointwise and Bochner cones differ in both directions [L].
- **(a) Just work.** (a3) The window results stand at `21/200` for all profiles; pushing the pole-conductance window toward `log 2/2` (the first prime) is finite calculus and stays nonselective. (a2) Deciding whether the RW/SZ/CHMD common-core equality extends to the completed source norms (shared with SZ).
- **(b) Open, B-RH.** The sign of the complete form on arbitrary compact tests at every aperture, that is `μ(A) ≥ 0` for every `A`: the ground-energy path must not cross zero between the proved floor at `21/200` and its proved limit `0`. In every coordinate system the unpaid term is the signed prime−polar pairing `Σ Λ(n)n^{−1/2}h(log n) − ∫ 2cosh(x/2)h(x)dx`, which equals a Chebyshev-error gauge [L], against the nonnegative Markov energy; so this is O2 with O1 inside it. ### FU — CCM-FU signed prime scheduling

### PA — Prime–archimedean source passivity

- **Receiver.** Continued exact prime−archimedean trace → high-frequency passivity, a thin Bernstein mismatch envelope, or a signed compact moment completion → RH.
- **Established.** Finite-frequency trace and source recovery are exact; on `Re s > 1/2` the continued trace is the weighted WL lag-pole series plus an explicit affine mass term [L]; signed current multiplier positivity on all upper half-strip slices is RH-equivalent; arbitrarily thin exponential envelopes for the coupled-Sonin Bernstein mismatch imply RH with every Hausdorff positivity premise discharged.
