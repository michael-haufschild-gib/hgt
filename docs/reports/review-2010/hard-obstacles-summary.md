# Routine work, open problems, and the shape of the missing mathematics

Companion: [per-route assessment of all 29 routes, 19 candidates and 15 walls](hard-obstacles.md). Evidence tags as there: [L] committed umbrella-imported Lean, [S] source not audited into a committed umbrella or in dependency conflict, [P] paper only.

## 1. Assessment

Every route in the [route ledger](../rh-current-frontier-map.md) ends in one unproved statement that implies RH or in one unconstructed object whose construction would be such a statement. None of these remaining steps is routine, and no routine task on the list below moves any of them. What is routine is small: the repair of one normalization conflict, the third ingredient of Voronin universality, a few stated equivalences and converses, and sharper named constants.

The open steps collapse into six families (§2.2). Two of them, square-root cancellation of a signed prime sum and positivity of the complete Weil form, are the same problem in different coordinates and carry the others. The corpus states both in their sharpest known form: on the prime side, RH is one real inequality per dyadic window `(X/2, 2X)` for every large `X`, with no height parameter [L]; on the operator side, RH is the statement that the restricted Weil ground energy `μ(A)`, which is `≥ 1/30` for apertures `A ≤ 21/200` and tends to `0` as `A → ∞` unconditionally, never crosses zero in between [L]. The corpus proves that the identity layer, the positivity-of-pieces layer, the finite-data layer and the averaging layer are each insufficient, and it names the five mechanisms one of which any proof must supply. No existing field supplies one. §2.3 states what a field that does would have to be.

## 2.2 Open mathematical obstacles

Six families. They are not proved independent; a theorem closing one would close others.

### O1. Square-root cancellation of a signed prime sum (Wall 1)

**Routes.** WL, MC, PM, CTF, PBM, RB, FU, RP, LI, SRT (minimal receiver), CHMD (discrepancy pairing); BVI, MSC, EGC, DR as candidates.

**Exact receivers** [L]. `ψ(x) − x = O(√x log² x)`; `M(x) = O_ε(x^{1/2+ε})`; the one-sided `±∫₀ᵀ(eᵗ − ψ(eᵗ))dt ≤ Be^{T/2}`; the linear band `∫₀^L Ẽ² = O(L)`; `d_N → 0`; cofinal admission of the power-damped Möbius source; convergence of the regularized prime series at every `σ > 1/2`; the Robin inequality on colossally abundant integers; the one-sided bound on `∫₀^∞ ∂_s K_y(s) Δ(s) ds`; and the sharpest prime-side form: for one generic bump width `0 < w < log 2/2`, the window functional `P_v = h_k(i/2) + h_k(−i/2) − Σ_{|log n − v| ≤ 2w} Λ(n)n^{−1/2}(k(log n) + k(−log n)) + (1/2π)∫h_kΓ` of the difference test `κ_w(t + v/2) − κ_w(t − v/2)` is nonnegative at every lag `v ≥ V`. Each is RH-equivalent or RH-sufficient. The last is one real inequality per dyadic window `(X/2, 2X)`, `X = e^v`: a one-sided bound at exponent zero on a smoothed, `√X`-normalized prime window at every scale, whose pole and archimedean terms pay the main term exactly.

**What is known to be insufficient.** Divisor-sum (Type I) information gives the constant `2`, never `1` (parity). Exponential-sum methods save `exp(−c(log x)^{3/5})`, never a power. Zero-density estimates count in strips, not at every scale. Unsigned majorants stop at absolute convergence. The positive absolute envelope of the signed covariance grows at the wrong exponent [L]. Mertens is `Ω±(√x)`; the receiver is one-sided at exponent zero with a logarithmic loss. Lag grids lose exactly the non-attained tops: a summable nonresonant chain is nonpositive at every integer lag and unbounded on the continuum, so a grid criterion needs attainment of a rightmost zero [L]. On the Möbius side, every finite law is owned: the renormalized defect is nonpositive at every prefix beyond `10N² + 8N`, uniformly in damping; the fixed-lag correlations are the Hardy–Littlewood singular series; the variance and dissipation have second-order expansions; and none of this bounds the coefficient-weighted complete energy below the first period [L].

**What a mechanism must satisfy** (walls ledger, [Wall 1 note](../walls/wall1.md)): it must fail for Beurling systems (use the additive structure of `ℕ`); survive the degenerate point of one character, modulus one, shift zero, with no family average; carry information beyond polynomial length equal to height at square-root precision; and distinguish `log 4` from `log 6`. Additive tools (large sieve, dispersion, Poisson) meet the first and third only after averaging over a family; multiplicative tools are Beurling-invariant. No known tool meets all four.

### O2. Positivity of the complete Weil form (Walls 8, 11, 12, 13)

**Routes.** RW, EWU, OWU, CHMD, HF, ACT, SRT (height-localized receiver), TT, PA; JAS at level one; AIP as candidate.

**Exact equivalent forms** [L]. The odd capacity inequality `sinhPairing(g)² ≤ 2·markovEnergy(g)` on compact odd tests; the single-shift inequality `D_a(u) ≤ 2S_a(u) + V_a(u)` at any one `a > 0` for even profiles; the nonnegative-cone inequality `Lévy_arch(v) + Lévy_prime(v) + 2(∫Φv e^{x/2})(∫Φv e^{−x/2}) ≥ ∫Φ cosh(x/2) v²` with no signed term; the pure jump form `J_M(u) + V_μ(u) ≥ 0` in any smeared gauge, with conductance `M(x)M(y)(dm(|x−y|) + 2 sinh(x/2) sinh(y/2))` per unordered pair; prime-blind positivity on the full integer-log grid; the even blind minimum nonnegative at every aperture; absence of sign-changing odd CHMD eigenstates above `1/2`; and the ground-energy path: `μ(A) ≥ 0` for every aperture `A`, equivalently `μ(A) → 0`.

**What is known.** The cone is the point `Λ`; prime-blind positivity fails if any prime-power node is deleted; the form is critical in every term (no Hardy weight, minimal prime rates, indispensable variance, capacity ratio tending to `1`), and near-null families exist under RH, so a certificate must cancel exactly. The ground-energy path is pinned at both ends: `μ(A) ≥ 1/30` for `A ≤ 21/200`, `μ(A) ≥ −log(2A) − 3A/2 − 2·archConstantReal − 4A³` for `2A < log 2` (Suzuki's main term), `μ(A) ≤ K exp((11/2)A − πe^{2(A−2)})` for `A ≥ 3`, and under RH it decreases to `0` without crossing while off RH it tends to `−∞` [L]. Positivity holds unconditionally on apertures `≤ 1/100`, on supports `≤ 21/200` for all even profiles, on supports `≤ 7/20` for smooth even `μ_a`-mean-zero profiles (past the prime `2`, with margin `1/20`), at single heights for `√X ≤ (¼ − ε) log T`, on far-separated antisymmetric two-bump tests and wide plateaus, and on every restricted family whose archimedean energy grows: factorial-tent patterns on fixed supports, Müntz-repaired tails, the critical factorial family on high dyadic blocks, on polynomial windows `[M, M^{1+δ}]`, and on its compact truncations of arbitrarily long support, where the energy is the literal full Weil functional of ζ [L]. All of these are nonselective. In every coordinate system the unpaid term is the signed prime−polar pairing `Σ Λ(n)n^{−1/2}h(log n) − ∫2cosh(x/2)h`, which is a Chebyshev-error gauge: O2 contains O1. Every pointwise supersolution with a nonnegative profile fails at both ends; one added jump repairs infinity iff `e^{−2a} + e^{−2ℓ} < 1` but never the origin; a supersolution, if one exists, is sign-changing with `∫M dm ≤ 0` and `∫₀^∞ M cosh ≤ 0` [L]. Every certificate of positivity is the critical-zero measure (Wall 12): a positivity producer is a spectral realization of the zeros. Pointwise cones transfer to the Bochner cone only on the window sector (Wall 13). The archimedean part of the jump kernel has crossing symbol `S(s) = cosh(s/2) − ω(s)` with threshold `s₀ ∈ (log 5, log 6)`: every crossing pair with sum below `s₀` has negative conductance, beyond it only axis collars of width `< 3e^{−3s/4}` do, and the pole pays the archimedean density off the collars but never a prime atom near the axes [L]/[S].

### O3. Uniformity through a critical parameter (Walls 3, 4)

**Routes.** TH, HCF (all degrees at one heat time), PA (all Hausdorff orders at one shift), LA, SZ, TT (the critical shift, time or axis), GTA (rank-uniform decay), XN (all discs), WD (all zero blocks).

**Shape.** Each route has a fixed-parameter theorem (eventual in another parameter) and needs the common-parameter theorem. Fixed rank `N` is blind above height `O(N√log N)`; the unconditional de Bruijn–Newman bound is the strip width `1/8`; shifts, differences and heat flow are real-rooted exactly beyond the strip edge. The missing step is control at the critical parameter itself, which in each case is O1 or O2 again.

### O4. Complete signed history and transport (Walls 1, 10, 14)

**Routes.** FU, RP, LI, SZ (Wronskian), TG, EGC.

**Shape.** The quantity is a sum over a history (blocks, fibres, cutoffs, contour copies); the positive pieces are proved and the signed cross terms between pieces (seed, inherited action, covariance, high-LCM fibres, moving core, endpoints, Gamma floor) are not. Absolute payment fails (fibre variation diverges, trace masses stay positive, envelopes fail); strong convergence does not pay norm or trace costs; the receivers are one-sided bounds whose exponent is the zero abscissa.

### O5. Every height, not almost every (Walls 3, 7)

**Routes.** BAG (recurrence on a prescribed disc), JAS (exceptional vectors), LA (near-double pairs), WL/MC (exceptional lags); SRT only in its height-localized alternate receiver (visibility of a non-attained top at a controlled lag at ζ-density).

**Shape.** Every unconditional statement about the zeros near the line is averaged: density, proportion, typical phase, almost-all intervals, pair correlation in the diagonal range. RH is a statement about one function at every height. Around an off-line zero the recurrence set has density zero; any target with an interior zero is approximated by `ζ` only on a density-zero set of heights; the head's self-return heights never pay the Abel pole [L]. Finite prime polynomials attain every phase pattern at suitable heights (Kronecker–Bohr), so pointwise positivity must come from the coupling, through the functional equation, between finitely many primes and the infinite tail. Conditioning on an event of density `κ` costs `1/κ` generically. Once Voronin universality's third ingredient is formalized, recurrence on a disc is equivalent to the disc being zero-free, so the every-height question for BAG is exactly zero exclusion with no intermediate estimate. The minimal SRT receiver does not belong here: it has no height parameter.

### O6. Arithmetic realization (Walls 5, 12, 14, 15)

**Routes.** HF (cohomology), RN and TG (native completion, transgression); candidates APF, BSH, CDC, DR, EWJ, FEL, HJ, IRK, MSC, PCS, PTR, RFHM, SC, SEC, W1C, WBF, PW-B.

**Shape.** The object sought is a space, operator, form or geometry that produces the prime powers, carries the Gamma and polar data, has a trace recovering the explicit formula, and places the zero support on the line. Constraints proved: self-adjointness is cheap (any real diagonal spectrum is realizable); unitary producers have unit-modulus Euler factors; the prime part is not trace class, finite rank or finite deficiency and is as large as `S(T)`; compact models fail Weyl density; positive currents and positive-definite transforms are orientation-blind; zero-indexed constructions (zero algebra, `XiZeroCopy`, divisor-support tests, lossless stacks with the zeros in the impedances) are circular until their decisive premise is proved from the source. This is the only family where a construction rather than an estimate is sought, and the only one where the function-field proofs give a template.

### How the families relate

O2 and O1 are one problem: the Chebyshev-error gauge identifies the unpaid term of every positivity route with the one-sided prime bound, and the SRT lag family writes RH as one window inequality per scale on the prime side and as the no-crossing of `μ(A)` on the operator side. O3 and O4 are O1/O2 with a limiting parameter attached. O5 is the quantifier gap of every averaged method and sits inside every route that uses density or typicality. O6 is the construction form of O2: by Wall 12 a certificate of positivity is a spectral realization of the zeros, so an arithmetic realization and a positivity proof are the same object seen from two sides.

## 2.3 Does closing these obstacles need a new field?

### The case

Nothing proves that a new field is necessary; one unexpected estimate inside existing analytic number theory would suffice. But the corpus does prove, route by route, why each existing layer stops where it stops, and the stopping points share one description. Sieve and bilinear methods see the additive structure of `ℕ` but only after averaging over a family, and divisor-sum information is parity-blind. Multiplicative, spectral and explicit-formula methods are Beurling-invariant: everything they prove holds for generalized integer systems with off-line zeros. Dirichlet-form and criticality methods give the right sign framework, but their identities hold for any even positive kernel; the arithmetic enters only through the equation of motion of `Θ`, and every nonnegative gauge fails. Restricted-family positivity is always paid by an archimedean gain of logarithmic size against a prime cost of bounded size; on the RH-complete cone that gain is absent and the limiting prime channel is negative definite. Arithmetic geometry has the template (Frobenius, finite-dimensional cohomology, the Hodge index theorem) and no object over `ℤ`. Each field has one of the three properties a closing theorem must have at once: it must see both operations of `ℕ` at a single modulus; it must carry a sign structure (one negative square, the pole plane); and it must be resolved at the zero spacing, that is at square-root precision at every scale. No existing field has its basic objects built with all three. In that sense, yes: the missing step appears to lie in a field that does not yet exist. Its name is immaterial; its content is fixed by the walls.

### What the field is about

The sign of the critical arithmetic form as a property of the incidence structure of the integers: which integers are multiples of which, and which are consecutive, read at a window `(X/2, 2X)` with precision `√X`. Equivalently, on the prime side, the one-parameter window inequality `P_v ≥ 0` for every late lag; on the operator side, the no-crossing law for the ground-energy path `μ(A)`.

### Objects and language it is built on

- **The window and its two structures.** A dyadic window of integers with multiplication (divisibility, prime powers, the node set `{log n}` and its criticality at `log 4` versus `log 6`; inside one window the only divisor edges are doublings and triplings and the only crossing prime-power edges are consecutive powers of `2` or `3`) and addition (`gcd(n, n+1) = 1`, the Bézout relation `pa − qb = h`, residue classes omitted modulo `p`, the Diophantine spacing of jumps of `{N/p}` and `{N/q}`). The corpus's Selberg coordinates, pronic Gram, valuation clocks and radical states are its finite coordinates.
- **The signed conductance form.** The jump form `J_M(u) + V_μ(u)` of an odd gauge `M`: conductance `M(x)M(y)(dm(|x−y|) + 2 sinh(x/2) sinh(y/2))` per unordered pair, where `dm` is the archimedean density `ω` plus the prime atoms `Λ(n)n^{−1/2}` at `log n`. Same-side pairs are positive. A crossing pair `x < 0 < y` is negative where the kernel is positive: everywhere below the threshold `s₀ ∈ (log 5, log 6)` of the crossing symbol `cosh(s/2) − ω(s)`, in axis collars beyond it, and on every prime anti-diagonal `|x| + y = log n`. The pole (the variance term) pays the archimedean density off the collars and the atom only at the interior of its anti-diagonal, never at the axes. RH is the nonnegativity of this form; its criticality is proved. This is the object on which "positivity" has a definite arithmetic meaning: an inequality between anti-diagonal and same-side prime atoms, with the pole as the only positive crossing source.
- **The ground-energy path.** `μ(A)`, the restricted Weil ground energy at aperture `A`: a single real function of one variable, positive on `[0, 21/200]`, equal to Suzuki's main term plus a bounded correction below `log 2/2`, decaying to `0` doubly exponentially under RH and to `−∞` off it. The pole plane, the Lorentz form `(B² − A²)/2` on the theta span, is the radical of the full zero form; the archimedean place behaves as a one-negative-square (Hodge-index-type) form.
- **The lag family.** The difference tests `κ_w(t + v/2) − κ_w(t − v/2)` of one generic width and their window functionals `P_v`; the crossing of the first negative lag on a crystal at `v ∈ (L − 2w, L]`; the attainment premise that grids need and continua do not.
- **Tests and gauges.** Even profiles in a Doob gauge; prime-blind autocorrelations; `μ_a`-mean-zero profiles (the pole-free sector, where the inequality is proved past the first prime); the critical factorial sources (the family on which long-support positivity is proved without RH); the configuration of zeros as a tempered configuration determined by the all-aperture form.
- **Scale language.** Height `T`, aperture `a`, lag `v`, bandwidth `λ = log X/log T`, depth `δ`, resolution, horizon ratio, damping. Every statement names its aperture or lag and the strongest unconditional value known.

### Mechanisms it must discover

1. **A prime-side inequality with no slack.** Payment of each anti-diagonal atom `Λ(n)n^{−1/2}` by same-side atoms and the variance, on all even profiles, at one shift. Its smallest open instance is the first prime window for profiles that are not `μ_a`-mean-zero: supports between `21/200` and `log 2/2`, where the single atom of `2` must be paid by the potential without the pole-free margin. The paying object cannot be a nonnegative function; the field needs a theory of sign-changing supersolutions, or of positive solutions for forms with negative conductances on an arithmetic set of pairs: a ground-state representation and a Harnack-type propagation across the prime shifts `log 2, log 3, …` for which no analogue exists.
2. **Single-modulus bilinear dispersion.** Cancellation in `Σ_{p∼P} Σ_{q∼Q} a_p b_q 𝒢^{ec}(p,q)` through the reciprocity `{N/pq} ↔ p̄/q + q̄/p` and the modular inverses, without an external character or modulus average. Classical dispersion crosses the Nyquist threshold `θ = 1` only after averaging; the receiver sits at `θ = 2` for one character and modulus one. The Vasyunin reciprocity law is a theorem with `O(p+q)` error; the dispersion estimate on the pronic Gram is not.
3. **A no-crossing law for the ground-energy path.** A proof that `μ(A)` stays nonnegative between its explicit floor at small aperture and its explicit limit at infinity: the first aperture at which the restricted Weil operator would acquire a negative direction is a contact aperture, and any argument that excludes contact must see every prime at critical strength (rate-criticality) while losing nothing at the archimedean place. In the lag family the same law reads: the first negative lag, which exists on every crystal and every finite off-line set, does not exist for ζ.
4. **A definite spectral realization with a large prime part.** By Wall 12 the certificate of positivity is the zero measure; a proof must therefore exhibit the Hilbert space and self-adjoint operator realizing it, with a prime modification of the archimedean reference that is not trace class and is as large as `S(T)`, and whose Euler factors are not unit-modulus. The field's positivity theorem and its realization theorem are the same theorem.
5. **A parity-breaking additive input at a single modulus.** The minimal observable that separates two weights with identical divisor-sum data, Ramanujan covariances and pronic Gram data but opposite trans-horizon energy growth. Its existence as a pair is the sharp statement of what sieve data cannot see; its negation is a Type-II input that does not exist in present analytic number theory.

### Criteria the field must meet

- **Selectivity.** Every theorem states what it says for `ζ(s−a)ζ(s+a)`, for a Beurling system with an off-line zero, and for the Liouville-twisted weight. A statement surviving all three is not about RH.
- **No family average.** Statements hold for one character, modulus one, shift zero.
- **Square-root precision beyond the diagonal.** Prime sums of length at least the height, controlled beyond the mean-value regime; on the lag family, one inequality per window at every scale.
- **Node criticality.** The mechanism distinguishes `log 4` from `log 6`; sampling-density, Nyquist and generic uncertainty arguments are excluded in advance.
- **Every scale, not almost every.** A statement about an exceptional set, a proportion or a typical member is infrastructure, not progress.
- **Complete expression, no margin.** Mean, diagonal, mixed terms, Gamma and polar charges, endpoints and inherited history are retained; the inequality to be proved is critical, so certificates with slack are impossible and exact cancellation is the only form a proof can take.
- **No archimedean subsidy.** Positivity obtained from an archimedean gain of size `log(1/w)` or `log M` against a bounded prime cost is nonselective by construction; a closing theorem must hold where that gain is absent.
- **Units.** Progress on positivity is reported as the aperture `A` through which `μ(A) ≥ 0` is proved or the support through which the variation inequality holds for all profiles; progress on cancellation as the exponent `θ` actually obtained or the lag `V` beyond which `P_v ≥ 0` is proved; progress on localization as the depth `δ(n, R)` excluded below height `T(n, R)`. Identities, finite classifications and nonselective positivity count as zero in these units.
- **Calibration.** A method claiming a cofinal sign or cancellation should either reproduce the corresponding theorem over `F_q[T]` or say why it cannot.

### Questions it must answer

1. Is the odd arithmetic jump form critical or supercritical, and what object certifies the answer? (RH is "not supercritical"; equivalently, `μ(A)` never crosses zero.)
2. Which profiles leave the anti-diagonal prime mass unpaid, and what classifies them? For a given odd gauge, which atoms does the pole pay in the integrated sense, and what must the same-side atoms pay for `n ∈ {2,3,4,5,7}`?
3. What in the one-parameter lag family replaces the attainment premise of grids: why does the actual ζ configuration have no first negative lag when every crystal and every finite off-line set has one?
4. What is the smallest additive input breaking parity at a single modulus, and what is its exact loss?
5. In which Schatten or deficiency class, on which Hilbert space, do the primes modify the archimedean operator to realize the critical-zero measure?
6. What in the multiplicative incidence of the node set `{log n}` makes `log 4` indispensable and `log 6` redundant, and how is that read by a positivity argument?
7. What is the exact tradeoff between aperture, height localization and arithmetic length needed to make a given off-line configuration visible, and what is the strongest estimate available at that length?

### Tools it aims to develop

- Ground-state representations in sign-changing gauges and a Harnack inequality for the arithmetic jump kernel across prime shifts.
- Localized explicit formulas at height `T` with the off-diagonal pair structure at bandwidth `λ > 1`, organized around the square-root threshold rather than the cutoff.
- Internal bilinear dispersion with modular inverses at a single modulus, on the pronic Gram.
- Profile decompositions in the complete-form norm (origin bubbles, distant copies, oscillatory waves) reducing positivity on all tests to positivity on single-frequency profiles; a passage from the critical factorial family to the compact tests of the receiver without an archimedean subsidy.
- Conditioned-tail estimates for the deterministic zeta orbit on a prescribed disc, beating the generic `1/κ` loss.
- Quantitative selectivity units and a function-field calibration module.

### What it would look like in practice

Theorems come in pairs: every invisibility theorem with a visibility witness, every identity with the estimate it enables or the sharp boundary it proves. Every statement is calibrated by aperture or lag and the strongest unconditional value. Positivity statements are checked against the shifted pair and against Beurling systems before they count. The first theorems within reach are falsifiable and not routine: the variation inequality for all profiles through the first prime window; existence or impossibility of a sign-changing folded supersolution satisfying both moment conditions; the integrated atom-versus-pole law for the shifted ground state; the parity pair for trans-horizon sieve data; a single-modulus dispersion estimate on the prime-pair Gram; `P_v ≥ 0` beyond an explicit lag for one generic width, or `μ(A) ≥ 0` through an aperture past `log 2/2`, which would be the first selective, cofinal, weight-specific estimate the corpus does not have. Each of these either produces such an estimate or sharpens the obstruction into a positive survivor. That, and not another equivalent formulation of RH, is what would constitute progress.
