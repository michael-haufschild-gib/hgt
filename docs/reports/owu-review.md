### I. Why We Are Stuck on the Minimum's Sign (`blindMinimum a ≥ 0`)

#### 1. The Uncancelled Rank-One Negative Pole Square
In the even route, [`BlindUnitSource`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/EvenSectorBlindVariationalAttainment.lean#L23-L26) imposes the linear pole constraint $\hat{u}(\pm i/2) = 0$, so both prime terms and pole terms vanish on blind sources, leaving purely the archimedean integral ([`sourceQuadratic_blind_eq_gamma`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/EvenSectorBlindVariationalAttainment.lean#L145-L148)).

In **OWU**, oddness only antisymmetrizes the two poles ($\langle \text{polarVector}(-1/2), Ju\rangle = -\langle \text{polarVector}(1/2), Ju\rangle$) without killing them. On an odd blind unit source $u \in \text{BlindUnitSource}(a)$ ([`OddSectorBlindVariationalAttainment.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorBlindVariationalAttainment.lean#L23-L26)), the prime terms vanish, **but the rank-one negative pole square survives** ([`sourceQuadratic_blind_eq_gamma_sub_pole`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorBlindVariationalAttainment.lean#L144-L149)):
$$\mathcal{Q}_a(u) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} 2\,\gamma_{\text{Hardy}}(2\pi\xi)\,|u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi \;-\; 2\,\bigl|\langle \text{polarVector}(1/2), Ju\rangle\bigr|^2.$$
Nothing in the definition of `BlindUnitSource` cancels $-2|\langle \text{polarVector}(1/2), Ju\rangle|^2$:
- At small apertures ($a < \frac{1}{2}\log 2$) where no prime nodes are active, blindness is vacuous ([`primeBlindCounterterm_eq_zero_of_support_half_log_two`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WeilPhiDoobGroundStateCancellationAndShortSupportNoGo.lean#L147-L153)), so the archimedean integral must beat the pole square by a sharp Hardy–Poincaré inequality.
- At large apertures $a$, $\gamma_{\text{Hardy}}(2\pi\xi) < 0$ at low frequencies AND $-2|\langle \text{polarVector}(1/2), Ju\rangle|^2 < 0$ simultaneously, so the quadratic prime-blindness constraints $\langle T_n Ju, Ju\rangle = 0$ must prevent $Ju$ from concentrating on the low-frequency / pole mode.

#### 2. Shift-Gauge Invariance of the Ground-Space & Interpolation Machinery
All of our de Branges / Krein ground-state theorems—[`OddSectorArithmeticGroundOperator.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorArithmeticGroundOperator.lean), [`OddSectorPairedGroundDivision.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorPairedGroundDivision.lean), [`OddSectorGroundPolynomialStructure.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorGroundPolynomialStructure.lean), [`OddSectorGroundZeroInterpolation.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorGroundZeroInterpolation.lean), and [`OddSectorExactSpectralInterpolation.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorExactSpectralInterpolation.lean)—operate on [`arithmeticShiftedForm`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorArithmeticGroundOperator.lean#L92-L95):

where $A_a = \sum_{j \in \text{apertureNodes}(a)} \lambda_j T_j$ is [`arithmeticCorrection`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorArithmeticGroundOperator.lean#L22-L24) and $m_{\text{odd}}(a)$ is [`blindMinimum`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddSectorExactDualAttainment.lean#L21-L22).

As recorded in [owu-blind-ground-source-sign-2026-10-17.md](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/docs/drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/owu-blind-ground-source-sign-2026-10-17.md#L48-L51), $q_a \ge 0$ on odd vectors holds **tautologically by subtracting $m_{\text{odd}}(a)$**. Positive interpolation weights $\rho_i > 0$ only measure energy *above* $m_{\text{odd}}(a)$; they are gauge-invariant under shifting $m_{\text{odd}}(a)$ and cannot determine its sign.

### II. Why We Are Stuck on the Sign Producer (Cut Removal & Ray Sign)

Suppose we *do* have an odd-positive weight $\lambda$. By [`OddWeilSpectralMeasure.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddWeilSpectralMeasure.lean), odd positivity gives a Nevanlinna spectral measure $\nu$ on the unit circle (mapping to the critical line $\gamma > 0$, the central point $\gamma = 0$ at $\zeta = -1$, and the real cut $\gamma \in [-1/4, 0)$) plus a constant $c \in \mathbb{R}$ such that for every $C^2_c$ test $f$ ([`re_weilFullFunctional_eq_integral_spectralWeight_sub`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WeilCutEvenMeanZeroDeficit.lean#L74-L85)):
$$\operatorname{Re} W_\lambda(f \star \tilde{f}) = \int \Theta_f(\zeta)\,d\nu(\zeta) - c\,|\hat{f}(0)|^2.$$

#### 1. The Parity Sign Flip on the Real Cut ($\gamma \in [-1/4, 0)$)
On the real cut $\gamma = -r^2 \in [-1/4, 0)$ ($r \in (0, 1/2]$, corresponding to real slit points $s = \tfrac{1}{2} \pm r \in (0, 1) \setminus \{1/2\}$), the test transform evaluated at the conjugate pair $\pm ir$ obeys opposite parity rules:
- **For an odd test $f$:** $\hat{f}(0) = 0$ and $\hat{f}(-ir) = -\hat{f}(ir)$, so $\hat{f}(-ir)\overline{\hat{f}(ir)} = -|\hat{f}(ir)|^2 \le 0$. Substituting this into $\Theta_f$ gives ([`oddSpectralWeight_eq_of_neg`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/OddWeilSpectralMeasure.lean#L624-L628)):
  $$\Theta_f(\zeta) = \frac{(1 + \gamma^2)\,|\hat{f}(ir)|^2}{-\gamma} \ge 0 \quad (\gamma < 0), \qquad \Theta_f(-1) = |\hat{f}'(0)|^2 \ge 0.$$
  **Every odd test has $\Theta_f(\zeta) \ge 0$ everywhere on the real cut and at $\zeta = -1$, and is blind to $c|\hat{f}(0)|^2$!** Positive measure $\nu$ on the real cut only makes $\int \Theta_f\,d\nu$ *more* positive on odd tests. Thus, odd positivity alone can never penalize or rule out real-cut mass or $c \ne 0$.
- **For an even test $f$:** $\hat{f}(-ir) = +\hat{f}(ir)$, which flips the sign on the cut ([`spectralWeight_eq_of_neg_of_even`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WeilCutEvenMeanZeroDeficit.lean#L272-L278)):
  $$\Theta_f(\zeta) = \frac{|\hat{f}(0)|^2 - (1 + \gamma^2)\,|\hat{f}(ir)|^2}{-\gamma} \quad (\gamma < 0).$$
  This minus sign is the *only* reason even mean-zero tests (`twoBumpTest` in [`WeilCutEvenMeanZeroDeficit.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WeilCutEvenMeanZeroDeficit.lean#L597-L607)) and even dilation rays (`dilate T` in [`WeilDilationRayCriterion.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WeilDilationRayCriterion.lean#L497-L500)) produce a negative cut contribution ($-T^4 r^4$ as $T \to \infty$) that forces $\nu(\{\gamma < 0\}) = 0$ and $\nu(\{-1\}) = 0$.

#### 2. Why EWU's Twisted-Arc Sign Producer Breaks in OWU
In **EWU**, cut removal does not require assuming extra positivity outside the sector: [`EvenSectorTwistedArcNull.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/EvenSectorTwistedArcNull.lean#L519-L522) pairs the spectral measure against the **twisted-arc null family** $\Phi_j = \Xi Q_j$. Because each $\Phi_j$ is $\Xi$-divisible ($W_\Lambda(\Phi_j) = 0$) and the nonnegative weights $w_j \ge 0$ make $\sum_j w_j (\Phi_j \star \tilde{\Phi}_j)$ prime-blind, the identity $\sum_j w_j W_\lambda(\Phi_j \star \tilde{\Phi}_j) = 0$ holds for **every** arithmetic weight $\lambda$, while on the imaginary cut $|y| \le \sqrt{2}$ the kernel has a strict sign that kills the cut measure.

As documented in [odd-sector-weil-uniqueness.md](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/docs/drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/odd-sector-weil-uniqueness.md#L51-L64) and [owu-real-cut-structural-loop-2026-10-16.md](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/docs/drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/owu-real-cut-structural-loop-2026-10-16.md#L41-L65), transporting this mechanism to OWU hits a **3-way obstruction**:
1. **Multiplication by $z$ destroys prime-blindness:** Turning an even test $\Phi_j$ into an odd test $z\Phi_j$ multiplies the spectral density by $z^2$, which is $-\partial_x^2$ in log-time. Applying $-\partial_x^2$ to the autocorrelation $C_j(x)$ destroys the finite-node vanishing $\sum_j w_j C_j(\log n) = 0$.
2. **Imaginary-axis sign flip:** On the imaginary segment $z = iy$, the factor $z^2 = -y^2 < 0$ reverses sign relative to the real axis $z \in \mathbb{R}$.
3. **The 6th-order pole/sign dilemma on the arc:** To build an odd-compatible prime-blind null family directly on the arc, its spectral multiplier $M(\tau)\Sigma(\tau)$ must vanish at $\tau = 0$ ($\hat{G}(0)=0$), be $\ge 0$ for $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$, and be $\le 0$ on the entire cut $\tau = iy$, $|y| \le 1$.
   - The untwisted arc kernel $\Sigma(\tau)$ has poles at $\tau = \pm i/2$ ($1/|x|$ logarithmic tail), requiring a factor $(\tfrac{1}{4} + \tau^2)$ to clear.
   - Using a single factor $M(\tau) = \tau^2(\tfrac{1}{4} + \tau^2)$ (the $a = 1/4$ twist in `TwistedArcFamilyN1Reduction`) gives $-y^2(\tfrac{1}{4} - y^2)$ on $\tau = iy$, which **changes sign at $y = 1/2$** right in the middle of the cut!
   - Keeping a uniform sign $\le 0$ across the entire cut $|y| \le 1$ while clearing the pole at $y = 1/2$ requires squaring the pole-clearing factor:
     $$M(\tau) = \tau^2\left(\frac{1}{4} + \tau^2\right)^2.$$
   - In log-arc coordinates, $\tau^2(\tfrac{1}{4}+\tau^2)^2$ is a **6th-order differential operator** $(\mathfrak{M} - \tfrac{1}{4})\mathfrak{M}^2$, for which the 4th-order clamped-beam Bohl/Green positivity theorem no longer applies.

### III. What We Are Missing (How to Unstick OWU)

To advance OWU on either front, we need something that breaks out of the two structural loops above:

1. **For the Minimum's Sign (`blindMinimum a ≥ 0`) — Breaking Shift-Gauge Invariance:**
   - **What cannot work:** Further qualitative analysis of $q_a \ge 0$, paired root division, or Riesz basis weights $\rho_i > 0$ inside $S = \ker q_a|_{\text{odd}}$, because all of these are invariant under shifting $Q_a \mapsto Q_a - C\cdot I$.
   - **What is missing:** Either:
     - **(a) Ground-state simplicity ($\dim S = 1$) or sign control on `blindMultiplier a`:** If $\dim S = 1$, then the root-free generator $G$ *is* the blind minimizer $u$, so $G$ is both prime-blind AND root-free on $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$. Proving $\dim S = 1$ requires constraining the oscillation of the Farkas multipliers $\lambda_j$ (e.g., via the Chebyshev/moment structure of the shift operators $T_j$ at prime nodes $\log n$).
     - **(b) A direct unshifted archimedean-vs-pole lower bound on `BlindUnitSource a`:** A quantitative bound showing that for any odd $u$ whose autocorrelation vanishes at $\{\log 2, \dots, \log N\}$, the archimedean integral $\int 2\gamma_{\text{Hardy}}(2\pi\xi)|u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi$ dominates the rank-one pole term $2|\langle \text{polarVector}(1/2), Ju\rangle|^2$.

2. **For the Sign Producer (Cut Removal & $\lambda = \Lambda$) — Overcoming Parity Blindness on the Cut:**
   - **What cannot work:** Testing the odd spectral measure $\nu$ against odd tests alone (since $\Theta_f \ge 0$ on the cut for every odd $f$), or using the 4th-order $a = 1/4$ twisted-arc operator (since $\tau^2(\tfrac{1}{4}+\tau^2)$ flips sign at $y = 1/2$).
   - **What is missing:** Either:
     - **(a) A 6th-order twisted-arc positivity principle:** Proving that the 6th-order operator $(\mathfrak{M} - \tfrac{1}{4})\mathfrak{M}^2$ (multiplier $\tau^2(\tfrac{1}{4}+\tau^2)^2$) admits a positive Green's kernel / finite-codimension prime-blind null family on the arc. That would give a $\Xi$-divisible, prime-blind, mean-zero kernel that is $\ge 0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and $\le 0$ across the *entire* cut $|y| \le 1$, killing the cut without needing even-sector positivity.
     - **(b) Joint parity-sector coupling instead of pure OWU:** As already formalized in [`ParitySectorJointRigidity.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ParitySectorJointRigidity.lean) and [`EvenSectorFullPositivity.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/EvenSectorFullPositivity.lean), the even sector *already* has the exact sign needed to kill the real cut and the central atom, while the odd sector controls the pole weight via the prime-blind hyperplane. If OWU cannot produce an internal 6th-order null family, its natural role is not as a standalone route, but coupled to EWU where the cut-removal problem is already solved.
