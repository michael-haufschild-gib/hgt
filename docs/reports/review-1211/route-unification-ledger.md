# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 12 candidates are faces of three core problems: **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection, reflection and position). Their RH-level endpoints meet in one object with four exact forms, on one scale, at one door.

- **One inequality per prime.** `P(p)≤A(p)+K` on the primes of sparse logarithmic windows, off power-thin exceptions; equivalently the upper side, `ℕ`-counts on the surviving rays, one moment exponent, two-bump comb limits, or the absolute value [L*].
- **One matrix at finite scale.** The step-packet matrix of every window is twice the product of its two charges plus a neutral part, and the neutral part is a potential plus the Dirichlet form of a weighted graph whose edges are the jets and the floor maps. RH ⇔ the neutral part has exactly one negative square on every window `(R,R+J]`, `R≤J`; the block matrix has as many negative squares as ζ has right zeros, the neutral part one more [L*].
- **One path.** The index of the restricted Weil form at aperture `b` is the number of crossings of the path of aperture conormals with the graph of Weil's operator; its supremum is the number of right zeros; RH ⇔ no crossing. The poles see this path through a path of Lagrangian planes in `ℂ²×ℂ²` [L*].
- **One scale.** Saving `γ`, rung `κ`, depth `δ`, mollifier `θ`, level `σ`, signal exponent `b`, twist exponent, floor exponent, moment ratio, critical-comb growth, relaxation rate: one number [L*]; [D]. U-Σ reads it as a rung, U-Π as an exponent, U-Γ as a depth or a rate. `b=7/8` is `[X]`; the open part is `½<b<7/8`.

Transversal to the three cores:
- **Two worlds.** A failure of RH is world I (a finite isolated top layer) or world II (a tail), with top `≤7/8` [X]; RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing [L*]/[F].
- **Off the scale.** Homogeneous floors, unit gaps, certificates, amplification, symmetrization, replication, positivity of a prime density, the count as a power, prime edits with a count rate: each kind has a receiver and a kill [L*].
- **One producer, with an end.** A completed sum over the twists of ζ over `ℚ(ζ₃)`, bounded directly and compared with its signal, gives `Re s>7/8` [X]. Its estimates reach `2/3` with square-root cancellation; its frame is a coupled tower reaching `3/5` [L*]; [D].
- **One door.** Every structure set weaker than the Euler product with the functional equation as an identity (or with a count below the floor of the edits) has a model with a finite layer; with the identity the system is the rational primes [L*]; [D].

**The common input [D].** The reflection `s↦1−s` is where the three cores meet. U-Π: the reflection of the producer's theta row needs the completed index, and the completion couples the target to the phase `L`-function `L(6s−3,ψ₆η⁶)`: the wall. U-Γ: the reflection, as an identity, is the one structure no model of world I has. U-Σ: the archimedean term of every sign form is the reflection's Gamma factor, and the neutral part is the form of the zeros minus the poles.

**Why there are three cores [D].** No test is compact on both sides of the explicit formula. A compact prime side (U-Σ) leaves infinitely many zero modes and only an exact sign decides. A localized zero side (U-Π) lets a crude bound decide and leaves the prime side at square-root precision. U-Γ is the involution `ρ↦1−ρ̄`, the selection of ζ among systems with its local structures, and the position of its zeros in height. The scale interpolates between the first two; the reflection is common to all three.

- **One shape of world I [L*].** Four sectors of the normalized trace; AAL ⇔ a unit gap ⇔ no accelerating Euler filter; non-attainment and one moment exponent give AAL.
- **One freedom [L*].** Every tangent datum, every finite layer, every tail that the structures allow is realized by a model of each structure set weaker than the Euler product with the FE as an identity; over the rational primes with multiplicities `0,1,2` and a count rate: a non-real pair alone, any finite layer, a pair above an infinite tail, landing failure, sparse tails, quartets symmetric in a strip.

- **One count for every regulator [L*].** Clock below the form; tower and heat flow with the count of their limit; price paths RH at every parameter.

- **Law and point [D].** One pointwise theorem past Vinogradov–Korobov (`7/8`, [X]); everything else on the zeros of ξ is in law.
