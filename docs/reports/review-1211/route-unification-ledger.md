# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 12 candidates are faces of three core problems: **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection, reflection and position). Their RH-level endpoints meet in one object with four exact forms, on one scale, at one door.

- **One inequality per prime.** `P(p)≤A(p)+K` on the primes of sparse logarithmic windows, off power-thin exceptions; equivalently the upper side, `ℕ`-counts on the surviving rays, one moment exponent, copositive prime planes [L*].
- **One matrix at finite scale.** The step-packet matrix of every window is the product of its two charges plus a neutral part. RH ⇔ the neutral part has exactly one negative square on every window `(R,R+J]`, `R≤J`; over all windows the block matrix has as many negative squares as ζ has right zeros, the neutral part one more [L*]. Two windows glue through their charges, and the glued form has one negative square iff the susceptibilities multiply to at least one [L*].
- **One path.** The index of the restricted Weil form at aperture `b` is the number of crossings below `b`; the crossing multiplicity is a Lagrangian intersection number; the supremum is the number of right zeros; RH ⇔ no crossing. Below the polar ground energy every crossing is charged [L*].
- **One scale.** Saving `γ`, rung `κ`, depth `δ`, mollifier `θ`, level `σ`, signal exponent `b`, twist exponent, floor exponent, critical-comb growth, relaxation rate: one number [L*]; [D]. U-Σ reads it as a rung, U-Π as an exponent, U-Γ as a depth or a rate. `b=7/8` is `[X]`; the open part is `½<b<7/8`.

Transversal to the three cores:
- **Two worlds.** A failure of RH is world I (a finite isolated top layer) or world II (a tail), with top `≤7/8` [X]; RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing [L*].
- **One critical object.** A failure of RH is a critical half-line state at a finite critical ratio, unique up to sign, with a Laplace transform that has no zero in the open right half-plane [L*].
- **Off the scale.** Homogeneous floors, unit gaps, certificates, amplification, symmetrization, replication, positivity of a prime density, the count as a power, the functional equation as a bound, prime edits with a count rate: each kind has a receiver and a kill [L*]/[F].
- **One producer, with an end.** A completed sum over the twists of ζ over `ℚ(ζ₃)`, bounded directly and compared with its signal, gives `Re s>7/8` [X]. Its estimates reach `2/3` with square-root cancellation; its frame is a coupled tower with fixed point `3/5`, and `(4r−1)/(6r−1)≥3/5` at every period `6r` [L*]; [D].
- **One door.** No fake has a decaying FE defect; fakes with a defect of mean size `T^ε` exist if `Θ<1`; fakes exist at every count exponent above the pair, and an odd fake zero needs a count exponent of at least half its real part [L*]. An argument against a finite layer uses the reflection up to a decaying error, or a count below half the layer [D].

**The common input [D].** The reflection `s↦1−s` is where the three cores meet. U-Π: the reflection of the producer's theta row needs the completed index, and the completion couples the target to a Grössencharacter `L`-function at `6s−3`: the wall. U-Γ: the reflection, up to a decaying error, is the one structure no fake has; the defect of a twin is `2iζ(½+2it)` times the imaginary part of its cofactor, so its size is the size of ζ on the line. U-Σ: the archimedean term of every sign form is the reflection's Gamma factor, and the neutral part is the form of the zeros minus the poles.

**Why there are three cores [D].** No test is compact on both sides of the explicit formula. A compact prime side (U-Σ) leaves infinitely many zero modes and only an exact sign decides. A localized zero side (U-Π) lets a crude bound decide and leaves the prime side at square-root precision. U-Γ is the involution `ρ↦1−ρ̄`, the selection of ζ among systems with its local structures, and the position of its zeros in height. The scale interpolates between the first two; the reflection is common to all three.

## 0. Unifying statements

- **One inequality per prime [L*].** RH ⇔ the floor, the upper side, the counts, the moments or the copositivity of the prime planes on the primes of late logarithmic windows; `Θ<1` ⇔ the floor `−Kp^α`, `α<½` (log §8, §15).
- **One resolution [L*]/[F].** Prescribed sublogarithmic windows do not bound the class; finite layers are resolved by floors at every length; one-sided floors of one length have models.
- **One matrix [L*].** RH ⇔ one negative square of every neutral window; certified on windows with `50J≤53R` at every block size, on narrow windows, on the first blocks of ratio four, on thin packets at every ratio; a margin above one scale passes to every refinement.
- **One gluing [L*].** Index one of a glued pair ⇔ susceptibility product `≥1`; index two if both susceptibilities are negative with product `<1`; a union of adjacent windows is bounded by the glued model with lowered potentials.
- **One index [L*].** Index law in the aperture and in the energy; crossing multiplicity = Lagrangian intersection number; far end = right zeros; below the polar ground energy the rank is at most two and every crossing is charged.
- **One index for number rings [L*].** Dedekind zeta functions, the Artin character of `X³−2`, `ζ_{ℚ(∛2)}`, quotients of configurations and their powers: the index counts zeros and poles off the line; the neutral form of a number ring has index one plus the right zeros. Brauer induction holds for finite groups; the reciprocity law is absent.
- **One selection [L*].** Constant, weights and contacts are forced; feasible systems converge to `Λ`.
- **One scale [L*]; [D].** `γ=δ=1/(2κ)=1/(2θ)`; signal exponent = abscissa; twist exponent `½+(1−1/n)θ/2`; critical-comb growth = width; rate `δ` ⇔ strip `1−2δ`; sizes to rung `1−o(1)`.
- **Two worlds, three atoms [L*].** `Θ<1⇔Θ∞<1`; RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing; B13 ⇔ packet index `0` or `∞`; AAL ⇔ at most one or infinitely many zeros right of every line.
- **One freedom [L*].** Over the rational primes with multiplicities `0,1,2` and a count rate: a non-real pair alone, any finite layer, a pair above an infinite tail, landing failure, zeros rising to a line, sparse tails, quartets symmetric in a strip, a layer of ζ removed. Under `Θ<1` the same with a subpolynomial mean FE defect, for a pair at `½<β₀<3/2−Θ`.
- **One rigidity [L*].** The Euler product with an `L¹` or polynomially bounded `L²` FE defect is the rational primes; two unequal coefficients give an Abel floor; a fake edit has defect energy `≥cT log T`; an odd zero of an edit exponential needs a count exponent of at least half its real part.
- **One count for every regulator [L*]/[F].** A Green–Weil floor gives RH; the tower quotient has a unit gap iff RH; a contact altered and paid at its price is RH at every parameter.
- **One critical curve, one critical state [L*].** RH, or `R_polar<W₀`, or `R_polar=W₀` with a polar-orthogonal state; the state is unique up to sign, solves the weak equation, and its Laplace transform has no zero on `Re z>0`.
- **One horizon [L*].** RH ⇔ the quarter ringdown rate; pulse rate = horocycle rate; the cusp spectrum has the Weyl order; no Maass cusp form below `3/16` on congruence groups.
- **One source [X]; [L*]; [D].** The root number of a row is `G(u)γ₂(u)²`; two estimates give `Re s>7/8`; the class ends at `2/3` (estimates) and at `(4r−1)/(6r−1)≥3/5` (frame, every period); the theta-square series has its double pole at `4/3`.
- **One field [L*].** The centred zeros generate a field normal over the field of complete-fibre traces; the two fields agree iff RH; over the prime trace field the orbits lie in `{±δ}`.
- **Law and point [D].** One pointwise theorem past Vinogradov–Korobov (`7/8`, [X]); everything else on the zeros of ξ is in law.
- **One dominance [L*].** The Goss Riemann hypothesis: simple zeros of strictly decreasing norms.
