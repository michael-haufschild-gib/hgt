# Route-unification ledger

**Status at 2026-10-24.** The 31 live routes, 17 standalone candidates and PW-B are faces of three construction problems: **U1, critical arithmetic cancellation; U2, complete signed positivity; U3, faithful arithmetic realization and orientation.** At the receiver level the corpus proves much of this unification: the `Δ`-readings form one isomorphism class of a receiver category, SZ's critical first variation is the Weil energy, the HF ceiling implies AIP's allocation, a Weil margin implies heat-Hankel positivity on a window, and ACS's Riemann–Roch is AIP's allocation and an arithmetic Hasse–Weil bound. Identical receivers do not mean identical producers.

Companions: [complete route report](hard-obstacles.md), [summary and research programme](hard-obstacles-summary.md), [walls ledger](walls-ledger.md).

## 1. Levels of unification

| Level | Justified | Not justified |
|---|---|---|
| **Exact identity** | Two expressions are equal on a stated source and normalization. | Extension to another completion or norm. |
| **Receiver isomorphism** | Same object in the receiver category (same threshold at every `σ`). | Equal finite tests, rates of unconditional bounds, or difficulty. |
| **Directional transfer** | A proved bound implies another under stated hypotheses. | The converse; dropping the hypotheses. |
| **Shared construction problem** | Same missing estimate, positivity or object. | A theorem that one producer yields the others. |
| **Analogy** | A model suggests a mechanism. | Validity for actual ζ. |

## 2. The three families

### U1 — Critical arithmetic cancellation

Primary data: actual prime or Möbius coefficients. Observables differ (lag, spline packet, Abel moment, history, regularized series, quotient head, oscillating probe, pronic energy, covariance, divisor sum); the missing input is a signed estimate at every positive slack. **Proved unification:** the width functor makes spectral width, abscissa, Möbius and Liouville damping, ξ half-plane and Suzuki formation receivers isomorphic (O:TheWidthFunctor). **Entry barrier:** in every member a fixed rate below `1/2` is quasi-RH.

Two subfamilies: *width readings* (WL, SRT, PBM, MC, JAS, FU, RP) and *critical energy* (PM, CTF and the BVI/ACCE/ACCC/SAEI machinery). LI and RB belong to U1 with different currencies (reciprocal depth; Boolean extremal inequality). BAG and DR reach U1 through local recurrence and continuation.

### U2 — Complete signed positivity

Primary object: the complete prime–Gamma–polar Weil form. RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF and TT attack its sign directly; ACS identifies it with Hodge index; APT, SAEI and AOT supply restricted positivity and payment tools. **Proved unification:** fixed-aperture sign transfer RW ⇔ SZ ⇔ CHMD with equivalent norms and identified domains; SZ's critical first variation equals the Weil energy; HF's window ceiling implies AIP's allocation; ACS's strict-cone RR is AIP's allocation; one-shift variation, odd capacity and blind minima are the parity components. **Common localization:** at each aperture the obstruction is a finite low-frequency sector; in the incidence picture frustration is carried only by prime-sum edges.

LA, TH, HCF, GTA, XN and PA use positivity, real-rootedness or contraction with other quantifiers (degree, rank, heat time, order). The bridge from Weil margins to heat-Hankel positivity ties TH/HCF to U2 with a coupled rank–time window.

### U3 — Faithful realization and orientation

Carriers: geometric (ACS effectivity), operator-theoretic (RN, GTA, PCS, IRK), probabilistic/statistical-mechanical (W1C, FEL), photonic (PTR), categorical/formal (CDC, SC, WBF, RFHM), divisor-diagnostic (HJ, SEC, BSH, APF, EWJ, EGC). **Proved unification:** on the Ξ divisor the HJ residual, SEC seam, CDC confluence, BSH mate gain, IRK noninvertibility and W1C Clifford energy all read `Re ρ = 1/2` with exact depth laws (S:TheZeroIndexedRoutesMeasureOneSide). **Common missing object:** a pre-divisor source with a forcing identity; for ACS, the arithmetic Hasse–Weil bound; for HJ/SEC, the global stabilization weld.

## 4. Exact common currencies

### 4.1 Width (U1)

For `σ ≥ 0`, `Δ ≤ σ` is equivalent to the graded lag, spline, deficit-primitive, band and regularized-series bounds, to admission of every damping above `σ` in the damped Möbius/Liouville receivers, to the ξ half-plane bound, and to the Suzuki formation exponent `1+2σ` (S:TheRoutesMeasureOneZeroWidth, S:TheWallsMeasureOneZeroWidth). These six receivers are canonically isomorphic objects of one category; morphisms are threshold inequalities (O:TheWidthFunctor). For damping, the infimum of accumulation points of admitted dampings is `Δ`; admission is equivalent to an empty Bohr obstruction hull and to rank zero of the repairing-ideal quotient, and these are invariant under critical-Wiener units (O:AnIndexFormulaForTheActualReceiver, O:UnitsOfTheWienerMonoidPreservePayment). Every-point recurrence of ζ to the right of `1/2+σ` is the lag floor at rate `σ` (S:EveryHeightOrbitAndConditionedTailBridge).

### 4.3 Geometric currency (U2/U3)

The scaling surface pairing is the pole-plane form minus the Weil form; Hodge index, RR+ample effectivity, one positive square in every intersection Gram and zero sector defects are RH (O:TheWidthDictionary). The negative index counts distinct right zeros. The arithmetic Lefschetz–adjunction identity [S] rewrites the defect as Weil's point-count decomposition; RH holds iff the cone of nef cycles obeying the Hasse–Weil Lefschetz bound at every scale equals the positive cone, and on curves the Rosati cone has RR exactly for unitary data (K:LambdaRingEffectivityAndHeckeLefschetzRigidity). This is the geometric face of the same sign that U2 attacks analytically.

### 4.4 Ground rate, mass and clustering

The compensated sampling floor at aperture `a` equals the ground floor; compensated floors at every `σ > Δ` are equivalent to equality of the ground rate with `Δ`; failure forces unbounded local off-line depth mass (C:CompensatedWidthFloorAndGroundEnergyRate [S]). Count-only data cannot give a mass floor (R:OffLineClusterWeilUnbounded); a rate excess needs collapsed clusters at doubly exponential height (D:MasterDCIPincerTheorem). This currency is shared by RW, SRT, ACS, EWU and AIP.

### 4.5 Depth on the divisor (U3)

## 6. Consequences

1. **Count each producer once.** A small-damping budget counts once across PM, CTF, BVI, MC; a complete Weil sign counts once across RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF, ACS, TT.
2. **Reformulation is finished at the receiver level.** The isomorphisms and transfers above leave no gain in new equivalent endpoints; progress requires an estimate or a construction.
3. **The three families reduce to six bridges.**
   - U1: break the rate-`1/2` barrier (quasi-RH first).
   - U1/U2: convert relative compensation into absolute energy.
   - U2: pay the finite low-frequency sector uniformly in aperture.
   - U2/W3: exclude collapsed off-line clusters and escape to height.
   - U2/U3: an arithmetic Hasse–Weil principle on the scaling surface.
   - U3: a pre-divisor forcing identity (stabilization weld, compactness, confluence, centered limit).
4. **U1 and U2 meet at one threshold.** At height `T` the square-root aperture `½ log T` (prime horizon `T`, test length `√T`) separates exactly diagonal mean values and blind sign readings from undersampled ones (O:TheNyquistBoundAtTheDiagonal); beyond it the missing input is the signed correlation of prime pairs. TTPC's `3/4` problem sits exactly there (prime cutoff `X = T`). In lag coordinates the same threshold is the rate-`1/2` barrier: the prime and polar lag measures both have density of order `e^{t/2}`, so AIP's reflected-prime cost and MC's balanced rows need cancellation below the square-root scale.
5. **U2 and U3 meet at one inequality.** The Weil sign, ACS's RR and the Hasse–Weil Lefschetz bound are the same statement; an analytic payment of the low sector and a geometric positivity principle would be two proofs of it.
6. **No reviewed theorem requires a new field;** the summary explains why the remaining bridges resist every existing mechanism tested in the corpus.
