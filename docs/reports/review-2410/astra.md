# Astra: research beyond the RH walls

Research date: 2026-10-24.

## 1. Answer

**There are opportunities for new mechanisms, but I found no established bypass of all three walls.** The promising change is to study which *signed defects the actual arithmetic source can sustain*, especially defects escaping to large heights or horizons. Constructing another detector of those defects would add little.

My priorities are:

1. **Quantitative rigidity of escaping arithmetic defects.** Couple actual Möbius/prime data, successor relations, cofactor dilations, residue phases and the polar correction in one estimate. The missing theorem concerns their joint signed covariance.
2. **For WD, source-derived positivity of local finite-factor coefficients.** The exact tail and even the heated finite-head transfer are already available. The retained polynomial is the sharply localized unpaid object.
3. **Directed residue transport with one global budget.** Preserve singularities, ordering and multiplicities through the prime–Gamma landing, then bound a genuine collision charge or force the Ward cap to vanish.

These are research directions, not proofs. Their claimed novelty is the proposed *producer theorem*, not the existing receivers, cocycles, compensation identities, Schur complements or orientation language.

A new field would best develop as **arithmetic defect rigidity**: a quantitative extension of Arithmetic Compensation Theory, Arithmetic Orientation Theory and Arithmetic Orientation Geometry. For WD alone, its useful specialization would be **arithmetic local divisor geometry**.

The walls do not prove that mathematics lacks suitable tools, or that a new field is necessary. They exclude specified mechanisms and identify missing theorems. Existing mathematics might still supply those theorems. The blueprints below explain what additional mathematics would have to accomplish.

## 3. What the walls now ask

The [route-unification ledger](route-unification-ledger.md) distinguishes three producer families:

| Family | Actual object requiring control | Remaining question |
|---|---|---|
| U1: critical cancellation | Möbius/prime receivers, signed continuation and critical pronic energy | Why does the actual source cancel at the required rate or have bounded energy? |
| U2: complete signed positivity | The full prime–Gamma–polar Weil form | Why can its low sector and mixed terms be paid from one budget? |
| U3: realization and orientation | A source construction landing faithfully on the divisor | Why does the constructed source force the required orientation? |

These are not proved equivalent construction problems. Equality of zero-width invariants, equivalence of receivers, and identification of several forms do not identify their possible producers.

### 3.1 Wall 1: source-specific quantitative cancellation

Write

\[
\Delta=\sup_{\xi(\rho)=0}\left|\Re\rho-\tfrac12\right|,
\qquad \Theta=\tfrac12+\Delta.
\]

For a receiver that reads $\Delta$, one fixed exponent $\sigma<1/2$ already gives $\Theta<1$. Classical savings of the form $\exp(v/2-v^\alpha)$, $\alpha<1$, retain exponent $1/2$. Repeated amplification multiplies exponents; it does not remove this barrier.

The useful question is therefore **which arithmetic relation supplies a genuinely stronger signed estimate?** A conjectural power saving must be labelled as quasi-RH strength where the corpus proves that equivalence. It is not an inexpensive first lemma.

For the critical-energy programmes, the issue is more precise than “find cancellation.” Diagonal terms, predecessor terms, several scalar works and very small *relative* remainders are already controlled. The unpaid quantity is an absolute signed interior/covariance contribution, uniformly in the horizon.

### 3.2 Wall 3: preserve information and pay its cost

The corpus already has exact recovery, complete invariants, stable observations in some norms, and fixed-aperture compactness. The remaining questions concern:

- conditioning and observation horizon;
- the norm in which an estimate transfers;
- off-line multiplicity mass and collapsed clusters;
- dependence on degree, rank, aperture, damping and height;
- allocation of the same Gamma/polar reserve across overlapping pieces.

For example, ACCE's dilation-step observation is already injective **and stable in the critical norm**. Its actual arithmetic output remains unbounded by the available argument. “Replace recovery by stability” would misdiagnose that result.

The square-root aperture $\frac12\log T$ connects several faces of this wall: test length reaches $\sqrt T$, prime horizon reaches $T$, and missing signed prime-pair correlations enter. A complete finite reading below the required scale does not resolve the sign above it.

### 3.3 Wall 14: derive orientation from arithmetic

Integrality, functional-equation symmetry, lambda-ring identities, Adams operations, section counts and formal correspondences do not themselves force purity. The golden Beurling datum and other countermodels make this concrete.

Likewise, a source defined through zeros can be an exact diagnostic without being a producer. A source-defined positive quantity can be genuine but still require an unproved identification with the adverse divisor quantity.

The question is **which independently established arithmetic law forces the landing to have the right sign?** Source uniqueness is insufficient: the signed Euler–Poisson rigidity theorem already identifies the source without proving its complete energy nonnegative.

### 3.4 Constraints any proposed field must respect

The [walls ledger](walls-ledger.md) supplies unusually strong design constraints:

- The obstruction is finite at each aperture, but its low-frequency dimension grows, with available bounds doubly exponential.
- A positive aperture-independent gap is incompatible with the proved small ground-energy ceiling. An upper bound tending to zero does not prove the ground energy tends to zero.
- A mass-only $L^2$ floor needs additional control of local off-line depth mass. The corresponding $H^1$ statements must not be silently identified with unrestricted $L^2$ statements.
- Weil mass, packet mass and negative index count different things: multiplicity $m$, quadratic multiplicity $m^2$, and distinct spectral directions.
- Critical Wiener units preserve the **whole repairing ideal**, not merely the truth of admission.
- Finite Euler-factor deletion is already invisible to damped Möbius payment, its ideal and its hull. Below half damping, the full Möbius kernel is not an element of the critical Wiener algebra.
- An isolated admitted damping gives the corpus's translation constraints on zeros; one cannot replace those with a strip conclusion without the necessary interval or accumulation argument.

These constraints rule out shortcuts, not all routes.

## 5. Opportunities for new mechanisms

### 5.1 Joint arithmetic rigidity on the escaping energy region

This is the strongest general structural opportunity.

Let $A_\varepsilon(n)$ be the actual centered damped Möbius divisor prefix, and

\[
E_\varepsilon(T)=\sum_{n=1}^{T}
 \frac{A_\varepsilon(n)^2}{n(n+1)},\qquad
u_{\varepsilon,T}(n)=\frac{A_\varepsilon(n)}{\sqrt{E_\varepsilon(T)}}.
\]

The [normalized-profile source](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCompensationTheory/TheNormalizedReceiverBlowUpProfile.lean) already proves that, if energy diverges, normalized mass escapes every fixed window. It retains exact dilation, origin-shift and frequency data, including

\[
u_{\varepsilon,T}(n)+
\sum_{j=2}^{n}j^{-\varepsilon}
u_{\varepsilon,T}(\lfloor n/j\rfloor)
=\frac{\rho_\varepsilon(n)}{\sqrt{E_\varepsilon(T)}},
\qquad 0\le \rho_\varepsilon(n)\le n.
\]

**A crucial limitation:** the forcing is known to vanish on fixed cells, or uniformly where $n=o(\sqrt E)$. It has not thereby vanished where the normalized energy actually lives.

The proposed new mechanism would classify **joint limits of mass, cofactor phase, successor data and forcing on that escaping region**, then exclude the adverse limits for the actual source. A scalar weak limit is inadequate: it can be zero while total normalized energy remains one.

What would count as progress:

1. A compactness/extraction result preserving the *signed bilinear pairings* and forcing needed by the exact energy identity.
2. A source-specific relation satisfied by every extracted state, beyond universal cocycle or multiplicativity identities.
3. A rigidity theorem excluding nonzero adverse states, or forcing a uniformly priced corrective flux.

This changes the object being controlled. It avoids repeatedly estimating an arbitrary row by absolute values. It does **not** make the missing rigidity automatic.

The existing majorant, drifting-origin and oscillating-probe examples must survive the extraction theorem and fail a precisely identified arithmetic condition. If a proposed compactification simply loses those examples, it has probably discarded the obstruction.

### 5.2 A mixed successor–cofactor estimate, rather than a universal successor floor

The successor laws give an arithmetic relation absent from the purely multiplicative countermodels, but universal successor constraints still admit the conductor-three twins. The next target should use the actual infinite prime prescription together with mixed scale data.

A concrete place to search is the compensated Bézout interior or the mixed entries of the signed Vaughan Gram matrix. One would retain the arithmetic residue relation, apply reciprocity or an additive transform to the *mixed term*, and sum with its true pronic weights before taking absolute values.

**Unproved target:** obtain a signed estimate for that aggregate stronger than the current coefficient-one identity, with all boundary and cofactor-tail terms included. A schematic sufficient output is

\[
E_\varepsilon(T)\le
\theta_\varepsilon E_\varepsilon(T)+C_\varepsilon,
\qquad \theta_\varepsilon<1,\quad C_\varepsilon<\infty,
\]

uniformly in $T$, for an appropriate family of positive dampings approaching zero. The constants need not stay bounded as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$; the receiver's actual damping quantifiers must be met.

The displayed contraction is already recognizable as the endpoint. The new work would be an explicit decomposition whose constants follow from independently proved arithmetic estimates. Calling the residual “curvature” or “dissipation” does not establish that estimate.

This direction could use a weaker integrated signed statement than a uniform pointwise short-window power saving. It still must cross the corpus's critical barrier somewhere. It is not a way to obtain quasi-RH from estimates already known at exponent $1/2$.

### 5.3 Act on the repairing obstruction with genuinely new arithmetic structure

The [repairing-ideal theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticOrientationTheory/UnpayabilityIsLocatedOnTheBohrPolydisk.lean) gives

\[
1\in I(c)\iff c\text{ is paid},\qquad
I(u*c)=I(c)\quad\text{for critical Wiener units }u.
\]

Thus another invertible multiplicative filter cannot repair the obstruction.

A more interesting question is whether a **boundary-corrected successor operation** can be constructed on repairing data, together with multiplicative dilation, and impose extra restrictions on the obstruction module. This would have to change or eliminate the obstruction through a proved arithmetic relation.

Three separate problems must be solved:

- define the operation and its domain;
- prove its continuity or energy cost and identify the correction;
- prove its action rules out the actual obstruction, rather than merely restating payment.

None follows from the ideal definition. In particular, I do not assume that the repairing ideal is closed, or that successor preserves it. The inspected source constructs an algebraic ideal in a Banach algebra and locates proper ideals through characters.

This is a plausible new interface between AOT and the successor programmes. It is less mature than the explicit receiver identities. Its first useful result may be a classification of which successor actions are impossible, together with a viable domain-sensitive replacement.

### 5.4 Global low-sector control by a signed boundary flux

An aperture flow or Schur evolution can organize how the unpaid sector changes. However, Schur reduction, signed Kron elimination and fixed-aperture compactness already exist.

The missing mechanism would be a **source-specific inequality for the newly entering boundary and its mixed coupling**, with the Gamma/polar reserve carried forward exactly once. A recursion that cancels only the unchanged interior does not control newly added cells.

A legitimate target could be a permitted vanishing or negative floor, or a nonnegative complete form. It should not demand a fixed positive spectral gap contradicted by the ground-energy ceiling.

I rank this below the preceding directions as a claim of novelty: it is a natural development of existing programmes. Its value depends entirely on producing the new signed boundary inequality.

### 5.5 Useful results that would not themselves prove RH

Arithmetic anti-collapse bounds would repair an important width-to-$L^2$ transfer. Sharper nonbox tower capacities could reduce shared-budget losses. TTPC's actual surplus bound could improve the proved proportion of critical-line zeros.

These are substantial targets. They should not be advertised as complete routes until the final sign and quantifiers are supplied. In particular, a density-one statement permits finitely many off-line zeros.

## 6. Blueprint: arithmetic defect rigidity

### 6.1 Central question and viewpoint

**Which defects can arise from the actual arithmetic source after normalization, localization and passage to infinity, and which exact arithmetic relations forbid them?**

The field would study compatibility *with quantitative consequences*. It would treat a receiver equivalence as a description of an obstruction, then seek a source theorem restricting that obstruction.

This is an extension of existing ACCC/AOT/AOG work, not a claim that their objects or questions are new. The proposed addition is a calculus capable of controlling signed defects on their moving support.

### 6.2 Objects and language

| Object | Data that must remain attached |
|---|---|
| Finite arithmetic source | Actual coefficients, damping, horizon, centering and boundary terms |
| Joint arithmetic state | Residues, valuations, successor phase and cofactor action; the multiplicative observation state alone is not successor-complete |
| Signed energy system | Full Gram matrix and mixed terms, with one explicit Gamma/polar reserve |
| Defect state | Escaping mass, location/scale, oscillation, cross-covariance and surviving forcing |
| Repairing module | Ideal, hull and local vanishing orders; support alone can lose multiplicity |
| Source-to-divisor map | Domain, normalization, residues, multiplicities and quantitative transfer cost |
| Certificate | Independently evaluable source expression plus a proved inequality landing in an existing receiver |

The language should distinguish:

- **observation** from **estimate**;
- **reconstruction** from **bounded output**;
- **source uniqueness** from **coercivity**;
- **finite-scale compatibility** from **uniform control**;
- **spectral support** from **multiplicity and local algebra**;
- **signed cancellation** from **absolute domination**.

A commutator or cocycle is useful only after its contribution to the actual energy has been priced. For example, floor dilation and successor have elementary noncommutation identities for arbitrary sequences. Those identities alone contain no Möbius cancellation.

### 6.3 Foundational principles

These are requirements for theorem design, not additional axioms about zeta.

1. **Construct from the source.** Arithmetic coefficients, theta data and exact identities come first. Divisor landing is proved afterward.
2. **Retain signs through the interaction.** Absolute values are taken only where their loss is explicitly affordable.
3. **Use one budget.** Local certificates share the same reserve; overlap, boundary and polar costs remain visible.
4. **Preserve the relevant defect.** A quotient or limit may not erase an adverse mode and then claim positivity.
5. **Retain forcing on moving scales.** Pointwise disappearance of a residual does not justify its disappearance in the global pairing.
6. **Track all receiver quantifiers.** Constants may depend on declared parameters; they may not secretly depend on the unbounded horizon.
7. **Respect countermodels.** A proposed sufficient condition must fail for the relevant impure or unpaid models.
8. **Allow singular and growing carriers.** Fixed finite frames, bounded landings and positive gaps are not default assumptions.
9. **Separate theorem strength.** Record whether a result gives a local estimate, a proportion, a fixed strip, or RH.

### 6.4 A concrete target theorem

A useful flagship would be an **arithmetic no-escape theorem**:

> For the actual centered damped Möbius prefixes, every sequence with diverging pronic energy has a nonzero normalized defect state preserving the full cofactor, successor and forcing identities; the actual arithmetic identities exclude every such state for dampings in a family sufficient for the admission receiver.

This contains two research tasks, not a proved theorem. The extraction half must prevent mass or signed covariance from disappearing. The exclusion half must use an explicit source property, not “the state is positive” or an RH-equivalent hypothesis.

An alternative flagship is a constructive estimate of the form in §5.2. Either would turn existing compensation and continuation machinery into a producer.

The hardest issue is likely **diffuse escape**. There need not be one compact “minimal bad packet” carrying a fixed proportion of the energy. The theory must handle mass spread over increasingly many logarithmic shells and phase classes.

### 6.5 Research topics and useful first outputs

| Topic | First worthwhile output | Inadequate substitute |
---|---|---|
| Joint defect extraction | A limit preserving the exact signed energy pairing and forcing | Weak convergence to zero |
| Successor selectivity | A quantitative statistic using actual prime data that excludes conductor-three and other surviving models | Universal multiplicativity cocycle |
| Mixed Bézout covariance | A summable signed gain after correct residue centering | A qualitative small leading coefficient with no usable rate |
| Repairing-module dynamics | A defined successor action with proved boundary cost | Assuming ideal invariance |
| Absolute compensation | Control of new horizon cells and cross terms | Summable relative errors alone |
| Continuum shared allocation | One global certificate including frustration and pole | Reusing local Gamma surplus |
| Arithmetic anti-collapse | A local depth-mass estimate with correct multiplicity | Total zero count $O(\log T)$ |
| Source effectivity | An independent section/energy law implying the desired sign | Defining sections by that sign |

The analogue of a finite-field proof must include a valid transfer theorem. The existing Jacobi-sum identity is an excellent calibration; integer arithmetic has no automatic finite-field orthogonality.

### 6.6 Relation to existing mathematical methods

Concentration–compactness suggests comparing a problem with its possible limits at infinity. Lions's theorem derives compactness from substantive strict inequalities; it does not supply those inequalities for arithmetic. Here the corresponding task is to retain and exclude every arithmetic escape mode. [Lions, *The concentration-compactness principle*, part 1](https://ems.press/journals/aihpc/articles/4077966).

Hypocoercivity suggests recovering control through interacting structures when one alone is degenerate. Its kinetic theorems require quantitative coercivity and boundedness inputs. Those would have to be proved anew for successor/cofactor arithmetic; they cannot be imported by analogy. [Dolbeault–Mouhot–Schmeiser](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1495).

The absence of a fixed gap is not fatal to this viewpoint: hypocoercivity without confinement develops algebraic-decay mechanisms. But no arithmetic application follows from that fact. The relevant lesson is to seek the correct scale-dependent estimate, not assume exponential coercivity. [Bouin–Dolbeault–Mischler–Mouhot–Schmeiser](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.06180).

### 6.7 Acceptance criteria

A proposed result advances this field only if it answers all of these:

- Which actual source property does it use?
- Which surviving countermodel violates that property or the resulting estimate?
- Which signed residual does it control?
- In which norm, and with what parameter dependence?
- How are overlap, Gamma and pole charged?
- Does it retain multiplicity and moving boundary data?
- Which established receiver consumes the output?
- Is the asserted input independently proved, or merely another form of the desired conclusion?

The field earns its name by producing such theorems. A new vocabulary for the existing equivalences does not suffice.
