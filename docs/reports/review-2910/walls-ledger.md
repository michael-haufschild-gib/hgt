## The three families

| Family | Question | What would cross it |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall 1 — Selective critical sign** | What bounds the actual signed prime/Möbius source, the complete Weil form or a computed defect at the critical scale, using a property \(\Lambda\) has and its twins lack? | A new inequality that is parity-breaking (fails for Liouville), sees every prime (fails for single large-prime flips), is precision-faithful (fails for \(\Lambda+\varepsilon n^{-\eta}\)) and keeps all tails and quantifiers. |
| **Wall 3 — Transfer and exhaustion** | Does an estimate survive the needed norm, source realization, shared reserve and simultaneous limit? | A source-faithful comparison in a topology that carries the sign or index, with the right uniformity. |
| **Wall 14 — Realization and forcing** | Which independently built structure produces the inequality? | A faithful object with a proved selective law, not a calibration, a receiver or an assumed structure field. |

How they interlock:

- **Global law (Wall 1 = Wall 14 at the endpoint) [S].** Index zero of the Weil kernel is the one-sided count \(\psi(x)-x\le C\sqrt x\log^2x\); for curve data over \(\mathbb F_q\) it is the one-sided point count. Wall 14 asks for a producer of exactly the count that Wall 1 asks to prove.
- **Entries (Wall 1 inside Wall 3).** Separated entries of the complete form are prime-error integrals; cross-level prime couplings are prime-error sequences; their positivity is one-prime tower positivity, which is RH.
- **Strata (Wall 3).** The selective margin is proved at narrow widths; carrying it to overlap is Wall 3. Index bounds pass only to pointwise kernel limits; locality fails on \(\mathbb Z^2\) and needs two primes.
- **Precision (Wall 1 → Wall 3).** A deciding input must act at unbounded aperture (inference).

Organizing identities:
\[
\operatorname{Re}W(f,f)=D(f,f)-\mathfrak d(f),\qquad
W(f,g)=-\int_0^\infty\big(e^h-\psi(e^h)-c\big)\,q'_{f,g}(h)\,dh-\Gamma_{\rm tail}(f,g),\qquad
\kappa(K_\zeta)=\#\{\rho:\operatorname{Re}\rho>\tfrac12\}.
\]

**Strata**, weakest first:

1. **[open]**: intermediate.
2. **[≥QRH]**: implies a fixed zero-free strip \(\Theta<1\).
3. **[LH]**: implies the Lindelöf hypothesis; "zeros approach the line" is LH plus finiteness for every \(\cos(hD)\Xi\), and implies \(\Theta<1\) [L].
4. **[FS]**: finitely many off-line zeros \(\iff\) all zeros above some height critical \(\iff\) finite index \(\iff\) bounded heat counts [S]. Implies [LH].
5. **[RH]**: equivalent to RH.
6. **[RH+]**: stronger than RH.
