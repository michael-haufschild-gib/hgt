## Answer this question

**What does bounded overlap know? Prove that the complete Weil form of ζ is eventually nonnegative on the atomic families at the overlap ratio \(c=2\), by a mechanism that pays the successor pairs \((kn,kn\pm1)\) at their full weight; and determine, for the single-prime flips \(\omega_p\), the overlap ratio \(c^*(p)\) at which their complete form first fails, so that the arithmetic content of bounded overlap is known: parity only, or every prime's sign.**

**The objects.** Fix a \(C^2\) profile \(\varphi\) supported in \([0,1]\) with \(\int\varphi^2>0\) (for instance `transMetzlerProfile = plateau (1/1000)`, a smooth plateau with transition width \(1/1000\)). At horizon \(N\) and width \(w\) the **atomic family** is
\[
f_x(u)=\sum_{n\le N}x_n\,\varphi\!\Bigl(\frac{u-\log n}{w}\Bigr),\qquad x\in\mathbb R^N
\]
(`atomicTest N x (atomDilate φ w)`, `SpacingScaleHarmonicAnalysis/TheRobinConstantOfAnAtom.lean`). One atom sits at each \(\log n\); the atoms are disjoint when \(w\) is below the top spacing \(\delta_N=\log(1+1/N)\) (`atomSpacing N`) and overlap beyond it. The **overlap ratio** is \(c=w/\delta_N\); the width \(w=c/N\) is used throughout. The **complete Weil form** is the real quadratic form of the explicit formula,
\[
W(f)=\Gamma(f)+\text{(both pole terms)}-\sum_{k}\frac{\Lambda(k)}{\sqrt k}\bigl(\langle f,T_{\log k}f\rangle+\langle f,T_{-\log k}f\rangle\bigr),
\]
with the archimedean (Gamma) energy, both poles and every prime power retained (`weilQuad`); by the explicit formula \(W(f)=\sum_\rho m_\rho\,\hat f(\rho)\overline{\hat f(1-\bar\rho)}\) over the nontrivial zeros, so \(W\ge0\) on all tests is the Riemann hypothesis. Its Gram matrix on the atoms at width \(c/N\), in atom-mass normalization, is `completeWeilMatrix φ c N` (`RenormalizedArithmeticPotentialTheory/TheCompleteWeilMatrixHasAnExactErrorDecomposition.lean`). For a completely multiplicative sign \(\omega\) the **twisted form** replaces \(\Lambda\) by \(\omega\Lambda\) in the prime sum and keeps Gamma and both poles unchanged (`weilQuadTw (fun n => ω n * Λ n)`, `SpacingScaleHarmonicAnalysis/TheGaugeBecomesVisibleAtTheCriticalExponent.lean`). The **Liouville twin** is \(\omega=\lambda\), \(\lambda(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}\); the **flip at \(p\)** is \(\omega_p(n)=(-1)^{v_p(n)}\).

The statements to prove, in two lines:
\[
\textbf{(Core)}\qquad \exists\,\varphi:\quad\forall^{\infty}N,\quad(\texttt{completeWeilMatrix }\varphi\ 2\ N).\texttt{PosDef}\quad\bigl(\text{equivalently }\forall x,\ \texttt{weilQuad}(f_x)\ge0\text{ at }w=2/N\bigr),
\]
\[
\textbf{(Map)}\qquad c^*(p):=\inf\{c>0:\ \neg\,\forall^\infty N\ \forall x,\ \texttt{weilQuadTw}(\omega_p\Lambda)(f_x)\ge0\text{ at }w=c/N\},\quad\text{decide }c^*(p)<\infty\text{ or }=\infty\text{ for }p\ge11,
\]
with, as the first case of the map, the status of \(\omega_{11}\) at the ratios \(c_0=1+10^{-18}\) and \(2\).

Answering it fully means answering four parts:

- **(Q1) The core at \(c=2\).** Positivity for every coefficient vector, with an explicit horizon \(N_0\), by a stated mechanism that does not treat the successor pairs as a small perturbation. Then the horizon function \(N_0(c)\) for every fixed \(c\), or the first \(c\) at which the mechanism stops.
- **(Q2) Selectivity beyond parity.** \(c^*(p)\) for the flips: finite for every \(p\ge11\) (bounded overlap sees every prime, at a \(p\)-dependent ratio) or infinite (bounded overlap sees only parity, and the primes enter under growth alone). Expected (inference, see Context): finite, with \(c^*(p)\to\infty\) as \(p\to\infty\); the growth of \(c^*(p)\) is the selectivity spectrum of the overlap ratio.
- **(Q3) The zero side of \(c=2\).** What eventual positivity at ratio \(2\) implies about the zeros, and whether finitely many off-line zeros together with ζ's zero density \(\tfrac{\log T}{2\pi}\) imply it for every fixed \(c\). A tempered configuration with one off-line pair and a constant-density on-line lattice is negative at every \(c\), so density is indispensable [S].
- **(Q4) Growth.** Whether every schedule \(c_N\to\infty\) is RH-complete, or where between fixed \(c\) and \((\log\log N)(\log\log\log N)^{2}\) the receiver stops; reported, not required.

The core deliverable is (Q1) at \(c=2\) with the mechanism, together with (Q2) decided at least for \(p=11\) at \(c_0\) and at \(c=2\).

**The overlap ratio is a Dirichlet-polynomial length (inference).** The Fourier side of \(f_x\) is \(\hat\varphi(ct/N)\,D_x(\tfrac12+it)\) with \(D_x(s)=\sum_{n\le N}x_nn^{-s}\) of length \(N\) (`TheAtomicFamilyIsADirichletPolynomial`), and the window \(\hat\varphi(ct/N)\) cuts the height at \(T\asymp N/c\). So ratio \(c\) at horizon \(N\) is length \(N\) at height \(T=N/c\); the spacing scale \(c\asymp1\) is the Nyquist scale, where the zero density \(\log T/2\pi\) matches the density of length-\(N\) polynomials. The proven positivity is positivity at length \(N\le(1+10^{-18})\,T\), exactly the square-root threshold \(X=T\) of Montgomery–Vaughan; RH is positivity at length \(T(\log\log T)^{1+\varepsilon}\); the census of critical zeros needs length \(T^{4/3}\) but only for a proportion. The question asks for the first positivity at length twice the height.

**Why \(c=1\) is a wall and how it was crossed.** Below the spacing the atomic intersection form is Metzler (nonnegative off-diagonal) in the Euler gauge and Weil positivity is a trickle-down inequality (`ArithmeticLorentzianGeometry`, `TheAtomicFormIsMetzlerBelowTheSpacing`, `TheHodgeIndexTricklesDown`) [L]. For every profile positive on \((0,1)\) and every \(c>1\), the atoms \(N-1,N\) (overlapping, positive pairing) and the atom \(1\) (separated, negative pairings) form an odd triangle and no unimodular gauge makes the form Metzler (`TheMetzlerThresholdOfTheOverlapRatioIsOne.lean`, `metzlerThreshold_overlapRatio`) [L]. The crossing to \(c_0\) was done by a perturbation around touching: the normalized complete matrix is a main matrix (reference operator with floor \(1/25\) on the Ramanujan modes, plus prime-defect coercivity from the plateau's variance reserve \(1-q_\varphi\), \(q_\varphi=(\int\varphi)^2/\int\varphi^2<1\)) plus a perturbation bounded by \((2000\,\delta_0+C_{\rm short}/\log(N/3))(\texttt{poleDefect}\,N\,x+\|x\|^2)\) for widths \(w\le(1+\delta_0)/N\) (`TransMetzlerDecomposition.lean`, `exists_perturbation_bound_transMetzler`); support overlaps occur only on adjacent pairs in the top band (`top_adjacent_of_log_gap_le`) and the non-ratio prime form carries the factor \(\delta_0+2/N\) (`abs_nonRatioForm_transMetzler_le`). Here `poleDefect N x` \(=\sum_{mt\le N}\Lambda(t)(x_{mt}-x_m/\sqrt t)^2\) is the Dirichlet energy of the pole relations along prime-power edges (`RenormalizedArithmeticPotentialTheory/TheDeficitOfThePrimeFormIsADirichletEnergy.lean`).

**Why the perturbative mechanism is spent.** Optimizing the convex interpolation over the certified inequalities gives the exact ceiling \(\delta_0<1/107520002048050000\approx9.3\cdot10^{-18}\) (`OptimizedTransMetzlerFloor.lean`, `certifiedOverlapIncrement_eq`, `eventually_completeWeilMatrix_posDef_certified`) [S]. No refinement of constants reaches a macroscopic ratio; the question is a change of mechanism.

**Where the arithmetic enters at \(c=2\).** The prime sum of an atomic test is \(\sum_k\Lambda(k)\,\mathrm{lagForm}_k(x)\), and a pair of atoms \((n,m)\) contributes at lag \(\log k\) iff \(|\log(m/n)-\log k|<w\) (`SpacingScaleHarmonicAnalysis/TheGaugeEntersThroughNonRatioLags.lean`: `primeSum_atomicTest_eq_lags`, `lagForm`). Ratio lags \(m=kn\) form the prime matrix \(P_N\) with entries \(\Lambda(k)/\sqrt k\) at \((n,kn)\); its norm is \(\log N-\gamma+o(1)\), attained on the pole vector \(n^{-1/2}\), and on the pole hyperplane \(\sum x_n/\sqrt n=0\) it has the sharp gap \(2\log2\) below that (`TheSpectralGapOfThePrimeFormIsTwoLogTwo`) [L]. Non-ratio lags are \(m=kn\pm r\) with \(r<c\,kn/N\); below the spacing there are none, and the gauge defect of every twist lives only on them (`exists_nonRatio_pair`). At \(w=2/N\):

- the shells are exactly \(r=1\): the pairs \((n,\,kn\pm1)\) with \(k\ge2\) a prime power and \(kn\in(N/2,N]\), weighted \(\Lambda(k)k^{-1/2}\,G_\varphi\bigl(N/(2kn)\bigr)\); the overlap factor runs from \(0\) at \(kn=N/2\) to \(G_\varphi(1/2)\) at \(kn=N\). The second shells \(r=2\) enter only for \(c>2\): \(c=2\) is the last ratio at which the successor is the only new arithmetic;
- for an atom \(n\le N/2\) the shell partners have \(k\in(N/2n,N/n]\), a dyadic range, so per unit atom mass the shell row sum is \(\sum_{k\in(N/2n,N/n]}\Lambda(k)/k\cdot G\approx(\log2)\,\bar G\): a dyadic Chebyshev increment. Its *size* is prime-number-theorem information; no cancellation in \(k\) is available or needed. The difficulty is the geometry of the shells, not their size;
- the archimedean side gains the overlaps of adjacent atoms \((n,n+1)\) with \(n>N/2\): positive, of the same order per unit mass (overlap times self-energy, constant one; `TheArchimedeanPairingOfTwoAtoms`);
- the two new families have nonnegative entries, row sums \(\asymp1\), and disjoint supports (additive neighbours in the top half; prime-power dilates plus one unit across scales). Neither can be paid by cancellation in operator norm. Positivity is a Schur-complement statement: the shells couple each scale \(N/k\) to the top half, the overlaps live inside the top half, and the only reserves are the gap \(2\log2\) on the pole hyperplane, the pole, and the archimedean overlap energy. With \(A\) the overlap block on the top half, \(S\) the shell block from lower scales to the top half, and \(M\) the margin on the pole hyperplane, (Q1) reads
  \[
  M\,I-S\,(M\,I+A)^{-1}S^{\!\top}\ \ge\ 0\quad\text{on the lower scales, up to the pole correction,}
  \]
  and as \(c\to\infty\) this comparison tends to Weil positivity on smooth tests (inference). CriticalArithmeticCorrelationTheory names this shape as the admissible one: one Schur-complement reserve for interacting directions, with the signed remainder identified before any absolute estimate.

**Why \(c=2\) is the right rung.** (i) The perturbative ceiling is certified at \(1+10^{-17}\); any macroscopic \(c\) needs the new mechanism, and \(c=2\) is the first ratio at which the successor pairs reach a bounded-below fraction of their weight while no second shell is present. (ii) The arithmetic input is a dyadic Chebyshev increment at every scale \(N/n\), so a proof cannot hide an unproved prime estimate; the obligation is purely the signed geometry. (iii) Under RH the statement is true for every \(c\) (`weilQuad_atomicTest_nonneg_of_riemannHypothesis`), so a counterexample would disprove RH: the inequality is true, and the question is whether its truth is reachable from primes alone. (iv) One zero weighs at most about \(c\) times the diagonal energy: for a zero \(\rho\) with \(\sigma=\operatorname{Re}\rho-\tfrac12\), the term of \(\rho\) in \(W(f_x)\) is at most \(m_\rho e^{2|\sigma|c/N}(\|\varphi\|_1^2/\|\varphi\|_2^2)\,c\,(1+1/N)/\sqrt{1-4\sigma^2}\) times \(\|x\|^2\|\varphi_w\|_2^2\) (`norm_Wsummand_atomicTest_dilate_le`, `TheWeightOfOneZero.lean`) [L]. So at \(c=2\) no single off-line zero is excluded by positivity, and the statement sits strictly below finite off-line support in the ladder; it is not a disguised RH.

**Why the flips decide what bounded overlap knows.** Every selective theorem of the corpus separates ζ from its Liouville twin: the pole-hyperplane gap (\(2\log2\) for ζ, \(0\) for Liouville), the Robin–Euler breakdown, the matching coefficient of the pole (\(+1\) for ζ, \(-1\) for Liouville, `RenormalizedArithmeticPotentialTheory/liouville_matching`). These are parity statements. The single flips \(\omega_p\), \(p\ge11\), keep the pole, keep polar matching, and attain the maximal gap \(2\log2\) (`ArithmeticCountingPositivity/primeFlip_hasHyperplaneGap_two_mul_log_two_iff`) [L], yet fail complete Weil positivity on some compact \(C^2\) test, because the full Weil cone is \(\{\Lambda\}\) under RH and empty otherwise (`primeFlip_exists_negative_complete_test`) [L]. At which overlap? Below the spacing a sign gauge is a congruence of the prime matrix, \(P_N^\omega=D_\omega P_ND_\omega\), and invisible (`ArithmeticElectrostatics/TheGaugeIsInvisibleAtAtomicResolution.lean`). Beyond the spacing the gauge lives only on the non-ratio lags. For \(\omega_p\) the flipped non-ratio terms at \(c=2\) are the shells with \(k=p^a\), \(a\) odd: for each \(a\) one dyadic block of atoms \(n\in[N/2p^a,N/p^a)\) is coupled to the top half through \((n,p^an\pm1)\) with weight \(2\log p\;p^{-a/2}\,G\) and the *opposite* sign to ζ's. Hence (inference): \(W_{\omega_p}(f_x)-W(f_x)\) at \(c=2\) is an explicit sparse bilinear form of size \(\asymp\log p/\sqrt p\) per unit mass of that block; the flip is positive at ratio \(c\) iff ζ's margin on vectors anti-correlated across the \(p\)-shells exceeds it. Since ζ's margin per unit mass tends to zero as \(c\to\infty\) (the family tends to Weil positivity, where equality is approached) while the flipped term keeps a fixed weight, every flip should fail at some finite \(c^*(p)\), and \(c^*(p)\) should grow with \(p\) because the weight \(\log p/\sqrt p\) decays. If this is right, bounded overlap sees every prime, one at a time, and \(p\mapsto c^*(p)\) is the first quantitative selectivity law beyond parity; its divergence is why no bounded overlap is RH-complete. If instead \(c^*(p)=\infty\), bounded overlap is parity-only and the primes enter only under growth. Either answer settles the item the walls ledger lists as 1.38 (flip selectivity at bounded overlap).

## Not an answer is

- Any ratio in \((1+10^{-18},\,1+10^{-6})\) obtained by sharpening the perturbative constants: the mechanism is the deliverable, not the constant. The core is \(c=2\), where the successor pairs carry weight bounded below.
- Positivity on vectors supported in one dyadic block, or on nonnegative vectors, or with a horizon depending on \(x\): the shells vanish on a single block, and on nonnegative tests ζ is the worst member of its sign orbit.
- A diagonal or unitary gauge of the atomic basis (excluded for every \(c>1\), `metzlerThreshold_overlapRatio`), or a compression of the kernel by a measure vanishing at zero characters (a receiver, `ArithmeticKreinLangerTheory/CompressionsAreReceivers.lean`).
- Paying the shells by their absolute values: per unit mass they are \(\asymp\log2\cdot\bar G\), larger than the certified floor \(1/120\); the estimate must use the Schur structure across scales.
- Positivity of a part of the form (Markov form alone, prime matrix alone, pole hyperplane alone) or of a corrected matrix (a positive correction changes the form); positivity for a twin or a model in place of ζ; any conditional statement (RH, finite off-line support, a zero-free strip, Lindelöf, pair correlation).
- Re-proving any ratio \(c\le c_0\), with or without a horizon uniform in \(c\): that is done [L].
- For (Q2): a negative test for \(\omega_p\) at unbounded aperture or under overlap growth (known by cone uniqueness and by the selection theorem); (Q2) asks for the threshold ratio at *bounded* overlap, with the family fixed as above.
- For (Q3): a new RH-equivalence of \(c=2\) positivity is not the stratum; the stratum is proved implications in both directions or a precise obstruction.
- A Lean statement carrying the conclusion as a hypothesis, structure field, typeclass instance or axiom; any `sorry`, `admit`, `native_decide`, raised heartbeat limits.

## Question is answered if

**Core (positive).** An unconditional, Lean-formalized theorem
\[
\exists\,\varphi:\quad\forall^\infty N,\ (\texttt{completeWeilMatrix }\varphi\ 2\ N).\texttt{PosDef}
\]
(or `weilQuad (atomicTest N x (atomDilate φ (2/N))) ≥ 0` for all \(x\)), with \(\varphi\) explicit and the horizon \(N_0\) explicit or explicitly bounded, under the project rules (no `sorry`/`admit`/`native_decide`/axioms, no heartbeat increases, `#print axioms` standard), with a pipeline card in `docs/drafts/pipeline/` written first and moved to `3-formalized` after. The proof must state the mechanism that pays the shells \((n,kn\pm1)\) against the top-half overlap block and the pole-hyperplane gap, and must report the same argument's behaviour on \(\omega_{11}\) at \(c=2\) (holds, fails, undecided).

**Map (required with the core).** (Q2) decided for \(p=11\) at \(c_0\) and at \(c=2\) as Lean theorems (eventual positivity, or an eventual negative witness `∃ x, weilQuadTw (ω₁₁ Λ) (f_x) < 0`), with the conjectured shape of \(c^*(p)\) stated and whatever is proved about it (finite for one \(p\), finite for all \(p\ge11\), growth in \(p\)). (Q3) proved in at least one direction for \(c=2\) (the strongest implication from positivity to the zeros, or finite off-line support plus ζ's density implies positivity), or the obstruction located. (Q4) reported as answered or open with the exact obstacle in the terms of `TheCriticalExponent` and `TheSecondOrderSynthesis`.

**Negative answer.** A Lean-formalized theorem that eventual positivity at \(c=2\) is equivalent to RH, to finite off-line support, or to a fixed zero-free strip. This closes the question as a new no-go (the ladder jumps between \(1+10^{-18}\) and \(2\)) and must come with its positive survivor: the largest ratio or the largest subfamily at \(c=2\) that remains unconditionally positive. (Eventual negativity of ζ's matrix at \(c=2\) would refute RH and is not expected.)

**Reporting.** Progress in units of the overlap ratio \(c\) with its horizon \(N_0(c)\), and, for the map, in the threshold \(c^*(p)\) per prime. New equivalences, finite-aperture certificates, twin positivity below the spacing, or improvements of \(\delta_0\) inside the perturbative range are recorded on the card as context and are not progress on this question.
