# Walls ledger — status quo

**Corpus checkpoint:** commit `b23b54cd36` with its working tree (2026-11-11). Fact log: `logs/review-1111-rev2-facts.md` (identifiers in parentheses are entries of log §4, §6, §8 and §9; `D1–D9` of log §7). Companions: [route-obstacles.md](route-obstacles.md), [route-unification-ledger.md](route-unification-ledger.md), [obstacles-summary.md](obstacles-summary.md).

Three wall families carry the frontier. **Σ** is the critical sign of the window form, **Π** the precision and production of the prime count, **Γ** the selection of ζ, the reflection of its zeros and their position in height. They meet in one minimal face, in one lattice and on one scale.

- **The minimal face.** RH ⇔ `P(p)≤A(p)+K` at the primes of arbitrarily late logarithmic windows `|log p−T|≤32 log(T+2)`, outside at most `Ce^{δT}` primes per window; the same with the upper side alone, with `ℕ`-counts on the surviving prime rays, with local even moments under one exponent, with the absolute value, and as the limit of two-bump comb forms [L*] (MF1–MF5, MF9). At the level of configurations no window below the logarithmic radius decides, at any prescribed radius [L*] (SL2, MF8).
- **The lattice.** RH ⇔ exactly one positive square of the comb pairing on packets; the negative index of the defect is the number of distinct right zeros, for ζ with Dirichlet characters, for Dedekind zeta functions, for the class characters together, and for the degree-two Artin character of the splitting field of `X³−2` [L*] (AG3, AR3). At finite scale RH is the sign of explicit finite matrices on step packets, with an explicit distance to the comb at every block size [L*] (AG5, HF1, SP1). Constant, contacts and tree lengths are forced [L*] (AG1).
- **The scale.** Saving `γ`, rung `κ`, depth `δ`, mollifier exponent `θ`, level `σ`, signal exponent `b`, twist exponent, floor exponent, moment ratio, tail growth, relaxation rate: `γ=δ=1/(2κ)=1/(2θ)`, `b=σ−½`, depth `=A/2k`, `Θ≤½+(1−1/n)θ/2`, `Θ≤1−a+σ`, `Θ≤1−2δ` [L*] (SA-g, JA1, FC1, TF1, FF1, FF4, PT2, RD1). Every graded receiver is an equivalence at each level of this scale [D] (D1). The level `b=7/8` is a theorem [X]; the open part of the scale is `½<b<7/8`. The one producer on the scale has three nested limits above the line: the room `[13/15,7/8]` of its estimates, the floor `5/6` of its squares, the wall `2/3` of its frame [L*] (TB, FG, MV); [D] (D3, D4).
- **Off the scale.** A homogeneous floor, a unit gap, a certificate, an amplification over all orders, a symmetrization, a replication, positivity of a prime density, the count as a power: each kind has a receiver and a kill in Lean [L*] (FF1–FF14, PL1, IF4, IF5).

[Frontier](../rh-current-frontier-map.md): **38 live formulations, 12 candidates**. [Scoped obstructions](../rh-killed-routes.md) separate failed mechanisms from open replacements.

**Status.** **Solved** (the face is a theorem), **Partially solved**, **Sharpened** (open, exact form a theorem), **Open**, **No-go** (mechanism refuted), **Classified**, **Reduced** (theorem-level equivalent named), **Settled** (a method question answered). **Strength:** RH, ≥qRH, AAL, sub, indep., identity, method, producer. **Evidence:** `[L*]` Lean statement read at the declaration for this ledger; `[F]`, `[KR]` stated by the frontier map and the scoped-obstruction ledger at the checkpoint, both read in full; `[S]` module graded unfinished or unintegrated by the frontier map; `[D]` deduction, not a Lean theorem; `[C]` classical, quoted from memory; `[A]` assessment; `[X]` the theorem of the external source cached in the repository (OpenAI, 30 September 2026), taken as proved: no zero of ζ, of a Dirichlet `L`-function or of a finite-order Hecke `L`-function over `ℚ(√−3)` in `Re s>7/8`; its formalization in the Lean graph is incomplete (A118). **≥qRH** marks a statement that implies a zero-free half-plane, Lindelöf or ZAL: it is open where it gives a half-plane below `7/8` or is not known to follow from [X].
