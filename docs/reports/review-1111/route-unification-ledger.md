# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 12 candidates are faces of three core problems: **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection, reflection and position). Their RH-level endpoints meet at one object, in two exact forms, on one scale.

- **One inequality per prime, on the primes of sparse logarithmic windows, off power-thin exceptions.** `P(p)≤A(p)+K`; equivalently the floor of the prime trace; the upper side alone; `ℕ`-counts on the ray `Γ_1+Γ_p` with the plane defect as quadratic limit; one growth exponent for all even local moments; the limit of the two-bump comb form; and, with no sign, the absolute value [L*] (MF1–MF5, MF9).
- **One matrix on the integers.** The comb pairing has the limit `2d₁d₂−Z(f)` on packets; RH ⇔ exactly one positive square; the negative index of the defect is the number of distinct right zeros, for ζ with Dirichlet characters, for Dedekind zeta functions, for the class characters together and for one Artin character of a non-abelian extension [L*] (AG3, AR3). At finite scale it is the sign of explicit finite matrices on step packets, at an explicit distance from the comb [L*] (AG5, SP1).
- **One scale.** Every graded receiver reads the same number: a saving `n^{½+γ}` of the contacts, the rung `κ=1/(2γ)`, the depth `δ=1/(2κ)`, the mollifier exponent `θ=κ`, the level `σ=½+b` of the dressed aggregate, the exponent `b` of the signal, the exponent of a twist family, the exponent of a floor, the ratio `A/2k` of a moment bound, the growth of a power tail of the comb, the relaxation rate of the horizon [L*] (SA-g, JA1, FC1, TF1, FF1, FF4, PT2, RD1); [D] (D1). U-Σ reads it as a sign, U-Π as a cancellation exponent, U-Γ as a depth or a rate. The level `b=7/8` is a theorem [X]; the open part of the scale is `½<b<7/8`.

Transversal to the three cores:
- **Two worlds.** A failure of RH is world I (¬AAL: a finite isolated top layer) or world II (AAL ∧ ¬ZAL: a tail), with top at most `7/8` [X]. RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing [L*] (AT1); `Θ<1⇔Θ∞<1` [L*] (AT2).
- **Off the scale.** Statements that carry no exponent: a homogeneous floor, a unit gap, a certificate, an amplification over all orders, a symmetrization, a replication, positivity of a prime density, the count as a power. Each has a receiver and a kill [L*] (FF1–FF14, PL1, IF4, IF5). AAL is of the second kind: at most one or infinitely many zeros right of every line [L*] (FF2).
- **One producer.** One mechanism has produced a pointwise theorem about the zeros: a completed sum over a family with a reciprocity identity, bounded directly and compared with its signal [X]. On the scale it has three nested limits above the line: the room `[13/15,7/8]` of its estimates, the floor `5/6` of its squares, the wall `2/3` of its frame [L*] (TB, FG, MV); [D] (D3, D4).

Three structural gaps lie under all three cores: the **dark range** (prime sums shorter than the Vinogradov length in the Lean graph, Dirichlet polynomials longer than the height); **Bézout from a property of the object** (every structure of the square in the corpus returns an identity, and every one-parameter family through ζ has the count of its limit or is RH at every parameter) [L*]; and the **joint input**: an inequality that uses the Euler product, the functional equation and the count of the integers together and is pointwise in the zeros. Each structure alone, and each pair in a weakened form, has a model with zeros off the line; the exact count alone, and the exact functional equation on a countable system alone, identify ζ [L*]/[KR] ([obstacles-summary.md](obstacles-summary.md) §1.3). One such inequality is a theorem: the half-plane `Re s>7/8` [X]; it is not in the Lean graph. None is known below `7/8`.

**Why there are three cores [D].** No test is compact on both sides of the explicit formula. A compact prime side (U-Σ) leaves infinitely many zero modes and only an exact sign decides. A localized zero side (U-Π) lets a crude bound decide and leaves the prime side at square-root precision. U-Γ is the involution `ρ↦1−ρ̄`, the selection of ζ and the position of its zeros in height. The scale is the one-parameter family between the two extremes.

## 0. Unifying statements

- **One inequality per prime [L*].** RH ⇔ the floor, the upper side, the counts, the moments or the two-bump comb limit on the primes of arbitrarily late logarithmic windows (MF1–MF5).
- **One resolution [L*].** At the level of configurations no window below the logarithmic radius decides, at every prescribed radius `R=o(log T)`; a density-matched configuration exists for some `R→∞`. Finite layers are resolved on bounded windows by floors at every length; one-sided floors on windows of one length have models (SL2, MF8, MF7, FF5).
- **One matrix [L*].** One positive square; exact index; the same matrix for Dirichlet characters, ray class characters of number fields, Dedekind zeta functions and one Artin character; at finite scale: step packets, ratio windows, mass floors, with an explicit block error (AG3, AR3, AG5, HF1, SP1).
- **One selection [L*].** Bézout holds for one constant, one set of contacts, one tree length; feasible systems at apertures tending to infinity converge to `Λ` (AG1, FF10).
- **One scale [L*]; [D].** `γ=δ=1/(2κ)=1/(2θ)`, `b=σ−½`, depth `=A/2k`, `Θ≤½+(1−1/n)θ/2`, `Θ≤1−a+σ`, `Θ≤1−2δ`; sizes pay every dominated system below rung one; the Liouville twist fails at `1+o(1)` (SA-a, SA-g, D1).
- **One index in the aperture [L*]/[F].** Immediately right of an aperture the index is the negative index plus the nullity; resonant apertures are locally finite; the supremum of the index is the number of right zeros; RH ⇔ no aperture carries a null mode [D] (IA1, HF2, HF3).
- **Two worlds, three atoms [L*].** `Θ<1⇔Θ∞<1`; RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing; AAL ⇔ B13 ∧ extreme DH; B13 ⇔ the packet index is `0` or `∞` (AT1, AT2, AC1).
- **One shape of world I [L*].** At `b_*=Θ−½` the normalized trace is in exactly one of four sectors; world I is the sector with a nonzero trigonometric-polynomial limit and an isolated top line. AAL ⇔ a unit gap ⇔ no finite Euler filter accelerates the horocycle rate. Non-attainment on windows, one growth exponent for all even window moments, and the absence of a nonzero trigonometric-polynomial limit each give AAL; the last is not equivalent to it (FF2–FF4, FF14; log §4.8). The tangent data of `μ` and `λ` are their residues at the top zeros, with the same support and degrees (TD1).
- **One freedom [L*].** Every tangent datum is realized by a symmetric tempered configuration, by a positive prime density, by an Euler product over natural norms with linear counting, and right of `17/30` by rational primes with repetition; above the frontier of ζ a one-off pair occurs in a meromorphic Euler product over the rational primes with weights in `[0,2]`. The Euler product read through the zero configuration has floor `½` (FF6, MD3, FF1).
- **One rigidity [L*].** A countable system with an integrable functional-equation defect is the rational primes; so is a system with integer norms and a bounded count error at slope one. A count rate is not rigid: fakes at every exponent above `½`, and below `½` from a half-plane of ζ; each fake built by deletion has a real zero on top, and additions alone never fake. Continuous prime measures keep every zero; no bounded or Wiener constituent has a zero; certificates converge to `Λ` (RG1, CR1, IF1–IF8, AO1, CT1, FF10).
- **One count for every regulator [L*].** The clock lies below Weil's form; the scattering tower and the heat flow have the count of their limit; price paths are RH at every parameter (FF11, AG2).
- **One critical curve [F]/[L*].** `R_polar=R_halfline≤W`; a three-way classification (AH1).
- **One horizon [L*]/[F].** Ringdown, horocycle, cavity energy and reflection Krein index read the same resonances; a rate `δ` is the strip `Re ρ≤1−2δ`; the horocycle exponent is `1−Θ/2`, filtered `1−Θ∞/2`; at every fixed scattering height the energy floor is RH (RD1, HA1; log §4.8).
- **One source [X]; [L*]; [D].** The Möbius sign is one Jacobi sum; two estimates for one completed sum give the half-plane `Re s>7/8` [X]. A generic mean square over the principal `n`-th-power twists at the exponent `θ` is the half-plane `½+(1−1/n)θ/2`, and the least `θ` is `(n/(n−1))(2Θ−1)`: the law of a twist family is a level of the scale (TF1). In the twist exponent the estimates have the room `[22/25,9/10]`, every Cauchy–Schwarz row sieve has the floor `(n−2)/(n−1)`, and a boundary below it is a saving taken before Cauchy–Schwarz (TB, FG, MV). The correction of the probe is an `L`-function of a second family at `6s−3`, so the frame is unconditional on `Re s>2/3` only [D] (D3).
- **Law and point [D].** One pointwise theorem holds past the Vinogradov–Korobov region: no zero right of `7/8` [X]. Left of `7/8` every unconditional theorem about the zeros of ξ is in law: twisted moments, a positive proportion on the line, exceptional heights on a rung, the depth sum, cameras [L*] (TM1, LV1, SA-c, DS1, CM3). The pointwise theorems act on constituents, finite layers, models and regulators.
- **One dominance [L*].** One Riemann hypothesis of the corpus is proved with no sign: the Goss zeta function, by strict log-concavity of coefficient norms in an ultrametric field (CG1). No corresponding norm is identified for ξ [F].
