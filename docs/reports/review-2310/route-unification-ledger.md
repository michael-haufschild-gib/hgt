# Route-unification ledger

**Status at 2026-10-23.** The 30 live routes, 18 standalone candidates and embedded PW-B organize into three construction families: **U1, critical arithmetic cancellation; U2, complete signed positivity; U3, faithful arithmetic realization and orientation.** Many endpoint criteria are provably equivalent to RH. That does not make their positive-rate invariants, fixed-aperture statements or missing constructions identical.

Companions: [complete route report](hard-obstacles.md), [summary and research programme](hard-obstacles-summary.md), [walls ledger](walls-ledger.md). Evidence and verification limits are shared with the complete report.

## 1. Levels of unification

| Level | What is justified | What is not justified |
|---|---|---|
| **Exact identity** | Two expressions are equal on a stated source/domain, with the stated normalization. | Extending identity to another completion, dropping Gamma/poles, or changing a norm. |
| **Exact receiver equivalence** | Either complete condition implies the other, sometimes through RH. | Equality of finite tests, numerical rates, or proof difficulty. |
| **Directional transfer** | A proved source bound implies a receiver under explicit hypotheses. | Its converse or removal of those hypotheses. |
| **Shared construction problem** | Routes need similar source estimates, positivity, effectivity or faithful landing. | A theorem that a producer for one automatically constructs the others. |
| **Analogy** | A model suggests a mechanism worth testing. | Actual-ζ arithmetic validity. |

Only the first three levels are theorem-level arrows. The final two guide research. In particular, “all these endpoints say RH” is logically correct but too coarse to organize the missing mathematics.

## 2. Three core families

### U1 — Critical arithmetic cancellation

The primary data are actual prime or Möbius coefficients. Routes change the observable: lag, band, Abel moment, matched history, regularized Dirichlet series, approximation residual, pronic energy, covariance or divisor sum. Their common missing ingredient is a source-derived estimate at the required rate, including signs and tails.

Two especially tight subfamilies are present:

- **Width readings:** prime-lag/packet exponential rates, regularized-series abscissae, Wold radius and the accumulation threshold of admitted damping read `Δ` through proved dictionaries.
- **Critical approximation/energy:** PM, MC and CTF have RH-equivalent endpoints, with BVI/THIM/ACCE exposing concrete energy and continuation identities. Several transports are exact, but a generic operator norm is not the same requirement as one actual-source orbit or first column.

LI and Robin belong here as arithmetic-estimate routes. LI reads a different height-weighted depth; Robin's Boolean receiver is not a proved positive-rate width dictionary. BAG and DR enter through source-dependent recurrence or continuation, with additional realization obligations.

### U2 — Complete signed positivity

RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, ACT, AIP, EWU and HF attack the actual Weil sign or a complete equivalent sign. APT and SAEI seek positive source families and payment mechanisms. ACS identifies the same sign with Hodge index. These are the tightest common-form connections in the corpus.

LA, TH, HCF, GTA and XN also use positivity, real-rootedness, contraction or curvature. Their complete endpoints meet RH, but their finite tests are not all the same Weil window. Rank, heat time, coefficient cone, quotient and pole constraints matter.

The common missing construction is an arithmetic reason for the complete sign. Small windows, positive restricted cones, uniqueness of the arithmetic weight and exact source reconstruction do not produce it.

### U3 — Faithful realization and orientation

These routes try to build a carrier on which positivity, losslessness, an index bound, descent or curvature makes line support inevitable. The carrier may be geometric, operator-theoretic, probabilistic, photonic, categorical or dynamical. Many diagnostics are exact; the missing step is an independent source construction with faithful landing.

U3 has three distinct construction types: positive realizations (HF/MSC/PTR/W1C/WBF), orientation/vanishing producers (TG/WD/APF/BSH/CDC/HJ/SEC and related candidates), and effectivity (ACS). They are not presently connected by a general producer theorem. An arithmetic RR construction might yield U2 positivity; a signed path estimate might also yield U2 positivity. No theorem makes those two constructions interchangeable.

## 4. The exact common currencies

### 4.1 Zero width and its arithmetic readings

For `σ≥0`, the width dictionary identifies `Δ≤σ` with the appropriately quantified prime-lag/packet bounds, regularized-series convergence to the right of `1/2+σ`, and the stated covariance/energy radius conditions. Endpoint/slack conventions belong to each theorem. The core dictionary is [TheRoutesMeasureOneZeroWidth](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheRoutesMeasureOneZeroWidth.lean); concrete examples are [RegularizedLogZetaAbscissa](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/RegularizedLogZetaAbscissa.lean) and [PaperWoldDirectedCovarianceWidthSpectrum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PaperWoldDirectedCovarianceWidthSpectrum.lean).

For damping, let `D={ε>0 : sup_T Eε(T)<∞}`. The proved statements are:

- every `ε>Δ` belongs to `D`;
- the infimum of accumulation points of `D` equals `Δ`;
- cofinal admission as `ε↓0` is RH-equivalent.

An isolated admitted damping is not being assigned the same converse as an accumulation family. Working source identifies the Möbius and Liouville positive admission sets. The shrinking unconditional near-half band remains a horizon-dependent estimate, not a fixed below-half strip. [AdmissionBelowHalfDampingAboveTheZeroWidth](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/AdmissionBelowHalfDampingAboveTheZeroWidth.lean); [SmallDampingMoebiusAndLiouvilleCancellationLaws](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/TransHorizonIncidenceMechanics/SmallDampingMoebiusAndLiouvilleCancellationLaws.lean).

### 4.2 Aperture and the common Weil form

Write `W_A` for all real compact `C²` tests in `[-A,A]`, and `W_A^even`, `W_A^odd` for the parity restrictions. The exact fixed-aperture arrows are:

| Statement | Proved relation | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Full window positivity | `W_A≥0 ⇔ (W_A^even≥0 and W_A^odd≥0)` | Both parity sectors required. |
| Odd window positivity | Equivalent to the specified sinh-capacity inequality at cutoff `⌈e^(2A)⌉`. | Full odd form includes the polar contribution. |
| Full window to ground | `W_A≥0 ⇒ μ(A)≥0`. | Ground/form realization and test class as defined. |
| Ground to windows | `μ(A)≥0 ⇔ W_A′≥0 for every 0<A′<A`. | Strict-interior approximation and continuity are part of the theorem. Do not erase the endpoint distinction. |
| Odd window to ACT | Implies the corresponding supported shifted Markov sign. | A directional fixed-aperture arrow, not its asserted converse. |
| Full window/ground to EWU | Implies the relevant even blind minimum is nonnegative. | No fixed-aperture equivalence of all blind/constrained cones is asserted. |
| Ground to lag | Nonnegative `μ(v/2+w)` gives the supported two-bump lag sign. | Only that family and normalization. |
| Cofinal/all apertures | Complete full positivity, full odd positivity, global odd Markov sign, ACT completeness and AIP full allocation each have RH endpoints. | Global equivalence does not upgrade the preceding one-way local arrows. |

Source: [TheRoutesMeasureOneAperture](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheRoutesMeasureOneAperture.lean), [SingleShiftCompleteness](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCriticalityTheory/SingleShiftCompleteness.lean), [GlobalIncidenceAllocationCondition](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticIncidencePositivity/GlobalIncidenceAllocationCondition.lean).

The full aperture is certified through `0.3`; the odd aperture through `0.362`. Joint 2/3 strip-cost payment at `0.575` is an ingredient, not a third full positivity radius. APT's positive families have arbitrarily large supports but do not exhaust all tests with a proved uniform norm cost.

### 4.3 Same form, different completions

Suzuki's primitive has a proper dense image in the Weil form domain. The derivative and Weil norms are inequivalent, yet density transfers nonnegativity in both directions. CHMD's graph norm is equivalent to the Weil form norm on the fixed-aperture common core; its quotient coordinate is continuous there. These are compatible facts about different norms and maps. Neither identifies the whole CHMD quotient with the Suzuki completion. [SuzukiScrewCompletionInsideTheWeilFormDomain](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/Suzuki/SuzukiScrewCompletionInsideTheWeilFormDomain.lean); [CHMDGraphNormIsTheWeilFormNormOnAnAperture](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CHMDGraphNormIsTheWeilFormNormOnAnAperture.lean).

This closes a genuine comparison problem. It does not prove the common form nonnegative. A new lower bound for the quotient map or global control of aperture-dependent constants would be another theorem, not a corollary of continuity.

### 4.4 The ground path: contact, rate and index are related but distinct

The ground path `A↦μ(A)` is antitone and continuous. Under RH it is nonnegative and tends to zero. Off RH it has a finite contact aperture, is negative beyond contact and tends to `−∞`. The current positive-window result gives contact at least `0.3`; strict positivity throughout every pre-contact aperture is not asserted by the contact theorem. [TheContactApertureOfTheGroundEnergyPath](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheContactApertureOfTheGroundEnergyPath.lean).

Define the mass-floor rate using eventual bounds `μ(A)≥−Cσ exp(2σA)`. It dominates `Δ`. Equality is proved under a uniform local off-line squared-depth bound, and in particular for finitely many off-line copies. The finite top-layer case has two-sided exponential ground growth. Without that extra control, the plain-mass converse remains open; derivative and logarithmic Fourier floors are available instead. The theorem's dichotomy is rate equality **or** unbounded local squared-depth mass, not an unconditional rate identity. [TheWidthIsTheGroundEnergyRateWhenOffLineZerosDoNotCluster](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheWidthIsTheGroundEnergyRateWhenOffLineZerosDoNotCluster.lean).

ACS adds another exact reading: the supremum of negative-square dimensions of the Weil test Gram forms is the number of distinct right zeros. Equivalently, this is the scaling surface’s positive index in excess of its single pole-plane positive direction. The degree/aperture filtration is monotone and exhausts that count. This is finer than a Boolean sign test, but it does not determine zero heights/depths from one scalar ground value. The ground path is a useful common object, not a proved complete invariant of the entire divisor. [TheHodgeDefectCountsTheRightZeros](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/TheHodgeDefectCountsTheRightZeros.lean); [TheDegreeFiltrationOfTheHodgeDefect](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/TheDegreeFiltrationOfTheHodgeDefect.lean).

### 4.5 Pointwise depth diagnostics

For `dρ=|Reρ−1/2|`, the first-prime seam charge satisfies

`‖charge(ρ)‖ = √2 log 2 sinh((log 2)dρ)`.

The mate gain satisfies `|log gain(n,ρ)|=2dρ log n`. The Hurwitz residual factors as `(2Reρ−1)` times its explicit Mellin factor; in the stated critical strip its zero criterion matches the first-prime seam criterion. Taking suprema recovers width for the proved seam/mate readings. [TheSideIsTheDepthAndTheWidthIsItsSupremum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheSideIsTheDepthAndTheWidthIsItsSupremum.lean).

Thus SEC/HJ and the calibrated radial faces of BSH/CDC are not new independent zero-location invariants. Their **producers** remain distinct. Nor does this theorem automatically provide per-zero depth formulas for APF, EWJ, EGC, RFHM, SC or WBF. Those require their own maps.

## 6. Consequences for organizing research

The shared work units are **source estimates and proved transfers**, not route labels. A new actual small-damping bound should be counted once even when it enters MC, PM and CTF. A complete full-Weil positivity theorem should be counted once across RW/SZ/CHMD/OWU/ACT/AIP/HF/ACS endpoint formulations. Conversely, a local graph-norm comparison and a global positivity producer are different achievements.

The sharp unresolved bridges are:

1. Relative arithmetic alignment → absolute signed receiver control.
2. Restricted positive families/local strip budgets → complete all-profile payment.
3. Width and signed companion geometry → unrestricted mass floor.
4. Finite/aperture/rank observations → the exact cofinal uniformity of a receiver.
5. Arithmetic source data → faithful positive carrier or independent effectivity.
6. Divisor diagnostic → independently forced vanishing/orientation.

The first four are mainly Walls 1 and 3; the last two are Wall 14 with arithmetic estimates still required. A successful proof may cross several at once. No reviewed theorem requires the invention of a new field, and no reorganization of equivalent receivers removes the need for a genuine arithmetic input.
