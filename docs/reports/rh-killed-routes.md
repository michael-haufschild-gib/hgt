### KR-ISG — Prime-Ising and invalid ferromagnetic limits

- **Path:** Balanced prime Ising trees → Lee–Yang Xi limit.
- **Obstruction [L]:** Balanced prime trees and integer-anchored Γ-site chains fail the Xi limit. For every ferromagnetic Ising graph sequence, constant normalization cannot converge to `E·Ξ`: total field must diverge and phase must asymptotically center at half-field. Centred Griffiths–Simon sequences containing harmonic masses `1/(2k)` from any `K≤7` also fail. GHS plus Lebowitz inequalities do not imply Lee–Yang. At a prime Γ-site pivot, nonnegative-fugacity dual-spectrum containment forces active walls into a divisibility chain of at most `⌊log₂(M−1)⌋` members. Each nondividing wall pair yields a nondual two-wall frequency with nonzero coefficient polynomial; signed cancellation lies on a proper algebraic locus.
- **Survivor [L]:** Arbitrary ferromagnetic graphs satisfy Lee–Yang; centred convergence in law, moments or low-frequency real-rooted approximation to `Ξ` implies RH. Slow-field producers require vanishing free variance and interaction-driven magnetization. For nonuniform prime-Ising trees, descendant-cluster mass equals the sum of rootwise nontriviality probabilities and is bounded by vertex count; this finite bound does not construct a centred Xi limit. Field-density and first-zero budgets remain necessary.
- **Needs:** Source-faithful centred critical graph sequence outside harmonic/anchored classes; W1C.
- **E:** R:PrimeIsingTreeBalancedCentering; R:PrimeIsingSubtreeMass; R:LeeYangResolutionLaw; R:LeeYangPrimeStabilityNoGo; R:RiemannSiegelLeeYangChain; R:GammaSiteLeeYangChainXiObstruction; R:LeeYangProducerFieldDensity; R:ComplexFugacityChainFieldDensity; R:LeeYangIsingGraphDatum; R:IsingGraphCenteringLaw; R:IsingHarmonicMagnetizationNoGo; R:IsingSlowFieldCriticality; R:IsingLawConvergenceRH; R:IsingMomentConvergenceRH; R:FerromagneticInequalitiesLeeYangBlind; R:LeeYangPrimePivotSpinPartitionSpectrum.
