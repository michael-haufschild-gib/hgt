# The walls ledger

**Status at 2026-10-22.** The corpus has closed substantial construction, local positivity, observation and compensation problems. It has not supplied an unconditional arithmetic estimate or faithful positive construction closing a reviewed RH route. This ledger records the remaining walls, their exact scope, the positive statements exposed by each obstruction, and the local walls already removed.

Companions: [complete route and programme report](hard-obstacles.md), [routine tasks and research agenda](hard-obstacles-summary.md). Route names cover 30 live routes, 17 standalone candidates and PM's embedded PW-B candidate. The nine programme dossiers supply the detailed theorem boundaries behind this ledger.

## 1. Reading the ledger

The established labels **Wall 1, Wall 3 and Wall 14** are retained. They are organizing families, not a theorem that every obstruction is equivalent to one scalar invariant.

- **Wall 1 — arithmetic estimates:** exact receivers are available; a complete source estimate is missing. Some receivers are RH-equivalent, others only sufficient. Positive-width estimates can be genuinely weaker than RH.
- **Wall 3 — information, norms and shared resources:** a particular observation, restriction or local payment does not imply the complete receiver. These are scoped statements about explicit classes and maps.
- **Wall 14 — source-faithful realization:** a proposed carrier must retain the actual source, divisor, multiplicity, domain and sign. Obstructions to finite-rank or particular ideal-class carriers do not constrain every possible proof of RH.

**Status vocabulary:** **open estimate** = unproved arithmetic target; **proved obstruction** = a counterexample, sharp boundary or classification in the stated class; **resolved locally** = the specified former obstacle is closed, while a stronger transfer remains; **paper-proved** = a complete recorded argument awaiting formal integration; **candidate** = outstanding proof obligations remain.

Evidence follows the [complete report](hard-obstacles.md#1-scope-and-meaning-of-open): tracked Lean declarations [L], working-source declarations [S], paper proofs [P] and unfinished candidates [C]. A theorem implication is not a proof of its premise. The residue-mode result has a separately recorded successful leaf/axiom audit. This review does not claim a new global compiler audit.

## 2. Wall 1 — source estimates at the critical rate

Write `Δ=supρ |Re ρ−1/2|=Θ−1/2`. RH says `Δ=0`. Several surveyed receivers read this width exactly; others detect its vanishing without measuring the same numerical rate.

The admissible damping **accumulation-rate infimum** equals `Δ`, and every `ε>Δ` is admitted. An isolated admitted damping does not automatically establish RH or the same half-plane conclusion as an accumulation family. A weighted ground-floor rate is not automatically the unrestricted mass-floor rate. The HF window excess exponent currently obeys only `Δ≤θ*≤Δ/2+1/4`. Heat time and width share a zero criterion but are not identified as the same number.

### Face 1.1 — critical signed prime and Möbius cancellation

**Open estimate.** Prove a complete source-derived band/packet bound, integrated Chebyshev floor, signed history estimate, regularized-series convergence or extremal divisor inequality at its closing rate.

**Where current estimates stop.** Exact decompositions leave signed off-diagonal, high-LCM, inherited-history, boundary and tail terms. Absolute-value bounds lose the critical cancellation. Typical phases or a fixed modulus do not control the complete deterministic source.

**Resolved locally.** Explicit universal spline construction; fixed-prime Möbius cancellation; centered-prime bias identification; actual-row short-window improvements. The remaining row estimates must keep their modulus/support restrictions, block mass and higher-order tails. [CubicSplineUniversalProbe](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/CubicSplineUniversalProbe.lean); [SingleModulusCancellationOfTheMoebiusBlock](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/SingleModulusCancellationOfTheMoebiusBlock.lean); [DualNormOfTheActualRowsBeatsTheLargeSieve](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/DualNormOfTheActualRowsBeatsTheLargeSieve.lean).

**Positive survivor / next target.** Prove a smaller positive admissible exponent with exact range and constants, or close one critical receiver. Such intermediate exponent progress is meaningful; it is not automatically equivalent to RH.

**Affected routes:** WL, FU, RP, MC, LI, JAS, PBM, RB; exterior EGC and parts of PA/HF. **Research:** O1/O3; APT-1–3, BS-1, BV-2, AI-5–6, SR-1/4–5.

### Face 1.2 — axis orientation from the actual source

**Open estimate/construction.** Prove an all-height logarithmic-derivative/Jensen/heat sign, the source's boundary-work orientation, the complete Ward-cap vanishing, or a pre-divisor law forcing a seam/mate diagnostic to vanish.

**Proved obstruction.** Symmetry, a functional equation, a zero-indexed diagnostic or integer-valued count does not supply its sign/vanishing. Finite parity selectors, a fixed-degree automatic Jensen regime and arbitrary cone-positive compact operators do not force the required full spectrum.

**Positive survivor.** Exact spectral receivers, multiplicity-preserving cap identities, collision criteria and pre-divisor transgression formulations specify the required input. The integral-index conclusion becomes routine after a real sub-unit source bound.

**Affected routes:** LA, TH, RN, WD, XN, TG; DR, HJ, SEC and mate/seam candidates. **Research:** O6/O8/O9.

### Face 1.3 — ground energy and critical positivity

**Open estimate.** Exclude a negative ground at every aperture, or prove the full odd capacity/one-shift variation/global incidence allocation.

**Resolved locally.** Full `0.3` and odd `0.362` floors; a complete one-shift receiver; harmonic radical and quantitative null rates; large restricted APT positive families; completed SZ/RW sign transfer. These are substantial local and structural results. [ThreeTenthsSharpGaugeWeilPositivity](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCriticalityTheory/ThreeTenthsSharpGaugeWeilPositivity.lean); [SchurLendingOddWindowThreeHundredSixtyTwoThousandths](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticIncidencePositivity/SchurLendingOddWindowThreeHundredSixtyTwoThousandths.lean); [SingleShiftCompleteness](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCriticalityTheory/SingleShiftCompleteness.lean); [CompactCriticalPolynomialWindowAndTemperednessBridge](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/CompactCriticalPolynomialWindowAndTemperednessBridge.lean).

**Proved obstruction.** The original radical-containing space admits no positive uniform Hardy improvement. In the specified rapidly decaying class, a nonzero nonnegative folded gauge has the wrong endpoint moments. A cofinal positive lower bound bounded away from zero contradicts the unconditional ground-energy upper ceiling.

**Positive survivor.** Signed endpoint repair, quotient-space coercivity questions with an explicitly chosen norm, finite-product floor transport allowed to decay, and complete allocation retaining cross-nodal terms. The ceiling tending to zero does not prove the ground itself tends to zero; that limit is RH-equivalent. [FoldedSourceOriginJumpMomentLaw](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCriticalityTheory/FoldedSourceOriginJumpMomentLaw.lean); [TheContactApertureOfTheGroundEnergyPath](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheContactApertureOfTheGroundEnergyPath.lean).

**Affected routes:** RW, EWU, OWU, ACT, AIP, CHMD, SZ, HF, TT. **Research:** O4/O5; APT-4, AC-1–6, AI-1–5, CE-5, SR-3.

### Face 1.4 — arithmetic cone and node rigidity

**Proved obstruction.** Full-positive arithmetic weights must equal `Λ`. The full cone is therefore contained in `{Λ}`, equals it under RH, and is empty otherwise. Approximate weights, finite-prefix agreement and an admixture do not provide another member. Full super-residue cones at pole weight `β>1` are empty; the even-only question is different. [WeilConeUniquenessUnconditional](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WeilConeUniquenessUnconditional.lean).

**Resolved locally.** Single-tower blind slack and deletion laws; prime-power node incidence classification; restricted positive-family and midpoint/quarter/collar classifications.

**Open estimate.** Positivity of the literal `Λ` form; full blind polar infimum; exact two-tower and prime-power-only slack; even-sector source control with the required global quantifiers.

**Positive survivor.** Exact rigidity says what a source must preserve. Node positivity requires RH **and** every prime-power node; node density alone is the wrong variable. Non-prime-power nodes can nevertheless affect a different tower extremum. [PrimePowerZeroPoleNodeCriticality](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/PrimePowerZeroPoleNodeCriticality.lean).

**Affected routes:** HF, EWU, OWU, ACT, AIP. **Research:** O4; BS-3 and APT-4–6.

### Face 1.5 — Jensen/contrast equality

**Open estimate.** Force the exact BSY/Jensen/likelihood residual to vanish from the arithmetic source.

**Proved limitation.** Correct KL/entropy accounting, a finite reveal budget and positive individual saddle terms leave the complete contrast. Vanishing individual defects need not give vanishing accumulated work.

**Positive survivor.** Exact zero-sum and adaptive-history identities locate every debt. They give a precise cofinal source target rather than an automatic equality. [BalazardSaiasYorZeroSum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/BalazardSaiasYorZeroSum.lean); [PaperCircleAdaptiveHistory](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PaperCircleAdaptiveHistory.lean).

**Affected routes:** FEL, PA, parts of CDC. **Research:** O9.

### Face 1.6 — correct quantifiers and uncontrolled tails

**Open estimate.** One bound must hold across the ranks, degrees, horizons, heights, phases or cutoffs demanded by the receiver.

**Proved limitation.** `∀rank ∃later time` is not `∃critical time ∀rank`. Strong convergence of finite truncations is not rank-uniform norm decay. A discrete-grid theorem with an attained-top premise is not a theorem for every continuous configuration.

**Resolved locally.** Explicit continuous lag visibility without top attainment, exact finite histories, global finite-cutoff recovery, local recurrence on zero-free discs, complete inner-contour cancellation and positive-damping admission for several sources.

**Positive survivor.** State a quantitative tail/uniformity theorem on the actual source. Uniformity may be unnecessary in some parameters: PM permits a different finite bound for every positive damping. Overstrengthening that quantifier can create a false target.

**Affected routes:** PA, TT, LA, TH, HCF, GTA, WD, JAS, LI, SRT, BAG; APF, DR, EWJ, SEC, WBF, PW-B. **Research:** O6 and the relevant programme-specific tail tasks.

### Face 1.7 — amplification without new arithmetic information

**Proved obstruction.** Taking powers of a scalar error multiplies its admissible exponential rate. An RH-equivalent bound is not weakened merely by writing it as a higher-moment or amplified criterion. [AmplificationMultipliesTheZeroWidthExponent](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/AmplificationMultipliesTheZeroWidthExponent.lean).

**Positive survivor.** Exact exponent bookkeeping. A genuinely new correlation or source estimate can improve a rate; scalar reparameterization alone cannot.

**Open estimate.** Supply the arithmetic input improving the rate, with the complete source and moment normalization retained. **Affected routes:** all amplified versions of Wall 1.1; tensor/Frobenius proposals also meet Wall 14.2.

### Face 1.8 — signed pronic energy below half damping

**Open estimate.** For arbitrarily small positive `ε`, prove `sup_T Eε(T)<∞` for the actual centered Möbius source.

**Proved obstruction.** On the full magnitude-bounded coefficient class, sign-blind admission stops at `ε>1/2`. Actual Möbius admission at `ε=1/2` uses arithmetic. Period variance and period primitive norms do not control short pronic prefixes. Generic Type-II thresholds are class-specific: cross-scale `6/5` and pronic-window `1` use different scale ratios.

**Resolved locally.**

- All sufficiently large-horizon prefix defects have the required sign, uniformly for `ε∈[0,1]`.
- Canonically compensated diagonal and predecessor brackets are bounded for every fixed positive damping.
- The actual-row low-frequency estimates improve the generic leading loss in explicit ranges.
- The shrinking VK damping band is controlled; admission above actual width and its exact accumulation-rate dictionary are proved.

The unpaid object is now sharply specified: the **compensated Bézout interior martingale**, or an equivalent complete signed energy bound. Summation by parts returns that energy with coefficient one and does not create a strict contraction. [RoughPrimeBoundaryUniformDampingDefectSign](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/TransHorizonIncidenceMechanics/RoughPrimeBoundaryUniformDampingDefectSign.lean); [UniformRenormalizedInteriorEnergyRemainder](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/UniformRenormalizedInteriorEnergyRemainder.lean); [AdmissionBelowHalfDampingAboveTheZeroWidth](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/AdmissionBelowHalfDampingAboveTheZeroWidth.lean).

**Positive survivor.** Critical Wiener preservers, exact conditional means, finite Gram/dual formulations and actual-row savings delimit viable estimates. Dual approximation requires both near-unit readout and vanishing centered energy; the `1/log N` lower floor is compatible with convergence to zero.

**Affected routes:** PM, MC, CTF; THIM/BVI support. **Research:** O2/O3; THIM-1–2/6, BV-1–4.

### Face 1.9 — signed continuation and the critical norm

**Newly explicit face.** The exact continuation criterion is

`sup_T ∫_a^c [−2Wε(T)/(1+Eε(T))]dε < ∞`

for an admitted `c`. It is equivalent to admission at `a`. Constants may depend on `a`. The positive part of the integrand is a stronger condition, not this equivalence. [TheDampingAverageOfTheRelativeLossSuffices](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/TheDampingAverageOfTheRelativeLossSuffices.lean); [TheAveragedBudgetIsExactlyAdmission](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/TheAveragedBudgetIsExactlyAdmission.lean).

**Proved obstruction / source-derived exclusion.** Global nonnegative work is false at horizon one near damping zero. Hence its universal core-dominance/Poincaré sufficient premises are unusable unchanged. A positive core or tangent Gram diagonal does not control the signed source/tangent pairing. The prime-harmonic debt proved from zero damping does not establish divergence on every fixed positive interval.

**Resolved locally.** Scalar budget, renormalization, cutoff transport, exact dilation/Dirichlet/Hardy block identities and source inversion. Compact `c₀` dynamics and total residue modes are available; spectrum/core are paper-proved. The entrance-law critical-pronic dual norm has the sharp half-damping threshold; reciprocal weighting gives a different admitted class. [TheDilationHardyInequalityOnThePronicMeasure](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/TheDilationHardyInequalityOnThePronicMeasure.lean); [ResidueModeCompleteness](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/ResidueModeCompleteness.lean); [PronicDualNormOfTheEntranceLaw](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/PronicDualNormOfTheEntranceLaw.lean).

**Positive survivor / next targets.** Signed integrated work, one-sided sublinear inversion pairings, or an actual-source contraction retaining linear cross terms, quadratic covariance, polar forcing and boundary flux. A norm bridge from `c₀` must be proved in the pronic space. No stable basis or bounded spectral projections follow from totality.

**Affected routes:** PM, MC, CTF and zero-damping PW-B; ACCE/BVI. **Research:** O2/O7; CE-1–6, BV-4–5.

## 3. Wall 3 — what a reading, norm or local payment leaves unresolved

### Face 3.1 — position and observation horizon

**Proved obstruction.** A bounded-aperture reading has a Nyquist threshold on the specified configuration classes. Finite-rank heat readers have a height horizon. Periodic finite sieve signals have exact recurrence dimension and polynomial observation laws; mean-square comparison has a sharp quadratic horizon.

**Resolved locally.** Position/depth Nyquist laws, finite relation classifications, exact fifth/sixth recovery and global sixth-root physical stability.

**Positive survivor.** Sharp recovery theorems specify how much data determine the finite signal. Their cost must be carried into a proposed arithmetic application. They do not turn an observation horizon into a bound on the unseen tail. [NyquistResolutionOfTheWeilForm](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/NyquistResolutionOfTheWeilForm.lean); [SievePhysicalTranslationStability](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/SievePhysicalTranslationStability.lean).

**Open questions:** sharper windowed recovery costs, multiple-gap observability, actual-ζ realizable readings, and discrete-grid visibility with the required hypotheses. **Research:** BS-4–6, THIM-5, BV-5, SR-2, SI-1–2.

### Face 3.2 — depth, aperture and support escape

**Proved obstruction.** On the broad tempered-configuration class, every bounded aperture admits positive off-line mimics of any permitted depth. A **true** bounded positivity certificate therefore cannot force zero width on that class. A negative test can refute positivity. These are synthetic countermodels, not off-line zeros of actual ζ.

**Resolved locally.** Unbounded-aperture positivity is complete. Exact lattice tolerance/depth laws and model multi-band inversion are available. APT supplies positivity on much larger restricted scale families than a fixed small aperture.

**Positive survivor.** A source argument may use additional arithmetic structure excluding the mimics. It must identify that additional input, or prove a cofinal family of complete positivity estimates. High-height lower bounds do not supply height cutoffs; restricted large-support positivity does not automatically cover every test.

**Open questions:** complete-family transfer, beyond-horizon phase control and source-realizable finite-precision depth inference. **Research:** APT-4–5, SR-2/4–5, SI-1/4.

### Face 3.3 — cone and norm transfer

**Proved obstruction.** Pointwise source positivity, Toeplitz/translation positivity, Bochner positivity and the reflected Weil/Hankel sign are different conditions. Derivative, logarithmic Fourier, mass, graph and pronic norms are not interchangeable.

**Resolved locally.** The Suzuki primitive is injective with **proper dense** range in the Weil form domain. Norms are inequivalent, but density transfers the sign in both directions. Width gives the stated weighted floors; local squared-depth control gives the sharp mass-floor rate. [SuzukiScrewCompletionInsideTheWeilFormDomain](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/Suzuki/SuzukiScrewCompletionInsideTheWeilFormDomain.lean); [UnsignedDepthSamplingDetectsLocalMass](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/UnsignedDepthSamplingDetectsLocalMass.lean).

**Positive survivor.** Work with the completed form and actual map. A norm-equivalence theorem is unnecessary for the already proved sign transfer; an identification with the entire CHMD quotient is not supplied by it.

**Open questions:** unrestricted signed mass-floor converse, quotient Hardy norm, APT complete-family transfer and `c₀`-to-pronic source control. **Research:** O4/O5/O7; APT-4, AC-3, BV-4, CE-6, SR-3.

### Face 3.4 — local payments share a global budget

**Newly explicit face.** A proof that every individual atom can be paid from its own strip does not prove simultaneous payment when those strips overlap. A gauge redistributes charges; it does not change their full sum.

**Proved obstruction.** Odd-cost-only estimates fail on explicit profiles; one-tower and two-tower resonant trains defeat automatic cross-prime cancellation. The complete cross aggregate is not always nonpositive. An indefinite signed remainder cannot be dropped.

**Resolved locally.** Own microstrips, the `0.575` joint `2/3` cost theorem, successful chain allocations, quarter/collar classifications, and full odd `0.362` positivity. Signed collars conserve autocorrelation charge; half-line one-signed profiles have nonnegative trans-half-width net charge; specified edge bands have exactly zero net charge. [OverlappingPrimeStripsTwentyThreeFortieths](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticIncidencePositivity/OverlappingPrimeStripsTwentyThreeFortieths.lean); [MasterSignChangingScrewGaugeTheorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignChangingScrewGauge/MasterSignChangingScrewGaugeTheorem.lean).

**Positive survivor.** Keep favorable even gain and use one global incidence ledger. Classify adverse sign-changing profiles and prove a dual/shared allocation with the pole and every remainder. The exact global condition is RH-equivalent; naming it is not proving it. [GlobalIncidenceAllocationCondition](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticIncidencePositivity/GlobalIncidenceAllocationCondition.lean).

**Open questions:** AI-1–6, AC-2/4 and THIM-3; O4.

### Face 3.5 — periodic reconstruction does not determine pronic admission

**Newly explicit face.** Finite signal recovery is now solved much more strongly than a magnitude-only model. Yet even complete translated periodic moments can preserve a parity ambiguity that changes pronic receiver admission in the studied squarefree class.

**Proved obstruction.** “Add higher periodic moments until the receiver is determined” fails for that family. Exact signal recovery up to translation need not preserve a prefix functional anchored at the origin.

**Positive survivor.** Pronic/non-periodic observations distinguish information lost by periodic translations; logarithmic signed hierarchies and critical Wiener preservers provide concrete replacement structures. What remains is a quantitative energy estimate, not merely another injectivity theorem.

**Open questions:** THIM-2/5–6, BS-5, SI-3, BV-4. This refines Wall 1.8 and does not assert that every possible arithmetic observation is blind.

## 4. Wall 14 — the carrier must preserve the source and the property that closes the route

### Face 14.1 — size and operator-ideal capacity

**Proved obstruction.** Particular finite-rank, fixed finite passive, trace-class-relative and local carrier proposals have insufficient capacity for the actual divisor or source. Strong convergence does not imply trace-norm or operator-norm convergence. The stated smooth-reference zero-count remainder is not integrable.

**Positive survivor.** Hilbert–Schmidt/regularized determinants, exact singular defects, growing carriers and quantitative counting-capacity laws. The current renewal operator already has a regularized determinant and the correct resonance multiplicities; positivity of its cone alone does not orient the entire spectrum. [ThetaRenewalFredholmSpectrum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ThetaRenewalFredholmSpectrum.lean).

**Open construction:** a source-faithful realization in an adequate topology with an independently proved sign/zero-preserving property. **Affected routes:** HF, RN, TG, GTA; APF, EWJ, PTR, W1C, WBF. **Research:** O6/O7/O8.

### Face 14.2 — integral Frobenius, torsion and orientation

**Proved obstruction.** The specified finite-rank integral-lattice prime actions and torsion/unitarity laws do not admit the desired nonreal divisor representation. Tensoring or amplifying an un-oriented diagnostic does not force its vanishing.

**Positive survivor.** Precise finite-rank exclusions, infinite-rank alternatives, framed curvature and seam diagnostics. No theorem says every RH proof needs a Frobenius fibration or this carrier architecture.

**Open construction:** source-defined arithmetic compatibility/orientation before choosing zeros, with an explicit coupling to the actual divisor. **Affected routes:** HF, BSH, SC, CDC and related diagnostics. **Research:** O7/O8.

### Face 14.3 — completion, defects and noncircular landing

**Newly explicit face.** A representation can exist and still miss the property needed by its receiver. A proper dense map is not a surjection; an inverse-covariant sewing law does not select a real part; an equivalence whose converse is built from the unknown zeros does not construct a pre-divisor model.

**Proved obstructions.** Native PCS shift/co-shift cannot intertwine the whole carrier with invertible chronological dilation. Fixed-gap lossless, local cosine-to-Ξ and generic positive Poisson zero-preservation proposals fail in their stated classes. Finite Lee–Yang real-rootedness does not identify an arithmetic Ξ limit. [PCSChronologicalDilationCarrierObstruction](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PCSChronologicalDilationCarrierObstruction.lean); [PhotonicLosslessProductPortRH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PhotonicLosslessProductPortRH.lean); [IsingLawConvergenceRH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/IsingLawConvergenceRH.lean).

**Resolved locally.** Admitted squarefree critical source; RFHM support/forward creation; exact nonlocal sech/Poisson handoff; proper dense RW/SZ completion; PW-B fixed-positive-damping admission; `c₀` residue-mode completeness.

**Positive survivor / next targets.** Carry the defect and original source through the completion. Construct a gapless/nonlocal port with actual-source zero control, a centered arithmetic Lee–Yang sequence, positive Sonin/Weil transport, or a pre-divisor action-to-receiver map. For damping dynamics, formalize the paper spectrum and strong-continuity/core results, then prove the separate critical-norm estimate.

**Affected candidates:** MSC, PCS, PTR, W1C, RFHM, SC, SEC, IRK, CDC, BSH, WBF, PW-B; HF/RN/CHMD/SZ where the proposed bridge requires it. **Research:** O7/O8 and BV-4/CE-6.
