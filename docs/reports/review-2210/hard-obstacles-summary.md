# RH obstacles: routine work, open mathematics, and a possible research programme

**Status at 2026-10-22:** 30 live routes, 17 standalone candidates, and PM's PW-B sub-branch. None has a remaining closure that the reviewed research reduces to routine work. The corpus has many exact receivers and substantial local estimates; it still lacks the source-derived global sign, cancellation, or faithful construction needed to discharge a receiver.

Companions: [complete route report](hard-obstacles.md), [updated walls ledger](walls-ledger.md). Evidence includes all nine requested research programmes, current proof cards, the six original reports and searched/inspected Lean sources. No outside literature is used. Programme-specific proof scopes and links appear in the complete report.

## 1. What the classifications mean

**Just work** means a task whose method is already available and whose remaining obligation is a controlled derivation, specialization, verification or integration. “The expression is explicit,” “only one inequality remains,” and “the next aperture is finite” do not establish that classification.

**B-RH** means the corpus proves that the proposed closing target implies RH; a stated equivalence is stronger evidence and is identified explicitly. **B-C** means an unresolved construction or estimate in our research. A B-C item is not automatically an internationally open problem: it may be an unsearched connection, a false strengthening, or a question with no completed receiver. With repository-only evidence, “nobody has a solution” is justified here as the status of the RH-strength closing problems, not as a universal novelty claim for every auxiliary lemma.

An implication with a false hypothesis is a valid theorem but an unusable closing strategy. An equivalence to RH is a precise target but not an advance in the arithmetic estimate. A theorem on synthetic configurations or arbitrary coefficient rows constrains that class; it does not prove that the actual ζ source cannot have additional structure.

## 3. Open mathematical obstacles, without duplicate counting

### O1 — Critical signed estimates for the actual prime source

**Target.** Prove one complete source-derived estimate already sufficient for RH: a one-sided universal packet bound at every positive exponential slack; the critical Chebyshev-deficit or linear-band estimate; the full Abel/history floor; the regularized prime-series convergence boundary; or the eventual extremal divisor inequality.

**Why it remains open.** Exact identities express the quantity in prime data, but do not provide the critical signed cancellation. Current unconditional error envelopes retain a positive exponential gap. Off-diagonal, inherited-history and endpoint terms cannot be charged to absolute values at the required scale.

**Strength and scope.** B-RH for the stated closing targets. Positive-width bounds are meaningful weaker targets and must not be dismissed as identical to RH. HF's `e^(L/2)+O(L)` ceiling is sufficient; the corpus does not supply the converse.

**Routes.** WL, FU, RP, PA, LI, JAS, PBM, RB; HF's window branch; EGC's exterior branch.

**What would resolve it.** A bound with its full source, constants, quantifiers and tails, inserted into an existing receiver. Another transform of the same uncontrolled expression does not resolve it.

### O2 — Signed damping continuation and critical pronic energy

Write `Eε(T)≥0` for actual centered Möbius energy and `∂εEε(T)=2Wε(T)`. Set

`Lε(T)=−2Wε(T)/(1+Eε(T)).`

The established identity is

`∫_a^c Lε(T)dε = log(1+Ea(T)) − log(1+Ec(T)).`

For an admitted `c`, a uniform upper bound on this integral for every horizon is equivalent to admission at `a`. Cofinal such `a↓0` is RH-equivalent. [TheAveragedBudgetIsExactlyAdmission](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/TheAveragedBudgetIsExactlyAdmission.lean).

**Missing estimate.** Control the complete signed integral uniformly in `T` while retaining both linear terms, the quadratic covariance, boundary flux and polar forcing. The bound may depend on `a`. There is no need for one bound uniform down to `a=0`.

**What is insufficient.** Fixed-horizon convergence; pointwise `c₀` compactness; a positive core; the ordered zero-damping trace; or a bound on magnitudes alone. A uniform tangent-norm estimate is stronger than the signed-work requirement and is not known to be necessary. Abstract smooth spike models separate pointwise derivative control from admission; they do not settle necessity for the literal Möbius source. The canonical BVI diagonal and predecessor remainders are already bounded, so their compensated interior martingale is a concrete remaining target. The alternative inversion identity exposes two one-sided sublinear pairing targets.

**False strengthening.** `Wε(T)≥0` at every positive damping and horizon fails already in the horizon-one small-damping limit. The corresponding global dilation Poincaré/core-dominance premise cannot be presented as a viable open conjecture without modification. Likewise, the prime-harmonic core divergence proved from damping zero must not be asserted on every fixed positive damping interval.

**Routes.** PM, MC, CTF; BVI/THIM and ACCE are supporting frameworks. **Strength:** B-RH at cofinal zero damping; isolated fixed-damping questions have more nuanced strength.

### O3 — Arithmetic structure beyond generic dispersion losses

**Target.** Turn actual Möbius and centered-prime row cancellation into bounds uniform over the growing moduli, supports and horizons required by complete energy.

**Established footholds.** Fixed-prime Möbius statistics vanish in the stated normalization; the prime row has an explicit nonzero bias requiring centering. Restricted short-window actual-row estimates beat the generic leading large-sieve loss. [SingleModulusCancellationOfTheMoebiusBlock](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/SingleModulusCancellationOfTheMoebiusBlock.lean); [OmittedResidueClassLawOfThePrimeRow](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/OmittedResidueClassLawOfThePrimeRow.lean); [DualNormOfTheActualRowsBeatsTheLargeSieve](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/DualNormOfTheActualRowsBeatsTheLargeSieve.lean).

**Missing mechanism.** Retain low-frequency residue information, conditional means, shared-start covariance, mass and higher-order tails through the growing-parameter limit. A finite packet theorem is not a uniform all-packet theorem.

**Strength.** B-C for specific improved estimates; B-RH when sufficient for the full half-power receiver. In the corpus's short-window criteria, a genuine power saving already corresponds to `Θ<1`, a nontrivial zero-free strip, rather than a routine strengthening.

**Routes.** MC, PM, CTF, with applications to O1. The universal coefficient-class obstruction remains valid alongside the actual-row improvements.

### O4 — Global positivity with one shared arithmetic budget

**Target.** Prove full Weil positivity, odd capacity, fixed-shift variation or the global AIP allocation for every required compact profile.

**Current boundary.** All tests through `A=0.3`; odd tests through `A=0.362`; restricted polar-neutral classes have their own larger windows. None is all-aperture positivity.

**Missing mechanism.** Pay all prime charges and the pole using one conserved surplus. Neighboring strips overlap; sign-changing profiles generate cross-nodal debt. Signed collars can change where a charge appears but preserve the full autocorrelation balance. Favorable strip mass must be retained.

**Positive new information.** For a one-signed odd half-line profile, trans-half-width net charges are nonnegative. Edge-supported profiles have exactly zero net charge on the specified thin band. These constrain possible adverse profiles; they do not exclude all of them. [MasterSignChangingScrewGaugeTheorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignChangingScrewGauge/MasterSignChangingScrewGaugeTheorem.lean).

**Strength and routes.** B-RH for complete positivity/allocation: RW, CHMD, SZ, OWU, ACT, AIP, EWU, HF and TT. B-C for a genuinely larger finite window or a new usable profile decomposition.

### O5 — Width, clustering, normalization and visibility

**Target.** Complete the missing width-to-unrestricted-mass-floor arrow, or find the exact signed replacement for local depth control. Where discrete lag sampling is used, supply its attainment/tail hypothesis.

**Established boundary.** Width controls derivative-normalized and logarithmic Fourier-energy floors. A uniform height-window squared-depth bound is sufficient for the sharp plain-`L²` rate. Uniform unsigned depth sampling is equivalent to that local depth bound. The unconditional counting estimate grows logarithmically with height. [UnsignedDepthSamplingDetectsLocalMass](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/UnsignedDepthSamplingDetectsLocalMass.lean).

**What remains unknown.** Whether signed positive/negative contributions yield the sharp unrestricted floor without the unsigned hypothesis; whether the sufficient depth condition is necessary for that signed floor; and how to control actual high-height near-top configurations in a chosen discrete scheme.

**Strength.** B-C. This dictionary problem is not itself the same assertion as RH: an exact positive-width rate could be proved even if `Δ>0`. Its zero-rate specialization has RH strength.

**Routes.** RW, SRT, HF and localization aspects of LA/XN; SquareRootThreshold, SSIC and the pincer framework.

### O6 — Uniformity across rank, degree, scale and exceptional configurations

**Target.** Obtain one estimate valid over every rank/degree at the same critical parameter, or every relevant block/phase with all tails controlled.

**Current boundary.** Fixed-rank heat positivity, fixed-order eventual moment signs, finite-history jets, typical-phase bounds, finite Schur convergence and restricted-family positivity are proved in their stated regimes.

**Missing mechanism.** A uniform quantitative bound or profile/compactness theorem that controls the escaping rank, source frequency, height, degree or exceptional phase. Merely taking a diagonal sequence can change the target and lose the critical parameter.

**Strength and routes.** B-RH for the complete criteria in PA, TT, LA, TH, HCF, GTA, WD and JAS; B-C for EWJ exhaustion and intermediate compactness statements. This is a family of specific quantifier problems, not a single proved impossibility of taking limits.

### O7 — Faithful positive or spectral realization

**Target.** Construct an arithmetic carrier with the correct Ξ divisor/multiplicities, complete prime−Gamma−polar trace and sufficient positivity or spectral orientation.

**Established boundary.** Several faithful source/determinant constructions exist; others are zero-built. Hilbert–Schmidt regularized determinants, dense domain embeddings and nonlocal ports have genuine positive survivors. Particular finite-rank, trace-class-relative, fixed-depth or whole-quotient completions are obstructed.

**Missing mechanism.** A source-defined positive form or zero-preserving limit in an adequate topology, without inserting the unknown zeros, assuming simplicity, or dropping the defect on completion. Compact cone-positivity alone does not constrain the full spectrum to the desired axis.

**Strength and routes.** B-C for the constructions; B-RH once the stated source-faithful landing and sign are achieved. HF, RN, SZ; MSC, PCS, PTR, W1C, WBF and PW-B.

### O8 — Arithmetic forcing before the divisor is chosen

**Target.** Turn a correct diagnostic at a parameter or zero into a source law that forces the diagnostic's vanishing/orientation.

**Established boundary.** Radial mate characters, dagger confluence, prime-2 seam charge, compact-ideal costs, Fock poles, rotation-algebra invertibility and cross-fibre reconstructions can distinguish line/off-line parameters exactly.

**Missing mechanism.** A prime/Gamma-defined object with a noncircular coupling to actual Ξ zeros and an independently proved conservation, orientation or index bound.

**Strength and routes.** B-C for BSH, CDC, APF, IRK, RFHM, SC and the unfinished source legs of HJ/SEC/DR; B-RH for their exact all-divisor vanishing targets. TG also needs the source residual and sub-unit estimate.

**Necessary distinction.** Eliminating a variable named “zero” is not enough. A construction whose coefficients encode the unknown zero set remains zero-built.

### O9 — Deterministic recurrence and Jensen/contrast equality

**Target.** For BAG, prove the needed self-return on every strict-right disc without assuming its zero-freeness. For FEL/PA, prove the full source contrast balance or exact Jensen equality.

**Established boundary.** Recurrence on already zero-free discs and exact entropy/BSY accounting are available. Nonvanishing-target approximation is separated from approximation to a target with zeros.

**Missing mechanism.** Conditioned deterministic tail control or a sharp source equality that retains exceptional phases and every reveal/spot debt.

**Strength.** B-RH at the full closing statements; B-C for proposed conditioned estimates. Probabilistic accounting is useful but does not force the exact deterministic equality.

## 4. Does this require a new field of mathematics?

**The corpus does not establish that a new field is necessary.** A new theorem using existing analytic number theory, harmonic analysis, spectral theory or operator theory could discharge one receiver. Neither a Frobenius fibration nor a spectral realization is a mandatory form of every RH proof.

There is nevertheless a coherent possible research programme connecting O2, O3, O4, O5 and O7. A useful provisional description is **signed arithmetic energy and incidence theory**. This is a proposal, not an existing field whose foundational theorems have been proved.

Its subject would be the way exact multiplicative arithmetic incidence constrains signed quadratic energies across scale. It would study why actual prime/Möbius sources can exhibit cancellations unavailable to arbitrary coefficient classes, and how those cancellations survive changes of coordinates, regularization and completion.

| **ACCE** | Energy derivatives, signed relative-loss criterion, dilation/inversion identities and finite-product transport. | An a priori integrated-work estimate, or self-improving pairing bound, for the actual source. |
| **SRT** | Width/rate dictionaries, quantitative visibility, weighted-floor and conditional mass-floor laws. | An unrestricted signed norm transfer and source bounds below the current rate. |
| **SSIC** | Explicit readings, fibres, refinements and scoped resolution limits. | A design language for source-realizable readings whose quantitative control survives the receiver limit. |

Three interfaces would determine whether these ingredients become a useful common theory:

1. **Arithmetic cancellation to a norm bound.** What exact identity of the literal Möbius/prime row excludes the generic bad coefficients, and does its gain survive growing moduli, block mass and the higher-order tails? A theorem only about fixed residues or averaged signs does not cross this interface.
2. **Local payment to global conservation.** How do one-strip/one-block gains combine without spending a resource twice or losing cross-nodal terms? A common decomposition must retain the same pole and boundary terms in AIP allocation, ACT variation and ACCE work.
3. **Reconstruction to quantitative control.** Dense residue modes, exact moment recovery and a faithful divisor identify an object. They do not bound it in pronic energy, plain `L²` or a source graph norm. Every useful bridge needs a norm and an explicit parameter-dependent loss.

### Tools worth trying to develop

These are proposed research tools, **not statements asserted to exist**.

| Proposed tool | Concrete input and desired output | How it could fail / success test |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic allocation duality | A finite signed incidence ledger with capacity, overlap, gain and pole data; output a complete primal allocation or a dual adverse profile. | Finite duality alone does not give a cofinal certificate. Success requires uniform losses and a controlled limit for the actual arithmetic graph. |
| Covariance continuation estimate | Actual source/tangent pair and boundary forcing; output a horizon-uniform bound for `∫−2W/(1+E)`. | Discarding negative work changes the target. A positive-core estimate can miss both linear cross terms. |
| Arithmetic profile decomposition | A sequence of nearly adverse tests/rows; output classified concentration, modulation, sign-change and scale-escape profiles with controlled energy splitting. | Compactness can fail at origin, height, aperture or modulus. Success must retain the signed cross terms needed to exclude each profile. |
| Graded functional calculus | Damping residue modes plus explicit embeddings among `c₀`, pronic energy and form domains. | Dense eigenmodes need not be a stable basis; a compact `c₀` operator need not be bounded in the critical norm. Success specifies admissible source class and every loss. |
| Signed sampling/observability inequalities | Actual prime-power relations or depth data; output an energy bound at a stated horizon and precision. | Injectivity, all-orders periodic moments and synthetic two-band inversion can each leave the receiver uncontrolled. Success identifies the missing non-periodic information and its cost. |
| Source-faithful completion with defect accounting | Arithmetic finite objects, their normalization, singular domains and defect spaces; output a limit preserving the divisor and the sign-producing property. | Strong convergence, positive kernels or pointwise reconstruction can lose trace, covariance or orientation. Success proves exactly what the topology preserves. |
| Boundary-sensitive rigidity | A source law valid before selecting a zero; output the vanishing/orientation of a known seam, mate or index diagnostic on the actual divisor. | A diagnostic already indexed by zeros can merely restate RH. Success gives an independent arithmetic coupling and estimate. |

The language should therefore carry a **source, domain, norm, observation, signed ledger, parameter range and limit order** in each theorem. Useful new invariants might measure unpaid overlap, integrated covariance loss, projection instability or the non-periodic information missing from a periodic reading. None should be declared an invariant of the actual source until its independence and transport laws are proved.

“Signed” is substantive: positive and negative pieces are not replaced by separate magnitudes before the arithmetic relation is used. “Incidence” means the exact relations between prime powers, divisors, common factors and translated supports; a density statistic alone need not retain them.

### Mechanisms the programme would seek

1. **Coercivity on the actual source class.** A theorem that an indefinite generic form is controlled on rows produced by a specific arithmetic law. It must identify the law, not assume the desired norm bound in another notation.
2. **Conserved allocation across overlaps.** A dual certificate or profile decomposition that combines local strip gains without spending the same surplus twice. Signed gauges would be coordinate choices whose conservation identities are enforced.
3. **Continuation with integrated loss.** A priori bounds for the signed relative-loss integral. This targets the exact admission criterion and permits local negative work, avoiding the refuted global-monotonicity strengthening.
4. **Signed sampling in the correct norm.** Replace an excessive unsigned local-density hypothesis by a theorem about the cancellation in the complete form, or prove precisely that such replacement is impossible in a stated class.
5. **Completion with a recorded defect.** Carry source norms, boundary flux and compact-ideal gaps through limits. A topology change must have an explicit theorem saying what it preserves and what it forgets.
6. **Arithmetic profile compactness.** Describe all ways a putative negative or unbounded source can escape: sign changes, concentration, vertical modulation, growing rank, origin mass or moving cutoff. Exclude those profiles using arithmetic input rather than discarding them.

Each mechanism is a research question. None is supplied merely by naming a framework, defining a norm, or proving a universal algebraic identity.

### Criteria for a useful theory

- **Source fidelity.** Inputs are actual primes, Möbius weights or theta/Gamma source data. Unknown zeros are not inserted into the construction whose purpose is to constrain them.
- **Exact target.** State which receiver is discharged and in which direction the connecting theorem runs.
- **Complete accounting.** Retain mean, diagonal, mixed covariance, polar and endpoint terms; name every remainder and the norm that pays it.
- **Correct quantifiers.** Bounds have the horizon/rank/aperture uniformity actually needed. Fixed-damping constants may depend on damping where the receiver permits it.
- **Arithmetic sensitivity.** Explain why a counterexample in the claimed class is excluded. Prime-power deletion, parity twins and shifted configurations are relevant tests when the mechanism uses those readings; they are not universal axioms for every possible proof.
- **Honest norm transfer.** Weighted energy is not called mass; strong convergence is not called norm convergence; proper dense range is not called surjectivity.
- **Measured progress.** Report an improved actual aperture, exponent, admissible damping range, all-rank bound or source-faithful limit. A new identity can be valuable infrastructure without being a smaller RH obstruction.
- **Positive survivors.** A no-go result should state the surviving sharp boundary, replacement hypothesis, decomposition or invariant.

### Questions it must answer

1. Which sign-changing odd profiles can create net unpaid prime demand after all favorable gains are restored?
2. Can those profiles be reduced to a controlled family without a norm loss that grows faster than the available arithmetic budget?
3. What signed arithmetic estimate bounds the averaged damping loss uniformly in horizon?
4. Which residue observable gives actual-row cancellation uniformly over the growing moduli and tails required by that estimate?
5. Does the signed Weil floor have sharp width rate in unrestricted `L²` without uniform unsigned depth mass?
6. Which topology preserves both the full source normalization and the sign needed by a carrier construction?
7. Can a pre-divisor conservation or orientation law force the already known seam/mate diagnostics to vanish?
8. What mechanism supplies the all-rank/all-height uniformity absent from fixed-rank and typical-case theorems?

### Falsifiable intermediate objectives

| Objective | Value of success | What it would not yet establish |
|---|---|---|
| Larger all-test positivity window with a complete certificate | Pays a currently missing local regime and tests allocation methods. | All-aperture positivity or RH. |
| Classification of adverse sign-changing strip profiles | Identifies where local gain must be shared and where a gauge cannot help. | A global shared budget. |
| Growing-modulus actual-row bound with all tails explicit | Goes beyond fixed-modulus cancellation and generic-class bounds. | RH unless its rate and range meet the receiver. |
| Horizon-uniform averaged continuation to a specified smaller damping | Extends actual admission and tests O2 directly. | Cofinal zero damping; an isolated damping is not RH. |
| Sharp mass-only ground-floor/width theorem | Completes a genuine dictionary gap. | `Δ=0`. |
| Source-faithful nonlocal carrier or completion | Removes a construction obstacle in a candidate. | The spectral/energy sign, unless separately proved. |
| Uniform all-degree source sign at a critical parameter | Discharges the corresponding moment receiver. | This is already RH-strength, not a preliminary routine milestone. |

A new field would become warranted by reusable theorems solving several of these problems with common objects and mechanisms. The present evidence warrants a research programme and a precise obstacle list; it does not warrant announcing that such a field already exists or that RH cannot be solved without it.

## 5. Present decision boundary

The strongest useful next result is a new **source estimate** or a **faithful construction with a proved sign-producing property**. The choice need not solve all O1–O9: one complete receiver suffices. Shared estimates should be counted once even when they improve several routes.
