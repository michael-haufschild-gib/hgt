# RH: where a new mechanism could enter

**Assessment:** the best-supported opportunity is an arithmetic estimate for the *joint* successor-shell, Gamma and pole expression, measured relative to its completed conductor energies. The more ambitious opportunity is a source-side bound for a non-holomorphic reflection defect. Neither is presently proved. I do not see a defensible, completely unrelated RH route that avoids every identified difficulty.

This report uses the six requested documents and Lean-corpus searches, following the 58 programme entries in their listed order, including repeated programmes. Linked programme READMEs were not opened. Relevant Lean statements were inspected; no builds, web searches or dependency audits were performed. “Established” retains the ledgers’ evidence scope, including the imported Zeta23 basis of the census results. Proposals below are research targets, not new theorems or claims of established novelty. Research record: [log](../../../logs/astra-rh-research-2026-10-30.txt).

## What the programme sequence has established

Read oldest to newest, the programmes show a mathematical progression:

| Stage | Main acquisition | Difficulty that survived |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic positivity, uncertainty, trans-horizon incidence, BVI and criticality | Exact source domains, half-damping thresholds, signed covariance and variation criteria | Admission and boundedness do not supply the critical sign |
| Incidence, compensation, orientation and local divisor geometry | Allocation, depth/rate dictionaries, geometric index and heat detectors | A detector amplifies an exception; it does not bound its arithmetic source |
| Successor arithmetic, defect rigidity, biarithmetic geometry, positive duality and correlation | Source selection, escape classification, affine positivity and gauge distinctions | Almost-everywhere cancellation does not select the specified arithmetic point |
| Polarization, effectivity, conjugate diagonal and electrostatics | Same-point versus Weil forms; exact defect, work and effectivity targets | Reconstructing a positive defect does not make it vanish |
| Intersection, renormalized potential, spacing-scale and Lorentzian programmes | Ramanujan spectrum, arithmetic sections and positivity below spacing | Local or separated structures leave signed interactions unpaid |
| Census, index descent, signed Hodge, counting positivity and Krein–Langer theory | Quantitative census certificates, complete-index receivers and selective finite-scale tests | Global index control and displacement-sensitive arithmetic estimates |
| Square-root precision, critical correlations, completed forms, gauge and harmonic analysis | Sharp producer costs, complete fixed-overlap positivity, conductor floors and precise remainder targets | Uniform control of growing conductor families at a complete resolution |
| Hilbertian explicit formulas, successor shells and resonance theory | A precise obstruction to holomorphic same-point substitution; exact lattice repayment; constraints on prime dynamics | A nonlinear source estimate or signed multiscale identity |

The strongest advance is therefore **not another RH equivalence**. It is that the complete fixed-overlap operator has an explicitly positive arithmetic spectrum. That gives a concrete object whose failure of uniform control can be studied.

The [unification ledger](route-unification-ledger.md) correctly concentrates attention on critical sign, square-root precision and arithmetic selection. Two qualifications matter:

- These are proved endpoint identifications and a useful classification of this corpus. They are not a theorem classifying every possible proof of RH.
- A vanishing positivity margin does not forbid estimates. It forbids spending a fixed absolute error against that margin. A sufficiently strong *relative* estimate can still work. Likewise, countermodels show that specified inputs are insufficient; they do not prove that every future proof must use one prescribed extra input.

## 1. Opportunities to cross a wall

### A. Turn conductorwise lattice repayment into a complete relative estimate

For a profile \(\phi\), write \(M=\int\phi^2\). The established completed energies are

\[
E_m(c)=\frac{2}{M\varphi(m)}
\sum_{\substack{k\ge1\\(k,m)=1}}\frac{m}{k}
\int_{kc/m}^{\infty}|\widehat\phi(2\pi t)|^2\,dt\ge0.
\]

This is a substantial mechanism: Möbius signs collapse to a coprimality indicator after the discrete shells are assembled. The shells repay a logarithmic loss that continuum averaging fails to repay. See [TheShellsRepayEveryConductor](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RenormalizedArithmeticPotentialTheory/TheShellsRepayEveryConductor.lean).

**Proposed extension:** perform that cancellation before estimating arbitrary vectors, retaining the pole and Gamma terms throughout. Seek a source-defined positive reference form and control the complete discrepancy relative to that form. Do not estimate shell size and polar size separately.

Three concrete questions make this proposal testable:

1. Can nonpositive complete-Weil vectors satisfy a defect bound \(D_N(x)\le C\log(2+c)\), instead of the current \(Cc^{16}\), for unit \(x\)?
2. Can the resulting conductor cutoff and approximation constants be made effective simultaneously in conductor and overlap?
3. Can the full complementary block and mixed Schur debit be paid in the same relative energy?

The first question is appropriately restricted to vectors with nonpositive *complete* energy. The proved exponential cutoff obstruction concerns all vectors of bounded prime defect; it does not rule out stronger confinement on this smaller set. A logarithmic defect bound would make an exponential-in-defect cutoff polynomial in overlap.

**What would count as the mechanism:** a joint discrete identity, or an inequality preserving its signed cancellation, that controls the actual Schur complement. Positivity of individual conductor packets is insufficient. A model factorization without the physical frame Gram and complementary space is insufficient.

**What this does not yet buy:** even logarithmic confinement would not automatically close RH. The tilted-spline packet residual still carries the \(c^{19}/\log N\) cost, whereas its familiar receiver requires \(c^4/\log N\to\infty\). Both the confinement and the relative error must improve. The Steklov profile has a much smaller completeness schedule, but its floor and error estimates must be proved for that same profile; it cannot borrow the spline estimates.

This is the best-supported way around \(\Sigma1a\)–\(\Sigma1b\). It uses information deliberately discarded by the failed separate-payment estimates. The sharp separate-shell lower-bound work remains at the ledgers’ [S] status; the recommendation does not depend on promoting it.

### B. Count dangerous directions instead of demanding a uniform gap

There is a second possible use of the completed spectrum: seek a quantitative bound on the *number* of directions that lose positivity.

For a finite complete matrix, a proposed source-defined positive reference \(A_N\) would give

\[
W_N=A_N^{1/2}(I-K_N)A_N^{1/2}.
\]

Negative directions correspond to eigenvalues of \(K_N\) exceeding \(1\). A uniform arithmetic bound on \(\operatorname{Tr}(K_N)_+\), along a family known to exhaust the full index, would bound that index. A bound below \(2\) would allow at most one negative direction; the actual ζ index is even, so it would force zero.

These are spectral implications, **not a supplied trace estimate**. Birman–Schwinger inertia is already in the corpus: [restricted-Weil inertia](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PaperRestrictedWeilInertia.lean). The proposed research is a uniform, source-derived bound for a suitably compensated relative operator.

Two obstacles must remain visible. First, the conductor inverse is compact but its inverse-power sums diverge: a trace bound cannot come from summing the bare Green eigenvalues. Second, finite index is already a major theorem: it implies finitely many off-line zeros and Lindelöf. See [the finite-index stratum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HeckeKreinToeplitz/TheFiniteIndexStratumOfZetaIsLindelofDeep.lean).

This changes the estimate being sought; it is not a new route independent of the index programme.

### C. Obtain arithmetic control of the non-holomorphic defect

The distinction between the same-point form \(S\) and the reflection-paired Weil form \(W\) is essential. Their nonnegative difference detects off-line support. A line-calibrated holomorphic explicit formula computes \(W\); it cannot be substituted for \(S\). The relevant result is [TheExplicitImageIsTheWeilForm](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HilbertianExplicitFormulas/TheExplicitImageIsTheWeilForm.lean).

The finite-configuration resolvent identity gives a particularly concrete opening. With \(u=\Re s-\tfrac12>0\), \(d_\rho=\Re\rho-\tfrac12\), and \(\rho^*=1-\overline\rho\),

\[
uS(s)=E(s)+D(s),\qquad
D(s)=\sum_\rho
\frac{2u\,m_\rho d_\rho^2}{|s-\rho|^2|s-\rho^*|^2}\ge0.
\]

The prime side supplies \(E\) in its convergence region. Finite theta readings supply a route to same-point data. **The missing input is an upper estimate for their difference, derived from their common arithmetic source.** Positivity of either reading, or accurate reconstruction of both, does not provide it.

A plausible programme would compare finite theta mean squares with the complete prime trace *before* either limit, retaining mixed terms and boundary terms. Its first useful output should be a bound uniform in the moving parameters that detect shallow, high zeros. Another fixed-window identity would not cross the wall.

The [resolvent formula](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HilbertianExplicitFormulas/TheResolventHilbertianExplicitFormula.lean) also exposes a necessary distinction: pointwise absolute convergence on the full divisor does not justify a finite integral over the whole vertical line. The latter reads an unweighted sum of depth moments and may diverge. Height cutoffs and their tails are part of the mathematics.

## 2. A wildly different, wall-free RH approach?

**None that I can currently justify.** The corpus already covers most concrete ways I would otherwise propose: spectral realization, arithmetic Hodge theory, heat flow, Markov ground states, recurrence, integrality, affine states, nonlinear defects and prime dynamics.

For example, replacing the present operators by a dynamical system is not automatically independent. The newest resonance work distinguishes two explicit constructions: disjoint prime loops reproduce ζ but encounter a nuclearity boundary; the free prime shift has dynamics but produces the wrong zeta function and a pole above \(1\). See [TheTwoRealizationsOfThePrimes](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticResonanceTheory/TheTwoRealizationsOfThePrimes.lean). A faithful, independently controlled realization would be new mathematics, but it belongs to an existing programme.

Nor does “RH implies Weil positivity” mean every future proof must estimate Weil matrices. A genuinely different proof could establish a ζ-specific structural theorem and imply positivity only afterwards. The ledgers do not exclude that possibility.

I would recognize a genuinely new route only after it supplies all three:

- an arithmetic object constructed independently of the unknown zeros;
- a restrictive theorem about that object, proved without importing an RH-equivalent positivity or continuation assumption;
- a transparent implication excluding off-line zeros.

At present I see opportunities for new **producer theorems** inside the existing framework, rather than evidence for a wholly separate mechanism. Giving a new name to an unconstructed arithmetic object would not answer this question.

## 3. Improving Quasi-RH beyond the \(1/8,7/8\) strip

**There are precise improvement targets, but no established improvement follows from the material read.**

First, the [frontier map](../rh-current-frontier-map.md#external-literature) labels the external \(7/8\) Dirichlet zero-free claim [E]. Local density and detector components are established; the independent continuation argument remains open there. I therefore treat \(7/8\) as a proposed benchmark, not an unconditional local starting theorem.

The heat-flow statement is different again: [newmanConstant_lt_eighth](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DeBruijnNewmanStrictBound.lean) concerns a heat parameter; its docstring identifies the corresponding standard normalization as \(<1/2\). It does not assert a spatial zero strip.

### Detector improvement: \(23/27\), then \(5/6\)

The inspected [detector tradeoff](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/QuasiRHDetectorGramTradeoff.lean) retains the Gram profile

\[
1+P^\nu+\frac{P^\kappa}{Y}.
\]

Its exact budget calculations identify where an improvement would have to come from:

| Proposed boundary | Established budget statement | Missing arithmetic input |
|---|---|---|
| \(23/27\approx0.85185<7/8\) | Removing the exceptional-frequency growth while retaining \(P^2/Y\) gives sharp model minimum \(23/27\), at \(x=5/18\), slot length \(1/6\) | Realize that improved Gram bound and complete the zero-exclusion argument |
| \(5/6\) | Feasible exactly when \(\nu\le1/15\) and \(\kappa+4\nu\le4/3\), for \(\nu\ge0\) | Improve ordinary and exceptional contributions jointly |
| \(3/4\) | Impossible by changing those two exponents alone: the retained unit/reflection allowance has floor \(29/36>3/4\) | Change the allowance, normalization or detector mechanism |

There is an explicit \(5/6\) budget witness:

\[
(x,\text{slots},\nu,\kappa)
=\left(\frac5{48},\frac16,\frac1{30},\frac65\right).
\]

This makes \(5/6\) a concrete target rather than an invitation to optimize unspecified exponents. It does not prove the required Gram estimate. Even deleting exceptional frequencies entirely cannot reach \(5/6\) while the ordinary exponent remains \(2\).

### Arithmetic remainder improvement: a direct target for ζ

The cyclotomic completion isolates a nonnegative remainder \(R_N\) with

\[
R_N\le4E_N,\qquad E_N\le60N+\frac13R_N.
\]

Consequently the corpus proves the exact equivalences

\[
\begin{aligned}
\tfrac16\le\Re\rho\le\tfrac56\quad\text{for every ξ zero}
&\iff R_N=O_\varepsilon(N^{5/3+\varepsilon})
 \quad\text{for every }\varepsilon>0,\\
\tfrac14\le\Re\rho\le\tfrac34\quad\text{for every ξ zero}
&\iff R_N=O_\varepsilon(N^{3/2+\varepsilon})
 \quad\text{for every }\varepsilon>0.
\end{aligned}
\]

See [ChebyshevCyclotomicCompletion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CompletedArithmeticForms/ChebyshevCyclotomicCompletion.lean). These are closed-strip statements, not strict exclusion of their boundary lines.

**My preference for ζ:** study the actual remainder’s arithmetic structure, with \(5/3+\varepsilon\) as the first target. The explicit coefficient channels already cost only \(O(N)\); the superlinear difficulty is isolated. A proof must supply cancellation or another upper-bound mechanism for the remainder, not merely use its nonnegativity.

The source-orbit alternative is equally precise: for a suitable generic test, growth \(O(e^{\sigma t})\) is equivalent to zero width at most \(\sigma\). Thus \(5/6\) and \(3/4\) require rates \(1/3\) and \(1/4\). The [orbit-rate theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticOrientationGeometry/TheOrientationRateIsTheDepthOfTheDeepestSeenZero.lean) supplies the amplifier, not its arithmetic bound.

Improving zero-density exponents alone cannot exclude one exceptional zero. Finite index would give some fixed strip, but does not by itself locate the remaining finite exceptions inside \(5/6\) or \(3/4\). Uniform Dirichlet versions also require their own conductor and exceptional-real-zero inputs.

## 4. At least 75% on the critical line

**Yes: there is a credible, sharply specified research opportunity. There is no completed proof in the results inspected.**

The established local baseline is

\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_{\mathrm{simple,\ critical}}(T)}{N(T)}
\ge0.67250071,
\]

with the Zeta23 dependency scope recorded in the frontier map. The exact declaration is [simple_critical_proportion_ge_67250071](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/IncommensurableGaborPinching/CriticalLineZeroProportion.lean).

**Preferred target: the signed coalescent surplus at bandwidth \(4/3\).** The actual certificate is

\[
N_0^*(T,2T)\ge
\left(\frac{29}{36}-32\pi\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon\right)N(T,2T)
-\mathcal S_{u,\eta}(T).
\]

The remaining surplus is the normalized prime-pair expression on
\(0<|\log(n/m)|\le T^{-1+\eta}\), with the prescribed window. Since
\(29/36-1/18=3/4\), a bound

\[
\mathcal S_{u,\eta}(T)\le
\left(\frac1{18}+o(1)\right)N(T,2T)
\]

for admissible profiles with arbitrarily small collars would give asymptotically at least 75% simple critical zeros. The profile choices and limits must match the [proved certificate](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/TransThresholdPairCorrelation/SmoothWeightedCertificateAtFourThirds.lean).

This is more focused than proving a complete prime-pair conjecture: only the required signed, weighted aggregate needs control. Its cancellation is essential. A one-sided prime-pair majorant does not retain the main-term cancellation needed at this threshold.

Other possibilities remain:

| Approach | Required advance | Limitation of current evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Higher signed Gram moments | Actual census-normalized moments near bandwidth \(1\), including the fourth moment and column bound | The \(16/21\) model certificate is not a ζ census theorem; existing ζ moments have smaller frequency support |
| Displacement credit | A census-scale lower bound for projected imaginary evaluation energy | Projection can erase displacement; nonzero depth alone supplies no credit |
| Collision charge | Arithmetic control \(\mathcal O_T\le(1/4+o(1))N(T)\), with matching multiplicities and windows | The quantum and census implication are proved; the arithmetic upper bound is missing |
| Exclude the near-line alternative | Bound simple off-line zeros in the shrinking strip below the dichotomy’s compulsory mass | An upper bound for all nearby zeros cannot distinguish critical from off-line zeros |

The last option is especially precise. The [three-quarters dichotomy](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/Zeta23SimpleOffLineNearLine.lean) says that failure of 75% in a large dyadic window forces at least
\((HD(1)-1/2-1/A-\varepsilon)N\) simple off-line zeros within \(A/\log T\). At \(A=10\), this is approximately \(0.0725007N\). An arithmetic upper bound strictly below that would eliminate the second branch.

**The crucial issue is arbitrarily shallow displacement.** Small second or fourth depth moments do not bound the number of off-line zeros: many zeros can have extremely small nonzero depth. Any purported 75% argument must pay for this possibility. Likewise, 75% *near* the line is not 75% *on* it; the \(3/4\) strip target and the gauge-exponent \(3/4\) obstruction are separate statements.

## 5. The three questions to prioritize

### 1. Can exact lattice repayment control the full low-energy space at growing overlap?

Prove a joint shell–Gamma–pole estimate with effective conductor dependence, including the mixed Schur block. The first substantial target is complete-energy confinement substantially stronger than \(D\ll c^{16}\); the decisive target is a relative bound on a *matched, RH-complete* family.

**Why first:** this extends an actual positive arithmetic identity already proved for every fixed conductor and every fixed overlap. It directly addresses the most developed route’s missing estimate.

### 2. Can the actual arithmetic source bound a non-holomorphic reflection defect uniformly as resolution increases?

Seek a theta/prime comparison retaining same-point information, or a compensated relative operator whose dangerous spectral mass has a uniform arithmetic bound. A finite global index bound would already prove Lindelöf and eventual criticality; a subquantum bound would prove RH.

**Why second:** this targets information that linear holomorphic explicit formulas cannot supply. It could change the available proof mechanisms, rather than improve constants inside a restricted certificate.

### 3. Can the actual signed coalescent surplus at bandwidth \(4/3\) be bounded by \(N/18+o(N)\)?

Preserve the exact window, main-term subtraction and multiplicity normalization. Seek a weighted cancellation theorem, not an absolute prime-pair upper estimate.

**Why third:** it has a numerical success criterion, yields a major 75% result, and demands a real arithmetic producer beyond current near-diagonal control. A successful signed-correlation technique could also inform the Quasi-RH remainder problem, but no transfer between these different forms should be assumed.

These questions should outrank further receiver equivalences, isolated fixed-rank certificates and additional fixed-conductor examples unless those developments establish a uniform estimate needed here.

## 6. What new field could supply the missing tools?

**Working name: arithmetic defect analysis.** Its purpose would be to control the loss of positivity, arithmetic specificity and exact zero detection under simultaneous limits. A new label is not enough: the field would become substantive only through a new compactness, compensation or rigidity theorem for actual arithmetic sources.

### Point of view and central questions

The primary object is the interaction of addition and multiplication at a critical scale. Addition enters through theta/Poisson summation and successor shifts; multiplication enters through prime powers, Möbius inversion and Hecke actions. Their comparison must retain the discrete relation \(n=km+h\), including the signed contribution of \(h\ne0\).

The fundamental questions would be:

- When does a positive limiting arithmetic spectrum imply positivity or bounded negative index for the finite source that realizes it?
- Which escape mechanisms can carry negative energy: large conductor, large height, very shallow depth, or drifting gauge?
- Which joint identities prevent those mechanisms for ζ while allowing them for its twins?
- Can additive and multiplicative readings determine the mass exactly on a symmetry locus, rather than only moments of distance from it?
- What replaces finite genus as an independently bounded arithmetic quantity?

### Objects and language

The theory would work with **pointed arithmetic sources**: coefficients and their distinguished gauge, together with additive and multiplicative actions, complete Gamma/pole terms, test spaces and explicit scale maps. The point must remain marked; Haar averaging cannot stand in for evaluation there.

Its analytic objects would include:

| Object | Role |
|---|---|
| Complete quadratic forms and their relative Schur complements | Preserve signed compensation and detect negative directions |
| Conductor- and height-resolved defect measures | Record where mass escapes when finite observations converge |
| Same-point/reflection-paired form pairs | Retain the non-holomorphic information absent from a linear explicit formula |
| Boundary and renormalization cocycles | Track costs created by changing horizons or transporting sources |
| Multiplicity-weighted depth measures and integer collision charges | Separate approximate localization from exact criticality |

For illustration, on zeros with \(0<\Im\rho\le T\), define the finite measure

\[
\nu_T=\sum_\rho m_\rho\,\delta_{|\Re\rho-1/2|}.
\]

This separates three genuinely different goals:

- a fixed strip bounds the support of every \(\nu_T\);
- 75% on-line bounds the atom \(\nu_T(\{0\})/N(T)\);
- RH puts the entire measure at \(0\).

The HEF resolvent already reads positive depth moments. A useful proposed observable for exact census is

\[
C_T(\epsilon)=\int\frac{d^2}{d^2+\epsilon^2}\,d\nu_T(d).
\]

For a fixed finite window, its limit as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) is off-line mass. The kernel is bounded and detects every nonzero depth eventually. **The research problem is an arithmetic upper bound uniform in the required resolution**, not the elementary zero-side definition. This is not asserted to be a new formalized invariant or an available explicit formula. In particular, taking \(T\to\infty\) at fixed \(\epsilon\) may lose precisely the shallow off-line mass being sought.

### Foundational principles

1. **Source before spectrum.** Constructions start from integer arithmetic and theta/prime data. Unknown zero locations may diagnose them, but may not define the input whose positivity is claimed.
2. **Complete signed compensation.** Prime, Gamma, polar, mixed and boundary terms travel together. A renormalization must identify its counterterm and its effect on the original form.
3. **Relative control at criticality.** Errors are measured in the actual shrinking energy, or through a justified index bound. No fixed gap is assumed.
4. **Explicit order of limits.** Height, overlap, conductor, rank, gauge and regulator limits have named uniformity requirements. Escape mass is recorded, not discarded.
5. **Arithmetic selection.** Theorems must distinguish the actual marked source from admissible countermodels using proved source structure. Finite-coordinate agreement and typical-gauge behaviour are insufficient.
6. **Covariance with defects.** Exact simultaneous successor/dilation invariance collapses to pole/Haar forms in the corpus. Arithmetic information must therefore survive in controlled defects or cocycles, not be erased by imposing excessive invariance.
7. **Purity is a conclusion.** Positivity, effectivity, finite index and arithmetic adjoints cannot be installed as axioms when they already encode the desired result.

The starting laws are ordinary arithmetic identities—Möbius inversion, \(\Lambda*1=\log\), integer incidence, theta/Poisson identities and the complete explicit formula. The missing foundational theorem would explain why their *joint constraints* control defect at a critical limit.

### Research topics and criteria for success

The first research topics should be:

- **Arithmetic concentration compactness:** classify limits of normalized nonpositive complete-Weil vectors while retaining conductor, horizon and gauge escape.
- **Joint lattice compensation:** extend the conductorwise coprimality/Fourier-tail identity to growing mixed families.
- **Relative spectral ideals:** identify compensated operators whose dangerous spectral mass is summable although bare conductor inverse powers are not.
- **Non-holomorphic arithmetic comparison:** derive source estimates for same-point quantities, with height boundaries and shallow-depth resolution retained.
- **Signed correlation estimates:** prove the particular weighted cancellation needed for the cyclotomic remainder or the \(4/3\) census surplus.
- **Infinite-genus purity:** find a source-bounded relative invariant that plays the role served by genus and Rosati positivity over finite fields.

A proposed general theorem should be tested against the actual finite-field calibration, golden impure data, remote prime flips, shallow coalescing pairs and escaping conductor sequences. It must allow the known countermodels where their hypotheses apply. It must also recover fixed-overlap positivity without erasing the discrete gain that makes that theorem true.

**The decisive first product would be one arithmetic estimate unavailable today:** a complete growing-family relative bound, a uniform source-derived index ceiling, the \(5/6\) remainder bound, or the \(N/18\) signed-surplus bound. That would justify speaking of new tools. Another collection of equivalent RH formulations would not.
