# RH proof routes

**RH is open. 58 live mechanisms · 12 candidates.** [Live routes](#live-routes) · [Candidates](#route-candidates) · [Killed mechanisms and scoped obstructions](rh-killed-routes.md)

**Counting rule.** A live route has a defined arithmetic object, a specific construction or estimate to try, and a Lean theorem showing why success for that object implies RH. A candidate is missing the object, its identification with ζ, or the implication from its own data to an RH criterion. A conditional implication is not a proof of its premise. Count the proposed mathematical operation, not the module, equivalent criterion, test parity, or number of proved lemmas.

<a id="find-a-route"></a><a id="shared-frontier"></a><a id="finite-height"></a><a id="external-literature"></a>
**Common baseline.** In this corpus the [strict strip](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/SubSevenEighthsZeroFreeHalfPlane.lean) puts the supremum of zero real parts at most 20999/24000. It does not give half-power cancellation. The [3/5 barrier](rh-killed-routes.md#cubic-tower-half-plane-barrier) limits a class of factorwise Gauss completions, not ζ. Finite height, fixed rank and one finite aperture do not settle the corresponding uniform problems.

## Boundaries

- **Dual descriptions are not extra mechanisms.** The [cut kernel](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RigidArithmeticOfCuts/TheKernelBeyondPowerLaws.lean) is the dual of CTF approximation. By contrast, AAD constructs integer floor-sum certificates, ALST constructs polynomial multiples, and AIF minimizes a holomorphic boundary moment. Each has its own witnesses and RH implication.
- **Partial results keep their scope.** Excluding finite off-line configurations alone would not prove RH. [The three-part reduction](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean) also needs approach to the line and a proof that approach forces finite off-line support. Under a single quartet, [Lindelöf and real-rootedness after removing the quartet factor](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SingleQuartetLindelofCosineAndQuotientLaguerrePolya.lean) both hold; neither test distinguishes that case from RH.
