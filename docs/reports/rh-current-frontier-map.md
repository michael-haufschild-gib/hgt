## Gates every route must pass

Distilled from STATUS; each is a proved obstruction, not a preference.
- No slack. RH sits exactly on its boundary (de Bruijn–Newman `Λ=0`; lag ceiling `a_1` approached along the powers of every base). A proof is an exact identity or a rigidity, never an inequality with margin; log-lossy estimates are worthless (CANNOT-411, MUST-239/240, CANNOT-437).
- Exact primes. If `Λ+w` is Weil-positive and `Σ_{k≤n}w(k)=κ₁(n−1)+κ₂(√n−1)+E(n)`, `Σ|E(n)|/(n√n)<∞`, then `w=0` (MUST-507). In particular, nonzero `ℓ¹(n⁻¹ᐟ²)` or partial-sum `O(n^θ)`, `θ<½`, perturbations fail (MUST-502/503); weights supported on `O(N^θ)` positions, `θ<½`, fail. Sieve majorants, sparse weights and regular residue/prime-power shifts cannot replace exact weights.
- Rigidity, not estimate. Any inequality whose hypotheses survive a single-prime Euler-factor deformation gives neither RH nor a zero-free region nor an off-line count (MUST-389; CANNOT-64/65).
- Orientation. The separating datum is reflection-odd and two-torsion; reflection-even real readers, localized/equivariant invariants and averages are blind to it (CANNOT-399, CANNOT-405/407, CANNOT-425, MUST-433).
- Individual zeros. Unconditional arithmetic identities see only pair-even smoothed densities; no fixed functional individuates a zero (CANNOT-182r).
- Exponent, not constant. The missing step moves an exponent by ½ at once; no method gives any exponent below ½ in the lag/Weil lanes (MUST-250, MUST-261).
- Cofinal source law. Finite data, fixed jets and finite packets are nonselective; the law must be proved on pre-divisor source data before specializing at Xi zeros (CANNOT-02, MUST-01, MUST-29/30).
- Mellin-diagonal inputs are blind. Dilations, dyadic/prime-2 self-similarity and the Hecke semigroup act on Mellin transforms by units or characters; common eigenvectors include Helson series with off-line zeros; together with the functional equation they pin ζ and leave no deformation handle (KR-R2).
- Holomorphic readers are unquantized. Zero-free anchored holomorphic approximants with bounded boundary argument have zero-free limits [L] (R:AnchoredPhasePassageRigidity); a holomorphic clock unimodular on the line gives one growing mode per free orbit (CANNOT-458). Source realizations of zero-side quantized forms are 0/∞ growth criteria: sub-unit margins are idle, any finite bound is RH; the open core is finiteness at the `√X` scale (KR-R1, KR-R3; CANNOT-461).
