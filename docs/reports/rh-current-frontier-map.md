## External mathematical status

RH remains [unsolved](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/Riemann-Hypothesis/). A September 2026 [quasi-RH manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/paper.pdf) claims Dirichlet zero-freeness for Re s>7/8 [E], including ζ. This is a fixed-strip claim, not the critical endpoint or a local Lean result. A corpus obstruction to a cardinality-only character estimate does not by itself refute an argument using additional arithmetic structure.

A September 2026 [ordinary-correlation manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Ordinary-two-point-correlations-of-multiplicative-functions-September-24-2026/final.pdf) claims fixed-affine-form Liouville correlations O_forms(X/(log X)^c) [E]. It does not supply power saving or uniformity for growing shifts/moduli. The established [logarithmic two-point theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05422), this ordinary-correlation claim, local formalization, and the moving-source estimates needed here are separate statuses.
