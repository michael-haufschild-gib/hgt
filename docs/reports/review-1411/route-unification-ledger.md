# Route unification ledger

The routes organize around a small number of arithmetic objects and limit problems. They do not become one proof merely because their terminal statements are equivalent to RH. This ledger distinguishes four kinds of connection:

- **Identity:** the same source or invariant, with an explicit formula.
- **Quantitative transfer:** a theorem transports a bound with stated losses and quantifiers.
- **Endpoint equivalence:** both complete assertions characterize RH, without an independent transfer of partial estimates.
- **Proposed bridge:** an additional theorem or construction remains necessary.

Evidence conventions and source scope: [route report](route-obstacles.md), [fact log](../../../logs/review-1411-current-facts.txt). The three wall families **P/U/R** mean arithmetic production, uniform exclusion and faithful realization. They classify obligations; they are not three interchangeable mathematical propositions.

## Strongest proved connections

### 1. Zero width, Möbius prefixes, long moments and JAS

Write `Θ` for the full rightmost abscissa and `w=Θ−1/2`. MMD gives `w≤3/8`. For `β≥0`, WD's fixed-height criterion reads the statement `w≤β` through all prefix lengths and every slack exponent `ε>0`. Its `[0,1]` ζ-mollified second-moment version has exponent `2(β+ε)`. [MMD] [WD]

The long-moment dictionary has the quantifiers

`∃K≥0, ∀θ₀>0, ∃θ≥θ₀, ∃Cθ≥0, ∃ᶠT→∞: moment(T,⌊T^θ⌋) ≤ Cθ T^(K+2βθ)`.

This is equivalent to `w≤β`; pure and ζ-mollified moments are equivalent at this level. The common height exponent `K`, arbitrarily large `θ`, and cofinal `T` are essential. Requiring a common constant for every `θ,T` is a different, false assertion. [WD-LONG]

The exact μ kernel signal has exponent controlled by `Θ`: for `1/2≤b<1`, `Θ≤b` iff its bound holds at every admissible exponent `b'>b`. A signal bound `O(Z^b)` gives large-prime JAS positivity at levels `b+1/2+ε`. Full dressed level one, including its above-a-cutoff form, is equivalent to RH without a chain premise. [SIGNAL] [SIGNAL-WIDTH] [SIGNAL-JAS] [JAS]

**Unification:** these are proved quantitative routes from actual cancellation to width or ancestry levels. **Limit:** a fixed-strip estimate does not supply the critical exponent, and no theorem here makes an available subcubic *reciprocity defect* estimate into the missing μ *signal* estimate. That identification is a producer problem. [INCOMPLETE] [TRUNCATION]

### 2. Window form, neutral margin, critical threshold and geometry

The neutral arithmetic form is the complete Weil form with its polar charge contribution separated. A uniform finite scalar lower margin on all admissible neutral matrices is equivalent to RH. Whole-space critical positivity and polar payment have equal thresholds; their infinite-threshold assertion is equivalent to RH. These are exact invariant relations, stronger than a list of unrelated RH criteria. [ACS] [POLAR]

Parity floors at `log 2` transfer to full complex windows and then block matrices. Their stated cutoff/range conditions give zero overlap levels in a finite ladder. A lower gap for every fixed cutoff on its already index-one windows gives another RH receiver. It does not require one gap uniform over all cutoffs, nor establish index one on all windows by definition. [FLOOR] [OVERLAP] [GAP]

**Unification:** RW/EWU/OWU, ACS and critical HF/atomic support expose different restrictions or degenerations of a complete arithmetic form. **Limit:** a source-independent geometric index theorem is still missing. Correct contact ideals and logarithmic diagonals constrain such a realization; they do not construct it. The complete contact operator cannot generally be compressed into fixed rank with the specified absolute-error budget. [CONTACT] [DIAGONAL] [RANK-WALL]

### 3. Finite exceptions, tail laws and negative index

For the actual zero configuration:

| Assertion | Exact equivalent content |
|---|---|
| AAL | `Θ=Θ∞`; equivalently, every right zero can be approximated in real part by zeros beyond every height. [AAL] |
| ¬AAL | A finite nonempty layer right of `1/2+h` for some `h≥0`, above a narrower infinite tail. [AAL-LAYER] |
| B13 | `FiniteOffLine→RH`; equivalently, real packet negative index is `0` or `∞`. This index counts **distinct right zeros**, not multiplicity. [INDEX] |
| Landing | `ZAL→FiniteOffLine`; under ZAL, a height threshold uniform as depth decreases to zero. [LANDING] |
| RH | `ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing`. [LANDING] |
| AAL decomposition | `B13 ∧ Landing ∧ (¬AAL→ZAL)`. The last implication is a genuine extra assertion. [AAL-ATOMS] |

**Unification:** AAL/ACS finite index, ZCG heat descent and BAG recurrence share the need to exclude finite or increasingly shallow defects. **Limit:** they are not interchangeable tail laws. A single off-line zero falsifies RH but need not falsify AAL. A fixed strip is neither ZAL nor AAL. Quasi-RH's tail/full equivalence does not change that distinction. [QUASITAIL]

### 4. Arithmetic Galois groups and heat monodromy

Two arithmetic field constructions must remain separate. Complete-fibre moments give monic polynomials whose roots are centered ξ zeros, algebraic over the associated source field. Prime-trace fields instead encode the values `p^ρ`; their finite residue-field degrees equal arithmetic orbit sizes. Neither is a proof that ξ zeros are algebraic over ℚ. [FIBRES] [ORBIT]

The prime-trace criterion requires **both** a lift of functional reflection and membership of every prime weight in the base. Degree-two orbits alone do not force criticality. Algebraic independence of trace functions is conditional on independence of the heights and concerns functions, not their numerical values. [REFLECTION] [TRACE-INDEP]

Arithmetic action matches heat monodromy on continued finite selections in distinct reflection pairs, with a selection-dependent time interval. A global group identification is not available: the specified whole-fibre comparisons are disproved. Proper heat monodromy is equivalent to a turn-parity law; it permits positive collision masses divisible by eight. [HEAT-FINITE] [HEAT-NOGO] [HEAT-PARITY] [HEAT-EIGHT]

**Unification:** finite local actions have an actual bridge. **Limit:** transporting centrality, orbit finiteness or conjugation across the whole heat fibre is not a valid purity proof.

### 5. Physical strip and arithmetic abscissa

For `0<δ≤1/4`, the actual thermofield strip of width δ corresponds to the zero bound `Reρ≤1−2δ`; exact pole orders prevent hidden cancellation in that dictionary. Thus δ=`1/16` is available and δ=`1/4` is RH. The Bianchi analogue controls the product with `L(χ₋₃)`. [ADS] [BIANCHI]

**Unification:** PM's physical rate is a quantitative reading of the same arithmetic abscissa. **Limit:** causality, FE, passivity and axis unitarity alone do not force any common positive decay rate in the constructed deformation class. Additional source-selective dynamics are required. [ADS-NOGO]
