# Route unification: identities, equivalences and missing bridges

Many routes detect the same failure of RH. That does not make their mathematical mechanisms identical, nor allow one route's missing hypothesis to be discharged by another route's receiver. This ledger distinguishes five relationships:

| Mark | Meaning | What it licenses |
|---|---|---|
| **I — identity/transfer** | A proved equality or source-preserving transformation of the specified objects. | Transfer the stated estimate with its constants and domains. |
| **Q — quantitative implication** | A proved bound on one observable gives a specified bound on another. | Transfer only in the proved direction and parameter range. |
| **E — terminal equivalence** | Each complete proposition is equivalent to RH or another named terminal property. | Logical equivalence of the complete propositions; no independent estimate. |
| **C — conditional bridge** | A construction/estimate would connect the mechanisms but is unavailable. | A research task, not an arrow usable in a proof. |
| **A — analogy** | Similar form or interpretation without a proved source-preserving map. | Motivation only. |

Evidence consists of checked source statements and inspected dependencies, with no builds or fresh kernel audit. [Fact log](../../../logs/review-1411-facts.txt). The three wall families P/U/R classify required work; the clusters below classify mathematical objects. Neither classification is a theorem reducing all mathematics to three scalar quantities.

## 1. Arithmetic forms and the window tower

**Routes:** RW, HF, HAT-C, EWU, OWU, ACS; supported by WTZ, AGZ, Green calculus and MAFT.

These are the closest to genuinely different faces of one arithmetic form. The real/complex Weil form, its parity restrictions, closed aperture ground energy and step-packet matrices have source-preserving transfers. Neutral matrices separate the charge contribution. Exact Schur identities express overlap through the middle response and end coupling. **I** applies to these stated identities, not to an unproved sign conclusion. [BLOCK-FLOOR] [HATC] [GREEN-LOSS]

At aperture `1881/5000`, the even certificate yields the full floor and all-block first-overlap theorem. That transfer is complete. The critical half-line state is a limiting obstruction to global positivity; it is not literally the finite-window matrix, and its existence alternative is not an exclusion theorem. **E** connects the complete global criteria; **C** marks the missing global signed estimate. [FLOOR] [GREEN-FIRST] [HF] [ACS]

| Same-source object | Current common mathematical content | Unpaid condition |
|---|---|---|
| Even/odd tests and full complex form | Parity splitting and complexification | Control at every aperture, not only the certified one |
| Closed form and step matrices | Approximation/transfer with exact support and cutoff conditions | Uniform lower margin over all windows |
| Neutral matrix and overlap Schur complement | Exact index/response calculus | Bound the actual signed complement |
| Ground-energy threshold and critical state | Existence and structural alternative under failure of RH | Rule out the positive threshold |
| Arithmetic square and contact/jet form | Explicit finite contact and jet identities | Global geometric realization/index theorem |

The fixed-rank gluing obstruction concerns a comparison model, not an impossibility of the common arithmetic form. A geometric realization must retain the logarithmic negative diagonal. [RANK-WALL] [SELF-PAIRING]

## 2. Universal sign and moment detectors

**Routes:** PA/HJ, TH, RN, GTA, HCF, ACT, AIP, SC, ECP; also the all-shell form of JAS.

These routes encode criticality in a complete family of inequalities: Hausdorff differences, Hermite flags, theta energy, quartic charge decay, polynomial heat tests on two grids, a fixed-shift profile inequality, a compensated ledger, Gaussian cone coupling, or finite character Grams. Their terminal propositions are **E**-equivalent to RH, or are a proved RH-sufficient receiver where only that direction is cited. They are not automatically equal kernels with interchangeable test classes. [PA] [TH] [RN] [GTA] [HCF] [ACT] [AIP] [SC] [ECP]

The common wall is a universal arithmetic assertion. Reducing to one shift or two grids does not reduce to finitely many tests; the unrestricted profile/order quantifier remains. A positive character, renewal model or moment sequence built independently of the arithmetic source does not satisfy the source-identification requirement by resemblance.

There are useful internal transfers, such as charge/flag conversions and exact compensation identities. A claimed cross-route transfer must name one of those theorems. “Both are positivity” is only **A**, and “both imply RH” is **E**, not a new producer.

## 3. Arithmetic cancellation and growth

**Routes:** WL, FU, CFC, RP, MC, SQF, TT, CHMD, WD, LI, SZ, CTF, PBM, RB; JAS also enters through arithmetic shell sums.

The common mechanism is source arithmetic whose excessive growth detects off-critical zeros. Within this cluster, different degrees of unification are proved:

| Connection | Strength | Precise qualification |
|---|---|---|
| WL deficit, normalized Chebyshev primitive and coprime cross-work | **I/Q/E**, through the proved rate dictionary | Use the actual normalized observables and the theorem's closed-rate range. [WL] |
| CHMD arithmetic wave and zero-width growth | **Q/E** | Boundedness of that wave is an RH criterion; arbitrary causal waves are irrelevant. [CHMD] |
| MC Hilbert form and its cancellation criterion | **E**, with internal exact identities | Subpower growth must hold for the arithmetic Möbius source. [MC] |
| WD fixed-window mollified second moment, pointwise prefix growth and low-set criterion | **E**, via separate proved receivers | Preserve each statement's order of quantifiers. One fixed positive exponent is not the all-ε assertion. [WD] [WD-PROPORTION] |
| WD cofinal long-prefix moments | **E**, and a graded **Q** bound | Arbitrarily large length exponents θ with cofinal-height bound `C_θ T^K` and fixed K characterize RH; `T^(K+2βθ)` yields width at most β. [WD-COFINAL] |
| JAS shells and width/exception conditions | **E**, with conjuncts | Small-shell positivity requires the stated chain/tail condition; the strip-dependent variant retains two sign conditions. [JAS] [JAS-STRIP] |
| FU, CFC, RP, SQF, TT, LI, SZ, CTF, PBM, RB | Primarily **E** or a cited sufficient implication | Their one-sided bounds, completions and averaging windows differ. Do not infer numerical norm equivalence from terminal RH equivalence. [FU] [CFC] [RP] [SQF] [TT] [LI] [SZ] [CTF] [PBM] [RB] |

MMD is a **producer feeding this cluster**, not another terminal RH equivalence. Its fixed strip can be inserted into a receiver with that hypothesis. It does not automatically provide critical cancellation. Decompletion offers a possible **C** bridge to a new producer, while the Gauss-monomial barrier fixes the scope of the existing completion architecture. [MMD] [DECOMPLETE] [GAUSS]

## 4. Heat, real-rootedness, zero geometry and topology

**Routes:** LA, ZCG, PTR, TG, XN; supported by heat monodromy and verified-height rungs.

Unbounded Jensen real-rootedness, every positive cosine shift, heat conjugation compatibility, a universal sub-unit topological gate and line-centred Newton discs are terminal **E** descriptions of criticality. Their finite approximations are different. A finite Jensen rung is not a finite heat-time theorem; a Newton margin is not a winding bound without a comparison. [LA] [ZCG] [PTR] [TG] [XN]

Heat monodromy provides **I** transport within its analytic cover and explicit local collision permutations. It does not provide **I** identification with arithmetic Galois actions. Its largeness is not a reality principle. Fixed-positive-time horizons and finite verified-height rungs provide **Q** control with parameters that must remain explicit. [MONODROMY] [HEAT-TURN] [HEAT-HORIZON] [JENSEN-RUNG]

The missing common ingredient is arithmetic information controlling the full zero configuration through an unbounded parameter or a singular limit. The heat constant, matrix margin and zero abscissa share an RH terminal locus where proved, but no general equality of these numerical quantities is asserted.

## 5. Recurrence, localization and the exceptional coordinate

**Routes:** BAG, SRT, JAS and tail-oriented branches of SC; supported by AAL and exceptional-layer theory.

BAG concerns a particular arithmetic target on every closed strip disc. S3 concerns transfer to heavy *identity* returns. These are different return problems; the necessary connecting implication is **C**. The available strip removes one S3 premise, not the transfer itself. [BAG] [S3] [MMD]

SRT has a quantitative prime-logarithmic-window criterion for zero width. JAS has several equivalent formulations whose shell assertion is paired with a chain, loud-set, tail or strip condition. They must be used as complete statements. [SRT] [JAS] [JAS-SUM] [JAS-STRIP]

The exceptional coordinate is independent of “how close the tail is”:

| Quantity/property | What it measures | What it does not settle |
|---|---|---|
| `Θ = sup Re ρ` | Full rightward zero extent | Whether that extent recurs arbitrarily high |
| `Θ∞` | Extent of the infinite high-zero tail | A finite exceptional top layer |
| AAL: `Θ∞=Θ` | No separate finite top layer | Criticality of the common extent |
| Tail approach to `1/2` | Asymptotic criticality | Low or finitely many off-line zeros |
| AAL plus tail approach | RH in the established framework | No extra arithmetic estimate is generated by the equivalence |

The finite-layer and height-transport characterizations are genuine **E** identifications of AAL. They are not consequences of zero-density bounds alone. Countermodel edits preserve enough coarse arithmetic to expose that distinction. [AAL] [AAL-LAYER] [AAL-TRANSPORT] [AAL-HIERARCHY] [EDIT]

AAL also decomposes into finite-stratum rigidity, landing of a tail approaching the line, and the defined implication from AAL failure to tail approach. This is a proved logical decomposition, not three independently discharged arithmetic inputs. [AAL-LANDING]

## 6. Physical and representation routes

**Route:** PM. **Candidates:** APF, IRK, W1C, WBF, MSC; ECP has both a representation and a sign aspect.

The modular/Bianchi scattering dictionaries give real **I/Q** relationships between specified arithmetic divisors and pulse decay. In Bianchi normalization, decay at every rate below `δ` detects the boundary `Re ρ≤1−2δ` for `ζL(χ₋₃)`. At `δ=1/4`, this is the corresponding Dedekind critical-line statement, not ζ alone. An AdS identification is a further **C/A** proposal, not part of this established dictionary. [PM] [RINGDOWN]

The candidates share R1, full-divisor landing, but their objects are distinct: APF traces, IRK resolutions, W1C approximants, WBF genealogy determinants and MSC conormal source data. A reconstruction restricted to real-labelled zeros cannot exhaust hypothetical off-line zeros by definition. A family constructed from the desired zero set is not automatically an independent arithmetic model. [APF] [IRK] [W1C] [WBF] [MSC]

Arithmetic Galois theory belongs here as a further faithful-source programme. It has actual finite equations and normal extensions. Its prime-weight/orbit and reflection-lift criteria are proved; a purity theorem is not. The prime-trace field and the complete-fibre source field have different definitions and must be compared by theorem. [TRACE-FIELD] [WEIGHT-ORBIT] [REFLECTION] [FIBRE-FIELD]

## 9. The actual unification

**One shared arithmetic form** unifies the window/Hodge/overlap cluster particularly tightly. **One critical-growth problem with several observables** links the cancellation cluster, subject to the proved dictionaries. **One full zero configuration with a separate exceptional coordinate** underlies the remaining terminal criteria. Faithful source realization connects these descriptions only where an exact theorem exists.

The useful Frankenstein strategy therefore joins an independently proved input to a source-preserving transfer and then to a receiver. It cannot close a cycle made entirely of terminal equivalences. The priority opportunities are a signed retained-charge overlap estimate, a decompleted phase-preserving producer, and an arithmetic invariant sensitive to finite exceptions. Each would add a missing theorem to the graph rather than another name for RH.
