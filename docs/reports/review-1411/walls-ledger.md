# RH wall ledger

Three core families organize the closure problems:

| Family | Mathematical question | Typical failure |
|---|---|---|
| **P — arithmetic production** | Can the actual prime/Möbius source satisfy the required signed bound? | Absolute values, averaging or completion erase the saving or the target. |
| **U — uniform exclusion** | Does control cover every relevant scale, height, degree, profile and finite exceptional zero? | Fixed-window or tail information leaves an uncontrolled limit or finite stratum. |
| **R — faithful realization** | Does an algebraic, geometric, physical or probabilistic object retain the exact arithmetic source and possess the required structure independently? | The model changes the divisor, hides the endpoint in a hypothesis, or forgets phases. |

This is a classification of research obligations, not a theorem that all walls are equivalent. A route may meet several families. **Open** means unresolved in the inspected Lean corpus; **excluded** names a proved impossible construction class; **available** names a proved input. Source-review scope and evidence are recorded in the [route report](route-obstacles.md) and [fact log](../../../logs/review-1411-current-facts.txt). No builds or fresh kernel audit underpin these labels.

## R — faithful realization

| Wall | Status and exact obstruction | Surviving target | Routes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 Full arithmetic geometry** | **Open.** Prime contacts and conductor ideals exist. Nonnegative geometric diagonals fail; admissible diagonals must carry logarithmic negative self-pairings. Big-Witt `VF` has a divisibility mask. [CONTACT] [CONTACT-EX] [DIAGONAL] [WITT] | A global intersection/cohomology theory with exact arithmetic diagonal, correct operator relations and a proved index theorem. | ACS, F₁, HF, PM |
| **R2 Finite-rank absolute gluing** | **Excluded class.** For ratio-four halves and `600(r+1)≤H`, every rank-r model with the prescribed absolute-error lowering leaves at least two negative squares. [RANK-WALL] | Full contact/jet operators, growing rank, or genuinely signed coupling estimates. | ACS, RW, HF |
| **R3 Global arithmetic/heat identification** | **Excluded comparisons.** Infinite heat transitivity cannot intertwine faithfully with finite arithmetic orbits; one finitary heat element cannot realize conjugation on a whole injected ξ fibre. [HEAT-NOGO] | Compatible finite-window data with controlled losses, without claiming a whole-fibre group identification. | ZCG, LA, arithmetic Galois |
| **R4 Galois purity** | **Open.** Reflection lift plus all prime-weight membership iff RH. Degree two alone includes rational off-line real parts; complete-fibre source fields already contain zero information. [REFLECTION] [ORBIT] [FIBRES] | Arithmetic construction/control of the base field, lift and weights. Centrality alone is not purity. | ZCG, LA, Galois programme |
| **R5 Physical axioms** | **Excluded inference.** Retarded prime2-deformations preserve the named causal/Euler/FE/passive/unitary properties yet have decay thresholds tending to zero. [ADS-NOGO] | An independently proved arithmetic dynamical principle distinguishing the actual prime gates and controlling decay. Dictionary construction is available. [ADS] | PM |
| **R6 Local algebra versus global structure** | **Open realization; counterexamples to automatic promotion.** Cyclic-local splitting can coexist with global nonsplitting; finite resolutions and divisor-order identities do not imply projectivity or sign. [LOCAL-GLOBAL] [IRK] [MSC] | A proved global obstruction-vanishing or exact structural transport theorem for the actual source. | IRK, MSC, ACS |
| **R7 Reversible sewing** | **Excluded class.** All-prime reversible single-valued RFHM sewing has zero nonempty-support output. [RFHM] | A source-faithful nonreversible or larger-carrier construction. | RFHM, JAS |
| **R8 Global roots / parity** | **Excluded global target; open regional construction.** ξ has odd critical-line zeros, so global square-root/even-order requirements fail. [PARITY] [ROOTS] | Source-driven regional divisibility with compatibility, restricted away from the critical line. | Topology, DR support, ZCG |

## What counts as crossing a wall

A quantitative theorem must state its source, signs, domain, constants and quantifier order. A construction must prove its arithmetic matching before its abstract positivity, topology or dynamics can be used. A no-go must retain its precise class and positive survivor. These requirements prevent three false closures: improving a receiver without producing its input; solving each finite problem without a valid limit; constructing a well-behaved model that is not the arithmetic object.

The routine applications and research programmes are collected in the [summary](obstacles-summary.md). None of the open walls is discharged merely by relabelling it in another route.
