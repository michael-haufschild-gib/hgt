# RH routes: current mathematics and closure obstacles

The corpus supplies an unconditional strip `1/8 ≤ Re ρ ≤ 7/8`, strong finite-window arithmetic theorems, and many exact RH criteria. No unconditional RH closure is identified in the inspected source. Most remaining route endpoints require an arithmetic estimate or exclusion principle; another equivalence theorem does not supply that input. [MMD]

Scope: 38 named routes, 12 candidates, and shared supporting programmes. Evidence is current Lean source, with statements, hypotheses and relevant proof interfaces inspected. This is not a fresh compilation or transitive-axiom audit. The [fact log](../../../logs/review-1411-current-facts.txt), [search evidence](../../../logs/review-1411-current-evidence.jsonl) and [source manifest](../../../logs/review-1411-current-source-manifest.json) record the evidence. The two reference maps supply nomenclature, not proof authority. No external search result is used.

**A — work** means an application, normalization or finite calculation with an available mathematical method. It does not include an unproved sign, rate or uniformity claim. **B — open research** means no solution supplied by the inspected corpus; this cannot certify what every mathematician knows. **Excluded** means a precise construction class has a proved obstruction. An excluded construction is not a refutation of its whole route. A dash in A means the receiver is available and no routine task removes the blocker.

## Shared frontier

### Arithmetic production and its limits

The MMD source proves the `7/8` zero-free half-plane for ζ, the stated Dirichlet specializations and its defined Eisenstein Hecke family. It is not an arbitrary-number-field GRH result. Quasi-RH is therefore an available input, not an open milestone. [MMD]

Incomplete reciprocity retains the target phases and has a genuine subcubic boundary-defect saving. On a fixed complex rectangle, with `N(c) ≤ X^(1−δ)`, `0<δ<1`, and `1/6<η<1/3`, its bound is `C X^(1/2−min(3η−1/2,1))`. This bounds the defect, not the entire target family. Family continuation amplifies the local sextic exponent by `Q²`: `1−6σ` becomes `3−6σ`. Truncation levels `L≥3` retain correction zeros at unbounded heights in `7/12≤Re s<2/3`; the absolute sixth-power tail costs at least `cZ^(2/3)`. The signed aggregate is a different quantity. [INCOMPLETE] [TRUNCATION]

For the specified Möbius-signed Gauss-monomial pairing class, the uncoupled boundary is at least `2/3`, the coupled fixed point at least `3/5`, and the single GRH-step boundary at least `7/12`. The `3/5` optimum is attained. Changing orders or arity inside this class cannot reach `1/2`. Uniform six-phase averaging removes the target reciprocal mode along with its reciprocal partner. A viable replacement must retain the target while controlling signed family interactions outside these mechanisms. [GAUSS] [AVERAGE]

Fixed-shift logarithmic two-point Liouville cancellation is proved. It supplies neither a critical prefix rate nor uniformity over growing shifts. Formal shifted logarithmic jets of ζ, μ and λ are algebraically independent over the specified prime-density-zero coefficient field; this constrains exact formal algebraic identities, not analytic estimates or zero coordinates. [CHOWLA] [JETS]

### Windows, overlap and critical scales

At aperture `A=log 2`, the actual Weil form has odd floor `10⁻¹⁰`, even/full/complex floor `10⁻¹³`, and

`10⁻¹³ ≤ groundEnergy(log 2) < 10⁻⁹`.

For cutoff `Q≥3`, this gives the block floor at every block size when `R+J<4R`; index one also uses `R≤J`. Ratio `≤3` is covered for `Q≥2`. The ladder has `levelAt Q k=0` for `k≤3,Q≥2`, and for `k≤4,Q≥3`. The `Q=2` fourth excess remains a distinct question; its output ratio exceeds three. Neither `ratio<4` nor a finite number of zero levels is an all-ratio theorem. [FLOOR] [OVERLAP]

The full tilted-spline matrix has a `c⁻⁷` floor for every fixed exponent `0<α<1/8`, uniformly over `1≤c≤(log N)^α`, eventually in `N`. One fixed small critical multiple is also positive on the whole space. A larger explicit ratio interval has complement coercivity, retaining its low-packet and Schur obligations. Whole-space critical threshold equals polar-payment threshold; an infinite threshold is equivalent to RH. The Beurling-profile criterion at `2 loglog N (logloglog N)²` uses a different profile: tilted-spline positivity at a larger numerical clock does not prove it. [SUBCRITICAL] [CRITICAL-SMALL] [COMPLEMENT] [POLAR] [BEURLING]

The actual first-overlap sign separates ζ from a sign twin with `ω₂=−1`; the twin admits a negative packet already in the first-step ratio range. Absolute-magnitude gluing cannot explain this distinction. Fixed-rank approximation with the specified absolute-error diagonal lowering fails at ratio four when `600(r+1)≤H`. The bare finite-cutoff dilation process has no gap. These obstructions leave full signed, growing-rank coupling available as a research direction. [TWIN] [RANK-WALL] [TREE-GAP]

### Global exclusion and realization

A critical half-line state is available as an alternative to RH. If that alternative occurs, the state is unique up to sign, outer, causally cyclic, and its Laplace transform is zero-free on the closed right half-plane. These properties describe the possible obstruction; they do not exclude it. Endpoint selection for a separate source-tilted confining problem does not prove endpoint sign or identify its minimizer with this critical state. [HF] [HF-OUTER] [HF-CYCLIC] [HF-BOUNDARY] [ENDPOINT]

Arithmetic Galois actions can be realized as heat monodromy on continued finite selections of zeros in distinct reflection pairs. Multiple zeros admit continuations; the positive time and permutation depend on the selection. Whole-fibre intertwining is obstructed: arithmetic orbits are finite, whereas heat monodromy is transitive on an infinite fibre; one finitary heat permutation cannot realize arithmetic conjugation on an entire injected fibre. A common specialization interval preserving all distinct off-line zeros as nonreal heat zeros would force finite off-line support. [HEAT-FINITE] [HEAT-NOGO] [HEAT-UNIFORM]

For `Θ` the full abscissa and `Θ∞` the tail abscissa, AAL is `Θ=Θ∞`, ZAL is `Θ∞=1/2`, and RH is AAL plus ZAL. AAL failure is a finite nonempty top layer above a narrower tail. Let `B13` mean `FiniteOffLine → RH`, and Landing mean `ZAL → FiniteOffLine`. Then RH is exactly `ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing`. AAL additionally has a decomposition through these two exclusions and `¬AAL → ZAL`. Neither a fixed strip nor finiteness at each positive heat time supplies these inputs. [AAL] [AAL-LAYER] [AAL-ATOMS] [LANDING]
