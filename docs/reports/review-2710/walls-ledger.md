# Walls ledger

Status: 2026-10-27, corpus `7eaf4d7ef` with working tree. Every obstacle in the corpus belongs to one of three core families:

- **Wall 1 — critical arithmetic sign.** Estimates, signs and oscillatory payment at the critical endpoint, at the gauge point `0`, and at the ordinates of zeros.
- **Wall 3 — transfer.** Readings, norms, completions, limits, shared budgets, localization, selection, and the passage from local to global.
- **Wall 14 — realization and forcing.** Faithful arithmetic objects, correspondences and their local types, invariants, counts, deformations, renormalizations, polarization, and the forcing of the actual source into its blind carrier.

**Organizing principle.** At an off-line reflection pair `{ρ, 1 − ρ̄}` the local form has signature `(1, 1)`; multiplicative correspondences act on it by boosts, the successor unipotently, the functional equation and its conjugates `σ_γ` by the swap. The test space of `ζ` carries two computed forms: Weil's form `W` (explicit formula), Hecke equivariant, whose positivity is RH; and the collided form `D = Σ m_ρ|f̂(ρ)|²` (the current `Δ log|ξ|`, polynomially many theta values), positive, faithful, the log-Sobolev norm, whose Hecke defect is the transverse moment of inertia `M_f` and whose equivariance is RH; `Re W = D − Δ`. Three further computed objects come from the same potential:

- the **field** of the zero divisor, read off `(ζ′/ζ)² = L(Λ ⋆ Λ)`; RH iff every right zero feels a transverse field `≤ 1`, or `< √(2c log γ)`; the field is outward at every off-line terminal;
- the **transverse energy** `𝓔`, quantized: `0` iff RH, otherwise `≥ 1`; finite iff the off-line index is finite; a window with an off-line zero carries at least `2`;
- the **self-energy of torsion cycles**, positive at `r < 1` and Hecke similar up to the cutoff; its counterterm is the identity, so nothing survives the renormalized limit, while the archimedean germ survives on the polar hyperplane and the arithmetic point completes it.

Wall 1 measures the hyperbolic part as a rate, a sign, a gauge charge or an alignment of `γ log p`; Wall 3 governs how positivity transfers between readings, norms, localizations, limits, budgets and from each prime to the sum over primes; Wall 14 asks what structure makes one form positive and equivariant at once, or bounds the field.

**Nineteen exhaustion laws** bound every mechanism tested:

- **Gauge.** Multiplicative data determine a source up to its gauge orbit; a coupling is blind under every real sign gauge iff it is of square class; finite multiplicative lattice forms are blind; the actual source is one point (1.18, 3.1).
- **Hyperbolicity.** Positivity invariant under a correspondence that is a boost at an off-line pair forces isotropy there (14.10, 14.17).
- **Resonance.** Inside correspondence-closed carriers a symmetric dilation is elliptic at an off-line point only when `γ log a ∈ πℤ`; the only reflection-compatible affine localizer is `σ_γ` (3.18, 14.17).
- **Conductor.** Twisted positivity is a theorem only below `e^a ≲ log γ/4`, where it is source-blind; the companion certificate needs `e^{2δa} > 768A log(|γ| + 3)`; the two never meet (3.19).
- **Localization.** Positivity constrains a fibre only where the carrier localizes; non-localizing positivity holds for every prime weight (3.18).
- **Dominated twins.** On nonnegative tests the Weil form is antitone in the source (1.4, 1.18).
- **Linear loss.** Moments, family averages, products and relative powers lose at least linearly; twists are rate neutral; dyadic amplification converts only rates (1.15, 1.17).
- **Pole order and one variable.** Pole order pays inserted zeros; a complex quartet contracts only against a real anchor; the positive polar-configuration cone is classified (1.16, 14.13, 14.19, 14.20, 14.23).
- **Dilution.** A positive packet on `ℚ`-independent ordinates buys at most its pole share (14.11, 3.19).
- **Collision.** Kernel counts, coupled counts, escape Cauchy forms and `D` pair `ρ` with `ρ̄`; their positivity is automatic, faithful and computed, and sees no sign (3.1, 3.15, 14.21).
- **Counting.** A section count with RR and Bézout, an effectivity function, a naive or convex height, a one-sided count, a fixed tensor loss, horizon torsion and the Farey potential are each RH; large-sieve majorants inflate; Stepanov auxiliaries need a lattice spectrum (1.1, 1.17, 14.4, 14.6, 14.18).
- **Deformation.** Positive deformations keep the off-line divisor and cannot be reversed; positive rigidity, chirality and central ceilings at an ordinate are exactly the absence of zeros there; surplus residues are even central drops (14.22, 14.25).
- **Survival.** Positivity passes a scale limit exactly on the survival cone of the counterterm; scalar counterterms leave nothing, rank-one counterterms a hyperplane (14.24, 14.27).
- **Renormalization.** The torsion germ has identity counterterm: survival cone `{0}`, minimal counterterm `≍ log N`, divergence at the arithmetic point, Hecke cocycle not a bounded coboundary (14.24).
- **Local to global.** Each prime's tower form satisfies a local Hodge index; the sum over primes has no `ℓ²` ceiling (3.23).
- **Completion.** Counting reaches incomplete sums only through a period; the arithmetic point has none (1.20).
- **Detection.** Zero-side objects — the field, the outward reserve, the lens, the local theta detector, the computed Hecke defect and moment of inertia — see each off-line zero at its own height and bound nothing (1.21, 3.22, 14.26).
- **Exterior.** The prime-side anti-alignment that a deep zero forces at its ordinate is proved for `σ > 1` and does not continue into the strip (1.21).
- **Statistics.** Averages, positive proportions, almost-every-ordinate positivity and the census place most zeros near the line and exclude none; only a quantized statistic below one quantum excludes (3.14).

Over `𝔽_q` one structure escapes all nineteen: a polarization of `C × C`, from Riemann–Roch and an ample class, makes the Rosati trace positive and Frobenius-equivariant at once; the self-intersection of the diagonal is the finite number `2 − 2g`; the Frobenius spectrum lies on a lattice; local and global index agree on one `H¹`. Over `ℤ` identities, the theorem of the square and the budget are free, the receivers are complete, a positive form and the field of the zeros are computed, positive equivariant families exist before renormalization, and local Hodge index holds at each prime; nothing yet joins positivity and equivariance through the diagonal, pays the sum over primes, or bounds the field.

## 6. What counts as crossing a wall

- **Wall 1.** Insert a newly proved source estimate into an existing receiver with every tail and quantifier intact. Any graded gain beyond Vinogradov–Korobov is a fixed strip; a non-graded output (a non-isolation law, a gauge margin, an exclusion of deep zeros at non-exceptional ordinates, a field bound below `√log γ` at selected heights) is progress of another kind. A new mechanism must contain a term that does not scale with amplification, must use non-square-class (gauge-charged) couplings, must read the arithmetic point, and for `ζ`'s own zeros must bound `Δ`, `M_f`, `𝓔` or the field.
- **Wall 3.** Prove a transfer, sharp boundary or reconstruction on a stated class, norm and limit, and show that the source satisfies its hypothesis. For 3.14, selection below the zero spacing or a computed window-energy bound. For 3.15, exclusion of infinite-height escape. For 3.18, a non-multiplicative localizer. For 3.19, the odd-sector sign beyond the conductor horizon at zero ordinates. For 3.21, payment of the pole-neutralized continuum form. For 3.23, archimedean payment of the sum of local prime forms.
- **Wall 14.** Construct the arithmetic object independently and prove fidelity, then a forcing law with input outside every mapped class: invariant carrier positivity; resonance and below-horizon twists; non-localizing positivity; one-variable positive systems and their cone; positive and rational deformations and their quantization; collided positivity, computed or not; zero-side detection; counting reformulations of the sign; square-class data; identities, square theorem, budget, involution; scalar-counterterm renormalization; local positivity at single primes. The law must fail on the relaxation models, the coupled-class deformations, the golden datum and complex fake, the sign and Liouville twins and multi-pair synthetic configurations; it must reproduce the `κ = 1` collapse, `κ ≥ 3`, `W = D − Δ`, the log-Sobolev norm, `E_f ≍ M_f`, transverse equilibrium and quantization, the torsion survival cone `{0}`, the polar hyperplane and the `𝔽_q` budget slopes; and it must supply a form that is positive and equivariant at once — through a coupled counterterm of lower rank with a Hecke-covariant finite part, an inequality for `M_f`, or a field bound from an alignment law at zero ordinates.
