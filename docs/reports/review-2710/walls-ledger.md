# Walls ledger

Status: 2026-10-27, corpus `6046e5478` with working tree. Every obstacle in the corpus belongs to one of three core families:

- **Wall 1 — critical arithmetic sign.** Estimates, signs and oscillatory payment at the critical endpoint, at the gauge point `0`, and at the ordinates of zeros.
- **Wall 3 — transfer.** Readings, norms, completions, limits, shared budgets, localization, selection, regulators, and the passage from local to global.
- **Wall 14 — realization and forcing.** Faithful arithmetic objects, correspondences, invariants, counts, deformations, renormalizations, polarization, the base, and the forcing of the actual source into its blind carrier.

**Organizing principle.** At an off-line reflection pair `{ρ, 1 − ρ̄}` the local form has signature `(1, 1)`; multiplicative correspondences act by boosts, the successor unipotently, the functional equation and `σ_γ` by the swap, rotations of the circle elliptically but on the wrong dual variable. The test space carries Weil's form `W` (equivariant; its positivity is RH) and the collided form `D` (positive, computed; its equivariance is RH); `Re W = D − Δ`. Every pairing of the explicit formula is a regularized self-energy, and **the rank of the counterterm is a property of the regulator**: the prime place alone keeps no positivity under any regulator tested; the archimedean place pays the prime place below the spacing of the atoms `log n`, with matched leading constants, and nothing arithmetic is seen there. Wall 1 measures the hyperbolic part as a rate, a sign, a gauge charge or an alignment; Wall 3 governs how positivity transfers between readings, regulators, widths and places; Wall 14 asks what structure makes one form positive and equivariant at once.

**Twenty-four exhaustion laws** bound every mechanism tested:

- **Gauge.** Multiplicative data determine a source up to its gauge orbit; a coupling is gauge blind iff it is of square class; the finite intersection of each sign twin is ζ's in the gauge `x ↦ ωx`; at atomic resolution twins' Weil forms differ only by the archimedean charged–neutral cross term, and on nonnegative tests `ζ` is the orbit minimum (1.18, 3.1).
- **Hyperbolicity.** Positivity invariant under a boost at an off-line pair forces isotropy (14.10, 14.17).
- **Resonance.** Elliptic localization in correspondence-closed carriers is resonance; `σ_γ` is the only reflection-compatible affine localizer; rotations of the circle localize in `ℚ/ℤ`, not at zero ordinates (3.18, 14.17).
- **Conductor.** Twisted positivity is a theorem only below `e^a ≲ log γ/4`, where it is source blind; the companion certificate needs `e^{2δa} > 768A log γ`; the two never meet (3.19).
- **Localization.** Positivity constrains a fibre only where the carrier localizes (3.18).
- **Dominated twins.** On nonnegative tests the Weil form is antitone in the source (1.4, 1.18).
- **Linear loss.** Moments, family averages, products and relative powers lose linearly; twists are rate neutral; dyadic amplification is soft (1.15, 1.17).
- **Pole order and one variable.** Pole order pays inserted zeros; quartets contract only against a real anchor (1.16, 14.13, 14.19, 14.20, 14.23).
- **Dilution.** Packets on independent ordinates buy at most their pole share; every finite lattice cloud a finite multiple (14.11, 3.19).
- **Collision.** Kernel counts, coupled counts, escape forms and `D` pair `ρ` with `ρ̄`; their positivity sees no sign (3.1, 14.21).
- **Counting.** Every counting currency is RH; Stepanov auxiliaries need a lattice spectrum (1.1, 14.4, 14.6, 14.18).
- **Deformation.** Positive deformations keep the off-line divisor and are irreversible (14.22, 14.25).
- **Survival.** Positivity passes a scale limit exactly on the null cone of the counterterm (14.24).
- **Regulator.** Abel radius: identity counterterm, survival `{0}`; spacing radius: scalar, covariant up to tame ramification, indefinite; horizon: polar on uniform profiles, identity on torsion orbits, unbounded rank (3.24, 14.24).
- **Partner rank.** A coupled partner pays at most as many prime directions as the rank of its own counterterm; the archimedean place pays only the arithmetic point (14.27).
- **Covariance.** A diagonal renormalization is Hecke covariant iff scalar; covariance and survival exclude each other (14.28).
- **Local to global.** Each prime's tower form has a local Hodge index; the sum has no `ℓ²` ceiling; summing in absolute value is the square-root threshold; there is no common ladder (3.23, 14.29).
- **Atomic.** Below the spacing the archimedean self-energy pays the prime form with a rank-one pole counterterm, for every sign twin alike (3.25).
- **Completion.** Counting reaches incomplete sums only through a period; the arithmetic point has none (1.20).
- **Detection.** Zero-side objects see each off-line zero at its own height and bound nothing (1.21, 3.22, 14.26).
- **Strip.** Uniform ceilings on terminals and on Hecke defects, and every graded reading, are zero-free strips (1.0, 1.16, 1.21).
- **Exterior.** The anti-alignment proved at `σ > 1` does not continue into the strip (1.21).
- **Moment.** Zero-ordinate moments are Rosati pairings; alignment counts bound frequencies, not individual zeros (3.14, 14.30).
- **Statistics.** Averages, proportions and the census exclude no zero; only a quantized statistic below one quantum would (3.14).

Over `𝔽_q` one structure escapes all of them: a polarization of `C × C` with all places acting on one Frobenius ladder, positive and Frobenius-equivariant at once, with finite diagonal self-intersection and a lattice spectrum. Over `ℤ` identities, the square theorem and the budget are free; a positive form, the field and the moment of inertia are computed; local Hodge index holds at each prime; the archimedean place pays the prime place below the spacing; nothing yet reaches the spacing, carries the gauge into a form, joins the transverse ladders, or bounds the field.

## 6. What counts as crossing a wall

- **Wall 1.** Insert a newly proved source estimate into an existing receiver with every tail and quantifier intact. Any graded gain is a strip; a non-graded output — a non-isolation law, a form gauge margin, an exclusion of deep zeros at non-exceptional ordinates, a non-uniform field bound — is progress of another kind. A new mechanism must contain a term that does not scale with amplification, use charged couplings, read the arithmetic point, and for `ζ`'s own zeros bound `Δ`, `M_f`, `𝓔` or the field non-uniformly.
- **Wall 3.** Prove a transfer on a stated class, norm, regulator and width, and show that the source satisfies its hypothesis. For 3.14, selection below the zero spacing. For 3.19, the odd-sector sign beyond the horizon or the interior anti-alignment law. For 3.25, archimedean payment at the spacing scale and into the overlap regime.
- **Wall 14.** Construct the arithmetic object independently, prove fidelity, then a forcing law with input outside every mapped class: invariant carriers; resonance and below-horizon twists; non-localizing positivity; one-variable systems; deformations; collided positivity; zero-side detection; counting reformulations; square-class data; identities, square theorem, budget; prime-only renormalization under any tested regulator; coupling of the torsion block; diagonal covariant counterterms; atomic-resolution positivity; local positivity at single primes. The law must fail on the relaxation models, the golden datum, complex fake, sign twins and synthetic configurations; reproduce `κ = 1`, `κ ≥ 3`, `W = D − Δ`, `E_f ≍ M_f`, equilibrium and quantization, the regulator table, the partner-rank law, the covariance law, the atomic stability law and its gauge blindness, and the `𝔽_q` slopes; and supply a form positive and equivariant at once — at the spacing scale, through a base, or through a non-uniform field bound.
