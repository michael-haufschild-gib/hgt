# Walls ledger

Status: 2026-10-27, corpus `3fd844e5f` with working tree. Every obstacle in the corpus belongs to one of three core families:

- **Wall 1 — critical arithmetic sign.** Estimates, signs and oscillatory payment at the critical endpoint, at the gauge point `0`, and at the ordinates of zeros.
- **Wall 3 — transfer.** Readings, norms, completions, limits, shared budgets, localization, selection, regulators, widths and overlap, and the passage from local to global.
- **Wall 14 — realization and forcing.** Faithful arithmetic objects, correspondences, invariants, counts, deformations, renormalizations, polarization, trickle-down, symmetry, and the forcing of the actual source into its blind carrier.

**Organizing principle.** At an off-line reflection pair `{ρ, 1 − ρ̄}` the local form has signature `(1, 1)`; Hecke correspondences act by boosts (isometries, conformal factor one), the successor unipotently, the functional equation and `σ_γ` by the swap, rotations of the circle on the wrong dual variable. The test space carries Weil's form `W` (equivariant; its positivity is RH) and the collided form `D` (positive, computed; its equivariance is RH); `Re W = D − Δ`. In Lorentzian terms RH is hyperbolicity of the scaling-surface Grams; for Metzler forms hyperbolicity is the trickle-down inequality, and the Metzler sign holds for the atomic kernel only in the Euler gauge. On atomic families the decisive parameter is the **overlap ratio** `K = w/δ_N`: below `c₀` the archimedean place pays the prime place and the pole, for every bounded twist alike; at `K = ∞` positivity is RH; bounded overlap is open. Wall 1 measures the hyperbolic part as a rate, a sign, a gauge charge or a quantum; Wall 3 governs how positivity transfers between readings, regulators, widths, overlaps and places; Wall 14 asks what structure makes one form positive and equivariant at once.

**Thirty exhaustion laws** bound every mechanism tested:

- **Gauge.** Multiplicative data determine a source up to its gauge orbit; a coupling is gauge blind iff it is of square class; the finite intersection of each sign twin is `ζ`'s in the gauge `x ↦ ωx` (1.18, 3.1).
- **Universality.** In the separated regime (`K ≤ c₀`) the Markov form sees only `|a(k)| ≤ Λ(k)`: every bounded twist, multiplicative or not, satisfies the same stability law (1.18, 3.25).
- **Diamagnetism.** On `|·|`-closed families `ζ` is the ground state of its gauge orbit; a margin for `ζ` needs the pole, signed bumps or overlap (1.18).
- **Polar matching.** Divergences read only the density of the gauge: every finite-flip twin is matched as `ζ` is (1.18, 14.31).
- **Hyperbolicity.** Positivity invariant under a boost at an off-line pair forces isotropy (14.10, 14.17).
- **Conformality.** No correspondence of the atoms over `ℤ` is conformal with factor `≠ 1`; Hecke maps are isometries (14.33).
- **Resonance.** Elliptic localization in correspondence-closed carriers is resonance; `σ_γ` is the only reflection-compatible affine localizer; rotations of the circle localize in `ℚ/ℤ` (3.18, 14.17).
- **Localization.** Positivity constrains a fibre only where the carrier localizes (3.18).
- **Conductor.** Twisted positivity is a theorem only below `e^a ≲ log γ/4`, where it is source blind; the companion certificate needs `e^{2δa} > 768A log γ`; they never meet (3.19).
- **Dominated twins.** On nonnegative tests the Weil form is antitone in the source (1.4).
- **Linear loss.** Moments, family averages, products and relative powers lose linearly; dyadic amplification is soft (1.15, 1.17).
- **Pole order and one variable.** Pole order pays inserted zeros; quartets contract only against a real anchor (1.16, 14.13, 14.19, 14.23).
- **Dilution.** Packets on independent ordinates buy at most their pole share; every finite lattice cloud a finite multiple (14.11).
- **Collision.** Kernel counts, coupled counts, escape forms and `D` pair `ρ` with `ρ̄` (3.1, 14.21).
- **Counting.** Every counting currency is RH; Stepanov auxiliaries need a lattice spectrum (1.1, 14.4, 14.6, 14.18).
- **Closure.** The Euler-product closure of nonnegative local data is Lorentzian for every datum; it cannot select (14.2, 14.29).
- **Deformation.** Positive deformations keep the off-line divisor and are irreversible (14.22, 14.25).
- **Survival.** Positivity passes a limit exactly on the null cone of the counterterm; a Gårding inequality is survival with a finite-rank counterterm (14.24).
- **Regulator.** Abel: identity, survival `{0}`; spacing radius: scalar, indefinite; horizon: polar on uniform profiles, tensor and covariant on class profiles, unbounded rank under exhaustion (3.24, 14.24, 14.28).
- **Partner rank.** A coupled partner pays at most as many prime directions as its own counterterm rank (14.27).
- **Covariance.** A diagonal renormalization is Hecke covariant iff scalar (14.28).
- **Local to global.** Each prime's tower form has a local Hodge index but at least two positive squares on a ladder; summing in absolute value is the square-root threshold; sums of hyperbolic forms are not hyperbolic (3.23, 14.32).
- **Separated payment.** Up to overlap ratio `c₀` the archimedean self-energy (constant one) pays the prime form and, on independent-profile sections, the pole — for every twin alike (3.25).
- **Overlap.** At unbounded overlap (fixed width) positivity of the atomic Weil Grams is RH; their index counts the distinct right zeros (3.26).
- **Completion.** Counting reaches incomplete sums only through a period; the arithmetic point has none (1.20).
- **Detection.** Zero-side objects see each off-line zero at its own height and bound nothing; finite indices are paid by energy, not bounded by arithmetic (1.16, 1.21, 3.22).
- **Strip.** Uniform ceilings on terminals and Hecke defects, and every graded reading, are zero-free strips (1.0, 1.16, 1.21).
- **Exterior.** The anti-alignment proved at `σ > 1` does not continue into the strip (1.21).
- **Moment.** Zero-ordinate moments are Rosati pairings; alignment counts bound frequencies, not individual zeros (3.14, 14.30).
- **Statistics.** Averages, proportions, alignment counts and the census exclude no zero (3.14).

Over `𝔽_q` one structure escapes all of them: a polarization of `C × C` in which Frobenius acts conformally with factor `q`, Bézout makes the Gram Metzler, and the trickle-down inequality holds on one Frobenius ladder. Over `ℤ` identities, the square theorem and the budget are free; positive forms, the field and the moment of inertia are computed; the archimedean place pays the prime place and the pole while atoms are separated; the horizon renormalization is covariant with local finite parts; the atomic kernel is Metzler exactly at the Euler gauge. Nothing yet crosses bounded overlap, supplies trickle-down links, provides a conformal correspondence, or bounds source work below the quantum.

## 6. What counts as crossing a wall

- **Wall 1.** Insert a newly proved source estimate into an existing receiver with every tail and quantifier intact. Any graded gain is a strip; a non-graded output — a non-isolation law, a form gauge margin, an arithmetic upper bound on source work off a sparse set, a non-uniform field bound — is progress of another kind. A new mechanism must contain a term that does not scale with amplification, use charged data (pole, non-ratio lags, finite parts, successor phases), and for `ζ`'s own zeros bound `Δ`, `M_f`, `𝓔` or the work non-uniformly.
- **Wall 3.** Prove a transfer on a stated class, norm, regulator, width and overlap ratio, and show that the source satisfies its hypothesis. For 3.14, selection below the zero spacing. For 3.19, the odd-sector sign or the interior anti-alignment law. For 3.26, positivity at a fixed overlap ratio beyond `c₀`, uniformly in `N`. For 3.23, arithmetic trickle-down links.
- **Wall 14.** Construct the arithmetic object independently, prove fidelity, then a forcing law with input outside every mapped class: invariant carriers; resonance and below-horizon twists; non-localizing positivity; one-variable systems; deformations; collided positivity; zero-side detection and index bounds; counting reformulations; square-class data; identities, square theorem, budget, Euler-product closure; prime-only renormalization; coupling of the torsion block; diagonal covariant counterterms; separated-regime, section or `|·|`-closed positivity; local positivity at single primes; repaired or glued forms; isometric correspondences. The law must fail on the relaxation models, the golden datum (which fails `(TD_π)`), the complex fake, sign twins (which fail the Metzler sign) and synthetic configurations; reproduce `κ = 1`, `κ ≥ 3`, `W = D − Δ`, `E_f ≍ M_f`, equilibrium and quantization, the closed threshold `2`, the regulator table, the partner-rank law, the covariance law, the separated payment law and its universality, the tower sign law, and the `𝔽_q` slopes; and supply a form positive and equivariant at once — through bounded overlap, through trickle-down links, through a conformal correspondence, or through an upper bound on source work.
