# Route-unification ledger

**Status 2026-10-27, corpus `3fd844e5f` with working tree.** The 31 live routes, 17 candidates and PW-B are faces of three construction problems:

- **U1 — critical arithmetic cancellation**: the *rate* reading; pointwise, the *alignment* of `γ log p` at zero ordinates; globally, the *quantum* of source work.
- **U2 — complete signed positivity**: the *sign* reading; on atomic families, positivity across the *overlap ratio*; in Lorentzian terms, *hyperbolicity*.
- **U3 — faithful realization and forcing**: the *carrier* reading, with *invariant and deformation*, *counting*, *renormalization*, *field*, *gauge*, *trickle-down*, *symmetry* and *base* sub-readings.

All three ask one question in different currencies: **what forces the actual arithmetic source to be blind to off-line zeros?** The corpus states it through one potential `log|ξ|`, the characteristic circle, the atomic families `Σ x_n g(· − log n)`, and the scaling-surface Grams:

- **U2 is the positivity of the equivariant form.** On atomic families positivity is a theorem up to overlap ratio `c₀`, for every bounded twist alike, and is RH at unbounded overlap; on scaling-surface Grams it is hyperbolicity; for Metzler forms it is the trickle-down inequality.
- **U3 asks for a form positive and equivariant at once.** `D` is positive and computed; its equivariance defect is the moment of inertia; uniform caps are strips. The prime place renormalized at the horizon is covariant with local finite parts, positive at large primes, with deficits at small primes. The Lorentzian sign holds only in the Euler gauge; the conformal symmetry that proves Weil's theorem over `𝔽_q` has no counterpart among Hecke, power and successor maps.
- **U1 excludes by comparing a collided object with a Rosati one, a budget with a spectral count, a short prime sum with a zero-side reserve, or the source work with its quantum `2`.**

The readings are equivalent at their endpoints:

- **U1.** Escaped Möbius `θ`-decay, receiver rate `1/2 − θ`, `Θ ≤ 1 − θ`, the Hecke strip cap, dyadic rate `1 − 2θ`, `Re ξ′/ξ ≥ 0` on `Re s > 1 − θ` and prefix reading rates are one statement at each `θ`; RH is `θ = 1/2`; also cofinal source work `≤ 2`.
- **U2.** Orbit Hodge positivity iff the orbit is blind; zero negative index; positivity of the atomic spline Grams at every fixed width; hyperbolicity of every scaling-surface Gram.
- **U3.** The maximal blind ideal is everything; `M_f = 0` for every test; `𝓔 ≤ 2`; no right terminal; positive chirality at every ordinate.

**Fourteen readings cut across the families.**

- **The local type.** Hecke correspondences boost (isometries, conformal factor one), the successor is unipotent, `σ_γ` swaps, torsion rotations are elliptic in the additive dual; nothing over `ℤ` is conformal with factor `≠ 1`.
- **The gauge point.** Square-class couplings, the finite intersection, the separated regime, `|·|`-closed families, divergences and the Euler-product closure are gauge blind; the pole, non-ratio lags, finite parts, cells and the archimedean successor phase are charged; `ζ` is the ground state of its orbit on `|·|`-closed families.
- **The pairing.** Collided objects pair `ρ` with `ρ̄`, Rosati objects with `1 − ρ̄`; zero-ordinate moments are Rosati.
- **The regulator.** The rank of a counterterm is a property of the regulator: identity (Abel), scalar (spacing), polar (horizon, uniform profiles), tensor torsion ⊗ polar (horizon, classes), unbounded under exhaustion.
- **The finite part.** `−2γ` on uniform profiles, tower forms with local Euler constants on classes; nonnegative for `p ≥ 2^16` (numerically from `p = 47`), deficit `2d_p` at small primes.
- **The partner.** A coupled partner pays at most its own counterterm's rank.
- **The overlap ratio.** Up to `c₀` the archimedean place pays the prime place and the pole, universally; at `K ≈ 4` non-ratio lags appear; at `K = ∞` positivity is RH.
- **The sampling rate.** Below the spacing the zeros oversample the atomic Dirichlet polynomials, at the spacing they sample at the Nyquist rate, beyond it they undersample; the Nyquist rank bound puts the same transition at the diagonal `N ≍ T` [L/R].
- **The Lorentzian signature.** Hyperbolicity is not closed under sums; trickle-down defects are; every proved atomic positivity is the stability regime of a Metzler form.
- **The field, energy and work.** Computed, quantized with closed threshold `2`; indices paid by energy; uniform ceilings are strips.
- **The moment of inertia.** The Hecke defect of `D`; dyadic amplification is soft.
- **The alignment.** Beyond the conductor, at terminals and at the field threshold an off-line zero is a large value of a short prime polynomial; such values are rare; the implication from zero to alignment is proved only at `σ > 1`.
- **The central order.** Deformations detect zero-free ordinates and are irreversible.
- **The statistic.** Averages, proportions and alignment counts exclude no zero.

Sub-readings of U3: invariant and deformation — closed; counting — closed as a reduction; renormalization — closed for diagonal and coupled regulators, covariant with local finite parts at the horizon, open as a uniform lower bound on the survival space; field and work — exact thresholds, open as an upper bound; gauge — closed structurally and on separated forms, open on overlap forms; trickle-down — calibrated over `𝔽_q`, open for `ζ`; symmetry — no conformal map; base — open. Identical receivers do not mean identical producers.

## 6. Consequences

1. **Count each producer once.**
   - a small-damping signed budget, equivalently positive gauge mass: PM, CTF, BVI, MC;
   - the positivity of the equivariant form: RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF, TT, ACS, SRT;
   - a residual heat-region sign: LA, WD, TH, HCF;
   - a forcing law into the maximal blind ideal: TG, RN, ACS, HF and all candidates;
   - **positivity at bounded overlap beyond universality**: AIP, HF, ACS, SRT, OWU (and through the atomic families every U2 route);
   - **trickle-down for the Euler-gauge form**: AIP, HF, ACS;
   - a uniform lower bound for the horizon finite part: AIP, HF, ACS;
   - an interior anti-alignment law with exceptional-set extinction, or an upper bound on source work: SRT, OWU, HF, ACS;
   - a non-uniform bound on `M_f` or the field: HF, ACS;
   - an actual linear budget: WL, MC.
2. **Finished below RH strength:** reformulation; carrier maximality; local-type classification including rotations and conformality; the forcing gap; invariants; packets and lattice clouds; budgets; contraction, collision, deformations; escape; counting and Nyquist sampling; the computation of `D` and `M_f` and the strip character of uniform caps; local detection; electrostatic forms, the closed quantum and index descent; alignment frequencies; zero moments as Rosati pairings; renormalization of the prime place under every tested regulator, including the covariant horizon tensor and its finite parts; coupling of the torsion block; covariance of diagonal counterterms; archimedean payment of the prime place and the pole at separated widths, universally; the RH-equivalence of fixed-width positivity; local Hodge index and its failure to sum; Lorentzian characterizations, the `𝔽_q` calibration and the gauge-fixing law; gauge-charged couplings and the source margin; the width currency; the census. New equivalent endpoints, graded readings, prime-only renormalizations, separated or universal positivities, constructed forms, coefficient margins, alignment counts, detectors and counting reformulations add nothing.
3. **The three families reduce to three constructions and one law.**
   - **U1 — an interior anti-alignment law** with extinction of the exceptional set, or an arithmetic upper bound on source work below the quantum `2`.
   - **U2 — the positivity of `W` at every overlap ratio**, approached from `c₀` by estimates that must stop being universal at `K_univ`.
   - **U3 — a form positive and equivariant at once**: trickle-down links for the Euler-gauge form, a conformal correspondence, or a base across the ladders.
   - **The common law:** arithmetic forces the zero spectrum to be elliptic — conjugation and the functional-equation reflection agree on the zeros. It appears as rate zero (U1), positivity of `W` at every width (U2), `M_f = 0`, `𝓔 ≤ 2`, no right terminal (U3), and — in Lorentzian terms — as the trickle-down inequality of a Metzler form whose sign hypothesis only `ζ` satisfies.
4. **Where the families meet: bounded overlap.** Below overlap ratio `c₀` the archimedean place pays the prime place and the pole for every bounded twist (U2/U3), the gauge cannot favour `ζ` (U1/U3), and the zeros oversample the Dirichlet polynomials; at `K ≈ 4` non-ratio lags carry the gauge; at the Nyquist rate sampling stops being injective — the regime of mean values at `T ≍ N` where the alignment counts of U1 also live, and the trickle-down regime of the Metzler dichotomy [R]. The conductor horizon and the square-root threshold are the same phenomenon in the aperture and moment variables.
5. **Partial progress is family-specific.** U1 is blocked at quasi-RH and has frequency bounds and a closed quantum; U2 has windows, short cycles, local parts, averages, the census, twisted positivity, separation from Liouville, and universal separated positivity including the pole; U3 has computed positive forms, quantized electrostatics, the full regulator table with a covariant horizon and local finite parts, the Lorentzian characterizations and the gauge-fixing law. A result converting one family's progress into another's would be a mechanism: separated positivity carried past `K_univ` (U2 → U3), the Metzler sign turned into trickle-down (U3 → U2), or alignment counts turned into exclusion by an interior law (U1 → U3).
6. **Seventeen obstructions are common to every mechanism tested.** Depth homogeneity; gauge invariance (square class, finite intersection); universality; diamagnetism; polar matching; hyperbolicity; conformality; Landau–Fejér positivity and dilution; deformation rigidity; the counting law; the survival law; the regulator law; the partner-rank law; the covariance law; the local–global law (hyperbolic sums fail); the detection law; the strip law. Collided positivity escapes the first seven and sees nothing; Rosati positivity sees everything and is RH; separated archimedean payment comes closest from the analytic side — positive, covariant, pole included, and unselective because it is universal; the Euler-gauge Metzler form comes closest from the algebraic side — selective by hypothesis, positive only in its stability regime. Over `𝔽_q` Frobenius makes positivity and equivariance one; over `ℤ` none is known.
