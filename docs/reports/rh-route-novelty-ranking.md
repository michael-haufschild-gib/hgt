# RH proof-route novelty against published approaches

**Scope:** Every entry in the [RH proof-route ledger](rh-current-frontier-map.md): 31 live routes, 17 standalone candidates and PM's PW-B candidate sub-branch (49 entries). BVI and the other supporting frameworks are not routes and are excluded; retired mechanisms are in [killed routes](rh-killed-routes.md). The score judges the *whole proposed proof architecture* (source, distinctive mechanism, RH receiver), not only its open lemma. These are descriptions of proposed routes, not claims of proof or priority. Proof status lives in the ledger.

**M (prior-art proximity, 0–5):**

| M | Meaning |
|:---:|---|
| 0 | No published RH approach with this receiver or this mechanism found, even as a suggestion. |
| 1 | Only remote precedent: an ingredient exists in the literature, but not used toward RH this way. |
| 2 | Recognizable published family; the producing mechanism is distinct. |
| 3 | Broadly similar route already proposed; our version changes the producer or a key coordinate. |
| 4 | Close to an established programme or classical criterion; our contribution is a refinement. |
| 5 | Classical RH route or equivalent, or a classical route in new words. |

A route whose established content is a classical criterion re-expressed in new coordinates is scored as classical. In particular, the **zero-indexed candidates** (HJ, SEC, BSH, CDC; partly IRK, PCS) read the critical-line symmetry `ρ ↦ 1−ρ̄` through a new quantity (for example `n^{2Reρ−1}=1` or `|2^ρ|=|2^{1−ρ̄}|`) and have no pre-divisor mechanism; new vocabulary does not lower their score. M is a literature-search judgement (web search, October 2026), never proof that no one has mentioned the idea.

**Novelty rank** orders entries by how new the proposed proof chain is in substance: M first, then whether the distinctive mechanism is actually formulated. An entry with low M but no defined mechanism ranks below concrete M2 routes. L = live route; C = candidate.

**Reading:** No entry reaches M0. The closest are WD, TG and JAS (M1), whose distinctive mechanisms have no published counterpart and whose receivers have only remote precedent. FU, RP, TT, ACT, HCF and RN (M2) add a new producing mechanism to a recognizable receiver family. RFHM and WBF have low M only because their mechanism is not yet formulated. Twenty-six entries (M4–M5) are classical criteria or established programmes, refined or restated.
