# RH proof-route novelty against published approaches

**Scope:** All 18 live routes and 24 candidates in the [RH proof-route ledger](rh-current-frontier-map.md); retired mechanisms excluded. Rank compares the *entire proposed proof architecture*—arithmetic/theta source, distinctive mechanism, RH receiver—not just its unproved lemma. These are conditional descriptions of proposed routes, not claims of proof, priority, or exhaustive literature coverage.

**M (mainstreamness):** Prior-art proximity of the *whole proposed route*, independent of correctness or proof status. 0 = no close external RH approach found, even as a suggestion; 1 = only remote precedent; 2 = recognizable proposed family with a distinct mechanism; 3 = broadly similar route already proposed; 4 = close established program; 5 = classical RH route/equivalent. A zero is a provisional literature-search judgment, never proof that no one has ever mentioned the idea. L = live route; C = candidate. Detailed proof status is in the [frontier ledger](rh-current-frontier-map.md). Novelty rank separately assesses the substance of the proposed proof chain; an unfamiliar reformulation can be M0 yet rank low.

## Published comparison baselines

- Explicit formula and classical RH equivalents: [Bombieri, *The Riemann Hypothesis*](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/riemann.pdf).
- Weil positivity / spectral and cohomological programs: [Connes–Consani, *Weil positivity and Trace formula*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13771); [Connes–Consani–Moscovici, *Zeta Spectral Triples*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22755); [Deninger, arithmetic Hodge strategy](https://www.uni-muenster.de/SFB878/publications/files/php7aMxKR3029.pdf).
- Approximation and Sonine spaces: [Báez-Duarte, strengthened Nyman–Beurling criterion](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202141); [Burnol, Sonine spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0105120).
- Screw and Li receivers: [Suzuki, screw function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03682); [Suzuki, Weil form via screw function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09096); [Suzuki, Li coefficients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05779).
- Jensen, heat, recurrence: [Griffin–Ono–Rolen–Zagier, Jensen polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07321); [Rodgers–Tao, de Bruijn–Newman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914); [Nakamura, Bagchi recurrence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.1778).
- Horizontal zero correlation: [Baluyot–Goldston–Suriajaya–Turnage-Butterbaugh](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14545).
- Theta spectral route: [Hedenmalm, *Spectral interpretation of Riemann zeta zeros*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17494).
- Zeta/statistical-mechanics and Lee–Yang proposals: [Contucci–Knauf, *The Low Activity Phase of Some Dirichlet Series*](https://empslocal.ex.ac.uk/people/staff/mrwatkin/zeta/contucci-knauf.pdf); [Wolf, RH and physics survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.1214); [Lee–Yang, circle theorem](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.87.410).
