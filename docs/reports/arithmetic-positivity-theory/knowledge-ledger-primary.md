# Arithmetic positivity: primary research ledger

This ledger records mathematical facts, failed mechanisms, and precise unresolved questions. It does not replace the [research programme](research-foundational.md) or its individual proof cards.

## Source facts

1. **The correction is literal.** `factorialMuntzBoundaryCorrected_sub_ramp` identifies the corrected source minus the compact ramp with `factorialMuntzCorrectedTail`, including the nonpositive endpoint convention. Source: [FactorialMuntzUniformTail.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/FactorialMuntzUniformTail.lean).
2. **The collar costs no additional scale power.** The same module proves \(|T_n(x)|\le12e^{3x/2}/\sqrt{n+1}\) for \(x\le0\), with \(T_n=0\) for \(x>0\). Readouts are admitted for \(\operatorname{Re}s>-1\). This supplies value control on a wider strip, not a bound on the complete zero-sampling sum.
3. **Smoothing retains the actual tail.** [FactorialMuntzTailSmoothing.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/FactorialMuntzTailSmoothing.lean) gives exact convolution readout factorization and a value envelope uniform in the smoothing index. Its smooth-test derivative constants are not asserted uniform in that index.
4. **Mixed comparison is already unconditional.** [XiWeilLineConjHilbert.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/XiWeilLineConjHilbert.lean) supplies Cauchy–Schwarz using the independent absolute square-sampling form. This does not assert positivity of the signed Weil form.
5. **The arithmetic scale has a concrete translation certificate.** The new field's [phase](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailPhase.lean), [weighted mass](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailWeightedMass.lean), [translation](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailTranslation.lean), and [logarithmic integration](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailLogTranslation.lean) modules prove the exact identity, the uniform mass bound \(96/N\), \(D(h)\le C\min\{1/N,h\}\), and \(\int_0^1D(h)\,dh/h\le C(1+\log N)/N\). The chosen translation constant is \(C=4050e^6+25344\); optimizing that numerical constant is unnecessary for the scale argument.
6. **The corrected-source zero vectors occupy at most one dimension.** The exact readout \(F_{H_n}(\rho)=c_n/\rho\) gives \(F_{\sum_n a_nH_n}(\rho)=(\sum_n a_nc_n)/\rho\) for every finite coefficient family. For a common smoothing index, all vectors acquire the same kernel multiplier and still span at most one dimension. If \(\sum_n a_nc_n=0\), the smoothed compact-ramp combination and the negative smoothed-tail combination have identical zero readouts. This exact reduction exposes the cross-scale tail Gram problem; positivity on the corrected-source family alone cannot supply a dense positive test family. The claim concerns the raw family or a fixed common smoothing index, not an arbitrary mixture of smoothing indices.
7. **The smaller correction budget is certified on the literal zeta carrier.** [FactorialCorrectedSampling.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialCorrectedSampling.lean) defines the multiplicity-weighted square mass, proves its summability for every actual corrected source, and bounds it by \(C_H/N\) for every smoothing index. The proof uses the exact reciprocal readout, actual zero separation, and the convergent genus-one majorant.

## Boundaries

- A bound at every fixed zero does not bound a sum over the full zero divisor. Uniformity in height and summability must be proved.
- A smoothing-uniform value bound does not supply a smoothing-uniform derivative bound. Endpoint jumps are a specific obstruction to an unqualified \(H^1\) argument.
- The diagonal sampling bounds and Cauchy–Schwarz alone leave normalized cross-scale couplings of constant order. The factors \(N^{-1/2}\) cancel under diagonal normalization; larger scales do not by themselves give a small off-diagonal operator.
- The absolute square-sampling budget \(O(\log N/N)\) is sufficient for the factorial mixed application but may be stronger than necessary. A suitably uniform \(o(\log^2N/N)\) budget would also make the mixed term lower order than the known positive tail diagonal.

## Logarithmic energy mechanism

The [sampling proof](../../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/arithmetic-positivity-factorial-log-sampling.md) establishes the following source-specific statements, with all upper sampling, lower logarithmic scale, and relative-margin bounds certified in Lean (`FactorialTailSampling.lean` and `FactorialTailRelativeMargin.lean`).

- The actual tail is \(a_ne^{3x/2}R_n(Ne^{-x})\mathbf1_{x\le0}\), with \(a_n\le15/\sqrt N\), \(|R_n|\le25/32\), and Lipschitz constant at most \(14\) on nonnegative arguments.
- For every Mellin weight \(\sigma\in[-1/4,5/4]\), its weighted translation square energy satisfies \(D_{n,\sigma}(h)\le C\min\{N^{-1},h\}\) for \(0<h\le1\). This retains the endpoint jump.
- Integrating against \(dh/h\) gives \(O((1+\log N)/N)\). Plancherel converts this to logarithmic Fourier energy; local zero counts and the holomorphic disk mean-value inequality convert that to absolute square sampling.
- Consequently \(\mathcal Z(T_n)+\mathcal Z(U_{n,k})\le C(1+\log N)/N\) for every \(n,k\). [FactorialTailSampling.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailSampling.lean) certifies the bound and summability on the literal zeta carrier. The smoothing threshold in the proposed upper bound is unnecessary.
- Existing positive tail energy forces a matching lower bound for fine smoothing. The sampling mass and signed tail energy both have sharp order \(\log N/N\), and their ratio to physical square mass has order \(\log N\). An \(O(1/N)\) spectral budget for this family is false.
- The same two-sided bounds give an absolute \(\delta>0\) with \(\operatorname{Re}\mathcal W(U_{n,k},U_{n,k})\ge\delta\mathcal Z(U_{n,k})\) eventually in \(n\), then in \(k\). This is a relative sign margin for actual individual sources. It does not control all cross-scale Gram matrices or imply positivity of their linear span.

## Rejected dependencies

- `norm_quantizationCompletion_le_of_le` in `WeilWorkQuantizationRadialGrowth.lean` assumes `WeilPositiveFull`. It cannot supply an independent positivity proof here. The independent absolute zero-sampling comparison remains usable.
- The existing public disk mean-square theorems in `HolomorphicBallMeanSq.lean` and `HolomorphicBallL2Submean.lean` assume an entire function. The actual tail readout is holomorphic on a half-plane; applying those declarations directly would strengthen the domain without proof. Near zero, \(\omega(h)\le1/h\); beyond unit displacement, the square-mass bound controls the cost.
- [FactorialTailFourierEnergy.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailFourierEnergy.lean) admits the actual complexified weighted source to \(L^1\cap L^2\), identifies its positive Gamma integral with physical mass plus jump energy, and proves the logarithmic Fourier bound with constant \(C_\Gamma(96+C+384A)\). Integrability of the spectral density is proved before its integral is bounded. The constant is uniform over the whole enlarged Mellin strip; sampling the actual zeros is still a separate step.
- [FactorialTailReadout.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailReadout.lean) proves holomorphy on \(\Re s>-1\) and the literal identity \(F_{T_n}(\sigma+it)=\widehat{g_{n,\sigma}}(-t/(2\pi))\). Its zeroth coefficient supplies the local circle estimate. Localizing the polar-integration proof is therefore sufficient; a new entire-function extension is unnecessary. The raw tail's half-plane holomorphy can also be obtained from the exact corrected-minus-ramp readout: the corrected readout is analytic on \(\operatorname{Re}s>-1\), and the compact ramp transform is entire.
- [XiGlobalPacketDensity.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/XiGlobalPacketDensity.lean) supplies `exists_xiUnitHeightPacket_global_log_bound` for every real height and every multiplicity copy, including bounded and negative heights. ## Certified foundational pillars and remaining frontier

The [Bohr coercivity paper proof](../../drafts/pipeline/2-fully-proven/arithmetic-positivity-factorial-bohr-coercivity.md) and its Lean certificates ([FactorialCollarIrrationalityAndSpacing.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialCollarIrrationalityAndSpacing.lean), [GeneralDivisorBernoulliFilterClassification.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/GeneralDivisorBernoulliFilterClassification.lean), [DyadicBernoulliFilterAndBohrCoercivity.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/DyadicBernoulliFilterAndBohrCoercivity.lean)) isolate an exact arithmetic mechanism. The phase is \(\Phi_N(v)=P(\lambda_Nv)-P(Nv)\), where \(\lambda_N^3=N^2(4N+1)/4\). Each \(\lambda_N\) is irrational (`irrational_collarLambda`), so shifted and integer harmonic families are orthogonal in the Bohr mean (`bohr_cross_mean_shifted_unshifted_eq_zero`), and \(k\lambda_N = \ell\lambda_M \iff k^3 N^2(4N+1) = \ell^3 M^2(4M+1)\) (`collarLambda_rat_mul_eq_iff_cubic`). The dyadic Bernoulli filter \((4,-2,1/4)\) is the unique 3-scale filter on \(\{1,2,4\}\) annihilating both polynomial collar drifts (`dyadic_stencil_unique_iff`), while no 2-scale filter on \(\{1,2\}\) exists (`two_scale_stencil_no_simultaneous_cancellation`). Its dominant first harmonic gives \(\mathcal B(\sum b_N\Phi_N)\ge\sum|b_N|^2/(8\pi^4)\) (`factorialBohr_first_harmonic_coercivity`) and linear independence of the actual tails.

The [finite-window paper proof](../../drafts/pipeline/2-fully-proven/arithmetic-positivity-factorial-window-coercivity.md) and its Lean certificate ([FactorialTailWindowRieszCoercivity.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialTailWindowRieszCoercivity.lean)) strengthen this to physical norm equivalence. The exact collar has \(1/15\le\lambda_N-N\le1/12\), so \(\{\pm N,\pm\lambda_N\}\) is uniformly separated. Applying Montgomery–Vaughan within each harmonic layer on \([K,L]\) (\(K=390/\pi\), \(L=16K\)), then using first-harmonic dominance (\(\sqrt{L-K} - \frac{3}{4}\sqrt{L+K} \ge \sqrt{L}/8\)), proves two-sided Riesz bounds \(144A\sum|b_n|^2 \le \dots \le 225B\sum|b_n|^2\) (`physical_riesz_window_bounds`), uniformly over all finite coefficient families.

The [signed span paper proof](../../drafts/pipeline/2-fully-proven/arithmetic-positivity-factorial-span-coercivity.md) and its Lean certificates ([EnvelopeCriticalPrimeTransitionAndDilationGain.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/EnvelopeCriticalPrimeTransitionAndDilationGain.lean), [NonstationaryMellinLowFrequencyExclusion.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/NonstationaryMellinLowFrequencyExclusion.lean)) control the whole high-scale span. Writing \(f_b=e^{3x/2}\sum b_N\Phi_N(e^{-x})\mathbf1_{x\le0}\), they prove \(\Re\mathcal W(f_b,f_b)\ge((A/2)\log M-C_{\text{tot}})\sum|b_N|^2\) (`span_weil_coercivity_lower_bound`) for coefficients supported on \(N\ge M\). Dilation-uniform Bessel bounds give \(|C_b(\log r)|\le B r^{-3/2}\sum|b|^2\), hence a convergent full prime budget for every envelope exponent \(\beta>1/2\) (`summable_envelopePrimeMajorant_of_gt_half`), with an exact divergence phase transition at \(\beta\le 1/2\) (`summable_envelopeLogMajorant_iff`). Unit-interval bounds control both polar branches (`polar_readout_product_bound`). Nonstationary Mellin phase bounds (`nonstationary_mellin_bound`) remove the low-frequency band \(|\xi|\le 1/2\), forcing logarithmic Gamma energy. Density and all-source extension remain unresolved.

The [complete ramp–tent margin paper proof](../../drafts/pipeline/2-fully-proven/arithmetic-positivity-factorial-ramp-gram-margin.md) and its Lean certificates ([SourceRelativeSchurMarginAndPerturbationCalculus.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/SourceRelativeSchurMarginAndPerturbationCalculus.lean), [RankOnePoleReadoutAndZeroNullspace.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/RankOnePoleReadoutAndZeroNullspace.lean), [FactorialRampSmoothingAndGramMargin.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/FactorialRampSmoothingAndGramMargin.lean)) derive \(\mathcal Z(H_{n,k})\le C_H/N\) from the literal rank-one readout \(1/(2\sqrt N\rho)\), prove the sharp Krein relative stability threshold \(\varepsilon < (\sqrt{1+\delta}-1)^2\) (`relative_perturbation_pos_iff_lt_critical`, `twoMode_relative_margin_threshold_sharp`), establish the smoothed compact-ramp lower bound \(\Re\mathcal W(r_{n,k},r_{n,k})\ge\log(n+1)/(4096(n+1))\) (`factorialMuntzSmoothedRamp_weil_log_lower`), and transfer it to the two-dimensional Gram margin \(Q_n(c,d)\ge c^2\log N/(8192N)+Nd^2/2\) (`factorialRampTent_gram_margin_of_tendsto`). Furthermore, [MarkovPoleHyperbolicCapacityAndApertureThresholds.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/arithmetic-positivity-theory/MarkovPoleHyperbolicCapacityAndApertureThresholds.lean) proves exact orthogonal parity decoupling of the hyperbolic pole plane (`hyperbolicPoleForm_even_odd_decoupling`), isolating anti-Markovian pole instability to the odd parity sector (`weil_odd_nonneg_iff_sinh_sq_le_two_markovForm`).
