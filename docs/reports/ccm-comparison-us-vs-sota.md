# Our Lean corpus vs. Connes–Consani–Moscovici

## Scope and verdict

“CCM” below denotes the Connes–Consani and Connes–Consani–Moscovici programme, not every RH route in this repository. Comparison is theorem-by-theorem against the cited papers, with Lean declarations checked in source after focused `scripts/formal_tools.py research` searches. Corpus reference: `206d504e9`. No build or proof-dependency audit was run for this report. “Not located” means no matching *end-to-end statement* was found in this review, not a proof of absence from every module.

**Verdict.** The corpus formalizes much more than surrounding algebra: the zeta-cycle characterization, compressed circle spectral triple, exact restricted Weil form/operator, Laurent approximation, and several archimedean/semilocal ingredients are present. It also proves quantitative and structural facts not asserted in the surveyed CCM papers. The conspicuous missing *published-result* closures are the exact arbitrary-finite-place Sonin-space isomorphism, the 2020 Sonin-trace domination inequality, and an integrated Lean counterpart of the 2025 normalized `D_log` determinant theorem. Separately, CCM itself leaves ground-state simple-evenness and convergence to Ξ open; these are research obstacles, not merely formalization backlog.

## Status key

| Label | Meaning |

| Substantial partial | Key exact pieces exist, but a published conclusion is not connected end-to-end. |

## Published results still lacking an identified complete Lean counterpart

1. **2020 Sonin-trace domination.** [Connes–Consani, *Weil positivity and Trace formula, the archimedean place*, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.13771v1) proves W∞(g*g*) ≥ Tr(ϑ(g)Sϑ(g)*) when g is supported in [2^(−1/2), 2^(1/2)] and its Fourier transform vanishes at i/2 and 0. The corpus has archimedean trace identities and Sonin compression/trace-class pieces, but the exact test-space, support, moment conditions, and domination inequality were **not located together**. The positive trace carrier [`SoninSemilocalProlateFinitePart`](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/SoninSemilocalProlateFinitePart.lean#L1) explicitly contains no prime, Euler, Gamma, or Weil term; its positivity alone does not prove that inequality.

2. **Full finite-S semilocal Sonin stability.** [CCM, *Zeta zeros and prolate wave operators*, Theorem 4.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.18423v2) gives an exact Hilbertian isomorphism θₛ: Soninλ(ℝ) ≃ Soninλ(Xₛ) for every finite set of places S containing ∞, including Fourier/Hardy–Titchmarsh compatibility. The one-prime square, finite-Euler measure, and factorwise semilocal Hilbert tensor [`FieldOneSemilocalFactorwiseHilbertTensor`](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FieldOneSemilocalFactorwiseHilbertTensor.lean#L1) are real components, but no declaration asserting this literal θₛ isomorphism was located. A bare equivalence of weighted L² lines or of tensor completions is weaker.

3. **The published conditional `D_log` determinant package.** [CCM, *Zeta Spectral Triples*, Theorem 5.10](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1) assumes a simple even least eigenvector of the actual finite matrix; on that hypothesis it constructs a self-adjoint rank-one-modified logarithmic operator and proves det_reg(D_log^(λ,N) − z) = −i λ^(−iz) ξ̂(z), hence real zeros. The corpus has the literal matrix, parity blocks [`CCMPublishedRestrictedWeilParityBlocks`](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCMPublishedRestrictedWeilParityBlocks.lean), general radical/source-response completion [`CCMSourceResponseDeterminantDichotomy`](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCMSourceResponseDeterminantDichotomy.lean), and an odd-ground real-zero branch [`CCMPublishedOddGroundRealZeros`](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCMPublishedOddGroundRealZeros.lean). An integrated declaration identifying the paper’s exact D_log^(λ,N), its operator domain/metric, and the stated normalized **regularized** determinant was **not located**. Generic finite characteristic-polynomial or real-rootedness theorems do not supply this identity. [`CCMGrowingApertureMellin`](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCMGrowingApertureMellin.lean#L6) explicitly leaves the determinant identity and prolate-seed convergence outside its statements.

4. **Full metaplectic representation theorem.** For [Theorem 5.2(iv)](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.18423v2), the exact Jacobi recurrence and Gamma moment determinacy above should be linked to a constructed Lie algebra representation and its identification with the even part of the metaplectic representation. No such end-to-end Lean certificate was located. This is a formalization gap in a *published* result, unlike the speculative semilocal prolate operator, which the paper only proposes.

These are statement-level assessments, not accusations of erroneous local code. They also do not imply that formalizing them alone would prove RH.

## Genuinely open CCM-to-RH bridge

The [2025 CCM paper’s §8](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1) explicitly leaves two steps: (i) prove the relevant least eigenvalue simple and its eigenvector even throughout the needed finite family; (ii) prove quantitative proximity of the prolate candidate to the true Weil ground state strong enough for normalized Fourier transforms/determinants to converge to Ξ. Numerical zero agreement does not establish (ii). The corpus’s [real-rooted ground receiver](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCMRestrictedWeilFullFormRealRootedGround.lean#L458) makes the same logical boundary sharp: **if** normalized real-rooted ground transforms converge locally uniformly to Ξ in the required strip, then RH. This premise remains unproved. Proving only small-aperture positivity or only a real-zero theorem at each fixed aperture cannot cross that limit.

The [2024 semilocal prolate paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.18423v2) constructs semilocal Sonin stability and cyclic-pair data but presents the decisive semilocal prolate operator/Weil-positivity comparison as a programme, not a theorem already available for transcription. A Lean module that constructs a positive compression energy with no arithmetic source is not that comparison.

## Priority if the aim is faithful CCM formalization

1. Close literal 2020 Sonin-trace inequality, including support and both vanishing conditions, from the existing trace-class and archimedean identities.
2. State and prove arbitrary-finite-S θₛ Sonin-space isomorphism on actual semilocal spaces; use existing one-prime and finite-Euler facts as lemmas, not as substitutes for the theorem.
3. Package the conditional 2025 finite-matrix D_log self-adjointness and exact normalized regularized determinant in one statement matching Theorem 5.10.
4. Connect metaplectic coefficient and Gamma-moment certificates to the exact representation identification in Theorem 5.2(iv).

These four are published-result formalization targets. Ground-state simple-evenness, source-to-ground convergence, and semilocal Weil positivity are **new mathematical work** until proved on paper, regardless of how much adjacent Lean infrastructure exists.
