## Reading the ledger

A **wall** is a proved limit of a class of arguments: a position that no argument of the class passes. A wall is not a statement about ζ unless marked FRONTIER.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FRONTIER** | a proved statement about ζ or its L-functions; the position is where proof stands |
| **SHARP** | the limit of the class is proved from both sides (an equality, an infimum, or an attained example) |
| **ONE-SIDED** | a proved bound for the class; the exact position, or attainment, is not determined |
| **OPEN** | no proved position |

The three core families:

| Family | Question | The walls say |
|---|---|---|
| **P — producer** | Which exponent can an estimate with cancellation reach? | Each completion class and each sieve class has a fixed exponent it cannot pass; a lever valid on a class of models bites only where the class realizes no abscissa; the sign costs half a unit and the Gauss family meets the sign line only at periods 3, 4, 6 |
| **R — sign** | Where is the sign of Weil's form proved, and what can produce it? | The sign is certified at fixed apertures; every floor with a rate is the width in other coordinates; the critical state carries every exact property tested so far without contradiction; no property short of the sign separates ζ from its fakes except the nonnegative Euler logarithm on finite rational edits |
| **U — uniformity** | What excludes a finite layer, a collision, or a loss growing with the order? | Every finite stage is available and every uniform statement is equivalent to RH, to AAL or to the width |
