# Which routes solve the same mathematical problem?

Two RH equivalences yield a logical implication by passing through RH. For research, the valuable transfer is one that works **before** RH, preserves a useful exponent, removes a construction, or leaves room for a weaker input. The ledger distinguishes exact translations, one-way transfers and analogies. The [route register](route-obstacles.md) gives the individual gates. The [wall ledger](walls-ledger.md) identifies their unpaid inputs.

The two most economical junctions have complementary risks. The **prime midpoint** removes the separate quartic conductor heights while retaining a single ordinary-prime estimate at every n. The **quartet/index splice** permits two negative directions while retaining a separate ordinary-prime or confined-state problem on each possible failure branch. Neither junction currently supplies its own arithmetic inequality. [Proof programmes](obstacles-summary.md)
