# Mathematical obstacles ranked by impact on RH

Here $\Theta$ is the supremum of the real parts of nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$, $\theta(x)=\sum_{p\le x}\log p$, and $[u]_+=\max(u,0)$. Items 1–10 each imply RH. Their order favors estimates that also yield a numerical strip improvement or isolate a specific signed sum. Items 11–12 improve the zero-free half-plane, 13–17 eliminate exceptional configurations, and 18 tests whether one proposed construction can reach RH at all. Equivalent formulations with the same proof input are listed under their common obstacle. ### 1. Bound the composite contribution to the prime midpoint

- **Prove that** for every $1/2<t<1$ and every integer $n\ge1$, the signed price of the odd balanced composites minus the all-integer unitary-divisor center is at most $C_t n^t$. Both prices use $(3n,4n]$ minus $(2n,3n]$, restricted to integers coprime to $n$. For a contributing composite $k$, the number of prime-power blocks is odd and at least three, and each block is smaller than $\sqrt{k}$. In both sums $k$ has weight $\log k\sum_{d\mid k,\ (d,k/d)=1,\ d\le\sqrt{k}}(-1)^{\omega(d)}$. [Exact definitions](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheOddSmoothCompositesAtThePrimeMidpoint.lean#L18)
- **Impact** — This upper bound gives RH. For any fixed $1/2\le b<1$, proving it for every $b<t<1$ gives exactly $\Theta\le b$. [Composite criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheOddSmoothCompositesAtThePrimeMidpoint.lean#L180) · [Midpoint RH theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/ThePrimeMidpointInTwoAdjacentGcdHorizons.lean#L342)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — The centered price differs from $D(n)=2\theta(3n)-\theta(2n)-\theta(4n)$ only by a proper-power term of size $O_\varepsilon(n^{1/2+\varepsilon})$. The full center can already be replaced at that cost by an explicit integral at each of $2n,3n,4n$. Start with the signed composite sum against those three integrals. Every balanced three-block integer has coefficient $-2$, forced by canceling the two-block products. The higher odd ranks remain in the sum. In the separate cube-root decomposition the selected lower and upper prime-triple boxes already have a positive lower-minus-upper surplus asymptotic to $c n/\log^2(3n)$. Its leading term must be combined with the other rows before taking absolute values. [Half-power error](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheOddSmoothCompositesAtThePrimeMidpoint.lean#L146) · [Integral-center criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheOddSmoothCompositesAtThePrimeMidpoint.lean#L227) · [Endpoint error](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheIntegralCenterOfTheSmallUnitaryDivisors.lean#L120) · [Rigidity of the triple coefficient](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheParityOfTheSmallUnitaryDivisors.lean#L289) · [Priced triple surplus](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/ThePrimeCountsInTheTwoMidpointTripleBoxes.lean#L632) · [Positive constant](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheSurplusOfTheLowerPrimeTriples.lean#L28)

### 2. Bound the real part of the conductor tail at cubic order

- **Prove that** for $K=\lfloor N^{17/9}\rfloor$ there is one $C$ such that $\Re\,\texttt{centeredFixedPrimeTail}(N,K)\le C N^3$ for every integer $N\ge1$. After summing the conductors, its two terms are $N^2\sum_{K<p\le H}\log p\,W(p/N^2)$ and $+\lambda K(K+1)/2$, with $W$ the integrated conductor spline, $H$ its terminal prime cutoff and $\lambda$ its slope at the origin. [Tail and normalization](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheFixedPrimeCutAfterSummingConductors.lean#L134)
- **Impact** — This one-sided cubic estimate is equivalent to RH. The same upper estimate with exponent $3+2a$ gives $\Theta\le1/2+a$ for $0\le a<1/2$. [Exact exponent](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheFixedPrimeCutAfterSummingConductors.lean#L250) · [Cubic endpoint](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheFixedPrimeCutAfterSummingConductors.lean#L300)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — Below the first spline knot the omitted prime weight is exactly linear. Its sum is a known multiple of $\sum_{p\le K}p\log p$, which fixes the counterterm. Apply summation by parts to the remaining primes with the signed spline intact. In the conductor-ordered decomposition, the terminal primes and their complement have opposite quartic leading terms. Bounding those absolute values separately already loses a power of $N$. [Linear part](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheFixedPrimeCutAfterSummingConductors.lean#L77) · [Centered comparison](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheFixedPrimeCutAfterSummingConductors.lean#L176) · [Opposite quartic terms](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheLastFirstPrimesInTheSignedProduct.lean#L212)

### 3. Cancel the Möbius-weighted cofactors remaining below $X^{3/7}$

- **Prove that** $|\texttt{primePowerInteriorAtExponent}(4/7,X)|\le C_\varepsilon X^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ for every $\varepsilon>0$ and integer $X\ge1$. At $y=\lfloor\log(X+1)\rfloor^2$ and $T=\lfloor X^{4/7}\rfloor$, this sums the rough Möbius weight over $m\le\lfloor X/(T+1)\rfloor$, against the rough Mangoldt prefix at $\lfloor X/m\rfloor$ minus its counting center there. [Finite sum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RestrictedFactorizationCalculus/ThePrimeReadingsBeyondTheHyperbola.lean#L261) · [Summand](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RestrictedFactorizationCalculus/RoughDivisorInversionAtTheHyperbola.lean#L114)
- **Impact** — This yields the square-root-plus-$\varepsilon$ Mertens bound for every prefix and hence RH. [Interior-to-Mertens transfer](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RestrictedFactorizationCalculus/ThePrimeReadingsBeyondTheHyperbola.lean#L268) · [Mertens-to-RH transfer](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/MobiusMertensLittlewood.lean#L87)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — At this cutoff the remaining cofactors are at most $X^{3/7}$. Removing the head costs $O(X^{1/2-1/84000})$, and the smooth center is already affordable. For any one admissible matching, the signed-flux identity expresses the interior as the two endpoint differences contributed by its fixed vertices and its crossing pairs. Estimate their sum at the same $X$. Moving the head to $X^{3/5}$ while retaining uniform half-plus control would itself prove $\Theta\le5/6$. ### 4. Bound the accumulated positive deficit at square endpoints

- **Prove that** for every $\delta>0$ there are $C_\delta$ and arbitrarily large integers $K$ for which
  $\sum_{m\le K,\ m\ge1}[\log 2-m^{-3}\log G(m^2,m^3)]_+\le C_\delta K^\delta$,
  where $\log G(M,X)=\sum_{M<d\le2M}\lfloor X/d\rfloor\Lambda(d)$. [Gcd product identity](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheGcdProductOnOneMangoldtOctave.lean#L69) · [Cofinal quantifier](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/SquareAndSixthPowerSamplesOfTheReciprocalPrimeOctave.lean#L498)
- **Impact** — This sparse, one-sided estimate gives RH. Replacing $(m^2,m^3)$ by $(m^6,m^7)$, with normalization $m^{-7}$, gives exactly $\Theta\le5/6$ and the $11/3$ conductor estimate at every endpoint. [Square criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/SquareAndSixthPowerSamplesOfTheReciprocalPrimeOctave.lean#L523) · [Sixth-power transfer](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/SquareAndSixthPowerSamplesOfTheReciprocalPrimeOctave.lean#L534)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — The total error from replacing the gcd deficits by reciprocal-Mangoldt octave deficits is at most $14(1+\log K)$. Work on the latter and bound how often and how far each octave falls below $\log 2$. The eighth-power gcd deficit already equals $\tfrac12\log K+C+o(1)$ on its own grid. Cancellation between positive and negative errors in a signed floor sum cannot bound the displayed positive parts. [Rounding after taking positive parts](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/SquareAndSixthPowerSamplesOfTheReciprocalPrimeOctave.lean#L484) · [Eighth-power comparison](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheGcdDeficitAtTheEighthPowerEndpoints.lean#L244)

### 5. Pay the mixed prime terms on the complete unit-spaced Weil comb

- **Prove that** for each integer $K\ge1$, all sufficiently large $N$, and every real profile $c$ on $S=(N,(1+3K)N]\cap\mathbb Z$, the mixed von Mangoldt debit is at most the diagonal and real-place budget of that same $c$. [Complete comb criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L1583)
- **Impact** — This proves RH and supplies the full Weil sign, including mixed terms across prime contacts. [Eventual positivity and RH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HodgeTheoryOfAFixedPart/TheRiemannHypothesisIsExactEventualCombPositivity.lean#L168)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — Every $c$ has a centered-divisibility representation with $(\sum_d|\alpha_d|)^2\le C_K N\sum_S c^2$. Payment is proved for a sufficiently small constant at this scale. The gap is payment at a constant large enough to represent every profile. Split the matrix into even and odd sites. The even block $A$ has an eventual positive floor by spacing two. It remains to bound the odd quadratic form after minimization over the even coefficients, $C-B^\top A^{-1}B$. The Liouville-twisted form has negative examples at full span, so this estimate must use the actual prime weights. [Representation](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L968) · [Small-cost bound](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L355) · [Spacing theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheSpacingThresholdForDivergentCombFloors.lean#L106) · [Liouville examples](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L1364)

### 6. Bound simultaneous opposite prime excursions at the same numerator

- **Prove that** the following joint upper-tail moment is $O_{a,g,d,\varepsilon,\eta}(X^{1/2+\varepsilon})$. Fix $a,g,\varepsilon>0$ and $0<d\le1/4$. For every sufficiently small fixed $\eta>0$, take all large $X=e^T$, $L=\lceil X^{1+\eta}\rceil$, $X^{1/(1+\eta)}\le k\le X$, and $s=\pm1$. Use $I=(k,L]$ and either flank $J_H=(\lfloor\sqrt H\rfloor,k]$ or $(L,H]$, choosing the same flank throughout the sum. With $\Delta(I,H)=\sum_{m\in I,\ \lfloor H/m\rfloor\ {\rm odd}}(\mathbf1_{m\ {\rm prime}}\log m-1)$, the moment is
  $L\sum_H [s\Delta(I,H)]_+[-s\Delta(J_H,H)]_+/H^2$.
  Sum only over $X\le H<L^2$, $H^{1/2+d}<L$, $\sqrt H<k$, where **both** signed discrepancies exceed $aX^g\sqrt H$. [Coefficient](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OddQuotientsInTheFractionalPrimeTest.lean#L23) · [Numerators and product](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OppositePrimeExcursionsOnOneFixedBand.lean#L87)
- **Impact** — Failure of RH forces fixed $a,c,g,d,r>0$ and, for **every** $0<\eta\le r$, arbitrarily late tests with this very sum greater than $ca^2X^{1/2+3g}$. Choosing $\varepsilon<3g$ excludes the critical state and proves RH. The uniform upper bound is sufficient. An estimate restricted to the forced sequence would also give the contradiction. [Forced lower bound and order of quantifiers](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OppositePrimeExcursionsOnOneFixedBand.lean#L180)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — Split at $H=L$. Below it the right flank is empty. On the left flank above level $B\sqrt H$, the mixed sum agrees with the interior second moment up to relative error $O(1/B)$. Use $B=aX^g$ there. Above $L$, count simultaneous exceedances with the same $H$ and weight $L/H^2$. [Below-endpoint second-moment reduction](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OppositePrimeExcursionsOnOneFixedBand.lean#L306)

### 7. Give long Möbius polynomials one growth exponent independent of length

- **Prove that** there is one $K\ge0$ such that for arbitrarily large fixed $\theta>0$ there is $C_\theta$ and an unbounded sequence of $T$ with
  $\int_1^T|\sum_{n\le T^\theta}\mu(n)n^{-1/2-it}|^2\,dt\le C_\theta T^K$. The sequence of heights may depend on $\theta$. [Pure Möbius criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ConjugateDiagonalGeometry/NearLineLogMean/LongMoebiusSecondMomentRigidity.lean#L776)
- **Impact** — This proves RH using only the Möbius polynomial. The corresponding moment with an extra $\zeta(1/2+it)$ factor can then be bounded for every length exponent and every $T\ge1$. [All-length consequence](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ConjugateDiagonalGeometry/NearLineLogMean/LongMoebiusSecondMomentRigidity.lean#L721)
- **Lean facts and proof start** — Writing $w=\Theta-1/2$, the available pure second moment costs $O_\theta(T^{4+2w\theta})$. Expand the square with the original coefficients. Its diagonal is $(T-1)\sum_{n\le T^\theta}\mu(n)^2/n$. The off-diagonal pairs with small $|\log(m/n)|$ require the new cancellation. Only the exponent $K$ must be uniform in $\theta$. [Current exponent](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ConjugateDiagonalGeometry/NearLineLogMean/LongMoebiusSecondMomentRigidity.lean#L111)

### 8. Prove cumulative decay for every modular scattering pulse

- **Prove that** for every compact smooth pulse $h$ and every $0<\sigma<1/4$, its reflected tail energy satisfies $E_h(T)\le C_{h,\sigma}e^{-2\sigma T}$ for all $T\ge0$. [Energy criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TailEnergy/RegularityAndReceiverEquivalence.lean#L831)

[Strip calibration](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TailEnergy/RegularityAndReceiverEquivalence.lean#L737)

Estimate interference in the accumulated return sum, restoring the mean term for general pulses. Actual pulses can have arbitrarily many prescribed stalls, which rules out a fixed fractional loss per step. ### 9. Bound the total heat-discriminant winding on an exhaustion

- **Prove that** the winding computed from the theta heat transform is zero on a family of regular windows covering all positive real times and all spatial heights. Equivalently, it suffices to bound the absolute total change of argument strictly below $2\pi$ on each window, including all transitions where the spatial cut changes. [Positive-time exhaustion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/GaloisTheoryOfZeros/TheLocalDiscriminantOfAHeatCollision.lean#L250)

- **Lean facts and proof start** — Sufficiently thin complex-time boxes isolate the collisions of the real slice. The spatial boundary moments give the squared Vandermonde, and the correction on a shared edge is the integral of the logarithmic derivative of the ratio of the two discriminants. Bound their combined argument from theta boundary data. The existing global winding formula constructs its discriminant using the enclosed collision divisor. ### 14. Force high zeros to approach the critical line

- **Prove that** for every $\sigma>1/2$ there is $T_\sigma$ such that $\Re(\xi'/\xi)(s)\ge0$ whenever $\Re s\ge\sigma$ and $|\Im s|\ge T_\sigma$. [Eventual Hadamard positivity](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/XiLogDerivEventualPositivity.lean#L198)
- **Impact** — This proves the Lindelöf hypothesis on the critical line and leaves only finitely many zeros at each fixed positive distance from that line (the approach law, ZAL). Together with item 15 it proves RH. [Lindelöf implication](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/XiLogDerivEventualPositivity.lean#L203) · [Two-input RH theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ZetaWeakRecurrenceLadderCollapse.lean#L292)

### 18. Test for a strict obstruction to the integer floor-alphabet construction

- **Prove that** there is some $1/4<a_*<1/2$ such that, for every sufficiently large alphabet bound $K$, **every** admissible balanced integer floor row supported in $[1,K]$ has normalized rate greater than $K^{-a_*}$.
