# Three families of walls between arithmetic and RH

A wall is the mathematical input a proof still has to supply. **P** asks how much *signed ordinary arithmetic* remains after exact cancellations. **R** asks what forces the *complete form or critical state* to have the required sign, index or realization. **U** asks what permits an inference *at every relevant scale or over the entire zero configuration*. The same failure can appear in all three descriptions. A paid entry settles its stated range. A counterexample rules out only the indicated inference.
