| Family | Question that a proof must answer | Most discriminating current test |
|---|---|---|
| **P · Cancellation on ordinary integers** | What forces the required signed remainder to be small at the actual integer endpoints? | For 5/6, one of five disjoint prime bands may be chosen at each midpoint.
