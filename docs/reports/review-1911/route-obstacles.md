# What stands between each route and a proof of RH?

**The missing result is a uniform theorem about ordinary arithmetic.** There are several ways to recognize success. For example, suppressing the positive excess of prime logarithms in two adjacent intervals, paying the full Weil form on integer combs, or excluding every positive-time collision of the heat transform would each suffice. Each asks for a different proof. The corpus also gives a useful intermediate destination, the half-plane Re s>5/6. **Inventory.** The 58 live mechanisms comprise 21 integer or prime constructions, 12 histories or approximations, 16 form or state constructions, and 9 degree or geometric tests. EWU/OWU count once and have separate entries below. The 12 candidates have an additional construction or identification to make. The last section covers the strip machinery and zero-configuration subproblems shared across routes. Retired constructions and their scoped counterexamples are catalogued separately. [Frontier map](../rh-current-frontier-map.md) · [Retired constructions](../rh-killed-routes.md)

**Reading the columns.** “Just work” means an operation available with the stated inputs, including a bounded calculation or an application of a proved transfer. A task in the last column requires a new argument. Where a criterion implies full RH, a solution would settle an open problem. For a proposed intermediate lemma or candidate object the narrower assertion is that no solution is supplied by the cited results. Θ denotes the supremum of real parts of nontrivial zeros. Half-plus means every exponent 1/2+ε, ε>0, with an ε-dependent constant. Verification here is at Lean source level. Links identify the statements and hypotheses. ## Shared subproblems and supporting constructions

**Strip detector.** The project proves zero-freeness strictly to the right of 20999/24000. Its marked-moment minimax calculation bounds a specified comparison system. A further analytic gain needs estimates on the long correlated rows, restoration of ordinary powers, or an independently controlled angular family. Short diagonal Grams and factorwise completions leave explicit obstructions. Liu's separately stated analytic bound is 0.8733458561067185768… . Both exponents exceed 5/6. **AAL, ZAL and finite exceptions.** AAL forbids a finite nonempty strict right tail beyond any depth h≥0. ZAL says there are only finitely many zeros beyond each *positive* depth. Together they imply RH by taking h strictly between zero and the depth of a hypothetical exception. Another route needs ZAL, landing (ZAL implies finite total off-line support), and exclusion of every nonempty finite off-line divisor. A single quartet is one case of the last input. The full finite-stratum input has six equivalent critical-state formulations and leaves isolated outer layers over infinite lower backgrounds open. **Other realizations.** The quadratic transverse-work quantum and subpower linear-mollifier defect measure zero exclusion and prime error. An independent upper bound would turn either into a proof. A three-term Deninger realization would need a compatible pairing and a faithful ξ action.
