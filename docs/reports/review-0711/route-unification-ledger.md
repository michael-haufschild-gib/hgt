# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 9 candidates are faces of three core problems: **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection and reflection). Their RH-level endpoints meet at the **arithmetic Hardy inequality** `P≤H` on every window, equivalently the **band** `|𝒞_m(f)|≤W(f)` of a test and its mirror, equivalently nonnegativity of one quadratic form on integer probes. U-Σ reads it as a form on the window, U-Π on the prime side, U-Γ on the zero side.

Transversal to the three, RH splits into **ZAL**, read by height-local receivers, and **AAL**, read by sparse receivers and recurrence. Below the three lie three structural gaps that every family meets: the **dark range**, primes past the Kronecker scale where conditioning on the head needs a Dirichlet polynomial longer than the height; the **missing count**, a nonnegative arithmetic function at bounded distance from the form; and the **critical-line input**, the functional equation acting on `Re s=½`, which every one-off countermodel lacks.

Evidence: `[L]` Lean source; `[K]` Lean with transitive axiom audit; `[S]` unfinished, unintegrated or uncommitted; `[D]` deduction; `[N]` numerical; `[C]` classical. **Equivalence through RH transfers truth, not mechanism.** Only an explicit source identity or a proved transport moves an estimate between routes. A shared endpoint is not a shared proof. Section 3 separates the two.

## 0. Unifying statements

- **One split [L].** RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ AAL ⇔ ZAL ∧ zero-disc weak recurrence ⇔ ZAL ∧ subexponential polar ground energy. ZAL ⇒ qRH. Under AAL eight RH-level statements coincide. `qRH ∨ AAL` [D].
- **One rigid alternative [L].** ¬AAL ⇔ an attained top excess ⇔ finite top and bottom layers ⇔ `ψ(x)−x` is one finite oscillation at leading order ⇔ two-prime dilation annihilation of `ψ`.
- **One countermodel boundary [L/S/D].** Positive Euler products — continuous, discrete, integer-norm, rational-prime with multiplicative linear counting — carry a finite top layer; none continues to `Re s=½` with a small functional-equation defect, and defect `≤1/6400` forces the rational primes. The AAL half is a critical-line statement.
- **One scalar [L].** Every window route measures `a*(ζ)`. Contact is `Ψ=½log H₁` blowing up; RH ⇔ `Ψ` locally bounded.
- **The critical curve is the window [S/L].** At ratio `R`, positivity on the high-conductor complement holds exactly below `⨅_A windowThreshold(A)`, and `log R_high≥6a*(ζ)+log c₀` [S]; the low-packet payment at every ratio is RH [L]. The critical-curve problem of HF, SRT, AIP and ACS is the window problem of RW.
- **One splitting order [S].** Physical tearing at the collar boundary fails at Schur ratio `4/13`; conductor-adapted splitting decouples. Conductor first, position second.
- **One sign plane [L/D].** `Ω(a,X)`: size reaches all heights to `a≈log log X`; typical heights to `a=δlog T/(64M²)` with Gaussian tails of the bad set; visibility, cut crystal and sharp location are the other curves.
- **One frequency diagonal [L].** Weil's form is the average of its frequency packets; a negative test is negative on a positive-measure set of frequencies.
- **One scale [L/D].** A near-unit mollifier needs length at least `exp(c log²T)`, the length of an Euler mollifier over the primes `≤c log²T`; absolute non-alignment of prime phases begins at length `≈L²`; conditional tail laws hold only while the head resonator is shorter than the height. The near-line wall, the short-sum barrier and the Kronecker scale are one range of primes.
- **One tail [L].** Zeros of ζ are zeros of the Euler tail `ζM_y` at every cut. The head is frozen below resolution `1/log y`. A return at a right zero is tail-bad at every cut and has density zero, while nonvanishing targets return at positive density. Every certificate past `Re s=1` is a tail-good condition.
- **One first return [L].** `R(y,η)` is bracketed at every precision; a weak return is an extreme value of `ζ(1+it)`; at bounded harmonic defect `R≥exp(c(log N)^{3/2}/log log N)`; at precision `≍y^{−9/2}` the RH bracket holds; proportional saving is the absence of early heavy returns.
- **One collision arithmetic [L].** Mean squares of smooth × prime polynomials are their diagonal plus collision counts fixed by gcd classes; the cost depends on the head, not on the product length.
- **One width axis [L/D].** Floor rate, damping rate, abscissa, gauge exponent, stability line, long-moment slope, Möbius zero abscissa, integrality exponent, stencil rate, PTR `h`, TH `a`, Hecke level, ringdown gap, horocycle exponent: interior values are zero-free strips; all unconditional savings sit at VK.
- **One Mertens level [L].** Stencil rate = Mertens level; RH places the exponent in `[¼,½]`; a producer is a mollifier on `Re s=1`; Farey discrepancy and the nerve Euler characteristic are `M(N)`.
- **One horocycle [L/S].** Long closed horocycles of the modular horizon equidistribute at `y^{1/2−ε}`; rate `y^α` forces `Re ρ≤2−2α`; RH ⇔ rate `y^{3/4−ε}` (Zagier). In time the same poles give the Lax–Phillips decay; RH ⇔ every resonant mode decays at `e^{−t/4}`.
- **One blindness [L].** Floor identities annihilate every zero mode; exact ledgers are holomorphic off `s=1`; polynomial ledgers read the twin only at `s=1,2`.
- **One kernel [L].** Zeros are the power-law kernel vectors of the cut system; strip superpositions are spanned by zeros; finite-energy kernel mass annihilation ⇒ RH.
- **One parity [L].** The exact identity `Σ_{k≤x}F(⌊x/k⌋)=⌊√x⌋` pins `λ`; a power-saving Liouville bound is a zero-free strip; twin pairs carry a quarter floor; parity is a size statement.
- **One edge [L/D].** Pronic domination, flux, Hermite–Biehler at `σ=1`, Euler coordinates, `3+4cos+cos2`, the Poisson measure of the Hecke algebra: every positivity with a proved mechanism stops at `Re s=1`.
- **One inner factor [L].** On `Re s>½`, `E` has inner factor the Blaschke product of right zeros; RH ⇔ `I(w)=0` at one point; Selberg's mollifier bounds `I` at infinity; BSY, `N(ρ)`, Krein–Langer index and the dilation surplus read it.
- **One second expression [L].** `exp(2I(1))=inf_Q∫|ζ exp Q|²dP₁`; near-extremal families converge to `1/E`; the shifted/critical Szegő ratio decides RH.
- **One log-mean [L].** Eight forms of `T⁻¹∫log|ζ|→0`, the Abel inner mass at infinity, depth density `o(T)`, density-zero damping deficits.
- **One certificate [L].** Causal: outer predictor, Schur band, sum of squares with margins as weights.
- **One threshold [L].** Slack `s>0` Szegő, `s<0` negative, `s=0` RH.
- **One column law [L].** On independent reflectors infima add; only amplitudes oscillating with the height cross columns; convergence is essential to torus positivity.
- **One Eisenstein carrier [L].** TH spectrum, PM carrier, BHR packets and horocycle means use `E*(z,½+ib)`; Hecke at `p` is time translation by `log p`; every Hecke operator is diagonal on it.
- **One Lax–Phillips model [L/S].** `φ(s)(s−1)/s` is a Blaschke product over `(1+ρ)/2`; resonant states are eigenvectors of the compressed dilation semigroup; on graphs the compression has rank two and its Krein metric is Hilbert exactly under damping.
- **One Krein law [L].** On every system the form multiplies by `q` per double tick and decays at the universal rate; its flux sign is universal damping.
- **One count [L].** A degree-two form is nonnegative iff a function bounded below lies at bounded distance. Curve: Manin's height is that function. Fake: count, not quadratic. ℤ: quadratic, not known count.
- **One isolation [L].** ζ is a singleton in FE defect `≤1/6400`, in count distance `o(log n)` among positive integer-norm systems, and under the exact parity identity. Above these thresholds one-offs exist (continuous, discrete, integer-norm, rational-prime), fakes exist (`O(log²)`), new zeros exist (signed generators).
- **One functional equation is not enough [L].** On graphs FE + flux + unitarity + Hecke do not force damping at genus two; integrality bounds genus by trivial zeros; the count law forces damping at genus one.
- **One hull [L].** Haar-typical, fair-coin-typical and Liouville-antipodal fibres extend zero-free to `Re s>½` with natural boundary `½`; the Liouville point extends iff RH.
- **One finite-rung rule [L].** Each fixed window, rank, degree, row and entry follows from verified zeros; limit pencils at a fixed ratio are window rungs; window positivity is a count with power saving on its range.
- **One budget [L].** A floor `−C_σe^{2σr}∫F²` for every `σ>0` implies RH.
- **One blind form [L].** RH is prime-blind positivity; an exact null blind test exists.
- **One cone [L].** `𝒜(c)` pins every weight to `Λ(n)`; RH ⇔ `λ*→0`.
- **Positive mimic = window sign [L].** Below contact, sampling atoms are a positive on-line mimic.
- **One-sided signs [L].** BSY `I≥0`, PM `D_s≤0`, TT `D+Q/2≥0`, budgets `≥1`, dilation surplus `≥0`: each proved sign has RH as its opposite.
- **Returns are prime returns [L].** A return forces `n^{−iτ_j}→1` and a spike near the pole; the first nontrivial return of the primes `≤N` is above `N`.
- **One maximal-angle zero [L].** RH ⇔ equal rotated moduli at zeros; the extremal law alone has a polynomial model.
