Three wall families carry the frontier. **Σ** is the critical sign of the window form, **Π** the precision and production of the prime count, **Γ** the selection of ζ, the reflection of its zeros and the transport of its zeros in height. All three meet in one minimal face, and that face is local in scale.

- **One inequality per prime, at sparse scales.** RH ⇔ `c_B(log p)≥−K` for every prime ⇔ `c_B(log p)≤K` for every prime ⇔ `P(p)≤A(p)+K` (smoothed von Mangoldt count in the window of `p` against its archimedean-plus-pole prediction) ⇔ Weil positivity of the two-bump test `B+τ_{log p}B` at every prime [L]; sign-free, RH ⇔ `|c_B(log p)|≤K` [L]. The primes need not all be used: for every `r>0`, RH ⇔ the lower bound holds for the primes of arbitrarily late windows `|log p−T|≤r√T` (Σ145) [L]. Decisive samples exist with arbitrarily large gaps and of zero convergence exponent (Σ138) [L]; the resolution limit of the face is open (Σ146). Unconditionally `|P(n)−A(n)|≤C√n·exp(−(log n)^{3/5}/(10⁸log log n))` (Π135) [L].
- **Σ is the sign of every window, and nothing else.** RH ⇔ some weight is admissible on every window ⇔ `Ψ=½log H₁` locally bounded ⇔ one Weil test per prime [L]. At the critical curve `R_c=R_PSD=R_polar=R_halfline≤R_prim=R_high=W`; ¬RH produces a unit half-line ground state at finite `W`, polar-orthogonal if `R_polar=W` (Σ129, Σ135, Σ139) [L]. The theta all-rank problem is the sign of a head whose leading term is `|ζ(½+ib)|²` (Σ133) [L].
- **Π is the same sign on the prime side.** All unconditional savings sit at the VK curve. Crude regimes exist in the exceptional coordinate, on thick sets of scales, on Bohr-dense samples and at the prime degrees (Π111–Π114, Σ137, Σ138, Σ147) [L]. Positivity of even tensor powers is free; the constituents of every tensor power are Tate twists and uniformity in `k` is RH (Π124, Π143) [L]. Exact vanishing over ℤ lives on the torsion; the exact Stepanov count over ℤ is Chebyshev's; the only exact symmetries of the prime orbit are the integer dilations (Π125, Π126, Π139) [L]. The gauge family of twists has big monodromy and pure generic fibres, and ζ is its jump fibre (Π141, Π142) [L]. Returns of the primes are counted collectively beyond a transcendence depth floor that grows with the number of primes (Π140) [L].
- **Γ is the same sign on the zero side, plus transport.** RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing, and AAL ⇔ B13 ∧ Landing ∧ DH; no two atoms fail together (Γ183) [L]. Landing has eleven equivalent forms and fails for FE-only and Euler-only models (Γ182, Γ185) [L]; DH is classified on models, classical Deuring–Heilbronn holds for quadratic `L(s,χ)`, and ζ has no Siegel regime (Γ184) [L]. Under ¬AAL damping filters read the exceptional divisor, but the Euler-region bound and the detection range are disjoint (Γ186) [L]. Off the diagonal Weil's kernel is the pole minus the Green functions of all places at the critical parameter (Γ158, Γ168) [L]; ordered Gram coupling gives finite locked limits on every packet with an exact defect (Γ187) [L]; a discrete adelic quotient carries the degree configuration as points (Γ189) [L]; the sign sits in the diagonal. Every horizon observable is on the width axis; one cavity function of Hecke eigenforms is a complete and sharp receiver (Γ134, Γ171) [L].

**How the three families split one problem [D].** No test is compactly supported on both sides of the explicit formula. Σ fixes a compact prime side (windows); the zero side then has infinite rank and only an exact sign decides. Π localizes the zero side (Gaussian or band-limited weights, geometric scales of a tower, the prime degrees); a crude bound then suffices — Landau's theorem, or sign-free the Laplace rate theorem, turns a constant into RH — but the prime side needs square-root precision. Γ is the involution `ρ↦1−ρ̄`, the selection of ζ and the vertical transport of its zeros. The curve proof has finite rank (ZAL in tower form is automatic) and zeros periodic in height (AAL is automatic); ζ has neither [L]. The prime side removes one freebie: decisive samples make attainment free, and positive deletion makes them local in scale [L].
