## The three families

| Family | Question | What would cross it |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall 1 — Selective critical sign** | What bounds the actual signed prime/Möbius source, the complete Weil form or a computed defect at the critical scale, using a property \(\Lambda\) has and its twins lack? | A new inequality meeting an existing receiver that is parity-breaking (fails for Liouville), precision-faithful (fails for \(\Lambda+\varepsilon n^{-\eta}\)) and keeps all tails and quantifiers. |
| **Wall 3 — Transfer and exhaustion** | Does an estimate survive the needed norm, source realization, shared reserve and simultaneous limit? | A source-faithful comparison in a topology that carries the sign or index, with the right uniformity. |
| **Wall 14 — Realization and forcing** | Which independently built structure forces the inequality? | A faithful object with a proved selective law, not a calibration or an assumed structure field. |

The families interlock:

- A smooth plateau has a positive complete-matrix floor at every fixed separated width [S]; carrying that sign through touching and overlap is a Wall 3 problem.
- The cross-level prime couplings of Wall 3 are Wall 1 prime-error sequences, and their positivity is one-prime tower positivity, which is RH.
- Over \(\mathbb F_q\) the Wall 14 forcing is a count; over \(\mathbb Z\) the corresponding count is a Wall 1 estimate.
- Precision rigidity forces any deciding input to act at unbounded aperture, which is Wall 3 (inference).

The organizing identities:
\[
\operatorname{Re}W(f,f)=D(f,f)-\mathfrak d(f),\qquad
W(f,g)=-\int_0^\infty\big(e^h-\psi(e^h)-c\big)\,q'_{f,g}(h)\,dh-\Gamma_{\rm tail}(f,g)\quad(\operatorname{supp}f<b<\operatorname{supp}g).
\]
Here \(W\) is the full Weil form, \(D\) the positive collided form and \(\mathfrak d\) the off-line defect. The diagonal carries the collided mass; the off-diagonal carries the prime error.

**Strata.** Inputs are classified by strength, weakest first:

1. **[open]**: intermediate.
2. **[≥QRH]**: implies a fixed zero-free strip \(\Theta<1\).
3. **[LH]**: zeros approach the line, equivalently LH together with finitely many nonreal zeros of every \(\cos(hD)\Xi\). Implies the Lindelöf hypothesis and \(\Theta<1\) [L].
4. **[FS]**: finitely many off-line zeros. Implies [LH] [L].
5. **[RH]**: equivalent to RH.
6. **[RH+]**: stronger than RH.
