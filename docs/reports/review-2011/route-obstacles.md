# What stands between each route and a proof of RH?

**Every route reads the same number.** Write Θ for the supremum of the real parts of the nontrivial zeros of ζ. In Lean the corpus proves Θ ≤ 20999/24000 and ζ(s) ≠ 0 for Re s > 7/8. Almost every live route is an exact equivalence with RH, and a large group are *exact meters*: their observable has an unconditional exponent law in terms of Θ, or an equivalence with Θ ≤ b at every b. An exact meter has no internal slack. A bound on it is a bound on Θ, and the construction that realizes the bound from a zero-free strip is a converse, not a producer. What remains open on such a route is one independent inequality for its observable. [Strip 20999/24000](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/SubSevenEighthsZeroFreeHalfPlane.lean#L180) · [7/8](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/QuasiRiemannHypothesis.lean#L626)

**Inventory.** 60 live mechanisms: 22 on ordinary integers, 12 histories and approximations, 16 forms, sections and states, 10 moment and zero-geometry tests. 12 candidates lack an object, its identification with ζ, or an implication to an RH criterion. EWU and OWU count once. The last section collects the subproblems shared by many routes. Retired constructions are listed in the [killed-routes ledger](../rh-killed-routes.md).

**Reading the columns.** *Lean status* gives the theorem tying the route to RH, with its quantifiers, and marks exact meters. *(a) Just work* lists obstacles removable with known mathematics: formal transfers, calibrations, explicit computations, model checks. None of them closes a route. *(b) Open mathematics* gives the input nobody can supply, first at full strength and then, where one exists, the weakest form that would still beat the corpus strip or exclude a class of exceptional zero configurations. "5/6 face" means the route's statement at Θ ≤ 5/6. Facts and searches behind every row are in the [research log](../../../logs/review-2011-research-log.md).
