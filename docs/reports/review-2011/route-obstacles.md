# What stands between each route and a proof of RH?

**Every route reads one number.** Write Θ for the supremum of the real parts of the nontrivial zeros of ζ. Lean proves that every zero with positive real part lies in [3001/24000, 20999/24000] and that ζ ≠ 0 for Re s > 7/8. The producer behind both theorems is the metaplectic cubic Möbius cancellation on SL₂(ℤ[ω])\ℍ³; no other producer of a zero-free half-plane exists in the corpus. [Strip](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/SubSevenEighthsZeroFreeHalfPlane.lean#L180) · [7/8](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/QuasiRiemannHypothesis.lean#L626)

**Three structural facts organize the table.**
1. *Exact meters.* Most observables on the integers have an unconditional exponent law in Θ or an equivalence with Θ ≤ b at every b. Every contracting positive dilation stencil of θ is such a meter in either sign: the halving increment, the prime midpoint and every Jensen gap θ(x) − Σ a_j θ(λ_j x) read Θ one-sidedly. A meter has no internal slack; a bound on it is a bound on Θ. [Every stencil](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticProducerTheory/EveryContractingStencilOfThePrimeErrorIsAOneSidedMeter.lean#L252)
2. *A critical scale for positivity.* The complete Weil matrix is positive definite below a critical overlap and RH is positivity at every bounded multiple of it: for the cubic spline c ≤ (log N)^α with α < 1/8 is proved and RH is positivity along every critical curve c⁸ = R log N; for Denjoy–Carleman profiles ε·c*_N is proved, c*_N ≍ log log N·(log log log N)², and RH is every multiple K·c*_N. Weil's form is certified with floor 10⁻¹³ on every window of ratio below 4 (aperture log 2) at every block size. [Below 1/8](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CriticalArithmeticCorrelationTheory/TheCompleteWeilMatrixIsPositiveDefiniteBelowTheEighthRoot.lean#L185) · [ε·c*](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/TransMetzlerWeilPositivity/ReceiverRate/TheLogarithmicProfileIsPositiveBelowCriticalOverlap.lean#L281) · [Every multiple](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/TransMetzlerWeilPositivity/ReceiverRate/ABoundedOverlapRateSeesASemilocalWindow.lean#L645) · [Ratio < 4](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/WindowTowerOfSpecZ/TheTwoPrimeWindowHasAPositiveWeilFloorAtEveryBlockSize.lean#L158)
3. *Two atoms.* RH ⇔ approach to the line ∧ the asymptotic abscissa law (AAL). About sixty Lean theorems produce AAL from conditions weaker than RH. [RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ZetaWeakRecurrenceLadderCollapse.lean#L292)

**Inventory.** 62 live mechanisms: 22 on ordinary integers, 12 histories and approximations, 18 forms, indices, sections and states, 10 moment and zero-geometry tests. The critical-scale complete Weil matrix (CWS) and the window index (WIN) are the two live mechanisms not listed in the [frontier map](../rh-current-frontier-map.md). 12 candidates lack an object, its identification with ζ, or an implication to an RH criterion. EWU and OWU count once. Retired constructions are in the [killed-routes ledger](../rh-killed-routes.md). Facts and the source lines behind every row are in the [fact log](../../../logs/review-2011-status-quo-facts.md).

**Columns.** *Lean status* gives the theorem tying the route to RH, with its quantifiers, and marks exact meters. *(a) Just work* lists obstacles removable with known mathematics: transfers, calibrations, certificates, packaging. None closes a route. *(b) Open mathematics* gives the missing input at full strength and, where it exists, the weakest form that would beat the corpus strip or remove an exceptional stratum. "5/6 face" is the route's statement at Θ ≤ 5/6. Statements were read in source; compile status was not checked.
