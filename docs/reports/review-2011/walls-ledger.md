**Reading a row.** *Settled boundary* lists Lean theorems: identities, equivalences, power savings on stated ranges, counterexamples. *Open input* is the theorem still missing. *Status* takes one of four values. **Open:** no unconditional inequality of the needed strength. **Exact:** the wall is an exact meter of Θ with no internal slack. **Reduced:** its open input is literally another wall's input. **Settled:** the mathematical question is closed in Lean and remains only as a boundary for other walls. Bracketed codes give the wall numbers in the route report. [Route report](route-obstacles.md) · [Unification ledger](route-unification-ledger.md)
