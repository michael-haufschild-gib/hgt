**The R → P chain.** If positivity first fails at a finite critical ratio, the failure is carried by the critical half-line ground state at that threshold, and on the terminal slab its forced joint height is the squared halving increment up to 95√H times its size. A one-sided half-plus bound for the halving increment therefore excludes the critical state, and with it every failure of R. The chain is a theorem in each link; its only open input is P15.
