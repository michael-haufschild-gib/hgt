# Research programme for a formal proof of RH

**Diagnosis.** Every quantitative statement on every wall is an exact meter of Θ, the supremum of the real parts of the zeros of ζ. On ordinary integers every contracting positive dilation stencil of θ reads Θ in either sign, and eleven observables have exact faces at Θ ≤ 5/6 and at RH. On forms the window margin is a meter as well: neutral windows of ratio 3K+1 keep one negative square after a diagonal shift CK^θ for every θ above the width w = Θ − 1/2, and such a shift with θ < 1/2 confines the zeros to width θ. The complete Weil matrix has a critical scale; it is positive definite below it and RH is positivity at every bounded multiple of it. A meter has no slack, so a bound on one is a bound on Θ and the construction that realizes it from a strip is a converse. Every remaining task produces one inequality. The only producer of a zero-free half-plane in the corpus is the metaplectic cubic Möbius cancellation (Θ ≤ 20999/24000, ζ ≠ 0 for Re s > 7/8). Over finite fields Lean has three proofs of purity: Stepanov's method, the two pairings of Jacobi sums, and purity as the equality case of a mean-value comparison. [Stencils](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticProducerTheory/EveryContractingStencilOfThePrimeErrorIsAOneSidedMeter.lean#L252) · [Margin ⇒ width](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MixingArithmeticOfTheFloorTrees/TheRateOfTheMarginOfTheWindowMatricesBoundsTheAbscissaOfTheZeros.lean#L724) · [Width ⇒ margin](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExponentDynamicsOfTheCriticalStrip/TheMarginOfTheWindowMatricesSinksAtTheRateOfTheWidth.lean#L789) · [Strip](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/SubSevenEighthsZeroFreeHalfPlane.lean#L180) · [Route report](route-obstacles.md) · [Walls](walls-ledger.md) · [Unification](route-unification-ledger.md) · [Fact log](../../../logs/review-2011-status-quo-facts.md)

**The three walls in their sharpest form.** **P:** a one-sided half-plus bound for one stencil, for instance the halving increment E(H) − E(H/2), E = θ − id. **R:** positivity at every critical ratio, equivalently Weil positivity at every aperture; certified at aperture log 2 (every window of ratio below 4, at every block size, floor 10⁻¹³) and on the whole subcritical range of the critical scale. **U:** the asymptotic abscissa law, RH ⇔ approach ∧ AAL, with about sixty Lean producers; first rung the lone quartet. **R and P are chained:** if R fails, a critical half-line ground state exists at the first failing ratio, and on its terminal slab the forced joint height is the squared halving increment up to 95√H times it. A one-sided bound for the halving increment excludes every failure of R. [Square](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheTerminalJointHeightIsTheSquaredHalvingIncrement.lean#L136)

**Best full-proof prospect (§2.20):** the continuity method for the window index. Start from the certified windows below ratio 4, produce a lossy Poincaré inequality for the window chains (a strip), and at the endpoint prove an a priori gap on the windows that are already bound, which is the exclusion of the critical state. **Best intermediate question (§2.19):** AAL.

**Scope.** RH is open. Lean statements are linked and were read in source; compile status was not checked. The 7/8 theorem and the strip 20999/24000 are Lean theorems of this project. Proposals, pilots and priorities are research judgments. No literature comparison is made.

## 2.4 A breakthrough below RH and below quasi-RH: exclude a lone quartet

**Question.** Can ζ have exactly one off-line quartet {ρ, ρ̄, 1−ρ, 1−ρ̄}, with every other zero on the line?

A negative answer is the first unconditional exclusion of an off-line configuration at every depth up to the strip. It is strictly weaker than RH and independent of the strip. In Lean the lone quartet has five equivalent forms: exactly two right zeros; realPacketNegIndex = 2; the one-wave law ψ(x) − x = −2m Re(x^ρ/ρ) + O(√x log²x); failure of the three-probe law; failure of the two-interval radial law. ## 2.5 A breakthrough for a positivity producer: a lossy Poincaré inequality for the window chains

**Question.** Is there a proof, using Λ ≥ 0 and the exact archimedean diagonal but no zero-free region, that the Doob chain of every neutral window of ratio 3K+1 (at one block size ≥ K^10 for each K) has Poincaré constant at least its binding energy minus CK^θ, for one θ < 8999/24000?

**The objects (Lean).** A neutral window N is a symmetric matrix with off-diagonal entries ≤ 0 (minus the jets and the floor-map contacts Λ(k)/k), so it has a positive ground vector φ, Nφ = λφ. Conjugation by φ gives a reversible chain with weights φ_i² and conductances −N_ij φ_iφ_j ≥ 0, and μ = −λ is the binding energy. The window has one bound state iff the chain's Poincaré constant is at least μ; a set with small cut conductance gives a second bound state. RH ⇔ the Poincaré inequality on every window ⇔ a uniform defect C on every window. The binding energy has exact order √ρ at ratio ρ, created by the floor maps of the integers of size ρ that join the two ends of the window. A shift CK^θ on the windows of ratio 3K+1 gives width ≤ θ, and the width gives the shift back (with a factor 1 + log R at general block size). [Doob form](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MixingArithmeticOfTheFloorTrees/TheBoundStatesOfAWindowAreTheSlowModesOfItsDoobChain.lean#L471) · [Bottleneck](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MixingArithmeticOfTheFloorTrees/TheBoundStatesOfAWindowAreTheSlowModesOfItsDoobChain.lean#L369) · [Uniform defect](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MixingArithmeticOfTheFloorTrees/AUniformLowerBoundOnTheMarginOfTheWindowMatricesForcesTheRiemannHypothesis.lean#L505) · [Two ends](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MixingArithmeticOfTheFloorTrees/TheBindingOfALongWindowJoinsItsTwoEnds.lean#L346) · [Receiver](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExponentDynamicsOfTheCriticalStrip/TheMarginOfTheWindowMatricesIsARulerAtPolynomialBlockSize.lean#L616) · [Converse](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExponentDynamicsOfTheCriticalStrip/TheMarginOfTheWindowMatricesSinksAtTheRateOfTheWidth.lean#L789)

**Why this question.** Every positivity theorem in the corpus is one of three kinds: a finite certificate at a fixed aperture (every window of ratio below 4 at every block size, floor 10⁻¹³), subcritical domination of the prime defect by the Γ reserve (cubic spline up to (log N)^α, α < 1/8; Denjoy–Carleman up to ε·c*_N), or selection at fixed overlap through the pole. None carries information about the zeros: fixed apertures are blind to width, and the reserve argument ends at the critical scale. The window defect is the one quantitative positivity statement with a receiver in both directions. A defect proved by positivity methods below the strip is the first positivity-produced zero-free region and the first producer not from metaplectic cancellation.

**Why it is hard.** The Poincaré constant and the binding energy are both of order √K; θ < 1/2 asks to match them to relative precision K^(θ−1/2). Constant-factor tools (Cheeger's inequality, canonical paths, comparison of chains) give nothing: the comparison must hold to leading order. The sink bound (index ≤ number of rows where Nv < 0 for a positive trial vector v) has the same requirement: v must be an approximate ground vector with row deficits ≤ CK^θ off one row; the constant vector works only on narrow windows (16J ≤ R). ## 2.13 A programme for 𝔽₁ geometry

**What exists (AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ).** The arithmetic square is a sequence ring with Frobenius endomorphisms F_n; it is a δ-ring. The graph Γ_n of F_n is a ghost component. ## 2.20 The most likely route to a proof of RH

**The guess.** RH will be proved as a positivity statement for the neutral windows of the floor graph of ℤ, by a continuity method in the window ratio. The only possible obstruction is the critical state, and one arithmetic inequality excludes it.

**Why this route and not another.**
1. *Exact calculus and a certified start.* R has an exact recursion in Lean. Every window of ratio below 4 holds one bound state at every block size; the ratio-squaring step carries an explicit excess; losses add below the binding energy of the middle; and the gap decreases with the window and along the refinement tower and is lower semicontinuous. 2. *The continuity method is a theorem.* If at every cutoff the windows that hold exactly one bound state have a common positive gap, RH holds; the estimate is asked only of windows that are already bound. The obstruction is sharp: if some window is unbound, then bound windows of no larger ratio have arbitrarily small gaps. [A priori gap ⇒ RH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/GreenCalculusOfThePrimeFreeWindow/TheBoundRangeEndsOnlyWhereTheGapOfTheBoundWindowsCloses.lean#L308) · [Gap closes](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/GreenCalculusOfThePrimeFreeWindow/TheBoundRangeEndsOnlyWhereTheGapOfTheBoundWindowsCloses.lean#L288)
3. *Selection by form.* The windows are Z-matrices because Λ ≥ 0, so they have positive ground vectors and Doob chains. The Liouville and pole-free sign twins have neither; this is the only R object that rejects the sign twins at every ratio by its structure, where positivity at fixed overlap rejects them only through the pole. 4. *Measurable partial progress.* The margin is a receiver in both directions (margin rate θ ⇔ width θ), so every intermediate result is a zero-free strip.
5. *One endpoint object.* Off RH the first failure is a single critical state with a Lean list of forced properties. On the terminal slab its joint height is the squared halving increment, so a one-sided P bound for one stencil also excludes it.

The alternatives are each blocked at a named point. The two-atom route (approach ∧ AAL) needs approach, which contains Lindelöf. Stepanov over ℤ needs a size comparison that the polynomial-operator no-go excludes for Frobenius-linear operators. Detector improvements reach at most 5/6 with the known budget. **Mechanism.** At ratio ρ the binding energy μ(ρ) ≍ √ρ is created by the floor maps of the integers of size ρ, which join the two ends of the window. RH says that the Doob chain of every window mixes at rate at least μ. The continuity method raises ρ. The index stays one as long as the gap of the bound windows stays bounded below, and the first failure is a closing gap whose limit object is the critical state, the zero-energy minimizer of the half-line system at the threshold W₀. **Failures to expect, and what each teaches.**
- *R is P on forms.* If the Poincaré defect of every test is a Θ-meter, S2 is a strip problem in disguise and the route reduces to O1. The positive survivor is the Doob form of the meter.
- *Beurling selection.* The over-regular Beurling system has Λ ≥ 0 and an integer count with constant error and fails Weil positivity. An argument that does not see the Γ diagonal at leading order fails there.
- *Gluing loss.* Two coupled windows hold at most the bound states of the product model with potentials lowered by the coupling excess. If that excess grows like the binding energy, the recursion cannot reach large ratios without loss, and the continuity method must run at fixed ratio along the block-size tower instead. - *Zero-side consistency.* Tempered zero configurations with a finite off-line layer have critical states, so an exclusion that uses only zero-side information fails.
- *Finite certificates.* Fixed apertures and finite windows prove nothing uniform; S3 needs the whole ratio range.

**Lessons from the corpus that shape the route.**
1. Every quantitative statement is an exact meter. A route needs an endpoint mechanism, here the continuity method, and not a sharper estimate.
2. Positivity at fixed overlap selects ζ from the sign twins only through the pole. The Z-matrix structure is the selection that persists at every ratio.
3. The physical axiom class collapses to ζ, so arithmetic must enter quantitatively.
4. Subcritical reserve arguments end at the critical scale. Positivity beyond it must be produced, not borrowed from the Γ reserve.
5. Over 𝔽_q purity is an equality case. The endpoint of this route is the ℤ-analogue: at the threshold the form vanishes on the state, and excluding the state is proving that no equality case exists.

This is a research judgment. S0 is in Lean and S1 is known mathematics; no result in the corpus shows that S2 or S3 is provable.
