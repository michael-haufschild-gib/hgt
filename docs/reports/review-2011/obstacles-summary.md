# Research programme for a formal proof of RH

**Diagnosis.** The corpus has turned almost every route into an exact statement about Θ, the supremum of real parts of ζ zeros. Eleven observables have *exact* calibrations at both Θ ≤ 5/6 and RH, and several have unconditional exponent laws: integer floor rows read Θ−1, long Möbius energy 2(Θ−1/2), r-grid gcd shortfalls max(0,1−r(1−Θ)), Mertens masses Θ, forced at every large cutoff, every rough interior beyond X^α reads Θ, one Steklov pulse reads the strip. A meter has no slack to harvest. Every remaining task is therefore of one of three kinds. **P:** produce one upper bound for one signed arithmetic remainder. **R:** prove one complete sign using what ζ has and its twins lack. **U:** exclude one exceptional stratum. The producers available in Lean are classical analytic inputs, the detector strip Θ ≤ 20999/24000 (with ζ ≠ 0 beyond 7/8), and, over finite fields, Stepanov's method, which proves Hasse–Weil for every hyperelliptic curve. [Route report](route-obstacles.md) · [Walls](walls-ledger.md) · [Unification](route-unification-ledger.md) · [Research log](../../../logs/review-2011-research-log.md)

**Three reductions define the frontier.** ECS reduces at its terminal slab to the halving increment E(H)−E(H/2), E=θ−id, so exclusion of the critical state and one-signed prime error are one theorem. [Joint height](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/JointOscillationsAtTheLastHalfOfThePrimeRow.lean#L151) · [Halving increment](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheTerminalOddQuotientHalfDetectsTheZeroAbscissa.lean#L204) The Weil comb reduces to its odd-sector Schur complement: the even block is coercive and the Liouville-twisted comb, with identical contact sizes, is negative. [Odd sector](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheMinimizingCorrectionOnASublatticeOfTheWeilComb.lean#L171) · [Liouville comb](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L1364) Finite-layer exclusion has ten equivalent forms. Two-probe separation is unconditional, but separating probes by prime scale provably fails. [Probe separation](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeDisplacementIndexAmplificationPayoff.lean#L364)

**Best full-proof prospect (§2.20).** An arithmetic Bombieri–Stepanov argument: an auxiliary section on the arithmetic square vanishing to high order along Frobenius graphs, giving a one-sided upper bound for the halving increment. The one-sided ⇒ RH step is already proved. The single missing ingredient is identified in Lean: inseparability at the real place. **Best intermediate question (§2.19).** The asymptotic abscissa law. RH ⇔ approach to the line ∧ AAL, and AAL has more than a dozen Lean producers from at least six routes.

**Scope.** RH remains open. Lean statements are linked. Proposals, pilots and priorities are research judgments. The corpus strip and the 7/8 theorem are Lean theorems of this project. No literature comparison is made here.

## 2.3 Frankenstein routes

A Frankenstein route is worth pursuing only when its pieces are proved, or posed, for *different* objects whose open inputs are strictly weaker than RH, and when a Lean theorem assembles them. Four assemblies meet this standard or come close.

**F1 · Two atoms (wired in Lean).** RH ⇔ approach to the line ∧ the asymptotic abscissa law (AAL). [RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ZetaWeakRecurrenceLadderCollapse.lean#L292) The two atoms come from different routes and different mathematics.

- **AAL** (finitely many zeros right of 1/2+h forces none) has more than a dozen Lean producers, each weaker than RH, from at least six routes. Four of them follow; the others are bounded Weil-lag readings on powers or progressions (WL), subexponential polar ground energy (RW), dressed JAS positivity and twisted plateaus. [Lag on powers ⇒ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/AsymptoticAbscissaLawProducers.lean#L102) · [Ground energy ⇒ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/PolarGroundEnergySubexponentialZerosApproachLine.lean#L49) **BAG:** weak self-recurrence of ζ, return shifts beyond every height on every disc in the strip, with no density. **Prime towers:** the ζ kernel bounded on infinitely many prime towers. **SC:** bounded coupling on one positive two-prime cone ⟨p⟩₊×⟨q⟩₊. **JAS:** constant-vector positivity on infinitely many dyadic prime shells. [Weak recurrence ⇒ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ZetaWeakRecurrenceLadderCollapse.lean#L287) · [Towers ⇒ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticKreinLangerTheory/PrimeTowersAndConeCouplingProduceAsymptoticAbscissaLaw.lean#L155) · [Cone ⇒ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticKreinLangerTheory/PrimeTowersAndConeCouplingProduceAsymptoticAbscissaLaw.lean#L232) · [Shells ⇒ AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticKreinLangerTheory/PrimeTowersAndConeCouplingProduceAsymptoticAbscissaLaw.lean#L264)
- **Approach** (PTR) is Lindelöf on the line plus finiteness of nonreal zeros of every cosine shift. [Approach](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/LindelofCosineFiniteDefectDepth.lean#L191)

Under AAL, RH ⇔ finite off-line support, and every large-height receiver reads the global abscissa: Hadamard positivity, central-gap budgets, finite index. [Under AAL](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticKreinLangerTheory/PrimeTowersAndConeCouplingProduceAsymptoticAbscissaLaw.lean#L313) A three-atom variant also exists in Lean. If one negative prime witness forces unbounded index (U1), RH ⇔ approach ∧ landing. [Three atoms](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeDisplacementIndexAmplificationPayoff.lean#L338) The value of F1 is that its atoms are different kinds of mathematics: recurrence or prime-tower boundedness for AAL, growth on the line for approach. Each is strictly weaker than RH. The cost is that approach contains Lindelöf on the critical line, itself open, and that every AAL producer, though weaker than RH, is open.

**F2 · ACS through its composite face (5/6).** The 11/3 conductor prefix carries opposite quartic terms in the terminal band and its complement. Bounding them separately loses. [Quartic](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheLastFirstPrimesInTheSignedProduct.lean#L212) The same statement is a one-sided 5/12 threshold for the square-corrected balanced-composite remainder. [5/12 ⇔ 11/3](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheHalfAbscissaOfTheBalancedCompositeRemainder.lean#L308) The composite remainder involves no band/complement split. It is governed by unitary-divisor combinatorics, which the corpus controls in detail: low-rank defects form intersecting families (Erdős–Ko–Rado), pivotal blocks give Sperner antichains, the prime-power part is supported near the diagonal, and the √n bias is priced with error n^(7/16). [Bias](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheSquareRootBiasOfTheOddBalancedComposites.lean#L70) The assembly: prove the one-sided 5/12 bound on the composite side, then read Θ ≤ 5/6 and the ACS prefix through the Lean equivalence. The blocker is that every value of the odd unitary midpoint occurs cofinally, so combinatorics alone fixes no sign. The arithmetic of which integers are balanced has to enter.

**F3 · Stepanov side plus real-zero exclusion.** The Stepanov-side lower bound is blind exactly to real off-line zeros: a configuration with a real off-line pair satisfies it, and a real zero masks shallower nonreal ones. [Blind to real zeros](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SurfaceOfSpecZ/TheStepanovSideIsBlindToRealZeros.lean#L154) · [Masking](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SurfaceOfSpecZ/ARealZeroMasksTheShallowerZeros.lean#L113) For ζ, real zeros in (1/2,1) are excluded classically. A Stepanov-side producer, valid for every configuration with an integer Euler product and the FE, combined with real-zero exclusion therefore gives RH for ζ. The real-zero part is the easy half. The producer is §2.6.

**F4 · Sieve-ordered Schur elimination (SAC × HF).** On the window (N,(1+3K)N] two integers coprime to every prime ≤ 1+3K have no prime contact, so all contacts run along prime rays from multiples of small primes. The comb's multiples of d ≥ 2 carry arbitrarily large floors at every fixed relative aperture. [Spacing ≥ 2](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheSpacingThresholdForDivergentCombFloors.lean#L85) The proposal: eliminate prime by prime, multiples of 2, then of 3, and so on, keeping each Schur correction explicit. Use the HF two-probe separation to control the cross terms between eliminated classes. [Two-probe determinant](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/PrimeDisplacementGramAndModeQuotients.lean#L319) The decisive test is the Liouville comb. Its contacts have the same sizes and it is negative at macroscopic cost, so any elimination step that bounds a Schur correction through \|B\|² alone proves a false statement. A valid step must exploit the sign pattern of Λ along prime rays, where all contacts are positive. [Prime rays](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/PrimeRayGram.lean)

Two assemblies are excluded by Lean results. Combining bounds on different P meters does not lower Θ, since the rows are exact. Combining a fixed-aperture R sign with any P bound proves nothing, since fixed-aperture positivity holds for configurations of every width. [Fixed aperture](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheZeroWidthIsAnUnboundedApertureProperty.lean#L135)

## 2.4 A breakthrough below RH and below quasi-RH: exclude a lone quartet

**Question.** Can ζ have exactly one off-line quartet {ρ, ρ̄, 1−ρ, 1−ρ̄}, with every other zero on the line?

A negative answer would be the first unconditional exclusion of an off-line configuration at *every* depth up to the strip. It is strictly weaker than RH, does not depend on the strip, and in Lean it has three equivalent forms. **Three-probe law:** any one negative prime witness can be amplified into three disjoint negative-definite high-prime probes. **Two-interval radial law:** the ordinary-prime radius test. **The statement itself.** [Three-probe law](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeDisplacementIndexAmplificationPayoff.lean#L157) · [Radial law](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheOrdinaryPrimeRadiusDetectsExactlyOneOffLineQuartet.lean#L52)

**Payoff.** The PDR dilation residuals at 2, 3, 6 become an RH test. The two-wave regime of PPC disappears. Under a lone quartet the BAG accurate returns lie near two shifts, and the alternative to RH in QIC becomes the third confined wave. Above all it opens U1 at its first rung. [PDR payoff](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeDisplacementIndexAmplificationPayoff.lean#L210) · [Third wave](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/OppositeExcursionsOfTheThirdConfinedPrimeWave.lean#L202)

**Why it is tractable in principle.** Under a lone quartet everything off the line is three numbers β, γ, m. By the explicit formula the prime error is −2m·Re(x^ρ/ρ) − 2m·Re(x^(1−ρ̄)/(1−ρ̄)) plus O(x^(1/2)log²x) from the remaining zeros, all on the line by hypothesis. The corpus already computes the adversary's effect on every route. The obstacle is structural: a smooth ordinary-power wave satisfies every *linear* transport identity, including the reflected-composite compatibility, exactly at a ζ zero. Linear identities cannot exclude it, nor can two dilations, quarter-period intervals or 0/1 occupancy. [Linear compatibility](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/ReflectedCompositeWaveTransmission.lean#L1580) · [Excluded attacks](../rh-killed-routes.md#selection)

**Proposed attack.** Use a nonlinear arithmetic constraint at the actual frequency. The cleanest is integrality combined with the exact Euler product at one prime. The quartet wave x^β cos(γ log x + φ) must be reproduced by integer jumps of θ in every dilation class modulo 2^k. At the same time the scattering sector of the prime 2 must cancel the repeated 2-power path with weight exactly 1/2, at a rate that a lone quartet fixes to (1−β)/2. [Repeated path](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TailEnergy/PrimePowerReturnsForceSignedCancellation.lean#L113) First test: the twin test suite. The argument must fail for the prime-2 Schur twin, whose off-line zero is real, and for Euler-symmetric quartet models. **Falsification:** a construction of an Euler product with the exact ζ coefficients except for a lone quartet. The FloorFree models realize lone quartets with Euler-symmetric data, so an attack must use the coefficients 1, not only Euler structure. [Euler-symmetric models](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/FloorFreeArithmetic/TheZeroConfigurationDoesNotSeeTheEulerProduct.lean#L260)

## 2.5 A breakthrough for a positivity producer: a sign-sensitive odd sector

**Question.** Is there a sign-sensitive inequality, using that every contact along a prime ray carries +log p, which makes the odd-sector Schur complement C − BᵀA⁻¹B of the unit-spacing comb nonnegative on a profile family where the Liouville comb is negative?

**Setting.** On aligned windows S=(2N, 2(N+3KN)], RH ⇔ the comb form is nonnegative on profiles whose rows vanish at every even site ⇔ every even/odd 2×2 parity minor is nonnegative. The even block has an unconditional unit floor. [Parity minors](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheParityMinorsOfTheCompleteWeilComb.lean#L105) · [Even-row equilibria](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheMinimizingCorrectionOnASublatticeOfTheWeilComb.lean#L171) The Liouville comb has contact weights (−1)^k log p, the same sizes, and macroscopic-cost negative profiles built from centred multiples. [Liouville negative](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L1364)

**The first rung.** Take the Liouville-negative centred-multiple profiles and evaluate the actual Λ comb on them. Prove nonnegativity there, or a lower bound −o(‖c‖²). This is far below RH: it is one explicit family. It would be the first theorem in which positivity of the comb *uses the sign of Λ*. Every positivity statement in the corpus holds equally for the twin: small aperture, spacing ≥ 2, fixed shapes.

**The producer.** Extend from that family to all even-row equilibria by sieve-ordered elimination (F4). Order the small primes. At each stage the eliminated block (multiples of p) has a divergent floor. The cross terms along p-rays have a fixed sign for Λ and alternating signs for the twin. The target identity writes each Schur correction as a sum of squares plus a remainder linear in the positive ray sums, which the next stage's floor pays. The prime-ray Gram has an exact lower spectral endpoint attained only when every ray sum vanishes, which locates the extremal direction. [Joint kernel of ray sums](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/TheJointKernelOfThePrimeRaySums.lean#L233)

**Failure modes.** If the Λ comb is negative on the Liouville family at macroscopic cost, RH fails in a quantified way through the debit/budget equivalence. [Debit ⇔ RH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SignedArithmeticCorrespondences/ControlledDivisibilityPositivityAndTheMacroscopicBarrier.lean#L1583) If it is positive there but the ray-sign identity cannot pay the next stage, the failing stage names the prime and conductor class where cancellation must come from interaction between rays. That would show the producer must be global in the primes.

## 2.6 Without positivity, Weil positivity or spectral input: archimedean inseparability

**Question.** Is there an operator on integer auxiliaries over the prime torus that kills the Frobenius (Adams) action *at the real place*, in the way d/dx kills x^q in characteristic p, so that an auxiliary can vanish to high order on the prime orbit without paying length?

**Why this is the right question.** Over 𝔽_q Stepanov's method proves the Riemann hypothesis for curves with no positivity and no spectral theory. An auxiliary polynomial vanishes to order m at every rational point because x^q − x has vanishing derivative contribution from x^q. Comparing degree with multiplicity gives N ≤ q + O(g√q), and the twist gives the other sign. Lean contains this proof for every hyperelliptic curve and the absence of negative squares in Stepanov's order kernel. [Hasse–Weil](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/TheCountLawOfEveryHyperellipticCurve.lean#L851) · [Order kernel](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticKreinLangerTheory/StepanovsOrderOverTheFiniteField.lean#L116) Over ℤ the corpus isolates exactly what fails. The fixed-point auxiliary has minimal length lcm(1,…,N), so its logarithm *is* ψ(N). RH ⇔ that length is ≤ N + C√N log²N. Exact vanishing order costs length at every point, and the Frobenius on torsion is the Galois/Adams action, inseparable only at finite places. [Length ⇔ RH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/TheLefschetzNumbersAreStepanovDegrees.lean#L236) · [Length = lcm](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/TheLefschetzNumbersAreStepanovDegrees.lean#L178) The ghost ring is a δ-ring with Frobenius = x^p + p·δ(x), but δ-order does not descend to integer vanishing order. [δ-structure](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ/TheArithmeticSquareIsCofree.lean#L212) · [δ-order](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ/DeltaOrderDoesNotDescendAtEveryIntegerPoint.lean#L87)

**What a positive answer gives.** A one-sided upper bound for ψ(N) − N, or for the halving increment, by counting alone. The one-sided ⇒ RH step is proved. [One sign suffices](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheTerminalOddQuotientHalfDetectsTheZeroAbscissa.lean#L204)

**Calibration that any proposal must pass.** It must fail on the integer-norm one-off Euler product with zeros 3/5 ± i, which has an Euler product but no FE. It may hold on the real one-off with zero 3/5, since the Stepanov side is blind to real zeros. So it must use the real place through the Γ factor. [Fails on one-off](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/TheStepanovBoundSeparatesZetaFromTheOneOff.lean#L127) · [Holds on real one-off](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/TheStepanovBoundSeparatesZetaFromTheOneOff.lean#L171) A first experiment: on the torsion points of one circle (u^q = u), find a derivation-like operator D with D(ψ_a f) = 0 to first order along the orbit t ↦ (p^(it)) and integral on auxiliaries. Then measure the vanishing order it buys at one return of the orbit against the Rouché cap. The corpus already proves that polynomial syzygies force exact derivative vanishing on the torsion grid and that this is still paid in degree. The operator has to break that payment. [Syzygies](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/PolynomialSyzygiesForceTheMixedJets.lean#L172)

## 2.7 RH as a physical phenomenon

**The phenomenon.** The modular surface PSL₂(ℤ)\ℍ is the horizon of a topological AdS black hole. Eisenstein wave packets lift to exact solutions smooth across the horizon. Every ζ zero is a quasinormal mode, a pole of the retarded correlator with exact multiplicity. The reflected signal of a pulse is an echo train with delays 2 log n and weights φ(n)/n. In this system RH is a *universal ringdown law*: every pulse decays at e^(−τ/4)·e^(ετ), and this is the same exponent as horocycle thermalization at y^(3/4). Cusp forms are undamped modes with λ ≥ 1/4. The ζ modes lie exactly 1/4 deeper iff RH. [One exponent](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TheTwoRatesOfTheHorizonAreOneExponent.lean#L34) · [Rate ⇔ strip](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TailEnergy/RegularityAndReceiverEquivalence.lean#L737) · [Horizon programme](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown.lean)

**What physics alone cannot do.** For every δ>0 some deformed horizon fails rate δ. ## 2.8 RH as topology or geometry

**The geometric object.** The de Bruijn–Newman heat flow H_t is a homotopy of finite-index Pontryagin states. At each t>0 the Hodge defect of the heat dilation kernel equals the number of conjugate pairs that collide on the real axis after time t. The defect is nonincreasing, jumps exactly at collisions, and is zero from t=1/8. RH ⇔ the Hodge index holds along the whole flow. **A compactness the corpus has proved.** The local constant satisfies c·d²/log X ≤ Λ_X ≤ (6600/9991+ε)/log X. Hence at heat t>0 every zero above height 2π·exp((6600/9991+ε)/t) is real, and the defect at time t is computed on a *bounded* window. [Reality horizon](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ZeroConfigurationGeometry/HeatScaleRepulsion/SymmetricHalfCollisionLaw.lean#L230) All noncompactness sits in the corner t→0⁺, height→∞, along the curve X(t)=2π·exp(C/t).

**Breakthrough question.** Is there a boundary integer, a winding of the θ-boundary discriminant along the moving reality horizon, that equals the Hodge defect for every t>0 and whose limit as t→0⁺ can be computed from θ alone? ## 2.9 Does a proof of RH need a new field?

**Yes, in a specific sense.** Exactness is the corpus's strongest result and also its verdict. The integers carry many exact meters of Θ and no producer. Every producer in Lean either is a classical analytic bound, which stops at a strip, or lives over a finite field. A proof needs a theory whose *output* is an inequality with slack for an integer observable, obtained by a mechanism that sees what twins do not. No such theory exists. The candidate is a **theory of arithmetic producers**.

**What it is about.** A producer is a construction C(N), built from integers and primes without reference to zeros, together with a comparison of two sizes of C(N) that forces an upper bound for a signed remainder. Over 𝔽_q the model producer is Stepanov–Bombieri: degree against multiplicity. A second model is Weil's: Castelnuovo positivity of an intersection pairing. The field must say which comparisons exist over ℤ, what they cost, and which twins they separate.

**Criteria it must meet.**
1. **Calibration.** Reprove inside the theory the Riemann hypothesis for curves, starting with the hyperelliptic case already in Lean, and Riemann–Roch with exact constants. 3. **Non-circularity.** The bound must not be computed from ψ. ALST shows the minimal auxiliary length *is* e^ψ, so a construction that evaluates it is a meter. [Length = lcm](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/TheLefschetzNumbersAreStepanovDegrees.lean#L178)

**Questions it must answer.** What replaces inseparability at the real place? ## 2.20 The most likely proof route: arithmetic Bombieri–Stepanov

**Judgment.** The corpus shows that reformulation is exhausted, and that every RH-equivalent statement is either an exact meter, a complete sign, or a stratum exclusion. In the function-field case three proofs exist: Weil's positivity, Stepanov–Bombieri's auxiliary polynomial, and Deligne's weights. The corpus has tested the integer analogue of each. **Weil:** comb positivity, reduced to the odd-sector Schur complement, a sign that twins with identical contact sizes violate. **Deligne:** the tensor-power bootstrap is retired, because it reads the existing width. **Stepanov:** the integer auxiliary is formalized and fails at *exactly one point*, the absence of inseparability at the real place. A route whose failure is localized to one missing operator is the most promising. Stepanov's method is also the only one of the three that the corpus executes completely over finite fields: Hasse–Weil for every hyperelliptic curve, and hence Hasse for elliptic curves in odd characteristic. Weil's mechanism appears only as an implication from an abstract Hodge index. [Elliptic Hasse](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/EllipticCurveHasseBound.lean#L119) · [Hodge index ⇒ bound](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/AdjunctionThresholdLaw.lean#L134) Its output is a *one-sided upper bound*, and for ζ the corpus proves that one-sided bounds on the halving increment, or on D(n), are equivalent to RH. [Hasse–Weil in Lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/TheCountLawOfEveryHyperellipticCurve.lean#L851) · [Integer Stepanov ⇔ RH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/TheLefschetzNumbersAreStepanovDegrees.lean#L236) · [One sign](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheTerminalOddQuotientHalfDetectsTheZeroAbscissa.lean#L204) [Retired TPP](../rh-killed-routes.md#tpp)

**Proof mechanism.**
1. **Surface and graphs.** Work on the arithmetic square with Frobenius graphs Γ_n, whose contacts give Λ(n)+1+1/(n²−1), with conductor sections of square conductors as the space of auxiliary functions (ACS/MSM machinery). [Contacts](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ/ThePlanarSquareHasTheContactsOfTheSquareAndPaysThem.lean#L333) · [Sections](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/OneMultiplicityPerSquareConductor.lean#L312)
2. **Riemann–Roch count.** Bombieri's step: sections of bounded height with prescribed vanishing exist by a Siegel/Riemann–Roch count with the Γ factor in the constants. The corpus has Siegel auxiliaries on smooth numbers and function-field Riemann–Roch. [Siegel](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticLefschetzStepanovTheory/SiegelAuxiliariesOnTheTorsion.lean#L119) · [Riemann–Roch](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CurvesOverFiniteFields/Duality.lean#L549)

4. **Count.** Length ≥ multiplicity × (number of closed points), giving an upper bound for θ(N) or for the halving increment with error N^(1/2+ε).
5. **Close.** The one-sided bound gives RH by the proved equivalences.
