# Route-unification ledger

**Status 2026-10-25, corpus snapshot `86591dd4b` plus the working tree.** The 31 live routes, 17 candidates and PW-B are faces of three construction problems:

- **U1 — critical arithmetic cancellation**: the *rate* reading of blindness.
- **U2 — complete signed positivity**: the *sign* reading of blindness.
- **U3 — faithful realization and forcing**: the *carrier* reading of blindness.

All three ask the same question in different currencies: **what forces the actual arithmetic source to be blind to off-line zeros?** The corpus proves the readings equivalent at their endpoints:

- **U1.** A generic orbit's growth rate equals the depth of the deepest zero it sees.
- **U2.** Orbit Hodge positivity holds iff the orbit is blind to off-line pairs. Family positivity holds iff the family is jointly blind, and every scale-shift-invariant positive test space is blind.
- **U3.** The maximal sharp-stable positive Meyer ideal is exactly the off-line-blind ideal, and it equals the whole source iff RH.

Identical receivers do not mean identical producers. Each family still needs its own missing construction.

Evidence: corpus search, statement reading and umbrella reachability (`logs/review-2510-update/`). Labels as in the complete report: [L] reachable from a package umbrella, [S] not.

## 1. Levels of unification

| Level | What it justifies | What it does not |
|---|---|---|
| **Exact identity** | Two expressions are equal on a stated source and normalization. | Extension to another completion or norm. |
| **Receiver isomorphism** | Same threshold at every rate `σ` (width functor). | Equal finite tests, equal unconditional rates, equal difficulty. |
| **First-rung coincidence** | The weakest nontrivial conclusions coincide; for every U1 receiver, and for multiplicative forcing, this is quasi-RH. | Equality at later rungs or of producers. |
| **Maximality** | Every member of a class lies in one extremal object. Positive single-channel carriers lie in the blind ideal. | That the extremal object is reached by the actual source. |
| **Directional transfer** | A proved bound implies another under stated hypotheses. | The converse; dropping the hypotheses. |
| **Shared construction problem** | The same missing estimate, sign or object. | That one producer yields the others. |
| **Analogy** | A model suggests a mechanism. | Validity for actual ζ. |

## 2. The three families

### U1 — Critical arithmetic cancellation (rate reading)

**Primary data:** actual prime or Möbius coefficients. Observables: lag, spline, deficit, Abel moment, history, regularized series, quotient head, probe, pronic energy, Vaughan Gram, cofactor module, divisor sums.

**Missing input:** a signed estimate at every positive slack.

**Proved unifications [L]:**

- The width functor makes these receivers isomorphic: spectral width, abscissa, damped Möbius and Liouville admission, the ξ half-plane, and the Suzuki formation exponent.
- **First-rung coincidence.** Each graded U1 receiver's first gain beyond Vinogradov–Korobov is a fixed zero-free strip: the balanced statistic, centred-prime band, short-window Möbius and prime saving, Vaughan empty leg, JAS budget, PBM graded abscissa and WL rate `σ`.
- **Vaughan leading order is classified.** Every subcritical moving-cutoff limit is a rank-≤ 1 smooth matrix at an amplitude from a compact countable set. Only the residual is open.

**Subfamilies:**

- **Width readings:** WL, SRT, PBM, MC, JAS, FU, RP, DR.
- **Critical energy:** PM, CTF, together with the BVI, ACCE, ADR, SAEI, SSA and THIM machinery.

**Separate currencies:** LI (reciprocal depth) and RB (extremal inequality). BAG enters through local recurrence. U1's only handles that are not graded are frequency averaging (the filtered deficit) and the escape alternative (cofactor modules).

### U2 — Complete signed positivity (sign reading)

**Primary object:** the complete prime–Gamma–polar Weil form.

- **Direct attacks on its sign:** RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF, TT.
- **Identification with Hodge index:** ACS.
- **Sign under other quantifiers:** LA, TH, HCF, GTA, XN, PA (degree, rank, heat time, order).

**Proved unifications [L]:**

- Fixed-aperture sign transfer RW ⇔ SZ ⇔ CHMD, with identified domains.
- The Suzuki first variation equals the Weil energy, including on RH-complete tent families.
- HF ceiling ⇒ AIP allocation.
- AIP allocation ⇔ ACS strict-cone RR.
- One-shift variation, odd capacity and blind minima are the parity components of the form.
- Weil margin ⇒ heat-Hankel positivity in a coupled rank–time window.
- **Averaged positivity at logarithmic aperture** is common to the whole family through the exact all-parity identity.
- **Finite-level existence.** RH ⇔ some weight system is positive at every finite aperture. U2 positivity is a family of finite feasibility problems glued by compactness and uniqueness.
- The ground-energy rate equals the polar rate, so the rate currencies of RW, SRT and ACS merge.

**Common localization:** at each aperture the obstruction is a finite low-frequency sector. In the incidence picture, frustration comes only from prime-sum edges. Across heights, the obstruction is selection.

### U3 — Faithful realization and forcing (carrier reading)

**Carriers:**

- geometric: ACS effectivity, the Meyer surface, AOG orbits and families;
- operator-theoretic: RN, GTA, PCS, IRK;
- probabilistic: W1C, FEL;
- photonic: PTR;
- categorical: CDC, SC, WBF, RFHM;
- divisor-diagnostic: HJ, SEC, BSH, APF, EWJ, EGC, PW-B;
- topological: TG;
- heat and divisor: WD cap, ALDG.

**Proved unifications:**

- **Maximality [L].** Every sharp-stable positive convolution ideal of the Meyer source lies in `meyerTraceBoundedIdeal`, the off-line-blind ideal. The ideal keeps every critical jet, carries Hodge index, RR, Bézout and ampleness, and equals the source iff RH. Every U3 carrier with single-channel sharp-stable positivity is therefore a sub-carrier of one object. Such carriers differ only in how much of it they see.
- On the Ξ divisor, the HJ residual, SEC seam, CDC confluence, BSH mate gain, IRK noninvertibility and W1C Clifford energy all read `Re ρ = 1/2` with exact depth laws. They are detectors.
- The Meyer surface Hodge index is equivalent to RH, and its Bézout and ampleness are each equivalent to RH [L].
- Operator-norm faithful positivity (faithful degrees, the Hilbert–Schmidt reserve, the bounded-orbit carrier) is equivalent to RH plus simple zeros.

**Common missing object:** a pre-divisor source with a **forcing law** that places it in the maximal blind ideal, using input outside single-channel positivity. Examples of where the law would act:

- the ACS effectivity source;
- the HJ/SEC weld;
- the TG sub-unit residual;
- the RN boundary orientation;
- exhaustion of the maximal blind ideal.

### The forcing currency: where U3 meets U1

The one proved forcing mechanism sits between the families.

- **Mechanism.** Positivity of `Λ` coupled across dilated channels, with a simple pole, no harmonic poles and integer zero orders, forces zero order 0 on `Re s = 1` [L]. It is a U3 mechanism, an exhaustion statement on the boundary line.
- **Output.** With growth input the same positivity yields the dlVP, Littlewood and VK regions [L]. That output is a U1 width statement.
- **Next rung.** It is the fixed strip of the U1 first-rung coincidence.

So multiplicative forcing and arithmetic cancellation share one graded currency below RH.

### 4.3 Geometric currency (U2 ↔ U3)

- **Scaling surface.** Pairing = pole plane − Weil form. Hodge index, RR plus ample effectivity, and zero sector defect are each equivalent to RH. The negative index counts distinct right zeros.
- **Meyer surface.** Trace RR holds unconditionally; Bézout ⇔ ampleness ⇔ RH; numerical = principal ⇔ simple zeros.
- **Maximal blind ideal.** All axioms hold; it is the greatest sharp-stable positive ideal; it equals the source ⇔ RH.
- **Bounded-orbit carrier [S].** All axioms hold, and exhaustion ⇔ RH plus simple zeros.
- **Function-field calibration.**
  - Rosati cones have Bézout without unitarity, and RR ⇔ unitarity.
  - In genus one, Hodge index on the window ⇔ degree positivity of `ℤ[F]`, i.e. Hasse.
  - The Hecke graphs of `ℤ` have no theorem of the square.

The Weil sign, ACS RR + Bézout, the Meyer Hodge index, exhaustion of the maximal blind ideal and the Lefschetz–Hasse–Weil bound are the same statement. An analytic payment of the low sector and a geometric forcing law would be two proofs of it.

### 4.4 Ground rate and clustering

`Δ ≤ polarRate = groundRate ≤ 1/2`, and RH ⇔ `groundRate = 0` [L]. Compensated floors above `Δ` ⇔ `polarRate = Δ`. Bounded local depth mass suffices; a rate excess needs collapsed clusters at doubly exponential height. RW, SRT, ACS, EWU and AIP share this currency.

### 4.5 Depth on the divisor (U3)

The first-prime seam charge `√2·log 2·sinh(δ log 2)`, the mate gain `2δ log n` and the other zero-indexed diagnostics have exact depth laws whose supremum is `Δ`. Positive Hermitian Meyer degrees see critical values only. **Diagnostics read depth; none forces it to vanish.**

### 4.6 Invariants (U3 selectivity)

The forcing law on `Re s = 1` needs four invariants, and each pole condition has a sharpness model. The functional equation excludes regular counts. The countermodel battery fails one invariant per model. Whether FE plus Beurling Euler positivity plus a simple pole characterize ζ (Q2) decides whether a forcing law using all invariants can be refuted by any model.

## 6. Consequences

1. **Count each producer once.** Each of the following is a single producer, shared by the routes listed:
   - a small-damping signed budget: PM, CTF, BVI, MC;
   - a complete Weil sign: RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF, TT, ACS;
   - a residual heat-region sign: LA, WD, TH, HCF;
   - a forcing law into the maximal blind ideal: TG, RN, ACS and all candidates.
2. **Reformulation is finished at the receiver level, and carrier maximality is settled.** The isomorphisms, first-rung coincidences and transfers leave nothing to gain from new equivalent endpoints. The maximality theorem leaves nothing to gain from new single-channel positive carriers.
3. **The three families reduce to three bridges and one law.**
   - **U1 — escape exclusion (Q3).** The only non-graded handle below quasi-RH.
   - **U2 — selection (Q1).** Pass from averaged positivity, already proved beyond Nyquist, to individual heights.
   - **U3 — selectivity of invariants (Q2), then forcing.** Decide whether ζ's invariants characterize it, then force the source into the maximal blind ideal with input outside the single-channel class.
   - **The common law:** arithmetic forces blindness. In the cancellation family it would appear as rate zero, in the positivity family as a complete sign, and in the realization family as exhaustion of the maximal blind ideal.
4. **Where the families meet.**
   - **U1 and U2** meet at the square-root threshold. At height `T`, the aperture `½ log T` separates diagonal readings from undersampled ones. Averaged positivity now crosses this threshold, up to `X ≤ Γ^{4/3}`, so the meeting point has moved from aperture to selection.
   - **U2 and U3** meet at one inequality: the Weil sign is the Meyer and scaling Hodge index, and equals exhaustion of the maximal blind ideal.
   - **U1 and U3** meet twice. First at the cofactor module: admission gives a faithful infinite module, escape a module annihilated by the paid ideal. Second at multiplicative forcing, whose graded continuation is the U1 width scale with first rung quasi-RH.
5. **Partial progress is family-specific.**
   - U1's rate axis is blocked at quasi-RH, and multiplicative forcing is blocked at the same rung.
   - U2 has genuine partial results: aperture windows, averages and finite-level feasibility.
   - U3 has blind sub-carriers, now all inside one maximal ideal, and the forcing law on `Re s = 1`.

   A result that converts one family's partial progress into another's would itself be a new mechanism. Example: short-window averaged positivity (U2) that controls zero ordinates would give U1 selection data.
6. **No reviewed theorem proves that a new field is needed.** The [summary](hard-obstacles-summary.md#4-would-the-b-tier-obstacles-require-a-new-field-of-mathematics) explains why the two known positivity mechanisms, single-channel quadratic and finite-degree multiplicative, are exhausted below RH strength.
