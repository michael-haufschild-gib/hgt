# Route-unification ledger

**Status 2026-10-25, corpus `eb175d946` plus the working tree of that day.** The 31 live routes, 17 candidates and PW-B are faces of three construction problems:

- **U1 — critical arithmetic cancellation**: the *rate* reading of blindness.
- **U2 — complete signed positivity**: the *sign* reading of blindness.
- **U3 — faithful realization and forcing**: the *carrier* reading of blindness, now with an *invariant* sub-reading.

All three ask one question in different currencies: **what forces the actual arithmetic source to be blind to off-line zeros?** The corpus proves the readings equivalent at their endpoints:

- **U1.** A generic orbit's growth rate equals the depth of the deepest zero it sees.
- **U2.** Orbit Hodge positivity holds iff the orbit is blind; family positivity iff joint blindness; every scale-shift-invariant positive test space is blind.
- **U3.** The maximal sharp-stable positive Meyer ideal is the off-line-blind ideal and equals the whole source iff RH. The degree-one invariants determine ζ. Pole order pays for off-line zeros in every model where they are inserted.

Identical receivers do not mean identical producers. Each family still needs its own missing construction.

## 1. Levels of unification

| Level | What it justifies | What it does not |
|---|---|---|
| **Exact identity** | Two expressions are equal on a stated source and normalization. | Extension to another completion or norm. |
| **Receiver isomorphism** | Same threshold at every rate `σ` (width functor). | Equal finite tests, rates or difficulty. |
| **First-rung coincidence** | The weakest nontrivial conclusions coincide; for every U1 receiver and for multiplicative forcing this is quasi-RH. | Equality of later rungs or of producers. |
| **Maximality** | Every member of a class lies in one extremal object. | That the actual source reaches it. |
| **Rigidity** | A set of invariants has exactly one solution. | That the solution's zeros are located. |
| **Directional transfer** | A proved bound implies another under hypotheses. | The converse. |
| **Shared construction problem** | The same missing estimate, sign or object. | That one producer yields the others. |
| **Analogy** | A model suggests a mechanism. | Validity for ζ. |

## 2. The three families

### U1 — Critical arithmetic cancellation (rate reading)

**Primary data:** prime or Möbius coefficients. Observables: lag, spline, deficit, Abel moment, history, regularized series, probe, pronic energy, Vaughan Gram, cofactor module, divisor sums.

**Missing input:** a signed estimate at every positive slack.

**Proved unifications:**

- The width functor makes the spectral, abscissa, damped Möbius and Liouville, ξ half-plane and Suzuki formation receivers isomorphic.
- **First-rung coincidence.** The first gain beyond Vinogradov–Korobov of each graded receiver is a fixed strip: the balanced statistic, centred-prime band, short windows, Vaughan empty leg, JAS budget, PBM abscissa, WL rate `σ`, and every Vaughan power improvement (convolution, residual Gram, factorial-order-one, tail gains) [S].
- **Vaughan phase transition at half damping.** Admission at and above `ε = 1/2` (actual Gram rank two; a rank-three ray of moving limits at the endpoint); below it, leading order classified and every power gain a strip [S].
- **Escape structure.** Record-minimum limits with positive diagonals; singleton-or-continuum families; contraction calculus; rank-one sign law.

**Subfamilies:**

- **Width readings:** WL, SRT, PBM, MC, JAS, FU, RP, DR.
- **Critical energy:** PM, CTF, with the BVI, ACCE, ADR, SAEI, SSA and THIM machinery.

**Separate currencies:** LI (reciprocal depth) and RB (extremal inequality); BAG through local recurrence. U1's non-graded handles are frequency averaging (filtered deficit) and the escape alternative.

### U2 — Complete signed positivity (sign reading)

**Primary object:** the complete prime–Gamma–polar Weil form.

- **Direct attacks on its sign:** RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF, TT.
- **Identification with Hodge index:** ACS.
- **Sign under other quantifiers:** LA, TH, HCF, GTA, XN, PA (degree, rank, heat time, order).

**Proved unifications:**

- Fixed-aperture sign transfer RW ⇔ SZ ⇔ CHMD with identified domains; Suzuki first variation = Weil energy, including on RH-complete tents.
- HF ceiling ⇒ AIP allocation ⇔ ACS strict-cone RR.
- One-shift variation, odd capacity and blind minima are parity components of the form.
- Weil margin ⇒ heat-Hankel positivity in a coupled rank–time window.
- **Averaged positivity** at logarithmic aperture on `[Γ, 2Γ]` for the whole family, through the exact all-parity identity; its main term is the local critical-zero weight.
- **Finite-level existence.** RH ⇔ some weight system is positive at every finite aperture.
- Ground rate = polar rate, merging the rate currencies of RW, SRT and ACS.

**Common localization:** at each aperture the obstruction is a finite low-frequency sector; in the incidence picture frustration comes only from prime-sum edges; across heights it is selection.

### U3 — Faithful realization and forcing (carrier and invariant readings)

**Carriers:**

- geometric: ACS effectivity, the Meyer surface, AOG orbits and families;
- operator-theoretic: RN, GTA, PCS, IRK;
- probabilistic: W1C, FEL;
- photonic: PTR;
- categorical: CDC, SC, WBF, RFHM;
- divisor-diagnostic: HJ, SEC, BSH, APF, EWJ, EGC, PW-B;
- topological: TG;
- heat and divisor: WD cap, ALDG.

**Proved unifications:**

- **Maximality.** Every sharp-stable positive convolution ideal lies in `meyerTraceBoundedIdeal`, the off-line-blind ideal, which keeps every critical jet, carries the full restricted geometry and equals the source iff RH. Single-channel positive carriers differ only in how much of it they see.
- **Detectors.** The HJ residual, SEC seam, CDC confluence, BSH mate gain, IRK noninvertibility and W1C Clifford energy all read `Re ρ = 1/2` with exact depth laws.
- **Faithful branch.** Operator-norm faithful positivity is RH plus simple zeros.
- **Invariant rigidity.** Coefficient positivity on `[1, ∞)`, Riemann's functional equation, the simple pole and the growth premises determine ζ (APD). Every relaxation breaks this: pole order 2 admits a real stray, pole order 3 a complex quartet, strip poles a quartet; without the functional equation over-regular systems have off-line zeros; without positivity uniqueness fails.
- **Zero–pole budget.** On the boundary, zero order ≤ pole order. In the interior, for finite corrections of ζ, `ord₁F − 1` = inserted off-line multiplicity by frequency pairs. Critical zeros force `κ = d`, `T = ζ^d`.

**Common missing object:** a pre-divisor source with a **forcing law** that places it in the maximal blind ideal, with input outside single-channel positivity, beyond finite certificates and sensitive to pole order. Where it would act: ACS effectivity; the HJ/SEC weld; the TG sub-unit residual; the RN boundary orientation; the absolute pole budget.

### The forcing currency: where the families meet

Two forcing mechanisms are proved, and both sit between families.

| Mechanism | Family of origin | What it forces | Where it stops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiplicative positivity (boundary divisor positive definite) | U3 | `ζ(1+it) ≠ 0`; with growth input the dlVP, Littlewood and VK regions | Interior gain only from growth input; coupled certificates split into rational fibres; next rung a fixed strip (U1). |
| Linear rigidity (Fejér-tent squeeze on the theta law) | U3 | The coefficient measure is ζ's | Zeros are nonlinear in the measure; no zero location. |

Their invariants run in parallel. Rows pair invariants with the same role where one exists; integrality of the divisor has no separate interior hypothesis, and the functional equation has no boundary counterpart.

| Boundary line `Re s = 1` | Interior (degree one) |
|---|---|
| Nonnegative weights (N) | Coefficient positivity on `[1, ∞)` (N) |
| Integer divisor (Z) | Riemann's functional equation (FE) |
| Simple pole (S) | Simple pole (S) |
| Pole isolation (I) | Holomorphy of the completion in the strip (H) |
| N + Z + S + I ⇒ no boundary zeros; each necessary | N + FE + S (+ growth) ⇒ the system is ζ; dropping S or H admits off-line zeros |
| Zero order ≤ pole order; zero at `u` ⇒ pole at `2u` | Finite corrections: `ord₁F − 1` = inserted multiplicity; complex quartet costs 2 |

On the boundary the invariants force the conclusion (no zeros). In the interior they force only the identity of the source, and the zero conclusion (RH) is the absolute budget at pole order one.

### 4.3 Geometric currency (U2 ↔ U3)

- **Scaling surface.** Pairing = pole plane − Weil form. Hodge index, RR plus ample effectivity, and zero sector defect are each equivalent to RH.

## 6. Consequences

1. **Count each producer once.** Each of the following is a single producer, shared by the routes listed:
   - a small-damping signed budget: PM, CTF, BVI, MC;
   - a complete Weil sign: RW, SZ, CHMD, OWU, EWU, ACT, AIP, HF, TT, ACS;
   - a residual heat-region sign: LA, WD, TH, HCF;
   - a forcing law into the maximal blind ideal: TG, RN, ACS and all candidates.
2. **Reformulation, carrier maximality and invariant completeness are finished.** New equivalent endpoints add nothing; new single-channel positive carriers add nothing; new finite multiplicative certificates add nothing; new countermodels with a proper subset of the invariants add nothing.
3. **The three families reduce to three bridges and one law.**
   - **U1 — escape exclusion (Q3).** The decisive sub-question is whether the paid ideal is nonzero on escape.
   - **U2 — selection (Q1).** From averaged positivity, proved beyond Nyquist, to individual heights.
   - **U3 — coupled positivity (Q2), then forcing.** Positivity outside the single-channel class, sensitive to pole order.
   - **The common law:** arithmetic forces blindness. It would appear as rate zero in U1, as a complete sign in U2, as exhaustion of the maximal blind ideal in U3, and in the invariant reading as the absolute pole budget.
4. **Where the families meet.**
   - **U1 and U2** meet at the square-root threshold. Averaged positivity crosses it (up to `X ≤ Γ^{4/3}`), so the meeting point has moved from aperture to selection.
   - **U2 and U3** meet at one inequality: the Weil sign is the Meyer and scaling Hodge index, the exhaustion of the maximal blind ideal and the absolute budget at pole order one.
   - **U1 and U3** meet at the cofactor module (admission gives a faithful infinite module, escape one annihilated by the paid ideal) and at multiplicative forcing, whose graded continuation is the U1 width scale.
5. **Partial progress is family-specific.**
   - U1's rate axis is blocked at quasi-RH, and so is multiplicative forcing.
   - U2 has aperture windows, averages and finite-level feasibility.
   - U3 has blind sub-carriers inside one maximal ideal, the forcing law on `Re s = 1`, rigidity, and the finite budget.

   A result that converts one family's partial progress into another's would itself be a new mechanism. For example, short-window averaged positivity (U2) that controls zero ordinates would supply U1 selection data.
6. **No reviewed theorem proves that a new field is needed.** The [summary](hard-obstacles-summary.md#4-would-the-b-tier-obstacles-require-a-new-field-of-mathematics) explains why the three known mechanisms (single-channel quadratic, finite multiplicative, linear rigidity) are exhausted below RH strength and what a fourth must satisfy.
