**Verdict.** Fable has not found all the routes suggested by the corpus. More seriously, its ranking overlooks developed arithmetic mechanisms while promoting a Morse target that is impossible on the cited nerve. Its repeated demand for exact evaluation or sharp constants contradicts stronger receivers already in Lean.

**The exclusions need repairing first.**

The user's A excludes Weil positivity as a proof mechanism. Fable substitutes the stronger exclusion of *every RH-equivalent Hermitian positivity mechanism*, then retains theta Hankel positivity. Those are inconsistent choices. An independent construction of the signed theta source could survive A even though its conclusion is equivalent to Weil positivity. Equivalence of conclusions does not establish identity of proof mechanisms.

B excludes positivity and spectral mechanisms, including transfer-operator, compact-operator, index and positive-measure realizations when those do the decisive work. Merely calling a two-site positive-definiteness condition a “prime estimate” does not make it independent. Likewise, the ten pre-divisor proposals cannot all pass B as one undifferentiated row.

Ordinary inequalities, convergence of sums of squares, and contour residues remain tools for a signed arithmetic estimate; proving an RH-equivalent positive form or real spectrum is an excluded engine. The distinction must be demonstrated in the proposed proof. Where only a zero-sum or operator construction has been inspected, this report does not certify a non-spectral source mechanism.

**1. Two proposed frontiers fail before any new research.**

**Morse matching: Fable's proposed bound is false on the cited complex.** The actual complex is

\[
\mathcal N_N=\{S:\ S\text{ finite set of primes},\ \prod_{p\in S}p\le N\}.
\]

It is the nerve of prime-multiple stars, not a complex incorporating successor relations. Its signed face count, including the empty face, is `nerveEulerChar = M(N)`; its reduced Euler characteristic is **−M(N)**. The product map injects its faces into `[1,N]`, so it has at most **N faces**, not exponentially many. These facts follow directly from `squarefreeNerve`, `mem_squarefreeNerve` and the proof of `nerveEulerChar_eq_mertens` in [TheNerveOfTheStarCover.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HodgeTheoryWithoutVarieties/TheNerveOfTheStarCover.lean). `[L/D]`

Every prime `N/2 < p ≤ N` gives an isolated vertex: adding another prime makes the product exceed N. Every ordinary discrete Morse matching therefore leaves that vertex critical. Even allowing a match with the empty face removes at most one. Thus

\[
\#\text{critical cells}\ge \pi(N)-\pi(\lfloor N/2\rfloor)-1\asymp N/\log N.
\]

The asymptotic uses the corpus's `tendsto_primeCounting_nat_ratio` in [WoldBooleanAcceleratedType.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/WoldBooleanAcceleratedType.lean). This contradicts the proposed `O(N^(1/2+ε))` critical-cell count whenever `0<ε<1/2`. This obstruction is an elementary paper deduction here, **not a Lean theorem added by this review**. `[D]`

Positive survivor: signed Euler characteristic remains an exact RH receiver. Cancellation between contributions of different dimensions can remain substantial despite many unavoidable critical cells. A method retaining that cancellation, or a different complex with a separately proved arithmetic identity, survives. Bounding the total number of critical cells of this nerve does not.

**Local sign pairing: the corpus has both stronger support and a much stronger no-go.** Fable's “no involution or path-count module” misses a developed programme:

- [MobiusBoundaryOppositeSignTransport.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/MobiusBoundaryOppositeSignTransport.lean) proves sign-reversing prime replacements, a Hall-to-Laplace transport consumer, displacement costs and fractional-transport identities. [RouteEThreeToggleCommonNeighborRigidity.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/RouteEThreeToggleCommonNeighborRigidity.lean) proves sign reversal for three toggles and exact common-neighbor constraints, including the `choose 6 3` fibre at support distance six.
- The stronger [MobiusBoundaryFixedArityLocalHallMassNoGo.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/MobiusBoundaryFixedArityLocalHallMassNoGo.lean) kills **every fixed replacement arity with a retained common spectator and local displacement**, when unmatched positive mass must fit the required bound. The corpus specifies what must change; “try an involution” is far behind this frontier.

**3. The missing arithmetic frontiers.**

**Damping continuation and the long-modulus remainder — highest priority under B.** This is not just “MC in other coordinates”: the corpus has an exact derivative, a paid part, a localized unpaid part, and obstructions to specific attempted estimates.

For `ε>0`, let

\[
A_\varepsilon(n)=\sum_{d\le n}\mu(d)d^{-\varepsilon}\lfloor n/d\rfloor
 -\frac{n}{\zeta(1+\varepsilon)},\qquad
E_T(\varepsilon)=\sum_{n\le T}\frac{A_\varepsilon(n)^2}{n(n+1)}.
\]

“Admission” means `sup_T E_T(ε)<∞`. Admission at positive dampings accumulating at zero is RH. Half damping is paid. The stronger current threshold law is `inf receiverDampings = Θ−1/2`, with admission for every `ε>Θ−1/2`; the older `Θ/2` bound is not the frontier. [SignedAveragedContinuationAndTheZeroWidthBand.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/SignedAveragedContinuationAndTheZeroWidthBand.lean) gives this law and eight equivalent continuation formulations. A bound stated above the unknown Θ threshold is not an unconditional numerical improvement of that threshold. `[L]`

The exact derivative is `E'_T=2W_T`, with `W_T` the signed source–tangent pairing. A primitive budget for `−2W_T/(1+E_T)`, independent of T, transports admission downward. Dyadic horizons or appropriate Cesàro bounds suffice. [TheDampingContinuationCriterion.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/TheDampingContinuationCriterion.lean) supplies the consumer. The averaged budget is equivalent to admission, so merely asserting it is circular as a proof strategy; the research is to obtain it from arithmetic. `[L]`

More concretely,

\[
A_\varepsilon(n)=S_\varepsilon(n,\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor)
 +L_\varepsilon(n,\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor),
\]

where the short sawtooth already satisfies, for `0<ε<1`,

\[
\sum_{n\le T}\frac{S_\varepsilon(n,\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor)^2}{n(n+1)}
\le \left(1+\frac1{1-\varepsilon}\right)^2\left(1+\frac1\varepsilon\right).
\]

The long term retains **both** the signed sawtooth for `sqrt(n)<d≤n` and the full harmonic mean tail past n. See [LongModulusExactDecomposition.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/LongModulusExactDecomposition.lean) and [LongModulusShortEnergy.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/LongModulusShortEnergy.lean). `[L]`

Thus the exact remaining target is bounded pronic energy of **L**, for dampings cofinal at zero. Equivalence with admission follows by the triangle inequality and the paid S bound. Constants may depend on ε; no uniform bound as ε tends to zero is required. `[D/O]` A first unconditional fixed damping below `1/2` would already give a fixed zero-free strip, and would be a substantive breakthrough.

The corpus also prevents two easy mistakes: both finite-horizon endpoints can be paid while an unbounded interior bump escapes with T; and demanding globally nonnegative W is false. Absolute dilation estimates stop at half damping. The viable question is signed interaction of the long remainder, not positive tangent correlations or another absolute majorant. The latter positivity route is excluded under B in any case. [ACCE's exact identities and obstruction inventory](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticContinuationAndCriticalEnergy/README.md).

**Sparse one-sided bounds — a substantially easier receiver than Fable states.** Let F be the literal `mobiusDivisorSquareRootAbelMoment`, defined by Möbius inversion of the prime-free divisor defect followed by square-root Abel increments. Its exact arithmetic value is

\[
F(N)=\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}(\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k)
\bigl(\psi(k)-k+2\gamma\bigr).
\]

See the definition and `mobiusDivisorSquareRootAbelMoment_eq_coherent_add_gammaBoundary` in [CCMFUSuzukiMobiusDivisorAbelProducer.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCM/CCMFUSuzukiMobiusDivisorAbelProducer.lean). This is not the trivial convolution `μ*1`; a misleading shorthand in a module header must not replace the definition. `[L]`

For **each fixed integer m≥1**, the corpus proves separately

\[
\mathrm{RH}\iff\sup_{j^m\ge2}\frac{F(j^m)}{j^m}<\infty,
\qquad
\mathrm{RH}\iff\inf_{j^m\ge2}\frac{F(j^m)}{j^m}>-\infty.
\]

More flexibly, one bounded sample in every sufficiently large backward window of length `N^(1−δ)`, for some `δ>0`, suffices. See `riemannHypothesis_iff_exists_powerWindow_mobiusDivisorSquareRootAbel_le` in [MobiusDivisorOneSidedPowerGapBootstrap.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/MobiusDivisorOneSidedPowerGapBootstrap.lean), and its lower counterpart in [MobiusDivisorLowerStripPowerGapBootstrap.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/MobiusDivisorLowerStripPowerGapBootstrap.lean). These improve even the corpus's quartic-sampling theorem. `[L]`

The mechanism is endpoint monotonicity plus a two-step bootstrap of the zero abscissa; it is not a demand for a positive form. [ObserverJetSignedCurvatureAndPrefixTelescoping.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ObserverJetSignedCurvatureAndPrefixTelescoping.lean) retains signed history, proves its difference from `(8/3)F(N)` is `o(N)`, and supplies corresponding upper/lower polynomial-schedule criteria. Its initial-prefix estimates and exact terminal telescoping identify where further cancellation is needed. `[L]`

**Next attack:** construct one controlled sample per power window from the actual signed history or divisor source. Do not replace signed history by its positive part; do not assume its drift and terminal contribution are independently bounded when only their combination is required. A sampled estimate remains unproved, but its quantifiers are much weaker than Fable's blanket demand for sharp control at every scale. `[O]`

**Joint ancestry has a non-positivity branch that Fable omits.** [JointAncestryDampedShellArithmetic.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/JointAncestryDampedShellArithmetic.lean) identifies the damped shell powers with literal arithmetic Riesz means, not just an operator spectrum. The stronger [JointAncestrySquareShellFinitePowerCriterion.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/JointAncestrySquareShellFinitePowerCriterion.lean) proves

\[
\mathrm{RH}\iff\forall\varepsilon>0\ \text{for arbitrarily large integers }m,\quad
\forall\,m<k\le m+\frac m{\log(m+1)},\quad
|\operatorname{jasShellRieszSum}(m^2,k)|\le m^{\varepsilon k}.
\]

Only this finite power window on infinitely many square shells is required. Failure of RH forces a violation on every sufficiently large shell. The proof uses complex power-sum detection and the actual prime-shell mass; no deepest zero or density premise is assumed. `[L]` This is an arithmetic amplification target, separate from JAS's prime-product positivity branch. It needs an independent family estimate uniform in that moving k-window. Calling the arithmetic Riesz identity a finished producer would repeat Fable's mistake. Under B, keep this scalar arithmetic branch; discard any attempted proof based on a positive spectral realization.

**Weak recurrence already supplies the analytic zero–one bridge.** [ZetaWeakRecurrenceLadderCollapse.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ZetaWeakRecurrenceLadderCollapse.lean) asks only for arbitrarily high self-return shifts on discs, **without positive density**. A return on an isolating disc transports an off-line zero to arbitrarily high ordinates at almost the same real part. Consequently, under weak recurrence, finitely many zeros beyond any fixed depth implies none. In particular,

\[
\mathrm{RH}\iff\mathrm{ZerosApproachLine}
 \ \wedge\ \mathrm{ZetaZeroDiscWeaklyRecurrent}.
\]

Both inputs remain open. `ZerosApproachLine` concerns **all** sufficiently high zeros at each fixed depth, not density-one clustering. Weak returns alone can coexist with a sparse infinite exceptional sequence. This is a precise two-input alternative to both Bagchi's stronger positive-density demand and Fable's unspecified analytic zero–one law. Lindelöf by itself does not supply the missing implication. `[L/D]`

**Heat also has an analytic branch that should not be excluded by its family name.** [DeBruijnNewmanCollisionCriterion.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DeBruijnNewmanCollisionCriterion.lean) proves RH equivalent to **simplicity of every zero of `H_t` for every `t>0`**. If the Newman constant is positive, its last collision occurs in a bounded region and produces a multiple zero. The proof uses uniform high-height reality, compactness and local injectivity at a simple zero. [TheLocalDeBruijnNewmanConstant.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/Heat/TheLocalDeBruijnNewmanConstant.lean) also bounds the local constant by `(2/3+ε)/log((X−1)/(2π))` at large X. `[L]` An independent analytic exclusion of simultaneous equations `H_t(z)=H'_t(z)=0` is therefore a distinct possible B target. A proof through positive forms, real-rooted approximation or a spectral model would fail B. No independent collision-exclusion mechanism is supplied; generic simplicity is insufficient because the criterion quantifies over **every** positive time. This deserves retention as an open analytic branch, not promotion to the main arithmetic priorities.

**Integer auxiliaries and signed inversion are omitted, too.** [TheIntegralityExponentIsTheZeroWidth.lean](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticOfSquareRootPrecision/TheIntegralityExponentIsTheZeroWidth.lean) proves each of the Siegel and Liouville halves of the Nair-integral problem equivalent to RH at `sqrt(x) log²x` precision. Recomputing the integrality floor or imposing a fixed ideal cannot advance it. **6. Where to attack next.**

1. **Concentrate the main B effort on the actual long-modulus source.** Keep the full harmonic tail and signed cross terms. Seek horizon-uniform control at one explicit damping below `1/2`, then a downward continuation law. The paid short head and exact energy derivative make progress and failure measurable. Do not substitute tangent-norm control or a positive correlation conjecture for the signed work budget.
2. **Run a separate sparse-sampling attack on the literal Abel/history source.** Exploit endpoint monotonicity and the signed curvature identity to produce one controlled sample per power window. Either upper or lower bounds suffice; all-scale sharp constants are unnecessary. Compare this with the finite-power JAS shell receiver before demanding estimates uniform over every tensor order.
3. **Keep arithmetic sign transport, but enforce its no-go boundary.** Prime-marker capacity is already available. The unsolved problem is compatible transport of composite signed mass. Fixed-arity local spectator moves cannot do it; growing-arity/nonlocal transport or signed residual cancellation are the actual alternatives. An exact cost decomposition for one of those alternatives would be useful even before an RH-strength estimate.
4. **Keep a distinct analytic rigidity lane: weak recurrence plus all-zero approach to the line.** This has an explicit finite-exception elimination mechanism. Density-one statistics, Lindelöf alone, or an index zero–one statement without finiteness do not fill its two inputs. Regional Kummer roots and cyclic source landing are higher-risk construction alternatives, with their missing constructions stated explicitly.
5. **Under A only, preserve the independent theta-source lane.** Rank eight and the two-entry repair are concrete source progress. The immediate finite problem is the remaining compact-middle sign; the RH problem is a uniform all-rank mechanism. Low-frequency real-rooted approximation is another exact target. Neither should be conflated with the toy Lee–Yang examples or carried into B under a different name.

The corpus does prove that many receivers measure the same zero width. It does **not** prove that their mechanisms carry the same information or face identical research costs. ASRP even separates the precision of prime counting from tower positivity: an inverse-power perturbation can preserve the former while destroying the latter. The right question is therefore not “which label avoids the wall?” but **which exact arithmetic identity retains information that the failed estimates discarded, and which remaining term must be controlled?** Fable's reports do not answer that question reliably. The signed remainder, sparse-history and constrained-transport frontiers above do.
