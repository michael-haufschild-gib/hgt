## 1. The big picture

### 1.1 A window is a module over a finite local λ-ring
RH holds iff Weil's form `Q` is nonnegative on every test supported in `[−A, 0]`, for every `A` [L].
The arithmetic acting on such a window is the Dirichlet ring truncated at `⌊e^A⌋`: `ρ_A` is a ring map
with kernel the truncation ideal, `ζ ≡ exp(Λ/log)` on every window, Weil's arithmetic operator is the
commutator `ρ_A(Λ) = [M, log Z]` of the position with the logarithm of the window zeta operator, and
`ζ ≡ exp Σ_a ψ^a(P)/a` is the plethystic exponential of the prime folds, so the prime powers of a window
are the primes of the windows `A/2, A/3, …` [L]. The Hilbert function of the tower is Landau's
`π_k(N)`; the socle is spanned by `(N/2, N]`, so the window algebra is Gorenstein only below `log 3`
[L]. On finite configurations `S ⊂ ℚ_{>0}` the arithmetic form is the Hecke operator of `ℤ[ℚ^×_{>0}]`:
fold correlations count multiplicative pairs, and the positivity horizons of a window are thresholds of
finite weighted rational Hecke graphs [L].

### 1.2 Dimension zero is classified: positive dimension supplies no positivity
Weil's form is the 1-jet at `k = 0` of the Tate family `τ_H(k) = ⟨ρ_A(J_k)H,H⟩`, the point counts of
`𝔸^k∖0` [L]. The question whether an archimedean family in positive dimension can dominate the counts
and hand positivity down to `k = 0` is answered [L]:
- bare domination with the prescribed 1-jet is equivalent to `Q(H) ≥ 0`, with an explicit prime-blind
  canonical family (`kD(H) + 2R_A(k)‖H‖²`, `R_A(k) = Σ_{n≤e^A}(n^k − 1 − k log n)n^{−1/2}`);
- no combination `αΓ + β·pole` (nor any Gamma-tilted family plus a pole multiple) dominates the counts
  across all windows at any fixed `k > 0`: on harmonic plateaus the counts grow like `e^{kA}` while the
  Gamma form stays bounded and the pole grows like `A²`;
- on the translated Riemann kernel any linear domination horizon is `≤ C·exp(−exp(A−2))`, driven by
  Selberg's second jet `Λ₂(2) = (log 2)²`;
- a shifted pole weight is pinched to the Euler-product value `1/ζ(1+k) < k` and refuted for every real
  weight (`4ξ(1+k)² < kζ(1+k)`);
- Tate positivity is itself non-uniform: for every `k > 0` some window has `τ_H(k) < 0` (prime star,
  `Σ1/p = ∞`); horizons are `log₂(1+2√2)` at the first prime stage, `1.19966…` at the second, and
  shrink like `e^{−A/2}`;
- the completed centred Tate family `Λ(½+it−k/2)/Λ(½+it+k/2)` is unimodular on the line: for it,
  domination is the functional equation;
- higher Taylor coefficients (Bombieri's `Λ_j`) are generated by `M` and `ρ_A(Λ)` and carry no
  arithmetic beyond `Λ`; the wall `k ↦ −k` is Dirichlet inversion, `μ` on squarefree integers;
- on short windows positivity survives into `|k| ≤ 1/200`; the imaginary Tate twist is unitary and
  preserves positivity: positivity lives on the line `Re k = 0`.

Survivors: domination in positive dimension forces an arithmetic (Euler-product) pole weight; null tests
carry the Selberg constraint `∂²A ≥ 2⟨ρ_A(Λ₂)H,H⟩`.

### 1.3 The stage step lives on two collars, and the new fold is a positive source
Crossing a stage adds one fold: `Q = Q_N − 2w_{N+1}c_H(log(N+1))`, and for `A = log(N+1) + ε` the
correlation involves only the left collar `[−A, −A+ε]` and the right collar `[−ε, 0]` [L]. For every
weight `φ > 0`, `Q = Q_N − wE_φ(H) + w∫φ(u)φ(u+ℓ)(H(u)/φ(u) − H(u+ℓ)/φ(u+ℓ))²` (ground-state
identity) [L]. If the frozen form is positive definite on the interior (an earlier stage), positivity on
`W_A` is the block inequality `S^N_ε − w_{N+1}(J + J*) ⪰ 0` on `L²(collar) ⊕ L²(collar)`, `S^N_ε` the
Schur complement of the frozen form onto the collars and `J` translation by `log(N+1)` [D].

The frozen form is indefinite: a real C² test on `[−21/20, 0]` has `Q₁ < 0 < Q` (prime 2 repairs a
negative frozen direction) [L], and on the unit window the frozen odd form is negative only along one
cubic moment, `Q₁ + 20M² ≥ ‖F‖²/125` [L/W]. Node criticality says the same at the level of the whole
datum: deleting a single prime-power node destroys positivity [L]. Hence the new fold is a positive
source, and every payment that treats it as a cost — the exit charge, the collar loan, the weighted
transfer criterion `wE_φ ≤ Q_N`, Barta and Picone certificates that bound `|c_n(H)|` — cannot certify
the frozen-negative tests [L/D]. Zero modes of a nonnegative window are its radical and, at a first
critical length, nonvanishing on both collars [L]: an edge is born exactly where the new fold acts.

### 1.4 Exact balance, and the gap between soft and exact horizons
- The Weil cone is a point: every real weight with full Weil positivity equals `Λ`; every certificate has
  pole weight `1` and node weights `Λ` [L].
- Window margins collapse doubly exponentially: `μ(a) ≤ K exp((11/2)a − πe^{2(a−2)})`; at half-width
  `½log 3` the Galerkin ground energy is `≈ 5.6×10⁻⁸` (even), `1.5×10⁻⁵` (odd) [L/N].
- Every soft deformation the counts pay unconditionally has horizon `≍ 1/deg K_A ≍ e^{−A/2}`: Tate
  dimension, the critical multiple (`K*(H) ≥ βJ/(κ(10 + 72e^{A/2}))`), the floor [L/D]. The gap between
  `e^{−A/2}` (soft) and `exp(−e^A)` (exact) is the no-slack wall in quantitative form.
- No finite-square identity at the first prime; no finite-dimensional window realization; no finite-rank
  realization of `K_ζ` [L].

Every estimate that loses a factor fails, every finite identity fails, every finite carrier fails, and
positive dimension adds nothing. A proof is an exact identity of unbounded rank per window, compatible
along the tower — or a soft structural argument that loses nothing.

### 1.5 The complete family: subcritical solved; at the critical curve only Weil's form is unsigned
The complete tilted-spline matrix is positive definite for every `α < 1/8`, uniformly
`1 ≤ c ≤ (log N)^α` [L]. Along the critical curve `c⁸ ≍ K log N` every twisted direction is a positive
count [L]: Ramanujan windows (`Σa_m²J_{β+1}(m)`, diagonal in the Ramanujan basis), prime windows
(`J(1)φ(p)#ℙ⁷(𝔽_p)`), every mean-zero periodic twist (a spectral Dirichlet series, coercive), every real
frequency (`½(ζ(β+1,u) + ζ(β+1,1−u))`), primitive characters (count `± q^{s−1}‖L(s,ψ̄²)‖`, graded by
parity), trigonometric twists graded by frequency [W]. Twisted directions add no negative square;
polarization is inherited on twisted sectors, short windows, the cosh hyperplane and below `K₀(A)`.
The bulk reads only the archimedean genus `2 + γ − log 4π` at the pole from all zeros below `N^θ`.
Untwisted windows carry `κQ(H) + (βJ/K)‖H‖²_{−(β+1)}`: Weil's form is the only unsigned piece.

Over `𝔽_q` the same family of a pure curve is a sum of squares at every resolution, weighted by Hurwitz
forms of the Frobenius angles; a twist frequency `u` over `ℤ` contributes exactly the Hurwitz form an
angle `u` contributes over `𝔽_q`; the golden fake has exponential monodromy and a fixed hyperbolic plane
[L]. The untwisted window is the trivial angle `u = 0`, where the Hurwitz form is singular: the whole
sign problem sits at the singular angle.

### 1.6 The reflection exists on both sides; the window algebra has no Frobenius
- Window side: `σ_A` is the Rosati involution of the window algebra; its multiplier is unitary exactly on
  `Re s = ½` [L]. `Q`-similitudes of the window algebra are scalars, its reflection centralizer is
  scalar [L]: Weil's `𝔽_q` argument (Rosati positivity plus a Frobenius similitude) cannot be run inside
  a window. The Frobenius analogue changes the window: `T_p − Id` realizes `√p H(· + log p)` in the
  window `A + log p` and scales complete Weil energy by `p` [L] — a similitude that carries no sign.
- Mayer side: `I_s† = I_{1/2−s̄}`, `I_s` bounded iff `Re s = ¼`, `D_s ≤ 0` with equality iff `Re s = ¼`,
  Krein positivity ⇔ RH, finite dyadic cusp energy ⇔ resonance; source Hecke eigenvalues
  `1 + p·p^{−2s}` from Barnes cones [L].
- Bridge: `U_LP` intertwines `I_s` with the window multiplier; complete Weil energy is the limit of twice
  the real Krein squares of finite core sums, with conjugation and an exponential twist; no real-linear
  isometry identifies Serre duality with the Krein reflection; finite core projections keep every
  negative PM direction, compensated by the omitted tail [L].

### 1.7 Selection and the prime-blind cone
Selection after positivity is a theorem; every selector without positivity is blind [L]. Prime-blind
positivity (the archimedean and pole part on combinations of autocorrelations vanishing at every
`±log n`) is RH; it is node-critical (every prime power needed, composites free), dual at each finite
level to a Farkas problem in finitely many node weights, equal to zero-side positivity on blind tests,
solved on nonnegative tests and on separated narrow-bump cones, and open on signed blind combinations
with dense cancellation [L].

### 1.8 Precision is one mode and one density statement
Every eventually positive cyclotomic band of the Chebyshev error, the fixed first band included, has
energy abscissa exactly `2Θ` [L]: precision is one Fourier coefficient. Log-mean, mollified log-mean,
horizontal moment, critical-scale clustering and Hecke readings are one statement; almost-all window
certificates ⇔ log-free occupancy; the inner caps `[½, σ_m]` are the zero-free-region problem [L].
Off-line clusters are detectable: every zero of depth `≥ 1/a` and height `≤ exp(a/(10⁵A₀ log a))` has a
smooth negative Weil test of support `[−(a+1), a+1]` [L].

### 1.9 What the three walls are
| Wall | Window form |
|---|---|
| Σ sign | the exact signed stage step: on two-collar data the frozen Schur complement must dominate the new fold's translation, with the fold acting as a positive source, without loss, uniformly in the stage |
| Π precision / production | one Fourier mode of the prime error at square-root size; parity classified at dimension zero; production of the window `h¹` by an exact identity of unbounded rank |
| Γ selection / reflection | the reflection is present on both sides; the window algebra has no Frobenius; positivity of the reflection is RH; selection after positivity is automatic |

They are three readings of one statement: **every window carries a positive ground state of its
arithmetic jump process, and each prime kick preserves it.**

### F3. The reflection assembly (PM ⊕ WTZ ⊕ DZG)
PM supplies the reflection as a Hilbert adjoint, a Krein space whose negative index is the polar defect,
source Hecke operators with Barnes-cone eigenvalues, Mellin translations `T_p − Id` into longer windows,
and complete Weil energy as a limit of Krein squares of finite core sums [L]. The window side supplies

of Serre duality with the Krein reflection is impossible, finite core projections keep every negative
direction, and boundary-subtracted cores are not Hecke eigenvectors [L]. Residual: positivity of the
reflection (B30, B34); the exact completion (B36) is an identity that would put Weil's `𝔽_q`
architecture in place except positivity.

### F4. The prime-blind tower (PBAP ⊕ CWS ⊕ WTZ)
At each level the Weil cone `C_A` is a finite convex set with a Farkas dual; RH ⇔ `C_A ≠ ∅` for every
`A`; nonnegative and separated-bump blind combinations are positive; node criticality forces every prime
power [L]. Residual: signed blind combinations (B40) — the near-null directions of long windows, the same
directions as F1's core.

### F5. Density–index–cluster (WD ⊕ AHD ⊕ CWS ⊕ AKLT ⊕ SRT)
Off-line zeros live in a density-zero set of windows [L], are detected by smooth tests at aperture
`≍ log|γ|·log log|γ| + 1/δ` [L], each raising the window index; an attained positive depth is seen by
almost every prime tower [L]. Residual: zero–one law plus index ceiling (B10, B11), ≥qRH as a whole.

## 2.4 A wildly different route

**Verdict: none that escapes the three walls.**

- The prime-blind, existence-first route is developed in the corpus and is not far: PBAP is node-critical,
  dual at each level to a finite Farkas problem, and on blind tests it is zero-side positivity [L]. It
  meets Σ as Poisson rigidity (Γ8) on the blind subspace; its open part is the same near-null directions
  as the window sign.
- Dimension zero (counts in positive dimension, archimedean Tate families) is classified as equivalent to
  the window sign [L].
- Transfer from function fields fails at the archimedean place: the obstruction at the first prime is
  archimedean (Π20), the archimedean place alone is not Weil positive [L], and the window algebra has no
  Frobenius [L]; the angle dictionary shows that the sign sits exactly at the trivial angle, which has no
  `𝔽_q` counterpart carrying a sign.
- Every route class that starts elsewhere (scheduled sums, Laguerre–Pólya, realization, pre-divisor
  sections, typicality, heat, density) has its decisive wall identified as a face of Σ, Π or Γ
  (`route-unification-ledger.md`).

A route would be far only if its positivity came from a source that is neither a quadratic-form
estimate, nor a count, nor a spectral realization. The most distant formulation not developed in the
corpus is dynamical [D]: Weil's form on a window is a translation-invariant form with kernel
`Ω(0)δ + Lévy kernel − Σ Λ(n)n^{−1/2}(δ_{log n} + δ_{−log n}) + pole`, so by Krein–Gelfand–Levitan theory
positivity on every window is the global existence of a causal evolution in the window length (a
canonical system / continuous Schur algorithm) kicked by `Λ(n)/√n` at each `A = log n`. RH would be the
statement that this evolution never leaves the positive cone — a no-blow-up statement, attackable by
invariant regions and Lyapunov functionals, which are exact rather than lossy. It does not escape Σ (the
evolution passes doubly-exponentially close to the boundary of the cone), it inherits the selection
problem (only the kicks `Λ` keep it alive), positivity conditions of de Branges type are known to fail as
sufficient criteria for ζ, and its nearest corpus carrier is the Suzuki screw / Krein–Schur machinery
of SZ. It changes the toolset, not the wall.

## 2.5 Breakthrough questions below RH and quasi-RH

### 2.5.1 Whole programme — the two-collar recursion
**Question.** For every prime-power stage `N+1` and collar width `ε`, compute the Schur complement
`S^N_ε` of the frozen window form onto the two collars that the new fold couples, derive its evolution
in `ε` and across stages (a Riccati-type law driven by the archimedean kernel and the folds already
present, with an impulse at each prime power), and give its collar coupling in closed form at the first
two prime stages.
- **Below RH.** An identity: no sign of Weil's form is used; each stage is a finite problem. The first
  stage involves only the prime-free archimedean datum (A8).
- **Advance.** The stage step becomes an explicit 2×2 operator inequality on `L²[0, ε]` per prime power:
  `S_LL ⪰ 0`, `S_RR − (S_RL − wJ*)S_LL^{−1}(S_LR − wJ) ⪰ 0`. It shows exactly where and by how much the
  frozen datum pre-installs the next fold, turns the tower into one evolution with arithmetic kicks, and
  joins WTZ's transfer, DZG's ground-state identity, the zero-mode structure on collars and SZ's
  Krein–Schur blocks in one object.

### 2.5.2 Route PM — the Hecke-equivariant completion
**Question.** Construct one bounded real-linear map from window tests into the completed PM resonant
space — boundary-corrected cores, conjugation and the exponential twist built in — under which complete
Weil window energy equals the Krein form exactly (not as a limit of finite core sums), and the source
Hecke operators correspond to the Mellin translations `T_p − Id` into longer windows.
- **Below RH.** An identity; consistent with the refuted linear Serre–Krein isometry, which it replaces by
  the twisted correspondence already proved at finite level [L].
- **Advance.** PM's Krein positivity becomes literally Weil window positivity with a Hecke action by
  window translations: the full architecture of Weil's `𝔽_q` proof except positivity, with finite
  projections and tail compensation made exact.

### 2.5.3 Route WD — log-free window occupancy
**Question.** Prove log-free unit-window occupancy `D_win`: for every `A > 0`, the number of unit windows
`[t, t+1] ⊂ [V, 2V]` containing a zero of depth `≥ A/log V` is `≤ ε(A)V` with `ε(A) → 0`.
Equivalently, almost all unit windows satisfy the log-stage Bessel–Szegő certificate [L].
- **Below RH.** A density statement, strictly weaker than log-free multiplicity density and independent
  of the log-mean law on generic density data [L].
- **Advance.** WD's certificate machinery is complete; this is its one analytic input at the log stage.
  The unconditional double-log stage (all but `O(V/log log V)` windows) shows the mechanism works one
  logarithm short of it; with a bounded per-window multiplicity second moment it recovers `D_zero` [L].

### 2.5.4 Route HF — growing conductors at the critical curve
**Question.** Along `c⁸ = K log N`, prove that the complete tilted-spline form on all vectors is
asymptotically the orthogonal sum of Weil's form on untwisted horizon windows and the positive counts of
every twisted direction, uniformly for conductors `m ≤ M(N)` with `M(N) → ∞`, with vanishing mixed and
bulk–horizon terms; then push `M(N)` to `N`.
- **Below RH.** An identity of limits; every fixed finite family is a theorem [L]; large conductors meet
  the `√N` barrier.
- **Advance.** With the eighth-root theorem in place, HF/SRT/AIP/ACS/SC become literally Weil's window
  criterion plus positive counts on the whole space, and fixed-multiple positivity (2.8) reduces to a
  statement about Weil's form alone.

## 2.6 Breakthrough questions that open the path to RH

### 2.6.1 Whole programme — a positive ground state that survives every prime kick
**Question.** For every window length `A`, construct a positive function `φ_A` with
`(L_A φ_A)/φ_A ≥ 0`, where `L_A` is the window jump operator of Weil's form (archimedean jumps with kernel
`e^{x/2}/sinh x`, prime jumps of length `log n` with conductance `Λ(n)/√n`, potential `V_A`, the pole
pair as two constraints), such that crossing each prime-power stage changes `φ` only by a correction on
the two collars.
- **Why it opens the path.** It is RH [D: fold-capacity criterion, Allegretto–Piepenbrink]. The
  ground-state representation then writes Weil's form exactly as a sum of jump-difference squares of
  `H/φ_A` plus a nonnegative potential — an identity of unbounded rank, the only kind the no-slack facts
  allow — with the signed new fold inside the squares rather than paid as a cost. Each new fold acts only
  on the collars, where zero modes would have to live, so a stage-uniform construction is a statement
  about two-collar data (2.5.1). Barta and Picone certificates exist [L] but are sign-blind; the
  construction must use the signs of the folds, as the first prime stage already requires.

### 2.6.2 Route PM — finite cusp energy forces zero defect
**Question.** Prove that every Lewis period of finite dyadic cusp energy has zero co-isometry defect:
`∫_1^∞ |ψ(2a) − 2^{1−2s}ψ(a)|² da/a < ∞ ⇒ D_s(ψ) = 0`.
- **Why it opens the path.** Finite cusp energy ⇔ resonance; `D_s = 0` ⇔ `Re s = ¼` at a resonance [L];
  so the implication is RH, stated between two explicit quadratic functionals of one function on
  `(0, ∞)`. Three-term plus inversion invariance is refuted off the line [L]; the missing input is
  arithmetic, and the Hecke operators `T_{p,s}`, which commute with `I_s` and intertwine `1 − M_s` [L],
  are its natural carriers.

### 2.6.3 Route WD — inner-cap cancellation
**Question.** On every Burnol unit window, at every polynomial stage with prefix `Θ_k(√m)`, cancel the
right-port flux through the inner caps `[½, σ_m]` by a source identity, so that `e^{−xt}|P₊(t)|²` is
integrable at `x = 1/(2n_{m,k})`.
- **Why it opens the path.** Across all degrees this is RH; at fixed degree it is the inverse-polynomial
  corridor `|β − ½| ≤ C/|γ|^k` [L]. The exterior caps cancel to `σ_m = 1 − 1/(116log(m+5))`; the
  functional-equation image never cancels the right edge. Everything else in WD is an equivalence; this
  is its single producer.

### 2.6.4 Route HF — the untwisted window as the trivial Frobenius angle
**Question.** Decompose the untwisted horizon window over additive frequencies `u ∈ [0, 1)`: the part
`u > 0` with the positive Hurwitz weights `½(ζ(β+1,u) + ζ(β+1,1−u))` already computed [L], and the
boundary `u → 0` through the resonance `u ≍ e^A/N`, renormalized — and decide whether Weil's form
`κQ(H)` appears there with a nonnegative weight, i.e. whether the complete family is a sum of squares at
every resolution, as it is for a pure curve over `𝔽_q`.
- **Why it opens the path.** Over `𝔽_q` positivity at every resolution is the statement that every
  Frobenius angle enters with a positive Hurwitz weight and the trivial angle carries only the main term
  [L]; over `ℤ` every nontrivial frequency already enters with a positive Hurwitz weight [L]. The
  decomposition is the precise `ℤ`-analogue of the mechanism that proves RH for curves, with every
  ingredient except the boundary term a theorem; a nonnegative boundary weight is RH.

## 2.7 Does closing RH need a new field?

**Assessment: yes — an exact theory of growing windows: the arithmetic criticality and canonical-system
theory of `Spec ℤ` along its window tower.** Its dimension-zero chapter is written and negative:
positive Tate dimension supplies no positivity. Its remaining content is the exact passage of a positive
structure through each prime kick.

*What it is about.* For every length `A`, a module over a finite local λ-ring carrying a
translation-invariant arithmetic jump process (archimedean Lévy jumps and prime jumps at rates
`Λ(n)/√n`) and a reflection, organized as a causal evolution in `A` with one kick per prime power; its
invariants are ground states, two-collar forms, zero modes and indices; its positivity is the global
existence of a positive ground state.

*What it would be like.* Exact, not estimative: identities, integers, invariant regions and rigidity
laws, because Weil positivity has no slack. Infinite-dimensional at every level, local in `n ≤ e^A`,
causal in `A`, functorial along small extensions, with the arithmetic entering only as the kicks.

*Criteria it must meet.*
1. Locality and causality: `W_A` depends on `n ≤ e^A` and the Gamma factor; the state at `A` determines
   the state just after `A`; entrances paid (proved); composite stages free (proved).
2. Exactness: positivity by identities of unbounded rank (ground-state representations, canonical
   systems), never by finite squares (refuted), cost-only payments (refuted) or lossy estimates.
3. Signed kicks: each prime kick is a positive source on two-collar data, as at the first prime (proved
   there); the theory must explain the sign the frozen datum pre-installs.
4. Reflection: Rosati involution of the window algebra, unitary multiplier on `Re s = ½`; no Frobenius
   inside a window (proved); a Frobenius only as a window-changing translation; positivity of Rosati
   type.
5. Selection by positivity: fails for flip twins, `ζ(s)ζ(s−ε)`, Epstein and `m² + 64n²` fakes, the golden
   curve; reproduces curves over `𝔽_q` (angle-weighted sums of squares); node-critical in the prime powers.
6. Prime-blindness of the output: `Λ` comes out (Weil cone a point, unique repair), it does not go in.
7. Dimension: a line of positive forms in imaginary Tate dimension; no positivity in real dimension.

*Questions it must answer.*
- What is the two-collar form of a stage, and what law carries it across a prime kick (2.5.1)?
- Does the window jump process have a positive ground state at every length, and how does it pass a
  stage (2.6.1)?
- Is there a unique-continuation principle for the window Euler–Lagrange equation that forbids a zero mode
  supported with full mass on both collars?
- Which positive involution on two-collar data makes the reflection `σ_A` a Rosati positivity?
- What is the `ℤ`-analogue of the trivial Frobenius angle, and what weight does Weil's form carry there
  (2.6.4)?
- How do the soft horizons `≍ e^{−A/2}` become the exact doubly-exponential margins?

*Tools it aims to develop.* Criticality theory for nonlocal operators on intervals (Allegretto–Piepenbrink,
Agmon ground states, Doob transforms, Keller–Pinchover–Pogorzelski); Krein–Gelfand–Levitan theory,
canonical systems and continuous Schur algorithms for kernels with atoms; invariant regions and Lyapunov
functionals for impulsive Riccati evolutions; Perron theory of growing rational Hecke graphs; Krein and
Pontryagin spaces; Lewis–Mayer transfer operators and Barnes-cone Hecke relations; Hurwitz/angle
Plancherel theory; the logarithmic Laplacian (Chen–Weth) with arithmetic shifts.

*Objects, language, mechanisms.*
- Objects: windows, stages, folds, collars, the two-collar form `S^N_ε`, the window λ-ring and its socle,
  rational Hecke graphs, ground states `φ_A`, zero modes, the end profile, the canonical class, the window
  involution, the Lewis period space and its Krein form, the prime-blind cone, Frobenius angles and twist
  frequencies.
- Mechanism A (criticality): positive ground state at every length ⇒ exact sum of squares ⇒ RH.
- Mechanism B (causal evolution): two-collar Riccati evolution with prime kicks stays in the positive cone
  ⇒ RH.
- Mechanism C (reflection): Rosati positivity on two-collar data + window-changing Frobenius ⇒ RH.
- Mechanism D (prime-blind): archimedean positivity on signed blind combinations ⇒ Weil cone nonempty ⇒ `Λ`
  by uniqueness ⇒ RH.
- Mechanism E (angles): Plancherel decomposition of the horizon layer with nonnegative weight at the
  trivial angle ⇒ RH.

No corpus result shows that this field closes RH. Its first tests are A8 (first two-collar form), A6
(odd unit window), A5 (first prime stage), 2.5.1 at the second stage, and A10 (twisted curve families).

## 2.8 The one question

**Is the complete Weil family positive on the whole space at one fixed critical multiple: is there
`K₁ > 0` such that `completeWeilMatrix tiltedUnitSpline (2K₁(log N)^{1/8}) N` is eventually positive
(definite, or on the pole hyperplane as in the horizon criterion)?**

By the critical-curve decomposition this is two statements: growing-conductor uniformity (2.5.4) and the
weighted window sign `κQ(H) + (βJ/K₁)∫e^{−(β+1)y}H² ≥ 0` on windows of every length (B5).

- **Why this is the frontier question.** Below the critical curve the family is positive [L]; at the
  critical curve every direction except untwisted windows is a positive count [L]; RH is positivity at
  every multiple [L]; the dimension-zero alternative is closed [L]. Fixed-multiple positivity is the first
  statement of the RH-complete schedule that is not yet RH: the horizon layer at `K₁` contains windows of
  every length, but the aliasing weight `e^{−(β+1)y}` grows exponentially toward the left end of a window
  (`y = log(n/N) → −A`; rate `8` for the tilted spline) while window negativity grows at rate at most `½`
  and must carry mass on both collars (zero modes) [L]. So a fixed multiple sees only windows up to a
  finite length `L(K₁)` [D], consistent with the radius `½log K` of the tests a multiple synthesizes [L].
  On two-bump tests the weighted ratio `Q/∫e^{8|y|}H²` of a negative direction tends to `0` with the
  window length even under ¬RH [D].
- **Why it is less hard than RH.** It is sub-RH: implied by RH, and decided by a localization law for
  where window negativity can live. For small `K₁`, windows of length `A` are paid by the count itself
  whenever `κ(10 + 72e^{A/2})K₁ < βJ` [L], i.e. up to `A(K₁) ≈ 2log(βJ/(72κK₁))`; what remains is the
  weighted sign on windows longer than `A(K₁)`, where the left-end weight is at least `e^{8A(K₁)}`
  [D]. No zero information beyond the floors is needed.
- **Why the answer opens the door.** A proof gives the first unconditional full-space positivity theorem
  on the RH-complete schedule and the first law in the window length (the `K ↔ A` dictionary at full-space
  level). It turns RH into a monotone continuation in `K`: the pencil index is monotone in `K`, zero at
  `K₁`, and can change only at a jump multiple, where a weighted zero mode must appear — a radical vector
  of the weighted window form, translation stable, carrying mass on both collars, solving one explicit
  nonlocal Euler–Lagrange equation [L]. RH becomes the nonexistence of such a vector along one parameter,
  a rigidity question for one equation instead of a sign over all tests; with the two-collar form
  (2.5.1) it is a question about collar data only. If instead fixed-multiple positivity turns out to be
  RH-equivalent, the whole problem is one explicit matrix family at one multiple, as for the Beurling
  schedule [L] — equally decisive for where the effort goes.
