# BiScaleUncertainty: mathematical development checklist

Date: 18 October 2026. Companion: [field-status verdict](status-1810.md).

**Objective: develop a reusable theory whose strongest conclusions depend essentially on the interaction between arithmetic incidence, correlation geometry, and energy.** The checklist below proposes a concrete mathematical route toward that objective. Field status is not a theorem, and completing a list cannot guarantee it. The targets are research problems, not assertions that the desired strongest versions are true.

Every item requires an exact statement, a proof or counterexample, and explicit hypotheses. A failed strong conjecture should produce the sharp surviving classification or bound. Formalize proved results on the actual objects; do not replace the missing mathematical input with a hypothesis that assumes the desired conclusion. No publication, paper-writing, outreach, or external-review tasks are included.

The order below puts foundations and finite arithmetic questions first. Items 8–12 investigate the analytic connections. If those connections fail, retain a narrower coherent theory with proved boundaries instead of forcing unrelated results into one framework.

- [ ] **1. Specify a common mathematical object with nontrivial models.** Define the state space, actual translation and dilation actions, arithmetic incidence data, allowed observations, and energy. State operator domains and normalizations; finite dilations need not be invertible or unitary. Construct finite divisor-sieve and continuum source models explicitly. **Completion test:** both models satisfy the structural assumptions without assuming recovery or coercivity; identify what information and operations a map between models must preserve. Merely bundling unrelated structures in a record does not complete this item.

- [ ] **2. Determine the exact classical content of the proposed foundations.** Derive the residual inequality from orthogonal projection and Cauchy–Schwarz, the allocation theorem from its norm/equality geometry, and the hyperbolic identity from the addition formula. Establish precisely which joint-energy conclusions already follow from additive energy alone. Resolve the incorrect squared hyperbolic identity identified in the verdict by the correct formula and a counterexample. **Completion test:** the remaining candidate principles have an explicit mathematical conclusion beyond these reductions; no renamed identity is counted as that advance.

- [ ] **3. Classify the full arithmetic observation ambiguity.** Start from actual Selberg coordinates and literal translated/affine moments. Extend the existing within-valuation result to supports spanning several powers of two and several odd factors. Use the Fourier moment formula to identify the realized zero-sum frequency relations; derive the equations these impose on coordinate signs. Treat zero coordinates, invisible coordinates, and genuine translation symmetries explicitly. **Completion test:** an exact `same observations ↔ specified ambiguity group` theorem on a stated general family, with actual sieve witnesses for every surviving ambiguity. Do not presume the residual ambiguity is always one common sign.

- [ ] **4. Compute sharp observation order from arithmetic support.** For each support class in item 3, determine the least translated-moment degree needed to eliminate every removable ambiguity, both with and without affine data. Begin with mixed valuation blocks and repeated prime-power orders. **Completion test:** an explicit arithmetic formula or sharp bounds, a separating observation at the claimed order, and indistinguishable actual signals at every smaller order. Recover the known prime-family degree-six boundary as a special case. Defining the answer as “the least separating degree” without computing it is insufficient.

- [ ] **5. Prove sharp stability of arithmetic reconstruction.** Normalize coefficients and observations before estimating errors. For the prime family, start with the existing normalized sixth readouts `P⁻⁶ R_pq = (a_p a_q)³`, where `P=N!`. Determine a bound of the form

  \[
  \inf_{\gamma\in G}\|a-\gamma b\|
  \leq C\,\|\mathcal O(a)-\mathcal O(b)\|^{\alpha},
  \]

  with the true ambiguity group `G`, specified norms, and explicit parameter dependence. **Completion test:** optimal or sharply bounded exponent near vanishing coordinates, matching degeneration examples, and stronger estimates on quantitatively nondegenerate strata. Then test the classification from item 3. Exact injectivity alone does not complete this item.

- [ ] **6. Determine the measurement complexity of recovery.** Construct finite collections of actual lag/affine observations that recover the chosen family, with an explicit reconstruction procedure. Bound the number of measurements in terms of active modes and arithmetic structure, and prove lower bounds. **Completion test:** a matched or quantitatively controlled gap between necessity and sufficiency, including unknown zero support. Count raw observations: a Fourier readout assembled from an entire `P`-period lag array is not one raw measurement. Combine the construction with item 5 to control noise amplification.

- [ ] **7. Prove a coercivity theorem that needs both scales.** For a specified infinite family of arithmetic models, determine the joint nullspace `K` and the best achievable dependence in

  \[
  \operatorname{dist}(f,K)^2
  \leq C\bigl(E_{\mathrm{add}}(f)+E_{\mathrm{mult}}(f)\bigr).
  \]

  Compute or sharply bound `C` from arithmetic incidence, rather than naming it as the inverse smallest eigenvalue. **Completion test:** a nonvacuous arithmetic criterion, extremizers or near-extremizers, and a family showing that neither individual energy gives the same conclusion or parameter dependence. If a uniform bound fails, prove its sharp deterioration law and identify the obstruction. The existing zero-energy constancy theorem is not this result.

- [ ] **8. Characterize constrained single-source realization and its cost.** Choose a nontrivial class of prescribed signed correlation data, including nonzero off-grid values. Determine when it is realized by one real source with specified regularity, normalization, logarithmic-grid constraints, and, where imposed, polar balance. Distinguish exact realization from closure and approximation. **Completion test:** necessary conditions, an actual source construction under sufficient conditions, and quantitative bounds on energy and support size; exhibit an obstruction where necessity and sufficiency differ. Positive-semidefinite Gram data or a spectral mixture alone do not establish the required source realization. Extend the existing finite constructions instead of reproving them.

- [ ] **9. Make continuum recovery quantitative on controlled classes.** Extend all-orders closed-cycle completeness to normalized correlations with specified support radius `L`, a positive origin collar `h(x)≥κ>0` for `|x|≤ρ`, and an explicit continuity modulus. Derive a sufficient cycle order, a finite sampling rule, and a noise-to-reconstruction bound. **Completion test:** explicit dependence on `L/ρ`, `κ`, and sampling error, with examples showing unavoidable deterioration. Test whether the energy/incidence bounds from items 7–8 supply the collar and continuity estimates. Respect the existing distant-copy obstruction: there is no fixed order valid uniformly over unrestricted separation.

- [ ] **10. Prove source realization and conservation in the limiting process.** Starting with actual constrained sources, determine additional quantitative conditions that produce a limit in a specified source class, retain all logarithmic-grid conditions and polar balance, and account exactly for singular-energy loss. Derive those conditions from concrete bounds or constructions, rather than assuming the desired convergence. **Completion test:** a source-level convergence theorem with explicit regularity, an exact defect statement, and witnesses showing which assumptions cannot be dropped. The existing [strict energy-loss example](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/InfiniteGridStrictEnergyLoss.lean) rules out treating common compact support or strong `L²` convergence as sufficient for energy convergence. Use [signed-trace compactness](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BiScaleUncertainty/SignedGridTraceCompactness.lean) as an ingredient, not as a substitute for source realization or polar control.

- [ ] **11. Establish a substantive discrete-to-continuum transfer.** Construct an explicit map from a defined arithmetic signal family to actual continuum sources. Prove the exact identities, or controlled approximation errors, for selected observations and both scale actions. Track support, normalization, and energy through the map. **Completion test:** transport a sharp recovery obstruction or quantitative bound into a new theorem in the other model, with useful parameter dependence. A formal analogy between two Gram matrices is insufficient. If a proposed exact transfer is impossible, prove the obstruction and the strongest useful approximate or restricted transfer that survives.

- [ ] **12. Determine which conclusions depend on arithmetic incidence rather than spacing alone.** Compare logarithmic-grid models with explicit perturbed grids or grids having comparable spacing but different multiplicative incidences. Separate exact zero constraints from approximate ones, and calculate the resulting change in recovery or coercivity. **Completion test:** a stability theorem under stated perturbations and a counterexample identifying a necessary incidence condition, or a proved universality result showing that the apparent arithmetic distinction disappears for the chosen observable. A translated or rescaled copy of the same model is not an independent example.

- [ ] **13. Prove an organizing theorem with several substantial consequences.** Use the previous results to formulate and prove one principle connecting available arithmetic/metric relations, observation ambiguity, and quantitative recovery or energy. One candidate mechanism is that the shortest sign-sensitive relations control recovery order, while source geometry and energy control which relations can be used stably. This is a research proposal, not an assumption. **Completion test:** derive existing sharp special cases and new results in meaningfully different model families from the same mechanism. A theorem formed by conjoining independently proved statements does not qualify. If no such connection survives, identify the narrower theory that the results actually support.

Each completed box should provide a reusable theorem with sharp scope, actual examples, and a proof whose premises have been discharged in those examples. Lean verification should check that exact mathematical result, including boundary and zero cases. Counts of files, definitions, or supporting lemmas are not completion criteria.

The key evidence would be a connected chain: **an explicit class of coupled systems → a computable structural invariant or classification → sharp recovery/coercivity → actual realization and stable transfer.** RH is not a required endpoint. Several exact results already exist along this chain; the work is to establish the missing mathematical connections and genuinely stronger conclusions.

Scope note: baseline statements were checked in current sources. Internal searches and exhaustive matching of the chosen source terms informed the proposed extensions, but do not certify that every target is absent under every name. Before pursuing an item, compare its exact statement with the current corpus and discharge any already-proved part by reuse. No new theorem in this checklist is claimed proved.
