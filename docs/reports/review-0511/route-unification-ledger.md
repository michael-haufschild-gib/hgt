# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 9 candidates are organized by three core problems — **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision
and production), **U-Γ** (selection and reflection). Their RH-level endpoints meet at the **arithmetic Hardy
inequality** `P ≤ H` on every window, equivalently the **band**
`|𝒞_m(f)| ≤ W(f)` of a test `f` and its mirror `f∘R`, `R(v) = 2m − v`,
`𝒞_m(f) = 𝓗_m(f) − Σ_nΛ(n)n^{−1/2}(f⋆f)(2m − log n)`. U-Σ reads it as a form on the window, U-Π on the prime
side, U-Γ on the zero side. RH ⇔ `κ = 0` ⇔ `a*(ζ) = ⊤` ⇔ `1 ∈ ℋ(c)` ∀c ⇔ `λ*(c) → 0` ⇔ `N_d = 0` for
infinitely many `d`. Here `κ` counts distinct right zeros, whereas `sup_d N_d` counts off-line multiplicity;
neither count is known finite. Reflection pairs decide window positivity. These equivalences do not construct
transport between route-specific sources or discharge their arithmetic bounds.

Live source-criterion branches include ZCG, PTR, SEC, ACS, CDC and PCS. HJ→PA and BSH→CDC are aliases, not
independent routes. FEL's BSY criterion supports CTF; its theta-history producer remains a candidate.
Candidates: APF, EGC, EWJ, FEL, IRK, MSC, RFHM, W1C, WBF. Programme/support labels below add no routes.

- **Distinct missing inputs.** Over `𝔽_q`, Hodge index and integral purity supply sign after their stated
  hypotheses [L]. Over ℤ, ACS's exact source cone and Riemann–Roch are constructed, but all-aperture Bézout/sign
