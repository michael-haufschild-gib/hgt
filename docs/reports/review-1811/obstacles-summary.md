# Research programme for a formal proof of RH

**Best prospect:** use the freedom to average the signed upper PVC product over square conductors before trying to estimate it. ACS already accepts precisely such a prefix. This permits cancellation between different conductors as well as between prime/cofactor pairs. The first decisive test is a power saving on one specified product range (§2.4). The most informative independent question is whether one off-line quartet could produce the ordinary-prime counts we actually have (§2.17). The former tests a proof mechanism; the latter tests an exceptional zero configuration. **Evidence and notation.** Θ is the rightmost zero abscissa and w=Θ−½. The working-tree declaration gives Θ≤20999/24000. This is a project-source result; published explicit regions such as Bellotti’s have a boundary depending on height. RH remains publicly open. “Proposed” designates a research question, and “deduction” designates an inference with its inputs shown. Claims of world priority for specialized lemmas would require independent comparison. ## 2.3 Combining PVC, ACS and finite summation

Let q_n be the positive rational ordinary-prime generator at the successor-square divisor with multiplicity n; write P_N=∏_{1≤n≤N}q_n. Let D_n be the signed dyadic prime contact. Write T_n(U,M) for Lean’s **cappedUpperProductReading** with product cutoff U and cofactor cap M, and set R_n=T_n(n⌊√n⌋,n). All floors, log-prime weights and coprimality conditions are those of this reading. Put L_n=1+log(2n). [Finite product](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/SquareGeneratorPrefix.lean) · [Prime contact](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheDyadicPrimeWeightOfSquareConductors.lean) · [Capped reading](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheCompleteUpperProductTail.lean)

**Deduction.** At m=n the mesh estimate gives log q_n=n²·ordinaryPrimeContact+O(n²). Replacing this contact by D_n costs O(1) before multiplication. The capped-tail theorem gives D_n=R_n+O(L_n⁶). The product cutoff contributes O(log(2n)) since U²≤n³; the cofactor cutoff costs O(log(2n)) since M=n and the spline horizon is O(n²). Summing these finite identities yields

$$
\left|\log P_N-\sum_{n=1}^N n^2 R_n\right| \ll N^3(1+\log(2N))^6.
$$

Thus it suffices to prove, for one fixed e∈{−1,+1}, that for **every** η>0 and **every** N the sum e∑n²R_n is at most Cη N^(3+η). For each 0<a<½ take η<2a and absorb the logarithms in N^(2a). The one-sided prefix theorem gives w≤a. Let a decrease to zero. This argument permits individual R_n to be large with opposite contributions across n. It uses no conjectural estimate in deriving the displayed transfer. The subpower **upper bound on the sum** is the unsolved step. ## 2.4 A breakthrough below a zero-free strip

**Proposed restricted theorem.** For n≥1 put V_n=⌊n^(7/4)⌋ and B_n=T_n(V_n,n)−T_n(2V_n,n). Can one prove, for some explicit δ>0, uniformly in N,

$$
\left|\sum_{N<n\le 2N}n^2 B_n\right|\ll N^{7/2-\delta}\,?
$$

The shell V_n<pv≤2V_n lies beyond the automatically paid n^(3/2) cutoff. Applying the proved two-cutoff inequality twice gives O(n^(1/2)log(2n)+L_n⁶) per shell and O(N^(7/2)log(2N)) after weighting and summing. A power saving would therefore beat a stated benchmark for a named part of the actual prime row. A useful first experiment is to fix p,v, sum the *signed* spline differences over the admissible n interval, and measure the two boundary terms separately. A result treating all p as integers must price its replacement error. [Primitive estimate](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheThreeQuarterProductWindow.lean) · [Cutoff formula](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheCompleteUpperProductTail.lean)

This restricted theorem would test whether the extra conductor variable buys cancellation. It leaves every other product band unpaid and implies no new ζ strip by the stated receivers. The exponent 7/4 is a proposed interior test between 3/2 and 2; no optimality claim is attached. Success would justify seeking the same gain on neighbouring shells. Failure with a lower bound would identify a range that must cancel *against* its neighbours.

## 2.5 A breakthrough for a positivity producer

Start with two independent signed displacements on disjoint finite prime supports. ## 2.6 A breakthrough without positivity or spectral machinery

**Can the signed factorial prime-removal sum be reorganized by an exact cancellation of factorization histories, leaving a boundary of square-root size?** Seek an algorithm on ordered factorization histories for n≤X, with an explicit sign-reversing operation and a deterministic encoding of the residual histories. Prove an upper bound on their number by direct integer enumeration, allowing the partner to change cofactor. Start on a prescribed band of factor sizes and record the exact signed boundary passed to neighbouring bands. On all bands combined the target is Oε(X^(½+ε)) at every integer cutoff. Fixed-cofactor matching has a proved large debt; the operation must genuinely change fibres. [Prime-removal test](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeErrorAtTheLinearGrowthBoundary.lean) · [Fibre obstruction](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/QuantitativeFactorizationMatching/GlobalDyadicGraphAndCofactorFibres.lean)

For the stipulated proof style there is a direct analytic finish. The elementary factorial convolution transfers this cancellation to M(X). Partial summation then makes ∑μ(n)n^(−s) holomorphic on Re s>½. On Re s>1 it equals 1/ζ(s); analytic continuation of the product away from the pole at 1 excludes right zeros. Functional reflection gives RH. This proposed chain uses signed algebra, counting and ordinary complex analysis. The full boundary encoding is the breakthrough. A construction on a band supplies only that band’s signed boundary. ## 2.7 A physical phenomenon and a falsifiable prediction

**Hypothesis:** RH expresses a universal relaxation rate for a pulse scattered by the modular cusp after its mean response is removed. In the corpus model this is a statement about the lifetime of a disturbance: at fixed observation point and height each compact pulse decays at rate ¼−ε for every ε>0. The strip-to-pulse equivalence fixes the prediction; it supplies no microscopic reason why the rate should hold. [Rate law](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TheRingdownRateIsTheZeroFreeStrip.lean#L625)

**Discriminating question.** Can one derive a bound on the causal return kernel from the actual arithmetic scattering dynamics, using a rule fixed before looking at the ξ poles, that already pays a rate δ>3001/48000 for all pulses in a specified source class? Show how that class controls the full pulse space with constants sufficient for the rate theorem. Test the identical derivation on a passive Euler twin sharing a prescribed finite head. Such twins are contractive and can have an off-line zero. A premise equally valid for twins with a pole incompatible with the predicted decay falsifies the proposed *mechanism*. A finite time trace can calibrate the kernel. Only an all-time estimate plus the class-extension theorem improves the strip. This is a mathematical scattering hypothesis; no physical identification in nature is established. ## 2.8 A geometric breakthrough

**Can arithmetic correspondences define a conserved, locally excisive intersection number for each colliding pair of sheets of the ξ heat cover?** Its change at a simple real fold should equal the local loss of a conjugate pair. It must distinguish two distinct folds with cancelling permutation product, and be computable independently from arithmetic data on the boundary of a height box. The geometric task is to match the formal local fold index with a global boundary calculation that forces the count of actual obstructing folds to vanish. ## 2.9 Would RH require a new field?

There is no necessity theorem. One sufficiently strong estimate on the explicit sum in §2.3 would suffice within existing analytic number theory. If a new subject is needed, my choice is a theory of **signed arithmetic correspondences with quantitative boundary estimates**. Its objects would be prime/cofactor incidences with two projections, conductor cutoffs, signed weights and explicit endpoint terms. Its central question would be when pushforward along one projection preserves a signed saving proved along the other.

The tools to develop are exact finite disintegration, estimates for incomplete fibres at moving boundaries, and composition rules that retain signed error. An identity of correspondences must certify which weight and centering it preserves. The first benchmark is the paid range pv≤n⌊√n⌋. The next is the interior shell of §2.4. A general theorem must explain the prime-order escape constant under inverse-fourth weighting and identify the ordinary-prime hypothesis lost under edits. These are concrete acceptance tests for the proposed theory. Its RH application succeeds only with an all-endpoint saving for the full arithmetic sum. ## 2.10 A field for proving or falsifying AAL

AAL is the **asymptotic abscissa law**. For each h≥0 the strict-right zeros with Re ρ>½+h are either absent or infinite in number. A failure has a finite nonempty outer layer above a cut, possibly with infinitely many shallower off-line zeros. Equivalently, the far-right abscissa falls when finitely many outliers are removed. The finite-total assertion B13, the landing law ZAL⇒finite off-line support and the existential *linear* abscissa law have different content. The last is equivalent to B13 and landing. [Outer layer](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ZeroConfigurationGeometry/AbscissaLaws/TheFailureOfTheAbscissaLawIsAFiniteLayer.lean) · [Abscissa criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/FloorFreeArithmetic/NonAttainmentGivesTheAbscissaLaw.lean) · [Linear law](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L90)

A focused theory of **isolated leading terms in arithmetic explicit formulae** could suffice. Its objects are an actual prime-jump measure, a finite list of top ordinates with multiplicities, a positive gap to the remainder and signed interval observations with controlled resolution. Develop reconstruction stable under close frequencies, tests that distinguish a true prime source from a planted finite wave, and a closure rule that forces new high-ordinate contributors whenever a nonempty top layer exists. The rule must survive a remainder smaller than the top by some positive power; demanding a square-root remainder would discard cases that falsify AAL.

First handle one quartet by §2.17. Then distinguish an isolated top pair with shallower zeros from a single quartet in the entire divisor. For several top frequencies, use finitely many dilations with independently justified conditioning for that finite set. Multipliers that identify frequencies give no arithmetic contradiction by themselves. A proof programme succeeds if it forces every strict nonempty right layer to be infinite. Falsification requires an actual ζ zero beyond a specified cut **and a proof there are only finitely many there**. Seeing repeated fitted frequencies or constructing an edited divisor satisfies neither requirement. Even a proof of AAL leaves ZAL for RH. ## 2.11 A field for a proof by signed cancellation alone

The proposed subject would study **effective cancellation in integer convolutions**. Its objects would be finite signed sets of factorizations, partial involutions, maps between divisor fibres and the residual chains on adjacent quotient intervals. Equality would mean an exact signed identity plus an explicit finite boundary. Define composition by tracing that boundary to its final unmatched integers. A conservation identity for sign is easy; controlling the size of the final boundary is the substance.

Three tools would make it useful. First, a normal form for integer cutoff floors under Dirichlet convolution. Second, a cross-cofactor rewriting rule preserving the Möbius weight. Third, a counting lemma with constants uniform under iteration, charged only to surviving endpoints. Calibrate with the factorial transform that preserves Mertens powers for s≥½ and with the linear-size unaveraged profile. Demand a nontrivial saving on a single restricted dyadic graph before extending the rewrites. If every admissible rewrite stays in fixed fibres the Ω(X/log³X) debt rejects that calculus. Success is a signed prefix bound that feeds the elementary continuation argument in §2.6. No operator, moment-positivity criterion, Weil form or zero spectrum enters the proposed proof. ## 2.12 A field for a topological or geometric proof

A candidate field would be **arithmetic intersection theory for families of analytic divisors with noncompact ends**. Local objects would be marked zero sheets, their conjugation/reflection involutions and the ramification divisor of projection to deformation time. Global objects would include height boundaries and the arithmetic cycles whose intersections are supposed to fix the deformation. Morphisms would preserve multiplicities, the order of branch turns and the flux of sheets through each boundary component.

The tools required are a gluing theorem for local collision indices, a relative intersection group retaining boundary escape, and a comparison from arithmetic graph contacts to a computable class of the actual ξ family. At finite height the local model must recover the Hermite collision and distinguish repeated transpositions. At infinite height it must quantify both the heat cutoff and escape as time tends to zero. A theory that only computes total parity or the branch divisor of an arbitrary symmetric entire function has failed the arithmetic comparison. A successful local comparison theorem on one nontrivial family would be an advance. The RH conclusion requires a vanishing mechanism on *all* boxes and exhaustion with no missing divisor. **Research judgment.** Pursue the signed **conductor-prefix** theorem of §2.3, using ordinary-prime arithmetic on the capped PVC products.
