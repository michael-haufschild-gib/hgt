## 2.3 A combination that removes a genuine blocker

**Arithmetic Castelnuovo–Severi (ACS) × paired Vaughan counts (PVC) × conductor smoothing.** Write $T_n(U,n)$ for the actual spline reading with coprime cofactor $v\le n$, product $pv>U$ and the original moving lower endpoint. For the rational conductor product $P(N)$ the source gives

$$
 B(N)=\sum_{n=1}^{N} n^2 T_n(\lfloor n^{17/9}\rfloor,n),
 \qquad |\log P(N)-B(N)|\ll N^3(1+\log(2N))^6 .
$$

ACS supplies the signed log price. PVC expresses it through ordinary primes. Summing periodic quotient differences across conductors pays one extra order at the spline knots. The estimate holds at every terminal $N$ and bypasses comparison of two individual quartic log heights. [Complete Upper Product Tail](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheCompleteUpperProductTail.lean) · [Partial-octave bound](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheSeventeenNinthsConductorPrefix.lean#L236) · [Height–prefix comparison](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/SquareConductors/TheSeventeenNinthsConductorPrefix.lean#L807) · [Quartic Heights of Reduced Square Prefix](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/QuarticHeightsOfTheReducedSquarePrefix.lean)

The proposed next splice is with exact divisor inversion **inside the remaining finite sum**. Group by product pv and outer quotient ⌊k/(pv)⌋, then split divisor sums into the full identity Σ_{d∣q}Λ(d)=log q and the exact complementary terms from the cap, coprimality, ordinary-prime restriction and endpoints. The identity pays the complete divisor sum only. The research target is cancellation between the omitted terms and the moving endpoints. A bound B(N)≤Cη N^(3+η) for every η>0 closes RH. The displayed comparison leaves precisely that upper bound open. ## 2.7 A physical hypothesis and a way to reject it

The proposed phenomenon is **escape of a wave from the cusp of the modular surface**. A compact pulse produces a delayed arithmetic return. The slowest persistent return controls the decay measurable by a receiver in the cusp. Every compact pulse decays at rate δ up to ε exactly when Θ≤1−2δ, for 0<δ≤¼. RH is the quarter-rate law in this model. A source that is causal, passive, unitary on the real axis and agrees on any preassigned finite arithmetic prefix can still have an off-line zero. [Rate–strip equivalence](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/TheRingdownRateIsTheZeroFreeStrip.lean#L625) · [Passive twin](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BlackHoleRingdown/ThePositiveEulerTwinsArePassive.lean#L360)

**Question.** Can the return from repeated traversal of a primitive prime path be used to derive a quantitative escape estimate for a *mixed* pulse, with all interference terms retained? At one prime the passive twin has a negative logarithmic coefficient at p², whereas the Euler logarithm of ζ contributes +1/2 at p². Predeclare the receiver, the time window and the prime-square and mixed-prime terms. Derive an inequality from the modular dynamics, test it on the undeformed pulse and the passive twin, then seek constants uniform as the window grows. A twin satisfying all proposed dynamical premises with slower decay falsifies those premises as an explanation. A finite-time inequality distinguishing them would give the hypothesis a measurable consequence below RH. A fit to a finite decay curve cannot decide the asymptotic exponent. ## 2.9 What new mathematics might a full proof need?

No deduction here requires the invention of a field. The missing capability is a way to deduce a global estimate for a completed arithmetic object from the finite operations that generate it. A theory of **signed arithmetic correspondences** is one possibility. Its objects would be integer divisors, correspondences indexed by prime powers, their real-place metrics and sections on opposing degrees. Its operations would include composition, intersection, duality and taking a finite part with a controlled error. Its language must distinguish a raw count, its main term, and its signed defect. Reduced numerator and denominator can both have quartic log height. [Quartic Heights of Reduced Square Prefix](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/QuarticHeightsOfTheReducedSquarePrefix.lean) · [Carrier Kernel Is Weil Minus Contact](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/AbsoluteGeometryOfSpecZ/TheCarrierKernelIsWeilMinusTheContact.lean)

A decisive theorem should control mixed arithmetic intersections after gluing, uniformly in a growing support, and give a quantitative bound below RH before addressing all supports. If ordinary analysis of the PVC prefix already yields that control, the estimate suffices without a new language. ## 2.10 What a theory for proving or falsifying AAL would study

**AAL**, the asymptotic abscissa law, says that for every h≥0 the distinct right zeros above ½+h are empty or infinite. A falsifier needs a zero above one actual threshold **and proof that only finitely many exceed it**. Arbitrarily many verified zeros on the line cannot establish the latter. Failure permits an infinite lower background. Conversely, AAL permits infinitely many displaced zeros. ZAL, that only finitely many stay beyond each positive depth, combined with AAL forces RH. [Finite-tail criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ZeroConfigurationGeometry/AbscissaLaws/TheFailureOfTheAbscissaLawIsAFiniteLayer.lean#L33) · [Landing Law for Zeta](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean)

The needed theory could fit inside harmonic analysis of exponential sums. Its objects would be a finite outer layer, a strictly slower remainder and phase-sensitive samples of the prime lag at log p. Its basic quantities would be top growth rate, multiplicities, separation of rates and a modulus for detecting an entire finite layer on a moving interval. A finite top attains its rate on prime samples in every sufficiently late window of each fixed length. A path to proof is to derive, for each eligible positive top depth b, some length on which all prime samples are arbitrarily small relative to pᵇ on arbitrarily late intervals. A path to falsification is to reconstruct a nonempty layer with certified slower remainder for actual ζ. A method must separate both paths on models with a finite top and on models with infinitely many zeros at nearly the same depth. ## 2.13 Programme for an absolute base 𝔽₁

The intended construction is an **absolute base for arithmetic geometry** on which the compactified arithmetic curve and its self-product have meaningful Frobenius intersections. An ordinary field cannot have one element because its zero and one must be distinct. Existing 𝔽₁ geometries already supply models for comparison.
