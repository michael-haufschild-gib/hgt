# Which routes can use the same new theorem?

The strongest common object is an **ordinary-prime error with its sign and scale intact**. It has several exact readings below RH. A second common object is the complete Weil form; here the normalizations and test spaces decide whether a partial estimate transports. A third is the exceptional zero configuration; here the distinction between one quartet, a finite outer layer and the whole divisor decides what a contradiction would prove. The rows below distinguish exact identities, implications and proposed constructions. [Route-by-route hypotheses](route-obstacles.md) · [Wall families](walls-ledger.md)

## Similar endpoints with distinct missing inputs

| Routes | What must survive a proposed transfer |
|---|---|
| FU, CFC, RP, TT, LI, SZ, CHMD, WL | A sample covering every late power window, one lower floor over an aperture tail, a linear variance floor, fixed-t covariance bounds, all subexponential lower rates, a bounded complete amplitude and the two possible wave signs are different quantifiers. A comparison must preserve time, normalization, endpoint terms and direction. [Signed histories](route-obstacles.md#fu) |
| QFM, RB, AAD, ALST | A graph matching across cofactors, a strict inequality at every late extremum, a balanced integer alphabet and a short nonzero auxiliary are different witnesses. The common prime-count endpoint does not construct any of them. [Prime arithmetic](route-obstacles.md#qfm) |
| BAG, CTF, AIF, WD | Positive-density returns to the ζ target, convergence of bounded rows to a specified vector, moment tending to one, and a uniform growth exponent across unbounded lengths are distinct. Target mass and long-length control are separate inputs. [Approximation routes](route-obstacles.md#bag) |
| PA, TH, HCF, ECP, LA, GTA | The index runs over shifts within a difference row, polynomial degree, two grids, mixed permutation shapes, unshifted Jensen degrees, or theta-stage packets. To merge results one needs a map controlling errors over that entire family. [All-order criteria](route-obstacles.md#pa) · [Quartic charge](route-obstacles.md#gta) |
| ZCG, PTR, XN, TG | A heat branch point, cosine-shift double root, failing Newton-disc cover and rectangle trace-norm defect can each obstruct a proof. A local solution for one analytic family supplies no uniform bound in the others. [Zero geometry](route-obstacles.md#zcg) |
| IRK, SEC, CDC/BSH, EGC | Proposed invariants select a projective class, test a strip parameter, read a marked residue or integrate a whole divisor. The missing operation must select ζ and force a contradiction on actual adverse fibres. [Candidate register](route-obstacles.md#irk) |
| APF, EWJ, RFHM, WBF | The proposed constructions retain, respectively, real trace labels, multiplicity jets, support grades and determinant magnitudes. The unpaid information is exhaustion, an effective endpoint, surviving orientation and relative phase. [Candidate register](route-obstacles.md#apf) |
| MSC, FEL, W1C, S3 | Each has a different promotion requirement: a positive transport of the squarefree source, a signed log-mean comparison, actual ferromagnetic approximants, or a homogeneous-return-to-trace theorem. [Candidate register](route-obstacles.md#msc) |

**Priority for combining routes.** PVC already gives an arithmetic decomposition of the ACS test. The freedom to sum over conductors *before* estimating is additional to the freedom to sum over prime/cofactor pairs. By contrast the ECS, PDR and ZCG quartet tests already observe the same predicted wave. Combining those tests is valuable for falsification only when at least one supplies independent prime arithmetic.
