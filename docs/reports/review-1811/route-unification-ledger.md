# Which RH routes share a mathematical problem?

Two routes genuinely share work when an estimate, witness or obstruction on one side gives something on the other **before anyone has proved RH**. An equivalence obtained by going through RH tells us which conclusions would follow at the end. It gives no recipe for constructing the other route's missing input. The distinctions below matter when choosing a theorem to prove. [Wall families](walls-ledger.md) · [Route definitions and individual obligations](route-obstacles.md)

## Where a shared endpoint leaves independent work

| Mechanisms | Distinction that a proposed combination must overcome |
|---|---|
| FU · CFC · RP · TT · LI · SZ · CHMD · WL | Respectively a normalized Abel moment, a first-clock lower bound, a linear variance floor, fixed-parameter directed covariance, every subexponential rate, an absolute amplitude, one side of a wave, and a bounded critical energy. Comparing these requires a signed identity with the correct normalization and uniform constants. [History routes](route-obstacles.md#fu) |
| RB · QFM · AAD · ALST | A bound at all late divisor extrema, an actual matching, a balanced integer row, and a short polynomial multiple are four different witnesses. Only the unrestricted matching and the polynomial minimum have the stated exact counting identifications. [Prime arithmetic](route-obstacles.md#qfm) |
| BAG · CTF · AIF · WD | Self-return density, bounded-coefficient L² approximation, a holomorphic budget tending to 1, and long Möbius moments impose different coefficient and length constraints. The cut kernel genuinely is dual to CTF. A minimizing sequence for AIF of unknown value produces no chronological approximants. [Kernel Beyond Power Laws](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RigidArithmeticOfCuts/TheKernelBeyondPowerLaws.lean) · [Inner Mass Is AMollifier Infimum](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticInnerFunctionTheory/TheInnerMassIsAMollifierInfimum.lean) · [Long Moebius Second Moment Rigidity](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ConjugateDiagonalGeometry/NearLineLogMean/LongMoebiusSecondMomentRigidity.lean#L704) |
| PA · TH · HCF · ECP · LA · GTA | The indices range over Hausdorff shifts, theta jets, two grids, mixed representations, Jensen degrees, and an entire stage with its tail. Transferring one finite certificate requires an explicit map between the tests and a tail estimate. [Degree routes](route-obstacles.md#pa) |
| ZCG · PTR · XN · TG · RN · SC · PM | A heat branch point, a real double zero of a shift, an invariant Newton disc, an integer formation count, a joint renewal node, a coupled index, and a visible scattering resonance all constrain the divisor. Transport of *location* alone gives no independent norm bound, collision exclusion or decay. [Geometry](route-obstacles.md#zcg) · [Renewal and coupling](route-obstacles.md#rn) |
| IRK · SEC · CDC/BSH · EGC | A genuine projective class, a line-selective seam, a marked repair price and a total contour count each need a separate arithmetic selection theorem. A contour enclosing off-line and on-line points supplies none of the other three. [Candidates](route-obstacles.md#candidates) |
| APF · EWJ · RFHM · WBF | They respectively retain paired labels, arbitrary-depth jets, prime support and phase, or Gram data up to gauge. A common model would have to preserve all relevant divisor data while independently controlling orientation. [Candidates](route-obstacles.md#candidates) |
| MSC · FEL · W1C · S3 | Their proposed bridges are, respectively, a positive transport, an entropy/log-mean inequality, ferromagnetic approximation, and a return-to-trace estimate. The objects on the two sides already exist in several cases. None of those facts constructs the missing bridge. [Candidates](route-obstacles.md#candidates) |
