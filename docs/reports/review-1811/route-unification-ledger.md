# Which routes would one theorem actually unlock?

A shared RH endpoint is cheap: if two propositions are equivalent to RH, each implies the other. A useful identification transfers a **subcritical exponent, a signed quantity with its normalization, a counterexample, or a finite-stratum conclusion**. The following ledger states what a proof on one side would buy on the other. [Route definitions](route-obstacles.md) · [Three wall families](walls-ledger.md)

## Exact transfers short of RH

| **Right edge Θ and width w=Θ−½** | JAS signal growth reads Θ with arbitrarily small exponent slack. For 0≤s<½, the ACS fixed dyadic upper bound O((n+1)^(2s)) is equivalent to w≤s. The WD optimal doubling-slope loss is 2w. The RW logarithmic floor rate reads w. The infimum of admissible window-doubling factors in the prescribed polynomial schedules is 2^w. | **The ordinary-prime error exponent** | LPF replaces ψ(N)−N by a largest-prime-factor mass centered at π²N²/12, increasing the power by one. PSM detects a right zero via both harmonic sign masses. | **Dyadic Möbius imbalance and Hall excess** | For any admissible relation, minimum unmatched = abs(dyadic Möbius sum) + 2 × Hall excess. The full relation eliminates the second term. | **The signed Weil degree on integer packets** | ACS writes the full form as M−D. A section ray recovers max(degree,0). | **One off-line quartet, any multiplicity m** | At ρ=β+iγ with β>½, the one-quartet law gives ψ(x)−x=−2m Re(x^ρ/ρ)+O(√x log²x). The bare index is two. ## Similar endpoints with different proof obligations

| Routes | What any common construction would have to preserve |
|---|---|
| MC, SQF, RB, AAD, ALST | Möbius prefixes require signed cancellation. The smoothed squarefree law permits a single side at quarter power. Robin requires eventual strict inequalities at extrema. AAD asks for balanced integer coefficients with bounded alphabet. ALST asks for a degree bound on a polynomial multiple. An independent ψ/Θ gain can price some of these inputs; none produces the five witnesses simply by changing notation. [Prime routes](route-obstacles.md#prime-counts-factors-and-interpolation) |
| FU, CFC, RP, TT, LI, SZ, CHMD | Preserve the sign, horizon, allowable growth and polar normalization. A linear lower floor on a matched-clock defect, a subexponential Li lower bound, bounded complete Suzuki amplitude and bounded-below prime wave involve different cancellations. [Signed histories](route-obstacles.md#signed-histories-recurrence-and-approximation) |
| CTF, AIF, WD, BAG | Approximation tolerance, mollifier budget near one, one exponent across cofinal lengths, and positive density of ζ-target returns are different forms of uniformity. A small average norm does not construct the BAG returns or the AIF polynomials. [Approximation and recurrence](route-obstacles.md#signed-histories-recurrence-and-approximation) |
| PA, TH, ECP, HCF, LA, SRT | One all-order bound can exclude an exterior root; the order ranges over difference rows, theta jets, Young diagrams, grid polynomials, Jensen degrees or prime traces. Transport requires a theorem preserving the whole test family and its constants. [All-order tests](route-obstacles.md#all-orders-zeros-and-topology) |
| GTA, SC, TG, XN | The threshold is respectively arbitrarily small charge, bounded coupling or minimal index, trace norm <1, or a covering Newton self-map. An integer conclusion from each does not identify the quantity that must be bounded. [Complete forms](route-obstacles.md#complete-forms-arithmetic-sections-and-scattering) · [Topological gates](route-obstacles.md#all-orders-zeros-and-topology) |
| SEC, CDC/BSH, EGC, IRK | SEC/CDC measure displacement once a parameter is given; EGC integrates many parameters together; IRK needs a marked projective realization. A common idea must both select actual ζ zeros and force the value of a source-defined invariant on their fibres. [Candidate interfaces](route-obstacles.md#candidate-mechanisms) |
| APF, EWJ, RFHM, WBF | Reconstructing traces, jets, grades or determinant magnitudes can preserve the wrong information. The missing pieces are divisor exhaustion, a nondegenerate endpoint, surviving orientation and relative phase respectively. [Candidate interfaces](route-obstacles.md#candidate-mechanisms) |
| S3, MSC, FEL, W1C | S3 would feed SRT through a return-to-trace theorem. MSC needs positive Sonin transport, FEL needs a boundary-mean inequality, W1C needs actual ferromagnetic approximants. Each has a distinct missing arrow. [Candidate interfaces](route-obstacles.md#candidate-mechanisms) |

## Where combinations have leverage

1. **PVC → ACS.** Try to estimate the surviving long-prime products *after* applying the fixed ACS dyadic weight. Its cancellation of the density term can help only if the bilinear estimate exploits the two scales jointly. An existing unsmoothed ψ bound, followed by partial summation, merely transfers its exponent. This is a proposed bridge. [Remaining products](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/RemovingSmallOrdinaryPrimesAtBothWidths.lean) · [Target weight](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/TheDyadicPrimeWeightOfSquareConductors.lean)
2. **BPF + PVC + ECS/ZCG.** The quartet waveform fixes frequency and multiplicity. Adjacent counts recover the same signed wave exactly. Fixed-depth distinct-factor arithmetic gives a different way of inspecting ordinary-prime carries. It could disprove the wave if it predicts a phase defect inconsistent with square-root remainder. Reconstructing the wave a third time is a diagnostic, with no exclusion attached. [Identity](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheSingleQuartetWaveInAdjacentPrimeCounts.lean) · [Distinct factors](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDistinctPrimeFactorsOfOneCentralBinomialDetectEveryRightZero.lean) · [State readout](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheTwoQuartetRungsRecoverTheComplexPrimeWave.lean)
3. **Finite-field geometry → ACS → HF.** A genuine arithmetic section correspondence controlling the loss of midpoint products could reach the signed degree and then the index. Test its factors and denominators on a known curve and on an impure arithmetic model. An abstract injection obtained by comparing the two cardinalities after assuming their order is circular. [Exact injection criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/MidpointProductsOfSmallSections.lean) · [Precision obstruction](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/Sections/LocalCongruencesOfMidpointInjections.lean) · [Curve control](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/FunctionField/HermitianPointClassOrders.lean)
4. **ECS integer primitive → zero geometry.** Its transform equals a nonvanishing state factor times ζ(s)/s. The zero set is identified exactly even though the primitive is integrable and square-integrable. The squared ordinary-polar error transfers to integer centering up to an exp(rt)-integrable absolute difference, 0≤r<1−Θ. A proof using this bridge needs a new zero-excluding property of the *arithmetic* input on 0<Re s<½. The critical line itself contains zeros and cannot be included in a blanket nonvanishing claim.
