# Walls between the source theorems and RH

There are three mathematical expenses. **P** is cancellation in an arithmetic quantity defined before consulting the zeros. **R** is the sign of a completed form or the exclusion of its adverse state. **U** is control of all relevant scales, heights, orders or exceptional zeros. A row marked **resolved** names the exact piece available to the next proof. A **boundary** rules out a specified mechanism. A **receiver** prices the remaining input. It supplies no independent payment. IDs allow cross-reference when one proof touches more than one expense. [Route register](route-obstacles.md) · [Identifications](route-unification-ledger.md)
