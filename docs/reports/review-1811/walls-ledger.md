# Walls between the arithmetic data and RH

The outstanding work falls into three families. **P** concerns the size of a signed sum on the ordinary integers. **R** concerns the sign, index or effectivity of a complete arithmetic object. **U** concerns the quantifiers under which a local result can exclude every zero. A route can meet all three. For example, the ACS prime sum is P33, its comparison with a section height is available, and its bound must hold through every terminal conductor (U25). A solved entry below settles only its stated range. [Individual routes](route-obstacles.md) · [Transfers between them](route-unification-ledger.md)
