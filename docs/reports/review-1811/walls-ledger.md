# Walls between the current theorems and RH

Three independent questions organize the ledger. **P** asks what ordinary primes cancel. **R** asks what enforces the sign or index of the complete arithmetic form. **U** asks whether the estimate covers every required configuration or limiting parameter. Some problems have two addresses. On square conductors, for example, reduction to ordinary-prime products is available, their signed sum is open (P33), and an estimate on occasional blocks misses the required endpoints (U25). **Where the three walls meet.** The smooth upper tail removes lower products (P33). A one-sided generator-prefix estimate would pay the section comparison (R36). The estimate must hold at every terminal N (U25). This gives a concrete chain by which a single new arithmetic bound could discharge all three obligations for one route.
