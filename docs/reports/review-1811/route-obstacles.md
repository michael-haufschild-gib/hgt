# Routes to a formal proof of RH

**52 live mechanisms and 12 candidates.** EWU and OWU are displayed separately because either parity criterion can close the pole-weight route. CDC/BSH is one candidate with two proposed constructions. A live mechanism has a specified object and a proof that the stated bound or construction would settle RH. A candidate still needs an arithmetic construction or a bridge from its own data to that implication. [Common walls](walls-ledger.md) · [Which routes share work](route-unification-ledger.md) · [Research programme](obstacles-summary.md)

The “work with existing inputs” column gives finite operations or deductions with known premises. The last column gives the new mathematics required for closure. Throughout, Θ is the supremum of real parts of nontrivial zeros and w=Θ−½. The working Lean source gives Θ≤20999/24000. Citations point to Lean statements. In particular, the finite radial converse, the adelic prime-plane results, the conductor-shell estimates and the joint ECS prefix bounds are reported at source level. This review adds no kernel or transitive axiom audit. [Project strip](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/SubSevenEighthsZeroFreeHalfPlane.lean#L180)
