# Routes to a formal proof of RH

**The most explicit unpaid estimate is a signed sum over ordinary primes and coprime cofactors at square conductors.** Its lower product range, the cost of truncating the complementary factor, and the transfer to zero width all have Lean declarations. A second comparatively sharp target is to contradict the two consecutive prime-interval increments forced by one off-line quartet. The first could close RH. The second settles a specific exceptional configuration. ## Scope of the exclusions

PW-B and SEC illustrate the difference between detecting the line and selecting ζ zeros. DR’s regional roots require an independent existence theorem. TPP’s factorwise completions have a 3/5 boundary. HAT-G’s common torus functional loses the degree dependence. These exclusions concern the specified operations. KR-TOR contributes an actual target-density transfer to BAG. HAT-C, PM and CFC retain respectively a normalized Green inequality, arithmetic pulse comparison and a first-clock floor. For legacy labels marked *unlocated* the inventory supplies no declaration establishing impossibility.
