# RH routes and their missing theorems

**The most concentrated arithmetic targets are a signed sum over ordinary primes at square conductors (ACS), a two-width sum over coprime products (PVC), and a first mean of the number of distinct prime factors of central binomial coefficients (BPF).** Each has an RH receiver in Lean. The existence of the receiver settles which observable suffices. Estimating it from integer arithmetic is the research problem. On the geometric side, two confined states already reconstruct the wave of a hypothetical single quartet. Excluding that wave would settle one shared stratum. [Exact bridges](route-unification-ledger.md) · [Research programmes](obstacles-summary.md)

**Coverage.** The register contains 47 live mechanisms, counting the even and odd polar tests as one mechanism with two entries, and 12 candidates. The fixed-cutoff smooth-integer count is an additional exact realization of BPF.

**Reading the register.** Each row gives the current theorem, an operation with identified inputs, and the missing producer. “Work” includes finite evaluations and deductions under the stated inputs. “Open” means an independent proof is missing. For an RH-equivalent endpoint, a solution would settle the public RH problem. For a narrower problem, the evidence establishes a corpus gap, without establishing worldwide priority or impossibility. Evidence comes from commit `da3facf356` and the working-tree declarations identified in the source log. Validation was at statement level. Θ is the rightmost zero abscissa, w = Θ − ½. An exponent specified for every ε allows its constant to depend on ε. [Corpus evidence](../../../logs/review-1811-rh-corpus-facts.md) · [Clay problem description](https://www.claymath.org/riemann/)
