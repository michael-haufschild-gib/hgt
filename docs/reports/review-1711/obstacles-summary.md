# RH research priorities

**Full-proof programme:** construct an arithmetic count for the Weil form on signed integer spline probes. The source already specifies the quantity the count must recover, including its mixed terms. Convergence along each fixed integer ray suffices. The decisive problem is to realize the renormalized *complete* form, including the reflection debit, as the growth of actual finite objects. Section 2.18 gives a staged programme and rejection tests. ## 2.4 A bounded question with consequences for the whole programme

**At three distinct coprime primary primes, does the normalized cubic-dilate residue factor into the three pair interactions, or is there a genuine three-prime term?** For two primes the source obtains the individual double pole by peeling the local coefficients before continuing. Relative to the product normalization the correction is (1+χ)/2 for χ=(p/q)₃. Its modulus is one when χ=1 and one half otherwise. In particular the naïve product of independent one-prime factors is already wrong. [TheCompositeDilateCorrelationHasADoublePole](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/OrderSixMetaplecticTheory/TheCompositeDilateCorrelationHasADoublePole.lean)

Fix three primes before any averaging. Predict the Laurent coefficient at 4/3 from their one-prime factors and three oriented pair symbols, making the reciprocity convention explicit. Compute the trace in two orders of peeling. If both give the pair product, test whether a compositional law survives a fourth prime. If they give a new term, isolate its phase and determine whether the signed family sees it. This question asks for a fixed-conductor local calculation, without any zero-free half-plane or growing-conductor error. A solution would decide whether the analytic programme should average pair characters or first develop a genuinely higher-order residue calculation. A fixed triple would not yet deliver that average.

## 2.6 Breakthrough question with positivity and spectral methods excluded

**Can multiplication of ordinary integers force a second independent prime-error frequency whenever ψ(x)−x has one isolated leading frequency above square root?** Use only Λ, counting multiples, divisor convolution, finite differences under rational dilation and complex continuation of ordinary Dirichlet series. Write E(x)=ψ(x)−x. A single quartet forces, for x≥16,

\[
E(x)=-2m\,\Re(x^\rho/\rho)+O(\sqrt{x}\log^2 x),\qquad \rho=\beta+i\gamma,\quad \beta>1/2.
\]

For any fixed integer b≥2 this implies that
\[
E(b^2x)-2b^\beta\cos(\gamma\log b)E(bx)+b^{2\beta}E(x)
 = O_b(\sqrt{x}\log^2 x).
\]
The latter formula is a deduction: each of the two leading powers is a root of the dilation polynomial. It gives a pair of exact tests at b=2 and b=3. [hasSingleOffLineQuartet_iff_chebyshevPsi_singleWave](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/SingleQuartetSecularAndWallRigidity.lean#L2230)

Look for a *nonlinear* arithmetic consequence of satisfying both recurrences with that remainder. One possibility is a count of two distinct prime factors with a shared cofactor, with diagonals and continuum main terms explicitly subtracted. Its evaluation by unique factorization would have to force an additional term larger than the error, while substitution of the hypothesized wave forbids it. Substituting the wave separately in two **linear** identities annihilates it twice and produces no contradiction. First prove or disprove the two-frequency claim for the one-quartet case. Extending it to an arbitrary isolated finite list targets AAL. To reach RH by replication alone would require a further result driving a replicated zero outside the proved strip, or else an independent proof of ZAL. ## 2.7 A physical phenomenon and a falsification test

The mathematical identification is unusually precise: a strip ending at 1−2δ is equivalent to decay at every δ−ε, for all compact pulses and positive observation heights. The RH endpoint is ¼. **My choice is ACS through arithmetic section counts on signed integer probes, using the explicit real-place subtraction from the surface programme and ECS as an independent failure test.** The reason is the quantifier in the Tate theorem.
