## 1. In short

**B13 is the implication FOL ⇒ RH**: if only finitely many zeros of ζ lie off the critical line, then none does. It is the abscissa law at the single abscissa ½ ([`abscissaLawAt_zero_zetaZeroConfig_iff`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L81)) and one of the three atoms of RH:

> RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing ([`riemannHypothesis_iff_three_atoms`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L68)), existential linear AAL ⇔ B13 ∧ Landing ([`zetaLinearAbscissaLaw_iff_b13_and_landing`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L90)).

Three equivalent pictures (all proved in Lean, §3):

- **Index.** The negative index of Weil's form on real packets equals the number of distinct zeros right of the line ([`realPacketNegIndex_eq_rightZeros`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/TheFiniteStratumIsThePacketIndexDichotomy.lean#L85)). B13 says this index is 0 or ∞, never finite and positive ([`finiteOffLine_imp_rh_iff_packetIndex_zero_or_infinite`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/TheFiniteStratumIsThePacketIndexDichotomy.lean#L99)).
- **Primes.** B13 says: if ψ(x) − x = −Re Σ_(q∈S) x^q/q + O(√x·log²x) for a finite set S of zeros, then S contains no off-line zero ([`stageTwo_primeSide_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/StageTwoHasPrimeSideRows.lean#L99)). The smallest case: ψ(x) − x = −2m·Re(x^ρ/ρ) + O(√x·log²x) for one zero ρ with Re ρ > ½ is exactly "ζ has a single off-line quartet" ([`hasSingleOffLineQuartet_iff_chebyshevPsi_singleWave`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/SingleQuartetSecularAndWallRigidity.lean#L1697)).
- **Critical state.** In the finite stratum there is no null vector of the confined half-line form at any ratio ([`finiteLayer_ecs_b13_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/FiniteLayerKreinStructureAndRigidity.lean#L1067)).

B13 is weaker than RH and than AAL; it is the base case of the abscissa law (*readme* IRW: "ZAL is its crude statement, AAL its self-improvement, B13 its base case at σ = ½").

## 2. Why it matters

1. **It is an atom of RH.** Every proof of RH contains a proof of B13. Together with Landing it is existential linear AAL; with the extreme Deuring–Heilbronn law (¬AAL ⇒ FOL) it *is* AAL ([`zetaExtremeDeuringHeilbronn_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L285), [`asymptoticAbscissaLaw_orthogonal_factorization_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L305)), and with both the nine receivers ZAL, FOL, κ(K_ζ) < ∞, bounded heat counts, finite critical source energy, eventual Hadamard positivity, central-gap budget and a finite odd bound-state space all become equivalent to RH ([`riemannHypothesis_tfae_of_extremeDeuringHeilbronn_and_b13`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L356)).
2. **It is the minimal shape of a counterexample.** A world with a finite off-line layer satisfies almost everything that RH gives: ZAL ([`zerosApproachLine_of_finiteOffLine`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/InfiniteRankWeilTheory/TheCrudeStepAndTheCount.lean#L216)), the Lindelöf hypothesis and a zero-free half-plane Re s > ½ + α, α < ½ ([`lindelof_of_negIndex_zetaKernel_lt_top`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HeckeKreinToeplitz/TheFiniteIndexStratumOfZetaIsLindelofDeep.lean#L119), [`quasiRH_of_negIndex_zetaKernel_lt_top`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HeckeKreinToeplitz/TheFiniteIndexStratumOfZetaIsLindelofDeep.lean#L127)), finite Krein–Langer index, bounded heat counts and the critical line above a height ([`zetaKernel_finiteIndex_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HeckeKreinToeplitz/TheFiniteIndexStratumOfZetaIsLindelofDeep.lean#L104)), and the explicit formula exact up to finitely many terms ([`abs_chebyshevPsi_sub_add_offLineWave_le`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FiniteOffLineVonKoch.lean#L569)). The methods that work "on average" — densities, high-height statistics, bounded-height verification — are blind to this world: neither Deuring–Heilbronn law is an asymptotic or a bounded-height property ([`not_isAsymptotic_deuringHeilbronn`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L189), [`not_isLocalBelow_deuringHeilbronn`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L208)).
3. **It is the Siegel-zero problem of ζ.** A finite layer is an exceptional set, but nonreal. The classical exceptional-zero mechanism (Deuring–Heilbronn repulsion) is proved in the corpus for a real zero of a quadratic Dirichlet L-function ([`exists_deuring_heilbronn_repulsion`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnRepulsionLaw.lean#L283)) and has nothing to act on for ζ: every pair of zeros of ζ satisfies (1 − Re ρ₁)·log(|Im ρ₁||Im ρ₂|) ≥ 1/58 (review 0911 §1.4, "no Siegel regime").
4. **It is where the structures of ζ must be used together.** Every proper subcollection of ζ's structures has a model with a finite nonempty off-line layer, and the Euler product together with the exact Γ_ℝ functional equation determines ζ (§4.4). B13 is therefore a statement about one object, with no model on which a proof can be tested.
5. **It turns receivers into proofs.** Under Landing, RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ([`zetaLandingLaw_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L145) lists eleven forms of Landing). In the finite stratum, a positive count, a thick-set bounded lag, or infinitely many subexponential lag readings on a positive-measure set of bases each gives RH (§3).

## 7. Open obstacles that are not just work

**T1. B13 itself.** The admission test for a proof, from §4: it must (i) use the Euler product with nonnegative logarithm and the exact Γ_ℝ functional equation together — every proper subset has a finite-layer model; (ii) be floor-free — the layer may sit at any depth δ ∈ (0, 8999/24000], so a mechanism that excludes zeros only beyond a positive depth, or that has any finite-stratum model, proves nothing ([`hasFiniteStratumModel_windowFloor`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/FloorFreeArithmetic/FloorsOnWindowsOfOneLengthHaveModels.lean#L182), FloorFreeArithmetic `HasFiniteStratumModel`); (iii) be neither asymptotic nor of bounded height ([`not_isAsymptotic_deuringHeilbronn`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L189), [`not_isLocalBelow_deuringHeilbronn`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L208)); (iv) use something the finite stratum lacks: Lindelöf, quasi-RH, ZAL, a finite Krein index and an explicit formula with finitely many off-line terms all hold there.

**T2. One quartet (E-Q6).** The smallest case is finite-dimensional — a 4 × 4 secular determinant, two rungs, one real wave in ψ — and it is the first place where Λ ≥ 0 and the exact functional equation must meet in a finite computation. Two coordinate systems are ready: ECS (§3) and CCM parity (B13-J3). Neither has an arithmetic input that a quartet violates. The models with a single quartet (two-sided edits, over-regular Beurling, positive twins) show that the input must be the joint one of T1 ([`singleQuartet_realized_in_models`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/SingleQuartetSecularAndWallRigidity.lean#L1908)).

**T3. Connes' step 1.** By B13-J2, step 1 at all large apertures excludes every finite layer except near-coincident odd-relation tops. Step 1 is a sign statement about ground states at growing aperture (family R); the corpus contains receivers that turn properties of ground states into RH ([CCMFullGroundZeroSideEigen](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCM/CCMFullGroundZeroSideEigen.lean), [CCMSymmetricDecreasingGroundStateRH](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCM/CCMSymmetricDecreasingGroundStateRH.lean)) but no proof of step 1 and no proved relation between step 1 and RH.

**T4. An independent upper bound for the filtered prime current.** The goal-loop architecture detects the top zero ρ by a prime filter and needs an unconditional bound of the same filter below the rate a(Re ρ − σ)/2 of its mode. The proved bounds stop at a(1 − σ)/2 minus a subexponential saving (Vinogradov–Korobov); the open centred cofactor cancellation (card 1-in-progress/b13-centred-cofactor-cancellation) would have to close the fixed gap a(1 − Re ρ)/2. *Reading:* such a bound does not use the finiteness of the layer; for a layer at depth δ it is a zero-free half-plane Re s > ½ + δ, and because δ is unknown it must hold at every depth, which is RH-strength cancellation. The architecture is a producer of family P; it is not a B13-specific route.

**T5. Stage 4 of ECS.** An identity of the critical state in which Λ ≥ 0 enters with a definite sign (E-O3 of the ECS report). Its finite-layer version is the ECS form of T1.

**T6. The extreme Deuring–Heilbronn law** ¬AAL ⇒ FOL. It would identify AAL with B13 ([`zetaExtremeDeuringHeilbronn_tfae`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L285)). It fails on integer-norm Euler products ([`oneOff_euler_product_extremeDeuringHeilbronn_not_linear`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnQuestionForZeta.lean#L463)); a proof needs the functional equation.

**T7. A unit-gap mechanism.** B13 ⇔ (FOL ⇒ κ(K_ζ) ≤ 1), because the index is even ([`negIndex_zetaKernel_le_one_iff`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HeckeKreinToeplitz/TheFiniteIndexStratumOfZetaIsLindelofDeep.lean#L75)). The Eisenstein constant term is the template: its only source of negativity is the pole, which has index one, and conjugation then excludes nonreal off-line zeros ([`lineLaw`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/InfiniteRankPurityTheory/TheEisensteinConstantTermIsPureByParity.lean#L197)). For K_ζ the negativity is carried by the primes, not by a rank-one source.

**T8. The model gap.** For a compact surface with exceptional real zero set A, the quotient Z(s+ih)Z(s−ih)/Z(s)² has nonnegative Euler logarithmic coefficients and an exact shifted Selberg functional equation. Its off-line zeros are A ± ih and its off-line poles are A. Thus these two structures permit finite nonreal layers for meromorphic functions. Requiring holomorphy throughout the open strip is still open. [Lean theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CompactHyperbolicSurfaces/TheSymmetricShiftOfTheSelbergEulerProduct.lean)

## 8. Missed opportunities: connections that exist in Lean and are not made

### 8.1 CCM parity is a B13 theorem in all but name

[`exists_aperture_ground_odd`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCM/CCMParityTopLayerNoGo.lean#L451) and [`not_finiteTopLayer_of_eventually_ground_not_odd`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCM/CCMParityTopLayerNoGo.lean#L566) prove: under a finite off-line set whose top layer has only even integer relations among its ordinates — every single quartet, every layer with ℚ-independent top ordinates — the CCM restricted Weil form has only reflection-odd ground states at arbitrarily large apertures; the parity even alternates ([`exists_aperture_ground_odd_of_even_relations`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/CCM/CCMParityTopLayerNoGo.lean#L550); *card*: `ground_parity_alternates_of_even_relations`). The finite layer is supplied for ζ by [`exists_finiteTopLayer_of_finite_offLine`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheWidthIsTheGroundEnergyRateWhenOffLineZerosDoNotCluster.lean#L282). The B13 literature of the corpus (frontier map, review 1611, ELT, ECS reports, the B13 cards) does not cite these modules; the killed-routes ledger records them as KR-CCP, a kill of the even-ground selector. Read as a B13 statement (B13-J2): **step 1 ⇒ B13 except for near-coincident odd-relation top layers**. This is the only implication in the corpus from a statement not equivalent to RH-on-the-finite-stratum to (most of) B13. It moves the question "is there an arithmetic reason for B13?" to "is there an arithmetic reason for even ground parity at large aperture?", and it isolates an explicit finite-dimensional residue (B13-J6).

### 8.2 The metric form is the weakest crude step

ZetaMetricTopDetection proves that under ¬AAL the lag exceeds every sub-top exponential eventually at almost every base, and that RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ a positive-measure set of bases with infinitely many subexponential readings. Since FOL gives ZAL, this is a form of B13 (B13-J1) weaker than the thick-set form [`b13_iff_thick_lag_bounded`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/InfiniteRankWeilTheory/TheCrudeStepAndTheCount.lean#L242) used by IRW, ECS and the review-1611 programme. A crude step in the finite stratum therefore only needs **infinitely many small readings on a positive-measure set of bases**, not bounds on long runs of levels.

### 8.3 Every proved finite-exception theorem has real exceptions, and ξ has none

In the corpus, finite exceptional sets that survive are real: the exceptional eigenvalues of a compact surface ([`exists_selbergZeta_small_eigenvalue`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CompactHyperbolicSurfaces/EntireSelbergZeta.lean#L264)), the at-most-one exceptional zero of the Eisenstein constant term ([`lineLaw`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/InfiniteRankPurityTheory/TheEisensteinConstantTermIsPureByParity.lean#L197)), the real zero of a quadratic L-function in Deuring–Heilbronn ([`exists_deuring_heilbronn_repulsion`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDeuringHeilbronnRepulsionLaw.lean#L283)). In each case a self-adjoint or index-one structure forces the exceptional parameter to be real. ξ is positive on the real axis ([`xi_real_pos`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/XiGlobalKummerSquareClasses.lean#L32)), so a finite layer of ζ consists of nonreal quartets. *Reading:* B13 is a statement about **nonreal** exceptions, and the mechanisms that handle exceptional zeros elsewhere act only on real ones. Two consequences: (a) the compact Selberg zeta is not a model against a B13 argument that uses non-reality, and its Euler logarithm has the opposite sign to ζ's (Z = Π(1 − N^(−s−k)) has nonpositive logarithm; 1/Z carries the exceptional points as poles, i.e. positive secondary main terms, not as zeros); (b) a "reality lever" — a structure under which off-line parameters of ζ must be real — would prove B13 with [`xi_real_pos`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/XiGlobalKummerSquareClasses.lean#L32). The ECS ladder already converts the nonreal right zeros into real critical ratios ([`criticalLadder_iff_rightZeros`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/UnderAFiniteLayerTheLadderHasOneRungPerRightZero.lean#L31)); what it lacks is a reason for the ratios not to exist.

### 8.4 Heat monodromy and the single quartet

B13-J4 connects the forward monodromy group of the heat flow (Galois theory of zeros) to the finite stratum through the right limit of the nonreal count ([`eventually_nonrealZeroCount_eq_of_finite`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/DeBruijnNonrealCountRightLimit.lean#L184)). The Galois-theory route to B13 by parity (review 1411 fable, Z1) closes if G₊ is the full group, as expected; the assembly records exactly what it would have given.

### 8.5 What finite certificates can and cannot do

A finite off-line set is refuted at an explicit aperture ([`finiteOffLine_visible`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/FiniteOffLineSetsAreVisible.lean#L454)), and certified fixed-aperture floors exist (log 2, 1881/5000; review 1611 J7). *Reading:* a certified floor at aperture A excludes exactly the layer shapes whose visibility aperture is at most A — layers that are deep, low and isolated — and never decides B13 (refutation is finite, confirmation is not). A quantitative "excluded region" for single quartets would follow by combining the two; it is bookkeeping, not progress on B13.

### 8.6 The B13 goal loop answered a different question

The ~35 formalized `b13-*` cards build the detection side completely and the Vaughan decomposition of the filtered current to exponentially small error. The finiteness of the layer is used only to isolate modes (detection); the upper bound never uses it, and by T4 it cannot use it without becoming the explicit formula. *Reading:* the loop is a producer programme of family P at the depth of the layer; its open centred-cancellation step is a zero-free-region estimate, and its natural home is the producer obstacles O1–O5 of review 1611, not B13.

## 9. A research programme for B13: Finite-Stratum Arithmetic (FSA)

Proposed folder `FiniteStratumArithmetic/`, umbrella `FiniteStratumArithmetic.lean`, namespace `GaloisForLFunctions.FiniteStratumArithmetic`. Inputs: ExceptionalLayerTheory (atoms, Deuring–Heilbronn, filters), InfiniteRankWeilTheory (towers, metric detection), ExclusionOfTheCriticalState (finite-layer Krein structure, single quartet), RHTower/CCM (parity), HeckeKreinToeplitz and ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri (index), GaloisTheoryOfZeros and RHTower/Heat (monodromy, right limit), WorldsOfTheZeroConfiguration, FloorFreeArithmetic, ArithmeticPositiveDuality and EulerFETransportTheory (models and rigidity), CompactHyperbolicSurfaces and InfiniteRankPurityTheory (calibrations).

### 9.1 Motivation

The finite stratum is the world in which only finitely many zeros of ζ lie off the line. It satisfies ZAL, Lindelöf and a zero-free half-plane; its Weil form is a Pontryagin space of finite index; its prime error is a finite wave plus a square-root remainder; its critical states are finitely many and solve a finite secular equation. Every structure of ζ taken without the others has a model inside it. FSA is the theory of this world: it proves the exact laws every finite layer must obey, in all coordinates at once, and it looks for the one law that the joint structure of ζ — Euler product with nonnegative logarithm and the exact Γ_ℝ functional equation — violates. It is floor-free by construction: every statement is uniform in the depth, the height and the number of quartets of the layer.

### 9.3 Language

- **Finite stratum**: FOL. **Layer**: its off-line set. **Top layer**, **depth**, **height**, **exceptional rank**.
- **Wave law**: ψ minus its layer wave is O(√x·log²x). **Empty wave**: RH.
- **Even / odd relation**: an integer relation among top ordinates with even / odd coefficient sum.
- **Rung**: a critical ratio of the confined form; **isolator**: a combination of rungs that sees one left pair.
- **Soft law**: holds for every tempered reflection-symmetric configuration with a finite layer. **Joint law**: uses Euler product and exact Γ_ℝ functional equation together. **Separator**: a joint law violated by every finite layer.
- **Real lever**: a structure forcing off-line parameters to be real. **Unit gap**: an index bound κ ≤ 1, which is κ = 0 by parity.
- **Floor**: the least depth a mechanism excludes; **floor-free**: floor 0. **Finite-stratum model**: a model of a mechanism with a finite nonempty layer (FloorFreeArithmetic).

### 9.4 Mechanisms (proved and transferable)

1. **Finite subtraction.** The explicit formula with finitely many off-line terms and a square-root remainder, in both directions (von Koch and one-sided Landau; [`abs_chebyshevPsi_sub_add_offLineWave_le`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FiniteOffLineVonKoch.lean#L569), [`finite_offLine_iff_exists_offLineWave`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/FiniteOffLineConverse.lean#L507)).
2. **Vandermonde extraction.** Finitely many readings (heights, damping scales, levels, apertures) recover each top multiplicity; Kronecker returns on the aperture or height line (local filters, metric detection, CCM returns).
3. **Index counting.** Every Krein-type form of ζ has index = number of right zeros; hyperbolic pairs have signature (1, 1); the index is even.
4. **Birman–Schwinger reduction.** A critical state of a finite layer is a kernel vector of a finite secular matrix built from the on-line resolvent.
5. **Parity on the aperture line.** Even floor, odd test, parity balance antisymmetric under the half-turn; positive somewhere iff positive at arbitrarily large apertures.
6. **Heat right limit and monodromy.** N(t) is right-continuous at 0 on the finite stratum; a proper G₊ forces 8 | N(t).
7. **Rigidity.** Beurling systems with Λ ≥ 0 and the Γ_ℝ functional equation up to an L² or decaying defect are ζ.

### 9.5 Pillar theorems

**Proved (inherited, cited above).**

- P1 (atoms). RH = ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing; AAL = B13 ∧ extreme DH.
- P2 (depth). The finite stratum is Lindelöf-deep and quasi-RH.

- P4 (wave). FOL ⇔ a finite wave law; one quartet ⇔ one real wave.
- P5 (detection). A finite layer is seen in every window, at almost every base, by prime-only filters, at a finite aperture.

- P7 (one quartet). Secular determinant, two rungs, Bessel trace bound, single-wave law.
- P8 (parity). Under an even-relation top layer every ground state is odd at arbitrarily large apertures.

### 9.9 Place in the corpus

FSA owns B13 and the finite stratum. ECS owns the critical state (B13 is its stage 2), IRW owns towers and the crude step, ELT owns the interaction of a top layer with an infinite remainder (AAL and Deuring–Heilbronn), WZC and FFA own models and floors, CCM owns the restricted Weil form, GTZ owns the heat monodromy. FSA imports their finite-stratum statements and states them in one language; it does not duplicate their receivers.

### 9.10 Big picture

The corpus has turned B13 from "finitely many exceptions" into a statement about one object with no models: every structure of ζ but the joint one admits a finite layer, the joint one admits only ζ. In that position, three kinds of argument remain:

1. **A sign law on a finite-dimensional object** — the parity of the restricted Weil ground state. It already excludes every finite layer with even top relations, conditional on Connes' step 1, and leaves a finite-dimensional residue (FSA-2). This is the nearest approach to B13 in the corpus.
2. **A reality lever** — in every proved finite-exception theorem the exceptions are real, and ξ has no real zeros. A structure of ζ that forces off-line parameters to be real would finish B13 (FSA-4).
3. **A joint identity** — Λ ≥ 0 inside the exact functional equation (ECS stage 4, FSA-1 for one quartet).

The prime-filter estimates, the thick-set crude counts and the Deuring–Heilbronn repulsions are, on the finite stratum, producers or detectors; they belong to the other atoms and to family P.

## 10. Summary

- **What B13 is:** finitely many off-line zeros force none; the index of Weil's form is 0 or ∞; a finite wave in ψ(x) − x with square-root remainder is empty.
- **Why it matters:** it is an atom of RH, the minimal counterexample shape, the Siegel-zero problem of ζ for nonreal exceptions, and the place where the Euler product and the exact functional equation must be used together.
- **Answered:** about twenty equivalent forms; the finite stratum is Lindelöf-deep; detection is complete; every proper subset of ζ's structures has a finite-layer model and the joint structure is rigid; one quartet reduces to a 4 × 4 secular problem and a single real wave; step 1 of Connes' programme excludes every layer with even top relations; nine approaches are closed (§4.5).
- **Just work:** B13-J1 (metric form), B13-J2 (step 1 ⇒ B13 up to odd relations), B13-J3 (step 1 excludes one quartet), B13-J4 (heat parity of quartets), B13-J5 (spectral model row), B13-J6 (the odd-relation residue, first case).
- **Not just work:** B13; one quartet; step 1; an independent filter bound (a producer); stage 4; extreme DH; a unit-gap mechanism; the model gap.
- **Missed connections:** the CCM parity modules are a B13 theorem in all but name; the metric form is the weakest crude step; all proved finite-exception theorems have real exceptions and ξ has none; heat monodromy meets the single quartet; the B13 goal loop is a producer programme.
