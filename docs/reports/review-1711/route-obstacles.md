# Routes to a formal proof of RH

The shortest *statement* of a missing theorem depends on the route. For MC it is cancellation of actual Möbius coefficients. For ACS it is an arithmetic construction paying a reflection defect. For ECS it is an arithmetic property impossible for the ground state. These give different choices of what to prove next. Translating a bound on zero width through all three gives no improvement in width.

This register covers 39 live mechanisms and 12 candidates. A receiver is a Lean implication whose input suffices for RH. **Work** means a deduction, specialization or calculation with identified inputs. **Open** means the corpus supplies no solution to the stated mathematical step. A finite subproblem may itself be open. Findings refer to Lean source, including working-tree statements. No new build or dependency audit is part of these grades.
