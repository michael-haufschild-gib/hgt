# Which routes share a mathematical problem?

Two routes can both characterize RH while requiring entirely different mathematics on the prime side. This ledger identifies the quantity held in common, the direction of each bridge and the input still required. The three wall families are arithmetic cancellation, production of a sign, and uniform exclusion of exceptions. [Wall ledger](walls-ledger.md) · [individual route statements](route-obstacles.md)

## Where assembling routes can pay

**A source theorem can enter late.** An independent count for the integer spline probes could prove their full Weil sign directly; via ACS, HF and ECS no construction of a global self-adjoint flow would then be necessary. Fixed-ratio Green bounds can check the proposed count's normalization. A true cubic-curve count supplies a positive control and the golden datum a negative control. The Kummer approximation actually converges to a square term containing D, so a count matching that square alone solves the wrong problem. [Tate count criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/CountingHolography/TheTateLimitOfACount.lean) · [KummerActualZetaComparison](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/Intersection/KummerActualZetaComparison.lean) · [CurveCriticalStatesAndStepanovExclusion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Models/CurveCriticalStatesAndStepanovExclusion.lean)

**A stratum can be removed once for several routes.** A single-wave exclusion from the prime side would prohibit a two-dimensional negative packet space without estimating arbitrary packet spaces. Conversely an index dichotomy could forbid the wave. This is a smaller, falsifiable theorem with several exact receivers. A sequence of exclusions for every *fixed* finite dimension would settle B13; the remaining infinite-layer problem would still require ZAL and landing, or a direct width/sign argument. [Quartet-exclusion dichotomy](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/SingleQuartetSecularAndWallRigidity.lean#L2275) · [Packet-index formulation of B13](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticCastelnuovoSeveri/TheFiniteStratumIsThePacketIndexDichotomy.lean) · [Three-part RH criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L68)

**A tempting false identification is especially costly.** Under a single quartet, the bare index is two. The same hypothetical state has state-weighted tail matrices of arbitrarily large inertia. One kernel evaluates the logarithmic derivative of ξ; the other tests a signed state tail on polynomials. No compression, positive congruence or domination theorem identifies them. Such a transfer, if derived from arithmetic and with the correct direction of inequality, would be new mathematics.
