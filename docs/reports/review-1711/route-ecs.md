## 1. Summary

### 1.1 What the route is

Weil's quadratic form, restricted to real profiles F on the half-line u < 0 and confined by the weight e^(−8u), is

  Q_R(F) = B(F) + a(R)·W₈(F),  W₈(F) = ∫F(u)²e^(−8u)du,

with B Weil's bare form (Gamma part − prime part + pole part) and a(R) a constant divided by R ([`criticalAliasCoefficient`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/TheCriticalFormHasAGammaLiminf.lean#L37)). The window threshold W₀ is the infimum of the thresholds of the windows [−A, 0] ([`windowThreshold`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/TheCriticalThresholdOfAWindow.lean#L52)); RH holds exactly when every window threshold is infinite ([`riemannHypothesis_iff_windowThreshold_eq_top`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/TheCriticalThresholdOfAWindow.lean#L88)). Off RH, W₀ is finite and Q_(W₀) has a unit null vector F, the **critical state** ([`riemannHypothesis_or_exists_criticalHalfLineGroundState`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/HalfLinePolarThresholdAndGroundStateOrthogonality.lean#L339); definition [`IsCriticalHalfLineGroundState`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/HalfLinePolarThresholdAndGroundStateOrthogonality.lean#L328)). The state

- is unique up to sign ([`criticalHalfLineGroundState_unique_up_to_sign`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/TheCriticalHalfLineStateIsUniqueUpToSign.lean#L67));
- has a transform LF(w) = ∫F(u)e^(wu)du with no zero on Re w ≥ 0 ([`criticalHalfLineGroundState_closedRightHalfPlaneLaplace_ne_zero`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/CausalReflection/TheCriticalHalfLineStateHasNoBoundaryLaplaceZero.lean#L126));
- solves the weak equation Q_(W₀)(F, G) = 0 for every G of the energy domain H ([`criticalHalfLineMixedEnergy_groundState_eq_zero`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/TheCriticalHalfLineStateSatisfiesTheWeakEquation.lean#L136); domain [`CriticalHalfLineEnergyDomain`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/TheCriticalHalfLineFormHasItsActualDomain.lean#L23));
- satisfies |F(u)| ≤ C e^(βu) for every β < 15/2 ([`criticalHalfLineGroundState_exponential_bounds`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/CausalReflection/ConfiningMinimizerExponentialBarriers.lean#L243)), and a(W₀) > 0 ([`criticalState_zeroNorm_limit_neg`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheZeroValuesOfTheStateAreANegativeVectorOfTheZeroPairing.lean#L783)).

RH is the statement that this one function does not exist.

### 1.2 How it is supposed to work

The programme proves every exact property of F as a theorem and looks for one property that contradicts the arithmetic of ζ while failing for models with off-line zeros (*readme*). F is a null vector, so every relation it satisfies is an identity and no constant is lost between steps. The proved picture:

1. **Prime coordinates.** Tested on the probes e_p(u) = e^(pu)·1_(u<0), which lie in H for p > 4, the weak equation is a closed formula in transforms of F: a·LF(p−8) + Gamma pairing − LF(p)·ΣΛ(n)n^(−½−p) − TF(p) + pole terms = 0, where TF collects the backward prime translates ([`criticalState_probeEquation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateEquationOnProbes.lean#L118)).
2. **Residue transport.** Replacing ΣΛ(n)n^(−½−p) by −ζ'/ζ(½+p) continues the equation in p and moves the poles of ζ'/ζ eight units to the left. LF continues to a meromorphic function on ℂ whose poles are exactly the points ρ − 17/2 for the nontrivial zeros ρ, with residue −m_ρ·LF(ρ−½)/a, nonzero when Re ρ ≥ ½ ([`riemannHypothesis_or_criticalState_meromorphic`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheTransformOfTheStateIsMeromorphicInThePlane.lean#L441), [`differentiableAt_stateContinuation_iff`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheTransformOfTheStateHasPolesAtTheZeros.lean#L193)).
3. **Three regions.** Right of the strip the equation is in prime coordinates. On the strip the Gamma pairing is the functional equation in Lévy form ([`levyGamma_eq_reflected_euler`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheGammaPairingIsTheFunctionalEquationOnTheStrip.lean#L504)). Left of the strip the Euler parts cancel, and a·LF(w) is minus the archimedean head, the prime heads and the two charges at w + 8 ([`stateMeromorphic_eq_left`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheTransformOfTheStateIsMeromorphicInThePlane.lean#L350)).
4. **Zero coordinates.** The values c_z = LF(z) at the centred zeros z = ρ − ½ are square-summable ([`criticalState_summable_norm_sq_zeroValues`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheZeroPairingOfTheStateConvergesAbsolutely.lean#L101)), and the state is their Cauchy transform: (★) a·LF(w) = −Σ_z LF(z)/(w + 8 − z), absolutely convergent at every point of Re w > −4 ([`criticalState_hasSum_starEquation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateIsTheCauchyTransformOfItsZeroValues.lean#L160)) and, for the continuation, off the poles ([`criticalState_hasSum_stateMeromorphic`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateIsTheCauchyTransformOfItsZeroValues.lean#L177)). Evaluated at the zeros, (★) is a closed linear system for the zero values ([`criticalState_hasSum_zeroValues_cauchy`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateIsTheCauchyTransformOfItsZeroValues.lean#L195)), and Σ_(on-line) |LF(iγ)|² + Σ_(off-line) LF(z)LF(−z) = −a∫F²e^(−8u) with no hypothesis ([`criticalState_zeroParseval`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheZeroPairingOfTheStateConvergesAbsolutely.lean#L180)).
5. **One series.** As distributions on the line, Σ_z LF(z)e^(−zu) is −a·e^(−8u)F(u) for u < 0 and a·F⁺(u) for u > 0, where F⁺ is the prime side of the state ([`criticalState_hasSum_heated_zeroSeries`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheZeroSeriesIsTheStateLeftOfTheWallAndThePrimeSideBeyond.lean#L363), [`criticalState_hasSum_zeroSeries_test`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheExplicitFormulaHoldsAtTheStateAgainstSmoothTests.lean#L312)). Almost everywhere on x > 0, −a·F⁺(x) is the Lévy head of F, minus the charge LF(½)e^(−x/2), plus the remainder of the prime number theorem averaged by F: Σ_n Λ(n)n^(−½)F(x − log n) − ∫_(y>x) F(x−y)e^(y/2)dy ([`beyondWallProfile_pntForm`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Wall/TheProfileBeyondTheWallInPrimeForm.lean#L229)).
6. **The decay law.** For κ ≥ 0, every centred zero has Re z ≤ κ exactly when ∫F²e^(−bu) < ∞ for every b < 16 − 2κ ([`forall_re_centredZero_le_iff_integrable_sq_mul_exp`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateDecaysExactlyAtTheRateOfTheZeros.lean#L313)); the supremum of the finite moments is 17 − 2Θ, Θ the supremum of the real parts of the zeros ([`isLUB_integrable_sq_mul_exp`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateDecaysExactlyAtTheRateOfTheZeros.lean#L362)).
7. **The ladder.** Some confined Gram matrix has n negative squares exactly when ζ has at least n zeros right of the line ([`confinedIndex_law`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/TheConfinedFormCountsTheZerosRightOfTheLine.lean#L236)), and the critical state is the first rung of a ladder of W₈-orthogonal null vectors of the confined form, one for each zero right of the line ([`exists_criticalLadder`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/TheCriticalStatesFormAnOrthogonalLadder.lean#L787)).
8. **The wall law.** The translate τ_kF(u) = F(u + k) is a profile of H, and its full form against G is the pairing of F⁺ on (0, k) with G(· − k) plus a confinement term ([`criticalState_mixedEnergy_translate`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/ThePrimeSideBeyondTheWallIsTheDefectOfTheTranslatedState.lean#L406)). Against F itself: ∫_0^k F⁺(x)F(x−k)dx = −(1 − e^(−8k))∫F(u)F(u+k)e^(−8u)du ([`criticalState_beyondWallPairing_translate`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/ThePrimeSideBeyondTheWallIsTheDefectOfTheTranslatedState.lean#L434)). At the wall this becomes the law of the wall: the terminal mean of F times its logarithmic moment tends to 16a∫F²e^(−8u), and the terminal mean has one sign ([`criticalState_tendsto_terminalMean_mul_wallLogMoment`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateHasOneSignAtTheWallInTheMean.lean#L731), [`criticalState_exists_sign_at_wall`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheStateHasOneSignAtTheWallInTheMean.lean#L813)).

The route closes when one exact property of F contradicts a property of ζ that the models with off-line zeros lack.

### 1.3 Where it ends

The last step has a Lean criterion. For a profile F of H, the beyond-the-wall prime-pole balance

  Φ_F(p) = ŜF(p) + LF(−½)/(p + ½),  ŜF(p) = Σ_n Λ(n) n^(p−½) ∫_(−log n)^0 F(u)e^(pu)du,

is holomorphic on Re p < −½. The property "Φ_F continues holomorphically to Re p < 0 for every F in H" ([`ZetaBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheBeyondTheWallPrimeBalanceExcludesTheState.lean#L268))

- excludes the state ([`no_criticalState_of_beyondTheWallPrimeContinuation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheBeyondTheWallPrimeBalanceExcludesTheState.lean#L399)) and is equivalent to RH ([`zetaBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation_iff_riemannHypothesis`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheBeyondTheWallPrimeBalanceExcludesTheState.lean#L452));
- fails for the state's own balance ([`criticalState_not_hasBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheBeyondTheWallPrimeBalanceExcludesTheState.lean#L380)), because every state sees a zero with 0 < Re ρ < ½ ([`criticalState_exists_seen_left_zetaZero`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Zeros/TheCriticalStateSeesALeftZero.lean#L1878));
- is sharp: for every δ > 0, continuation to Re p < δ fails already for the probe e_5 ([`not_forall_wallPrimeBalance_continues_past_zero`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheBeyondTheWallPrimeBalanceExcludesTheState.lean#L563));
- fails on the prime-two deformations, on positive Euler twins that agree with ζ through any prefix, and on curve data with a non-unitary Frobenius root ([`primeTwo_not_modelBeyondTheWallPrimeContinuation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Models/BeyondTheWallContinuationFailsOnTheModels.lean#L551), [`exists_positive_euler_twin_failing_beyondTheWallContinuation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Models/BeyondTheWallContinuationFailsOnTheModels.lean#L601), [`curveCriticalState_not_beyondWallContinuation`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Models/CurveCriticalStatesAndStepanovExclusion.lean#L1524)), and holds for the cubic Kummer curves, where the state is excluded by a theorem ([`cubicKummerDatum_beyondWallContinuation_and_no_criticalState`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Models/CurveCriticalStatesAndStepanovExclusion.lean#L1837)).

*Reading.* Φ_F is the Laplace transform of the right-of-wall wave of step 5, and the polar term is the continuum main term of the prime number theorem ([`polarWallTerm_eq_continuumMainTerm`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheBeyondTheWallPrimeBalanceExcludesTheState.lean#L217)). Continuation to Re p < 0 for every F is the bound ψ(x) − x = O(x^(½+ε)) in F-averaged form. The state is the profile at which this average is worst: it sees every zero on or right of the line. Stage 5 is RH in the form of the prime number theorem with square-root remainder, averaged by one function.

### 2.17 What the proved layer says

*Reading.*

- Every proved property is compatible with the existence of the state. Each is a theorem about a hypothetical object.
- The state is an explicit object in three coordinate systems. In time: a null vector of Q_(W₀) with exact decay rate 17 − 2Θ. In transform: a meromorphic function with the zeros as poles. In zero coordinates: a square-summable vector c with a·c_(z') = −Σ_z c_z/(z' + 8 − z) and negative Krein norm Σ_z c_z c_(−z) = −a∫F²e^(−8u).
- The soft part is closed. Zero pairing, (★), Parseval, the index law, the ladder, the collar, the mode expansions and the decay law hold for the state of every tempered symmetric configuration with a finite off-line layer, and model states exist in Lean (§2.15). A finite layer with these identities does not force the absence of states ([`soft_finiteLayer_does_not_force_zero_states`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/FiniteLayerKreinStructureAndRigidity.lean#L889)).
- Arithmetic enters at five places: the Euler product through ζ'/ζ (residue transport); ζ ≠ 0 on (½, 1) (sign law, continuation along the real axis); Λ(n) in the prime heads and in F⁺ (criterion, wall law); the zero-free half-plane Re s > 20999/24000 (unconditional decay rates); the sublogarithmic size of ζ'/ζ right of the zeros (decay law).
- Every decay result is a ruler: rate b gives the zero-free half-plane Re s > (17 − b)/2, and conversely. Decay moves the width, not RH.
- The function-field calibration is complete: for curves the state exists exactly when a root is not unitary, and both proofs of the curve RH available in Lean exclude it, one through the sign of the bare form (Hodge index), one through a square-root bound of the traces at all degrees (Stepanov), which is the stage-5 continuation.
- The two inputs that no model has together are Λ ≥ 0 and the exact functional equation. Stage 2 and stage 4 must use both, and no identity of the state uses both with a sign.

### E-O4. The decay side (off the RH path)

The decay law is proved (§2.7). Open:

- **(a)** the endpoint moment b = 17 − 2Θ when Θ is not attained;
- **(b)** pointwise decay of the state at the rate of the zeros: almost-everywhere bounds Ce^(βu) hold for β < 15/2 ([`criticalHalfLineGroundState_exponential_bounds`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ArithmeticHodgeTheoryOfDegenerations/CausalReflection/ConfiningMinimizerExponentialBarriers.lean#L243)), and beyond 15/2 only the averaged state is controlled ([`criticalState_averaged_decay_beyond_barrier`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheAveragedStateDecaysAtTheRateOfTheZeros.lean#L701));
- **(c)** the shifted zero pairing on 7/2 − Θ ≤ c < 17/2 − 2Θ;
- **(d)** the unheated growth law of F⁺: on the lines left of the zeros the multiplier −ζ'/ζ(½ − p) of the state equation gains no logarithm, and the continuation is known in L²(dt/log) only ([`criticalState_integral_norm_sq_stateMeromorphic_line_le_seed`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheZeroCauchyTransformIsSquareIntegrableUpToALogarithm.lean#L892)); the rate of the zeros is known for the heated profile ([`criticalState_norm_heatedBeyondWall_le`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Modes/TheHeatedProfileBeyondTheWallGrowsAtTheRateOfTheZeros.lean#L169));
- **(e)** an L² mode expansion modulo a finite layer.

*Reading.* The five items stop at the same place: one logarithm. The Hilbert inequality on the zeros holds with the weight 1/log, and a cluster of N zeros loses a factor of order N, so the zero count alone cannot remove the weight (*readme*). Removing it needs regularity of the state (E-T7) or a spacing property of the zeros. All five are rulers: a rate is a zero-free half-plane ([`criticalState_not_integrable_of_zero`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheDecayOfTheStateReadsTheZeros.lean#L316)), and levers that are valid on the multiplicative twins of one prime move no seed s ≥ Θ ([`wall_eq_seed_of_zetaTwins`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExponentDynamicsOfTheCriticalStrip/TheMultiplicativeTwinsRealizeEveryAbscissaAboveTheZeros.lean#L125)). Solving them moves no wall.

### E-O6. The growth of the index

With infinitely many zeros right of the line the number of critical ratios below R is finite for each R ([`exists_bound_orthonormal_nonpositive`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/TheConfinedFormHasFiniteIndexAtEveryRatio.lean#L98)) and unbounded ([`confinedIndex_law`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Index/TheConfinedFormCountsTheZerosRightOfTheLine.lean#L236)); its growth in R is not known. Bounds exist for the first rung ([`criticalAliasCoefficient_le_offLineWeight`](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExclusionOfTheCriticalState/Pick/TheOffLineZerosBoundTheCriticalRatioFromBelow.lean#L226)) and, as work, for the sum of the rungs of a finite layer (E-J13); for an infinite layer the off-line weight diverges and gives nothing. *Reading:* a growth law is a density statement for the zeros off the line, read in the ratio; it is off the RH path, and its bounded case is Lindelöf-deep.

## 9. The route in one paragraph

*Reading.* ECS has reduced RH to the non-existence of one function and made the reduction exact in three coordinate systems: a null vector with decay rate 17 − 2Θ, a meromorphic transform with the zeros as poles, and a square-summable vector of zero values with negative Krein norm that solves a Cauchy system. Stages 0 and 1 are theorems, the collar is decided, and the soft part is closed: model states exist, and a finite layer with every soft identity does not exclude them. What is open is arithmetic. Stage 2 is B13 in six forms, Lindelöf-deep, and its smallest case is one quartet (E-Q6). Stage 4 must use Λ ≥ 0 and the exact functional equation together, and the corpus proves that only ζ has both, so no model can test it. Stage 5 is the F-averaged prime number theorem with square-root remainder (E-O1); the route has no mechanism for it. The work that is ready is the pointwise edge law at the wall (E-J14), the sign law and the mean squares at the rate of the zeros (E-J11, E-J12), the ladder in Pick and trace form (E-J10, E-J13) and the prime-side rows of stage 2 (E-J15). The connections with the largest return are the window regularity theory (E-M10) and the cyclicity of the state combined with the wall law (E-M11).
