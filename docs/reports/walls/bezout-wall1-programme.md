### 0. Baseline: What We Have Already Built (and Why It Meets 3 of the 4 Wall 1 Criteria)
[Wall 1](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/docs/reports/wall1.md#L40-L47) states that any mechanism capable of breaking the square-root barrier must simultaneously:
1. **Fail for Beurling generalized primes** (use the exact additive structure of $\mathbb{N}$),
2. **Operate at the degenerate point** (trivial character, modulus $1$, no family averaging),
3. **Distinguish $\log 4$ from $\log 6$** (separate prime powers $p^a$ from squarefree composites $pq$, i.e. Wall 11),
4. **Carry cancellation across polynomial horizons $H \ll L_N = \operatorname{lcm}(1, \dots, N)$ at square-root precision.**

In Modules 1–63 we have already formalized exact algebraic machinery satisfying **(1), (2), and (3)**:
- **Beurling-falsifying additive identity:** $d \mid n \implies \gcd(d, n+1) = 1$ and the pronic weight $\frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}$ rely on the successor equation $(n+1) - n = 1$ in $\mathbb{Z}$, which fails in continuous Beurling systems ([`PronicBezoutSplitAndPrimePowerIndecomposability.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/PronicBezoutSplitAndPrimePowerIndecomposability.lean)).
- **Wall 11 ($\log 4$ vs. $\log 6$) separation at modulus $1$:** Under the non-periodic pronic weight $\frac{1}{M(M+1)}$, the discrepancy of a pure Selberg mode $q$ sums to $-\Lambda(q)$ (`-log 2` at $q = 4$, `0` at $q = 6$, proved in [`ShortHorizonPronicDiscrepancyAndVonMangoldtDuality.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/ShortHorizonPronicDiscrepancyAndVonMangoldtDuality.lean#L107-L135)), and under the compressed lag algebra $\widehat{T}_1^2 \ne \widehat{T}_2$ at $q = 4$ with revival defect $-\frac{1}{p-1} = -1$ whereas $\widehat{T}_1^2 = \widehat{T}_2$ on all squarefree modes $q = 6$ ([`NonMarkovianLagOperatorAlgebraAndPrimePowerRevival.lean`](file:///Users/michael/Documents/code/hgt/formal/GaloisForLFunctions/BezoutValuationIncidence/NonMarkovianLagOperatorAlgebraAndPrimePowerRevival.lean)).

### Step 3: Extract the $\frac{1-A}{2} \ge \frac{7}{24}$ Negative Drift from the Local Impulse Cross-Sum
- **Where we are:** After Step 2, the entire sign and drift of Bracket (3) is governed by the local impulse cross-sum
  $$-2\sum_{n=1}^{T-1} \operatorname{addDiff}(S_\varepsilon)(n+1)\,\operatorname{addDiff}(I_\varepsilon)(n)\,w_T(n)$$
  together with the non-positive interior gradient dissipation $-\sum_{n=1}^{T-1} (\operatorname{addDiff} I_\varepsilon(n))^2 w_T(n)$. - **Mathematical problem to solve:** Compute the exact harmonic drift rate of each lag-$r$ shell cross-correlation with the active shell $\{n+1, n+2\}$:
  $$\lim_{T \to \infty} \frac{1}{\log T}\sum_{n=1}^{T-1} \operatorname{addDiff}(S_\varepsilon)(n+1)\,\Delta^{(r)} I_\varepsilon(n)\,w_T(n),$$
  and prove that summing over all shells $r \ge 2$ (plus the interior gradient dissipation) produces the exact compensating drift $+\bigl(\frac{1}{2}v(\varepsilon) + \kappa(\varepsilon)\bigr)H_T^{\text{comp}} \sim \frac{1-A}{2}\log T$ required to cancel Brackets (1) and (2).
- **Roadblock to overcome (The Multi-Shift Chinese Remainder Barrier):** For a fixed lag $r \ge 2$, the correlation between divisors $d_1 \mid (n+1)$ and $d_2 \mid (n+r)$ depends on $\gcd(d_1, d_2) \mid (r - 1)$ (coprime only when $d_1, d_2$ are coprime to $r - 1$). Summing these correlations over all lags $1 \le r \le R$ is valid while $R$ is fixed as $T \to \infty$, **but** the remainder after $R$ shells still contains divisors $d > 2R$. Closing the sum over $r$ up to $d/2$ without leaving an uncontrolled tail is the exact point where local Bézout coprimality meets global sieve parity.
