# Walls ledger

Status: 2026-10-26, corpus `8765b382d` with the working tree of that day. Every obstacle in the corpus belongs to one of three core families:

- **Wall 1 — critical arithmetic sign.** Estimates, signs and oscillatory payment at the critical endpoint, at the gauge point `0`.
- **Wall 3 — transfer.** Readings, norms, completions, limits, shared budgets, localization and selection.
- **Wall 14 — realization and forcing.** Faithful arithmetic objects, correspondences and their local types, invariants, polarization, and the forcing of the actual source into its blind carrier.

**Organizing principle.** At an off-line reflection pair `{ρ, 1 − ρ̄}` the Rosati local form has signature `(1, 1)`, and every multiplicative correspondence — Hecke operators, the archimedean flow, every gauge twist — acts on it by a boost; on the critical line the same correspondences act by rotations; the successor acts unipotently; the functional equation acts by the reflection swap. RH says the whole zero spectrum is elliptic. Wall 1 measures the hyperbolic part as a rate, Wall 3 governs how positivity and estimates transfer between readings, localizations, limits and budgets, Wall 14 asks what positive structure forces the source to be elliptic. Seven exhaustion laws bound every mechanism tested:

- **Gauge.** Multiplicative data determine a source up to its orbit under the gauge torus `(ℝ/ℤ)^{primes}`; whatever they prove holds at Haar-almost every twist; the actual source is one point of the orbit (1.18, 3.1).
- **Hyperbolicity.** Positivity on a carrier invariant under a correspondence that is a boost at an off-line pair forces isotropy there: single-channel ideals are blind or half-blind, coupled point-stable carriers isotropic (14.10, 14.17).
- **Localization.** Positivity constrains a fibre only where the carrier localizes; non-localizing positivity holds for every prime weight (3.18).
- **Dominated twins.** On nonnegative tests the Weil form is antitone in the source; positivity there holds for every dominated twin of `ζ` (1.4, 1.18).
- **Linear loss.** A rate from an amplified observable needs a loss law `b_k = o(k)`; moments, family averages, tensor products and relative powers lose at least linearly (1.15, 1.17).
- **Pole order.** Pole order pays exactly for inserted zeros; real off-line pairs contract for free; a complex quartet contracts only against a real anchor, which is a boundary pole (the tax), a real zero or a collision; `ζ`'s own zeros, if paid, are paid by oscillation of the zero sum (1.16, 14.13, 14.19).
- **One variable (Landau–Fejér) [R].** A one-variable positive object sees its rightmost singularities through a real singularity that dominates them (Landau; the `2 : 1` real-anchor law), and a harmonic coupling weights a collided zero less than twice its pole (Fejér, `a₁ < 2a₀`). Boundary forcing, collars, contractions and couplings stop at these two laws; collisions of conjugate zeros exist only ordinate by ordinate, at conductor `log γ`, with the classical local zero count as budget (14.11, 14.19, 14.20).

Over `𝔽_q` one structure escapes all seven: a polarization of `C × C`, from Riemann–Roch and an ample class, makes Rosati positivity a theorem; hyperbolic localization by Frobenius polynomials then excludes off-line eigenvalues; and `Λ²H¹`, inside the cohomology of the surface, collides conjugate eigenvalues at no cost. Over `ℤ` the identities, the theorem of the square and the budget of Weil's argument are free, the receiver side is complete (one prime, one test, short cycles, one shift), and the polarization is missing (14.18).

## 6. What counts as crossing a wall

- **Wall 1.** Insert a newly proved source estimate into an existing receiver with every tail and quantifier intact. Because of face 1.0, any graded gain beyond Vinogradov–Korobov is a fixed strip. A new mechanism must contain a term that does not scale with its amplification (1.15, 1.17), must be gauge charged — read the sign of prime-power atoms or the pole, not only multiplicative data or nonnegative tests (1.4, 1.18) — and for ζ's own zeros must measure oscillatory payment, not pole charge (1.16). The Lefschetz part on short cycles at one prime (1.3) is the sharpest single target.
- **Wall 3.** Prove a transfer, sharp boundary or quantitative reconstruction on a stated class, norm and limit, and show that the source satisfies its hypothesis. For 3.14, selection below the zero spacing; a shorter window is graded progress. For 3.15, the explicit-formula split of the cofactor profile. For 3.16, a self-dual identity with the gap on the spectral side. For 3.17, a gauge-charged estimate at the point `0` beyond the threshold. For 3.18, a localizing carrier whose positivity is not induced by invariance.
- **Wall 14.** Construct the arithmetic object independently and prove fidelity at the strength needed, then a forcing law placing the source in the maximal blind ideal with input outside every mapped class: invariant (hyperbolic) carrier positivity (14.10, 14.17); non-localizing positivity (3.18); finite or height-coupled multiplicative positivity (14.11); linear rigidity in the coefficient measure (14.12); pole order, positive contraction and one-variable collisions (14.13, 14.19, 14.20); successor prescription (14.15); gauge-invariant data (1.18); identities, the theorem of the square and the budget (14.2, 14.16). The law must fail on the relaxation models, the complex fake and the Liouville twin, reproduce the κ = 1 collapse and `5d < 9κ`, and supply the sign layer: a polarization with a section count (14.18), an elliptic localizer (14.17), or a structure with fixed tensor loss (14.14). Blindness of a sub-carrier does not cross the wall; neither does a transfer from a zero-free region.
