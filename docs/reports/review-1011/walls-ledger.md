# Walls ledger — status quo

**Corpus checkpoint:** commit `1cec78e529` with its working tree (2026-11-10). Working log: `logs/review-1011-status-quo-facts.md`. Companions: [route-obstacles.md](route-obstacles.md), [route-unification-ledger.md](route-unification-ledger.md), [obstacles-summary.md](obstacles-summary.md).

Three wall families carry the frontier. **Σ** is the critical sign of the window form, **Π** the precision and production of the prime count, **Γ** the selection of ζ, the reflection of its zeros and their position in height. They meet in one minimal face, in one lattice and on one ladder.

- **The minimal face.** RH ⇔ `P(p)≤A(p)+K` at the primes of arbitrarily late logarithmic windows `|log p−T|≤32 log(T+2)`, outside at most `Ce^{δT}` primes per window [L*]; the same with the upper side alone, with `ℕ`-counts on the surviving prime rays, with local even moments under one exponent, and with the absolute value [L*]/[K]. At the level of configurations no shorter window decides, also at ζ's density [L*].
- **The lattice.** RH ⇔ exactly one positive square of the comb pairing on the two rulings and every finite family of packets; the negative index of the defect is the number of right zeros [L*]. Weights, constant, scale and tree lengths are forced; the sign has no margin [L*]/[L].
- **The ladder.** A system has a mass and a packet an aperture; the rung is their ratio in logarithms. Sizes prove the sign up to rung `1−o(1)` in the conductor and the height aspect, both one-structure fakes fail at `1+o(1)`, and one rate exchanges cancellation of the contacts, the rung and the depth of a zero: `γ=δ=1/(2κ)` [L*].
- **A level and a half-plane.** RH ⇔ positivity of the dressed lag aggregate at level one on the characters of all large primes [L*]. A half-plane is a power saving of one smoothed Möbius sum [L*]; `[X]` states one at `7/8` outside the native graph.

[Frontier](../rh-current-frontier-map.md): **38 live formulations, 12 candidates**. [Scoped obstructions](../rh-killed-routes.md) separate failed mechanisms from open replacements.

**Status.** **Solved** (the face is a theorem), **Partially solved**, **Sharpened** (open, exact form a theorem), **Open**, **No-go** (mechanism refuted), **Classified**, **Reduced** (theorem-level equivalent named), **Settled** (a method question answered). **Strength:** RH, ≥qRH, ≥B56, AAL, sub, indep., identity, method, producer. **Evidence:** `[L*]` Lean statement read for this ledger (working log §5); `[L]`, `[K]`, `[S]` as graded by the frontier map and the scoped-obstruction ledger at the checkpoint, not re-read; `[D]` deduction; `[C]` classical; `[A]` assessment; `[X]` the external half-plane theorem, cached, unchecked, outside the native import graph.
