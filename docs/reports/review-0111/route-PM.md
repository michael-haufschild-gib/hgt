PM has two branches, the Gauss–Mayer branch and the energy and escape branch. Already proved for the Gauss–Mayer branch: the regularized determinant D = A + ζB, the nonvanishing and holomorphy of D(w, −1), the cone for real w, and the coding of hyperbolic SL₂(ℤ) classes by Gauss words.

The Gauss–Mayer branch:

1. The Mayer–Selberg trace identity. This is known mathematics; the remaining work is formalization. Assemble the multiplicities of oriented and unoriented primitive geodesics into the trace and product identity, so that D(w, ±1) becomes the Selberg zeta function and scattering determinant of SL₂(ℤ).
2. Faithful identification. Show that the zeros of ζ with ½ < Re w < 1 are exactly the events where R(w) has eigenvalue ±1, located in the scattering sector of the Mayer determinant.
3. A Krein structure near Re w = 1, sub-RH. Put the Lewis–Zagier, or Lax–Phillips, Hermitian structure on the eigenfunctions of R(w), including the scattering sector. Its first test is to reprove the classical zero-free region by transfer-operator positivity. That would show the realization actually moves zeros, which no realization in the corpus does yet.
4. Separating the two kinds of events, sub-RH. The Krein form must tell cusp-form events on the line from scattering events just left of it. Cone contraction alone provably cannot do this.
5. Pushing into the strip, ≥qRH. Carry the Krein positivity from near Re w = 1 to a fixed zero-free strip. The tool would be a quantitative spectral gap of the transfer operator on the scattering sector, of Dolgopyat type.
6. Closing step. Exclude off-line resonances. This is partner saturation of the resonant Eisenstein states, the PM form of Frobenius at infinity, and it is equivalent to RH.

The energy and escape branch:

- a. The escape bridge, sub-RH. Show that a diverging receiver energy produces a row obeying the Euler laws at the primes 2 and 3, with an error of finite energy. The proved zero detector then turns escape into a statement about zeros of ζ.
- b. Gauge mass, sub-RH. Show that some escape state, at some damping below ½, has positive mass on the gauge torus.
- c. Exterior tightness, ≥qRH. Exclude energy escaping to infinite height, which is the unattained-width case.
- d. Closing step. Bound the complete energy cofinally as ε ↓ 0. Equivalently, show positive gauge mass for every actual subcritical cofactor limit. This is equivalent to RH.
