# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 12 candidates are faces of three core problems: **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection, reflection and position). Their RH-level endpoints meet in one object with six exact forms, on one scale, at one door.

- **One inequality per prime.** Width `≤σ` ⇔ the prime trace is `≥−Kp^σ` on the primes of cofinal logarithmic windows, off power-thin exceptions; equivalently the upper side; for two fixed primes a bound at their difference lags on windows of radius `r√T` [F].
- **One matrix at finite scale.** The block matrix of every window is its degrees plus a neutral part; the neutral part has negative off-diagonal entries, the jets and the floor-map contacts `Λ(k)/k`. RH ⇔ on every window two disjointly supported nonnegative packets, one of negative energy, satisfy `N(x,x)N(y,y)≤N(x,y)²` ⇔ every bound state has one sign ⇔ the Doob chain of the window has gap at least its binding energy [L*].
- **One recursion.** A window is its dyadic middle and two ends. It holds one bound state iff its two overlapping parts do and the conditional coupling of the ends is a contraction between their conditional energies; RH ⇔ the contraction on every window. Parts of ratio `≤ρ` give ratio `≤ρ²/2` [L*]; [D1].
- **One margin.** RH ⇔ the window matrices have a bounded margin ⇔ one explicit two-end family is bounded below. The rate of the two-end family is the zero width; a margin `K^θ` of the matrices, `θ<½`, gives the strip of width `θ`; the unconditional exponent is `½` [L*].
- **One path.** The index of the restricted Weil form at an aperture is the number of crossings below it; the crossing multiplicity is a Lagrangian intersection number [L*].
- **One cover.** The zeros of the heat family form a cover of the time plane. Complex conjugation of the zeros over a positive real time is a forward monodromy; RH ⇔ it is the identity ⇔ no branch time is real and positive. The group has finite supports, is generated by turns, and its orbits are the classes of simple joins [L*].
- **One scale.** Saving `γ`, rung `κ`, depth `δ`, mollifier `θ`, level `σ`, signal exponent `b`, critical-comb growth, two-end rate, heat constant, relaxation rate: one number [L*]; [D]; the margin exponent of the matrices bounds it from above. `b=7/8` is `[X]`; the open part is `½<b<7/8`.

Transversal to the three cores:
- **Two worlds.** A failure of RH is world I (a finite isolated top layer) or world II (a tail), with top `≤7/8` [X]; RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing [F].
- **One critical object.** A failure of RH is a critical half-line state at a finite critical ratio: unique up to sign, outer, cyclic, stable, exponentially bounded [F]/[S]. On the cover it is a positive real branch time; on a window it is a second bound state, which changes sign; in the odd sector it is an odd bound state [L*].
- **One producer, with an end.** A completed sum over the twists of ζ over `ℚ(ζ₃)` gives `Re s>7/8` [X]. Its frame is a coupled tower with fixed point `3/5`, and `(4r−1)/(6r−1)≥3/5` at every period `6r` [L*].
- **One door.** Among countable systems with unit multiplicities, a decaying functional-equation defect forces the rational primes. Among positive real weights the defect can grow arbitrarily slowly with prescribed off-line zeros. Under `Θ+2β<2` a signed grouped edit of the primes counts below the square root with a pair alone on top [L*].

**The common input [D].** The small primes and the jets are where the three cores meet. U-Σ: the contacts of 2 and 3 hold every window of ratio three and four together, and they do so only jointly with the jets: the contacts alone have zero gap and zero curvature. U-Π: the producer is a sum of cubic and sextic Gauss sums over `ℚ(ζ₃)`, whose wall is a Grössencharacter `L`-function at `6s−3`. U-Γ: the door of world I is unit multiplicity at every prime together with the reflection. In receiver form two primes suffice (two-prime lags, two heat grids at `log 2` and `log 3`); in producer form the binding has rank proportional to the block size. Sampling is cheap, binding is not.

**Why there are three cores [D].** No test is compact on both sides of the explicit formula. A compact prime side (U-Σ) leaves infinitely many zero modes and only an exact sign decides. A localized zero side (U-Π) lets a crude bound decide and leaves the prime side at square-root precision. U-Γ is the involution `ρ↦1−ρ̄`, the selection of ζ among systems with its local structures, and the position of its zeros. The scale interpolates between the first two; conjugation of the zeros is a monodromy of one cover.

## 0. Unifying statements

- **One inequality per prime [F].** The minimal face of SRT; two primes at radius `r√T`; every radius `o(log T)` has a countermodel (log §9).
- **One resolution [L*].** Floors at every bounded length give AAL; one-sided floors of one length have models (log §17).
- **One matrix [L*].** Hodge inequality on positive packets without common blocks ⇔ one-sign bound states ⇔ RH; certified: one bound state with second eigenvalue `≥1/5000` for `50J≤53R`; first step packets of ratio four and seven; thin packets at every ratio (log §10, §12; [D4]).
- **One chain [L*].** For a symmetric matrix with nonpositive off-diagonal entries and a positive eigenvector: index `≤1` ⇔ Poincaré inequality of its Doob chain with constant the binding energy. RH ⇔ this on every window; unconditionally the gap is at most the binding energy plus a constant (log §5).
- **One recursion [L*].** The overlap law and its RH form (log §12). At the first step both conditional energies are `⪰1/5000` [D10].
- **One binding [L*]/[KR].** Rank-one gluing iff block size one; no rank-`r` model for `600(r+1)≤H`; two bound states with weakened or twisted contacts; zero gap and zero curvature of the contacts alone; on components the chain energy is the jet energy (log §4, §5, §10, §11).
- **One margin, one exponent [L*].** Bounded margin ⇔ RH (forms, matrices, initial windows, step packets); exponent `θ<½` ⇒ width `≤θ`; unconditional `−C√K`; a positive margin is incompatible with RH (log §5, §11).
- **One family [L*].** `N(o_v)=2Re S(0)−2Re S(v)`: the two-end family of the window matrices is the lag structure of the zeros. Bounded below ⇔ RH; rate `e^{θv}` ⇔ width `≤θ` (log §5).
- **One index [L*].** Index law in the aperture and in the energy; crossings of the aperture conormals with the graph of Weil's operator (log §16).
- **One index for number rings [L*].** The neutral form of a number ring has the index of its zeta function plus one; the index of a difference of tempered configurations counts zeros and poles off the line; every Artin Euler product is a product of integer powers of Hecke `L`-series, with reciprocity for linear characters (log §7, §17).
- **One selection [KR].** Constant, weights and contacts are forced; the full-positive cone is `{Λ}` under RH and empty otherwise.
- **One self-pairing law [L*].** A completion of the couplings of a window with at most one negative square has self-pairings of logarithmic size on one of every two separated runs; nonnegative self-pairings give two positive squares; the dyadic completion attains the rate (log §11).
- **One scale [L*]; [D].** Isolated depth `δ` shows at rung `κ` with `2δκ>1`; signal exponent = abscissa; critical-comb growth = width; two-end rate = width `≤` margin exponent; rate `δ` ⇔ strip `1−2δ`; `δ²/(200 log γ)≤Λ_dBN≤w²/2`.
- **Two worlds, three atoms [F].** RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ AAL ⇔ ZAL ∧ B13 ∧ Landing; B13 ⇔ supremal negative index `0` or `∞`.
- **One freedom [KR]/[L*].** Over the rational primes with multiplicities `0,1,2` and a count rate: every finite layer; below the square root under `Θ+2β<2`. With positive real weights: prescribed off-line zeros with a defect below any unbounded function.
- **One rigidity [L*].** Unit multiplicities with a decaying defect are the rational primes; real-weighted fakes on the integers have an Abel floor; a fake edit has a non-decaying defect with energy bounded below.
- **One critical curve, one critical state [F].** RH, or a finite-`W` ground state, charged or polar-orthogonal; the energy branch is attained, unique, strictly concave; the state is outer, cyclic and stable; trial sublevels are compact [L*].
- **One cover [L*].** Transport over forward times; finite supports; generation by turns; conjugation as a monodromy; orbits as simple-join classes; RH ⇔ no positive real branch time. Pair preservation of the whole group is false [S].
- **One horizon [L*].** Strip `1−2δ` ⇔ ringdown rate `δ`; pulse rate = horocycle rate; `λ₁≥3/16` on congruence groups, attained in weight `½`.
- **One source [X]; [L*].** Two estimates give `Re s>7/8`; the frame of every `6r`-periodic pairing has fixed point `≥3/5`.
- **One field [L*]/[KR].** `K_even⊆ℝ` ⇔ RH; the Jensen equations have the full signed-permutation group or their moments are dependent.
- **Law and point [D].** One pointwise theorem past Vinogradov–Korobov (`7/8`, [X]); everything else on the zeros of ξ is in law: positive proportions, runs of simple zeros, logarithmic Chowla, short-interval mean squares.
- **One dominance [L*].** The Goss Riemann hypothesis: roots of strictly decreasing norms.
