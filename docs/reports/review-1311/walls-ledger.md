# Walls ledger — status quo

**Corpus checkpoint:** `065c78c2b5` (2026-11-13), working-tree sources included; the frontier map at the same checkpoint. Fact log: `logs/review-1311-frontier-facts.md` (cited as "log §n"). Companions: [route-obstacles.md](route-obstacles.md), [route-unification-ledger.md](route-unification-ledger.md), [obstacles-summary.md](obstacles-summary.md).

Three wall families carry the frontier. **Σ** is the critical sign of the window form, **Π** the precision and production of the prime count, **Γ** the selection of ζ, the reflection of its zeros and their position. They meet in one inequality per prime, one finite-scale matrix with one recursion on its ratio, one cover of the time plane, and one scale.

- **The minimal face.** Width `≤σ` ⇔ the prime trace is `≥−Kp^σ` on cofinal logarithmic windows after `≤Ce^{δT}` omissions; the upper side alone is equivalent; two fixed primes on difference lags within `r√T` suffice. No window below the logarithmic radius decides [F] (log §9).
- **The finite-scale matrix.** RH ⇔ the Hodge inequality `N(x,x)N(y,y)≤N(x,y)²` for disjoint nonnegative packets with `N(x,x)<0` on every window ⇔ every bound state has one sign [L*] (log §10). Certified: one bound state for `50J≤53R` with floor `1/5000`; first step packets of ratio four and seven; thin step packets at every ratio [L*] (log §12).
- **The recursion.** A window with a dyadic middle holds one bound state iff its two overlapping parts do and the conditional coupling of the ends is a contraction between their conditional energies; RH ⇔ this contraction on every window [L*] (log §12). Parts of ratio `≤ρ` give ratio `≤ρ²/2` [D1]. At the first step the conditional energies are `⪰1/5000` [D10]; the coupling bound is open (B194).
- **Binding.** Every fixed-rank model of the coupling of two halves fails beyond block size one; weakened or Liouville-twisted contacts give two bound states; the contacts alone have zero gap and zero curvature; the jets enter at the binding scale [L*]/[KR] (log §4, §10, §11).
- **The margin and the exponent.** RH ⇔ a bounded margin of the window matrices ⇔ a bounded two-end family; a margin or step-packet floor of size `K^θ`, `θ<½`, gives the strip of width `θ`, and the rate `e^{θv}` of the two-end family is that strip; the unconditional floor is `−C√K` [L*] (log §5, §11). The open part is every `θ<½` (B195); whether the width gives the floor on all packets is open (B198).
- **The path.** The index of the restricted Weil form is the number of crossings of the aperture path [L*] (log §16).
- **The critical object.** RH, or a finite critical ratio with a critical half-line state: unique up to sign, outer, cyclic, stable, exponentially bounded [F]/[S] (log §11). Its exclusion is B104.
- **The cover.** RH ⇔ no positive real branch time of the heat cover ⇔ the conjugation monodromy is trivial at every positive real non-branch time. The forward monodromy group has finite supports, is generated by turns, and its orbits are the classes of simple joins [L*] (log §6, §11).
- **The scale.** Saving, rung, depth, mollifier exponent, level, signal exponent, critical-comb growth, two-end rate, heat constant, relaxation rate: one number [D]; the margin exponent of the window matrices bounds it from above [L*]. `b=7/8` is `[X]`; the open part is `½<b<7/8`.
- **The producer.** One class with an exact end: every `6r`-periodic Gauss pairing has its coupled fixed point at `(4r−1)/(6r−1)≥3/5` [L*] (log §10).
- **The door of world I.** Among unit multiplicities a decaying defect forces the rational primes; among positive real weights defects grow arbitrarily slowly with prescribed off-line zeros; under `Θ+2β<2` a signed grouped edit counts below the square root with a pair alone on top [L*] (log §8, §10).
