# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 9 candidates are faces of three core problems, **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection and reflection). Their RH-level endpoints meet at the **arithmetic Hardy inequality** `P≤H` on every window, equivalently the **band** `|𝒞_m(f)|≤W(f)` of a test and its mirror. U-Σ reads it as a form on the window, U-Π on the prime side, U-Γ on the zero side.

Corpus checkpoint: commit `8c88f0f9a6` plus the working tree of 2026-11-06. Evidence: `[L]` Lean source; `[S]` unfinished or uncommitted source; `[D]` deduction; `[N]` numerical. Scope and source locators: [frontier map](../rh-current-frontier-map.md), [scoped obstructions](../rh-killed-routes.md).

**Equivalence through RH transfers truth, not mechanism.** Only an explicit source identity or a proved transport moves an estimate between routes. A shared endpoint is not a shared proof.

## 0. Unifying statements

- **One inner factor [D].** On `Re s>½`, `(s−1)ζ(s)/s` has inner factor `I_ζ`, the Blaschke product of right zeros. RH ⇔ `I_ζ=1`. BSY reads its mass at 1. `N(ρ)≤Πϱ` reads it at a zero [S]. Krein–Langer index reads its degree. Schur parameters and `r(a)` peel it. Carleson separation is its regularity.
- **One scalar flow [D from L].** `r′=(1−r²)q−2r`, `(log K)′=2(1+r²)/(1−r²)`. Lattice Schur parameters are its discrete form. Contact is the only way the tower can fail.
- **One passive medium [D].** Reflectors at `½log n`, strength from `Λ(n)/√n`, archimedean background. Window sign says the echo train is passive. RW, PA, PTR, CHMD and SC read the same requirement.
- **One Laguerre form [D].** `Ξ′²−ΞΞ″` and its depth-`a` version: TH current as `a→0`, PTR at `h`, heat collision exclusion on `H_t`, `J`-level Turán for LA. Each route's source is a different object, and only the limit form is common.
- **One Eisenstein carrier [D from L].** TH's spectrum is `Σ_Nτ_b(N)A(N;a,b)` with `τ_b(N)K_{ib}(2πNe^r)`: the Fourier expansion of `E*(iy,½+ib)`. PM's Mayer carrier is the same family. Edge positivity by Maass–Selberg is shared; off-axis positivity is open on both.
- **One width axis [L/D].** Floor rate `2σ`, damping rate, abscissa `2Θ`, gauge exponent, stability line `σ`, long-moment slope `2w`, PTR `h`, TH `a`, heat coefficient `≥cd²`. Interior values are zero-free strips. All unconditional savings sit at Vinogradov–Korobov [L].
- **One finite-rung rule [L].** Each fixed window, rank, degree, row and entry follows from verified zeros, with explicit transfers. Limit pencils at a fixed critical ratio are rungs too [D]. Rungs measure verified height.
- **One budget [L].** A floor `−C_σe^{2σr}∫F²` for every `σ>0`, or one constant at all apertures, implies RH. All sign routes need only this.
- **One blind form [L].** RH is prime-blind positivity. Blind energy is `(1/π)∫|𝓜f|²(θ′+1−Re ζ(½+it))`.
- **One cone [L].** `𝒜(c)` pins every weight to `Λ(n)` within `Ce^{Kn−c/1104}`. `λ*(c)` is below a doubly exponential ceiling, above `(1/20000)/2^k` at the tower. RH ⇔ `λ*→0`. The datum of ζ is the critical point of the family.
- **One format [L].** On every window both sectors are represented exactly: neutral signed theta gauge (even), shifted ground state (odd). Gauge freedom acts only in the pole direction.
- **One head [L].** Dirichlet-shift companions cancel every tail; what remains of a long window is its head of length `log d`.
- **One frustration [L].** Even sector past `log ρ`, in every gauge. Actual odd source whenever a negative prime reflection acts. Trivial zeros are paid at the centre.
- **One end of size [L].** `H<0` by half-width `21/40`; edge law crosses one in `(½log 5,½log 6]`; templates end at spread 6.
- **Positive mimic = window sign [L].** Below contact, sampling atoms are a positive on-line mimic of ζ's image. ZCG's mimic hypothesis and RW's sign are one statement.
- **One-sided signs [L].** BSY `I≥0`, PM `D_s≤0`, TT `D+Q/2≥0`: each proved sign has RH as its opposite. What is missing is a second expression.
- **Sign → location [L].** Level `a` gives an exponential corridor; `λL_T`, `λ>1`, gives `e^{−c log³T}`. Both are receivers.
- **Two cluster walls [D].** MZF's obstruction is a local fraction `≳1/8` of comparably deep zeros; NGF's quadratic depth law is `≍log X` zeros at distance `≍d`. Different thresholds, same local-excess shape. Both vanish at typical heights.
- **Repulsion is Diophantine and sign-free [L/D].** Finite carriers repel by Baker; a.e. value repels; `0∉E` bears on no real part.
