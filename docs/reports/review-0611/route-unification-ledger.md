# Route unification ledger — status quo

The 38 live formulations and 9 candidates are faces of three core problems, **U-Σ** (sign), **U-Π** (precision and production) and **U-Γ** (selection and reflection). Their RH-level endpoints meet at the **arithmetic Hardy inequality** `P≤H` on every window, equivalently the **band** `|𝒞_m(f)|≤W(f)` of a test and its mirror. U-Σ reads it as a form on the window, U-Π on the prime side, U-Γ on the zero side. Transversal to the three, RH splits into two halves: **ZAL**, read by height-local receivers, and **AAL**, read by sparse receivers and recurrence.

Corpus checkpoint: commit `a079423f8d` plus the working tree of 2026-11-07. Evidence: `[L]` Lean source; `[L]` unfinished, unintegrated or uncommitted; `[D]` deduction; `[N]` numerical; `[C]` classical. Scope and source locators: [frontier map](../rh-current-frontier-map.md), [scoped obstructions](../rh-killed-routes.md).

**Equivalence through RH transfers truth, not mechanism.** Only an explicit source identity or a proved transport moves an estimate between routes. A shared endpoint is not a shared proof. Section 3 separates the two.

- **One inner factor [L].** On `Re s>½`, `E` has inner factor the Blaschke product of right zeros. RH ⇔ `I(w)=0` at one point. BSY reads its mass at 1. `N(ρ)` reads it at a zero.
