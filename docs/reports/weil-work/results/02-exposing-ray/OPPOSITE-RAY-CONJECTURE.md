## Consequence to audit, not a replacement proof

For any existing global polar Weil-positive coefficient sequence lam, weak duality against the proposed opposite families would give exposingValue(lam)>=E after taking N to infinity. Existing exposure gives the reverse inequality, and equality forces lam(n)=vonMangoldt(n) for n>=2. Thus the candidate would impose cone uniqueness without proving cone nonemptiness. This is a substantive consequence that could kill the candidate by an existing nontrivial cone example; it is not supplied as an assumption.

Likewise, appending an opposite family to a below-threshold positive-ray family produces a zero-trace family after matching cutoffs and controlling slack. This is motivation for searching for a source construction, not a new conditional RH theorem to formalize. Ordinary zero-trace positivity remains unpaid.
