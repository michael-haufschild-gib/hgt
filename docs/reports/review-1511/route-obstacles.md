# RH routes: current mathematics and closure obstacles

The corpus has one seed — no zero of `ζ` or of any Dirichlet `L`-function with `Re s > 7/8` — and many exact readings of the zero width `w = Θ − ½`, of the number of zeros right of the line, and of the tail of the zero configuration. No unconditional RH closure exists in the corpus. Every route endpoint below needs a lever on `w`, a uniform exclusion (an atom), or a faithful realization that uses the Euler product and the functional equation of `ζ` together [MMD] [MMD_DIR] [THREEATOMS] [EULERWORLD] [COSH].

**Scope.** 38 route formulations, 12 candidates and the supporting programmes named in the frontier map. **Evidence.** Lean statements read in source at checkpoint `cf08f8dfc5` with the working tree of 15 Nov 2026; [W] marks untracked working-tree modules, [S] modules the frontier map tags unintegrated. Every cited module was located; the 112 declarations cited by the review-1411 reports were re-read and match their claims ([working log](../../../logs/review-1511-working-log.md)). No build, compile or axiom audit was run.

**A — work**: an application, normalization, finite certificate or classical argument with a known method. It never contains an unproved sign, rate or uniformity. **B — open**: no proof in the corpus and no known method; this does not certify the state of all mathematics. **Excluded**: a stated construction class has a proved obstruction. A dash in A means that no routine task moves the route.

## Shared frontier: three faces of one configuration

**Width.** `w ≤ 3/8` is the seed [MMD_STRIP]. It is read exactly by many rulers — the zero abscissa, Möbius prefixes at one height, one unit window of `ζM_X`, the zeros of `M_X`, the zeta-kernel signal, the two-end antisymmetric state, the Weil floor in the logarithmic energy, the neutral-window margin at polynomial block size, long Möbius moments, Weil-lag endpoints, prime-log windows, the regularized prime series [RULERS] [LOGENERGY] [MARGINRULER] [WD-LONG] [WL] [SRT] [PBM]. A lever proved on one ruler is a lever on all, and an improvement of a reading is an improvement of `w` [LEVERTRANSPORT] [MEASURING]. No lever for `ζ` is proved. The producer class of Möbius-signed Gauss pairings has wall `3/5`, also for mixed towers, composites and minima [GAUSS] [MIXED] [WALLGEN]. Levers valid on Euler worlds cannot bite on `(½, 2/3)`; levers valid on cosh models bite nowhere; the seed fails on prime edits with count `Ax + O(x^{14/15})` [EULERWORLD] [COSH] [EDIT].

**Index.** Some neutral window has `r` negative squares iff `r ≤ 1 + #(right zeros)` [INDEXLAW]; real packets attain the full index [INDEX]. Margins bounded below on all windows ⇔ RH [ACS]; an a priori gap on bound windows at each cutoff gives RH [GAP]. The two-prime window has a floor at `log 2` [FLOORLOG2] [S]; ratios `≤ 3` (`Q ≥ 2`) and `< 4` (`Q ≥ 3`) hold at every block size [OVERLAP] [LOGLADDER]. With no hypothesis every window of ratio `3K+1` has at most one eigenvalue below `−C(1+log K)²K^{3/8}(1+log R)` [MARGIN38].

**Tail.** AAL fails exactly at a finite top layer [AAL-LAYER]; RH ⇔ ZAL ∧ `FiniteOffLine → RH` ∧ Landing [THREEATOMS]. Fakes realize each atom's failure separately (ELT, FFA, WZC, compact Selberg); in world I an edit of the primes removes the top layer [WORLDI].
