# Binary Goldbach: killed routes and surviving results

**Lean baseline:** `aa34244d8b80a1d35a2bf646468a50c245d533fa`. No new retirements since `4c6ee6b23220`. [Live routes and candidates](goldbach-current-frontier-map.md).

| Retired mechanism | Obstruction | Surviving result |
|---|---|---|
| **Smooth balanced products as an ordinary short-interval sum** | At q = 2, X = 4j² and width √X, nonuniform multiplication fibres force a [squared smoothing error ≥ cX²](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueProfile.lean#L450). | The [exact signed pairing](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueProfile.lean#L406) separates the periodic profile from centered covariance. Bounding that covariance remains open. |
| **Subtract one fixed periodic profile and repeat the norm bound** | Equal-product collisions force the compensated squared norm divided by X² to diverge for growing H = o(X²) along multiples of the fixed modulus. [Paper obstruction](https://github.com/michael-haufschild-gib/hgt/blob/b1ae42543dd9e8e752e32a1d83f8f41877e43e65/docs/drafts/pipeline/5-failed/goldbach-compensated-product-collision-cost.md) | Lean [counts zero-shift collisions exactly](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/BalancedProductCollisions.lean#L335) and [bounds positive shifts by divisor mass](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/BalancedProductCollisions.lean#L196). The signed amplifier remains open. |
| **Reverse parity with a polylogarithmic family of fixed projective maps** | A non-affine fractional-linear map with integer coefficients and nonzero determinant Δ has at most 2τ(abs Δ) integral returns. At polynomial height this gives vanishing coverage of balanced rectangles with polynomial side lengths. Diagonal maps preserve the product. [Paper coverage argument](https://github.com/michael-haufschild-gib/hgt/blob/b1ae42543dd9e8e752e32a1d83f8f41877e43e65/docs/drafts/pipeline/5-failed/goldbach-projective-parity-returns.md) | Lean proves the finite cap with [sharpness for inversion](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProjectiveIntegralReturns.lean#L144) and the [family bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProjectiveIntegralReturns.lean#L177). An adaptive correspondence needs its own multiplicity bound. |

| Invalid shortcut | Exact boundary and surviving input |
|---|---|
| Negative Liouville signs, fixed roughness, or coprimality detect two primes. | Distinct [prime cubes](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ParityBoundary.lean#L88) are coprime composites with negative sign, and their least prime factors can exceed any fixed cutoff. The strict least-factor cube test detects primes on the required support. |
| Mark divisors of the complement to detect its primes. | A [proper-divisor mark vanishes at primes](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/twin-prime/DivisorMarkTransfer.lean#L26). Mark the predecessor or estimate the unmarked residual. |
| Freeze the affine slope and reuse the short modulus. | For a ≡ r mod m with m > 0 and b ≠ 0, ba−T has least positive period abs(bm). The required long-modulus estimate remains open. [Period theorem](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/AffineProgressionConductor.lean#L32) |
| RH boxed averages, positive damping, or Gram positivity force pointwise prime pairs. | [Boxed mass equals θ(x)²](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L159), damping includes composites, and the [odd Gram form](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/OddCharacterGram.lean#L98) measures diagonal minus reflected mass. DAMP and ODD require strict inequalities at a fixed target. |
| Symmetry or Hilbert–Schmidt class forces Li–Liu’s zero exclusion. | A rank-one model can have arbitrarily large normalized eigenvalue μ. The sharp single-factor bounds are [4μ²η(1−η) ≤ 1 for exclusion in Re z > 1−η](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/LiLiu/OperatorSpectralBounds.lean#L24), for 0 < η < 1/2, and [μ² ≤ 1 for critical-line zeros](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/SpectralSquareProduct.lean#L67). The [Hardy error bound under local GRH](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/LiLiu/Boundary/CorrelationForm.lean#L54) leaves a small-norm estimate for the arithmetic operator. |
| The complete product-residue profile gives the prime odd moment. | The profile’s [odd-character moments vanish](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueOddMoments.lean#L57). Its [additive Ramanujan moments](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueRamanujanMoments.lean#L21) retain nonunit fibres and give local main terms. |
