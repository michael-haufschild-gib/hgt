# Binary Goldbach: killed routes and surviving results

These are failures of **specific proof mechanisms**, not counterexamples to Goldbach. The first table records the project’s three retired Goldbach mechanisms; the second distinguishes invalid shortcuts from open routes. [Live routes and candidates](goldbach-current-frontier-map.md).

| Retired mechanism | What kills it | What survives |
|---|---|---|
| **Smooth the balanced product count as an ordinary short-interval sum.** | Complete multiplication fibres modulo q are not uniform. At q = 2 and window width √X the [actual squared smoothing error is already ≥ cX²](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueProfile.lean#L450), not o(X²). | The [exact signed pairing](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueProfile.lean#L406) separates the periodic profile from the covariance of the centered rows ([failed transfer](../../drafts/pipeline/5-failed/goldbach-balanced-product-smoothing.md)). Estimate that covariance, not the unsigned smoothing cost. |
| **Subtract one fixed periodic profile, then apply the same norm bound.** | After fixed-modulus subtraction, repeated factorizations with identical product still contribute on the diagonal. The [paper obstruction](../../drafts/pipeline/5-failed/goldbach-compensated-product-collision-cost.md) makes the residual squared norm divided by X² unbounded; it does not rule out signed cancellation. | Lean gives the [exact zero-shift collision count](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/BalancedProductCollisions.lean#L335) and a [divisor bound for positive shifts](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/BalancedProductCollisions.lean#L196). DIL retains the diagonal in its amplifier. |
| **Reverse parity using a small fixed family of rational projective maps.** | A non-affine integer fractional-linear map has at most 2τ(abs(det)) integral returns. At polynomial height, polylogarithmically many such maps cannot cover a positive fraction of both long integer intervals; the diagonal rescalings leave the product unchanged. | The [finite return cap and its sharp inversion case](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProjectiveIntegralReturns.lean#L144) extend to [families](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProjectiveIntegralReturns.lean#L177). The [asymptotic coverage argument](../../drafts/pipeline/5-failed/goldbach-projective-parity-returns.md) is on paper. An adaptive correspondence would need its own multiplicity bound. |

| Invalid shortcut | Boundary and useful replacement |
|---|---|
| Negative Liouville signs, roughness alone, or coprimality imply two primes. | A [prime cube](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ParityBoundary.lean#L88) has negative sign and is composite, even above arbitrarily large fixed cutoffs. Two distinct prime cubes are coprime as well. The strict least-factor cube test survives; sign arguments must keep that support. Likewise a prime plus an almost-prime is not yet a prime pair. |
| A mark on divisors of the complement can detect its prime values. | A [proper-divisor mark vanishes at every prime](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/twin-prime/DivisorMarkTransfer.lean#L26). Better estimates where the mark is nonzero cannot determine the signed mass it misses. The [Goldbach avoidance estimate](../../drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/goldbach-mark-avoidance-mass.md) is not a formalized asymptotic. Mark the predecessor instead, or bound the unmarked residual directly. |
| Freeze an affine slope and use the original short modulus. | From a ≡ r (mod m), the exact progression for ba−T has step bm, not m. The [period theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/AffineProgressionConductor.lean#L32) invalidates that invocation of Type I when the enlarged modulus is out of range. The long-modulus average is open, not disproved. |
| RH boxed averages, positive damping, or a positive Gram matrix force each additive coefficient to be positive. | [Boxed prime-pair mass is θ(x)²](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L159), not G(N). Positive damping also counts composites. The [odd Gram matrix](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/OddCharacterGram.lean#L98) measures diagonal **minus** reflected mass; its positivity is not the strict deficit needed for a pair. Keep N fixed and prove the DAMP or ODD inequality. |
| Reflection symmetry or Hilbert–Schmidt class forces Li–Liu’s spectral hypothesis. | Even one real eigenvalue μ = 2 gives a symmetric finite-rank model with off-line zeros: the [single-factor criterion](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/SpectralSquareProduct.lean#L67) is exactly μ² ≤ 1. This is not a counterexample for the arithmetic operator. The surviving task is an arithmetic norm/error bound, not another determinant identity. |
| The complete product-residue profile supplies the signed odd moment. | Its [odd-character moments vanish identically](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueOddMoments.lean#L57), even though its [additive Ramanujan moments](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ProductResidueRamanujanMoments.lean#L21) retain nonunit fibres. These are useful local main terms, not an estimate for the odd moment of the **prime** profile. |
