# Binary Goldbach: current proof frontier

**Open in the Lean corpus.** The target is every even integer N ≥ 4, including 4 = 2 + 2. Let G(N) be the sum of log p · log q over ordered **prime** pairs p + q = N. Lean proves [G(N) > 0 iff such a pair exists](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L82). The [diagonal correction](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/CoprimePrimePairCorrection.lean#L65) settles N = 2p for prime p. The remaining targets have composite N/2 and require two distinct primes. An eventual theorem must also cover its initial range; an almost-all theorem can leave exceptions anywhere. [Killed routes and survivors](goldbach-killed-routes.md).

## Live routes

A live route has a Lean theorem reducing **actual prime-pair existence** to its proposed test. A candidate below has relevant estimates but no such completed transfer for its proposed method. An equivalence proves neither side; the last column names the input that would change the verdict.

| Route | What Lean proves | Hard step still open |
|---|---|---|
| **PAR — sieve to the cubic threshold, then use Liouville parity** | Below the cube of its least prime factor, a number is prime iff its Liouville sign is −1. On the doubly rough support Lean gets the [exact count](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ParityBoundary.lean#L186): four times the number of prime pairs is R₀ − 2R₁ + R₂ (mass, one-point sign, two-point sign). | Make that combination **strictly positive** for each remaining target at a prime-detecting cutoff z of order ∛N. One sufficient estimate is R₁ < 0 on this support, since R₂ ≥ −R₀. Unsifted sign cancellation does not estimate this sum. |
| **DAMP — separate primes from composites by changing scale** | For the odd part of the squarefree damped divisor source, write O(N, ε) for its reflected overlap. A pair exists iff [16O(N, ε) − O(N, 2ε) > 0 for some ε > 0](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L971). The [normalized lower bound improves under halving](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L954) and tends to G(N). | Derive a strictly positive lower bound at a scale chosen for each N **without assuming a prime pair**. Convergence from below allows a zero limit. Positive mass at one damping can come entirely from composites. |
| **ODD — bound the odd Dirichlet-character moment (Li–Liu spectral approach)** | On reduced residues, [distinct prime pairs exist iff twice the odd-character moment is strictly below φ(N) times the diagonal prime mass](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/PrimePairCharacterMoment.lean#L58). For any supplied real ℓ² spectrum the [spectral product has only critical-line zeros iff its diagonal operator has norm ≤ 1](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/InfiniteSpectralSquareProduct.lean#L206). | Link the **actual N-dependent arithmetic residual operator** to the smoothed prime-pair error in Lean and prove its magnitude is smaller than the positive main term for every large even N. The [Li–Liu paper](../../goldbach-conditional.pdf) ([authors’ statement](https://mrc.sdu.edu.cn/english/info/1018/1514.htm)) uses Dirichlet GRH **plus** spectral zero exclusion (S), or the stronger critical-line condition (R). Neither the abstract norm equivalence nor GRH alone supplies this input. |

## Candidates: possible sources of the missing estimates

| Candidate | Foothold | Result needed |
|---|---|---|
| **DIL — prime dilations of balanced products** | Lean counts [nonunit modular rectangles with error](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ModularProductRectangleDecomposition.lean#L294) and keeps the [exact reflected-row conductor](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ReflectedProductConductor.lean#L176). The [off-diagonal dispersion bound for flat product counts](../../drafts/pipeline/2-fully-proven/goldbach/goldbach-flat-prime-dilation-dispersion.md) is on paper, not a Lean capstone. | Complete the signed amplifier, including its diagonal. Prove cancellation for the actual bilinear coefficients and marks against μ(N−mn), uniformly in N. A flat count has no such coefficients. |
| **MARK — mark the predecessor of the prime complement** | For n = N−p with p prime, a divisor mark on **n−1** can retain n prime. [Type I, the one-variable estimate](../../drafts/pipeline/2-fully-proven/goldbach/goldbach-predecessor-mark-type-i.md), is on paper; Lean [bounds the discarded square divisors in mean square](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/SquareDivisorTail.lean#L270). | Bound the [far-slope Type II covariance](../../drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/goldbach-transverse-mark-correlation.md) of two affine Möbius values, retaining squarefreeness and the congruence restrictions. Even at modulus one it is open; then average over the root moduli and obtain a negative signed mass on prime-detecting support. |
| **AFF — signed affine dispersion** | Lean gives the [exact period of a progression under an affine map](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/AffineProgressionConductor.lean#L32). It identifies why the proposed second Type I estimate does not apply. | [Repair the long-modulus estimate](../../drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/goldbach-signed-affine-dispersion-audit.md) with the slope retained, including semiprime cofactors and the signed term lost by premature Cauchy–Schwarz. The cited source is not a Lean proof. |
| **ARC — major and minor arcs for the prime sum** | The corpus has [major-arc prime-character estimates](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/PrimeExponentialSums/MajorArcs.lean#L251) and a [minor-arc von Mangoldt estimate](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/PrimeExponentialSums/VinogradovMinorArc.lean#L408). These concern one exponential sum. | For the prime log-weight sum S, show that the minor arcs’ contribution to the **Nth Fourier coefficient of S²** is smaller than the positive major-arc contribution for *each* even N. Preserve the additive phase and remove proper prime powers if using von Mangoldt weights. |

**Not extra routes.** The [RH boxed-mass equivalence](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L215) controls a sum over targets, not its smallest coefficient. Reflection-even/odd energies re-express G(N), not an estimate for it. A [subproduct congruent to −1, or confinement to an odd-character kernel](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/Characters/MaximalOddKernels.lean#L70), concerns multiplicative residues, not two actual prime representatives summing to N. The vanishing-damping prime detector belongs to DAMP; common-cofactor and reflected-root averages are inputs to the marked sieve, not separate proofs.
