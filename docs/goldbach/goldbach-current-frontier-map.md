# Binary Goldbach: current proof frontier

**Open for even N ≥ 4.** Lean identifies [prime-pair existence with G(N) > 0](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L82), where G(N) counts ordered prime pairs with log weights. The [diagonal term](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/CoprimePrimePairCorrection.lean#L65) covers N = 2p for prime p, including 4. Targets with composite N/2 require distinct primes. An eventual theorem leaves an initial range to verify.

**Lean baseline:** `69ddb4658356380b7c49e1d2e47de56930506661`. Since `6b6eb094c948`, ODD and ARC gained inputs. No new Goldbach routes, candidates, or retirements. [Killed mechanisms and survivors](goldbach-killed-routes.md).

## Live routes

| Route | What is established | Hard step still open |
|---|---|---|
| **PAR · Cubic roughness and parity** | On the doubly rough support below the least-prime-factor cube, Liouville sign −1 detects primes. [The exact prime-pair count is (R₀ − 2R₁ + R₂)/4](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/ParityBoundary.lean#L186), where the terms are mass, one-point sign, and two-point sign. | Prove strict positivity at a prime-detecting cutoff of order ∛N for each unresolved N. A sufficient estimate is R₁ < 0 on that support, since R₂ ≥ −R₀. |
| **DAMP · Two-scale damping** | For the odd squarefree damped divisor source’s reflected overlap O, existence is equivalent to [16O(N, ε) − O(N, 2ε) > 0 for some ε > 0](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L971). The [normalized lower bound increases under halving](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/RiemannHypothesisBridge.lean#L954) toward G(N). | Find a scale with a strictly positive lower bound for each unresolved N. A zero limit is compatible with the convergence, and a single overlap includes composite mass. |
| **ODD · Odd-character deficit and Li–Liu** | The [strict odd-character deficit](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/PrimePairCharacterMoment.lean#L58) detects distinct prime pairs. Lean verifies Li–Liu’s [weighted asymptotic](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/LiLiu/Conclusions/PrimePairs.lean#L75) and [central prime-pair lower bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/LiLiu/Conclusions/PrimePairs.lean#L94) under eventual Dirichlet GRH plus S(h) or R. The new [windowed Landau reduction](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/WindowedLandauTheory/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ZeroFreeAllOfTopG.lean#L27) reduces the GRH input to character host supply and connected families of windows. | Prove the strict deficit directly, or prove GRH and bound the arithmetic Hardy operator norm by o(𝔖(N)), where 𝔖 is the singular series. The windowed reduction still needs the uniform Selmer bound and connected families for every primitive character. S(h) supplies ‖Tₙ‖ ≤ 𝔖(N)/h(N) with h(N) → ∞. [Window obligations](../riemann-hypothesis/rh-current-frontier-map.md#wlt), [norm scale](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/goldbach/LiLiu/Spectral/SpectralScales.lean#L40). |
