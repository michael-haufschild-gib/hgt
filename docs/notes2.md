## Alternative endpoint
Source-FE covariance of a regular nonlinear evolution at a nonzero fixed clock is equivalent to RH

## big picture
Zeros are connected through the inverse geometry of ζ: following solutions of ζ(s)=w can link a nontrivial zero to a trivial zero, or connect nonadjacent zeros through a critical point.

For RH, the crucial distinction remains w=0. Moving roots off the critical line while w≠0 does not challenge RH. A useful proof mechanism would need to explain why the zero fibre is rigidly confined to that line.

## Question 1
The question is whether arithmetic restricts these connections strongly enough to distinguish the zero fibre from ordinary value fibres.

## Question 2
The question is whether the connection geometry reveals such a constraint—or shows why a proposed geometric argument cannot supply it.


## Observation
- Both Dirichlet L-functions show the same predecessor pattern in the tested first five positive zeros, but join it to different trivial zeros. That weakens the case for the pattern being a zeta-specific RH mechanism.

- Critical-line placement is generated by complementary branch symmetry, but zeta’s arithmetic height information is absent from this reduced radial source. A proof cannot stop at this layer; arithmetic selection must enter through angular pairing/global face coherence or a still-missing invariant coupling them. This kills “pointwise radial extinction” but exposes a stronger organizing fact: complementary symmetry alone generates an entire moving critical-line spectral family.
