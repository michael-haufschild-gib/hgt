# RH proof routes

**RH is open. 72 live mechanisms · 13 candidates.** [Live routes](#live-routes) · [Candidates](#route-candidates) · [Killed mechanisms and scoped obstructions](rh-killed-routes.md)

**Counting rule.** A live route has a defined arithmetic object, a specific construction or estimate to try, and a Lean theorem showing why success for that object implies RH. A candidate is missing the object, its identification with ζ, or the implication from its own data to an RH criterion. A conditional implication is not a proof of its premise. Count the proposed mathematical operation, not the module, equivalent criterion, test parity, or number of proved lemmas.

<a id="find-a-route"></a><a id="shared-frontier"></a><a id="finite-height"></a><a id="external-literature"></a>
**Common baseline.** In this corpus the [strict strip](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/MetaplecticMobiusDescent/Detector/SubSevenEighthsZeroFreeHalfPlane.lean) puts the supremum of zero real parts at most 20999/24000. The [coupled Bohman bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/VerifiedHeightRungs/HadamardBudget/TheVerifiedHeightOneHundredEightyFour.lean#L3522) puts every nontrivial zero through height **184** on the line by keeping the actual prime phases t log p at bandwidth L=11/2. [The fixed-band inequality reverses at 185](rh-killed-routes.md#bohman-fixed-band); this is a limit on the test, not on ζ. [Five equivalent tests for Θ≤5/6](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheFiveSixthsFacesOfTheZeroAbscissa.lean#L118): PSM and MC require exponent 5/6+ε; ECS and WD require at most 2/3 in the exponent; AAD needs every rate a<1/6. Only the [weaker bounds from the existing strip](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/SquareRootThreshold/TheFiveSixthsFacesOfTheZeroAbscissa.lean#L143) are unconditional. The [3/5 barrier](rh-killed-routes.md#cubic-tower-half-plane-barrier) limits a class of factorwise Gauss completions, not ζ. Finite height, fixed rank and one finite aperture do not settle the corresponding uniform problems.

## Boundaries

- **Dual descriptions are not extra mechanisms.** The [cut kernel](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RigidArithmeticOfCuts/TheKernelBeyondPowerLaws.lean) is the dual of CTF approximation. By contrast, AAD constructs integer floor-sum certificates, ALST constructs polynomial multiples, and AIF minimizes a holomorphic boundary moment. Each has its own witnesses and RH implication.
- **Prime-probe ranks have different payoffs.** Rank two follows from a negative witness. The [rank-three amplification law](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeDisplacementIndexAmplificationPayoff.lean#L157) would remove the lone-quartet alternative in [QIC](#qic), [PDR](#pdr) and [PPC](#ppc); it does not prove any prime-error estimate. [Positive orbit counts at every degree plus three Weil count tests](rh-killed-routes.md#prime-five-quartet) do not exclude a quartet in curve models. [All-rank amplification](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/ThePrimeDisplacementIndexAmplificationPayoff.lean#L236) is equivalent to finite-layer exclusion, not RH. [ZCG](#zcg) still needs approach to the line and [landing](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheLandingLawForZeta.lean#L145): if landing fails, [all right-zero depths form a descending sequence to zero](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/ExceptionalLayerTheory/TheDescendingStripLadderAndShallowZeroAnnihilators.lean#L183). Gaps between consecutive depths do not exclude that sequence.
