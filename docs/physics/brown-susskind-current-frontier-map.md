# Brown–Susskind · live proof routes

**The fixed-Hamiltonian second law remains open.** For every sufficiently long prefix of almost every Gaussian Pauli chain, low-cost returns recorded at unit time resolution have doubly exponential mean spacing, even starting at the first robust crossing $\sigma$. The proved exclusion interval immediately after $\sigma$ is singly exponential. Matching linear growth, exponential onset and the first post-crossing return on the predicted scale remain open. [Target](second-law.md) · [Killed routes](brown-susskind-killed-routes.md)

**Model.** Draw the three onsite and nine nearest-neighbour bond Pauli coefficients independently from the standard normal law, once on an infinite chain. On its open $K$-site prefix put $D=2^K$. Cost means minimum number of arbitrary two-qubit gates, no ancillas, projective operator-norm error $1/10$. All three claims must hold on the same typical draw. A lower bound witnessed on a state or in Choi distance can certify operator hardness. A return needs whole-operator control.

## Live routes

| Route | What the proof gives | Hard mathematical obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| **RAMP** · count gates touching active sites | Independent cuts give a short linear ramp on engineered chains and a lower ramp on a positive-measure Gaussian neighbourhood. On iid prefixes, isolated fields give the typical fixed-horizon floor above. [Explicit chain](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalLaboratoryComplexityUpper.lean) · [Gaussian neighbourhood](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/NearestNeighborGaussianSameDrawGrowthLaw.lean#L324) | A touched-site count saturates at $O(K)$. Certify repeated gate use on the same qubits for exponentially long times on typical draws. |
| **WORD** · certify minimum circuit length | A per-gate witness bounds word length. Geometric endpoint hardness plus an increment compiler gives a continuous ramp. Scalar energy shifts preserve distance to every circuit budget. For spectral width $\Delta E$ and cell half-width $h$, the mean certifies the whole cell with margin $h\Delta E/2$. The speed $\Delta E/2$ is sharp at zero gates. | **HIT** · gate-walk first entrance | A short preparation word contributes to first-entrance mass. Capacity retains the cost of the entire family, and dephasing gives a late-time transfer. [Capacity](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/CapacityEntropyBudget.lean#L68) · [Transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/AnnealedAccessibilityPlateau.lean#L97) | At ramp times, upper-bound entrance mass into the finite-error operator target sharply enough to exclude short preparations. |
| **LAG** · compare to the easy past | A Choi hit splits into a return near an earlier anchor or a large anchor-orthogonal overlap. [Choi split](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/LagResolvedStaticChoiShortcutDichotomyLaw.lean#L117) · [Endpoint cost](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/PersistentCircuitFloor.lean#L67) | Beyond fixed horizons, control both returns to affordable anchors and residual overlaps with every fresh circuit target. |
| **PHASE** · count short circuits on the spectral torus | For sufficiently long even Gaussian paths, affordable time in $[0,T]$ lies between $T/100^{D-1}$ and $(T+S_H)/M$ for every $s\le r$. From every $a\ge0$ on a typical Pauli chain, some $u\in[a,a+G_K]$ begins a window of width $W$ throughout which cost exceeds $r$. On even paths the maximum recurs on intervals and has positive lower frequency along each injective integer clock fixed before the draw. [Occupation](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PhysicalCircuitReturnDeadline.lean#L296) · [Maximum](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/AttainedOrbitCircuitMaximum.lean#L236) · [Prescribed clocks](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PrescribedClockCircuitOccupation.lean#L130) · [Dated excursions](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/DatedExcursion.lean#L36) | Find a hard visit by $\exp(O(K))$. | **INC** · count circuit targets actually met | Nonidentity Pauli tubes are avoided with high probability on prescribed polynomial windows in the Gaussian two-local model. For continuous circuits the replica span has an explicit rank bound, and a small enough return-excised incidence integral excludes all hits. [Pauli targets](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliContinuousLaboratoryReturnLaw.lean#L395) · [Continuous circuits](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ContinuousCircuitReturnExcisedFirstEntryLaw.lean#L369) | Bound that integral on the fixed-H trajectory for growing budgets and the whole forward window, including repeated visits. |
| **DES** · weight target moments | Optimal-design weights reduce forward exclusion to moment bounds for circuit targets. A draw-probability bound covers a bounded continuum. [Weighted reduction](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/StaticChoiOptimalDesignWeightedCrowdingFirstReturnLaw.lean#L373) · [Continuum](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/QuenchedBoundedTargetFirstEntryLaw.lean) | Prove the return-excised moments at replica orders large enough to pay for the full circuit family. |
| **CLOCK** · bound phase hitting times | Almost surely on every sufficiently long prefix, every interval of deterministic length $G_K$ visits every relative-phase box of radius $1/80$, including the identity box. The coefficient bound and summable failure probabilities put all these visits on the same draw. [Prefix filling theorem](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/PrefixPhaseHorizon.lean#L106) · [Identity visit](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/TimedReflection.lean#L58) | The all-labelings horizon has triple-exponential size in $K$, with $2^{2^{D-1}}\le G_K\le 2^{2^{512D^2}}$. [Finite-sample bounds](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/CircuitWindows/GaussianMeanReturns.lean#L101) | The additive error $S_H$ can absorb the first step.
