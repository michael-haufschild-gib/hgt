# Brown–Susskind · live proof routes

**The fixed-Hamiltonian second law remains open.** Almost surely, every sufficiently long prefix of one iid Gaussian Pauli chain reaches an exponential circuit threshold and stays hard for an exponential time around its first crossing. What is missing is a matching linear ramp to that crossing, its onset by exponential time, and a doubly exponential wait from there to the first polynomial-cost return. [Target](second-law.md) · [Killed routes](brown-susskind-killed-routes.md)

**Model.** Draw the three onsite and nine nearest-neighbour bond Pauli coefficients independently from the standard normal law, once on an infinite chain. On its open $K$-site prefix put $D=2^K$. Cost means minimum number of arbitrary two-qubit gates, no ancillas, projective operator-norm error $1/10$. All three claims must hold on the same typical draw. A lower bound witnessed on a state or in Choi distance can certify operator hardness. A return needs whole-operator control.

| Phase | Proved in Lean | Open at the target scale |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Almost surely on every sufficiently long prefix, cost is $\Theta(K)$ throughout a window of width $\Theta(1/K)$ around $\pi/\mathrm{diam}(H)$. For each fixed $T>0$ it is at least $c(T)K$ throughout $[A(T)/K,T]$. [Matching microscopic bounds](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPauliEarlyLateCircuitLaw.lean#L100) · [Fixed-horizon floor](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/PersistentCircuitFloor.lean#L67) | Prove matching bounds of order $v_Kt$ from a physically admissible start to $\exp(\Theta(K))$, uniformly against circuits chosen after seeing the draw. |
| Plateau | Put $P=\lfloor D/[64(K+1)]\rfloor$, $r=\lfloor P/2\rfloor$. The first time $\sigma$ at distance $9/80$ from the $P$-gate set exists. For $M=(r+1)^r$, at most a $1/M$ limiting upper fraction of starts see an $r$-gate hit during the next $(M-1)/[40(1+\lVert H\rVert)]$ time units. [First crossing](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPauliFirstThreshold.lean#L71) · [Window occupation](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPauliEarlyLateCircuitLaw.lean#L21) | Date $\sigma$ by $\exp(O(K))$ and quantify occupation by distance **below the actual maximum** after onset. One hard threshold gives only one part of the fluctuation profile. |
| First low-cost return | Almost surely on every sufficiently long prefix, $\sigma>B=2^{\lfloor K/2\rfloor}/[C(K+1)^2]$ for deterministic $C>0$. Every budget $s\le r$ has an attained first return $v_s$ after $\sigma$, with $\sigma+B<v_s\le(\sigma+1)100^{D-1}$. [First-return theorem](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPauliFirstThreshold.lean#L71) | For polynomial $s$, exclude **all** $s$-gate hits for doubly exponential time from $\sigma$. The upper deadline still contains $\sigma$. Doubly exponential hard intervals are proved at other, undated starts. |

## Live routes

| Route | What the proof gives | Hard mathematical obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| **RAMP** · count gates touching active sites | Independent cuts give a short linear ramp on engineered chains and a lower ramp on a positive-measure Gaussian neighbourhood. On iid prefixes, isolated fields give the typical fixed-horizon floor above. [Explicit chain](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalLaboratoryComplexityUpper.lean) · [Gaussian neighbourhood](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/NearestNeighborGaussianSameDrawGrowthLaw.lean#L324) | A touched-site count saturates at $O(K)$. Certify repeated gate use on the same qubits for exponentially long times on typical draws. |
| **WORD** · certify minimum circuit length | A per-gate Lipschitz witness bounds all words. Hardness at geometric times plus a uniform increment compiler transfers to a continuous ramp. A time-cell mean distance exceeding $\epsilon+\lVert H\rVert h$ excludes the full budget throughout a cell of half-width $h$. [Word witness](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticOrbitNetBellmanLaw.lean#L345) · [Geometric transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GeometricSkeletonBrownRamp.lean#L69) · [Cell certificate](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/CircuitCellAverage.lean#L122) | Prove the endpoint or cell-mean lower bound along the identity-origin orbit, at budgets proportional to time, together with a matching simulation rate. |
| **HIT** · gate-walk first entrance | A short preparation word contributes to first-entrance mass. Capacity retains the cost of the entire family, and dephasing gives a late-time transfer. [Capacity](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/CapacityEntropyBudget.lean#L68) · [Transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/AnnealedAccessibilityPlateau.lean#L97) | At ramp times, upper-bound entrance mass into the finite-error operator target sharply enough to exclude short preparations. |
| **LAG** · compare to the easy past | A Choi hit splits into a return near an earlier anchor or a large anchor-orthogonal overlap. For physical circuits at two times, the sum of endpoint costs is at least $c(T)K$ at every lag in $[A(T)/K,T]$. [Choi split](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/LagResolvedStaticChoiShortcutDichotomyLaw.lean#L117) · [Endpoint cost](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/PersistentCircuitFloor.lean#L67) | Beyond fixed horizons, control both returns to affordable anchors and residual overlaps with every fresh circuit target. |
| **PHASE** · count short circuits on the spectral torus | For sufficiently long even Gaussian paths, affordable time in $[0,T]$ lies between $T/100^{D-1}$ and $(T+S_H)/M$ for every $s\le r$. The actual maximum recurs on intervals. Along any injective integer clock fixed before the draw, both maxima and identity returns have positive lower index density. [Occupation](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PhysicalCircuitReturnDeadline.lean#L296) · [Maximum](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/AttainedOrbitCircuitMaximum.lean#L236) · [Prescribed clocks](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PrescribedClockCircuitOccupation.lean#L130) | Bound short-circuit occupation starting at identity on the **first** exponential time window. Asymptotic clock densities leave the initial observations uncontrolled. |
| **INC** · count circuit targets actually met | Nonidentity Pauli tubes are avoided with high probability on prescribed polynomial windows in the Gaussian two-local model. For continuous circuits the replica span has an explicit rank bound, and a small enough return-excised incidence integral excludes all hits. [Pauli targets](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliContinuousLaboratoryReturnLaw.lean#L395) · [Continuous circuits](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ContinuousCircuitReturnExcisedFirstEntryLaw.lean#L369) | Bound that integral on the fixed-H trajectory for growing budgets and the whole forward window, including repeated visits. |
| **DES** · weight target moments | Optimal-design weights reduce forward exclusion to moment bounds for circuit targets. A draw-probability bound covers a bounded continuum. [Weighted reduction](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/StaticChoiOptimalDesignWeightedCrowdingFirstReturnLaw.lean#L373) · [Continuum](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/QuenchedBoundedTargetFirstEntryLaw.lean) | Prove the return-excised moments at replica orders large enough to pay for the full circuit family. |
| **CLOCK** · bound phase hitting times | Phase alignment gives a zero-gate deadline. For a Gaussian Pauli prefix, the probability of both the energy cutoff $\max_i\lvert E_i\rvert\le R$ and a missed phase cube from some origin within the explicit filling time is at most $2\,\#S\,D!\,a$ for $0<a\le1$, with $S$ the retained balanced modes. [Identity deadline](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PhysicalCircuitReturnDeadline.lean#L39) · [Gaussian filling clock](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/GaussianPhaseClock.lean#L63) | The certified detuning contains $a^{D!}$. Reach a horizon $\exp(\mathrm{poly}(D))$ with high probability, after accounting for the cutoff tail. First-return **lower** bounds also require avoidance of nonidentity short circuits. |
| **First crossing by circuit composition** | At $\sigma$, reserve half the gate budget for a sparse compiler for physical time increments. This excludes every $r$-gate circuit throughout $[\sigma-B,\sigma+B]$, even with odd prefix lengths. [Composition](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/FirstCircuitSaturationBoundary.lean#L115) · [Gaussian bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPauliFirstThreshold.lean#L71) | Control circuits *forward from this first crossing*. Sparsely compiling increments protects an exponential interval. A doubly exponential interval requires a stronger avoidance estimate. |
