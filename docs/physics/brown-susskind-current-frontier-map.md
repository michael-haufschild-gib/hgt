# Brown–Susskind · live proof routes

**The fixed-Hamiltonian second law remains open.** Almost surely, every sufficiently long prefix of one iid Gaussian Pauli chain has a deterministic deadline for its first robust crossing and its next polynomial-cost return. Filling the whole phase torus already takes doubly exponential time, so exponential onset needs a bound for a single hard visit. Matching linear growth and doubly exponential exclusion of early returns remain open. [Target](second-law.md) · [Killed routes](brown-susskind-killed-routes.md)

**Model.** Draw the three onsite and nine nearest-neighbour bond Pauli coefficients independently from the standard normal law, once on an infinite chain. On its open $K$-site prefix put $D=2^K$. Cost means minimum number of arbitrary two-qubit gates, no ancillas, projective operator-norm error $1/10$. All three claims must hold on the same typical draw. A lower bound witnessed on a state or in Choi distance can certify operator hardness. A return needs whole-operator control.

## Live routes

|---|---|---|
| **RAMP** · count gates touching active sites | Independent cuts give a short linear ramp on engineered chains and a lower ramp on a positive-measure Gaussian neighbourhood. On iid prefixes, isolated fields give the typical fixed-horizon floor above. | **WORD** · certify minimum circuit length | A per-gate Lipschitz witness bounds all words. Hardness at geometric times plus a uniform increment compiler transfers to a continuous ramp.
