# Brown–Susskind · live proof routes

**The fixed-Hamiltonian second law remains open.** Typical Gaussian Pauli chains need at least $c\sqrt K$ gates on $[A/K,K^{1/48}/4]$. Resolved low-cost returns have doubly exponential mean spacing. At the first robust crossing itself, the proved exclusion lasts singly exponential time. Matching linear growth and exponential plateau onset remain open. [Target](second-law.md) · [Killed routes](brown-susskind-killed-routes.md)

**Model.** Draw the three onsite and nine nearest-neighbour bond Pauli coefficients independently from the standard normal law, once on an infinite chain. On its open $K$-site prefix put $D=2^K$. Cost means minimum number of arbitrary two-qubit gates, no ancillas, projective operator-norm error $1/10$. All three claims must hold on the same typical draw. A lower bound witnessed on a state or in Choi distance can certify operator hardness. A return needs whole-operator control.

## Live routes

| **RAMP** · count gates touching active sites | Independent cuts give a short linear ramp on engineered chains and a lower ramp on a positive-measure Gaussian neighbourhood. On iid prefixes, counts of weak isolated fields extend the lower bound to polynomial times. [Explicit chain](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalLaboratoryComplexityUpper.lean) · [Gaussian neighbourhood](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/NearestNeighborGaussianSameDrawGrowthLaw.lean#L324) | A touched-site count saturates at $O(K)$. | **WORD** · certify minimum circuit length | A per-gate witness bounds word length. Geometric endpoint hardness plus an increment compiler gives a continuous ramp. Scalar energy shifts preserve distance to every circuit budget. For spectral width $\Delta E$ and cell half-width $h$, the mean certifies the whole cell with margin $h\Delta E/2$. The speed $\Delta E/2$ is sharp at zero gates. [Word witness](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticOrbitNetBellmanLaw.lean#L345) · [Geometric transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GeometricSkeletonBrownRamp.lean#L69) · [Cell certificate](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/CircuitCellAverage.lean#L360) · [Sharp speed at zero gates](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/CircuitCellAverage.lean#L312) | The exact semicircle formula would give the zero-gate cutoff $\lvert t\rvert\Delta E=4\arcsin(1/20)$ at error $1/10$. On even paths the maximum has positive lower frequency on each injective integer clock fixed before the draw. [Occupation](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PhysicalCircuitReturnDeadline.lean#L296) · [Dated excursions](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/DatedExcursion.lean#L36) · [Prescribed clocks](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PrescribedClockCircuitOccupation.lean#L130) | Find a hard visit by $\exp(O(K))$. | **DES** · weight target moments | Optimal-design weights reduce forward exclusion to moment bounds for circuit targets. A draw-probability bound covers a bounded continuum. | **CLOCK** · bound phase hitting times | Almost surely on every sufficiently long prefix, every interval of deterministic length $G_K$ visits every relative-phase box of radius $1/80$, including the identity box.
