# Brown–Susskind: live proof routes

**Full second law: open.** Almost every sufficiently long Gaussian nearest-neighbour chain reaches a robust exponential circuit threshold. Its first crossing from identity excludes all polynomial-cost returns for a proved exponential duration. Matching linear growth up to that crossing, an exponential upper bound on its onset, and a doubly exponential first-return lower bound remain open. [Killed routes](brown-susskind-killed-routes.md).

## Target and strongest results

Use the [project’s second-law statement](second-law.md): fix the local ensemble, strength normalization, universal gate library, allowed ancillas and error ε > 0. Draw H once on K qubits (D = 2ᴷ). Starting at identity, measure **minimum gate count** to approximate the whole operator exp(−itH) in operator norm up to global phase. All three rows must hold on **one event of probability 1−o(1)**, with the same gate model and ε.

| Phase | Required | What Lean already supplies |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Matching bounds of order vₖt after resolution time up to t_sat = exp(Θ(K)); vₖ depends on strength normalization. | An [explicit inverse-square chain](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalLaboratoryComplexityUpper.lean) has matching two-qubit gate bounds of order t/ε on a window of length O(εK). A [positive Gaussian measure of path draws](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/NearestNeighborGaussianSameDrawGrowthLaw.lean#L324) retains a laboratory lower ramp. Positive measure is not probability tending to one. |
| Plateau | Reach an exponential level around t_sat; quantify how far complexity drops below it and for what fraction of later times. | [Generic connected networks](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ConnectedNetworkStaticChoiComplexityPlateauLaw.lean#L80) bound the long-time fraction near a prescribed finite Choi dictionary. [One path draw](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/NearestNeighborNearHilbertContinuousCircuitSameDrawLaw.lean#L28) excludes continuous circuits of order D/log D on recurring hard intervals. Neither identifies first saturation or the fluctuation profile there. |
| First low-cost return | Starting at first saturation, bound the first hit of **any** circuit of at most poly(K) gates from below and above on the scale exp(exp(Θ(K))). | [Finite dictionary, Choi error 1/200](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/NearestNeighborPolynomialCircuitQuantitativeFirstReturnLaw.lean#L87): arbitrarily late hard blocks of width (3/2)ᴰ/2 and a re-entry deadline depending on the chosen block. [Continuous gates, projective operator error 1/20](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/NearestNeighborNearHilbertContinuousCircuitFirstReturnLaw.lean#L29): consecutive exit/re-entry episodes on one draw, but with unspecified width and onset; its ramp uses 1/10000. Neither clock starts at first saturation. |

**Continuous-gate fluctuation bound.** On almost every sufficiently long fixed Gaussian path with K even, put r = ⌊D/[128(K+1)]⌋ and M = (r+1)ʳ. For projective operator error 1/10, at most a 1/M upper fraction of starting times admit any r-gate hit during the next (M−1)/[40(1+‖H‖)] time units. The estimate is uniform in observation origin and the same H retains its locality signal at every time. Its finite-time correction depends on H and supplies no first-saturation deadline. [Lean theorem](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/TranslatedCircuitOccupation.lean) · [proof and quantifiers](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/gaussian-path-short-circuit-window-occupation.md).

**Affordable time from identity.** For every gate budget s between zero and r, the time spent at cost at most s in [0,T] lies between T/100ᴰ⁻¹ and (T+S)/M on the same draw. The lower bound already holds for the empty circuit on every static Hamiltonian. The first affordable time after any u is attained by (|u|+1)100ᴰ⁻¹. These estimates quantify duration and a return deadline. A doubly exponential lower bound from first saturation remains open. [Occupation theorem](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/identity-prefix-short-circuit-occupation.md) · [first-entry theorem](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/projective-operator-first-short-circuit-deadline.md).

**First robust threshold from identity.** Let P = ⌊D/[64(K+1)]⌋ and r = ⌊P/2⌋. The first time σ at which distance to the P-gate set reaches 9/80 obeys σ > B = 2^⌊K/4⌋/(1+‖H‖). Throughout [σ−B,σ+B], every r-gate circuit fails at error 1/10. For any s ≤ r, the first s-gate return v after σ satisfies σ+B < v ≤ (σ+1)100ᴰ⁻¹. This lower bound starts at a specified first crossing and has single-exponential duration. [First crossing and first return](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/first-saturation-boundary-for-the-gaussian-path.md).

**Actual orbit maximum.** At error 1/10 the minimum gate count attains its own maximum on recurrent positive-length intervals. The maximum equals the largest cost on the full torus of independently assigned energy phases, exceeds r, and coexists with permanent two-query locality separation. The time spent near this exact height remains unquantified. [Maximum theorem](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/attained-orbit-maximum-and-permanent-locality.md).

**Doubling the observation time.** Among base times t sampled in (0,T], the limiting upper fraction admitting r gates at all of t,2t,…,2ᵏ⁻¹t is at most (r+1)⁻²ʳᵏ. The underlying square-root count also gives positive time density for k hard doublings followed by an open return neighbourhood. The onset of these histories remains undated. [Conditional rarity theorem](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/short-circuits-in-spectral-square-root-fibers.md).

**Microscopic gate floor.** For iid unit-variance Pauli coefficients, almost surely every sufficiently long N-site prefix needs at least cN two-qubit gates throughout an interval of width proportional to 1/N around π divided by the spectral diameter. The center is also of order 1/N. A size-uniform O(Nt) upper bound cannot start this early. [Lean theorem](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPathMicroscopicCircuitFloor.lean).

**Prescribed geometric clock.** On almost every draw the observations at 1,2,4,… attain the actual orbit maximum with positive lower index density. For each fixed ε > 0, empty-circuit returns also have positive lower index density. The same theorem holds for any injective integer sequence prescribed before the draw. It leaves the first O(n) clock ticks uncontrolled as n grows. [Physical theorem and proof](../drafts/pipeline/3-formalized/identity-anchored-dyadic-clock-for-static-gaussian-hamiltonians.md).

**Metric matters.** [Choi distance is bounded by operator distance](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/OrdinaryTwoLocalGaussianHarmonicChoiGrowthLaw.lean#L48): excluding every circuit in Choi distance gives an operator lower bound for the same gates. A Choi or product-state *return* does not give an operator return. A [fixed easy input’s late-time hardness](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/CubicSeedFullScalePlateau.lean#L67) is a lower-bound witness, not the cost of the whole evolution. If renewed growth after a dip is claimed, [reflection about an exact return](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalExactReturnReflectionLaw.lean#L256) is available for an engineered chain, not for a generic low-cost dip.

## Routes

A **live** route has a distinct mechanism and a formal result on a physical evolution, or a proved reduction to a quantitative estimate on that evolution. A **candidate** still needs a bridge from its surrogate to the physical circuit family. “If” below denotes a reduction, not a discharged estimate.

| Live route | What is proved | Open mathematical obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| **RAMP — charge gates to active pairs** | Independent cuts force distinct two-qubit gates. An [explicit harmonic matching and its Gaussian neighbourhood](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/OrdinaryTwoLocalGaussianHarmonicChoiSharpRamp.lean#L89) retain a linear Choi lower bound across successive time windows. | Only O(K) pairs can be charged. Find a witness that charges **repeated use** of qubits for exponential time on typical draws. |
| **WORD — certify minimum word length** | A [function that increases by at most one per gate](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticOrbitNetBellmanLaw.lean#L345) certifies a lower bound for all words. [Geometrically spaced hard times plus a simulation upper bound](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GeometricSkeletonBrownRamp.lean#L69) suffice for a continuous ramp with error slack. | Produce such hardness on the actual orbit at those times, against the best circuit chosen *after* seeing H. Simulation supplies only the upper direction. |
| **HIT — random gate-walk first entrance** | A short gate word contributes to first-entrance probability. The [capacity bound](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/CapacityEntropyBudget.lean#L68) retains the full price of the circuit family; a [static-chain dephasing theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/AnnealedAccessibilityPlateau.lean#L97) transfers a suitable upper bound to late times. | Bound first-entrance mass for the physical operator target at ramp times, including fixed-error gate approximation. Gate-walk time counts preparation steps, not Hamiltonian time. |
| **LAG — separate shortcuts from returns to the easy past** | [Exact Choi decomposition](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/LagResolvedStaticChoiShortcutDichotomyLaw.lean#L117): a circuit hit is either near an earlier chosen orbit point or has large overlap with the part of the circuit target orthogonal to that point. | Choose genuinely early, affordable reference times and bound both the returns and **all** residual overlaps. A spectral return bound alone says nothing about the latter. |
| **PHASE — count easy regions on the phase torus** | Dephasing gives the long-time occupation bound above. A [finite-window theorem](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/NearestNeighborGaussianSameDrawGrowthPlateauRecurrenceLaw.lean#L377) puts a hard block by 2T **if** the near-resonance bound at T beats the circuit count. | Prove that inequality at the predicted onset for typical H, then bound time spent at each lower gate threshold. Equidistribution alone gives no onset rate. |
| **INC — bound which circuit targets the orbit meets** | A Gaussian two-local draw [avoids every nonidentity Pauli target tube](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliContinuousLaboratoryReturnLaw.lean#L395) with high probability on any prescribed polynomial-length window. For [continuous circuits](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ContinuousCircuitReturnExcisedFirstEntryLaw.lean#L369), a bound on the occupied target dimension and incidence integral would exclude the entire family on an interval. | Pay for **all** short gates, not just Pauli targets, including repeated visits to the same circuit image. The physical estimate must survive growing gate budget and the full forward time window. |
| **DES — weighted moments of the target family** | [Optimal-design weights](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/StaticChoiOptimalDesignWeightedCrowdingFirstReturnLaw.lean#L373) turn weighted near-resonance control into forward exclusion; a [draw-probability version](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/QuenchedBoundedTargetFirstEntryLaw.lean) handles a bounded target continuum under moment bounds. | Prove the return-excised moments for the fixed-H ensemble at orders growing fast enough to pay for the full circuit family. |
| **CLOCK — simultaneous return of eigenphases** | Phase alignment supplies a [rank-dependent deadline](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/StaticChoiQuantitativeRecurrenceUpperLaw.lean); [physical observer exponents](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/ConnectedNetworkAllObserverRecurrenceLaw.lean#L195) hold at fixed system size as error shrinks. | At fixed ε and growing K, rule out **earlier nonidentity short circuits** after first saturation. An upper deadline for identity and shrinking-error exponents cannot give this lower bound. |

| Candidate | Existing foothold | What would promote it |
|---|---|---|
| **STRATA — classify fast-forwardable exceptions** | [Explicit easy families](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/FastForwardableStratumCodimension.lean#L257) force a minimum shortcut probability. | Prove an inverse theorem: every substantial transient shortcut family comes from an independently recognizable exception whose measure can be bounded. A list of examples only bounds probability from below. |
| **CAV — growing-replica expansion** | The [two-replica identity/swap kernel](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/TwoReplicaGlobalSingletEdgeCasimirBridge.lean) and [finite-replica Pauli word bounds](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/FiniteReplicaTwoLocalPauliWordHall.lean#L195) control particular replica spaces. | Turn these into a bound uniform in replica order and over physical circuits; feed it into INC or DES. |
| **PARENT — witness hardness by energy** | [Transporting an easy state’s parent Hamiltonian](../../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/DynamicalParentComplexityBridge.lean#L143) turns an independent energy penalty into a robust state-preparation lower bound, hence an operator witness on that input. | Construct a penalty uniform over all short circuits on a typical physical orbit. Defining the parent by conjugation preserves its ground state but does not prove that penalty. |

**What recurrence already gives:** once a plateau time T is known, sample at multiples of T+1. Divide each of the D−1 relative eigenphase circles into q arcs, with 2π/q ≤ ε. Two of the first qᴰ⁻¹+1 samples (including identity) share a box. Their time difference lies between T+1 and (T+1)qᴰ⁻¹ and returns the **whole operator** within ε of identity up to phase. This elementary upper bound costs no randomness; it gives no early-return exclusion.

**Next decisive test:** show with high probability that **no** circuit substantially shorter than vₖt approximates the operator at any geometric ramp time. The separate recurrence test is forward avoidance of the entire polynomial-gate set from first saturation.

**Literature boundary:** the [Brown–Susskind proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.01107) concerns a fixed Hamiltonian. Results for [random circuits or stochastic Hamiltonians](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09734) and for [late-time product-state complexity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26885) are not the three-phase operator theorem above.
