# Brown–Susskind · live proof routes

**The fixed-Hamiltonian second law remains open.** Almost surely, long prefixes of one Gaussian Pauli chain have doubly exponential intervals that exclude short circuits, with a deterministic deadline for finding such an interval from any starting time. At the first robust crossing, the proved exclusion lasts only singly exponential time. Matching linear growth to that crossing, exponential onset and the first low-cost return on the predicted scale remain open. [Target](second-law.md) · [Killed routes](brown-susskind-killed-routes.md)

**Model.** Draw the three onsite and nine nearest-neighbour bond Pauli coefficients independently from the standard normal law, once on an infinite chain. On its open $K$-site prefix put $D=2^K$. Cost means minimum number of arbitrary two-qubit gates, no ancillas, projective operator-norm error $1/10$. All three claims must hold on the same typical draw. A lower bound witnessed on a state or in Choi distance can certify operator hardness. A return needs whole-operator control.

## Live routes

| Route | What the proof gives | Hard mathematical obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| **RAMP** · count gates touching active sites | Independent cuts give a short linear ramp on engineered chains and a lower ramp on a positive-measure Gaussian neighbourhood. On iid prefixes, isolated fields give the typical fixed-horizon floor above. [Explicit chain](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalLaboratoryComplexityUpper.lean) · [Gaussian neighbourhood](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/NearestNeighborGaussianSameDrawGrowthLaw.lean#L324) | A touched-site count saturates at $O(K)$. Certify repeated gate use on the same qubits for exponentially long times on typical draws. |
| **WORD** · certify minimum circuit length | A per-gate witness bounds all words. Geometric endpoint bounds plus a compiler give a continuous ramp. Cell means certify whole intervals. So does a reference $J$ within $1/40$ of the start up to phase, if every transported grid point clears the circuit budget by $3/20$ and $\lVert H\rVert\,\mathrm{mesh}\le 1/40$. [Word witness](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticOrbitNetBellmanLaw.lean#L345) · [Geometric transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/GeometricSkeletonBrownRamp.lean#L69) · [Cell certificate](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/CircuitCellAverage.lean#L123) · [Reference-grid transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/CircuitWindows/ReferenceGrid.lean#L14) | Prove endpoint or grid hardness for budgets proportional to time on the identity-origin orbit, together with a matching simulation rate. |
| **HIT** · gate-walk first entrance | A short preparation word contributes to first-entrance mass. Capacity retains the cost of the entire family, and dephasing gives a late-time transfer. [Capacity](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/CapacityEntropyBudget.lean#L68) · [Transfer](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/AnnealedAccessibilityPlateau.lean#L97) | At ramp times, upper-bound entrance mass into the finite-error operator target sharply enough to exclude short preparations. |
| **LAG** · compare to the easy past | A Choi hit splits into a return near an earlier anchor or a large anchor-orthogonal overlap. For physical circuits at two times, the sum of endpoint costs is at least $c(T)K$ at every lag in $[A(T)/K,T]$. [Choi split](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/LagResolvedStaticChoiShortcutDichotomyLaw.lean#L117) · [Endpoint cost](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/PersistentCircuitFloor.lean#L67) | Beyond fixed horizons, control both returns to affordable anchors and residual overlaps with every fresh circuit target. |
| **PHASE** · count short circuits on the spectral torus | For sufficiently long even Gaussian paths, affordable time in $[0,T]$ lies between $T/100^{D-1}$ and $(T+S_H)/M$ for every $s\le r$. From every $a\ge0$ on a typical Pauli chain, some $u\in[a,a+G_K]$ begins a window of width $W$ throughout which cost exceeds $r$. On even paths the maximum recurs on intervals and has positive lower frequency along each injective integer clock fixed before the draw. [Occupation](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PhysicalCircuitReturnDeadline.lean#L296) · [Maximum](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/AttainedOrbitCircuitMaximum.lean#L236) · [Prescribed clocks](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/PrescribedClockCircuitOccupation.lean#L130) · [Dated excursions](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/DatedExcursion.lean#L36) | Find a hard visit by $\exp(O(K))$. Full coverage already takes doubly exponential time. [Cover-time bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/ReflectionVisitPacking.lean#L62) |
| **INC** · count circuit targets actually met | Nonidentity Pauli tubes are avoided with high probability on prescribed polynomial windows in the Gaussian two-local model. For continuous circuits the replica span has an explicit rank bound, and a small enough return-excised incidence integral excludes all hits. [Pauli targets](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliContinuousLaboratoryReturnLaw.lean#L395) · [Continuous circuits](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/ContinuousCircuitReturnExcisedFirstEntryLaw.lean#L369) | Bound that integral on the fixed-H trajectory for growing budgets and the whole forward window, including repeated visits. |
| **DES** · weight target moments | Optimal-design weights reduce forward exclusion to moment bounds for circuit targets. A draw-probability bound covers a bounded continuum. [Weighted reduction](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/StaticChoiOptimalDesignWeightedCrowdingFirstReturnLaw.lean#L373) · [Continuum](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/QuenchedBoundedTargetFirstEntryLaw.lean) | Prove the return-excised moments at replica orders large enough to pay for the full circuit family. |
| **CLOCK** · bound phase hitting times | Almost surely on every sufficiently long prefix, every interval of deterministic length $G_K$ visits every relative-phase box of radius $1/80$, including the identity box. The coefficient bound and summable failure probabilities put all these visits on the same draw. [Prefix filling theorem](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/PrefixPhaseHorizon.lean#L106) · [Identity visit](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/TimedReflection.lean#L58) | The all-labelings horizon has triple-exponential size in $K$, with $2^{2^{D-1}}\le G_K\le 2^{2^{512D^2}}$. Obtain an $\exp(\mathrm{poly}(D))$ return deadline. Earlier hits on nonidentity short circuits require a separate lower bound. [Horizon floor](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/AbsoluteCircuitClock.lean#L21) · [Upper bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/HorizonGrowth.lean#L79) |
| **First crossing by circuit composition** | A hard reflection is visited by $G_K$, so $B<\sigma\le G_K$. Compiling increments with half the gate budget excludes all $r$-gate circuits throughout $[\sigma-B,\sigma+B]$. [Timed reflection](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/TimedReflection.lean#L83) · [Absolute deadlines](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/AbsoluteCircuitClock.lean#L58) | Certify an early crossing from identity and move the start of a doubly exponential hard interval to that first crossing. |
