# Brown–Susskind: killed routes and surviving results

**No obstruction below refutes the [second law](second-law.md).** Each rules out a specific inference about the minimum gate count of one static evolution. The surviving routes are in the [frontier map](brown-susskind-current-frontier-map.md).

| Retired argument | Why it fails | Strongest survivor |
|---|---|---|
| **Ambient entropy ⇒ gate growth.** Treat static-H endpoints as Haar samples. | At fixed time the image of an m-parameter coefficient ball admits an [ε-net of size at most (1 + 2·abs(t)√m/ε)ᵐ](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticOrbitNetBellmanLaw.lean#L107). Unrestricted concentration at arbitrary centres cannot pay exponential gate entropy. Nor does time averaging repair this: a [single static orbit’s frame potential](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/AutonomousTemporalFramePotentialObstructionLaw.lean#L141) exceeds the Haar value in the stated range. | A small net is not a cheap circuit: its centres may themselves be hard. Count **physical circuit** balls on the orbit (WORD/PHASE); Haar mixing is unnecessary. |
| **Uniform rarity for every short word ⇒ ramp.** Pay the full word count times the worst single-word probability. | A nonzero one-Pauli Hamiltonian can be tuned to return exactly to identity at the tested time. A [whole coefficient tube](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/FiniteHorizonCStarPerturbationLaw.lean#L118) stays close. The [failed-route card](../drafts/pipeline/5-failed/static-circuitwise-temporal-min-entropy.md) gives the Gaussian mass comparison on paper; the periodic centre and perturbation bound are in Lean. | Count distinct endpoints with their actual weights, separating identity returns and explicit easy families. The [return-excised circuit bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliReturnExcisedCircuitContinuumLaw.lean) does this for a finite dictionary; it does not bound what was excised. |
| **Exact shortcut probability from observer rank or “organized formats.”** | For a product input, a [neighbourhood of a gapped easy eigenline](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliGaussianBoxShortcutFloor.lean) has time-uniform shortcut mass. This invalidates the proposed ensemble-averaged suppression, **not** operator hardness. The [organized-format formula](../drafts/pipeline/1-in-progress/cross-disciplinary/universal-laws-and-structures/autonomous-shortcut-free-energy-law.md) has no independent format class as proposed. | Explicit fast-forwardable families give a [one-sided codimension bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/FastForwardableStratumCodimension.lean#L257). A converse conditioned on independently defined exceptions remains open (STRATA). |
| **Scalar recurrence energy ⇒ no adaptive shortcuts.** Average the chosen circuit targets before estimating correlations. | At a single time the scalar energy is just the source norm squared. More sharply, [orthogonal fresh targets saturate the inequality](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticShortcutSelectorPartitionLaw.lean#L217) even when **every** sample is a perfect shortcut. Subtracting the diagonal would miss them all. | [Partition by selected target before estimating](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticShortcutSelectorPartitionLaw.lean#L241): repeated visits and hits on new targets are different costs. INC/DES must control both. |
| **Longer words or more pure replicas ⇒ a larger Gaussian exponent.** | Words can cancel or stay on two sites. For a pure-projector boundary, [replication rescales every commutator weight equally](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PureReplicaGaussianStableRankBarrier.lean#L130): the stable-rank exponent is unchanged and bounded by the number of physical generators. | Replicas can still compress the **target** family; they cannot manufacture independent draws of H. [Pauli-target avoidance](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PauliContinuousLaboratoryReturnLaw.lean#L395) survives for polynomial windows. For general circuits the missing estimate is occupied target mass (INC/DES). |
| **Rank, equidistribution or a late hard block ⇒ first dip from saturation.** | [Periodic and maximal-return-exponent spectra](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/PhysicalFirstReturnHorizonInstabilityLaw.lean#L420) can be arbitrarily close over a finite horizon. Worse, an [unrestricted normalized dictionary](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/StaticChoiArbitraryTargetFirstReturnNoGo.lean#L57) can plant the orbit point at time one. The latter is not a counterexample for a fixed physical gate library. | Rank gives a return **deadline**; a [selected hard block](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/RHTower/NearestNeighborPolynomialCircuitQuantitativeFirstReturnLaw.lean#L87) gives a genuine consecutive gap there. Neither excludes earlier visits to physical short circuits. |
| **Exact complexity or literal monotonicity ⇒ the robust growth law.** | [Finite resolution gives an initial zero-cost interval](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/ResolutionTimeNoncommutativity.lean#L54). Even a chain with rigorous growth has a [rise followed by a return drop](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalComplexityMonotonicityRigidityLaw.lean#L156). | Seek matching coarse bounds *after* resolution time, not monotonicity of every step. An explicitly periodic chain has an [exact reflection law](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/StaticLocalExactReturnReflectionLaw.lean#L256). |
| **Start an O(Nt) upper ramp at vanishing physical times.** | For Gaussian Pauli prefixes at fixed operator error, cost is at least cN throughout [A/N,1]. It exceeds every fixed multiple of Nt along every t → 0 in this interval. | The extensive floor persists to every fixed horizon. Sublinear-cost visits have microscopic or diverging separation. A ramp starting at a positive physical time remains possible. [Proof and survivor](../drafts/pipeline/5-failed/submacroscopic-start-for-a-linear-gaussian-circuit-ramp.md). |
| **Date the first plateau using a spectral-sign hit.** | If a 1/20 sign hit starts a 1/10-hard interval of width W at u ≥ 0, the empty circuit succeeds at 2u. Hence u > W. | Conditioning on an arbitrary doubled phase event still leaves a quantitative lower bound on long hard prehistories. [Obstruction and survivor](../drafts/pipeline/5-failed/spectral-sign-witness-for-early-first-saturation.md). |

**Narrow correction, not a route kill:** discounted gate-walk occupation counts revisits after success, so it is not the first-entrance probability. [The two are comparable within a fixed discount factor](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/RenewalOccupationPotential.lean#L242); HIT survives as a route to preparation cost, not physical recurrence time.
