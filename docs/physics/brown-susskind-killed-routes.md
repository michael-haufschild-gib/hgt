# Brown–Susskind · killed arguments and surviving results

The [fixed-Hamiltonian second law](second-law.md) remains open. Here $K$ is the qubit count and $D=2^K$. The surviving arguments appear in the [frontier map](brown-susskind-current-frontier-map.md).

| Retired argument | Obstruction | Positive result |
|---|---|---|
| **Start a uniform $O(Kt)$ upper ramp at vanishing time.** | On iid Gaussian Pauli prefixes, cost exceeds every fixed multiple of $Kt_K$ along any $t_K\to0$ eventually above $A/K$. A ramp starting earlier and extending to a fixed time also crosses this interval. [No-go](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/PersistentCircuitFloor.lean#L95) | Every fixed horizon has an extensive gate floor. At error $1/10$, two endpoint costs sum to at least $c(T)K$ at every lag in $[A(T)/K,T]$. A positive-time start remains open. [Floor and spacing](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/PersistentCircuitFloor.lean#L14) |
| **Date first saturation by filling the whole phase torus.** | A $1/80$ cover visits all $2^{D-1}$ anchored signs. Almost surely on every sufficiently long Gaussian prefix, every such interval has duration $T\ge c(2^{D-1}-1)/K$ for a deterministic $c>0$. This exceeds exponential onset time. [Gaussian cover-time bound](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/ReflectionVisitPacking.lean#L79) | At error $\epsilon<1/2$, any interval of length $T$ visits at most $1+\lfloor\lVert H\rVert T/(1-2\epsilon)\rfloor$ distinct signs. A single hard sign suffices for the threshold. [Sign count](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/ReflectionVisitPacking.lean#L13) · [Timed reflection](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/TimedReflection.lean#L83) |
| **Use a spectral sign to start a doubly exponential hard window at exponential time.** | A $1/20$ visit to a phased reflection at $u$ gives a $1/10$ empty-circuit return at $2u$. A hard window $[u,u+W]$ beginning at that visit forces $W<u$. [Sign obstruction](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/SpectralSignReturnClock.lean#L79) | On typical Gaussian prefixes, every $a\ge0$ admits a sign visit $u\in[a,a+G_K]$ with $W<u$. All budgets $s\le r$ stay excluded through $u+W$. Each has an attained first return by $2u$, when the empty circuit succeeds. Hard dyadic prehistories also have positive density before open return events. | **Obtain polynomial degree in $D$ by counting fewer modes in the full permutation product.** | For even one nonzero balanced mode, the product over all eigenvalue labelings is homogeneous of degree exactly $D!$. The resulting prefix deadline itself satisfies $G_K\ge 2^{2^{D-1}}$ for $K\ge4$. [Detuning](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/AbsoluteDetuning.lean#L81) · [Discriminant](../../formal/GaloisForLFunctions/HTGeometry/Quantum/GaussianPath/SpectralFilling/Discriminant.lean#L66) |
| **Infer gate growth from ambient Haar entropy.** | At fixed time, an $m$-parameter ball of Hamiltonians maps to a small endpoint net. Time averaging a single static orbit also leaves a frame-potential excess over Haar in the proved range.
