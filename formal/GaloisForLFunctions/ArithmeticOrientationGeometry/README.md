# Arithmetic Orientation Geometry

Arithmetic Orientation Geometry asks what could force a **scaling
correspondence to satisfy the Hasse–Weil bound**. Over a finite field, the
geometry of Frobenius graphs supplies that sign. Over $\mathbb Z$, analogous
Lefschetz and adjunction identities exist, but an independent arithmetic
reason for the sign remains to be found.

The programme studies one cycle together with its entire scale orbit. This
makes the target more specific than positivity of an unspecified ambient
space: determine which zeros the orbit sees, how its adverse directions grow,
and what source law could exclude them.

## Objects and language

A **scaling datum** consists of a polar surface, a group of scales and an
isometric action $\psi_g$ that scales the two fibre classes oppositely.
If $\delta$ is the Castelnuovo–Severi defect, the **orbit correlation** is

$$
c_x(g)=\delta(\psi_gx,x).
$$

The orbit of $x$ is **oriented** when the defect is nonnegative on its span:

$$
\sum_{g,h}a_ga_hc_x(g-h)\ge0
$$

for every finite real coefficient family. Thus orientation is positive
definiteness of one function on the scale group. It implies the Hasse–Weil
envelope $|c_x(g)|\le c_x(0)$. On the tempered zero configurations studied
here, bounded orbit correlation and orientation are equivalent.

A **correspondence** is a finite linear combination of scales. Convolution
composes correspondences; reversing the scale gives the **Rosati involution**.
The trace at $x$ is $\tau_x(\xi)=\delta(\xi\cdot x,x)$, and Rosati positivity
is the orbit form expressed as $\tau_x(\xi\xi')\ge0$.

Over $\mathbb Z$, scales are logarithmic translations of Weil tests. A
**spectrally generic** test sees every zero. Its orbit can therefore decide
RH; an arbitrary test may be blind to the adverse divisor.

## Foundational pillars

**Weil planes and correspondence identities.** Hasse–Weil at one scale is
Hodge index on the plane generated by a cycle and its translate. Lefschetz,
adjunction, pullback and Rosati formulas hold on every scaling datum. They
organize the sign problem, but also hold on impure model data.

**Orientation is spectral visibility.** A cycle is oriented exactly when its
orbit is blind to the relevant off-line zeros. Its orientation index counts
the distinct adverse zeros it sees; its exponential correlation rate measures
their depth. Consequently RH is equivalent to the all-scale envelope for one
real spectrally generic test. Genericity prevents the zero-test loophole.

**Orbit sections.** Restricting the scaling surface to one orbit gives an exact
section/effectivity criterion: the required Riemann–Roch supply exists there
precisely when the orbit is oriented. This localizes the geometric construction
needed. It does not rule out an independent proof of that supply merely
because the resulting criterion is equivalent to the sign.

**Finite segments and eventual bounds.** Archimedean energy orients specified
finite orbit segments. Eventual boundedness and rate laws can self-improve
under the proved spectral hypotheses. The distinction is the unbounded-scale
quantifier: a segment theorem cannot be iterated without paying its dependence
on the segment and source.

**A second index formula.** Let $D$ count distinct right-of-line zeros and,
when this count is finite, let $E=\sum_{\Re\rho>1/2}(m_\rho-1)$ be the
multiplicity excess. For sufficiently small positive heat time,

$$
\operatorname{collisionMassAfter}(t)=2(D+E).
$$

An infinite off-line divisor makes this mass diverge as heat tends to zero.
Uniform finite collision bounds are therefore equivalent to finitely many
off-line zeros, still allowing finite exceptions. Conjugation makes finite
$D$ even, so $D<2$ is exactly RH. The identity supplies a quantized target,
not an upper bound on it. See [TheIndexExit](TheIndexExit.lean).

## Research questions

- Which arithmetic sections or source inequality orient an orbit independently
  of the Weil sign criterion?
- Which weighted frustration, signed shell-flux or cofactor estimate can be
  transferred to the orbit form with constants uniform in scale?
- Can a source-derived collision budget exclude infinitely many exceptions,
  then fall below the first nonzero index quantum?
- Which actual-divisor hypotheses remove the unresolved accumulation and
  resonance obstructions on a single prime tower?

A proposed mechanism should distinguish the relevant shifted-pair, fake-curve
or clustered model, at its stated quantitative scale. Finite local factors,
positive point counts and nonnegative collision counts do not establish that
separation. Multiplicity also cannot be discarded when passing from the
Hodge index to heat collision mass.
