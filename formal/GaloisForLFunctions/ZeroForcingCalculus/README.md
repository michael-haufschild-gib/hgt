# Zero-Forcing Calculus (`ZeroForcingCalculus`)

Namespace `GaloisForLFunctions.ZeroForcingCalculus`; umbrella `ZeroForcingCalculus.lean`.

Every theorem named here is unconditional (`#print axioms` reports only `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`).

## 1. Purpose

This programme answers the zero-forcing question for the Riemann zeta function without positivity, Weil positivity, or spectral assumptions:

> Does one off-line zero $\rho$ of $\zeta$ ($\operatorname{Re}\rho \ne \tfrac12$) force a second zero outside its own four-element quartet $Q(\rho)=\{\rho,\bar\rho,1-\rho,1-\bar\rho\}$ by an exact identity using $\zeta$'s functional equation, without a coincidence hypothesis?

1. **Exact unconditional forcing stops at the quartet $Q(\rho)$, while transport closure rigidly classifies all strip configurations.** Every zero $\rho$ with $\operatorname{Re}\rho > \tfrac12$ satisfies $\tfrac12 < \operatorname{Re}\rho \le \tfrac78$ and $\operatorname{Im}\rho \ne 0$, forces the four pairwise distinct zeros $Q(\rho)$ with identical meromorphic and analytic order, and never forms a closed configuration under either $k$-fold descent ($\zeta(s)^m/\zeta(ks)$, $k \ge 2$) or dyadic doubling ($\zeta(2s)/\zeta(s)$).
2. **Obstructions to unconditional inter-quartet forcing and the exact $[1/2, 9/16)$ ruler.**
   - *Witt Euler-product monomials* $W_e(s)=\prod_{j=1}^K \zeta(js)^{e_j}$ satisfy $\operatorname{ord}_s W_e = \sum_{j=1}^K e_j\operatorname{ord}_{js}\zeta$ globally on $\mathbb{C}$ and $\operatorname{ord}_\rho W_e = e_1\operatorname{ord}_\rho\zeta$ on $\operatorname{Re}\rho > \tfrac12$; at $s=\rho/k$ ($k \ge 2$), turning a denominator zero into a numerator zero requires pole-freeness of $W_e$ across $\operatorname{Re}s > \operatorname{Re}\rho/k$.
   - *The unconditional $[1/2, 9/16)$ squarefree pole-abscissa ruler:* combining the $2/3$ halving-reflection cascade with the unconditional $7/8$ zero-free half-plane ($1 - (7/8)/2 = 9/16$) makes pole-freeness of $((s-1)\zeta(s))^m/\zeta(2s)$ on $\{\operatorname{Re}s > \beta/2\}$ unconditionally equivalent to $\Theta \le \beta$ for all $\beta \in [\tfrac12, \tfrac9{16})$ and all $m \in \mathbb{N}$ (and equivalent to `RiemannHypothesis` at $\beta = \tfrac12$, i.e. $\operatorname{Re}s > \tfrac14$).
   - *Ramanujan / Rankin–Selberg shift barrier:* in $\zeta(s)\zeta(s-a)\zeta(s-b)\zeta(s-a-b)/\zeta(2s-a-b)$, the denominator zero $s_*=(\rho+a+b)/2$ lies at distance $\ge 1 - \operatorname{Re}\rho/2 \ge \tfrac9{16}$ to the left of the polar abscissa $\sigma_{\mathrm{pole}}(a,b)=1+\max(0,\operatorname{Re}a,\operatorname{Re}b,\operatorname{Re}(a+b))$ for all $(a,b)\in\mathbb{C}^2$.
   - *Brauer permutation cancellation:* for every finite group $G$ and subgroup $H \le G$, $\sum_{g\in G}\operatorname{Ind}_H^G(1_H)(g) = |G|$, so every virtual permutation relation $\sum_i c_i\operatorname{Ind}_{H_i}^G(1_{H_i}) = 0$ satisfies $\sum_i c_i = 0$ and cancels the $\zeta(s)$ factor identically.
3. **Galois closure and mixed prime traces of an isolated off-line quartet.** Over the paired-ordinate prime trace field $B = \mathtt{primeTraceField}$:
   - *Regime A ($\tfrac12 + it$ not a zero, $U_t = \{\rho, 1-\bar\rho\}$):* the height reflection product $A_{t,p} = p(u_p(\rho) - v_p(\rho))$ is real and strictly negative at **every** prime $p$, so $A_{t,p}^2 \in B$ is strictly positive at every prime $p$ (opposite in sign to critical heights); $A_{t,p}^2$ is a square in $B$ iff $u_p(\rho) \in B$ iff $v_p(\rho) \in B$ iff $\operatorname{Gal}(E/B)$ contains no partial flip; and every mixed trace factors symmetrically as $T_t(m) = \operatorname{mult}(\rho)\,e^{L/2}(e^{aL}+e^{-aL})(e^{itL}+e^{-itL})$ ($a=\operatorname{Re}\rho-\tfrac12$, $L=\sum_p m_p\log p$).
   - *Regime B ($\tfrac12 + it$ is a zero):* any critical zero at ordinate $\pm t$ forces $u_p(\rho) \in B$ at all primes $p$ and eliminates all partial flips; when $Q(\rho)$ is the unique off-line quartet at ordinate $\pm t$, $T_t(2e_p) = p(\operatorname{mult}(\rho_0)u_p(\rho) + \operatorname{mult}(\rho)u_p(\rho)v_p(\rho))$ places both $u_p(\rho), v_p(\rho) \in B$ and restricts $\operatorname{Gal}(E/B)|_{Q(\rho)}$ to the Klein four-group $\{1,c,r,cr\}$.
4. **Consequences of expanding zero-forcing laws.** Combining band-expanding or depth-expanding forcing with the unconditional $7/8$ zero-free region for $\zeta$ and Dirichlet $L$-functions excludes abscissa bands, transports simultaneously across all `zetaZeroWidth` rulers and the eternal-AdS Wightman strip, and collapses $\Theta = \tfrac12$ (`RiemannHypothesis`) whenever $(1/2, 7/8]$ is covered.
