# Selective Multiscale Arithmetic Positivity (SMAP)

Namespace `GaloisForLFunctions.SelectiveMultiscaleArithmeticPositivity`; umbrella `SelectiveMultiscaleArithmeticPositivity.lean`.

**Question.** Every positivity the corpus proves on the complete arithmetic source also holds for the bounded twists $|\lambda|\le\Lambda$
(same Gamma and pole terms), yet the Liouville twin is not Weil-positive. Which inequality is true for $\Lambda$, false for its twins,
and survives the dyadic renormalization under which the complete source is a copy of itself?

SMAP studies $W=\Gamma+\text{poles}-P$ as a translation-invariant form on the complete multiplicative source, with three energy sources
(primes, Gamma, pole) and a renormalization group generated by Hecke dilations $T_{\log p}$. The subject is **selective positivity**:
positivity using a property of $\Lambda$ its twins lack, stated so it can survive infinite exhaustion.

## 2. Why

**Twin-blindness is a dead end.** On atomic families at any root of finite overlap order, positivity of a bounded weight *is* RH and
forces the weight to be $\Lambda$. A proof built from estimates shared by a twin family cannot reach an RH-complete family. The
high-detail floor holds for every twist, so the Liouville twin's negative directions live in the low and cross-level residual.

**The pole hyperplane is where $\Lambda$ differs.** On atomic tests the continuous pole moment $\int fe^{-u/2}$ is a multiple of $\sum x_n/\sqrt n$. On that
hyperplane the pole term vanishes, $\zeta$'s prime form has a uniform gap below its ceiling, the Liouville twin reaches the ceiling;
twins differ only in the prime form, so the gap transfers to full Weil forms and pays the whole off-pole component.

**Selectivity has a size: $2\log2$.** No uniform gap exceeds $2\log2$ (conductor-2 Ramanujan mode is the first excitation); Liouville's gap is 0;
no sign gauge beats $\zeta$. A margin needs **harmonic correlation** with the pole: $\sum_{n\le N}\omega(n)/n=o(\sqrt{\log N})$ ⇒ margin 0; full harmonic-density
agreement ⇒ full $2\log2$.

**Multiscale structure is exact.** Two adjacent cells fill the $\log2$-translate of one cell, so the source is a sum over levels of
translates of one generator plus level details; translation-invariant forms have level-Toeplitz Grams. The pole hyperplane is
invariant under the dyadic step; off it pole couplings grow like $2^{|k-j|/2}$ and form one hyperbolic plane. On the hyperplane the
entire arithmetic content of the multiscale problem is the **signed Chebyshev error along the dyadic orbit**.

## 4. Mechanisms

1. **Selection at every root**: atomic receiver + Weil cone uniqueness.
2. **Pole-hyperplane identity**: translation multiplies exponential moments by $e^{ac}$.
3. **Twin difference**: $W_\omega(f_x)-W(f_x)=(\int g^2)(P_N(x)-P_N(\omega x))$ below the spacing.
4. **Gap transfer**: on the hyperplane $W=M\ge(\varphi_0+c_0G)\|f\|^2$; selective Gårding inequality off it.
5. **Bottleneck**: $\log m+\tau(m)\ge2\log2$, equality only at $m=2$; two top eigenvectors span a plane meeting every hyperplane.
6. **Misalignment cost**: on $y\perp\omega b$, $(\sum y_nb_n)^2\le|y|^2\cdot2(H_N-S_N)$.
7. **Level payment and Schur transparency**: $2B+E\ge(1-\theta)\delta t^2-(\beta^2/\theta\delta)s^2$; a PD detail block leaves exactly the Schur-corrected coarse block.
8. **Doubled cell** $C_{2a}+C_{2a+1}=T_{\log2}C_a$.
9. **Exponential moments under translation**: pole pairing of two copies $=2A(f)B(f)\cosh$(half shift).
10. **Coupling between levels splits in three**: adjacent levels through one constant; separated levels: Gamma part $\le4^{-(k-j-1)}$ (summable),
    pole part hyperbolic (vanishes on the hyperplane), remainder = centred Chebyshev integral at lags between levels.
11. **Dyadic sign rigidity**: on the tower $g,T_{\log2}g,\dots$ Weil's form is Toeplitz in the lag; positivity of one window $\{0,d\}$ bounds the
    symmetrized lag correlation by the diagonal; two weights differ only by the prime pairing of their difference, so both cannot be
    dyadic positive when that difference has unbounded one-signed dyadic mass.

   `not_liouville_hasHyperplaneGap`).
6. **Harmonic correlation decides the margin** (`not_hasHyperplaneGap_of_harmonicSum_small`, `hasHyperplaneGap_two_mul_log_two_of_correlation`).

9. **Three-part level coupling** (`polePairing_shift`, `abs_weilPair_shift_add_chebyshev_le`, `abs_weilPair_add_primePart_le`).
10. **Dyadic sign rigidity**: if two weights are both dyadic positive along a generator, the dyadic prime mass of their difference is

    $\Lambda$ vs $\Lambda+\varepsilon n^{-\eta}$ (interval statements with $o(1)$ relative error cannot decide the sign) and $\Lambda$ vs Liouville — are not yet
    declared in the Lean file (open, §7).

## 6. Traps

| Trap | Truth |
|---|---|
| A strong twin-blind floor completes the proof | Twin-blind positivity at any finite-order root is impossible |
| The high-detail Haar floor is evidence for $\zeta$ | It holds for every twist, Liouville included |
| Identifying $\zeta$ gives positivity | $\zeta$ is the hardest member, not the most positive |
| Hyperplane separation is a sign separation | At spacing scale it is a margin; sign separation appears at unbounded overlap |
| $\zeta$ is the minimum of its gauge orbit | Only on nonnegative atomic tests; on the hyperplane Liouville lies below |
| The hyperplane gap can be large | $\le2\log2$, attained; no gauge does better |
| Agreement with $\zeta$ at most primes keeps a margin | The margin reads harmonic correlation over integers |
| A larger gap means more selective | Damping enlarges gaps without selecting (zero weight has every gap) |
| The pole is harmless across scales | Couplings grow like $2^{|j-k|/2}$; only on the hyperplane do they vanish |
| Separated levels can be paid in absolute value | Gamma part yes; Chebyshev part does not decay (Cramér) |
| The dyadic orbit is a one-prime problem | Only for exact atoms; windows read Chebyshev increments near $2^m$ |
| Adding a positively paid level cannot hurt | It leaves the Schur-corrected (lower) coarse block |
| Interval prime counts with $o(1)$ relative error decide the sign | Targeted by dyadic rigidity: perturbed weights share them (Lean application pending) |

## 7. Open problems

1. **Selective transfer at coarse scale**: a full-form lower bound on generator orbits using the hyperplane gap or Chebyshev main-term
   cancellation beyond the spacing scale.
2. **Signed Chebyshev costs between levels**: cancellation between levels making the costs fed to `rgMargin` summable.
3. **Generator-orbit Toeplitz symbols**: positivity of the Gamma symbol + Chebyshev increments near $2^m$ (must use windows: exact towers are
   blind; the two-prime torus is RH).
4. **Margin at intermediate correlation** $S_N/H_N\to\rho\in(0,1)$: is it a function of $\rho$ alone?
5. **A virial identity** giving an upper side for AID's canonical critical energy.
6. **Selective input on the low and cross-level residual** (low details, generator cell, inter-level Chebyshev couplings).
7. **Finish the dyadic rigidity applications**: $\Lambda$ and $\Lambda+\varepsilon n^{-\eta}$, and $\Lambda$ and its Liouville twin, are never both dyadic positive along a
   nonnegative generator (stated in `TheDyadicSignIsRigid`, not yet proved there).

## 8. Tools to build

Signed inter-level Chebyshev estimates; Toeplitz symbol calculus for generator orbits with windows; a quantitative dyadic
sign-rigidity theory beyond one-signed differences.

## 9. Contribution standard

Unconditional; state source, form, domain, growing parameter and error currency; report on the battery (Liouville, $c\Lambda$, quadratic
mixtures, finite flips, golden datum); say twin-blind or selective; keep Gamma and both poles or say which is dropped; record the
positive survivor of every no-go. Success: a selective inequality on a source-complete family (minimal instance: a full-form
inequality on a cofinal family that Liouville violates), or a proof that no inequality of a stated class can be selective there.
