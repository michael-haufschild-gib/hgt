# Tower Coupling (`AdelicKreinGeometry/TowerCoupling`)

Does there exist a sequence of separable prime-tower assemblies `K_S = Σ_{p ∈ S} k_p^{(S)}(v_p(·))` built from the `S`-truncated complete explicit formula (primes in `S`, Gamma, pole, running counterterm) whose archimedean-glued signature `κ(archGlue(K_S, c)) = Σ_{p ∈ S} κ₀(k_p^{(S)}) + 1 + ε_S` (`ε_S ∈ {0, 1}`) converges to `κ(K_ζ) + 1 = κ(weilArchState c)`?

## Answer

**No** for separable `S`-truncated explicit-formula assemblies (under all running counterterms), **no** for pointwise limits of arbitrary separable prime-tower assemblies, and **no** for fixed-`S` global `S`-truncated explicit-formula kernels at unbounded slope height; **yes** once non-separable inter-tower coupling is retained, in two unconditional forms (`coupledAssembly` for `|S| ≥ 2` and window-coupled `sTruncatedCorr` with zero counterterm).

1. **`PolarSecondDifference.lean`** — Discrete summation by parts on `ℤ` identifying the polar index `κ₀(k)` with `κ(Δ²k)` (`polarIndex_eq_negIndex_secondDiff`), the mixing dichotomy `κ₀(k) ∈ {0, ⊤}` when `(Δ²k)(n) → 0` (`polarIndex_eq_zero_or_top_of_secondDiff_tendsto`), and the exact `{0, 1, ⊤}` trichotomy for `pairKernel (valChar r) w + h` (`polarIndex_pairKernel_add_of_secondDiff_tendsto`).
2. **`STruncatedExplicitTowers.lean`** — Finite-`S` summability `Σ_{n ≥ 2} |Λ_S(n)| / √n < ∞` (`summable_vonMangoldtS_div_sqrt`); decay of local `S`-truncated prime and Gamma correlations along every prime tower; full glued signature classification (`negIndex_archGlue_sTruncatedAssembly`) showing primes in `S` and Gamma contribute `0` to every finite local polar index; unconditional divergence `≥ |S| + 1 → ⊤` of pole-retaining cubic-spline assemblies (`tendsto_negIndex_archGlue_poleRetaining_atTop`); the `{1, 2, ⊤}` gap and parity obstruction `κ(weilArchState c) ≠ 2` for pole-free assemblies (`not_tendsto_negIndex_archGlue_poleFree_of_finite_pos`); and unconditional twin-flip blindness under diagonal counterterms (`exists_prime_flip_obstruction`).
3. **`InterTowerCouplingRigidity.lean`** — The `S`-valuation retraction `projS S : posUnits →* posUnits` and exact canonical split `coupledAssembly S K = axialAssembly S K + interTowerCoupling S K` (`coupledAssembly_eq_axial_add_interTower`, `towerAssembly_eq_axialAssembly_of_agrees`, `interTowerCoupling_primeUnit_zpow`); continuous log-additivity rigidity of `coprimeCrossDiff` on `⟨2, 3⟩ × ⟨5, 7⟩` (`negIndex_le_two_of_coprimeCrossDiff_eq_zero`); unconditional nonvanishing of `coprimeCrossDiff ratCorr` and impossibility of pointwise approximation of `ratCorr` by arbitrary `towerAssembly` nets (`exists_coprimeCrossDiff_ratCorr_ne_zero`, `not_tendsto_towerAssembly_ratCorr`); and untruncated axial replication blow-up on `0 < κ(K_ζ) < ⊤` (`tendsto_negIndex_archGlue_axialAssembly_ratCorr_atTop`).
4. **`CoupledTowerSignatureFormula.lean`** — Two unconditional positive signature formulas computing `κ(K_ζ) + 1`:
   - **Coupled `S`-valuation assembly**: for every `S : Finset Nat.Primes` with `2 ≤ S.card` and `c > 0`, `κ(archGlue(coupledAssembly S ratCorr, c)) = κ(K_ζ) + 1 = κ(weilArchState c)` (`negIndex_archGlue_coupledAssembly_ratCorr`), and analogously for every tempered zero configuration and primitive Dirichlet `L(s, χ)` (`negIndex_archGlue_coupledAssembly_orbitCorr`, `negIndex_archGlue_coupledAssembly_dirichlet`).
   - **Window-coupled `S`-truncated explicit formula (zero counterterm)**: while fixed-`S` global `sTruncatedCorr S` satisfies `κ(archGlue(sTruncatedCorr S, c)) ∈ {1, ⊤}` (`negIndex_archGlue_sTruncatedCorr_mem`), on every slope-height window `|log |slope(g_i)|| ≤ L` with `windowPrimes L ⊆ S` (`p ≤ ⌊exp(2L + 8)⌋₊`), `gram (archGlue (sTruncatedCorr S) c) g = gram (weilArchState c) g` entrywise (`gram_archGlue_sTruncatedCorr_eq_weilArchState`), so the window-coupled supremum over `(L, S_L)` equals `κ(K_ζ) + 1` unconditionally (`negIndex_weilArchState_eq_iSup_windowPrimes`).
