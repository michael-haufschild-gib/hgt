# StripBochnerWeilForcing (SBWF)

Namespace `GaloisForLFunctions.StripBochnerWeilForcing`; umbrella `StripBochnerWeilForcing.lean`; 7 modules.
All theorems unconditional. Hardy's theorem (unconditional, RHTower) supplies critical zeros where needed.

## 1. Motivation

Single-zero harmonic certificates on the boundary hit the Fejér barrier (MPG): for pole order κ ≥ 2 they certify no
interior width. SBWF asks what height positivity (positive-definite vertical sections of Re(−F'/F)) forces once it is
coupled across many height channels and combined with a completed functional equation, inside the critical strip.
The answer is a complete, sharp theory for the abstract class of "completed positive systems": a 1/log T collar
with ⌊κ/2⌋ exceptions, a phase transition at κ = 1, and the identification of the κ = 1 class with ℝ^×·ζ.

## 3. Language

- **Pole budget κ**: order of the pole of F at s = 1 (inserted into Λ as (s(s−1))^κ). **Degree d**: number of Γ_ℝ factors.
- **Collar**: the region Re z > 1 − w(T), 0 < Im z ≤ T. **Interior forcing**: some zero pushed to Re ρ ≤ 1 − w.
- **Residue matching**: Poisson/Cauchy mass 2π per zero (ρ and ρ̄) versus κπ from the pole.
- **Gauge**: E = (s(s−1))^κG with G → 1 at +∞ and E(1−s) = εE(s), no archimedean factor.
- **Refund**: cancelling a critical zero pair returns one unit of pole budget.

## 4. Mechanisms

**A. Positivity and Hadamard**
1. Multi-channel Gram positivity of −F'/F at s_{jk} = 1+δ + i(x_j − x_k).
2. Hadamard zero-pair expansion of Λ'/Λ: every term has Re ≥ 0 on Re s > 1; dihedral orbits {ρ, ρ̄, 1−ρ, 1−ρ̄} are
   disjoint for distinct ordinates, or for any injective tuple in the fundamental domain {Re > ½, Im > 0}.
3. Decomposition −F'/F = κ(1/s + 1/(s−1)) + dΓ_ℝ'/Γ_ℝ − Λ'/Λ; all-height digamma bound Re Γ_ℝ'/Γ_ℝ ≤ log(3+|t|)/2 + ¼.

**B. Channel design**
1. Slow-doubling Følner sets in the semigroup generated by the ordinates (no separation, no independence).
2. Star configuration (weight √r at height 0) with separated inverse-square packing.
3. Smoothed additive-cube wavepacket: iterated box convolutions with autocorrelation ≥ 1 − 1/(8M) on windows around
   every quantized ordinate and controlled total mass; arctan bounds for discrete Cauchy sums.
4. Width extraction: B/(δ+η) ≤ A/δ + G for all δ ∈ (0,1] ⇒ η ≥ min(1, (√B − √A)²/G).

**C. Rigidity**
1. Phragmén–Lindelöf on the strip + polynomial growth ⇒ gauges are polynomials.
2. Landau's abscissa theorem: a nonnegative Laplace correction continues along the whole real axis; reflection at
   ¾ ↔ ¼ or at ½ then kills it.
3. Laplace–Bochner: Re 1/(a+ib) = ∫e^{−at}cos(bt)dt; twists with nonnegative Laplace densities preserve PD sections.
4. Complete monotonicity: second differences of PD functions are PD ⇒ even derivatives of −F'/F ≥ 0; √x growth ⇒ odd
   derivatives ≤ 0; Leibniz transfers to F; Bernstein–Widder gives a positive Dirichlet measure.

## 7. Open questions

1. Interior forcing beyond the collar for the class: impossible from height positivity alone (sharpness), so what
   additional structure, compatible with the functional equation, could work?
2. Equality case 2Σm = κ for even κ.
3. Systems with d > 1: which are products of standard L-functions; is there a degree analogue of the κ = 1 collapse?
4. Optimal `sharpCollarConst`; comparison with de la Vallée Poussin and Vinogradov–Korobov widths.
5. Explicit width in `collective_half_pole_interior_forcing` (Følner scale is uncontrolled).

| Trap | Fact |

| More channels remove exceptions | 2Σm ≤ κ is attained by boundary twists of ζ |
| Pole order bounds intrinsic zeros | It counts inserted charge; κ = 1 class is ζ itself |
| A gauge can carry an infinite zero budget | Without Γ_ℝ it is a polynomial of degree 2κ |

| A refunded complex quartet needs κ = 3 | κ = 2 suffices |

## 10. Big picture

SBWF extracts everything height positivity and a functional equation can say about zeros, and the answer is
sharp and modest: a zero-free collar of width ≍ 1/log T with at most ⌊κ/2⌋ exceptions, exactly matching what pole
charge can pay for. At pole order two the axioms already admit off-line zeros; at pole order one they admit only
real multiples of ζ. So the axiomatic class "positive Euler-type function + completed FE" is either too large (κ ≥ 2,
RH false in the class) or exactly ζ (κ = 1, RH is the original problem).

The lesson for the RH programme: no argument that uses only height positivity, pole order and the functional
equation can prove RH, because such an argument would apply to the whole class. A proof must use a property of ζ
that distinguishes it inside its own κ = 1 class — and since that class is ℝ^×·ζ, it must use arithmetic structure
beyond these axioms, not a sharper estimate within them.
