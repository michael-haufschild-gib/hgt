- Paper-proved source-perturbation no-go (Lean open): let `Phi=thetaCuspFullSource`, fix `0<beta<1/2`, and choose a nonnegative, nonzero even `C_c^infty` bump `phi` supported away from zero. For each high frequency `T`, the two even real compactly supported directions `g1,T(t)=phi(t) cos(Tt)` and `g2,T(t)=t phi(t) sin(Tt)` have Fourier--Laplace evaluations at `T+i beta` tending respectively to `hat(phi)(i beta)/2` (positive real) and `-hat(t phi)(i beta)/(2i)` (nonzero pure imaginary), with sign depending on Fourier convention. Thus their real-linear evaluations form an invertible `2x2` matrix for large `T`. Weighted all-derivative integrability of the literal theta source makes `hat(Phi)(T+i beta)` decay faster than every power by integration by parts. Solve two real coefficients to make the perturbed transform zero at `T+i beta`; these coefficients decay faster than every power. The perturbation then tends to zero in every exponentially weighted `C^k` seminorm, is supported in a fixed compact set away from zero, preserves *every local source seam jet exactly*, and preserves global positivity and strict log-concavity for large `T` (the latter are strict in Lean at `ThetaCuspKernelLogConcavity.lean:918,930`, and outside support the source is unchanged). Evenness also preserves the centered functional-equation reflection and Schwarz reality of the transform. For any fixed `m`, choose distinct positive frequency ratios `lambda_j T`; the `2m x 2m` evaluation matrix tends to the same invertible diagonal blocks because cross-frequency Fourier integrals rapidly decay. Hence an arbitrarily small such perturbation plants `m` prescribed physical-strip off-axis zero quartets at arbitrarily high heights. This does not alter the literal theta source and does not prove or refute RH; it falsifies any proposed SQB/Suzuki/source-positivity theorem that selects Xi zeros using only source properties open in this weighted smooth topology, reflection, and local seam jets. The missing producer must use a rigid, perturbation-unstable arithmetic identity or a truly global cofinal bound. Corpus search found the weaker polynomial finite-probe no-go (`FiniteProbeRHNonselection.lean`) and generic positive-source warnings, not this local-at-theta all-smooth-order escape theorem; literature novelty is not certified.

This analytic pivot cannot preserve *exact* agreement on an open tail (identity theorem), nor the exact square-lattice atom expansion. Thus source analyticity, finite seam jets, and all-order leading cusp asymptotics still fail to select Xi; the exact global theta lattice law remains the discriminator.
