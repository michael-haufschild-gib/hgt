# Root CCM-FU facts

- The corpus already proves exact late-prime head updates and an adverse cutoff schedule (`MobiusDivisorLatePrimeBlocks`, `MobiusDivisorFiniteClockCutoffGrowth`). A discrete shell-head blowup alone is therefore not a new frontier result.
- `MobiusDivisorDyadicShellDebtGrowth.lean` proves an unconditional stronger continuous result. For `P_N = primesInIoc (2N) (3N)` and `k_N = #P_N`, the physical mean `mobiusDivisorDilationMean P_N 1` is at least `k_N²/(40,960,000 N)` once `k_N ≥ 480`; the density product `∏_{p∈P_N} p/(p+1)` is at least `1/2`. The corpus prime-window lower bound then makes both the mean and its squarefree-density normalization tend to `+∞`.
- Mechanism: on `x ∈ (7N/2, 15N/4]`, two-prime shell divisors exceed `x`; every surviving one-prime dilation has a uniform positive first-block source value. The processed head is therefore uniformly negative of size `k_N√N`, on an interval of length `N/4`.
- Route implication: no core-uniform fixed-core debt budget can pay CCM-FU across growing dyadic shell cores. This does not refute a coupled exhaustive prime/cutoff schedule; any successful schedule must cancel the shell through inherited smaller-prime terms or another genuinely moving-core source interaction.
- `MobiusDivisorShellAnchorDebt.lean` proves that the same shell debt diverges after adjoining **any fixed finite old-prime core** `Q`: `mobiusDivisorDilationMean (Q ∪ P_N) 1 → +∞`. Exact two-shell-prime exclusion leaves the shell children at arguments below two, where every old prime is inactive. The old head contributes only a fixed `O_Q(√N)` amplitude, versus the shell's `k_N√N` negative mass.
- `MobiusDivisorShellAnchorComplexity.lean` upgrades this to a moving-core cardinality barrier: for every disjoint actual-prime core `Q` and `N≥1`, `480·2^|Q|≤k_N` implies `mobiusDivisorDilationMean (Q∪P_N) 1 ≥ k_N²/(40,960,000 N)`. The source-specific bound `|H_Q(x)|≤(3/5)·2^|Q|√x` is proved by prime-insertion recurrence. Thus any old-core cancellation that keeps this physical debt below the shell lower scale requires `|Q|>log₂(k_N/480)`; this is only a necessary complexity law, not a constructive payment schedule.
- Stronger insight for whole route: neither bounded old anchors nor moving anchors of sublogarithmic cardinality can neutralize dense fresh-shell debt. Remaining viable CCM-FU route needs many old primes with correlated signed transport across scales; scalar late-prime mass or endpoint-only balancing cannot substitute for that covariance. The all-cutoff payment theorem remains open.
- Limitation: `2^|Q|` is a crude absolute-amplitude ceiling. A realistic dense inherited core contains far more than `log₂ k_N` primes, so this obstruction alone cannot exclude or construct its payment. Next hard target is an actual arithmetic bound/sign law for `H_{Q_N}` and its cross-covariance when `Q_N` moves with `N`, not another cardinality estimate.
