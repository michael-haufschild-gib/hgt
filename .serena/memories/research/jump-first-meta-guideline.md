# Jump-first research rule

For theory-of-everything/Nature research, separate abduction from validation.

1. Jump first: propose ontology-changing physical equivalences, thought experiments, or axioms without pruning for literature proximity, current corpus fit, or easy formalization.
2. State layperson-level worldview flip and conjecture before choosing machinery.
3. Falsify second: collide with primary literature, no-go theorems, data, low-energy limits, and competing theories; retain strongest positive survivor.
4. Keep `[CONJECTURE]`, `[LITERATURE]`, `[MATHEMATICS]`, and empirical-evidence labels exact. Lean formalizes surviving mathematical claims only; it does not validate physical identifications.
5. Preserve time-ordered provenance separating human target constraints from AI-originated physical content.

## Sharpened conjecture rule

A conjecture is: “Based on everything presently known, I have a hunch that statement S is true, although I cannot yet say how it could be proved.” Provability is not part of conjecture formation; it comes afterward.

Operational consequences:
1. Freeze the declarative physical statement before looking for a proof route.
2. Do not use Lean tractability, available machinery, literature proximity, or likely publishability to generate or prune conjectures.
3. A statement reverse-engineered from a known theorem or visible proof route is a derived theorem candidate or bridge, not an abductive jump.
4. After freezing the conjecture, separately seek exact definitions, consequences, falsifiers, paper proofs, and unconditional Lean formalization.
5. Failure to find a proof does not by itself count against the conjecture; conflict with evidence, incoherence, or failed predictions does.
