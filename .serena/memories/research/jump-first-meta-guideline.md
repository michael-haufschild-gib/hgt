# Jump-first research rule

A conjecture is: “Based on everything presently known, I have a hunch that statement S could be true, although I could not presently tell how I would prove it.” Provability is not part of the conjecture. It begins afterward.

Operational consequences:
1. Absorb observations, established theory, failed routes, and primary literature.
2. Ask what physical statement seems true.
3. Freeze the statement before targeted proof or realization search.
4. Do not use Lean tractability, corpus fit, literature proximity, or publishability to generate, rank, weaken, strengthen, prune, or reject it.
5. A proof-shaped statement is a `[BRIDGE]`, not the primary conjecture.
6. After freeze, separately seek definitions, falsifiers, novelty, proof, prediction, and unconditional Lean.
7. Failure to prove is not evidence against a conjecture.
8. Preserve time-ordered provenance.
