# Independent validation of Dirichlet inverse paths

All 10 repetitions of five selected paths completed at 100/160 digits. The first-root attachments, fifth-root predecessor maps and conductor-5 small-loop exchange retained their discovery endpoints under finer steps.

A separately implemented Euler–Maclaurin formula for Hurwitz zeta, assembled with each character, checked nine path locations using two truncations. The largest independent target residual was 1.075e-88 at 100 digits and 1.15e-147 at 160 digits. These are residual checks, not certified whole-path error bounds.

The comparison therefore supplies reproducible evidence that the selected predecessor pattern occurs beyond zeta. That pattern alone cannot serve as a zeta-specific RH selector. The remaining scientific question is which additional source constraints force critical-line confinement; no answer or physical HB identification is supplied here.
