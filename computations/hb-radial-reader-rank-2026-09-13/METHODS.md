# Mechanical application of every conjecture-attack operation

| # | Operation | Application | Execution or rejection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Split | Separate source branches, reader sides, determinant, rank and null-vector validation. | Four complete entries and determinant are computed together. |
| 2 | Attack as false | Search for a rank-one matrix with no individual channel zero. | Contour/root scan; independently validate any witness. |
| 3 | Reverse | Nonzero individual entries do not imply injectivity; ask whether an extra reader sees the lost direction. | Third-reader test at each validated rank-one candidate. |
| 4 | Change viewpoint | Replace channel-by-channel survival by source-subspace observability. | Determinant and normalized null vector. |
| 5 | Make visual | Render determinant zero locations and source-to-reader arrows for a found witness. | Figure only if a validated candidate exists. |
| 6 | Action | Two linear measurements compress two source amplitudes. | Test whether compression loses a direction. |
| 7 | Strip essentials | Keep exact radial source and first endpoint subtraction; do not posit unavailable global action. | Explicit scope and omitted physical blocks. |
| 8 | Similar objects | Rank/kernel loss in recent Hermitian-form representation theory motivates quotient audit. | No theorem transfer; inspect actual2x2matrix. |
| 9 | Small cases | rho=1/2 real makes source columns equal. | Cheap implementation check; excluded from discovery region. |
| 10 | Extreme cases | Test low and high tiles, boundary approaches, and known individual-channel zeros. | Frozen finite domain and comparisons; no infinity claim. |
| 11 | Experiment | Find and challenge determinant zeros. | Two meshes, five seeds, up to four independently validated candidates. |
| 12 | Invariant | Rank is invariant under nonsingular reader/source basis changes. | Cheap transformed-matrix determinant check. |
| 13 | Monotone | No ordering exists for this complex determinant; reject magnitude-as-monotonicity inference. | Do not scan bigger norms as a success target. |
| 14 | Weaker bridge | Two-reader finite injectivity is weaker than full causal identification. | Label component results; do not change primary RH goal. |
| 15 | New hunch | A lost source direction may be seen by a reader outside the original span. | Frozen third reader, not one chosen after seeing the null vector. |
| 16 | Minimal counterexample | Choose first validated tile/root in deterministic height order. | Limit validation to four, record non-exhaustiveness. |
| 17 | Representation | Hypergeometric period versus endpoint-integrated convergent series. | Independent190-digit source calculation. |
| 18 | Theorem search | Ask corpus for matrix injectivity and common-reader survival. | Cached deep/method search; partial/source-only coverage retained. |
| 19 | Fake proof | All four entries survive, therefore every source survives: determinant is the missing step. | Test exactly that step. |
| 20 | Missing lemma | Injectivity of actual two-reader source map, with global-rate attachment still separate. | A numerical counterexample would forbid using strip-wide injectivity. |
| 21 | Dependency chain | Full identification would need rate-compatible source assembly, readers, boundary terms and injectivity only if used. | Rank calculation supplies at most one component; no hidden premise discharge. |
| 22 | Nearby false statement | Individual channel nonvanishing already fails at known controls. | Compare their matrix determinants as a cheap control, not rediscovery. |
| 23 | Boundary classification | Distinguish rank0,rank1,normalization pole, and real coincident-source degeneracy. | All entries, row norms, derivative and independent values retained. |
| 24 | Positive residue | If rank1, test a third reader; if no hit, retain only finite numerical non-detection. | Do not retire HB or promote a whole-strip theorem. |

Conjecture formation: no universal-law claim is made; this is a specifically identified bridge attack. Universal-shadow and conjecture-distance gates reject relabeling a finite determinant as a universal principle. No promotion to a new primary conjecture merely because it is computable. The stroke-of-genius move is to replace scalar survival with the rank of the source-to-reader map.
