NODE constraint: white-noise covariance can be positive only as a generalized object,
not as an ordinary trace-class/function-valued covariance.

Split subclaims for reciprocal seam moments:
  A. T_0 is finite.
  B. T_k for k>=1 are finite.
  C. shifted Hankels [T_{i+j+1}] can be ordinary finite PSD matrices.
  D. unshifted Hankels [T_{i+j}] with i,j=0 are ordinary finite PSD matrices.

Finding:
  The unshifted Hankel tower [T_{i+j}] in the proof route is not an ordinary
  finite Stieltjes moment matrix unless T0 is replaced, regularized, or the
  domain is restricted to test polynomials vanishing at 0. The natural ordinary
  finite tower is the shifted one [T_{i+j+1}], corresponding to the finite
  measure dnu=y dmu.
