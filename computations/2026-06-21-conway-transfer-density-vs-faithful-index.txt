SCALAR DENSITY VS FAITHFUL INDEX

all-positive reference
  eigs=[1. 1. 1. 1.]
  measured: sum_abs=4.000, frob=2.000, max_abs=1.000, trace=4.000
  faithful inertia (neg,zero,pos)=(0, 0, 4)

one signed defect, same absolute spectrum
  eigs=[-1.  1.  1.  1.]
  measured: sum_abs=4.000, frob=2.000, max_abs=1.000, trace=2.000
  faithful inertia (neg,zero,pos)=(1, 0, 3)

two signed defects, same absolute spectrum
  eigs=[-1. -1.  1.  1.]
  measured: sum_abs=4.000, frob=2.000, max_abs=1.000, trace=0.000
  faithful inertia (neg,zero,pos)=(2, 0, 2)

same absolute eigenvalue density and Frobenius norm do not determine the negative index.

reverse check: same faithful index can have different scalar densities
