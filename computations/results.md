# Computational facts (short, for agents)

- AFE for q^{s/2}Γ_ℝ(s)^d L(s): the weight g(w)=exp(a w² − i(dπ/4)w) removes the Gamma decay; double precision gives 1e-13 accuracy with N≈4(t/2π)^{d/2} terms (a=0.02).
- FE-null-space numerics: d=1 and d=2 have no visible freedom (Hamburger, Kaczorowski–Perelli confirmed); d≥3 freedom switches on above an onset height (≈45 for d=3). Window-local count (40-unit windows, threshold 0.01·top): W₃ dimension = ζ's zero count ϑ/π in the window (22/22.0, 23/23.2, 24/24.2 for windows above 180). L399's density is exact asymptotically; the earlier 'half density' was an onset-lag artifact of cumulative counts.
- Cumulative visible dim W₃ on [0,T]: 0,4,10,19,32 at T=40,80,120,160,220 (lags because of the onset; the local density converges to ϑ'/π).
- ζ³ has no Gram-law twins: the LP is infeasible at T=30,50,80 even with unbounded coefficients.
- ζ³+W (coefficients ≤1000·d₃) keeps only ζ's own sign changes up to T=120: ≈2/3 of zeros stay off the line. SCOPE: only directions visible at ≥1e-6 relative level; fine-structure twins (tiny E with Z_E(γ_j)=0 and the right sign of Z_E′ at each low zero, splitting triple zeros into tight real triplets) are not excluded. Certified pinning forces any twin into exactly this regime at low n.
- Weil form on B-spline span over [−L/2,L/2] with all weights λ(n), n≤e^L, free: positivity pins λ(2) to log 2 within 1e-2 (L=1.0), 1e-4 (L=1.2), 1.6e-7 (L=1.3); λ(n) is pinned once L ≳ log n + 0.3, super-exponentially. Adding half-integer support points widens the ranges only ~10×.
- The min eigenvalue of the Weil form at Λ on support L falls super-exponentially: 6e-3 (L=0.7), 8e-6 (1.0), 1e-8 (1.2), 4e-10 (1.3), then reaches the spline-basis floor ~1e-11 by L=2 (hk=0.05).
- Unconstrained LPs over near-null directions exploit rounding noise; certify directions by weight-independence and bound coefficients.
- Prime-blind positivity thresholds (no pole, free integer weights): even Γ_ℝ(s) → q*=4.03628, odd Γ_ℝ(s+1) → q*=2.86055 (stable for L≥2.5); smallest real conductors are 5 and 3, so fake producers exist on [4.036,5) and [2.861,3).
- Thresholds multiply over Γ_ℝ factors; pole factors are critical (contribute 1): Γ_ℂ+pole → 2.8605, Γ_ℝ²+pole → 4.0363, Γ_ℂ no pole → 11.546, Γ_ℝ² no pole → 16.29, ζ² datum → 1.
- ζ datum (Γ_ℝ+pole, q=1) is exactly critical: κ*_L → 0, and the positivity-maximizing integer weights reproduce Λ(2..7) to 4 decimals.
- The extremal prime-blind certificate is universal (the same for even and odd data); ∫g* = log 2 and its spectrum vanishes at 2πj/log 2. Discrete Turán: max ∫g over PD g with g(0)=1, g(log n)=0 ∀n≥2 equals log 2 (Poisson on log2·ℤ).
- Real Dirichlet L-functions (χ₅, χ₈, χ₋₃, χ₋₄) and quadratic Dedekind zetas have min eig(Q at own weights) → 0⁺ (no slack), but their data admit other weights with slack (W fat), unlike ζ.
- Positivity pins the whole ζ datum at q=1: residue w within [−3.6e-10, +3.0e-6] of 1 and archimedean scale within ±2e-8 at L=1.6 (all integer weights free); only the conductor direction has slack.
- Critical producer of the even no-pole datum at q*=4.036276 converges: λ_c(2..11) = −1.3505, 0.9306, −2.3715, 1.3614, −0.3468, 1.5397, −3.829, 0.977, −0.64, 1.906 (not an Euler product). - Pinning (W ⊆ {Λ}) implies no real-rooted twins: a twin's producer Λ_F/3 must equal Λ. Numerically any twin has |Λ_F(2)/3 − log 2| ≤ 1e-7.
- Threshold constants are basis-independent: at L=2.5, q*(even)=4.03627 and q*(odd)=2.86055 for B-spline knot spacing hk = 0.05, 0.035, 0.025, 0.018.

- The prime-free threshold q*(datum) has a strict, kinked local minimum exactly 1 at ζ's datum along Gamma shift μ (slopes of κ*: +0.38 / −0.048), pole location τ, residue w, and scale α; the critical producer passes smoothly through Λ (λ(6) crosses 0 at μ=0).

- Visible W₃ coefficient vectors are arithmetically generic: no multiplicativity, Ω-distribution equal to the base rate, no residue bias, no correlation with d₃ or μ. They carry no mass at n≤30 at heights ≤160.
- Low-coefficient cost law: unit-norm approximate W₃ elements (FE defect ≤1e-6) carry at most ~1e-12..1e-18 mass at n≤5, ~1e-6 at n≤10, and 0.06..0.4 at n≤20 (W₄ similar, slightly softer). - Twin counting in degree 3 is exactly critical: local dim W₃ = ζ's zero count, while perturbative twins need one condition per ζ-zero. Counting neither excludes nor allows twins (degree 2, also critical, is rigid).
- Evaluation of W₃ at ζ's zeros is not special: the smallest singular value on the window plateau (3e-4..5e-4) is in the range of Gram points, midpoints and shifted zeros (3.5e-4..2e-3). No near-kernel and no repulsion: W₃ does not see individual zeros.
- Degree-2 abundance threshold (Γ_ℝ(s)², conductor N, entire part): no visible FE freedom for N ≤ 4.05, onset at 4.1 (window 100–140), sv 0.21 at N=4.2, freedom growing with N (19 directions at N=32). Slack producers from degree-2 families exist for every c > ½ log 2.

- Exact-null tests (ĝ = Ξ²·ĥ, ĥ ≥ 0; Q_Λ(g) = 0 unconditionally) pin producers only from one side: LP certificates give λ(n) ≤ Λ(n)+ε, but the deficit side is blind (stuck at the a-priori tail bound).
