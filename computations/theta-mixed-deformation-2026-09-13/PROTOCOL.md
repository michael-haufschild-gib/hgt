# Frozen family D1: constrained theta source interactions

Question: does the moment-compensated interaction of two independently constrained theta-source deformations change the smallest located zero-collision threshold or its responsible zero pair materially, compared with the additive model? Generic failure of symmetry or finitely many moments is already known and is NOT the discovery target.

Source: the corpus thetaCuspFullSource mu(x), with Xi(t)=integral_R mu(x) exp(i t x) dx. For positive x, mu=sum_n exp(x/2)*2pi*(2pi*(n exp x)^4-3*(n exp x)^2)*exp(-pi*(n exp x)^2); use its established even extension. Numerical transform evaluation uses Xi(t)=xi(1/2+i t), xi(s)=s(s-1)Lambda(s)/2 away from poles. Source normalization was read directly in ThetaCuspFinitePrimeSource and PrimeArchThetaFirstMoment.

Define bounded even trigonometric functions h1 using frequencies (2,4,6), h2 using (3,5,7). In each, set the highest-frequency coefficient to 1, solve the two linear constraints integral mu*h=integral x^2*mu*h=0, then normalize the sum of absolute cosine coefficients to 1. Define h12=h1*h2-c0-c4*cos(4x), choosing c0,c4 to enforce the same two moment constraints. Coefficients are computed at 70 digits; constraint residuals and conditioning are retained. These are intended exact definitions with finite-precision realizations, not formalized new theorems.

Compare sources mu*[1+a*h1+b*h2] and mu*[1+a*h1+b*h2+a*b*h12]. Both retain evenness and the two chosen moment constraints in their mathematical definitions. The source is strictly positive wherever |a|+|b|+|a*b|*sum|coeff(h12)|<1 (triangle-bound sufficient region); do not assert positivity outside it. No other modular/automorphic relation or full HB source equation is preserved by assumption.

Multiple experiments: four signed coordinate-axis interventions; four diagonal directions (a,b)=lambda*(+/-1,+/-1)/sqrt(2), each with interaction absent/present; each of the first 64 cached zero gaps as a collision search basin. Axes need no duplicate interaction calculation because ab=0 is a known identity. Search F=F_t=0 with independent source interaction switch. Record all failures and escaping iterates; the smallest found collision is not a certified global first collision or an exhaustive zero count.

Risky discriminant: an interaction-induced change of responsible gap, or threshold ratio outside [1/2,2], counts as a candidate needing validation. A ratio within that band does not prove additive control. Freeze these thresholds before the scan. Also retain candidates outside the positive-source sufficient region but label them; they cannot establish failure within that region.

Prediction attempt: product-to-sum identities, moment preservation, evenness, and a collision's local square-root splitting are predictable checks. Gamma asymptotics suggest shifted transforms can be sensitive, but do not identify the first located collision, its pair, or compensation's effect on the threshold. The corpus already excludes generic moment-only proof routes; this experiment tests the missing interaction quantitatively rather than rediscovering that exclusion.

Method: change arena to deformation-parameter space; promote nonlinear compensation to observable; compare nearby constructions; attack individual-effect explanations; seek a positive survivor after failures. Broad recent memristor search found Lipan et al., arXiv:2605.27342v1, on discriminant surfaces separating topological regimes. Only the discriminant viewpoint is transferred: F=F_t=0 is derived directly here, no device theorem assumed.
