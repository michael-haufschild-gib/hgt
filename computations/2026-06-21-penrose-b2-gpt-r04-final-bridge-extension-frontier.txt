Finding:
  A finite positive slab can have genuine positive margins and still give no certificate
  for the first unseen Stieltjes extension. The final bridge theorem therefore needs an
  extension-frontier passport: at each scale, the completed weld must place the next
  reciprocal moment inside the projectively compatible PSD cone with controlled slack.
