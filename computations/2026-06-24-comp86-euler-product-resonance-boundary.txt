=== DIAGNOSIS (Wheeler, post-run) ===
UNRELIABLE RESULTS. Three bugs:
1. Truncation bug: euler_product_jet truncates at 10 Dirichlet terms. For s=1.5, 2^{-10·1.5}=2^{-15}≈3e-5
   — NOT negligible vs the ODE relation scale. The 2^{-s} sanity test (should show near-null at deg 1)
   fails because the truncated sum doesn't satisfy the exact ODE.
2. Underdetermination bug: degree ≤ 3 gives 20×35 matrix — underdetermined (20 < 35), so near-null
   signals are EXPECTED ARTIFACTS regardless of algebraic structure.
3. Threshold too loose: "near-null" threshold 10^{-70} applied at 80 dps (machine eps ~10^{-80}).
   Ratios 10^{-91} and 10^{-171} are below machine epsilon — pure numerical noise, NOT real relations.
Conclusion: ALL "near-null" signals in this run are false positives. Needs comp86b with:
- Exact Euler product via mpmath.nprod or closed-form expression
- ≥ 20 sample points for degree ≤ 2, ≥ 38 for degree ≤ 3
- Threshold relative to machine epsilon (use < 10^{-75} at 80 dps)
