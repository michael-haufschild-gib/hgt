Quartet: w = +/- a +/- i gamma, y=-1/w^2
T_1 = -4*(a - gamma)*(a + gamma)/(a**2 + gamma**2)**2
T_2 = 4*(a**2 - 2*a*gamma - gamma**2)*(a**2 + 2*a*gamma - gamma**2)/((a - I*gamma)**4*(a + I*gamma)**4)
T_3 = -4*(a - gamma)*(a + gamma)*(a**2 - 4*a*gamma + gamma**2)*(a**2 + 4*a*gamma + gamma**2)/((a - I*gamma)**6*(a + I*gamma)**6)
T_4 = 4*(a**4 - 4*a**3*gamma - 6*a**2*gamma**2 + 4*a*gamma**3 + gamma**4)*(a**4 + 4*a**3*gamma - 6*a**2*gamma**2 - 4*a*gamma**3 + gamma**4)/((a - I*gamma)**8*(a + I*gamma)**8)

det H_0(1) = -64*a**2*gamma**2/(a**2 + gamma**2)**6
det H_1(1) = -64*a**2*gamma**2/(a**2 + gamma**2)**8

For a>0 and gamma>0 both determinants are strictly negative.
At a=0 the quartet collapses to on-line duplicated atoms and the minors are PSD-rank-one.
