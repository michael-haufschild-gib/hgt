ATTEMPT: compute the arithmetic Jensen polynomials J^{d,n} of xi, their hyperbolicity
margins, and a Hermite-polynomial comparison (the §83.4 "Hermite engine"), as a concrete
necessary-condition test beyond GORZ's eventual-hyperbolicity asymptotic.

OUTCOME: the referee reported the Jensen polynomials as NON-hyperbolic (max|Im(root)| ~ 1-10).
This CONTRADICTS the established result (Griffin-Ono-Rolen-Zagier 2019; and the field's own
correct computation `computations/jensen_hyperbolicity_margin`, which found all J^{d,n}
hyperbolic for d=2..6, n=0..4). Therefore the referee is WRONG, not the known hyperbolicity.

ROOT CAUSE: the referee built the wrong Pólya-Jensen sequence. It used the raw Taylor
moments of Xi(t)=xi(1/2+it) (with/without sign and (2n)! factors); the correct Pólya-Jensen
sequence for xi is the Li/secular coefficient sequence (a Laguerre-Pólya moment sequence,
expanded at s=1, not the s=1/2 Xi-Taylor moments). The naive Xi-Taylor moments are not a
Laguerre-Pólya sequence under this normalization, so their Jensen polynomials are not hyperbolic.

STATUS: discarded. The correct arithmetic Jensen margins are in `jensen_hyperbolicity_margin`
(verified). The concrete Hermite-root comparison (§83.4 engine) is DEFERRED pending a correct
Li/secular-sequence implementation. No claim is drawn from this referee.
