Step-12 follow-up: boundary cases
  integer-slope wrapping makes observed covariance vanish while true covariance is large;
  irrational-ish slopes avoid exact lock-in but still show quotient distortion.

Finding:
  Observed-volume covariance needs a wrapping/lift passport. If the observed variable
  is periodic or quotient-valued, covariance on the wrapped representative can be a
  trajectory-order artifact. A monotone unwrapped lift or winding count must accompany
  any claim that the covariance is a genuine measure invariant.
